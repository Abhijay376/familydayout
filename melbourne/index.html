Things to do with kids on the weekend

Weekends are busy at the big attractions, so mix one paid outing with free family activities nearby: a playground after the museum, a trail walk before lunch. Our day planner builds a day around your kids' ages, the weather and how far you want to drive, including split days when toddlers and teens want different things.

Most councils run free playgrounds, splash parks, skate parks and libraries with free story time. Every suburb page has a free list, for example free things to do near Werribee, St Kilda, Windsor, Prahran, Balaclava, South Yarra.
