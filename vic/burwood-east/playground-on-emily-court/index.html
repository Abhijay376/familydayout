Playground on Emily Court

Playground in Burwood East · Basic info

Playground on Emily Court in Burwood East is a public playground in the Whitehorse City area. The Whitehorse EW6 passes about 100 m away, and Burwood Teppanyaki House is about 370 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 550 m away, and bus 735 stops at Blackburn English Language School/Bonview Cres, a 4-minute walk away.
