Blackburn Velodrome

Skate, BMX or pump track in Burwood East · Basic info

Blackburn Velodrome is a skate, bmx or pump track in Burwood East, in the Whitehorse City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Mahoneys Rd/Burwood Hwy (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 99 m away.
