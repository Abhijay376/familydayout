Playground at Donaldson Reserve

Playground in Burwood East · Basic info

This is a public playground in Burwood East. The Whitehorse EW6 is about 110 m away, and for food, Gotcha Fresh Tea is about 430 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 500 m from the playground, and bus 735 stops at Davis St/Royton St, a 6-minute walk away.
