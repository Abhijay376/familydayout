East Ringwood Reserve Playground

Playground in Ringwood East · Basic info

East Ringwood Reserve Playground in Ringwood East is a public playground in the Maroondah City area. The Mount Dandenong Road Connection is about 20 m away, and for food, Yang's Place is about 460 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 190 m from the playground, and bus 670 stops at Braeside Ave/Maroondah Hwy, a 8-minute walk away.
