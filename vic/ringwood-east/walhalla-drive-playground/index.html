Walhalla Drive Playground

Playground in Ringwood East · Basic info

This is a public playground in Ringwood East. The Eastwood Walking Route passes about 200 m away, and Hender Street Playground is another option about 340 m away. There's a public toilet about 530 m away. It's easiest by car, about 4 minutes from Ringwood East.
