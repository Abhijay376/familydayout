Playground at Freeman Street Reserve

Playground in Ringwood East · Basic info

This is a public playground in Ringwood East. The Ringwood to Croydon Rail Trail passes about 120 m away, and Little Hub Pizza & Pasta is about 250 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 790 m away, and trains stop at Ringwood East Railway Station, a 8-minute walk away.
