Wombolano Park

Park in Ringwood East · Basic info

Wombolano Park is a park in Ringwood East, in the Maroondah City Council area, good for all ages. Bedford Rd/Canterbury Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 75 m away.
