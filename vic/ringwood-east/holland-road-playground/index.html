Holland Road Playground

Playground in Ringwood East · Basic info

Holland Road Playground in Ringwood East is a public playground in the Maroondah City area. The Mount Dandenong Road Connection is about 370 m away, and Aurora Art is about 300 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 650 m from the playground, and bus 670 stops at Seares Dr/Maroondah Hwy, a 4-minute walk away.
