Smithdene Avenue Reserve Playground

Playground in Ringwood East · Basic info

Smithdene Avenue Reserve Playground in Ringwood East is a public playground in the Maroondah City area. The Eastwood Walking Route passes about 140 m away, and Shane Crescent Reserve Playground is another option about 490 m away. There's a public toilet about 560 m away, and bus 669 stops at Smithdene Ave/Railway Ave, a 2-minute walk away.
