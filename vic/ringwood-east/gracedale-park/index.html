Gracedale Park

Playground in Ringwood East · Basic info

Gracedale Park is a public playground in Ringwood East, in the Maroondah City area. The Bayswater-Lilydale Trail passes about 110 m away, and Walhalla Drive Playground is another option about 470 m away. There's a public toilet about 80 m away, and buses 664 and 967 stop at Berry Rd/Bayswater Rd, a 9-minute walk away.
