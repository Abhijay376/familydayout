Rosanna Primary School Reserve

Park in Rosanna · Basic info

Rosanna Primary School Reserve is a park in Rosanna, in the Banyule City Council area, good for all ages. Dougharty Rd/Waiora Rd (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 821 m away.
