Playground at Rosanna Parklands

Playground in Rosanna · Basic info

Playground at Rosanna Parklands in Rosanna is a public playground in the Banyule City area. The LBN1 - Watsonia to East Ivanhoe passes about 80 m away, and Macleod Village Fish & Chips is about 490 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 560 m away, and bus 514 stops at Stanton Cres/Thomson Dr, a 1-minute walk away.
