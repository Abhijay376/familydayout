Station Road Reserve

Park in Rosanna · Basic info

Station Road Reserve is a park in Rosanna, in the Banyule City Council area, good for all ages. Grove Rd/Station Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 315 m away.
