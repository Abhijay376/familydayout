Rosanna is in the Banyule area, and our data lists 9 playgrounds and 9 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Rosanna has a library.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Rosanna is Rosanna Parklands, covering about 20 hectares. De Winton Park and Rosanna Primary School Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Rosanna include Haward Walkway, Maleela Reserve and Station Road Reserve. Playgrounds in Rosanna include Daniel Park, the playground at De Winton Park and the playground at Haward Walkway. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Rosanna. In Rosanna you'll also find Rosanna Library. For walks and rides, LBN1 - Watsonia to East Ivanhoe, Banyule LBN2 and Banyule LBN6 cross Rosanna. Our data lists 11 cafes and family restaurants in Rosanna for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Rosanna Railway Station (metro: Hurstbridge Line) in Rosanna. Buses 513, 514, 517, 548 and 551 stop in Rosanna. By car from Rosanna, Box Hill is about 19 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 23 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 21 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Olympic Leisure in Heidelberg West is about 5 minutes' drive from Rosanna. The closest beach to Rosanna is Horse Arena in Abbotsford, about 19 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the bmx track at Malahang Reserve in Heidelberg West is about 4 minutes' drive from Rosanna. The closest cinema to Rosanna is Hoyts in Preston, about 6 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Northland Timezone in Preston is about 6 minutes' drive from Rosanna. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bundoora Park Farm in Bundoora is about 11 minutes' drive from Rosanna.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 9 playgrounds across Rosanna. Rainy days at the library in Rosanna. Free days on the LBN1 - Watsonia to East Ivanhoe and in Rosanna's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Rosanna, Viewbank has 8 playgrounds. Next door to Rosanna, Macleod has 8 playgrounds. Yallambie, next to Rosanna, has 6 playgrounds. Heidelberg Heights, next to Rosanna, has 3 playgrounds. Heidelberg also borders Rosanna.
