Monegeetta is in the Macedon Ranges area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Gallaghers Ford Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Monegeetta, at about 8 hectares. Monegeetta Streamside Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Monegeetta has no train station; Clarkefield Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 7.8 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Lancefield - Sunbury/Clarkefield via Romsey & Monegeeta and Lancefield - Gisborne via Romsey, Monegeeta & Riddells Creek routes stop in Monegeetta. By car from Monegeetta, Broadmeadows is about 41 minutes away, Kyneton about 41 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 56 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Monegeetta is Gisborne Aquatic Centre in Gisborne, about 18 minutes' drive. For a library, Romsey Library in Romsey is about 7 minutes' drive from Monegeetta. The closest skate park to Monegeetta is Romsey Skate Park in Romsey, about 7 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Reading Cinema in Sunbury is about 24 minutes' drive from Monegeetta. For an indoor play centre, Timezone in Craigieburn is about 30 minutes' drive from Monegeetta. Broadmeadows is the closest bigger centre to Monegeetta, about 41 minutes away by car, and has 12 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Monegeetta are Romsey Skate Park in Romsey (7 min), Romsey Library in Romsey (7 min), Low Ropes Course in Riddells Creek (14 min), the playground at Ross Watt Reserve in New Gisborne (17 min), Gisborne Library in Gisborne (18 min) and Old Gisborne Court House in Gisborne (18 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Monegeetta.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Monegeetta, Romsey has 2 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Monegeetta, Riddells Creek has 4 playgrounds. Bolinda also borders Monegeetta.
