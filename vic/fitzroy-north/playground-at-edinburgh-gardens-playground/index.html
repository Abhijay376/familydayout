Playground at Edinburgh Gardens

Playground in Fitzroy North · Basic info

Playground at Edinburgh Gardens in Fitzroy North is a public playground in the Yarra City area. The Fairfield to CBD passes about 60 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 60 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 120 m away, and tram 11 stops at Alfred Cres/St Georges Rd #21, a 2-minute walk away.
