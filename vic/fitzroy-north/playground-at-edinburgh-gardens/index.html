Playground at Edinburgh Gardens

Playground in Fitzroy North · Basic info

Playground at Edinburgh Gardens is a public playground in Fitzroy North, in the Yarra City area. The Inner Circle Rail Trail passes about 130 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 280 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 90 m away, and bus 546 stops at Grant St/Queens Pde, a 3-minute walk away.
