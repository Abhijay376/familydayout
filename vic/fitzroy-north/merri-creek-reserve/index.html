Merri Creek Reserve

Park in Fitzroy North · Basic info

Merri Creek Reserve is a park in Fitzroy North, in the Merri-Bek City Council area, good for all ages. Ida St/Miller St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 874 m away.
