Playground on Boston Place

Playground in Fitzroy North · Basic info

Playground on Boston Place is a public playground in Fitzroy North, in the Yarra City area. The Canning Street passes about 310 m away, and Bargoonga Nganjin North Fitzroy Library is about 500 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 420 m away, and tram 11 stops at Fitzroy Bowls Club/Brunswick St #20, a 3-minute walk away.
