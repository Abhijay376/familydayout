Wootong Vale in the Southern Grampians area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Giant Rock Scenic Reserve is the largest mapped park in Wootong Vale, at about 6 hectares. Our data shows 1 off-leash dog park in Wootong Vale.

Getting around

Wootong Vale has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 116 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wootong Vale, so most trips start by car. By car from Wootong Vale, Hamilton is about 36 minutes away and Portland about 83 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Merino Swimming Pool in Merino is about 38 minutes' drive from Wootong Vale. The closest library to Wootong Vale is Hamilton Library in Hamilton, about 37 minutes' drive. For a skate park, coleraine skate park in Coleraine is about 9 minutes' drive from Wootong Vale. For a cinema, Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton is about 37 minutes' drive from Wootong Vale. Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Wootong Vale, about 36 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Wootong Vale are coleraine skate park in Coleraine (9 min), the playground at Queens Park in Coleraine (9 min), Silvester Oval Playground in Coleraine (9 min), Merino Swimming Pool in Merino (38 min), Gorman's art gallery in Casterton (28 min) and Casterton Pool in Casterton (28 min).

Best for
