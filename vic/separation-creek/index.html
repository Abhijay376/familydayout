Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Separation Creek in the Colac Otway area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it. Separation Creek does have a beach.

Landmarks

Our data shows 2 public toilets in Separation Creek. In Separation Creek you'll also find Godfrey Creek. For walks and rides, Staircase & Allambee Beek Falls track crosses Separation Creek.

Getting around

Separation Creek has no train station; Birregurra Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 35 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Separation Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Separation Creek, Torquay is about 68 minutes away, Colac about 70 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Separation Creek is Lorne Sea Baths in Lorne, about 17 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Kay Langan Skate Park in Lorne is about 17 minutes' drive from Separation Creek. For a cinema, Lorne Theatre in Lorne is about 17 minutes' drive from Separation Creek. For a zoo or wildlife park, Live Wire Park in Lorne is about 19 minutes' drive from Separation Creek. The closest places for kids by road from Separation Creek are Grey River in Grey River (11 min), the playground at Lorne - Queenscliff Coastal Reserve in Lorne (17 min), Kay Langan Skate Park in Lorne (17 min), Lorne Theatre in Lorne (17 min), Lorne Sea Baths in Lorne (17 min) and the playground at Lorne - Queenscliff Coastal Reserve in Lorne (19 min).

Best for

Beach days at Godfrey Creek. Longer rides and walks from Separation Creek on the Staircase & Allambee Beek Falls track.
