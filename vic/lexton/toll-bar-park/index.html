Toll Bar Park

Park in Lexton · Basic info

Toll Bar Park is a park in Lexton, in the Pyrenees Shire Council area, good for all ages. Williamson St/Sunraysia Hwy (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 11 m away with a baby change table.
