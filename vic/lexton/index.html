Lexton in the Pyrenees area has 2 playgrounds and 5 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Lexton Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Lexton, at about 257 hectares. Lexton H5 Bushland Reserve and Lexton H6 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Lexton also has Toll Bar Park and Burbank Creek Streamside Reserve. Playgrounds in Lexton include the playground at Toll Bar Park. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Lexton.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lexton. The closest to Lexton is Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line), about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Ballarat - Maryborough route stop in Lexton. By car from Lexton, Maryborough is about 34 minutes away, Ballarat about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lexton is Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot, about 19 minutes' drive. For a library, Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort is about 28 minutes' drive from Lexton. For a skate park, the skate park at Alexander Park in Learmonth is about 20 minutes' drive from Lexton. For a cinema, Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central is about 39 minutes' drive from Lexton. The closest indoor play centre to Lexton is Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point, about 42 minutes' drive. Maryborough is the closest bigger centre to Lexton, about 34 minutes away by car, and has a library. The closest places for kids by road from Lexton are Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground in Talbot (19 min), Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (19 min), Courthouse in Avoca (21 min), the skate park at Alexander Park in Learmonth (20 min), the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (28 min) and Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort (28 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lexton.
