Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Old Tallangatta in the Towong area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

The High Country Rail Trail, Tallangatta - Firebrace Loop and Tallangatta - Lake Road Loop all pass through Old Tallangatta.

Getting around

There's no train station in Old Tallangatta. The closest to Old Tallangatta is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 32 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Old Tallangatta, so most trips start by car. By car from Old Tallangatta, Wodonga is about 42 minutes away and Wangaratta about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Old Tallangatta is Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest library to Old Tallangatta is Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta, about 8 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Skate Park in Baranduda is about 34 minutes' drive from Old Tallangatta. The closest cinema to Old Tallangatta is Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana, about 37 minutes' drive. Wodonga is the closest bigger centre to Old Tallangatta, about 42 minutes away by car, and has 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Old Tallangatta are Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta (8 min), Tallangatta Art & Photography in Tallangatta (9 min), Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta (9 min), Granya Museum in Granya (21 min), the playground at Banksia Park in Tangambalanga (21 min) and the playground at Ludlows Reserve in Ebden (26 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Old Tallangatta on the High Country Rail Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Old Tallangatta, Tallangatta has 1 playground, a pool and a library.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Old Tallangatta, without traffic.
