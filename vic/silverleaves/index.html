Landmarks

Phillip Island Nature Park is the largest mapped park in Silverleaves, at about 2,007 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Silverleaves. The closest to Silverleaves is Stony Point Railway Station (metro), about 9.7 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Silverleaves, so most trips start by car. By car from Silverleaves, Wonthaggi is about 43 minutes away, Leongatha about 72 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Silverleaves is Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Wonthaggi, about 41 minutes' drive. For a library, Phillip Island Library in Cowes is about 6 minutes' drive from Silverleaves. The closest beach to Silverleaves is Silverleaves in Cowes, about 6 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Wonthaggi Railway Historic Reserve in Kilcunda is about 30 minutes' drive from Silverleaves. For a cinema, Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi is about 41 minutes' drive from Silverleaves. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Silverleaves is Phillip Island Wildlife Park in Cowes, about 8 minutes' drive. Wonthaggi is the closest bigger centre to Silverleaves, about 43 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Silverleaves, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Shearwater Reserve in Cowes (4 min), Pyramid Rock Gallery in Cowes (5 min), Silverleaves in Cowes (6 min), Phillip Island Library in Cowes (6 min), the playground at Erewhon Point Reserve in Cowes (6 min) and Mingara Gallery in Cowes (6 min).
