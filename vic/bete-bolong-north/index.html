Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bete Bolong North is Wood Point Flora Reserve, covering about 47 hectares. Bete Bolong North also has Long Point, Sandy Point and Stringers Knob Historic Fire Tower. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Bete Bolong North. Picnic areas in Bete Bolong North include Sandy Point and Stringers Knob Historic Fire Tower. For camping, there's Long Point.

Getting around

Bete Bolong North has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 64 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bete Bolong North, so most trips start by car. By car from Bete Bolong North, Lakes Entrance is about 83 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bete Bolong North is Buchan: Natural Spring in Buchan, about 39 minutes' drive. From Bete Bolong North, the nearest places for kids by road are Buchan: Natural Spring in Buchan (39 min), the playground at Forest Park in Orbost (48 min), Orbost Library in Orbost (49 min), Ewings Marsh in Waygara (60 min) and Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost (49 min).
