Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Yangoura, in the Wellington area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Yangoura is Glenmaggie State Forest, covering about 15,014 hectares. Twenty Acre Creek Reference Area is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Yangoura include Store Point. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Yangoura. For camping, there's Store Point. The Crinoline Walking Track, Morning Star Walk and Store Point Historic Loop all pass through Yangoura.

Getting around

Yangoura has no train station; Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 46 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yangoura, so most trips start by car. By car from Yangoura, Traralgon is about 2 hours away and Sale about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest places for kids by road from Yangoura are Fire station in Walhalla (58 min), the playground at Ned Stringer's Park in Walhalla (59 min), Apex Park Playground in Heyfield (85 min), Heyfield and District Museum in Heyfield (86 min), Pump Track in Heyfield (86 min) and Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield (86 min).

Best for

Free days on the Crinoline Walking Track and in Yangoura's parks.
