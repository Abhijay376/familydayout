Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Coojar in the Southern Grampians area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Coojar. The closest to Coojar is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 111 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Coojar, so most trips start by car. By car from Coojar, Hamilton is about 52 minutes away and Horsham about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Coojar is Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Coojar is coleraine skate park in Coleraine, about 24 minutes' drive. Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Coojar, about 52 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Coojar are Harrow Discovery Centre in Harrow (18 min), Bake House Studio in Harrow (18 min), Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral (22 min), Glendinning Street Playground in Balmoral (23 min), coleraine skate park in Coleraine (24 min) and the playground at Queens Park in Coleraine (24 min).
