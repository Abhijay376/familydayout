Pier Milan is in the Buloke area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Pier Milan is Mittyack Prange Road Bushland Reserve, covering about 279 hectares. Pier Millan Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Pier Milan has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 81 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Pier Milan, so most trips start by car. By car from Pier Milan, Swan Hill is about 78 minutes away and Mildura about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake is about 31 minutes' drive from Pier Milan. From Pier Milan, the nearest places for kids by road are Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake (31 min), the playground at Apex Park in Sea Lake (31 min), Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang (36 min) and the playground at Tempy Recreation Reserve in Tempy (40 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Pier Milan.

Nearby suburbs

Tempy, next to Pier Milan, has 1 playground and a pool. Gerahmin and Bimbourie also border Pier Milan.
