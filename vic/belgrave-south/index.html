Our data lists 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in Belgrave South, in the Yarra Ranges area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Belgrave South is Baluk Willam Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 67 hectares. Kerr's Park and Belgrave South Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Belgrave South. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Belgrave South for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Belgrave South has no train station; Belgrave Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 2.7 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 695, 697 and 695F stop in Belgrave South. By car from Belgrave South, Dandenong is about 20 minutes away, Cranbourne about 24 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 40 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Belgrave Heated Outdoor Swimming Pool in Belgrave is about 5 minutes' drive from Belgrave South. For a library, Belgrave Library in Belgrave is about 6 minutes' drive from Belgrave South. For a beach, Edithvale Beach in Edithvale is about 33 minutes' drive from Belgrave South. For a skate park, Upwey Skate Park in Upwey is about 9 minutes' drive from Belgrave South. The closest cinema to Belgrave South is Cameo Belgrave in Belgrave, about 6 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Little Rascals Play Centre in Ferntree Gully is about 12 minutes' drive from Belgrave South. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale is about 29 minutes' drive from Belgrave South. The nearest larger centre is Dandenong, about 20 minutes from Belgrave South by car, with 21 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Belgrave South.

Nearby suburbs

Belgrave, next to Belgrave South, has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Emerald, next to Belgrave South, has 6 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Belgrave South, Lysterfield has 8 playgrounds. Next door to Belgrave South, Belgrave Heights has 2 playgrounds. Selby, Menzies Creek and Narre Warren East also border Belgrave South.
