Taylor Bay is in the Murrindindi area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Taylor Bay is Eildon Water Reserve, covering about 1,246 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Taylor Bay. The closest to Taylor Bay is Euroa Railway Station (V/Line), about 58 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Taylor Bay, so most trips start by car. By car from Taylor Bay, Mansfield is about 71 minutes away, Healesville about 74 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Eildon Swimming Pool in Eildon is about 7 minutes' drive from Taylor Bay. The closest library to Taylor Bay is Alexandra Library in Alexandra, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Taylor Bay are Eildon Swimming Pool in Eildon (7 min), the playground at W.J Moore Park in Eildon (7 min), Baanh ba Djila Splash Park in Eildon (7 min), Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra (17 min), Alexandra Timber Tramway and Museum in Alexandra (18 min) and Alexandra Library in Alexandra (17 min).
