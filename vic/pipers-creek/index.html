Pipers Creek is in the Macedon Ranges area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Pipers Creek, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Pipers Creek has no train station; Kyneton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 7.9 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Pipers Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Pipers Creek, Kyneton is about 14 minutes away, Castlemaine about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 76 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Gisborne Aquatic Centre in Gisborne is about 32 minutes' drive from Pipers Creek. The closest library to Pipers Creek is Kyneton Library in Kyneton, about 14 minutes' drive. For a beach, Canoeing in Tylden is about 25 minutes' drive from Pipers Creek. The closest skate park to Pipers Creek is Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton, about 15 minutes' drive. Kyneton is the closest bigger centre to Pipers Creek, about 14 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Pipers Creek are Jean Haynes Playground in Kyneton (13 min), Kyneton Museum in Kyneton (14 min), Kyneton Library in Kyneton (14 min), Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton (15 min), Kyneton Community Park in Kyneton (15 min) and Canoeing in Tylden (25 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Pipers Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Pipers Creek, Kyneton has 10 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Pipers Creek, Newham has 1 playground. Carlsruhe also borders Pipers Creek.
