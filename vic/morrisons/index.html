Morrisons is in the Moorabool area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Lal Lal State Forest is the largest mapped park in Morrisons, at about 869 hectares. Morrisons Recreation Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Morrisons. The closest to Morrisons is Ballan Railway Station (V/Line), about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Morrisons, so most trips start by car. By car from Morrisons, Ballarat is about 51 minutes away, Geelong about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Ballan Swimming Pool in Ballan is about 41 minutes' drive from Morrisons. For a library, Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn is about 35 minutes' drive from Morrisons. The closest beach to Morrisons is Disability beach in Steiglitz, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Morrisons is Meredith Skate Park in Meredith, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Morrisons is Fairy Park in Anakie, about 36 minutes' drive. Ballarat is the closest bigger centre to Morrisons, about 51 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Morrisons are Meredith Skate Park in Meredith (15 min), Meredith Pioneer Park Reserve Playground in Meredith (15 min), the playground at Mount Wallace Hall in Mount Wallace (22 min), Disability beach in Steiglitz (31 min), Fairy Park in Anakie (36 min) and the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan (40 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Morrisons.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Morrisons, Mount Wallace has 1 playground. Meredith, next to Morrisons, has 1 playground and a skate park. Next door to Morrisons, Elaine has 1 playground. Next door to Morrisons, Beremboke has 1 playground. Durdidwarrah and Bungal also border Morrisons.
