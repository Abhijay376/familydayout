Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Iraak, in the Mildura area. Beyond the parks, Iraak has a beach.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Iraak is Karadoc Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 110 hectares. Carwarp West I224 Bushland Reserve and Carwarp 2 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Places to visit in Iraak include Rudd's Road Sandbar.

Getting around

There's no train station in Iraak. The closest to Iraak is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 150 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Iraak, so most trips start by car. By car from Iraak, Mildura is about 41 minutes away and Swan Hill about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Iraak is Irymple Swimming Pool in Irymple, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest library to Iraak is A S Kenyon Library Red Cliffs in Red Cliffs, about 26 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Skatepark in Red Cliffs is about 27 minutes' drive from Iraak. The closest cinema to Iraak is Deakin Cinema Complex in Mildura, about 42 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Sunraysia Tenpin in Mildura is about 41 minutes' drive from Iraak. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Iraak is Putt Putt Family Fun Centre in Mildura, about 42 minutes' drive. Mildura is the closest bigger centre to Iraak, about 41 minutes away by car, and has 26 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Iraak are Ovens Park Playground in Red Cliffs (28 min), the playground at Barclay Square in Red Cliffs (26 min), Red Cliffs Military Museum in Red Cliffs (26 min), A S Kenyon Library Red Cliffs in Red Cliffs (26 min), Work Space Gallery 3496 in Red Cliffs (26 min) and Skatepark in Red Cliffs (27 min).

Best for

Beach days at Rudd's Road Sandbar.
