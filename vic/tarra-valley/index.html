Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Tarra Valley in the Wellington area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Tarra Valley. For walks and rides, Diaper Track crosses Tarra Valley.

Getting around

Tarra Valley has no train station; Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tarra Valley, so most trips start by car. By car from Tarra Valley, Traralgon is about 49 minutes away, Sale about 79 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Yarram Public Pool in Yarram is about 24 minutes' drive from Tarra Valley. For a library, Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram is about 24 minutes' drive from Tarra Valley. The closest skate park to Tarra Valley is the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram, about 24 minutes' drive. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Tarra Valley, about 49 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Tarra Valley are Yarram Historical Society Museum in Yarram (23 min), the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram (24 min), Yarram Public Pool in Yarram (24 min), Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram (24 min), the playground at Memorial Park in Yarram (24 min) and Port Albert Maritime Museum in Port Albert (36 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Tarra Valley on the Diaper Track.
