Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Yarroweyah in the Moira area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Yarroweyah. The closest to Yarroweyah is Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line), about 53 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Yarroweyah. By car from Yarroweyah, Shepparton is about 53 minutes away and Benalla about 67 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah is about 26 minutes' drive from Yarroweyah. For a library, Barooga Library in Cobram is about 12 minutes' drive from Yarroweyah. For a beach, Ski Beach in Cobram is about 11 minutes' drive from Yarroweyah. The closest skate park to Yarroweyah is Strathmerton Community Skate Park in Strathmerton, about 7 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Cobram Cinema in Cobram is about 8 minutes' drive from Yarroweyah. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Yarroweyah is Tunzafun in Yarrawonga, about 39 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 53 minutes from Yarroweyah by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Yarroweyah, the nearest places for kids by road are Strathmerton Community Skate Park in Strathmerton (7 min), the playground at Federation Park in Cobram (8 min), the skate park at Federation Park in Cobram (8 min), Cobram Cinema in Cobram (8 min), Ski Beach in Cobram (11 min) and Thompsons Beach in Cobram (11 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Yarroweyah.

Nearby suburbs

Cobram, next to Yarroweyah, has 4 playgrounds, a library and a beach. Next door to Yarroweyah, Koonoomoo has 1 playground, a library and a beach. Next door to Yarroweyah, Strathmerton has a skate park. Katunga also borders Yarroweyah.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Yarroweyah, without traffic.
