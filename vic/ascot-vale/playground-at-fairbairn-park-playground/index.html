Playground at Fairbairn Park

Playground in Ascot Vale · Official details

Playground at Fairbairn Park in Ascot Vale is a public playground in the Moonee Valley City area. The Maribyrnong River Trail passes about 120 m away, and middle child is about 330 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 50 m away, and bus 468 stops at Maribyrnong Park/Maribyrnong Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
