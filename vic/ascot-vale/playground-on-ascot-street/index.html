Playground on Ascot Street

Playground in Ascot Vale · Basic info

Playground on Ascot Street is a public playground in Ascot Vale, in the Moonee Valley City area. Older kids have a skate park about 250 m away, and Move is about 560 m away. There's a public toilet about 670 m away, and tram 57 stops at Sandown Rd/Epsom Rd #32, a 3-minute walk away.
