Playground at Walter Street Reserve

Playground in Ascot Vale · Official details

Playground at Walter Street Reserve is a public playground in Ascot Vale, in the Moonee Valley City area. Playground at Fairbairn Park is another option about 350 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 450 m from the playground, and bus 472 stops at All Saints Anglican Church/Charles St, a 7-minute walk away.
