Victory Park Playground

Playground in Ascot Vale · Official details

Victory Park Playground is a public playground in Ascot Vale, in the Moonee Valley City area. Move is about 100 m away, and for food, Charcoal Chicken is about 410 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 80 m from the playground, and bus 472 stops at Epsom Rd/Charles St, a 4-minute walk away.
