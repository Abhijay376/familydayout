Playground on Churchill Avenue

Playground in Ascot Vale · Basic info

This is a public playground in Ascot Vale. Older kids have a skate park about 60 m away, and for food, Corner Fish & Chip Shop is about 320 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 760 m from the playground, and bus 472 stops at Rothwell St/Francis St, a 5-minute walk away.
