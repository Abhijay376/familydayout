Playground on Fernhill Street

Playground in Ascot Vale · Basic info

Playground on Fernhill Street is a public playground in Ascot Vale, in the Moonee Valley City area. The Myrnong Crescent Popup Bike Lane Route is about 80 m away, and for food, Pizza Depot is about 370 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.1 km from the playground, and bus 959 stops at 28-Middle St/Mt Alexander Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
