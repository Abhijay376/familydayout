Our data lists 6 parks and reserves in Sheep Hills, in the Yarriambiack area.

Landmarks

Nullan I106 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Sheep Hills, at about 29 hectares. Kellalac Bushland Reserve and Nullan I108 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Sheep Hills also has Nullan I104 Bushland Reserve, Nullan I105 Bushland Reserve and Nullan I107 Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Sheep Hills.

Getting around

There's no train station in Sheep Hills. The closest to Sheep Hills is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 110 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Sheep Hills. By car from Sheep Hills, Horsham is about 52 minutes away and Stawell about 83 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Sheep Hills is Minyip Swimming Pool in Minyip, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Sheep Hills is the skate park at Rabl Park in Murtoa, about 35 minutes' drive. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to Sheep Hills, about 52 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Sheep Hills are the playground at Minyip Wetlands in Minyip (15 min), Lions Playground in Minyip (15 min), Minyip Swimming Pool in Minyip (16 min), Wheatlands Warracknabeal Agricultural Machinery Museum in Warracknabeal (13 min), Historical Centre in Warracknabeal (15 min) and Rupanyup Swimming Pool in Rupanyup (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Sheep Hills.

Nearby suburbs

Minyip, next to Sheep Hills, has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Warracknabeal, next to Sheep Hills, has 5 playgrounds. Boolite, Bangerang and Areegra also border Sheep Hills.
