Our data lists 5 playgrounds and 2 parks and reserves in Redan, in the Ballarat area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Redan is Morshead Park, covering about 35 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Redan include Latrobe Street Reserve. Playgrounds around Redan include the playground at Morshead Park, the playground at Trekardo Park and the playground at Latrobe Street Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Redan. For walks and rides, Yarrowee River Trail and Ballarat Circuit: The Great Dividing Trail Network cross Redan. Our data lists 3 cafes and family restaurants in Redan for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Redan has no train station; Ballarat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 2.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 22, 24 and 25 stop in Redan. By car from Redan, Ballarat is about 3 minutes away, Daylesford about 43 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Redan is Ballarat Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Lake Gardens, about 6 minutes' drive. For a library, Sebastopol Library in Sebastopol is about 4 minutes' drive from Redan. For a skate park, the skate park at Len T Frazer Reserve in Ballarat East is about 5 minutes' drive from Redan. The closest cinema to Redan is Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central, about 4 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Redan is Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Redan is Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East, about 9 minutes' drive.

Best for

Younger kids at Redan's 5 local playgrounds. Free days on the Yarrowee River Trail and in Redan's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Sebastopol, next to Redan, has 14 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Ballarat Central, next to Redan, has 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. Delacombe, next to Redan, has 8 playgrounds. Next door to Redan, Mount Pleasant has 5 playgrounds. Newington also borders Redan.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Redan, without traffic.
