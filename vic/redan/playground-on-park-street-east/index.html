Playground at Pleasant Street Reserve

Playground in Redan · Basic info

Playground at Pleasant Street Reserve is a public playground in Redan, in the Ballarat City area. The Victrack passes about 240 m away, and Caa Waa Restaurant is about 170 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 1.3 km away, and bus 24 stops at Pleasant St/La Trobe St, a 5-minute walk away.
