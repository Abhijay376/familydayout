Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Seaview, in the Baw Baw area.

Landmarks

Mount Worth State Park is the largest mapped park in Seaview, at about 1,032 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Seaview. The Moonlight Divide Track, Moonlight Creek Track and Valley Views Trail all pass through Seaview.

Getting around

Seaview has no train station; Warragul Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Seaview, so most trips start by car. By car from Seaview, Warragul is about 24 minutes away, Leongatha about 47 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul is about 22 minutes' drive from Seaview. For a library, Warragul Library in Warragul is about 23 minutes' drive from Seaview. For a skate park, Warragul Skate Park in Warragul is about 22 minutes' drive from Seaview. The closest cinema to Seaview is Warragul Cinema in Warragul, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Seaview is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 22 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Wildlife Trail in Tynong North is about 44 minutes' drive from Seaview. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Seaview, about 24 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Seaview are Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul (22 min), Warragul Skate Park in Warragul (22 min), the playground at Burke Street Park in Warragul (22 min), Warragul Leisure Centre in Warragul (22 min), Burke Street Pump Track in Warragul (22 min) and Warragul Cinema in Warragul (22 min).

Best for

Free days on the Moonlight Divide Track and in Seaview's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Ferndale, Ellinbank, Cloverlea and Allambee also border Seaview.
