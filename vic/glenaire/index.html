Glenaire in the Colac Otway area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Glenaire is Aire River Heritage River, covering about 1,080 hectares. Aire River Wildlife Reserve (hunting) and Calder River Reference Area are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Glenaire include Greater Otway NP (addition). Our data shows 2 public toilets in Glenaire. The Great Ocean Walk passes through Glenaire.

Getting around

Glenaire has no train station; Colac Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 50 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glenaire, so most trips start by car. By car from Glenaire, Colac is about 67 minutes away and Warrnambool about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Glenaire is Apollo Bay Community Pool in Apollo Bay, about 33 minutes' drive. For a beach, Station Beach in Hordern Vale is about 13 minutes' drive from Glenaire. The closest cinema to Glenaire is Mechanics Hall in Apollo Bay, about 30 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Wildlife Wonders in Apollo Bay is about 25 minutes' drive from Glenaire. From Glenaire, the nearest places for kids by road are Station Beach in Hordern Vale (13 min), Glenaire East in Hordern Vale (13 min), Wildlife Wonders in Apollo Bay (25 min), Mechanics Hall in Apollo Bay (30 min), the playground at Foreshore Reserve in Apollo Bay (31 min) and Skate Bowl in Apollo Bay (31 min).

Best for

Free days on the Great Ocean Walk and in Glenaire's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Hordern Vale, next to Glenaire, has a beach. Next door to Glenaire, Cape Otway has 1 playground. Next door to Glenaire, Beech Forest has 1 playground. Johanna and Aire Valley also border Glenaire.
