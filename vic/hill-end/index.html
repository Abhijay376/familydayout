Hill End is in the Baw Baw area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Hill End, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Hill End. The closest to Hill End is Trafalgar Railway Station (V/Line), about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Hill End, so most trips start by car. By car from Hill End, Warragul is about 38 minutes away, Traralgon about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar is about 21 minutes' drive from Hill End. For a library, Morwell Library in Morwell is about 35 minutes' drive from Hill End. For a skate park, Moe Skate Park in Moe is about 22 minutes' drive from Hill End. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 37 minutes' drive from Hill End. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 37 minutes' drive from Hill End. The nearest larger centre is Warragul, about 38 minutes from Hill End by car, with 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Hill End are the playground at Blue Rock Lake West picnic area in Willow Grove (7 min), the playground at Ferguson Street Reserve in Moe (21 min), Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar (21 min), Moe Skate Park in Moe (22 min), Diving Pool in Moe (23 min) and Old Brown Coal Mine Museum in Yallourn North (27 min).
