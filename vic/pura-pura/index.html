Our data lists 4 parks and reserves in Pura Pura, in the Moyne area.

Landmarks

Lake Terrinallum Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Pura Pura, at about 189 hectares. Terrinallum N9 Lake Reserve and Terrinallum N10 Lake Reserve are the next largest. Pura Pura also has Pura Pura Recreation Reserve.

Getting around

Pura Pura has no train station; Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 46 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Pura Pura, so most trips start by car. By car from Pura Pura, Ararat is about 64 minutes away, Ballarat about 73 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum is about 32 minutes' drive from Pura Pura. The closest skate park to Pura Pura is Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake, about 35 minutes' drive. From Pura Pura, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Simpson Park in Streatham (19 min), Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum (32 min), Market Square Playground in Mortlake (34 min), Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake (34 min) and Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Pura Pura.

Nearby suburbs

Darlington and Carranballac also border Pura Pura.
