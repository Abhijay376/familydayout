Karkarook Park (water authority land)

Park in Heatherton · Basic info

Karkarook Park (water authority land) is a park in Heatherton, in the Kingston City Council area, good for all ages. South Rd/Warrigal Rd (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 301 m away.
