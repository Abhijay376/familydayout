Karkarook Park

Park in Heatherton · Official details

Karkarook Park is a park in Heatherton, in the Kingston City Council area, good for all ages. Karkarook Park/Warrigal Rd (bus) is a 8-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 316 m away and dogs are allowed on a lead.

Questions parents ask

Where is Karkarook Park?
Karkarook Park is in Heatherton, Victoria.
Is Karkarook Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Karkarook Park?
Yes, on a lead.
Are there toilets at Karkarook Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 316 m away.
How do I get to Karkarook Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Karkarook Park/Warrigal Rd (routes 903), a 8-minute walk.
