Barnawartha is in the Indigo area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Barnawartha is Barnawartha Scenic Reserve, covering about 131 hectares. Fryingpan Creek Streamside Reserve and Indigo Creek Park are the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Barnawartha.

Getting around

There's no train station in Barnawartha. The closest to Barnawartha is Chiltern Railway Station (V/Line), about 8.2 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Albury - Corowa via Rutherglen route stop in Barnawartha. By car from Barnawartha, Wodonga is about 20 minutes away and Wangaratta about 39 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Barnawartha is Howlong Swimming Pool in Gooramadda, about 19 minutes' drive. For a library, Howlong Library and Resource Centre in Gooramadda is about 18 minutes' drive from Barnawartha. For a beach, Finns Beach in Bandiana is about 23 minutes' drive from Barnawartha. For a skate park, the bmx track at Lowe Square in Gooramadda is about 19 minutes' drive from Barnawartha. The closest cinema to Barnawartha is Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana, about 22 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 20 minutes from Barnawartha by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Barnawartha, the nearest places for kids by road are Chiltern Athenaeum Museum in Chiltern (8 min), Martin Park Playground in Chiltern (8 min), Howlong Library and Resource Centre in Gooramadda (18 min), Howlong Swimming Pool in Gooramadda (19 min), the bmx track at Lowe Square in Gooramadda (19 min) and Lowe Square Playground in Gooramadda (19 min).
