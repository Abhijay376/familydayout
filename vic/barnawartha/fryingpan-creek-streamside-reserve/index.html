Fryingpan Creek Streamside Reserve

Park in Barnawartha · Basic info

Fryingpan Creek Streamside Reserve is a park in Barnawartha, in the Indigo Shire Council area, good for all ages. Havelock St/High St (bus) is a 15-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1363 m away.
