Pompapiel is in the Loddon area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Pompapiel, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Pompapiel. The closest to Pompapiel is Dingee Railway Station (V/Line), about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Pompapiel, so most trips start by car. By car from Pompapiel, Bendigo is about 51 minutes away, Echuca about 70 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Pompapiel is Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo, about 27 minutes' drive. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Pompapiel, about 51 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Pompapiel are the playground at R J Phelan Reserve in Mitiamo (27 min), Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo (27 min), East Loddon Historical Society in Mitiamo (28 min), Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood (34 min) and Pyramid Hill & Districts Historical Museum in Pyramid Hill (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Pompapiel.

Nearby suburbs

Calivil, Bears Lagoon and Auchmore also border Pompapiel.
