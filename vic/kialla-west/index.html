Getting around

There's no train station in Kialla West. The closest to Kialla West is Mooroopna Railway Station (V/Line), about 7.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kialla West, so most trips start by car. By car from Kialla West, Shepparton is about 12 minutes away, Seymour about 52 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

The closest places for kids by road from Kialla West are Emerald Bank Leisure Land in Kialla (4 min), Museum of Vehicle Evolution - MOVE in Kialla (4 min), Riviera Park Estate Playground in Kialla (5 min), Shepparton Art Museum in Shepparton (9 min), the skate park at Skate Park in Shepparton (10 min) and Aquamoves in Shepparton (11 min).
