Cambarville is in the Yarra Ranges area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Cambarville is Walsh Creek Reference Area, covering about 915 hectares. Mount Gregory Reference Area is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Cambarville include Snowy Camp 1, 2 & 3. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Cambarville. For camping, there's Snowy Camp 1, 2 & 3. For walks and rides, Cascades Track and Cumberland Walk cross Cambarville.

Getting around

Cambarville has no train station; Longwarry Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 62 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cambarville, so most trips start by car. By car from Cambarville, Healesville is about 50 minutes away, Lilydale about 72 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cambarville is Marysville Outdoor Swimming Pool in Marysville, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Cambarville is the skate park at Gallipolli Park in Marysville, about 19 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Healesville, about 50 minutes from Cambarville by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Cambarville, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Gallipolli Park in Marysville (18 min), Flying fox and slide in Marysville (18 min), Replica of the original police station in Marysville (18 min), Marysville Outdoor Swimming Pool in Marysville (18 min), the skate park at Gallipolli Park in Marysville (19 min) and Bruno's Art and Sculpture Garden in Marysville (19 min).

Best for

Free days on the Cascades Track and in Cambarville's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Eildon, next to Cambarville, has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Cambarville, Marysville has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. McMahons Creek, Matlock and Jericho also border Cambarville.
