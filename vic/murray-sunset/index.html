Murray-sunset in the Mildura area has 19 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Murray-sunset is Murray-Sunset National Park, covering about 665,735 hectares. Sunset Wilderness Zone and Minook Wilderness Zone are the next largest. Murray-sunset also has Danyo Reference Area, Purnya Reference Area and Sunset Reference Area. Our data shows 9 public toilets in Murray-sunset. For walks and rides, Murray-Sunset Remote Track, Kline Loop Nature Walk and Lake Hardy Loop Nature Walk cross Murray-sunset.

Getting around

There's no train station in Murray-sunset. The closest to Murray-sunset is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 183 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Murray-sunset, so most trips start by car. By car from Murray-sunset, Mildura is about 2.5 hours away and Swan Hill about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Murray-sunset is Underbool Swimming Pool in Underbool, about 33 minutes' drive. From Murray-sunset, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Outen Park in Underbool (32 min), Underbool Swimming Pool in Underbool (33 min), Walpeup Library in Walpeup (47 min) and the playground at Walpeup Wayside Stop in Walpeup (47 min).

Best for

Free days on the Murray-Sunset Remote Track and in Murray-sunset's parks.
