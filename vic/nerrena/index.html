Nerrena is in the South Gippsland area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Nerrena, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Nerrena has no train station; Yarragon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 34 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nerrena, so most trips start by car. By car from Nerrena, Leongatha is about 12 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 42 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha is about 11 minutes' drive from Nerrena. For a library, Leongatha Library in Leongatha is about 11 minutes' drive from Nerrena. For a beach, The Caves in Inverloch is about 38 minutes' drive from Nerrena. The closest skate park to Nerrena is BMX track in Leongatha, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Nerrena is Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha, about 11 minutes' drive. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Nerrena, about 12 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Nerrena are BMX track in Leongatha (10 min), Leongatha Library in Leongatha (11 min), Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha (11 min), South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha (11 min), Leongatha Skate Park in Leongatha (11 min) and Splash Pools and Stadium in Leongatha (12 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Nerrena.

Nearby suburbs

Leongatha, next to Nerrena, has 4 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Koorooman, Koonwarra and Dumbalk also border Nerrena.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Nerrena, without traffic.
