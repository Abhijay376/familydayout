Charles McCarthy Memorial Reserve

Park in St Leonards · Basic info

Charles McCarthy Memorial Reserve is a park in St Leonards, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. McBeth St/Murradoc Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 591 m away.
