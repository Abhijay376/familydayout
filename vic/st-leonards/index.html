St Leonards in the Greater Geelong area has 9 playgrounds and 4 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, St Leonards has a beach.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in St Leonards is Swan Bay - Edwards Point Wildlife Reserve, covering about 281 hectares. Salt Lagoon, St Leonards Wildlife Reserve and Charles McCarthy Memorial Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in St Leonards include Henty Reserve. Playgrounds around St Leonards include Leviens Reserve, Harvey Park North and Lake View Reserve. Our data shows 5 public toilets in St Leonards. In St Leonards you'll also find Red Bluff. The Foreshore passes through St Leonards. Our data lists 3 cafes and family restaurants in St Leonards for a lunch stop.

Getting around

St Leonards has no train station; Werribee Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 30 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 60 stops in St Leonards. By car from St Leonards, Geelong is about 33 minutes away, Torquay about 53 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Northern Bellarine Aquatic Centre in Curlewis is about 17 minutes' drive from St Leonards. The closest library to St Leonards is Drysdale Library in Drysdale, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to St Leonards is Portarlington Skate Park in Portarlington, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to St Leonards is Village Cinemas in Geelong, about 33 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Laserquest in Geelong is about 34 minutes' drive from St Leonards. For a zoo or wildlife park, Adventure Park in Wallington is about 23 minutes' drive from St Leonards.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 9 playgrounds across St Leonards. Beach days at Red Bluff. Free days on The Foreshore and in St Leonards's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to St Leonards, Portarlington has 10 playgrounds, a beach and a skate park. Next door to St Leonards, Indented Head has 6 playgrounds and a beach. Next door to St Leonards, Drysdale has 5 playgrounds and a library. Mannerim also borders St Leonards.
