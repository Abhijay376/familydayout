Leviens Reserve

Playground in St Leonards · Basic info

Leviens Reserve is a public playground in St Leonards, in the Greater Geelong City area. The Foreshore is about 330 m away, and St Leonards Lake Reserve is another option about 450 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 510 m from the playground, and bus 60 stops at Cole St/Leviens Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
