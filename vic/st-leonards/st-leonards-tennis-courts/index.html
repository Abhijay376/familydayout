St Leonards Tennis Courts

Playground in St Leonards · Basic info

St Leonards Tennis Courts is a public playground in St Leonards, in the Greater Geelong City area. Leviens Reserve is another option about 460 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 130 m from the playground, and bus 60 stops at Ward St/Cole St, a 3-minute walk away.
