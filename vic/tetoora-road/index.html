Tetoora Road is in the Baw Baw area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Tetoora Road, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Tetoora Road has no train station; Warragul Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tetoora Road, so most trips start by car. By car from Tetoora Road, Warragul is about 18 minutes away, Leongatha about 42 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul is about 16 minutes' drive from Tetoora Road. For a library, Warragul Library in Warragul is about 17 minutes' drive from Tetoora Road. For a skate park, Warragul Skate Park in Warragul is about 16 minutes' drive from Tetoora Road. The closest cinema to Tetoora Road is Warragul Cinema in Warragul, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Tetoora Road is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Tetoora Road is Wildlife Trail in Tynong North, about 38 minutes' drive. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Tetoora Road, about 18 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Tetoora Road, the nearest places for kids by road are Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul (16 min), Warragul Skate Park in Warragul (16 min), the playground at Burke Street Park in Warragul (16 min), Warragul Leisure Centre in Warragul (16 min), Burke Street Pump Track in Warragul (16 min) and Warragul Cinema in Warragul (16 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tetoora Road.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Tetoora Road, Hallora has 1 playground. Ellinbank also borders Tetoora Road.
