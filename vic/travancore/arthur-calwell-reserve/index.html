Arthur Calwell Reserve

Park in Travancore · Official details

Arthur Calwell Reserve is a park in Travancore, in the Moonee Valley City Council area, good for all ages. 26-Wellington St/Mt Alexander Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1013 m away with a baby change table.
