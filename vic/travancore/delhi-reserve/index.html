Delhi Reserve

Park in Travancore · Basic info

Delhi Reserve is a park in Travancore, in the Moonee Valley City Council area, good for all ages. 24-Mooltan St/Mt Alexander Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 480 m away with a baby change table.
