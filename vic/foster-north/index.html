Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Foster North in the South Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Foster North has no train station; Trafalgar Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 45 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Foster North, so most trips start by car. By car from Foster North, Leongatha is about 27 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 47 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Foster North is Foster Outdoor Pool in Foster, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest library to Foster North is Foster Library in Foster, about 6 minutes' drive. For a beach, Toora Beach in Toora is about 23 minutes' drive from Foster North. The closest skate park to Foster North is the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Foster North is Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha, about 27 minutes' drive. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Foster North, about 27 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Foster North are Luminous Glass Studio in Foster (6 min), Stockyard Gallery in Foster (6 min), Foster Library in Foster (6 min), Foster Outdoor Pool in Foster (7 min), the playground at Stanley Street Playground in Toora (15 min) and Toora Pool in Toora (14 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Foster North.

Nearby suburbs

Foster, next to Foster North, has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Foster North, Fish Creek has 1 playground. Buffalo, next to Foster North, has 1 playground. Turtons Creek, Stony Creek and Dollar also border Foster North.
