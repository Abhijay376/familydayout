Skate park at M R Power Recreation Reserve

Skate, BMX or pump track in Sebastopol · Basic info

Skate park at M R Power Recreation Reserve is a skate, bmx or pump track in Sebastopol, in the Ballarat City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Howard St/Grant St (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1490 m away.
