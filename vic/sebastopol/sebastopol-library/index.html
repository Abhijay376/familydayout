Sebastopol Library

Library in Sebastopol · Basic info

Sebastopol Library is a library in Sebastopol, in the Ballarat City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. CFA/Albert St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1 m away.
