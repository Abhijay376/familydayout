Playground at N F Donald Park (Neville Donald Park)

Playground in Sebastopol · Basic info

Playground at N F Donald Park (Neville Donald Park) is a public playground in Sebastopol, in the Ballarat City area. Sebastopol Library is about 50 m away, and Rio Bravo is about 130 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 60 m away, and bus 22 stops at Bridge St/Albert St, a 1-minute walk away.
