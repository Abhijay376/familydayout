Redan Wetlands/Yuilles Wetlands

Park in Sebastopol · Basic info

Redan Wetlands/Yuilles Wetlands is a park in Sebastopol, in the Ballarat City Council area, good for all ages. Bala St/Albert St (bus) is a 12-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1209 m away with a baby change table.
