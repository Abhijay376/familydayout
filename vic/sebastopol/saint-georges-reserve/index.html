Saint Georges Reserve

Park in Sebastopol · Basic info

Saint Georges Reserve is a park in Sebastopol, in the Ballarat City Council area, good for all ages. Vickers St/Albert St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 256 m away.
