Sebastopol in the Ballarat area has 14 playgrounds and 7 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, Sebastopol has a library and 2 skate parks.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Sebastopol is Redan Wetlands/Yuilles Wetlands, covering about 52 hectares. M R Power Recreation Reserve and Saint Georges Reserve are the next largest. Sebastopol also has Victory Park, Teague Court Reserve and Kowree Crescent Reserve. Playgrounds in Sebastopol include Vickers Tait Reserve, Keane Street Playground and the playground at Victory Park. Our data shows 8 public toilets and 1 off-leash dog park in Sebastopol. Places to visit in Sebastopol include Sebastopol Library. For older kids in Sebastopol there's BMX Reserve and the skate park at M R Power Recreation Reserve. For walks and rides, Yarrowee River Trail and Ballarat Circuit: The Great Dividing Trail Network cross Sebastopol. Our data lists 7 cafes and family restaurants in Sebastopol for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Sebastopol. The closest to Sebastopol is Ballarat Railway Station (V/Line), about 4.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 22, 24 and 25 stop in Sebastopol. V/Line coaches stop in Sebastopol. By car from Sebastopol, Ballarat is about 6 minutes away, Daylesford about 45 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Ballarat Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Lake Gardens is about 9 minutes' drive from Sebastopol. For a cinema, Showbiz Cinemas Ballarat in Smythes Creek is about 6 minutes' drive from Sebastopol. The closest indoor play centre to Sebastopol is Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point, about 6 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East is about 11 minutes' drive from Sebastopol.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 14 playgrounds across Sebastopol. Rainy days at the library in Sebastopol. Teens who skate or ride, with BMX Reserve in Sebastopol. Free days on the Yarrowee River Trail and in Sebastopol's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Sebastopol, Delacombe has 8 playgrounds. Mount Clear, next to Sebastopol, has 6 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Sebastopol, Redan has 5 playgrounds. Mount Pleasant, next to Sebastopol, has 5 playgrounds. Ross Creek, Bonshaw, Magpie and Cambrian Hill also border Sebastopol.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Sebastopol, without traffic.
