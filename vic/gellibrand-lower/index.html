Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Gellibrand Lower, in the Corangamite area. Gellibrand Lower also has 3 beaches.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Gellibrand Lower is Latrobe Bushland Reserve, covering about 298 hectares. In Gellibrand Lower you'll also find Gellibrand East and Gellibrand East Beach. The Great Ocean Walk, Ryans Den to Moonlight Head and Coast track all pass through Gellibrand Lower.

Getting around

There's no train station in Gellibrand Lower. The closest to Gellibrand Lower is Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line), about 56 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gellibrand Lower, so most trips start by car. By car from Gellibrand Lower, Colac is about 76 minutes away and Warrnambool about 79 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Gellibrand Lower is Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon, about 41 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell is about 27 minutes' drive from Gellibrand Lower. The closest places for kids by road from Gellibrand Lower are Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell (27 min), Art Reach Studio in Beech Forest (41 min), the playground at John H Gardner Reserve in Beech Forest (41 min), the playground at Peterborough Coastal Reserve (COM) in Peterborough (38 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (41 min) and the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (41 min).

Best for

Beach days at Gellibrand East and Gellibrand East Beach. Free days on the Great Ocean Walk and in Gellibrand Lower's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Princetown, next to Gellibrand Lower, has 1 playground and a beach.
