Landmarks

Other parks and reserves in Bullumwaal include Blacks Camp. Picnic areas in Bullumwaal include Blacks Camp.

Getting around

Bullumwaal has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bullumwaal, so most trips start by car. By car from Bullumwaal, Bairnsdale is about 47 minutes away and Lakes Entrance about 81 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The nearest larger centre is Bairnsdale, about 47 minutes from Bullumwaal by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Bullumwaal, the nearest places for kids by road are Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale (46 min), Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale (46 min), Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale (47 min), Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale (46 min), Krowathunkoolong Keeping Place in Bairnsdale (48 min) and All Abilities Playground in Bairnsdale (48 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bullumwaal.
