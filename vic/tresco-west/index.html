Tresco West is in the Swan Hill area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Tresco West Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Tresco West, at about 91 hectares.

Getting around

Tresco West has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tresco West, so most trips start by car. By car from Tresco West, Swan Hill is about 22 minutes away and Echuca about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tresco West is Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill, about 21 minutes' drive. For a library, Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill is about 22 minutes' drive from Tresco West. For a skate park, the skate park at Riverside Park in Swan Hill is about 21 minutes' drive from Tresco West. For a cinema, Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill is about 21 minutes' drive from Tresco West. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Tresco West, about 22 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Tresco West are the playground at Gray Park in Lake Boga (8 min), Lake Boga Flying Boat Museum in Lake Boga (9 min), the playground at Guastalegname Park in Swan Hill (19 min), Swan Hill Regional Art Gallery in Swan Hill (20 min), the skate park at Riverside Park in Swan Hill (21 min) and Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tresco West.
