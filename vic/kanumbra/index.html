Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Kanumbra in the Murrindindi area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Great Victorian Rail Trail crosses Kanumbra.

Getting around

Kanumbra has no train station; Euroa Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 35 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kanumbra, so most trips start by car. By car from Kanumbra, Mansfield is about 38 minutes away, Seymour about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kanumbra is Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra, about 23 minutes' drive. For a library, Alexandra Library in Alexandra is about 22 minutes' drive from Kanumbra. For a skate park, BMX Track in Caveat is about 45 minutes' drive from Kanumbra. The closest cinema to Kanumbra is Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa, about 34 minutes' drive. Mansfield is the closest bigger centre to Kanumbra, about 38 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Kanumbra, the nearest places for kids by road are Yarck Recreation Reserve Playground in Yarck (7 min), Alexandra Library in Alexandra (22 min), the playground at Leckie Park in Alexandra (23 min), Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra (23 min), Alexandra Timber Tramway and Museum in Alexandra (24 min) and BMX Track in Caveat (45 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Kanumbra on the Great Victorian Rail Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Kanumbra, Yarck has 1 playground. Fawcett also borders Kanumbra.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Kanumbra, without traffic.
