Poowong Swimming Pool

Pool or water play in Poowong · Basic info

Poowong Swimming Pool is a pool or water play in Poowong, in the South Gippsland Shire Council area, good for all ages. Poowong Post Office/Drouin Poowong Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 200 m away.
