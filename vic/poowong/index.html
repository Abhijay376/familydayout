Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Poowong, in the South Gippsland area. Poowong also has a library and a pool.

Landmarks

Nyora Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Poowong, at about 22 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Poowong. Places to visit in Poowong include Poowong Swimming Pool and Poowong library.

Getting around

Poowong has no train station; Drouin Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Warragul Station - Poowong East via Drouin, Ripplebrook route stop in Poowong. By car from Poowong, Leongatha is about 30 minutes away, Warragul about 31 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 89 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest skate park to Poowong is Nyora skate park in Nyora, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Poowong is Warragul Cinema in Warragul, about 29 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 29 minutes' drive from Poowong. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Poowong is Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville, about 34 minutes' drive. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Poowong, about 30 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Poowong are the playground at Sunnyside Park in Loch (7 min), Nyora skate park in Nyora (10 min), the playground at Nyora Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Nyora (10 min), Korumburra Outdoor Pool in Korumburra (18 min), Coal Creek Community Park & Museum in Korumburra (20 min) and Drouin Skate Park in Drouin (25 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the library in Poowong.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Poowong, Korumburra has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Nyora, next to Poowong, has 3 playgrounds and a skate park. Loch, next to Poowong, has 1 playground. Poowong East also borders Poowong.
