Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Dewhurst in the Cardinia area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

The Lyrebird Walk passes through Dewhurst.

Getting around

Dewhurst has no train station; Cardinia Road Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 10 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dewhurst, so most trips start by car. By car from Dewhurst, Pakenham is about 22 minutes away, Dandenong about 29 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 51 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Dewhurst is Jump Swim School Berwick in Berwick, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest library to Dewhurst is Emerald Library in Emerald, about 8 minutes' drive. For a beach, Carrum Beach in Carrum is about 43 minutes' drive from Dewhurst. For a skate park, the bmx track at Beaconsfield Upper Recreation Reserve in Beaconsfield Upper is about 7 minutes' drive from Dewhurst. The closest cinema to Dewhurst is Village Cinemas in Narre Warren, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Dewhurst is Oz Tenpin in Narre Warren, about 20 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, MonkeyG's in Pakenham is about 24 minutes' drive from Dewhurst. The nearest larger centre is Pakenham, about 22 minutes from Dewhurst by car, with 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play. From Dewhurst, the nearest places for kids by road are the bmx track at Beaconsfield Upper Recreation Reserve in Beaconsfield Upper (7 min), the playground at Beaconsfield Upper Recreation Reserve in Beaconsfield Upper (7 min), the playground at Sutherland Park Recreation Reserve in Beaconsfield Upper (8 min), Emerald Museum in Emerald (8 min), Worrell Reserve Skate Park in Emerald (7 min) and Emerald Library in Emerald (8 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Dewhurst on the Lyrebird Walk.

Nearby suburbs

Emerald, next to Dewhurst, has 6 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Beaconsfield Upper, next to Dewhurst, has 3 playgrounds and a skate park.
