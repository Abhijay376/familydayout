Warrandyte is in the Manningham area, and our data lists 5 playgrounds and 16 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Warrandyte has 2 libraries, 2 museums or galleries and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Warrandyte is Warrandyte State Park, covering about 680 hectares. Currawong Bush Park and Warrandyte Reserve are the next largest. Playground at Warrandyte Reserve and Federation Playspace are among the council's larger district playgrounds. Warrandyte also has Black Flat, Diane Reserve and wonguim wilam. Other playgrounds in Warrandyte include Lynette Reserve Playground and Stiggants Reserve Playground. Our data shows 10 public toilets in Warrandyte. In Warrandyte you'll also find Stonehouse Gallery, Warrandyte Historical Society, Warrandyte Library and Warrandyte high school library. For older kids in Warrandyte there's Warrandyte Skate Park. Goldfields Shopping Centre is the local shopping centre. For walks and rides, Gold Heritage Walk, Main Yarra Trail Extension and Powerline Track cross Warrandyte. Our data lists 4 cafes and family restaurants in Warrandyte for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Warrandyte has no train station; Ringwood Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 8.7 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 364, 578, 579 and 906 stop in Warrandyte. By car from Warrandyte, Ringwood is about 13 minutes away, Lilydale about 20 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 34 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Croydon Memorial Pool in Croydon is about 16 minutes' drive from Warrandyte. The closest beach to Warrandyte is Horse Arena in Abbotsford, about 31 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Hoyts in Ringwood is about 14 minutes' drive from Warrandyte. For an indoor play centre, Timezone in Ringwood is about 14 minutes' drive from Warrandyte. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Warrandyte is Bundoora Park Farm in Bundoora, about 33 minutes' drive.

Best for

Younger kids at Warrandyte's 5 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the library and museum or gallery in Warrandyte. Teens who skate or ride, with Warrandyte Skate Park in Warrandyte. Free days on the Gold Heritage Walk and in Warrandyte's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Warrandyte, Doncaster East has 44 playgrounds. Next door to Warrandyte, Templestowe has 25 playgrounds. Eltham, next to Warrandyte, has 15 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Donvale, next to Warrandyte, has 14 playgrounds. Wonga Park, Park Orchards, Warrandyte South and North Warrandyte also border Warrandyte.
