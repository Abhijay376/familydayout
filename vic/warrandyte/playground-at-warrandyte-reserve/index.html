Playground at Warrandyte Reserve

Playground in Warrandyte · Basic info

Playground at Warrandyte Reserve in Warrandyte is one of the council's larger playgrounds in the Manningham City area. Older kids have a skate park about 80 m away, and Stiggants Reserve Playground is another option about 600 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 170 m from the playground, and buses 578 and 579 stop at Melbourne Hill Rd/Heidelberg-Warrandyte Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
