Black Flat

Park in Warrandyte · Basic info

Black Flat is a park in Warrandyte, in the Manningham City Council area, good for all ages. Mullens Rd/Yarra St (bus) is a 15-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1858 m away.
