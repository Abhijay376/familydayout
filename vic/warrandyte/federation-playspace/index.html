Federation Playspace

Playground in Warrandyte · Basic info

This is one of the council's biggest regional playgrounds in Warrandyte, looked after by Manningham City Council. Warrandyte Library is about 140 m away, and Warrandyte Historical Society is about 320 m away. There's a public toilet about 280 m away, and buses 364, 578, 579 and 906 stop at Webb St/Yarra St, a 3-minute walk away.
