Pound Bend Reserve

Park in Warrandyte · Official details

Pound Bend Reserve is a park in Warrandyte, in the Manningham City Council area, good for all ages. Melbourne Hill Rd/Heidelberg-Warrandyte Rd (bus) is a 16-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 629 m away with a baby change table and dogs are not allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Pound Bend Reserve?
Pound Bend Reserve is in Warrandyte, Victoria.
Is Pound Bend Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Pound Bend Reserve?
No, dogs are not allowed.
Are there toilets at Pound Bend Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 629 m away, with a baby change table.
How do I get to Pound Bend Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Melbourne Hill Rd/Heidelberg-Warrandyte Rd (routes 578, 579), a 16-minute walk.
