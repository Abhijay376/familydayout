Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Driffield in the Latrobe area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Driffield has no train station; Morwell Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 6.9 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Driffield, so most trips start by car. By car from Driffield, Traralgon is about 20 minutes away, Warragul about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Diving Pool in Moe is about 16 minutes' drive from Driffield. The closest library to Driffield is Morwell Library in Morwell, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Driffield is Morwell Skate Park in Morwell, about 8 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 11 minutes' drive from Driffield. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Driffield, about 20 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Driffield are the playground at Yinnar Centenary Garden in Yinnar (9 min), ARC Yinnar in Yinnar (9 min), Railway Trolley Shed in Yinnar (10 min), the playground at Helen Street Reserve in Morwell (8 min), Morwell Skate Park in Morwell (8 min) and Morwell Library in Morwell (9 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Driffield.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Driffield, Morwell has 14 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. Yinnar, next to Driffield, has 3 playgrounds. Hazelwood, Delburn and Coalville also border Driffield.
