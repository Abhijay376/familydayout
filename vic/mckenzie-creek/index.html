McKenzie Creek in the Horsham area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in McKenzie Creek is Bungalally I50 Bushland Reserve, covering about 65 hectares. Tyer Swamp Bushland Reserve and Bungalally I49 Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

McKenzie Creek has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 86 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside McKenzie Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from McKenzie Creek, Horsham is about 11 minutes away and Stawell about 58 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham is about 10 minutes' drive from McKenzie Creek. The closest library to McKenzie Creek is Dimboola Library in Dimboola, about 39 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Velodrome in Horsham is about 9 minutes' drive from McKenzie Creek. For a cinema, Centre Cinemas in Horsham is about 10 minutes' drive from McKenzie Creek. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to McKenzie Creek, about 11 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From McKenzie Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Uebergang Park in Horsham (9 min), Velodrome in Horsham (9 min), Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham (10 min), Horsham Water Play in Horsham (11 min), Centre Cinemas in Horsham (10 min) and Horsham Skate Park in Horsham (11 min).

Best for
