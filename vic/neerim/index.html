Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Neerim, in the Baw Baw area.

Landmarks

Neerim G206 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Neerim, at about 2 hectares. For walks and rides, Rokeby to Neerim Trail crosses Neerim.

Getting around

Neerim has no train station; Drouin Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 89 stops in Neerim. By car from Neerim, Warragul is about 24 minutes away, Pakenham about 51 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul is about 26 minutes' drive from Neerim. The closest library to Neerim is Warragul Library in Warragul, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Neerim is Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South, about 5 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 24 minutes' drive from Neerim. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 25 minutes' drive from Neerim. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Neerim is Wildlife Trail in Tynong North, about 37 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Warragul, about 24 minutes from Neerim by car, with 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Neerim, the nearest places for kids by road are Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South (5 min), the playground at LaTrobe and Loch Rivers, Noojee Streamside Reserve in Noojee (11 min), the playground at Ellen Close Linear Reserve in Warragul (23 min), Warragul Library in Warragul (25 min), Warragul Cinema in Warragul (24 min) and The Alley Warragul in Warragul (25 min).

Best for

Free days on the Rokeby to Neerim Trail and in Neerim's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Neerim, Neerim South has a skate park. Gentle Annie also borders Neerim.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Neerim, without traffic.
