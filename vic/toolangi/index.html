Toolangi in the Murrindindi area has 11 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Toolangi is Toolangi State Forest, covering about 23,461 hectares. Paul Range State Forest and Sylvia Falls Scenic Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Toolangi include Tanglefoot, Lukes Creek and Monda Horseyards. Our data shows 7 public toilets in Toolangi. Picnic areas in Toolangi include Jimmys Old Tool Display, Monda Horseyards and Tanglefoot. For camping, there's Lukes Creek. For walks and rides, Tanglefoot Loop, Myrtle Gully Circuit and Track 11 cross Toolangi.

Getting around

There's no train station in Toolangi. The closest to Toolangi is Lilydale Railway Station (metro), about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Toolangi, so most trips start by car. By car from Toolangi, Healesville is about 18 minutes away, Lilydale about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 75 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Toolangi is Jack Hore Memorial Pool in Healesville, about 16 minutes' drive. For a library, Yarra Plenty Regional Library in Christmas Hills is about 30 minutes' drive from Toolangi. For a skate park, the skate park at McKenzie Reserve in Yarra Glen is about 23 minutes' drive from Toolangi. The closest cinema to Toolangi is The Memo, Healesville in Healesville, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Toolangi is GAMR Laser Tag & VR in Chirnside Park, about 39 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Toolangi is Healesville Sanctuary in Badger Creek, about 22 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Healesville, about 18 minutes from Toolangi by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema.

Best for

Free days on the Tanglefoot Loop and in Toolangi's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Healesville, next to Toolangi, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. Kinglake, next to Toolangi, has 1 playground. Next door to Toolangi, Glenburn has 1 playground. Dixons Creek, next to Toolangi, has 1 playground. Narbethong, Murrindindi, Fernshaw and Chum Creek also border Toolangi.
