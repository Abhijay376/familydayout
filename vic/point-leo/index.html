Getting around

Point Leo has no train station; Bittern Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 782 stops in Point Leo. By car from Point Leo, Mornington is about 29 minutes away, Frankston about 34 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 77 minutes, without traffic.
