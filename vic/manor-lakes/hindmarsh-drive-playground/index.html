Hindmarsh Drive Playground

Playground in Manor Lakes · Official details

Hindmarsh Drive Playground is a neighbourhood playground in Manor Lakes, in the Wyndham City area. McDonald's is about 410 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 660 m away, and bus 191 stops at Tyrrell Pl/Hindmarsh Dr, a 1-minute walk away.
