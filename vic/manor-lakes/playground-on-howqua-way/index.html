Playground on Howqua Way

Playground in Manor Lakes · Official details

This is a public playground in Manor Lakes. Wyndham Library Service - Manor Lakes Branch is about 310 m away, and Playground at Eureka Drive Park is another option about 110 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 380 m from the playground, and trains stop at Wyndham Vale Railway Station, a 4-minute walk away.
