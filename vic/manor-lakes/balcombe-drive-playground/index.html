Balcombe Drive Playground

Playground in Manor Lakes · Official details

This is a neighbourhood playground in Manor Lakes, looked after by Wyndham City Council. Wyndham Library Service - Manor Lakes Branch is about 510 m away, and Playground on Howqua Way is another option about 590 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.2 km away, and bus 191 stops at Balcombe Dr/Manor Lakes Bvd, a 3-minute walk away.
