Ashburton Reserve

Park in Manor Lakes · Official details

Ashburton Reserve is a park in Manor Lakes, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Manor Lakes College/Manor Lakes Bvd (bus) is a 9-minute walk away.

Details

Good to know
Public BBQ about 50 m away (Council)

Questions parents ask

Where is Ashburton Reserve?
Ashburton Reserve is in Manor Lakes, Victoria.
Is Ashburton Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
How do I get to Ashburton Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Manor Lakes College/Manor Lakes Bvd (routes 191), a 9-minute walk.
