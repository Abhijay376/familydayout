Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Dixie, in the Corangamite area.

Landmarks

Lake Mumblin Lake Reserve is the largest mapped park in Dixie, at about 15 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Dixie. The closest to Dixie is Terang Railway Station (V/Line), about 7.2 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dixie, so most trips start by car. By car from Dixie, Warrnambool is about 41 minutes away, Colac about 54 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon is about 22 minutes' drive from Dixie. The closest library to Dixie is Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake, about 28 minutes' drive. For a beach, Buttress Point in The Cove is about 35 minutes' drive from Dixie. For a skate park, the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon is about 22 minutes' drive from Dixie. For a cinema, Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool is about 41 minutes' drive from Dixie. For an indoor play centre, The Bowl in Warrnambool is about 41 minutes' drive from Dixie. For a zoo or wildlife park, Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee is about 34 minutes' drive from Dixie. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 41 minutes from Dixie by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Dixie, the nearest places for kids by road are the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (22 min), the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve in Camperdown (30 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (22 min), Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (26 min), the playground at Tea Tree Lake Reserve in Mortlake (28 min) and Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake (28 min).

Best for
