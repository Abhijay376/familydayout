Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Coonooer West in the Northern Grampians area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Coonooer West has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 85 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Coonooer West, so most trips start by car. By car from Coonooer West, Stawell is about 80 minutes away and Maryborough about 83 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud is about 18 minutes' drive from Coonooer West. For a skate park, Donald BMX Track in Donald is about 25 minutes' drive from Coonooer West. The closest cinema to Coonooer West is Rex Theatre in Charlton, about 22 minutes' drive. From Coonooer West, the nearest places for kids by road are Apex Park Playground in St Arnaud (17 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (18 min), the playground at Queen Mary Botanic Gardens in St Arnaud (18 min), Donald BMX Track in Donald (25 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (21 min) and Rex Theatre in Charlton (22 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Coonooer West.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Coonooer West, Donald has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Cope Cope and Coonooer Bridge also border Coonooer West.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Coonooer West, without traffic.
