Lavers Hill is in the Colac Otway area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Lavers Hill, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 4 public toilets in Lavers Hill. For walks and rides, Madsens Track Nature Walk crosses Lavers Hill.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lavers Hill. The closest to Lavers Hill is Colac Railway Station (V/Line), about 41 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Lavers Hill. By car from Lavers Hill, Colac is about 56 minutes away, Warrnambool about 1.5 hours and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest beach to Lavers Hill is Wreck Beach in Gellibrand Lower, about 27 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell is about 45 minutes' drive from Lavers Hill. For a cinema, Mechanics Hall in Apollo Bay is about 43 minutes' drive from Lavers Hill. For a zoo or wildlife park, Wildlife Wonders in Apollo Bay is about 38 minutes' drive from Lavers Hill. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Lavers Hill, about 56 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Lavers Hill are Art Reach Studio in Beech Forest (21 min), the playground at John H Gardner Reserve in Beech Forest (21 min), Wreck Beach in Gellibrand Lower (27 min), Station Beach in Hordern Vale (26 min), the playground at Rex Norman Park in Gellibrand (35 min) and Wildlife Wonders in Apollo Bay (38 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Lavers Hill on the Madsens Track Nature Walk.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Lavers Hill, Carlisle River has 1 playground. Chapple Vale also borders Lavers Hill.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Lavers Hill, without traffic.
