Playground on Railway Road

Playground in Caldermeade · Basic info

Playground on Railway Road in Caldermeade is a public playground in the Cardinia Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground. It's easiest by car, about 6 minutes from Caldermeade.
