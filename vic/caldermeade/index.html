Getting around

Caldermeade has no train station; Nar Nar Goon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Caldermeade, so most trips start by car. By car from Caldermeade, Pakenham is about 24 minutes away, Cranbourne about 30 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 67 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Caldermeade is Koo Wee Rup Swimming Pool in Koo Wee Rup, about 10 minutes' drive. For a library, Pakenham Library in Pakenham is about 23 minutes' drive from Caldermeade. The closest beach to Caldermeade is Corinella Beach in Corinella, about 32 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Cochrane Park in Koo Wee Rup is about 9 minutes' drive from Caldermeade. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Frankston is about 38 minutes' drive from Caldermeade. The closest indoor play centre to Caldermeade is Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Caldermeade is Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville, about 20 minutes' drive. Pakenham is the closest bigger centre to Caldermeade, about 24 minutes away by car, and has 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play. From Caldermeade, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Thwaites Park in Lang Lang (6 min), the playground at Dick Jones Park in Lang Lang (6 min), the skate park at Cochrane Park in Koo Wee Rup (9 min), Koo Wee Rup Swimming Pool in Koo Wee Rup (10 min), Fisherman's Cottage in Tooradin (16 min) and Nyora skate park in Nyora (20 min).
