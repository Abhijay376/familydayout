Wilkur in the Buloke area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Wilkur is Wilkur Bushland Reserve, covering about 2 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wilkur. The closest to Wilkur is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 111 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wilkur, so most trips start by car. By car from Wilkur, Horsham is about 73 minutes away and Swan Hill about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem is about 26 minutes' drive from Wilkur. For a skate park, Birchip Skate Park in Birchip is about 24 minutes' drive from Wilkur. From Wilkur, the nearest places for kids by road are Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem (26 min), Birchip History Museum in Birchip (24 min), the playground at Birchip Shire Soldiers Memorial Park in Birchip (24 min), the playground at Lake Watchem Lake Reserve in Watchem (28 min), Birchip Skate Park in Birchip (24 min) and Historical Centre in Warracknabeal (29 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wilkur.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Wilkur, Brim has 1 playground. Bangerang and Ballapur also border Wilkur.
