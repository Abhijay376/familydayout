Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Boolite, in the Yarriambiack area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Boolite is Dunmunkle Bushland Reserve, covering about 4 hectares.

Getting around

Boolite has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 107 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Boolite, so most trips start by car. By car from Boolite, Horsham is about 59 minutes away and Stawell about 87 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Boolite is Minyip Swimming Pool in Minyip, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Boolite is Donald BMX Track in Donald, about 36 minutes' drive. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to Boolite, about 59 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Boolite are the playground at Minyip Wetlands in Minyip (19 min), Minyip Swimming Pool in Minyip (19 min), Lions Playground in Minyip (19 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (34 min), Donald BMX Track in Donald (36 min) and Woods' Farming and Heritage Museum in Rupanyup (38 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Boolite.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Boolite, Minyip has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Bangerang and Areegra also border Boolite.
