Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Watsons Creek in the Nillumbik area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Watsons Creek has no train station; Hurstbridge Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 6.6 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Watsons Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Watsons Creek, Ringwood is about 25 minutes away, Healesville about 28 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 43 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Diamond Creek Outdoor Pool in Diamond Creek is about 13 minutes' drive from Watsons Creek. The closest library to Watsons Creek is Yarra Plenty Regional Library in Christmas Hills, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Watsons Creek is Horse Arena in Abbotsford, about 40 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Hurstbridge Half-pipe in Hurstbridge is about 9 minutes' drive from Watsons Creek. The closest cinema to Watsons Creek is Hoyts in Greensborough, about 22 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Rare Bears Indoor Play Centre in Eltham is about 17 minutes' drive from Watsons Creek. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bundoora Park Farm in Bundoora is about 32 minutes' drive from Watsons Creek. Ringwood is the closest bigger centre to Watsons Creek, about 25 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Watsons Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are Andrew Ross Museum in Kangaroo Ground (5 min), Yarra Plenty Regional Library in Christmas Hills (7 min), Hurstbridge Half-pipe in Hurstbridge (9 min), Hurstbridge Skate Park in Hurstbridge (10 min), the playground at Anzac Memorial Park in Hurstbridge (10 min) and the playground at Gordon & Sheila Ferguson Park in Hurstbridge (10 min).
