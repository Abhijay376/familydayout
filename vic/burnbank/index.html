Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Burnbank in the Pyrenees area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Burnbank has no train station; Talbot Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Burnbank, so most trips start by car. By car from Burnbank, Maryborough is about 25 minutes away, Ballarat about 41 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot is about 10 minutes' drive from Burnbank. For a library, Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough is about 26 minutes' drive from Burnbank. For a skate park, the skate park at Alexander Park in Learmonth is about 23 minutes' drive from Burnbank. For a cinema, Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central is about 42 minutes' drive from Burnbank. Maryborough is the closest bigger centre to Burnbank, about 25 minutes away by car, and has a library. From Burnbank, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Toll Bar Park in Lexton (8 min), Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground in Talbot (10 min), Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (10 min), Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (26 min), the skate park at Alexander Park in Learmonth (23 min) and Lee Medlyn Bottle Museum in Clunes (23 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Burnbank.
