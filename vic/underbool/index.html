Underbool is in the Mildura area, and our data lists 1 playground and 16 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Underbool has a pool.

Landmarks

Welshmans Plain Flora and Fauna Reserve is the largest mapped park in Underbool, at about 234 hectares. Mamengoroock I72 Bushland Reserve and Gnarr Flora Reserve are the next largest. Underbool also has Outen Park, Daalko Bushland Reserve and Gnarr I75 Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds in Underbool include the playground at Outen Park. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Underbool. Places to visit in Underbool include Underbool Swimming Pool.

Getting around

Underbool has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 161 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Ouyen - Pinnaroo via Mallee Hwy route stop in Underbool. V/Line coaches stop in Underbool. By car from Underbool, Mildura is about 2 hours away and Swan Hill about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Walpeup Library in Walpeup is about 16 minutes' drive from Underbool. The closest cinema to Underbool is Roxy in Ouyen, about 37 minutes' drive. From Underbool, the nearest places for kids by road are Walpeup Library in Walpeup (16 min), the playground at Walpeup Wayside Stop in Walpeup (16 min), Roxy in Ouyen (37 min) and Ouyen Swimming Pool in Ouyen (37 min).
