Henry Lawsons Green Playground

Playground in Point Cook · Official details

This is a neighbourhood playground in Point Cook, looked after by Wyndham City Council. KFC is about 340 m away if you need food, and Baltimore Drive Playground is another option about 350 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 680 m away, and bus 498 stops at Rowland Dr/Dunnings Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
