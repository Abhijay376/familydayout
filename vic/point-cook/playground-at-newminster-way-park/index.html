Playground at Newminster Way Park

Playground in Point Cook · Official details

Playground at Newminster Way Park in Point Cook is a neighbourhood playground in the Wyndham City area. Older kids have a skate park about 30 m away, and Playground at Spindrift Way Park is another option about 470 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.1 km from the playground, and buses 496, 497 and 949 stop at Newminster Way/Point Cook Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
