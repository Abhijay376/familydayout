Lewiston Drive Playground

Playground in Point Cook · Official details

Lewiston Drive Playground is a neighbourhood playground in Point Cook, in the Wyndham City area. The Palmers Road Trail is about 220 m away, and Playground at Elidon Crescent Park is another option about 400 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and buses 494, 497 and 949 stop at Lancaster Dr/Palmers Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
