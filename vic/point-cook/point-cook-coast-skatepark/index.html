Point Cook Coast Skatepark

Skate, BMX or pump track in Point Cook · Basic info

Point Cook Coast Skatepark is a skate, bmx or pump track in Point Cook, in the Wyndham City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. South Shore Ave/Sanctuary Lakes South Bvd (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 46 m away.
