Sanctuary Lakes South Blvd Playground

Playground in Point Cook · Official details

Sanctuary Lakes South Blvd Playground in Point Cook is a neighbourhood playground in the Wyndham City area. Playground at Central Park is another option about 440 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 690 m from the playground, and bus 496 stops at South Shore Ave/Sanctuary Lakes South Bvd, a 2-minute walk away.
