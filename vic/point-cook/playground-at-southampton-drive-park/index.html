Playground at Southampton Drive Park

Playground in Point Cook · Official details

Playground at Southampton Drive Park in Point Cook is a neighbourhood playground in the Wyndham City area. The Skeleton Creek Trail passes about 390 m away, and Lewiston Drive Playground is another option about 590 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 960 m away, and bus 495 stops at Foxwood Dr/The Strand, a 4-minute walk away.
