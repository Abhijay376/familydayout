Silverbay Avenue Park

Park in Point Cook · Official details

Silverbay Avenue Park is a park in Point Cook, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Tidal Way/Saltwater Prom (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 753 m away.
