Yuruga Boulevard Playground

Playground in Point Cook · Official details

This is a neighbourhood playground in Point Cook, looked after by Wyndham City Council. Playground at Leap Frog Park is another option about 320 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 540 m from the playground, and bus 497 stops at Tristania Dr/Point Cook Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
