Point Cook Town Centre

Shopping centre in Point Cook · Basic info

Point Cook Town Centre is a shopping centre in Point Cook, in the Wyndham City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Point Cook Shopping Centre/Boardwalk Bvd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 56 m away.
