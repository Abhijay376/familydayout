Kellerman Drive Playground

Playground in Point Cook · Official details

This is a neighbourhood playground in Point Cook, looked after by Wyndham City Council. Playground at Eccles Vista Park is another option about 330 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 900 m from the playground, and bus 498 stops at Sneydes Rd/Hacketts Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
