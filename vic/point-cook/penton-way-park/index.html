Penton Way Park

Park in Point Cook · Basic info

Penton Way Park is a park in Point Cook, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Rowland Dr/Dunnings Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1092 m away with a baby change table.
