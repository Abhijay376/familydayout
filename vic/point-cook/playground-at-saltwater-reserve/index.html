Playground at Saltwater Reserve

Playground in Point Cook · Official details

This is a neighbourhood playground in Point Cook, looked after by Wyndham City Council. Playground at Paragon Park is another option about 270 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 350 m from the playground, and bus 497 stops at Parliament St/Saltwater Prom, a 4-minute walk away.
