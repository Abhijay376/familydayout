Playground at Friar Place Park

Playground in Point Cook · Official details

Playground at Friar Place Park is a neighbourhood playground in Point Cook, in the Wyndham City area. The Palmers Road Trail passes about 200 m away, and Playground at Lennon Boulevard Park is another option about 440 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 850 m away, and bus 497 stops at Palmers Rd/Dunnings Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
