Tom Roberts Parade Reserve

Park in Point Cook · Basic info

Tom Roberts Parade Reserve is a park in Point Cook, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Sneydes Rd/Boardwalk Bvd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 520 m away with a baby change table.
