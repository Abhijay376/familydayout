Pepperjack Way Playground

Playground in Point Cook · Official details

Pepperjack Way Playground is a neighbourhood playground in Point Cook, in the Wyndham City area. The Canal is about 320 m away, and for food, Oh Happy Day is about 270 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 500 m from the playground, and bus 497 stops at Miami Dr/Saltwater Prom, a 5-minute walk away.
