Playground at Boardwalk Park

Playground in Point Cook · Official details

This is a neighbourhood playground in Point Cook, looked after by Wyndham City Council. Older kids have a skate park about 90 m away, and Point Cook Library is about 210 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 130 m from the playground, and buses 495 and 949 stop at Central Park/The Strand, a 2-minute walk away.
