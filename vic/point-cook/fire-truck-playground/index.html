Fire truck playground

Playground in Point Cook · Official details

Fire truck playground in Point Cook is a neighbourhood playground in the Wyndham City area. The Hobsons Bay Coastal Trail is about 20 m away, and for food, Coast Café is about 440 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 740 m from the playground, and bus 497 stops at Tidal Way/Saltwater Prom, a 5-minute walk away.
