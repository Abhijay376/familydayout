Featherbrook Drive Playground

Playground in Point Cook · Official details

Featherbrook Drive Playground in Point Cook is a small local playground in the Wyndham City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus 495 stops at Wildcherry Pl/The Boardwalk, a 8-minute walk away.
