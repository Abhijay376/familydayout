Playground at Miles Franklin Park

Playground in Point Cook · Official details

Playground at Miles Franklin Park is a neighbourhood playground in Point Cook, in the Wyndham City area. For food, Flaming Healthy is about 410 m away, and Henry Lawsons Green Playground is another option about 420 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.1 km from the playground, and bus 495 stops at Juniper Ave/Boardwalk Bvd, a 6-minute walk away.
