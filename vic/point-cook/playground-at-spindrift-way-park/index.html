Playground at Spindrift Way Park

Playground in Point Cook · Official details

Playground at Spindrift Way Park in Point Cook is a neighbourhood playground in the Wyndham City area. The Palmers Road Trail passes about 330 m away, and Playground at Elidon Crescent Park is another option about 340 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and buses 497 and 498 stop at Willowgreen Way/Dunnings Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
