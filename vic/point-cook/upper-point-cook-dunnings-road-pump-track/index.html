Upper Point Cook (Dunnings Road pump track)

Skate, BMX or pump track in Point Cook · Basic info

Upper Point Cook (Dunnings Road pump track) is a skate, bmx or pump track in Point Cook, in the Wyndham City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Waterhaven Bvd/Dunnings Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1082 m away with a baby change table.
