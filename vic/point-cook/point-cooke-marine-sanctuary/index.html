Point Cooke Marine Sanctuary

Park in Point Cook · Official details

Point Cooke Marine Sanctuary is a park in Point Cook, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages.

Details

Seasons
Jellyfish swarms over the reef in late summer (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
Resident snakes and blue ringed octopus: avoid long grass and rock pool crevices; Rocks are slippery and uneven; Quite remote: tell someone where you are going (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Point Cooke Marine Sanctuary?
Point Cooke Marine Sanctuary is in Point Cook, Victoria.
Is Point Cooke Marine Sanctuary free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
