Saltwater Reserve Bouldering Wall

Climbing in Point Cook · Official details

Saltwater Reserve Bouldering Wall is a climbing in Point Cook, in the Wyndham City Council area, best for kids 5 and up and teens. Atlantis Dr/Point Cook Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 257 m away with a baby change table.

Good to know
