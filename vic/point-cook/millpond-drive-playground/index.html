Millpond Drive Playground

Playground in Point Cook · Official details

Millpond Drive Playground is a neighbourhood playground in Point Cook, in the Wyndham City area. Swanview Walk Playground is another option about 380 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 800 m from the playground, and buses 495 and 949 stop at Grassbird Dr/The Boardwalk, a 4-minute walk away.
