Playground at Sanctuary Lakes East Boulevard Park

Playground in Point Cook · Official details

Playground at Sanctuary Lakes East Boulevard Park is a public playground in Point Cook, in the Wyndham City area. The Skeleton Creek Trail is about 70 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.5 km from the playground, and bus 496 stops at Coastal Prom/Sanctuary Lakes East Bvd, a 1-minute walk away.
