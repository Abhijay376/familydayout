Gallery Place Playground

Playground in Point Cook · Official details

This is a neighbourhood playground in Point Cook, looked after by Wyndham City Council. Pizza Hut is about 320 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 660 m away, and bus 496 stops at Point Cook Rd/Sanctuary Lakes Bvd, a 4-minute walk away.

Features

  • Size: Neighbourhood (council data)
  • Fenced: No (council data)
  • Ground: Soft fall

  • Food nearby: Pizza Hut (320 m), McDonald's (430 m)
  • Also nearby: Sanctuary Lakes Shopping Centre (470 m)
