Swanview Walk Playground

Playground in Point Cook · Official details

Swanview Walk Playground in Point Cook is a neighbourhood playground in the Wyndham City area. Millpond Drive Playground is another option about 380 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 610 m away, and bus 495 stops at Pangana Dr/The Boardwalk, a 6-minute walk away.
