Kingsford Drive Park

Park in Point Cook · Official details

Kingsford Drive Park is a park in Point Cook, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Sneydes Rd/Hacketts Rd (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1030 m away with a baby change table.

Questions parents ask

Where is Kingsford Drive Park?
Kingsford Drive Park is in Point Cook, Victoria.
Is Kingsford Drive Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Kingsford Drive Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 1030 m away, with a baby change table.
How do I get to Kingsford Drive Park by public transport?
