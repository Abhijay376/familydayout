Island Way Traffic Playground

Playground in Point Cook · Official details

Island Way Traffic Playground in Point Cook is a neighbourhood playground in the Wyndham City area. Flower pot playground is another option about 220 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.7 km from the playground, and bus 494 stops at Island Way/Alamanda Bvd, a 5-minute walk away.
