Saltwater Crocodile Park

Playground in Point Cook · Official details

Saltwater Crocodile Park in Point Cook is a public playground in the Wyndham City area. The Hobsons Bay Coastal Trail passes about 380 m away, and Coast Café is about 110 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 330 m away, and bus 497 stops at Tidal Way/Saltwater Prom, a 1-minute walk away.
