Pirate ship playground

Playground in Point Cook · Official details

Pirate ship playground is one of the council's larger playgrounds in Point Cook, in the Wyndham City area. Island Way Traffic Playground is another option about 240 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.3 km from the playground, and bus 494 stops at Bayview Park/Alamanda Bvd, a 2-minute walk away.
