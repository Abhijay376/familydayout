Prudence Parade Reserve

Park in Point Cook · Official details

Prudence Parade Reserve is a park in Point Cook, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Alamanda College/Alamanda Bvd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away.

Details

Good to know

Questions parents ask

Where is Prudence Parade Reserve?
Prudence Parade Reserve is in Point Cook, Victoria.
Is Prudence Parade Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
How do I get to Prudence Parade Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Alamanda College/Alamanda Bvd (routes 494), a 6-minute walk.
