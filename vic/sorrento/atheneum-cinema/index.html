Atheneum Cinema

Cinema in Sorrento · Basic info

Atheneum Cinema is a cinema in Sorrento, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire Council area, good for school-age kids and teens. Ocean Beach Rd/Kerferd Ave (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 56 m away with a baby change table.
