Stringers Corner Park

Park in Sorrento · Basic info

Stringers Corner Park is a park in Sorrento, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire Council area, good for all ages. Ferry Terminal/Esplanade (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 235 m away with a baby change table.
