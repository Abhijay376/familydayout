Our data lists 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in Seville East, in the Yarra Ranges area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Seville East is Wandin Yallock G65 Bushland Reserve, covering about 3 hectares. Woori Yallock Creek Streamside Reserve is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Seville East include Glenvalley Crescent Park. Playgrounds around Seville East include the playground at Glenvalley Crescent Park. The Lilydale-Warburton Rail Trail and Warburton Rail Trail all pass through Seville East.

Getting around

Seville East has no train station; Lilydale Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 683 and 965 stop in Seville East. By car from Seville East, Lilydale is about 17 minutes away, Healesville about 21 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 58 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Seville Water Play Park in Seville is about 4 minutes' drive from Seville East. The closest library to Seville East is Mount Evelyn Reading Room in Mount Evelyn, about 14 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Mount Evelyn Skate Park in Mount Evelyn is about 14 minutes' drive from Seville East. For a cinema, Reading Cinemas in Chirnside Park is about 22 minutes' drive from Seville East. The closest indoor play centre to Seville East is PlayPlace in Mooroolbark, about 23 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Lyre Bird enclosure in Badger Creek is about 17 minutes' drive from Seville East. The nearest larger centre is Lilydale, about 17 minutes from Seville East by car, with 12 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Seville East, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Valley Reserve in Seville (4 min), Seville Water Play Park in Seville (4 min), Mont De Lancey Museum in Wandin North (11 min), Mount Evelyn Skate Park in Mount Evelyn (14 min), the pump track at Lilydale-Warburton in Mount Evelyn (14 min) and Mount Evelyn Reading Room in Mount Evelyn (14 min).

Best for
