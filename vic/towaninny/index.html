Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Towaninny in the Buloke area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Towaninny. The closest to Towaninny is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 56 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Towaninny, so most trips start by car. By car from Towaninny, Swan Hill is about 58 minutes away and Echuca about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Towaninny is Quambatook Memorial Pool in Quambatook, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest library to Towaninny is Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook, about 14 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 58 minutes from Towaninny by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Towaninny are Quambatook Historical Centre in Quambatook (14 min), Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook (14 min), Quambatook Lions Club Playground in Quambatook (15 min), Quambatook Memorial Pool in Quambatook (15 min), the playground at Hall Corner Park in Nullawil (22 min) and Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof (25 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Towaninny.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Towaninny, Quambatook has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Ninyeunook, Kalpienung and Cokum also border Towaninny.
