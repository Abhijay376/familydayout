Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Kardella in the South Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Great Southern Rail Trail crosses Kardella.

Getting around

Kardella has no train station; Warragul Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kardella, so most trips start by car. By car from Kardella, Leongatha is about 17 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 42 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kardella is Korumburra Outdoor Pool in Korumburra, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest library to Kardella is Leongatha Library in Leongatha, about 16 minutes' drive. For a beach, The Caves in Inverloch is about 40 minutes' drive from Kardella. The closest skate park to Kardella is Leongatha Skate Park in Leongatha, about 17 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha is about 16 minutes' drive from Kardella. The nearest larger centre is Leongatha, about 17 minutes from Kardella by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Kardella, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Coal Creek Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Korumburra (9 min), the playground at Coleman Park in Korumburra (9 min), Korumburra Outdoor Pool in Korumburra (9 min), Coal Creek Community Park & Museum in Korumburra (10 min), Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha (16 min) and Leongatha Library in Leongatha (16 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Kardella on the Great Southern Rail Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Korumburra, next to Kardella, has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Arawata also borders Kardella.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Kardella, without traffic.
