Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Milnes Bridge in the Gannawarra area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Milnes Bridge. The closest to Milnes Bridge is Kerang Railway Station (V/Line), about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Milnes Bridge, so most trips start by car. By car from Milnes Bridge, Swan Hill is about 62 minutes away and Echuca about 63 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Milnes Bridge is Cohuna Pool in Cohuna, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest library to Milnes Bridge is Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Milnes Bridge is Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Milnes Bridge are Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna (12 min), the playground at C L King Memorial Park in Cohuna (12 min), the playground at Cohuna Apex Park in Cohuna (12 min), Cohuna Pool in Cohuna (13 min), Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (15 min) and Swimming Pool in Kerang (15 min).
