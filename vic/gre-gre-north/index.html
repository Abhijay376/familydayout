Gre Gre North is in the Northern Grampians area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Gre Gre North is Gre Gre Bushland Reserve, covering about 42 hectares.

Getting around

Gre Gre North has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 78 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gre Gre North, so most trips start by car. By car from Gre Gre North, Stawell is about 60 minutes away and Horsham about 73 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud is about 17 minutes' drive from Gre Gre North. The closest skate park to Gre Gre North is Donald BMX Track in Donald, about 33 minutes' drive. Stawell is the closest bigger centre to Gre Gre North, about 60 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds and a pool. From Gre Gre North, the nearest places for kids by road are Apex Park Playground in St Arnaud (16 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (17 min), Market Square Park Playground in St Arnaud (17 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (29 min), Donald BMX Track in Donald (33 min) and Woods' Farming and Heritage Museum in Rupanyup (38 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Gre Gre North.

Nearby suburbs

Moolerr, Gre Gre South and Gre Gre also border Gre Gre North.
