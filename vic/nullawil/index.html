Nullawil in the Buloke area has 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Nullawil is Nullawil Recreation Reserve, covering about 6 hectares. Nullawill West Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Nullawil include Hall Corner Park. Playgrounds around Nullawil include the playground at Hall Corner Park. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Nullawil.

Getting around

Nullawil has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 67 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Nullawil. By car from Nullawil, Swan Hill is about 65 minutes away and Stawell about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Nullawil is Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest library to Nullawil is Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Nullawil is Birchip Skate Park in Birchip, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Nullawil are the playground at Culgoa Memorial Park in Culgoa (13 min), Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof (19 min), Willandra Museum in Wycheproof (20 min), Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock (22 min), Birchip Skate Park in Birchip (30 min) and Birchip History Museum in Birchip (30 min).
