Kinnabulla in the Buloke area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Box Swamp Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Kinnabulla, at about 65 hectares. Bulls Swamp Wildlife Reserve (no hunting) and Lake Danaher Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

Kinnabulla has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 93 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kinnabulla, so most trips start by car. By car from Kinnabulla, Horsham is about 1.5 hours away and Swan Hill about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kinnabulla is Woomelang Recreation Reserve Swimming Pool in Woomelang, about 25 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Birchip Skate Park in Birchip is about 16 minutes' drive from Kinnabulla. The closest places for kids by road from Kinnabulla are Birchip History Museum in Birchip (15 min), the playground at Birchip Shire Soldiers Memorial Park in Birchip (16 min), Birchip Skate Park in Birchip (16 min), the playground at J.E Taylor Sunset Park in Birchip (16 min), Woomelang Recreation Reserve Swimming Pool in Woomelang (25 min) and Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Kinnabulla.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Kinnabulla, Beulah has 2 playgrounds. Curyo and Ballapur also border Kinnabulla.
