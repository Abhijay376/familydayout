Wangaratta South is in the Wangaratta area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Wangaratta South, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 3 public toilets in Wangaratta South. For walks and rides, Salisbury Falls Walk and Alpine Views Track cross Wangaratta South.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wangaratta South. The closest to Wangaratta South is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wangaratta South, so most trips start by car. By car from Wangaratta South, Wangaratta is about 8 minutes away, Benalla about 28 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Moore than Swimming in Wangaratta is about 6 minutes' drive from Wangaratta South. The closest library to Wangaratta South is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 9 minutes' drive. For a beach, Sandy Beach in Beechworth is about 39 minutes' drive from Wangaratta South. The closest skate park to Wangaratta South is the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Wangaratta South is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 9 minutes' drive. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Wangaratta South, about 8 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Wangaratta South are Moore than Swimming in Wangaratta (6 min), the playground at Dodsworth Street Reserve in Wangaratta (7 min), Brien Crescent Playground in Wangaratta (8 min), Splashpark in Wangaratta (8 min), the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta (9 min) and Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (9 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Wangaratta South on the Salisbury Falls Walk.

Nearby suburbs

Wangaratta, next to Wangaratta South, has 9 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Wangaratta South, Glenrowan has 1 playground. Laceby also borders Wangaratta South.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Wangaratta South, without traffic.
