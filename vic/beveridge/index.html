Beveridge in the Mitchell area has 4 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in our data. Beyond the parks, Beveridge has a skate park.

Landmarks

Beveridge Recreation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Beveridge, at about 10 hectares. Mandalay Park and Newpark Reserve are the next largest. Playgrounds around Beveridge include the playground at Mandalay Park, the playground at Beveridge Recreation Reserve and the playground at Beveridge Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Beveridge. For older kids in Beveridge there's the skate park at Mandalay Park. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Beveridge for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Beveridge has no train station; Wallan Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 6.7 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 511 stops in Beveridge. By car from Beveridge, Broadmeadows is about 27 minutes away, Sunshine about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 46 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Birdy Cove in Whittlesea is about 19 minutes' drive from Beveridge. For a library, Wallan Library in Wallan is about 7 minutes' drive from Beveridge. For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 45 minutes' drive from Beveridge. The closest cinema to Beveridge is United Cinemas in Craigieburn, about 18 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Timezone in Craigieburn is about 18 minutes' drive from Beveridge. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Beveridge is Funfields in Whittlesea, about 19 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Broadmeadows, about 27 minutes from Beveridge by car, with 12 playgrounds, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Beveridge's 4 local playgrounds. Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at Mandalay Park in Beveridge.

Nearby suburbs

Wallan, next to Beveridge, has 13 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Beveridge, Donnybrook has 5 playgrounds. Mickleham, next to Beveridge, has 4 playgrounds. Next door to Beveridge, Kalkallo has 3 playgrounds and a skate park. Woodstock, Upper Plenty, Eden Park and Darraweit Guim also border Beveridge.
