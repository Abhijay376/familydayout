Playground at Mandalay Park

Playground in Beveridge · Basic info

Playground at Mandalay Park is a public playground in Beveridge, in the Mitchell Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 60 m away, and Playground on Mandalay Circuit is another option about 170 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 80 m from the playground, and bus 511 stops at Ramble Cl/Mandalay Cct , a 5-minute walk away.
