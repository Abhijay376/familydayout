Capel Sound in the Mornington Peninsula area has 2 playgrounds and 6 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Nepean Miniature Aerosport Association is the largest mapped park in Capel Sound, at about 26 hectares. Tootgarook Sports Reserve and Vern Wright Reserve are the next largest. Capel Sound also has Rosebud West Reserve, Elizabeth Avenue Reserve and Tootgarook Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds around Capel Sound include the playground at Vern Wright Reserve and the playground at Port Phillip Bay Coastal Reserve (COM). Our data shows 4 public toilets in Capel Sound. The Mornington Peninsula Bay Trail passes through Capel Sound. Our data lists 4 cafes and family restaurants in Capel Sound for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Capel Sound has no train station; Bittern Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 787, 788 and 887 stop in Capel Sound. By car from Capel Sound, Mornington is about 25 minutes away, Frankston about 31 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 74 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Yawa Aquatic Centre in Rosebud is about 4 minutes' drive from Capel Sound. For a library, Rosebud Library in Rosebud is about 5 minutes' drive from Capel Sound. The closest beach to Capel Sound is Rosebud Beach in Rosebud, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Capel Sound is Rosebud Skate Park in Rosebud, about 3 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Rosebud Cinemas in Rosebud is about 5 minutes' drive from Capel Sound. The closest indoor play centre to Capel Sound is Top Fun Shack in Rosebud, about 4 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale is about 36 minutes' drive from Capel Sound. Mornington is the closest bigger centre to Capel Sound, about 25 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a library, indoor play and a beach.

Best for

Free days on the Mornington Peninsula Bay Trail and in Capel Sound's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Capel Sound, Rosebud has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Tootgarook, next to Capel Sound, has 1 playground.
