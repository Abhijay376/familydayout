Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Sunday Creek in the Mitchell area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Sunday Creek has no train station; Broadford Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 2.9 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Sunday Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Sunday Creek, Seymour is about 25 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 48 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 67 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore is about 19 minutes' drive from Sunday Creek. The closest library to Sunday Creek is Broadford Library in Broadford, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Sunday Creek is Broadford Skate Park in Broadford, about 5 minutes' drive. For a cinema, United Cinemas in Craigieburn is about 39 minutes' drive from Sunday Creek. The closest indoor play centre to Sunday Creek is Timezone in Craigieburn, about 39 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Sunday Creek is Funfields in Whittlesea, about 34 minutes' drive. Seymour is the closest bigger centre to Sunday Creek, about 25 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Sunday Creek are Rupert Street Playground in Broadford (5 min), Broadford Skate Park in Broadford (5 min), Sutherland Street Playground in Broadford (6 min), Broadford Library in Broadford (7 min), the skate park at Wandong and Heathcote Junction Memorial Park in Wandong (12 min) and Kilmore Library in Kilmore (18 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Sunday Creek.

Nearby suburbs
