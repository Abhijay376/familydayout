Bundalong is in the Moira area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bundalong, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 2 public toilets in Bundalong.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bundalong. The closest to Bundalong is Springhurst Railway Station (V/Line), about 33 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Wangaratta - Yarrawonga route stop in Bundalong. By car from Bundalong, Wangaratta is about 34 minutes away and Wodonga about 61 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bundalong is Rutherglen War Memorial Swimming Pool in Rutherglen, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest library to Bundalong is Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga, about 14 minutes' drive. For a beach, Forges Beach No 2 in Yarrawonga is about 24 minutes' drive from Bundalong. The closest skate park to Bundalong is the pump track on Victoria Street in Wahgunyah, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Bundalong is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 34 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Tunzafun in Yarrawonga is about 16 minutes' drive from Bundalong. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Bundalong, about 34 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Bundalong are Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga (14 min), Tunzafun in Yarrawonga (16 min), Yarrawonga Mulwala Pioneer Museum in Yarrawonga (17 min), Lonsdale Reserve Playground in Yarrawonga (18 min), Mulwala Waterslide in Yarrawonga (18 min) and Swimming Pool Reserve in Yarrawonga (18 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bundalong.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Bundalong, Yarrawonga has 6 playgrounds, a library and a beach. Brimin, Boomahnoomoonah and Bathumi also border Bundalong.
