Bolinda is in the Macedon Ranges area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bolinda, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Bolinda.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bolinda. The closest to Bolinda is Clarkefield Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bolinda, so most trips start by car. By car from Bolinda, Broadmeadows is about 38 minutes away, Kyneton about 43 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 54 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Gisborne Aquatic Centre in Gisborne is about 21 minutes' drive from Bolinda. The closest library to Bolinda is Romsey Library in Romsey, about 9 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Romsey Skate Park in Romsey is about 9 minutes' drive from Bolinda. The closest cinema to Bolinda is Reading Cinema in Sunbury, about 22 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Timezone in Craigieburn is about 28 minutes' drive from Bolinda. The nearest larger centre is Broadmeadows, about 38 minutes from Bolinda by car, with 12 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Bolinda, the nearest places for kids by road are Romsey Skate Park in Romsey (9 min), Romsey Library in Romsey (9 min), Low Ropes Course in Riddells Creek (16 min), the playground at Village Green in Sunbury (16 min), Gisborne Library in Gisborne (20 min) and Old Gisborne Court House in Gisborne (20 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bolinda.
