Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Pomborneit East in the Corangamite area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Pomborneit East has no train station; Colac Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Pomborneit East, so most trips start by car. By car from Pomborneit East, Colac is about 36 minutes away, Warrnambool about 1.5 hours and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Pomborneit East is Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac, about 39 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt is about 41 minutes' drive from Pomborneit East. The closest cinema to Pomborneit East is Colac Cinemas in Colac, about 37 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Pomborneit East is Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac, about 37 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 36 minutes from Pomborneit East by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Pomborneit East, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (35 min), the playground at Western Bay Playground in Colac (35 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (37 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (37 min), Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (39 min) and the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (41 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Pomborneit East.
