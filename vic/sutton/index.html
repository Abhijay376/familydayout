Sutton is in the Buloke area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Sutton, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Sutton. The closest to Sutton is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 69 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Sutton, so most trips start by car. By car from Sutton, Swan Hill is about 63 minutes away and Horsham about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock is about 11 minutes' drive from Sutton. The closest skate park to Sutton is Birchip Skate Park in Birchip, about 23 minutes' drive. From Sutton, the nearest places for kids by road are Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock (11 min), the playground at Culgoa Memorial Park in Culgoa (19 min), the playground at Hall Corner Park in Nullawil (20 min), Birchip Skate Park in Birchip (23 min), Birchip History Museum in Birchip (23 min) and Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Sutton.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Sutton, Berriwillock has a pool. Next door to Sutton, Culgoa has 1 playground. Marlbed and Jil Jil also border Sutton.
