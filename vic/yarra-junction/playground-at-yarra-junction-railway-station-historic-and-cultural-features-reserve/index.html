Playground at Yarra Junction Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve

Playground in Yarra Junction · Basic info

Playground at Yarra Junction Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Yarra Junction is a public playground in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. The Lilydale-Warburton Rail Trail is about 20 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 120 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 100 m from the playground, and bus 683 stops at Yarra Junction Memorial Reserve/Warburton Hwy, a 1-minute walk away.
