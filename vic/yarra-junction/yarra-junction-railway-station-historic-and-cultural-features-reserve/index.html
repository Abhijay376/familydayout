Yarra Junction Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve

Park in Yarra Junction · Basic info

Yarra Junction Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve is a park in Yarra Junction, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Station St/Warburton Hwy (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 459 m away.
