Skate park at Yarra Junction Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve

Skate, BMX or pump track in Yarra Junction · Basic info

Skate park at Yarra Junction Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve is a skate, bmx or pump track in Yarra Junction, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Yarra Junction Memorial Reserve/Warburton Hwy (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 165 m away.
