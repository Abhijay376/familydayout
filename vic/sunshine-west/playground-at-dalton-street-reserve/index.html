Playground at Dalton Street Reserve

Playground in Sunshine West · Official details

Playground at Dalton Street Reserve in Sunshine West is a public playground in the Brimbank City area. Older kids have a skate park about 320 m away, and for food, Old Dang Noodle Bar is about 360 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 480 m from the playground, and bus 428 stops at Fairbairn Rd/Wright St, a 4-minute walk away.
