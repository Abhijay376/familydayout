Gresham Way Reserve Playground

Playground in Sunshine West · Basic info

Gresham Way Reserve Playground is a public playground in Sunshine West, in the Brimbank City area. The Derrimut Trail passes about 100 m away, and Playground at Austral/Sheldon Reserve is another option about 530 m away. There's a public toilet about 770 m away, and bus 428 stops at The Avenue SC/The Avenue, a 1-minute walk away.
