Norm Talintyre Reserve Skate Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Sunshine West · Basic info

Norm Talintyre Reserve Skate Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Sunshine West, in the Brimbank City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Talintyre Rd/Fairbairn Rd (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 252 m away.
