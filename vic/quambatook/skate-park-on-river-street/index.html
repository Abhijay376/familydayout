Skate park on River Street

Skate, BMX or pump track in Quambatook · Basic info

Skate park on River Street is a skate, bmx or pump track in Quambatook, in the Gannawarra Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Quambatook Hotel/Guthrie St (coach) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 532 m away.
