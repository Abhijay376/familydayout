Our data lists 2 playgrounds and 4 parks and reserves in Quambatook, in the Gannawarra area. Beyond the parks, Quambatook has a library, a pool and a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

Towaninny North Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Quambatook, at about 40 hectares. Quambatook I204 Bushland Reserve and Cannie Nature Conservation Reserve are the next largest. Quambatook also has Bev Nalder Memorial Park. Playgrounds in Quambatook include Quambatook Lions Club Playground. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Quambatook. In Quambatook you'll also find Quambatook Historical Centre, Quambatook Memorial Pool and Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch.

Getting around

There's no train station in Quambatook. The closest to Quambatook is Kerang Railway Station (V/Line), about 39 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Quambatook. By car from Quambatook, Swan Hill is about 71 minutes away and Echuca about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Quambatook, the nearest places for kids by road are Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (31 min), Swimming Pool in Kerang (31 min) and the playground at Hall Corner Park in Nullawil (36 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the library and museum or gallery in Quambatook.

Nearby suburbs

Ninyeunook, Meering West, Gredgwin and Cannie also border Quambatook.
