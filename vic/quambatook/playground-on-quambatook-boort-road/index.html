Playground on Quambatook - Boort Road

Playground in Quambatook · Basic info

This is a public playground in Quambatook. Older kids have a skate park about 200 m away, and Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch is about 460 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 70 m from the playground, and coaches stop at Quambatook Hotel/Guthrie St , a 7-minute walk away.
