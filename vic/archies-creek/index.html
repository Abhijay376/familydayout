Archies Creek is in the Bass Coast area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Archies Creek, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Archies Creek has no train station; Stony Point Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 36 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Archies Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Archies Creek, Wonthaggi is about 15 minutes away, Leongatha about 45 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Wonthaggi is about 14 minutes' drive from Archies Creek. For a library, Wonthaggi Principal Library in Wonthaggi is about 14 minutes' drive from Archies Creek. The closest beach to Archies Creek is Kilcunda East Beach in Kilcunda, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Archies Creek is Wonthaggi Skate Park in Wonthaggi, about 14 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi is about 14 minutes' drive from Archies Creek. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Archies Creek is Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville, about 28 minutes' drive. Wonthaggi is the closest bigger centre to Archies Creek, about 15 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Archies Creek are Kids Playground in Wonthaggi (12 min), the playground at South Dudley Reserve in South Dudley (14 min), Kilcunda East Beach in Kilcunda (12 min), Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Wonthaggi (14 min), Powlett River West in Kilcunda (15 min) and Bass Coast Arts Society in Wonthaggi (14 min).
