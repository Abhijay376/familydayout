Leslie Street Pocket Park

Park in St Albans · Basic info

Leslie Street Pocket Park is a park in St Albans, in the Brimbank City Council area, good for all ages. Leslie St/Arthur St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 615 m away.
