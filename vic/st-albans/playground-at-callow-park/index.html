Playground at Callow Park

Playground in St Albans · Basic info

Playground at Callow Park in St Albans is a public playground in the Brimbank City area. For food, McDonald's is about 250 m away, and Alice’s Playspace is another option about 480 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 390 m from the playground, and bus 423 stops at Bingo Centre/Gratz St, a 3-minute walk away.
