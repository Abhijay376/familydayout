Abe Wood Reserve

Playground in Lovely Banks · Basic info

This is a public playground in Lovely Banks. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground. It's easiest by car, about 5 minutes from Lovely Banks.
