Tungamah in the Moira area has 1 playground and 6 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Tungamah Natural Features Reserve is the largest mapped park in Tungamah, at about 317 hectares. Tungamah Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting) and Unnamed P0093 Private Nature Reserve are the next largest. Tungamah also has Tungamah Lions Park, Tungumah Bushland Reserve and Tharanbegga H47 Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds in Tungamah include the playground at Tungamah Lions Park. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Tungamah.

Getting around

Tungamah has no train station; Benalla Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 43 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Tungamah. By car from Tungamah, Benalla is about 46 minutes away and Wangaratta about 46 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tungamah is Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah, about 42 minutes' drive. For a library, Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga is about 21 minutes' drive from Tungamah. The closest beach to Tungamah is Duffys Beach No 1 in Burramine, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Tungamah is the skate park at Federation Park in Cobram, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Tungamah is Cobram Cinema in Cobram, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Tungamah is Tunzafun in Yarrawonga, about 23 minutes' drive. Benalla is the closest bigger centre to Tungamah, about 46 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Tungamah are Duffys Beach No 1 in Burramine (21 min), Katamatite Historical Society Museum in Katamatite (18 min), the playground at E.W Kilgour Reserve in Katamatite (18 min), Gravel Bend Beach in Burramine (24 min), Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga (21 min) and Tunzafun in Yarrawonga (23 min).
