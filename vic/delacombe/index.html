Delacombe in the Ballarat area has 8 playgrounds and 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Delacombe is Leawarra Crescent Reserve, covering about 1 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Delacombe include Sandon Place Reserve, Woolway Court Reserve and Leawarra Crescent Park. Playgrounds around Delacombe include Holstein Close Playground, Continuance Park Playground and the playground at Doug Dean Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet and 1 park or playground with a council BBQ close by in Delacombe. The Bonshaw Creek Trail passes through Delacombe.

Getting around

There's no train station in Delacombe. The closest to Delacombe is Wendouree Railway Station (V/Line), about 4.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 24 and 25 stop in Delacombe. By car from Delacombe, Ballarat is about 7 minutes away, Daylesford about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Delacombe is Ballarat Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Lake Gardens, about 6 minutes' drive. For a library, Sebastopol Library in Sebastopol is about 5 minutes' drive from Delacombe. For a skate park, the skate park at M R Power Recreation Reserve in Sebastopol is about 6 minutes' drive from Delacombe. For a cinema, Showbiz Cinemas Ballarat in Smythes Creek is about 4 minutes' drive from Delacombe. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 7 minutes' drive from Delacombe. For a zoo or wildlife park, Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East is about 12 minutes' drive from Delacombe.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 8 playgrounds across Delacombe. Free days on the Bonshaw Creek Trail and in Delacombe's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Delacombe, Sebastopol has 14 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Delacombe, Alfredton has 17 playgrounds and a skate park. Winter Valley, next to Delacombe, has 6 playgrounds. Smythes Creek, next to Delacombe, has a cinema. Redan, Newington and Bonshaw also border Delacombe.
