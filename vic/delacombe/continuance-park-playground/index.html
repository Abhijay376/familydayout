Continuance Park Playground

Playground in Delacombe · Basic info

Continuance Park Playground in Delacombe is a public playground in the Ballarat City area. The Bonshaw Creek Trail is about 300 m away, and Holstein Close Playground is another option about 500 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 960 m from the playground, and bus 25 stops at Bellview Ct/Smythes Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
