Getting around

Balnarring Beach has no train station; Morradoo Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 7.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Balnarring Beach, so most trips start by car. By car from Balnarring Beach, Mornington is about 26 minutes away, Frankston about 29 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 71 minutes, without traffic.
