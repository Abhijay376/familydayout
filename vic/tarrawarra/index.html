Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tarrawarra is Tarrawarra G61 Bushland Reserve, covering about 5 hectares.

Getting around

Tarrawarra has no train station; Lilydale Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 685 and 965 stop in Tarrawarra. By car from Tarrawarra, Healesville is about 10 minutes away, Lilydale about 19 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 58 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tarrawarra is Healesville Outdoor Pool in Healesville, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest library to Tarrawarra is Yarra Plenty Regional Library in Christmas Hills, about 15 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at McKenzie Reserve in Yarra Glen is about 5 minutes' drive from Tarrawarra. The closest cinema to Tarrawarra is The Memo, Healesville in Healesville, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Tarrawarra is GAMR Laser Tag & VR in Chirnside Park, about 21 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Lyre Bird enclosure in Badger Creek is about 14 minutes' drive from Tarrawarra. Healesville is the closest bigger centre to Tarrawarra, about 10 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Tarrawarra are TarraWarra Museum of Art in Healesville (5 min), Eva and Marc Besen Centre in Healesville (5 min), the playground at McKenzie Reserve in Yarra Glen (5 min), the skate park at McKenzie Reserve in Yarra Glen (5 min), the playground at Hubbard Reserve in Yarra Glen (6 min) and The Memo, Healesville in Healesville (9 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tarrawarra.
