Fawcett is in the Murrindindi area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Fawcett, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Fawcett. The closest to Fawcett is Euroa Railway Station (V/Line), about 42 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Fawcett, so most trips start by car. By car from Fawcett, Mansfield is about 59 minutes away, Seymour about 67 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Fawcett is Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra, about 23 minutes' drive. For a library, Alexandra Library in Alexandra is about 21 minutes' drive from Fawcett. For a skate park, the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea is about 39 minutes' drive from Fawcett. Mansfield is the closest bigger centre to Fawcett, about 59 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Fawcett are Alexandra Library in Alexandra (21 min), the playground at Leckie Park in Alexandra (22 min), Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra (23 min), Alexandra Timber Tramway and Museum in Alexandra (23 min), Yarck Recreation Reserve Playground in Yarck (25 min) and the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea (39 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Fawcett.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Fawcett, Alexandra has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Yarck, next to Fawcett, has 1 playground. Koriella and Kanumbra also border Fawcett.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Fawcett, without traffic.
