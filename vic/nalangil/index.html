Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Nalangil, in the Colac Otway area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Nalangil is Nalangil Lake Reserve, covering about 49 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Nalangil. The closest to Nalangil is Colac Railway Station (V/Line), about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nalangil, so most trips start by car. By car from Nalangil, Colac is about 19 minutes away, Geelong about 83 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac is about 22 minutes' drive from Nalangil. For a skate park, the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt is about 24 minutes' drive from Nalangil. The closest cinema to Nalangil is Colac Cinemas in Colac, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Nalangil is Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac, about 20 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 19 minutes from Nalangil by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Nalangil, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Red Rock Scenic Reserve in Alvie (20 min), the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (18 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (19 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (20 min), Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (22 min) and the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (24 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Nalangil.
