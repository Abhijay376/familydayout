Nullawarre North is in the Moyne area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Nullawarre North, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Nullawarre North. The closest to Nullawarre North is Terang Railway Station (V/Line), about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nullawarre North, so most trips start by car. By car from Nullawarre North, Warrnambool is about 33 minutes away and Colac about 75 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon is about 22 minutes' drive from Nullawarre North. For a library, Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool is about 34 minutes' drive from Nullawarre North. For a beach, Buttress Point in The Cove is about 18 minutes' drive from Nullawarre North. The closest skate park to Nullawarre North is the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon, about 21 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool is about 33 minutes' drive from Nullawarre North. The closest indoor play centre to Nullawarre North is The Bowl in Warrnambool, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Nullawarre North is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 26 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 33 minutes from Nullawarre North by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Nullawarre North are Buttress Point in The Cove (18 min), Flaxman Beach in Nullawarre (19 min), the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (21 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (22 min), Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (26 min) and the playground at Peterborough Coastal Reserve (COM) in Peterborough (27 min).
