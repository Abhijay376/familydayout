Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Towaninny South, in the Buloke area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Towaninny South is Towaninny South Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 36 hectares. Ninyeunook Grassland Reserve and Ninyeunook I198 Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

Towaninny South has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 56 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Towaninny South, so most trips start by car. By car from Towaninny South, Swan Hill is about 75 minutes away and Stawell about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Towaninny South is Quambatook Memorial Pool in Quambatook, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest library to Towaninny South is Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook, about 27 minutes' drive. From Towaninny South, the nearest places for kids by road are Quambatook Lions Club Playground in Quambatook (27 min), Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook (27 min), Quambatook Historical Centre in Quambatook (27 min), Quambatook Memorial Pool in Quambatook (27 min), the playground at Hall Corner Park in Nullawil (29 min) and Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof (32 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Towaninny South.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Towaninny South, Wycheproof has 1 playground and a pool. Dumosa and Bunguluke also border Towaninny South.
