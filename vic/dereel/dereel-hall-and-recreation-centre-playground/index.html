Dereel Hall & Recreation Centre Playground

Playground in Dereel · Basic info

Dereel Hall & Recreation Centre Playground in Dereel is a public playground in the Golden Plains Shire area. The Kuruc-a-ruc trail passes about 40 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 10 m away. There's a public toilet about 70 m away, and bus Ballarat - Rokewood via Ross Creek stops at Community Centre/Colac - Ballarat Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
