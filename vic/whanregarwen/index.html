Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Whanregarwen, in the Murrindindi area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Whanregarwen is Black Range State Forest, covering about 266 hectares. Black Range State Forest is the next largest.

Getting around

Whanregarwen has no train station; Euroa Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 50 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Whanregarwen, so most trips start by car. By car from Whanregarwen, Seymour is about 47 minutes away, Mansfield about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Whanregarwen is Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra, about 11 minutes' drive. For a library, Alexandra Library in Alexandra is about 10 minutes' drive from Whanregarwen. For a skate park, the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea is about 19 minutes' drive from Whanregarwen. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 47 minutes from Whanregarwen by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Whanregarwen, the nearest places for kids by road are Alexandra Library in Alexandra (10 min), Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra (11 min), the playground at Leckie Park in Alexandra (11 min), Alexandra Timber Tramway and Museum in Alexandra (12 min), Yarck Recreation Reserve Playground in Yarck (15 min) and the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea (19 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Whanregarwen.

Nearby suburbs

Alexandra, next to Whanregarwen, has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Cathkin and Acheron also border Whanregarwen.
