Chisholm Street Reserve

Park in Black Hill · Official details

Chisholm Street Reserve is a park in Black Hill, in the Ballarat City Council area, good for all ages. Chisholm Street Reserve/Chisholm St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 1167 m away and dogs are allowed.
