Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Boomahnoomoonah in the Moira area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Boomahnoomoonah has no train station; Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 34 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Boomahnoomoonah, so most trips start by car. By car from Boomahnoomoonah, Wangaratta is about 34 minutes away and Benalla about 51 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Boomahnoomoonah is Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta, about 33 minutes' drive. For a library, Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga is about 18 minutes' drive from Boomahnoomoonah. The closest beach to Boomahnoomoonah is Forges Beach No 2 in Yarrawonga, about 25 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track on Victoria Street in Wahgunyah is about 34 minutes' drive from Boomahnoomoonah. The closest cinema to Boomahnoomoonah is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 34 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Tunzafun in Yarrawonga is about 20 minutes' drive from Boomahnoomoonah. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 34 minutes from Boomahnoomoonah by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Boomahnoomoonah are the playground at Peechelba Reserve in Peechelba (16 min), Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga (18 min), Tunzafun in Yarrawonga (20 min), Yarrawonga Mulwala Pioneer Museum in Yarrawonga (20 min), Lonsdale Reserve Playground in Yarrawonga (22 min) and Mulwala Waterslide in Yarrawonga (22 min).
