Ascot (Greater Bendigo) in the Greater Bendigo area has 3 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Ascot (Greater Bendigo) is Ascot Bushland Reserve, covering about 1 hectares. Collins Park is the next largest. Playgrounds around Ascot (Greater Bendigo) include the playground at Collins Park. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Ascot (Greater Bendigo).

Getting around

Ascot (Greater Bendigo) has no train station; Epsom Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 1.4 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 50 stops in Ascot (Greater Bendigo). By car from Ascot (Greater Bendigo), Bendigo is about 12 minutes away, Castlemaine about 44 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.
