Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Rathscar West in the Pyrenees area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Rathscar West has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Rathscar West, so most trips start by car. By car from Rathscar West, Maryborough is about 33 minutes away, Ararat about 62 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly is about 32 minutes' drive from Rathscar West. For a library, Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough is about 34 minutes' drive from Rathscar West. Maryborough is the closest bigger centre to Rathscar West, about 33 minutes away by car, and has a library. The closest places for kids by road from Rathscar West are the playground at Avoca Lions Park in Avoca (12 min), Courthouse in Avoca (12 min), the playground at Toll Bar Park in Lexton (32 min), Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (31 min), Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (32 min) and Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (37 min).
