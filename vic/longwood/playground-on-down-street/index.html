Longwood Recreation Reserve Playground

Playground in Longwood · Basic info

Longwood Recreation Reserve Playground is a public playground in Longwood, in the Strathbogie Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. You'll find a public toilet about 60 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 2 minutes from Longwood.
