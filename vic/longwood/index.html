Longwood in the Strathbogie area has 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Tenneriffe Flora Reserve is the largest mapped park in Longwood, at about 107 hectares. Monea North Nature Conservation Reserve and Monea South I41 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Playgrounds around Longwood include Longwood Recreation Reserve Playground. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Longwood.

Getting around

There's no train station in Longwood. The closest to Longwood is Euroa Railway Station (V/Line), about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Longwood, so most trips start by car. By car from Longwood, Seymour is about 35 minutes away, Benalla about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Longwood is Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa, about 15 minutes' drive. For a library, Euroa Library in Euroa is about 16 minutes' drive from Longwood. The closest skate park to Longwood is Euroa Skate Park in Euroa, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Longwood is Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa, about 16 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 35 minutes from Longwood by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Longwood, the nearest places for kids by road are Creightons Creek Recreation Reserve Playground in Creightons Creek (28 min), Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa (15 min), Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa (16 min), Farmers Arms Hotel Museum in Euroa (16 min), Euroa Skate Park in Euroa (16 min) and Euroa Library in Euroa (16 min).
