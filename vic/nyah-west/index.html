Getting around

Nyah West has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus tv stops in Nyah West. By car from Nyah West, Swan Hill is about 29 minutes away and Echuca about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Nyah West is Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill, about 28 minutes' drive. For a library, Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill is about 28 minutes' drive from Nyah West. The closest skate park to Nyah West is Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South, about 25 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill is about 29 minutes' drive from Nyah West. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Nyah West, about 29 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Nyah West, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Nyah Heritage Park in Nyah (9 min), the playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV) in Nyah (10 min), Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South (25 min), the bmx track at Piangil Memorial Park in Piangil (23 min), Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill (28 min) and Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill (28 min).
