Skate park on Monash Avenue

Skate, BMX or pump track in Nyah West · Basic info

Skate park on Monash Avenue is a skate, bmx or pump track in Nyah West, in the Swan Hill Rural City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. General Store/Monash Ave (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 95 m away with a baby change table.
