Our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in Cape Clear, in the Golden Plains area.

Landmarks

Cape Clear Recreation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Cape Clear, at about 3 hectares. Playgrounds around Cape Clear include Cape Clear & District Lions Park Playground. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Cape Clear.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cape Clear. The closest to Cape Clear is Wendouree Railway Station (V/Line), about 33 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cape Clear, so most trips start by car. By car from Cape Clear, Ballarat is about 36 minutes away, Colac about 67 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood is about 17 minutes' drive from Cape Clear. The closest library to Cape Clear is Sebastopol Library in Sebastopol, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Cape Clear is Dereel Skate Park in Dereel, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Cape Clear is Showbiz Cinemas Ballarat in Smythes Creek, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Cape Clear is Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point, about 36 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East is about 41 minutes' drive from Cape Clear. Ballarat is the closest bigger centre to Cape Clear, about 36 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Cape Clear, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Berringa Recreation Reserve in Berringa (13 min), Dereel Skate Park in Dereel (21 min), Rokewood Skate Park in Rokewood (17 min), Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood (17 min), Haddon Tramway Preservation Association in Haddon (26 min) and Showbiz Cinemas Ballarat in Smythes Creek (28 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Cape Clear.
