Goughs Bay is in the Mansfield area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Goughs Bay is Loyola G109 Bushland Reserve, covering about 1 hectares. Goughs Bay also has Forest Camp and Bracks Bridge. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Goughs Bay. Picnic areas in Goughs Bay include Forest Camp. For walks and rides, Mansfield - Goughs Bay, Goughs Bay Lookout and Forest Camp cross Goughs Bay.

Getting around

Goughs Bay has no train station; Benalla Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 71 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Goughs Bay, so most trips start by car. By car from Goughs Bay, Mansfield is about 25 minutes away and Benalla about 76 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Mansfield Outdoor Swimming Pool in Mansfield is about 26 minutes' drive from Goughs Bay. The closest library to Goughs Bay is Mansfield Library in Mansfield, about 25 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield is about 26 minutes' drive from Goughs Bay. For a cinema, Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield is about 25 minutes' drive from Goughs Bay. For a zoo or wildlife park, Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield is about 17 minutes' drive from Goughs Bay. The nearest larger centre is Mansfield, about 25 minutes from Goughs Bay by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Goughs Bay, the nearest places for kids by road are Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield (17 min), Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield (25 min), Mansfield Library in Mansfield (25 min), the playground at Mansfield Botanic Park in Mansfield (25 min), the playground at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (25 min) and the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (26 min).
