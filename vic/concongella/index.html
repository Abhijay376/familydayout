Concongella in the Northern Grampians area has 10 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Holden Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Concongella, at about 20 hectares. Kirkella I10 Bushland Reserve and Leviathan Historic and Cultural Features Reserve are the next largest. Concongella also has Bulgana Bushland Reserve, Walshs Streamside Reserve and Cocks Farm Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Concongella. The closest to Concongella is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Concongella, so most trips start by car. By car from Concongella, Stawell is about 6 minutes away, Ararat about 31 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell is about 6 minutes' drive from Concongella. The closest library to Concongella is Ararat Library in Ararat, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Concongella is Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans, about 27 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell is about 10 minutes' drive from Concongella. The closest cinema to Concongella is Astor Cinema in Ararat, about 31 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell is about 7 minutes' drive from Concongella. Stawell is the closest bigger centre to Concongella, about 6 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds and a pool. From Concongella, the nearest places for kids by road are Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell (6 min), Stawell Skate Park in Stawell (6 min), Gladstone Park Playground in Stawell (6 min), Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell (7 min), Railway Station Art and Crafts Gallery in Stawell (7 min) and the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell (10 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Concongella.

Nearby suburbs

Stawell, next to Concongella, has 6 playgrounds, a pool and a zoo or wildlife park. Next door to Concongella, Great Western has 1 playground. Greens Creek, Campbells Bridge and Bulgana also border Concongella.
