Stradbroke in the Wellington area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Stradbroke is Giffard (Rifle Range) Flora Reserve, covering about 1,092 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Stradbroke.

Getting around

Stradbroke has no train station; Sale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Stradbroke, so most trips start by car. By car from Stradbroke, Sale is about 20 minutes away, Traralgon about 55 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Indoor pool in Sale is about 19 minutes' drive from Stradbroke. For a library, Sale Library in Sale is about 18 minutes' drive from Stradbroke. For a beach, 90 Mile Beach in Seaspray is about 30 minutes' drive from Stradbroke. The closest skate park to Stradbroke is Sale Skate Park in Sale, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Stradbroke is Sale Cinemas in Sale, about 19 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Sale, about 20 minutes from Stradbroke by car, with 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Stradbroke are 90 Mile Beach in Seaspray (30 min), Historic Gunpowder Magazine in Sale (17 min), 90 Mile Beach Marine National Park in Giffard (35 min), Sale Historical Museum in Sale (18 min), Little McMillan Park Playground in Sale (18 min) and Sale Skate Park in Sale (18 min).

Best for

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Stradbroke, Seaspray has 1 playground and a beach. Giffard, next to Stradbroke, has a beach. Longford and Giffard West also border Stradbroke.
