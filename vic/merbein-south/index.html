Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Merbein South, in the Mildura area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Merbein South is Merbein South Flora Reserve, covering about 3 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Merbein South. The closest to Merbein South is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 188 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Merbein South, so most trips start by car. By car from Merbein South, Mildura is about 15 minutes away and Swan Hill about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Merbein Pool in Merbein is about 11 minutes' drive from Merbein South. The closest library to Merbein South is Merbein Library in Merbein, about 11 minutes' drive. For a beach, Apex Beach in Mildura is about 23 minutes' drive from Merbein South. The closest skate park to Merbein South is Merbein Skate Park in Merbein, about 15 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Deakin Cinema Complex in Mildura is about 15 minutes' drive from Merbein South. The closest indoor play centre to Merbein South is Laserpunk Laserforce in Mildura, about 15 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Putt Putt Family Fun Centre in Mildura is about 15 minutes' drive from Merbein South. The nearest larger centre is Mildura, about 15 minutes from Merbein South by car, with 26 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Merbein South are Merbein Library in Merbein (11 min), Merbein Pool in Merbein (11 min), The RAAF Air Museum in Mildura (9 min), the playground at Chaffey Park in Merbein (13 min), Merbein Skate Park in Merbein (15 min) and the playground at Hollywood Boulevard Drainage Reserve in Mildura (11 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Merbein South.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Merbein South, Wargan has 6 playgrounds and a library. Merbein, next to Merbein South, has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Koorlong and Birdwoodton also border Merbein South.
