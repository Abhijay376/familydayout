Our data lists 1 park or reserve in St Germains, in the Greater Shepparton area.

Landmarks

St Germains also has St Germains Bushland Reserve. For walks and rides, Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks crosses St Germains.

Getting around

St Germains has no train station; Mooroopna Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside St Germains, so most trips start by car. By car from St Germains, Shepparton is about 28 minutes away, Echuca about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to St Germains is Community Swimming Pool in Merrigum, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest library to St Germains is Kyabram Library And Community Hub in Kyabram, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest beach to St Germains is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 30 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the bmx track on Racecourse Road in Kyabram is about 26 minutes' drive from St Germains. The closest cinema to St Germains is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 28 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 28 minutes' drive from St Germains. The closest zoo or wildlife park to St Germains is Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram, about 26 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 28 minutes from St Germains by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From St Germains, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Undera Youth Spot in Undera (7 min), the playground at Drum Street Reserve in Kyabram (24 min), Kyabram Library And Community Hub in Kyabram (24 min), Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram (26 min), the bmx track on Racecourse Road in Kyabram (26 min) and Tatura Irrigation and Wartime Camps Museum in Tatura (21 min).

Best for

Free days on the Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks and in St Germains's parks.
