Playground at McKenzie Reserve

Playground in Yarra Glen · Basic info

This is a public playground in Yarra Glen. The Northern Yarra Valley Trail (under construction) passes about 100 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 100 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and buses 685 and 965 stop at Herbert St/Melba Hwy, a 3-minute walk away.
