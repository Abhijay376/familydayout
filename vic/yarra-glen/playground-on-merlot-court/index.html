Playground on Merlot Court

Playground in Yarra Glen · Basic info

Playground on Merlot Court is a public playground in Yarra Glen, in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. The Northern Yarra Valley Trail (under construction) passes about 300 m away, and Playground at Hubbard Reserve is another option about 450 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.2 km away, and buses 685 and 965 stop at Melba Hwy/Armstrong Gr, a 9-minute walk away.
