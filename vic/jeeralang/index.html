Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Jeeralang in the Latrobe area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Jeeralang. The closest to Jeeralang is Morwell Railway Station (V/Line), about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Jeeralang, so most trips start by car. By car from Jeeralang, Traralgon is about 45 minutes away, Warragul about 71 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Latrobe Leisure - Churchill in Churchill is about 28 minutes' drive from Jeeralang. For a library, Churchill Library in Churchill is about 27 minutes' drive from Jeeralang. The closest skate park to Jeeralang is Churchill Skate Park in Churchill, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Jeeralang is Village 8 in Morwell, about 36 minutes' drive. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Jeeralang, about 45 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. From Jeeralang, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Reservoir Park in Churchill (27 min), Walkley Park in Churchill (28 min), Churchill Library in Churchill (27 min), Churchill Skate Park in Churchill (28 min), Latrobe Leisure - Churchill in Churchill (28 min) and ARC Yinnar in Yinnar (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Jeeralang.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Jeeralang, Churchill has 9 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Jeeralang Junction, Hazelwood South, Hazelwood North and Grand Ridge also border Jeeralang.
