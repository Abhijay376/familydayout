Glenorchy Playground

Playground in Glenorchy · Official details

Glenorchy Playground is a public playground in Glenorchy, in the Northern Grampians Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and coaches stop at Wills St/Edwards St, a 2-minute walk away.
