Getting around

Skinners Flat has no train station; Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 57 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Skinners Flat, so most trips start by car. By car from Skinners Flat, Bendigo is about 70 minutes away, Maryborough about 82 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Skinners Flat is Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn, about 7 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 23 minutes' drive from Skinners Flat. The closest places for kids by road from Skinners Flat are Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (7 min), Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (7 min), Rex Theatre in Charlton (23 min), Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (23 min), the playground at Rotary Park in Charlton (23 min) and the playground at Gordon Park in Charlton (24 min).
