Shepparton North is in the Greater Shepparton area, and our data lists 4 playgrounds and 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Grammar Park Gardens is the largest mapped park in Shepparton North, at about 1 hectares. Playgrounds in Shepparton North include Northside Estate Playground, the playground at Grammar Park Gardens and Warrumbungle Drive Shepparton Playground. Our data shows 3 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Shepparton North.

Getting around

There's no train station in Shepparton North. The closest to Shepparton North is Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 4 stops in Shepparton North. By car from Shepparton North, Shepparton is about 7 minutes away, Benalla about 56 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.
