Hibiscus-Buxton Walkway

Park in Meadow Heights · Basic info

Hibiscus-Buxton Walkway is a park in Meadow Heights, in the Hume City Council area, good for all ages. Kanooka Gr/Lightwood Cres (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1894 m away with a baby change table.
