Meadow Heights in the Hume area has 8 playgrounds and 52 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Broadmeadows Valley Park is the largest mapped park in Meadow Heights, at about 12 hectares. Broadmeadows Valley Park and Buchan Street Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Meadow Heights include Fantasy Park, Tyrell Reserve and Redwood Reserve. Playgrounds in Meadow Heights include the playground at Redwood Reserve, the playground at Canadian Walkway and the playground at Rokewood Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Meadow Heights. Meadow Heights Shopping Centre is the local shopping centre. For walks and rides, Broadmeadows Valley Trail, Greenvale Creek Trail and Yuroke Creek Trail cross Meadow Heights. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Meadow Heights for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Meadow Heights. The closest to Meadow Heights is Coolaroo Railway Station (metro), about 1.2 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 484, 541, 542, 543, 901 and 953 stop in Meadow Heights. By car from Meadow Heights, Broadmeadows is about 8 minutes away, Sunshine about 20 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 27 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Splash Aqua Park and Leisure Centre in Craigieburn is about 11 minutes' drive from Meadow Heights. For a library, Hume Global Learning Centre - Broadmeadows in Broadmeadows is about 6 minutes' drive from Meadow Heights. For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 30 minutes' drive from Meadow Heights. The closest skate park to Meadow Heights is the skate park at Arena Recreation Reserve in Roxburgh Park, about 7 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Hoyts in Broadmeadows is about 6 minutes' drive from Meadow Heights. The closest indoor play centre to Meadow Heights is Timezone in Craigieburn, about 12 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 8 playgrounds across Meadow Heights. Free days on the Broadmeadows Valley Trail and in Meadow Heights's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Meadow Heights, Greenvale has 21 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Meadow Heights, Westmeadows has 10 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Meadow Heights, Roxburgh Park has 14 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Meadow Heights, Attwood has 3 playgrounds. Coolaroo also borders Meadow Heights.
