Kenley-Uplands-Ceres Reserve

Park in Meadow Heights · Basic info

Kenley-Uplands-Ceres Reserve is a park in Meadow Heights, in the Hume City Council area, good for all ages. Hazeldene St/Barry Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 508 m away.
