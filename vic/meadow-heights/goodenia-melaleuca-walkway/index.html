Goodenia-Melaleuca Walkway

Park in Meadow Heights · Basic info

Goodenia-Melaleuca Walkway is a park in Meadow Heights, in the Hume City Council area, good for all ages. Banksia Pl/Lightwood Cres (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1154 m away.
