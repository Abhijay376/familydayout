Lightwood-Redwood Walkway

Park in Meadow Heights · Basic info

Lightwood-Redwood Walkway is a park in Meadow Heights, in the Hume City Council area, good for all ages. Magnolia Bvd/Lightwood Cres (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1537 m away with a baby change table.
