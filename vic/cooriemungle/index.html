Cooriemungle in the Corangamite area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Cooriemungle Creek Flora Reserve is the largest mapped park in Cooriemungle, at about 853 hectares. Cooriemungle Reference Area is the next largest.

Getting around

Cooriemungle has no train station; Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 34 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cooriemungle, so most trips start by car. By car from Cooriemungle, Warrnambool is about 61 minutes away and Colac about 66 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cooriemungle is Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Cooriemungle is Rutledge Creek Beach in Port Campbell, about 21 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon is about 17 minutes' drive from Cooriemungle. The closest places for kids by road from Cooriemungle are Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (17 min), the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (17 min), Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell (17 min), Rutledge Creek Beach in Port Campbell (21 min), Sherbrooke Estuary in Port Campbell (22 min) and the playground at Peterborough Coastal Reserve (COM) in Peterborough (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Cooriemungle.

Nearby suburbs

Princetown, next to Cooriemungle, has 1 playground and a beach. Next door to Cooriemungle, Port Campbell has 1 playground, a beach and a skate park. Scotts Creek, Newfield, Kennedys Creek and Cowleys Creek also border Cooriemungle.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Cooriemungle, without traffic.
