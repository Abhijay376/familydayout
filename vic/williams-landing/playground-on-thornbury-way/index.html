Thornbury Way Playground

Playground in Williams Landing · Official details

Thornbury Way Playground in Williams Landing is a small local playground in the Wyndham City area. The Federation Trail passes about 350 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 800 m away, and buses 151 and 153 stop at Forsyth Rd/Ashcroft Ave, a 4-minute walk away.

Features

  • Size: Small local (council data)
  • Fenced: No (council data)
  • Toilet: 800 m walk, baby change, accessible toilet, Mon-Wed 9am-5:30pm, Thu-Fri 9am-9pm, Sun 10am-5pm, Sat 9am-5pm
  • Trail: Federation Trail (350 m)
  • Public transport: Buses 151 and 153 at Forsyth Rd/Ashcroft Ave (4 min walk); Williams Landing Railway Station (1.2 km)
  • Drive time: 3 min from Williams Landing, 5 min from Seabrook, 6 min from Laverton
