Playground at Packer Way Park

Playground in Williams Landing · Official details

This is a neighbourhood playground in Williams Landing, looked after by Wyndham City Council. Playground at Williams Landing Boulevard Reserve is another option about 350 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 710 m away, and buses 150 and 152 stop at Delaney Bvd/Palmers Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
