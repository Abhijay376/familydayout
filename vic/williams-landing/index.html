Williams Landing is on the eastern edge of Wyndham, with 7 playgrounds and 11 parks and reserves in our data. It has its own train station and shopping centre, and the Federation Trail runs through it.

Landmarks

Williams Landing Boulevard Reserve is the largest park, at about 9 hectares, and Addison Park is the next largest at about 5 hectares. The Federation Trail crosses the suburb, so there's a long off-road route for bikes and scooters on the doorstep. Williams Landing Shopping Centre is the main place for food and supplies. Playgrounds sit in parks around the suburb, including the one at Bushlark Crescent Park, about 240 metres from the suburb centre by road.

Getting around

Williams Landing Station is on the metro Werribee line, about 1.8 kilometres from the suburb centre. Buses 150, 151, 152, 153, 494, 495, 497 and 949 run through the suburb, and all of them stop at or near the station. By car, Hoppers Crossing is about 7 minutes away, Point Cook about 10, Werribee about 11 and the Melbourne CBD about 26, without traffic. The Federation Trail also gives a car-free route to Hoppers Crossing.

Best for

Toddlers, with seven playgrounds across the suburb. Families on bikes, because the Federation Trail passes right through. Quick trips into the city by train. On a rainy day, head to Hoppers Crossing or Point Cook, which have the cinema and a library.

Nearby suburbs

Point Cook is close by and has the coast, three skate parks and the RAAF Museum. Hoppers Crossing, about 7 minutes away, has Village Cinemas, Timezone and AquaPulse. Laverton North has Lawrie Emmins Reserve and its skate park.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Williams Landing, without traffic.
