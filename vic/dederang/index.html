Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Dederang, in the Alpine area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Dederang is Dederang Streamside Reserve, covering about 7 hectares.

Getting around

Dederang has no train station; Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 42 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Albury - Mt Beauty via Baranduda and Tawonga South route stop in Dederang. By car from Dederang, Wodonga is about 41 minutes away and Bright about 52 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Dederang is the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford, about 40 minutes' drive. The closest library to Dederang is Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Dederang is Pebble Beach in Tawonga South, about 30 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Skate Park in Yackandandah is about 26 minutes' drive from Dederang. For a cinema, Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana is about 35 minutes' drive from Dederang. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 41 minutes from Dederang by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Dederang, the nearest places for kids by road are Skate Park in Yackandandah (26 min), the playground at Sir Isaac Isaacs Park in Yackandandah (26 min), the playground at Banksia Park in Tangambalanga (26 min), Pebble Beach in Tawonga South (30 min), Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford (38 min) and Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford (38 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Dederang.

Nearby suburbs

Kancoona and Glen Creek also border Dederang.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Dederang, without traffic.
