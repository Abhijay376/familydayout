Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Dhurringile, in the Greater Shepparton area.

Landmarks

Murchison North Scenic Reserve is the largest mapped park in Dhurringile, at about 2 hectares.

Getting around

Dhurringile has no train station; Murchison East Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 8.1 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dhurringile, so most trips start by car. By car from Dhurringile, Shepparton is about 30 minutes away, Seymour about 54 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Dhurringile is Tatura Swimming complex in Tatura, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest library to Dhurringile is Tatura Library in Tatura, about 16 minutes' drive. For a beach, Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton is about 32 minutes' drive from Dhurringile. The closest skate park to Dhurringile is the skate park at Tatura Memorial Park in Tatura, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Dhurringile is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Dhurringile is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 30 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kidstown in Mooroopna is about 28 minutes' drive from Dhurringile. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 30 minutes from Dhurringile by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Dhurringile, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Murchison Youth Spot in Murchison (14 min), the playground at Hopkins Street Park in Tatura (16 min), Tatura Library in Tatura (16 min), Tatura Irrigation and Wartime Camps Museum in Tatura (16 min), Tatura Swimming complex in Tatura (18 min) and the skate park at Tatura Memorial Park in Tatura (18 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Dhurringile.

Nearby suburbs

Tatura, next to Dhurringile, has 8 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Toolamba, next to Dhurringile, has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Dhurringile, Murchison has 2 playgrounds. Toolamba West and Murchison North also border Dhurringile.
