Cornishtown is in the Indigo area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Grand Junction Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Cornishtown, at about 10 hectares. Chiltern I90 Bushland Reserve and Chiltern West H125 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. For walks and rides, Bowser-Wagunya Preferred Route and Rutherglen - Chiltern cross Cornishtown.

Getting around

Cornishtown has no train station; Chiltern Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 8.1 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cornishtown, so most trips start by car. By car from Cornishtown, Wodonga is about 38 minutes away and Wangaratta about 47 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rutherglen War Memorial Swimming Pool in Rutherglen is about 17 minutes' drive from Cornishtown. The closest library to Cornishtown is Rutherglen Public Library in Rutherglen, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Cornishtown is Sandy Beach in Beechworth, about 37 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Cornishtown is the pump track on Victoria Street in Wahgunyah, about 23 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana is about 41 minutes' drive from Cornishtown. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 38 minutes from Cornishtown by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Cornishtown are Chiltern Athenaeum Museum in Chiltern (15 min), Martin Park Playground in Chiltern (15 min), Rutherglen Public Library in Rutherglen (16 min), Apex Park Playground in Rutherglen (16 min), Rutherglen War Memorial Swimming Pool in Rutherglen (17 min) and the pump track on Victoria Street in Wahgunyah (23 min).

Best for

Free days on the Bowser-Wagunya Preferred Route and in Cornishtown's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Cornishtown, Rutherglen has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Next door to Cornishtown, Chiltern has 2 playgrounds. Barnawartha also borders Cornishtown.
