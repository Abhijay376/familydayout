R J Phelan Reserve

Park in Mitiamo · Basic info

R J Phelan Reserve is a park in Mitiamo, in the Loddon Shire Council area, good for all ages. Glossop St/Bendigo - Pyramid Rd (coach) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 52 m away.
