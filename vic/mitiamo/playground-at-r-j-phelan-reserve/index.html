Playground at R J Phelan Reserve

Playground in Mitiamo · Basic info

Playground at R J Phelan Reserve is a public playground in Mitiamo, in the Loddon Shire area. Mitiamo Swimming Pool is about 70 m away, and East Loddon Historical Society is about 170 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 60 m from the playground, and coaches stop at Glossop St/Bendigo - Pyramid Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
