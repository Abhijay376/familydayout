Playground at Kett Street Reserve

Playground in Nunawading · Basic info

Playground at Kett Street Reserve in Nunawading is a public playground in the Whitehorse City area. The Koonung Creek Trail is about 200 m away, and Darvall Playspace Playground is another option about 360 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.1 km from the playground, and buses 271 and 273 stop at Busana Way/Junction Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
