Our data lists 14 playgrounds and 19 parks and reserves in Nunawading, in the Whitehorse area. Beyond the parks, Nunawading has a library.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Nunawading is Yarrandoo Park, covering about 12 hectares. Walker Park and R.E.Gray Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Nunawading include Nicoll Park, Tunstall Park and Dagola Reserve. Playgrounds around Nunawading include Yamminga Park, Wood Park Playground and the playground at Nicoll Park. Our data shows 8 public toilets in Nunawading. Places to visit in Nunawading include Nunawading Library. Brand Smart Outlet Mall is the local shopping centre. For walks and rides, EastLink Trail, Koonung Creek Trail and Whitehorse EW4 cross Nunawading. Our data lists 13 cafes and family restaurants in Nunawading for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Nunawading Railway Station (metro: Belgrave Line and Lilydale Line) in Nunawading. Buses 270, 271, 273, 303, 735, 736, 738, 901, 902 and 907 stop in Nunawading. V/Line coaches stop in Nunawading. By car from Nunawading, Ringwood is about 7 minutes away, Box Hill about 9 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 26 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Nunawading is Aqualink Nunawading in Forest Hill, about 5 minutes' drive. For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 23 minutes' drive from Nunawading. For a skate park, Ringwood Skate Park in Ringwood is about 6 minutes' drive from Nunawading. For a cinema, Hoyts in Forest Hill is about 5 minutes' drive from Nunawading. The closest indoor play centre to Nunawading is Zone Bowling in Forest Hill, about 4 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale is about 20 minutes' drive from Nunawading.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 14 playgrounds across Nunawading. Rainy days at the library in Nunawading. Free days on the EastLink Trail and in Nunawading's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Nunawading, Blackburn has 17 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Nunawading, Mitcham has 16 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Forest Hill, next to Nunawading, has 9 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. Donvale, next to Nunawading, has 14 playgrounds. Vermont and Blackburn North also border Nunawading.
