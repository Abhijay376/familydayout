Playground at Tunstall Park

Playground in Nunawading · Basic info

Playground at Tunstall Park is a public playground in Nunawading, in the Whitehorse City area. The Whitehorse EW2-A is about 30 m away, and for food, The Peddler is about 350 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 630 m from the playground, and buses 273 and 902 stop at Tunstall Ave/Springvale Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
