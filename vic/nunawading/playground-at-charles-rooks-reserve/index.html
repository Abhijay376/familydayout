Playground at Charles Rooks Reserve

Playground in Nunawading · Basic info

Playground at Charles Rooks Reserve is a public playground in Nunawading, in the Whitehorse City area. The Whitehorse EW4 is about 30 m away, and Playground at Dagola Reserve is another option about 230 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 880 m from the playground, and bus 738 stops at Charles Rooks Reserve/Rooks Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
