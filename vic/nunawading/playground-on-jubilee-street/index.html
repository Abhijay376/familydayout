Playground on Jubilee Street

Playground in Nunawading · Basic info

Playground on Jubilee Street in Nunawading is a public playground in the Whitehorse City area. The Whitehorse EW2-A passes about 60 m away, and Playground on Elm Street is another option about 350 m away. There's a public toilet about 690 m away, and bus 271 stops at Elm St/Goodwin St, a 3-minute walk away.
