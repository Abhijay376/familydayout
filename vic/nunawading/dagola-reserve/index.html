Dagola Reserve

Park in Nunawading · Basic info

Dagola Reserve is a park in Nunawading, in the Whitehorse City Council area, good for all ages. Charles Rooks Reserve/Rooks Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1378 m away.
