Kalkee is in the Horsham area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Kalkee, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kalkee. The closest to Kalkee is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 104 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kalkee, so most trips start by car. By car from Kalkee, Horsham is about 20 minutes away and Stawell about 75 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham is about 22 minutes' drive from Kalkee. For a library, Dimboola Library in Dimboola is about 33 minutes' drive from Kalkee. For a skate park, Velodrome in Horsham is about 22 minutes' drive from Kalkee. The closest cinema to Kalkee is Centre Cinemas in Horsham, about 22 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Horsham, about 20 minutes from Kalkee by car, with 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Kalkee are the playground at Charisma Park in Horsham (20 min), the playground at Elbourne Park in Horsham (21 min), Centre Cinemas in Horsham (22 min), Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham (22 min), Velodrome in Horsham (22 min) and Horsham Skate Park in Horsham (23 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Kalkee.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Kalkee, Dimboola has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Longerenong, next to Kalkee, has 1 playground. Murra Warra and Dooen also border Kalkee.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Kalkee, without traffic.
