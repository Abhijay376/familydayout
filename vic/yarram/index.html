Yarram in the Wellington area has 1 playground and 5 parks and reserves in our data. Yarram also has a library, a pool, a museum or gallery and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Yarram is Won Wron Flora Reserve, covering about 125 hectares. Woodside Flora Reserve and Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve are the next largest. Yarram also has Memorial Park and Woranga Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds around Yarram include the playground at Memorial Park. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Yarram. In Yarram you'll also find Yarram Historical Society Museum, Yarram Public Pool and Yarram Library - Temporary Site. For older kids in Yarram there's the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve. Yarram Plaza is the local shopping centre. The Great Southern Rail Trail passes through Yarram. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Yarram for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Yarram. The closest to Yarram is Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line), about 42 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Yarram - Traralgon via Gormandale route stop in Yarram. V/Line coaches stop in Yarram. By car from Yarram, Sale is about 58 minutes away, Traralgon about 62 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest beach to Yarram is Woodside_Beach in Woodside Beach, about 28 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Sale, about 58 minutes from Yarram by car, with 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Yarram, the nearest places for kids by road are Port Albert Maritime Museum in Port Albert (13 min), Woodside_Beach in Woodside Beach (28 min), Woodside Beach Road Playground in Woodside Beach (28 min), Woolami in Woodside Beach (30 min), Toora Pool in Toora (32 min) and the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora (34 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the library and museum or gallery in Yarram. Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram. Free days on the Great Southern Rail Trail and in Yarram's parks.
