Playground at Memorial Park

Playground in Yarram · Basic info

Playground at Memorial Park in Yarram is a public playground in the Wellington Shire area. The Great Southern Rail Trail passes about 260 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 280 m away. There's a public toilet about 970 m away, and bus Yarram - Traralgon via Gormandale stops at Memorial Park/Grant St, a 6-minute walk away.
