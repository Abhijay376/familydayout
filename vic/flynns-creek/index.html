Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Flynns Creek in the Latrobe area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Flynns Creek has no train station; Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Flynns Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Flynns Creek, Traralgon is about 24 minutes away, Sale about 54 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon is about 25 minutes' drive from Flynns Creek. For a library, Morwell Library in Morwell is about 31 minutes' drive from Flynns Creek. For a skate park, Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon is about 26 minutes' drive from Flynns Creek. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 30 minutes' drive from Flynns Creek. The nearest larger centre is Traralgon, about 24 minutes from Flynns Creek by car, with 38 playgrounds and a pool. From Flynns Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Hourigan Park in Traralgon (22 min), the playground at Eric Taylor Reserve in Traralgon (22 min), Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon (25 min), Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon (26 min), Rosedale Swimming Pool in Rosedale (30 min) and Powerworks in Morwell (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Flynns Creek.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Flynns Creek, without traffic.
