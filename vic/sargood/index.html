Sargood is in the Wellington area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Sargood, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Wellington River Walking Track, Riggall Spur Track and Clive Lanigan Walking Track cross Sargood.

Getting around

There's no train station in Sargood. The closest to Sargood is Stratford Railway Station (V/Line), about 54 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Sargood, so most trips start by car. By car from Sargood, Sale is about 4 hours away and Traralgon about 4 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Sargood on the Wellington River Walking Track.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Sargood, without traffic.
