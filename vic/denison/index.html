Denison is in the Wellington area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Denison, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Denison has no train station; Rosedale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Denison, so most trips start by car. By car from Denison, Sale is about 27 minutes away, Traralgon about 34 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield is about 6 minutes' drive from Denison. For a library, Sale Library in Sale is about 26 minutes' drive from Denison. For a skate park, Pump Track in Heyfield is about 6 minutes' drive from Denison. The closest cinema to Denison is Sale Cinemas in Sale, about 27 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Sale, about 27 minutes from Denison by car, with 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Denison, the nearest places for kids by road are Apex Park Playground in Heyfield (5 min), Heyfield and District Museum in Heyfield (5 min), Pump Track in Heyfield (6 min), Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield (6 min), Graves Court - Perry Court - Wickham Court Playground in Heyfield (7 min) and Rosedale Swimming Pool in Rosedale (16 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Denison.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Denison, Heyfield has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Denison, Cowwarr has 1 playground. Riverslea, Nambrok and Fulham also border Denison.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Denison, without traffic.
