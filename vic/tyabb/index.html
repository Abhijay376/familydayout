Tyabb is in the Mornington Peninsula area, and our data lists 5 playgrounds and 9 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tyabb is Western Port Coastal Reserve (COM), covering about 728 hectares. Tyabb Foreshore Reserve and Olivers Creek Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Tyabb include Rotaract Park, Gordon Rolfe Reserve and Delepan Drive Reserve. Playgrounds around Tyabb include the playground at Rotaract Park, the playground at Delepan Drive Reserve and the playground at Tyabb Central Reserve. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Tyabb. For walks and rides, Western Port Bay Trail crosses Tyabb. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Tyabb for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Tyabb has a train station: Tyabb Railway Station (metro: Stony Point Line). Buses 782, 783 and 886 stop in Tyabb. By car from Tyabb, Frankston is about 14 minutes away, Mornington about 16 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 55 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Pelican Park Recreation Centre in Hastings is about 7 minutes' drive from Tyabb. For a library, Hastings Library in Hastings is about 7 minutes' drive from Tyabb. For a beach, Micks Beach in French Island is about 10 minutes' drive from Tyabb. The closest skate park to Tyabb is Somerville Skate Park in Somerville, about 6 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Frankston is about 16 minutes' drive from Tyabb. The closest indoor play centre to Tyabb is Kidz Shed in Hastings, about 4 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Tyabb is Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale, about 10 minutes' drive.

Best for

Younger kids at Tyabb's 5 local playgrounds. Free days on the Western Port Bay Trail and in Tyabb's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Tyabb, Hastings has 11 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Tyabb, Somerville has 13 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Tyabb, Moorooduc has 3 playgrounds.
