Tom Groggin is in the Towong area, and our data lists 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Indi Wilderness Zone is the largest mapped park in Tom Groggin, at about 13,384 hectares. Davies Plain Remote and Natural Area and Tom Groggin Reference Area are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Tom Groggin include Mount Murphy Historic Area. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Tom Groggin. For walks and rides, Australian Alps Walking Track and Mount Pinnibar Track cross Tom Groggin.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tom Groggin. The closest to Tom Groggin is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 119 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tom Groggin, so most trips start by car. By car from Tom Groggin, Wodonga is about 4 hours away and Wangaratta about 5 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Free days on the Australian Alps Walking Track and in Tom Groggin's parks.
