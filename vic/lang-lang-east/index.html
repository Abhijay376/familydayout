Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Lang Lang East in the Cardinia area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Lang Lang East has no train station; Garfield Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lang Lang East, so most trips start by car. By car from Lang Lang East, Pakenham is about 30 minutes away, Warragul about 38 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 73 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lang Lang East is Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest library to Lang Lang East is Poowong library in Poowong, about 20 minutes' drive. For a beach, Corinella Beach in Corinella is about 39 minutes' drive from Lang Lang East. The closest skate park to Lang Lang East is Nyora skate park in Nyora, about 15 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 36 minutes' drive from Lang Lang East. The closest indoor play centre to Lang Lang East is Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Lang Lang East is Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville, about 27 minutes' drive. Pakenham is the closest bigger centre to Lang Lang East, about 30 minutes away by car, and has 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play. From Lang Lang East, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at James Street Reserve in Lang Lang (12 min), Nyora skate park in Nyora (15 min), the playground at Dick Jones Park in Lang Lang (11 min), Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong (20 min), Poowong library in Poowong (20 min) and the skate park at Cochrane Park in Koo Wee Rup (21 min).
