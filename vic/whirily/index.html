Whirily in the Buloke area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Karyrie I193 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Whirily, at about 32 hectares. Karyrie I194 Bushland Reserve and Karyrie I296 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Whirily include Carapugna Tennis Court Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Whirily has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 81 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Whirily, so most trips start by car. By car from Whirily, Swan Hill is about 74 minutes away and Horsham about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Whirily is Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Whirily is Birchip Skate Park in Birchip, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Whirily are the playground at Birchip Shire Soldiers Memorial Park in Birchip (11 min), Birchip Skate Park in Birchip (11 min), Birchip History Museum in Birchip (11 min), the playground at J.E Taylor Sunset Park in Birchip (12 min), Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock (22 min) and Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Whirily.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Whirily, Birchip has 2 playgrounds and a skate park. Nullawil, next to Whirily, has 1 playground. Next door to Whirily, Narraport has 1 playground. Karyrie, Jil Jil and Dumosa also border Whirily.
