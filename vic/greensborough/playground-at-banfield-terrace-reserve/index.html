Playground at Banfield Terrace Reserve

Playground in Greensborough · Basic info

Playground at Banfield Terrace Reserve is a public playground in Greensborough, in the Banyule City area. The Greensborough Bypass Trail is about 90 m away, and Playground at Kalparrin Gardens is another option about 490 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 620 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 4 minutes from Greensborough.
