Kalparrin Gardens

Park in Greensborough · Basic info

Kalparrin Gardens is a park in Greensborough, in the Banyule City Council area, good for all ages. Warriparri Cres/Plenty River Dr (bus) is a 12-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 11 m away.
