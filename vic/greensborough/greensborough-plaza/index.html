Greensborough Plaza

Shopping centre in Greensborough · Official details

Greensborough Plaza is a shopping centre in Greensborough, in the Banyule City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Church St/Grimshaw St (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 10 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: Mon: 9:00am to 5:30pm; Tue: 9:00am to 5:30pm; Wed: 9:00am to 5:30pm; Thu: 9:00am to 9:00pm; Fri: 9:00am to 9:00pm; Sat: 9:00am to 5:00pm; Sun: 10:00am to 5:00pm.
