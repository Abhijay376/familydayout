Kirrip Playspace

Playground in Greensborough · Basic info

Kirrip Playspace in Greensborough is a public playground in the Nillumbik Shire area. The Greensborough Bypass Trail passes about 140 m away, and Diamond Valley Library is about 210 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and buses 343 and 901 stop at Civic Dr/Diamond Creek Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
