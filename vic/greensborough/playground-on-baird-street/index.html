Playground on Baird Street

Playground in Greensborough · Basic info

Playground on Baird Street is a public playground in Greensborough, in the Banyule City area. The Plenty River Trail is about 90 m away, and Playground at Montmorency Park is another option about 340 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 690 m from the playground, and buses 293 and 901 stop at Montmorency Park/Para Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
