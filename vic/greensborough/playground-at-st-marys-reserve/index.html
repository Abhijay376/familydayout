Playground at St Marys Reserve

Playground in Greensborough · Basic info

Playground at St Marys Reserve in Greensborough is a public playground in the Banyule City area. The Greensborough Bypass Trail is about 180 m away, and Greensborough War Memorial Sculptures is about 440 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 460 m from the playground, and buses 566 and 902 stop at St Mary's Parish Primary School/Grimshaw St , a 2-minute walk away.
