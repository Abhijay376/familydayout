Plenty River Reserve

Park in Greensborough · Basic info

Plenty River Reserve is a park in Greensborough, in the Banyule City Council area, good for all ages. Joyce Ave/Main St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 182 m away.
