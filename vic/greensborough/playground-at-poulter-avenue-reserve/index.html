Playground at Poulter Avenue Reserve

Playground in Greensborough · Basic info

Playground at Poulter Avenue Reserve in Greensborough is a public playground in the Banyule City area. The Plenty River Trail passes about 10 m away, and WaterMarc is about 410 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and trains stop at Greensborough Railway Station, a 7-minute walk away.
