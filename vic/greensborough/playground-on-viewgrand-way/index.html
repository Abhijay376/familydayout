Playground on Viewgrand Way

Playground in Greensborough · Basic info

Playground on Viewgrand Way is a public playground in Greensborough, in the Nillumbik Shire area. The Banyule LBN9 passes about 380 m away, and Playground on Oldstead Road is another option about 200 m away. There's a public toilet about 480 m away, and buses 343 and 901 stop at Plenty River Dr/Diamond Creek Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
