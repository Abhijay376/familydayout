Playground at Collendina Reserve

Playground in Greensborough · Basic info

Playground at Collendina Reserve is a public playground in Greensborough, in the Nillumbik Shire area. The M80 Trail passes about 20 m away, and Worcester East Park Playground is another option about 290 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.1 km away, and bus 566 stops at Hamilton Ct/Gillingham St, a 7-minute walk away.
