Playground on Oldstead Road

Playground in Greensborough · Basic info

Playground on Oldstead Road in Greensborough is a public playground in the Nillumbik Shire area. The Banyule LBN9 passes about 290 m away, and Playground on Viewgrand Way is another option about 200 m away. There's a public toilet about 680 m away, and buses 343 and 901 stop at Oldstead Rd/Diamond Creek Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
