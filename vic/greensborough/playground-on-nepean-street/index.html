Playground on Nepean Street

Playground in Greensborough · Basic info

Playground on Nepean Street is a public playground in Greensborough, in the Banyule City area. The Plenty River Trail is about 80 m away, and Playground at Montmorency Park is another option about 330 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 300 m from the playground, and buses 293 and 901 stop at Montmorency Secondary College/Para Rd , a 6-minute walk away.
