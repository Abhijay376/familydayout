Plenty River Walk

Park in Greensborough · Basic info

Plenty River Walk is a park in Greensborough, in the Nillumbik Shire Council area, good for all ages. Punkerri Cct/Plenty River Dr (bus) is a 9-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1430 m away with a baby change table.
