Lethbridge Russell Street Reserve Playground

Playground in Lethbridge · Basic info

Lethbridge Russell Street Reserve Playground in Lethbridge is a public playground in the Golden Plains Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 40 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and coaches stop at General Store/Midland Hwy, a 3-minute walk away.
