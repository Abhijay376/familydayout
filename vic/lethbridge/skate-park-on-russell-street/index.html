Skate park on Russell Street

Skate, BMX or pump track in Lethbridge · Basic info

Skate park on Russell Street is a skate, bmx or pump track in Lethbridge, in the Golden Plains Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. General Store/Midland Hwy (coach) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 46 m away.
