Lethbridge is in the Golden Plains area, and our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lethbridge is Boonderoo Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 186 hectares. Playgrounds around Lethbridge include Lethbridge Russell Street Reserve Playground. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Lethbridge.

Getting around

Lethbridge has no train station; North Shore Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 25 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Lethbridge. By car from Lethbridge, Geelong is about 33 minutes away, Torquay about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 81 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Goodfellow Aquatic Centre in Bell Post Hill is about 23 minutes' drive from Lethbridge. The closest library to Lethbridge is Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn, about 11 minutes' drive. For a beach, Disability beach in Steiglitz is about 26 minutes' drive from Lethbridge. The closest skate park to Lethbridge is Bannockburn Skate Park in Bannockburn, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Lethbridge is Village Cinemas in Geelong, about 31 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, All 4 Kids Play Centre in Geelong West is about 29 minutes' drive from Lethbridge. For a zoo or wildlife park, Fairy Park in Anakie is about 39 minutes' drive from Lethbridge. The nearest larger centre is Geelong, about 33 minutes from Lethbridge by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Lethbridge, the nearest places for kids by road are Maude Tennis Reserve Playground in Maude (12 min), Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn (11 min), Bannockburn Skate Park in Bannockburn (12 min), the skate park at Turtle Bend Park in Teesdale (23 min), Disability beach in Steiglitz (26 min) and Goodfellow Aquatic Centre in Bell Post Hill (23 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lethbridge.

Nearby suburbs

Bannockburn, next to Lethbridge, has 6 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Teesdale, next to Lethbridge, has 4 playgrounds and a skate park. Maude, next to Lethbridge, has 2 playgrounds. Shelford, next to Lethbridge, has 1 playground. Meredith, She Oaks, Russells Bridge and Bamganie also border Lethbridge.
