Mount Camel is in the Greater Bendigo area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

State Forest is the largest mapped park in Mount Camel, at about 780 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Mount Camel. For walks and rides, Wattle Gully Walk, Lagoon Walk and Dargile Forest Walk cross Mount Camel.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mount Camel. The closest to Mount Camel is Goornong Railway Station (V/Line), about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mount Camel, so most trips start by car. By car from Mount Camel, Bendigo is about 51 minutes away, Seymour about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mount Camel is Rushworth Pool in Rushworth, about 35 minutes' drive. For a library, Heathcote Library in Heathcote is about 17 minutes' drive from Mount Camel. For a skate park, Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote is about 17 minutes' drive from Mount Camel. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Mount Camel, about 51 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Mount Camel are Heathcote Play Space in Heathcote (16 min), Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote (17 min), Heathcote Library in Heathcote (17 min), Mudwood Studio in Heathcote (17 min), Powder Magazine in Heathcote (18 min) and Tooborac Recreation Reserve Playground in Tooborac (29 min).

Best for

Free days on the Wattle Gully Walk and in Mount Camel's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Mount Camel, Heathcote has 1 playground, a library and a skate park. Ladys Pass and Costerfield also border Mount Camel.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Mount Camel, without traffic.
