Bannockburn Central

Shopping centre in Bannockburn · Basic info

Bannockburn Central is a shopping centre in Bannockburn, in the Golden Plains Shire Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. McPhillips Rd/High St (coach) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 40 m away with a baby change table.
