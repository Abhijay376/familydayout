Cape Nelson State Park

Park in Portland West · Official details

Cape Nelson State Park is a park in Portland West, in the Glenelg Shire Council area, good for all ages. The nearest public toilet is about 301 m away. Opening hours: Things To Do Cape Nelson Lighthouse Be sure to visit this heritage-listed lighthouse which is open daily with free access to the grounds..
