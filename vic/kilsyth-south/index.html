Kilsyth South in the Maroondah area has 3 playgrounds and 6 parks and reserves in our data. Kilsyth South also has a skate park.

Landmarks

Appletree Hill Reserve is the largest mapped park in Kilsyth South, at about 2 hectares. Unnamed P0056 Private Nature Reserve and Grayswood Park are the next largest. Kilsyth South also has W Jackson Reserve, Leeds Drive Reserve and Chandra Avenue Reserve. Playgrounds around Kilsyth South include the playground at Grayswood Park, the playground at Leeds Drive Reserve and the playground at Chandra Avenue Reserve. For older kids in Kilsyth South there's Kilsyth South Skatepark. For walks and rides, Dandenong Creek Trail, Liverpool Road Connection and The Basin Trail cross Kilsyth South. Our data lists 5 cafes and family restaurants in Kilsyth South for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kilsyth South. The closest to Kilsyth South is Boronia Railway Station (metro), about 4.3 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 679, 690 and 967 stop in Kilsyth South. By car from Kilsyth South, Ringwood is about 12 minutes away, Lilydale about 14 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 39 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kilsyth South is Croydon Memorial Pool in Croydon, about 8 minutes' drive. For a library, Miller's Homestead in Boronia is about 6 minutes' drive from Kilsyth South. For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 37 minutes' drive from Kilsyth South. For a cinema, Metro Cinemas in Boronia is about 8 minutes' drive from Kilsyth South. For an indoor play centre, PlayPlace in Mooroolbark is about 8 minutes' drive from Kilsyth South. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale is about 30 minutes' drive from Kilsyth South. The nearest larger centre is Ringwood, about 12 minutes from Kilsyth South by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Kilsyth South's 3 local playgrounds. Teens who skate or ride, with Kilsyth South Skatepark in Kilsyth South. Free days on the Dandenong Creek Trail and in Kilsyth South's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Kilsyth South, Boronia has 29 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Kilsyth, next to Kilsyth South, has 11 playgrounds and a skate park. Bayswater North, next to Kilsyth South, has 9 playgrounds.
