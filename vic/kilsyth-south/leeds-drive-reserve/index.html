Leeds Drive Reserve

Park in Kilsyth South · Basic info

Leeds Drive Reserve is a park in Kilsyth South, in the Maroondah City Council area, good for all ages. Liverpool Rd/Canterbury Rd (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 531 m away.
