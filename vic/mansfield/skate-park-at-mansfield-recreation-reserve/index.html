Skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve

Skate, BMX or pump track in Mansfield · Basic info

Skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve is a skate, bmx or pump track in Mansfield, in the Mansfield Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. MMBL Depot/High St (coach) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 169 m away with a baby change table.
