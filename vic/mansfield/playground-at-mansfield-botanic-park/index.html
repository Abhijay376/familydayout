Playground at Mansfield Botanic Park

Playground in Mansfield · Basic info

Playground at Mansfield Botanic Park is a public playground in Mansfield, in the Mansfield Shire area. The Mansfield - Stoneys Road Loop passes about 30 m away, and Mansfield Library is about 320 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus Mansfield - Woods Point via Jamieson stops at High St/Erril St, a 6-minute walk away.
