Playground on Highett Street

Playground in Mansfield · Basic info

Playground on Highett Street in Mansfield is a public playground in the Mansfield Shire area. The Mansfield - Stoneys Road Loop is about 40 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 290 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 750 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 1 minute from Mansfield.
