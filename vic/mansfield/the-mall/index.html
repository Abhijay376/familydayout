The Mall

Shopping centre in Mansfield · Basic info

The Mall is a shopping centre in Mansfield, in the Mansfield Shire Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. High St/Erril St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 174 m away.
