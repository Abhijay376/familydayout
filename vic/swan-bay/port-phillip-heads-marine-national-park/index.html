Port Phillip Heads Marine National Park

Park in Swan Bay · Official details

Port Phillip Heads Marine National Park is a park in Swan Bay, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Dogs are not allowed.

Details

Dogs
not allowed (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
Dogs allowed on lead at all times in Point Lonsdale Sector only; Take rubbish away; Fishing prohibited (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Port Phillip Heads Marine National Park?
Port Phillip Heads Marine National Park is in Swan Bay, Victoria.
Is Port Phillip Heads Marine National Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Port Phillip Heads Marine National Park?
No, dogs are not allowed.
