Meadow Creek in the Wangaratta area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Meadow Creek is Carboor Upper Education Area, covering about 414 hectares. For walks and rides, Vines 2 Vines: Whitfield to Milawa crosses Meadow Creek.

Getting around

There's no train station in Meadow Creek. The closest to Meadow Creek is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Meadow Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Meadow Creek, Wangaratta is about 33 minutes away and Benalla about 50 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Meadow Creek is Splashpark in Wangaratta, about 29 minutes' drive. For a library, Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta is about 32 minutes' drive from Meadow Creek. The closest skate park to Meadow Creek is the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Meadow Creek is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 32 minutes' drive. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Meadow Creek, about 33 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Meadow Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Apex Park in Moyhu (8 min), Myrrhee Recreation Reserve in Myrrhee (25 min), Splashpark in Wangaratta (29 min), the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta (30 min), Glenrowan Museum in Glenrowan (29 min) and Cobb & Co in Glenrowan (29 min).

Best for

Free days on the Vines 2 Vines: Whitfield to Milawa and in Meadow Creek's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Meadow Creek, Moyhu has 1 playground. Next door to Meadow Creek, Milawa has 1 playground. Docker, Carboor and Bobinawarrah also border Meadow Creek.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Meadow Creek, without traffic.
