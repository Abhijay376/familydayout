Calrossie is in the Wellington area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Calrossie, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Calrossie has no train station; Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 34 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Calrossie, so most trips start by car. By car from Calrossie, Traralgon is about 60 minutes away, Sale about 68 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Calrossie is Yarram Public Pool in Yarram, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest library to Calrossie is Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Calrossie is Woodside_Beach in Woodside Beach, about 37 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram is about 13 minutes' drive from Calrossie. The nearest larger centre is Traralgon, about 60 minutes from Calrossie by car, with 38 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Calrossie are Yarram Historical Society Museum in Yarram (12 min), the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram (13 min), Yarram Public Pool in Yarram (13 min), Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram (13 min), the playground at Memorial Park in Yarram (13 min) and Port Albert Maritime Museum in Port Albert (25 min).
