Picola is in the Moira area, and our data lists 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Picola is Barmah National Park, covering about 28,510 hectares. Nathalia Nature Conservation Reserve and Picola Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Picola also has Narioka Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Picola.

Getting around

There's no train station in Picola. The closest to Picola is Echuca Railway Station (V/Line), about 36 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Picola. By car from Picola, Echuca is about 56 minutes away and Shepparton about 59 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Nathalia Memorial Pool in Nathalia is about 14 minutes' drive from Picola. For a library, Nathalia Library in Nathalia is about 12 minutes' drive from Picola. The closest beach to Picola is Scotts Beach in Yalca, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Picola is Riverland Skate Park in Numurkah, about 40 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 56 minutes from Picola by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Picola, the nearest places for kids by road are Barmah Forest Heritge and Education Centre in Nathalia (12 min), Nathalia Library in Nathalia (12 min), the playground at Apex Park in Nathalia (13 min), Nathalia Memorial Pool in Nathalia (14 min), Scotts Beach in Yalca (30 min) and Paradise Beach in Yalca (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Picola.

Nearby suburbs

Nathalia, next to Picola, has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Kotupna and Barmah also border Picola.
