Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Larpent in the Colac Otway area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Larpent.

Getting around

There's no train station in Larpent. The closest to Larpent is Colac Railway Station (V/Line), about 8.2 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Larpent, so most trips start by car. By car from Larpent, Colac is about 9 minutes away, Geelong about 74 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac is about 12 minutes' drive from Larpent. The closest skate park to Larpent is the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Larpent is Colac Cinemas in Colac, about 10 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac is about 10 minutes' drive from Larpent. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Larpent, about 9 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Larpent, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (8 min), the playground at Western Bay Playground in Colac (9 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (10 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (10 min), Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (12 min) and the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (14 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Larpent.
