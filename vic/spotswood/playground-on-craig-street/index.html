Playground on Craig Street

Playground in Spotswood · Basic info

Playground on Craig Street is a public playground in Spotswood, in the Hobsons Bay City area. The Bay Trail is about 70 m away, and Scienceworks is about 50 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.4 km from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 2 minutes from Spotswood.
