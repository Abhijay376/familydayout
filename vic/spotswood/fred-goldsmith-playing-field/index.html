Fred Goldsmith Playing Field

Park in Spotswood · Official details

Fred Goldsmith Playing Field is a park in Spotswood, in the Hobsons Bay City Council area, good for all ages. Spotswood Railway Station (train) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 873 m away.
