Donald McLean Reserve

Park in Spotswood · Basic info

Donald McLean Reserve is a park in Spotswood, in the Hobsons Bay City Council area, good for all ages. The Avenue/Melbourne Rd (bus) is a 9-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 69 m away.
