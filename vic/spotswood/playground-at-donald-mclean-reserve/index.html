Playground at Donald McLean Reserve

Playground in Spotswood · Official details

Playground at Donald McLean Reserve is a public playground in Spotswood, in the Hobsons Bay City area. The Federation Trail passes about 170 m away, and Angela Sparks Gallery is about 540 m away. There's a public toilet about 220 m away, and bus 472 stops at The Avenue/Melbourne Rd, a 10-minute walk away.
