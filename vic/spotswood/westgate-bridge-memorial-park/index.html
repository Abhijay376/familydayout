Westgate Bridge Memorial Park

Park in Spotswood · Basic info

Westgate Bridge Memorial Park is a park in Spotswood, in the Hobsons Bay City Council area, good for all ages. Hyde St/Francis St (bus) is a 9-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1493 m away.
