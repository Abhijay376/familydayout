Playground at Craig Street Park

Playground in Spotswood · Official details

Playground at Craig Street Park is a public playground in Spotswood, in the Hobsons Bay City area. The Bay Trail is about 20 m away, and Industrial Art Sign Co is about 200 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 810 m from the playground, and trains stop at Spotswood Railway Station, a 4-minute walk away.
