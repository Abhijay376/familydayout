Craig Street Park

Park in Spotswood · Official details

Craig Street Park is a park in Spotswood, in the Hobsons Bay City Council area, good for all ages. Spotswood Railway Station (train) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 811 m away.

Where is Craig Street Park?
Craig Street Park is in Spotswood, Victoria.
