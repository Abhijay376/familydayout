Miga Lake is in the West Wimmera area, and our data lists 7 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Miga Lake is Mount Arapiles-Tooan State Park, covering about 7,457 hectares. Kalingur State Forest and Tooan Reference Area are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Miga Lake include Miga Lake Lake Reserve, Black Waterhole Education Area and The Reserve Wildlife Reserve (hunting).

Getting around

There's no train station in Miga Lake. The closest to Miga Lake is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 121 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Miga Lake, so most trips start by car. By car from Miga Lake, Horsham is about 53 minutes away and Hamilton about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Miga Lake is Goroke Swimming Centre in Goroke, about 40 minutes' drive. The closest library to Miga Lake is West Wimmera Shire Goroke Library in Goroke, about 40 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk is about 32 minutes' drive from Miga Lake. The nearest larger centre is Horsham, about 53 minutes from Miga Lake by car, with 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Miga Lake are Harrow Discovery Centre in Harrow (31 min), Bake House Studio in Harrow (32 min), the playground at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk (32 min), the skate park at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk (32 min), the playground at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk (32 min) and West Wimmera Shire Goroke Library in Goroke (40 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Miga Lake.
