Our data lists 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in Bangholme, in the Greater Dandenong area.

Landmarks

Firman Park is the largest mapped park in Bangholme, at about 13 hectares. National Water Sports Centre is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Bangholme. For walks and rides, Dandenong Creek Trail and Bike: Thompsons Road cross Bangholme.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bangholme. The closest to Bangholme is Bonbeach Railway Station (metro), about 4.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 833, 857, 901 and 902 stop in Bangholme. By car from Bangholme, Dandenong is about 12 minutes away, Frankston about 14 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 35 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bangholme is Grenda Aquatic Centre in Keysborough, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest library to Bangholme is Bhai Vir Singh Library in Keysborough, about 5 minutes' drive. For a beach, Chelsea Beach in Chelsea is about 8 minutes' drive from Bangholme. The closest skate park to Bangholme is Chelsea Skate Park in Chelsea, about 6 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Reading Cinema in Dandenong is about 13 minutes' drive from Bangholme. For an indoor play centre, Gravity Zone Indoor Trampoline Centre in Seaford is about 8 minutes' drive from Bangholme. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale is about 23 minutes' drive from Bangholme. Dandenong is the closest bigger centre to Bangholme, about 12 minutes away by car, and has 21 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Bangholme, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Heights Park in Chelsea Heights (4 min), the playground at Third Avenue Reserve in Chelsea Heights (4 min), Bhai Vir Singh Library in Keysborough (5 min), Chelsea Skate Park in Chelsea (6 min), Chelsea Library in Chelsea (7 min) and Chelsea Beach in Chelsea (8 min).

Best for

Free days on the Dandenong Creek Trail and in Bangholme's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Keysborough, next to Bangholme, has 32 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Carrum Downs, next to Bangholme, has 22 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Bangholme, Patterson Lakes has 7 playgrounds and a library. Lyndhurst, next to Bangholme, has 7 playgrounds. Aspendale Gardens, Chelsea Heights, Waterways and Dandenong South also border Bangholme.
