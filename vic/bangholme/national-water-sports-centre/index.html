National Water Sports Centre

Park in Bangholme · Official details

National Water Sports Centre is a park in Bangholme, in the Greater Dandenong City Council area, good for all ages. Fielding Dr/Wells Rd (bus) is a 60-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 957 m away. Opening hours: Public access Tuesdays and Fridays; closed public holidays and during events.
