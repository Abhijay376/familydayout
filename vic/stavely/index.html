Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Stavely in the Ararat area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Stavely. The closest to Stavely is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 45 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Stavely, so most trips start by car. By car from Stavely, Ararat is about 45 minutes away and Hamilton about 60 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Willaura Outdoor Pool in Willaura is about 18 minutes' drive from Stavely. The closest library to Stavely is Lake Bolac Library in Lake Bolac, about 31 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Astor Cinema in Ararat is about 44 minutes' drive from Stavely. Ararat is the closest bigger centre to Stavely, about 45 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Stavely, the nearest places for kids by road are Glenthompson Lions Park Playground in Glenthompson (18 min), Glenthompson Rec Reserve Playground in Glenthompson (20 min), Willaura Outdoor Pool in Willaura (18 min), Lake Bolac Library in Lake Bolac (31 min), Ararat Gallery TAMA in Ararat (44 min) and Ararat Library in Ararat (44 min).
