Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Bruarong, in the Indigo area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bruarong is Bruarong Bushland Reserve, covering about 87 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bruarong. The closest to Bruarong is Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line), about 34 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bruarong, so most trips start by car. By car from Bruarong, Wodonga is about 36 minutes away and Bright about 49 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bruarong is the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest library to Bruarong is Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford, about 21 minutes' drive. For a beach, Sandy Beach in Beechworth is about 32 minutes' drive from Bruarong. The closest skate park to Bruarong is Skate Park in Yackandandah, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Bruarong is Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana, about 32 minutes' drive. Wodonga is the closest bigger centre to Bruarong, about 36 minutes away by car, and has 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Bruarong, the nearest places for kids by road are Skate Park in Yackandandah (12 min), the playground at Sir Isaac Isaacs Park in Yackandandah (12 min), the playground at Swinburne Park in Myrtleford (20 min), Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford (21 min), Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford (21 min) and The Old School Museum in Myrtleford (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bruarong.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Bruarong, Yackandandah has 1 playground and a skate park.
