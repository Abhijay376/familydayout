Playground at Selby Park

Playground in Selby · Basic info

Playground at Selby Park in Selby is a public playground in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and buses 695 and 695F stop at Lyons Dr/Belgrave-Gembrook Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
