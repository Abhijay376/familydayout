Green Gully is in the Mount Alexander area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Green Gully, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Green Gully has no train station; Castlemaine Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Green Gully, so most trips start by car. By car from Green Gully, Castlemaine is about 13 minutes away, Maryborough about 34 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Newstead Swimming Pool in Newstead is about 5 minutes' drive from Green Gully. The closest library to Green Gully is Castlemaine Library in Castlemaine, about 13 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Bike Jumps in Castlemaine is about 12 minutes' drive from Green Gully. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 40 minutes' drive from Green Gully. The nearest larger centre is Castlemaine, about 13 minutes from Green Gully by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Green Gully are Newstead Swimming Pool in Newstead (5 min), the playground at Newstead Memorial Park in Newstead (6 min), Lawrence Park Playground in Castlemaine (11 min), Bike Jumps in Castlemaine (12 min), BMX Track in Castlemaine (12 min) and Castlemaine CASPA in Castlemaine (13 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Green Gully.
