Kergunyah in the Indigo area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Kergunyah is Kergunyah I32 Bushland Reserve, covering about 17 hectares. Kergunyah I33 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kergunyah. The closest to Kergunyah is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Albury - Mt Beauty via Baranduda and Tawonga South route stop in Kergunyah. By car from Kergunyah, Wodonga is about 26 minutes away and Bright about 67 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Edge Aquatics in Baranduda is about 17 minutes' drive from Kergunyah. The closest library to Kergunyah is Upper Murray Regional Library - Wodonga in Wodonga, about 25 minutes' drive. For a beach, Finns Beach in Bandiana is about 31 minutes' drive from Kergunyah. For a skate park, Skate Park in Baranduda is about 17 minutes' drive from Kergunyah. For a cinema, Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana is about 21 minutes' drive from Kergunyah. Wodonga is the closest bigger centre to Kergunyah, about 26 minutes away by car, and has 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Kergunyah, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Banksia Park in Tangambalanga (11 min), Skate Park in Baranduda (17 min), Mel Read Park in Baranduda (17 min), Edge Aquatics in Baranduda (17 min), Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana (21 min) and Army Museum Bandiana in Bandiana (23 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Kergunyah.

Nearby suburbs

Yackandandah, next to Kergunyah, has 1 playground and a skate park. Gundowring, Glen Creek and Allans Flat also border Kergunyah.
