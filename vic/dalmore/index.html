Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Dalmore in the Cardinia area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Dalmore. The closest to Dalmore is Cardinia Road Railway Station (metro), about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dalmore, so most trips start by car. By car from Dalmore, Cranbourne is about 18 minutes away, Pakenham about 18 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 56 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Dalmore is Koo Wee Rup Swimming Pool in Koo Wee Rup, about 9 minutes' drive. For a library, Cranbourne Library in Cranbourne East is about 17 minutes' drive from Dalmore. For a beach, Frankston Beach in Frankston is about 32 minutes' drive from Dalmore. The closest skate park to Dalmore is the skate park at Cochrane Park in Koo Wee Rup, about 9 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Narre Warren is about 25 minutes' drive from Dalmore. For an indoor play centre, Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham is about 19 minutes' drive from Dalmore. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Dalmore is MonkeyG's in Pakenham, about 18 minutes' drive. Cranbourne is the closest bigger centre to Dalmore, about 18 minutes away by car, and has 27 playgrounds. From Dalmore, the nearest places for kids by road are Fisherman's Cottage in Tooradin (6 min), the playground at Lighthouse Playground in Tooradin (6 min), the playground at Cochrane Park in Koo Wee Rup (9 min), the skate park at Cochrane Park in Koo Wee Rup (9 min), Koo Wee Rup Swimming Pool in Koo Wee Rup (9 min) and King Swim in Clyde North (17 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Dalmore.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Dalmore, Clyde has 11 playgrounds and a skate park. Koo Wee Rup, next to Dalmore, has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Cardinia, next to Dalmore, has 4 playgrounds. Tooradin, next to Dalmore, has 2 playgrounds.
