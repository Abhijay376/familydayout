Scotchman's Creek Linear Park

Park in Mount Waverley · Basic info

Scotchman's Creek Linear Park is a park in Mount Waverley, in the Monash City Council area, good for all ages. Hardner Rd/Stephensons Rd (bus) is a 17-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1776 m away.
