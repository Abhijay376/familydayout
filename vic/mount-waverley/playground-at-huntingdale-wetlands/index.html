Playground at Huntingdale Wetlands

Playground in Mount Waverley · Basic info

This is a public playground in Mount Waverley. The Scotchmans Creek Trail is about 220 m away, and for food, Basil & Macrina is about 450 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 760 m from the playground, and buses 693 and 742 stop at Kevin St/Ferntree Gully Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
