Playground on Vasey Avenue

Playground in Mount Waverley · Basic info

Playground on Vasey Avenue is a public playground in Mount Waverley, in the Monash City area. Huntingtower Sports and Aquatic Centre is about 510 m away, and Playground on Montgomery Avenue is another option about 90 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 870 m away, and buses 703 and 737 stop at Savige Ave/Blackburn Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
