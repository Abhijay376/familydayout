Monash Historic Society

Museum or gallery in Mount Waverley · Basic info

Monash Historic Society is a museum or gallery in Mount Waverley, in the Monash City Council area, best for school-age kids. Mt Waverley Station/Miller Cres (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 3 m away with a baby change table.
