Playground on Beverley Grove

Playground in Mount Waverley · Basic info

Playground on Beverley Grove is a public playground in Mount Waverley, in the Monash City area. The Waverley Rail Trail is about 360 m away, and Playground on Winbourne Road is another option about 420 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 740 m from the playground, and bus 623 stops at Heany St/Waverley Rd, a 9-minute walk away.
