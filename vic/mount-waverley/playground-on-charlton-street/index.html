Playground on Charlton Street

Playground in Mount Waverley · Basic info

Playground on Charlton Street is a public playground in Mount Waverley, in the Monash City area. For food, Marisa Pizza & Pasta is about 140 m away, and Playground at Fairway Reserve is another option about 580 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 610 m from the playground, and buses 623 and 733 stop at Kemp Ave/Stephensons Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
