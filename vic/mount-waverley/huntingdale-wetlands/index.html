Huntingdale Wetlands

Park in Mount Waverley · Basic info

Huntingdale Wetlands is a park in Mount Waverley, in the Monash City Council area, good for all ages. Estelle St/Huntingdale Rd (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 919 m away.
