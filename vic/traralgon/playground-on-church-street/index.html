Playground on Church Street

Playground in Traralgon · Basic info

Playground on Church Street in Traralgon is a public playground in the Latrobe City area. Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre is about 500 m away, and Wan Loy is about 80 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 150 m away, and bus Wonthaggi - Traralgon via Leongatha stops at Traralgon Railway Station/Princes St , a 2-minute walk away.
