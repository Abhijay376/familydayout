Blake Park

Park in Traralgon · Basic info

Blake Park is a park in Traralgon, in the Latrobe City Council area, good for all ages. Hyde Park Rd/Elliot St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 990 m away with a baby change table.
