Oxford Place Reserve

Park in Traralgon · Basic info

Oxford Place Reserve is a park in Traralgon, in the Latrobe City Council area, good for all ages. St Georges Rd/Breed St (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1038 m away with a baby change table.
