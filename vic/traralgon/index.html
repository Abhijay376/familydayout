Our data lists 38 playgrounds and 90 parks and reserves in Traralgon, in the Latrobe area. Beyond the parks, Traralgon has a pool and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Traralgon is Traralgon Railway Reservoir Conservation Reserve, covering about 32 hectares. Victory Park and Apex Park are the next largest. Traralgon also has Ann Park, Bond Park and Dyer Park. Playgrounds around Traralgon include Explorers Park, Pax Hill Playground and Rod Dunbar Playground. Our data shows 17 public toilets in Traralgon. In Traralgon you'll also find Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre. For older kids in Traralgon there's Traralgon Skate Park. Traralgon Centre Plaza is the local shopping centre. For walks and rides, Traralgon to Morwell shared path crosses Traralgon. Our data lists 31 cafes and family restaurants in Traralgon for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Traralgon has a train station: Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line: Bairnsdale - Melbourne via Sale & Traralgon and Traralgon - Melbourne via Morwell, Moe & Pakenham). Buses on the 1, 3, 5 and 6 routes stop in Traralgon. V/Line coaches stop in Traralgon. By car from Traralgon, Sale is about 45 minutes away, Warragul about 48 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Morwell Library in Morwell is about 14 minutes' drive from Traralgon. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 11 minutes' drive from Traralgon.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 38 playgrounds across Traralgon. Teens who skate or ride, with Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon. Free days on the Traralgon to Morwell shared path and in Traralgon's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Morwell, next to Traralgon, has 14 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. Next door to Traralgon, Traralgon East has 2 playgrounds. Tyers, next to Traralgon, has 1 playground. Glengarry, next to Traralgon, has 1 playground and a skate park. Traralgon South, Maryvale, Loy Yang and Hazelwood North also border Traralgon.
