Explorers Park

Playground in Traralgon · Basic info

Explorers Park is a public playground in Traralgon, in the Latrobe City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.4 km away, and bus 45 stops at Explorers Park/Franklin St, a 4-minute walk away.
