Playground at Duncan Cameron Memorial Park

Playground in Traralgon · Basic info

Playground at Duncan Cameron Memorial Park is a public playground in Traralgon, in the Latrobe City area. For food, KFC is about 360 m away, and Playground on Church Street is another option about 490 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 600 m from the playground, and bus 42 stops at Bank St/Francis St, a 5-minute walk away.
