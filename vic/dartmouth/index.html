Dartmouth in the Towong area has 5 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Banimboola Creek Natural Catchment Area is the largest mapped park in Dartmouth, at about 2,308 hectares. Mount Tabor Creek Natural Catchment Area and Mount Cravensville Natural Features and Scenic Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Dartmouth include Lightwood Reference Area and Wombat Creek Reference Area. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Dartmouth. For walks and rides, Australian Alps Walking Track crosses Dartmouth.

Getting around

Dartmouth has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 71 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dartmouth, so most trips start by car. By car from Dartmouth, Wodonga is about 76 minutes away and Wangaratta about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Eskdale Swimming Pool in Eskdale is about 25 minutes' drive from Dartmouth. From Dartmouth, the nearest places for kids by road are Eskdale Swimming Pool in Eskdale (25 min).

Best for

Free days on the Australian Alps Walking Track and in Dartmouth's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Glen Valley and Benambra also border Dartmouth.
