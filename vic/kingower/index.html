Kingower is in the Loddon area, and our data lists 5 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Kingower is Kingower Reference Area, covering about 308 hectares. Kingower Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Kingower include Orchid Dam, Ironbark Dam and Butchers Camp Site. Picnic areas in Kingower include Butchers Camp Site, Ironbark Dam and Orchid Dam.

Getting around

Kingower has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 49 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kingower, so most trips start by car. By car from Kingower, Bendigo is about 49 minutes away, Maryborough about 59 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn is about 25 minutes' drive from Kingower. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 49 minutes from Kingower by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Kingower are Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (25 min), Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (25 min), Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (34 min), Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (34 min), Bi-Centennial Park Playground in St Arnaud (36 min) and the playground at Queen Mary Botanic Gardens in St Arnaud (37 min).
