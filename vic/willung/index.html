Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Willung in the Wellington area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Willung. The closest to Willung is Rosedale Railway Station (V/Line), about 10 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Willung, so most trips start by car. By car from Willung, Traralgon is about 32 minutes away, Sale about 35 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Willung is Rosedale Swimming Pool in Rosedale, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest library to Willung is Sale Library in Sale, about 33 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Pump Track in Heyfield is about 28 minutes' drive from Willung. The closest cinema to Willung is Sale Cinemas in Sale, about 33 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Traralgon, about 32 minutes from Willung by car, with 38 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Willung are Edward Crooke Park Playground in Rosedale (11 min), Rosedale Swimming Pool in Rosedale (11 min), Prince Street Reserve Playground in Rosedale (12 min), Heyfield and District Museum in Heyfield (28 min), Pump Track in Heyfield (28 min) and Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield (28 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Willung.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Willung, Rosedale has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Gormandale, next to Willung, has 1 playground. Longford, Hiamdale and Flynn also border Willung.
