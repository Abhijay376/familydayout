Woodside is in the Wellington area, and our data lists 5 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Woodside is Jack Smith Lake Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 2,781 hectares. Woodside H26 Bushland Reserve and Woodside H27 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Woodside include Bruthen Flora Reserve and Woodside H25 Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Woodside.

Getting around

There's no train station in Woodside. The closest to Woodside is Rosedale Railway Station (V/Line), about 41 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Woodside, so most trips start by car. By car from Woodside, Sale is about 42 minutes away and Traralgon about 66 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Woodside is Yarram Public Pool in Yarram, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest library to Woodside is Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Woodside is Woodside_Beach in Woodside Beach, about 12 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram is about 17 minutes' drive from Woodside. Sale is the closest bigger centre to Woodside, about 42 minutes away by car, and has 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Woodside, the nearest places for kids by road are Woodside_Beach in Woodside Beach (12 min), Woodside Beach Road Playground in Woodside Beach (12 min), Woolami in Woodside Beach (15 min), McLoughlins Beach Playground in McLoughlins Beach (14 min), Yarram Historical Society Museum in Yarram (14 min) and Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram (16 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Woodside.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Woodside, Yarram has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Woodside Beach, next to Woodside, has 1 playground and a beach. Next door to Woodside, McLoughlins Beach has 1 playground. Hunterston, Darriman and Carrajung South also border Woodside.
