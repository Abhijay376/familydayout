Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Pomborneit in the Corangamite area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Pomborneit has no train station; Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Pomborneit, so most trips start by car. By car from Pomborneit, Colac is about 23 minutes away, Warrnambool about 72 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Pomborneit is Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac, about 26 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown is about 16 minutes' drive from Pomborneit. The closest cinema to Pomborneit is Colac Cinemas in Colac, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Pomborneit is Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac, about 24 minutes' drive. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Pomborneit, about 23 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Pomborneit, the nearest places for kids by road are Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (16 min), the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve in Camperdown (24 min), the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (22 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (24 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (24 min) and Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Pomborneit.
