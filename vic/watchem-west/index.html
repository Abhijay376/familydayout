Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Watchem West, in the Yarriambiack area.

Landmarks

Watchem I122 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Watchem West, at about 39 hectares. Watchem I123 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Watchem West. The closest to Watchem West is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 123 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Watchem West, so most trips start by car. By car from Watchem West, Horsham is about 78 minutes away and Stawell about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Watchem West is Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Watchem West is Birchip Skate Park in Birchip, about 26 minutes' drive. From Watchem West, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Lake Watchem Lake Reserve in Watchem (11 min), Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem (10 min), the playground at J.E Taylor Sunset Park in Birchip (24 min), Birchip History Museum in Birchip (25 min), Birchip Skate Park in Birchip (26 min) and Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Watchem West.

Nearby suburbs

Watchem, next to Watchem West, has 1 playground and a pool. Litchfield, Carron, Bangerang and Areegra also border Watchem West.
