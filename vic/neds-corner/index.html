Landmarks

The biggest green space in Neds Corner is Unnamed P0268 Private Nature Reserve, covering about 29,803 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Neds Corner. The closest to Neds Corner is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 225 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Neds Corner, so most trips start by car. By car from Neds Corner, Mildura is about 85 minutes away and Swan Hill about 4 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids
