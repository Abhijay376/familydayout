Fairbank is in the South Gippsland area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Fairbank, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Fairbank. The closest to Fairbank is Yarragon Railway Station (V/Line), about 25 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Fairbank, so most trips start by car. By car from Fairbank, Leongatha is about 14 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 45 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Fairbank is South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest library to Fairbank is Leongatha Library in Leongatha, about 14 minutes' drive. For a beach, The Caves in Inverloch is about 41 minutes' drive from Fairbank. The closest skate park to Fairbank is Leongatha Skate Park in Leongatha, about 12 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha is about 14 minutes' drive from Fairbank. The nearest larger centre is Leongatha, about 14 minutes from Fairbank by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Fairbank, the nearest places for kids by road are Leongatha Skate Park in Leongatha (12 min), South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha (13 min), Splash Pools and Stadium in Leongatha (14 min), Leongatha Library in Leongatha (14 min), Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha (14 min) and BMX track in Leongatha (14 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Fairbank.
