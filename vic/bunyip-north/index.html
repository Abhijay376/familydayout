Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Bunyip North, in the Cardinia area.

Landmarks

Cannibal Creek Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Bunyip North, at about 21 hectares. Bunyip G13 Streamside Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Bunyip North has no train station; Bunyip Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 4.2 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bunyip North, so most trips start by car. By car from Bunyip North, Pakenham is about 22 minutes away, Warragul about 23 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 66 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rock Pool in Tynong North is about 8 minutes' drive from Bunyip North. The closest library to Bunyip North is Drouin Library in Drouin, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Bunyip North is the bmx track at Greenland Court Reserve in Garfield, about 7 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 21 minutes' drive from Bunyip North. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 21 minutes' drive from Bunyip North. For a zoo or wildlife park, Wildlife Trail in Tynong North is about 8 minutes' drive from Bunyip North. The nearest larger centre is Pakenham, about 22 minutes from Bunyip North by car, with 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play. From Bunyip North, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Bunyip Sanctuary in Bunyip (7 min), the playground at Koolangarra Park in Bunyip (7 min), the bmx track at Greenland Court Reserve in Garfield (7 min), Wildlife Trail in Tynong North (8 min), Rock Pool in Tynong North (8 min) and Lazy River in Tynong North (8 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bunyip North.
