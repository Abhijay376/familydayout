Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Thorpdale South in the Baw Baw area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Thorpdale South. The closest to Thorpdale South is Trafalgar Railway Station (V/Line), about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Thorpdale South, so most trips start by car. By car from Thorpdale South, Warragul is about 36 minutes away, Leongatha about 38 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar is about 18 minutes' drive from Thorpdale South. The closest library to Thorpdale South is Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Thorpdale South is Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North in Mirboo North, about 16 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 31 minutes' drive from Thorpdale South. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 35 minutes' drive from Thorpdale South. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Thorpdale South, about 36 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Thorpdale South are Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North in Mirboo North (16 min), the playground at Baromi Park in Mirboo North (15 min), Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North (15 min), the playground at Brindlewood Reserve in Trafalgar (17 min), Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar (18 min) and Railway Trolley Shed in Yinnar (24 min).
