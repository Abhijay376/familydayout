Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Chocolyn in the Corangamite area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Chocolyn. The closest to Chocolyn is Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Chocolyn, so most trips start by car. By car from Chocolyn, Colac is about 43 minutes away, Warrnambool about 61 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Lismore Pool in Lismore is about 27 minutes' drive from Chocolyn. The closest library to Chocolyn is Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Chocolyn is Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown, about 8 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 43 minutes from Chocolyn by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Chocolyn are Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (8 min), the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve in Camperdown (16 min), Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum (29 min), Lismore Pool in Lismore (27 min), Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake (33 min) and the playground at Tea Tree Lake Reserve in Mortlake (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Chocolyn.
