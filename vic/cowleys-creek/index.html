Cowleys Creek is in the Corangamite area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Cowleys Creek, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cowleys Creek. The closest to Cowleys Creek is Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line), about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cowleys Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Cowleys Creek, Warrnambool is about 59 minutes away and Colac about 65 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cowleys Creek is Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Cowleys Creek is Rutledge Creek Beach in Port Campbell, about 23 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon is about 15 minutes' drive from Cowleys Creek. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Cowleys Creek, about 59 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Cowleys Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (15 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (15 min), Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell (19 min), Rutledge Creek Beach in Port Campbell (23 min), Sherbrooke Estuary in Port Campbell (23 min) and the playground at Peterborough Coastal Reserve (COM) in Peterborough (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Cowleys Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Cowleys Creek, Timboon has a pool and a skate park.
