Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Jancourt East, in the Corangamite area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Jancourt East is Jancourt Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 3,357 hectares. Carpendeit Reference Area and Carpendeit Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

Jancourt East has no train station; Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Jancourt East, so most trips start by car. By car from Jancourt East, Colac is about 48 minutes away, Warrnambool about 58 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon is about 29 minutes' drive from Jancourt East. For a beach, Clifton Beach in Princetown is about 41 minutes' drive from Jancourt East. The closest skate park to Jancourt East is Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown, about 25 minutes' drive. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Jancourt East, about 48 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Jancourt East are Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (25 min), the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve in Camperdown (27 min), the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (29 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (29 min), Clifton Beach in Princetown (41 min) and Gibson Beach in Princetown (41 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Jancourt East.

Nearby suburbs

Cobden, next to Jancourt East, has 1 playground. Glenfyne, Carpendeit and Bungador also border Jancourt East.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Jancourt East, without traffic.
