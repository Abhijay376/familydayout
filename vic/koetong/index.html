Koetong in the Towong area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Dry Forest Creek Reference Area is the largest mapped park in Koetong, at about 1,382 hectares. Trestle Bridge, Koetong Creek (1) Historic Reserve and Trestle Bridge, Koetong Creek (2) Historic Reserve are the next largest. Koetong also has Red Rooster. For walks and rides, High Country Rail Trail and Tallangatta - Firebrace Loop cross Koetong.

Getting around

There's no train station in Koetong. The closest to Koetong is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 52 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Koetong, so most trips start by car. By car from Koetong, Wodonga is about 60 minutes away and Wangaratta about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Koetong is Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta, about 27 minutes' drive. For a library, Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta is about 27 minutes' drive from Koetong. The closest beach to Koetong is Pebble Beach in Walwa, about 39 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Koetong is Corryong Cinema in Corryong, about 37 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 60 minutes from Koetong by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Koetong are Granya Museum in Granya (28 min), Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta (27 min), Tallangatta Art & Photography in Tallangatta (27 min), Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta (27 min), Pebble Beach in Walwa (39 min) and Corryong Cinema in Corryong (37 min).

Best for

Free days on the High Country Rail Trail and in Koetong's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Bungil and Bullioh also border Koetong.
