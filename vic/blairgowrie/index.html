Blairgowrie in the Mornington Peninsula area has 1 playground and 10 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, Blairgowrie has 6 beaches.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Blairgowrie is Stringer Road Reserve, covering about 5 hectares. Clayton Park and Harry Blythe Playground are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Blairgowrie include Slips Reserve, Sid Baker Reserve and E G Ritchie Reserve. Playgrounds around Blairgowrie include the playground at Harry Blythe Playground. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Blairgowrie. In Blairgowrie you'll also find Bridgewater Bay Beach and Dimmicks Beach. The Melbourne Road Trail and Coppins Track all pass through Blairgowrie. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Blairgowrie for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Blairgowrie. The closest to Blairgowrie is Bittern Railway Station (metro), about 36 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 787 and 788 stop in Blairgowrie. By car from Blairgowrie, Mornington is about 37 minutes away, Frankston about 43 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 86 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Blairgowrie is Alba Thermal Springs & Spa ARCH in Fingal, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest library to Blairgowrie is Rosebud Library in Rosebud, about 17 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Rosebud Skate Park in Rosebud is about 15 minutes' drive from Blairgowrie. The closest cinema to Blairgowrie is Atheneum Cinema in Sorrento, about 6 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Top Fun Shack in Rosebud is about 17 minutes' drive from Blairgowrie. The nearest larger centre is Mornington, about 37 minutes from Blairgowrie by car, with 22 playgrounds, a library, indoor play and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Blairgowrie are Koonya Ocean Beach in Sorrento (3 min), the playground at Port Phillip Bay Coastal Reserve (COM) in Sorrento (4 min), Atheneum Cinema in Sorrento (6 min), Nepean Historical Society in Sorrento (6 min), Nura Gallery in Sorrento (5 min) and Alba Thermal Springs & Spa ARCH in Fingal (12 min).

Best for

Beach days at Bridgewater Bay Beach and Dimmicks Beach. Free days on the Melbourne Road Trail and in Blairgowrie's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Blairgowrie, Sorrento has 2 playgrounds, a beach and a cinema. Next door to Blairgowrie, Rye has 3 playgrounds and a beach.
