Playground on Whitehorse Road

Playground in Blackburn · Basic info

Playground on Whitehorse Road in Blackburn is a public playground in the Whitehorse City area. The Whitehorse NS6 is about 100 m away, and Blackburn Library is about 580 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 850 m from the playground, and buses 271, 279 and 901 stop at Blackburn PS/Surrey Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
