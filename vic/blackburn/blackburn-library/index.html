Blackburn Library

Library in Blackburn · Basic info

Blackburn Library is a library in Blackburn, in the Whitehorse City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Blackburn Rd/Central Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 328 m away with a baby change table.
