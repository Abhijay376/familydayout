McCubbin Park

Park in Blackburn · Basic info

McCubbin Park is a park in Blackburn, in the Whitehorse City Council area, good for all ages. Parkside St/Central Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 371 m away with a baby change table.
