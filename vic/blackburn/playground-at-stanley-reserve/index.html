Playground at Stanley Reserve

Playground in Blackburn · Basic info

Playground at Stanley Reserve in Blackburn is a public playground in the Whitehorse City area. The Whitehorse EW2-A passes about 30 m away, and China Bar is about 500 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 660 m away, and buses 271 and 279 stop at Downing St/Whitehorse Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
