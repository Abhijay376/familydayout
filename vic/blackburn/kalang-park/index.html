Kalang Park

Park in Blackburn · Basic info

Kalang Park is a park in Blackburn, in the Whitehorse City Council area, good for all ages. Heath St/Main St (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 355 m away with a baby change table.
