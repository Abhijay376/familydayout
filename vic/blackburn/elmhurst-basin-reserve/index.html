Elmhurst Basin Reserve

Park in Blackburn · Basic info

Elmhurst Basin Reserve is a park in Blackburn, in the Whitehorse City Council area, good for all ages. Downing St/Whitehorse Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 894 m away with a baby change table.
