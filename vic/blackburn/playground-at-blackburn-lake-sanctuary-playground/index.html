Playground at Blackburn Lake Sanctuary

Playground in Blackburn · Basic info

Playground at Blackburn Lake Sanctuary is a public playground in Blackburn, in the Whitehorse City area. The Whitehorse EW4 passes about 130 m away, and Playground at Jean-Lake Reserve is another option about 430 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 820 m away, and bus 736 stops at Blackburn Lake PS/Milton St, a 3-minute walk away.
