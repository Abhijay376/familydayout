Playground at Blackburn Lake Sanctuary

Playground in Blackburn · Basic info

This is a public playground in Blackburn. The Bike: Blackburn Lake shimmy passes about 50 m away, and Playground at Morton Park is another option about 510 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 100 m away, and bus 736 stops at Gwenda Ave/Central Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
