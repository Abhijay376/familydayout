Urban Climb, Blackburn

Climbing in Blackburn · Basic info

Urban Climb is a climbing in Blackburn, in the Whitehorse City Council area, best for kids 5 and up and teens. Maple St/Whitehorse Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 749 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: opening_hours: Mo-Fr 10:00-22:00; Sa,Su 08:00-20:00.
