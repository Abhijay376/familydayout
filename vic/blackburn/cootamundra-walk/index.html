Cootamundra Walk

Park in Blackburn · Basic info

Cootamundra Walk is a park in Blackburn, in the Whitehorse City Council area, good for all ages. Surrey Rd/Springfield Rd (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 780 m away with a baby change table.
