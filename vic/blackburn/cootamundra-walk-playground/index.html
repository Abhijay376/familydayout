Cootamundra Walk Playground

Playground in Blackburn · Basic info

Cootamundra Walk Playground in Blackburn is a public playground in the Whitehorse City area. The Whitehorse EW2-A passes about 10 m away, and Sushi Sushi is about 270 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 960 m away. It's easiest by car, about 3 minutes from Blackburn.
