Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Tarrengower in the Mount Alexander area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Tarrengower has no train station; Castlemaine Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tarrengower, so most trips start by car. By car from Tarrengower, Castlemaine is about 34 minutes away, Maryborough about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tarrengower is Maldon Swimming Pool in Maldon, about 17 minutes' drive. For a library, Athenaeum Library in Maldon is about 16 minutes' drive from Tarrengower. The closest skate park to Tarrengower is Bike Jumps in Castlemaine, about 34 minutes' drive. Castlemaine is the closest bigger centre to Tarrengower, about 34 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Tarrengower are Maldon District Museum in Maldon (16 min), the playground at Maldon Shire Gardens in Maldon (17 min), Maldon Vintage Machinery Museum in Maldon (17 min), Athenaeum Library in Maldon (16 min), Maldon Swimming Pool in Maldon (17 min) and Newstead Swimming Pool in Newstead (25 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tarrengower.

Nearby suburbs

Maldon, next to Tarrengower, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Baringhup also borders Tarrengower.
