Playground at Phillip Street Reserve

Playground in Mentone · Basic info

Playground at Phillip Street Reserve in Mentone is a public playground in the Kingston City area. The Cheltenham - Parkdale is about 40 m away, and for food, Applehead Deli is about 300 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 840 m from the playground, and buses 708 and 825 stop at Latrobe St/Nepean Hwy, a 7-minute walk away.
