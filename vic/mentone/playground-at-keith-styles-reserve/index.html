Playground at Keith Styles Reserve

Playground in Mentone · Basic info

Playground at Keith Styles Reserve is a public playground in Mentone, in the Kingston City area. The Cheltenham - Parkdale passes about 280 m away, and Mentone Dog Beach is about 580 m away. There's a public toilet about 450 m away, and bus 903 stops at Venice St/Mentone Pde, a 1-minute walk away.
