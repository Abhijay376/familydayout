Playground on Broome Avenue

Playground in Mentone · Basic info

Playground on Broome Avenue is a public playground in Mentone, in the Kingston City area. For food, Fishy Feast is about 400 m away, and Playground on Blackwood Avenue is another option about 160 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 900 m from the playground, and buses 811 and 812 stop at Blackwood Ave/Lower Dandenong Rd , a 7-minute walk away.
