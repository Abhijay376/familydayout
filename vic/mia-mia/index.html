Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Mia Mia, in the Greater Bendigo area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Mia Mia is Mia Mia Bushland Reserve, covering about 9 hectares. Mia Mia "Flat" is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mia Mia. The closest to Mia Mia is Malmsbury Railway Station (V/Line), about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mia Mia, so most trips start by car. By car from Mia Mia, Kyneton is about 35 minutes away, Bendigo about 52 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Heathcote Library in Heathcote is about 15 minutes' drive from Mia Mia. For a skate park, Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote is about 15 minutes' drive from Mia Mia. The nearest larger centre is Kyneton, about 35 minutes from Mia Mia by car, with 10 playgrounds and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Mia Mia are Heathcote Play Space in Heathcote (14 min), Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote (15 min), Heathcote Library in Heathcote (15 min), Mudwood Studio in Heathcote (15 min), Powder Magazine in Heathcote (17 min) and Metcalfe Recreation Reserve Playground in Metcalfe (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mia Mia.

Nearby suburbs

Heathcote, next to Mia Mia, has 1 playground, a library and a skate park. Glenhope East, Glenhope, Derrinal and Argyle also border Mia Mia.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Mia Mia, without traffic.
