Glenpatrick is in the Pyrenees area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Glenpatrick, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Glenpatrick. The closest to Glenpatrick is Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line), about 32 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glenpatrick, so most trips start by car. By car from Glenpatrick, Ararat is about 43 minutes away, Maryborough about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat is about 43 minutes' drive from Glenpatrick. The closest library to Glenpatrick is Ararat Library in Ararat, about 43 minutes' drive. For a skate park, David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat is about 44 minutes' drive from Glenpatrick. The closest cinema to Glenpatrick is Astor Cinema in Ararat, about 43 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Ararat, about 43 minutes from Glenpatrick by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Glenpatrick are the playground at Avoca Lions Park in Avoca (35 min), Courthouse in Avoca (35 min), the playground at Toll Bar Park in Lexton (36 min), David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat (44 min), Ararat Gallery TAMA in Ararat (43 min) and Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat (43 min).
