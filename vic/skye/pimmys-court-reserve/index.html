Pimmys Court Reserve

Park in Skye · Basic info

Pimmys Court Reserve is a park in Skye, in the Frankston City Council area, good for all ages. Flora Park Way/Ballarto Rd (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 727 m away.
