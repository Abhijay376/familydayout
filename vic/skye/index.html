Our data lists 7 playgrounds and 11 parks and reserves in Skye, in the Frankston area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Skye is Skye Reserve, covering about 4 hectares. Cranwell Mews Reserve and Langwarrin Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Skye also has Heyson Reserve, Skye Valley Park and Lady Emily Reserve. Playgrounds around Skye include Skye Valley Park Playground, the playground at Heyson Reserve and the playground at Lady Emily Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Skye. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Skye for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Skye has no train station; Kananook Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 5.7 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 760 and 832 stop in Skye. By car from Skye, Cranbourne is about 12 minutes away, Frankston about 12 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 45 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Skye is Pines Forest Aquatic Centre in Frankston North, about 8 minutes' drive. For a library, Frankston Library in Frankston is about 12 minutes' drive from Skye. The closest beach to Skye is Seaford Beach in Seaford, about 11 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Carrum Downs Skatepark in Carrum Downs is about 7 minutes' drive from Skye. The closest cinema to Skye is Village Cinemas in Frankston, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Skye is Gravity Zone Indoor Trampoline Centre in Seaford, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Skye is Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale, about 16 minutes' drive.

Younger kids at Skye's 7 local playgrounds.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Skye, Langwarrin has 22 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Skye, Cranbourne West has 21 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Skye, Carrum Downs has 22 playgrounds and a skate park. Lyndhurst, next to Skye, has 7 playgrounds. Cranbourne South and Sandhurst also border Skye.
