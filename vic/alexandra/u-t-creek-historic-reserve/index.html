U.T. Creek Historic Reserve

Park in Alexandra · Basic info

U.T. Creek Historic Reserve is a park in Alexandra, in the Murrindindi Shire Council area, good for all ages. Perkins St/Grant St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 267 m away with a baby change table.
