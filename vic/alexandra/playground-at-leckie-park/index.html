Playground at Leckie Park

Playground in Alexandra · Basic info

Playground at Leckie Park is a public playground in Alexandra, in the Murrindindi Shire area. Alexandra Library is about 300 m away, and Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool is about 180 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 310 m from the playground, and bus Alexandra - Marysville via Taggerty & Buxton stops at Perkins St/Grant St, a 4-minute walk away.
