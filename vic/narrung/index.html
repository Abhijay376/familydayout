Narrung is in the Swan Hill area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Narrung is Heywood Lake Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 564 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Narrung. The closest to Narrung is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 72 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Narrung, so most trips start by car. By car from Narrung, Swan Hill is about 63 minutes away and Mildura about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest skate park to Narrung is the bmx track at Piangil Memorial Park in Piangil, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Narrung are the playground at Boundary Bend Memorial Park in Boundary Bend (8 min), the playground at Mensforth Park in Piangil (28 min) and the bmx track at Piangil Memorial Park in Piangil (30 min).

Best for
