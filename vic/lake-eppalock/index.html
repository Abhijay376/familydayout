Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Lake Eppalock in the Greater Bendigo area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, O'Keefe Rail Trail crosses Lake Eppalock.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lake Eppalock. The closest to Lake Eppalock is Goornong Railway Station (V/Line), about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lake Eppalock, so most trips start by car. By car from Lake Eppalock, Kyneton is about 66 minutes away, Bendigo about 69 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Lake Eppalock is Heathcote Library in Heathcote, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Lake Eppalock is Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Lake Eppalock are Heathcote Play Space in Heathcote (34 min), Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote (35 min), Heathcote Library in Heathcote (35 min), Mudwood Studio in Heathcote (35 min), Powder Magazine in Heathcote (37 min) and Tooborac Recreation Reserve Playground in Tooborac (47 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Lake Eppalock on the O'Keefe Rail Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Eppalock and Derrinal also border Lake Eppalock.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Lake Eppalock, without traffic.
