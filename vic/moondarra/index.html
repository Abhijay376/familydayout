Moondarra in the Baw Baw area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Moondarra is Moondarra State Park, covering about 6,336 hectares. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Moondarra. For walks and rides, Wirilda Walking Track and Moe–Walhalla narrow-gauge railway cross Moondarra.

Getting around

There's no train station in Moondarra. The closest to Moondarra is Moe Railway Station (V/Line), about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Moondarra, so most trips start by car. By car from Moondarra, Traralgon is about 40 minutes away, Warragul about 52 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Moondarra is Rawson Swimming Pool in Rawson, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest library to Moondarra is Morwell Library in Morwell, about 37 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Moondarra is Moe Skate Park in Moe, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Moondarra is Village 8 in Morwell, about 39 minutes' drive. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Moondarra, about 40 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Moondarra are Rawson Swimming Pool in Rawson (11 min), the playground at Erica State Forest in Rawson (12 min), the playground at Croxford Park in Rawson (12 min), Fire station in Walhalla (24 min), Moe Skate Park in Moe (24 min) and Diving Pool in Moe (25 min).

Best for

Free days on the Wirilda Walking Track and in Moondarra's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Moondarra, Yallourn North has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Tyers, next to Moondarra, has 1 playground. Erica, next to Moondarra, has 1 playground. Tanjil, Jacob Creek, Caringal and Boola also border Moondarra.
