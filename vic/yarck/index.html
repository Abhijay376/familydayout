Yarck is in the Murrindindi area, and our data lists 1 playground and 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Yarck is Yarck Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 546 hectares. Molesworth Bushland Reserve and Home Creek, Yarck Streamside Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Yarck include Yarck Recreation Reserve. Playgrounds in Yarck include Yarck Recreation Reserve Playground. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Yarck. For walks and rides, Great Victorian Rail Trail crosses Yarck.

Getting around

There's no train station in Yarck. The closest to Yarck is Euroa Railway Station (V/Line), about 39 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Yarck. By car from Yarck, Mansfield is about 43 minutes away, Seymour about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Yarck is Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra, about 17 minutes' drive. For a library, Alexandra Library in Alexandra is about 16 minutes' drive from Yarck. For a skate park, BMX Track in Caveat is about 39 minutes' drive from Yarck. The closest cinema to Yarck is Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa, about 39 minutes' drive. Mansfield is the closest bigger centre to Yarck, about 43 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Yarck are Alexandra Library in Alexandra (16 min), the playground at Leckie Park in Alexandra (17 min), Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra (17 min), Alexandra Timber Tramway and Museum in Alexandra (18 min), BMX Track in Caveat (39 min) and the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea (22 min).

Best for

Free days on the Great Victorian Rail Trail and in Yarck's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Yarck, Caveat has 1 playground and a skate park. Kanumbra, Gobur, Fawcett and Cathkin also border Yarck.
