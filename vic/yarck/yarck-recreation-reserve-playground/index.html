Yarck Recreation Reserve Playground

Playground in Yarck · Basic info

Yarck Recreation Reserve Playground is a public playground in Yarck, in the Murrindindi Shire area. The Great Victorian Rail Trail is about 220 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and coaches stop at Wrights St/Maroondah Hwy, a 3-minute walk away.
