Playground on Patience Avenue

Playground in Doreen · Basic info

Playground on Patience Avenue is a public playground in Doreen, in the Whittlesea City area. Shanghai Blossom is about 140 m away if you need food, and Playground on Navigate Road is another option about 110 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.2 km away, and bus 385 stops at Orchard Rd/Elation Bvd, a 4-minute walk away.
