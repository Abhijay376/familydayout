Playground at Serle Wetland Park

Playground in Doreen · Basic info

Playground at Serle Wetland Park is a public playground in Doreen, in the Whittlesea City area. For food, Shanghai Blossom is about 440 m away, and Playground at Jorgensen Park is another option about 330 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.2 km from the playground, and bus 381 stops at Walling Way/Serle St, a 2-minute walk away.
