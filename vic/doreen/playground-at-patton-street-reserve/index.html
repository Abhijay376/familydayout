Playground at Patton Street Reserve

Playground in Doreen · Basic info

Playground at Patton Street Reserve is a public playground in Doreen, in the Whittlesea City area. Playground at Ferlie Park is another option about 160 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 580 m from the playground, and bus 389 stops at Independence Bvd/Bridge Inn Rd , a 8-minute walk away.
