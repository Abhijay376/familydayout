Cathedral Walkway

Park in Doreen · Basic info

Cathedral Walkway is a park in Doreen, in the Whittlesea City Council area, good for all ages. Cathedral Rise/Timbertop Dr (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 760 m away.
