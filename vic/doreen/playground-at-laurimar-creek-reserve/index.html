Playground at Laurimar Creek Reserve

Playground in Doreen · Basic info

Playground at Laurimar Creek Reserve in Doreen is a public playground in the Whittlesea City area. The Laurimar Creek Trail is about 20 m away, and Playground at Red Angus Park is another option about 410 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 960 m from the playground, and bus 381 stops at Waterway Bvd/Overland Dr, a 3-minute walk away.
