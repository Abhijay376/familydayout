Playground at Elation Park South

Playground in Doreen · Basic info

Playground at Elation Park South is a public playground in Doreen, in the Whittlesea City area. Pier 4 Fish & Chippery is about 170 m away if you need food, and Playground on Patience Avenue is another option about 290 m away. There's a public toilet about 920 m away, and bus 385 stops at Orchard Rd/Elation Bvd, a 2-minute walk away.
