Smokey Park

Park in Doreen · Basic info

Smokey Park is a park in Doreen, in the Whittlesea City Council area, good for all ages. Ashgate Dr/Orchard Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1140 m away.
