Mulberry Street Walkway

Park in Doreen · Basic info

Mulberry Street Walkway is a park in Doreen, in the Whittlesea City Council area, good for all ages. Eliot Ave/Flaxen Hills Rd (bus) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 467 m away with a baby change table.
