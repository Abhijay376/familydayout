Walker Tree Reserve

Park in Doreen · Basic info

Walker Tree Reserve is a park in Doreen, in the Whittlesea City Council area, good for all ages. Ballam Way/Hazel Glen Dr (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 902 m away with a baby change table.
