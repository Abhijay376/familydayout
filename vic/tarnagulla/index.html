Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Tarnagulla. For walks and rides, Dunolly Cycle Tracks and Tarnagulla Cycle Track cross Tarnagulla.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tarnagulla. The closest to Tarnagulla is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 32 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Tarnagulla. By car from Tarnagulla, Maryborough is about 38 minutes away, Bendigo about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly is about 13 minutes' drive from Tarnagulla. The closest library to Tarnagulla is Athenaeum Library in Maldon, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Tarnagulla is Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk, about 35 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 40 minutes' drive from Tarnagulla. The closest indoor play centre to Tarnagulla is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 40 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 45 minutes' drive from Tarnagulla. Maryborough is the closest bigger centre to Tarnagulla, about 38 minutes away by car, and has a library. From Tarnagulla, the nearest places for kids by road are Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (13 min), Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (14 min), the playground at Market Reserve in Carisbrook (39 min), Athenaeum Library in Maldon (34 min), Maldon Swimming Pool in Maldon (34 min) and the playground at Maldon Shire Gardens in Maldon (34 min).

Best for

Free days on the Dunolly Cycle Tracks and in Tarnagulla's parks.
