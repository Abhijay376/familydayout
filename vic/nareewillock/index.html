Landmarks

The biggest green space in Nareewillock is Narrewillock Bushland Reserve, covering about 1 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Nareewillock. The closest to Nareewillock is Kerang Railway Station (V/Line), about 63 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nareewillock, so most trips start by car. By car from Nareewillock, Bendigo is about 1.5 hours away and Swan Hill about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Boort Swimming Pool in Boort is about 20 minutes' drive from Nareewillock. The closest cinema to Nareewillock is Rex Theatre in Charlton, about 17 minutes' drive. From Nareewillock, the nearest places for kids by road are Rex Theatre in Charlton (17 min), Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (17 min), the playground at Rotary Park in Charlton (17 min), the playground at Gordon Park in Charlton (17 min), Boort Swimming Pool in Boort (20 min) and Boort Court House Historical Museum in Boort (20 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Nareewillock.

Nearby suburbs

Charlton, next to Nareewillock, has 3 playgrounds and a cinema. Glenloth, Bunguluke and Buckrabanyule also border Nareewillock.
