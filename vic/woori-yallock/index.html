Woori Yallock is in the Yarra Ranges area, and our data lists 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Woori Yallock Reserve is the largest mapped park in Woori Yallock, at about 5 hectares. Lone Star Creek Bushland Reserve and Symes Road Park are the next largest. Playgrounds in Woori Yallock include the playground at Symes Road Park. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Woori Yallock. For walks and rides, Lilydale-Warburton Rail Trail and Warburton Rail Trail cross Woori Yallock.

Getting around

There's no train station in Woori Yallock. The closest to Woori Yallock is Lilydale Railway Station (metro), about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 683 and 965 stop in Woori Yallock. By car from Woori Yallock, Healesville is about 16 minutes away, Lilydale about 20 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 61 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Seville Water Play Park in Seville is about 6 minutes' drive from Woori Yallock. For a library, Yarra Junction public library in Yarra Junction is about 10 minutes' drive from Woori Yallock. The closest skate park to Woori Yallock is the skate park at Yarra Junction Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Yarra Junction, about 10 minutes' drive. For a cinema, The Memo, Healesville in Healesville is about 16 minutes' drive from Woori Yallock. For an indoor play centre, PlayPlace in Mooroolbark is about 26 minutes' drive from Woori Yallock. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Woori Yallock is Lyre Bird enclosure in Badger Creek, about 13 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Healesville, about 16 minutes from Woori Yallock by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema.

Best for

Free days on the Lilydale-Warburton Rail Trail and in Woori Yallock's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Woori Yallock, Healesville has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. Next door to Woori Yallock, Seville East has 1 playground. Gruyere, next to Woori Yallock, has 1 playground. Mount Toolebewong, Launching Place and Hoddles Creek also border Woori Yallock.
