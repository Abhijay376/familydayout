Neerim Junction is in the Baw Baw area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Nayook Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Neerim Junction, at about 9 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Neerim Junction.

Getting around

Neerim Junction has no train station; Drouin Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 25 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 89 stops in Neerim Junction. By car from Neerim Junction, Warragul is about 27 minutes away, Pakenham about 54 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

The closest places for kids by road from Neerim Junction are the playground at LaTrobe and Loch Rivers, Noojee Streamside Reserve in Noojee (8 min), Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South (9 min), the playground at Ellen Close Linear Reserve in Warragul (26 min), Warragul Library in Warragul (28 min), Warragul Cinema in Warragul (28 min) and The Alley Warragul in Warragul (28 min).
