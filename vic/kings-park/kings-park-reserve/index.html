Kings Park Reserve

Park in Kings Park · Official details

Kings Park Reserve is a park in Kings Park, in the Brimbank City Council area, good for all ages. Maplewood Rd/Gillespie Rd (bus) is a 9-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 75 m away.

Good to know

Questions parents ask

Where is Kings Park Reserve?
Kings Park Reserve is in Kings Park, Victoria.
Is Kings Park Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Kings Park Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 75 m away.
How do I get to Kings Park Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Maplewood Rd/Gillespie Rd (routes 425), a 9-minute walk.
