Kings Park is in the Brimbank area, and our data lists 10 playgrounds and 8 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Kings Park is Kings Park Reserve, covering about 7 hectares. Cyril Clements Reserve and Kurung Drive Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Kings Park include Myuna Drive Reserve, Cromwell Road Reserve and Beverley Street Reserve. Playgrounds around Kings Park include the playground at Kings Park Reserve, the playground at Myuna Drive Reserve and the playground at Kurung Drive Reserve. Our data shows 4 public toilets and 1 park or playground with a council BBQ close by in Kings Park. For walks and rides, Hume Drive Trail crosses Kings Park.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kings Park. The closest to Kings Park is Keilor Plains Railway Station (metro), about 1.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 418, 420, 424 and 425 stop in Kings Park. By car from Kings Park, Sunshine is about 12 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 21 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 31 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kings Park is Warm Water in Keilor Downs, about 4 minutes' drive. For a library, Brimbank Libraries Deer Park Branch in Deer Park is about 5 minutes' drive from Kings Park. For a beach, Altona Beach in Altona is about 26 minutes' drive from Kings Park. For a skate park, the skate park at Packard Street Reserve in Keilor Downs is about 4 minutes' drive from Kings Park. For a cinema, Hoyts in Taylors Lakes is about 7 minutes' drive from Kings Park. For an indoor play centre, Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes is about 8 minutes' drive from Kings Park. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Kings Park is Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville, about 26 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 10 playgrounds across Kings Park. Free days on the Hume Drive Trail and in Kings Park's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Kings Park, St Albans has 33 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Kings Park, Taylors Hill has 18 playgrounds. Next door to Kings Park, Burnside has 6 playgrounds. Next door to Kings Park, Delahey has 5 playgrounds. Burnside Heights and Albanvale also border Kings Park.
