Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Yambuna in the Campaspe area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks crosses Yambuna.

Getting around

Yambuna has no train station; Echuca Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yambuna, so most trips start by car. By car from Yambuna, Echuca is about 37 minutes away and Shepparton about 64 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Kyabram Library And Community Hub in Kyabram is about 38 minutes' drive from Yambuna. The closest skate park to Yambuna is the bmx track on Racecourse Road in Kyabram, about 40 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca is about 38 minutes' drive from Yambuna. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Yambuna is Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram, about 40 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 37 minutes from Yambuna by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Yambuna, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at McCormick Reserve in Kyabram (40 min), the playground at Drum Street Reserve in Kyabram (37 min), Kyabram Library And Community Hub in Kyabram (38 min), Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram (40 min), the bmx track on Racecourse Road in Kyabram (40 min) and Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca (38 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Yambuna on the Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks.

Nearby suburbs

Kotupna and Kanyapella also border Yambuna.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Yambuna, without traffic.
