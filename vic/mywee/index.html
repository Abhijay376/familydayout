Mywee is in the Moira area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb. Mywee also has 6 beaches.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Mywee is Strathmerton Bushland Reserve, covering about 35 hectares. In Mywee you'll also find Breens Beach and Forest Beach.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mywee. The closest to Mywee is Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line), about 59 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mywee, so most trips start by car. By car from Mywee, Shepparton is about 65 minutes away and Benalla about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah is about 38 minutes' drive from Mywee. For a library, Tocumwal Library in Koonoomoo is about 24 minutes' drive from Mywee. For a skate park, Strathmerton Community Skate Park in Strathmerton is about 21 minutes' drive from Mywee. For a cinema, Cobram Cinema in Cobram is about 31 minutes' drive from Mywee. The closest places for kids by road from Mywee are Bouchiers Beach in Koonoomoo (21 min), Carter Beach in Ulupna (22 min), Strathmerton Community Skate Park in Strathmerton (21 min), Tocumwal Library in Koonoomoo (24 min), the playground at Federation Park in Cobram (30 min) and the skate park at Federation Park in Cobram (31 min).

Best for

Beach days at Breens Beach and Forest Beach.

Nearby suburbs

Koonoomoo, next to Mywee, has 1 playground, a library and a beach. Next door to Mywee, Ulupna has a beach. Strathmerton, next to Mywee, has a skate park.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Mywee, without traffic.
