Hadfield in the Merri-Bek area has 4 playgrounds and 5 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Martin Reserve is the largest mapped park in Hadfield, at about 4 hectares. Reddish Reserve and Glenroy Lions Park are the next largest. Hadfield also has Kennan Reserve and Mommsen Reserve. Playgrounds around Hadfield include the playground at Martin Reserve, the playground at Mommsen Reserve and the playground at Reddish Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Hadfield. The Upfield Bike Path and Upfield Shared Path all pass through Hadfield. Our data lists 4 cafes and family restaurants in Hadfield for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Fawkner Railway Station (metro: Upfield Line) in Hadfield. Buses 527, 530, 531, 534, 536 and 951 stop in Hadfield. By car from Hadfield, Broadmeadows is about 8 minutes away, Sunshine about 20 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 22 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Elite Swimming in Pascoe Vale is about 5 minutes' drive from Hadfield. The closest library to Hadfield is Little free library in Coburg North, about 3 minutes' drive. For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 23 minutes' drive from Hadfield. The closest skate park to Hadfield is Coburg Velodrome in Coburg North, about 5 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Pentridge Cinema in Coburg is about 7 minutes' drive from Hadfield. The closest indoor play centre to Hadfield is The Keys in Preston, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Hadfield is Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville, about 16 minutes' drive. Broadmeadows is the closest bigger centre to Hadfield, about 8 minutes away by car, and has 12 playgrounds, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Hadfield's 4 local playgrounds. Free days on the Upfield Bike Path and in Hadfield's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Coburg North, next to Hadfield, has 14 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Hadfield, Glenroy has 17 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Hadfield, Fawkner has 12 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Pascoe Vale, next to Hadfield, has 12 playgrounds and a pool.
