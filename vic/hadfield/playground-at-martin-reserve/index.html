Playground at Martin Reserve

Playground in Hadfield · Official details

This is a public playground in Hadfield. The Reservoir - Merri Creek LCN passes about 100 m away, and Playground at LaTrobe Park is another option about 520 m away. There's a public toilet about 510 m away, and buses 527 and 536 stop at Fairleigh St/Box Forest Rd, a 2-minute walk away.

  • Fenced: Not checked yet
  • Toilet: 510 m walk, accessible toilet, daylight hours
  • Trail: Reservoir - Merri Creek LCN (100 m)
