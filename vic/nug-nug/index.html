Nug Nug is in the Alpine area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Nug Nug, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Nug Nug. The closest to Nug Nug is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 49 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nug Nug, so most trips start by car. By car from Nug Nug, Bright is about 46 minutes away and Wangaratta about 60 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Nug Nug is the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford, about 19 minutes' drive. For a library, Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford is about 20 minutes' drive from Nug Nug. The closest beach to Nug Nug is Sandy Beach in Beechworth, about 45 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford is about 20 minutes' drive from Nug Nug. Bright is the closest bigger centre to Nug Nug, about 46 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Nug Nug are the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford (19 min), Myrtleford Swimming Pool in Myrtleford (20 min), The Old School Museum in Myrtleford (20 min), Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford (20 min), Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford (20 min) and Cundy Park Playground in Myrtleford (20 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Nug Nug.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Nug Nug, Myrtleford has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Buffalo River, next to Nug Nug, has 1 playground. Eurobin also borders Nug Nug.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Nug Nug, without traffic.
