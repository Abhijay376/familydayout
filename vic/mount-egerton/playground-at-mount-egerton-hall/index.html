Playground at Mount Egerton Hall

Playground in Mount Egerton · Basic info

Playground at Mount Egerton Hall is a public playground in Mount Egerton, in the Moorabool Shire area. Playground at Mount Egerton Recreation Reserve is another option about 460 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 640 m from the playground, and bus Ballan - Mount Egerton via Gordon stops at Mt Egerton General Store/Main Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
