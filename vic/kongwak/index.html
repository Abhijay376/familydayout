Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Kongwak in the South Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Kongwak.

Getting around

Kongwak has no train station; Drouin Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 44 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kongwak, so most trips start by car. By car from Kongwak, Wonthaggi is about 19 minutes away, Leongatha about 20 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Korumburra Outdoor Pool in Korumburra is about 15 minutes' drive from Kongwak. For a library, Wonthaggi Principal Library in Wonthaggi is about 18 minutes' drive from Kongwak. For a beach, The Caves in Inverloch is about 18 minutes' drive from Kongwak. For a skate park, Wonthaggi Skate Park in Wonthaggi is about 17 minutes' drive from Kongwak. The closest cinema to Kongwak is Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi, about 18 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville is about 25 minutes' drive from Kongwak. The nearest larger centre is Wonthaggi, about 19 minutes from Kongwak by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Kongwak, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Peterson Street Reserve in Korumburra (15 min), the playground at Newton Reserve in Inverloch (12 min), Korumburra Outdoor Pool in Korumburra (15 min), Bunurong Environment Centre & Shell Museum in Inverloch (12 min), Coal Creek Community Park & Museum in Korumburra (18 min) and Wonthaggi Skate Park in Wonthaggi (17 min).

Best for

Nearby suburbs
