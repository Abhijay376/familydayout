Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Tenby Point in the Bass Coast area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Guy Road Shared Pathway crosses Tenby Point.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tenby Point. The closest to Tenby Point is Stony Point Railway Station (metro), about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tenby Point, so most trips start by car. By car from Tenby Point, Wonthaggi is about 34 minutes away, Pakenham about 48 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tenby Point is Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Wonthaggi, about 33 minutes' drive. For a library, San Remo Library in San Remo is about 22 minutes' drive from Tenby Point. For a beach, Corinella Beach in Corinella is about 10 minutes' drive from Tenby Point. The closest skate park to Tenby Point is the skate park at Wonthaggi Railway Historic Reserve in Kilcunda, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Tenby Point is Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi, about 33 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville is about 10 minutes' drive from Tenby Point. Wonthaggi is the closest bigger centre to Tenby Point, about 34 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Tenby Point are Corinella Beach in Corinella (10 min), Chicory Kiln in French Island (12 min), Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville (10 min), the playground at George Bass Memorial Park in Bass (14 min), Art Fusion in Anderson (17 min) and Shelly Beach in Kilcunda (21 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Tenby Point on the Guy Road Shared Pathway.
