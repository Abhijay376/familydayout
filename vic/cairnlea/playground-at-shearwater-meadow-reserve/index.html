Playground at Shearwater Meadow Reserve

Playground in Cairnlea · Official details

This is a public playground in Cairnlea. Playground on Shepperd Way is another option about 330 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 700 m away, and bus 423 stops at Shearwater Meadow/Furlong Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
