Playground at Station Waters Reserve

Playground in Cairnlea · Basic info

This is a public playground in Cairnlea. The Kororoit Creek Trail is about 90 m away, and Playground at Waterfield Park (Stage 1) is another option about 480 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 890 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 4 minutes from Cairnlea.
