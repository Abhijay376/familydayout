Playground at Waterfield Park (Stage 1)

Playground in Cairnlea · Official details

Playground at Waterfield Park (Stage 1) is a public playground in Cairnlea, in the Brimbank City area. The Kororoit Creek Trail is about 360 m away, and Playground on Shepperd Way is another option about 320 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 710 m from the playground, and bus 423 stops at Clare Brennan Dr/Furlong Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
