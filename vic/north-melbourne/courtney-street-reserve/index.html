Courtney Street Reserve

Park in North Melbourne · Official details

Courtney Street Reserve is a park in North Melbourne, in the Melbourne City Council area, good for all ages. Queensberry St/Peel St #11 (tram) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 284 m away.
