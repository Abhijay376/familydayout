North Melbourne Community Centre Playground

Playground in North Melbourne · Official details

This is a public playground in North Melbourne. The Ascot Vale to CBD is about 220 m away, and for food, Moroccan Soup Bar is about 130 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 380 m from the playground, and bus 402 stops at Boundary Rd/Canning St, a 4-minute walk away.
