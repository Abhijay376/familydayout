Curzon Street Reserve

Park in North Melbourne · Basic info

Curzon Street Reserve is a park in North Melbourne, in the Melbourne City Council area, good for all ages. Curzon St/Haines St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 364 m away.
