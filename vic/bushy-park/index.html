Bushy Park is in the Wellington area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bushy Park, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Bushy Park has no train station; Stratford Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bushy Park, so most trips start by car. By car from Bushy Park, Sale is about 28 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 63 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford is about 23 minutes' drive from Bushy Park. The closest library to Bushy Park is Sale Library in Sale, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Bushy Park is Skate Park in Stratford, about 24 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Former Drive-in site in Sale is about 28 minutes' drive from Bushy Park. The nearest larger centre is Sale, about 28 minutes from Bushy Park by car, with 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Bushy Park, the nearest places for kids by road are Boisdale Recreation Reserve Playground in Boisdale (3 min), Anzac Park Playground in Briagolong (8 min), Skate Park in Stratford (24 min), Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford (23 min), Former Drive-in site in Sale (28 min) and Sale Historical Museum in Sale (29 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bushy Park.

Nearby suburbs

Briagolong, next to Bushy Park, has 2 playgrounds. Boisdale, next to Bushy Park, has 1 playground.
