Kilmore is in the Mitchell area, and our data lists 8 playgrounds and 9 parks and reserves in the suburb. On top of that, Kilmore has a library, a pool and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Kilmore is Monument Hill Reserve, covering about 70 hectares. J. J. Clancy Reserve and Apex Park are the next largest. Kilmore also has Hudson Park, Link Reserve and Centenary Reserve. Playgrounds around Kilmore include Link Reserve Playground, the playground at Hudson Park and Centenary Drive Playground. Our data shows 11 public toilets in Kilmore. Places to visit in Kilmore include Kilmore Leisure Centre and Kilmore Library. For older kids in Kilmore there's Kilmore Skate Park. Our data lists 8 cafes and family restaurants in Kilmore for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kilmore. The closest to Kilmore is Kilmore East Station (V/Line), about 2.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Kilmore Town Service (Link Bus) route stop in Kilmore. V/Line coaches stop in Kilmore. By car from Kilmore, Seymour is about 33 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 44 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 63 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a cinema, United Cinemas in Craigieburn is about 35 minutes' drive from Kilmore. For an indoor play centre, Timezone in Craigieburn is about 34 minutes' drive from Kilmore. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Kilmore is Funfields in Whittlesea, about 31 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 8 playgrounds across Kilmore. Rainy days at the library in Kilmore. Teens who skate or ride, with Kilmore Skate Park in Kilmore.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Kilmore, Wandong has 1 playground and a skate park. Next door to Kilmore, Kilmore East has 1 playground. Forbes, Clonbinane and Bylands also border Kilmore.
