Hudson Park

Park in Kilmore · Basic info

Hudson Park is a park in Kilmore, in the Mitchell Shire Council area, good for all ages. Hudson Park/Sydney St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 7 m away.

This page has basic info from the map: name, location and type. We have not checked the details yet. Know this place? Tell us.
