Mincha is in the Loddon area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Mincha, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Mincha has no train station; Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 6.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mincha, so most trips start by car. By car from Mincha, Echuca is about 73 minutes away and Swan Hill about 83 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mincha is Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest library to Mincha is Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Mincha are Pyramid Hill & Districts Historical Museum in Pyramid Hill (10 min), the playground at C L King Memorial Park in Cohuna (48 min), Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna (49 min), the playground at Cohuna Apex Park in Cohuna (49 min), Cohuna Pool in Cohuna (50 min) and East Loddon Historical Society in Mitiamo (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mincha.
