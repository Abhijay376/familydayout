Playground at Timberglades Reserve

Playground in Montrose · Basic info

Playground at Timberglades Reserve in Montrose is a public playground in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. The Ways of Seeing (ngurrak barring | RidgeWalk) passes about 400 m away, and Playground at Montrose Recreational Reserve is another option about 460 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.0 km away, and bus 688 stops at Old Coach Rd/Mt Dandenong Tourist Rd, a 8-minute walk away.
