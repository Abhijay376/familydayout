The People’s Garden

Park in Montrose · Basic info

The People’s Garden is a park in Montrose, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Montrose Rd/Mt Dandenong Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 296 m away.
