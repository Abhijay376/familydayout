Montrose Road Playspace

Park in Montrose · Basic info

Montrose Road Playspace is a park in Montrose, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Tasman Ct/Montrose Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 528 m away with a baby change table.
