Gentle Annie in the Baw Baw area has 7 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Latrobe State Forest is the largest mapped park in Gentle Annie, at about 14,854 hectares. Tarago River Forest Reserve and Labertouche State Forest are the next largest. Gentle Annie also has Limberlost, Latrobe River and Labertouche Cave Geological Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Gentle Annie. Picnic areas in Gentle Annie include Latrobe River, Latrobe River Picnic and Camping Area and Limberlost. The Powelltown Tramways Railtrails, Walk into history Trail and Freemans Mill Track all pass through Gentle Annie.

Getting around

Gentle Annie has no train station; Drouin Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gentle Annie, so most trips start by car. By car from Gentle Annie, Warragul is about 36 minutes away, Healesville about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Warburton Water World in Warburton is about 39 minutes' drive from Gentle Annie. For a library, Yarra Junction public library in Yarra Junction is about 30 minutes' drive from Gentle Annie. The closest skate park to Gentle Annie is Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South, about 17 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 36 minutes' drive from Gentle Annie. The closest indoor play centre to Gentle Annie is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 37 minutes' drive. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Gentle Annie, about 36 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Free days on the Powelltown Tramways Railtrails and in Gentle Annie's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Gembrook, next to Gentle Annie, has 6 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Gentle Annie, Powelltown has 1 playground. Noojee, next to Gentle Annie, has 1 playground. Neerim South, Neerim Junction, Neerim and Nayook also border Gentle Annie.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Gentle Annie, without traffic.
