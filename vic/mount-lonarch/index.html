Mount Lonarch is in the Pyrenees area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Mount Lonarch is Mt Lonarch Berumgower State Forest, covering about 1,164 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Mount Lonarch include Mount Lonarch. Picnic areas in Mount Lonarch include Mount Lonarch.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mount Lonarch. The closest to Mount Lonarch is Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line), about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mount Lonarch, so most trips start by car. By car from Mount Lonarch, Maryborough is about 40 minutes away, Ararat about 44 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mount Lonarch is Beaufort Pool in Beaufort, about 27 minutes' drive. For a library, Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort is about 26 minutes' drive from Mount Lonarch. For a skate park, the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort is about 27 minutes' drive from Mount Lonarch. Maryborough is the closest bigger centre to Mount Lonarch, about 40 minutes away by car, and has a library. The closest places for kids by road from Mount Lonarch are the playground at Toll Bar Park in Lexton (11 min), the playground at Avoca Lions Park in Avoca (18 min), Courthouse in Avoca (18 min), Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort (26 min), the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (27 min) and Beaufort Pool in Beaufort (27 min).
