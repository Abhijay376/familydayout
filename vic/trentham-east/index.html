Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Trentham East, in the Macedon Ranges area.

Landmarks

Trentham East also has Firth Park. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Trentham East. Picnic areas in Trentham East include Firth Park. The Anderson Mill Walk and Anderson Mill Walking Track all pass through Trentham East.

Getting around

Trentham East has no train station; Woodend Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Trentham East, so most trips start by car. By car from Trentham East, Kyneton is about 25 minutes away, Daylesford about 31 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 78 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford is about 31 minutes' drive from Trentham East. The closest library to Trentham East is Kyneton Library in Kyneton, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Trentham East is Canoeing in Tylden, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Trentham East is Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton, about 23 minutes' drive. Kyneton is the closest bigger centre to Trentham East, about 25 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Trentham East are Little Gallery in Trentham (8 min), the playground at Quarry Street Reserve in Trentham (8 min), the playground at J P Gleeson Memorial Park in Lyonville (16 min), Canoeing in Tylden (19 min), Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton (23 min) and Kyneton Library in Kyneton (25 min).
