Nandaly is in the Buloke area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Nandaly is Nandaly Bushland Reserve, covering about 1 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Nandaly.

Getting around

There's no train station in Nandaly. The closest to Nandaly is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 79 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nandaly, so most trips start by car. By car from Nandaly, Swan Hill is about 80 minutes away and Mildura about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Nandaly is Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Nandaly are Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake (24 min), the playground at Apex Park in Sea Lake (24 min), the playground at Tempy Recreation Reserve in Tempy (32 min) and Tempy Outdoor Swimming Pool in Tempy (32 min).

Best for
