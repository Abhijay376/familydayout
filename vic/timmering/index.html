Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Timmering in the Campaspe area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Timmering. The closest to Timmering is Rochester Railway Station (V/Line), about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Timmering, so most trips start by car. By car from Timmering, Echuca is about 35 minutes away, Shepparton about 52 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Timmering is Memorial Pool in Rochester, about 12 minutes' drive. For a library, Kyabram Library And Community Hub in Kyabram is about 19 minutes' drive from Timmering. The closest skate park to Timmering is the bmx track on Racecourse Road in Kyabram, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Timmering is Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca, about 35 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram is about 21 minutes' drive from Timmering. Echuca is the closest bigger centre to Timmering, about 35 minutes away by car, and has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Timmering are Memorial Pool in Rochester (12 min), the playground at Apex Park in Rochester (13 min), the playground at McCormick Reserve in Kyabram (18 min), Kyabram Library And Community Hub in Kyabram (19 min), the bmx track on Racecourse Road in Kyabram (20 min) and Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Timmering.

Nearby suburbs

Kyabram, next to Timmering, has 8 playgrounds, a library and a zoo or wildlife park. Girgarre and Corop also border Timmering.
