Banyan is in the Buloke area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Banyan is Green Lake Regional Park, covering about 306 hectares. Banyan Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Banyan has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 80 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Banyan, so most trips start by car. By car from Banyan, Swan Hill is about 69 minutes away and Horsham about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Banyan is Woomelang Recreation Reserve Swimming Pool in Woomelang, about 11 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Birchip Skate Park in Birchip is about 38 minutes' drive from Banyan. The closest places for kids by road from Banyan are Woomelang Recreation Reserve Swimming Pool in Woomelang (11 min), the playground at Woomelang Rec Reserve in Woomelang (11 min), the playground at High way Park Woomelang in Woomelang (11 min), Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock (18 min), Birchip Skate Park in Birchip (38 min) and Birchip History Museum in Birchip (38 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Banyan.

Nearby suburbs

Woomelang, next to Banyan, has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Sea Lake, next to Banyan, has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Banyan, Berriwillock has a pool. Myall and Boigbeat also border Banyan.
