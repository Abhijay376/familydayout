Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Bungil, in the Towong area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bungil is Mount Lawson State Park, covering about 13,359 hectares. Bungil Reference Area is the next largest. The High Country Rail Trail and Tallangatta - Firebrace Loop all pass through Bungil.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bungil. The closest to Bungil is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 39 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bungil, so most trips start by car. By car from Bungil, Wodonga is about 52 minutes away and Wangaratta about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta is about 33 minutes' drive from Bungil. For a library, Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta is about 32 minutes' drive from Bungil. Wodonga is the closest bigger centre to Bungil, about 52 minutes away by car, and has 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Bungil, the nearest places for kids by road are Granya Museum in Granya (7 min), Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta (32 min), Tallangatta Art & Photography in Tallangatta (32 min), the playground at Roy Williams Park in Bellbridge (30 min) and Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta (33 min).

Best for

Free days on the High Country Rail Trail and in Bungil's parks.
