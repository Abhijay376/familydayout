Myrrhee is in the Wangaratta area, and our data lists 1 playground and 5 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Myrrhee is Drum Top Reference Area, covering about 402 hectares. Unnamed P0074 Private Nature Reserve and Myrrhee H2 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Myrrhee include Whitfield Scenic Reserve and Whitfield H1 Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds around Myrrhee include Myrrhee Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Myrrhee.

Getting around

There's no train station in Myrrhee. The closest to Myrrhee is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 37 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Myrrhee, so most trips start by car. By car from Myrrhee, Wangaratta is about 42 minutes away and Mansfield about 57 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Splashpark in Wangaratta is about 38 minutes' drive from Myrrhee. The closest library to Myrrhee is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 41 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta is about 38 minutes' drive from Myrrhee. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 41 minutes' drive from Myrrhee. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Myrrhee, about 42 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Myrrhee, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Apex Park in Moyhu (17 min), Splashpark in Wangaratta (38 min), the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta (38 min), Cobb & Co in Glenrowan (38 min), Glenrowan Museum in Glenrowan (38 min) and Moore than Swimming in Wangaratta (39 min).

Best for

Nearby suburbs

Whitfield, next to Myrrhee, has 1 playground. Tolmie, next to Myrrhee, has 1 playground. Next door to Myrrhee, Moyhu has 1 playground. Tatong, King Valley, Hansonville and Greta South also border Myrrhee.
