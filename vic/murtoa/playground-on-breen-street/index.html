Playground at Lake Marma Park- Lions

Playground in Murtoa · Basic info

Playground at Lake Marma Park- Lions is a public playground in Murtoa, in the Yarriambiack Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 250 m away, and Water play at Rabl Park is about 90 m away. There's a public toilet about 160 m away, and coaches stop at McDonald St/Marma St, a 8-minute walk away.
