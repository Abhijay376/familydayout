Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Elizabeth Island, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Elizabeth Island. The closest to Elizabeth Island is Stony Point Railway Station (metro), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Elizabeth Island, so most trips start by car. By car from Elizabeth Island, Wonthaggi is about 38 minutes away, Pakenham about 51 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Wonthaggi is about 36 minutes' drive from Elizabeth Island. The closest library to Elizabeth Island is San Remo Library in San Remo, about 25 minutes' drive. For a beach, Corinella Beach in Corinella is about 2 minutes' drive from Elizabeth Island. For a skate park, the skate park at Wonthaggi Railway Historic Reserve in Kilcunda is about 25 minutes' drive from Elizabeth Island. For a cinema, Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi is about 36 minutes' drive from Elizabeth Island. For a zoo or wildlife park, Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville is about 14 minutes' drive from Elizabeth Island. Wonthaggi is the closest bigger centre to Elizabeth Island, about 38 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Elizabeth Island, the nearest places for kids by road are Chicory Kiln in French Island (1 min), Corinella Beach in Corinella (2 min), Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville (14 min), the playground at George Bass Memorial Park in Bass (17 min), Art Fusion in Anderson (21 min) and Shelly Beach in Kilcunda (24 min).
