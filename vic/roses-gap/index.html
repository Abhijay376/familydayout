Roses Gap is in the Northern Grampians area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Roses Gap, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

The Grampians Peaks Trail, Briggs Bluff Walk and Gar Waterfalls Walk all pass through Roses Gap.

Getting around

Roses Gap has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 56 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Roses Gap, so most trips start by car. By car from Roses Gap, Stawell is about 31 minutes away and Horsham about 44 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Halls Gap Swimming Pool in Halls Gap is about 33 minutes' drive from Roses Gap. The closest skate park to Roses Gap is the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell, about 31 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Centre Cinemas in Horsham is about 43 minutes' drive from Roses Gap. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Roses Gap is Halls Gap Zoo in Halls Gap, about 36 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Stawell, about 31 minutes from Roses Gap by car, with 6 playgrounds and a pool. From Roses Gap, the nearest places for kids by road are Glenorchy Playground in Glenorchy (27 min), Halls Gap Swimming Pool in Halls Gap (33 min), the playground at Halls Gap Picnic Reserve in Halls Gap (33 min), Brambuk Cultural Centre in Halls Gap (37 min), Halls Gap Zoo in Halls Gap (36 min) and MOCO Gallery in Halls Gap (37 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Roses Gap on the Grampians Peaks Trail.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Roses Gap, without traffic.
