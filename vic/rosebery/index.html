Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Rosebery in the Yarriambiack area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Rosebery has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 117 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Rosebery. By car from Rosebery, Horsham is about 82 minutes away and Swan Hill about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Rosebery is Hopetoun Swimming Pool in Hopetoun, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Rosebery are the playground at Beulah Lions Park in Beulah (10 min), the playground at Beulah Lake Park in Beulah (11 min), Hopetoun Swimming Pool in Hopetoun (12 min), Woomelang Recreation Reserve Swimming Pool in Woomelang (37 min), Historical Centre in Warracknabeal (38 min) and Wheatlands Warracknabeal Agricultural Machinery Museum in Warracknabeal (39 min).

Best for
