Playground at Bristow Reserve

Playground in Seddon · Basic info

Playground at Bristow Reserve in Seddon is a public playground in the Maribyrnong City area. The Footscray Popup Bike routes is about 250 m away, and for food, Miss An'am is about 260 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 540 m from the playground, and buses 411, 412, 414 and 947 stop at John St/Buckley St, a 3-minute walk away.
