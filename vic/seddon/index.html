Landmarks

Mappin Reserve is the largest mapped park in Seddon, at about 1 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Seddon include Seddon Reserve, Bristow Reserve and Manallack Reserve. Playgrounds around Seddon include the playground at Mappin Reserve and the playground at Bristow Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Seddon. Places to visit in Seddon include Polish Museum and Archives in Australia. For walks and rides, Footscray Popup Bike routes crosses Seddon. Our data lists 10 cafes and family restaurants in Seddon for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Seddon Railway Station (metro: Werribee Line and Williamstown Line) in Seddon. Buses 223, 409, 411, 412, 414, 472 and 947 stop in Seddon. By car from Seddon, Sunshine is about 12 minutes away, Werribee about 24 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 15 minutes, without traffic.
