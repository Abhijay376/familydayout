Nambrok is in the Wellington area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Nambrok, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Nambrok has no train station; Rosedale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nambrok, so most trips start by car. By car from Nambrok, Sale is about 22 minutes away, Traralgon about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rosedale Swimming Pool in Rosedale is about 15 minutes' drive from Nambrok. For a library, Sale Library in Sale is about 21 minutes' drive from Nambrok. For a skate park, Pump Track in Heyfield is about 14 minutes' drive from Nambrok. For a cinema, Sale Cinemas in Sale is about 22 minutes' drive from Nambrok. The nearest larger centre is Sale, about 22 minutes from Nambrok by car, with 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Nambrok, the nearest places for kids by road are Queen Street Reserve Playground in Rosedale (15 min), Rosedale Swimming Pool in Rosedale (15 min), Prince Street Reserve Playground in Rosedale (15 min), Heyfield and District Museum in Heyfield (14 min), Pump Track in Heyfield (14 min) and Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield (14 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Nambrok.

Nearby suburbs

Rosedale, next to Nambrok, has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Denison also borders Nambrok.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Nambrok, without traffic.
