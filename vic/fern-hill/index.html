Fern Hill is in the Hepburn area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Fern Hill, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Fern Hill. The closest to Fern Hill is Kyneton Railway Station (V/Line), about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Fern Hill, so most trips start by car. By car from Fern Hill, Kyneton is about 23 minutes away, Daylesford about 31 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 79 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford is about 31 minutes' drive from Fern Hill. The closest library to Fern Hill is Kyneton Library in Kyneton, about 23 minutes' drive. For a beach, Canoeing in Tylden is about 17 minutes' drive from Fern Hill. The closest skate park to Fern Hill is Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton, about 21 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Kyneton, about 23 minutes from Fern Hill by car, with 10 playgrounds and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Fern Hill are Little Gallery in Trentham (10 min), the playground at Quarry Street Reserve in Trentham (10 min), Canoeing in Tylden (17 min), Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton (21 min), the playground at J P Gleeson Memorial Park in Lyonville (16 min) and Kyneton Library in Kyneton (23 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Fern Hill.

Nearby suburbs

Tylden, next to Fern Hill, has 1 playground and a beach. Trentham, next to Fern Hill, has 2 playgrounds.
