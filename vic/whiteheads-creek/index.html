Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Whiteheads Creek in the Mitchell area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Whiteheads Creek. The Great Victorian Rail Trail passes through Whiteheads Creek.

Getting around

There's no train station in Whiteheads Creek. The closest to Whiteheads Creek is Seymour Railway Station (V/Line), about 8.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Whiteheads Creek. By car from Whiteheads Creek, Seymour is about 14 minutes away, Shepparton about 76 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Seymour Swimming Centre in Seymour is about 13 minutes' drive from Whiteheads Creek. For a library, Seymour Library in Seymour is about 14 minutes' drive from Whiteheads Creek. For a skate park, Seymour Skate Park in Seymour is about 14 minutes' drive from Whiteheads Creek. Seymour is the closest bigger centre to Whiteheads Creek, about 14 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Whiteheads Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at M O'Sullivan Reserve in Seymour (13 min), Seymour Swimming Centre in Seymour (13 min), Seymour Skate Park in Seymour (14 min), the playground at O'Shea Park in Seymour (15 min), Seymour Library in Seymour (14 min) and Memorial Swimming pool in Seymour (16 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Whiteheads Creek on the Great Victorian Rail Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Seymour, next to Whiteheads Creek, has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Whiteheads Creek, Tallarook has 1 playground. Avenel, next to Whiteheads Creek, has 1 playground. Tarcombe, Highlands and Ghin Ghin also border Whiteheads Creek.
