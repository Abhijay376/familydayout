Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Coragulac in the Colac Otway area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Coragulac. The closest to Coragulac is Colac Railway Station (V/Line), about 10 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Colac - Alvie via Warrion route stop in Coragulac. By car from Coragulac, Colac is about 14 minutes away, Geelong about 79 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Coragulac is Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac, about 17 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt is about 19 minutes' drive from Coragulac. The closest cinema to Coragulac is Colac Cinemas in Colac, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Coragulac is Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac, about 15 minutes' drive. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Coragulac, about 14 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Coragulac are the playground at Red Rock Scenic Reserve in Alvie (8 min), the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (13 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (15 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (15 min), Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (17 min) and the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (19 min).
