Rich Avon West in the Northern Grampians area has 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Rich Avon West is Guthrie Park, covering about 7 hectares. Playgrounds around Rich Avon West include Guthrie Park Playground.

Getting around

There's no train station in Rich Avon West. The closest to Rich Avon West is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 86 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Rich Avon West, so most trips start by car. By car from Rich Avon West, Stawell is about 57 minutes away and Horsham about 60 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Rich Avon West is Donald Swimming Pool in Donald, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Rich Avon West is Donald BMX Track in Donald, about 26 minutes' drive. Stawell is the closest bigger centre to Rich Avon West, about 57 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Rich Avon West are the playground at Shanahan Park in Marnoo (18 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (24 min), Donald BMX Track in Donald (26 min), Rupanyup Swimming Pool in Rupanyup (24 min), Woods' Farming and Heritage Museum in Rupanyup (24 min) and Murtoa Museum Complex in Murtoa (37 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Rich Avon West.

Nearby suburbs

Rupanyup, next to Rich Avon West, has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Rich Avon West, Minyip has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Laen and Banyena also border Rich Avon West.
