Playground at Duke Street Reserve

Playground in Altona North · Basic info

Playground at Duke Street Reserve is a public playground in Altona North, in the Hobsons Bay City area. Altona North Community Library is about 200 m away, and for food, Subway is about 230 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 910 m from the playground, and buses 232, 411, 903 and 947 stop at McArthurs Rd/Millers Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
