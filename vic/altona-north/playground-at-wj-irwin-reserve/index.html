Playground at WJ Irwin Reserve

Playground in Altona North · Basic info

Playground at WJ Irwin Reserve in Altona North is a public playground in the Hobsons Bay City area. For food, Grill'd is about 430 m away, and Playground at SJ Clement Reserve is another option about 390 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 720 m from the playground, and bus 412 stops at Fourth Ave/McIntosh Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
