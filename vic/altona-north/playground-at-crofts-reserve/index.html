Playground at Crofts Reserve

Playground in Altona North · Basic info

Playground at Crofts Reserve is a public playground in Altona North, in the Hobsons Bay City area. The Kororoit Creek Trail is about 240 m away, and Playground at G J Hosken Reserve is another option about 270 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 520 m from the playground, and bus 471 stops at Knapp St/Blackshaws Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
