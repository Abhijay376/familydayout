Playground at A. W. Langshaw Reserve

Playground in Altona North · Basic info

Playground at A. W. Langshaw Reserve in Altona North is a public playground in the Hobsons Bay City area. Altona North Community Library is about 470 m away, and Playground on McArthurs Road is another option about 570 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.3 km from the playground, and bus 412 stops at Cresser St/Mills St, a 4-minute walk away.
