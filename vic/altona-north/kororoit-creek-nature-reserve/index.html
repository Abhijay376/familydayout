Kororoit Creek Nature Reserve

Park in Altona North · Basic info

Kororoit Creek Nature Reserve is a park in Altona North, in the Hobsons Bay City Council area, good for all ages. Knapp St/Blackshaws Rd (bus) is a 27-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 2108 m away.
