Playground at Haven Central Park

Playground in Altona North · Basic info

Playground at Haven Central Park in Altona North is a public playground in the Hobsons Bay City area. For food, Blackshaws Rd Fish & Chips is about 250 m away, and Playground at R.J. Cooper Reserve is another option about 550 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.2 km from the playground, and buses 432 and 471 stop at Kyle Rd/Blackshaws Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
