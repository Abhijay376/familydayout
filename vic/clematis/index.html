Clematis is in the Cardinia area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Clematis is Clematis Park Bushland Reserve, covering about 1 hectares. For walks and rides, Eastern Dandenong Ranges Trail crosses Clematis.

Getting around

There's no train station in Clematis. The closest to Clematis is Belgrave Railway Station (metro), about 6.4 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 695 and 695F stop in Clematis. By car from Clematis, Dandenong is about 26 minutes away, Pakenham about 29 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 46 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Belgrave Heated Outdoor Swimming Pool in Belgrave is about 12 minutes' drive from Clematis. For a library, Emerald Library in Emerald is about 4 minutes' drive from Clematis. The closest beach to Clematis is Edithvale Beach in Edithvale, about 39 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Worrell Reserve Skate Park in Emerald is about 4 minutes' drive from Clematis. The closest cinema to Clematis is Cameo Belgrave in Belgrave, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Clematis is Little Rascals Play Centre in Ferntree Gully, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Clematis is MonkeyG's in Pakenham, about 30 minutes' drive. Dandenong is the closest bigger centre to Clematis, about 26 minutes away by car, and has 21 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Clematis are the playground at Puffing Billy Park in Emerald (3 min), Worrell Reserve Skate Park in Emerald (4 min), Emerald Library in Emerald (4 min), Emerald Museum in Emerald (5 min), the playground at Puffing Billy Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Emerald (5 min) and BMX track in Emerald (5 min).

Best for

Free days on the Eastern Dandenong Ranges Trail and in Clematis's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Clematis, Emerald has 6 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Menzies Creek, next to Clematis, has 1 playground.
