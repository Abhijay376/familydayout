Beazley Reserve

Park in Chelsea Heights · Basic info

Beazley Reserve is a park in Chelsea Heights, in the Kingston City Council area, good for all ages. First Ave/Thames Prom (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 140 m away.
