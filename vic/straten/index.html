Straten is in the Buloke area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Straten, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Straten. The closest to Straten is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 90 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Straten, so most trips start by car. By car from Straten, Swan Hill is about 85 minutes away and Mildura about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Tempy Outdoor Swimming Pool in Tempy is about 17 minutes' drive from Straten. From Straten, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Tempy Recreation Reserve in Tempy (17 min), Tempy Outdoor Swimming Pool in Tempy (17 min), Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake (28 min) and the playground at Apex Park in Sea Lake (28 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Straten.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Straten, Tempy has 1 playground and a pool. Nyarrin also borders Straten.
