Lismore in the Corangamite area has 6 parks and reserves in our data. Beyond the parks, Lismore has a pool.

Landmarks

Lake Gnarpurt Lake Reserve is the largest mapped park in Lismore, at about 2,472 hectares. Lake Tooliorook Lake Reserve and Lake Struan Wildlife Reserve (hunting) are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Lismore include Lake Gnarpurt Reserve, Lismore Recreation Reserve and Lismore Classic Scramble Club. Our data shows 6 public toilets in Lismore. Places to visit in Lismore include Lismore Pool.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lismore. The closest to Lismore is Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line), about 35 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Lismore. By car from Lismore, Colac is about 55 minutes away, Ballarat about 61 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum is about 10 minutes' drive from Lismore. The closest skate park to Lismore is Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown, about 33 minutes' drive. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Lismore, about 55 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Lismore, the nearest places for kids by road are Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum (10 min), the playground at Cressy Picnic Area in Cressy (24 min), Cape Clear & District Lions Park Playground in Cape Clear (26 min), Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (33 min), Rokewood Skate Park in Rokewood (38 min) and Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood (38 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lismore.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Lismore, Derrinallum has a library. Leslie Manor, Kariah, Foxhow and Berrybank also border Lismore.
