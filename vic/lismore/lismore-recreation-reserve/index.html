Lismore Recreation Reserve

Park in Lismore · Basic info

Lismore Recreation Reserve is a park in Lismore, in the Corangamite Shire Council area, good for all ages. Newsagency/Hamilton Hwy (coach) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 526 m away.
