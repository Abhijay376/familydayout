Playground at Koornang Park

Playground in Carnegie · Official details

This is a public playground in Carnegie. Diving Pool is about 260 m away, and Playground at Rosanna Street Reserve is another option about 480 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and buses 625 and 626 stop at Moonya Rd/Koornang Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
