Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Little Desert, in the Hindmarsh area.

Landmarks

Coynallan Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Little Desert, at about 4 hectares. Our data shows 13 public toilets in Little Desert. For walks and rides, Desert Discovery Walk, Wallaby Track and Salt Lake Walking Track cross Little Desert.

Getting around

Little Desert has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 146 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Little Desert, so most trips start by car. By car from Little Desert, Horsham is about 60 minutes away and Stawell about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Little Desert is Goroke Swimming Centre in Goroke, about 33 minutes' drive. For a library, Nhill Library in Nhill is about 17 minutes' drive from Little Desert. For a skate park, Nhill Skate Park in Nhill is about 16 minutes' drive from Little Desert. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to Little Desert, about 60 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Little Desert are Nhill Playground in Nhill (15 min), Nhill Skate Park in Nhill (16 min), Wagonin in Nhill (16 min), Gallery Central in Nhill (16 min), Nhill Library in Nhill (17 min) and the playground at Goldsworthy Park in Nhill (17 min).

Best for

Free days on the Desert Discovery Walk and in Little Desert's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Little Desert, Goroke has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Dimboola, next to Little Desert, has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Nhill, next to Little Desert, has 3 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Peronne, Nurcoung, Lawloit and Kiata also border Little Desert.
