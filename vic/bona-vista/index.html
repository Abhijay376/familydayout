Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bona Vista in the Baw Baw area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Bona Vista has no train station; Warragul Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 5.8 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bona Vista, so most trips start by car. By car from Bona Vista, Warragul is about 11 minutes away, Pakenham about 44 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 88 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul is about 9 minutes' drive from Bona Vista. For a library, Warragul Library in Warragul is about 10 minutes' drive from Bona Vista. The closest skate park to Bona Vista is Warragul Skate Park in Warragul, about 9 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 9 minutes' drive from Bona Vista. The closest indoor play centre to Bona Vista is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 9 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Wildlife Trail in Tynong North is about 31 minutes' drive from Bona Vista. The nearest larger centre is Warragul, about 11 minutes from Bona Vista by car, with 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Bona Vista are Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul (9 min), the playground at Burke Street Park in Warragul (9 min), Warragul Skate Park in Warragul (9 min), Warragul Leisure Centre in Warragul (9 min), Burke Street Pump Track in Warragul (9 min) and Warragul Cinema in Warragul (9 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bona Vista.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Bona Vista, Warragul has 22 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Darnum, next to Bona Vista, has 2 playgrounds. Gainsborough, Ellinbank and Cloverlea also border Bona Vista.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Bona Vista, without traffic.
