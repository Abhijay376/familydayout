Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Joyces Creek in the Mount Alexander area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Joyces Creek. The Castlemaine-Maryborough Rail Trail (Under Construction) passes through Joyces Creek.

Getting around

Joyces Creek has no train station; Castlemaine Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Joyces Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Joyces Creek, Castlemaine is about 23 minutes away, Maryborough about 24 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Newstead Swimming Pool in Newstead is about 8 minutes' drive from Joyces Creek. For a library, Athenaeum Library in Maldon is about 23 minutes' drive from Joyces Creek. The closest skate park to Joyces Creek is Bike Jumps in Castlemaine, about 22 minutes' drive. Castlemaine is the closest bigger centre to Joyces Creek, about 23 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Joyces Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Newstead Memorial Park in Newstead (8 min), Newstead Swimming Pool in Newstead (8 min), the playground at Market Reserve in Carisbrook (18 min), Maldon District Museum in Maldon (23 min), Athenaeum Library in Maldon (23 min) and Maldon Vintage Machinery Museum in Maldon (24 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Joyces Creek on the Castlemaine-Maryborough Rail Trail (Under Construction).

Nearby suburbs

Newstead, next to Joyces Creek, has 1 playground and a pool. Sandon, Moolort and Baringhup also border Joyces Creek.
