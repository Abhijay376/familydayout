Our data lists 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in Yallourn, in the Latrobe area.

Landmarks

Graeme Edwards Memorial Park is the largest mapped park in Yallourn, at about 4 hectares. Yallourn also has Fairway Drive Reserve. Playgrounds around Yallourn include the playground at Fairway Drive Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Yallourn. The Moe-Yallourn Rail Trail passes through Yallourn.

Getting around

There's no train station in Yallourn. The closest to Yallourn is Morwell Railway Station (V/Line), about 7.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yallourn, so most trips start by car. By car from Yallourn, Traralgon is about 29 minutes away, Warragul about 42 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Yallourn is Yallourn North Outdoor Pool in Yallourn North, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest library to Yallourn is Morwell Library in Morwell, about 18 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Yallourn North Skate Park and BMX Track in Yallourn North is about 11 minutes' drive from Yallourn. The closest cinema to Yallourn is Village 8 in Morwell, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Yallourn is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 40 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Traralgon, about 29 minutes from Yallourn by car, with 38 playgrounds and a pool. From Yallourn, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Hallston Street Reserve in Newborough (12 min), Old Brown Coal Mine Museum in Yallourn North (11 min), Yallourn North Outdoor Pool in Yallourn North (11 min), Yallourn North Skate Park and BMX Track in Yallourn North (11 min), Moe Skate Park in Moe (15 min) and Diving Pool in Moe (16 min).

Best for

Free days on the Moe-Yallourn Rail Trail and in Yallourn's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Yallourn, Morwell has 14 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. Newborough, next to Yallourn, has 11 playgrounds. Yallourn North, next to Yallourn, has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Hernes Oak also borders Yallourn.
