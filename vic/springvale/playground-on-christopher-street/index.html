Playground at Norman Luth Reserve

Playground in Springvale · Official details

Playground at Norman Luth Reserve is a public playground in Springvale, in the Greater Dandenong City area. Playground at Olinda Reserve is another option about 580 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 60 m from the playground, and bus 814 stops at Heather Gr/Regent Ave, a 5-minute walk away.
