Our data lists 16 playgrounds and 10 parks and reserves in Springvale, in the Greater Dandenong area. Springvale also has 2 libraries and an indoor play centre.

Landmarks

Warner Reserve is the largest mapped park in Springvale, at about 17 hectares. Springvale Reserve and Edinburgh Road Reserve are the next largest. Springvale also has Davis Reserve, Olinda Reserve and Library Reserve. Playgrounds in Springvale include Playground, the playground at Davis Reserve and the playground at Olinda Reserve. Our data shows 12 public toilets and 7 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Springvale. Places to visit in Springvale include Småland, Springvale Library and The Little Library. Golden Plaza, Springvale Centre and Springvale Homemaker Centre are the main shopping centres. For walks and rides, Djerring Trail crosses Springvale. Our data lists 65 cafes and family restaurants in Springvale for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Springvale Railway Station (metro: Cranbourne Line and Pakenham Line) in Springvale. Buses 631, 705, 800, 811, 813, 814, 824, 848, 885, 902, 978 and 979 stop in Springvale. By car from Springvale, Dandenong is about 11 minutes away, Ringwood about 20 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 25 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Springvale is Noble Park Aquatic Centre in Noble Park, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Springvale is Edithvale Beach in Edithvale, about 15 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Noble Park Skatepark in Noble Park is about 6 minutes' drive from Springvale. The closest cinema to Springvale is Village Cinemas in Clayton, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Springvale is Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale, about 11 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 16 playgrounds across Springvale. Rainy days at the library and indoor play centre in Springvale. Free days on the Djerring Trail and in Springvale's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Noble Park, next to Springvale, has 19 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Springvale, Mulgrave has 23 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Springvale, Clayton has 7 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Springvale, Clayton South has 12 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Noble Park North and Springvale South also border Springvale.
