Playground on Kintore Street

Playground in Springvale · Basic info

Playground on Kintore Street is a public playground in Springvale, in the Greater Dandenong City area. The Djerring Trail is about 80 m away, and The Little Library is about 370 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 370 m from the playground, and bus 811 stops at Elm Gr/Lightwood Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
