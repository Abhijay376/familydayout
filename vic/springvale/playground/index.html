Playground

Playground in Springvale · Official details

This is a public playground in Springvale. Springvale Library is about 100 m away, and for food, Pitta Pan's Kebab is about 170 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 90 m from the playground, and buses 811, 813 and 902 stop at Springvale Library/Springvale Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
