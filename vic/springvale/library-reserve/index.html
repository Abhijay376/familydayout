Library Reserve

Park in Springvale · Official details

Library Reserve is a park in Springvale, in the Greater Dandenong City Council area, good for all ages. Springvale Library/Springvale Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 103 m away with a baby change table.

Questions parents ask

Where is Library Reserve?
Library Reserve is in Springvale, Victoria.
Is Library Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Library Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 103 m away, with a baby change table.
How do I get to Library Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Springvale Library/Springvale Rd (routes 811, 813, 902), a 2-minute walk.
