Playground at Springvale Services for Children

Playground in Springvale · Basic info

Playground at Springvale Services for Children in Springvale is a public playground in the Greater Dandenong City area. The Djerring Trail is about 70 m away, and Springvale Library is about 530 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 580 m from the playground, and bus 811 stops at Elm Gr/Lightwood Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
