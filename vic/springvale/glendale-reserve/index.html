Glendale Reserve

Park in Springvale · Official details

Glendale Reserve is a park in Springvale, in the Greater Dandenong City Council area, good for all ages. Stephenson St/Springvale Rd (bus) is a 10-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 553 m away.

Where is Glendale Reserve?
Glendale Reserve is in Springvale, Victoria.
