Springvale Library

Library in Springvale · Basic info

Springvale Library is a library in Springvale, in the Greater Dandenong City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Springvale Library/Springvale Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 147 m away with a baby change table.
