Playground at Edinburgh Road Reserve

Playground in Springvale · Basic info

Playground at Edinburgh Road Reserve is a public playground in Springvale, in the Greater Dandenong City area. Pita Bar is about 110 m away if you need food, and Playground at Knell Street Reserve is another option about 530 m away. There's a public toilet about 350 m away, and buses 631, 813, 814 and 978 stop at Wellington Sec College/Police Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
