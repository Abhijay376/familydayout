Getting around

There's no train station in Wurruk. The closest to Wurruk is Sale Railway Station (V/Line), about 1.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 3 stops in Wurruk. V/Line coaches stop in Wurruk. By car from Wurruk, Sale is about 6 minutes away, Traralgon about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.
