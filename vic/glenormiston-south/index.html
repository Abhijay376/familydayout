Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Glenormiston South in the Corangamite area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Glenormiston South has no train station; Terang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 9.0 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glenormiston South, so most trips start by car. By car from Glenormiston South, Warrnambool is about 53 minutes away, Colac about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon is about 42 minutes' drive from Glenormiston South. The closest library to Glenormiston South is Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake, about 21 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake is about 22 minutes' drive from Glenormiston South. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Glenormiston South is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 39 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 53 minutes from Glenormiston South by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Glenormiston South are Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake (22 min), the playground at Tea Tree Lake Reserve in Mortlake (22 min), Market Square Playground in Mortlake (21 min), Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake (21 min), Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (22 min) and Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (39 min).
