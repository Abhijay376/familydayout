Landmarks

The biggest green space in Allendale is Loughlin Mine Historical Reserve, covering about 5 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Allendale. The closest to Allendale is Creswick Railway Station (V/Line), about 6.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Allendale. By car from Allendale, Daylesford is about 26 minutes away, Ballarat about 27 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Allendale is (Diving tower, restricted) in Creswick, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest library to Allendale is Creswick Library in Creswick, about 9 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick is about 9 minutes' drive from Allendale. For a cinema, Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central is about 27 minutes' drive from Allendale. The closest indoor play centre to Allendale is Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point, about 31 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek is about 27 minutes' drive from Allendale. Daylesford is the closest bigger centre to Allendale, about 26 minutes away by car, and has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Allendale are Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick (9 min), (Diving tower, restricted) in Creswick (9 min), children in Creswick (9 min), Creswick Library in Creswick (9 min), Skate Park in Creswick (9 min) and Apex Park Playground in Creswick (10 min).
