Colac West is in the Colac Otway area, and our data lists 1 playground in the suburb.

Landmarks

Our data shows 2 public toilets in Colac West. The MBN SW3 passes through Colac West.

Getting around

Colac West has no train station; Colac Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 3.1 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Colac - Alvie via Warrion route stop in Colac West. By car from Colac West, Colac is about 4 minutes away, Geelong about 68 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac is about 7 minutes' drive from Colac West. For a skate park, the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt is about 9 minutes' drive from Colac West. The closest cinema to Colac West is Colac Cinemas in Colac, about 5 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac is about 5 minutes' drive from Colac West. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Colac West, about 4 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Colac West, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (3 min), the playground at Western Bay Playground in Colac (3 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (5 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (5 min), Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (7 min) and the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (9 min).

Best for

Free days on the MBN SW3 and in Colac West's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Colac, next to Colac West, has 13 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. Next door to Colac West, Elliminyt has 1 playground.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Colac West, without traffic.
