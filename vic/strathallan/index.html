Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Strathallan in the Campaspe area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Strathallan. The closest to Strathallan is Rochester Railway Station (V/Line), about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Strathallan, so most trips start by car. By car from Strathallan, Echuca is about 17 minutes away, Bendigo about 69 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Strathallan is Memorial Pool in Rochester, about 13 minutes' drive. For a library, Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca is about 18 minutes' drive from Strathallan. The closest skate park to Strathallan is Moama Echuca Community Skate Park in Echuca, about 20 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca is about 17 minutes' drive from Strathallan. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram is about 34 minutes' drive from Strathallan. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 17 minutes from Strathallan by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Strathallan, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Apex Park in Rochester (12 min), Memorial Pool in Rochester (13 min), the playground at Echuca Lions Park in Echuca (15 min), Holden Museum in Echuca (16 min), Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca (17 min) and Port of Echuca Discovery Centre in Echuca (17 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Strathallan.

Nearby suburbs

Echuca, next to Strathallan, has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. Bamawm and Ballendella also border Strathallan.
