Playground at W E Newton Reserve

Playground in Portsea · Basic info

Playground at W E Newton Reserve is a public playground in Portsea, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire area. Portsea Front Beach is about 390 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus 788 stops at Nepean Pl/Point Nepean Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
