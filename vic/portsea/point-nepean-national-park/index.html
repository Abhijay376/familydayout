Point Nepean National Park

Park in Portsea · Official details

Point Nepean National Park is a park in Portsea, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire Council area, good for all ages. The nearest public toilet is about 819 m away. Opening hours: Open daily. Vehicles 6am-6pm (6am-8pm daylight saving); pedestrians and cyclists any time. Quarantine Station buildings 9am-4:30pm. Information Centre from 10am.

Details

Opening hours
Open daily. Vehicles 6am-6pm (6am-8pm daylight saving); pedestrians and cyclists any time. Quarantine Station buildings 9am-4:30pm. Information Centre from 10am (Parks Victoria)

on site (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
Four electric BBQs at the Quarantine Station; no open fires or charcoal; Helmets must be worn when cycling; Swimming at bay beach and Quarantine Station permitted; watch for strong currents and rips; Shuttle bus runs between Quarantine Station and Fort Nepean (Parks Victoria)
