Police Point Shire Park

Park in Portsea · Basic info

Police Point Shire Park is a park in Portsea, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire Council area, good for all ages. Point Nepean National Park Entrance/Point Nepean Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 236 m away with a baby change table.
