Portsea Back Beach

Beach in Portsea · Official details

Portsea Back Beach is a beach in Portsea, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire Council area, good for all ages. Franklin Rd/Hotham Rd (bus) is a 15-minute walk away.

Toilets
yes (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Portsea Back Beach?
Portsea Back Beach is in Portsea, Victoria.
Is Portsea Back Beach free?
Yes. It is a public beach, free to visit.
How do I get to Portsea Back Beach by public transport?
The closest stop is Franklin Rd/Hotham Rd (routes 788), a 15-minute walk.
