Rythdale is in the Cardinia area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Rythdale, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Rythdale. The closest to Rythdale is Pakenham Railway Station (metro), about 6.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Rythdale, so most trips start by car. By car from Rythdale, Pakenham is about 8 minutes away, Cranbourne about 21 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 51 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Rythdale is Pakenham Outdoor Swimming Pool in Pakenham, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest library to Rythdale is Pakenham Library in Pakenham, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Rythdale is Seaford Beach in Seaford, about 37 minutes' drive. For a skate park, P.B Ronald Skate Park in Pakenham is about 10 minutes' drive from Rythdale. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Narre Warren is about 20 minutes' drive from Rythdale. For an indoor play centre, Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham is about 10 minutes' drive from Rythdale. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Rythdale is MonkeyG's in Pakenham, about 8 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Pakenham, about 8 minutes from Rythdale by car, with 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play. From Rythdale, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Ron Andrews Park in Pakenham (7 min), the playground at William Bruce Ronald Reserve in Pakenham (7 min), MonkeyG's in Pakenham (8 min), Pakenham Library in Pakenham (9 min), Historic Council Shire Offices in Pakenham (9 min) and Pakenham Outdoor Swimming Pool in Pakenham (10 min).
