St Deny's Reserve Playground

Playground in Wonga Park · Basic info

St Deny's Reserve Playground is a small local playground in Wonga Park, in the Manningham City area. For food, Little Lofty Cafe is about 170 m away, and Old Yarra Road Playground is another option about 240 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 360 m from the playground, and bus 672 stops at Toppings Rd/Yarra Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
