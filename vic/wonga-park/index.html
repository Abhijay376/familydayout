Wonga Park in the Manningham area has 9 playgrounds and 21 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Wonga Park Reserve is the largest mapped park in Wonga Park, at about 9 hectares. Penderel Court Reserve and Arthur John Upton Reserve are the next largest. Wonga Park also has Stane Brae, Yarra Brae and Bend of Isles. Playgrounds around Wonga Park include Joroma Place Playground, Old Yarra Road Playground and St Deny's Reserve Playground. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Wonga Park. The Village Centre is the local shopping centre. For walks and rides, Blue Tongue Bend Walk crosses Wonga Park. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Wonga Park for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wonga Park. The closest to Wonga Park is Croydon Railway Station (metro), about 6.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 671 and 672 stop in Wonga Park. By car from Wonga Park, Lilydale is about 12 minutes away, Ringwood about 14 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 41 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Wonga Park is Kingswim in Chirnside Park, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest library to Wonga Park is Warrandyte Library in Warrandyte, about 8 minutes' drive. For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 38 minutes' drive from Wonga Park. For a skate park, Bike Jumps in Croydon Hills is about 7 minutes' drive from Wonga Park. The closest cinema to Wonga Park is Reading Cinemas in Chirnside Park, about 8 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, GAMR Laser Tag & VR in Chirnside Park is about 8 minutes' drive from Wonga Park. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Wonga Park is Bundoora Park Farm in Bundoora, about 40 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 9 playgrounds across Wonga Park. Free days on the Blue Tongue Bend Walk and in Wonga Park's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Wonga Park, Chirnside Park has 9 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. Warrandyte, next to Wonga Park, has 5 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Croydon North, next to Wonga Park, has 10 playgrounds. Next door to Wonga Park, Croydon Hills has 6 playgrounds and a skate park. Warranwood, Christmas Hills, Warrandyte South and North Warrandyte also border Wonga Park.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Wonga Park, without traffic.
