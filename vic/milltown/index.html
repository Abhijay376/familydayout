Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Milltown, in the Glenelg area.

Landmarks

Milltown State Forest is the largest mapped park in Milltown, at about 444 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Milltown. The closest to Milltown is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 77 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Milltown. By car from Milltown, Portland is about 32 minutes away and Hamilton about 39 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood is about 12 minutes' drive from Milltown. For a library, Heywood Library in Heywood is about 12 minutes' drive from Milltown. For a beach, Whalers Point in Portland is about 33 minutes' drive from Milltown. For a skate park, hamilton skate park in Hamilton is about 41 minutes' drive from Milltown. The closest cinema to Milltown is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 39 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Portland, about 32 minutes from Milltown by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Milltown are Heywood Library in Heywood (12 min), the playground at Apex Park in Heywood (12 min), Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood (12 min), the playground at Heywood Youth Park in Heywood (12 min), Whalers Point in Portland (33 min) and Powerhouse Motor Car Museum in Portland (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Milltown.

Nearby suburbs

Heywood, next to Milltown, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Breakaway Creek also borders Milltown.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Milltown, without traffic.
