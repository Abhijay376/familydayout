Buckrabanyule is in the Buloke area, and our data lists 5 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Nardoo Hills Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Buckrabanyule, at about 1,186 hectares. Mount Buckrabanyule Conservation Reserve and Mount Buckra Scenic Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Buckrabanyule include Woosang H45 Bushland Reserve and Woosang I163 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Buckrabanyule. The closest to Buckrabanyule is Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line), about 59 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Buckrabanyule, so most trips start by car. By car from Buckrabanyule, Bendigo is about 81 minutes away and Maryborough about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn is about 18 minutes' drive from Buckrabanyule. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 16 minutes' drive from Buckrabanyule. The closest places for kids by road from Buckrabanyule are Rex Theatre in Charlton (16 min), Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (16 min), the playground at Rotary Park in Charlton (17 min), the playground at Gordon Park in Charlton (17 min), Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (18 min) and Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (18 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Buckrabanyule.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Buckrabanyule, Charlton has 3 playgrounds and a cinema. Wedderburn, next to Buckrabanyule, has 1 playground and a pool. Woolshed Flat, Terrappee, Skinners Flat and Nareewillock also border Buckrabanyule.
