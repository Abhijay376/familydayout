Our data lists 7 playgrounds and 14 parks and reserves in Narre Warren North, in the Casey area. Narre Warren North also has a museum or gallery and 2 skate parks.

Landmarks

Bayview Park is the largest mapped park in Narre Warren North, at about 51 hectares. Narre Warren North Recreation Reserve and Troups Creek Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Narre Warren North include Kurll Park, Kalora Park and Ackland Park. Playgrounds around Narre Warren North include Jack Thomas Reserve, the playground at Ackland Park and Marrawah Place Reserve Playground. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Narre Warren North. In Narre Warren North you'll also find Janet Matthews Artist. For older kids in Narre Warren North there's Pump Track and Pump Track. For walks and rides, Welcome Walk, Upper Blair Witch Trail and Troups Creek Track cross Narre Warren North. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Narre Warren North for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Narre Warren North. The closest to Narre Warren North is Narre Warren Railway Station (metro), about 5.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 695, 697, 834, 841, 842, 863, 982 and 695F stop in Narre Warren North. By car from Narre Warren North, Dandenong is about 13 minutes away, Cranbourne about 17 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 34 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Narre Warren North is Casey ARC in Narre Warren, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest library to Narre Warren North is Narre Warren Library in Narre Warren, about 7 minutes' drive.

Best for

Younger kids at Narre Warren North's 7 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Narre Warren North. Teens who skate or ride, with Pump Track in Narre Warren North. Free days on the Welcome Walk and in Narre Warren North's parks.
