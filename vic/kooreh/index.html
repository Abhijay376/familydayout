Kooreh is in the Northern Grampians area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Moolerr I45 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Kooreh, at about 26 hectares.

Getting around

Kooreh has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 56 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kooreh, so most trips start by car. By car from Kooreh, Maryborough is about 66 minutes away, Stawell about 72 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kooreh is St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud, about 11 minutes' drive. From Kooreh, the nearest places for kids by road are Bi-Centennial Park Playground in St Arnaud (9 min), the playground at Queen Mary Botanic Gardens in St Arnaud (10 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (11 min), Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (29 min), Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (28 min) and Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (41 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Kooreh.

Nearby suburbs

Carapooee also borders Kooreh.
