Playground at Pioneer Park

Playground in Sydenham · Basic info

Playground at Pioneer Park is a public playground in Sydenham, in the Brimbank City area. Playground at Kenswick Drive Reserve is another option about 520 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.2 km from the playground, and buses 460, 462, 463 and 943 stop at Arran Ct/Melton Hwy, a 10-minute walk away.
