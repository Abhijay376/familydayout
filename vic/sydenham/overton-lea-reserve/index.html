Overton Lea Reserve

Park in Sydenham · Official details

Overton Lea Reserve is a park in Sydenham, in the Brimbank City Council area, good for all ages. Overton Lea Reserve/Overton Lea Bvd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 169 m away.

Where is Overton Lea Reserve?
