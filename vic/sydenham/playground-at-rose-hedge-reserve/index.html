Playground at Rose Hedge Reserve

Playground in Sydenham · Official details

Playground at Rose Hedge Reserve in Sydenham is a public playground in the Brimbank City area. The Hume Drive Trail passes about 320 m away, and Caroline II Thai Restaurant is about 330 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 1.3 km away, and buses 420, 461 and 464 stop at Roseleigh Bvd/Kings Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
