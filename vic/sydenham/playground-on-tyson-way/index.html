Playground on Tyson Way

Playground in Sydenham · Basic info

Playground on Tyson Way in Sydenham is a public playground in the Brimbank City area. For food, Seafood D'lite is about 70 m away, and Playground at Hillside Reserve is another option about 200 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 820 m from the playground, and bus 462 stops at Community Hub/Calder Park Dr, a 1-minute walk away.
