Playground on Kings Road

Playground in Sydenham · Basic info

This is a public playground in Sydenham. The Hume Drive Trail is about 300 m away, and for food, Priya Indian Cuisine is about 250 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.1 km from the playground, and buses 461 and 464 stop at Roseleigh Bvd/Kings Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
