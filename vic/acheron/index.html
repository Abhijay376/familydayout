Acheron is in the Murrindindi area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Acheron, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Acheron. The closest to Acheron is Euroa Railway Station (V/Line), about 57 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 684 stops in Acheron. By car from Acheron, Healesville is about 52 minutes away, Seymour about 59 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Acheron is Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest library to Acheron is Alexandra Library in Alexandra, about 9 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Gallipolli Park in Marysville is about 30 minutes' drive from Acheron. Healesville is the closest bigger centre to Acheron, about 52 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Acheron, the nearest places for kids by road are Alexandra Library in Alexandra (9 min), Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra (9 min), the playground at Leckie Park in Alexandra (10 min), Alexandra Timber Tramway and Museum in Alexandra (11 min), the playground at Buxton Recreation Reserve in Buxton (17 min) and Replica of the original police station in Marysville (29 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Acheron.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Acheron, Alexandra has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Limestone also borders Acheron.
