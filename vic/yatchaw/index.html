Yatchaw is in the Southern Grampians area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Yatchaw Sandpit Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Yatchaw, at about 4 hectares. Our data shows 1 off-leash dog park in Yatchaw.

Getting around

There's no train station in Yatchaw. The closest to Yatchaw is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 72 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yatchaw, so most trips start by car. By car from Yatchaw, Hamilton is about 14 minutes away and Warrnambool about 73 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Yatchaw is Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Hamilton, about 16 minutes' drive. For a library, Hamilton Library in Hamilton is about 13 minutes' drive from Yatchaw. The closest skate park to Yatchaw is hamilton skate park in Hamilton, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Yatchaw is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 13 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 14 minutes from Yatchaw by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Yatchaw are Tarrington Rec Reserve Playground in Tarrington (5 min), mitchell park playground in Hamilton (11 min), Sir Reginald Ansett Transport Museum in Hamilton (12 min), hamilton skate park in Hamilton (12 min), Hamilton Library in Hamilton (13 min) and Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton (13 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Yatchaw.

Nearby suburbs

Hamilton, next to Yatchaw, has 23 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Yatchaw, Tarrington has 1 playground. Buckley Swamp also borders Yatchaw.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Yatchaw, without traffic.
