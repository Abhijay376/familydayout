Bandiana in the Wodonga area has 6 playgrounds and 6 parks and reserves in our data. Beyond the parks, Bandiana has a museum or gallery, a cinema and a beach.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bandiana is Wodonga Bushland Reserve, covering about 5 hectares. Fromholtz Park and Eastern Hill Reserve are the next largest. Bandiana also has Kosovar Park, Fellowes Park and Wintersun Park. Playgrounds around Bandiana include the playground at Kosovar Park, the playground at Fellowes Park and the playground at Fromholtz Park. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Bandiana. Places to visit in Bandiana include Army Museum Bandiana, Bandiana Area Cinema and Finns Beach. The High Country Rail Trail, Kiewa Trail and Bananduda Bike Path all pass through Bandiana. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Bandiana for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Bandiana has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 6.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bandiana, so most trips start by car. By car from Bandiana, Wodonga is about 7 minutes away and Wangaratta about 59 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, WAVES in Wodonga is about 5 minutes' drive from Bandiana. For a library, Upper Murray Regional Library - Wodonga in Wodonga is about 6 minutes' drive from Bandiana. For a skate park, James Scott Memorial Skate Park in Wodonga is about 7 minutes' drive from Bandiana.

Best for

Younger kids at Bandiana's 6 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the cinema and museum or gallery in Bandiana. Beach days at Finns Beach. Free days on the High Country Rail Trail and in Bandiana's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Bandiana, Wodonga has 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Baranduda, next to Bandiana, has 8 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Bandiana, Leneva has 3 playgrounds. Bonegilla, next to Bandiana, has 3 playgrounds. Killara also borders Bandiana.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Bandiana, without traffic.
