Playground on Broad Street

Playground in Bandiana · Basic info

Playground on Broad Street in Bandiana is a public playground in the Wodonga City area. The Albury-Thurgoona Trail is about 10 m away, and Playground at Fellowes Park is another option about 530 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus NSW903 stops at Borella Rd/Schubach St , a 4-minute walk away.
