Playground on Kim Avenue

Playground in Bandiana · Basic info

Playground on Kim Avenue in Bandiana is a public playground in the Wodonga City area. The Hume and Hovell Walking Track passes about 100 m away, and Playground at Eastern Hill Reserve is another option about 530 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus NSW903 stops at Denmar St/Jamieson St , a 1-minute walk away.
