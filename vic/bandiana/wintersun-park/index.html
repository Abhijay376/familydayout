Wintersun Park

Park in Bandiana · Basic info

Wintersun Park is a park in Bandiana, in the Wodonga City Council area, good for all ages. Spring Ave/Chapple St (bus) is a 21-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1178 m away with a baby change table.
