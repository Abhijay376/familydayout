Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Slaty Creek, in the Northern Grampians area.

Landmarks

Gowar Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Slaty Creek, at about 79 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Slaty Creek. The closest to Slaty Creek is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 69 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Slaty Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Slaty Creek, Stawell is about 70 minutes away and Maryborough about 73 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud is about 8 minutes' drive from Slaty Creek. For a skate park, Donald BMX Track in Donald is about 40 minutes' drive from Slaty Creek. The closest cinema to Slaty Creek is Rex Theatre in Charlton, about 25 minutes' drive. From Slaty Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are Apex Park Playground in St Arnaud (8 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (8 min), Market Square Park Playground in St Arnaud (8 min), Rex Theatre in Charlton (25 min), Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (25 min) and Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (36 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Slaty Creek.

Nearby suburbs

St Arnaud, next to Slaty Creek, has 4 playgrounds and a pool. Gooroc and Coonooer Bridge also border Slaty Creek.
