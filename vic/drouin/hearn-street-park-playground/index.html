Hearn Street Park Playground

Playground in Drouin · Basic info

Hearn Street Park Playground in Drouin is a public playground in the Baw Baw Shire area. Ozzy Charcoal Chicken and Souvlaki is about 130 m away if you need food, and Playground on McLean Street is another option about 260 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus 86 stops at McNeilly Rd/Princes Way, a 3-minute walk away.
