Water Play

Playground in Drouin · Basic info

Water Play is a public playground in Drouin, in the Baw Baw Shire area. The Warragul - Drouin Two Towns Trail is about 10 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 340 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 200 m from the playground, and bus 86 stops at Neerim St/Walker Dr, a 4-minute walk away.
