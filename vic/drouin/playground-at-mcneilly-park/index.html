Playground at McNeilly Park

Playground in Drouin · Basic info

Playground at McNeilly Park is a public playground in Drouin, in the Baw Baw Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet right by the playground. It's easiest by car, about 3 minutes from Drouin.
