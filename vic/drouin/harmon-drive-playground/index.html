Harmon Drive Playground

Playground in Drouin · Basic info

This is a public playground in Drouin. The Warragul - Drouin Two Towns Trail passes about 10 m away, and Playground at L. G. Boeyen Reserve is another option about 520 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 970 m away, and bus 86 stops at Buscombe Cres/Hopetoun Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
