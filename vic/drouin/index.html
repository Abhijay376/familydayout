Drouin in the Baw Baw area has 19 playgrounds and 13 parks and reserves in our data. Beyond the parks, Drouin has a library, a pool and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Drouin is Thomas Maddock Reserve, covering about 9 hectares. Drouin Nature Reserve and McNeilly Park are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Drouin include Civic Park, Hearn Park and Balmoral Park. Playgrounds around Drouin include Water Play, Harmon Drive Playground and Waddell Park Playground. Our data shows 10 public toilets in Drouin. In Drouin you'll also find Drouin Swimming Pool and Drouin Library. For older kids in Drouin there's Drouin Skate Park. The Warragul - Drouin Two Towns Trail and Two Towns Trail all pass through Drouin. Our data lists 11 cafes and family restaurants in Drouin for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Drouin Railway Station (V/Line: Bairnsdale - Melbourne via Sale & Traralgon and Traralgon - Melbourne via Morwell, Moe & Pakenham) in Drouin. Buses on the 85, 86, Garfield Station - Traralgon Plaza and Warragul Station - Poowong East via Drouin, Ripplebrook routes stop in Drouin. V/Line coaches stop in Drouin. By car from Drouin, Warragul is about 11 minutes away, Pakenham about 34 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 77 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 9 minutes' drive from Drouin. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 9 minutes' drive from Drouin. For a zoo or wildlife park, Wildlife Trail in Tynong North is about 20 minutes' drive from Drouin.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 19 playgrounds across Drouin. Rainy days at the library in Drouin. Teens who skate or ride, with Drouin Skate Park in Drouin. Free days on the Warragul - Drouin Two Towns Trail and in Drouin's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Drouin, Warragul has 22 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Longwarry, next to Drouin, has 3 playgrounds. Longwarry North, Lardner, Drouin West and Drouin South also border Drouin.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Drouin, without traffic.
