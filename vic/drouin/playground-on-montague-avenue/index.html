Playground on Montague Avenue

Playground in Drouin · Basic info

Playground on Montague Avenue is a public playground in Drouin, in the Baw Baw Shire area. Drouin Swimming Pool is about 590 m away, and Drouin Thai is about 250 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 500 m away, and bus 85 stops at Porter Pl/Lampard Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
