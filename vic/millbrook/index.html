Millbrook is in the Moorabool area, and our data lists 1 playground in the suburb.

Landmarks

Playgrounds around Millbrook include the playground at Millbrook Community Centre.

Getting around

Millbrook has no train station; Ballan Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Ballarat - Mt Egerton via Gordon route stop in Millbrook. By car from Millbrook, Ballarat is about 25 minutes away, Bacchus Marsh about 34 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 79 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Millbrook is Brown Hill Swimming Pool in Brown Hill, about 19 minutes' drive. For a library, Central Highlands Regional Library Corp Ballarat branch in Ballarat Central is about 24 minutes' drive from Millbrook. The closest beach to Millbrook is Lionhead Beach in Pentland Hills, about 30 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan is about 20 minutes' drive from Millbrook. The closest cinema to Millbrook is Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central, about 24 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 25 minutes' drive from Millbrook. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Millbrook is Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek, about 14 minutes' drive. Ballarat is the closest bigger centre to Millbrook, about 25 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Millbrook, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Paddock Creek Recreation Reserve in Gordon (8 min), Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek (14 min), Brown Hill Swimming Pool in Brown Hill (19 min), Eureka Swimming Complex in Eureka (21 min), Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East (21 min) and the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan (20 min).
