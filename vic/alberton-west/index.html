Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Alberton West in the Wellington area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Alberton West. The closest to Alberton West is Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line), about 45 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Alberton West, so most trips start by car. By car from Alberton West, Leongatha is about 66 minutes away, Sale about 73 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Yarram Public Pool in Yarram is about 16 minutes' drive from Alberton West. The closest library to Alberton West is Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram, about 15 minutes' drive. For a beach, Chinaman Beach in Wilsons Promontory is about 27 minutes' drive from Alberton West. For a skate park, the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram is about 17 minutes' drive from Alberton West. From Alberton West, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Memorial Park in Yarram (15 min), Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram (15 min), Yarram Public Pool in Yarram (16 min), Port Albert Maritime Museum in Port Albert (16 min), the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram (17 min) and Yarram Historical Society Museum in Yarram (17 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Alberton West.

Nearby suburbs

Yarram, next to Alberton West, has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Devon North and Alberton also border Alberton West.
