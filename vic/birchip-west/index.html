Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Birchip West, in the Buloke area.

Landmarks

Rileys Tank Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Birchip West, at about 32 hectares. Dunstan Bushland Reserve and Heggety Tank Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

Birchip West has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 100 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Birchip West, so most trips start by car. By car from Birchip West, Horsham is about 89 minutes away and Swan Hill about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Birchip West is Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Birchip West is Birchip Skate Park in Birchip, about 14 minutes' drive. From Birchip West, the nearest places for kids by road are Birchip History Museum in Birchip (14 min), the playground at Birchip Shire Soldiers Memorial Park in Birchip (14 min), Birchip Skate Park in Birchip (14 min), the playground at J.E Taylor Sunset Park in Birchip (15 min), Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem (18 min) and Woomelang Recreation Reserve Swimming Pool in Woomelang (39 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Birchip West.

Nearby suburbs

Birchip, next to Birchip West, has 2 playgrounds and a skate park. Ballapur also borders Birchip West.
