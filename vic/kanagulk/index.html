Kanagulk is in the Horsham area, and our data lists 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Lake Kanagulk Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Kanagulk, at about 454 hectares. Red Hill State Forest and Kanagulk State Forest are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Kanagulk include Telangatuk I40 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Kanagulk has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 98 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kanagulk, so most trips start by car. By car from Kanagulk, Horsham is about 56 minutes away and Hamilton about 80 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kanagulk is Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral, about 16 minutes' drive. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to Kanagulk, about 56 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Kanagulk, the nearest places for kids by road are Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral (16 min), Glendinning Street Playground in Balmoral (16 min), Harrow Discovery Centre in Harrow (37 min) and Bake House Studio in Harrow (37 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Kanagulk.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Kanagulk, Balmoral has 1 playground and a pool. Telangatuk East, next to Kanagulk, has 1 playground. Douglas also borders Kanagulk.
