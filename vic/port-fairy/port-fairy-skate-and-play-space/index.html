Port Fairy Skate and Play space

Playground in Port Fairy · Basic info

Port Fairy Skate and Play space in Port Fairy is a public playground in the Moyne Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 40 m away, and Southcombe Beach is about 580 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus 8 stops at Sackville St/Campbell St, a 5-minute walk away.
