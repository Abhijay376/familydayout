Fiddlers Green

Park in Port Fairy · Basic info

Fiddlers Green is a park in Port Fairy, in the Moyne Shire Council area, good for all ages. Port Fairy Visitor Centre/Bank St (coach) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 128 m away with a baby change table.

Where is Fiddlers Green?
