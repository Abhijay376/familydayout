Pea Soup Beach

Beach in Port Fairy · Basic info

Pea Soup Beach is a beach in Port Fairy, in the Moyne Shire Council area, good for all ages. Reardon St/Powling St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 530 m away.
