Skate park at Russell Clark Reserve

Skate, BMX or pump track in Port Fairy · Basic info

Skate park at Russell Clark Reserve is a skate, bmx or pump track in Port Fairy, in the Moyne Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Sackville St/Campbell St (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 53 m away with a baby change table.
