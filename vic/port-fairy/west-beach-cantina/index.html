West Beach Cantina

Cafe or restaurant in Port Fairy · Basic info

Bank St/Gipps St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 175 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: opening_hours: Mo, Fr-Su 11:00-20:00.
