Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Trafalgar South, in the Baw Baw area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Trafalgar South is Uralla Nature Reserve, covering about 44 hectares. For walks and rides, Donaldson Track and Bullocky Track cross Trafalgar South.

Getting around

Trafalgar South has no train station; Trafalgar Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 4.6 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Trafalgar South, so most trips start by car. By car from Trafalgar South, Warragul is about 28 minutes away, Traralgon about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Trafalgar South is Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar, about 9 minutes' drive. For a library, Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North is about 26 minutes' drive from Trafalgar South. The closest skate park to Trafalgar South is Moe Skate Park in Moe, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Trafalgar South is Warragul Cinema in Warragul, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Trafalgar South is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 26 minutes' drive. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Trafalgar South, about 28 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Trafalgar South are the playground at Brindlewood Reserve in Trafalgar (9 min), Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar (9 min), Yarragon Childrens Playground Reserve in Yarragon (16 min), Station Gallery in Yarragon (16 min), Moe Skate Park in Moe (19 min) and Diving Pool in Moe (20 min).

Best for

Free days on the Donaldson Track and in Trafalgar South's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Trafalgar, next to Trafalgar South, has 4 playgrounds and a pool. Narracan, Moe South and Childers also border Trafalgar South.
