St Andrews is in the Nillumbik area, and our data lists 1 playground and 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in St Andrews is Queenstown G57 Bushland Reserve, covering about 40 hectares. Queenstown G58 Bushland Reserve and St Andrews Nature Conservation Reserve are the next largest. St Andrews also has Lakeview Road Reserve. Playgrounds around St Andrews include Childrens Playground. Our data shows 2 public toilets in St Andrews. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in St Andrews for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in St Andrews. The closest to St Andrews is Hurstbridge Railway Station (metro), about 8.4 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside St Andrews, so most trips start by car. By car from St Andrews, Ringwood is about 34 minutes away, Healesville about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 52 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to St Andrews is Diamond Creek Outdoor Pool in Diamond Creek, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest library to St Andrews is Yarra Plenty Regional Library in Christmas Hills, about 14 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Hurstbridge Half-pipe in Hurstbridge is about 9 minutes' drive from St Andrews. For a cinema, Hoyts in Greensborough is about 26 minutes' drive from St Andrews. The closest indoor play centre to St Andrews is Rare Bears Indoor Play Centre in Eltham, about 26 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bundoora Park Farm in Bundoora is about 36 minutes' drive from St Andrews. The nearest larger centre is Ringwood, about 34 minutes from St Andrews by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from St Andrews.

Nearby suburbs

Christmas Hills, next to St Andrews, has a library. Kinglake, next to St Andrews, has 1 playground. Next door to St Andrews, Arthurs Creek has 1 playground. Smiths Gully, Panton Hill and Cottles Bridge also border St Andrews.
