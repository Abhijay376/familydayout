Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Traynors Lagoon in the Northern Grampians area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Traynors Lagoon. The closest to Traynors Lagoon is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 79 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Traynors Lagoon, so most trips start by car. By car from Traynors Lagoon, Stawell is about 55 minutes away and Horsham about 67 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Traynors Lagoon is St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud, about 19 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Donald BMX Track in Donald is about 28 minutes' drive from Traynors Lagoon. Stawell is the closest bigger centre to Traynors Lagoon, about 55 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds and a pool. From Traynors Lagoon, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Shanahan Park in Marnoo (16 min), Guthrie Park Playground in Rich Avon West (17 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (19 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (24 min), Donald BMX Track in Donald (28 min) and Woods' Farming and Heritage Museum in Rupanyup (32 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Traynors Lagoon.
