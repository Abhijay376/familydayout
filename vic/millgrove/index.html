Millgrove in the Yarra Ranges area has 3 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, Millgrove has a library.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Millgrove is Dee River Streamside Reserve, covering about 6 hectares. Millgrove Recreation Reserve is the next largest. Millgrove also has Mckenzie King Drive Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Millgrove. Places to visit in Millgrove include Street Library. The Lilydale-Warburton Rail Trail, Warburton Rail Trail and O'Shannassy Aqueduct Trail all pass through Millgrove. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Millgrove for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Millgrove. The closest to Millgrove is Lilydale Railway Station (metro), about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 683 stops in Millgrove. By car from Millgrove, Healesville is about 27 minutes away, Lilydale about 34 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 75 minutes, without traffic.
