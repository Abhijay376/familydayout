Rotary Park

Park in Rochester · Basic info

Rotary Park is a park in Rochester, in the Campaspe Shire Council area, good for all ages. Gilles St/Moore St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 17 m away with a baby change table.

Where is Rotary Park?
