Memorial Pool

Pool or water play in Rochester · Basic info

Memorial Pool is a pool or water play in Rochester, in the Campaspe Shire Council area, good for all ages. Gilles St/Moore St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 86 m away.
