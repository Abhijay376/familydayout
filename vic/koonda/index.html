Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Koonda, in the Strathbogie area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Koonda is Upotipotpon Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 5 hectares.

Getting around

Koonda has no train station; Violet Town Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Koonda, so most trips start by car. By car from Koonda, Benalla is about 35 minutes away, Shepparton about 37 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Koonda is Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla, about 35 minutes' drive. For a library, Shepparton Library in Shepparton is about 38 minutes' drive from Koonda. For a beach, Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton is about 40 minutes' drive from Koonda. For a skate park, Pump Track in Benalla is about 36 minutes' drive from Koonda. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 37 minutes' drive from Koonda. The closest indoor play centre to Koonda is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 37 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kidstown in Mooroopna is about 41 minutes' drive from Koonda. Benalla is the closest bigger centre to Koonda, about 35 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds and a pool. From Koonda, the nearest places for kids by road are Violet Town Recreation Reserve West Playground in Violet Town (24 min), Violet Town Lions Park Playground in Violet Town (24 min), Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (35 min), Benalla Costume and Pioneer Museum in Benalla (35 min), Pump Track in Benalla (36 min) and Benalla Art Gallery in Benalla (35 min).
