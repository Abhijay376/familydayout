Our data lists 6 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in Eynesbury, in the Melton area.

Landmarks

Eynesbury Polo Field is the largest mapped park in Eynesbury, at about 44 hectares. Werribee River Water Frontage is the next largest. Eynesbury also has Eynesbury Village Green. Playgrounds around Eynesbury include Redbank St Reserve Playground, Goulburn Way Reserve Playground and Castlemaine Drive Reserve Playground.

Getting around

There's no train station in Eynesbury. The closest to Eynesbury is Melton Railway Station (V/Line), about 9.8 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 452 stops in Eynesbury. By car from Eynesbury, Melton is about 17 minutes away, Werribee about 21 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 49 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Melton Swimmers in Melton South is about 14 minutes' drive from Eynesbury. For a library, Melton Library & Learning Hub in Melton is about 17 minutes' drive from Eynesbury. For a beach, Needles Beach in Pentland Hills is about 33 minutes' drive from Eynesbury. For a skate park, Rockbank Skate Park in Rockbank is about 18 minutes' drive from Eynesbury. For a cinema, Reading Cinemas in Melton West is about 18 minutes' drive from Eynesbury. For an indoor play centre, Timezone in Hoppers Crossing is about 23 minutes' drive from Eynesbury. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Eynesbury is Werribee Open Range Zoo in Werribee South, about 30 minutes' drive.

Nearby suburbs

Wyndham Vale, next to Eynesbury, has 27 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Eynesbury, Weir Views has 4 playgrounds. Next door to Eynesbury, Exford has 1 playground. Parwan, Mount Cottrell and Balliang East also border Eynesbury.
