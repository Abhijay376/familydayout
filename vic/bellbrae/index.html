Bellbrae in the Surf Coast area has 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in our data. Bellbrae also has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bellbrae is Bellbrae Reserve, covering about 6 hectares. Playgrounds around Bellbrae include the playground at Bellbrae Reserve. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Bellbrae. Places to visit in Bellbrae include Salt and Pepper Gallery.

Getting around

Bellbrae has no train station; Waurn Ponds Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Bellbrae. By car from Bellbrae, Torquay is about 9 minutes away, Geelong about 26 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 84 minutes, without traffic.

The nearest larger centre is Torquay, about 9 minutes from Bellbrae by car, with 15 playgrounds, a library and a beach.
