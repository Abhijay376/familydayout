Landmarks

Playgrounds in Newry include Play Equipment. The Nicholson Rocks Walking Track passes through Newry.

Getting around

Newry has no train station; Stratford Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 17 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Newry, so most trips start by car. By car from Newry, Sale is about 30 minutes away, Traralgon about 55 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Newry is Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest library to Newry is Sale Library in Sale, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Newry is Pump Track in Heyfield, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Newry is Former Drive-in site in Sale, about 30 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Sale, about 30 minutes from Newry by car, with 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Newry are Boisdale Recreation Reserve Playground in Boisdale (17 min), Pump Track in Heyfield (21 min), Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield (21 min), Heyfield and District Museum in Heyfield (22 min), Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford (26 min) and Skate Park in Stratford (27 min).

Best for

Free days on the Nicholson Rocks Walking Track and in Newry's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Newry, Maffra has 6 playgrounds. Boisdale, next to Newry, has 1 playground. Monomak and Maffra West Upper also border Newry.
