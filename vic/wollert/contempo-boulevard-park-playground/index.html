Contempo Boulevard Park Playground

Playground in Wollert · Basic info

This is a public playground in Wollert. The Wollert Transport Corridor is about 300 m away, and Playground on Werribee Crescent is another option about 480 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 860 m from the playground, and bus 358 stops at Evolve Esp/Contempo Bvd, a 1-minute walk away.
