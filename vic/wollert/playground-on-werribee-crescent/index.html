Playground on Werribee Crescent

Playground in Wollert · Basic info

This is a public playground in Wollert. The Wollert Transport Corridor is about 110 m away, and Playground on Barham Way is another option about 190 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 720 m from the playground, and bus 357 stops at Edgars Creek Secondary College/Edgars Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
