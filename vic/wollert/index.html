Wollert is in the Whittlesea area, and our data lists 12 playgrounds and 17 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Wollert is galgi gnarrk (Craigieburn Grassland Nature Conservation Reserve), covering about 344 hectares. Future Conservation Area 32 - Craigieburn Road (West), Wollert and Future Conservation Area 31 - Craigieburn Road (East), Wollert are the next largest. Wollert also has Thyme Park, Fulham Park and Kendon Park. Playgrounds in Wollert include the playground at Thyme Park, Steadman Park Playground and the playground at Fulham Park. Our data shows 14 public toilets in Wollert. For walks and rides, Galada Tamboore Trail, Galada Tamboore Pathway and Edgars Creek Trail cross Wollert. Our data lists 5 cafes and family restaurants in Wollert for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wollert. The closest to Wollert is Epping Railway Station (metro), about 6.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 356, 357, 358 and 390 stop in Wollert. By car from Wollert, Broadmeadows is about 21 minutes away, Sunshine about 32 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 34 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Wollert is Mill Park Leisure Centre in Mill Park, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest library to Wollert is Mernda Library in Mernda, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Wollert is Horse Arena in Abbotsford, about 33 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Wollert is Epping Skate Park in Epping, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Wollert is Reading Epping Cinema in Epping, about 8 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Timezone in Epping is about 8 minutes' drive from Wollert. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Wollert is Funfields in Whittlesea, about 13 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 12 playgrounds across Wollert. Free days on the Galada Tamboore Trail and in Wollert's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Craigieburn, next to Wollert, has 54 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Wollert, Epping has 43 playgrounds, a cinema and a skate park. South Morang, next to Wollert, has 26 playgrounds. Next door to Wollert, Mernda has 20 playgrounds and a library. Donnybrook and Woodstock also border Wollert.
