Playground on Barham Way

Playground in Wollert · Basic info

This is a public playground in Wollert. The Edgars Creek Trail passes about 150 m away, and Playground on Werribee Crescent is another option about 190 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 800 m away, and bus 390 stops at Edgars Rd/Craigieburn Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
