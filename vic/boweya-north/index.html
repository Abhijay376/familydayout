Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Boweya North in the Moira area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Boweya North. The closest to Boweya North is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Boweya North, so most trips start by car. By car from Boweya North, Wangaratta is about 29 minutes away and Benalla about 45 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta is about 28 minutes' drive from Boweya North. For a library, Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga is about 28 minutes' drive from Boweya North. For a beach, Forges Beach No 2 in Yarrawonga is about 35 minutes' drive from Boweya North. For a skate park, the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta is about 33 minutes' drive from Boweya North. The closest cinema to Boweya North is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Boweya North is Tunzafun in Yarrawonga, about 30 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 29 minutes from Boweya North by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Boweya North are the playground at Peechelba Reserve in Peechelba (18 min), the playground at Tungamah Lions Park in Tungamah (23 min), Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga (28 min), Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta (28 min), Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (29 min) and Tunzafun in Yarrawonga (30 min).
