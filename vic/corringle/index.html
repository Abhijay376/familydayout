Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Corringle, in the East Gippsland area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Corringle is Lake Corringle Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 921 hectares. First and Second Islands Flora Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Corringle.

Getting around

Corringle has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 72 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Corringle, so most trips start by car. By car from Corringle, Lakes Entrance is about 59 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 86 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost is about 19 minutes' drive from Corringle. The closest library to Corringle is Orbost Library in Orbost, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Corringle is Ewings Marsh in Waygara, about 31 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Lakes Entrance, about 59 minutes from Corringle by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a beach. From Corringle, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Forest Park in Orbost (19 min), Orbost Library in Orbost (19 min), Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost (19 min), Ewings Marsh in Waygara (31 min) and Sailors Grave Beach in Cape Conran (47 min).
