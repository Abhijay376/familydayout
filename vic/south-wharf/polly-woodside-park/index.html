Polly Woodside Park

Park in South Wharf · Basic info

Polly Woodside Park is a park in South Wharf, in the Melbourne City Council area, good for all ages. Casino/MCEC/Clarendon St #124A (tram) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 384 m away.
