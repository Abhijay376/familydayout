Skyline Precinct Melbourne

Zoo, farm or wildlife park in South Wharf · Official details

Skyline Precinct Melbourne is a zoo, farm or wildlife park in South Wharf, in the Melbourne City Council area, good for all ages. Casino/MCEC/Clarendon St #124A (tram) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 387 m away. Opening hours: Monday - Friday 11am - 10.30pm; Saturday & Sunday 10am - 10.30pm.
