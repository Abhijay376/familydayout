Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Korobeit in the Moorabool area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Korobeit. The closest to Korobeit is Ballan Railway Station (V/Line), about 10 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Korobeit, so most trips start by car. By car from Korobeit, Bacchus Marsh is about 15 minutes away, Melton about 24 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 59 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Korobeit is Ballan Swimming Pool in Ballan, about 12 minutes' drive. For a library, Lerderderg Library in Bacchus Marsh is about 14 minutes' drive from Korobeit. For a beach, Lionhead Beach in Pentland Hills is about 10 minutes' drive from Korobeit. For a skate park, the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan is about 12 minutes' drive from Korobeit. The closest cinema to Korobeit is Reading Cinemas in Melton West, about 22 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 39 minutes' drive from Korobeit. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek is about 29 minutes' drive from Korobeit. The nearest larger centre is Bacchus Marsh, about 15 minutes from Korobeit by car, with 6 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Korobeit are the playground at Myrniong Recreation Reserve in Myrniong (4 min), the playground at Greendale Recreation Reserve in Greendale (4 min), Lionhead Beach in Pentland Hills (10 min), the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan (12 min), Ballan Swimming Pool in Ballan (12 min) and Paul Saddler Swimland in Darley (13 min).
