Woodlands Industrial Estate Linear Reserve

Park in Braeside · Basic info

Woodlands Industrial Estate Linear Reserve is a park in Braeside, in the Kingston City Council area, good for all ages. Bell Gr/Lower Dandenong Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1111 m away.
