Playground on Governor Road

Playground in Braeside · Basic info

Playground on Governor Road in Braeside is a public playground in the Kingston City area. The Mordialloc - Waterways - Braeside passes about 340 m away, and Nando's is about 120 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 290 m away, and buses 709 and 902 stop at Governor Rd/Springvale Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
