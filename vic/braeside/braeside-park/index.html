Braeside Park

Park in Braeside · Official details

Braeside Park is a park in Braeside, in the Kingston City Council area, good for all ages. Braeside Park Entrance/Lower Dandenong Rd (bus) is a 23-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 1076 m away and dogs are not allowed. Opening hours: Sunrise to sunset; Governor Road entrance closes 4pm daily.
