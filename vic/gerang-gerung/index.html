Gerang Gerung is in the Hindmarsh area, and our data lists 6 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Gerang Gerung is Gerang Gerung (north) Flora Reserve, covering about 108 hectares. Gerang Gerung I71 Bushland Reserve and Gerang Gerung (south) Flora Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Gerang Gerung include Woraigworm I72 Bushland Reserve, Woraigworm I73 Bushland Reserve and Coker Dam Wildlife Reserve (hunting). For walks and rides, Red Gum Circuit crosses Gerang Gerung.

Getting around

There's no train station in Gerang Gerung. The closest to Gerang Gerung is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 138 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gerang Gerung, so most trips start by car. By car from Gerang Gerung, Horsham is about 41 minutes away and Stawell about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Gerang Gerung is Dimboola Swimming Pool in Dimboola, about 13 minutes' drive. For a library, Dimboola Library in Dimboola is about 13 minutes' drive from Gerang Gerung. The closest skate park to Gerang Gerung is Nhill Skate Park in Nhill, about 19 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Horsham, about 41 minutes from Gerang Gerung by car, with 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Gerang Gerung, the nearest places for kids by road are Dimboola Library in Dimboola (13 min), Dimboola Swimming Pool in Dimboola (13 min), Nhill Library in Nhill (18 min), the playground at Goldsworthy Park in Nhill (18 min), Gallery Central in Nhill (18 min) and Nhill Skate Park in Nhill (19 min).

Best for

Free days on the Red Gum Circuit and in Gerang Gerung's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Gerang Gerung, Dimboola has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Antwerp also borders Gerang Gerung.
