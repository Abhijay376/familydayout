Landmarks

Apollo Bay Coastal Reserve is the largest mapped park in Petticoat Creek, at about 229 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Petticoat Creek. The closest to Petticoat Creek is Birregurra Railway Station (V/Line), about 43 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Petticoat Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Petticoat Creek, Colac is about 67 minutes away, Torquay about 1.5 hours and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Apollo Bay Community Pool in Apollo Bay is about 9 minutes' drive from Petticoat Creek. For a beach, White Crest in Wongarra is about 5 minutes' drive from Petticoat Creek. The closest skate park to Petticoat Creek is the skate park at Forrest Common in Forrest, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Petticoat Creek is Mechanics Hall in Apollo Bay, about 9 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Skate Bowl in Apollo Bay is about 9 minutes' drive from Petticoat Creek. From Petticoat Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are Old Cable Station Museum in Apollo Bay (5 min), White Crest in Wongarra (5 min), Skate Bowl in Apollo Bay (9 min), the playground at Foreshore Reserve in Apollo Bay (9 min), Mechanics Hall in Apollo Bay (9 min) and Apollo Bay Community Pool in Apollo Bay (9 min).
