Yangery is in the Moyne area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Yangery, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Yangery has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 9.0 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yangery, so most trips start by car. By car from Yangery, Warrnambool is about 13 minutes away and Hamilton about 75 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Yangery is Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest library to Yangery is Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool, about 14 minutes' drive. For a beach, Shelly Beach in Warrnambool is about 14 minutes' drive from Yangery. For a skate park, Koroit Youth Space in Koroit is about 8 minutes' drive from Yangery. For a cinema, Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool is about 12 minutes' drive from Yangery. For an indoor play centre, The Bowl in Warrnambool is about 14 minutes' drive from Yangery. For a zoo or wildlife park, Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee is about 25 minutes' drive from Yangery. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Yangery, about 13 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Yangery are W P O'Sullivan Playground in Dennington (8 min), Koroit Youth Space in Koroit (8 min), the playground at Bushfield Recreation Reserve in Bushfield (9 min), Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool (12 min), Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool (14 min) and Warrnambool Art Gallery in Warrnambool (14 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Yangery.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Yangery, Warrnambool has 45 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Yangery, Dennington has 1 playground. Southern Cross, Mailors Flat and Illowa also border Yangery.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Yangery, without traffic.
