Morrl Morrl is in the Northern Grampians area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Bolangum Flora Reserve is the largest mapped park in Morrl Morrl, at about 19 hectares. Monaghans North Bushland Reserve and Monaghans South Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

Morrl Morrl has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 47 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Morrl Morrl, so most trips start by car. By car from Morrl Morrl, Stawell is about 26 minutes away and Ararat about 52 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Morrl Morrl is Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell, about 25 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell is about 30 minutes' drive from Morrl Morrl. For a zoo or wildlife park, Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell is about 28 minutes' drive from Morrl Morrl. Stawell is the closest bigger centre to Morrl Morrl, about 26 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Morrl Morrl are the playground at Shanahan Park in Marnoo (21 min), Navarre Playground in Navarre (22 min), Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell (25 min), Railway Station Art and Crafts Gallery in Stawell (27 min), Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell (28 min) and the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Morrl Morrl.

Nearby suburbs

Callawadda also borders Morrl Morrl.
