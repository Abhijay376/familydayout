Reedy Lake is in the Gannawarra area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Middle and Reedy Lake Nature Reserve is the largest mapped park in Reedy Lake, at about 430 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Reedy Lake.

Getting around

There's no train station in Reedy Lake. The closest to Reedy Lake is Kerang Railway Station (V/Line), about 9.4 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Reedy Lake, so most trips start by car. By car from Reedy Lake, Swan Hill is about 39 minutes away and Echuca about 85 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Reedy Lake is Swimming Pool in Kerang, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest library to Reedy Lake is Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang, about 8 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Barham Skate Park in Koondrook is about 31 minutes' drive from Reedy Lake. For a cinema, Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill is about 39 minutes' drive from Reedy Lake. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 39 minutes from Reedy Lake by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Reedy Lake, the nearest places for kids by road are Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (8 min), Swimming Pool in Kerang (8 min), the playground at Lake Meran W.R in Lake Meran (22 min), the playground at Gray Park in Lake Boga (26 min), Lake Boga Flying Boat Museum in Lake Boga (27 min) and Barham Branch Library in Koondrook (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Reedy Lake.
