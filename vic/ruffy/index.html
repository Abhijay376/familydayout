Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Ruffy, in the Strathbogie area.

Landmarks

Ruffy Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Ruffy, at about 27 hectares. Bishops Mitre Rock Scenic Reserve and Ruffy Flora Reserve are the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Ruffy.

Getting around

There's no train station in Ruffy. The closest to Ruffy is Euroa Railway Station (V/Line), about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Ruffy, so most trips start by car. By car from Ruffy, Seymour is about 63 minutes away, Mansfield about 70 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Ruffy is Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa, about 44 minutes' drive. For a library, Euroa Library in Euroa is about 45 minutes' drive from Ruffy. The closest skate park to Ruffy is BMX Track in Caveat, about 31 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa is about 44 minutes' drive from Ruffy. The closest places for kids by road from Ruffy are Creightons Creek Recreation Reserve Playground in Creightons Creek (25 min), BMX Track in Caveat (31 min), Yarck Recreation Reserve Playground in Yarck (34 min), Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa (44 min), Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa (44 min) and Farmers Arms Hotel Museum in Euroa (44 min).
