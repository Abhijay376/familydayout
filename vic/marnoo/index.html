Our data lists 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in Marnoo, in the Northern Grampians area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Marnoo is Wirchilleba Bushland Reserve, covering about 10 hectares. Blind Creek Streamside Reserve is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Marnoo include Shanahan Park. Playgrounds in Marnoo include the playground at Shanahan Park. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Marnoo.

Getting around

There's no train station in Marnoo. The closest to Marnoo is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 68 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the St Arnaud - Stawell via Marnoo route stop in Marnoo. V/Line coaches stop in Marnoo. By car from Marnoo, Stawell is about 41 minutes away and Horsham about 56 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rupanyup Swimming Pool in Rupanyup is about 20 minutes' drive from Marnoo. The closest skate park to Marnoo is Donald BMX Track in Donald, about 33 minutes' drive. Stawell is the closest bigger centre to Marnoo, about 41 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Marnoo are Guthrie Park Playground in Rich Avon West (17 min), Rupanyup Swimming Pool in Rupanyup (20 min), Woods' Farming and Heritage Museum in Rupanyup (20 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (30 min), Murtoa Museum Complex in Murtoa (32 min) and Donald BMX Track in Donald (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Marnoo.

Nearby suburbs

Kanya, Grays Bridge, Bolangum and Banyena also border Marnoo.
