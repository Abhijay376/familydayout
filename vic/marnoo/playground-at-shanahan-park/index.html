Playground at Shanahan Park

Playground in Marnoo · Basic info

Playground at Shanahan Park in Marnoo is a public playground in the Northern Grampians Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus St Arnaud - Stawell via Marnoo stops at Park Rd/Donald Stawell Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
