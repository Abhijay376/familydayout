Shanahan Park

Park in Marnoo · Basic info

Shanahan Park is a park in Marnoo, in the Northern Grampians Shire Council area, good for all ages. Park Rd/Donald Stawell Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 14 m away.
