Our data lists 5 parks and reserves in Noradjuha, in the Horsham area.

Landmarks

Boundary Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Noradjuha, at about 156 hectares. Lake Clarke Wildlife Reserve (hunting) and Lake Carchap Wildlife Reserve (no hunting) are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Noradjuha include Noradjuha Recreation Reserve and Cookes Reserve Wildlife Reserve (hunting).

Getting around

Noradjuha has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 100 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Noradjuha, so most trips start by car. By car from Noradjuha, Horsham is about 30 minutes away and Stawell about 78 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham is about 29 minutes' drive from Noradjuha. For a skate park, the skate park at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk is about 15 minutes' drive from Noradjuha. For a cinema, Centre Cinemas in Horsham is about 29 minutes' drive from Noradjuha. The nearest larger centre is Horsham, about 30 minutes from Noradjuha by car, with 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Noradjuha are Arapiles Climbing Museum in Natimuk (15 min), the skate park at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk (15 min), the playground at Natimuk Showgrounds Recreation Reserve in Natimuk (16 min), the playground at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk (15 min), The Goat Gallery Natimuk in Natimuk (16 min) and Horsham Skate Park in Horsham (28 min).
