Our data lists 5 parks and reserves in Limestone, in the Murrindindi area.

Landmarks

Black Range State Forest is the largest mapped park in Limestone, at about 87 hectares. Black Range State Forest and Black Range State Forest are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Limestone include Stanley Homestead and Whanregarwen Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Limestone. For camping, there's Stanley Homestead. The Great Victorian Rail Trail passes through Limestone.

Getting around

Limestone has no train station; Seymour Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 40 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Limestone, so most trips start by car. By car from Limestone, Seymour is about 48 minutes away, Healesville about 59 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra is about 41 minutes' drive from Limestone. The closest library to Limestone is Alexandra Library in Alexandra, about 40 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea is about 19 minutes' drive from Limestone. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 48 minutes from Limestone by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Limestone, the nearest places for kids by road are the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea (19 min), TLM Moto Guzzi in Yea (27 min), Yarck Recreation Reserve Playground in Yarck (33 min), the playground at Leckie Park in Alexandra (42 min), Alexandra Library in Alexandra (40 min) and BMX Track in Caveat (58 min).

Best for

Free days on the Great Victorian Rail Trail and in Limestone's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Limestone, Yea has a skate park. Molesworth and Acheron also border Limestone.
