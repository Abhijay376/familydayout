Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Coomboona in the Greater Shepparton area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

The Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks passes through Coomboona.

Getting around

Coomboona has no train station; Mooroopna Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Coomboona, so most trips start by car. By car from Coomboona, Shepparton is about 28 minutes away, Echuca about 59 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Coomboona is Aquamoves in Shepparton, about 28 minutes' drive. For a library, Mooroopna Library in Mooroopna is about 22 minutes' drive from Coomboona. The closest beach to Coomboona is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Coomboona is the skate park at Skate Park in Shepparton, about 28 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 28 minutes' drive from Coomboona. The closest indoor play centre to Coomboona is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 28 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kidstown in Mooroopna is about 26 minutes' drive from Coomboona. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 28 minutes from Coomboona by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Coomboona, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Undera Youth Spot in Undera (17 min), the playground at Craigmuir Lake Park in Mooroopna (18 min), Mooroopna Library in Mooroopna (22 min), Kidstown in Mooroopna (26 min), Shepparton Heritage Centre in Shepparton (27 min) and Shepparton Library in Shepparton (28 min).
