James Penrose Reserve

Park in Coolaroo · Basic info

James Penrose Reserve is a park in Coolaroo, in the Hume City Council area, good for all ages. Etna Ct/Longford Cres (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 979 m away with a baby change table.
