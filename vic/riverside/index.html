Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Riverside, in the Horsham area.

Landmarks

Wimmera River Water / Stream Frontage is the largest mapped park in Riverside, at about 38 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Riverside. The closest to Riverside is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 90 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Riverside, so most trips start by car. By car from Riverside, Horsham is about 5 minutes away and Stawell about 57 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham is about 5 minutes' drive from Riverside. For a library, Dimboola Library in Dimboola is about 33 minutes' drive from Riverside. For a skate park, Velodrome in Horsham is about 5 minutes' drive from Riverside. The closest cinema to Riverside is Centre Cinemas in Horsham, about 6 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Horsham, about 5 minutes from Riverside by car, with 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Riverside, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Lawrence Street Park in Horsham (4 min), the playground at Charisma Park in Horsham (4 min), Velodrome in Horsham (5 min), Centre Cinemas in Horsham (6 min), Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham (5 min) and Horsham Skate Park in Horsham (7 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Riverside.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Riverside, Horsham has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. Next door to Riverside, Haven has 1 playground. Bungalally also borders Riverside.
