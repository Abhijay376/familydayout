Warrayure is in the Southern Grampians area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Warrayure, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Warrayure has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 79 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Warrayure, so most trips start by car. By car from Warrayure, Hamilton is about 15 minutes away and Ararat about 72 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Warrayure is Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Hamilton, about 17 minutes' drive. For a library, Hamilton Library in Hamilton is about 15 minutes' drive from Warrayure. For a skate park, hamilton skate park in Hamilton is about 13 minutes' drive from Warrayure. The closest cinema to Warrayure is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 15 minutes' drive. Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Warrayure, about 15 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Warrayure, the nearest places for kids by road are Dunkeld Memorial Park Playground in Dunkeld (12 min), Tarrington Rec Reserve Playground in Tarrington (12 min), Sir Reginald Ansett Transport Museum in Hamilton (13 min), hamilton skate park in Hamilton (13 min), Hamilton Library in Hamilton (15 min) and Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton (15 min).
