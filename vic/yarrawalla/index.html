Getting around

There's no train station in Yarrawalla. The closest to Yarrawalla is Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line), about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yarrawalla, so most trips start by car. By car from Yarrawalla, Echuca is about 63 minutes away and Bendigo about 69 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo is about 20 minutes' drive from Yarrawalla. From Yarrawalla, the nearest places for kids by road are Pyramid Hill & Districts Historical Museum in Pyramid Hill (12 min), the playground at R J Phelan Reserve in Mitiamo (20 min), Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo (20 min), East Loddon Historical Society in Mitiamo (20 min), A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park in Boort (31 min) and Boort Swimming Pool in Boort (32 min).
