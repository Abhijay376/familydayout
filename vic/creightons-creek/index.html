Our data lists 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in Creightons Creek, in the Strathbogie area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Creightons Creek is Euroa I49 Bushland Reserve, covering about 22 hectares. Euroa I48 Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Playgrounds around Creightons Creek include Creightons Creek Recreation Reserve Playground. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Creightons Creek.

Getting around

There's no train station in Creightons Creek. The closest to Creightons Creek is Euroa Railway Station (V/Line), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Creightons Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Creightons Creek, Seymour is about 56 minutes away, Benalla about 60 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Creightons Creek is Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest library to Creightons Creek is Euroa Library in Euroa, about 25 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Euroa Skate Park in Euroa is about 24 minutes' drive from Creightons Creek. The closest cinema to Creightons Creek is Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa, about 24 minutes' drive. Seymour is the closest bigger centre to Creightons Creek, about 56 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Creightons Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are Longwood Recreation Reserve Playground in Longwood (29 min), Farmers Arms Hotel Museum in Euroa (24 min), Euroa Skate Park in Euroa (24 min), Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa (24 min), Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa (24 min) and Euroa Library in Euroa (25 min).

Nearby suburbs
