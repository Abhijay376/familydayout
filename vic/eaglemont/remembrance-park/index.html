Remembrance Park

Park in Eaglemont · Basic info

Remembrance Park is a park in Eaglemont, in the Banyule City Council area, good for all ages. Austin Hospital/Burgundy St (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 476 m away.
