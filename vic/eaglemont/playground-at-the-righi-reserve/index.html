Playground at The Righi Reserve

Playground in Eaglemont · Basic info

This is a public playground in Eaglemont. The LBN1 - Watsonia to East Ivanhoe is about 210 m away, and for food, Cat Jump Thai Kitchen is about 250 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.0 km from the playground, and trains stop at Eaglemont Railway Station, a 5-minute walk away.
