Eaglemont in the Banyule area has 2 playgrounds and 10 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Eaglemont is Remembrance Park, covering about 2 hectares. Albert Jones Reserve and Homestead Park Reserve (Private) are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Eaglemont include The Righi Reserve, Banksia Street Reserve and Glen Park Reserve (Private). Playgrounds around Eaglemont include the playground at The Righi Reserve and the playground at Albert Jones Reserve. For walks and rides, Main Yarra Trail and LBN1 - Watsonia to East Ivanhoe cross Eaglemont. Our data lists 3 cafes and family restaurants in Eaglemont for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Eaglemont Railway Station (metro: Hurstbridge Line) in Eaglemont. Bus 546 stops in Eaglemont. By car from Eaglemont, Box Hill is about 16 minutes away, Ringwood about 21 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 17 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Ivanhoe Aquatic and Fitness Centre in Ivanhoe is about 5 minutes' drive from Eaglemont. The closest library to Eaglemont is Ivanhoe Library in Ivanhoe, about 4 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Eaglemont is Horse Arena in Abbotsford, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Eaglemont is the bmx track at Malahang Reserve in Heidelberg West, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Eaglemont is Hoyts in Preston, about 8 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Northland Timezone in Preston is about 7 minutes' drive from Eaglemont. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Eaglemont is Bundoora Park Farm in Bundoora, about 14 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Box Hill, about 16 minutes from Eaglemont by car, with 11 playgrounds, a pool and a library.

Best for

Free days on the Main Yarra Trail and in Eaglemont's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Ivanhoe, next to Eaglemont, has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Eaglemont, Bulleen has 16 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Eaglemont, Ivanhoe East has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Eaglemont, Heidelberg has 1 playground.
