Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Barunah Plains in the Colac Otway area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Barunah Plains. The closest to Barunah Plains is Birregurra Railway Station (V/Line), about 33 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Barunah Plains, so most trips start by car. By car from Barunah Plains, Colac is about 48 minutes away, Geelong about 58 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Barunah Plains is Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Barunah Plains is the skate park at Turtle Bend Park in Teesdale, about 39 minutes' drive. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Barunah Plains, about 48 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Barunah Plains, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Cressy Picnic Area in Cressy (17 min), Shelford Recreation Reserve Playground in Shelford (32 min), the skate park at Turtle Bend Park in Teesdale (39 min), Teesdale BMX Track in Teesdale (39 min), Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood (36 min) and Lismore Pool in Lismore (37 min).
