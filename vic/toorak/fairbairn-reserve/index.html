Fairbairn Reserve

Park in Toorak · Basic info

Fairbairn Reserve is a park in Toorak, in the Stonnington City Council area, good for all ages. Mathoura Rd/Malvern Rd #35 (tram) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 106 m away.
