Toorak in the Stonnington area has 2 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Toorak is Winifred Crescent Reserve, covering about 1 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Toorak include Fairbairn Reserve and Carters Avenue Reserve. Playgrounds in Toorak include the playground at Carters Avenue Reserve and the playground at Winifred Crescent Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Toorak. Tok H Centre is the local shopping centre. The Main Yarra Trail, Gardiners Creek Trail and South Yarra to Toorak Bicycle Route all pass through Toorak.

Getting around

Trains stop at Heyington Railway Station (metro: Glen Waverley Line) in Toorak. Trams 16, 58 and 72 stop in Toorak. Buses 603, 604 and 605 stop in Toorak. By car from Toorak, Box Hill is about 18 minutes away, Dandenong about 25 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 8 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Prahran Aquatic Centre in Prahran is about 4 minutes' drive from Toorak. For a library, Toorak/South Yarra Library in South Yarra is about 4 minutes' drive from Toorak. For a beach, West Beach in St Kilda West is about 9 minutes' drive from Toorak. The closest skate park to Toorak is the pump track at HA Smith Reserve in Hawthorn, about 5 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Palace Cinemas in South Yarra is about 3 minutes' drive from Toorak. For an indoor play centre, Rush Escape Game in South Yarra is about 4 minutes' drive from Toorak. For a zoo or wildlife park, Luna Park in St Kilda is about 9 minutes' drive from Toorak. Box Hill is the closest bigger centre to Toorak, about 18 minutes away by car, and has 11 playgrounds, a pool and a library.

Best for

Free days on the Main Yarra Trail and in Toorak's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Toorak, Hawthorn has 15 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Toorak, South Yarra has 12 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. Next door to Toorak, Prahran has 7 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Malvern, next to Toorak, has 3 playgrounds and a library. Burnley, Armadale and Kooyong also border Toorak.
