Tok H Centre

Shopping centre in Toorak · Official details

Tok H Centre is a shopping centre in Toorak, in the Stonnington City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Toorak Village/Toorak Rd #132 (tram) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 188 m away. Opening hours: 4th April – Easter Saturday: 10am – 5pm; 5th April – Easter Sunday: 10am – 5pm; April – Easter Monday: 10am – 5pm; 4th April – Easter Saturday: 7am – 10pm; 5th April – Easter Sunday: 8am – 9pm; April – Easter Monday: 7am – 10pm; 4th April – Easter Saturday: 7am – 6pm.
