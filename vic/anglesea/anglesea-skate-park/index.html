Anglesea Skate Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Anglesea · Basic info

Anglesea Skate Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Anglesea, in the Surf Coast Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Anglesea Motor Inn/Great Ocean Rd (coach) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 203 m away.
