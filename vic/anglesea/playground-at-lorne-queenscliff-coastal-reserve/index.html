Playground at Lorne - Queenscliff Coastal Reserve

Playground in Anglesea · Basic info

Playground at Lorne - Queenscliff Coastal Reserve in Anglesea is a public playground in the Surf Coast Shire area. The Surf Coast Walk is about 60 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 50 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 160 m from the playground, and coaches stop at Anglesea Motor Inn/Great Ocean Rd, a 8-minute walk away.
