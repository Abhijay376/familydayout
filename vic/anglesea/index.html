Anglesea is in the Surf Coast area, and our data lists 3 playgrounds and 8 parks and reserves in the suburb. Anglesea also has a museum or gallery, 5 beaches and a skate park.

Landmarks

Anglesea Depot is the largest mapped park in Anglesea, at about 362 hectares. Eumeralla Education Area and Anglesea Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Anglesea include Coogoorah Park, Team Challenge and Challenge Valley. Playgrounds in Anglesea include the playground at Coogoorah Park, the playground at Lorne - Queenscliff Coastal Reserve and the playground at Lorne - Queenscliff Coastal Reserve. Our data shows 16 public toilets in Anglesea. Places to visit in Anglesea include Anglesea Museum, Anglesea and Eumeralla. For older kids in Anglesea there's Anglesea Skate Park. For walks and rides, Surf Coast Walk, Yan Wirring Mirr Loop and 4 Grasstrees cross Anglesea. Our data lists 6 cafes and family restaurants in Anglesea for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Anglesea. The closest to Anglesea is Waurn Ponds Railway Station (V/Line), about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Anglesea. By car from Anglesea, Torquay is about 19 minutes away, Geelong about 38 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.
