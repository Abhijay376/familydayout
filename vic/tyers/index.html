Tyers is in the Latrobe area, and our data lists 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Bert Christensen Reserve is the largest mapped park in Tyers, at about 1 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Tyers include Tyers Community Park and Jean Galbraith Flora Reserve. Playgrounds in Tyers include the playground at Tyers Community Park. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Tyers. For walks and rides, Wirilda Walking Track crosses Tyers.

Getting around

Tyers has no train station; Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 7.8 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 5 stops in Tyers. By car from Tyers, Traralgon is about 12 minutes away, Warragul about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tyers is Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest library to Tyers is Morwell Library in Morwell, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Tyers is Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon, about 13 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 18 minutes' drive from Tyers. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Tyers, about 12 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. From Tyers, the nearest places for kids by road are Stockdale Fields Playground in Traralgon (8 min), Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon (11 min), Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon (13 min), Glengarry Skatepark in Glengarry (10 min), Yallourn North Outdoor Pool in Yallourn North (10 min) and Old Brown Coal Mine Museum in Yallourn North (10 min).

Best for

Free days on the Wirilda Walking Track and in Tyers's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Tyers, Traralgon has 38 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Yallourn North, next to Tyers, has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Glengarry West and Boola also border Tyers.
