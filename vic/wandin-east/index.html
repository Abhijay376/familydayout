Wandin East is in the Yarra Ranges area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Wandin East, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Wandin East.

Getting around

Wandin East has no train station; Lilydale Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wandin East, so most trips start by car. By car from Wandin East, Lilydale is about 18 minutes away, Healesville about 28 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 58 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Wandin East is Seville Water Play Park in Seville, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest library to Wandin East is Mount Evelyn Reading Room in Mount Evelyn, about 13 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Monbulk Skate & BMX Park in Monbulk is about 12 minutes' drive from Wandin East. For a cinema, Cameo Belgrave in Belgrave is about 21 minutes' drive from Wandin East. The closest indoor play centre to Wandin East is PlayPlace in Mooroolbark, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Wandin East is Lyre Bird enclosure in Badger Creek, about 25 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Lilydale, about 18 minutes from Wandin East by car, with 12 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Wandin East are Seville Pool Playground in Seville (5 min), Seville Water Play Park in Seville (6 min), the playground at Greenglades Court Reserve in Wandin North (9 min), Mont De Lancey Museum in Wandin North (10 min), Monbulk Skate & BMX Park in Monbulk (12 min) and Monbulk Aquatic Centre in Monbulk (13 min).

Best for
