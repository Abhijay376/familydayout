Our data lists 3 playgrounds and 5 parks and reserves in Junction Village, in the Casey area.

Landmarks

Junction Village Recreation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Junction Village, at about 5 hectares. Bandicoot Park is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Junction Village include Spring Road Park, The Arcade Reserve and Houlder Avenue Reserve. Playgrounds around Junction Village include the playground at Spring Road Park, the playground at Houlder Avenue Reserve and the playground at Junction Village Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Junction Village.

Getting around

Junction Village has no train station; Cranbourne Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 3.7 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 795 and 796 stop in Junction Village. By car from Junction Village, Cranbourne is about 5 minutes away, Dandenong about 20 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 45 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Junction Village is Casey RACE in Cranbourne East, about 4 minutes' drive. For a library, Cranbourne Library in Cranbourne East is about 4 minutes' drive from Junction Village. The closest beach to Junction Village is Seaford Beach in Seaford, about 21 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Criterium Cycling Track in Cranbourne East is about 6 minutes' drive from Junction Village. The closest cinema to Junction Village is Village Cinemas in Narre Warren, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Junction Village is Oz Tenpin in Narre Warren, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Junction Village is Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale, about 12 minutes' drive. Cranbourne is the closest bigger centre to Junction Village, about 5 minutes away by car, and has 27 playgrounds.

Best for

Younger kids at Junction Village's 3 local playgrounds.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Junction Village, Cranbourne has 27 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Junction Village, Cranbourne East has 16 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Botanic Ridge, next to Junction Village, has 3 playgrounds.
