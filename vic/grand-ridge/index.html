Grand Ridge is in the Latrobe area, and our data lists 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Grand Ridge is Gunyah Rainforest Scenic Reserve, covering about 150 hectares. Traralgon Creek (Yerang Park) Flora Reserve and Unnamed P0267 Private Nature Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Grand Ridge include Cooks Gully Flora Reserve.

Getting around

Grand Ridge has no train station; Morwell Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Grand Ridge, so most trips start by car. By car from Grand Ridge, Traralgon is about 74 minutes away, Leongatha about 83 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Grand Ridge, the nearest places for kids by road are Boolarra Skate Park in Boolarra (51 min), Boolarra BMX track in Boolarra (51 min), the playground at Railway Park in Boolarra (51 min), Boolarra Old School Museum in Boolarra (51 min), Railway Trolley Shed in Yinnar (58 min) and the playground at Yinnar Centenary Garden in Yinnar (58 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Grand Ridge.

Nearby suburbs

Jeeralang, Hiawatha, Gunyah and Budgeree also border Grand Ridge.
