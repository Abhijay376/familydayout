Wargan is in the Mildura area, and our data lists 6 playgrounds and 8 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Wargan has a library and 3 museums or galleries.

Landmarks

Wargan-Mallee Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Wargan, at about 1,443 hectares. Curlwaa Recreation Reserve and Junction Park are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Wargan include Lock 10, Fotherby Park and O'Donell Park. Playgrounds in Wargan include O'Donell Park Playground, the playground at Fotherby Park and the playground at Junction Park. Places to visit in Wargan include Old Wentworth Gaol, Riverboat Rod's Model Paddlesteamer Display and Wentworth Shire Library.

Getting around

Wargan has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 192 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wargan, so most trips start by car. By car from Wargan, Mildura is about 22 minutes away and Swan Hill about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Merbein Pool in Merbein is about 18 minutes' drive from Wargan. For a beach, Apex Beach in Mildura is about 30 minutes' drive from Wargan. For a skate park, Merbein Skate Park in Merbein is about 21 minutes' drive from Wargan. The closest cinema to Wargan is Deakin Cinema Complex in Mildura, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Wargan is Laserpunk Laserforce in Mildura, about 22 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Putt Putt Family Fun Centre in Mildura is about 22 minutes' drive from Wargan.

Best for

Younger kids at Wargan's 6 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the library and museum or gallery in Wargan.

Nearby suburbs

Yelta, next to Wargan, has 1 playground and a library. Next door to Wargan, Werrimull has a library. Next door to Wargan, Cullulleraine has 1 playground. Merbein West and Merbein South also border Wargan.
