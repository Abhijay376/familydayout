Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bonnie Doon is Maintongoon G108 Bushland Reserve, covering about 518 hectares. Tallangallook-Dry Creek Historic Area and Maintongoon G49 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Bonnie Doon also has Tallangallook Mine Area, Tallangallook Bushland Reserve and Tallangallook Road Picnic Area. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Bonnie Doon. Picnic areas in Bonnie Doon include Tallangallook Mine Area and Tallangallook Road Picnic Area. The Great Victorian Rail Trail passes through Bonnie Doon.

Getting around

Bonnie Doon has no train station; Euroa Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 40 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Bonnie Doon. By car from Bonnie Doon, Mansfield is about 19 minutes away, Benalla about 55 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.
