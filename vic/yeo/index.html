Yeo is in the Colac Otway area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Yeo, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Yeo. The closest to Yeo is Colac Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yeo, so most trips start by car. By car from Yeo, Colac is about 14 minutes away, Geelong about 74 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac is about 11 minutes' drive from Yeo. The closest skate park to Yeo is the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Yeo is Colac Cinemas in Colac, about 14 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac is about 14 minutes' drive from Yeo. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 14 minutes from Yeo by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Yeo, the nearest places for kids by road are Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (11 min), the playground at Lawrence Court Park in Colac (12 min), the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (13 min), the playground at Inglis Court Park in Colac (13 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (14 min) and Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (14 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Yeo.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Yeo, Elliminyt has 1 playground. Colac East and Barongarook also border Yeo.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Yeo, without traffic.
