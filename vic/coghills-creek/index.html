Coghills Creek is in the Ballarat area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Coghills Creek, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Coghills Creek has no train station; Clunes Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 10 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Coghills Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Coghills Creek, Ballarat is about 26 minutes away, Daylesford about 45 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Coghills Creek is Clunes Swimming Pool in Clunes, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest library to Coghills Creek is Creswick Library in Creswick, about 21 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Alexander Park in Learmonth is about 21 minutes' drive from Coghills Creek. For a cinema, Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central is about 27 minutes' drive from Coghills Creek. The closest indoor play centre to Coghills Creek is Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Coghills Creek is Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East, about 29 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Ballarat, about 26 minutes from Coghills Creek by car, with 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Coghills Creek are the playground at Ascot Hall Reserve in Ascot (8 min), the playground at Centenary Park in Blowhard (7 min), the skate park at Alexander Park in Learmonth (21 min), Lee Medlyn Bottle Museum in Clunes (20 min), Clunes Museum in Clunes (19 min) and Clunes Swimming Pool in Clunes (20 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Coghills Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Learmonth, next to Coghills Creek, has 2 playgrounds and a skate park. Blowhard, next to Coghills Creek, has 1 playground. Ascot, next to Coghills Creek, has 1 playground. Mount Bolton and Glendaruel also border Coghills Creek.
