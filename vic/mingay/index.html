Mingay is in the Corangamite area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Mingay, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mingay. The closest to Mingay is Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line), about 46 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mingay, so most trips start by car. By car from Mingay, Ballarat is about 60 minutes away, Colac about 67 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Lismore Pool in Lismore is about 14 minutes' drive from Mingay. For a library, Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum is about 17 minutes' drive from Mingay. The closest skate park to Mingay is Linton Skate Park in Linton, about 30 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Ballarat, about 60 minutes from Mingay by car, with 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Mingay are Lismore Pool in Lismore (14 min), Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum (17 min), Linton Skate Park in Linton (30 min), Linton BMX park in Linton (30 min), Linton Recreation Reserve Playground in Linton (30 min) and the playground at Simpson Park in Streatham (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mingay.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Mingay, Lismore has a pool. Derrinallum, next to Mingay, has a library. Bradvale also borders Mingay.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Mingay, without traffic.
