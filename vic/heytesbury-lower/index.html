Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Heytesbury Lower in the Corangamite area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

The Poorpa Yanyeen Meerreeng - The Timboon to Port Campbell Trail passes through Heytesbury Lower.

Getting around

There's no train station in Heytesbury Lower. The closest to Heytesbury Lower is Terang Railway Station (V/Line), about 37 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Heytesbury Lower, so most trips start by car. By car from Heytesbury Lower, Warrnambool is about 44 minutes away and Colac about 75 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Heytesbury Lower is Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Heytesbury Lower is Wild Dog Cove 5 in Peterborough, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Heytesbury Lower is Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Heytesbury Lower is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 38 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 44 minutes from Heytesbury Lower by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Heytesbury Lower, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Peterborough Coastal Reserve (COM) in Peterborough (7 min), Wild Dog Cove 5 in Peterborough (8 min), Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell (9 min), Falls of Halladale in Peterborough (9 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (11 min) and the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (12 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Heytesbury Lower on the Poorpa Yanyeen Meerreeng - The Timboon to Port Campbell Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Heytesbury Lower, Port Campbell has 1 playground, a beach and a skate park. Next door to Heytesbury Lower, Peterborough has 1 playground and a beach. Nirranda South, next to Heytesbury Lower, has a beach. Paaratte and Curdievale also border Heytesbury Lower.
