Brimpaen is in the Horsham area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Brimpaen is Black Range State Forest, covering about 2,337 hectares. Mockinya Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Brimpaen.

Getting around

Brimpaen has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 69 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Brimpaen, so most trips start by car. By car from Brimpaen, Horsham is about 34 minutes away and Stawell about 57 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham is about 33 minutes' drive from Brimpaen. The closest skate park to Brimpaen is Velodrome in Horsham, about 33 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Centre Cinemas in Horsham is about 34 minutes' drive from Brimpaen. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to Brimpaen, about 34 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Brimpaen are the playground at Uebergang Park in Horsham (33 min), Velodrome in Horsham (33 min), Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham (33 min), Horsham Water Play in Horsham (34 min), Centre Cinemas in Horsham (34 min) and Horsham Skate Park in Horsham (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Brimpaen.

Nearby suburbs

Telangatuk East, next to Brimpaen, has 1 playground. Mockinya, Laharum and Cherrypool also border Brimpaen.
