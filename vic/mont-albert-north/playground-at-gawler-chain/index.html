Playground at Gawler Chain

Playground in Mont Albert North · Basic info

Playground at Gawler Chain is a public playground in Mont Albert North, in the Whitehorse City area. The Gawler Chain Trail passes about 10 m away, and Playground on Moselle Street is another option about 280 m away. There's a public toilet about 650 m away, and buses 302 and 304 stop at Valda Ave/Belmore Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
