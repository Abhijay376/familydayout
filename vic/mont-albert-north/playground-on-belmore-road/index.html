Playground on Belmore Road

Playground in Mont Albert North · Basic info

Playground on Belmore Road in Mont Albert North is a public playground in the Whitehorse City area. The Koonung Creek Trail is about 20 m away, and Playground on Manniche Avenue is another option about 280 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 600 m from the playground, and buses 302 and 304 stop at Milne Rd/Belmore Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
