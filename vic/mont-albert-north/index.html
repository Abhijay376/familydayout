Mont Albert North is in the Whitehorse area, and our data lists 8 playgrounds and 5 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Koonung Creek Park is the largest mapped park in Mont Albert North, at about 12 hectares. Elgar Park and The Box Hill Miniature Steam Railway Society are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Mont Albert North include Gawler Chain and Ashmole Reserve. Playgrounds around Mont Albert North include the playground at Gawler Chain, the playground at Ashmole Reserve and the playground at Koonung Creek Park. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Mont Albert North. The Koonung Creek Trail, Whitehorse EW2-An and Whitehorse NS1 all pass through Mont Albert North. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Mont Albert North for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mont Albert North. The closest to Mont Albert North is Union Railway Station (metro), about 1.7 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 281, 293, 302, 304 and 612 stop in Mont Albert North. By car from Mont Albert North, Box Hill is about 5 minutes away, Ringwood about 15 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 20 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Boroondara Sports Complex in Balwyn North is about 4 minutes' drive from Mont Albert North. For a library, Balwyn Library in Balwyn is about 5 minutes' drive from Mont Albert North. The closest beach to Mont Albert North is Horse Arena in Abbotsford, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Mont Albert North is Box Hill South Skate Park in Box Hill South, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Mont Albert North is Palace Cinemas in Balwyn, about 6 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 8 playgrounds across Mont Albert North. Free days on the Koonung Creek Trail and in Mont Albert North's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Doncaster, next to Mont Albert North, has 37 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. Balwyn North, next to Mont Albert North, has 14 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Box Hill, next to Mont Albert North, has 11 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Mont Albert North, Balwyn has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. Box Hill North and Mont Albert also border Mont Albert North.
