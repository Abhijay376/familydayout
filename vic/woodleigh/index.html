Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Woodleigh, in the Bass Coast area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Woodleigh is Hurdy Gurdy Creek Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 37 hectares. For walks and rides, Gippsland Odyssey Extension - Woolamai to Nyora (Proposed) crosses Woodleigh.

Getting around

There's no train station in Woodleigh. The closest to Woodleigh is Bunyip Railway Station (V/Line), about 34 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Woodleigh, so most trips start by car. By car from Woodleigh, Leongatha is about 33 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 38 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 86 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Woodleigh is Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest library to Woodleigh is Poowong library in Poowong, about 16 minutes' drive. For a beach, Corinella Beach in Corinella is about 37 minutes' drive from Woodleigh. For a skate park, Nyora skate park in Nyora is about 13 minutes' drive from Woodleigh. For a cinema, Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi is about 37 minutes' drive from Woodleigh. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 45 minutes' drive from Woodleigh. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Woodleigh is Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville, about 25 minutes' drive. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Woodleigh, about 33 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Woodleigh, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Sunnyside Park in Loch (10 min), the playground at Nyora Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Nyora (13 min), Nyora skate park in Nyora (13 min), Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong (16 min), Poowong library in Poowong (16 min) and Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville (25 min).

Best for

Free days on the Gippsland Odyssey Extension - Woolamai to Nyora (Proposed) and in Woodleigh's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Woodleigh, Lang Lang has 4 playgrounds. Next door to Woodleigh, Loch has 1 playground. Grantville, next to Woodleigh, has 1 playground and a zoo or wildlife park. Krowera and Kernot also border Woodleigh.
