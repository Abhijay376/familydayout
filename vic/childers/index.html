Childers is in the Baw Baw area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Childers, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Childers. The closest to Childers is Trafalgar Railway Station (V/Line), about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Childers, so most trips start by car. By car from Childers, Warragul is about 36 minutes away, Traralgon about 45 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Childers is Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest library to Childers is Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Childers is Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North in Mirboo North, about 26 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 34 minutes' drive from Childers. The closest indoor play centre to Childers is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 35 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Warragul, about 36 minutes from Childers by car, with 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Childers, the nearest places for kids by road are Yarragon Childrens Playground Reserve in Yarragon (24 min), Station Gallery in Yarragon (24 min), the playground at Brindlewood Reserve in Trafalgar (19 min), Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar (20 min), Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North in Mirboo North (26 min) and Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Childers.
