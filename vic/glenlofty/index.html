Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Glenlofty in the Pyrenees area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Pyrenees Endurance Walk crosses Glenlofty.

Getting around

There's no train station in Glenlofty. The closest to Glenlofty is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 31 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glenlofty, so most trips start by car. By car from Glenlofty, Ararat is about 41 minutes away, Stawell about 55 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Glenlofty is Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat, about 41 minutes' drive. For a library, Learning Resource Centre in Ararat is about 45 minutes' drive from Glenlofty. For a skate park, David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat is about 42 minutes' drive from Glenlofty. For a cinema, Astor Cinema in Ararat is about 41 minutes' drive from Glenlofty. Ararat is the closest bigger centre to Glenlofty, about 41 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Glenlofty, the nearest places for kids by road are Community Playground in Landsborough (23 min), Navarre Playground in Navarre (36 min), Courthouse in Avoca (40 min), David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat (42 min), Ararat Gallery TAMA in Ararat (41 min) and Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat (41 min).

Best for
