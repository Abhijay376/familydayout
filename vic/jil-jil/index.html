Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Jil Jil in the Buloke area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Jil Jil has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 75 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Jil Jil, so most trips start by car. By car from Jil Jil, Swan Hill is about 69 minutes away and Horsham about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Jil Jil is Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Jil Jil is Birchip Skate Park in Birchip, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Jil Jil are the playground at Hall Corner Park in Nullawil (13 min), the playground at Birchip Shire Soldiers Memorial Park in Birchip (16 min), Birchip Skate Park in Birchip (17 min), Birchip History Museum in Birchip (17 min), Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock (17 min) and Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem (32 min).

Best for
