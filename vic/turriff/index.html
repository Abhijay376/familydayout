Landmarks

The biggest green space in Turriff is Turriff West School Bushland Reserve, covering about 20 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Turriff.

Getting around

There's no train station in Turriff. The closest to Turriff is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 100 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Turriff. By car from Turriff, Swan Hill is about 89 minutes away and Mildura about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Turriff is Tempy Outdoor Swimming Pool in Tempy, about 11 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Roxy in Ouyen is about 35 minutes' drive from Turriff. The closest places for kids by road from Turriff are the playground at Tempy Recreation Reserve in Tempy (10 min), Tempy Outdoor Swimming Pool in Tempy (11 min), the playground at Lascelles Caravan Park in Lascelles (15 min), Woomelang Recreation Reserve Swimming Pool in Woomelang (28 min) and Roxy in Ouyen (35 min).

Best for
