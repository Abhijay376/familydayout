Drik Drik in the Glenelg area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Drik Drik is Keegans Bend Reference Area, covering about 925 hectares. Balrook Bushland Reserve and Drik Drik H51 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Drik Drik.

Getting around

Drik Drik has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 112 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Drik Drik, so most trips start by car. By car from Drik Drik, Portland is about 54 minutes away and Hamilton about 67 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood is about 33 minutes' drive from Drik Drik. For a library, Heywood Library in Heywood is about 33 minutes' drive from Drik Drik. The nearest larger centre is Portland, about 54 minutes from Drik Drik by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. From Drik Drik, the nearest places for kids by road are Dartmoor Coach House Museum in Dartmoor (10 min), the playground at Cameron Park in Nelson (26 min), the playground at Glenelg River Heritage River in Nelson (26 min), Heywood Library in Heywood (33 min), Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood (33 min) and Merino Swimming Pool in Merino (37 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Drik Drik.

Nearby suburbs

Nelson, next to Drik Drik, has 3 playgrounds. Greenwald and Dartmoor also border Drik Drik.
