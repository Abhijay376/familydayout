Our data lists 4 parks and reserves in Chute, in the Pyrenees area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Chute is Ben Major State Forest, covering about 1,860 hectares. Ben Major Flora Reserve and Fortes Bush State Forest are the next largest. Chute also has Raglan Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Chute has no train station; Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Chute, so most trips start by car. By car from Chute, Ararat is about 47 minutes away, Maryborough about 47 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Beaufort Pool in Beaufort is about 20 minutes' drive from Chute. For a library, Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort is about 19 minutes' drive from Chute. The closest skate park to Chute is the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort, about 20 minutes' drive. Ararat is the closest bigger centre to Chute, about 47 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Chute, the nearest places for kids by road are Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort (19 min), the playground at Wotherspoon Reserve in Beaufort (20 min), the playground at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (20 min), the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (20 min), Beaufort Pool in Beaufort (20 min) and Courthouse in Avoca (26 min).
