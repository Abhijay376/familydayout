Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Gelantipy, in the East Gippsland area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Gelantipy is Mount Stewart Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 1,622 hectares. Bald Hills Road Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Gelantipy include Dingo Hill Lookout. Picnic areas in Gelantipy include Dingo Hill Lookout.

Getting around

Gelantipy has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 88 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gelantipy, so most trips start by car. By car from Gelantipy, Lakes Entrance is about 1.5 hours away and Bairnsdale about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Buchan: Natural Spring in Buchan is about 43 minutes' drive from Gelantipy. From Gelantipy, the nearest places for kids by road are Buchan: Natural Spring in Buchan (43 min).
