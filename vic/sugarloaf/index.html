Sugarloaf is in the Colac Otway area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Sugarloaf, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Sugarloaf. The closest to Sugarloaf is Birregurra Railway Station (V/Line), about 41 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Sugarloaf, so most trips start by car. By car from Sugarloaf, Colac is about 73 minutes away, Torquay about 83 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Apollo Bay Community Pool in Apollo Bay is about 16 minutes' drive from Sugarloaf. The closest beach to Sugarloaf is Carisbrook Creek in Grey River, about 1 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Sugarloaf is Kay Langan Skate Park in Lorne, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Sugarloaf is Mechanics Hall in Apollo Bay, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Sugarloaf is Skate Bowl in Apollo Bay, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Sugarloaf are Carisbrook Creek in Grey River (1 min), White Crest in Wongarra (1 min), Old Cable Station Museum in Apollo Bay (11 min), Skate Bowl in Apollo Bay (15 min), the playground at Foreshore Reserve in Apollo Bay (15 min) and Mechanics Hall in Apollo Bay (16 min).
