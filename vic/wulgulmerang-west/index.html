Wulgulmerang West is in the East Gippsland area, and our data lists 6 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Wulgulmerang West is Nunnett State Forest, covering about 33,789 hectares. Dawson-Murrindal Nature Conservation Reserve and Green Hills Nature Conservation Reserve are the next largest. Wulgulmerang West also has Glenmore Huts, W Tree Falls Natural Features and Scenic Reserve and Mount Stewart Natural Features and Scenic Reserve. Picnic areas in Wulgulmerang West include Glenmore Huts.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wulgulmerang West. The closest to Wulgulmerang West is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 79 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wulgulmerang West, so most trips start by car. By car from Wulgulmerang West, Lakes Entrance is about 2.5 hours away and Bairnsdale about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby suburbs
