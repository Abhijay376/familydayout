Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Rowsley, in the Moorabool area.

Landmarks

Rowsley Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Rowsley, at about 7 hectares.

Getting around

Rowsley has no train station; Bacchus Marsh Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 6.8 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Rowsley, so most trips start by car. By car from Rowsley, Bacchus Marsh is about 17 minutes away, Melton about 30 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 65 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Rowsley is Bacchus Marsh Public childrens pool in Bacchus Marsh, about 15 minutes' drive. For a library, Lerderderg Library in Bacchus Marsh is about 17 minutes' drive from Rowsley. The closest beach to Rowsley is Needles Beach in Pentland Hills, about 19 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the bmx track at Bacchus Marsh Racecourse and Recreation Reserve in Maddingley is about 11 minutes' drive from Rowsley. For a cinema, Reading Cinemas in Melton West is about 28 minutes' drive from Rowsley. The closest indoor play centre to Rowsley is Timezone in Hoppers Crossing, about 44 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bacchus Marsh, about 17 minutes from Rowsley by car, with 6 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Rowsley, the nearest places for kids by road are the bmx track at Bacchus Marsh Racecourse and Recreation Reserve in Maddingley (11 min), Australian Gliding Museum in Parwan (21 min), the playground at Guy Place Recreation Reserve in Maddingley (12 min), the playground at Sarino Park Recreation Reserve in Maddingley (13 min), Bacchus Marsh Public childrens pool in Bacchus Marsh (15 min) and the skate park at Rotary Park in Bacchus Marsh (17 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Rowsley.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Rowsley, Maddingley has 8 playgrounds and a skate park. Balliang, next to Rowsley, has 1 playground. Glenmore also borders Rowsley.
