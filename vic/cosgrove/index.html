Cosgrove is in the Greater Shepparton area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Cosgrove is Pine Lodge H54 Bushland Reserve, covering about 12 hectares. Dookie H52 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cosgrove. The closest to Cosgrove is Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line), about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cosgrove, so most trips start by car. By car from Cosgrove, Shepparton is about 22 minutes away, Benalla about 41 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Aquamoves in Shepparton is about 24 minutes' drive from Cosgrove. For a library, Shepparton Library in Shepparton is about 22 minutes' drive from Cosgrove. The closest beach to Cosgrove is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Cosgrove is Shepparton Velodrome in Shepparton, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Cosgrove is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 23 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 22 minutes' drive from Cosgrove. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Cosgrove is Kidstown in Mooroopna, about 26 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 22 minutes from Cosgrove by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Cosgrove are the playground at CWA Gardens in Dookie (5 min), the playground at Dookie Recreation Reserve in Dookie (7 min), StarBowl in Shepparton (22 min), Village Cinemas in Shepparton (23 min), Shepparton Library in Shepparton (22 min) and Shepparton Heritage Centre in Shepparton (23 min).

Best for
