Beverford is in the Swan Hill area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Beverford, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Beverford has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Beverford, so most trips start by car. By car from Beverford, Swan Hill is about 13 minutes away and Echuca about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Beverford is Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill, about 12 minutes' drive. For a library, Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill is about 12 minutes' drive from Beverford. The closest skate park to Beverford is Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South, about 16 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill is about 13 minutes' drive from Beverford. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 13 minutes from Beverford by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Beverford are Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South (16 min), the playground at Nyah Heritage Park in Nyah (11 min), the playground at Adams Park in Swan Hill (12 min), Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill (12 min), the skate park at James Belsar Reserve in Swan Hill (12 min) and Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill (12 min).
