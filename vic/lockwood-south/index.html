Our data lists 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in Lockwood South, in the Greater Bendigo area.

Landmarks

Lockwood Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Lockwood South, at about 11 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Lockwood South include Lockwood South State Forest. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Lockwood South. For camping, there's Lockwood South State Forest.

Getting around

Lockwood South has no train station; Kangaroo Flat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 9.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Lockwood South. By car from Lockwood South, Bendigo is about 20 minutes away, Castlemaine about 22 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lockwood South is Faith Leech Aquatic Centre in Bendigo, about 21 minutes' drive. For a library, Kangaroo Flat Library in Kangaroo Flat is about 12 minutes' drive from Lockwood South. For a skate park, the pump track on Water Street in Bendigo is about 22 minutes' drive from Lockwood South. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 20 minutes' drive from Lockwood South. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 17 minutes' drive from Lockwood South. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 26 minutes' drive from Lockwood South. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Lockwood South, about 20 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Lockwood South, the nearest places for kids by road are Graemar Court Playspace in Kangaroo Flat (12 min), Kangaroo Flat Library in Kangaroo Flat (12 min), William Drive Playspace in Kangaroo Flat (13 min), Intagolf in Golden Gully (17 min), Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo (20 min) and Bendigo RSL Museum in Bendigo (20 min).
