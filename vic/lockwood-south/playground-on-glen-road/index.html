Playground on Glen Road

Playground in Lockwood South · Basic info

This is a public playground in Lockwood South. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and coaches stop at Roadside Store/Calder Alternative Hwy, a 4-minute walk away.
