Lockwood Streamside Reserve

Park in Lockwood South · Basic info

Lockwood Streamside Reserve is a park in Lockwood South, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, good for all ages. Roadside Store/Calder Alternative Hwy (coach) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 288 m away with a baby change table.
