Willowvale is in the Golden Plains area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Willowvale, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Willowvale. The closest to Willowvale is Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line), about 44 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Willowvale, so most trips start by car. By car from Willowvale, Ballarat is about 59 minutes away, Colac about 71 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Lismore Pool in Lismore is about 24 minutes' drive from Willowvale. For a library, Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum is about 33 minutes' drive from Willowvale. The closest skate park to Willowvale is Linton Skate Park in Linton, about 30 minutes' drive. Ballarat is the closest bigger centre to Willowvale, about 59 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Willowvale, the nearest places for kids by road are Linton Skate Park in Linton (30 min), Linton BMX park in Linton (30 min), Linton Recreation Reserve Playground in Linton (31 min), Lismore Pool in Lismore (24 min), Cape Clear & District Lions Park Playground in Cape Clear (24 min) and Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Willowvale.
