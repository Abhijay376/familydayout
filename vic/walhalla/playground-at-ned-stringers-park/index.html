Playground at Ned Stringer's Park

Playground in Walhalla · Basic info

Playground at Ned Stringer's Park in Walhalla is a public playground in the Baw Baw Shire area. The Australian Alps Walking Track passes about 60 m away, and Fire station is about 80 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground. It's easiest by car, about 1 minute from Walhalla.
