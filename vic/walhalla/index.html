Our data lists 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in Walhalla, in the Baw Baw area. Beyond the parks, Walhalla has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Walhalla is Deep Creek State Forest, covering about 8,779 hectares. Walhalla Historic Area is the next largest. Walhalla also has Ned Stringer's Park. Playgrounds around Walhalla include the playground at Ned Stringer's Park. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Walhalla. Places to visit in Walhalla include Fire station. The Australian Alps Walking Track and Bruntons Bridge Walk all pass through Walhalla.

Getting around

Walhalla has no train station; Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 30 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Walhalla, so most trips start by car. By car from Walhalla, Traralgon is about 43 minutes away, Warragul about 70 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rawson Swimming Pool in Rawson is about 13 minutes' drive from Walhalla. The closest skate park to Walhalla is Glengarry Skatepark in Glengarry, about 41 minutes' drive. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Walhalla, about 43 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Walhalla are the playground at Croxford Park in Rawson (12 min), Rawson Swimming Pool in Rawson (13 min), Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon (42 min), Glengarry Skatepark in Glengarry (41 min), Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon (43 min) and Old Brown Coal Mine Museum in Yallourn North (41 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Walhalla. Free days on the Australian Alps Walking Track and in Walhalla's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Rawson, next to Walhalla, has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Walhalla, Toongabbie has 2 playgrounds. Toombon, Jacob Creek, Baw Baw and Amor also border Walhalla.
