Getting around

There's no train station in Cardinia. The closest to Cardinia is Cardinia Road Railway Station (metro), about 8.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cardinia, so most trips start by car. By car from Cardinia, Pakenham is about 13 minutes away, Cranbourne about 16 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 51 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cardinia is King Swim in Clyde North, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest library to Cardinia is Pakenham Library in Pakenham, about 14 minutes' drive. For a beach, Seaford Beach in Seaford is about 32 minutes' drive from Cardinia. For a skate park, Clyde North Skatepark (Bernardins Street) in Clyde North is about 14 minutes' drive from Cardinia. The closest cinema to Cardinia is Village Cinemas in Narre Warren, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Cardinia is Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Cardinia is MonkeyG's in Pakenham, about 13 minutes' drive. Pakenham is the closest bigger centre to Cardinia, about 13 minutes away by car, and has 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play.

Nearby suburbs

Clyde North, next to Cardinia, has 34 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Cardinia, Clyde has 11 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Cardinia, Koo Wee Rup has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Tooradin, next to Cardinia, has 2 playgrounds. Officer South, Rythdale, Pakenham South and Dalmore also border Cardinia.
