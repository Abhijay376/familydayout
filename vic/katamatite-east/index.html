Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Katamatite East in the Moira area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Katamatite East has no train station; Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 51 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Katamatite East, so most trips start by car. By car from Katamatite East, Shepparton is about 55 minutes away and Benalla about 65 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah is about 41 minutes' drive from Katamatite East. The closest library to Katamatite East is Barooga Library in Cobram, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Katamatite East is Backhouse Beach in Cobram East, about 13 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Federation Park in Cobram is about 21 minutes' drive from Katamatite East. For a cinema, Cobram Cinema in Cobram is about 21 minutes' drive from Katamatite East. For a zoo or wildlife park, Tunzafun in Yarrawonga is about 30 minutes' drive from Katamatite East. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Katamatite East, about 55 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Katamatite East, the nearest places for kids by road are Backhouse Beach in Cobram East (13 min), the playground at E.W Kilgour Reserve in Katamatite (15 min), Katamatite Historical Society Museum in Katamatite (15 min), the playground at C R Wood Park in Katamatite (15 min), Duffys Beach No 1 in Burramine (20 min) and Cobram Cinema in Cobram (21 min).
