Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Waldara in the Wangaratta area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

The MBN passes through Waldara.

Getting around

Waldara has no train station; Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 4.9 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Waldara, so most trips start by car. By car from Waldara, Wangaratta is about 7 minutes away and Benalla about 41 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta is about 6 minutes' drive from Waldara. The closest library to Waldara is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Waldara is Sandy Beach in Beechworth, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Waldara is the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta, about 11 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 7 minutes' drive from Waldara. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 7 minutes from Waldara by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Waldara are Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta (6 min), Brien Crescent Playground in Wangaratta (6 min), Donald Street Playground in Wangaratta (6 min), Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (7 min), Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta (7 min) and Moore than Swimming in Wangaratta (10 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Waldara on the MBN.
