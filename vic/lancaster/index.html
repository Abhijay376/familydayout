Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Lancaster in the Campaspe area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Lancaster has no train station; Mooroopna Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Bendigo Station - Shepparton Station via Kyabram route stop in Lancaster. V/Line coaches stop in Lancaster. By car from Lancaster, Shepparton is about 32 minutes away, Echuca about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lancaster is Community Swimming Pool in Merrigum, about 8 minutes' drive. For a library, Kyabram Library And Community Hub in Kyabram is about 9 minutes' drive from Lancaster. For a beach, Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton is about 34 minutes' drive from Lancaster. The closest skate park to Lancaster is the bmx track on Racecourse Road in Kyabram, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Lancaster is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Lancaster is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Lancaster is Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram, about 11 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 32 minutes from Lancaster by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Lancaster are the playground at Judd Park in Merrigum (8 min), Community Swimming Pool in Merrigum (8 min), the playground at Merrigum Lions Park in Merrigum (8 min), Kyabram Library And Community Hub in Kyabram (9 min), Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram (11 min) and the bmx track on Racecourse Road in Kyabram (11 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lancaster.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Lancaster, Kyabram has 8 playgrounds, a library and a zoo or wildlife park. Next door to Lancaster, Merrigum has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Undera, next to Lancaster, has 1 playground. Wyuna, St Germains and Gillieston also border Lancaster.
