Our data lists 6 playgrounds and 9 parks and reserves in Strathfieldsaye, in the Greater Bendigo area.

Landmarks

Strathfieldsaye Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Strathfieldsaye, at about 17 hectares. Strathfieldsaye Club Court Reserve and Strathfieldsaye H97 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Strathfieldsaye include Bark Reserve, Coomoora Park and Greater Bendigo (future addition). Playgrounds around Strathfieldsaye include Clydebank Court Playspace and the playground at Coomoora Park. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Strathfieldsaye. For walks and rides, Sheepwash Creek Trail crosses Strathfieldsaye. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Strathfieldsaye for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Strathfieldsaye has no train station; Bendigo Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 7.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 70 and 71 stop in Strathfieldsaye. By car from Strathfieldsaye, Bendigo is about 12 minutes away, Castlemaine about 43 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Brennan Park Swimming Pool in Kennington is about 10 minutes' drive from Strathfieldsaye. The closest library to Strathfieldsaye is Bendigo Library in Bendigo, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Strathfieldsaye is Ewing Park Skating in Bendigo, about 10 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 13 minutes' drive from Strathfieldsaye. The closest indoor play centre to Strathfieldsaye is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 13 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 13 minutes' drive from Strathfieldsaye.

Best for

Younger kids at Strathfieldsaye's 6 local playgrounds. Free days on the Sheepwash Creek Trail and in Strathfieldsaye's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Strathfieldsaye, Kennington has 7 playgrounds and a pool. Strathdale, next to Strathfieldsaye, has 4 playgrounds. Flora Hill, next to Strathfieldsaye, has 4 playgrounds. Junortoun, next to Strathfieldsaye, has 2 playgrounds. Spring Gully, Mandurang, Longlea and Eppalock also border Strathfieldsaye.
