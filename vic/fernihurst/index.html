Fernihurst in the Loddon area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Fernihurst is Kinipanial Creek Historic and Cultural Features Reserve, covering about 62 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Fernihurst. The closest to Fernihurst is Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line), about 31 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Fernihurst, so most trips start by car. By car from Fernihurst, Bendigo is about 72 minutes away and Echuca about 83 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Fernihurst is Boort Swimming Pool in Boort, about 37 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Fernihurst are A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park in Boort (36 min), Boort Court House Historical Museum in Boort (36 min), Boort Swimming Pool in Boort (37 min), the playground at R J Phelan Reserve in Mitiamo (39 min), Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo (40 min) and East Loddon Historical Society in Mitiamo (40 min).

Best for
