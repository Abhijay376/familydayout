Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Katunga, in the Moira area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Katunga is Katunga Bushland Reserve, covering about 4 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Katunga.

Getting around

Katunga has no train station; Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 43 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Katunga. By car from Katunga, Shepparton is about 41 minutes away and Benalla about 71 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah is about 14 minutes' drive from Katunga. For a library, Numurkah Library in Numurkah is about 14 minutes' drive from Katunga. For a beach, Carter Beach in Ulupna is about 33 minutes' drive from Katunga. For a skate park, Riverland Skate Park in Numurkah is about 15 minutes' drive from Katunga. For a cinema, Cobram Cinema in Cobram is about 25 minutes' drive from Katunga. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 41 minutes' drive from Katunga. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kidstown in Mooroopna is about 44 minutes' drive from Katunga. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Katunga, about 41 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Katunga are Numurkah Library in Numurkah (14 min), Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah (14 min), Riverland Skate Park in Numurkah (15 min), Strathmerton Community Skate Park in Strathmerton (15 min), Carter Beach in Ulupna (33 min) and Labbett Beach in Ulupna (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Katunga.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Katunga, Numurkah has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Next door to Katunga, Yalca has a beach. Strathmerton, Naring and Muckatah also border Katunga.
