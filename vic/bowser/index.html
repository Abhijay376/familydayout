Bowser is in the Wangaratta area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bowser is Peechelba - Wangaratta rail line Bushland Reserve, covering about 88 hectares. The Wangaratta - Eldorado Loop and Murray to Mountains Rail Trail: Wangaratta – Bright all pass through Bowser.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bowser. The closest to Bowser is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 6.2 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bowser, so most trips start by car. By car from Bowser, Wangaratta is about 11 minutes away and Benalla about 41 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bowser is Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest library to Bowser is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 10 minutes' drive. For a beach, Sandy Beach in Beechworth is about 36 minutes' drive from Bowser. For a skate park, the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta is about 13 minutes' drive from Bowser. The closest cinema to Bowser is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 8 minutes' drive. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Bowser, about 11 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Bowser are the playground at Apex Park in Wangaratta (8 min), Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta (8 min), the playground at King George V Gardens in Wangaratta (8 min), Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (10 min), Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta (12 min) and the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta (13 min).

Best for

Free days on the Wangaratta - Eldorado Loop and in Bowser's parks.
