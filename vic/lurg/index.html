Lurg in the Benalla area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Lurg I67 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Lurg, at about 3 hectares. Lurg I68 Bushland Reserve and Kelfeera Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Lurg also has Lurg H129 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Lurg has no train station; Benalla Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lurg, so most trips start by car. By car from Lurg, Benalla is about 24 minutes away, Wangaratta about 42 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lurg is Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest library to Lurg is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 43 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Pump Track in Benalla is about 24 minutes' drive from Lurg. The closest cinema to Lurg is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 43 minutes' drive. Benalla is the closest bigger centre to Lurg, about 24 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds and a pool. From Lurg, the nearest places for kids by road are Benalla Aviation Museum in Benalla (24 min), Benalla Migrant Camp in Benalla (24 min), Pump Track in Benalla (24 min), Benalla Splash Park in Benalla (24 min), Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (24 min) and the playground at Benalla Botanical Gardens in Benalla (25 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lurg.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Lurg, Benalla has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Glenrowan West also borders Lurg.
