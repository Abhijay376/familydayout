Nelson Park

Park in Portland · Basic info

Nelson Park is a park in Portland, in the Glenelg Shire Council area, good for all ages. Nelson Park/Cape Nelson Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 346 m away.
