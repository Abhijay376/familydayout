Whalers Point

Beach in Portland · Basic info

Whalers Point is a beach in Portland, in the Glenelg Shire Council area, good for all ages. Jubilee Ct/Milbanke St (bus) is a 9-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 589 m away.
