Husson Fauna Park

Park in Portland · Basic info

Husson Fauna Park is a park in Portland, in the Glenelg Shire Council area, good for all ages. Mitchell Cres/Otway St (bus) is a 17-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 343 m away.
