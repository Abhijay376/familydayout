Playground on Weatherall Road

Playground in Cheltenham · Basic info

Playground on Weatherall Road in Cheltenham is a public playground in the Bayside City area. Hole In One Pasta & Pizza is about 70 m away if you need food, and Playground on Glebe Avenue is another option about 400 m away. There's a public toilet about 110 m away, and bus 922 stops at Cheltenham Recreation Reserve/Weatherall Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
