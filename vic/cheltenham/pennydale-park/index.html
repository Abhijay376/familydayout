Pennydale Park

Park in Cheltenham · Basic info

Pennydale Park is a park in Cheltenham, in the Bayside City Council area, good for all ages. Victoria Golf Club/Park Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 905 m away and dogs are allowed on a lead.
