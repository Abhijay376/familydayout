Playground at Elliott Street Reserve

Playground in Cheltenham · Basic info

Playground at Elliott Street Reserve is a public playground in Cheltenham, in the Kingston City area. For food, Applehead Deli is about 480 m away, and Playground at Follett Road Reserve is another option about 290 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.0 km from the playground, and bus 903 stops at Elliott St/Warrigal Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
