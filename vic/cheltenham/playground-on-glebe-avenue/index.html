Playground on Glebe Avenue

Playground in Cheltenham · Basic info

Playground on Glebe Avenue is a public playground in Cheltenham, in the Bayside City area. The Cheltenham - Parkdale passes about 130 m away, and Cheltenham Library is about 270 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 380 m away, and bus 811 stops at Cheltenham Railway Station/Charman Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
