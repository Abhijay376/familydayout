Playground at Chelsea Street Reserve

Playground in Cheltenham · Basic info

Playground at Chelsea Street Reserve in Cheltenham is a public playground in the Kingston City area. For food, Golden Thai is about 330 m away, and Playground at Friendship Square Reserve is another option about 520 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.2 km from the playground, and buses 811 and 812 stop at Parnell St/Bernard St, a 4-minute walk away.
