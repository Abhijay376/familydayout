Oakwood Park

Park in Noble Park North · Official details

Oakwood Park is a park in Noble Park North, in the Greater Dandenong City Council area, good for all ages. Heatherton Rd/Princes Hwy (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 159 m away.

Where is Oakwood Park?
