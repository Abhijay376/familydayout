Our data lists 12 playgrounds and 7 parks and reserves in Noble Park North, in the Greater Dandenong area.

Landmarks

Barry J Powell Reserve is the largest mapped park in Noble Park North, at about 10 hectares. Oakwood Park and W J Turner Reserve are the next largest. Noble Park North also has Nimbin Reserve, Birkley Reserve and Oakwood Avenue Reserve. Playgrounds around Noble Park North include Oakwood Park, the playground at Nimbin Reserve and the playground at Coolavin Reserve. Our data shows 4 public toilets, 6 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by and 1 off-leash dog park in Noble Park North. For walks and rides, EastLink Trail crosses Noble Park North. Our data lists 3 cafes and family restaurants in Noble Park North for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Noble Park North has no train station; Noble Park Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 2.1 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 631, 800, 813, 814, 848, 850, 862 and 978 stop in Noble Park North. By car from Noble Park North, Dandenong is about 7 minutes away, Ringwood about 18 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 27 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Noble Park Aquatic Centre in Noble Park is about 5 minutes' drive from Noble Park North. The closest library to Noble Park North is Dandenong Library in Dandenong, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Noble Park North is Edithvale Beach in Edithvale, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Noble Park North is Noble Park Skatepark in Noble Park, about 4 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Reading Cinema in Dandenong is about 7 minutes' drive from Noble Park North. The closest indoor play centre to Noble Park North is Rush HQ in Rowville, about 8 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 12 playgrounds across Noble Park North. Free days on the EastLink Trail and in Noble Park North's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Dandenong, next to Noble Park North, has 21 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Noble Park North, Noble Park has 19 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Dandenong North, next to Noble Park North, has 24 playgrounds. Mulgrave, next to Noble Park North, has 23 playgrounds and a skate park. Springvale also borders Noble Park North.
