Birkley Reserve

Park in Noble Park North · Official details

Birkley Reserve is a park in Noble Park North, in the Greater Dandenong City Council area, good for all ages. Galos Pl/Police Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 609 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Birkley Reserve?
Birkley Reserve is in Noble Park North, Victoria, at 4 Birkley Court, Noble Park North, VIC 3174.
