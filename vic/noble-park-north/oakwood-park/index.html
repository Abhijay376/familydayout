Oakwood Park

Playground in Noble Park North · Official details

This is a public playground in Noble Park North. The EastLink Trail is about 20 m away, and Playground at Edith MacPherson Reserve is another option about 520 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 800 stops at Heatherton Rd/Princes Hwy, a 1-minute walk away.
