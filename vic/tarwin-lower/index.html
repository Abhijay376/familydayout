Tarwin Lower in the South Gippsland area has 1 playground and 7 parks and reserves in our data. Tarwin Lower also has 2 beaches.

Landmarks

Kings Flat Flora Reserve is the largest mapped park in Tarwin Lower, at about 83 hectares. Tarwin Lower Flora Reserve and Tarwin Lower Recreation Reserve are the next largest. Tarwin Lower also has RV Fisher Reserve, School Road Reserve and Tarwin South Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds around Tarwin Lower include the playground at RV Fisher Reserve. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Tarwin Lower. Places to visit in Tarwin Lower include Cape Liptrap and Diggers Island South Beach. For walks and rides, Venus Bay Tarwin Lower Bike Path and Five Mile Track cross Tarwin Lower.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tarwin Lower. The closest to Tarwin Lower is Yarragon Railway Station (V/Line), about 58 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tarwin Lower, so most trips start by car. By car from Tarwin Lower, Leongatha is about 29 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 29 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha is about 28 minutes' drive from Tarwin Lower. For a library, Leongatha Library in Leongatha is about 28 minutes' drive from Tarwin Lower. For a skate park, the skate park at Venus Bay Community Centre Reserve in Venus Bay is about 7 minutes' drive from Tarwin Lower. The closest cinema to Tarwin Lower is Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha, about 28 minutes' drive. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Tarwin Lower, about 29 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Beach days at Cape Liptrap and Diggers Island South Beach. Free days on the Venus Bay Tarwin Lower Bike Path and in Tarwin Lower's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Inverloch, next to Tarwin Lower, has 4 playgrounds and a beach. Venus Bay, next to Tarwin Lower, has 6 playgrounds and a skate park. Fish Creek, next to Tarwin Lower, has 1 playground. Next door to Tarwin Lower, Buffalo has 1 playground. Walkerville South, Walkerville, Pound Creek and Middle Tarwin also border Tarwin Lower.
