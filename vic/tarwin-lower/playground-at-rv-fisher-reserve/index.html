Playground at RV Fisher Reserve

Playground in Tarwin Lower · Basic info

Playground at RV Fisher Reserve is a public playground in Tarwin Lower, in the South Gippsland Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet right by the playground. It's easiest by car, about 1 minute from Tarwin Lower.
