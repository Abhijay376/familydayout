Burramboot is in the Campaspe area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

One Tree Swamp and Two Tree Swamp Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Burramboot, at about 856 hectares. Gaynor Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the next largest.

Getting around

Burramboot has no train station; Elmore Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Burramboot, so most trips start by car. By car from Burramboot, Echuca is about 43 minutes away, Shepparton about 56 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Memorial Pool in Rochester is about 20 minutes' drive from Burramboot. The closest library to Burramboot is Rushworth Library in Rushworth, about 25 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Lions Park in Rushworth is about 25 minutes' drive from Burramboot. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 43 minutes from Burramboot by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Burramboot are Campaspe Run Rural Discovery Centre in Elmore (17 min), Memorial Pool in Rochester (20 min), the playground at Apex Park in Rochester (21 min), Rushworth Museum in Rushworth (24 min), Rushworth Library in Rushworth (25 min) and Rushworth Pool in Rushworth (25 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Burramboot.

Nearby suburbs

Rushworth, next to Burramboot, has a pool, a library and a skate park. Colbinabbin, next to Burramboot, has 1 playground. Corop and Carag Carag also border Burramboot.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Burramboot, without traffic.
