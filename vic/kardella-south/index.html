Kardella South is in the South Gippsland area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Kardella South, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Kardella South has no train station; Warragul Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 32 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kardella South, so most trips start by car. By car from Kardella South, Leongatha is about 8 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kardella South is Korumburra Outdoor Pool in Korumburra, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest library to Kardella South is Leongatha Library in Leongatha, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Kardella South is The Caves in Inverloch, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Kardella South is Leongatha Skate Park in Leongatha, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Kardella South is Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha, about 7 minutes' drive. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Kardella South, about 8 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Kardella South, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Coal Creek Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Korumburra (5 min), Coal Creek Community Park & Museum in Korumburra (6 min), the playground at Coleman Park in Korumburra (7 min), Korumburra Outdoor Pool in Korumburra (7 min), Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha (7 min) and Leongatha Library in Leongatha (7 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Kardella South.

Nearby suburbs

Leongatha, next to Kardella South, has 4 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Kardella South, Korumburra has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Kardella also borders Kardella South.
