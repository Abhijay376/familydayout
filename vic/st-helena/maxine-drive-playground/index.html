Maxine Drive Playground

Playground in St Helena · Basic info

This is a public playground in St Helena. The Banyule LBN9 is about 300 m away, and Playground at Patrick Close Reserve is another option about 280 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 800 m from the playground, and bus 518 stops at Maxine Dr/St Helena Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
