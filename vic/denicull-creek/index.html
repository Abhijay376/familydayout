Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Denicull Creek in the Ararat area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Denicull Creek. The closest to Denicull Creek is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 7.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Denicull Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Denicull Creek, Ararat is about 8 minutes away, Stawell about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat is about 8 minutes' drive from Denicull Creek. For a library, Ararat Library in Ararat is about 8 minutes' drive from Denicull Creek. The closest skate park to Denicull Creek is David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat, about 12 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Astor Cinema in Ararat is about 8 minutes' drive from Denicull Creek. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Denicull Creek is Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell, about 32 minutes' drive. Ararat is the closest bigger centre to Denicull Creek, about 8 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Denicull Creek are Ararat Gallery TAMA in Ararat (8 min), Ararat Library in Ararat (8 min), Astor Cinema in Ararat (8 min), Langi Morgala Museum in Ararat (8 min), Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat (8 min) and Ararat Outdoor Pool in Ararat (12 min).
