Kackeraboite Creek Reserve

Park in Mount Eliza · Basic info

Kackeraboite Creek Reserve is a park in Mount Eliza, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire Council area, good for all ages. Ithaca Rd/Nepean Hwy (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1181 m away.
