John H Butler Reserve

Park in Mount Eliza · Basic info

John H Butler Reserve is a park in Mount Eliza, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire Council area, good for all ages. Wimborne Ave/Mount Eliza Way (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 104 m away.
