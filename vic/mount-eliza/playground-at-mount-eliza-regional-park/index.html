Playground at Mount Eliza Regional Park

Playground in Mount Eliza · Basic info

Playground at Mount Eliza Regional Park is a public playground in Mount Eliza, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire area. The Peninsula Link Trail passes about 210 m away, and Doppio Zero is about 360 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 380 m away. It's easiest by car, about 8 minutes from Mount Eliza.
