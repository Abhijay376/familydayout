The Ridge Reserve

Park in Mount Eliza · Basic info

The Ridge Reserve is a park in Mount Eliza, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire Council area, good for all ages. Wooralla Dr/Nepean Hwy (bus) is a 13-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1639 m away.
