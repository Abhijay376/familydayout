Howard Parker Playground

Playground in Mount Eliza · Basic info

Howard Parker Playground is a public playground in Mount Eliza, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire area. Manyung Gallery Mount Eliza is about 330 m away, and Kim & Li Chinese & Thai Restaurant is about 270 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and buses 781, 784, 785 and 788 stop at Mount Eliza SC/Mount Eliza Way, a 5-minute walk away.
