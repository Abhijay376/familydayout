Sunnyside Beach

Beach in Mount Eliza · Basic info

Sunnyside Beach is a beach in Mount Eliza, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire Council area, good for all ages. George Vowell Aged Care/Nepean Hwy (bus) is a 19-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 115 m away with a baby change table.

Where is Sunnyside Beach?
