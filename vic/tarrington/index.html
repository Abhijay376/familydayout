Getting around

There's no train station in Tarrington. The closest to Tarrington is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 76 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Hamilton - Penshurst via Tarrington route stop in Tarrington. V/Line coaches stop in Tarrington. By car from Tarrington, Hamilton is about 10 minutes away and Portland about 73 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tarrington is Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Hamilton, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest library to Tarrington is Hamilton Library in Hamilton, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Tarrington is hamilton skate park in Hamilton, about 8 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton is about 9 minutes' drive from Tarrington. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 10 minutes from Tarrington by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Tarrington, the nearest places for kids by road are White Avenue Playground in Hamilton (8 min), Sir Reginald Ansett Transport Museum in Hamilton (7 min), hamilton skate park in Hamilton (8 min), Hamilton Library in Hamilton (9 min), Hamilton Gallery in Hamilton (9 min) and Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton (9 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tarrington.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Tarrington, Hamilton has 23 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Strathkellar and Croxton East also border Tarrington.
