Playground on Hewat Drive

Playground in Highton · Basic info

Playground on Hewat Drive is a public playground in Highton, in the Greater Geelong City area. Red Rooster is about 420 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.2 km away, and buses 1, 40, 41 and 42 stop at Ficinia Mews/Pigdons Rd, a 8-minute walk away.
