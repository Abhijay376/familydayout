Barrabool Hills Plaza

Shopping centre in Highton · Official details

Barrabool Hills Plaza is a shopping centre in Highton, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Avondale Ave/Province Bvd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 416 m away. Opening hours: Monday – Sunday 7.00am – 10.00pm; EXCEPTIONS – GOOD FRIDAY, ANZAC DAY (9am - 1pm) AND CHRISTMAS DAY – CLOSED.
