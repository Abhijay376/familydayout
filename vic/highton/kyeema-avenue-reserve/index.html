Kyeema Avenue Reserve

Playground in Highton · Basic info

Kyeema Avenue Reserve in Highton is a public playground in the Greater Geelong City area. Lambhill Reserve is another option about 390 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.3 km from the playground, and bus 43 stops at Barrabool Hills Cemetery/Mt Pleasant Rd, a 8-minute walk away.
