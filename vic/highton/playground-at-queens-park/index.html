Playground at Queens Park

Playground in Highton · Basic info

Playground at Queens Park in Highton is a public playground in the Greater Geelong City area. The Barwon River Trail is about 260 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground. It's easiest by car, about 6 minutes from Highton.
