Highton Reserve

Playground in Highton · Basic info

Highton Reserve in Highton is a public playground in the Greater Geelong City area. Highton Library is about 220 m away, and Bluebird Sandwiches is about 290 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 80 m away, and bus 42 stops at Highton Library/Roslyn Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
