Bennett Reserve

Playground in Highton · Basic info

Bennett Reserve is a public playground in Highton, in the Greater Geelong City area. Playground on Mount Pleasant Road is another option about 130 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 720 m from the playground, and bus 43 stops at Montpellier Primary School/Mt Pleasant Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
