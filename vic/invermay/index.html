Invermay is in the Ballarat area, and our data lists 1 playground in the suburb.

Landmarks

Playgrounds in Invermay include the playground at Invermay Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet and 1 park or playground with a council BBQ close by in Invermay.

Getting around

There's no train station in Invermay. The closest to Invermay is Ballarat Railway Station (V/Line), about 4.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Invermay, so most trips start by car. By car from Invermay, Ballarat is about 9 minutes away, Daylesford about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Invermay is the water play at Midlands Reserve in Ballarat North, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest library to Invermay is Central Highlands Regional Library Corp Ballarat branch in Ballarat Central, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Invermay is the skate park at Len T Frazer Reserve in Ballarat East, about 11 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central is about 9 minutes' drive from Invermay. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 12 minutes' drive from Invermay. For a zoo or wildlife park, Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East is about 15 minutes' drive from Invermay. The nearest larger centre is Ballarat, about 9 minutes from Invermay by car, with 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Invermay, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Jack Toohey Park in Invermay Park (4 min), the water play at Midlands Reserve in Ballarat North (5 min), Central Highlands Regional Library Corp Ballarat branch in Ballarat Central (7 min), Black Hill Swimming Pool in Black Hill (9 min), Ballarat Fine Art Gallery in Ballarat Central (9 min) and Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central (9 min).
