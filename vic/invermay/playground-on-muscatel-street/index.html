Playground at Invermay Recreation Reserve

Playground in Invermay · Official details

Playground at Invermay Recreation Reserve is a public playground in Invermay, in the Ballarat City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet about 90 m away. It's easiest by car, about 1 minute from Invermay.
