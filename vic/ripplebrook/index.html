Ripplebrook is in the Baw Baw area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Ripplebrook, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Ripplebrook. The closest to Ripplebrook is Longwarry Railway Station (V/Line), about 9.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Warragul Station - Poowong East via Drouin, Ripplebrook route stop in Ripplebrook. By car from Ripplebrook, Warragul is about 23 minutes away, Pakenham about 35 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 79 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Ripplebrook is Drouin Swimming Pool in Drouin, about 14 minutes' drive. For a library, Drouin Library in Drouin is about 14 minutes' drive from Ripplebrook. For a skate park, Drouin Skate Park in Drouin is about 14 minutes' drive from Ripplebrook. The closest cinema to Ripplebrook is Warragul Cinema in Warragul, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Ripplebrook is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 21 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Wildlife Trail in Tynong North is about 23 minutes' drive from Ripplebrook. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Ripplebrook, about 23 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Ripplebrook are Drouin Skate Park in Drouin (14 min), Drouin Library in Drouin (14 min), Water Play in Drouin (14 min), the playground at Civic Park in Drouin (14 min), Drouin Swimming Pool in Drouin (14 min) and Bmx in Warragul (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Ripplebrook.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Ripplebrook, Longwarry has 3 playgrounds. Athlone also borders Ripplebrook.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Ripplebrook, without traffic.
