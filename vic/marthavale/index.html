Landmarks

Other parks and reserves in Marthavale include Marthavale Hut. Picnic areas in Marthavale include Marthavale Hut.

Getting around

Marthavale has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 43 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Marthavale, so most trips start by car. By car from Marthavale, Bairnsdale is about 2 hours away and Lakes Entrance about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Marthavale, the nearest places for kids by road are Great Alpine Gallery in Swifts Creek (108 min) and Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek (109 min).
