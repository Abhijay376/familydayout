Playground on Tallis Street

Playground in Norlane · Basic info

This is a public playground in Norlane. Older kids have a skate park about 170 m away, and The Pizza Box is about 420 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 530 m away, and buses 1 and 23 stop at Rose Ave/Station St, a 3-minute walk away.
