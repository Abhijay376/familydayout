Corio Bowls

Skate, BMX or pump track in Norlane · Basic info

Corio Bowls is a skate, bmx or pump track in Norlane, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Leisuretime Sports Precinct/Anakie Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 220 m away.
