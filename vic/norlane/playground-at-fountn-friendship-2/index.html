Playground at Fountn Friendship

Playground in Norlane · Basic info

This is a public playground in Norlane. The Cowies Creek Trail is about 140 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 220 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 190 m from the playground, and bus 22 stops at Leisuretime Sports Precinct/Anakie Rd , a 3-minute walk away.
