Windsor Park

Playground in Norlane · Basic info

This is a public playground in Norlane. Older kids have a skate park about 260 m away, and The Pizza Box is about 350 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 670 m away, and buses 20 and 22 stop at Sparks Rd/Princes Hwy, a 6-minute walk away.
