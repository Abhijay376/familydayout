Playground at Fountain of Friendship Park

Playground in Norlane · Basic info

This is a public playground in Norlane. The Cowies Creek Trail passes about 90 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 110 m away. There's a public toilet about 140 m away, and bus 22 stops at Leisuretime Sports Precinct/Anakie Rd , a 1-minute walk away.
