Toorongo in the Baw Baw area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Toorongo is Noojee State Forest, covering about 15,107 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Toorongo include Link Road. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Toorongo. For camping, there's Link Road. For walks and rides, Upper Yarra Walking Track and Toorongo Falls Walk cross Toorongo.

Getting around

There's no train station in Toorongo. The closest to Toorongo is Warragul Railway Station (V/Line), about 45 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Toorongo, so most trips start by car. By car from Toorongo, Warragul is about 79 minutes away, Traralgon about 1.5 hours and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Toorongo, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at LaTrobe and Loch Rivers, Noojee Streamside Reserve in Noojee (45 min) and Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South (61 min).

Best for

Free days on the Upper Yarra Walking Track and in Toorongo's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Toorongo, Noojee has 1 playground. Loch Valley, Jericho, Cambarville and Baw Baw also border Toorongo.
