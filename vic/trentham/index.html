Our data lists 2 playgrounds and 7 parks and reserves in Trentham, in the Hepburn area. On top of that, Trentham has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

Tylden South Education Area is the largest mapped park in Trentham, at about 120 hectares. Coliban River Scenic Reserve and Trentham Public Park are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Trentham include Trentham Town Square, Quarry Street Reserve and Trent Creek Public Park. Playgrounds in Trentham include the playground at Quarry Street Reserve. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Trentham. In Trentham you'll also find Little Gallery. For walks and rides, The Domino Rail Trail, Domino Trail Alternate and Wombat Trail cross Trentham. Our data lists 3 cafes and family restaurants in Trentham for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trentham has no train station; Kyneton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 4 stops in Trentham. V/Line coaches stop in Trentham. By car from Trentham, Kyneton is about 24 minutes away, Daylesford about 25 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 81 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Trentham is Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford, about 25 minutes' drive. For a library, Kyneton Library in Kyneton is about 24 minutes' drive from Trentham. For a beach, Canoeing in Tylden is about 17 minutes' drive from Trentham. The closest skate park to Trentham is Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton, about 22 minutes' drive. Kyneton is the closest bigger centre to Trentham, about 24 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds and a library. From Trentham, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at J P Gleeson Memorial Park in Lyonville (11 min), Canoeing in Tylden (17 min), Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton (22 min), Kyneton Library in Kyneton (24 min), Kyneton Museum in Kyneton (24 min) and Daylesford Library in Daylesford (24 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Trentham. Free days on The Domino Rail Trail and in Trentham's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Tylden, next to Trentham, has 1 playground and a beach. Next door to Trentham, Lyonville has 1 playground. North Blackwood, Newbury, Little Hampton and Lerderderg also border Trentham.
