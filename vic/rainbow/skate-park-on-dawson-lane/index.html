Skate park on Dawson Lane

Skate, BMX or pump track in Rainbow · Basic info

Skate park on Dawson Lane is a skate, bmx or pump track in Rainbow, in the Hindmarsh Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Chemist/Federal St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 206 m away.
