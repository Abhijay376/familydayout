Our data lists 1 playground and 11 parks and reserves in Rainbow, in the Hindmarsh area. Rainbow also has a pool.

Landmarks

Wimmera River Heritage River is the largest mapped park in Rainbow, at about 48,642 hectares. Birdcage Flora and Fauna Reserve and Albacutya I171 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Rainbow include Gregson Park, Rainbow Lake and Rainbow Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 8 public toilets in Rainbow. In Rainbow you'll also find Rainbow Pool. For walks and rides, Hermans Hill Walking Track crosses Rainbow.

Getting around

Rainbow has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 155 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Horsham - Hopetoun via Dimboola route stop in Rainbow. By car from Rainbow, Horsham is about 82 minutes away and Stawell about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Rainbow, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Yaapeet Rec Reserve in Yaapeet (17 min), Fitness Course in Jeparit (27 min), Wimmera-Mallee Pioneer Museum in Jeparit (27 min) and Hopetoun Swimming Pool in Hopetoun (38 min).

Best for

Free days on the Hermans Hill Walking Track and in Rainbow's parks.
