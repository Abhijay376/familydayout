Playground at Coot Picnic Area

Playground in Albert Park · Official details

This is a public playground in Albert Park. The Cecil Street is about 270 m away, and Melbourne Sports and Aquatic Centre is about 170 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and tram 12 stops at Melbourne Sports & Aquatic Centre/Albert Rd #131, a 5-minute walk away.
