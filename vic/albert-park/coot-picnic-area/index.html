Coot Picnic Area

Park in Albert Park · Basic info

Coot Picnic Area is a park in Albert Park, in the Port Phillip City Council area, good for all ages. Melbourne Sports & Aquatic Centre/Albert Rd #131 (tram) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 35 m away with a baby change table.
