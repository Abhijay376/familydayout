South Melbourne Beach

Beach in Albert Park · Basic info

South Melbourne Beach is a beach in Albert Park, in the Port Phillip City Council area, good for all ages. Beaconsfield Pde/Victoria Ave #32 (tram) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 103 m away.
