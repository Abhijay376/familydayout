Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Tol Tol, in the Swan Hill area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tol Tol is Toltol Flora and Fauna Reserve, covering about 262 hectares.

Getting around

Tol Tol has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 103 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tol Tol, so most trips start by car. By car from Tol Tol, Swan Hill is about 1.5 hours away and Mildura about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest skate park to Tol Tol is Robinvale Skatepark in Robinvale, about 9 minutes' drive. From Tol Tol, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Pisasale Park in Robinvale (8 min), the playground at Rotary Park in Robinvale (8 min), Rural Life Museum in Robinvale (8 min) and Robinvale Skatepark in Robinvale (9 min).

Best for
