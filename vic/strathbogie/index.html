Strathbogie is in the Strathbogie area, and our data lists 1 playground and 8 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Strathbogie is Seven Creeks Wildlife Reserve (no hunting), covering about 301 hectares. Bald Hill Bushland Reserve and Wondoomarook I60 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Strathbogie include Ruoaks, Goldworthys and Wondoomarook I58 Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds in Strathbogie include Strathbogie Recreation Reserve Playground. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Strathbogie. Picnic areas in Strathbogie include Ruoaks. For camping, there's Goldworthys. The Bridge to Bridge Walking Track passes through Strathbogie.

Getting around

Strathbogie has no train station; Euroa Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Strathbogie, so most trips start by car. By car from Strathbogie, Mansfield is about 55 minutes away, Benalla about 67 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Strathbogie is Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa, about 34 minutes' drive. For a library, Euroa Library in Euroa is about 34 minutes' drive from Strathbogie. For a skate park, Euroa Skate Park in Euroa is about 34 minutes' drive from Strathbogie. For a cinema, Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa is about 34 minutes' drive from Strathbogie. Mansfield is the closest bigger centre to Strathbogie, about 55 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Strathbogie, the nearest places for kids by road are Farmers Arms Hotel Museum in Euroa (34 min), Lions Park Playground in Euroa (33 min), Euroa Skate Park in Euroa (34 min), Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa (34 min), Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa (34 min) and Euroa Library in Euroa (34 min).

Best for
