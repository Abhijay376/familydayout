Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Bellellen, in the Northern Grampians area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bellellen is Reids Bushland Reserve, covering about 5 hectares. Bellellen I69 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bellellen. The closest to Bellellen is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 25 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bellellen, so most trips start by car. By car from Bellellen, Stawell is about 21 minutes away and Ararat about 44 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bellellen is Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest library to Bellellen is Ararat Library in Ararat, about 44 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Bellellen is Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Bellellen is the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell, about 19 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Astor Cinema in Ararat is about 44 minutes' drive from Bellellen. For a zoo or wildlife park, Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell is about 24 minutes' drive from Bellellen. The nearest larger centre is Stawell, about 21 minutes from Bellellen by car, with 6 playgrounds and a pool. From Bellellen, the nearest places for kids by road are the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell (19 min), the playground at Federation Park in Stawell (19 min), the playground at Taylors Gully Park in Stawell (20 min), Railway Station Art and Crafts Gallery in Stawell (21 min), Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans (24 min) and Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bellellen.

Nearby suburbs

Stawell, next to Bellellen, has 6 playgrounds, a pool and a zoo or wildlife park. Lake Fyans, next to Bellellen, has a beach. Black Range also borders Bellellen.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Bellellen, without traffic.
