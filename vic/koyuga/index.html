Koyuga is in the Campaspe area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Koyuga, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Koyuga has no train station; Echuca Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Koyuga, so most trips start by car. By car from Koyuga, Echuca is about 18 minutes away and Shepparton about 56 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Memorial Pool in Rochester is about 33 minutes' drive from Koyuga. The closest library to Koyuga is Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Koyuga is Moama Echuca Community Skate Park in Echuca, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Koyuga is Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca, about 19 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram is about 29 minutes' drive from Koyuga. Echuca is the closest bigger centre to Koyuga, about 18 minutes away by car, and has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Koyuga, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Echuca Lions Park in Echuca (19 min), Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca (19 min), McDonald's PlayPlace in Echuca (20 min), Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca (20 min), The Foundry Arts Space in Echuca (21 min) and Customs House Gallery in Echuca (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Koyuga.

Nearby suburbs

Echuca, next to Koyuga, has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. Strathallan, Nanneella, Kanyapella and Echuca Village also border Koyuga.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Koyuga, without traffic.
