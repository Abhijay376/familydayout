Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Tallangatta Valley, in the Towong area.

Landmarks

Cravensville Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Tallangatta Valley, at about 11 hectares. Tallangatta Creek K11 Streamside Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tallangatta Valley. The closest to Tallangatta Valley is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 47 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tallangatta Valley, so most trips start by car. By car from Tallangatta Valley, Wodonga is about 56 minutes away and Wangaratta about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta is about 22 minutes' drive from Tallangatta Valley. For a library, Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta is about 22 minutes' drive from Tallangatta Valley. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 56 minutes from Tallangatta Valley by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Tallangatta Valley, the nearest places for kids by road are Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta (22 min), Tallangatta Art & Photography in Tallangatta (22 min), Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta (22 min), Granya Museum in Granya (25 min) and the playground at Banksia Park in Tangambalanga (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tallangatta Valley.

Nearby suburbs

Lucyvale, Koetong, Dartmouth and Bullioh also border Tallangatta Valley.
