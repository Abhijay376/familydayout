Playground at Pearl Iris Park

Playground in Sunbury · Basic info

Playground at Pearl Iris Park in Sunbury is a public playground in the Hume City area. The Vineyard Road passes about 150 m away, and Playground at Logan Court Reserve is another option about 180 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.3 km away, and bus 475 stops at Rosenthal Shopping Centre/Elizabeth Dr , a 5-minute walk away.
