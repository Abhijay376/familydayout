Playground at Fraser Court Reserve

Playground in Sunbury · Basic info

Playground at Fraser Court Reserve is a public playground in Sunbury, in the Hume City area. For food, Batman Pizza is about 270 m away, and Playground at Lawson Street Reserve is another option about 180 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 770 m from the playground, and bus 475 stops at Carlson St/Mitchells Lane , a 4-minute walk away.
