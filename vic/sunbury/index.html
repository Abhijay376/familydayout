Sunbury in the Hume area has 43 playgrounds and 72 parks and reserves in our data. Sunbury also has a library, a pool, a cinema and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Sunbury is Holden Flora Reserve, covering about 88 hectares. Emu Bottom Reserve and Mount Holden Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Sunbury include The Nook, Apex Park and Tom's Park. Playgrounds in Sunbury include the playground at Apex Park, the playground at Waugh Park and the playground at Century Park. Our data shows 21 public toilets in Sunbury. In Sunbury you'll also find Reading Cinema, Sunbury Aquatic and Leisure Centre and Sunbury Library. For older kids in Sunbury there's Sunbury Bmx Club. HomeCo and Sunbury Square Shopping Centre are the main shopping centres. For walks and rides, Elizabeth Drive, Vineyard Road and Blind Creek cross Sunbury. Our data lists 20 cafes and family restaurants in Sunbury for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Sunbury has a train station: Sunbury Railway Station (metro: Bendigo - Melbourne via Gisborne, Echuca/Moama - Melbourne via Bendigo or Heathcote and Sunbury Line). Buses on the 475, 479, 481 and 483 routes stop in Sunbury. V/Line coaches stop in Sunbury. By car from Sunbury, Melton is about 24 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 28 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 40 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest beach to Sunbury is Horse Arena in Abbotsford, about 43 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes is about 19 minutes' drive from Sunbury. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Sunbury is Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville, about 35 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 43 playgrounds across Sunbury. Rainy days at the library and cinema in Sunbury. Teens who skate or ride, with Sunbury Bmx Club in Sunbury. Free days on the Elizabeth Drive and in Sunbury's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Gisborne, next to Sunbury, has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Diggers Rest, next to Sunbury, has 11 playgrounds. Next door to Sunbury, Riddells Creek has 4 playgrounds. Gisborne South, Clarkefield and Bulla also border Sunbury.
