Playground on Riddell Road

Playground in Sunbury · Basic info

Playground on Riddell Road is a public playground in Sunbury, in the Hume City area. The Blind Creek passes about 30 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 230 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 190 m away, and buses 487 and 489 stop at Sunbury Recreation Reserve/Riddell Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
