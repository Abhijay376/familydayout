Jack Maher Park

Park in Wodonga · Basic info

Jack Maher Park is a park in Wodonga, in the Wodonga City Council area, good for all ages. 163 De Kerilleau Dr (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 900 m away.
