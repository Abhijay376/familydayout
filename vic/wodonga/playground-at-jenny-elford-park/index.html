Playground at Jenny Elford Park

Playground in Wodonga · Basic info

Playground at Jenny Elford Park is a public playground in Wodonga, in the Wodonga City area. Wodonga Sports & Leisure Centre is about 490 m away, and for food, McDonald's is about 320 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 630 m from the playground, and buses 150 and F stop at Birallee Shops and Leisure Centre/Melrose Dr, a 7-minute walk away.
