Playground at Les Stone Park

Playground in Wodonga · Basic info

Playground at Les Stone Park in Wodonga is a public playground in the Wodonga City area. The House Creek Path passes about 30 m away, and Gino's Pizzas Wodonga is about 460 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 220 m away, and buses 160 and G stop at Kelly Park/Lawrence` St, a 5-minute walk away.
