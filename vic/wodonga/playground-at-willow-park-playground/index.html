Playground at Willow Park

Playground in Wodonga · Basic info

This is a public playground in Wodonga. The House Creek Pathway is about 10 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 390 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 480 m from the playground, and buses 150 and G stop at Flower Pl/Mcfarland Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
