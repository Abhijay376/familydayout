Walter Cottrell Park

Park in Wodonga · Basic info

Walter Cottrell Park is a park in Wodonga, in the Wodonga City Council area, good for all ages. Provincial Business Centre/High St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 520 m away with a baby change table.
