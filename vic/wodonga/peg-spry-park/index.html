Peg Spry Park

Park in Wodonga · Basic info

Peg Spry Park is a park in Wodonga, in the Wodonga City Council area, good for all ages. Rex Ct/De Kerilleau Dr (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1283 m away.
