Sister Willet Park

Park in Wodonga · Basic info

Sister Willet Park is a park in Wodonga, in the Wodonga City Council area, good for all ages. Jack In The Box Creek/Lawrence St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 993 m away.
