Willow Park

Park in Wodonga · Basic info

Willow Park is a park in Wodonga, in the Wodonga City Council area, good for all ages. Murray Valley Private Hospital/Nordsvan Dr (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 302 m away with a baby change table.
