Playground at Felix Grundy Park

Playground in Wodonga · Basic info

This is a public playground in Wodonga. The House Creek Path is about 10 m away, and Playground at Russell Cochrane Park is another option about 600 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 910 m from the playground, and 5 bus routes stop at Centro Wodonga/Smythe St, a 10-minute walk away.
