Wodonga is in the Wodonga area, and our data lists 36 playgrounds and 60 parks and reserves in the suburb. Wodonga also has a library, a pool, a museum or gallery and a skate park.

Landmarks

Wodonga Regional Park is the largest mapped park in Wodonga, at about 1,043 hectares. Castle Creek Conservation Reserve and Belvoir Park are the next largest. Wodonga also has McLean Park, Tooley Park and Willow Park. Playgrounds around Wodonga include the playground at Tooley Park, the playground at Willow Park and the playground at Willow Park. Our data shows 18 public toilets in Wodonga. Places to visit in Wodonga include Art Space Wodonga, WAVES and Upper Murray Regional Library - Wodonga. For older kids in Wodonga there's James Scott Memorial Skate Park. Wodonga Plaza is the local shopping centre. For walks and rides, High Country Rail Trail, House Creek Path and House Creek Pathway cross Wodonga. Our data lists 33 cafes and family restaurants in Wodonga for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Wodonga has a train station: Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line: Albury - Melbourne via Seymour). Buses on the 7, B, C and E routes stop in Wodonga. V/Line coaches stop in Wodonga. By car from Wodonga, Wangaratta is about 55 minutes away and Benalla about 81 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest beach to Wodonga is Finns Beach in Bandiana, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Wodonga is Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana, about 6 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 36 playgrounds across Wodonga. Rainy days at the library and museum or gallery in Wodonga. Teens who skate or ride, with James Scott Memorial Skate Park in Wodonga. Free days on the High Country Rail Trail and in Wodonga's parks.

Nearby suburbs

West Wodonga, next to Wodonga, has 20 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Wodonga, Gateway Island has 11 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. Next door to Wodonga, Bandiana has 6 playgrounds, a beach and a cinema. Leneva, next to Wodonga, has 3 playgrounds. Huon Creek and Castle Creek also border Wodonga.
