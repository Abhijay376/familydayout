Playground on Gordon Street

Playground in Wodonga · Basic info

Playground on Gordon Street in Wodonga is a public playground in the Wodonga City area. The House Creek Pathway is about 350 m away, and Playground at Willow Park is another option about 570 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 320 m from the playground, and bus S stops at Elm St/Gordon St, a 4-minute walk away.
