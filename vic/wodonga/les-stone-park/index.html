Les Stone Park

Park in Wodonga · Basic info

Les Stone Park is a park in Wodonga, in the Wodonga City Council area, good for all ages. Wodonga Secondary College/69 Woodland St (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 350 m away with a baby change table.

Questions parents ask

Where is Les Stone Park?
Les Stone Park is in Wodonga, Victoria.
Is Les Stone Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Les Stone Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 350 m away, with a baby change table.
How do I get to Les Stone Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Wodonga Secondary College/69 Woodland St (routes Albury - Mt Beauty via Baranduda and Tawonga South, Albury - Wodonga - Myrtleford), a 5-minute walk.
