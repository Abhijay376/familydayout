Playground at Bernie Cox Park

Playground in Wodonga · Basic info

Playground at Bernie Cox Park is a public playground in Wodonga, in the Wodonga City area. The Yarralumla & Castle Creek Loop is about 200 m away, and Playground at Veronica Egan Park is another option about 240 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus 150 stops at Clarendon Ave/Yarralumla Dr , a 4-minute walk away.
