Hibiscus Reserve

Playground in Newcomb · Basic info

This is a public playground in Newcomb. Newcomb Library is about 200 m away, and Domino's is about 230 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 380 m away, and buses 30, 32, 56 and 61 stop at Newcomb Central Shopping Centre/Bellarine Hwy , a 5-minute walk away.
