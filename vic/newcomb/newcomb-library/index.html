Newcomb Library

Library in Newcomb · Basic info

Newcomb Library is a library in Newcomb, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Newcomb Central Shopping Centre/Bellarine Hwy (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 119 m away.
