Lancaster Reserve

Playground in Newcomb · Basic info

Lancaster Reserve in Newcomb is a public playground in the Greater Geelong City area. The Bellarine Rail Trail is about 40 m away, and Newcomb Library is about 450 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 480 m from the playground, and buses 30, 32, 56 and 61 stop at Newcomb Secondary College/Bellarine Hwy, a 3-minute walk away.
