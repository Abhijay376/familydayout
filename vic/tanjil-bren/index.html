Tanjil Bren is in the Baw Baw area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Tanjil Bren, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Tanjil Bren. The Tanjil Bren Tramway Walk passes through Tanjil Bren.

Getting around

Tanjil Bren has no train station; Moe Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 40 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tanjil Bren, so most trips start by car. By car from Tanjil Bren, Warragul is about 67 minutes away, Traralgon about 83 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Tanjil Bren, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at LaTrobe and Loch Rivers, Noojee Streamside Reserve in Noojee (33 min), Rawson Swimming Pool in Rawson (55 min), the playground at Erica State Forest in Rawson (56 min) and Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South (48 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Tanjil Bren on the Tanjil Bren Tramway Walk.
