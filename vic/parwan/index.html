Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Parwan in the Moorabool area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it. Parwan does have a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

Places to visit in Parwan include Australian Gliding Museum.

Getting around

There's no train station in Parwan. The closest to Parwan is Bacchus Marsh Railway Station (V/Line), about 3.7 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Parwan, so most trips start by car. By car from Parwan, Bacchus Marsh is about 8 minutes away, Melton about 17 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 52 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bacchus Marsh Public childrens pool in Bacchus Marsh is about 6 minutes' drive from Parwan. The closest library to Parwan is Lerderderg Library in Bacchus Marsh, about 7 minutes' drive. For a beach, Needles Beach in Pentland Hills is about 14 minutes' drive from Parwan. For a skate park, the skate park at Rotary Park in Bacchus Marsh is about 7 minutes' drive from Parwan. For a cinema, Reading Cinemas in Melton West is about 16 minutes' drive from Parwan. The closest indoor play centre to Parwan is Timezone in Hoppers Crossing, about 31 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Fairy Park in Anakie is about 39 minutes' drive from Parwan. The nearest larger centre is Bacchus Marsh, about 8 minutes from Parwan by car, with 6 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Parwan are the playground at Maddingley Park in Maddingley (6 min), Bacchus Marsh Public childrens pool in Bacchus Marsh (6 min), McCrae playground in Maddingley (6 min), Lerderderg Library in Bacchus Marsh (7 min), the skate park at Rotary Park in Bacchus Marsh (7 min) and Paul Saddler Swimland in Darley (9 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Parwan.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Parwan, Bacchus Marsh has 6 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Brookfield, next to Parwan, has 9 playgrounds. Maddingley, next to Parwan, has 8 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Parwan, Eynesbury has 6 playgrounds. Hopetoun Park, Exford, Balliang and Rowsley also border Parwan.
