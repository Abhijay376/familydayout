Riley Reserve

Park in Murrumbeena · Basic info

Riley Reserve is a park in Murrumbeena, in the Glen Eira City Council area, good for all ages. Riley Reserve/Neerim Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 513 m away with a baby change table.
