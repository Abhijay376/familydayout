Playground at Boyd Park

Playground in Murrumbeena · Basic info

Playground at Boyd Park is a public playground in Murrumbeena, in the Glen Eira City area. The Anniversary Trail passes about 20 m away, and McDonald's is about 470 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 770 m away, and bus 624 stops at Springthorpe Gardens/Neerim Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
