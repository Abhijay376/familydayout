Staghorn Flat in the Indigo area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Baranduda Regional Park is the largest mapped park in Staghorn Flat, at about 3,409 hectares. Baranduda Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Staghorn Flat. The closest to Staghorn Flat is Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line), about 17 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 7 stops in Staghorn Flat. By car from Staghorn Flat, Wodonga is about 18 minutes away and Wangaratta about 61 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Staghorn Flat is Edge Aquatics in Baranduda, about 9 minutes' drive. For a library, Upper Murray Regional Library - Wodonga in Wodonga is about 17 minutes' drive from Staghorn Flat. For a beach, Finns Beach in Bandiana is about 23 minutes' drive from Staghorn Flat. For a skate park, Skate Park in Baranduda is about 8 minutes' drive from Staghorn Flat. The closest cinema to Staghorn Flat is Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana, about 13 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 18 minutes from Staghorn Flat by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Staghorn Flat are Skate Park in Baranduda (8 min), Mel Read Park in Baranduda (8 min), Edge Aquatics in Baranduda (9 min), the playground at Baranduda Reserve in Baranduda (9 min), Skate Park in Yackandandah (8 min) and Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana (13 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Staghorn Flat.

Nearby suburbs

Baranduda, next to Staghorn Flat, has 8 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Staghorn Flat, Leneva has 3 playgrounds. Bonegilla, next to Staghorn Flat, has 3 playgrounds. Tangambalanga, next to Staghorn Flat, has 2 playgrounds. Yackandandah, Kiewa and Allans Flat also border Staghorn Flat.
