Eaglehawk North is in the Greater Bendigo area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Eaglehawk North, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Bendigo Bushland Trail crosses Eaglehawk North.

Getting around

Eaglehawk North has no train station; Eaglehawk Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 3.1 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Eaglehawk North, so most trips start by car. By car from Eaglehawk North, Bendigo is about 15 minutes away, Castlemaine about 45 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Eaglehawk North is Peter Krenz Leisure Centre in Eaglehawk, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest library to Eaglehawk North is Bendigo Library in Bendigo, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Eaglehawk North is McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk, about 7 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 16 minutes' drive from Eaglehawk North. The closest indoor play centre to Eaglehawk North is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Eaglehawk North is The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo, about 17 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 15 minutes from Eaglehawk North by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Eaglehawk North are McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk (7 min), the playground at Canterbury Park in Eaglehawk (7 min), Eaglehawk Playspace in Eaglehawk (7 min), Peter Krenz Leisure Centre in Eaglehawk (8 min), the bmx track on Victoria Street in Eaglehawk (9 min) and Sloan Swim School in California Gully (9 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Eaglehawk North on the Bendigo Bushland Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Eaglehawk North, Eaglehawk has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park.
