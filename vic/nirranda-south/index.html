Nirranda South is in the Moyne area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Nirranda South, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids. Nirranda South does have a beach.

Landmarks

In Nirranda South you'll also find Radfords Beach.

Getting around

Nirranda South has no train station; Sherwood Park Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nirranda South, so most trips start by car. By car from Nirranda South, Warrnambool is about 35 minutes away and Colac about 78 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon is about 24 minutes' drive from Nirranda South. For a library, Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool is about 36 minutes' drive from Nirranda South. For a skate park, the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon is about 23 minutes' drive from Nirranda South. For a cinema, Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool is about 35 minutes' drive from Nirranda South. The closest indoor play centre to Nirranda South is The Bowl in Warrnambool, about 36 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee is about 28 minutes' drive from Nirranda South. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Nirranda South, about 35 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Nirranda South, the nearest places for kids by road are Flaxmans Hill Beach in Nirranda (15 min), the playground at Peterborough Coastal Reserve (COM) in Peterborough (10 min), the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (23 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (24 min), Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell (21 min) and Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (28 min).

Best for

Beach days at Radfords Beach.

Nearby suburbs

Peterborough, next to Nirranda South, has 1 playground and a beach. Nirranda, next to Nirranda South, has a beach.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Nirranda South, without traffic.
