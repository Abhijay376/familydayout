Cobram East is in the Moira area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Cobram East has 6 beaches.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Cobram East is Boosey H42 Bushland Reserve, covering about 17 hectares. In Cobram East you'll also find Backhouse Beach and Cobrawonga Beach No 1.

Getting around

Cobram East has no train station; Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 54 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cobram East, so most trips start by car. By car from Cobram East, Shepparton is about 53 minutes away and Benalla about 63 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah is about 39 minutes' drive from Cobram East. The closest library to Cobram East is Barooga Library in Cobram, about 11 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Federation Park in Cobram is about 8 minutes' drive from Cobram East. The closest cinema to Cobram East is Cobram Cinema in Cobram, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Cobram East is Tunzafun in Yarrawonga, about 26 minutes' drive. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Cobram East, about 53 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Cobram East are Fosters Beach in Cobram (9 min), Cobram Cinema in Cobram (8 min), the playground at Federation Park in Cobram (8 min), the skate park at Federation Park in Cobram (8 min), the playground at Cobram Regional Park in Cobram (10 min) and Barooga Library in Cobram (11 min).

Best for

Beach days at Backhouse Beach and Cobrawonga Beach No. 1.

Nearby suburbs

Cobram, next to Cobram East, has 4 playgrounds, a library and a beach. Katamatite, next to Cobram East, has 2 playgrounds. Boosey also borders Cobram East.
