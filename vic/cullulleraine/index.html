Cullulleraine is in the Mildura area, and our data lists 1 playground in the suburb.

Landmarks

Our data shows 5 public toilets in Cullulleraine. The Goonegul Parish Trail passes through Cullulleraine.

Getting around

Cullulleraine has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 216 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cullulleraine, so most trips start by car. By car from Cullulleraine, Mildura is about 45 minutes away and Swan Hill about 3.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Cullulleraine is Mildura Mobile Library stops here in Werrimull, about 11 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Mildura, about 45 minutes from Cullulleraine by car, with 26 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Cullulleraine are Mildura Mobile Library stops here in Werrimull (11 min), Millewa Pioneer Park in Meringur (27 min) and Merbein Library in Merbein (41 min).

Best for

Free days on the Goonegul Parish Trail and in Cullulleraine's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Cullulleraine, Wargan has 6 playgrounds and a library. Werrimull, next to Cullulleraine, has a library.
