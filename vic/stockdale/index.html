Stockdale in the Wellington area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Bow - Worrung State Forest is the largest mapped park in Stockdale, at about 1,830 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Stockdale. The closest to Stockdale is Stratford Railway Station (V/Line), about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Stockdale, so most trips start by car. By car from Stockdale, Sale is about 32 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 43 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford is about 18 minutes' drive from Stockdale. The closest library to Stockdale is Sale Library in Sale, about 33 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Stockdale is Skate Park in Stratford, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Stockdale is Former Drive-in site in Sale, about 30 minutes' drive. Sale is the closest bigger centre to Stockdale, about 32 minutes away by car, and has 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Stockdale, the nearest places for kids by road are Anzac Park Playground in Briagolong (23 min), Mountainview Drive Playground in Stratford (17 min), Skate Park in Stratford (19 min), Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford (18 min), Former Drive-in site in Sale (30 min) and Sale Historical Museum in Sale (32 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Stockdale.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Stockdale, Stratford has 8 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Briagolong, next to Stockdale, has 2 playgrounds. Fernbank also borders Stockdale.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Stockdale, without traffic.
