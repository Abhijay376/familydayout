Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Mount Sabine in the Colac Otway area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

The Sabine Falls Track passes through Mount Sabine.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mount Sabine. The closest to Mount Sabine is Birregurra Railway Station (V/Line), about 33 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mount Sabine, so most trips start by car. By car from Mount Sabine, Colac is about 48 minutes away, Torquay about 82 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mount Sabine is Apollo Bay Community Pool in Apollo Bay, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Mount Sabine is White Crest in Wongarra, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Mount Sabine is the skate park at Forrest Common in Forrest, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Mount Sabine is Mechanics Hall in Apollo Bay, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Mount Sabine is Skate Bowl in Apollo Bay, about 23 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 48 minutes from Mount Sabine by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Mount Sabine, the nearest places for kids by road are the skate park at Forrest Common in Forrest (15 min), the playground at Forrest Common in Forrest (15 min), the playground at John H Gardner Reserve in Beech Forest (21 min), Art Reach Studio in Beech Forest (22 min), Old Cable Station Museum in Apollo Bay (19 min) and Skate Bowl in Apollo Bay (23 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Mount Sabine on the Sabine Falls Track.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Mount Sabine, Wongarra has a beach.
