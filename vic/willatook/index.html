Willatook is in the Moyne area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Willatook, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Willatook. The closest to Willatook is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 32 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Willatook, so most trips start by car. By car from Willatook, Warrnambool is about 38 minutes away and Hamilton about 54 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Willatook is Hawkesdale Swimming Pool in Hawkesdale, about 11 minutes' drive. For a library, Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool is about 40 minutes' drive from Willatook. The closest beach to Willatook is Reef Point Beach in Port Fairy, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Willatook is Koroit Youth Space in Koroit, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Willatook is Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Willatook is The Bowl in Warrnambool, about 40 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 38 minutes from Willatook by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Willatook, the nearest places for kids by road are Hawkesdale Swimming Pool in Hawkesdale (11 min), the playground at Kirkstall Recreation Reserve in Kirkstall (17 min), Koroit Youth Space in Koroit (23 min), Reef Point Beach in Port Fairy (25 min), Killarney Beach in Killarney (29 min) and Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy (32 min).
