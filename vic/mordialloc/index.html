Mordialloc in the Kingston area has 11 playgrounds and 13 parks and reserves in our data. Mordialloc also has a library.

Landmarks

Doug Denyer Reserve is the largest mapped park in Mordialloc, at about 9 hectares. George Woods Reserve and Jack Grut Reserve are the next largest. Mordialloc also has Seymour Park, Bradshaw Park and Keefer Reserve. Playgrounds in Mordialloc include the playground at Doug Denyer Reserve, the playground at George Woods Reserve and the playground at George Woods Reserve. Our data shows 10 public toilets in Mordialloc. In Mordialloc you'll also find Kingston Toy Library. For walks and rides, Bay Trail, Longbeach Trail and Mordialloc Creek Trail cross Mordialloc. Our data lists 24 cafes and family restaurants in Mordialloc for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Mordialloc Railway Station (metro: Frankston Line) in Mordialloc. Buses 705, 706, 708, 709, 811, 812 and 903 stop in Mordialloc. By car from Mordialloc, Dandenong is about 14 minutes away, Frankston about 19 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 28 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Mentone Sports and Aquatic Centre in Mentone is about 4 minutes' drive from Mordialloc. For a beach, Mordialloc Beach in Parkdale is about 3 minutes' drive from Mordialloc. The closest skate park to Mordialloc is Mordialloc Skate Park in Aspendale, about 3 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Cheltenham is about 7 minutes' drive from Mordialloc. For an indoor play centre, Småland in Springvale is about 13 minutes' drive from Mordialloc. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Mordialloc is Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale, about 16 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 11 playgrounds across Mordialloc. Rainy days at the library in Mordialloc. Free days on the Bay Trail and in Mordialloc's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Mentone, next to Mordialloc, has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Mordialloc, Dingley Village has 18 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Mordialloc, Parkdale has 7 playgrounds, a library and a beach. Next door to Mordialloc, Aspendale has 10 playgrounds, a beach and a skate park. Braeside and Moorabbin Airport also border Mordialloc.
