Jack Grut Reserve

Park in Mordialloc · Basic info

Jack Grut Reserve is a park in Mordialloc, in the Kingston City Council area, good for all ages. J Grut Reserve/Governor Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 8 m away.
