Playground at George Woods Reserve

Playground in Mordialloc · Basic info

Playground at George Woods Reserve in Mordialloc is a public playground in the Kingston City area. The Longbeach Trail is about 100 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 350 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 130 m from the playground, and buses 705 and 708 stop at Chute St/Park St, a 3-minute walk away.
