White Street Reserve

Park in Mordialloc · Basic info

White Street Reserve is a park in Mordialloc, in the Kingston City Council area, good for all ages. Barkly St/Collocott St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1052 m away.
