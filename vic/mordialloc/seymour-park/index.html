Seymour Park

Park in Mordialloc · Basic info

Seymour Park is a park in Mordialloc, in the Kingston City Council area, good for all ages. Black Knight St/Hall Mark Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 450 m away.
