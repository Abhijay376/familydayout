MAZE

Playground in Sale · Basic info

MAZE is a public playground in Sale, in the Wellington Shire area. Indoor pool is about 570 m away, and Historic Gunpowder Magazine is about 360 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 550 m from the playground, and bus 1 stops at Lex Glover Regional Aquatic Complex/Guthridge Pde, a 9-minute walk away.
