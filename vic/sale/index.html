Landmarks

The biggest green space in Sale is Sale Common Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 325 hectares. Sale Botanic Gardens and Victoria Park are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Sale include Cullinan Park, McMillan Park and Stead Reserve. Playgrounds around Sale include MAZE, Apex Park Playground and Boulevard Playground. Our data shows 13 public toilets in Sale. Places to visit in Sale include Gippsland Art Gallery, Historic Gunpowder Magazine, Former Drive-in site, Sale Cinemas and AquaEnergy. For older kids in Sale there's Sale Skate Park. Gippsland Centre is the local shopping centre. Our data lists 33 cafes and family restaurants in Sale for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Sale Railway Station (V/Line: Bairnsdale - Melbourne via Sale & Traralgon) in Sale. Buses on the 1, 2, 3 and 4 routes stop in Sale. V/Line coaches stop in Sale. By car from Sale, Traralgon is about 45 minutes away, Bairnsdale about 55 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.
