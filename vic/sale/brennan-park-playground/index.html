Brennan Park Playground

Playground in Sale · Basic info

Brennan Park Playground in Sale is a public playground in the Wellington Shire area. Old train signals is about 470 m away, and Sale Rainbow Chinese Restaurant is about 310 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 780 m away, and buses 2, 5 and Sale - Stratford stop at Fitzroy St/Raymond St, a 6-minute walk away.
