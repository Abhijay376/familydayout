Gippsland Art Gallery

Museum or gallery in Sale · Official details

Gippsland Art Gallery is a museum or gallery in Sale, in the Wellington Shire Council area, best for school-age kids. Desailly St/Foster St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 718 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: Mon-Fri 9am - 5:30pm, Weekends 10am - 4pm, Public Holidays 10am-4pm; Mon - Fri 9AM-5:30PM; Monday - Friday 9.00am - 5.30pm.
