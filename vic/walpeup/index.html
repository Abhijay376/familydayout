Walpeup is in the Mildura area, and our data lists 1 playground and 13 parks and reserves in the suburb. On top of that, Walpeup has a library.

Landmarks

Dennying Channel Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Walpeup, at about 443 hectares. Walpeup Flora and Fauna Reserve and Paignie I23 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Walpeup also has Walpeup Wayside Stop, Symes Bushland Reserve and Paignie Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds around Walpeup include the playground at Walpeup Wayside Stop. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Walpeup. In Walpeup you'll also find Walpeup Library.

Getting around

There's no train station in Walpeup. The closest to Walpeup is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 142 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Ouyen - Pinnaroo via Mallee Hwy route stop in Walpeup. V/Line coaches stop in Walpeup. By car from Walpeup, Mildura is about 1.5 hours away and Swan Hill about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Walpeup is Underbool Swimming Pool in Underbool, about 17 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Roxy in Ouyen is about 22 minutes' drive from Walpeup. The closest places for kids by road from Walpeup are the playground at Outen Park in Underbool (17 min), Underbool Swimming Pool in Underbool (17 min), Roxy in Ouyen (22 min) and Ouyen Swimming Pool in Ouyen (22 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the library in Walpeup.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Walpeup, Ouyen has 4 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. Patchewollock, next to Walpeup, has 1 playground. Big Desert also borders Walpeup.
