Getting around

She Oaks has no train station; Corio Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside She Oaks, so most trips start by car. By car from She Oaks, Geelong is about 46 minutes away, Ballarat about 54 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

The closest library to She Oaks is Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest beach to She Oaks is Disability beach in Steiglitz, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to She Oaks is Meredith Skate Park in Meredith, about 16 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Geelong is about 45 minutes' drive from She Oaks. For an indoor play centre, All 4 Kids Play Centre in Geelong West is about 42 minutes' drive from She Oaks. For a zoo or wildlife park, Fairy Park in Anakie is about 33 minutes' drive from She Oaks. The nearest larger centre is Geelong, about 46 minutes from She Oaks by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From She Oaks, the nearest places for kids by road are Maude Tennis Reserve Playground in Maude (15 min), Maude Recreation Reserve Playground in Maude (16 min), Disability beach in Steiglitz (18 min), Meredith Skate Park in Meredith (16 min), Fairy Park in Anakie (33 min) and Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn (24 min).
