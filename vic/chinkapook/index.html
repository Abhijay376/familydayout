Chinkapook in the Swan Hill area has 11 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Lianiduck Flora and Fauna Reserve is the largest mapped park in Chinkapook, at about 307 hectares. Chinkapook Flora and Fauna Reserve and Box Flat Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Chinkapook also has Soaks Bushland Reserve, Eureka Bushland Reserve and Eureka West Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Chinkapook has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 59 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Chinkapook, so most trips start by car. By car from Chinkapook, Swan Hill is about 52 minutes away and Mildura about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Chinkapook is Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang, about 14 minutes' drive. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Chinkapook, about 52 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Chinkapook, the nearest places for kids by road are Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang (14 min), Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake (32 min), the playground at Apex Park in Sea Lake (32 min) and the playground at Nyah Heritage Park in Nyah (39 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Chinkapook.

Nearby suburbs

Manangatang, next to Chinkapook, has 1 playground and a pool. Gerahmin, Cocamba and Chillingollah also border Chinkapook.
