South Dudley is in the Bass Coast area, and our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

South Dudley also has South Dudley Reserve. Playgrounds in South Dudley include the playground at South Dudley Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in South Dudley. For walks and rides, Bass Coast Rail Trail crosses South Dudley.

Getting around

South Dudley has no train station; Stony Point Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 40 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus Dudley stops in South Dudley. By car from South Dudley, Wonthaggi is about 4 minutes away, Leongatha about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to South Dudley is Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Wonthaggi, about 6 minutes' drive. For a library, Wonthaggi Principal Library in Wonthaggi is about 5 minutes' drive from South Dudley. For a beach, Baxters Beach in Wonthaggi is about 10 minutes' drive from South Dudley. For a skate park, Wonthaggi Skate Park in Wonthaggi is about 6 minutes' drive from South Dudley. For a cinema, Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi is about 4 minutes' drive from South Dudley. For a zoo or wildlife park, Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville is about 28 minutes' drive from South Dudley. The nearest larger centre is Wonthaggi, about 4 minutes from South Dudley by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from South Dudley are the playground at Guide Park Reserve in Wonthaggi (3 min), Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi (4 min), Wonthaggi Principal Library in Wonthaggi (5 min), Bass Coast Arts Society in Wonthaggi (5 min), Wonthaggi & District Historical Museum in Wonthaggi (5 min) and Wonthaggi Skate Park in Wonthaggi (6 min).

Best for
