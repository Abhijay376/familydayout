Huntly North is in the Greater Bendigo area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Greater Bendigo National Park is the largest mapped park in Huntly North, at about 17,571 hectares. Huntly Natural Features Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Huntly North has no train station; Huntly Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 7.3 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Huntly North, so most trips start by car. By car from Huntly North, Bendigo is about 26 minutes away, Castlemaine about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, White Hills Pool in White Hills is about 19 minutes' drive from Huntly North. For a library, Bendigo Library in Bendigo is about 25 minutes' drive from Huntly North. The closest skate park to Huntly North is McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Huntly North is Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Huntly North is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 29 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 23 minutes' drive from Huntly North. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Huntly North, about 26 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Huntly North, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Epsom Village Green in Epsom (17 min), the playground at Pennington Reserve in Huntly (17 min), White Hills Pool in White Hills (19 min), Bendigo Joss House Temple in North Bendigo (23 min), The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo (23 min) and McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk (25 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Huntly North.
