Greater Bendigo National Park

Park in Huntly North · Official details

Greater Bendigo National Park is a park in Huntly North, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, good for all ages. Dogs are allowed on a lead.

Questions parents ask

Where is Greater Bendigo National Park?
Greater Bendigo National Park is in Huntly North, Victoria.
Is Greater Bendigo National Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Greater Bendigo National Park?
Yes, on a lead.
