Chatsworth is in the Moyne area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Picnic Lakes Lake Reserve is the largest mapped park in Chatsworth, at about 82 hectares. Lake Peawick-Gorra Lake Reserve and Lake Tim Dunn Lake Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Chatsworth. The closest to Chatsworth is Terang Railway Station (V/Line), about 48 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Chatsworth, so most trips start by car. By car from Chatsworth, Hamilton is about 68 minutes away and Ararat about 72 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Willaura Outdoor Pool in Willaura is about 44 minutes' drive from Chatsworth. For a library, Lake Bolac Library in Lake Bolac is about 28 minutes' drive from Chatsworth. The closest skate park to Chatsworth is Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake, about 40 minutes' drive. From Chatsworth, the nearest places for kids by road are Lake Bolac Library in Lake Bolac (28 min), Market Square Playground in Mortlake (39 min), Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake (39 min), Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake (40 min), the playground at Tea Tree Lake Reserve in Mortlake (40 min) and Willaura Outdoor Pool in Willaura (44 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Chatsworth.

Nearby suburbs

Lake Bolac, next to Chatsworth, has 1 playground and a library. Nareeb, Hexham and Caramut also border Chatsworth.
