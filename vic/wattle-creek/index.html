Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Wattle Creek in the Northern Grampians area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Wattle Creek has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 37 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wattle Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Wattle Creek, Stawell is about 41 minutes away and Ararat about 53 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Wattle Creek is Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell, about 40 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell is about 45 minutes' drive from Wattle Creek. For a zoo or wildlife park, Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell is about 42 minutes' drive from Wattle Creek. Stawell is the closest bigger centre to Wattle Creek, about 41 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Wattle Creek are Community Playground in Landsborough (13 min), Navarre Playground in Navarre (14 min), Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell (40 min), Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell (42 min), Railway Station Art and Crafts Gallery in Stawell (42 min) and the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell (45 min).

Best for
