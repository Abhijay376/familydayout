Muskerry in the Campaspe area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Barnadown Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Muskerry, at about 10 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Muskerry. The closest to Muskerry is Goornong Railway Station (V/Line), about 6.0 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Muskerry, so most trips start by car. By car from Muskerry, Bendigo is about 29 minutes away, Echuca about 59 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Muskerry is Goornong Swimming Pool in Goornong, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest library to Muskerry is Bendigo Library in Bendigo, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Muskerry is McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Muskerry is Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Muskerry is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 32 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 26 minutes' drive from Muskerry. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 29 minutes from Muskerry by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Muskerry are Goornong Swimming Pool in Goornong (10 min), Campaspe Run Rural Discovery Centre in Elmore (21 min), the playground at Pennington Reserve in Huntly (19 min), the playground at Collins Park in Ascot (18 min), White Hills Pool in White Hills (22 min) and Bendigo Joss House Temple in North Bendigo (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Muskerry.

Nearby suburbs

Goornong, next to Muskerry, has a pool. Axedale, next to Muskerry, has 1 playground. Fosterville and Barnadown also border Muskerry.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Muskerry, without traffic.
