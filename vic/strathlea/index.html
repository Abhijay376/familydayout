Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Strathlea in the Mount Alexander area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Strathlea has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Strathlea, so most trips start by car. By car from Strathlea, Castlemaine is about 30 minutes away, Maryborough about 33 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Newstead Swimming Pool in Newstead is about 15 minutes' drive from Strathlea. The closest library to Strathlea is Athenaeum Library in Maldon, about 30 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Bike Jumps in Castlemaine is about 30 minutes' drive from Strathlea. The nearest larger centre is Castlemaine, about 30 minutes from Strathlea by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Strathlea, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Newstead Memorial Park in Newstead (15 min), Newstead Swimming Pool in Newstead (15 min), the playground at Market Reserve in Carisbrook (27 min), Maldon District Museum in Maldon (30 min), Athenaeum Library in Maldon (30 min) and Maldon Vintage Machinery Museum in Maldon (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Strathlea.

Nearby suburbs

Campbelltown also borders Strathlea.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Strathlea, without traffic.
