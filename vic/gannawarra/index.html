Gannawarra in the Gannawarra area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Other parks and reserves in Gannawarra include Canoe Site and Black Swamp. Picnic areas in Gannawarra include Black Swamp and Canoe Site.

Getting around

There's no train station in Gannawarra. The closest to Gannawarra is Kerang Railway Station (V/Line), about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gannawarra, so most trips start by car. By car from Gannawarra, Echuca is about 61 minutes away and Swan Hill about 77 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Cohuna Pool in Cohuna is about 11 minutes' drive from Gannawarra. For a library, Barham Branch Library in Koondrook is about 17 minutes' drive from Gannawarra. The closest skate park to Gannawarra is Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Gannawarra are Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna (10 min), the playground at Cohuna Apex Park in Cohuna (11 min), the playground at C L King Memorial Park in Cohuna (11 min), Cohuna Pool in Cohuna (11 min), Barham Branch Library in Koondrook (17 min) and Barham Skate Park in Koondrook (18 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Gannawarra.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Gannawarra, Koondrook has 2 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Cohuna, next to Gannawarra, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Koroop and Cullen also border Gannawarra.
