Our data lists 3 playgrounds and 10 parks and reserves in Bonegilla, in the Wodonga area. On top of that, Bonegilla has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

Ryans Lagoon Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Bonegilla, at about 151 hectares. Mungabareena Reserve and Eastern Hill Reserve are the next largest. Bonegilla also has Red Gum Park, Beethang I28 Bushland Reserve and Bonegilla Wetland Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds in Bonegilla include Eastern Circuit Playground and the playground at Red Gum Park. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Bonegilla. Places to visit in Bonegilla include The Bonegilla Migrant Experience. Harvey Norman Centre is the local shopping centre. For walks and rides, High Country Rail Trail crosses Bonegilla. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Bonegilla for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bonegilla. The closest to Bonegilla is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 9.3 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bonegilla, so most trips start by car. By car from Bonegilla, Wodonga is about 13 minutes away and Wangaratta about 65 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, WAVES in Wodonga is about 10 minutes' drive from Bonegilla. The closest library to Bonegilla is Upper Murray Regional Library - Wodonga in Wodonga, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Bonegilla is Finns Beach in Bandiana, about 18 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Skate Park in Baranduda is about 11 minutes' drive from Bonegilla. The closest cinema to Bonegilla is Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana, about 8 minutes' drive. Wodonga is the closest bigger centre to Bonegilla, about 13 minutes away by car, and has 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library.

Best for

Younger kids at Bonegilla's 3 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Bonegilla. Free days on the High Country Rail Trail and in Bonegilla's parks.

Next door to Bonegilla, Ebden has 3 playgrounds. Next door to Bonegilla, Tangambalanga has 2 playgrounds. Killara and Staghorn Flat also border Bonegilla.
