Wroxham is in the East Gippsland area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Merragunegin Reference Area is the largest mapped park in Wroxham, at about 689 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wroxham. The closest to Wroxham is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 173 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wroxham, so most trips start by car. By car from Wroxham, Lakes Entrance is about 3.5 hours away and Bairnsdale about 4 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wroxham.
