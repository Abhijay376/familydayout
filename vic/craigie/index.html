Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Craigie in the Central Goldfields area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Talbot Heritage Trail and Cycle Central Goldfields cross Craigie.

Getting around

There's no train station in Craigie. The closest to Craigie is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 6.3 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Craigie, so most trips start by car. By car from Craigie, Maryborough is about 9 minutes away, Castlemaine about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Craigie is Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot, about 16 minutes' drive. For a library, Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough is about 9 minutes' drive from Craigie. For a skate park, Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick is about 42 minutes' drive from Craigie. Maryborough is the closest bigger centre to Craigie, about 9 minutes away by car, and has a library. The closest places for kids by road from Craigie are Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (9 min), the playground at Market Reserve in Carisbrook (9 min), Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground in Talbot (16 min), Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (16 min), Lee Medlyn Bottle Museum in Clunes (24 min) and The Warehouse in Clunes (25 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Craigie on the Talbot Heritage Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Craigie, Maryborough has a library. Next door to Craigie, Carisbrook has 1 playground.
