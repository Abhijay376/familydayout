Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Waggarandall, in the Moira area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Waggarandall is Moodie Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 193 hectares. Wattville Natural Features Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Waggarandall. The closest to Waggarandall is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 39 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Waggarandall, so most trips start by car. By car from Waggarandall, Benalla is about 35 minutes away and Shepparton about 50 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla is about 35 minutes' drive from Waggarandall. The closest library to Waggarandall is Numurkah Library in Numurkah, about 40 minutes' drive. For a beach, Fosters Beach in Cobram is about 32 minutes' drive from Waggarandall. The closest skate park to Waggarandall is the skate park at Federation Park in Cobram, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Waggarandall is Cobram Cinema in Cobram, about 32 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Benalla, about 35 minutes from Waggarandall by car, with 5 playgrounds and a pool. From Waggarandall, the nearest places for kids by road are Katamatite Historical Society Museum in Katamatite (15 min), the playground at E.W Kilgour Reserve in Katamatite (15 min), the playground at C R Wood Park in Katamatite (15 min), Fosters Beach in Cobram (32 min), Backhouse Beach in Cobram East (31 min) and Cobram Cinema in Cobram (32 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Waggarandall.

Nearby suburbs

Dookie, next to Waggarandall, has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Waggarandall, Tungamah has 1 playground. Youanmite, Yabba South, Yabba North and St James also border Waggarandall.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Waggarandall, without traffic.
