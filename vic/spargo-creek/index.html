Our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in Spargo Creek, in the Moorabool area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Spargo Creek is Spargo Creek Education Area, covering about 145 hectares. Playgrounds in Spargo Creek include the playground at Korweinguboora Recreation Reserve. For walks and rides, Werribee River Walk crosses Spargo Creek.

Getting around

There's no train station in Spargo Creek. The closest to Spargo Creek is Ballan Railway Station (V/Line), about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Ballan - Hepburn via Daylesford route stop in Spargo Creek. By car from Spargo Creek, Daylesford is about 16 minutes away, Ballarat about 30 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 75 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Spargo Creek is Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford, about 15 minutes' drive. For a library, Daylesford Library in Daylesford is about 16 minutes' drive from Spargo Creek. For a beach, Lionhead Beach in Pentland Hills is about 27 minutes' drive from Spargo Creek. For a skate park, the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan is about 15 minutes' drive from Spargo Creek. The closest cinema to Spargo Creek is Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central, about 29 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 30 minutes' drive from Spargo Creek. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek is about 20 minutes' drive from Spargo Creek. The nearest larger centre is Daylesford, about 16 minutes from Spargo Creek by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Spargo Creek are Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford (15 min), the playground at Wallace Recreation Reserve in Wallace (15 min), Daylesford Library in Daylesford (16 min), Daylesford Spa Country Railway in Daylesford (18 min), the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan (15 min) and Ballan Swimming Pool in Ballan (16 min).

Best for

Free days on the Werribee River Walk and in Spargo Creek's parks.
