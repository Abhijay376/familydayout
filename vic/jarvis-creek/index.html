Jarvis Creek is in the Towong area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Jarvis Creek Plateau Regional Park is the largest mapped park in Jarvis Creek, at about 2,517 hectares. For walks and rides, Tallangatta - Lake Road Loop crosses Jarvis Creek.

Getting around

There's no train station in Jarvis Creek. The closest to Jarvis Creek is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Jarvis Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Jarvis Creek, Wodonga is about 53 minutes away and Wangaratta about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta is about 20 minutes' drive from Jarvis Creek. The closest library to Jarvis Creek is Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Jarvis Creek is Skate Park in Baranduda, about 45 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 53 minutes from Jarvis Creek by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Jarvis Creek are Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta (19 min), Tallangatta Art & Photography in Tallangatta (19 min), Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta (20 min), the playground at Roy Williams Park in Bellbridge (30 min), Granya Museum in Granya (32 min) and the playground at Banksia Park in Tangambalanga (32 min).

Best for

Free days on the Tallangatta - Lake Road Loop and in Jarvis Creek's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Tallangatta, next to Jarvis Creek, has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Georges Creek and Bethanga also border Jarvis Creek.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Jarvis Creek, without traffic.
