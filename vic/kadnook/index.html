Kadnook in the West Wimmera area has 5 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Kadnook State Forest is the largest mapped park in Kadnook, at about 2,014 hectares. Connewirricoo State Forest and Kadnook I29 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Kadnook also has Kadnook I30 Bushland Reserve and Kadnook I31 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Kadnook has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 139 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kadnook, so most trips start by car. By car from Kadnook, Hamilton is about 79 minutes away and Horsham about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest places for kids by road from Kadnook are West Wimmera Community Association - Red Tail Gallery in Edenhope (18 min), Dachshund Museum & Gallery in Edenhope (18 min), the playground at Lions Park in Edenhope (18 min) and Henley Park Playground in Edenhope (19 min).
