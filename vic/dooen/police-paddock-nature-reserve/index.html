Police Paddock Nature Reserve

Park in Dooen · Basic info

Police Paddock Nature Reserve is a park in Dooen, in the Horsham Rural City Council area, good for all ages. Horsham Primary School - Rasmussen Campus/Albert St (bus) is a 15-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 131 m away.
