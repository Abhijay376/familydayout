Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Wooreen in the South Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wooreen. The closest to Wooreen is Yarragon Railway Station (V/Line), about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wooreen, so most trips start by car. By car from Wooreen, Leongatha is about 12 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 43 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Wooreen is South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest library to Wooreen is Leongatha Library in Leongatha, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Wooreen is The Caves in Inverloch, about 39 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Wooreen is BMX track in Leongatha, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Wooreen is Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha, about 12 minutes' drive. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Wooreen, about 12 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Wooreen, the nearest places for kids by road are BMX track in Leongatha (9 min), Leongatha Skate Park in Leongatha (10 min), South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha (10 min), Splash Pools and Stadium in Leongatha (11 min), Leongatha Library in Leongatha (11 min) and Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha (12 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wooreen.
