Merri Creek Park

Park in Epping · Basic info

Merri Creek Park is a park in Epping, in the Whittlesea City Council area, good for all ages. Barry Rd/Victoria Dr (bus) is a 20-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1066 m away with a baby change table.
