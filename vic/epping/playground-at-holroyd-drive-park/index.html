Playground at Holroyd Drive Park

Playground in Epping · Basic info

Playground at Holroyd Drive Park in Epping is a public playground in the Whittlesea City area. The Darebin Creek Trail passes about 110 m away, and Nando's is about 370 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 840 m away, and bus 556 stops at Grand Pde/Dalton Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
