Goldminers Park

Park in Epping · Basic info

Goldminers Park is a park in Epping, in the Whittlesea City Council area, good for all ages. Mecklenburg Cl/O'Herns Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1259 m away.
