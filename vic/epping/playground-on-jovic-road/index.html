Playground on Jovic Road

Playground in Epping · Basic info

Playground on Jovic Road is a public playground in Epping, in the Whittlesea City area. Timezone is about 420 m away, and Muffin Break is about 40 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus 556 stops at Epping Plaza SC/Cooper St, a 10-minute walk away.
