Playground at Lehmann’s Farm Park

Playground in Epping · Basic info

Playground at Lehmann’s Farm Park is a public playground in Epping, in the Whittlesea City area. The Edgars Creek Trail is about 10 m away, and Playground at Legg Park is another option about 410 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 690 m from the playground, and bus 357 stops at Zinnober Gdns/Edgars Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
