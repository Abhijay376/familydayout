Peppercorn Park Playground

Playground in Epping · Basic info

Peppercorn Park Playground in Epping is a public playground in the Whittlesea City area. The Darebin Creek Trail is about 80 m away, and Darebin Creek Parklands (centre) Playground is another option about 360 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 800 m from the playground, and bus 556 stops at Keith Ave/Greenbrook Dr, a 2-minute walk away.
