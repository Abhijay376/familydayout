Memorial Hall Park

Park in Epping · Basic info

Memorial Hall Park is a park in Epping, in the Whittlesea City Council area, good for all ages. Memorial Ave/High St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 301 m away.
