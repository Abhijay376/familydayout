Darebin Creek Parklands (centre) Playground

Playground in Epping · Basic info

Darebin Creek Parklands (centre) Playground is a public playground in Epping, in the Whittlesea City area. The Darebin Creek Trail is about 90 m away, and Darebin Creek Parklands (north) Playground is another option about 320 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 450 m from the playground, and buses 356, 357, 577 and 901 stop at McDonalds Rd/Davisson St, a 2-minute walk away.
