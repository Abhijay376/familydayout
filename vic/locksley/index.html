Locksley in the Strathbogie area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Locksley Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Locksley, at about 14 hectares. Burnt Creek Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Locksley. The closest to Locksley is Avenel Railway Station (V/Line), about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Locksley, so most trips start by car. By car from Locksley, Seymour is about 27 minutes away, Shepparton about 55 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Nagambie Pool in Nagambie is about 22 minutes' drive from Locksley. The closest library to Locksley is Nagambie Library in Nagambie, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Locksley is Euroa Skate Park in Euroa, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Locksley is Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa, about 21 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 27 minutes from Locksley by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Locksley, the nearest places for kids by road are Longwood Recreation Reserve Playground in Longwood (14 min), the playground at Jubilee Park in Avenel (13 min), Nagambie Pool in Nagambie (22 min), Nagambie Historical Society in Nagambie (23 min), Nagambie Library in Nagambie (24 min) and Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Locksley.

Nearby suburbs

Nagambie, next to Locksley, has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Locksley, Longwood has 1 playground. Next door to Locksley, Avenel has 1 playground.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Locksley, without traffic.
