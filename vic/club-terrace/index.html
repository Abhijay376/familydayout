Club Terrace in the East Gippsland area has 8 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Club Terrace is Bemm, Goolengook, Arte and Errinundra Rivers Heritage River, covering about 5,372 hectares. Lind National Park and Cobon Nature Conservation Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Club Terrace include Ada River, Bemm River Scenic Reserve and St George Plain Flora Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Club Terrace. Picnic areas in Club Terrace include Ada River.

Getting around

There's no train station in Club Terrace. The closest to Club Terrace is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 120 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Club Terrace, so most trips start by car. By car from Club Terrace, Lakes Entrance is about 1.5 hours away and Bairnsdale about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest beach to Club Terrace is Sailors Grave Beach in Cape Conran, about 37 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Club Terrace is the skate park at Mick Baum Park in Cann River, about 23 minutes' drive. From Club Terrace, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Mick Baum Park in Cann River (23 min), the skate park at Mick Baum Park in Cann River (23 min), Sailors Grave Beach in Cape Conran (37 min) and the playground at Luderick Point Memorial Park in Bemm River (38 min).
