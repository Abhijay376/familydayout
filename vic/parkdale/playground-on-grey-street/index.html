Playground on Grey Street

Playground in Parkdale · Basic info

Playground on Grey Street in Parkdale is a public playground in the Kingston City area. For food, Adriano's Pizza & Pasta is about 400 m away, and Playground on Brisbane Terrace is another option about 320 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 850 m from the playground, and buses 811 and 812 stop at Ewen St/Lower Dandenong Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
