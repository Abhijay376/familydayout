Playground on Meribah Court

Playground in Parkdale · Basic info

Playground on Meribah Court in Parkdale is a public playground in the Kingston City area. Mentone Pizza & Pasta is about 210 m away if you need food, and Playground at Southern Road Reserve is another option about 320 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.1 km away, and buses 811 and 812 stop at Ivy St/Lower Dandenong Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
