Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Vinifera in the Swan Hill area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Vinifera has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Vinifera, so most trips start by car. By car from Vinifera, Swan Hill is about 19 minutes away and Echuca about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill is about 18 minutes' drive from Vinifera. For a library, Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill is about 18 minutes' drive from Vinifera. The closest skate park to Vinifera is Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Vinifera is Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill, about 19 minutes' drive. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Vinifera, about 19 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Vinifera, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Nyah Heritage Park in Nyah (4 min), the playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV) in Nyah (5 min), Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South (17 min), the bmx track at Piangil Memorial Park in Piangil (18 min), Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill (18 min) and Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill (18 min).
