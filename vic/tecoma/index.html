Tecoma in the Yarra Ranges area has 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tecoma is Belgrave G185 Bushland Reserve, covering about 2 hectares. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Tecoma. The Ringwood - Belgrave Rail Trail, Country (ngurrak barring | RidgeWalk) and Belgrave Rail Trail all pass through Tecoma. Our data lists 4 cafes and family restaurants in Tecoma for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Tecoma Railway Station (metro: Belgrave Line) in Tecoma. Buses 693 and 699 stop in Tecoma. By car from Tecoma, Ringwood is about 21 minutes away, Dandenong about 23 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 39 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tecoma is Belgrave Heated Outdoor Swimming Pool in Belgrave, about 4 minutes' drive. For a library, Belgrave Library in Belgrave is about 2 minutes' drive from Tecoma. For a beach, Edithvale Beach in Edithvale is about 34 minutes' drive from Tecoma. The closest skate park to Tecoma is Upwey Skate Park in Upwey, about 3 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Tecoma is Cameo Belgrave in Belgrave, about 2 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Tecoma is Little Rascals Play Centre in Ferntree Gully, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Tecoma is Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale, about 28 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Ringwood, about 21 minutes from Tecoma by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Tecoma, the nearest places for kids by road are Belgrave Library in Belgrave (2 min), Cameo Belgrave in Belgrave (2 min), Upwey Skate Park in Upwey (3 min), Upwey / Belgrave RSL in Upwey (3 min), Wright Avenue Playground in Upwey (3 min) and the playground at Borthwick Park Reserve in Belgrave (4 min).

Best for

Free days on the Ringwood - Belgrave Rail Trail and in Tecoma's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Tecoma, Belgrave has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Lysterfield, next to Tecoma, has 8 playgrounds. Next door to Tecoma, Upwey has 7 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Tecoma, Belgrave Heights has 2 playgrounds. Sherbrooke and Ferny Creek also border Tecoma.
