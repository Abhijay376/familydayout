Jam Factory, South Yarra

Shopping centre in South Yarra · Basic info

Jam Factory is a shopping centre in South Yarra, in the Stonnington City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Arthur St/Chapel St #49 (tram) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 314 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: opening_hours: Mo-Su 10:00-00:00.
