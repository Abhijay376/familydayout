Fawkner Park Toddler Playspace

Playground in South Yarra · Official details

Fawkner Park Toddler Playspace in South Yarra is a public playground in the Melbourne City area. The St Kilda Road passes about 350 m away, and Bistro Gitan is about 230 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 140 m away, and tram 58 stops at Fawkner Park/Toorak Rd #123, a 5-minute walk away.
