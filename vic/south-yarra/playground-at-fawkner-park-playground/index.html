Playground at Fawkner Park

Playground in South Yarra · Official details

Playground at Fawkner Park is a public playground in South Yarra, in the Melbourne City area. The South Yarra to Toorak Bicycle Route passes about 190 m away, and France-Soir is about 370 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 390 m away, and bus 246 stops at Lang St/Punt Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
