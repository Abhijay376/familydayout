Cucinetta, South Yarra

Cafe or restaurant in South Yarra · Basic info

Cucinetta is a italian cafe or restaurant in South Yarra, in the Stonnington City Council area, a place to eat during a day out. South Yarra Railway Station/Toorak Rd #127 (tram) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 334 m away.
