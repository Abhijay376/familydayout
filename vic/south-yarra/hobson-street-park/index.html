Hobson Street Park

Park in South Yarra · Basic info

Hobson Street Park is a park in South Yarra, in the Stonnington City Council area, good for all ages. Hobson St/Malvern Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 277 m away.
