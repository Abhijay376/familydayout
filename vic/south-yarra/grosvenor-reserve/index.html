Grosvenor Reserve

Park in South Yarra · Basic info

Grosvenor Reserve is a park in South Yarra, in the Stonnington City Council area, good for all ages. Cliff St/Chapel St #48 (tram) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 262 m away with a baby change table.
