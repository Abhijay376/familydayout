Como Centre

Shopping centre in South Yarra · Basic info

Como Centre is a shopping centre in South Yarra, in the Stonnington City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Toorak Rd/Chapel St #50 (tram) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 18 m away.
