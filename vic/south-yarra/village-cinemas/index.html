Village Cinemas

Cinema in South Yarra · Basic info

Village Cinemas is a cinema in South Yarra, in the Stonnington City Council area, good for school-age kids and teens. Wilson St/Chapel St #48 (tram) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 18 m away with a baby change table.
