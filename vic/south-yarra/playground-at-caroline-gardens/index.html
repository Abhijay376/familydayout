Playground at Caroline Gardens

Playground in South Yarra · Basic info

Playground at Caroline Gardens in South Yarra is a public playground in the Stonnington City area. The South Yarra to Toorak Bicycle Route is about 310 m away, and Rush Escape Game is about 340 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and tram 58 stops at Punt Rd/Toorak Rd #125, a 2-minute walk away.
