Fawkner Park

Park in South Yarra · Official details

Fawkner Park is a park in South Yarra, in the Melbourne City Council area, good for all ages. Pasley St South/Punt Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 332 m away.
