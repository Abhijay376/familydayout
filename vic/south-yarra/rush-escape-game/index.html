Rush Escape Game

Indoor play in South Yarra · Official details

Rush Escape Game is a indoor play in South Yarra, in the Stonnington City Council area, best for younger kids (check the venue's age and height rules). South Yarra Railway Station/Toorak Rd #127 (tram) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 191 m away. Opening hours: Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri 10:00-22:00; Sat, Sun 10:00-23:00.
