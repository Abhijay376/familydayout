Thomas Oval, South Yarra

Park in South Yarra · Basic info

Thomas Oval is a park in South Yarra, in the Stonnington City Council area, good for all ages. Como Park/Alexandra Ave (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 490 m away.
