Smeaton in the Hepburn area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Smeaton is Andersons Mill, Smeaton Historic Reserve, covering about 5 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Smeaton.

Getting around

There's no train station in Smeaton. The closest to Smeaton is Creswick Railway Station (V/Line), about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Smeaton. By car from Smeaton, Daylesford is about 25 minutes away, Ballarat about 33 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Infants in Creswick is about 14 minutes' drive from Smeaton. The closest library to Smeaton is Creswick Library in Creswick, about 15 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick is about 15 minutes' drive from Smeaton. For a cinema, Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central is about 33 minutes' drive from Smeaton. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 36 minutes' drive from Smeaton. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek is about 32 minutes' drive from Smeaton. Daylesford is the closest bigger centre to Smeaton, about 25 minutes away by car, and has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Smeaton, the nearest places for kids by road are Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick (15 min), Infants in Creswick (14 min), (Diving tower, restricted) in Creswick (14 min), Creswick Library in Creswick (15 min), Skate Park in Creswick (15 min) and Apex Park Playground in Creswick (15 min).
