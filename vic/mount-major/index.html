Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Mount Major in the Greater Shepparton area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mount Major. The closest to Mount Major is Violet Town Railway Station (V/Line), about 30 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mount Major, so most trips start by car. By car from Mount Major, Benalla is about 35 minutes away, Shepparton about 35 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Aquamoves in Shepparton is about 36 minutes' drive from Mount Major. The closest library to Mount Major is Shepparton Library in Shepparton, about 36 minutes' drive. For a beach, Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton is about 38 minutes' drive from Mount Major. For a skate park, the skate park at Skate Park in Shepparton is about 36 minutes' drive from Mount Major. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 35 minutes' drive from Mount Major. The closest indoor play centre to Mount Major is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Mount Major is Kidstown in Mooroopna, about 39 minutes' drive. Benalla is the closest bigger centre to Mount Major, about 35 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds and a pool. From Mount Major, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at CWA Gardens in Dookie (12 min), the playground at Dookie Recreation Reserve in Dookie (14 min), Village Cinemas in Shepparton (35 min), StarBowl in Shepparton (35 min), Shepparton Heritage Centre in Shepparton (36 min) and the skate park at Skate Park in Shepparton (36 min).
