Mount Cole in the Ararat area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Mount Cole State Forest is the largest mapped park in Mount Cole, at about 10,201 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Mount Cole include Chinamans Campground and Victoria Mill Scenic Reserve. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Mount Cole. Picnic areas in Mount Cole include Chinamans Campground and Victoria Mill Scenic Reserve. For walks and rides, Borella Walk, Indistinct and Chinaman to Reservoir cross Mount Cole.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mount Cole. The closest to Mount Cole is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mount Cole, so most trips start by car. By car from Mount Cole, Ararat is about 41 minutes away, Stawell about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

The closest pool or water play area to Mount Cole is Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat, about 41 minutes' drive. For a library, Learning Resource Centre in Ararat is about 44 minutes' drive from Mount Cole. The closest skate park to Mount Cole is David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat, about 42 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Astor Cinema in Ararat is about 41 minutes' drive from Mount Cole. The nearest larger centre is Ararat, about 41 minutes from Mount Cole by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Mount Cole, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Smith Street Reserve in Ararat (39 min), David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat (42 min), Ararat Gallery TAMA in Ararat (41 min), Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat (41 min), Langi Morgala Museum in Ararat (41 min) and Learning Resource Centre in Ararat (44 min).
