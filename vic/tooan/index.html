Our data lists 6 parks and reserves in Tooan, in the Horsham area.

Landmarks

Jilpanger Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Tooan, at about 12,251 hectares. Jilpanger Reference Area and Bow Lake Wildlife Reserve (hunting) are the next largest. Tooan also has Tooan Bushland Reserve, St Marys Lake Lake Reserve and Heard Lake Wildlife Reserve (hunting).

Getting around

Tooan has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 118 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tooan, so most trips start by car. By car from Tooan, Horsham is about 41 minutes away and Stawell about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tooan is Goroke Swimming Centre in Goroke, about 35 minutes' drive. For a library, West Wimmera Shire Goroke Library in Goroke is about 35 minutes' drive from Tooan. The closest skate park to Tooan is the skate park at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Tooan is Centre Cinemas in Horsham, about 40 minutes' drive. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to Tooan, about 41 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Tooan, the nearest places for kids by road are The Goat Gallery Natimuk in Natimuk (19 min), the playground at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk (19 min), the skate park at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk (19 min), the playground at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk (20 min), Arapiles Climbing Museum in Natimuk (20 min) and West Wimmera Shire Goroke Library in Goroke (35 min).

Best for
