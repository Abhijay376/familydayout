Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Dumbalk North in the South Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Dumbalk North has no train station; Trafalgar Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 33 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dumbalk North, so most trips start by car. By car from Dumbalk North, Leongatha is about 32 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 55 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha is about 31 minutes' drive from Dumbalk North. For a library, Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North is about 25 minutes' drive from Dumbalk North. For a skate park, Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North in Mirboo North is about 26 minutes' drive from Dumbalk North. For a cinema, Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha is about 31 minutes' drive from Dumbalk North. The nearest larger centre is Leongatha, about 32 minutes from Dumbalk North by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Dumbalk North are the playground at Baromi Park in Mirboo North (25 min), Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North in Mirboo North (26 min), Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North (25 min), BMX track in Leongatha (30 min), Leongatha Library in Leongatha (31 min) and Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha (31 min).
