Eldorado Court Playground

Playground in Wyndham Vale · Official details

This is a neighbourhood playground in Wyndham Vale, looked after by Wyndham City Council. Subway is about 390 m away if you need food, and Aloma Avenue Playground is another option about 400 m away. There's a public toilet about 790 m away, and bus 191 stops at Valewood Dr/Honour Ave, a 2-minute walk away.
