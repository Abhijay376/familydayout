Skate park at Presidents Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Wyndham Vale · Basic info

Skate park at Presidents Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Wyndham Vale, in the Wyndham City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. McGrath Rd/Heaths Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 101 m away.
