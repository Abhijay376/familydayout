Mundara Drive Playground

Playground in Wyndham Vale · Official details

This is a neighbourhood playground in Wyndham Vale, looked after by Wyndham City Council. Haines Drive Playground is another option about 380 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.8 km away, and bus 192 stops at Ribblesdale Ave/Haines Dr, a 4-minute walk away.
