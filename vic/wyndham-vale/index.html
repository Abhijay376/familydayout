Wyndham Vale sits just west of Werribee and has 27 playgrounds and 20 parks and reserves in our data. Its standout is Presidents Park, one of the biggest parks in the council area.

Landmarks

Presidents Park covers 69 hectares. It has one of the council's larger district playgrounds and a skate park, so it suits kids of different ages on the same trip. Wyndham Vale Reserve, at about 10 hectares, and Oaktree Avenue Park are the next largest green spaces. The Werribee River Trail and the Lollypop Creek Trail both pass through the suburb. Wyndham Vale Square Shopping Centre is the local shopping centre.

Getting around

Wyndham Vale Station on the V/Line Geelong line is about 2.6 kilometres from the suburb centre, with Warrnambool services as well. Werribee Station on the metro Werribee line is about 3.2 kilometres away. Buses 166, 190, 191 and 192 run through the suburb. By car, Werribee is about 5 minutes away, Hoppers Crossing about 9, Point Cook about 18 and the Melbourne CBD about 35, without traffic. Geelong is about 43 minutes.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 27 playgrounds across the suburb. Mixed-age groups at Presidents Park, where younger kids can use the playground while older ones use the skate park. Free days on the Werribee River Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Werribee is only a few minutes away and has the outdoor pool, the library, the gallery and the museum. Manor Lakes has a library branch and a shopping centre. Tarneit has more playgrounds and the Julia Gillard Library.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Wyndham Vale, without traffic.
