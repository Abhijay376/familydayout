Memory Crescent Playground

Playground in Wyndham Vale · Official details

Memory Crescent Playground is a public playground in Wyndham Vale, in the Wyndham City area. Playground on Stanmore Crescent is another option about 380 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.1 km away, and bus 191 stops at Memory Cres/Welcome Pde, a 3-minute walk away.
