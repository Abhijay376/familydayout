Wiltshire Place Park

Park in Wyndham Vale · Basic info

Wiltshire Place Park is a park in Wyndham Vale, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Bolton Rd/Ballan Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 884 m away with a baby change table.
