Playground at Presidents Park

Playground in Wyndham Vale · Official details

Playground at Presidents Park is one of the council's biggest regional playgrounds in Wyndham Vale, in the Wyndham City area. Older kids have a skate park about 50 m away, and Playground at Burgundy Drive Park is another option about 430 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 80 m away, and bus 166 stops at McGrath Rd/Heaths Rd, a 2-minute walk away.

Features
