Playground at Honour Avenue Park

Playground in Wyndham Vale · Official details

Playground at Honour Avenue Park in Wyndham Vale is a neighbourhood playground in the Wyndham City area. The Lollypop Creek Trail passes about 110 m away, and Honour Fish & Chips is about 100 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 320 m away, and bus 191 stops at Intervale Dr/Honour Ave , a 3-minute walk away.
