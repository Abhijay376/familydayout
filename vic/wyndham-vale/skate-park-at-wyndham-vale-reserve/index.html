Skate park at Wyndham Vale Reserve

Skate, BMX or pump track in Wyndham Vale · Basic info

Skate park at Wyndham Vale Reserve is a skate, bmx or pump track in Wyndham Vale, in the Wyndham City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Wyndham Vale Reserve/Honour Ave (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 579 m away.
