Ventnor Beach Road Playground

Playground in Ventnor · Basic info

Ventnor Beach Road Playground in Ventnor is a public playground in the Bass Coast Shire area. Ventnor Beach is about 150 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus Cowes - Anderson - Wonthaggi stops at Ventnor Beach Rd/Harris Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
