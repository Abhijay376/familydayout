Kyabram Northern Oval

Park in Kyabram · Basic info

Kyabram Northern Oval is a park in Kyabram, in the Campaspe Shire Council area, good for all ages. Transit Centre/Union St (bus) is a 18-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1286 m away.
