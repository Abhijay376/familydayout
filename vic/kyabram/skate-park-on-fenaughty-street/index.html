Skate park on Fenaughty Street

Skate, BMX or pump track in Kyabram · Basic info

Skate park on Fenaughty Street is a skate, bmx or pump track in Kyabram, in the Campaspe Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Transit Centre/Union St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 139 m away with a baby change table.
