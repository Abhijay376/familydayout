John Pilley Reserve

Playground in Kyabram · Basic info

John Pilley Reserve is a public playground in Kyabram, in the Campaspe Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 400 m away, and Kyabram Fauna Park is about 400 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 210 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 2 minutes from Kyabram.
