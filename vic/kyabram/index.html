Kyabram in the Campaspe area has 8 playgrounds and 9 parks and reserves in our data. Kyabram also has a library and a zoo or wildlife park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Kyabram is Ern Miles Reserve, covering about 11 hectares. Kyabram Northern Oval and Edis Park are the next largest. Kyabram also has Jaycee Park, McCormick Reserve and Poplar Street Park. Playgrounds in Kyabram include John Pilley Reserve, the playground at Edis Park and the playground at Jaycee Park. Our data shows 7 public toilets in Kyabram. In Kyabram you'll also find Kyabram Fauna Park and Kyabram Library And Community Hub. Our data lists 3 cafes and family restaurants in Kyabram for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Kyabram has no train station; Mooroopna Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 30 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Bendigo Station - Shepparton Station via Kyabram route stop in Kyabram. V/Line coaches stop in Kyabram. By car from Kyabram, Echuca is about 33 minutes away, Shepparton about 41 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kyabram is Community Swimming Pool in Merrigum, about 16 minutes' drive. For a beach, Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton is about 43 minutes' drive from Kyabram. The closest skate park to Kyabram is the skate park at Tatura Memorial Park in Tatura, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Kyabram is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 41 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Kyabram is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 41 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 8 playgrounds across Kyabram. Rainy days at the library in Kyabram.

Nearby suburbs

Merrigum, next to Kyabram, has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Lancaster, Kyvalley, Kyabram South and Girgarre also border Kyabram.
