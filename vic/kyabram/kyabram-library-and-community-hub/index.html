Kyabram Library And Community Hub

Library in Kyabram · Basic info

Kyabram Library And Community Hub is a library in Kyabram, in the Campaspe Shire Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Transit Centre/Union St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 387 m away with a baby change table.
