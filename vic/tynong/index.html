Landmarks

Tynong Recreation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Tynong, at about 8 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Tynong.

Getting around

Trains stop at Tynong Railway Station (V/Line: Bairnsdale - Melbourne via Sale & Traralgon and Traralgon - Melbourne via Morwell, Moe & Pakenham) in Tynong. Buses on the Garfield Station - Traralgon Plaza route stop in Tynong. V/Line coaches stop in Tynong. By car from Tynong, Pakenham is about 15 minutes away, Warragul about 31 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 59 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tynong is Garfield Swimming Pool in Garfield, about 6 minutes' drive. For a library, Pakenham Library in Pakenham is about 14 minutes' drive from Tynong. For a skate park, Skate BMX Park in Garfield is about 5 minutes' drive from Tynong. The closest cinema to Tynong is Village Cinemas in Narre Warren, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Tynong is Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham, about 16 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Wildlife Trail in Tynong North is about 10 minutes' drive from Tynong. Pakenham is the closest bigger centre to Tynong, about 15 minutes away by car, and has 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play. From Tynong, the nearest places for kids by road are Skate BMX Park in Garfield (5 min), Garfield Swimming Pool in Garfield (6 min), Wildlife Trail in Tynong North (10 min), Lazy River in Tynong North (10 min), Gumbuya World in Tynong North (10 min) and Typhoon Island in Tynong North (10 min).
