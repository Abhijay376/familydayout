Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Kolora in the Moyne area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Kolora has no train station; Terang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kolora, so most trips start by car. By car from Kolora, Warrnambool is about 55 minutes away, Colac about 64 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kolora is Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon, about 45 minutes' drive. For a library, Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake is about 15 minutes' drive from Kolora. The closest skate park to Kolora is Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake, about 15 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee is about 42 minutes' drive from Kolora. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Kolora, about 55 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Kolora, the nearest places for kids by road are Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake (15 min), the playground at Tea Tree Lake Reserve in Mortlake (15 min), Market Square Playground in Mortlake (14 min), Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake (15 min), Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (30 min) and Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (42 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Kolora.

Nearby suburbs

Mortlake, next to Kolora, has 2 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Kolora, Terang has 1 playground. Next door to Kolora, Noorat has 1 playground. Glenormiston South, Glenormiston North, Darlington and Bookaar also border Kolora.
