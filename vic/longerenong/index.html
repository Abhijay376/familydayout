Longerenong in the Horsham area has 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Longerenong is Darlot Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 265 hectares. Longerenong Bushland Reserve and Peppertree Park are the next largest. Playgrounds in Longerenong include the playground at Peppertree Park. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Longerenong.

Getting around

There's no train station in Longerenong. The closest to Longerenong is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 84 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Longerenong, so most trips start by car. By car from Longerenong, Horsham is about 15 minutes away and Stawell about 59 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Longerenong is the water play at Rabl Park in Murtoa, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Longerenong is the skate park at Rabl Park in Murtoa, about 14 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Centre Cinemas in Horsham is about 16 minutes' drive from Longerenong. The nearest larger centre is Horsham, about 15 minutes from Longerenong by car, with 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Longerenong are the playground at Jung Recreation Reserve in Murtoa (19 min), the water play at Rabl Park in Murtoa (14 min), the skate park at Rabl Park in Murtoa (14 min), Murtoa Museum Complex in Murtoa (15 min), Velodrome in Horsham (15 min) and Centre Cinemas in Horsham (16 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Longerenong.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Longerenong, Murtoa has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Lubeck, next to Longerenong, has 1 playground. Kewell, Kalkee, Drung and Dooen also border Longerenong.
