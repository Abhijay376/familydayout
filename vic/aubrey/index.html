Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Aubrey, in the Yarriambiack area.

Landmarks

Cat Swamp Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Aubrey, at about 25 hectares.

Getting around

Aubrey has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 131 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Aubrey, so most trips start by car. By car from Aubrey, Horsham is about 53 minutes away and Stawell about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Aubrey is Dimboola Swimming Pool in Dimboola, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest library to Aubrey is Dimboola Library in Dimboola, about 27 minutes' drive. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to Aubrey, about 53 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Aubrey are the playground at Warrackanbeal Boat Ramp in Warracknabeal (11 min), Historical Centre in Warracknabeal (12 min), Lions Playground in Warracknabeal (13 min), Wheatlands Warracknabeal Agricultural Machinery Museum in Warracknabeal (15 min), Dimboola Swimming Pool in Dimboola (27 min) and Dimboola Library in Dimboola (27 min).
