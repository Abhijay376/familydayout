Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Kernot in the Bass Coast area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Kernot. The Gippsland Odyssey Extension - Woolamai to Nyora (Proposed) passes through Kernot.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kernot. The closest to Kernot is Tynong Railway Station (V/Line), about 38 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kernot, so most trips start by car. By car from Kernot, Wonthaggi is about 28 minutes away, Leongatha about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong is about 27 minutes' drive from Kernot. For a library, Poowong library in Poowong is about 27 minutes' drive from Kernot. The closest beach to Kernot is Corinella Beach in Corinella, about 27 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Nyora skate park in Nyora is about 24 minutes' drive from Kernot. For a cinema, Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi is about 26 minutes' drive from Kernot. For a zoo or wildlife park, Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville is about 15 minutes' drive from Kernot. The nearest larger centre is Wonthaggi, about 28 minutes from Kernot by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Kernot, the nearest places for kids by road are Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville (15 min), the playground at Sunnyside Park in Loch (21 min), the playground at Nyora Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Nyora (24 min), Nyora skate park in Nyora (24 min), Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong (27 min) and Poowong library in Poowong (27 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Kernot on the Gippsland Odyssey Extension - Woolamai to Nyora (Proposed).

Nearby suburbs

Grantville, next to Kernot, has 1 playground and a zoo or wildlife park. Almurta also borders Kernot.
