Balmoral in the Southern Grampians area has 1 playground and 6 parks and reserves in our data. Balmoral also has a pool.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Balmoral is Glenelg River, Fulham Streamside Reserve, covering about 827 hectares. Black Swamp, Balmoral Wildlife Reserve (hunting) and Balmoral Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Balmoral also has Seymour Park, Balmoral Swimming Pool Reserve and Longfella Park (Balmoral Nature Conservation Reserve). Playgrounds around Balmoral include Glendinning Street Playground. Our data shows 5 public toilets and 6 off-leash dog parks in Balmoral. Places to visit in Balmoral include Balmoral Swimming Pool.

Getting around

There's no train station in Balmoral. The closest to Balmoral is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 97 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Balmoral, so most trips start by car. By car from Balmoral, Hamilton is about 64 minutes away and Horsham about 72 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Balmoral, the nearest places for kids by road are Harrow Discovery Centre in Harrow (31 min), Bake House Studio in Harrow (31 min), the playground at Cavendish Town Square Memorial Park in Cavendish (42 min) and coleraine skate park in Coleraine (46 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Balmoral.

Nearby suburbs

Telangatuk East, next to Balmoral, has 1 playground. Kanagulk, Harrow and Englefield also border Balmoral.
