Yarraberb is in the Loddon area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Yarraberb, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Yarraberb has no train station; Raywood Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yarraberb, so most trips start by car. By car from Yarraberb, Bendigo is about 33 minutes away, Castlemaine about 58 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood is about 27 minutes' drive from Yarraberb. The closest library to Yarraberb is Bendigo Library in Bendigo, about 32 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk is about 26 minutes' drive from Yarraberb. The closest cinema to Yarraberb is Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo, about 33 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Yarraberb is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 35 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 35 minutes' drive from Yarraberb. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 33 minutes from Yarraberb by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Yarraberb, the nearest places for kids by road are Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood (27 min), Peter Krenz Leisure Centre in Eaglehawk (25 min), Eaglehawk Playspace in Eaglehawk (25 min), Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk (26 min), the playground at Canterbury Park in Eaglehawk (26 min) and McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Yarraberb.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Yarraberb, Raywood has 1 playground and a pool. Auchmore also borders Yarraberb.
