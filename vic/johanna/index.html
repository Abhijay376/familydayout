Johanna is in the Colac Otway area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Johanna Falls Scenic Reserve is the largest mapped park in Johanna, at about 12 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Johanna. For walks and rides, Great Ocean Walk crosses Johanna.

Getting around

There's no train station in Johanna. The closest to Johanna is Colac Railway Station (V/Line), about 49 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Johanna, so most trips start by car. By car from Johanna, Colac is about 72 minutes away and Warrnambool about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest beach to Johanna is Glenaire East in Hordern Vale, about 27 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Mechanics Hall in Apollo Bay is about 44 minutes' drive from Johanna. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Johanna is Wildlife Wonders in Apollo Bay, about 39 minutes' drive. From Johanna, the nearest places for kids by road are Glenaire East in Hordern Vale (27 min), Station Beach in Hordern Vale (27 min), Art Reach Studio in Beech Forest (37 min), the playground at John H Gardner Reserve in Beech Forest (37 min), Wildlife Wonders in Apollo Bay (39 min) and Mechanics Hall in Apollo Bay (44 min).

Best for

Free days on the Great Ocean Walk and in Johanna's parks.
