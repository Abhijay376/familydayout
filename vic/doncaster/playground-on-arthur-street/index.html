Walker Reserve Playground

Playground in Doncaster · Basic info

This is a small local playground in Doncaster, looked after by Manningham City Council. Doncaster Library is about 420 m away, and for food, Straits Cafe is about 390 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 930 m from the playground, and buses 280, 295, 902 and 907 stop at Clay Dr/Doncaster Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
