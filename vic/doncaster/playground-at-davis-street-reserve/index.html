Playground at Davis Street Reserve

Playground in Doncaster · Basic info

This is a small local playground in Doncaster, looked after by Manningham City Council. The Whitehorse EW2-A is about 330 m away, and Playground at Katrina Street Reserve is another option about 230 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 830 m from the playground, and buses 281, 293 and 304 stop at Davis St/Elgar Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
