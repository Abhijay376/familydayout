Playground at Sharon Reserve

Playground in Doncaster · Basic info

Playground at Sharon Reserve is a small local playground in Doncaster, in the Manningham City area. The Church St shimmy passes about 160 m away, and Rajam Restaurant is about 360 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 760 m away, and buses 279, 295 and 318 stop at Murphy Rd/Victoria St, a 5-minute walk away.
