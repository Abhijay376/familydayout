Playground at Katrina Street Reserve

Playground in Doncaster · Basic info

This is one of the council's larger playgrounds in Doncaster, looked after by Manningham City Council. The Whitehorse EW2-A passes about 180 m away, and Playground at Davis Street Reserve is another option about 230 m away. There's a public toilet about 780 m away, and buses 281, 293 and 304 stop at Katrina St/Elgar Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
