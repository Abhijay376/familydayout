Schramms Reserve Playground (North)

Playground in Doncaster · Basic info

Schramms Reserve Playground (North) in Doncaster is a small local playground in the Manningham City area. The Ruffey Lake Circuit is about 300 m away, and Schramms Reserve Playground (South) is another option about 430 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 400 m from the playground, and bus 305 stops at Earl St/George St, a 4-minute walk away.
