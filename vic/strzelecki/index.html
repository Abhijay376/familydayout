Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Strzelecki in the South Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Strzelecki. The closest to Strzelecki is Warragul Railway Station (V/Line), about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Strzelecki, so most trips start by car. By car from Strzelecki, Warragul is about 29 minutes away, Leongatha about 31 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong is about 16 minutes' drive from Strzelecki. For a library, Poowong library in Poowong is about 16 minutes' drive from Strzelecki. For a skate park, Nyora skate park in Nyora is about 25 minutes' drive from Strzelecki. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 27 minutes' drive from Strzelecki. The closest indoor play centre to Strzelecki is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 28 minutes' drive. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Strzelecki, about 29 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Strzelecki are Poowong library in Poowong (16 min), Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong (16 min), the playground at Coleman Park in Korumburra (18 min), Korumburra Outdoor Pool in Korumburra (19 min), the playground at Peterson Street Reserve in Korumburra (19 min) and Coal Creek Community Park & Museum in Korumburra (21 min).
