Our data lists 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in Steels Creek, in the Yarra Ranges area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Steels Creek is Unnamed GPN8228 Private Nature Reserve, covering about 18 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Steels Creek include Burgoyne G132 B.R. For walks and rides, Steel's Creek Track and Fire Access Track cross Steels Creek.

Getting around

There's no train station in Steels Creek. The closest to Steels Creek is Lilydale Railway Station (metro), about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Steels Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Steels Creek, Healesville is about 20 minutes away, Lilydale about 23 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 60 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Steels Creek is Lilydale Outdoor Pool in Lilydale, about 24 minutes' drive. For a library, Yarra Plenty Regional Library in Christmas Hills is about 14 minutes' drive from Steels Creek. For a skate park, the skate park at McKenzie Reserve in Yarra Glen is about 9 minutes' drive from Steels Creek. The closest cinema to Steels Creek is The Memo, Healesville in Healesville, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Steels Creek is GAMR Laser Tag & VR in Chirnside Park, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Steels Creek is Lyre Bird enclosure in Badger Creek, about 24 minutes' drive. Healesville is the closest bigger centre to Steels Creek, about 20 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Steels Creek are the playground at Hubbard Reserve in Yarra Glen (6 min), the skate park at McKenzie Reserve in Yarra Glen (9 min), the playground at McKenzie Reserve in Yarra Glen (9 min), Yarra Plenty Regional Library in Christmas Hills (14 min), House of Bottles Museum & Tearooms in Kinglake (18 min) and TarraWarra Museum of Art in Healesville (15 min).

Best for

Free days on the Steel's Creek Track and in Steels Creek's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Steels Creek, Christmas Hills has a library. Yarra Glen, next to Steels Creek, has 3 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Steels Creek, Kinglake has 1 playground. Dixons Creek, next to Steels Creek, has 1 playground. Castella also borders Steels Creek.
