Bob Pettitt Reserve

Park in Jan Juc · Basic info

Bob Pettitt Reserve is a park in Jan Juc, in the Surf Coast Shire Council area, good for all ages. Jan Juc Park/Domain Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 263 m away.
