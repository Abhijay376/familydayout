Jan Juc Park

Playground in Jan Juc · Basic info

Jan Juc Park is a public playground in Jan Juc, in the Surf Coast Shire area. The Surf Coast Walk is about 390 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 60 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 52 stops at Domain Rd/Sunset Strp, a 2-minute walk away.
