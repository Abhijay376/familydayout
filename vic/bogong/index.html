Bogong in the Alpine area has 1 playground in our data.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Bogong. The Grey Hills Track, Bogong Creek Tramway and Big Hill DH Track all pass through Bogong.

Getting around

Bogong has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 84 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bogong, so most trips start by car. By car from Bogong, Bright is about 46 minutes away and Wodonga about 87 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Bogong is Mt Beauty Library in Mount Beauty, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Bogong is Pebble Beach in Tawonga South, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Bogong is Mount Beauty BMX Bike Track in Mount Beauty, about 17 minutes' drive. Bright is the closest bigger centre to Bogong, about 46 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Bogong, the nearest places for kids by road are Mount Beauty BMX Bike Track in Mount Beauty (17 min), Mt Beauty Library in Mount Beauty (17 min), Falls Creek Museum in Falls Creek (18 min), Pebble Beach in Tawonga South (21 min), Bright River Pool in Bright (46 min) and Bright Art Gallery in Bright (46 min).

Best for

Free days on the Grey Hills Track and in Bogong's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Bogong, Mount Beauty has 1 playground, a library and a skate park. Tawonga South, next to Bogong, has a beach. Hotham Heights, next to Bogong, has 1 playground. Falls Creek, next to Bogong, has 1 playground. Nelse also borders Bogong.
