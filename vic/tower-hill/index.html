Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Tower Hill, in the Moyne area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tower Hill is Tower Hill Wildlife Reserve, covering about 627 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Tower Hill. The Lake's Edge Path and Peak Climb Track all pass through Tower Hill.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tower Hill. The closest to Tower Hill is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 8 stops in Tower Hill. By car from Tower Hill, Warrnambool is about 15 minutes away and Portland about 70 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool is about 17 minutes' drive from Tower Hill. The closest library to Tower Hill is Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Tower Hill is Killarney Beach in Killarney, about 8 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Koroit Youth Space in Koroit is about 8 minutes' drive from Tower Hill. For a cinema, Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy is about 13 minutes' drive from Tower Hill. The closest indoor play centre to Tower Hill is The Bowl in Warrnambool, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Tower Hill is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 31 minutes' drive. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Tower Hill, about 15 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Tower Hill, the nearest places for kids by road are Killarney Beach in Killarney (8 min), Koroit Youth Space in Koroit (8 min), Reef Point Beach in Port Fairy (7 min), W P O'Sullivan Playground in Dennington (9 min), the playground at Victoria Park in Warrnambool (12 min) and Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy (13 min).

Best for

Free days on the Lake's Edge Path and in Tower Hill's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Killarney, next to Tower Hill, has a beach. Koroit, next to Tower Hill, has 1 playground and a skate park.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Tower Hill, without traffic.
