Nangana is in the Cardinia area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Nangana, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Nangana. The closest to Nangana is Belgrave Railway Station (metro), about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nangana, so most trips start by car. By car from Nangana, Lilydale is about 27 minutes away, Healesville about 29 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 62 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Seville Water Play Park in Seville is about 14 minutes' drive from Nangana. The closest library to Nangana is Emerald Library in Emerald, about 13 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Cockatoo Skate park in Cockatoo is about 9 minutes' drive from Nangana. For a cinema, Cameo Belgrave in Belgrave is about 27 minutes' drive from Nangana. The closest indoor play centre to Nangana is Little Rascals Play Centre in Ferntree Gully, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Nangana is Lyre Bird enclosure in Badger Creek, about 25 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Lilydale, about 27 minutes from Nangana by car, with 12 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Nangana are Cockatoo Skate park in Cockatoo (9 min), the playground at Dunstan Road Reserve in Avonsleigh (11 min), Emerald Library in Emerald (13 min), Worrell Reserve Skate Park in Emerald (13 min), Emerald Museum in Emerald (14 min) and the playground at Puffing Billy Park in Emerald (14 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Nangana.

Nearby suburbs

Gembrook, next to Nangana, has 6 playgrounds and a skate park. Cockatoo, next to Nangana, has 3 playgrounds and a skate park.
