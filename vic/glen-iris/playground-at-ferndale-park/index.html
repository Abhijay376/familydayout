Playground at Ferndale Park

Playground in Glen Iris · Basic info

Playground at Ferndale Park is a public playground in Glen Iris, in the Boroondara City area. The Ferndale Trail passes about 30 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 60 m away, and bus 612 stops at Ferndale Rd/Glen Iris Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
