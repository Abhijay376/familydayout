Hill 'n' Dale BMX Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Glen Iris · Basic info

Hill 'n' Dale BMX Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Glen Iris, in the Boroondara City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Hilltop Ave/High St (bus) is a 10-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 784 m away.
