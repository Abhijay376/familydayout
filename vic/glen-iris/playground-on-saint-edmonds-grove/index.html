Playground on Saint Edmonds Grove

Playground in Glen Iris · Basic info

Playground on Saint Edmonds Grove in Glen Iris is a public playground in the Stonnington City area. The Gardiners Creek Trail is about 120 m away, and for food, Little Thai Princess is about 300 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 190 m from the playground, and tram 72 stops at Gardiner Station/Burke Rd #51, a 4-minute walk away.
