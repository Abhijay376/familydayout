Playground at Glenvale Road Playground

Playground in Glen Iris · Basic info

Playground at Glenvale Road Playground is a public playground in Glen Iris, in the Stonnington City area. For food, Glen Iris Fish & Chips is about 480 m away, and Playground at Gardiner Primary Reserve is another option about 470 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 930 m from the playground, and tram 6 stops at Burke Rd/High St #51, a 3-minute walk away.
