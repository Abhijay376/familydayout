Eric Raven Reserve

Park in Glen Iris · Basic info

Eric Raven Reserve is a park in Glen Iris, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Estella St/Albion Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 250 m away.
