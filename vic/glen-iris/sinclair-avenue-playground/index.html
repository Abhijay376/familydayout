Sinclair Avenue Playground

Playground in Glen Iris · Basic info

Sinclair Avenue Playground in Glen Iris is a public playground in the Boroondara City area. The Gardiners Creek Trail is about 50 m away, and for food, Little Thai Princess is about 320 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 350 m from the playground, and tram 72 stops at Harris Ave/Burke Rd #52, a 2-minute walk away.
