Getting around

There's no train station in Darnum. The closest to Darnum is Yarragon Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.4 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Garfield Station - Traralgon Plaza route stop in Darnum. V/Line coaches stop in Darnum. By car from Darnum, Warragul is about 9 minutes away, Pakenham about 42 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 86 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Darnum is Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul, about 7 minutes' drive. For a library, Warragul Library in Warragul is about 9 minutes' drive from Darnum. The closest skate park to Darnum is Warragul Skate Park in Warragul, about 7 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 8 minutes' drive from Darnum. The closest indoor play centre to Darnum is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Darnum is Wildlife Trail in Tynong North, about 28 minutes' drive. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Darnum, about 9 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Darnum, the nearest places for kids by road are Station Gallery in Yarragon (5 min), Yarragon Childrens Playground Reserve in Yarragon (7 min), the playground at Pederson Park in Warragul (9 min), Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul (7 min), Warragul Skate Park in Warragul (7 min) and Warragul Leisure Centre in Warragul (8 min).
