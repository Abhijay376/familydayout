Darlimurla in the South Gippsland area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Unnamed P0301 Private Nature Reserve is the largest mapped park in Darlimurla, at about 128 hectares. The Grand Ridge Rail Trail passes through Darlimurla.

Getting around

Darlimurla has no train station; Trafalgar Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Darlimurla, so most trips start by car. By car from Darlimurla, Leongatha is about 31 minutes away, Traralgon about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Darlimurla is Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest library to Darlimurla is Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North, about 9 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North in Mirboo North is about 9 minutes' drive from Darlimurla. The closest cinema to Darlimurla is Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha, about 31 minutes' drive. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Darlimurla, about 31 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Darlimurla, the nearest places for kids by road are Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North in Mirboo North (9 min), the playground at Baromi Park in Mirboo North (9 min), Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North (9 min), Boolarra BMX track in Boolarra (18 min), the playground at Railway Park in Boolarra (18 min) and Boolarra Old School Museum in Boolarra (19 min).

Best for

Free days on the Grand Ridge Rail Trail and in Darlimurla's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Mirboo North, next to Darlimurla, has 1 playground, a library and a skate park. Next door to Darlimurla, Boolarra has 1 playground and a skate park. Boolarra South also borders Darlimurla.
