Bruthen in the East Gippsland area has 5 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, Bruthen has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bruthen is The Glen Flora Reserve, covering about 11 hectares. Bruthen Recreation Reserve and Bruthen Community Recreation Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Bruthen include Main Street Park and Bruthen Walks Trailhead. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Bruthen. Picnic areas in Bruthen include Bruthen Walks Trailhead. Places to visit in Bruthen include Bruthen Gallery. For walks and rides, East Gippsland Rail Trail, Plane Crash Loop and Berry Farm Loop cross Bruthen.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bruthen. The closest to Bruthen is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 14 stops in Bruthen. By car from Bruthen, Bairnsdale is about 25 minutes away and Lakes Entrance about 36 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale is about 25 minutes' drive from Bruthen. For a library, Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale is about 22 minutes' drive from Bruthen. The closest beach to Bruthen is 90 Mile Beach in Boole Poole, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Bruthen is the skate park at Gilsenan Reserve in Paynesville, about 37 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Bruthen is Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Bruthen is Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow, about 23 minutes' drive. Bairnsdale is the closest bigger centre to Bruthen, about 25 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Bruthen are the playground at Howitt Park in Wy Yung (21 min), Howitt Park in Wy Yung (22 min), Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale (22 min), Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale (23 min), Krowathunkoolong Keeping Place in Bairnsdale (23 min) and Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale (25 min).

Best for
