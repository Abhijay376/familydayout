Main Street Park

Park in Bruthen · Basic info

Main Street Park is a park in Bruthen, in the East Gippsland Shire Council area, good for all ages. General Store/Main St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 137 m away with a baby change table.
