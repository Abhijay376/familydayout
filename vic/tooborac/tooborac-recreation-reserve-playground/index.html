Tooborac Recreation Reserve Playground

Playground in Tooborac · Basic info

Tooborac Recreation Reserve Playground is a public playground in Tooborac, in the Mitchell Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and coaches stop at General Store/Northern Hwy, a 7-minute walk away.
