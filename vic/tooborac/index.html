Tooborac is in the Mitchell area, and our data lists 1 playground and 7 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Tooborac State Forest is the largest mapped park in Tooborac, at about 365 hectares. Tooborac Nature Conservation Reserve and Puckapunyal Streamside Reserve are the next largest. Tooborac also has Baynton I18 Bushland Reserve, Tooborac I98 Bushland Reserve and Tooborac I99 Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds in Tooborac include Tooborac Recreation Reserve Playground. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Tooborac.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tooborac. The closest to Tooborac is Broadford Railway Station (V/Line), about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Tooborac. By car from Tooborac, Seymour is about 29 minutes away, Bendigo about 53 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 89 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore is about 27 minutes' drive from Tooborac. For a library, Heathcote Library in Heathcote is about 13 minutes' drive from Tooborac. The closest skate park to Tooborac is Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote, about 13 minutes' drive. Seymour is the closest bigger centre to Tooborac, about 29 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Tooborac, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Pyalong Recreation Reserve in Pyalong (8 min), Mudwood Studio in Heathcote (13 min), Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote (13 min), Heathcote Library in Heathcote (13 min), Powder Magazine in Heathcote (15 min) and Kilmore Skate Park in Kilmore (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tooborac.

Nearby suburbs

Heathcote, next to Tooborac, has 1 playground, a library and a skate park. Pyalong, next to Tooborac, has 1 playground. Heathcote South, Glenhope East, Glenaroua and Baynton also border Tooborac.
