Our data lists 12 parks and reserves in Harrow, in the West Wimmera area. Beyond the parks, Harrow has 2 museums or galleries.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Harrow is Yallakar State Forest, covering about 5,607 hectares. Edgars State Forest and Harrow State Forest are the next largest. Harrow also has Tommy's Paddock, Harrow Recreation Reserve and Lime Kiln Lake Lake Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Harrow. Places to visit in Harrow include Bake House Studio and Harrow Discovery Centre.

Getting around

Harrow has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 120 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Harrow, so most trips start by car. By car from Harrow, Hamilton is about 70 minutes away and Horsham about 82 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Harrow is Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral, about 30 minutes' drive. From Harrow, the nearest places for kids by road are Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral (30 min), Glendinning Street Playground in Balmoral (30 min) and the playground at Lions Park in Edenhope (37 min).

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Harrow, Balmoral has 1 playground and a pool. Edenhope, next to Harrow, has 2 playgrounds. Kanagulk, Kadnook, Douglas and Culla also border Harrow.
