Deakin University Library

Library in Waurn Ponds · Basic info

Deakin University Library is a library in Waurn Ponds, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Deakin University/Alfred Deakin Dr (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 167 m away.
