Our data lists 6 parks and reserves in Yalmy, in the East Gippsland area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Yalmy is Snowy River National Park, covering about 114,705 hectares. Rodger River and Mountain Creek Natural Catchment Area and Snowy River Wilderness Zone are the next largest. Yalmy also has Zig Zag Creek Reference Area, Mountain Creek Reference Area and Musk Creek Natural Catchment Area. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Yalmy. For walks and rides, Raymond Creek Walking Track crosses Yalmy.

Getting around

There's no train station in Yalmy. The closest to Yalmy is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 77 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yalmy, so most trips start by car. By car from Yalmy, Lakes Entrance is about 2 hours away and Bairnsdale about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Yalmy, the nearest places for kids by road are Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost (69 min), Orbost Library in Orbost (69 min), the playground at Forest Park in Orbost (69 min) and Ewings Marsh in Waygara (89 min).

Best for

Free days on the Raymond Creek Walking Track and in Yalmy's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Orbost, next to Yalmy, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Yalmy, Buchan has 1 playground and a pool. Jarrahmond, Goongerah, Gelantipy and Deddick Valley also border Yalmy.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Yalmy, without traffic.
