Nanneella is in the Campaspe area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Nanneella is Echuca South Bushland Reserve, covering about 28 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Nanneella. The closest to Nanneella is Rochester Railway Station (V/Line), about 9.1 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nanneella, so most trips start by car. By car from Nanneella, Echuca is about 27 minutes away, Shepparton about 64 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Memorial Pool in Rochester is about 16 minutes' drive from Nanneella. The closest library to Nanneella is Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Nanneella is Moama Echuca Community Skate Park in Echuca, about 31 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca is about 28 minutes' drive from Nanneella. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Nanneella is Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram, about 33 minutes' drive. Echuca is the closest bigger centre to Nanneella, about 27 minutes away by car, and has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Nanneella, the nearest places for kids by road are Memorial Pool in Rochester (16 min), the playground at Apex Park in Rochester (17 min), the playground at Echuca Lions Park in Echuca (28 min), Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca (28 min), Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca (29 min) and The Foundry Arts Space in Echuca (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Nanneella.

Nearby suburbs

Rochester, next to Nanneella, has 1 playground and a pool. Fairy Dell, Corop and Bonn also border Nanneella.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Nanneella, without traffic.
