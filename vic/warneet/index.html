Our data lists 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in Warneet, in the Casey area.

Landmarks

Warneet Natural Features Reserve is the largest mapped park in Warneet, at about 4 hectares. Warneet Balaka St Bushland Reserve and Warneet Iluka St Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Playgrounds around Warneet include the playground at Western Port Coastal Reserve (COM). Our data shows 2 public toilets in Warneet.

Getting around

Warneet has no train station; Cranbourne Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 795 stops in Warneet. By car from Warneet, Cranbourne is about 18 minutes away, Frankston about 25 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 58 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, King Swim in Clyde North is about 17 minutes' drive from Warneet. For a library, Cranbourne Library in Cranbourne East is about 17 minutes' drive from Warneet. For a beach, Frankston Beach in Frankston is about 29 minutes' drive from Warneet. For a skate park, Skate Park in Clyde is about 15 minutes' drive from Warneet. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Frankston is about 26 minutes' drive from Warneet. For an indoor play centre, Kidz Shed in Hastings is about 24 minutes' drive from Warneet. For a zoo or wildlife park, Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale is about 13 minutes' drive from Warneet. Cranbourne is the closest bigger centre to Warneet, about 18 minutes away by car, and has 27 playgrounds. From Warneet, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Blind Bight Bushland Reserve in Blind Bight (5 min), Fisherman's Cottage in Tooradin (10 min), Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale (13 min), Skate Park in Clyde (15 min), Criterium Cycling Track in Cranbourne East (16 min) and King Swim in Clyde North (17 min).
