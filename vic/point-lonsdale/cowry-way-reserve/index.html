Cowry Way Reserve

Playground in Point Lonsdale · Basic info

Cowry Way Reserve in Point Lonsdale is a public playground in the Greater Geelong City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus 56 stops at Pico Ave/Fellows Rd, a 10-minute walk away.
