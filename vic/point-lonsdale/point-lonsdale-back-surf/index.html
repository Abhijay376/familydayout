Point Lonsdale (Back) Surf

Beach in Point Lonsdale · Basic info

Point Lonsdale (Back) Surf is a beach in Point Lonsdale, in the Queenscliffe Borough Council area, good for all ages. Point Lonsdale SLSC/Ocean Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 412 m away.
