Boxwood in the Benalla area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Boxwood is Boxwood Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 50 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Boxwood. The closest to Boxwood is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Boxwood, so most trips start by car. By car from Boxwood, Benalla is about 32 minutes away and Shepparton about 42 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla is about 32 minutes' drive from Boxwood. For a library, Shepparton Library in Shepparton is about 42 minutes' drive from Boxwood. For a beach, Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton is about 45 minutes' drive from Boxwood. For a skate park, Pump Track in Benalla is about 33 minutes' drive from Boxwood. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 42 minutes' drive from Boxwood. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 42 minutes' drive from Boxwood. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kidstown in Mooroopna is about 45 minutes' drive from Boxwood. Benalla is the closest bigger centre to Boxwood, about 32 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Boxwood are the playground at CWA Gardens in Dookie (14 min), the playground at Dookie Recreation Reserve in Dookie (17 min), Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (32 min), Benalla Costume and Pioneer Museum in Benalla (32 min), Pump Track in Benalla (33 min) and Benalla Art Gallery in Benalla (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Boxwood.
