Almonds is in the Moira area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Almonds, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Almonds has no train station; Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 30 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Almonds, so most trips start by car. By car from Almonds, Wangaratta is about 31 minutes away and Benalla about 40 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Almonds is Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta, about 30 minutes' drive. For a library, Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga is about 23 minutes' drive from Almonds. The closest beach to Almonds is Forges Beach No 2 in Yarrawonga, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Almonds is the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Almonds is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 31 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Tunzafun in Yarrawonga is about 25 minutes' drive from Almonds. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 31 minutes from Almonds by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Almonds, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Tungamah Lions Park in Tungamah (18 min), the playground at Peechelba Reserve in Peechelba (20 min), Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga (23 min), Tunzafun in Yarrawonga (25 min), Yarrawonga Mulwala Pioneer Museum in Yarrawonga (25 min) and Mulwala Waterslide in Yarrawonga (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Almonds.
