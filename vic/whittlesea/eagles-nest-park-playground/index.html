Eagles Nest Park Playground

Playground in Whittlesea · Basic info

Eagles Nest Park Playground in Whittlesea is a public playground in the Whittlesea City area. Volcano Beach is about 560 m away, and Funfields is about 600 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 920 m away, and buses 382 and 385 stop at Whittlesea Primary School/Plenty Rd , a 5-minute walk away.
