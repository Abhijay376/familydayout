A.F. Walker Recreation Reserve Playground

Playground in Whittlesea · Basic info

This is a public playground in Whittlesea. Older kids have a skate park about 330 m away, and Whittlesea Swimming Centre is about 270 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 270 m from the playground, and bus 384 stops at Forest St/Beech St, a 7-minute walk away.
