Wandown is in the Swan Hill area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Wandown is Wandown Flora and Fauna Reserve, covering about 2,529 hectares.

Getting around

Wandown has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 82 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wandown, so most trips start by car. By car from Wandown, Swan Hill is about 1.5 hours away and Mildura about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang is about 36 minutes' drive from Wandown. The closest skate park to Wandown is Robinvale Skatepark in Robinvale, about 42 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Wandown are the playground at Boundary Bend Memorial Park in Boundary Bend (49 min), the playground at River Murray Reserve in Wemen (35 min), Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang (36 min), Rural Life Museum in Robinvale (41 min) and Robinvale Skatepark in Robinvale (42 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wandown.
