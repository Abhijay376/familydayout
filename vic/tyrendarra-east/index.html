Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Tyrendarra East, in the Moyne area. On top of that, Tyrendarra East has a beach.

Landmarks

Yambuk Coastal Reserve is the largest mapped park in Tyrendarra East, at about 526 hectares. Places to visit in Tyrendarra East include Eumeralla.

Getting around

Tyrendarra East has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 53 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tyrendarra East, so most trips start by car. By car from Tyrendarra East, Portland is about 29 minutes away and Warrnambool about 55 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood is about 25 minutes' drive from Tyrendarra East. The closest library to Tyrendarra East is Heywood Library in Heywood, about 25 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Russell Clark Reserve in Port Fairy is about 28 minutes' drive from Tyrendarra East. The closest cinema to Tyrendarra East is Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy, about 29 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Portland, about 29 minutes from Tyrendarra East by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Tyrendarra East are Fitzroy River Beach in Tyrendarra (14 min), Bay of Whales Gallery in Narrawong (21 min), the playground at Narrawong Recreation Reserve in Narrawong (19 min), Heywood Library in Heywood (25 min), the playground at Apex Park in Heywood (25 min) and Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood (25 min).
