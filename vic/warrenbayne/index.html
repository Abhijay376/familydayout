Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Warrenbayne in the Benalla area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Warrenbayne. The closest to Warrenbayne is Violet Town Railway Station (V/Line), about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Warrenbayne, so most trips start by car. By car from Warrenbayne, Benalla is about 35 minutes away, Mansfield about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Warrenbayne is Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Warrenbayne is Pump Track in Benalla, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Warrenbayne is Swanpool Cinema in Swanpool, about 26 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Benalla, about 35 minutes from Warrenbayne by car, with 5 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Warrenbayne are Swanpool Cinema in Swanpool (26 min), the playground at Waminda Park in Benalla (32 min), The Rocket Playground in Benalla (34 min), Benalla Art Gallery in Benalla (34 min), Benalla Costume and Pioneer Museum in Benalla (35 min) and Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Warrenbayne.

Nearby suburbs

Benalla, next to Warrenbayne, has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Swanpool, next to Warrenbayne, has a cinema. Boho South, Boho and Baddaginnie also border Warrenbayne.
