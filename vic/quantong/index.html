Quantong in the Horsham area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Quantong is Quantong Recreation Reserve, covering about 6 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Quantong. The closest to Quantong is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 102 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Horsham - Naracoorte via Natimuk, Goroke, Edenhope route stop in Quantong. By car from Quantong, Horsham is about 16 minutes away and Stawell about 69 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Quantong is Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham, about 15 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk is about 7 minutes' drive from Quantong. The closest cinema to Quantong is Centre Cinemas in Horsham, about 15 minutes' drive. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to Quantong, about 16 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Quantong, the nearest places for kids by road are Arapiles Climbing Museum in Natimuk (7 min), the playground at Natimuk Showgrounds Recreation Reserve in Natimuk (8 min), the skate park at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk (7 min), the playground at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk (7 min), The Goat Gallery Natimuk in Natimuk (7 min) and Horsham Skate Park in Horsham (14 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Quantong.

Nearby suburbs

Natimuk, next to Quantong, has 4 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Quantong, Vectis has 1 playground.
