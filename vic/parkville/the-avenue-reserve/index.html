The Avenue Reserve

Park in Parkville · Basic info

The Avenue Reserve is a park in Parkville, in the Melbourne City Council area, good for all ages. Brunswick Rd/Sydney Rd #19 (tram) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 180 m away.
