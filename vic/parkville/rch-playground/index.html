RCH playground

Playground in Parkville · Basic info

RCH playground in Parkville is a public playground in the Melbourne City area. The Royal Park to Docklands is about 70 m away, and for food, Shuji Sushi is about 110 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 620 m from the playground, and trams 57, 58 and 59 stop at Royal Childrens Hospital/Flemington Rd #19, a 4-minute walk away.
