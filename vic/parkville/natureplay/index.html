NaturePlay

Playground in Parkville · Official details

NaturePlay is a public playground in Parkville, in the Melbourne City area. The Royal Park to Docklands is about 380 m away, and for food, Three Pandas is about 210 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 130 m from the playground, and bus 959 stops at Gatehouse St/Flemington Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
