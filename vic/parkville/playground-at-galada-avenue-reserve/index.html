Playground at Galada Avenue Reserve

Playground in Parkville · Official details

This is a public playground in Parkville. The Moonee Ponds Creek Trail is about 160 m away, and Elite Swimming is about 450 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 540 m from the playground, and bus 505 stops at Parkville Ave/Garrard St, a 2-minute walk away.
