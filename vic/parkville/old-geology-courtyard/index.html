Old Geology Courtyard

Park in Parkville · Basic info

Old Geology Courtyard is a park in Parkville, in the Melbourne City Council area, good for all ages. Melbourne University/Swanston St #1 (tram) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 298 m away.
