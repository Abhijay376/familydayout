Playground at Ievers Reserve

Playground in Parkville · Official details

Playground at Ievers Reserve in Parkville is a public playground in the Melbourne City area. The Ascot Vale to CBD passes about 330 m away, and Grainger Museum is about 500 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 130 m away, and trams 57, 58 and 59 stop at Murphy St/Flemington Rd #15, a 2-minute walk away.
