McPherson Field

Park in Parkville · Basic info

McPherson Field is a park in Parkville, in the Melbourne City Council area, good for all ages. Royal Park Station/Poplar Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 288 m away.
