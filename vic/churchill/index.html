Churchill in the Latrobe area has 9 playgrounds and 16 parks and reserves in our data. Churchill also has a library, a pool and a skate park.

Landmarks

Mathison Park is the largest mapped park in Churchill, at about 40 hectares. Eel Hole Creek Walk and Gaskin Park South are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Churchill include Amaroo Park, Watson Park and Walkley Park. Playgrounds around Churchill include Walkley Park, Eli Court Playground and Watson Park Playground. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Churchill. In Churchill you'll also find Latrobe Leisure - Churchill and Churchill Library. For older kids in Churchill there's Churchill Skate Park. Hazelwood Village Shopping Centre is the local shopping centre. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Churchill for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Churchill. The closest to Churchill is Morwell Railway Station (V/Line), about 8.3 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 2, 3, 4, 7, 9 and 30 stop in Churchill. By car from Churchill, Traralgon is about 19 minutes away, Warragul about 45 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 10 minutes' drive from Churchill.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 9 playgrounds across Churchill. Rainy days at the library in Churchill. Teens who skate or ride, with Churchill Skate Park in Churchill.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Churchill, Yinnar has 3 playgrounds. Hazelwood North and Hazelwood also border Churchill.
