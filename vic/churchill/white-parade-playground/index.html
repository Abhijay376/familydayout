White Parade Playground

Playground in Churchill · Basic info

White Parade Playground in Churchill is a public playground in the Latrobe City area. Churchill Library is about 490 m away, and Walker Parade Playground is another option about 220 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 630 m from the playground, and bus 30 stops at Churchill Primary School/Howard Ave, a 2-minute walk away.
