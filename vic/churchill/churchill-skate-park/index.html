Churchill Skate Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Churchill · Basic info

Churchill Skate Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Churchill, in the Latrobe City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Churchill SC/Georgina Pl (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 86 m away with a baby change table.
