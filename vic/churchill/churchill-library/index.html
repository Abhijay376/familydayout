Churchill Library

Library in Churchill · Basic info

Churchill Library is a library in Churchill, in the Latrobe City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Churchill SC/Georgina Pl (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 6 m away with a baby change table.
