Walker Parade Playground

Playground in Churchill · Basic info

Walker Parade Playground in Churchill is a public playground in the Latrobe City area. Older kids have a skate park about 310 m away, and Churchill Library is about 320 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 460 m from the playground, and bus 30 stops at Churchill Primary School/Howard Ave, a 3-minute walk away.
