Silvan Reservoir Park

Park in Silvan · Official details

Silvan Reservoir Park is a park in Silvan, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. General Store/Monbulk Rd (bus) is a 14-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 355 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed on a lead. Opening hours: Daily 9am-4pm.
