All Abilities Playground

Playground in Northcote · Basic info

All Abilities Playground is a public playground in Northcote, in the Darebin City area. The Bike: Christmas Street Shimmy passes about 110 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 30 m away. There's a public toilet about 140 m away, and bus 552 stops at Northcote Shopping Plaza/Hunter St, a 3-minute walk away.
