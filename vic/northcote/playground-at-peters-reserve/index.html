Playground at Peters Reserve

Playground in Northcote · Basic info

This is a public playground in Northcote. The South Morang Line Shimmy is about 60 m away, and for food, Inthanon Thai is about 350 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 470 m from the playground, and trains stop at Merri Railway Station, a 5-minute walk away.
