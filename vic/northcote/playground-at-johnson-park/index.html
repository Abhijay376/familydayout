Playground at Johnson Park

Playground in Northcote · Basic info

Playground at Johnson Park is a public playground in Northcote, in the Darebin City area. The Great Eastern Cycleway is about 140 m away, and for food, Ash & Rye is about 230 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 140 m from the playground, and buses 250 and 251 stop at Bastings St/Victoria Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
