Johnson Park

Park in Northcote · Basic info

Johnson Park is a park in Northcote, in the Darebin City Council area, good for all ages. Bastings St/Victoria Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 138 m away and dogs are allowed on a lead.
