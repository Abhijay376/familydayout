Oldis Gardens

Park in Northcote · Basic info

Oldis Gardens is a park in Northcote, in the Darebin City Council area, good for all ages. Northcote Park/Westgarth St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 127 m away.
