McDonell Park

Park in Northcote · Basic info

McDonell Park is a park in Northcote, in the Darebin City Council area, good for all ages. Northcote Aquatic Centre/Victoria Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1039 m away.
