Playground at Batman Park

Playground in Northcote · Basic info

Playground at Batman Park in Northcote is a public playground in the Darebin City area. The St Georges Road Trail passes about 160 m away, and Farina 00 Wood Fired Pizza is about 310 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 150 m away, and bus 508 stops at St Georges Rd/Arthurton Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
