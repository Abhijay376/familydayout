Playground at Rubie Thomson Reserve

Playground in Northcote · Basic info

Playground at Rubie Thomson Reserve in Northcote is a public playground in the Darebin City area. The Great Eastern Cycleway is about 280 m away, and Northcote Aquatic & Recreation Centre is about 390 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 820 m from the playground, and bus 508 stops at Rubie Thomson Reserve/Separation St, a 4-minute walk away.
