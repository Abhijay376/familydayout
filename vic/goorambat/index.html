Goorambat is in the Benalla area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Goorambat is Bungeet H126 Bushland Reserve, covering about 8 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Goorambat.

Getting around

There's no train station in Goorambat. The closest to Goorambat is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Goorambat. By car from Goorambat, Benalla is about 18 minutes away, Wangaratta about 47 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla is about 18 minutes' drive from Goorambat. The closest skate park to Goorambat is Pump Track in Benalla, about 19 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Swanpool Cinema in Swanpool is about 38 minutes' drive from Goorambat. Benalla is the closest bigger centre to Goorambat, about 18 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Goorambat are Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (18 min), Benalla Splash Park in Benalla (18 min), Benalla Costume and Pioneer Museum in Benalla (18 min), Pump Track in Benalla (19 min), Benalla Art Gallery in Benalla (19 min) and The Rocket Playground in Benalla (19 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Goorambat.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Goorambat, Benalla has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Devenish, Chesney Vale, Bungeet West and Broken Creek also border Goorambat.
