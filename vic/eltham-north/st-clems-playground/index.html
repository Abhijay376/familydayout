St Clems Playground

Playground in Eltham North · Basic info

St Clems Playground in Eltham North is a public playground in the Banyule City area. The Banyule LBN9 passes about 90 m away, and Settlers Park Playground is another option about 230 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus 517 stops at Glen Katherine Dr/Weidlich Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
