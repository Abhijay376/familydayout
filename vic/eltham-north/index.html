Eltham North in the Nillumbik area has 16 playgrounds and 26 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Diamond Creek Linear Reserve is the largest mapped park in Eltham North, at about 3 hectares. St Helena Bushland Reserve and Diamond Creek Linear Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Eltham North include Dalvida Reserve, Macdhui Reserve and Macdhui Reserve. Playgrounds in Eltham North include St Clems Playground, Progress Road Playground and Ramptons Road Playground. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Eltham North. For walks and rides, Diamond Creek Trail and Banyule LBN9 cross Eltham North. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Eltham North for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Eltham North. The closest to Eltham North is Eltham Railway Station (metro), about 1.8 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 343, 381, 517 and 580 stop in Eltham North. By car from Eltham North, Box Hill is about 21 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 23 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 32 minutes, without traffic.

For a cinema, Hoyts in Greensborough is about 10 minutes' drive from Eltham North. The closest indoor play centre to Eltham North is Rare Bears Indoor Play Centre in Eltham, about 6 minutes' drive.
