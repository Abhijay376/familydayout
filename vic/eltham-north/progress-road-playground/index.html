Progress Road Playground

Playground in Eltham North · Basic info

Progress Road Playground in Eltham North is a public playground in the Banyule City area. The Banyule LBN9 is about 80 m away, and Playground at Rise Reserve is another option about 480 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 920 m from the playground, and bus 517 stops at Macka Ave/Weidlich Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
