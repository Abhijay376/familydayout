Playground at Calendonia Drive Reserve

Playground in Eltham North · Basic info

Playground at Calendonia Drive Reserve is a public playground in Eltham North, in the Banyule City area. The Banyule LBN9 is about 60 m away, and for food, Canteen is about 110 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 780 m from the playground, and bus 517 stops at Calendonia Dr/Weidlich Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
