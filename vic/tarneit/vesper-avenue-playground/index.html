Vesper Avenue Playground

Playground in Tarneit · Official details

Vesper Avenue Playground is a neighbourhood playground in Tarneit, in the Wyndham City area. Playground at Hummingbird Boulevard Reserve is another option about 520 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus 167 stops at Toucan Rd/Hummingbird Bvd, a 4-minute walk away.
