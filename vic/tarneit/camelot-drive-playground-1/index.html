Camelot Drive Playground 1

Playground in Tarneit · Official details

Camelot Drive Playground 1 is a neighbourhood playground in Tarneit, in the Wyndham City area. Penrose Promenade Playground is another option about 280 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.2 km from the playground, and bus 180 stops at Inverell Pkwy/Crossway Ave, a 2-minute walk away.
