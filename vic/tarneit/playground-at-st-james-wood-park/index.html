Playground at St James Wood Park

Playground in Tarneit · Official details

This is a neighbourhood playground in Tarneit, looked after by Wyndham City Council. The Skeleton Creek Trail passes about 240 m away, and Subway is about 360 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 800 m away, and bus 150 stops at St James Wood Dr/Sayers Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
