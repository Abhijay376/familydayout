Eventide Avenue Park

Park in Tarneit · Basic info

Eventide Avenue Park is a park in Tarneit, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Penrose Prom/Leakes Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1602 m away with a baby change table.
