Playground at Passion Crescent Park

Playground in Tarneit · Basic info

Playground at Passion Crescent Park is a playground in Tarneit, in the Wyndham City Council area, best for toddlers and primary-school kids. Homewood Ent/Leakes Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1485 m away with a baby change table.
