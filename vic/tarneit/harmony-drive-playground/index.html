Harmony Drive Playground

Playground in Tarneit · Official details

Harmony Drive Playground in Tarneit is a neighbourhood playground in the Wyndham City area. The Global Local is about 420 m away if you need food, and Playground at Wootten Road Reserve is another option about 460 m away. There's a public toilet about 640 m away, and buses 181 and 182 stop at Harmony Dr/Hogans Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
