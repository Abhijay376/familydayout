Tarneit skatepark/Ecoville Community Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Tarneit · Basic info

Tarneit skatepark/Ecoville Community Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Tarneit, in the Wyndham City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Tarneit P-9 College/Crossway Ave (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1257 m away.
