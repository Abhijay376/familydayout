The Ruins

Park in Tarneit · Official details

The Ruins is a park in Tarneit, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Riley Tce/Davis Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 854 m away with a baby change table.

Details

Good to know
Public BBQ about 30 m away (Council)

Questions parents ask

Where is The Ruins?
The Ruins is in Tarneit, Victoria.
Is The Ruins free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at The Ruins?
The nearest public toilet is about 854 m away, with a baby change table.
How do I get to The Ruins by public transport?
The closest stop is Riley Tce/Davis Rd (routes 182), a 2-minute walk.
