Playground at Clifton Circuit Park

Playground in Tarneit · Basic info

Playground at Clifton Circuit Park is a playground in Tarneit, in the Wyndham City Council area, best for toddlers and primary-school kids. Bethany Rd/Davis Rd (bus) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 669 m away with a baby change table.
