Edmund Drive Playground

Playground in Tarneit · Official details

This is a neighbourhood playground in Tarneit, looked after by Wyndham City Council. Playground at Moorookyle Avenue Park is another option about 180 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus 167 stops at Edmund Dr/Moorookyle Ave, a 4-minute walk away.
