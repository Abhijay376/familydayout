Riverdale Village Town Centre

Shopping centre in Tarneit · Basic info

Riverdale Village Town Centre is a shopping centre in Tarneit, in the Wyndham City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Riverdale Village Shopping Centre/Davis Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 118 m away with a baby change table.
