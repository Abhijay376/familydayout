Edmund Drive Park

Park in Tarneit · Basic info

Edmund Drive Park is a park in Tarneit, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Edmund Dr/Moorookyle Ave (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1384 m away with a baby change table.
