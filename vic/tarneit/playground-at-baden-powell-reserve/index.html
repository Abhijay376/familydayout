Playground at Baden Powell Reserve

Playground in Tarneit · Basic info

Playground at Baden Powell Reserve is a playground in Tarneit, in the Wyndham City Council area, best for toddlers and primary-school kids. Parklea Way/Baden Powell Dr (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 46 m away.
