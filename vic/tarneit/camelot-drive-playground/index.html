Camelot Drive Playground

Playground in Tarneit · Official details

Camelot Drive Playground is a neighbourhood playground in Tarneit, in the Wyndham City area. For food, Bubba Pizza Tarneit is about 170 m away, and Goddard Street Playground is another option about 310 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus 167 stops at Camelot Dr/Wickford Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
