Alfred Park Playground

Playground in Tarneit · Official details

This is a neighbourhood playground in Tarneit, looked after by Wyndham City Council. The Skeleton Creek Trail passes about 320 m away, and Julia Gillard Library is about 470 m away. There's a public toilet about 770 m away, and buses 152, 154 and 160 stop at Kulin Dr/Leakes Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
