Aeroplane Park

Park in Tarneit · Basic info

Aeroplane Park is a park in Tarneit, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Aspire St/Davis Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 870 m away with a baby change table.
