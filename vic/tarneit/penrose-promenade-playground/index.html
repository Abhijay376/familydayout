Penrose Promenade Playground

Playground in Tarneit · Official details

Penrose Promenade Playground in Tarneit is a neighbourhood playground in the Wyndham City area. For food, Bubba Pizza Tarneit is about 470 m away, and Camelot Drive Playground 1 is another option about 280 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus 180 stops at Clarafield Cres/Sayers Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
