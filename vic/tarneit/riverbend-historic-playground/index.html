Riverbend Historic Playground

Playground in Tarneit · Official details

Riverbend Historic Playground in Tarneit is a neighbourhood playground in the Wyndham City area. The Werribee River Trail passes about 20 m away, and Playground at Nottingham Crescent Park is another option about 510 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus 181 stops at Prominence Bvd/Thames Bvd, a 6-minute walk away.
