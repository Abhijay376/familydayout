Parklea Way Park

Park in Tarneit · Official details

Parklea Way Park is a park in Tarneit, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Parklea Way/Baden Powell Dr (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 308 m away.

Questions parents ask

Where is Parklea Way Park?
Parklea Way Park is in Tarneit, Victoria.
Is Parklea Way Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Parklea Way Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 308 m away.
How do I get to Parklea Way Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Parklea Way/Baden Powell Dr (routes 151), a 2-minute walk.
