Penrose Promenade Park

Park in Tarneit · Official details

Penrose Promenade Park is a park in Tarneit, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Clarafield Cres/Sayers Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away.

Details

Good to know
Public BBQ about 20 m away (Council)

Questions parents ask

Where is Penrose Promenade Park?
Penrose Promenade Park is in Tarneit, Victoria.
Is Penrose Promenade Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
How do I get to Penrose Promenade Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Clarafield Cres/Sayers Rd (routes 180), a 5-minute walk.
