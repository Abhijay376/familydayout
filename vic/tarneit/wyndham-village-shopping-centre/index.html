Wyndham Village Shopping Centre

Shopping centre in Tarneit · Basic info

Wyndham Village Shopping Centre is a shopping centre in Tarneit, in the Wyndham City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Wyndham Village Shopping Centre/Sayers Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1251 m away.
