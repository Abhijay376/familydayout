Murrindindi in the Murrindindi area has 12 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Murrindindi is Black Range State Forest, covering about 22,370 hectares. Murrindindi River Natural Features and Scenic Reserve and Black Range State Forest are the next largest. Murrindindi also has SEC, Ferns and Cascades. Our data shows 12 public toilets in Murrindindi. Picnic areas in Murrindindi include Cascades, SEC and Suspension Bridge. For camping, there's Blackwood and Bull Creek. The Acheron Ridge, Murrindindi River Walk and Mill Track Loop all pass through Murrindindi.

Getting around

There's no train station in Murrindindi. The closest to Murrindindi is Hurstbridge Railway Station (metro), about 44 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Murrindindi, so most trips start by car. By car from Murrindindi, Seymour is about 59 minutes away, Healesville about 64 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a skate park, the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea is about 29 minutes' drive from Murrindindi. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 59 minutes from Murrindindi by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Murrindindi, the nearest places for kids by road are the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea (29 min), TLM Moto Guzzi in Yea (36 min), Bollygum Park in Kinglake (49 min), House of Bottles Museum & Tearooms in Kinglake (49 min), Yarck Recreation Reserve Playground in Yarck (49 min) and the skate park at McKenzie Reserve in Yarra Glen (58 min).

Best for
