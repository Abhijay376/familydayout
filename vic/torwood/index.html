Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Torwood in the Baw Baw area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Torwood has no train station; Warragul Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Torwood, so most trips start by car. By car from Torwood, Warragul is about 19 minutes away, Leongatha about 43 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Torwood is Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul, about 17 minutes' drive. For a library, Warragul Library in Warragul is about 19 minutes' drive from Torwood. The closest skate park to Torwood is Warragul Skate Park in Warragul, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Torwood is Warragul Cinema in Warragul, about 18 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 18 minutes' drive from Torwood. For a zoo or wildlife park, Wildlife Trail in Tynong North is about 38 minutes' drive from Torwood. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Torwood, about 19 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Torwood, the nearest places for kids by road are Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul (17 min), the playground at Burke Street Park in Warragul (18 min), Warragul Skate Park in Warragul (18 min), Warragul Leisure Centre in Warragul (18 min), Burke Street Pump Track in Warragul (18 min) and Warragul Cinema in Warragul (18 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Torwood.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Torwood, Hallora has 1 playground. Ellinbank also borders Torwood.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Torwood, without traffic.
