Turoar is in the Swan Hill area, and our data lists 6 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Turoar is Towan Plains Flora and Fauna Reserve, covering about 1,181 hectares. Ryanby Bushland Reserve and Clarks Tank Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Turoar also has Plants Bushland Reserve, Turoar I142 Bushland Reserve and Turoar I150 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Turoar has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 48 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Turoar, so most trips start by car. By car from Turoar, Swan Hill is about 49 minutes away and Mildura about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang is about 23 minutes' drive from Turoar. The closest skate park to Turoar is the bmx track at Piangil Memorial Park in Piangil, about 40 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 49 minutes from Turoar by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Turoar, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Nyah Heritage Park in Nyah (29 min), Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang (23 min), the playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV) in Nyah (30 min), the bmx track at Piangil Memorial Park in Piangil (40 min) and Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South (44 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Turoar.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Turoar, Manangatang has 1 playground and a pool. Next door to Turoar, Piangil has 2 playgrounds and a skate park. Nyrraby, Chinkapook and Chillingollah also border Turoar.
