Tarrenlea is in the Southern Grampians area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Tarrenlea, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tarrenlea. The closest to Tarrenlea is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 102 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tarrenlea, so most trips start by car. By car from Tarrenlea, Hamilton is about 30 minutes away and Portland about 83 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Merino Swimming Pool in Merino is about 39 minutes' drive from Tarrenlea. The closest library to Tarrenlea is Hamilton Library in Hamilton, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Tarrenlea is coleraine skate park in Coleraine, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Tarrenlea is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 30 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 30 minutes from Tarrenlea by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Tarrenlea are the playground at Coleraine Railway Reserve in Coleraine (16 min), the playground at Peter Francis Points Arboretum in Coleraine (16 min), coleraine skate park in Coleraine (17 min), Big Woolbales & Campe Motor Museum in Hamilton (28 min), Merino Swimming Pool in Merino (39 min) and Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Hamilton (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tarrenlea.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Tarrenlea, Coleraine has 4 playgrounds and a skate park. Morgiana also borders Tarrenlea.
