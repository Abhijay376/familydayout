Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Kotta, in the Campaspe area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Kotta is Little Kotta Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 18 hectares. Dullard Waterhole Bushland Reserve is the next largest. For walks and rides, Lockington – Kotta Rail Trail crosses Kotta.

Getting around

Kotta has no train station; Echuca Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kotta, so most trips start by car. By car from Kotta, Echuca is about 22 minutes away, Bendigo about 66 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kotta is Lockington Pool in Lockington, about 7 minutes' drive. For a library, Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca is about 23 minutes' drive from Kotta. For a skate park, Moama Echuca Community Skate Park in Echuca is about 25 minutes' drive from Kotta. For a cinema, Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca is about 23 minutes' drive from Kotta. Echuca is the closest bigger centre to Kotta, about 22 minutes away by car, and has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Kotta, the nearest places for kids by road are Lockington Pool in Lockington (7 min), Lockington District Swimming Pool in Lockington (7 min), the playground at Echuca Lions Park in Echuca (20 min), Holden Museum in Echuca (22 min), Hopwood Gardens Playground in Echuca (22 min) and Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca (23 min).

Best for

Free days on the Lockington – Kotta Rail Trail and in Kotta's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Lockington, next to Kotta, has a pool. Bamawm Extension and Bamawm also border Kotta.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Kotta, without traffic.
