Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Allestree, in the Glenelg area.

Landmarks

Convincing Ground Historic and Cultural Features Reserve is the largest mapped park in Allestree, at about 20 hectares.

Getting around

Allestree has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 74 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Allestree, so most trips start by car. By car from Allestree, Portland is about 8 minutes away and Hamilton about 65 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Portland Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Portland is about 11 minutes' drive from Allestree. For a library, Heywood Library in Heywood is about 19 minutes' drive from Allestree. For a beach, Whalers Point in Portland is about 9 minutes' drive from Allestree. Portland is the closest bigger centre to Allestree, about 8 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Allestree are the playground at Narrawong Recreation Reserve in Narrawong (6 min), Whalers Point in Portland (9 min), the playground at Mitchell Park in Portland (10 min), Powerhouse Motor Car Museum in Portland (10 min), Bay of Whales Gallery in Narrawong (14 min) and Portland Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Portland (11 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Allestree.
