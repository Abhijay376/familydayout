Our data lists 7 parks and reserves in Leslie Manor, in the Corangamite area.

Landmarks

Lake Terangpom Wildlife Reserve (no hunting) is the largest mapped park in Leslie Manor, at about 220 hectares. Lake Coradgill Lake Reserve and Lake Punpundal Lake Reserve are the next largest. Leslie Manor also has Lake Tatutong Lake Reserve, Lake Bulkil Narra Lake Reserve and Leslie Manor Recreation Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Leslie Manor. The closest to Leslie Manor is Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line), about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Leslie Manor, so most trips start by car. By car from Leslie Manor, Colac is about 59 minutes away, Warrnambool about 83 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Leslie Manor is Lismore Pool in Lismore, about 42 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Leslie Manor is Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown, about 31 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 59 minutes from Leslie Manor by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Leslie Manor, the nearest places for kids by road are Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (31 min), the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve in Camperdown (39 min), the playground at Cressy Picnic Area in Cressy (39 min), Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum (48 min) and Lismore Pool in Lismore (42 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Leslie Manor.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Leslie Manor, Lismore has a pool. Next door to Leslie Manor, Weerite has 1 playground. Kariah, Foxhow, Dreeite and Cundare North also border Leslie Manor.
