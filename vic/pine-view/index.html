Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Pine View in the Gannawarra area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Pine View has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 25 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Pine View, so most trips start by car. By car from Pine View, Swan Hill is about 66 minutes away and Echuca about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Pine View is Quambatook Memorial Pool in Quambatook, about 16 minutes' drive. For a library, Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook is about 16 minutes' drive from Pine View. The closest places for kids by road from Pine View are Quambatook Memorial Pool in Quambatook (16 min), Quambatook Historical Centre in Quambatook (15 min), Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook (16 min), Quambatook Lions Club Playground in Quambatook (16 min), Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (21 min) and Swimming Pool in Kerang (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Pine View.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Pine View, Quambatook has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Budgerum East also borders Pine View.
