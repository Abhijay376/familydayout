Heathcote Junction is in the Mitchell area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Heathcote Junction, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

The Say G'day Trail passes through Heathcote Junction.

Getting around

There's no train station in Heathcote Junction. The closest to Heathcote Junction is Heathcote Junction Railway Station (V/Line), about 200 metres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Heathcote Junction, so most trips start by car. By car from Heathcote Junction, Seymour is about 34 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 55 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Heathcote Junction is Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore, about 11 minutes' drive. For a library, Wallan Library in Wallan is about 10 minutes' drive from Heathcote Junction. The closest skate park to Heathcote Junction is the skate park at Wandong and Heathcote Junction Memorial Park in Wandong, about 2 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Heathcote Junction is Reading Epping Cinema in Epping, about 28 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Timezone in Epping is about 28 minutes' drive from Heathcote Junction. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Heathcote Junction is Funfields in Whittlesea, about 21 minutes' drive. Seymour is the closest bigger centre to Heathcote Junction, about 34 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Heathcote Junction, the nearest places for kids by road are the skate park at Wandong and Heathcote Junction Memorial Park in Wandong (2 min), L B Davern Reserve Playground in Wandong (5 min), the playground at Merri Creek Park in Wallan (9 min), BMX Track in Wallan (9 min), Wallan Library in Wallan (10 min) and Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore (11 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Heathcote Junction on the Say G'day Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Wallan, next to Heathcote Junction, has 13 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Wandong, next to Heathcote Junction, has 1 playground and a skate park.
