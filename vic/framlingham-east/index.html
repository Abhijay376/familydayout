Framlingham East is in the Moyne area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Framlingham East, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Framlingham East. The closest to Framlingham East is Terang Railway Station (V/Line), about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Framlingham East, so most trips start by car. By car from Framlingham East, Warrnambool is about 42 minutes away and Colac about 88 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Framlingham East is Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool, about 45 minutes' drive. The closest library to Framlingham East is Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Framlingham East is Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Framlingham East is Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool, about 42 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, The Bowl in Warrnambool is about 43 minutes' drive from Framlingham East. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Framlingham East is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 38 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 42 minutes from Framlingham East by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Framlingham East are Market Square Playground in Mortlake (23 min), Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake (24 min), Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake (24 min), the playground at Tea Tree Lake Reserve in Mortlake (24 min), Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (38 min) and Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool (42 min).
