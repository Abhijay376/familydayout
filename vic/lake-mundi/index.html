Lake Mundi is in the Glenelg area, and our data lists 11 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Roseneath State Forest is the largest mapped park in Lake Mundi, at about 13,332 hectares. Tooloy-Lake Mundi Wildlife Reserve (hunting) and Argyle State Forest are the next largest. Lake Mundi also has Tooloy State Forest, Tooloy Flora Reserve and Lake Mundi State Forest.

Getting around

Lake Mundi has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 155 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lake Mundi, so most trips start by car. By car from Lake Mundi, Hamilton is about 78 minutes away and Portland about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Casterton Pool in Casterton is about 27 minutes' drive from Lake Mundi. The closest places for kids by road from Lake Mundi are Gorman's art gallery in Casterton (26 min) and Casterton Pool in Casterton (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lake Mundi.
