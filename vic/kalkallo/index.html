Kalkallo is in the Hume area, and our data lists 3 playgrounds and 7 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Kalkallo has a skate park.

Landmarks

Tall Trees Reserve is the largest mapped park in Kalkallo, at about 54 hectares. Kalkallo Recreation Reserve and Klandy Drive Reserve are the next largest. Kalkallo also has Mode Park, Quill Park and Bells Reserve. Playgrounds around Kalkallo include Toyon Playground, the playground at Mode Park and the playground at Bells Reserve. Our data shows 6 public toilets in Kalkallo. For older kids in Kalkallo there's the skate park at Bells Reserve. Kallo Town Centre is the local shopping centre. Our data lists 3 cafes and family restaurants in Kalkallo for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kalkallo. The closest to Kalkallo is Donnybrook Railway Station (V/Line), about 2.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 524 and 525 stop in Kalkallo. By car from Kalkallo, Broadmeadows is about 23 minutes away, Sunshine about 35 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 41 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Splash Aqua Park and Leisure Centre in Craigieburn is about 12 minutes' drive from Kalkallo. The closest library to Kalkallo is Craigieburn Library in Craigieburn, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Kalkallo is Horse Arena in Abbotsford, about 40 minutes' drive. For a cinema, United Cinemas in Craigieburn is about 13 minutes' drive from Kalkallo. For an indoor play centre, Timezone in Craigieburn is about 13 minutes' drive from Kalkallo. For a zoo or wildlife park, Funfields in Whittlesea is about 20 minutes' drive from Kalkallo. Broadmeadows is the closest bigger centre to Kalkallo, about 23 minutes away by car, and has 12 playgrounds, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Kalkallo's 3 local playgrounds. Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at Bells Reserve in Kalkallo.

Nearby suburbs

Craigieburn, next to Kalkallo, has 54 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Donnybrook, next to Kalkallo, has 5 playgrounds. Mickleham, next to Kalkallo, has 4 playgrounds. Next door to Kalkallo, Beveridge has 4 playgrounds and a skate park.
