Maiden Gully Bushland Reserve

Park in Maiden Gully · Basic info

Maiden Gully Bushland Reserve is a park in Maiden Gully, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, good for all ages. McNamara Dr/Calder Hwy (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 318 m away.
