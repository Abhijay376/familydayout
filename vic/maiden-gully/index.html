Maiden Gully in the Greater Bendigo area has 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Maiden Gully is Maiden Gully Recreation Reserve, covering about 5 hectares. Maiden Gully Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Maiden Gully include Maiden Gully Community Park. Playgrounds around Maiden Gully include Westbury Boulevard Playspace. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Maiden Gully.

Getting around

Maiden Gully has no train station; Eaglehawk Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 4.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 54 stops in Maiden Gully. V/Line coaches stop in Maiden Gully. By car from Maiden Gully, Bendigo is about 10 minutes away, Castlemaine about 33 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Maiden Gully is Sloan Swim School in California Gully, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest library to Maiden Gully is Kangaroo Flat Library in Kangaroo Flat, about 8 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk is about 5 minutes' drive from Maiden Gully. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 10 minutes' drive from Maiden Gully. The closest indoor play centre to Maiden Gully is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Maiden Gully is The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo, about 15 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 10 minutes from Maiden Gully by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Maiden Gully.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Maiden Gully, Kangaroo Flat has 13 playgrounds and a library. California Gully, next to Maiden Gully, has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Eaglehawk, next to Maiden Gully, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Maiden Gully, Long Gully has 4 playgrounds and a skate park. Golden Square, West Bendigo, Myers Flat and Marong also border Maiden Gully.
