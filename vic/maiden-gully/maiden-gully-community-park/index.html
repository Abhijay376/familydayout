Maiden Gully Community Park

Park in Maiden Gully · Basic info

Maiden Gully Community Park is a park in Maiden Gully, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, good for all ages. Maiden Gully CFA/Beckhams Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 109 m away with a baby change table.
