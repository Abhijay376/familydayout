Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Granite Rock in the East Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Granite Rock has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 6.1 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Granite Rock, so most trips start by car. By car from Granite Rock, Bairnsdale is about 13 minutes away and Lakes Entrance about 41 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale is about 12 minutes' drive from Granite Rock. The closest library to Granite Rock is Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Granite Rock is Boyfriend Beach in Forge Creek, about 26 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Gilsenan Reserve in Paynesville is about 25 minutes' drive from Granite Rock. For a cinema, Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale is about 12 minutes' drive from Granite Rock. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow is about 12 minutes' drive from Granite Rock. Bairnsdale is the closest bigger centre to Granite Rock, about 13 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Granite Rock are the playground at Howitt Park in Wy Yung (10 min), Howitt Park in Wy Yung (10 min), Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale (11 min), Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale (12 min), Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale (12 min) and Krowathunkoolong Keeping Place in Bairnsdale (12 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Granite Rock.

Nearby suburbs

Wy Yung, next to Granite Rock, has 3 playgrounds. Next door to Granite Rock, Lucknow has a zoo or wildlife park. Clifton Creek also borders Granite Rock.
