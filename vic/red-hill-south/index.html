Red Hill South is in the Mornington Peninsula area, and our data lists 1 playground and 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Red Hill South is Merricks Trail, covering about 14 hectares. Balnarring G95 Bushland Reserve and Bald Hills Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Red Hill South include Koopalanda Reserve. Playgrounds in Red Hill South include the playground at Koopalanda Reserve. For walks and rides, Red Hill Rail Trail crosses Red Hill South. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Red Hill South for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Red Hill South has no train station; Bittern Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Red Hill South, so most trips start by car. By car from Red Hill South, Mornington is about 22 minutes away, Frankston about 28 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 71 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Red Hill South is Yawa Aquatic Centre in Rosebud, about 20 minutes' drive. For a library, Rosebud Library in Rosebud is about 19 minutes' drive from Red Hill South. The closest beach to Red Hill South is Dromana Beach in Dromana, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Red Hill South is Pump Track in Dromana, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Red Hill South is Dromana Triple Drive-In Theatre in Dromana, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Red Hill South is Top Fun Shack in Rosebud, about 20 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale is about 32 minutes' drive from Red Hill South. Mornington is the closest bigger centre to Red Hill South, about 22 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a library, indoor play and a beach.

Best for

Free days on the Red Hill Rail Trail and in Red Hill South's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Shoreham, next to Red Hill South, has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Red Hill South, Red Hill has 1 playground. Main Ridge also borders Red Hill South.
