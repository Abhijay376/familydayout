Tolmie is in the Mansfield area, and our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Toombullup State Forest is the largest mapped park in Tolmie, at about 1,329 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Tolmie.

Getting around

Tolmie has no train station; Benalla Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 49 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tolmie, so most trips start by car. By car from Tolmie, Mansfield is about 22 minutes away, Benalla about 66 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Mansfield Library in Mansfield is about 21 minutes' drive from Tolmie. The closest skate park to Tolmie is the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield, about 23 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield is about 21 minutes' drive from Tolmie. For a zoo or wildlife park, Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield is about 29 minutes' drive from Tolmie. The nearest larger centre is Mansfield, about 22 minutes from Tolmie by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Tolmie are the playground at Mansfield Botanic Park in Mansfield (21 min), Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield (21 min), Mansfield Library in Mansfield (21 min), the playground at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (23 min), the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (23 min) and Mansfield Outdoor Swimming Pool in Mansfield (23 min).
