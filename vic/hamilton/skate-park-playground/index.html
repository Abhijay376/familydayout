Playground in Hamilton · Basic info

skate park playground in Hamilton is a public playground in the Southern Grampians Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 30 m away, and Sir Reginald Ansett Transport Museum is about 60 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 80 m from the playground, and bus 3 stops at Ansett Museum/Ballarat Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
