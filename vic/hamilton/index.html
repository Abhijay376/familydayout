Hamilton is in the Southern Grampians area, and our data lists 23 playgrounds and 14 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Hamilton has a library, a pool, 5 museums or galleries and a cinema.

Landmarks

Hamilton Community Parklands is the largest mapped park in Hamilton, at about 122 hectares. Pedrina Park and Victoria Park are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Hamilton include Apex Park, Bree Park and Hughan Park. Playgrounds in Hamilton include l.e.a.p playground, Bree Park Playground and skate park playground. Our data shows 34 public toilets and 14 off-leash dog parks in Hamilton. In Hamilton you'll also find Big Woolbales & Campe Motor Museum, Hamilton Gallery, Hamilton Cinema, Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre and Hamilton Library. For older kids in Hamilton there's hamilton skate park. John Thomson & Co and The Hub Hamilton are the main shopping centres. The Hamilton-Coleraine Rail Trail passes through Hamilton. Our data lists 12 cafes and family restaurants in Hamilton for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Hamilton. The closest to Hamilton is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 82 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the 1, 2, 3 and Hamilton - Penshurst via Tarrington routes stop in Hamilton. V/Line coaches stop in Hamilton. By car from Hamilton, Portland is about 68 minutes away and Warrnambool about 83 minutes, without traffic.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 23 playgrounds across Hamilton. Rainy days at the library, cinema and museum or gallery in Hamilton. Teens who skate or ride, with hamilton skate park in Hamilton. Free days on the Hamilton-Coleraine Rail Trail and in Hamilton's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Tarrington, next to Hamilton, has 1 playground. Next door to Hamilton, Branxholme has 1 playground. Strathkellar, Hensley Park, Byaduk North and Buckley Swamp also border Hamilton.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Hamilton, without traffic.
