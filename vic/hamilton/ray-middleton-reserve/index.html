Ray Middleton Reserve

Park in Hamilton · Official details

Ray Middleton Reserve is a park in Hamilton, in the Southern Grampians Shire Council area, good for all ages. McArthur St/King St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 1123 m away and dogs are allowed.
