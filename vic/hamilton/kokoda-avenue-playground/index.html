Kokoda Avenue Playground

Playground in Hamilton · Basic info

Kokoda Avenue Playground is a public playground in Hamilton, in the Southern Grampians Shire area. Rasmussen Avenue Playground is another option about 400 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 970 m from the playground, and bus 3 stops at Eventide Lutheran Homes/Ballarat Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
