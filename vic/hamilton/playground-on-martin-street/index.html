Botanic Gardens Playground

Playground in Hamilton · Basic info

Botanic Gardens Playground in Hamilton is a public playground in the Southern Grampians Shire area. Hamilton Library is about 580 m away, and Hamilton History Centre is about 410 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus 1 stops at Hamilton Croquet Club/Martin St, a 3-minute walk away.
