Hamilton Library

Library in Hamilton · Basic info

Hamilton Library is a library in Hamilton, in the Southern Grampians Shire Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Hamilton Town Centre/Brown St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 2 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: opening_hours: Mo-Fr 10:00-17:30; Sa 09:00-12:00.
