Hamilton Gallery

Museum or gallery in Hamilton · Official details

Hamilton Gallery is a museum or gallery in Hamilton, in the Southern Grampians Shire Council area, best for school-age kids. Hamilton Town Centre/Brown St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 2 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: MON – FRI, 10AM - 5PM; SAT – SUN, 10AM - 2PM; Mon – Fri 10am - 5pm; Sat – Sun 10am - 2pm.
