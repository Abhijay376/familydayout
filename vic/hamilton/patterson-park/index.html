Patterson Park

Park in Hamilton · Official details

Patterson Park is a park in Hamilton, in the Southern Grampians Shire Council area, good for all ages. Hamilton College/MacPherson St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 474 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed.
