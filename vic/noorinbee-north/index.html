Getting around

Noorinbee North has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 146 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Noorinbee North, so most trips start by car. By car from Noorinbee North, Lakes Entrance is about 2 hours away and Bairnsdale about 2.5 hours, without traffic.
