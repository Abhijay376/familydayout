Burrowye is in the Towong area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Burrowye, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Burrowye. The closest to Burrowye is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 57 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Burrowye, so most trips start by car. By car from Burrowye, Wodonga is about 78 minutes away and Wangaratta about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest beach to Burrowye is Pebble Beach in Walwa, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Burrowye are Pebble Beach in Walwa (24 min), The Spotted Goanna in Walwa (26 min) and Granya Museum in Granya (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Burrowye.

Nearby suburbs

Bungil also borders Burrowye.
