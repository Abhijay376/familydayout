Koonaburra Reserve

Park in Wandin North · Basic info

Koonaburra Reserve is a park in Wandin North, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Railway Pde/Warburton Hwy (bus) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 955 m away.
