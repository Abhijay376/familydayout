Our data lists 4 playgrounds and 8 parks and reserves in Wandin North, in the Yarra Ranges area. Wandin North also has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

Picnic Hill Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Wandin North, at about 9 hectares. Wandin Yallock Bushland Reserve and Wandin Yallock G173 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Wandin North include Koonaburra Reserve, John Street Reserve and Meadow Court Reserve. Playgrounds in Wandin North include the playground at Koonaburra Reserve, the playground at Meadow Court Reserve and the playground at Greenglades Court Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Wandin North. Places to visit in Wandin North include Mont De Lancey Museum. For walks and rides, Lilydale-Warburton Rail Trail and Warburton Rail Trail cross Wandin North. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Wandin North for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Wandin North has no train station; Lilydale Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 6.7 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 683 and 965 stop in Wandin North. By car from Wandin North, Lilydale is about 9 minutes away, Ringwood about 23 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 50 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Wandin North is Seville Water Play Park in Seville, about 7 minutes' drive. For a library, Mount Evelyn Reading Room in Mount Evelyn is about 8 minutes' drive from Wandin North. For a skate park, Mount Evelyn Skate Park in Mount Evelyn is about 8 minutes' drive from Wandin North. The closest cinema to Wandin North is Reading Cinemas in Chirnside Park, about 14 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, GAMR Laser Tag & VR in Chirnside Park is about 14 minutes' drive from Wandin North.

Best for

Younger kids at Wandin North's 4 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Wandin North. Free days on the Lilydale-Warburton Rail Trail and in Wandin North's parks.
