Playground at Greenglades Court Reserve

Playground in Wandin North · Basic info

This is a public playground in Wandin North. The Lilydale-Warburton Rail Trail is about 320 m away, and for food, China Man Restaurant is about 50 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 130 m from the playground, and buses 683 and 965 stop at George St/Warburton Hwy, a 2-minute walk away.
