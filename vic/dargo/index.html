Dargo is in the Wellington area, and our data lists 1 playground and 16 parks and reserves in the suburb. Dargo also has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Dargo is Boulung - Deera State Forest, covering about 50,776 hectares. Dargo State Forest and Lagoon Plateau Reference Area are the next largest. Dargo also has Black Flat, Dogs Grave and Meyers Flat. Playgrounds around Dargo include Dargo Public Hall Playground. Our data shows 8 public toilets in Dargo. Picnic areas in Dargo include Black Flat, Castleburn Creek and Collins Flat. Places to visit in Dargo include Dargo Museum. The McMillans Walking Track, Track Ninety Six and McMillan Alternative Walking Track all pass through Dargo.

Getting around

There's no train station in Dargo. The closest to Dargo is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 52 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dargo, so most trips start by car. By car from Dargo, Bairnsdale is about 73 minutes away and Sale about 84 minutes, without traffic.

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Dargo. Free days on the McMillans Walking Track and in Dargo's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Omeo, next to Dargo, has 2 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Dargo, Hotham Heights has 1 playground. Dinner Plain, next to Dargo, has 1 playground. Wentworth, Tabberabbera, Ryans and Moornapa also border Dargo.
