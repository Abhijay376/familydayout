Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Konongwootong in the Southern Grampians area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Konongwootong has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 121 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Konongwootong, so most trips start by car. By car from Konongwootong, Hamilton is about 39 minutes away and Portland about 86 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Konongwootong is Casterton Pool in Casterton, about 29 minutes' drive. For a library, Hamilton Library in Hamilton is about 39 minutes' drive from Konongwootong. The closest skate park to Konongwootong is coleraine skate park in Coleraine, about 11 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton is about 39 minutes' drive from Konongwootong. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 39 minutes from Konongwootong by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Konongwootong, the nearest places for kids by road are coleraine skate park in Coleraine (11 min), the playground at Queens Park in Coleraine (11 min), Silvester Oval Playground in Coleraine (11 min), Gorman's art gallery in Casterton (28 min), Casterton Pool in Casterton (29 min) and Harrow Discovery Centre in Harrow (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Konongwootong.

Nearby suburbs

Coleraine, next to Konongwootong, has 4 playgrounds and a skate park. Carapook and Brit Brit also border Konongwootong.
