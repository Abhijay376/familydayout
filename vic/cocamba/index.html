Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Cocamba, in the Swan Hill area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Cocamba is Cocamba Flora and Fauna Reserve, covering about 134 hectares. Degraves Tank Flora Reserve and Cocamba Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

Cocamba has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 66 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cocamba, so most trips start by car. By car from Cocamba, Swan Hill is about 60 minutes away and Mildura about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang is about 7 minutes' drive from Cocamba. The closest skate park to Cocamba is the bmx track at Piangil Memorial Park in Piangil, about 37 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 60 minutes from Cocamba by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Cocamba are Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang (7 min), the playground at Piangil Memorial Park in Piangil (37 min) and the bmx track at Piangil Memorial Park in Piangil (37 min).
