Torrita in the Mildura area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Torrita is Torrita Flora and Fauna Reserve, covering about 252 hectares. Kattyoong Bushland Reserve and Robinsons Tank Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Torrita include Kattyoong Flora Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Torrita.

Getting around

Torrita has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 150 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Ouyen - Pinnaroo via Mallee Hwy route stop in Torrita. By car from Torrita, Mildura is about 2 hours away and Swan Hill about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Torrita is Underbool Swimming Pool in Underbool, about 10 minutes' drive. For a library, Walpeup Library in Walpeup is about 8 minutes' drive from Torrita. For a cinema, Roxy in Ouyen is about 29 minutes' drive from Torrita. From Torrita, the nearest places for kids by road are Walpeup Library in Walpeup (8 min), the playground at Walpeup Wayside Stop in Walpeup (8 min), the playground at Outen Park in Underbool (10 min), Underbool Swimming Pool in Underbool (10 min), Roxy in Ouyen (29 min) and Ouyen Swimming Pool in Ouyen (29 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Torrita.
