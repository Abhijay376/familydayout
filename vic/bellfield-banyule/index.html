Our data lists 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in Bellfield (Banyule), in the Banyule area.

Landmarks

Ford Park is the largest mapped park in Bellfield (Banyule), at about 9 hectares. Cyril Cummins Reserve is the next largest. Playgrounds in Bellfield (Banyule) include the playground at Ford Park. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Bellfield (Banyule). For walks and rides, Darebin Creek Trail and Banyule LBN3 cross Bellfield (Banyule).

Getting around

There's no train station in Bellfield (Banyule). The closest to Bellfield (Banyule) is Ivanhoe Railway Station (metro), about 1.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 250, 350, 513, 514, 549 and 903 stop in Bellfield (Banyule). By car from Bellfield (Banyule), Box Hill is about 19 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 21 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 17 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bellfield (Banyule) is Ivanhoe Aquatic and Fitness Centre in Ivanhoe, about 3 minutes' drive. The closest library to Bellfield (Banyule) is Ivanhoe Library in Ivanhoe, about 4 minutes' drive. For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 15 minutes' drive from Bellfield (Banyule). For a skate park, the bmx track at Malahang Reserve in Heidelberg West is about 3 minutes' drive from Bellfield (Banyule). The closest cinema to Bellfield (Banyule) is Hoyts in Preston, about 4 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Northland Timezone in Preston is about 4 minutes' drive from Bellfield (Banyule). For a zoo or wildlife park, Bundoora Park Farm in Bundoora is about 12 minutes' drive from Bellfield (Banyule). Box Hill is the closest bigger centre to Bellfield (Banyule), about 19 minutes away by car, and has 11 playgrounds, a pool and a library.

Best for

Free days on the Darebin Creek Trail and in Bellfield (Banyule)'s parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Bellfield (Banyule), Heidelberg West has 6 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Bellfield (Banyule), Heidelberg Heights has 3 playgrounds.
