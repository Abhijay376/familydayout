Playground at Ford Park

Playground in Bellfield · Basic info

This is a public playground in Bellfield. The Banyule LBN3 passes about 40 m away, and Playground on Jellicoe Street is another option about 530 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 140 m away, and buses 250, 350 and 549 stop at Perkins Ave/Oriel Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
