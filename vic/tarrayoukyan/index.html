Tarrayoukyan is in the Southern Grampians area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Tarrayoukyan, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Tarrayoukyan has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 121 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tarrayoukyan, so most trips start by car. By car from Tarrayoukyan, Hamilton is about 57 minutes away and Horsham about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tarrayoukyan is Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral, about 31 minutes' drive. For a skate park, coleraine skate park in Coleraine is about 29 minutes' drive from Tarrayoukyan. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 57 minutes from Tarrayoukyan by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Tarrayoukyan are Bake House Studio in Harrow (21 min), Harrow Discovery Centre in Harrow (21 min), Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral (31 min), Glendinning Street Playground in Balmoral (31 min), coleraine skate park in Coleraine (29 min) and the playground at Queens Park in Coleraine (29 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tarrayoukyan.
