Kanyapella is in the Campaspe area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Kanyapella Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Kanyapella, at about 486 hectares. For walks and rides, Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks crosses Kanyapella.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kanyapella. The closest to Kanyapella is Echuca Railway Station (V/Line), about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kanyapella, so most trips start by car. By car from Kanyapella, Echuca is about 18 minutes away and Shepparton about 81 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Aqua Island in Wharparilla is about 40 minutes' drive from Kanyapella. For a library, Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca is about 19 minutes' drive from Kanyapella. For a skate park, Moama Echuca Community Skate Park in Echuca is about 20 minutes' drive from Kanyapella. The closest cinema to Kanyapella is Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca, about 19 minutes' drive. Echuca is the closest bigger centre to Kanyapella, about 18 minutes away by car, and has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Kanyapella are Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca (19 min), Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca (19 min), McDonald's PlayPlace in Echuca (19 min), The Foundry Arts Space in Echuca (19 min), Customs House Gallery in Echuca (19 min) and Hopwood Gardens Playground in Echuca (20 min).

Best for

Free days on the Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks and in Kanyapella's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Echuca Village also borders Kanyapella.
