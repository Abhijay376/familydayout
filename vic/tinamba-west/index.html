Tinamba West in the Wellington area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tinamba West is Glenmaggie Regional Park, covering about 571 hectares. Macalister River Streamside Reserve is the next largest. The Old Race Track, .1 and 8 all pass through Tinamba West.

Getting around

Tinamba West has no train station; Rosedale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tinamba West, so most trips start by car. By car from Tinamba West, Sale is about 39 minutes away, Traralgon about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tinamba West is Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest library to Tinamba West is Sale Library in Sale, about 40 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Pump Track in Heyfield is about 14 minutes' drive from Tinamba West. The closest cinema to Tinamba West is Former Drive-in site in Sale, about 39 minutes' drive. Sale is the closest bigger centre to Tinamba West, about 39 minutes away by car, and has 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Tinamba West, the nearest places for kids by road are Coongulla Community Centre Reserve Playground in Coongulla (13 min), Play Equipment in Newry (22 min), Pump Track in Heyfield (14 min), Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield (15 min), Heyfield and District Museum in Heyfield (15 min) and Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford (34 min).

Best for

Free days on the Old Race Track and in Tinamba West's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Newry, next to Tinamba West, has 1 playground. Next door to Tinamba West, Coongulla has 1 playground. Glenmaggie also borders Tinamba West.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Tinamba West, without traffic.
