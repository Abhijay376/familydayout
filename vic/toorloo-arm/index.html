Toorloo Arm in the East Gippsland area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Toorloo Arm is Lake Tyers State Park, covering about 8,696 hectares. Our data shows 6 public toilets in Toorloo Arm. For walks and rides, East Gippsland Rail Trail, Lonely Bay Walk and Nowa Nowa Walk Banksia Boulevard cross Toorloo Arm.

Getting around

Toorloo Arm has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 36 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 12 stops in Toorloo Arm. By car from Toorloo Arm, Lakes Entrance is about 9 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 44 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Toorloo Arm is Lakes Entrance Aquatic Centre in Lakes Entrance, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest library to Toorloo Arm is Lakes Entrance Library in Lakes Entrance, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Toorloo Arm is Lake Tyers Beach Sand Bar in Lake Tyers Beach, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Toorloo Arm is Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale, about 42 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow is about 35 minutes' drive from Toorloo Arm. Lakes Entrance is the closest bigger centre to Toorloo Arm, about 9 minutes away by car, and has 3 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a beach. From Toorloo Arm, the nearest places for kids by road are Lakes Entrance Aquatic Centre in Lakes Entrance (7 min), Lake Tyers Beach Sand Bar in Lake Tyers Beach (14 min), the playground at Lakes Entrance - Lake Tyers Coastal Reserve (COM) in Lake Tyers Beach (14 min), 90 Mile Beach in Lakes Entrance (7 min), Lakes Entrance Library in Lakes Entrance (9 min) and Bronsdon Street Park in Lakes Entrance (12 min).

Best for

Free days on the East Gippsland Rail Trail and in Toorloo Arm's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Toorloo Arm, Lakes Entrance has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Lake Tyers Beach, next to Toorloo Arm, has 1 playground and a beach. Nowa Nowa, Lake Tyers and Kalimna West also border Toorloo Arm.
