Our data lists 2 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in Willow Grove, in the Baw Baw area.

Landmarks

Neerim East G79 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Willow Grove, at about 8 hectares. Willow Grove Bushland Reserve and Blue Rock Lake West picnic area are the next largest. Playgrounds in Willow Grove include the playground at Blue Rock Lake West picnic area. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Willow Grove.

Getting around

There's no train station in Willow Grove. The closest to Willow Grove is Moe Railway Station (V/Line), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Willow Grove, so most trips start by car. By car from Willow Grove, Warragul is about 31 minutes away, Traralgon about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar is about 14 minutes' drive from Willow Grove. The closest library to Willow Grove is Morwell Library in Morwell, about 28 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Moe Skate Park in Moe is about 15 minutes' drive from Willow Grove. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 30 minutes' drive from Willow Grove. The closest indoor play centre to Willow Grove is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 30 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Warragul, about 31 minutes from Willow Grove by car, with 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Willow Grove, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Ferguson Street Reserve in Moe (14 min), Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar (14 min), Moe Skate Park in Moe (15 min), Diving Pool in Moe (16 min), Old Brown Coal Mine Museum in Yallourn North (20 min) and Yallourn North Skate Park and BMX Track in Yallourn North (20 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Willow Grove.

Nearby suburbs

Trafalgar, next to Willow Grove, has 4 playgrounds and a pool. Shady Creek, Neerim East and Hill End also border Willow Grove.
