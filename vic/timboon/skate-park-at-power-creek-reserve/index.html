Skate park at Power Creek Reserve

Skate, BMX or pump track in Timboon · Basic info

Skate park at Power Creek Reserve is a skate, bmx or pump track in Timboon, in the Corangamite Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Timboon-Nullawarre Rd/Bailey St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 267 m away.
