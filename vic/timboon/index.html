Timboon in the Corangamite area has 3 parks and reserves in our data. Timboon also has a pool and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Timboon is Timboon I1 Bushland Reserve, covering about 277 hectares. Power Creek Reserve is the next largest. Timboon also has Timboon Masonic Park. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Timboon. Places to visit in Timboon include Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool. For older kids in Timboon there's the skate park at Power Creek Reserve. For walks and rides, Camperdown to Timboon Rail Trail and Poorpa Yanyeen Meerreeng - The Timboon to Port Campbell Trail cross Timboon.

Getting around

Timboon has no train station; Terang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Camperdown - Timboon via Cobden and Warrnambool - Port Campbell - Timboon via Allansford, Nullawarre, Peterborough routes stop in Timboon. By car from Timboon, Warrnambool is about 45 minutes away and Colac about 65 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a beach, Wild Dog Cove 5 in Peterborough is about 19 minutes' drive from Timboon. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Timboon is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 38 minutes' drive. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Timboon, about 45 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Timboon are Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell (16 min), the playground at Peterborough Coastal Reserve (COM) in Peterborough (19 min), Wild Dog Cove 5 in Peterborough (19 min), Falls of Halladale in Peterborough (20 min), the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve in Camperdown (41 min) and Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (38 min).

Best for

Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon. Free days on the Camperdown to Timboon Rail Trail and in Timboon's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Newfield, Glenfyne, Curdievale and Curdies River also border Timboon.
