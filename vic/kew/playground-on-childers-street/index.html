Playground on Childers Street

Playground in Kew · Basic info

Playground on Childers Street is a public playground in Kew, in the Boroondara City area. The Victoria Park passes about 50 m away, and Kew Toy Library is about 480 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 850 m away, and buses 302 and 304 stop at Park Cres/Asquith St, a 8-minute walk away.
