Stevenson Street Reserve

Park in Kew · Basic info

Stevenson Street Reserve is a park in Kew, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Stevenson St/High St #31 (tram) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 668 m away with a baby change table.
