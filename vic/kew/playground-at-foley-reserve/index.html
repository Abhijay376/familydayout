Playground at Foley Reserve

Playground in Kew · Basic info

Playground at Foley Reserve is a public playground in Kew, in the Boroondara City area. The Elgin St Hawthorn Local Cycle Route is about 260 m away, and Playground at Smart Street Reserve is another option about 480 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.0 km from the playground, and trams 109 and 48 stop at Barkers Rd/High St #29, a 4-minute walk away.
