Reservoir Reserve

Park in Kew · Basic info

Reservoir Reserve is a park in Kew, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Kew Traffic School/Cotham Rd #41 (tram) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 113 m away with a baby change table.
