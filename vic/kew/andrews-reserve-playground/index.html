Andrews Reserve Playground

Playground in Kew · Basic info

Andrews Reserve Playground in Kew is a public playground in the Boroondara City area. The Main Yarra Trail is about 70 m away, and Horse Arena is about 540 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 800 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 5 minutes from Kew.
