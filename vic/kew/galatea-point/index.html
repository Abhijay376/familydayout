Galatea Point

Park in Kew · Basic info

Galatea Point is a park in Kew, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Yarra Bend Park/Studley Park Rd (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 881 m away.
