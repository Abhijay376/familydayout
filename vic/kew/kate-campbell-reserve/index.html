Kate Campbell Reserve

Park in Kew · Basic info

Kate Campbell Reserve is a park in Kew, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Princess St/Earl St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 597 m away with a baby change table.
