Jack O'Toole Reserve

Park in Kew · Basic info

Jack O'Toole Reserve is a park in Kew, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Kellett Gr/Willsmere Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 541 m away with a baby change table.
