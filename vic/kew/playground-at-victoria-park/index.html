Playground at Victoria Park

Playground in Kew · Basic info

Playground at Victoria Park is a public playground in Kew, in the Boroondara City area. The Anniversary Trail passes about 200 m away, and Vicky's Restaurant is about 290 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 140 m away, and buses 302 and 304 stop at East Kew Terminus/Valerie St, a 3-minute walk away.
