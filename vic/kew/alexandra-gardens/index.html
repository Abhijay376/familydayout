Alexandra Gardens

Park in Kew · Basic info

Alexandra Gardens is a park in Kew, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Charles St/Cotham Rd #35 (tram) is a 2-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 104 m away and dogs are allowed on a lead.
