Dorothy Rogers Reserve

Park in Kew · Basic info

Dorothy Rogers Reserve is a park in Kew, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Kew Junction/Studley Park Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 645 m away with a baby change table.
