Playground at Dickinson Reserve Picnic Area

Playground in Kew · Basic info

Playground at Dickinson Reserve Picnic Area is a public playground in Kew, in the Boroondara City area. The Main Yarra Trail is about 80 m away, and Horse Arena is about 440 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 390 m from the playground, and buses 200 and 207 stop at Walmer St/Studley Park Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
