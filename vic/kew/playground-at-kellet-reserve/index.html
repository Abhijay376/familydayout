Playground at Kellet Reserve

Playground in Kew · Basic info

Playground at Kellet Reserve in Kew is a public playground in the Boroondara City area. The Stevenson St Sackville St is about 190 m away, and for food, Kupid Espresso is about 260 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.2 km from the playground, and bus 624 stops at Fitzwilliam St/Wrixon St, a 2-minute walk away.
