Fenton Reserve

Park in Kew · Basic info

Fenton Reserve is a park in Kew, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. High St/Cotham Rd #34 (tram) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 230 m away with a baby change table.
