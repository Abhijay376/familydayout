Parks Victoria Flying Fox Management Area

Park in Kew · Basic info

Parks Victoria Flying Fox Management Area is a park in Kew, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Royal Talbot Rehab Centre/Yarra Bvd (bus) is a 17-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 398 m away.
