Playground on Racecourse Road

Playground in Haddon · Basic info

Playground on Racecourse Road is a public playground in Haddon, in the Golden Plains Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 80 m away, and Haddon Lions Park Playground is another option about 220 m away. There's a public toilet about 360 m away, and bus C27 stops at Memorial Dr/Sago Hill Rd, a 9-minute walk away.
