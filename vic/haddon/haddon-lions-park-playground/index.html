Haddon Lions Park Playground

Playground in Haddon · Basic info

Haddon Lions Park Playground is a public playground in Haddon, in the Golden Plains Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 10 m away, and Playground on Racecourse Road is another option about 220 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 190 m from the playground, and bus C27 stops at Memorial Dr/Sago Hill Rd, a 9-minute walk away.
