Playground on Fourteen Mile Road

Playground in Garfield · Basic info

Playground on Fourteen Mile Road is a public playground in Garfield, in the Cardinia Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 160 m away, and Garfield Swimming Pool is about 250 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 110 m from the playground, and bus Garfield Station - Traralgon Plaza stops at Garfield Station/Nar Nar Goon - Longwarry Rd , a 7-minute walk away.
