Barker Road Reserve

Park in Garfield · Basic info

Barker Road Reserve is a park in Garfield, in the Cardinia Shire Council area, good for all ages. Garfield Railway Station (vline) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 881 m away.
