Playground at Greenland Court Reserve

Playground in Garfield · Basic info

Playground at Greenland Court Reserve is a public playground in Garfield, in the Cardinia Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 30 m away, and Playground at Barker Road Reserve is another option about 410 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.1 km away. It's easiest by car, about 1 minute from Garfield.
