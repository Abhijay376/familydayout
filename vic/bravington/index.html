Bravington is in the Baw Baw area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bravington, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

The Rokeby Flora and Fauna Trail and Rokeby Flora and Fauna Rail Trail all pass through Bravington.

Getting around

Bravington has no train station; Warragul Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 9.4 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bravington, so most trips start by car. By car from Bravington, Warragul is about 9 minutes away, Pakenham about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 82 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul is about 11 minutes' drive from Bravington. The closest library to Bravington is Warragul Library in Warragul, about 10 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South is about 10 minutes' drive from Bravington. The closest cinema to Bravington is Warragul Cinema in Warragul, about 9 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 10 minutes' drive from Bravington. For a zoo or wildlife park, Wildlife Trail in Tynong North is about 25 minutes' drive from Bravington. The nearest larger centre is Warragul, about 9 minutes from Bravington by car, with 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Bravington are the playground at Ellen Close Linear Reserve in Warragul (8 min), Charles Street Park in Warragul (8 min), Warragul Library in Warragul (10 min), Warragul Cinema in Warragul (9 min), The Alley Warragul in Warragul (10 min) and Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South (10 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Bravington on the Rokeby Flora and Fauna Trail.

Nearby suburbs
