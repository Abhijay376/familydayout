Wemen in the Swan Hill area has 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Wemen Flora and Fauna Reserve is the largest mapped park in Wemen, at about 202 hectares. Playgrounds around Wemen include the playground at River Murray Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wemen. The closest to Wemen is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 105 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wemen, so most trips start by car. By car from Wemen, Mildura is about 80 minutes away and Swan Hill about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Wemen is Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Wemen is Robinvale Skatepark in Robinvale, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Wemen are the playground at Rotary Park in Robinvale (25 min), Rural Life Museum in Robinvale (25 min), Robinvale Skatepark in Robinvale (27 min) and Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang (38 min).
