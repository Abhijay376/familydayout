Getting around

Manifold Heights has no train station; Geelong Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 2.0 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 1, 24 and 25 stop in Manifold Heights. By car from Manifold Heights, Geelong is about 8 minutes away, Torquay about 27 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 65 minutes, without traffic.
