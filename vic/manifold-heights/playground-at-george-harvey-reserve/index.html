Playground at George Harvey Reserve

Playground in Manifold Heights · Basic info

Playground at George Harvey Reserve is a public playground in Manifold Heights, in the Greater Geelong City area. The Bike: Western Link is about 100 m away, and Shearers Arms Gallery is about 470 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 680 m from the playground, and bus 25 stops at Central Ave/Autumn St, a 2-minute walk away.
