George Harvey Reserve

Park in Manifold Heights · Basic info

George Harvey Reserve is a park in Manifold Heights, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Central Ave/Autumn St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 650 m away.
