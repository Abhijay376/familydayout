Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Adams Estate in the Bass Coast area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Adams Estate has no train station; Stony Point Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Adams Estate, so most trips start by car. By car from Adams Estate, Wonthaggi is about 32 minutes away, Pakenham about 41 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 85 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Koo Wee Rup Swimming Pool in Koo Wee Rup is about 27 minutes' drive from Adams Estate. For a library, San Remo Library in San Remo is about 20 minutes' drive from Adams Estate. The closest beach to Adams Estate is Corinella Beach in Corinella, about 15 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Wonthaggi Railway Historic Reserve in Kilcunda is about 19 minutes' drive from Adams Estate. For a cinema, Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi is about 31 minutes' drive from Adams Estate. The closest indoor play centre to Adams Estate is Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham, about 41 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Adams Estate is Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville, about 4 minutes' drive. Wonthaggi is the closest bigger centre to Adams Estate, about 32 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Adams Estate, the nearest places for kids by road are Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville (4 min), the playground at George Bass Memorial Park in Bass (11 min), Corinella Beach in Corinella (15 min), Chicory Kiln in French Island (17 min), Art Fusion in Anderson (15 min) and Shelly Beach in Kilcunda (19 min).
