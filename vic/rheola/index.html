Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Rheola, in the Loddon area.

Landmarks

Rheola Hill Historic & Cultural Features Res. is the largest mapped park in Rheola, at about 70 hectares. Kangderaar I106 Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Rheola include Rheola Pioneers Park.

Getting around

Rheola has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 44 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Rheola, so most trips start by car. By car from Rheola, Bendigo is about 50 minutes away, Maryborough about 53 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly is about 28 minutes' drive from Rheola. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 50 minutes from Rheola by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Rheola are Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (28 min), Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (28 min), Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (23 min), Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (23 min), Bi-Centennial Park Playground in St Arnaud (34 min) and the playground at Queen Mary Botanic Gardens in St Arnaud (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Rheola.

Nearby suburbs

Brenanah and Arnold West also border Rheola.
