Kemp Avenue Reserve

Park in Mount Evelyn · Basic info

Kemp Avenue Reserve is a park in Mount Evelyn, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Newton Ave/Hereford Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1022 m away.
