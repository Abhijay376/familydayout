Playground at Lilydale-Warburton

Playground in Mount Evelyn · Basic info

This is a public playground in Mount Evelyn. The Lilydale-Warburton Rail Trail passes about 40 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 30 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 190 m away, and buses 663 and 679 stop at Birmingham Rd/Wray Cres, a 3-minute walk away.
