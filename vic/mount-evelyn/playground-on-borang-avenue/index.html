Playground on Borang Avenue

Playground in Mount Evelyn · Basic info

Playground on Borang Avenue is a public playground in Mount Evelyn, in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet right by the playground. It's easiest by car, about 5 minutes from Mount Evelyn.
