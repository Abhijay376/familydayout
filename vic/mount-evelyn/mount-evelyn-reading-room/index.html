Mount Evelyn Reading Room

Library in Mount Evelyn · Basic info

Mount Evelyn Reading Room is a library in Mount Evelyn, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Birmingham Rd/Wray Cres (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 159 m away with a baby change table.
