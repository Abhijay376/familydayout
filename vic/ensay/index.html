Getting around

There's no train station in Ensay. The closest to Ensay is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 54 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 14 stops in Ensay. By car from Ensay, Bairnsdale is about 73 minutes away and Lakes Entrance about 83 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek is about 13 minutes' drive from Ensay. For a skate park, the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo is about 35 minutes' drive from Ensay. The closest places for kids by road from Ensay are Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek (13 min), Great Alpine Gallery in Swifts Creek (13 min), Petersens Art Gallery & Museum in Omeo (34 min), the playground at Omeo Historical Park & Museum in Omeo (35 min) and the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Ensay.

Nearby suburbs

Swifts Creek, next to Ensay, has 1 playground and a pool. Reedy Flat, Nunniong, Ensay North and Doctors Flat also border Ensay.
