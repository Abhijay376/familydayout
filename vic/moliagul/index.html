Moliagul is in the Central Goldfields area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Moliagul is Moliagul Historic and Cultural Features Reserve, covering about 997 hectares. Moliagul H51 Bushland Reserve and Moliagul I99 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Moliagul. The Dunolly Cycle Tracks and Moliagul Cycle Track all pass through Moliagul.

Getting around

There's no train station in Moliagul. The closest to Moliagul is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 34 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Moliagul, so most trips start by car. By car from Moliagul, Maryborough is about 42 minutes away, Bendigo about 54 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly is about 17 minutes' drive from Moliagul. The closest library to Moliagul is Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough, about 42 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 42 minutes from Moliagul by car, with a library. From Moliagul, the nearest places for kids by road are Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (17 min), Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (17 min), Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (42 min), the playground at Market Reserve in Carisbrook (47 min), Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (35 min) and Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (35 min).

Best for

Free days on the Dunolly Cycle Tracks and in Moliagul's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Bealiba, next to Moliagul, has 1 playground. Goldsborough and Cochranes Creek also border Moliagul.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Moliagul, without traffic.
