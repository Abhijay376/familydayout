Our data lists 5 parks and reserves in Dandongadale, in the Alpine area.

Landmarks

Yarrarabula Remote and Natural Area is the largest mapped park in Dandongadale, at about 12,750 hectares. Yarrarabula Creek Natural Catchment Area is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Dandongadale include Blades, Tea Tree and Manna Gum. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Dandongadale. Picnic areas in Dandongadale include Blades, Manna Gum and Tea Tree. For walks and rides, Whitfield - Lake Buffalo crosses Dandongadale.

Getting around

There's no train station in Dandongadale. The closest to Dandongadale is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 58 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dandongadale, so most trips start by car. By car from Dandongadale, Bright is about 67 minutes away and Wangaratta about 81 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford is about 40 minutes' drive from Dandongadale. The closest library to Dandongadale is Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford, about 41 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Dandongadale is Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford, about 41 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Dandongadale are the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford (40 min), Myrtleford Swimming Pool in Myrtleford (41 min), The Old School Museum in Myrtleford (41 min), Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford (41 min), Cundy Park Playground in Myrtleford (41 min) and Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford (41 min).

Best for

Free days on the Whitfield - Lake Buffalo and in Dandongadale's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Buffalo River, next to Dandongadale, has 1 playground. Carboor, Buckland and Abbeyard also border Dandongadale.
