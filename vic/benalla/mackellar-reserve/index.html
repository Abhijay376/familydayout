MacKellar Reserve

Park in Benalla · Basic info

MacKellar Reserve is a park in Benalla, in the Benalla Rural City Council area, good for all ages. Salisbury St/MacKellar St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 853 m away.
