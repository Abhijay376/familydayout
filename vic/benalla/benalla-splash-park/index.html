Benalla Splash Park

Playground in Benalla · Basic info

Benalla Splash Park in Benalla is a public playground in the Benalla Rural City area. The Jim Wood Walking Trail is about 20 m away, and Benalla Aquatic Centre is about 50 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground. It's easiest by car, about 1 minute from Benalla.
