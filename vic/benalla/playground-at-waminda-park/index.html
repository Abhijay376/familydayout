Playground at Waminda Park

Playground in Benalla · Basic info

Playground at Waminda Park is a public playground in Benalla, in the Benalla Rural City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet about 1.7 km away, and bus 1 stops at Mansfield Rd/Ballintine St, a 3-minute walk away.
