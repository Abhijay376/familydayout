Our data lists 5 playgrounds and 14 parks and reserves in Benalla, in the Benalla area. On top of that, Benalla has a pool, 5 museums or galleries and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Benalla is Reef Hills State Park, covering about 2,006 hectares. Goomalibee Nature Conservation Reserve and Reef Hills Reference Area are the next largest. Benalla also has Waminda Park, Hollands Bend and Taungurong Park. Playgrounds in Benalla include Benalla Splash Park, The Rocket Playground and the playground at Waminda Park. Our data shows 15 public toilets in Benalla. Places to visit in Benalla include Benalla Art Gallery, Benalla Aviation Museum and Benalla Aquatic Centre. For older kids in Benalla there's Pump Track. For walks and rides, Benalla-Mokoan Discovery Trail, Flat Track’n and Get In There cross Benalla. Our data lists 6 cafes and family restaurants in Benalla for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Benalla Railway Station (V/Line: Albury - Melbourne via Seymour) in Benalla. Buses 1 and 2 stop in Benalla. V/Line coaches stop in Benalla. By car from Benalla, Wangaratta is about 34 minutes away, Shepparton about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Benalla is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Benalla is Swanpool Cinema in Swanpool, about 22 minutes' drive.

Best for

Younger kids at Benalla's 5 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Benalla. Teens who skate or ride, with Pump Track in Benalla. Free days on the Benalla-Mokoan Discovery Trail and in Benalla's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Swanpool, next to Benalla, has a cinema. Tatong, Tarnook, Moorngag and Molyullah also border Benalla.
