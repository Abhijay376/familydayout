Barunah Park is in the Golden Plains area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Barunah Park, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Barunah Park. The closest to Barunah Park is Winchelsea Railway Station (V/Line), about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Barunah Park, so most trips start by car. By car from Barunah Park, Geelong is about 59 minutes away, Colac about 60 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Barunah Park is Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood, about 34 minutes' drive. For a library, Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn is about 36 minutes' drive from Barunah Park. The closest skate park to Barunah Park is the skate park at Turtle Bend Park in Teesdale, about 23 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Geelong, about 59 minutes from Barunah Park by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Barunah Park, the nearest places for kids by road are Shelford Recreation Reserve Playground in Shelford (16 min), Summer Sensations in Teesdale (20 min), the skate park at Turtle Bend Park in Teesdale (23 min), Teesdale BMX Track in Teesdale (23 min), Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood (34 min) and Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn (36 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Barunah Park.

Nearby suburbs

Rokewood, next to Barunah Park, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Shelford, next to Barunah Park, has 1 playground. Next door to Barunah Park, Cressy has 1 playground. Hesse and Barunah Plains also border Barunah Park.
