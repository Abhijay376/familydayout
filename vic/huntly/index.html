Our data lists 3 playgrounds and 6 parks and reserves in Huntly, in the Greater Bendigo area.

Landmarks

Huntly Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Huntly, at about 265 hectares. Whipstick Nature Conservation Reserve and Pennington Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Huntly include Huntly Lions Park, Wallawa Place Reserve and Huntly Greene St Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds in Huntly include the playground at Pennington Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Huntly. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Huntly for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Huntly has no train station; Huntly Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 3.2 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the 5 and Goornong routes stop in Huntly. By car from Huntly, Bendigo is about 16 minutes away, Castlemaine about 47 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Huntly is White Hills Pool in White Hills, about 9 minutes' drive. For a library, Bendigo Library in Bendigo is about 15 minutes' drive from Huntly. The closest skate park to Huntly is McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk, about 15 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 17 minutes' drive from Huntly. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 19 minutes' drive from Huntly. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 13 minutes' drive from Huntly. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 16 minutes from Huntly by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Nearby suburbs

Eaglehawk, next to Huntly, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Epsom, next to Huntly, has 3 playgrounds. Next door to Huntly, Ascot has 3 playgrounds. Wellsford, Huntly North and Bagshot also border Huntly.
