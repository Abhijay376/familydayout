Landmarks

Playgrounds in Cowwarr include Cricket Street Playground. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Cowwarr. For walks and rides, Gippsland Plains Rail Trail crosses Cowwarr. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Cowwarr for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cowwarr. The closest to Cowwarr is Rosedale Railway Station (V/Line), about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Cowwarr. By car from Cowwarr, Traralgon is about 26 minutes away, Sale about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cowwarr is Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield, about 11 minutes' drive. For a library, Sale Library in Sale is about 34 minutes' drive from Cowwarr. The closest skate park to Cowwarr is Pump Track in Heyfield, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Cowwarr is Village 8 in Morwell, about 36 minutes' drive. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Cowwarr, about 26 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. From Cowwarr, the nearest places for kids by road are Apex Park Playground in Heyfield (10 min), Heyfield and District Museum in Heyfield (10 min), Pump Track in Heyfield (10 min), Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield (11 min), the skate park at Apex Park in Glengarry (17 min) and Rosedale Swimming Pool in Rosedale (18 min).

Best for

Free days on the Gippsland Plains Rail Trail and in Cowwarr's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Heyfield, next to Cowwarr, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Cowwarr, Toongabbie has 2 playgrounds. Seaton, Denison and Dawson also border Cowwarr.
