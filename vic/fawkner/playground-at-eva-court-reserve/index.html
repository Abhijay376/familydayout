Playground at Eva Court Reserve

Playground in Fawkner · Basic info

Playground at Eva Court Reserve is a public playground in Fawkner, in the Merri-Bek City area. The M80 Trail is about 10 m away, and for food, 88 Place is about 340 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 780 m from the playground, and bus 531 stops at Anderson Rd/Sydney Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
