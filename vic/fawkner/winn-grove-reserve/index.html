Winn Grove Reserve

Park in Fawkner · Basic info

Winn Grove Reserve is a park in Fawkner, in the Merri-Bek City Council area, good for all ages. Mutton Rd/Lynch Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 846 m away.
