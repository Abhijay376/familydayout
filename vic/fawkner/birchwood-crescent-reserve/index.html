Birchwood Crescent Reserve

Park in Fawkner · Basic info

Birchwood Crescent Reserve is a park in Fawkner, in the Merri-Bek City Council area, good for all ages. Leighton Cres/Anderson Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1148 m away with a baby change table.
