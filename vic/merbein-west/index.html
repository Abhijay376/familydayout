Merbein West is in the Mildura area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Merbein West, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Merbein West has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 192 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Merbein West, so most trips start by car. By car from Merbein West, Mildura is about 21 minutes away and Swan Hill about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Merbein West is Merbein Pool in Merbein, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest library to Merbein West is Merbein Library in Merbein, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Merbein West is Apex Beach in Mildura, about 27 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Merbein Skate Park in Merbein is about 12 minutes' drive from Merbein West. For a cinema, Deakin Cinema Complex in Mildura is about 21 minutes' drive from Merbein West. For an indoor play centre, Laserpunk Laserforce in Mildura is about 21 minutes' drive from Merbein West. For a zoo or wildlife park, Putt Putt Family Fun Centre in Mildura is about 21 minutes' drive from Merbein West. Mildura is the closest bigger centre to Merbein West, about 21 minutes away by car, and has 26 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Merbein West, the nearest places for kids by road are Merbein Library in Merbein (8 min), Merbein Pool in Merbein (8 min), the playground at Chaffey Park in Merbein (11 min), Merbein Skate Park in Merbein (12 min), O'Donell Park Playground in Wargan (10 min) and BMX Track in Mildura (17 min).

Best for

Nearby suburbs
