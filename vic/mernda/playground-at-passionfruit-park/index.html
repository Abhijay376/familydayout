Playground at Passionfruit Park

Playground in Mernda · Basic info

This is a public playground in Mernda. The South Morang - Mernda Trail is about 140 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 390 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 660 m from the playground, and buses 382, 386 and 387 stop at Tangelo Gr/Plenty Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
