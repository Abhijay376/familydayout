Mayfield Park Playground

Playground in Mernda · Basic info

Mayfield Park Playground in Mernda is a public playground in the Whittlesea City area. The Simons Creek Trail is about 300 m away, and for food, Theo's Fish & Chips is about 380 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 620 m from the playground, and buses 382 and 387 stop at Francesca Dr/Plenty Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
