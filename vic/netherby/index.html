Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Netherby, in the Hindmarsh area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Netherby is Warraquil I57 Bushland Reserve, covering about 30 hectares. Netherby Memorial Park is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Netherby. The closest to Netherby is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 174 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Netherby, so most trips start by car. By car from Netherby, Horsham is about 81 minutes away and Stawell about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Netherby is Nhill Library in Nhill, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Netherby is Nhill Skate Park in Nhill, about 25 minutes' drive. From Netherby, the nearest places for kids by road are Nhill Library in Nhill (25 min), the playground at Goldsworthy Park in Nhill (25 min), Gallery Central in Nhill (25 min), Nhill Skate Park in Nhill (25 min), Wagonin in Nhill (26 min) and Nhill Playground in Nhill (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Netherby.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Netherby, Nhill has 3 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Netherby, Rainbow has 1 playground and a pool. Next door to Netherby, Jeparit has 3 playgrounds. Lorquon and Big Desert also border Netherby.
