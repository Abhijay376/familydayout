Playground at Thomas Street Recreation Reserve

Playground in Thomastown · Basic info

Playground at Thomas Street Recreation Reserve is a public playground in Thomastown, in the Whittlesea City area. The Edgars Creek Trail passes about 10 m away, and Main Street Recreation Reserve Playground is another option about 450 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 640 m away, and buses 555 and 557 stop at Travers St/High St, a 8-minute walk away.
