Playground at Winamura Park

Playground in Thomastown · Basic info

Playground at Winamura Park in Thomastown is a public playground in the Whittlesea City area. Barry Road Park Playground is another option about 410 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.0 km from the playground, and bus 357 stops at Winamurra Cres/Edgars Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
