H.R. Uren Recreation Reserve Playground

Playground in Thomastown · Basic info

H.R. Uren Recreation Reserve Playground is a public playground in Thomastown, in the Whittlesea City area. The Galada Tamboore Trail is about 210 m away, and Roma Park Playground is another option about 530 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus 554 stops at HR Uren Reserve/Victoria Dr, a 3-minute walk away.
