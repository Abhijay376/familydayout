Playground at Pandora Park

Playground in Thomastown · Basic info

This is a public playground in Thomastown. The Galada Tamboore Trail is about 190 m away, and H.R. Uren Recreation Reserve Playground is another option about 490 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.7 km from the playground, and bus 554 stops at The Close/Victoria Dr, a 4-minute walk away.
