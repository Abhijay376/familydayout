Thomastown Library and Community Centre

Library in Thomastown · Basic info

Thomastown Library and Community Centre is a library in Thomastown, in the Whittlesea City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Main Street Rec Reserve/Main St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 12 m away.
