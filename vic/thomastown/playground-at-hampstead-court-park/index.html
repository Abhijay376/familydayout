Playground at Hampstead Court Park

Playground in Thomastown · Basic info

This is a public playground in Thomastown. The Edgars Creek Trail passes about 360 m away, and Main Street Recreation Reserve Playground is another option about 500 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 790 m away, and bus 357 stops at Brookland Gr/Edgars Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
