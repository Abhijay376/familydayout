Main Street Recreation Reserve Playground

Playground in Thomastown · Basic info

This is a public playground in Thomastown. The Edgars Creek Trail is about 90 m away, and Thomastown Library and Community Centre is about 250 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 120 m from the playground, and bus 357 stops at Main Street Rec Reserve/Main St, a 5-minute walk away.
