Galada Tamboore and Marran Baba Parklands

Park in Thomastown · Basic info

Galada Tamboore and Marran Baba Parklands is a park in Thomastown, in the Whittlesea City Council area, good for all ages. Bendigo Cres/Victoria Dr (bus) is a 13-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1044 m away with a baby change table.
