Graytown is in the Strathbogie area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Graytown, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Graytown. The closest to Graytown is Nagambie Railway Station (V/Line), about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Graytown, so most trips start by car. By car from Graytown, Seymour is about 38 minutes away, Shepparton about 60 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Graytown is Nagambie Pool in Nagambie, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest library to Graytown is Nagambie Library in Nagambie, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Graytown is Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote, about 24 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 38 minutes from Graytown by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Graytown are the playground at Goulburn River Heritage River in Nagambie (16 min), Nagambie Recreation Reserve Playground in Nagambie (16 min), Nagambie Pool in Nagambie (16 min), Nagambie Historical Society in Nagambie (17 min), Nagambie Library in Nagambie (18 min) and Mudwood Studio in Heathcote (24 min).

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Graytown.
