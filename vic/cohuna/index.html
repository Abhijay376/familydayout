Cohuna is in the Gannawarra area, and our data lists 2 playgrounds and 11 parks and reserves in the suburb. Cohuna also has a pool and a skate park.

Landmarks

Spence Bridge Education Area is the largest mapped park in Cohuna, at about 384 hectares. Cohuna Bushland Reserve and Cohuna Apex Park are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Cohuna include Goat Island, Five Sleeper and Reedy Lagoon. Playgrounds around Cohuna include the playground at Cohuna Apex Park and the playground at C L King Memorial Park. Our data shows 6 public toilets in Cohuna. Picnic areas in Cohuna include Five Sleeper, Goat Island and Horse Shoe Lagoon. For camping, there's Gunbower Creek - Cohuna Camping Area 2 (CLWF) and Gunbower Creek - Cohuna Camping Area 3 (CLWF). Places to visit in Cohuna include Cohuna Pool. For older kids in Cohuna there's Cohuna Skate Park.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cohuna. The closest to Cohuna is Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line), about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Kerang - Echuca via Cohuna route stop in Cohuna. V/Line coaches stop in Cohuna. By car from Cohuna, Echuca is about 52 minutes away and Swan Hill about 74 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Cohuna is Barham Branch Library in Koondrook, about 28 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 52 minutes from Cohuna by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Cohuna are Leitchville Memorial Swimming Pool in Leitchville (14 min), Barham Branch Library in Koondrook (28 min), Barham Skate Park in Koondrook (28 min), Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (26 min) and Pyramid Hill & Districts Historical Museum in Pyramid Hill (43 min).

Best for

Teens who skate or ride, with Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Cohuna, Koondrook has 2 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Gannawarra, Daltons Bridge, Cullen and Burkes Bridge also border Cohuna.
