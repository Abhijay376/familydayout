Raywood in the Loddon area has 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, Raywood has a pool.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Raywood is Neilborough Creek Streamside Reserve, covering about 24 hectares. Yarraberb Bushland Reserve and Neilborough Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Raywood. Places to visit in Raywood include Raywood Aquatic Centre.

Getting around

Raywood has a train station: Raywood Railway Station (V/Line: Swan Hill - Melbourne via Bendigo). V/Line coaches stop in Raywood. By car from Raywood, Bendigo is about 27 minutes away, Castlemaine about 55 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Raywood is Bendigo Library in Bendigo, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Raywood is McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Raywood is Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Raywood is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 29 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 30 minutes' drive from Raywood. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 27 minutes from Raywood by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Raywood, the nearest places for kids by road are McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk (20 min), Peter Krenz Leisure Centre in Eaglehawk (19 min), Eaglehawk Playspace in Eaglehawk (20 min), the playground at Canterbury Park in Eaglehawk (20 min), the bmx track on Victoria Street in Eaglehawk (21 min) and Bendigo Steam Engine Museum in North Bendigo (25 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Raywood.

Nearby suburbs

Kamarooka, Campbells Forest and Auchmore also border Raywood.
