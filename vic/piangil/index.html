Our data lists 2 playgrounds and 7 parks and reserves in Piangil, in the Swan Hill area. Piangil also has a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Piangil is Bailey Plain Bushland Reserve, covering about 178 hectares. Bransons Bushland Reserve and Ryans Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Piangil include Mensforth Park, Harry Lacey Park and Piangil Memorial Park. Playgrounds in Piangil include the playground at Mensforth Park and the playground at Piangil Memorial Park. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Piangil. For older kids in Piangil there's the bmx track at Piangil Memorial Park. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Piangil for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Piangil has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 39 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus tv stops in Piangil. V/Line coaches stop in Piangil. By car from Piangil, Swan Hill is about 36 minutes away and Mildura about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Piangil is Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest library to Piangil is Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Piangil is Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill, about 36 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 36 minutes from Piangil by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Piangil, the nearest places for kids by road are Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South (34 min), Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang (31 min), Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill (35 min), Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill (35 min), Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill (36 min) and Swan Hill Regional Art Gallery in Swan Hill (38 min).

Best for
