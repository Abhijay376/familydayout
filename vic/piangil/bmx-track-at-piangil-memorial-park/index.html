BMX track at Piangil Memorial Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Piangil · Basic info

BMX track at Piangil Memorial Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Piangil, in the Swan Hill Rural City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. General Store/Murray St (coach) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 31 m away.
