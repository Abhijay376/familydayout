Henkel Street Private Cinema

Cinema in Brunswick · Official details

Henkel Street Private Cinema is a cinema in Brunswick, in the Merri-Bek City Council area, good for school-age kids and teens. Gardiner St/Victoria St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 374 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: Cinema 1 - Wednesday/Thursday 7.30-9.30pm, Saturday 9-11pm, Sunday 6-8pm.; Cinema 2 - Wednesday/Thursday 7-9pm, Friday 7-9pm, Saturday 8.15pm-10.15pm, Sunday 5.15-7.15pm..
