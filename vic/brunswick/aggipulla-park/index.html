Aggipulla Park

Park in Brunswick · Basic info

Aggipulla Park is a park in Brunswick, in the Merri-Bek City Council area, good for all ages. Barkly Square/Sydney Rd #20 (tram) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 206 m away with a baby change table.
