Clifton Park Skatepark

Skate, BMX or pump track in Brunswick · Basic info

Clifton Park Skatepark is a skate, bmx or pump track in Brunswick, in the Merri-Bek City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Gardiner St/Victoria St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 346 m away with a baby change table.
