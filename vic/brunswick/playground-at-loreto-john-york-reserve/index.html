Playground at Loreto John York Reserve

Playground in Brunswick · Basic info

Playground at Loreto John York Reserve is a public playground in Brunswick, in the Merri-Bek City area. The East Brunswick Shimmy is about 360 m away, and for food, Coburger & Co is about 150 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 520 m from the playground, and trams 1 and 6 stop at Moreland Rd/Holmes St #129, a 2-minute walk away.
