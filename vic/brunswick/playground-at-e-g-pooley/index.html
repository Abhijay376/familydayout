Playground at E G Pooley

Playground in Brunswick · Basic info

Playground at E G Pooley in Brunswick is a public playground in the Merri-Bek City area. The East Brunswick Shimmy passes about 20 m away, and Degani is about 220 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 760 m away, and bus 510 stops at Brunswick Private Hospital/Moreland Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
