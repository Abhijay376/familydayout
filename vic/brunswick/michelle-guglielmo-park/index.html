Michelle Guglielmo Park

Park in Brunswick · Basic info

Michelle Guglielmo Park is a park in Brunswick, in the Merri-Bek City Council area, good for all ages. Sydney Rd/Glenlyon Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 87 m away with a baby change table.
