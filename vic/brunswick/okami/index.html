Okami, Brunswick

Cafe or restaurant in Brunswick · Basic info

Okami is a japanese cafe or restaurant in Brunswick, in the Merri-Bek City Council area, a place to eat during a day out. Moreland Station/Moreland Rd #133 (tram) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 364 m away with a baby change table.
