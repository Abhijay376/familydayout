Mount Hooghly is in the Central Goldfields area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Mount Hooghly, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mount Hooghly. The closest to Mount Hooghly is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mount Hooghly, so most trips start by car. By car from Mount Hooghly, Maryborough is about 29 minutes away, Bendigo about 59 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly is about 7 minutes' drive from Mount Hooghly. The closest library to Mount Hooghly is Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough, about 29 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 29 minutes from Mount Hooghly by car, with a library. From Mount Hooghly, the nearest places for kids by road are Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (7 min), Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (7 min), the playground at Avoca Lions Park in Avoca (24 min), Courthouse in Avoca (24 min), Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (29 min) and the playground at Market Reserve in Carisbrook (36 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mount Hooghly.

Nearby suburbs

Dunolly, next to Mount Hooghly, has a pool. Bet Bet also borders Mount Hooghly.
