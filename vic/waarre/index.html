Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Waarre in the Corangamite area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Waarre has no train station; Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 39 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Waarre, so most trips start by car. By car from Waarre, Warrnambool is about 64 minutes away and Colac about 74 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Waarre is Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Waarre is Rutledge Creek Beach in Port Campbell, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Waarre is Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Waarre are Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell (14 min), Rutledge Creek Beach in Port Campbell (18 min), Sherbrooke Estuary in Port Campbell (18 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (20 min), the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (20 min) and the playground at Peterborough Coastal Reserve (COM) in Peterborough (22 min).

Best for
