Playground at Hopetoun Gardens

Playground in Elsternwick · Basic info

Playground at Hopetoun Gardens in Elsternwick is a public playground in the Glen Eira City area. Danny's is about 200 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and tram 67 stops at Parkside St/Glenhuntly Rd #48, a 3-minute walk away.
