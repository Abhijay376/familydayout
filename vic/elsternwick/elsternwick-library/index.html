Elsternwick Library

Library in Elsternwick · Basic info

Elsternwick Library is a library in Elsternwick, in the Glen Eira City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Elsternwick Shopping Centre/Glenhuntly Rd #45 (tram) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1 m away with a baby change table.
