Playground on Seymour Road

Playground in Elsternwick · Basic info

This is a public playground in Elsternwick. Elsternwick Library is about 540 m away, and for food, Mediterranean Greek Tavern is about 450 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 70 m from the playground, and bus 604 stops at Villiers St/Orrong Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
