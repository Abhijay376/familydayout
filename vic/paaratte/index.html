Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Paaratte in the Corangamite area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Poorpa Yanyeen Meerreeng - The Timboon to Port Campbell Trail crosses Paaratte.

Getting around

Paaratte has no train station; Terang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 34 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Paaratte, so most trips start by car. By car from Paaratte, Warrnambool is about 49 minutes away and Colac about 70 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Paaratte is Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon, about 7 minutes' drive. For a beach, Wild Dog Cove 5 in Peterborough is about 11 minutes' drive from Paaratte. The closest skate park to Paaratte is the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon, about 7 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 49 minutes from Paaratte by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Paaratte are Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (7 min), the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (7 min), the playground at Peterborough Coastal Reserve (COM) in Peterborough (10 min), Wild Dog Cove 5 in Peterborough (11 min), Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell (12 min) and Falls of Halladale in Peterborough (12 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Paaratte on the Poorpa Yanyeen Meerreeng - The Timboon to Port Campbell Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Port Campbell, next to Paaratte, has 1 playground, a beach and a skate park. Next door to Paaratte, Timboon has a pool and a skate park. Heytesbury Lower also borders Paaratte.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Paaratte, without traffic.
