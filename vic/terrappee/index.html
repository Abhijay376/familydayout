Terrappee in the Loddon area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Terrappee Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Terrappee, at about 18 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Terrappee. The closest to Terrappee is Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line), about 51 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Terrappee, so most trips start by car. By car from Terrappee, Bendigo is about 1.5 hours away and Swan Hill about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Boort Swimming Pool in Boort is about 20 minutes' drive from Terrappee. The closest cinema to Terrappee is Rex Theatre in Charlton, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Terrappee are Boort Swimming Pool in Boort (20 min), Boort Court House Historical Museum in Boort (20 min), A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park in Boort (20 min), Rex Theatre in Charlton (28 min), Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (28 min) and the playground at Rotary Park in Charlton (28 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Terrappee.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Terrappee, Boort has 1 playground and a pool. Catumnal and Buckrabanyule also border Terrappee.
