Eagle Rock Marine Sanctuary

Park in Aireys Inlet · Official details

Eagle Rock Marine Sanctuary is a park in Aireys Inlet, in the Surf Coast Shire Council area, good for all ages. Bambra Rd/Great Ocean Rd (coach) is a 13-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 513 m away.

Details

Good to know
Snorkel only in calm conditions; Eagle Rock lookout and Split Point Lighthouse nearby (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Eagle Rock Marine Sanctuary?
Eagle Rock Marine Sanctuary is in Aireys Inlet, Victoria.
Is Eagle Rock Marine Sanctuary free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Eagle Rock Marine Sanctuary?
The nearest public toilet is about 513 m away.
How do I get to Eagle Rock Marine Sanctuary by public transport?
The closest stop is Bambra Rd/Great Ocean Rd (routes Warrnambool - Melbourne via Apollo Bay & Geelong), a 13-minute walk.
