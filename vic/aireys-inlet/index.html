Aireys Inlet is in the Surf Coast area, and our data lists 2 playgrounds and 4 parks and reserves in the suburb. Aireys Inlet also has 5 beaches and a skate park.

Landmarks

Eagle Rock Marine Sanctuary is the largest mapped park in Aireys Inlet, at about 18 hectares. Aireys Inlet Bushland Reserve and Aireys Inlet Reserve are the next largest. Aireys Inlet also has Bark Hut Reserve. Playgrounds in Aireys Inlet include the playground at Bark Hut Reserve and the playground at Aireys Inlet Reserve. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Aireys Inlet. In Aireys Inlet you'll also find Red Rocks Beach and Sandy Gully Beach. For older kids in Aireys Inlet there's Aireys Inlet Skate Park. For walks and rides, Currawong Falls Track, Ironbark Gorge Track and Airies Inlet Clifftop Walk cross Aireys Inlet. Our data lists 3 cafes and family restaurants in Aireys Inlet for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Aireys Inlet has no train station; Winchelsea Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Aireys Inlet. By car from Aireys Inlet, Torquay is about 30 minutes away, Geelong about 49 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Aireys Inlet is Lorne Sea Baths in Lorne, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest library to Aireys Inlet is Torquay Library in Torquay, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Aireys Inlet is Lorne Theatre in Lorne, about 21 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Leisurelink Restaurant in Highton is about 43 minutes' drive from Aireys Inlet. For a zoo or wildlife park, Live Wire Park in Lorne is about 23 minutes' drive from Aireys Inlet. The nearest larger centre is Torquay, about 30 minutes from Aireys Inlet by car, with 15 playgrounds, a library and a beach.

Best for

Teens who skate or ride, with Aireys Inlet Skate Park in Aireys Inlet. Beach days at Red Rocks Beach and Sandy Gully Beach. Free days on the Currawong Falls Track and in Aireys Inlet's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Aireys Inlet, Anglesea has 3 playgrounds, a beach and a skate park. Eastern View, next to Aireys Inlet, has a beach.
