Aireys Inlet Skate Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Aireys Inlet · Basic info

Aireys Inlet Skate Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Aireys Inlet, in the Surf Coast Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Bambra Rd/Great Ocean Rd (coach) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 13 m away.
