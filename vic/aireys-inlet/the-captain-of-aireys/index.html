The Captain of Aireys

Cafe or restaurant in Aireys Inlet · Basic info

The Captain of Aireys is a pizza cafe or restaurant in Aireys Inlet, in the Surf Coast Shire Council area, a place to eat during a day out. Bambra Rd/Great Ocean Rd (coach) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 465 m away.
