Smelly Beach

Beach in Aireys Inlet · Basic info

Smelly Beach is a beach in Aireys Inlet, in the Surf Coast Shire Council area, good for all ages. Post Office/Great Ocean Rd (coach) is a 9-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 978 m away.

Where is Smelly Beach?
Smelly Beach is in Aireys Inlet, Victoria.
