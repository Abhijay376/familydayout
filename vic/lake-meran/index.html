Lake Meran is in the Gannawarra area, and our data lists 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Leaghur State Park is the largest mapped park in Lake Meran, at about 2,045 hectares. Lake Meran W.R. and Little Lake Natural Features Reserve are the next largest. Playgrounds around Lake Meran include the playground at Lake Meran W.R. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Lake Meran.

Getting around

Lake Meran has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lake Meran, so most trips start by car. By car from Lake Meran, Swan Hill is about 60 minutes away and Echuca about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Swimming Pool in Kerang is about 16 minutes' drive from Lake Meran. The closest library to Lake Meran is Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Lake Meran is Barham Skate Park in Koondrook, about 39 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 60 minutes from Lake Meran by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Lake Meran, the nearest places for kids by road are Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (16 min), Swimming Pool in Kerang (16 min), A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park in Boort (24 min), Boort Court House Historical Museum in Boort (24 min), Boort Swimming Pool in Boort (25 min) and Barham Branch Library in Koondrook (38 min).

Best for
