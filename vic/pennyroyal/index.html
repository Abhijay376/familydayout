Our data lists 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in Pennyroyal, in the Surf Coast area.

Landmarks

Penny Royal Creek Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Pennyroyal, at about 10 hectares. Pennyroyal also has Enchanted Forest.

Getting around

Pennyroyal has no train station; Birregurra Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Pennyroyal, so most trips start by car. By car from Pennyroyal, Colac is about 40 minutes away, Torquay about 60 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Lorne Sea Baths in Lorne is about 21 minutes' drive from Pennyroyal. The closest library to Pennyroyal is Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac, about 40 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Pennyroyal is Lorne Beach in Lorne, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Pennyroyal is Birregurra Skate Park in Birregurra, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Pennyroyal is Lorne Theatre in Lorne, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Pennyroyal is Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac, about 39 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Live Wire Park in Lorne is about 23 minutes' drive from Pennyroyal. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 40 minutes from Pennyroyal by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Pennyroyal, the nearest places for kids by road are Birregurra Skate Park in Birregurra (22 min), Lorne Theatre in Lorne (21 min), Lorne Sea Baths in Lorne (21 min), Kay Langan Skate Park in Lorne (21 min), the playground at Lorne - Queenscliff Coastal Reserve in Lorne (21 min) and the playground at Lorne - Queenscliff Coastal Reserve in Lorne (22 min).
