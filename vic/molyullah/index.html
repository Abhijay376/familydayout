Molyullah is in the Benalla area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Molyullah is Mollyullah Bushland Reserve, covering about 4 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Molyullah. The closest to Molyullah is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Molyullah, so most trips start by car. By car from Molyullah, Benalla is about 32 minutes away and Wangaratta about 57 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Molyullah is Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Molyullah is Pump Track in Benalla, about 32 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Swanpool Cinema in Swanpool is about 44 minutes' drive from Molyullah. The nearest larger centre is Benalla, about 32 minutes from Molyullah by car, with 5 playgrounds and a pool. From Molyullah, the nearest places for kids by road are Benalla Aviation Museum in Benalla (31 min), Benalla Migrant Camp in Benalla (31 min), Pump Track in Benalla (32 min), Benalla Splash Park in Benalla (32 min), Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (32 min) and the playground at Benalla Botanical Gardens in Benalla (32 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Molyullah.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Molyullah, Benalla has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Lurg also borders Molyullah.
