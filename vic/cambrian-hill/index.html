Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Cambrian Hill in the Golden Plains area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cambrian Hill. The closest to Cambrian Hill is Ballarat Railway Station (V/Line), about 8.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cambrian Hill, so most trips start by car. By car from Cambrian Hill, Ballarat is about 11 minutes away, Daylesford about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cambrian Hill is Buninyong Swimming Pool in Buninyong, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest library to Cambrian Hill is Sebastopol Library in Sebastopol, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Cambrian Hill is the skate park at M R Power Recreation Reserve in Sebastopol, about 6 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Showbiz Cinemas Ballarat in Smythes Creek is about 10 minutes' drive from Cambrian Hill. The closest indoor play centre to Cambrian Hill is Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point, about 12 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East is about 17 minutes' drive from Cambrian Hill. The nearest larger centre is Ballarat, about 11 minutes from Cambrian Hill by car, with 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Cambrian Hill, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Wall Street Reserve in Sebastopol (4 min), the skate park at M R Power Recreation Reserve in Sebastopol (6 min), the playground at M R Power Recreation Reserve in Sebastopol (6 min), the pump track on Darling Street in Sebastopol (6 min), Sebastopol Library in Sebastopol (6 min) and Showbiz Cinemas Ballarat in Smythes Creek (10 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Cambrian Hill.

Nearby suburbs

Sebastopol, next to Cambrian Hill, has 14 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Ross Creek, next to Cambrian Hill, has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Cambrian Hill, Napoleons has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Cambrian Hill, Bonshaw has 2 playgrounds. Scotchmans Lead and Magpie also border Cambrian Hill.
