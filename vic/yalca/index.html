Yalca is in the Moira area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Yalca has 2 beaches.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Yalca is Top End Reference Area, covering about 130 hectares. Yalca Bushland Reserve is the next largest. In Yalca you'll also find Paradise Beach and Scotts Beach.

Getting around

Yalca has no train station; Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 45 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yalca, so most trips start by car. By car from Yalca, Shepparton is about 55 minutes away and Echuca about 68 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Nathalia Memorial Pool in Nathalia is about 26 minutes' drive from Yalca. For a library, Nathalia Library in Nathalia is about 24 minutes' drive from Yalca. The closest skate park to Yalca is Strathmerton Community Skate Park in Strathmerton, about 23 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Cobram Cinema in Cobram is about 35 minutes' drive from Yalca. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 55 minutes from Yalca by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Yalca are Top End Beach in Bearii (26 min), Wasers Beach in Bearii (26 min), Barmah Forest Heritge and Education Centre in Nathalia (24 min), Nathalia Library in Nathalia (24 min), the playground at Apex Park in Nathalia (24 min) and Nathalia Memorial Pool in Nathalia (26 min).

Best for

Beach days at Paradise Beach and Scotts Beach.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Yalca, Nathalia has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Next door to Yalca, Bearii has a beach. Strathmerton and Katunga also border Yalca.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Yalca, without traffic.
