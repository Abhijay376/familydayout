Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Roslynmead, in the Campaspe area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Roslynmead is Pipit Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 33 hectares.

Getting around

Roslynmead has no train station; Echuca Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Roslynmead, so most trips start by car. By car from Roslynmead, Echuca is about 23 minutes away, Bendigo about 74 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Roslynmead is Lockington Pool in Lockington, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest library to Roslynmead is Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Roslynmead is Moama Echuca Community Skate Park in Echuca, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Roslynmead is Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca, about 22 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 23 minutes from Roslynmead by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Roslynmead, the nearest places for kids by road are Lockington Pool in Lockington (15 min), Lockington District Swimming Pool in Lockington (15 min), Holden Museum in Echuca (20 min), the playground at Echuca Lions Park in Echuca (21 min), Hopwood Gardens Playground in Echuca (20 min) and Port of Echuca Discovery Centre in Echuca (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Roslynmead.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Roslynmead, Wharparilla has a pool. Bamawm Extension also borders Roslynmead.
