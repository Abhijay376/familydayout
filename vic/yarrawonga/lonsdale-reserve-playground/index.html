Lonsdale Reserve Playground

Playground in Yarrawonga · Basic info

Lonsdale Reserve Playground is a public playground in Yarrawonga, in the Moira Shire area. Mulwala Civic Centre Library is about 350 m away, and Yarrawonga Mulwala Pioneer Museum is about 560 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and coaches stop at Melbourne St/Inglis St, a 4-minute walk away.
