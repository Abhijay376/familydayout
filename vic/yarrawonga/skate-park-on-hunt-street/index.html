Skate park on Hunt Street

Skate, BMX or pump track in Yarrawonga · Basic info

Skate park on Hunt Street is a skate, bmx or pump track in Yarrawonga, in the Moira Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Town Hall/Orr St (bus) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 5 m away.
