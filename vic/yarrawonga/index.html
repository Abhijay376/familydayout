Our data lists 6 playgrounds and 10 parks and reserves in Yarrawonga, in the Moira area. On top of that, Yarrawonga has 2 libraries, a museum or gallery, 2 zoos or wildlife parks and 2 beaches.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Yarrawonga is Yarrawonga Regional Park, covering about 314 hectares. Kyffins Reserve and Gorman Park are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Yarrawonga include Hammon Park, Purtle Park and Rotary Park. Playgrounds in Yarrawonga include Swimming Pool Reserve, Lonsdale Reserve Playground and Purtle Park All Ability Playground. Our data shows 13 public toilets in Yarrawonga. In Yarrawonga you'll also find Mulwala Waterslide, Tunzafun, Yarrawonga Mulwala Pioneer Museum, Mulwala Civic Centre Library and Yarrawonga Library. For walks and rides, Murray River Adventure Trail and McNamara Walk cross Yarrawonga. Our data lists 12 cafes and family restaurants in Yarrawonga for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Yarrawonga. The closest to Yarrawonga is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 47 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Wangaratta - Yarrawonga route stop in Yarrawonga. V/Line coaches stop in Yarrawonga. By car from Yarrawonga, Wangaratta is about 45 minutes away and Benalla about 53 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rutherglen War Memorial Swimming Pool in Rutherglen is about 38 minutes' drive from Yarrawonga. The closest skate park to Yarrawonga is the skate park at Federation Park in Cobram, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Yarrawonga is Cobram Cinema in Cobram, about 30 minutes' drive.

Best for

Younger kids at Yarrawonga's 6 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the library and museum or gallery in Yarrawonga. Beach days at Forges Beach No. 1 and Forges Beach No. 2. Free days on the Murray River Adventure Trail and in Yarrawonga's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Burramine, next to Yarrawonga, has a beach. Bathumi also borders Yarrawonga.
