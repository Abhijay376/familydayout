Kalinga Park

Park in Kialla · Basic info

Kalinga Park is a park in Kialla, in the Greater Shepparton City Council area, good for all ages. Guthrie Street Primary School/Abernethy St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1414 m away with a baby change table.
