Kialla in the Greater Shepparton area has 11 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in our data. Beyond the parks, Kialla has a museum or gallery and a zoo or wildlife park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Kialla is Kalinga Park, covering about 47 hectares. Bosco Park and Falcon Park are the next largest. Playground at Lowanna Park and Playground at Pelican Park are among the council's larger district playgrounds. Other playgrounds in Kialla include Sevens Park Playground, Sevens Creek Playground and the playground at Lowanna Waters. Our data shows 2 public toilets and 2 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Kialla. Places to visit in Kialla include Emerald Bank Leisure Land and Museum of Vehicle Evolution - MOVE. Riverside Plaza is the local shopping centre. The Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks, Broken River Path and The River Walk all pass through Kialla. Our data lists 3 cafes and family restaurants in Kialla for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kialla. The closest to Kialla is Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line), about 3.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the 8 and Shepparton - Euroa via Kialla routes stop in Kialla. By car from Kialla, Shepparton is about 7 minutes away, Benalla about 56 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Aquamoves in Shepparton is about 6 minutes' drive from Kialla. The closest library to Kialla is Shepparton Library in Shepparton, about 7 minutes' drive. For a beach, Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton is about 8 minutes' drive from Kialla. The closest skate park to Kialla is the skate park at Skate Park in Shepparton, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Kialla is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 7 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 11 playgrounds across Kialla. Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Kialla. Free days on the Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks and in Kialla's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Kialla, Shepparton East has 3 playgrounds. Next door to Kialla, Toolamba has 2 playgrounds. Kialla West, Karramomus, Arcadia and Orrvale also border Kialla.
