Riverwood Reserve Playground

Playground in Kialla · Basic info

Riverwood Reserve Playground in Kialla is a neighbourhood playground in the Greater Shepparton City area. The Seven Creeks Cycleway extention is about 10 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.3 km from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 4 minutes from Kialla.
