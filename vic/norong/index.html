Norong in the Indigo area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Norong is Doma Mungi Creek Streamside Reserve, covering about 28 hectares. Norong Central Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Norong. The closest to Norong is Springhurst Railway Station (V/Line), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Wangaratta - Yarrawonga route stop in Norong. By car from Norong, Wangaratta is about 39 minutes away and Wodonga about 55 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Norong is Rutherglen War Memorial Swimming Pool in Rutherglen, about 20 minutes' drive. For a library, Rutherglen Public Library in Rutherglen is about 20 minutes' drive from Norong. The closest skate park to Norong is the pump track on Victoria Street in Wahgunyah, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Norong is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 36 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Tunzafun in Yarrawonga is about 40 minutes' drive from Norong. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Norong, about 39 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Norong, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Peechelba Reserve in Peechelba (27 min), Apex Park Playground in Rutherglen (19 min), Rutherglen War Memorial Swimming Pool in Rutherglen (20 min), Rutherglen Public Library in Rutherglen (20 min), the pump track on Victoria Street in Wahgunyah (21 min) and Corowa Library in Wahgunyah (23 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Norong.

Nearby suburbs

Rutherglen, next to Norong, has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Next door to Norong, Springhurst has 1 playground. Brimin, Boorhaman North and Boorhaman East also border Norong.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Norong, without traffic.
