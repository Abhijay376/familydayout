Carrajung South in the Wellington area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Bruthen Creek Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Carrajung South, at about 4 hectares. Carrajung H34 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Carrajung South. The closest to Carrajung South is Rosedale Railway Station (V/Line), about 25 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Carrajung South, so most trips start by car. By car from Carrajung South, Traralgon is about 44 minutes away, Sale about 64 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Yarram Public Pool in Yarram is about 28 minutes' drive from Carrajung South. For a library, Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram is about 27 minutes' drive from Carrajung South. For a beach, Woodside_Beach in Woodside Beach is about 33 minutes' drive from Carrajung South. The closest skate park to Carrajung South is the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram, about 28 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Traralgon, about 44 minutes from Carrajung South by car, with 38 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Carrajung South are Yarram Historical Society Museum in Yarram (25 min), Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram (27 min), the playground at Memorial Park in Yarram (28 min), the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram (28 min), Yarram Public Pool in Yarram (28 min) and Woodside_Beach in Woodside Beach (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Carrajung South.
