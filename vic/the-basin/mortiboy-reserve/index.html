Mortiboy Reserve

Park in The Basin · Basic info

Mortiboy Reserve is a park in The Basin, in the Knox City Council area, good for all ages. Stuart St/Forest Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 640 m away with a baby change table.
