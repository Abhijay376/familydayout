Rose River is in the Wangaratta area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Rose River is Dandongadale (part in SF) Remote and Natural Area, covering about 9,330 hectares. Long Jack Creek Natural Catchment Area is the next largest. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Rose River. For walks and rides, Whitfield - Lake Buffalo crosses Rose River.

Getting around

Rose River has no train station; Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 60 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Rose River, so most trips start by car. By car from Rose River, Wangaratta is about 87 minutes away and Mansfield about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest places for kids by road from Rose River are Myrrhee Recreation Reserve in Myrrhee (64 min), the playground at Apex Park in Moyhu (62 min), the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford (80 min), Myrtleford Swimming Pool in Myrtleford (81 min), The Old School Museum in Myrtleford (80 min) and Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford (81 min).

Best for

Free days on the Whitfield - Lake Buffalo and in Rose River's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Cheshunt and Abbeyard also border Rose River.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Rose River, without traffic.
