Warragul West is in the Baw Baw area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Warragul West, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Warragul West has no train station; Drouin Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 3.9 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Warragul West, so most trips start by car. By car from Warragul West, Warragul is about 11 minutes away, Pakenham about 38 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 82 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Warragul Leisure Centre in Warragul is about 9 minutes' drive from Warragul West. For a library, Drouin Library in Drouin is about 9 minutes' drive from Warragul West. For a skate park, Warragul Skate Park in Warragul is about 9 minutes' drive from Warragul West. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 9 minutes' drive from Warragul West. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 9 minutes' drive from Warragul West. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Warragul West is Wildlife Trail in Tynong North, about 24 minutes' drive. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Warragul West, about 11 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Warragul West, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Kelly Koochew Park in Warragul (7 min), the playground at Kestle Park in Warragul (7 min), Warragul Leisure Centre in Warragul (9 min), Warragul Skate Park in Warragul (9 min), Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul (9 min) and Drouin Skate Park in Drouin (9 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Warragul West.

Nearby suburbs

Warragul, next to Warragul West, has 22 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Warragul West, Drouin has 19 playgrounds, a pool and a library.
