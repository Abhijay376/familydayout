Tim Hill Recreational Reserve

Park in Wandana Heights · Official details

Tim Hill Recreational Reserve is a park in Wandana Heights, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Roslyn Rd/Scenic Rd (bus) is a 7-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 959 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Tim Hill Recreational Reserve?
Tim Hill Recreational Reserve is in Wandana Heights, Victoria.
Is Tim Hill Recreational Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Tim Hill Recreational Reserve?
Yes, dogs are allowed.
Are there toilets at Tim Hill Recreational Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 959 m away, with a baby change table.
How do I get to Tim Hill Recreational Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Roslyn Rd/Scenic Rd (routes 43), a 7-minute walk.
