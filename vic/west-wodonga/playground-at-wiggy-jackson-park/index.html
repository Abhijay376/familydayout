Playground at Wiggy Jackson Park

Playground in West Wodonga · Basic info

Playground at Wiggy Jackson Park in West Wodonga is a public playground in the Wodonga City area. Playground at Herb Harris Park is another option about 230 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.2 km from the playground, and buses 150 and F stop at Federation Bottle Shop/Felltimber Creek Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
