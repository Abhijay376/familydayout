Gordon Craig Park

Park in West Wodonga · Basic info

Gordon Craig Park is a park in West Wodonga, in the Wodonga City Council area, good for all ages. Mayfair Dr/Moorefield Park Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1014 m away.
