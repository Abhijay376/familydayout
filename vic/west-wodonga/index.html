West Wodonga is in the Wodonga area, and our data lists 20 playgrounds and 46 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, West Wodonga has a pool.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in West Wodonga is Wonga Wetlands, covering about 500 hectares. Fell Timber Creek Nature Conservation Reserve and Gordon Craig Park are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in West Wodonga include Coyle Park, Wattle Park and Whitla Park. Playgrounds in West Wodonga include the playground at Gayview Park, the playground at Marimba Park and the playground at Hicks Reserve. Our data shows 4 public toilets in West Wodonga. In West Wodonga you'll also find Wodonga Sports & Leisure Centre. Birallee Village is the local shopping centre. The Quartz, Granite Walking Trail and Mudstone all pass through West Wodonga. Our data lists 8 cafes and family restaurants in West Wodonga for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in West Wodonga. The closest to West Wodonga is Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line), about 4.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the C, F, G and M routes stop in West Wodonga. V/Line coaches stop in West Wodonga. By car from West Wodonga, Wodonga is about 9 minutes away and Wangaratta about 49 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to West Wodonga is Upper Murray Regional Library - Wodonga in Wodonga, about 9 minutes' drive. For a beach, Finns Beach in Bandiana is about 12 minutes' drive from West Wodonga. For a skate park, James Scott Memorial Skate Park in Wodonga is about 11 minutes' drive from West Wodonga. The closest cinema to West Wodonga is Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana, about 11 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 20 playgrounds across West Wodonga. Free days on the Quartz and in West Wodonga's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to West Wodonga, Wodonga has 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to West Wodonga, Huon Creek has 1 playground.
