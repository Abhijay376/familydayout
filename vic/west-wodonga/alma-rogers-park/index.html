Alma Rogers Park

Park in West Wodonga · Basic info

Alma Rogers Park is a park in West Wodonga, in the Wodonga City Council area, good for all ages. Mayfair Dr/Lawrence St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 771 m away.
