Birallee Village

Shopping centre in West Wodonga · Official details

Birallee Village is a shopping centre in West Wodonga, in the Wodonga City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Birallee Shopping Centre/101 Melrose Dr (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 222 m away. Opening hours: Monday – Sunday 7:00am – 10:00pm; Monday – Sunday 7:00am – 10:00pm; Monday – Sunday 7:00am – 10:00pm.
