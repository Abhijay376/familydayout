Playground at John Byrne Park

Playground in West Wodonga · Basic info

Playground at John Byrne Park is a public playground in West Wodonga, in the Wodonga City area. Playground at Hicks Reserve is another option about 360 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 980 m from the playground, and buses 160 and G stop at Emerald Ave/Marshall St, a 5-minute walk away.
