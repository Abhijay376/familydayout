Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Mysia, in the Loddon area.

Landmarks

Mysia I120 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Mysia, at about 18 hectares. Mysia Nature Conservation Reserve and Mysia H47 Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

Mysia has no train station; Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 38 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Bendigo - Boort via Wedderburn route stop in Mysia. V/Line coaches stop in Mysia. By car from Mysia, Bendigo is about 1.5 hours away and Echuca about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Boort Swimming Pool in Boort is about 19 minutes' drive from Mysia. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 44 minutes' drive from Mysia. The closest places for kids by road from Mysia are A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park in Boort (18 min), Boort Court House Historical Museum in Boort (18 min), Boort Swimming Pool in Boort (19 min), Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (33 min), Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (33 min) and Rex Theatre in Charlton (44 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mysia.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Mysia, Boort has 1 playground and a pool. Borung also borders Mysia.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Mysia, without traffic.
