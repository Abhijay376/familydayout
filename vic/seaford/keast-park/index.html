Keast Park

Park in Seaford · Basic info

Keast Park is a park in Seaford, in the Frankston City Council area, good for all ages. Kalimna St/Eel Race Rd (bus) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 66 m away with a baby change table.
