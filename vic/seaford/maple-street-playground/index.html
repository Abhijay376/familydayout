Maple Street Playground

Playground in Seaford · Basic info

Maple Street Playground is a public playground in Seaford, in the Frankston City area. The Peninsula Link Trail passes about 20 m away, and Playground on Austin Road is another option about 560 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.0 km away, and bus 779 stops at Brunel Rd/Maple St, a 1-minute walk away.
