Maple Street Reserve

Park in Seaford · Basic info

Maple Street Reserve is a park in Seaford, in the Frankston City Council area, good for all ages. Maple St/Downs Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 722 m away and dogs are allowed.
