Our data lists 17 playgrounds and 16 parks and reserves in Seaford, in the Frankston area. Beyond the parks, Seaford has an indoor play centre, a beach and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Seaford is Seaford Foreshore Reserve, covering about 46 hectares. Seaford Wetland and Kananook Creek Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Seaford include Keast Park, Riviera Reserve and Seaford Reserve. Playgrounds in Seaford include Maple Street Playground, the playground at Keast Park and the playground at Kananook Reserve. Our data shows 16 public toilets in Seaford. Places to visit in Seaford include Gravity Zone Indoor Trampoline Centre and Seaford Beach. For older kids in Seaford there's the skate park at Seaford North Reserve. For walks and rides, Peninsula Link Trail, Bike: Carrum - Frankston and Kananook Creek Walk cross Seaford. Our data lists 28 cafes and family restaurants in Seaford for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Kananook Railway Station (metro: Frankston Line) and Seaford Railway Station (metro: Frankston Line) in Seaford. Buses 760, 778, 779, 780, 832, 833 and 901 stop in Seaford. By car from Seaford, Frankston is about 8 minutes away, Cranbourne about 19 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 42 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Seaford is Pines Forest Aquatic Centre in Frankston North, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest library to Seaford is Frankston Library in Frankston, about 8 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Hoyts in Frankston is about 9 minutes' drive from Seaford. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Seaford is Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale, about 22 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 17 playgrounds across Seaford. Rainy days at the indoor play centre in Seaford. Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at Seaford North Reserve in Seaford. Beach days at Seaford Beach. Free days on the Peninsula Link Trail and in Seaford's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Frankston, next to Seaford, has 27 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Seaford, Carrum Downs has 22 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Seaford, Patterson Lakes has 7 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Seaford, Frankston North has 7 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Carrum also borders Seaford.
