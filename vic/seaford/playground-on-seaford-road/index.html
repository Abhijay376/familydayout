Playground on Seaford Road

Playground in Seaford · Basic info

Playground on Seaford Road in Seaford is a public playground in the Frankston City area. The Bike: Carrum - Frankston passes about 20 m away, and McDonald's is about 260 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 300 m away, and bus 780 stops at Seaford Rd/Fortescue Ave, a 1-minute walk away.
