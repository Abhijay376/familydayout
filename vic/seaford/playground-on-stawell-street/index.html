Playground on Stawell Street

Playground in Seaford · Basic info

Playground on Stawell Street is a public playground in Seaford, in the Frankston City area. Seaford Kebab and Grill House is about 400 m away if you need food, and Playground on Sunderland Court is another option about 360 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.2 km away, and bus 760 stops at Barry St/Seaford Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
