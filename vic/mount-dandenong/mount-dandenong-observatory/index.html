Mount Dandenong Observatory

Park in Mount Dandenong · Basic info

Mount Dandenong Observatory is a park in Mount Dandenong, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Mount Dandenong Observatory/Observatory Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 226 m away.
