William Ricketts Sanctuary

Park in Mount Dandenong · Official details

William Ricketts Sanctuary is a park in Mount Dandenong, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. William Ricketts Sanctuary/Mt Dandenong Tourist Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 47 m away and dogs are not allowed. Opening hours: Daily 10am-4pm, last entry 3:30pm.
