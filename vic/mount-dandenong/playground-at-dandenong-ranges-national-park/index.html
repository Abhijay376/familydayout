Playground at Dandenong Ranges National Park

Playground in Mount Dandenong · Basic info

Playground at Dandenong Ranges National Park is a public playground in Mount Dandenong, in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. The Mechanics Walk is about 70 m away, and Playground on Old Coach Road is another option about 80 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 920 m from the playground, and bus 688 stops at 1376 Mt Dandenong Tourist Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
