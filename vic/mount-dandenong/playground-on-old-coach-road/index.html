Playground on Old Coach Road

Playground in Mount Dandenong · Basic info

This is a public playground in Mount Dandenong. The Mechanics Walk passes about 120 m away, and Playground at Dandenong Ranges National Park is another option about 80 m away. There's a public toilet about 760 m away, and bus 688 stops at Helen Rd/Mt Dandenong Tourist Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
