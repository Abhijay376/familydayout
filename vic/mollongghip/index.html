Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Mollongghip in the Hepburn area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

The Goldfields Track and Great Dividing Trail all pass through Mollongghip.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mollongghip. The closest to Mollongghip is Creswick Railway Station (V/Line), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mollongghip, so most trips start by car. By car from Mollongghip, Ballarat is about 29 minutes away, Daylesford about 31 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, children in Creswick is about 22 minutes' drive from Mollongghip. For a library, Creswick Library in Creswick is about 21 minutes' drive from Mollongghip. The closest beach to Mollongghip is Lionhead Beach in Pentland Hills, about 45 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Skate Park in Creswick is about 21 minutes' drive from Mollongghip. For a cinema, Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central is about 28 minutes' drive from Mollongghip. The closest indoor play centre to Mollongghip is Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Mollongghip is Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek, about 20 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Ballarat, about 29 minutes from Mollongghip by car, with 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Mollongghip are the playground at Bullarook Recreation Reserve in Bullarook (13 min), the playground at Bungaree Recreation Reserve in Bungaree (20 min), Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek (20 min), Creswick Library in Creswick (21 min), Skate Park in Creswick (21 min) and Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick (21 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Mollongghip on the Goldfields Track.

Nearby suburbs

Bullarook, next to Mollongghip, has 1 playground. Claretown and Barkstead also border Mollongghip.
