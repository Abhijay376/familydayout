Playground at Thomson Recreation Reserve

Playground in Thomson · Basic info

Playground at Thomson Recreation Reserve is a public playground in Thomson, in the Greater Geelong City area. The Bellarine Rail Trail passes about 380 m away, and Geelong Pizza Lovers is about 320 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 1.1 km away, and bus 31 stops at St Margarets Catholic Primary School/St Albans Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
