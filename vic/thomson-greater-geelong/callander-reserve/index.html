Callander Reserve

Park in Thomson · Basic info

Callander Reserve is a park in Thomson, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Callander St/St Albans Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 690 m away with a baby change table.
