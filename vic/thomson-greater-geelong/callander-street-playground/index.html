Callander Street Playground

Playground in Thomson · Basic info

Callander Street Playground is a public playground in Thomson, in the Greater Geelong City area. The Bellarine Rail Trail is about 260 m away, and for food, Kebabman is about 380 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 710 m from the playground, and bus 31 stops at Gordon Institute of TAFE/St Albans Rd , a 3-minute walk away.
