Playground at Mill Park

Playground in Ballan · Basic info

This is a neighbourhood playground in Ballan, looked after by Moorabool Shire Council. Older kids have a skate park about 60 m away, and Ballan Swimming Pool is about 90 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground. It's easiest by car, about 2 minutes from Ballan.
