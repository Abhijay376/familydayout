Skate park at Mill Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Ballan · Basic info

Skate park at Mill Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Ballan, in the Moorabool Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Ballan Station/Atkinson St (coach) is a 13-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 97 m away.
