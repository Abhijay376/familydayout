Playground at Harry Pottage Memorial Reserve

Playground in Macleod · Basic info

Playground at Harry Pottage Memorial Reserve is a public playground in Macleod, in the Banyule City area. The LBN1 - Watsonia to East Ivanhoe passes about 140 m away, and Playground on Birdwood Avenue is another option about 470 m away. There's a public toilet about 550 m away, and bus 561 stops at Kinlock St/Glenmore St, a 2-minute walk away.
