Playground at Dunvegan Parklands

Playground in Macleod · Basic info

Playground at Dunvegan Parklands is a public playground in Macleod, in the Banyule City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. You'll find a public toilet about 1.0 km from the playground, and bus 561 stops at Dunvegan Cres/Vincent St, a 1-minute walk away.
