John McRobbie Reserve

Park in Macleod · Basic info

John McRobbie Reserve is a park in Macleod, in the Banyule City Council area, good for all ages. Ruthven St/Macleod Pde (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 808 m away.
