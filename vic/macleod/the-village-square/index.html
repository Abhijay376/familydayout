The Village Square

Park in Macleod · Basic info

The Village Square is a park in Macleod, in the Darebin City Council area, good for all ages. Grand Ave/Springthorpe Bvd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1177 m away.
