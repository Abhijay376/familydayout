Playground at Harborne Street Reserve

Playground in Macleod · Basic info

Playground at Harborne Street Reserve is a public playground in Macleod, in the Banyule City area. The Banyule LBN7 passes about 120 m away, and Royal Australian Corps of Signals Museum is about 390 m away. There's a public toilet about 480 m away, and bus 514 stops at Delta Rd/Elder St, a 7-minute walk away.
