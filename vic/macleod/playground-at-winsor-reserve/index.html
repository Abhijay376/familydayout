Playground at Winsor Reserve

Playground in Macleod · Basic info

Playground at Winsor Reserve in Macleod is a public playground in the Banyule City area. The LBN1 - Watsonia to East Ivanhoe passes about 130 m away, and Playground at West Mayling Reserve is another option about 460 m away. There's a public toilet about 980 m away, and bus 514 stops at Torbay St/Greensborough Hwy, a 3-minute walk away.
