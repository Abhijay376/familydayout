Ouyen is in the Mildura area, and our data lists 4 playgrounds and 52 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Ouyen has a pool and a cinema.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Ouyen is Bronzewing Flora and Fauna Reserve, covering about 12,485 hectares. Timberoo Flora and Fauna Reserve and Bell Nature Conservation Reserve are the next largest. Ouyen also has Jardine Park, Ouyen Lake Park and Tiega Flora Reserve. Playgrounds in Ouyen include Jardine Park Playground and the playground at Ouyen Lake Park. Our data shows 8 public toilets in Ouyen. Places to visit in Ouyen include Roxy and Ouyen Swimming Pool. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Ouyen for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Ouyen has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 117 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Mildura - Ouyen - Sea Lake, Mildura - Horsham via Hopetoun and Ouyen - Pinnaroo via Mallee Hwy routes stop in Ouyen. V/Line coaches stop in Ouyen. By car from Ouyen, Mildura is about 77 minutes away and Swan Hill about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Walpeup Library in Walpeup is about 23 minutes' drive from Ouyen.

Best for

Younger kids at Ouyen's 4 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the cinema in Ouyen.

Nearby suburbs

Walpeup, next to Ouyen, has 1 playground and a library. Tempy, next to Ouyen, has 1 playground and a pool. Next door to Ouyen, Patchewollock has 1 playground. Mittyack, Kulwin and Hattah also border Ouyen.
