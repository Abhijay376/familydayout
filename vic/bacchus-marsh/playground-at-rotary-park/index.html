Playground at Rotary Park

Playground in Bacchus Marsh · Basic info

Playground at Rotary Park in Bacchus Marsh is a neighbourhood playground in the Moorabool Shire area. The Aqualink Central passes about 350 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 80 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 490 m away, and buses 433 and 434 stop at Rotary Park/Lerderderg St, a 1-minute walk away.
