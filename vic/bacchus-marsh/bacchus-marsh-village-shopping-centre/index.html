Bacchus Marsh Village Shopping Centre

Shopping centre in Bacchus Marsh · Basic info

Bacchus Marsh Village Shopping Centre is a shopping centre in Bacchus Marsh, in the Moorabool Shire Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Bacchus Marsh Village/Bennett St (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 286 m away with a baby change table.
