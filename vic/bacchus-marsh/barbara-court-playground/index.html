Barbara Court Playground

Playground in Bacchus Marsh · Basic info

This is a small local playground in Bacchus Marsh, looked after by Moorabool Shire Council. The Aqualink Central is about 130 m away, and Paul Saddler Swimland is about 550 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.1 km from the playground, and bus 433 stops at Clifton Dr/Gisborne Rd, a 7-minute walk away.
