Peppertree Park

Park in Bacchus Marsh · Basic info

Peppertree Park is a park in Bacchus Marsh, in the Moorabool Shire Council area, good for all ages. O'Hagan Pl/Main St (bus) is a 22-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1500 m away.
