Bacchus Marsh in the Moorabool area has 6 playgrounds and 7 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, Bacchus Marsh has a library, a pool and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bacchus Marsh is Peppertree Park, covering about 18 hectares. Masons Lane Reserve and Rotary Park are the next largest. Bacchus Marsh also has Barbara Court Reserve, Lidgett Street Reserve and Clarinda Street Reserve. Playgrounds around Bacchus Marsh include Barbara Court Playground, the playground at Rotary Park and Clarinda Street Playground. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Bacchus Marsh. Places to visit in Bacchus Marsh include Bacchus Marsh Public childrens pool and Lerderderg Library. For older kids in Bacchus Marsh there's the skate park at Rotary Park. Bacchus Marsh Village Shopping Centre is the local shopping centre. For walks and rides, Aqualink North, Aqualink East and Aqualink Central cross Bacchus Marsh. Our data lists 17 cafes and family restaurants in Bacchus Marsh for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Bacchus Marsh has no train station; Bacchus Marsh Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 1.4 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 433 and 434 stop in Bacchus Marsh. V/Line coaches stop in Bacchus Marsh. By car from Bacchus Marsh, Melton is about 14 minutes away, Sunshine about 34 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 50 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest beach to Bacchus Marsh is Lionhead Beach in Pentland Hills, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Bacchus Marsh is Reading Cinemas in Melton West, about 13 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes is about 33 minutes' drive from Bacchus Marsh. For a zoo or wildlife park, Fairy Park in Anakie is about 43 minutes' drive from Bacchus Marsh.

Best for

Younger kids at Bacchus Marsh's 6 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the library in Bacchus Marsh. Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at Rotary Park in Bacchus Marsh. Free days on the Aqualink North and in Bacchus Marsh's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Bacchus Marsh, Darley has 13 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Bacchus Marsh, Maddingley has 8 playgrounds and a skate park. Pentland Hills, next to Bacchus Marsh, has a beach. Hopetoun Park, next to Bacchus Marsh, has 2 playgrounds. Parwan and Merrimu also border Bacchus Marsh.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Bacchus Marsh, without traffic.
