Lerderderg Library, Bacchus Marsh

Library in Bacchus Marsh · Basic info

Lerderderg Library is a library in Bacchus Marsh, in the Moorabool Shire Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Bacchus Marsh Village/Bennett St (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 311 m away with a baby change table.
