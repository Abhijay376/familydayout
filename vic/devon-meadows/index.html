Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Devon Meadows in the Casey area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Devon Meadows. The closest to Devon Meadows is Cranbourne Railway Station (metro), about 7.2 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 795 and 796 stop in Devon Meadows. V/Line coaches stop in Devon Meadows. By car from Devon Meadows, Cranbourne is about 9 minutes away, Frankston about 19 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 49 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Casey RACE in Cranbourne East is about 8 minutes' drive from Devon Meadows. The closest library to Devon Meadows is Cranbourne Library in Cranbourne East, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Devon Meadows is Frankston Beach in Frankston, about 21 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Skate Park in Clyde is about 7 minutes' drive from Devon Meadows. The closest cinema to Devon Meadows is Village Cinemas in Frankston, about 17 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Zone Bowling in Frankston is about 16 minutes' drive from Devon Meadows. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Devon Meadows is Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale, about 8 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Cranbourne, about 9 minutes from Devon Meadows by car, with 27 playgrounds. From Devon Meadows, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Junction Village Recreation Reserve in Junction Village (5 min), the playground at Houlder Avenue Reserve in Junction Village (5 min), Skate Park in Clyde (7 min), Criterium Cycling Track in Cranbourne East (8 min), Cranbourne Library in Cranbourne East (8 min) and Casey RACE in Cranbourne East (8 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Devon Meadows.

Nearby suburbs

Cranbourne East, next to Devon Meadows, has 16 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Clyde, next to Devon Meadows, has 11 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Devon Meadows, Cranbourne South has 5 playgrounds and a skate park. Pearcedale, next to Devon Meadows, has 4 playgrounds, a zoo or wildlife park and a skate park. Junction Village, Botanic Ridge, Tooradin and Blind Bight also border Devon Meadows.
