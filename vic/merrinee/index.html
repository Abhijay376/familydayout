Our data lists 5 parks and reserves in Merrinee, in the Mildura area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Merrinee is Morkalla - Red Cliffs Railway Bushland Reserve, covering about 564 hectares. Merrinee Bushland Reserve and Pirlta Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Merrinee also has Karawinna Bushland Reserve and Former Recreation Reserve (southern section). Our data shows 1 public toilet in Merrinee. For walks and rides, Former Morkalla-Redcliffs Railway crosses Merrinee.

Getting around

There's no train station in Merrinee. The closest to Merrinee is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 194 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Merrinee, so most trips start by car. By car from Merrinee, Mildura is about 45 minutes away and Swan Hill about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Merbein Pool in Merbein is about 45 minutes' drive from Merrinee. The closest library to Merrinee is Mildura Mobile Library stops here in Werrimull, about 15 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Skatepark in Red Cliffs is about 31 minutes' drive from Merrinee. Mildura is the closest bigger centre to Merrinee, about 45 minutes away by car, and has 26 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Merrinee are Mildura Mobile Library stops here in Werrimull (15 min), the playground at Barclay Square in Red Cliffs (30 min), Woodbine Park Playground in Red Cliffs (30 min), Red Cliffs Military Museum in Red Cliffs (30 min), A S Kenyon Library Red Cliffs in Red Cliffs (30 min) and Work Space Gallery 3496 in Red Cliffs (30 min).

Best for

Free days on the Former Morkalla-Redcliffs Railway and in Merrinee's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Wargan, next to Merrinee, has 6 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Merrinee, Werrimull has a library. Next door to Merrinee, Cullulleraine has 1 playground. Merbein South, Koorlong and Carwarp also border Merrinee.
