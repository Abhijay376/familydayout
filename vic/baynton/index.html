Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Baynton, in the Macedon Ranges area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Baynton is Baynton I16 Bushland Reserve, covering about 3 hectares.

Getting around

Baynton has no train station; Kyneton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Baynton, so most trips start by car. By car from Baynton, Kyneton is about 34 minutes away, Castlemaine about 61 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 87 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore is about 33 minutes' drive from Baynton. The closest library to Baynton is Kyneton Library in Kyneton, about 35 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton is about 35 minutes' drive from Baynton. Kyneton is the closest bigger centre to Baynton, about 34 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds and a library. From Baynton, the nearest places for kids by road are Jean Haynes Playground in Kyneton (34 min), Kyneton Museum in Kyneton (34 min), Kyneton Library in Kyneton (35 min), Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton (35 min), Kyneton Community Park in Kyneton (36 min) and Romsey Library in Romsey (25 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Baynton.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Baynton, Tooborac has 1 playground. Glenhope East, Glenhope, Benloch and Barfold also border Baynton.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Baynton, without traffic.
