Fred Drury Reserve

Park in Maffra · Basic info

Fred Drury Reserve is a park in Maffra, in the Wellington Shire Council area, good for all ages. Munro St/Powerscourt St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 822 m away.
