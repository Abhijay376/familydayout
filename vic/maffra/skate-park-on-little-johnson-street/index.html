Skate park on Little Johnson Street

Skate, BMX or pump track in Maffra · Basic info

Skate park on Little Johnson Street is a skate, bmx or pump track in Maffra, in the Wellington Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Pearson St/Johnson St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 219 m away.
