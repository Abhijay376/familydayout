Mafeking Hill Reserve Playground

Playground in Maffra · Basic info

Mafeking Hill Reserve Playground is a public playground in Maffra, in the Wellington Shire area. The Gippsland Plains Rail Trail passes about 370 m away, and The Food Store is about 300 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 300 m away, and bus Maffra town service stops at Munro St/Powerscourt St, a 6-minute walk away.
