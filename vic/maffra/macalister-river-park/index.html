Macalister River Park

Park in Maffra · Basic info

Macalister River Park is a park in Maffra, in the Wellington Shire Council area, good for all ages. Pearson St/Johnson St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 12 m away with a baby change table.
