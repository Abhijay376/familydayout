Tonghi Creek in the East Gippsland area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tonghi Creek is Serpentine Creek Education Area, covering about 524 hectares. Tonghi Creek Streamside Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tonghi Creek. The closest to Tonghi Creek is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 130 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tonghi Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Tonghi Creek, Lakes Entrance is about 1.5 hours away and Bairnsdale about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest skate park to Tonghi Creek is the skate park at Mick Baum Park in Cann River, about 9 minutes' drive. From Tonghi Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Mick Baum Park in Cann River (8 min) and the skate park at Mick Baum Park in Cann River (9 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tonghi Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Tamboon, next to Tonghi Creek, has a beach. Cann River, next to Tonghi Creek, has 1 playground and a skate park.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Tonghi Creek, without traffic.
