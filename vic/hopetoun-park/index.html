Our data lists 2 playgrounds in Hopetoun Park, in the Moorabool area.

Landmarks

Playgrounds around Hopetoun Park include the playground at Carberry Drive Recreation Reserve and the playground at Hammond Circuit Recreation Reserve.

Getting around

Hopetoun Park has no train station; Bacchus Marsh Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 5.7 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Hopetoun Park, so most trips start by car. By car from Hopetoun Park, Melton is about 8 minutes away, Bacchus Marsh about 10 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 43 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Hopetoun Park is Melton Waves in Melton, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest library to Hopetoun Park is Lerderderg Library in Bacchus Marsh, about 9 minutes' drive. For a beach, Lionhead Beach in Pentland Hills is about 20 minutes' drive from Hopetoun Park. For a skate park, the skate park at Cambrian Way Reserve in Melton West is about 9 minutes' drive from Hopetoun Park. The closest cinema to Hopetoun Park is Reading Cinemas in Melton West, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Hopetoun Park is Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes, about 27 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville is about 44 minutes' drive from Hopetoun Park. The nearest larger centre is Melton, about 8 minutes from Hopetoun Park by car, with 15 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Hopetoun Park, the nearest places for kids by road are the skate park at Cambrian Way Reserve in Melton West (9 min), Reading Cinemas in Melton West (7 min), Melton Waves in Melton (8 min), Lerderderg Library in Bacchus Marsh (9 min), the skate park at Rotary Park in Bacchus Marsh (10 min) and Melton Library & Learning Hub in Melton (8 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Hopetoun Park.

Nearby suburbs

Bacchus Marsh, next to Hopetoun Park, has 6 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Hopetoun Park, Melton West has 8 playgrounds, a cinema and a skate park. Brookfield, next to Hopetoun Park, has 9 playgrounds. Merrimu and Long Forest also border Hopetoun Park.
