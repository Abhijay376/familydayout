Getting around

Newlyn North has no train station; Creswick Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 9.7 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Hepburn - Creswick via Daylesford route stop in Newlyn North. V/Line coaches stop in Newlyn North. By car from Newlyn North, Daylesford is about 15 minutes away, Ballarat about 26 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Infants in Creswick is about 10 minutes' drive from Newlyn North. The closest library to Newlyn North is Creswick Library in Creswick, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Newlyn North is Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick, about 10 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central is about 25 minutes' drive from Newlyn North. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 26 minutes' drive from Newlyn North. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Newlyn North is Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek, about 18 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Daylesford, about 15 minutes from Newlyn North by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Newlyn North are Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick (10 min), Infants in Creswick (10 min), (Diving tower, restricted) in Creswick (10 min), Creswick Library in Creswick (10 min), Skate Park in Creswick (10 min) and Apex Park Playground in Creswick (11 min).
