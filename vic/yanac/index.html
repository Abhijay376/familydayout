Our data lists 6 parks and reserves in Yanac, in the Hindmarsh area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Yanac is Yanac Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 201 hectares. Millers Rd No.1 Pit Bushland Reserve and Dahwedarre Wildlife Reserve (hunting) are the next largest. Yanac also has Yanac Recreation Reserve, Yanac-a-yanac I44 Bushland Reserve and Yanac-a-yanac I45 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Yanac has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 185 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yanac, so most trips start by car. By car from Yanac, Horsham is about 85 minutes away and Stawell about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Nhill Library in Nhill is about 27 minutes' drive from Yanac. For a skate park, Nhill Skate Park in Nhill is about 28 minutes' drive from Yanac. The closest places for kids by road from Yanac are Gallery Central in Nhill (27 min), Nhill Skate Park in Nhill (28 min), Wagonin in Nhill (28 min), Nhill Library in Nhill (27 min), Nhill Playground in Nhill (28 min) and the playground at Goldsworthy Park in Nhill (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Yanac.

Nearby suburbs
