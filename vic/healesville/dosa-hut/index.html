Dosa Hut

Cafe or restaurant in Healesville · Basic info

Dosa Hut is a indian cafe or restaurant in Healesville, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, a place to eat during a day out. Don Rd/Maroondah Hwy (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 314 m away with a baby change table.
