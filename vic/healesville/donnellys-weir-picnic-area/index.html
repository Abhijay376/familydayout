Donnellys Weir Picnic Area

Park in Healesville · Official details

Donnellys Weir Picnic Area is a park in Healesville, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. The nearest public toilet is about 6 m away and dogs are not allowed. Opening hours: opening_hours: 24/7.
