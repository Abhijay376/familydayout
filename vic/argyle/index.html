Argyle in the Greater Bendigo area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Spring Plains Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Argyle, at about 1,360 hectares.

Getting around

Argyle has no train station; Goornong Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 42 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Argyle, so most trips start by car. By car from Argyle, Seymour is about 38 minutes away, Bendigo about 44 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Argyle is Memorial Swimming pool in Seymour, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest library to Argyle is Heathcote Library in Heathcote, about 4 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Argyle is Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote, about 4 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 41 minutes' drive from Argyle. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 38 minutes from Argyle by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Argyle, the nearest places for kids by road are Mudwood Studio in Heathcote (3 min), Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote (4 min), Heathcote Library in Heathcote (4 min), Heathcote Play Space in Heathcote (4 min), Powder Magazine in Heathcote (6 min) and Tooborac Recreation Reserve Playground in Tooborac (11 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Argyle.

Nearby suburbs

Heathcote, next to Argyle, has 1 playground, a library and a skate park. Next door to Argyle, Tooborac has 1 playground.
