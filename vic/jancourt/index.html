Jancourt is in the Corangamite area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Jancourt, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Jancourt. The closest to Jancourt is Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line), about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Jancourt, so most trips start by car. By car from Jancourt, Colac is about 46 minutes away, Warrnambool about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Jancourt is Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon, about 28 minutes' drive. For a beach, Rutledge Creek Beach in Port Campbell is about 40 minutes' drive from Jancourt. For a skate park, Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown is about 24 minutes' drive from Jancourt. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 46 minutes from Jancourt by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Jancourt, the nearest places for kids by road are Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (24 min), the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve in Camperdown (26 min), the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (28 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (28 min), Rutledge Creek Beach in Port Campbell (40 min) and Sherbrooke Estuary in Port Campbell (41 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Jancourt.
