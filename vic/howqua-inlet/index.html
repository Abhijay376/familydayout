Howqua Inlet is in the Mansfield area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Howqua Inlet is Howqua G120 Bushland Reserve, covering about 3 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Howqua Inlet. The closest to Howqua Inlet is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 76 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Mansfield - Woods Point via Jamieson route stop in Howqua Inlet. By car from Howqua Inlet, Mansfield is about 22 minutes away, Benalla about 73 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.
