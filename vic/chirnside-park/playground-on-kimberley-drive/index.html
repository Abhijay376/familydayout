Playground on Kimberley Drive

Playground in Chirnside Park · Basic info

Playground on Kimberley Drive is a public playground in Chirnside Park, in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 390 m away, and bus 677 stops at Vista Dr/Edward Rd, a 8-minute walk away.
