Chirnside Park in the Yarra Ranges area has 9 playgrounds and 8 parks and reserves in our data. Chirnside Park also has a pool, a cinema and 3 indoor play centres.

Landmarks

Cherry Hill Way Reserve is the largest mapped park in Chirnside Park, at about 5 hectares. Botanica Reserve and Goldentree Drive Reserve are the next largest. Chirnside Park also has Lancaster Reserve, The Parkway Reserve and Billanook Way Reserve. Playgrounds in Chirnside Park include Cloverlea Playground, The Parkway Playground and Delamere Drive Playground. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Chirnside Park. In Chirnside Park you'll also find Reading Cinemas, Ace Space Indoor Play, GAMR Laser Tag & VR and Kingswim. Chirnside Homemaker Centre and Chirnside Park Shopping Centre are the main shopping centres. For walks and rides, Green Spine Shared Path and Brushy Creek Trail cross Chirnside Park. Our data lists 15 cafes and family restaurants in Chirnside Park for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Chirnside Park. The closest to Chirnside Park is Lilydale Railway Station (metro), about 1.7 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 664, 670, 671, 672, 675, 677, 679, 683 and 684 stop in Chirnside Park. By car from Chirnside Park, Lilydale is about 4 minutes away, Ringwood about 13 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 40 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Chirnside Park is Lilydale Library in Lilydale, about 6 minutes' drive. For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 38 minutes' drive from Chirnside Park. The closest skate park to Chirnside Park is Lilydale Skate Bowl in Lilydale, about 5 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Lyre Bird enclosure in Badger Creek is about 28 minutes' drive from Chirnside Park.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 9 playgrounds across Chirnside Park. Rainy days at the cinema and indoor play centre in Chirnside Park. Free days on the Green Spine Shared Path and in Chirnside Park's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Chirnside Park, Croydon has 24 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Chirnside Park, Mooroolbark has 18 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Chirnside Park, Lilydale has 12 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Chirnside Park, Croydon North has 10 playgrounds. Wonga Park, Christmas Hills and Coldstream also border Chirnside Park.
