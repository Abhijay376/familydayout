Ace Space Indoor Play

Indoor play in Chirnside Park · Official details

Ace Space Indoor Play is a indoor play in Chirnside Park, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, best for younger kids (check the venue's age and height rules). Old Melbourne Rd/Maroondah Hwy (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 306 m away. Opening hours: Monday to Friday (School Term & Summer Holidays):9:30am-3:30pm,; Saturday and Sunday & School Holidays: 9:30am-5.00pm..
