Chirnside Homemaker Centre

Shopping centre in Chirnside Park · Official details

Chirnside Homemaker Centre is a shopping centre in Chirnside Park, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Old Melbourne Rd/Maroondah Hwy (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 138 m away. Opening hours: Monday - Thursday 9:00am - 5:00pm; Friday 9:00am - 6:00pm; Saturday 9:00am - 5:00pm; Sunday 10:00am - 5:00pm; Monday - Thursday 8:00am - 9:00pm; Friday 8:00am - 8:00pm; Saturday 8:00am - 12:30pm.
