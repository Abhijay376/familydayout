Kingswim, Chirnside Park

Pool or water play in Chirnside Park · Official details

Kingswim is a pool or water play in Chirnside Park, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Maroondah Golf Park/Maroondah Hwy (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 684 m away. Opening hours: Mon, Tue 08:15-20:15; Thu, Wed 08:15-19:45; Fri 08:15-18:45; Sat, Sun 07:30-12:15.
