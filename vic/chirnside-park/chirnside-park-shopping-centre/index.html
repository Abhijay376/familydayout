Chirnside Park Shopping Centre

Shopping centre in Chirnside Park · Official details

Chirnside Park Shopping Centre is a shopping centre in Chirnside Park, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Chirnside Park SC/Maroondah Hwy (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 218 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: Monday - Friday from 8:45am until 9:30pm; Saturday - Sunday from 8:45am to 9:30pm.
