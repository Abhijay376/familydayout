The Parkway Playground

Playground in Chirnside Park · Basic info

The Parkway Playground in Chirnside Park is a public playground in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 990 m from the playground, and bus 677 stops at West Haven Ct/Edward Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
