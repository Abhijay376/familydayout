Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Whitfield. For walks and rides, Whitfield - Lake Buffalo and Vines 2 Vines: Whitfield to Milawa cross Whitfield.

Getting around

There's no train station in Whitfield. The closest to Whitfield is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 46 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the King Valley-Wangaratta route stop in Whitfield. By car from Whitfield, Wangaratta is about 42 minutes away and Mansfield about 51 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Splashpark in Wangaratta is about 38 minutes' drive from Whitfield. For a library, Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta is about 42 minutes' drive from Whitfield. The closest skate park to Whitfield is the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta, about 39 minutes' drive. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Whitfield, about 42 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Whitfield are Myrrhee Recreation Reserve in Myrrhee (19 min), the playground at Apex Park in Moyhu (18 min), Splashpark in Wangaratta (38 min), the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta (39 min), Cobb & Co in Glenrowan (38 min) and Glenrowan Museum in Glenrowan (38 min).

Best for

Free days on the Whitfield - Lake Buffalo and in Whitfield's parks.
