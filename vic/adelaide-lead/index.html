Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Adelaide Lead, in the Central Goldfields area.

Landmarks

Paddys Ranges State Park is the largest mapped park in Adelaide Lead, at about 2,015 hectares. Adelaide Lead Streamside Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Adelaide Lead. For walks and rides, Talbot Heritage Trail, Paddys Range Walking track Southern loop and Paddys Range Walking track Northern Loop cross Adelaide Lead.

Getting around

There's no train station in Adelaide Lead. The closest to Adelaide Lead is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 6.4 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Adelaide Lead, so most trips start by car. By car from Adelaide Lead, Maryborough is about 10 minutes away, Castlemaine about 54 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot is about 25 minutes' drive from Adelaide Lead. For a library, Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough is about 10 minutes' drive from Adelaide Lead. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 10 minutes from Adelaide Lead by car, with a library. From Adelaide Lead, the nearest places for kids by road are Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (10 min), the playground at Market Reserve in Carisbrook (17 min), Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground in Talbot (25 min), Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (25 min), Courthouse in Avoca (22 min) and Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (34 min).

Best for

Free days on the Talbot Heritage Trail and in Adelaide Lead's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Adelaide Lead, Maryborough has a library. Amherst also borders Adelaide Lead.
