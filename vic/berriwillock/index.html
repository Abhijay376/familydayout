Our data lists 4 parks and reserves in Berriwillock, in the Buloke area. On top of that, Berriwillock has a pool.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Berriwillock is Berriwillock Bushland Reserve, covering about 20 hectares. Boigbeat Tank Bushland Reserve and Angels Rest Flora Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Berriwillock include Tynan Park. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Berriwillock. Places to visit in Berriwillock include Berriwillock Swimming Pool.

Getting around

There's no train station in Berriwillock. The closest to Berriwillock is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 61 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Berriwillock. By car from Berriwillock, Swan Hill is about 53 minutes away and Horsham about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest skate park to Berriwillock is Birchip Skate Park in Birchip, about 34 minutes' drive. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Berriwillock, about 53 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Berriwillock, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Culgoa Memorial Park in Culgoa (12 min), the playground at Apex Park in Sea Lake (16 min), Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake (16 min), Birchip Skate Park in Birchip (34 min) and Birchip History Museum in Birchip (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Berriwillock.

Nearby suburbs

Culgoa, next to Berriwillock, has 1 playground. Boigbeat and Banyan also border Berriwillock.
