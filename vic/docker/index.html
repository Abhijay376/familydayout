Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Docker in the Wangaratta area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Docker has no train station; Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Docker, so most trips start by car. By car from Docker, Wangaratta is about 19 minutes away and Benalla about 38 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Splashpark in Wangaratta is about 16 minutes' drive from Docker. The closest library to Docker is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 19 minutes' drive. For a beach, Sandy Beach in Beechworth is about 44 minutes' drive from Docker. For a skate park, the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta is about 16 minutes' drive from Docker. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 19 minutes' drive from Docker. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Docker, about 19 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Docker are the playground at Apex Park in Moyhu (6 min), the playground at John McAleese Community Park in Milawa (13 min), Splashpark in Wangaratta (16 min), the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta (16 min), Moore than Swimming in Wangaratta (17 min) and Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (19 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Docker.
