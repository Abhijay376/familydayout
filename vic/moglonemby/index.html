Landmarks

The biggest green space in Moglonemby is Moglonemby Bushland Reserve, covering about 14 hectares. Seven Creeks Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Playgrounds around Moglonemby include Moglonemby Hall Playground.

Getting around

There's no train station in Moglonemby. The closest to Moglonemby is Euroa Railway Station (V/Line), about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Moglonemby, so most trips start by car. By car from Moglonemby, Shepparton is about 41 minutes away, Benalla about 44 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.
