Gelliondale is in the Wellington area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Gelliondale, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

The Great Southern Rail Trail passes through Gelliondale.

Getting around

Gelliondale has no train station; Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 47 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gelliondale, so most trips start by car. By car from Gelliondale, Leongatha is about 63 minutes away, Sale about 70 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Gelliondale is Yarram Public Pool in Yarram, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest library to Gelliondale is Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram, about 12 minutes' drive. For a beach, Chinaman Beach in Wilsons Promontory is about 24 minutes' drive from Gelliondale. For a skate park, the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram is about 13 minutes' drive from Gelliondale. From Gelliondale, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Memorial Park in Yarram (12 min), Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram (12 min), Yarram Public Pool in Yarram (13 min), Port Albert Maritime Museum in Port Albert (13 min), the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram (13 min) and Yarram Historical Society Museum in Yarram (14 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Gelliondale on the Great Southern Rail Trail.
