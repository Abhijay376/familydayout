Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Icy Creek in the Baw Baw area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Icy Creek has no train station; Yarragon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 36 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Icy Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Icy Creek, Warragul is about 47 minutes away, Traralgon about 63 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Icy Creek is Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Icy Creek is Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South, about 29 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Warragul, about 47 minutes from Icy Creek by car, with 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Icy Creek are the playground at LaTrobe and Loch Rivers, Noojee Streamside Reserve in Noojee (13 min), the playground at Blue Rock Lake West picnic area in Willow Grove (25 min), Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South (29 min), Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar (38 min), Moe Skate Park in Moe (40 min) and Diving Pool in Moe (40 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Icy Creek.
