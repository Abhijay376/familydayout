Landmarks

Marmal Flora Reserve is the largest mapped park in Barraport West, at about 11 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Barraport West. The closest to Barraport West is Kerang Railway Station (V/Line), about 45 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Barraport West, so most trips start by car. By car from Barraport West, Swan Hill is about 1.5 hours away and Bendigo about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Barraport West is Boort Swimming Pool in Boort, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest library to Barraport West is Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook, about 32 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 39 minutes' drive from Barraport West. From Barraport West, the nearest places for kids by road are Boort Court House Historical Museum in Boort (27 min), A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park in Boort (28 min), Boort Swimming Pool in Boort (28 min), Quambatook Lions Club Playground in Quambatook (32 min), Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook (32 min) and Quambatook Historical Centre in Quambatook (32 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Barraport West.
