Natya is in the Swan Hill area, and our data lists 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Piangil - Yungera Railway Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Natya, at about 63 hectares. Possum Flat Bushland Reserve and Natya Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Natya also has Coonimur I56 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Natya. The closest to Natya is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 53 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Natya, so most trips start by car. By car from Natya, Swan Hill is about 52 minutes away and Mildura about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest skate park to Natya is the bmx track at Piangil Memorial Park in Piangil, about 19 minutes' drive. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Natya, about 52 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Natya are the playground at Mensforth Park in Piangil (18 min), the playground at Piangil Memorial Park in Piangil (19 min) and the bmx track at Piangil Memorial Park in Piangil (19 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Natya.

Nearby suburbs

Manangatang, next to Natya, has 1 playground and a pool. Next door to Natya, Piangil has 2 playgrounds and a skate park. Bolton also borders Natya.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Natya, without traffic.
