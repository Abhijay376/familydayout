Landmarks

The biggest green space in Fairy Dell (East Gippsland) is Fairy Dell Flora Reserve, covering about 66 hectares. For walks and rides, Plane Crash Loop, Berry Farm Loop and Explosive Magazine Loop cross Fairy Dell (East Gippsland).

Getting around

Fairy Dell (East Gippsland) has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Fairy Dell (East Gippsland), so most trips start by car. By car from Fairy Dell (East Gippsland), Bairnsdale is about 33 minutes away and Lakes Entrance about 51 minutes, without traffic.
