Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Minjah in the Moyne area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Minjah. The closest to Minjah is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 39 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Minjah, so most trips start by car. By car from Minjah, Warrnambool is about 53 minutes away and Hamilton about 62 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Minjah is Hawkesdale Swimming Pool in Hawkesdale, about 22 minutes' drive. For a library, Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake is about 44 minutes' drive from Minjah. The closest beach to Minjah is Reef Point Beach in Port Fairy, about 45 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Minjah is Koroit Youth Space in Koroit, about 41 minutes' drive. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Minjah, about 53 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Minjah, the nearest places for kids by road are Hawkesdale Swimming Pool in Hawkesdale (22 min), Volcanoes Discovery Centre in Penshurst (36 min), the playground at Kirkstall Recreation Reserve in Kirkstall (37 min), Koroit Youth Space in Koroit (41 min), Penshurst Botanic Gardens Playground in Penshurst (36 min) and Penshurst Swimming Pool in Penshurst (36 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Minjah.

Nearby suburbs
