Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Purdeet in the Moyne area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Purdeet has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 51 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Purdeet, so most trips start by car. By car from Purdeet, Hamilton is about 43 minutes away and Warrnambool about 53 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Purdeet is Penshurst Swimming Pool in Penshurst, about 17 minutes' drive. For a library, Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake is about 38 minutes' drive from Purdeet. For a skate park, Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake is about 39 minutes' drive from Purdeet. The closest cinema to Purdeet is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 43 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 43 minutes from Purdeet by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Purdeet are Penshurst Rec Reserve Playground in Penshurst (17 min), Penshurst Swimming Pool in Penshurst (17 min), Penshurst Botanic Gardens Playground in Penshurst (18 min), Volcanoes Discovery Centre in Penshurst (18 min), Hawkesdale Swimming Pool in Hawkesdale (29 min) and Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake (38 min).
