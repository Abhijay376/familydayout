Mirranatwa is in the Southern Grampians area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Mirranatwa is Mirranatwa Bushland Reserve, covering about 43 hectares. Mirranatwa also has Grampians National Park (Mirranatwa Lot 188). Our data shows 2 off-leash dog parks in Mirranatwa. The Teddy Bear Gap Loop and Teddy Bear Gap Track all pass through Mirranatwa.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mirranatwa. The closest to Mirranatwa is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 48 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mirranatwa, so most trips start by car. By car from Mirranatwa, Hamilton is about 53 minutes away and Stawell about 63 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Halls Gap Swimming Pool in Halls Gap is about 39 minutes' drive from Mirranatwa. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 53 minutes from Mirranatwa by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Mirranatwa, the nearest places for kids by road are Dunkeld Memorial Park Playground in Dunkeld (26 min), Willaura Outdoor Pool in Willaura (54 min), MOCO Gallery in Halls Gap (34 min), Brambuk Cultural Centre in Halls Gap (37 min), the playground at Halls Gap Picnic Reserve in Halls Gap (39 min) and Halls Gap Swimming Pool in Halls Gap (39 min).

Best for

Free days on the Teddy Bear Gap Loop and in Mirranatwa's parks.
