Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Campbells Forest, in the Loddon area.

Landmarks

Myers Creek Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Campbells Forest, at about 30 hectares. Leichardt Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Campbells Forest has no train station; Raywood Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Campbells Forest, so most trips start by car. By car from Campbells Forest, Bendigo is about 25 minutes away, Castlemaine about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood is about 20 minutes' drive from Campbells Forest. For a library, Bendigo Library in Bendigo is about 24 minutes' drive from Campbells Forest. For a skate park, Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk is about 17 minutes' drive from Campbells Forest. The closest cinema to Campbells Forest is Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo, about 25 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 27 minutes' drive from Campbells Forest. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 27 minutes' drive from Campbells Forest. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 25 minutes from Campbells Forest by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Campbells Forest, the nearest places for kids by road are Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood (20 min), Peter Krenz Leisure Centre in Eaglehawk (17 min), Eaglehawk Playspace in Eaglehawk (17 min), Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk (17 min), the playground at Canterbury Park in Eaglehawk (17 min) and McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk (18 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Campbells Forest.

Nearby suburbs

Raywood, next to Campbells Forest, has 1 playground and a pool. Leichardt, Derby and Bridgewater also border Campbells Forest.
