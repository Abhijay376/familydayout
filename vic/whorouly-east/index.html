Whorouly East is in the Wangaratta area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Whorouly East, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Whorouly East. The closest to Whorouly East is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 33 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Whorouly East, so most trips start by car. By car from Whorouly East, Wangaratta is about 37 minutes away and Bright about 40 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford is about 14 minutes' drive from Whorouly East. The closest library to Whorouly East is Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford, about 13 minutes' drive. For a beach, Sandy Beach in Beechworth is about 28 minutes' drive from Whorouly East. For a skate park, Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford is about 13 minutes' drive from Whorouly East. The closest cinema to Whorouly East is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 34 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 37 minutes from Whorouly East by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Whorouly East, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Rotary Park in Myrtleford (11 min), The Old School Museum in Myrtleford (13 min), Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford (13 min), Cundy Park Playground in Myrtleford (13 min), Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford (13 min) and the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford (14 min).
