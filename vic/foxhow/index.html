Foxhow is in the Corangamite area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Foxhow, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Foxhow has no train station; Colac Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 35 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Foxhow, so most trips start by car. By car from Foxhow, Colac is about 46 minutes away, Ballarat about 70 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Lismore Pool in Lismore is about 17 minutes' drive from Foxhow. For a library, Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum is about 26 minutes' drive from Foxhow. For a skate park, Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown is about 30 minutes' drive from Foxhow. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Foxhow, about 46 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Foxhow are the playground at Cressy Picnic Area in Cressy (14 min), Lismore Pool in Lismore (17 min), Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum (26 min), Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (30 min), Rokewood Recreation Reserve Playground in Rokewood (36 min) and Rokewood Skate Park in Rokewood (36 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Foxhow.

Nearby suburbs

Lismore, next to Foxhow, has a pool. Cundare North and Berrybank also border Foxhow.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Foxhow, without traffic.
