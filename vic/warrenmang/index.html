Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Warrenmang, in the Pyrenees area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Warrenmang is Warrenmang Bushland Reserve, covering about 12 hectares. Warrenmang also has Camerons Track Campground. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Warrenmang. Picnic areas in Warrenmang include Camerons Track Campground. The Pyrenees Endurance Walk passes through Warrenmang.

Getting around

Warrenmang has no train station; Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 44 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Warrenmang, so most trips start by car. By car from Warrenmang, Maryborough is about 52 minutes away, Stawell about 69 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 52 minutes from Warrenmang by car, with a library. From Warrenmang, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Avoca Lions Park in Avoca (31 min), Courthouse in Avoca (30 min), Navarre Playground in Navarre (39 min), Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (56 min) and Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (52 min).

Best for

Free days on the Pyrenees Endurance Walk and in Warrenmang's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Warrenmang, Landsborough has 1 playground. Avoca, next to Warrenmang, has 1 playground. Nowhere Creek, Moonambel, Glenpatrick and Glenlofty also border Warrenmang.
