Catumnal is in the Loddon area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Boort (1) Flora Reserve is the largest mapped park in Catumnal, at about 21 hectares. Marmal H1 Bushland Reserve and Marmal H2 Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Catumnal. The closest to Catumnal is Kerang Railway Station (V/Line), about 46 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Catumnal, so most trips start by car. By car from Catumnal, Bendigo is about 1.5 hours away and Swan Hill about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Boort Swimming Pool in Boort is about 12 minutes' drive from Catumnal. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 38 minutes' drive from Catumnal. From Catumnal, the nearest places for kids by road are Boort Court House Historical Museum in Boort (10 min), A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park in Boort (11 min), Boort Swimming Pool in Boort (12 min), the playground at Centenary Park in Wycheproof (33 min), Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof (33 min) and Willandra Museum in Wycheproof (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Catumnal.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Catumnal, Boort has 1 playground and a pool. Barraport also borders Catumnal.
