Playground at Rayner Reserve

Playground in Oak Park · Official details

Playground at Rayner Reserve in Oak Park is a public playground in the Merri-Bek City area. Playground at H.M. Dowd Reserve is another option about 590 m away. There's a public toilet about 60 m away, and bus 542 stops at Rayner Reserve/Devon Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
