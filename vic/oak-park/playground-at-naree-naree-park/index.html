Playground at Naree Naree Park

Playground in Oak Park · Basic info

Playground at Naree Naree Park is a public playground in Oak Park, in the Merri-Bek City area. The Glenroy link to Moonee Ponds Creek Trail is about 170 m away, and Playground at G.E. Clarke Reserve is another option about 570 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 960 m from the playground, and buses 513 and 514 stop at Esperanto Ct/Plumpton Ave, a 3-minute walk away.
