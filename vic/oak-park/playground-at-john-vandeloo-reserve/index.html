Playground at John Vandeloo Reserve

Playground in Oak Park · Basic info

This is a public playground in Oak Park. The Traditional Fish & Chips Shop is about 300 m away if you need food, and Playground at Stevenson Reserve is another option about 130 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 490 m away, and trains stop at Oak Park Railway Station, a 5-minute walk away.
