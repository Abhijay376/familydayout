Chelsea in the Kingston area has 3 playgrounds and 5 parks and reserves in our data. Beyond the parks, Chelsea has a library, a beach and a skate park.

Landmarks

Chelsea Bicentennial Park is the largest mapped park in Chelsea, at about 30 hectares. Chelsea Recreation Reserve and Chelsea Park are the next largest. Chelsea also has Victory Park and Woolepe Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds around Chelsea include the playground at Victory Park and the playground at Chelsea Bicentennial Park. Our data shows 7 public toilets in Chelsea. In Chelsea you'll also find Chelsea Library and Chelsea Beach. For older kids in Chelsea there's Chelsea Skate Park. Chelsea Market is the local shopping centre. The Longbeach Trail passes through Chelsea. Our data lists 12 cafes and family restaurants in Chelsea for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Chelsea Railway Station (metro: Frankston Line) in Chelsea. Buses 706, 857, 858 and 902 stop in Chelsea. By car from Chelsea, Frankston is about 15 minutes away, Dandenong about 17 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 37 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Grenda Aquatic Centre in Keysborough is about 11 minutes' drive from Chelsea. The closest cinema to Chelsea is Village Cinemas in Cheltenham, about 16 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Gravity Zone Indoor Trampoline Centre in Seaford is about 9 minutes' drive from Chelsea. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Chelsea is Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale, about 24 minutes' drive. Frankston is the closest bigger centre to Chelsea, about 15 minutes away by car, and has 27 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Chelsea's 3 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the library in Chelsea. Teens who skate or ride, with Chelsea Skate Park in Chelsea. Beach days at Chelsea Beach. Free days on the Longbeach Trail and in Chelsea's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Edithvale, next to Chelsea, has 3 playgrounds, a beach and a skate park. Next door to Chelsea, Bonbeach has 3 playgrounds and a beach. Chelsea Heights, next to Chelsea, has 4 playgrounds.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Chelsea, without traffic.
