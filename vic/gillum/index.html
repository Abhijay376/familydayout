Gillum is in the Wellington area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Gillum is Ben Cruachan State Forest, covering about 21,794 hectares. For walks and rides, Dolodrook Track crosses Gillum.

Getting around

There's no train station in Gillum. The closest to Gillum is Rosedale Railway Station (V/Line), about 44 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gillum, so most trips start by car. By car from Gillum, Sale is about 82 minutes away and Traralgon about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest places for kids by road from Gillum are Coongulla Community Centre Reserve Playground in Coongulla (45 min), Play Equipment in Newry (58 min), Pump Track in Heyfield (54 min), Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield (54 min) and Heyfield and District Museum in Heyfield (55 min).

Best for

Free days on the Dolodrook Track and in Gillum's parks.
