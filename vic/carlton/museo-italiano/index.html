Museo Italiano

Museum or gallery in Carlton · Official details

Museo Italiano is a museum or gallery in Carlton, in the Melbourne City Council area, best for school-age kids. Faraday St/Lygon St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 56 m away. Opening hours: Tuesday - Friday: 10 am - 5pm; Saturday: 1pm - 5pm.
