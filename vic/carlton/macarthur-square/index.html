Macarthur Square

Park in Carlton · Basic info

Macarthur Square is a park in Carlton, in the Melbourne City Council area, good for all ages. Rathdowne St/Elgin St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 152 m away.
