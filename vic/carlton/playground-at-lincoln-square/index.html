Playground at Lincoln Square

Playground in Carlton · Official details

Playground at Lincoln Square is a public playground in Carlton, in the Melbourne City area. The Swanston St: west side is about 70 m away, and Law Library is about 260 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 250 m from the playground, and 8 tram routes stop at Lincoln Square/Swanston St #3, a 2-minute walk away.
