Canning and Palmerston Streets Playground

Playground in Carlton · Basic info

Canning and Palmerston Streets Playground is a public playground in Carlton, in the Melbourne City area. The Fairfield to CBD is about 20 m away, and Carlton Baths is about 320 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 340 m from the playground, and buses 250 and 251 stop at Pitt St/Rathdowne St, a 3-minute walk away.
