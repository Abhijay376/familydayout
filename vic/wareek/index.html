Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Wareek, in the Central Goldfields area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Wareek is Bet Bet Creek Bushland Reserve, covering about 14 hectares. Wareek H55 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Wareek has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wareek, so most trips start by car. By car from Wareek, Maryborough is about 15 minutes away, Castlemaine about 60 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly is about 20 minutes' drive from Wareek. For a library, Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough is about 16 minutes' drive from Wareek. Maryborough is the closest bigger centre to Wareek, about 15 minutes away by car, and has a library. The closest places for kids by road from Wareek are Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (16 min), the playground at Avoca Lions Park in Avoca (18 min), Courthouse in Avoca (18 min), the playground at Market Reserve in Carisbrook (23 min), Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (20 min) and Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (20 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wareek.

Nearby suburbs

Homebush, Dunluce, Bung Bong and Bowenvale also border Wareek.
