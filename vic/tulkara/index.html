Tulkara is in the Northern Grampians area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Tulkara, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Tulkara has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 39 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tulkara, so most trips start by car. By car from Tulkara, Stawell is about 35 minutes away and Ararat about 58 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell is about 34 minutes' drive from Tulkara. The closest skate park to Tulkara is the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell, about 39 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell is about 37 minutes' drive from Tulkara. Stawell is the closest bigger centre to Tulkara, about 35 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Tulkara are Navarre Playground in Navarre (16 min), Community Playground in Landsborough (18 min), Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell (34 min), Railway Station Art and Crafts Gallery in Stawell (36 min), Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell (37 min) and the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell (39 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tulkara.

Nearby suburbs

Navarre, next to Tulkara, has 1 playground. Greens Creek also borders Tulkara.
