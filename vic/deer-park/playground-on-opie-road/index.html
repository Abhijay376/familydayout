Playground at Isabella Williams Reserve

Playground in Deer Park · Official details

This is a public playground in Deer Park. The Kororoit Creek Trail passes about 140 m away, and Illawong Terrace Reserve Playground is another option about 290 m away. There's a public toilet about 60 m away, and buses 422 and 423 stop at Neale Rd/Tamar Dr, a 5-minute walk away.
