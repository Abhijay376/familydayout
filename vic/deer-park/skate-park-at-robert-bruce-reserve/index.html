Skate park at Robert Bruce Reserve

Skate, BMX or pump track in Deer Park · Basic info

Skate park at Robert Bruce Reserve is a skate, bmx or pump track in Deer Park, in the Brimbank City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Deer Park Library/Neale Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 153 m away.
