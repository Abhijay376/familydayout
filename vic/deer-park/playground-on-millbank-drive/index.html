Playground at Kororoit Creek (NS Station - Billingham)

Playground in Deer Park · Basic info

Playground at Kororoit Creek (NS Station - Billingham) in Deer Park is a public playground in the Brimbank City area. The Kororoit Creek Trail is about 10 m away, and Playground at Davitt Reserve is another option about 500 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 800 m from the playground, and bus 422 stops at Dumfries St SC/Dumfries St, a 6-minute walk away.
