Malmsbury in the Macedon Ranges area has 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Malmsbury is Edgecombe Streamside Reserve, covering about 9 hectares. Malmsbury Botanical Gardens and Malmsbury Reservoir Recreation Reserve are the next largest. Playgrounds in Malmsbury include the playground at Malmsbury Botanical Gardens. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Malmsbury. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Malmsbury for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Malmsbury Railway Station (V/Line: Bendigo - Melbourne via Gisborne, Echuca/Moama - Melbourne via Bendigo or Heathcote and Swan Hill - Melbourne via Bendigo) in Malmsbury. Buses on the Kyneton - Malmsbury route stop in Malmsbury. V/Line coaches stop in Malmsbury. By car from Malmsbury, Kyneton is about 11 minutes away, Castlemaine about 24 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 76 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Chewton Swimming Pool in Chewton is about 20 minutes' drive from Malmsbury. For a library, Kyneton Library in Kyneton is about 11 minutes' drive from Malmsbury. The closest beach to Malmsbury is Canoeing in Tylden, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Malmsbury is Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton, about 12 minutes' drive. Kyneton is the closest bigger centre to Malmsbury, about 11 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Malmsbury are the playground at Taradale Mineral Springs Reserve in Taradale (8 min), Kyneton Museum in Kyneton (10 min), Kyneton Library in Kyneton (11 min), Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton (12 min), Canoeing in Tylden (22 min) and Chewton Swimming Pool in Chewton (20 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Malmsbury.

Nearby suburbs

Kyneton, next to Malmsbury, has 10 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Taradale, next to Malmsbury, has 1 playground. Next door to Malmsbury, Metcalfe has 1 playground. Next door to Malmsbury, Lauriston has 1 playground. Metcalfe East, Greenhill, Edgecombe and Drummond North also border Malmsbury.
