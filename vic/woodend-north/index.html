Woodend North is in the Macedon Ranges area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Woodend North, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Woodend North has no train station; Woodend Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 4.1 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Woodend North, so most trips start by car. By car from Woodend North, Kyneton is about 12 minutes away, Castlemaine about 38 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 63 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Gisborne Aquatic Centre in Gisborne is about 19 minutes' drive from Woodend North. For a library, Kyneton Library in Kyneton is about 12 minutes' drive from Woodend North. For a beach, Canoeing in Tylden is about 19 minutes' drive from Woodend North. The closest skate park to Woodend North is Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Woodend North is Reading Cinema in Sunbury, about 31 minutes' drive. Kyneton is the closest bigger centre to Woodend North, about 12 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds and a library. From Woodend North, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Hanging Rock Reserve in Newham (13 min), Jean Haynes Playground in Kyneton (9 min), Kyneton Library in Kyneton (12 min), Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton (11 min), Kyneton Museum in Kyneton (12 min) and Canoeing in Tylden (19 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Woodend North.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Woodend North, Tylden has 1 playground and a beach. Next door to Woodend North, Woodend has 1 playground. Newham, next to Woodend North, has 1 playground. Cadello also borders Woodend North.
