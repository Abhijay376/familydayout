Wooragee is in the Indigo area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Wooragee is Magpie Creek Historic Reserve, covering about 47 hectares. Reedy Creek K18 Streamside Reserve and Woorragee I20 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Wooragee. For walks and rides, Beechworth to Yackandandah Rail Trail crosses Wooragee.

Getting around

Wooragee has no train station; Chiltern Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 7 stops in Wooragee. By car from Wooragee, Wodonga is about 25 minutes away and Wangaratta about 43 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Wooragee is Beechworth Swimming Pool in Beechworth, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest library to Wooragee is Upper Murray Regional Library - Wodonga in Wodonga, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Wooragee is Sandy Beach in Beechworth, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Wooragee is the pump track on Alma Road in Beechworth, about 11 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana is about 26 minutes' drive from Wooragee. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 25 minutes from Wooragee by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Wooragee are Beechworth Carriage Museum in Beechworth (9 min), The Burke Museum in Beechworth (9 min), Sandy Beach in Beechworth (10 min), the pump track on Alma Road in Beechworth (11 min), Beechworth Swimming Pool in Beechworth (11 min) and the playground at Newtown Park in Beechworth (12 min).

Best for

Free days on the Beechworth to Yackandandah Rail Trail and in Wooragee's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Beechworth, next to Wooragee, has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. Chiltern, next to Wooragee, has 2 playgrounds. Yackandandah, next to Wooragee, has 1 playground and a skate park. Indigo Valley also borders Wooragee.
