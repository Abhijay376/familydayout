Playground at Midlands Reserve

Playground in Ballarat North · Official details

Playground at Midlands Reserve in Ballarat North is a public playground in the Ballarat City area. Water play at Midlands Reserve is about 30 m away, and Playground on Landsborough Street is another option about 90 m away. There's a public toilet about 50 m away, and buses 10 and 13 stop at Lydiard St/Landsborough St, a 4-minute walk away.
