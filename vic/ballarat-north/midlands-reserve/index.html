Midlands Reserve

Park in Ballarat North · Official details

Midlands Reserve is a park in Ballarat North, in the Ballarat City Council area, good for all ages. Walker St/Lydiard St N (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 100 m away.

Questions parents ask

Where is Midlands Reserve?
Midlands Reserve is in Ballarat North, Victoria.
Is Midlands Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Midlands Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 100 m away.
How do I get to Midlands Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Walker St/Lydiard St N (routes 10, 13), a 4-minute walk.
