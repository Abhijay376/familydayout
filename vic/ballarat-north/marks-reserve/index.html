Marks Reserve

Park in Ballarat North · Basic info

Marks Reserve is a park in Ballarat North, in the Ballarat City Council area, good for all ages. Walker St/Lydiard St N (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 592 m away.
