Our data lists 4 playgrounds and 6 parks and reserves in Windsor, in the Stonnington area. Windsor also has a skate park.

Landmarks

Windsor Siding is the largest mapped park in Windsor, at about 1 hectares. Chris Gahan Reserve and Gladstone Gardens are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Windsor include F.G Hughes Reserve, James Street Reserve and Harry Gregory Reserve. Playgrounds around Windsor include the playground at Windsor Siding, the playground at Gladstone Gardens and the playground at James Street Reserve. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Windsor. For older kids in Windsor there's the pump track at Windsor Siding. The Upton Rd passes through Windsor. Our data lists 9 cafes and family restaurants in Windsor for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Windsor has a train station: Windsor Railway Station (metro: Sandringham Line). Trams 5, 6, 64 and 78 stop in Windsor. Buses 246 and 603 stop in Windsor. By car from Windsor, Box Hill is about 23 minutes away, Sunshine about 25 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 9 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Windsor is Prahran Aquatic Centre in Prahran, about 2 minutes' drive. For a library, Prahran Library in Prahran is about 1 minutes' drive from Windsor. The closest beach to Windsor is West Beach in St Kilda West, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Windsor is The Astor Theatre in St Kilda, about 2 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Windsor is Akira’s Arcade in Prahran, about 2 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Windsor is Luna Park in St Kilda, about 5 minutes' drive. Box Hill is the closest bigger centre to Windsor, about 23 minutes away by car, and has 11 playgrounds, a pool and a library.

Best for

Younger kids at Windsor's 4 local playgrounds. Teens who skate or ride, with the pump track at Windsor Siding in Windsor. Free days on the Upton Rd and in Windsor's parks.

Nearby suburbs

St Kilda, next to Windsor, has 9 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Prahran, next to Windsor, has 7 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Windsor, Melbourne has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Next door to Windsor, St Kilda East has 3 playgrounds.
