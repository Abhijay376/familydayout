Pump track at Windsor Siding

Skate, BMX or pump track in Windsor · Basic info

Pump track at Windsor Siding is a skate, bmx or pump track in Windsor, in the Stonnington City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Windsor Railway Station (train) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 76 m away.
