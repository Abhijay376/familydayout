Landmarks

The biggest green space in Mitta Mitta is Mitta Mitta Reference Area, covering about 681 hectares. Sunnyside Education Area and Mitta Mitta Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Mitta Mitta also has The Mill, Snowy Creek and The Walnuts. Our data shows 7 public toilets in Mitta Mitta. Picnic areas in Mitta Mitta include Lightning Creek, Snowy Creek and The Mill. For camping, there's The Cricket Pitch and The Walnuts. The Australian Alps Walking Track, Mitta - Callaghan Creek Loop and Trappers Gap: Mitta Mitta to Mount Beauty all pass through Mitta Mitta. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Mitta Mitta for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Mitta Mitta has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 65 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mitta Mitta, so most trips start by car. By car from Mitta Mitta, Wodonga is about 66 minutes away and Wangaratta about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Eskdale Swimming Pool in Eskdale is about 15 minutes' drive from Mitta Mitta. The closest places for kids by road from Mitta Mitta are Eskdale Swimming Pool in Eskdale (15 min) and Mt Beauty Library in Mount Beauty (100 min).

Best for

Free days on the Australian Alps Walking Track and in Mitta Mitta's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Eskdale, next to Mitta Mitta, has a pool. Nelse, Glen Wills, Glen Valley and Dartmouth also border Mitta Mitta.
