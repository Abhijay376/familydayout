Getting around

There's no train station in Murgheboluc. The closest to Murgheboluc is Waurn Ponds Railway Station (V/Line), about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Murgheboluc, so most trips start by car. By car from Murgheboluc, Geelong is about 22 minutes away, Torquay about 35 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 74 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Geelong Aquatic Centre in Newtown is about 19 minutes' drive from Murgheboluc. For a library, Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn is about 14 minutes' drive from Murgheboluc. The closest beach to Murgheboluc is Eastern Beach in Geelong, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Murgheboluc is Bannockburn Skate Park in Bannockburn, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Murgheboluc is Village Cinemas in Geelong, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Murgheboluc is Laserquest in Geelong, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Murgheboluc is Adventure Park in Wallington, about 37 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Geelong, about 22 minutes from Murgheboluc by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Murgheboluc are the playground at Lawsons Park in Inverleigh (6 min), Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn (14 min), Bannockburn Skate Park in Bannockburn (15 min), Shearers Arms Gallery in Geelong West (17 min), the skate park at Turtle Bend Park in Teesdale (23 min) and Western Heights College Library in Hamlyn Heights (17 min).
