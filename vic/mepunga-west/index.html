Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Mepunga West in the Moyne area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Mepunga West has no train station; Sherwood Park Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 10.0 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mepunga West, so most trips start by car. By car from Mepunga West, Warrnambool is about 17 minutes away and Colac about 77 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool is about 20 minutes' drive from Mepunga West. The closest library to Mepunga West is Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Mepunga West is Buttress Point in The Cove, about 18 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Warrnambool Skate Park in Warrnambool is about 20 minutes' drive from Mepunga West. For a cinema, Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool is about 17 minutes' drive from Mepunga West. The closest indoor play centre to Mepunga West is The Bowl in Warrnambool, about 18 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee is about 10 minutes' drive from Mepunga West. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 17 minutes from Mepunga West by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Mepunga West are Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (10 min), Grieve Street Playground in Warrnambool (15 min), the playground at Brierly Park in Warrnambool (16 min), SCOPE Galleries in Warrnambool (17 min), The Bowl in Warrnambool (18 min) and Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool (17 min).
