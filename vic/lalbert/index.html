Lalbert in the Gannawarra area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Lake Lalbert Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Lalbert, at about 639 hectares. Talgitcha Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Lalbert.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lalbert. The closest to Lalbert is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 41 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Swan Hill - Wycheproof via Lalbert route stop in Lalbert. V/Line coaches stop in Lalbert. By car from Lalbert, Swan Hill is about 45 minutes away and Echuca about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Quambatook Memorial Pool in Quambatook is about 28 minutes' drive from Lalbert. The closest library to Lalbert is Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Lalbert is the skate park at Riverside Park in Swan Hill, about 44 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Lalbert is Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill, about 44 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 45 minutes from Lalbert by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Lalbert, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Culgoa Memorial Park in Culgoa (22 min), Quambatook Historical Centre in Quambatook (27 min), Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook (27 min), Quambatook Lions Club Playground in Quambatook (27 min), Quambatook Memorial Pool in Quambatook (28 min) and Lake Boga Flying Boat Museum in Lake Boga (39 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lalbert.

Nearby suburbs

Kunat, Goschen, Cannie and Beauchamp also border Lalbert.
