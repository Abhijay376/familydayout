Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside South Purrumbete in the Corangamite area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

South Purrumbete has no train station; Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside South Purrumbete, so most trips start by car. By car from South Purrumbete, Colac is about 29 minutes away, Warrnambool about 59 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon is about 36 minutes' drive from South Purrumbete. The closest library to South Purrumbete is Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac, about 31 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown is about 22 minutes' drive from South Purrumbete. For a cinema, Colac Cinemas in Colac is about 29 minutes' drive from South Purrumbete. The closest indoor play centre to South Purrumbete is Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac, about 29 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 29 minutes from South Purrumbete by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from South Purrumbete are Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (22 min), the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve in Camperdown (24 min), the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (27 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (29 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (29 min) and Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from South Purrumbete.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to South Purrumbete, Cobden has 1 playground. Koallah, Carpendeit, Bullaharre and Bostocks Creek also border South Purrumbete.
