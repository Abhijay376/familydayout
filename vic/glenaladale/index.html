Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Glenaladale in the East Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Glenaladale has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glenaladale, so most trips start by car. By car from Glenaladale, Bairnsdale is about 33 minutes away and Sale about 51 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Glenaladale is Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale, about 33 minutes' drive. The closest library to Glenaladale is Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale, about 34 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Skate Park in Stratford is about 36 minutes' drive from Glenaladale. The closest cinema to Glenaladale is Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale, about 34 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow is about 41 minutes' drive from Glenaladale. Bairnsdale is the closest bigger centre to Glenaladale, about 33 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Glenaladale are the playground at Amy Shand Park in Lindenow (15 min), Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale (33 min), Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale (34 min), Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale (35 min), Krowathunkoolong Keeping Place in Bairnsdale (35 min) and Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale (34 min).
