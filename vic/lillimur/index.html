Lillimur in the West Wimmera area has 8 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Little Desert Area Remote and Natural Area is the largest mapped park in Lillimur, at about 15,836 hectares. Little Desert (West) Reference Area and Lillimur South Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Lillimur also has Yearinga I8 Bushland Reserve, Yearinga I9 Bushland Reserve and Dinyarrak I5 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Lillimur has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 191 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Lillimur. By car from Lillimur, Horsham is about 2 hours away and Stawell about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Kaniva Library in Kaniva is about 19 minutes' drive from Lillimur. The closest places for kids by road from Lillimur are the playground at Band Park in Kaniva (19 min) and Kaniva Library in Kaniva (19 min).
