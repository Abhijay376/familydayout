Getting around

There's no train station in Pioneer Bay. The closest to Pioneer Bay is East Pakenham Railway Station (metro), about 33 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Cowes - Fountain Gate via Anderson route stop in Pioneer Bay. V/Line coaches stop in Pioneer Bay. By car from Pioneer Bay, Wonthaggi is about 33 minutes away, Pakenham about 34 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 78 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Pioneer Bay is Koo Wee Rup Swimming Pool in Koo Wee Rup, about 20 minutes' drive. For a library, San Remo Library in San Remo is about 21 minutes' drive from Pioneer Bay. The closest beach to Pioneer Bay is Corinella Beach in Corinella, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Pioneer Bay is the skate park at Cochrane Park in Koo Wee Rup, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Pioneer Bay is Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Pioneer Bay is Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham, about 33 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville is about 5 minutes' drive from Pioneer Bay. Wonthaggi is the closest bigger centre to Pioneer Bay, about 33 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Pioneer Bay are Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville (5 min), the playground at George Bass Memorial Park in Bass (13 min), the playground at Thwaites Park in Lang Lang (13 min), Corinella Beach in Corinella (17 min), Chicory Kiln in French Island (18 min) and Art Fusion in Anderson (17 min).
