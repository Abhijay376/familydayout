Skate park on Sonia Crescent

Skate, BMX or pump track in Pioneer Bay · Basic info

Skate park on Sonia Crescent is a skate, bmx or pump track in Pioneer Bay, in the Bass Coast Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Pioneer Bay/Bass Hwy (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 16 m away.
