Our data lists 2 playgrounds and 5 parks and reserves in Beulah, in the Yarriambiack area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Beulah is Cookies Plain Bushland Reserve, covering about 20 hectares. Carori I174 Bushland Reserve and Carori I175 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Beulah include Beulah Bushland Reserve and Byanga Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds in Beulah include the playground at Beulah Lake Park and the playground at Beulah Lions Park. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Beulah.

Getting around

Beulah has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 123 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Mildura - Horsham via Hopetoun route stop in Beulah. V/Line coaches stop in Beulah. By car from Beulah, Horsham is about 73 minutes away and Stawell about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Beulah is Hopetoun Swimming Pool in Hopetoun, about 22 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Birchip Skate Park in Birchip is about 39 minutes' drive from Beulah. The closest places for kids by road from Beulah are Hopetoun Swimming Pool in Hopetoun (22 min), Historical Centre in Warracknabeal (29 min), Wheatlands Warracknabeal Agricultural Machinery Museum in Warracknabeal (30 min), Rainbow Pool in Rainbow (34 min) and Birchip Skate Park in Birchip (39 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Beulah.

Nearby suburbs

Hopetoun, next to Beulah, has 4 playgrounds and a pool. Rainbow, next to Beulah, has 1 playground and a pool. Next door to Beulah, Brim has 1 playground. Watchupga, Rosebery, Reedy Dam and Kinnabulla also border Beulah.
