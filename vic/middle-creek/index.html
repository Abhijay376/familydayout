Middle Creek in the Pyrenees area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Middle Creek is Woodnaggerak Bushland Reserve, covering about 5 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Middle Creek. The closest to Middle Creek is Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Middle Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Middle Creek, Ararat is about 29 minutes away, Ballarat about 56 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Middle Creek is Beaufort Pool in Beaufort, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest library to Middle Creek is Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort, about 19 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort is about 20 minutes' drive from Middle Creek. For a cinema, Astor Cinema in Ararat is about 28 minutes' drive from Middle Creek. The nearest larger centre is Ararat, about 29 minutes from Middle Creek by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Middle Creek are Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort (19 min), the playground at Wotherspoon Reserve in Beaufort (19 min), the playground at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (20 min), the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (20 min), Beaufort Pool in Beaufort (20 min) and Astor Cinema in Ararat (28 min).
