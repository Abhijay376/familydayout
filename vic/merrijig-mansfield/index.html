Landmarks

The biggest green space in Merrijig (Mansfield) is Delatite Education Area, covering about 390 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Merrijig (Mansfield) include Tomahawk Hut, Evans Creek Hut and Carters Mill Picnic Area. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Merrijig (Mansfield). Picnic areas in Merrijig (Mansfield) include Carters Mill Picnic Area and Tomahawk Hut. For camping, there's Buttercup Four Camping Area and Buttercup Three Camping Area. The Merrijig - Two Rivers Loop, Mansfield - Merrijig and Mirimbah Loop all pass through Merrijig (Mansfield).

Getting around

Merrijig (Mansfield) has no train station; Benalla Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 65 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Merrijig (Mansfield), so most trips start by car. By car from Merrijig (Mansfield), Mansfield is about 21 minutes away, Benalla about 72 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.
