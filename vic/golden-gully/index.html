Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Golden Gully in the Greater Bendigo area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it. Golden Gully does have an indoor play centre.

Landmarks

Places to visit in Golden Gully include Intagolf. The Goldfields Track and Leanganook Track all pass through Golden Gully.

Getting around

Golden Gully has no train station; Kangaroo Flat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 1.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Golden Gully, so most trips start by car. By car from Golden Gully, Bendigo is about 7 minutes away, Castlemaine about 30 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Golden Gully is Brennan Park Swimming Pool in Kennington, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest library to Golden Gully is Kangaroo Flat Library in Kangaroo Flat, about 6 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Ewing Park Skating in Bendigo is about 7 minutes' drive from Golden Gully. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 6 minutes' drive from Golden Gully. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Golden Gully is The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo, about 12 minutes' drive. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Golden Gully, about 7 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Golden Gully, the nearest places for kids by road are Kangaroo Flat Playground & Recreation Reserve in Kangaroo Flat (5 min), the playground at RF Turner Memorial Park in Golden Square (5 min), Kangaroo Flat Library in Kangaroo Flat (6 min), Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo (6 min), Discovery Science & Technology Centre in Bendigo (7 min) and Bendigo Library in Bendigo (7 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the indoor play centre in Golden Gully. Longer rides and walks from Golden Gully on the Goldfields Track.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Golden Gully, Kangaroo Flat has 13 playgrounds and a library. Golden Square, next to Golden Gully, has 4 playgrounds. Spring Gully, next to Golden Gully, has 1 playground. Next door to Golden Gully, Quarry Hill has 1 playground.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Golden Gully, without traffic.
