Worcester West Park

Park in Bundoora · Basic info

Worcester West Park is a park in Bundoora, in the Whittlesea City Council area, good for all ages. Enterprise Dr/Mammalia Dr (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 861 m away with a baby change table.
