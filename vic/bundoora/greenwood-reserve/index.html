Greenwood Reserve

Park in Bundoora · Basic info

Greenwood Reserve is a park in Bundoora, in the Banyule City Council area, good for all ages. Lea Cres/Greenwood Dr (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 12 m away.
