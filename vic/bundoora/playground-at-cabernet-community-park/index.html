Playground at Cabernet Community Park

Playground in Bundoora · Official details

Playground at Cabernet Community Park is a public playground in Bundoora, in the Whittlesea City area. The M80 Trail is about 110 m away, and Playground at Frost Court Park is another option about 280 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus 570 stops at Tasman Dr/Betula Ave, a 6-minute walk away.
