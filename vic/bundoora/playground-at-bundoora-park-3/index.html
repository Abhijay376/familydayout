Playground at Bundoora Park

Playground in Bundoora · Basic info

This is a public playground in Bundoora. Bundoora Park Farm is about 410 m away, and for food, Polaris Kebab House is about 310 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 280 m from the playground, and tram 86 stops at Bundoora Park/Plenty Rd #61, a 3-minute walk away.
