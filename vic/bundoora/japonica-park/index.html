Japonica Park

Park in Bundoora · Basic info

Japonica Park is a park in Bundoora, in the Whittlesea City Council area, good for all ages. Hibiscus Ave/Betula Ave (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 771 m away with a baby change table.
