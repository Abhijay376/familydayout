Playground at Taunton Park

Playground in Bundoora · Basic info

This is a public playground in Bundoora. The M80 Trail passes about 330 m away, and Hungry Jack's is about 180 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.3 km away, and buses 382 and 566 stop at Parade College/Plenty Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
