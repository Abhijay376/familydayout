Playground at Greenwood Reserve

Playground in Bundoora · Basic info

Playground at Greenwood Reserve is a public playground in Bundoora, in the Banyule City area. The Banyule LBN7 is about 30 m away, and Playground on Morwell Avenue is another option about 460 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 566 stops at Lea Cres/Greenwood Dr, a 1-minute walk away.
