Bundoora Park

Park in Bundoora · Basic info

Bundoora Park is a park in Bundoora, in the Darebin City Council area, good for all ages. Bundoora Park/Plenty Rd #61 (tram) is a 14-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 96 m away.
