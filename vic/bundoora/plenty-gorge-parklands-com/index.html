Plenty Gorge Parklands (COM)

Park in Bundoora · Official details

Plenty Gorge Parklands (COM) is a park in Bundoora, in the Whittlesea City Council area, good for all ages. Hamilton Ct/Gillingham St (bus) is a 21-minute walk away and dogs are allowed. Opening hours: Hawkstowe Picnic Area Gordons Road gate 7:00am-5:00pm (7:00am-7:00pm daylight saving).
