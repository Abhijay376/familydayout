Redmond Court Wetlands Reserve

Park in Bundoora · Basic info

Redmond Court Wetlands Reserve is a park in Bundoora, in the Banyule City Council area, good for all ages. Greenhills Rd/Plenty Rd #68 (tram) is a 14-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 968 m away.
