Elite Swimming

Pool or water play in West Footscray · Official details

Elite Swimming is a pool or water play in West Footscray, in the Maribyrnong City Council area, good for all ages. Hansen Reserve/Roberts St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 106 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: Mo 09:00-18:30, Tu 09:00-18:30, We 09:00-18:30, Th 09:00-18:30, Fr 09:00-18:30, Sa 09:00-14:30, Su 09:00-12:00.
