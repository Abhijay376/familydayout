Playground at Johnson Reserve

Playground in West Footscray · Basic info

Playground at Johnson Reserve in West Footscray is a public playground in the Maribyrnong City area. Older kids have a skate park about 20 m away, and Playground at Gaudion Reserve is another option about 590 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus 216 stops at Argyle St/Essex St, a 2-minute walk away.
