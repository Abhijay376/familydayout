Playground at Gaudion Reserve

Playground in West Footscray · Basic info

Playground at Gaudion Reserve is a public playground in West Footscray, in the Maribyrnong City area. The Sunshine Footscray Trail is about 260 m away, and Playground at Lions Park is another option about 590 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 940 m from the playground, and bus 220 stops at Rondell Ave/Barkly St, a 1-minute walk away.
