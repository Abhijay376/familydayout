Shorten Reserve

Park in West Footscray · Official details

Shorten Reserve is a park in West Footscray, in the Maribyrnong City Council area, good for all ages. Khartoum St/Essex St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and dogs are allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Shorten Reserve?
Shorten Reserve is in West Footscray, Victoria.
Is Shorten Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Shorten Reserve?
Yes, dogs are allowed.
How do I get to Shorten Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Khartoum St/Essex St (routes 216), a 2-minute walk.
