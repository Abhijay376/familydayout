Our data lists 6 parks and reserves in Flowerdale, in the Murrindindi area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Flowerdale is Mount Robertson State Forest, covering about 5,469 hectares. Yea G125 Bushland Reserve and Spring Valley Recreation Reserve are the next largest. Flowerdale also has Flowerdale Bushland Reserve, Junction Hill Bushland Reserve and Break ODay Creek Streamside Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Flowerdale. For walks and rides, Cycling Trail crosses Flowerdale.

Getting around

There's no train station in Flowerdale. The closest to Flowerdale is Wandong Railway Station (V/Line), about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Flowerdale. By car from Flowerdale, Seymour is about 48 minutes away, Healesville about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 76 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Whittlesea Swimming Centre in Whittlesea is about 30 minutes' drive from Flowerdale. For a library, Broadford Library in Broadford is about 36 minutes' drive from Flowerdale. The closest skate park to Flowerdale is the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Flowerdale is Funfields in Whittlesea, about 33 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 48 minutes from Flowerdale by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Flowerdale, the nearest places for kids by road are the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea (22 min), TLM Moto Guzzi in Yea (36 min), Rupert Street Playground in Broadford (33 min), Broadford Skate Park in Broadford (34 min), Sutherland Street Playground in Broadford (34 min) and Broadford Library in Broadford (36 min).

Best for

Free days on the Cycling Trail and in Flowerdale's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Flowerdale, Glenburn has 1 playground. Yea, next to Flowerdale, has a skate park. Kinglake Central, Homewood and Clonbinane also border Flowerdale.
