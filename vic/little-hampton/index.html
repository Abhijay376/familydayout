Little Hampton in the Hepburn area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Coliban I7 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Little Hampton, at about 3 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Little Hampton. The closest to Little Hampton is Kyneton Railway Station (V/Line), about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Little Hampton, so most trips start by car. By car from Little Hampton, Daylesford is about 19 minutes away, Kyneton about 26 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 84 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford is about 19 minutes' drive from Little Hampton. The closest library to Little Hampton is Daylesford Library in Daylesford, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Little Hampton is Canoeing in Tylden, about 20 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton is about 24 minutes' drive from Little Hampton. Daylesford is the closest bigger centre to Little Hampton, about 19 minutes away by car, and has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Little Hampton are the playground at J P Gleeson Memorial Park in Lyonville (5 min), the playground at Quarry Street Reserve in Trentham (6 min), Little Gallery in Trentham (6 min), Canoeing in Tylden (20 min), Daylesford Spa Country Railway in Daylesford (18 min) and Daylesford Library in Daylesford (19 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Little Hampton.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Little Hampton, Trentham has 2 playgrounds. Lyonville, next to Little Hampton, has 1 playground. Glenlyon also borders Little Hampton.
