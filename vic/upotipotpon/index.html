Upotipotpon in the Strathbogie area has 6 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Upotipotpon is Morphett Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 21 hectares. Nalinga Streamside Reserve and Upotipotpon H80 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Upotipotpon include Upotipotpon H65 Bushland Reserve, Upotipotpon H4A Streamside Reserve and Broken River K22 Streamside Reserve.

Getting around

Upotipotpon has no train station; Violet Town Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 17 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Upotipotpon, so most trips start by car. By car from Upotipotpon, Benalla is about 38 minutes away, Shepparton about 49 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Upotipotpon is Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla, about 38 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Pump Track in Benalla is about 39 minutes' drive from Upotipotpon. Benalla is the closest bigger centre to Upotipotpon, about 38 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds and a pool. From Upotipotpon, the nearest places for kids by road are Violet Town Recreation Reserve West Playground in Violet Town (31 min), Violet Town Lions Park Playground in Violet Town (31 min), Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (38 min), Benalla Costume and Pioneer Museum in Benalla (38 min), Pump Track in Benalla (39 min) and Benalla Art Gallery in Benalla (39 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Upotipotpon.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Upotipotpon, Violet Town has 3 playgrounds. Koonda, Gowangardie, Goomalibee and Earlston also border Upotipotpon.
