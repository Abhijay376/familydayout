Pira in the Swan Hill area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Pira is Pira Bushland Reserve, covering about 4 hectares.

Getting around

Pira has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Pira, so most trips start by car. By car from Pira, Swan Hill is about 20 minutes away and Echuca about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Pira is Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest library to Pira is Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Pira is Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Pira is Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill, about 20 minutes' drive. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Pira, about 20 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Pira, the nearest places for kids by road are Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South (12 min), the playground at Nyah Heritage Park in Nyah (16 min), the playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV) in Nyah (17 min), Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill (19 min), the skate park at James Belsar Reserve in Swan Hill (20 min) and Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill (19 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Pira.

Nearby suburbs

Nyah West, next to Pira, has 1 playground. Next door to Pira, Woorinen South has a skate park. Nyrraby, Nowie and Bulga also border Pira.
