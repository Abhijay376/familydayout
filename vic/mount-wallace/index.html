Getting around

Mount Wallace has no train station; Ballan Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mount Wallace, so most trips start by car. By car from Mount Wallace, Bacchus Marsh is about 39 minutes away, Daylesford about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 84 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mount Wallace is Ballan Swimming Pool in Ballan, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest library to Mount Wallace is Lerderderg Library in Bacchus Marsh, about 39 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Mount Wallace is Disability beach in Steiglitz, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Mount Wallace is the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan, about 20 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Fairy Park in Anakie is about 26 minutes' drive from Mount Wallace. The nearest larger centre is Bacchus Marsh, about 39 minutes from Mount Wallace by car, with 6 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Mount Wallace, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Beremboke Recreation Reserve in Beremboke (14 min), the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan (20 min), Ballan Swimming Pool in Ballan (21 min), Disability beach in Steiglitz (36 min), Fairy Park in Anakie (26 min) and Meredith Skate Park in Meredith (31 min).
