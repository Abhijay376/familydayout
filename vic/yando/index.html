Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Yando, in the Loddon area.

Landmarks

Lake Yando Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Yando, at about 87 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Yando. The closest to Yando is Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line), about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yando, so most trips start by car. By car from Yando, Bendigo is about 84 minutes away and Echuca about 86 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Boort Swimming Pool in Boort is about 10 minutes' drive from Yando. From Yando, the nearest places for kids by road are A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park in Boort (9 min), Boort Court House Historical Museum in Boort (9 min), Boort Swimming Pool in Boort (10 min), Pyramid Hill & Districts Historical Museum in Pyramid Hill (32 min), the playground at Lake Meran W.R in Lake Meran (30 min) and Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo (43 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Yando.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Yando, Boort has 1 playground and a pool. Canary Island also borders Yando.
