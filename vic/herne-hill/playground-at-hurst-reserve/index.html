Playground at Hurst Reserve

Playground in Herne Hill · Basic info

Playground at Hurst Reserve is a public playground in Herne Hill, in the Greater Geelong City area. The Gabrielle Blythe Linear Pathway is about 260 m away, and for food, Minerva Rd Fish Shop is about 320 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 280 m from the playground, and bus 24 stops at St Josephs Westcourt Campus/Minerva Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
