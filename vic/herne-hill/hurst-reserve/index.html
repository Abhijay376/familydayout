Hurst Reserve

Park in Herne Hill · Official details

Hurst Reserve is a park in Herne Hill, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. St Josephs Westcourt Campus/Minerva Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 0 m away and dogs are allowed.
