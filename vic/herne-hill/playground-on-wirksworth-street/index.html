Playground on Wirksworth Street

Playground in Herne Hill · Basic info

This is a public playground in Herne Hill. The Gabrielle Blythe Linear Pathway is about 200 m away, and for food, Minerva Rd Fish Shop is about 490 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 240 m from the playground, and bus 24 stops at St Josephs Westcourt Campus/Minerva Rd, a 7-minute walk away.
