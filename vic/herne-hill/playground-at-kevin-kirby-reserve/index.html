Playground at Kevin Kirby Reserve

Playground in Herne Hill · Basic info

Playground at Kevin Kirby Reserve in Herne Hill is a public playground in the Greater Geelong City area. The Gabrielle Blythe Linear Pathway passes about 70 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus 25 stops at Beckley St/McCurdy Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
