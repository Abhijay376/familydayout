Getting around

Pine Lodge has no train station; Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Pine Lodge, so most trips start by car. By car from Pine Lodge, Shepparton is about 17 minutes away, Benalla about 52 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.
