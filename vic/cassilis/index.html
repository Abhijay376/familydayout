Cassilis in the East Gippsland area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Gingee Munjie State Forest is the largest mapped park in Cassilis, at about 1,843 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cassilis. The closest to Cassilis is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 68 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cassilis, so most trips start by car. By car from Cassilis, Bairnsdale is about 2 hours away and Lakes Entrance about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek is about 35 minutes' drive from Cassilis. For a skate park, the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo is about 38 minutes' drive from Cassilis. From Cassilis, the nearest places for kids by road are Great Alpine Gallery in Swifts Creek (34 min), Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek (35 min), Old Omeo Courthouse in Omeo (38 min), the playground at Omeo Historical Park & Museum in Omeo (37 min) and the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo (38 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Cassilis.

Nearby suburbs

Swifts Creek, next to Cassilis, has 1 playground and a pool. Next door to Cassilis, Omeo has 2 playgrounds and a skate park.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Cassilis, without traffic.
