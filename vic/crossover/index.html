Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Crossover, in the Baw Baw area.

Landmarks

Crossover Regional Park is the largest mapped park in Crossover, at about 253 hectares. For walks and rides, Rokeby to Neerim Trail, Rokeby to Crossover Rail Trail and Traverse cross Crossover.

Getting around

Crossover has no train station; Warragul Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Crossover, so most trips start by car. By car from Crossover, Warragul is about 16 minutes away, Pakenham about 42 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 86 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Crossover is Drouin Swimming Pool in Drouin, about 17 minutes' drive. For a library, Warragul Library in Warragul is about 16 minutes' drive from Crossover. For a skate park, Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South is about 5 minutes' drive from Crossover. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 15 minutes' drive from Crossover. The closest indoor play centre to Crossover is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 16 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Wildlife Trail in Tynong North is about 29 minutes' drive from Crossover. The nearest larger centre is Warragul, about 16 minutes from Crossover by car, with 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Crossover are Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South (5 min), Conventry Drive Playground in Warragul (13 min), the playground at Pederson Park in Warragul (15 min), Warragul Library in Warragul (16 min), Warragul & District Historical Society Museum in Warragul (15 min) and Warragul Velodrome in Warragul (16 min).

Best for

Free days on the Rokeby to Neerim Trail and in Crossover's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Crossover, Rokeby has 1 playground. Neerim South, next to Crossover, has a skate park.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Crossover, without traffic.
