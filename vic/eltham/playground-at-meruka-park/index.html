Playground at Meruka Park

Playground in Eltham · Basic info

Playground at Meruka Park in Eltham is a public playground in the Nillumbik Shire area. Hungry Jack's is about 470 m away if you need food, and Playground at Sherbourne Road Reserve is another option about 220 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 980 m away, and buses 514 and 902 stop at Stephen St/Sherbourne Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
