Eltham Lower Park Playground

Playground in Eltham · Basic info

Eltham Lower Park Playground is a public playground in Eltham, in the Nillumbik Shire area. The Diamond Creek Trail passes about 30 m away, and Walk The Wok is about 220 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 100 m away, and buses 513 and 902 stop at Eltham Lower Park/Main Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
