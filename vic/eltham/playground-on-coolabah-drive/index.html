Playground on Coolabah Drive

Playground in Eltham · Basic info

Playground on Coolabah Drive in Eltham is a public playground in the Nillumbik Shire area. The Diamond Creek Trail is about 190 m away, and Eltham North Adventure Playground is another option about 250 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 870 m from the playground, and buses 578 and 579 stop at Coleman Cres/Main Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
