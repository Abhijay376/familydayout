Barak Bushlands

Park in Eltham · Basic info

Barak Bushlands is a park in Eltham, in the Nillumbik Shire Council area, good for all ages. Antoinette Bvd/Main Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 693 m away.
