Pasadena Avenue Park

Park in Beaumaris · Basic info

Pasadena Avenue Park is a park in Beaumaris, in the Bayside City Council area, good for all ages. Pasadena Ave/Haydens Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 537 m away with a baby change table.
