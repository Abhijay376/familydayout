Donald Macdonald Reserve

Park in Beaumaris · Basic info

Donald Macdonald Reserve is a park in Beaumaris, in the Bayside City Council area, good for all ages. Donald MacDonald Reserve/Haydens Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 321 m away.
