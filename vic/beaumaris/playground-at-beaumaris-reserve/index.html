Playground at Beaumaris Reserve

Playground in Beaumaris · Basic info

Playground at Beaumaris Reserve in Beaumaris is a public playground in the Bayside City area. Beaumaris Community Centre is about 80 m away, and for food, Mr Beau is about 360 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 200 m from the playground, and bus 922 stops at Beaumaris Community Centre/Reserve Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
