Ricketts Point Marine Sanctuary

Park in Beaumaris · Official details

Ricketts Point Marine Sanctuary is a park in Beaumaris, in the Bayside City Council area, good for all ages. Haydens Rd/Pacific Bvd (bus) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 125 m away.

Details

Water play
seasonal (Parks Victoria)
Seasons
Beach patrolled by Beaumaris Life Saving Club on weekends from November to Easter (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
Junior Ranger programs for rockpooling; Accessible by public transport; Great Victorian Fish Count in December for snorkellers (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Ricketts Point Marine Sanctuary?
Ricketts Point Marine Sanctuary is in Beaumaris, Victoria.
Is Ricketts Point Marine Sanctuary free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Ricketts Point Marine Sanctuary?
The nearest public toilet is about 125 m away.
How do I get to Ricketts Point Marine Sanctuary by public transport?
The closest stop is Haydens Rd/Pacific Bvd (routes 600, 922, 923), a 8-minute walk.
