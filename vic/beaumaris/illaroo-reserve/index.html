Illaroo Reserve

Park in Beaumaris · Basic info

Illaroo Reserve is a park in Beaumaris, in the Bayside City Council area, good for all ages. Wakool Ave/Charman Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 794 m away with a baby change table.
