Playground at Sue Kirkpatrick Park

Playground in Beaumaris · Basic info

Playground at Sue Kirkpatrick Park is a public playground in Beaumaris, in the Bayside City area. Hole In One Pasta & Pizza is about 420 m away if you need food, and Playground on Weatherall Road is another option about 480 m away. There's a public toilet about 660 m away, and bus 922 stops at Victor Ave/Weatherall Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
