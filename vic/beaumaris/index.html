Beaumaris in the Bayside area has 4 playgrounds and 10 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, Beaumaris has a library and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Beaumaris is Ricketts Point Marine Sanctuary, covering about 120 hectares. Donald Macdonald Reserve and Beaumaris Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Beaumaris include Banksia Reserve, Illaroo Reserve and WF Vale Reserve. Playgrounds around Beaumaris include the playground at Banksia Reserve, the playground at Illaroo Reserve and the playground at Beaumaris Reserve. Our data shows 12 public toilets in Beaumaris. In Beaumaris you'll also find Beaumaris Community Centre. For older kids in Beaumaris there's Beaumaris Skate Park. For walks and rides, Bay Trail crosses Beaumaris. Our data lists 9 cafes and family restaurants in Beaumaris for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Beaumaris has no train station; Cheltenham Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 2.1 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 600, 811, 812, 825, 922 and 923 stop in Beaumaris. By car from Beaumaris, Dandenong is about 19 minutes away, Frankston about 25 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 27 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Beaumaris is SwimRight Sandringham in Cheltenham, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Beaumaris is Mentone Dog Beach in Mentone, about 4 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Beaumaris is Village Cinemas in Cheltenham, about 6 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Småland in Springvale is about 18 minutes' drive from Beaumaris. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Beaumaris is Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale, about 17 minutes' drive. Dandenong is the closest bigger centre to Beaumaris, about 19 minutes away by car, and has 21 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Beaumaris's 4 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the library in Beaumaris. Teens who skate or ride, with Beaumaris Skate Park in Beaumaris. Free days on the Bay Trail and in Beaumaris's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Cheltenham, next to Beaumaris, has 20 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Beaumaris, Mentone has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Black Rock, next to Beaumaris, has 3 playgrounds and a beach.
