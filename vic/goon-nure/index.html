Our data lists 4 parks and reserves in Goon Nure, in the East Gippsland area.

Landmarks

Blond Bay Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Goon Nure, at about 1,923 hectares. Moormurng Flora and Fauna Reserve and Steel Bay - Newland Backwater Gippsland Lakes Reserve are the next largest. Goon Nure also has Blond Bay Gippsland Lakes Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Goon Nure.

Getting around

There's no train station in Goon Nure. The closest to Goon Nure is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 17 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Goon Nure, so most trips start by car. By car from Goon Nure, Bairnsdale is about 20 minutes away and Sale about 52 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale is about 20 minutes' drive from Goon Nure. The closest library to Goon Nure is Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Goon Nure is Boyfriend Beach in Forge Creek, about 29 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Gilsenan Reserve in Paynesville is about 28 minutes' drive from Goon Nure. For a cinema, Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale is about 21 minutes' drive from Goon Nure. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Goon Nure is Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow, about 27 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bairnsdale, about 20 minutes from Goon Nure by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Goon Nure, the nearest places for kids by road are Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale (20 min), Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale (21 min), Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale (22 min), Krowathunkoolong Keeping Place in Bairnsdale (21 min), Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale (21 min) and Bairnsdale Museum in Bairnsdale (22 min).
