Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Yuroke in the Hume area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Yuroke has no train station; Craigieburn Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 5.6 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yuroke, so most trips start by car. By car from Yuroke, Broadmeadows is about 15 minutes away, Sunshine about 23 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 33 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Yuroke is Splash Aqua Park and Leisure Centre in Craigieburn, about 5 minutes' drive. For a library, Craigieburn Library in Craigieburn is about 5 minutes' drive from Yuroke. For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 35 minutes' drive from Yuroke. For a skate park, the skate park at Aston Reserve in Craigieburn is about 4 minutes' drive from Yuroke. The closest cinema to Yuroke is United Cinemas in Craigieburn, about 6 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Timezone in Craigieburn is about 6 minutes' drive from Yuroke. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Yuroke is Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville, about 28 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Broadmeadows, about 15 minutes from Yuroke by car, with 12 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Yuroke are the playground at Destination Park in Greenvale (2 min), the playground at Napoli Park in Greenvale (3 min), the skate park at Aston Reserve in Craigieburn (4 min), Splash Aqua Park and Leisure Centre in Craigieburn (5 min), Timezone in Craigieburn (6 min) and United Cinemas in Craigieburn (6 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Yuroke.

Nearby suburbs

Craigieburn, next to Yuroke, has 54 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Yuroke, Greenvale has 21 playgrounds and a skate park. Mickleham, next to Yuroke, has 4 playgrounds.
