Muckatah in the Moira area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Muckatah is Muckatah Bushland Reserve, covering about 4 hectares.

Getting around

Muckatah has no train station; Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 46 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Muckatah, so most trips start by car. By car from Muckatah, Shepparton is about 46 minutes away and Benalla about 56 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah is about 29 minutes' drive from Muckatah. For a library, Barooga Library in Cobram is about 17 minutes' drive from Muckatah. For a beach, Ski Beach in Cobram is about 16 minutes' drive from Muckatah. For a skate park, the skate park at Federation Park in Cobram is about 13 minutes' drive from Muckatah. The closest cinema to Muckatah is Cobram Cinema in Cobram, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Muckatah is Tunzafun in Yarrawonga, about 34 minutes' drive. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Muckatah, about 46 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Muckatah are the playground at C R Wood Park in Katamatite (6 min), the playground at E.W Kilgour Reserve in Katamatite (6 min), Katamatite Historical Society Museum in Katamatite (6 min), the skate park at Federation Park in Cobram (13 min), Cobram Cinema in Cobram (13 min) and Ski Beach in Cobram (16 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Muckatah.

Nearby suburbs

Cobram, next to Muckatah, has 4 playgrounds, a library and a beach. Next door to Muckatah, Cobram East has a beach. Next door to Muckatah, Katamatite has 2 playgrounds. Naring and Katunga also border Muckatah.
