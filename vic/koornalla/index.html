Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Koornalla, in the Latrobe area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Koornalla is Callignee Bushland Reserve, covering about 2 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Koornalla. The closest to Koornalla is Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line), about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Koornalla, so most trips start by car. By car from Koornalla, Traralgon is about 21 minutes away, Warragul about 62 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Koornalla is Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon, about 22 minutes' drive. For a library, Morwell Library in Morwell is about 28 minutes' drive from Koornalla. For a skate park, Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon is about 23 minutes' drive from Koornalla. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 27 minutes' drive from Koornalla. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Koornalla, about 21 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. From Koornalla, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Hourigan Park in Traralgon (19 min), the playground at Eric Taylor Reserve in Traralgon (19 min), Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon (22 min), Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon (23 min), Powerworks in Morwell (27 min) and Village 8 in Morwell (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Koornalla.
