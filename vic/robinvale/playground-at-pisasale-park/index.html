Playground at Pisasale Park

Playground in Robinvale · Basic info

Playground at Pisasale Park is a public playground in Robinvale, in the Swan Hill Rural City area. Playground on Watkin Street is another option about 300 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus Mildura - Euston - Robinvale stops at Robinvale PO/Herbert St, a 7-minute walk away.
