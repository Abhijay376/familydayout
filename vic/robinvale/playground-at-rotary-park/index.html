Playground at Rotary Park

Playground in Robinvale · Basic info

Playground at Rotary Park is a public playground in Robinvale, in the Swan Hill Rural City area. Rural Life Museum is about 90 m away, and Robinvale Chinese Restaurant is about 60 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 130 m away, and coaches stop at Robinvale Railway Station/Bromley Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
