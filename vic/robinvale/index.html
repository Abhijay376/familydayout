Our data lists 5 playgrounds and 15 parks and reserves in Robinvale, in the Swan Hill area. Robinvale also has a museum or gallery and a skate park.

Landmarks

Gadsen Bend Park is the largest mapped park in Robinvale, at about 1,603 hectares. Bumbang Island Historic Reserve and Bumbang I262 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Robinvale also has Caix Square, Rotary Park and Anderson Park. Playgrounds in Robinvale include the playground at Rotary Park, the playground at Pisasale Park and the playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV). Our data shows 8 public toilets in Robinvale. Places to visit in Robinvale include Rural Life Museum. For older kids in Robinvale there's Robinvale Skatepark. Our data lists 7 cafes and family restaurants in Robinvale for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Robinvale. The closest to Robinvale is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 111 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Mildura - Euston - Robinvale route stop in Robinvale. V/Line coaches stop in Robinvale. By car from Robinvale, Mildura is about 2 hours away and Swan Hill about 2 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Younger kids at Robinvale's 5 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Robinvale. Teens who skate or ride, with Robinvale Skatepark in Robinvale.
