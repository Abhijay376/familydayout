Playground on Marine Parade

Playground in Marlo · Basic info

Playground on Marine Parade is a public playground in Marlo, in the East Gippsland Shire area. The Snowy River Estuary Walk is about 80 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and coaches stop at Marlo Primary School/Jorgensen St, a 8-minute walk away.
