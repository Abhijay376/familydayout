Marlo in the East Gippsland area has 1 playground and 5 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Marlo is Marlo Coastal Reserve, covering about 584 hectares. Beware Reef Marine Sanctuary and Brodribb River Flora and Fauna Reserve are the next largest. Marlo also has William Hunter Flora Reserve and Marlo Race Course Gazetted (temporary recreation reserve). Our data shows 4 public toilets in Marlo. For walks and rides, Marlo Walking Track Network, Snowy River Estuary Walk and Estuary view trail cross Marlo. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Marlo for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Marlo. The closest to Marlo is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 80 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Marlo. By car from Marlo, Lakes Entrance is about 60 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 86 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Marlo is Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost, about 13 minutes' drive. For a library, Orbost Library in Orbost is about 13 minutes' drive from Marlo. For a beach, Sailors Grave Beach in Cape Conran is about 18 minutes' drive from Marlo. Lakes Entrance is the closest bigger centre to Marlo, about 60 minutes away by car, and has 3 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Marlo are the playground at Forest Park in Orbost (13 min), Orbost Library in Orbost (13 min), Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost (13 min), Sailors Grave Beach in Cape Conran (18 min) and Ewings Marsh in Waygara (32 min).

Best for

Free days on the Marlo Walking Track Network and in Marlo's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Marlo, Orbost has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Marlo, Cape Conran has a beach. Bemm River, next to Marlo, has 2 playgrounds. Manorina, Corringle, Cabbage Tree Creek and Brodribb River also border Marlo.
