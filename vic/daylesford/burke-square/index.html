Burke Square

Park in Daylesford · Basic info

Burke Square is a park in Daylesford, in the Hepburn Shire Council area, good for all ages. CFA/Bridport St (bus) is a 9-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 824 m away with a baby change table.
