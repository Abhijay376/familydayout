Skate park at McCormack Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Laverton · Basic info

Skate park at McCormack Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Laverton, in the Hobsons Bay City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Laverton College/Bladin St (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 205 m away.
