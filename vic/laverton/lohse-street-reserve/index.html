Lohse Street Reserve

Park in Laverton · Basic info

Lohse Street Reserve is a park in Laverton, in the Hobsons Bay City Council area, good for all ages. Laverton Station/Maher Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 40 m away.
