Playground at Dick Murdock Reserve

Playground in Laverton · Basic info

Playground at Dick Murdock Reserve in Laverton is a public playground in the Hobsons Bay City area. Pirate Pizza is about 250 m away if you need food, and Playground at McCormack Park is another option about 450 m away. There's a public toilet about 690 m away, and buses 154, 400, 414 and 417 stop at Barwise St/Bladin St, a 5-minute walk away.
