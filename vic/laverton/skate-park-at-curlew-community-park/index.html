Skate park at Curlew Community Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Laverton · Basic info

Skate park at Curlew Community Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Laverton, in the Hobsons Bay City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Laverton Station/Railway Ave (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 190 m away.
