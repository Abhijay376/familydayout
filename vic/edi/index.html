Other parks and reserves in Edi include Edi Cutting. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Edi. For camping, there's Edi Cutting. The Vines 2 Vines: Whitfield to Milawa passes through Edi.

Getting around

Edi has no train station; Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 33 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the King Valley-Wangaratta route stop in Edi. By car from Edi, Wangaratta is about 31 minutes away and Benalla about 49 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Splashpark in Wangaratta is about 28 minutes' drive from Edi. For a library, Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta is about 31 minutes' drive from Edi. For a skate park, the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta is about 28 minutes' drive from Edi. The closest cinema to Edi is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 31 minutes' drive. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Edi, about 31 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Edi are the playground at Apex Park in Moyhu (7 min), Myrrhee Recreation Reserve in Myrrhee (20 min), Splashpark in Wangaratta (28 min), the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta (28 min), Glenrowan Museum in Glenrowan (27 min) and Cobb & Co in Glenrowan (27 min).
