Aberfeldie is in the Moonee Valley area, and our data lists 10 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Aberfeldie has a library.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Aberfeldie is Aberfeldie Park, covering about 15 hectares. Clifton Park and Riverside Park are the next largest. Playgrounds around Aberfeldie include the playground at Clifton Park, the playground at Clifton Park and the playground at Riverside Park. Our data shows 4 public toilets and 4 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Aberfeldie. Places to visit in Aberfeldie include Little library. For walks and rides, Maribyrnong River Trail crosses Aberfeldie. Our data lists 3 cafes and family restaurants in Aberfeldie for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Aberfeldie. The closest to Aberfeldie is Essendon Railway Station (metro), about 1.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 465, 467, 468 and 903 stop in Aberfeldie. By car from Aberfeldie, Sunshine is about 13 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 16 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 17 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Sydenham Street Swimming in Moonee Ponds is about 5 minutes' drive from Aberfeldie. The closest beach to Aberfeldie is Sandridge Beach in Port Melbourne, about 16 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Parkitect Pump Track in Essendon is about 5 minutes' drive from Aberfeldie. The closest cinema to Aberfeldie is Palace Cinemas Penny Lane in Moonee Ponds, about 6 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Strike Bowling Bar in Maribyrnong is about 6 minutes' drive from Aberfeldie. For a zoo or wildlife park, Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville is about 12 minutes' drive from Aberfeldie.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 10 playgrounds across Aberfeldie. Rainy days at the library in Aberfeldie. Free days on the Maribyrnong River Trail and in Aberfeldie's parks.
