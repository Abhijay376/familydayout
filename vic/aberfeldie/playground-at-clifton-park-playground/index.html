Playground at Clifton Park

Playground in Aberfeldie · Official details

Playground at Clifton Park in Aberfeldie is a public playground in the Moonee Valley City area. Pizza Raphael is about 380 m away if you need food, and Playground at Bradshaw Reserve is another option about 480 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and buses 465 and 903 stop at Ogilvie St/Buckley St, a 3-minute walk away.
