Playground at Aberfeldie Park

Playground in Aberfeldie · Official details

Playground at Aberfeldie Park is a public playground in Aberfeldie, in the Moonee Valley City area. Playground at Gym Equipment - Aberfeldie Park is another option about 200 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 750 m from the playground, and buses 467 and 468 stop at Corio St/Waverley St, a 4-minute walk away.
