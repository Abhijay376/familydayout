Playground at Clifton Park

Playground in Aberfeldie · Basic info

Playground at Clifton Park in Aberfeldie is a public playground in the Moonee Valley City area. The Maribyrnong River Trail is about 310 m away, and for food, Pizza Raphael is about 490 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 460 m from the playground, and buses 465 and 903 stop at Batman St/Buckley St, a 3-minute walk away.
