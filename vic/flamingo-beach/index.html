Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Flamingo Beach in the Wellington area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it. Flamingo Beach does have a beach.

Landmarks

Places to visit in Flamingo Beach include Flamingo Beach.

Getting around

There's no train station in Flamingo Beach. The closest to Flamingo Beach is Sale Railway Station (V/Line), about 30 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Flamingo Beach, so most trips start by car. By car from Flamingo Beach, Sale is about 57 minutes away and Traralgon about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

Sale is the closest bigger centre to Flamingo Beach, about 57 minutes away by car, and has 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Flamingo Beach, the nearest places for kids by road are Glomar Beach in Glomar Beach (7 min), Golden Beach Community Centre Playground in Golden Beach (15 min), Lions Park Playground in Loch Sport (51 min), Historic Gunpowder Magazine in Sale (54 min), Sale Historical Museum in Sale (55 min) and Sale Skate Park in Sale (55 min).

Best for

Beach days at Flamingo Beach.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Flamingo Beach, Golden Beach has 1 playground and a beach. Glomar Beach, next to Flamingo Beach, has a beach.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Flamingo Beach, without traffic.
