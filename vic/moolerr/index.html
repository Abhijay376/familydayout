Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Moolerr in the Northern Grampians area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Moolerr has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 66 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Moolerr, so most trips start by car. By car from Moolerr, Stawell is about 58 minutes away and Maryborough about 67 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud is about 5 minutes' drive from Moolerr. For a skate park, Donald BMX Track in Donald is about 38 minutes' drive from Moolerr. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 38 minutes' drive from Moolerr. The nearest larger centre is Stawell, about 58 minutes from Moolerr by car, with 6 playgrounds and a pool. From Moolerr, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Queen Mary Botanic Gardens in St Arnaud (5 min), Market Square Park Playground in St Arnaud (5 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (5 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (35 min), Donald BMX Track in Donald (38 min) and Rex Theatre in Charlton (38 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Moolerr.

Nearby suburbs

St Arnaud, next to Moolerr, has 4 playgrounds and a pool. Carapooee West and Beazleys Bridge also border Moolerr.
