Wonga is in the South Gippsland area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Wonga, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Wonga has no train station; Morwell Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 43 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wonga, so most trips start by car. By car from Wonga, Leongatha is about 47 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 67 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Foster Outdoor Pool in Foster is about 18 minutes' drive from Wonga. For a library, Foster Library in Foster is about 17 minutes' drive from Wonga. For a beach, Toora Beach in Toora is about 31 minutes' drive from Wonga. The closest skate park to Wonga is the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora, about 23 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Leongatha, about 47 minutes from Wonga by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Wonga, the nearest places for kids by road are Luminous Glass Studio in Foster (17 min), Stockyard Gallery in Foster (17 min), Foster Library in Foster (17 min), Foster Outdoor Pool in Foster (18 min), the playground at Stanley Street Playground in Toora (23 min) and Toora Pool in Toora (22 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wonga.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Wonga, Toora has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. Next door to Wonga, Foster has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Boolarong also borders Wonga.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Wonga, without traffic.
