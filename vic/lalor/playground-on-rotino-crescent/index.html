Whittlesea Public Gardens Playground

Playground in Lalor · Basic info

Whittlesea Public Gardens Playground in Lalor is a public playground in the Whittlesea City area. The Galada Tamboore Trail passes about 250 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 310 m away. It's easiest by car, about 7 minutes from Lalor.
