Playground at V. R Michael Reserve

Playground in Lalor · Basic info

Playground at V. R Michael Reserve is a public playground in Lalor, in the Whittlesea City area. For food, McDonald's is about 120 m away, and Playground on Jovic Road is another option about 570 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 220 m from the playground, and bus 555 stops at Childs Rd/High St, a 3-minute walk away.
