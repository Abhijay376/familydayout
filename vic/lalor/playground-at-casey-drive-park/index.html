Playground at Casey Drive Park

Playground in Lalor · Basic info

Playground at Casey Drive Park is a public playground in Lalor, in the Whittlesea City area. The Darebin Creek Trail passes about 150 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 250 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 800 m away, and bus 559 stops at Dennis St/Darebin Dr, a 7-minute walk away.
