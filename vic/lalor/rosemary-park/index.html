Rosemary Park

Park in Lalor · Basic info

Rosemary Park is a park in Lalor, in the Whittlesea City Council area, good for all ages. Gilwell Rd/Edgars Rd (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1666 m away.
