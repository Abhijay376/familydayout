Gillwell Park

Park in Lalor · Basic info

Gillwell Park is a park in Lalor, in the Whittlesea City Council area, good for all ages. Caringford Park/Kingsway Dr (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1308 m away with a baby change table.
