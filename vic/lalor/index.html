Lalor in the Whittlesea area has 20 playgrounds and 27 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, Lalor has a library.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lalor is Huskisson Recreation Reserve, covering about 6 hectares. V. R Michael Reserve and W A Smith Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Lalor include Lorne Park, Boston Park and Moffat Park. Playgrounds in Lalor include Sarissa Park Playground, the playground at Lorne Park and the playground at Moffat Park. Our data shows 8 public toilets in Lalor. In Lalor you'll also find Lalor Library. Lalor Hub and Lalor Plaza Shopping Centre are the main shopping centres. The Darebin Creek Trail, Galada Tamboore Trail and Galada Tamboore Pathway all pass through Lalor. Our data lists 14 cafes and family restaurants in Lalor for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Lalor Railway Station (metro: Mernda Line) in Lalor. Buses 357, 554, 555, 556, 557, 559, 566 and 570 stop in Lalor. By car from Lalor, Broadmeadows is about 14 minutes away, Sunshine about 25 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 27 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lalor is Thomastown Aquatic and Recreation Centre in Thomastown, about 5 minutes' drive. For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 26 minutes' drive from Lalor. The closest skate park to Lalor is the bmx track at Sycamore Reserve in Mill Park, about 7 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Reading Epping Cinema in Epping is about 4 minutes' drive from Lalor. The closest indoor play centre to Lalor is Timezone in Epping, about 4 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bundoora Park Farm in Bundoora is about 16 minutes' drive from Lalor.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 20 playgrounds across Lalor. Rainy days at the library in Lalor. Free days on the Darebin Creek Trail and in Lalor's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Mill Park, next to Lalor, has 44 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Epping, next to Lalor, has 43 playgrounds, a cinema and a skate park. Next door to Lalor, Bundoora has 41 playgrounds, a library and a zoo or wildlife park. Thomastown, next to Lalor, has 18 playgrounds, a pool and a library.
