Boston Park

Park in Lalor · Basic info

Boston Park is a park in Lalor, in the Whittlesea City Council area, good for all ages. Lalor Plaza/Darebin Dr (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 244 m away with a baby change table.
