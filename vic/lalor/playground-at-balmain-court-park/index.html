Playground at Balmain Court Park

Playground in Lalor · Basic info

This is a public playground in Lalor. The Edgars Creek Trail is about 200 m away, and for food, Romeo's Pizza is about 340 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.2 km from the playground, and bus 357 stops at Robert St/Edgars Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
