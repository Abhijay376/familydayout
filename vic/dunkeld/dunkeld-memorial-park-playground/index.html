Dunkeld Memorial Park Playground

Playground in Dunkeld · Basic info

Dunkeld Memorial Park Playground in Dunkeld is a public playground in the Southern Grampians Shire area. The Dunkeld to Mount Sturgeon Discovery Trail passes about 100 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and coaches stop at Information Centre/Glenelg Hwy, a 1-minute walk away.
