Dunkeld Arboretum

Park in Dunkeld · Official details

Dunkeld Arboretum is a park in Dunkeld, in the Southern Grampians Shire Council area, good for all ages. Information Centre/Glenelg Hwy (coach) is a 14-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 857 m away and dogs are allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Dunkeld Arboretum?
Dunkeld Arboretum is in Dunkeld, Victoria.

The nearest public toilet is about 857 m away.

The closest stop is Information Centre/Glenelg Hwy (routes Mount Gambier - Melbourne via Hamilton & Ballarat, Warrnambool - Melbourne via Ararat & Hamilton), a 14-minute walk.
