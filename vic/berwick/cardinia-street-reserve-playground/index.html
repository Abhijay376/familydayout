Cardinia Street Reserve Playground

Playground in Berwick · Basic info

Cardinia Street Reserve Playground is a public playground in Berwick, in the Casey City area. Older kids have a skate park about 370 m away, and SECAN Art Gallery is about 220 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 540 m from the playground, and bus 837 stops at Manuka Rd/Brisbane St, a 6-minute walk away.
