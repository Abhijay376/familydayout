Playground on Quarry Hills Drive

Playground in Berwick · Basic info

This is a public playground in Berwick. The Hallam Bypass Trail is about 400 m away, and for food, Gather: Food, coffee & Co is about 140 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 70 m from the playground, and buses 828, 838, 926 and 981 stop at Wilson Botanic Park/Princes Hwy, a 7-minute walk away.
