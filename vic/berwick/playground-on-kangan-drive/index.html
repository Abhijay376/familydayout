Playground on Kangan Drive

Playground in Berwick · Basic info

Playground on Kangan Drive in Berwick is a public playground in the Casey City area. The Haileybury College Trail passes about 280 m away, and Playground on Meg Way is another option about 470 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 250 m away, and bus 928 stops at Beaconhills College - Berwick/Kangan Dr , a 3-minute walk away.
