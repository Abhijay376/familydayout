Melbourne Water Pipe Reserve

Park in Berwick · Basic info

Melbourne Water Pipe Reserve is a park in Berwick, in the Casey City Council area, good for all ages. Irving Cl/Bridgewater Bvd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1394 m away with a baby change table.
