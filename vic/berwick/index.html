Berwick is in the Casey area, and our data lists 58 playgrounds and 59 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Berwick has 2 libraries, 2 pools, 2 museums or galleries and 2 skate parks.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Berwick is Cardinia Creek Parklands Reserve, covering about 162 hectares. Cardinia Creek Parklands Reserve and Cardinia Creek Parklands Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Berwick include Alira Park, Buchanan Park and Ivens Reserve. Playgrounds around Berwick include Bellbird Park, the playground at Alira Park and Timbarra Park PLayground. Our data shows 27 public toilets in Berwick. Places to visit in Berwick include Paperworks Gallery, SECAN Art Gallery, Jump Swim School Berwick, Splash's Swimming School and Berwick Library. For older kids in Berwick there's the bmx track at Kirsty Lottkowitz Reserve and the skate park at Buchanan Park. Eden Rise, Eden Rise Shopping Centre and Parkhill Plaza Shopping Centre are the main shopping centres. For walks and rides, Hallam Bypass Trail, Hallam Valley Trail and Berwick Town Creek Trail cross Berwick. Our data lists 31 cafes and family restaurants in Berwick for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Berwick has a train station: Berwick Railway Station (metro: Pakenham Line and Traralgon - Melbourne via Morwell, Moe & Pakenham). 16 bus routes stop in Berwick, including 828, 831, 834, 835, 836 and 837. By car from Berwick, Pakenham is about 14 minutes away, Cranbourne about 17 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 39 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a beach, Carrum Beach in Carrum is about 29 minutes' drive from Berwick. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Narre Warren is about 8 minutes' drive from Berwick.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 58 playgrounds across Berwick. Rainy days at the library and museum or gallery in Berwick. Teens who skate or ride, with the bmx track at Kirsty Lottkowitz Reserve in Berwick. Free days on the Hallam Bypass Trail and in Berwick's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Narre Warren, next to Berwick, has 30 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Berwick, Clyde North has 34 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Berwick, Narre Warren South has 18 playgrounds and a pool. Officer, next to Berwick, has 14 playgrounds. Cranbourne North, Beaconsfield, Narre Warren North and Harkaway also border Berwick.
