Warwick Glendenning Reserve Playground

Playground in Berwick · Basic info

Warwick Glendenning Reserve Playground is a public playground in Berwick, in the Casey City area. Bournevale Drive Reserve Playground is another option about 370 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.2 km from the playground, and bus 839 stops at W K Glendenning Reserve/Avebury Dr, a 2-minute walk away.
