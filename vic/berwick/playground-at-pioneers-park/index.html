Playground at Pioneers Park

Playground in Berwick · Basic info

This is a public playground in Berwick. The Hallam Bypass Trail passes about 390 m away, and Berwick Library is about 340 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and buses 837 and 839 stop at Berwick Inn/Lyall Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
