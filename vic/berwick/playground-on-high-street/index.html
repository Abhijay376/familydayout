Playground on High Street

Playground in Berwick · Basic info

Playground on High Street is a public playground in Berwick, in the Casey City area. The Haileybury College Trail is about 400 m away, and SECAN Art Gallery is about 310 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 210 m from the playground, and buses 836, 837, 838 and 926 stop at Haileybury College/Princes Hwy, a 6-minute walk away.
