Playground on Stefan Drive

Playground in Berwick · Basic info

Playground on Stefan Drive in Berwick is a public playground in the Casey City area. The Hallam Valley Trail passes about 350 m away, and Playground at Lily Vale Close Reserve is another option about 120 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.7 km away, and bus 847 stops at Ashfield Dr/Centre Rd, a 8-minute walk away.
