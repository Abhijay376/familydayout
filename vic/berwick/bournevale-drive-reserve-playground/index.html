Bournevale Drive Reserve Playground

Playground in Berwick · Basic info

Bournevale Drive Reserve Playground in Berwick is a public playground in the Casey City area. Warwick Glendenning Reserve Playground is another option about 370 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 900 m from the playground, and bus 839 stops at Wilson Botanic Park/Bournevale Dr, a 1-minute walk away.
