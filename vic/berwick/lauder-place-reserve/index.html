Lauder Place Reserve

Park in Berwick · Basic info

Lauder Place Reserve is a park in Berwick, in the Casey City Council area, good for all ages. Brentwood Park PS/Chirnside Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1737 m away with a baby change table.
