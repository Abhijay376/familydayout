Playground at Elwood Park

Playground in Elwood · Basic info

Playground at Elwood Park in Elwood is a public playground in the Port Phillip City area. The Bay Trail is about 120 m away, and Elwood Beach is about 180 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 230 m from the playground, and buses 600, 606, 922 and 923 stop at Pine Ave/Ormond Rd, a 7-minute walk away.
