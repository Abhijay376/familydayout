Chicken Park

Playground in Elwood · Basic info

Chicken Park is a public playground in Elwood, in the Port Phillip City area. The Elster Creek Trail passes about 330 m away, and Shaggy's Pizza is about 160 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and tram 67 stops at Scott St/Brighton Rd #40, a 5-minute walk away.
