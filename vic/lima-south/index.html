Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Lima South in the Benalla area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 3 public toilets in Lima South. The Mount Samaria Track and Wild Dog Creek Falls Track all pass through Lima South.

Getting around

Lima South has no train station; Benalla Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 33 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lima South, so most trips start by car. By car from Lima South, Mansfield is about 26 minutes away, Benalla about 32 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lima South is Mansfield Outdoor Swimming Pool in Mansfield, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest library to Lima South is Mansfield Library in Mansfield, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Lima South is the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield, about 27 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Swanpool Cinema in Swanpool is about 12 minutes' drive from Lima South. For a zoo or wildlife park, Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield is about 36 minutes' drive from Lima South. Mansfield is the closest bigger centre to Lima South, about 26 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Lima South, the nearest places for kids by road are Swanpool Cinema in Swanpool (12 min), the playground at Maindample Community Park in Maindample (22 min), Mansfield Library in Mansfield (26 min), Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield (26 min), the playground at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (27 min) and the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (27 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Lima South on the Mount Samaria Track.

Nearby suburbs

Swanpool, next to Lima South, has a cinema. Bridge Creek and Barjarg also border Lima South.
