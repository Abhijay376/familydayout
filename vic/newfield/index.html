Newfield is in the Corangamite area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Newfield, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Newfield has no train station; Terang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 37 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Newfield, so most trips start by car. By car from Newfield, Warrnambool is about 53 minutes away and Colac about 67 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon is about 9 minutes' drive from Newfield. For a beach, Rutledge Creek Beach in Port Campbell is about 10 minutes' drive from Newfield. The closest skate park to Newfield is Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell, about 6 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 53 minutes from Newfield by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Newfield, the nearest places for kids by road are Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell (6 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (9 min), the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (9 min), Rutledge Creek Beach in Port Campbell (10 min), Sherbrooke Estuary in Port Campbell (11 min) and the playground at Peterborough Coastal Reserve (COM) in Peterborough (15 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Newfield.

Nearby suburbs

Port Campbell, next to Newfield, has 1 playground, a beach and a skate park. Timboon, next to Newfield, has a pool and a skate park. Cowleys Creek and Cooriemungle also border Newfield.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Newfield, without traffic.
