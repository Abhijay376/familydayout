Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside St Clair in the Bass Coast area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in St Clair. The closest to St Clair is Stony Point Railway Station (metro), about 42 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside St Clair, so most trips start by car. By car from St Clair, Wonthaggi is about 7 minutes away, Leongatha about 32 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Wonthaggi is about 5 minutes' drive from St Clair. For a library, Wonthaggi Principal Library in Wonthaggi is about 6 minutes' drive from St Clair. For a beach, Baxters Beach in Wonthaggi is about 14 minutes' drive from St Clair. The closest skate park to St Clair is Wonthaggi Skate Park in Wonthaggi, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to St Clair is Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to St Clair is Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville, about 27 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wonthaggi, about 7 minutes from St Clair by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From St Clair, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Rodney Court Reserve in Wonthaggi (5 min), Wonthaggi Skate Park in Wonthaggi (5 min), Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Wonthaggi (5 min), the playground at Jacobs Park in North Wonthaggi (7 min), Bass Coast Arts Society in Wonthaggi (6 min) and Wonthaggi & District Historical Museum in Wonthaggi (6 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from St Clair.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to St Clair, Wonthaggi has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to St Clair, Inverloch has 4 playgrounds and a beach. Next door to St Clair, North Wonthaggi has 1 playground. Wattle Bank and Lance Creek also border St Clair.
