Nuggetty is in the Mount Alexander area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Nuggety Quarry Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Nuggetty, at about 11 hectares. Nuggety Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Nuggetty has no train station; Castlemaine Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 4 stops in Nuggetty. By car from Nuggetty, Castlemaine is about 30 minutes away, Bendigo about 41 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Nuggetty is Maldon Swimming Pool in Maldon, about 12 minutes' drive. For a library, Athenaeum Library in Maldon is about 12 minutes' drive from Nuggetty. The closest skate park to Nuggetty is BMX Track in Castlemaine, about 30 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 41 minutes' drive from Nuggetty. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 38 minutes' drive from Nuggetty. Castlemaine is the closest bigger centre to Nuggetty, about 30 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Nuggetty are Athenaeum Library in Maldon (12 min), Maldon Swimming Pool in Maldon (12 min), the playground at Maldon Shire Gardens in Maldon (12 min), Maldon District Museum in Maldon (12 min), Maldon Vintage Machinery Museum in Maldon (13 min) and Newstead Swimming Pool in Newstead (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Nuggetty.

Nearby suburbs

Maldon, next to Nuggetty, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Baringhup also borders Nuggetty.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Nuggetty, without traffic.
