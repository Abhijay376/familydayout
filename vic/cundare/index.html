Cundare is in the Colac Otway area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Cundare Lake Reserve is the largest mapped park in Cundare, at about 77 hectares.

Getting around

Cundare has no train station; Colac Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cundare, so most trips start by car. By car from Cundare, Colac is about 32 minutes away, Geelong about 76 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood is about 45 minutes' drive from Cundare. For a library, Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac is about 33 minutes' drive from Cundare. The closest skate park to Cundare is the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt, about 37 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Colac Cinemas in Colac is about 33 minutes' drive from Cundare. The closest indoor play centre to Cundare is Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac, about 32 minutes' drive. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Cundare, about 32 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Cundare, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Cressy Picnic Area in Cressy (21 min), the playground at Red Rock Scenic Reserve in Alvie (36 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (32 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (33 min), Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (33 min) and the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (37 min).

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Cundare.
