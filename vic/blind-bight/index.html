Blind Bight is in the Casey area, and our data lists 2 playgrounds and 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Blind Bight Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Blind Bight, at about 4 hectares. Blind Bight Bushland Reserve and Blind Bight Foreshore Reserve are the next largest. Blind Bight also has Anchorage Drive Reserve. Playgrounds in Blind Bight include the playground at Blind Bight Bushland Reserve and the playground at Blind Bight Foreshore Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Blind Bight. The Blind Bight Nature Reserve Track passes through Blind Bight.

Getting around

Blind Bight has no train station; Cranbourne Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 795 stops in Blind Bight. By car from Blind Bight, Cranbourne is about 14 minutes away, Frankston about 22 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 55 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Blind Bight is King Swim in Clyde North, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest library to Blind Bight is Cranbourne Library in Cranbourne East, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Blind Bight is Frankston Beach in Frankston, about 26 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Skate Park in Clyde is about 11 minutes' drive from Blind Bight. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Frankston is about 23 minutes' drive from Blind Bight. The closest indoor play centre to Blind Bight is Kidz Shed in Hastings, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Blind Bight is Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale, about 10 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Cranbourne, about 14 minutes from Blind Bight by car, with 27 playgrounds. From Blind Bight, the nearest places for kids by road are Fisherman's Cottage in Tooradin (7 min), Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale (10 min), Skate Park in Clyde (11 min), Criterium Cycling Track in Cranbourne East (13 min), King Swim in Clyde North (14 min) and Cranbourne Library in Cranbourne East (14 min).

Best for

Free days on the Blind Bight Nature Reserve Track and in Blind Bight's parks.
