Blind Bight Foreshore Reserve

Park in Blind Bight · Basic info

Blind Bight Foreshore Reserve is a park in Blind Bight, in the Casey City Council area, good for all ages. Harbour Way/Anchorage Dr (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 316 m away.
