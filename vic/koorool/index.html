Our data lists 5 parks and reserves in Koorool, in the Wellington area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Koorool is Avon - Mt Hedrick Natural Features and Scenic Reserve, covering about 5,636 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Koorool include Paddy Lees Crossing, Wombat Crossing Camp Site An and Wombat Crossing Camp Site B. Picnic areas in Koorool include Paddy Lees Crossing, Wombat Crossing Camp Site An and Wombat Crossing Camp Site B.

Getting around

There's no train station in Koorool. The closest to Koorool is Stratford Railway Station (V/Line), about 30 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Koorool, so most trips start by car. By car from Koorool, Sale is about 70 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Koorool, the nearest places for kids by road are Anzac Park Playground in Briagolong (45 min), Boisdale Recreation Reserve Playground in Boisdale (45 min), Skate Park in Stratford (62 min), Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford (62 min) and Former Drive-in site in Sale (70 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Koorool.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Koorool, Boisdale has 1 playground. Valencia Creek, Toolome and Monomak also border Koorool.
