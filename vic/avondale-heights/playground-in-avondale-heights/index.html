Playground in Avondale Heights

Playground in Avondale Heights · Basic info

Playground in Avondale Heights is a public playground in Avondale Heights, in the Moonee Valley City area. Avondale Heights Library and Learning Centre is about 130 m away, and McDonald's is about 230 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 160 m away, and bus 406 stops at Westminster Dr/Military Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
