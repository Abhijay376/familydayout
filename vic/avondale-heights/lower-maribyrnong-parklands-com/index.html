Lower Maribyrnong Parklands (COM)

Park in Avondale Heights · Basic info

Lower Maribyrnong Parklands (COM) is a park in Avondale Heights, in the Moonee Valley City Council area, good for all ages. Canning St/Herbert St (bus) is a 9-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1361 m away.
