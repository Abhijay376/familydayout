Avondale Heights Library and Learning Centre

Library in Avondale Heights · Basic info

Avondale Heights Library and Learning Centre is a library in Avondale Heights, in the Moonee Valley City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Alexander St/Military Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 152 m away with a baby change table.
