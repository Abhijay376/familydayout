Larwood Play Area

Playground in Avondale Heights · Basic info

Larwood Play Area is a public playground in Avondale Heights, in the Moonee Valley City area. The Maribyrnong River Trail passes about 120 m away, and Anchor Fish Bar & Grill is about 420 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 390 m away, and buses 406 and 407 stop at Canning Reserve/Canning St, a 3-minute walk away.
