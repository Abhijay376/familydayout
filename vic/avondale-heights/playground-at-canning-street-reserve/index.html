Playground at Canning Street Reserve

Playground in Avondale Heights · Official details

Playground at Canning Street Reserve in Avondale Heights is a public playground in the Moonee Valley City area. The Maribyrnong River Trail passes about 30 m away, and Larwood Play Area is another option about 340 m away. There's a public toilet about 250 m away, and buses 406 and 407 stop at Canning Reserve/Canning St, a 3-minute walk away.
