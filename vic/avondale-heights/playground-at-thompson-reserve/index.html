Playground at Thompson Reserve

Playground in Avondale Heights · Official details

Playground at Thompson Reserve is a public playground in Avondale Heights, in the Moonee Valley City area. The Maribyrnong River Trail is about 160 m away, and Playground at Mountainview Reserve is another option about 350 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 890 m from the playground, and bus 407 stops at Davis Ave/Skewes St, a 4-minute walk away.
