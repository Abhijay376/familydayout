Playground at Rhonda Davis Reserve

Playground in Avondale Heights · Basic info

Playground at Rhonda Davis Reserve is a public playground in Avondale Heights, in the Moonee Valley City area. The Maribyrnong River Trail is about 240 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 940 m from the playground, and bus 407 stops at Canning St/Herbert St, a 4-minute walk away.
