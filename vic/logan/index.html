Logan in the Loddon area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Logan is Logan Streamside Reserve, covering about 12 hectares. Synnotts Diggings Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Logan. The closest to Logan is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 53 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Logan, so most trips start by car. By car from Logan, Maryborough is about 59 minutes away, Bendigo about 68 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Logan is St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud, about 19 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 59 minutes from Logan by car, with a library. From Logan, the nearest places for kids by road are Bi-Centennial Park Playground in St Arnaud (17 min), the playground at Queen Mary Botanic Gardens in St Arnaud (18 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (19 min), Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (20 min), Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (20 min) and Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Logan.

Nearby suburbs

Cochranes Creek and Burkes Flat also border Logan.
