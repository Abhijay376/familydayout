Brumby in the East Gippsland area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Buchan Headwaters Wilderness Zone is the largest mapped park in Brumby, at about 30,326 hectares. Tambo State Forest and Upper Buchan River Heritage River are the next largest. The Mt Stradbroke Walking Track passes through Brumby.

Getting around

There's no train station in Brumby. The closest to Brumby is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 96 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Brumby, so most trips start by car. By car from Brumby, Lakes Entrance is about 3.5 hours away and Bairnsdale about 4 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Free days on the Mt Stradbroke Walking Track and in Brumby's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Brumby, Wulgulmerang East has 1 playground. Nunniong, Cobberas, Bindi and Benambra also border Brumby.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Brumby, without traffic.
