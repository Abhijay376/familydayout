Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Clarkefield in the Macedon Ranges area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Trains stop at Clarkefield Railway Station (V/Line: Bendigo - Melbourne via Gisborne and Echuca/Moama - Melbourne via Bendigo or Heathcote) in Clarkefield. Buses on the Lancefield - Sunbury/Clarkefield via Romsey & Monegeeta route stop in Clarkefield. V/Line coaches stop in Clarkefield. By car from Clarkefield, Broadmeadows is about 35 minutes away, Melton about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 50 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Sunbury Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Sunbury is about 18 minutes' drive from Clarkefield. The closest library to Clarkefield is Sunbury Library in Sunbury, about 18 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Romsey Skate Park in Romsey is about 14 minutes' drive from Clarkefield. The closest cinema to Clarkefield is Reading Cinema in Sunbury, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Clarkefield is Timezone in Craigieburn, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Clarkefield is Funfields in Whittlesea, about 41 minutes' drive. Broadmeadows is the closest bigger centre to Clarkefield, about 35 minutes away by car, and has 12 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Clarkefield, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Village Green in Sunbury (12 min), the playground at Turnberry Drive Reserve in Sunbury (13 min), Sunbury Library in Sunbury (18 min), Romsey Skate Park in Romsey (14 min), Romsey Library in Romsey (15 min) and Sunbury Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Sunbury (18 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Clarkefield.

Nearby suburbs

Sunbury, next to Clarkefield, has 43 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Riddells Creek, next to Clarkefield, has 4 playgrounds. Next door to Clarkefield, Mickleham has 4 playgrounds. Next door to Clarkefield, Beveridge has 4 playgrounds and a skate park. Wildwood, Oaklands Junction, Darraweit Guim and Bolinda also border Clarkefield.
