Westby is in the Gannawarra area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Westblades Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Westby, at about 70 hectares.

Getting around

Westby has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 9.7 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Westby, so most trips start by car. By car from Westby, Swan Hill is about 53 minutes away and Echuca about 87 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Swimming Pool in Kerang is about 11 minutes' drive from Westby. The closest library to Westby is Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang, about 11 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Barham Skate Park in Koondrook is about 32 minutes' drive from Westby. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Westby, about 53 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Westby, the nearest places for kids by road are Swimming Pool in Kerang (11 min), Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (11 min), the playground at Lake Meran W.R in Lake Meran (28 min), the playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV) in Koondrook (31 min), Barham Branch Library in Koondrook (31 min) and Barham Skate Park in Koondrook (32 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Westby.
