Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Wandiligong, in the Alpine area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Wandiligong is Alpine Park, covering about 5 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Wandiligong. The Murray to Mountains Rail Trail: Bright – Wandiligong, Bennetts Trail and Flowmingo all pass through Wandiligong.

Getting around

Wandiligong has no train station; Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 75 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wandiligong, so most trips start by car. By car from Wandiligong, Bright is about 10 minutes away and Wangaratta about 77 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Wandiligong is Bright Splash Park in Bright, about 9 minutes' drive. For a library, Bright Library in Bright is about 8 minutes' drive from Wandiligong. For a beach, Pebble Beach in Tawonga South is about 35 minutes' drive from Wandiligong. For a skate park, Bright BMX Track in Bright is about 7 minutes' drive from Wandiligong. The closest cinema to Wandiligong is Sun Cinema Bright in Bright, about 9 minutes' drive. Bright is the closest bigger centre to Wandiligong, about 10 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Wandiligong, the nearest places for kids by road are Bright BMX Track in Bright (7 min), Bright Library in Bright (8 min), Bright Art Gallery in Bright (9 min), Bright Museum in Bright (9 min), Bright Splash Park in Bright (9 min) and Sun Cinema Bright in Bright (9 min).

Best for

Free days on the Murray to Mountains Rail Trail: Bright – Wandiligong and in Wandiligong's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Bright, next to Wandiligong, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Harrietville, next to Wandiligong, has 2 playgrounds. Buckland also borders Wandiligong.
