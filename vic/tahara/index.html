Tahara in the Glenelg area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Tahara Recreation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Tahara, at about 11 hectares.

Getting around

Tahara has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 98 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tahara, so most trips start by car. By car from Tahara, Hamilton is about 29 minutes away and Portland about 58 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tahara is Merino Swimming Pool in Merino, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest library to Tahara is Hamilton Library in Hamilton, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Tahara is coleraine skate park in Coleraine, about 18 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton is about 29 minutes' drive from Tahara. Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Tahara, about 29 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Tahara are the playground at Peter Francis Points Arboretum in Coleraine (17 min), the playground at Coleraine Railway Reserve in Coleraine (18 min), coleraine skate park in Coleraine (18 min), Merino Swimming Pool in Merino (20 min), Hamilton History Centre in Hamilton (28 min) and Mud Gallery in Hamilton (29 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tahara.

Nearby suburbs

Branxholme, next to Tahara, has 1 playground. Tahara West, Tahara Bridge, Morgiana and Grassdale also border Tahara.
