Callignee North is in the Wellington area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Callignee Wildlife Reserve (no hunting) is the largest mapped park in Callignee North, at about 45 hectares.

Getting around

Callignee North has no train station; Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Callignee North, so most trips start by car. By car from Callignee North, Traralgon is about 30 minutes away, Sale about 71 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Callignee North is Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon, about 31 minutes' drive. For a library, Morwell Library in Morwell is about 37 minutes' drive from Callignee North. The closest skate park to Callignee North is Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon, about 33 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Callignee North is Village 8 in Morwell, about 36 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Traralgon, about 30 minutes from Callignee North by car, with 38 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Callignee North are the playground at Hourigan Park in Traralgon (28 min), the playground at Eric Taylor Reserve in Traralgon (29 min), Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon (31 min), Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon (33 min), Powerworks in Morwell (36 min) and Village 8 in Morwell (36 min).
