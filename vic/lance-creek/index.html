Lance Creek is in the Bass Coast area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Lance Creek, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Lance Creek has no train station; Longwarry Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 50 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lance Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Lance Creek, Wonthaggi is about 12 minutes away, Leongatha about 28 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lance Creek is Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Wonthaggi, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest library to Lance Creek is Wonthaggi Principal Library in Wonthaggi, about 11 minutes' drive. For a beach, Baxters Beach in Wonthaggi is about 19 minutes' drive from Lance Creek. The closest skate park to Lance Creek is Wonthaggi Skate Park in Wonthaggi, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Lance Creek is Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi, about 11 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville is about 26 minutes' drive from Lance Creek. The nearest larger centre is Wonthaggi, about 12 minutes from Lance Creek by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Lance Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Rodney Court Reserve in Wonthaggi (10 min), Wonthaggi Skate Park in Wonthaggi (9 min), Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Wonthaggi (10 min), the playground at Jacobs Park in North Wonthaggi (11 min), Bass Coast Arts Society in Wonthaggi (11 min) and Wonthaggi & District Historical Museum in Wonthaggi (11 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lance Creek.
