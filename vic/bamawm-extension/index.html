Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bamawm Extension is Cantwell Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 30 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bamawm Extension. The closest to Bamawm Extension is Echuca Railway Station (V/Line), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bamawm Extension, so most trips start by car. By car from Bamawm Extension, Echuca is about 17 minutes away, Bendigo about 72 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bamawm Extension is Lockington Pool in Lockington, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest library to Bamawm Extension is Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca, about 17 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Moama Echuca Community Skate Park in Echuca is about 19 minutes' drive from Bamawm Extension. For a cinema, Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca is about 17 minutes' drive from Bamawm Extension. Echuca is the closest bigger centre to Bamawm Extension, about 17 minutes away by car, and has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Bamawm Extension, the nearest places for kids by road are Lockington Pool in Lockington (13 min), Lockington District Swimming Pool in Lockington (13 min), the playground at Echuca Lions Park in Echuca (15 min), Holden Museum in Echuca (16 min), Hopwood Gardens Playground in Echuca (16 min) and Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca (17 min).
