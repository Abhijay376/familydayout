Glenrowan West is in the Benalla area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Glenrowan West is Eleven Mile Creek Bushland Reserve, covering about 5 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Glenrowan West. The closest to Glenrowan West is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glenrowan West, so most trips start by car. By car from Glenrowan West, Benalla is about 25 minutes away, Wangaratta about 31 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla is about 25 minutes' drive from Glenrowan West. For a library, Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta is about 33 minutes' drive from Glenrowan West. The closest skate park to Glenrowan West is Pump Track in Benalla, about 25 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 33 minutes' drive from Glenrowan West. The nearest larger centre is Benalla, about 25 minutes from Glenrowan West by car, with 5 playgrounds and a pool. From Glenrowan West, the nearest places for kids by road are Kate's Cottage in Glenrowan (16 min), Glenrowan Museum in Glenrowan (16 min), Pump Track in Benalla (25 min), Benalla Splash Park in Benalla (25 min), Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (25 min) and the playground at Benalla Botanical Gardens in Benalla (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Glenrowan West.

Nearby suburbs

Glenrowan, next to Glenrowan West, has 1 playground. Taminick, Lurg and Greta West also border Glenrowan West.
