Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Picola West in the Moira area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Picola West has no train station; Echuca Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 34 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Picola West, so most trips start by car. By car from Picola West, Echuca is about 62 minutes away and Shepparton about 69 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Nathalia Memorial Pool in Nathalia is about 24 minutes' drive from Picola West. The closest library to Picola West is Nathalia Library in Nathalia, about 22 minutes' drive. For a beach, Scotts Beach in Yalca is about 34 minutes' drive from Picola West. The closest skate park to Picola West is Strathmerton Community Skate Park in Strathmerton, about 37 minutes' drive. From Picola West, the nearest places for kids by road are Barmah Forest Heritge and Education Centre in Nathalia (22 min), Nathalia Library in Nathalia (22 min), the playground at Apex Park in Nathalia (22 min), Nathalia Memorial Pool in Nathalia (24 min), Scotts Beach in Yalca (34 min) and Paradise Beach in Yalca (34 min).
