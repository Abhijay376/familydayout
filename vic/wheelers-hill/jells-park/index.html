Jells Park

Park in Wheelers Hill · Official details

Jells Park is a park in Wheelers Hill, in the Monash City Council area, good for all ages. Petronella Ave/Ferntree Gully Rd (bus) is a 14-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 611 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed on a lead.

Details

Dogs
on lead (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
Changing Places facility at the Visitor Centre; Dogs not allowed in playgrounds or conservation areas; Playspaces at Yabbie Hill and Jells Park East; Fishing at Jells Lake needs a Recreational Fishing Licence (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Jells Park?
Jells Park is in Wheelers Hill, Victoria.
Is Jells Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Jells Park?
Yes, on a lead.
Are there toilets at Jells Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 611 m away, with a baby change table.
How do I get to Jells Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Petronella Ave/Ferntree Gully Rd (routes 693, 753), a 14-minute walk.
