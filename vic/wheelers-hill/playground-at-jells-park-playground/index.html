Playground at Jells Park

Playground in Wheelers Hill · Basic info

This is a public playground in Wheelers Hill. The Dandenong Creek Trail passes about 230 m away, and Playground on Jells Road is another option about 570 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 350 m away. It's easiest by car, about 6 minutes from Wheelers Hill.
