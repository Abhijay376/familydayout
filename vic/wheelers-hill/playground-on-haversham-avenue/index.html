Playground on Haversham Avenue

Playground in Wheelers Hill · Basic info

Playground on Haversham Avenue in Wheelers Hill is a public playground in the Monash City area. The EastLink Trail passes about 200 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.3 km away, and buses 693 and 753 stop at Marykirk Dr/Ferntree Gully Rd, a 8-minute walk away.
