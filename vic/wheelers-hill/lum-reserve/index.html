Lum Reserve

Park in Wheelers Hill · Basic info

Lum Reserve is a park in Wheelers Hill, in the Monash City Council area, good for all ages. Cintra Ave/Earlwood Dr (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 79 m away.
