Playground on Dirigo Drive

Playground in Wheelers Hill · Basic info

Playground on Dirigo Drive is a public playground in Wheelers Hill, in the Monash City area. Mulgrave Pizza House is about 20 m away if you need food, and Playground on Chancellor Drive is another option about 370 m away. There's a public toilet about 880 m away, and bus 848 stops at Brandon Park Dr/Lum Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
