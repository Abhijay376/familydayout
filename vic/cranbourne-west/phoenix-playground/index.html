Phoenix playground

Playground in Cranbourne West · Basic info

Phoenix playground is a public playground in Cranbourne West, in the Casey City area. Cranny Boy's Pizza is about 320 m away if you need food, and Playground on Strathlea Drive is another option about 370 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 790 m away, and buses 760 and 792 stop at Hayton Park Bvd/Hall Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
