Playground on Burgan Court

Playground in Cranbourne West · Basic info

Playground on Burgan Court is a public playground in Cranbourne West, in the Casey City area. The Cb Switch Yard Trail passes about 20 m away, and Aussiano Steaks & Pizza is about 230 m away if you need food. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and buses 897 and 982 stop at Geebung Rd/Central Parkway, a 4-minute walk away.
