Playground on Everlasting Boulevard

Playground in Cranbourne West · Basic info

Playground on Everlasting Boulevard is a public playground in Cranbourne West, in the Casey City area. Kami is about 40 m away if you need food, and Playground on Aquatic Drive is another option about 40 m away. There's a public toilet about 800 m away, and bus 792 stops at Barton PS/Everlasting Bvd, a 1-minute walk away.
