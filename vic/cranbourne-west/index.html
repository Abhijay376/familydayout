Cranbourne West is in the Casey area, and our data lists 21 playgrounds and 14 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Cranbourne West has a pool and 3 skate parks.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Cranbourne West is Scarborough Avenue Reserve, covering about 8 hectares. Cirai Crescent Reserve and Morningside Boulevard Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Cranbourne West include Princeton Park, Tony Way Reserve and Linda Drive Reserve. Playgrounds around Cranbourne West include Phoenix playground, Trueman Reserve Playground and the playground at Princeton Park. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Cranbourne West. Places to visit in Cranbourne West include JUMP! Swim Schools. For older kids in Cranbourne West there's Ambrosia Estate Skatepark and Cranbourne West BMX Track. For walks and rides, Cb Switch Yard Trail crosses Cranbourne West. Our data lists 30 cafes and family restaurants in Cranbourne West for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cranbourne West. The closest to Cranbourne West is Cranbourne Railway Station (metro), about 1.4 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 760, 791, 792, 863, 897 and 982 stop in Cranbourne West. By car from Cranbourne West, Cranbourne is about 4 minutes away, Dandenong about 17 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 42 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Cranbourne Library in Cranbourne East is about 7 minutes' drive from Cranbourne West. For a beach, Seaford Beach in Seaford is about 18 minutes' drive from Cranbourne West. The closest cinema to Cranbourne West is Village Cinemas in Frankston, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Cranbourne West is Oz Tenpin in Narre Warren, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Cranbourne West is Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale, about 16 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 21 playgrounds across Cranbourne West. Teens who skate or ride, with Ambrosia Estate Skatepark in Cranbourne West. Free days on the Cb Switch Yard Trail and in Cranbourne West's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Cranbourne, next to Cranbourne West, has 27 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Cranbourne West, Cranbourne North has 12 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Cranbourne West, Skye has 7 playgrounds. Next door to Cranbourne West, Lyndhurst has 7 playgrounds. Cranbourne South and Botanic Ridge also border Cranbourne West.
