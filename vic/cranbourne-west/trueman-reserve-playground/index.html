Trueman Reserve Playground

Playground in Cranbourne West · Basic info

This is a public playground in Cranbourne West. Kami is about 490 m away if you need food, and Playground on Everlasting Boulevard is another option about 400 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and buses 760 and 792 stop at Bradman Dr/Hall Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
