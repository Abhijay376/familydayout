Litchfield in the Buloke area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Witchipool I127 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Litchfield, at about 7 hectares. Witchipool I126 Bushland Reserve and Carron I125 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Litchfield include Carron I124 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Litchfield has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 110 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Litchfield, so most trips start by car. By car from Litchfield, Horsham is about 70 minutes away and Stawell about 79 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Litchfield is Donald Swimming Pool in Donald, about 14 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Donald BMX Track in Donald is about 16 minutes' drive from Litchfield. The closest places for kids by road from Litchfield are the playground at Donald Public Park in Donald (14 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (14 min), Donald BMX Track in Donald (16 min), Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem (14 min), the playground at Lake Watchem Lake Reserve in Watchem (17 min) and Birchip History Museum in Birchip (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Litchfield.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Litchfield, Donald has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Laen North and Carron also border Litchfield.
