Barnawartha North in the Wodonga area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Barnawartha North is Barnawartha South I16 Bushland Reserve, covering about 12 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Barnawartha North. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Barnawartha North for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Barnawartha North has no train station; Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Barnawartha North, so most trips start by car. By car from Barnawartha North, Wodonga is about 15 minutes away and Wangaratta about 44 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Barnawartha North is Wodonga Sports & Leisure Centre in West Wodonga, about 14 minutes' drive. For a library, Upper Murray Regional Library - Wodonga in Wodonga is about 15 minutes' drive from Barnawartha North. For a beach, Finns Beach in Bandiana is about 18 minutes' drive from Barnawartha North. The closest skate park to Barnawartha North is the bmx track on Silva Drive in West Wodonga, about 14 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana is about 17 minutes' drive from Barnawartha North. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 15 minutes from Barnawartha North by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Barnawartha North, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Valentina Gillard Park in West Wodonga (11 min), the playground at Bill Black Park in West Wodonga (13 min), the bmx track on Silva Drive in West Wodonga (14 min), Wodonga Sports & Leisure Centre in West Wodonga (14 min), Art Space Wodonga in Wodonga (15 min) and Upper Murray Regional Library - Wodonga in Wodonga (15 min).
