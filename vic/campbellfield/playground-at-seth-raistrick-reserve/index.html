Playground at Seth Raistrick Reserve

Playground in Campbellfield · Basic info

Playground at Seth Raistrick Reserve is a public playground in Campbellfield, in the Hume City area. For food, McDonald's is about 450 m away, and Playground at William Canning Reserve is another option about 280 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 240 m from the playground, and bus 531 stops at Reserve Ave/Campbell St, a 1-minute walk away.
