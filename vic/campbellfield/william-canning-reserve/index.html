William Canning Reserve

Park in Campbellfield · Basic info

William Canning Reserve is a park in Campbellfield, in the Hume City Council area, good for all ages. Fordson Rd/Sydney Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 909 m away.
