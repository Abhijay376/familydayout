Playground at Roebourne Crescent Reserve

Playground in Campbellfield · Basic info

Playground at Roebourne Crescent Reserve in Campbellfield is a public playground in the Hume City area. Campbellfield Kebab House is about 140 m away if you need food, and Playground at Eva Court Reserve is another option about 590 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 400 m away, and bus 530 stops at Campbellfield Plaza/Sydney Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
