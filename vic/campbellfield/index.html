Campbellfield is in the Hume area, and our data lists 6 playgrounds and 9 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Campbellfield has a skate park.

Landmarks

bababi marning (Cooper St Grassland Nature Conservation Reserve) is the largest mapped park in Campbellfield, at about 53 hectares. National Boulevard Reserve and Seth Raistrick Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Campbellfield include Foden Reserve, Somerton Park and Allan Barry Reserve. Playgrounds in Campbellfield include the playground at Allan Barry Reserve, the playground at Hughes Court Reserve and the playground at Seth Raistrick Reserve. Our data shows 6 public toilets in Campbellfield. For older kids in Campbellfield there's PARK4MX Broadmeadows. Campbellfield Plaza is the local shopping centre. For walks and rides, National Boulevard Crit Circuit crosses Campbellfield. Our data lists 22 cafes and family restaurants in Campbellfield for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Campbellfield has no train station; Upfield Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 1.2 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 530, 531, 532, 538, 901 and 902 stop in Campbellfield. V/Line coaches stop in Campbellfield. By car from Campbellfield, Broadmeadows is about 7 minutes away, Sunshine about 19 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 26 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Campbellfield is Fawkner Leisure Centre in Fawkner, about 6 minutes' drive. For a library, Fawkner Library in Fawkner is about 7 minutes' drive from Campbellfield. For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 26 minutes' drive from Campbellfield. The closest cinema to Campbellfield is Hoyts in Broadmeadows, about 8 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, MOPA in Thomastown is about 9 minutes' drive from Campbellfield. For a zoo or wildlife park, Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville is about 22 minutes' drive from Campbellfield.

Best for

Younger kids at Campbellfield's 6 local playgrounds. Teens who skate or ride, with PARK4MX Broadmeadows in Campbellfield. Free days on the National Boulevard Crit Circuit and in Campbellfield's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Campbellfield, Reservoir has 38 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Campbellfield, Epping has 43 playgrounds, a cinema and a skate park. Next door to Campbellfield, Thomastown has 18 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Fawkner, next to Campbellfield, has 12 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Broadmeadows, Roxburgh Park, Dallas and Coolaroo also border Campbellfield.
