Wulgulmerang is in the East Gippsland area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Wulgulmerang is Wulgulmerang Creek Bushland Reserve, covering about 40 hectares. Goodwin Creek Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Wulgulmerang.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wulgulmerang. The closest to Wulgulmerang is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 107 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wulgulmerang, so most trips start by car. By car from Wulgulmerang, Lakes Entrance is about 2 hours away and Bairnsdale about 2.5 hours, without traffic.
