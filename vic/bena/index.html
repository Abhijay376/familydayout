Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Bena. The Great Southern Rail Trail passes through Bena.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bena. The closest to Bena is Drouin Railway Station (V/Line), about 31 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bena, so most trips start by car. By car from Bena, Leongatha is about 20 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 89 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Korumburra Outdoor Pool in Korumburra is about 7 minutes' drive from Bena. The closest library to Bena is Poowong library in Poowong, about 13 minutes' drive. For a beach, The Caves in Inverloch is about 38 minutes' drive from Bena. The closest skate park to Bena is Nyora skate park in Nyora, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Bena is Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha, about 19 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 42 minutes' drive from Bena. For a zoo or wildlife park, Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville is about 34 minutes' drive from Bena. The nearest larger centre is Leongatha, about 20 minutes from Bena by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Bena, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Coleman Park in Korumburra (7 min), Korumburra Outdoor Pool in Korumburra (7 min), the playground at Peterson Street Reserve in Korumburra (8 min), Coal Creek Community Park & Museum in Korumburra (10 min), Nyora skate park in Nyora (15 min) and Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong (13 min).

Best for

Free days on the Great Southern Rail Trail and in Bena's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Bena, Korumburra has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Bena, Loch has 1 playground. Kongwak, Jumbunna and Jeetho also border Bena.
