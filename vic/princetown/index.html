Princetown is in the Corangamite area, and our data lists 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in the suburb. Princetown also has 5 beaches.

Landmarks

Twelve Apostles Marine National Park is the largest mapped park in Princetown, at about 7,512 hectares. Princetown Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the next largest. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Princetown. In Princetown you'll also find Brown Hill and Clifton Beach. The Great Ocean Walk passes through Princetown.

Getting around

There's no train station in Princetown. The closest to Princetown is Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line), about 52 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Princetown. By car from Princetown, Warrnambool is about 69 minutes away and Colac about 82 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Princetown is Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Princetown is Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell, about 17 minutes' drive. From Princetown, the nearest places for kids by road are Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell (17 min), the playground at Peterborough Coastal Reserve (COM) in Peterborough (27 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (31 min) and the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (31 min).

Best for

Beach days at Brown Hill and Clifton Beach. Free days on the Great Ocean Walk and in Princetown's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Princetown, Port Campbell has 1 playground, a beach and a skate park. Next door to Princetown, Gellibrand Lower has a beach. Chapple Vale also borders Princetown.
