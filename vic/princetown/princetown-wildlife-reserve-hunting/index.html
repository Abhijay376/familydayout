Princetown Wildlife Reserve (hunting)

Park in Princetown · Basic info

Princetown Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is a park in Princetown, in the Corangamite Shire Council area, good for all ages. Old Ocean Rd/Great Ocean Rd (coach) is a 19-minute walk away.
