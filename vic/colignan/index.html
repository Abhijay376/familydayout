Colignan in the Mildura area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Kemendok Nature Reserve is the largest mapped park in Colignan, at about 1,065 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Colignan. The closest to Colignan is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 142 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Colignan, so most trips start by car. By car from Colignan, Mildura is about 45 minutes away and Swan Hill about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Colignan is Irymple Swimming Pool in Irymple, about 37 minutes' drive. For a library, A S Kenyon Library Red Cliffs in Red Cliffs is about 29 minutes' drive from Colignan. The closest beach to Colignan is Rudd's Road Sandbar in Iraak, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Colignan is Skatepark in Red Cliffs, about 30 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Mildura, about 45 minutes from Colignan by car, with 26 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Colignan are Rudd's Road Sandbar in Iraak (17 min), Ovens Park Playground in Red Cliffs (32 min), the playground at Barclay Square in Red Cliffs (29 min), Red Cliffs Military Museum in Red Cliffs (29 min), A S Kenyon Library Red Cliffs in Red Cliffs (29 min) and Work Space Gallery 3496 in Red Cliffs (29 min).
