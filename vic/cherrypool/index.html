Cherrypool in the Southern Grampians area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Cherrypool State Forest is the largest mapped park in Cherrypool, at about 8,381 hectares. Our data shows 1 off-leash dog park in Cherrypool.

Getting around

Cherrypool has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 69 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cherrypool, so most trips start by car. By car from Cherrypool, Horsham is about 40 minutes away and Hamilton about 63 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cherrypool is Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham, about 38 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Velodrome in Horsham is about 38 minutes' drive from Cherrypool. The closest cinema to Cherrypool is Centre Cinemas in Horsham, about 39 minutes' drive. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to Cherrypool, about 40 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Cherrypool, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Uebergang Park in Horsham (38 min), Velodrome in Horsham (38 min), Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham (38 min), Horsham Water Play in Horsham (39 min), Centre Cinemas in Horsham (39 min) and Horsham Skate Park in Horsham (40 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Cherrypool.

Nearby suburbs

Telangatuk East, next to Cherrypool, has 1 playground. Brimpaen also borders Cherrypool.
