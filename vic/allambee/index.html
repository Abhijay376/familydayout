Allambee is in the Baw Baw area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Allambee State Forest is the largest mapped park in Allambee, at about 99 hectares. Allambee East G91 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Allambee has no train station; Yarragon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 7.4 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Allambee, so most trips start by car. By car from Allambee, Warragul is about 27 minutes away, Leongatha about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Allambee is Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar, about 21 minutes' drive. For a library, Warragul Library in Warragul is about 27 minutes' drive from Allambee. The closest skate park to Allambee is Warragul Skate Park in Warragul, about 25 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 26 minutes' drive from Allambee. The closest indoor play centre to Allambee is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 26 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Warragul, about 27 minutes from Allambee by car, with 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Allambee are Yarragon Childrens Playground Reserve in Yarragon (15 min), Station Gallery in Yarragon (15 min), Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar (21 min), the playground at Brindlewood Reserve in Trafalgar (23 min), Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul (25 min) and Warragul Skate Park in Warragul (25 min).
