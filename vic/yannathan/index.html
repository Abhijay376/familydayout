Yannathan is in the Cardinia area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Yannathan is Yannathan I97 Bushland Reserve, covering about 10 hectares.

Getting around

Yannathan has no train station; Garfield Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yannathan, so most trips start by car. By car from Yannathan, Pakenham is about 24 minutes away, Cranbourne about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 68 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Koo Wee Rup Swimming Pool in Koo Wee Rup is about 15 minutes' drive from Yannathan. The closest library to Yannathan is Poowong library in Poowong, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Yannathan is Corinella Beach in Corinella, about 37 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Cochrane Park in Koo Wee Rup is about 15 minutes' drive from Yannathan. The closest cinema to Yannathan is Warragul Cinema in Warragul, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Yannathan is Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham, about 23 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, MonkeyG's in Pakenham is about 22 minutes' drive from Yannathan. The nearest larger centre is Pakenham, about 24 minutes from Yannathan by car, with 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play. From Yannathan, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at James Street Reserve in Lang Lang (10 min), the playground at Dick Jones Park in Lang Lang (9 min), the skate park at Cochrane Park in Koo Wee Rup (15 min), Koo Wee Rup Swimming Pool in Koo Wee Rup (15 min), Nyora skate park in Nyora (20 min) and Garfield Swimming Pool in Garfield (24 min).
