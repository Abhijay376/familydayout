Mockinya is in the Horsham area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Mockinya is Donald (Dollin) Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 64 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mockinya. The closest to Mockinya is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 78 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mockinya, so most trips start by car. By car from Mockinya, Horsham is about 25 minutes away and Stawell about 66 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham is about 24 minutes' drive from Mockinya. For a skate park, Velodrome in Horsham is about 23 minutes' drive from Mockinya. For a cinema, Centre Cinemas in Horsham is about 25 minutes' drive from Mockinya. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to Mockinya, about 25 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Mockinya, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Uebergang Park in Horsham (23 min), Velodrome in Horsham (23 min), Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham (24 min), Horsham Water Play in Horsham (25 min), Centre Cinemas in Horsham (25 min) and Horsham Skate Park in Horsham (25 min).
