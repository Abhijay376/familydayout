Our data lists 16 playgrounds and 15 parks and reserves in Cranbourne East, in the Casey area. Cranbourne East also has a library, a pool and 4 skate parks.

Landmarks

Casey Fields is the largest mapped park in Cranbourne East, at about 86 hectares. Heathland Park and Mayfield Wetlands are the next largest. Cranbourne East also has Atrus Park, Cascades Park and Dinosaur Park. Playgrounds around Cranbourne East include the playground at Cascades Park, the playground at Dinosaur Park and the playground at Livingstone Park. Our data shows 8 public toilets in Cranbourne East. In Cranbourne East you'll also find Casey RACE and Cranbourne Library. For older kids in Cranbourne East there's Casey BMX Club and Criterium Cycling Track. Hunt Club Village is the local shopping centre. For walks and rides, Bike: Thompsons Road and Casey Fields Fitness Trail cross Cranbourne East. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Cranbourne East for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Cranbourne East has no train station; Cranbourne Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 1.9 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 795, 796, 798, 799, 881, 897, 898 and 981 stop in Cranbourne East. By car from Cranbourne East, Cranbourne is about 4 minutes away, Dandenong about 18 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 44 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest beach to Cranbourne East is Seaford Beach in Seaford, about 20 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Narre Warren is about 13 minutes' drive from Cranbourne East. The closest indoor play centre to Cranbourne East is Oz Tenpin in Narre Warren, about 12 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale is about 15 minutes' drive from Cranbourne East.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 16 playgrounds across Cranbourne East. Rainy days at the library in Cranbourne East. Teens who skate or ride, with Casey BMX Club in Cranbourne East. Free days on the Bike: Thompsons Road and in Cranbourne East's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Clyde North, next to Cranbourne East, has 34 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Cranbourne East, Cranbourne has 27 playgrounds and a skate park. Cranbourne North, next to Cranbourne East, has 12 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Cranbourne East, Clyde has 11 playgrounds and a skate park. Junction Village and Devon Meadows also border Cranbourne East.
