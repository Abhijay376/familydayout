Playground on Chapelton Road

Playground in Cranbourne East · Basic info

Playground on Chapelton Road is a public playground in Cranbourne East, in the Casey City area. Playground on Eliburn Drive is another option about 150 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 320 m from the playground, and buses 796, 897 and 981 stop at Casey Complex/Berwick-Cranbourne Rd, a 10-minute walk away.
