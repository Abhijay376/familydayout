Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Gladfield in the Loddon area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Gladfield has no train station; Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gladfield, so most trips start by car. By car from Gladfield, Bendigo is about 75 minutes away and Swan Hill about 75 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Gladfield is Boort Swimming Pool in Boort, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest library to Gladfield is Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang, about 28 minutes' drive. From Gladfield, the nearest places for kids by road are Pyramid Hill & Districts Historical Museum in Pyramid Hill (16 min), A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park in Boort (25 min), Boort Court House Historical Museum in Boort (25 min), Boort Swimming Pool in Boort (26 min), Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (28 min) and Swimming Pool in Kerang (28 min).
