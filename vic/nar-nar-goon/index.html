Getting around

Nar Nar Goon has a train station: Nar Nar Goon Railway Station (V/Line: Bairnsdale - Melbourne via Sale & Traralgon and Traralgon - Melbourne via Morwell, Moe & Pakenham). Buses on the Garfield Station - Traralgon Plaza route stop in Nar Nar Goon. V/Line coaches stop in Nar Nar Goon. By car from Nar Nar Goon, Pakenham is about 10 minutes away, Cranbourne about 30 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 55 minutes, without traffic.
