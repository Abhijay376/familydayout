Annuello is in the Swan Hill area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Moss Tank Flora and Fauna Reserve is the largest mapped park in Annuello, at about 326 hectares. Annuello Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Annuello has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 86 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Annuello. By car from Annuello, Swan Hill is about 85 minutes away and Mildura about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang is about 20 minutes' drive from Annuello. The closest skate park to Annuello is Robinvale Skatepark in Robinvale, about 27 minutes' drive. From Annuello, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at River Murray Reserve in Wemen (19 min), Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang (20 min), the playground at Rotary Park in Robinvale (26 min), Rural Life Museum in Robinvale (26 min) and Robinvale Skatepark in Robinvale (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Annuello.

Nearby suburbs

Wemen, next to Annuello, has 1 playground. Kulwin, Kooloonong, Bolton and Bannerton also border Annuello.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Annuello, without traffic.
