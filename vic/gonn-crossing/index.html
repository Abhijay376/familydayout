Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Gonn Crossing in the Gannawarra area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Gonn Crossing. The closest to Gonn Crossing is Kerang Railway Station (V/Line), about 25 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gonn Crossing, so most trips start by car. By car from Gonn Crossing, Swan Hill is about 45 minutes away and Echuca about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Gonn Crossing is Swimming Pool in Kerang, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest library to Gonn Crossing is Barham Branch Library in Koondrook, about 33 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Gonn Crossing is Barham Skate Park in Koondrook, about 34 minutes' drive. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Gonn Crossing, about 45 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Gonn Crossing, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Riverside Park in Koondrook (33 min), Barham Branch Library in Koondrook (33 min), Barham Skate Park in Koondrook (34 min), the playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV) in Koondrook (35 min), Swimming Pool in Kerang (25 min) and Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (25 min).
