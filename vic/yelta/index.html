Yelta is in the Mildura area, and our data lists 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Yelta has a library and a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Yelta is Tapio Park, covering about 1 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Yelta include Lions Sunset Park. Playgrounds around Yelta include the playground at Tapio Park. In Yelta you'll also find Military Museum and Dareton Library.

Getting around

There's no train station in Yelta. The closest to Yelta is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 197 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yelta, so most trips start by car. By car from Yelta, Mildura is about 22 minutes away and Swan Hill about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Yelta is Merbein Pool in Merbein, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Yelta is Apex Beach in Mildura, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Yelta is Merbein Skate Park in Merbein, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Yelta is Deakin Cinema Complex in Mildura, about 22 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Laserpunk Laserforce in Mildura is about 22 minutes' drive from Yelta. For a zoo or wildlife park, Putt Putt Family Fun Centre in Mildura is about 22 minutes' drive from Yelta. The nearest larger centre is Mildura, about 22 minutes from Yelta by car, with 26 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Yelta are O'Donell Park Playground in Wargan (3 min), Wentworth Shire Library in Wargan (9 min), Riverboat Rod's Model Paddlesteamer Display in Wargan (9 min), Merbein Pool in Merbein (9 min), Merbein Skate Park in Merbein (13 min) and BMX Track in Mildura (18 min).
