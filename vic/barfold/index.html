Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Barfold in the Mount Alexander area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Barfold. The closest to Barfold is Malmsbury Railway Station (V/Line), about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Barfold, so most trips start by car. By car from Barfold, Kyneton is about 25 minutes away, Castlemaine about 51 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Barfold is Kyneton Library in Kyneton, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Barfold is Canoeing in Tylden, about 36 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton is about 26 minutes' drive from Barfold. Kyneton is the closest bigger centre to Barfold, about 25 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds and a library. From Barfold, the nearest places for kids by road are Metcalfe Recreation Reserve Playground in Metcalfe (27 min), Jean Haynes Playground in Kyneton (25 min), Kyneton Museum in Kyneton (25 min), Kyneton Library in Kyneton (25 min), Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton (26 min) and Canoeing in Tylden (36 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Barfold.

Nearby suburbs

Metcalfe, next to Barfold, has 1 playground. Langley, Glenhope and Baynton also border Barfold.
