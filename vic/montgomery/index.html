Montgomery is in the Wellington area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Montgomery, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Montgomery. The closest to Montgomery is Sale Railway Station (V/Line), about 7.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Montgomery, so most trips start by car. By car from Montgomery, Sale is about 8 minutes away, Bairnsdale about 48 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Montgomery is Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest library to Montgomery is Sale Library in Sale, about 9 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Skate Park in Stratford is about 8 minutes' drive from Montgomery. The closest cinema to Montgomery is Former Drive-in site in Sale, about 6 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Sale, about 8 minutes from Montgomery by car, with 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Montgomery, the nearest places for kids by road are Former Drive-in site in Sale (6 min), Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford (7 min), BMX Track in Stratford (7 min), Apex Park Playground in Stratford (8 min), Skate Park in Stratford (8 min) and Sale Historical Museum in Sale (9 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Montgomery.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Montgomery, Stratford has 8 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Airly also borders Montgomery.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Montgomery, without traffic.
