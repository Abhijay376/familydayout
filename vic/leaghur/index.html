Leaghur in the Loddon area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Leaghur is Lake Leaghur Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 83 hectares. Leaghur Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Leaghur. The closest to Leaghur is Kerang Railway Station (V/Line), about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Leaghur, so most trips start by car. By car from Leaghur, Swan Hill is about 70 minutes away and Bendigo about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Leaghur is Boort Swimming Pool in Boort, about 15 minutes' drive. For a library, Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang is about 25 minutes' drive from Leaghur. From Leaghur, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Lake Meran W.R in Lake Meran (10 min), A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park in Boort (14 min), Boort Court House Historical Museum in Boort (15 min), Boort Swimming Pool in Boort (15 min), Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (25 min) and Swimming Pool in Kerang (25 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Leaghur.

Nearby suburbs

Lake Meran, next to Leaghur, has 1 playground. Canary Island, Barraport and Appin South also border Leaghur.
