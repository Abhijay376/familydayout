Apsley in the West Wimmera area has 1 playground and 12 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Apsley is Bring Albert State Forest, covering about 343 hectares. Apsley Bushland Reserve and Leah Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting) are the next largest. Apsley also has War Memorial Park, Jackie White's Waterhole and Apsley Recreation Reserve. Playgrounds in Apsley include the playground at War Memorial Park. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Apsley.

Getting around

There's no train station in Apsley. The closest to Apsley is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 168 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Horsham - Naracoorte via Natimuk, Goroke, Edenhope route stop in Apsley. By car from Apsley, Horsham is about 1.5 hours away and Hamilton about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Apsley, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Lions Park in Edenhope (17 min), Dachshund Museum & Gallery in Edenhope (17 min) and West Wimmera Community Association - Red Tail Gallery in Edenhope (17 min).
