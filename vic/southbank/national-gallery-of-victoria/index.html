National Gallery of Victoria

Museum or gallery in Southbank · Official details

National Gallery of Victoria is a museum or gallery in Southbank, in the Melbourne City Council area, best for school-age kids. Arts Precinct/St Kilda Rd #14 (tram) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 20 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: opening_hours: Mo-Su 10:00-17:00.
