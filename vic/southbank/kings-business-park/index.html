Kings Business Park

Park in Southbank · Official details

Kings Business Park is a park in Southbank, in the Melbourne City Council area, good for all ages. Sturt St/Kings Way #118 (tram) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 226 m away.
