Boyd Community Park

Park in Southbank · Official details

Boyd Community Park is a park in Southbank, in the Melbourne City Council area, good for all ages. City Rd/Kings Way #116 (tram) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 140 m away.

Where is Boyd Community Park?
Boyd Community Park is in Southbank, Victoria, at 207 City Road, Southbank, 3006.
