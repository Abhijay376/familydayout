Rocks on Wheels

Playground in Southbank · Official details

Rocks on Wheels is a public playground in Southbank, in the Melbourne City area. The Southbank to Capital City Trail passes about 20 m away, and NGV Library is about 310 m away. There's a public toilet about 360 m away, and bus 605 stops at Kavanagh St/Southbank Bvd, a 1-minute walk away.
