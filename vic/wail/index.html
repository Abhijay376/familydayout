Wail in the Horsham area has 8 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Wail State Forest is the largest mapped park in Wail, at about 3,462 hectares. Wail Nursery is the next largest. Wail also has Big Bend, Wail 2 Campsite and Wail Site 1 Campsite. Picnic areas in Wail include Big Bend, Wail 2 Campsite and Wail Site 1 Campsite. The Scenic Loop, Wail Scenic Loop and Wail Trail all pass through Wail.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wail. The closest to Wail is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 114 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wail, so most trips start by car. By car from Wail, Horsham is about 22 minutes away and Stawell about 76 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Dimboola Swimming Pool in Dimboola is about 9 minutes' drive from Wail. The closest library to Wail is Dimboola Library in Dimboola, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Wail is Horsham Skate Park in Horsham, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Wail is Centre Cinemas in Horsham, about 22 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Horsham, about 22 minutes from Wail by car, with 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Wail are Dimboola Swimming Pool in Dimboola (9 min), Dimboola Library in Dimboola (9 min), the playground at Elbourne Park in Horsham (20 min), Playland in Horsham (21 min), Horsham Skate Park in Horsham (22 min) and Centre Cinemas in Horsham (22 min).

Best for

Free days on the Scenic Loop and in Wail's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Wail, Dimboola has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Kalkee and Duchembegarra also border Wail.
