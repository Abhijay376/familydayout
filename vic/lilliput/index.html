Lilliput is in the Indigo area, and our data lists 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lilliput is Chiltern Valley Extended Mine Historic and Cultural Features Reserve, covering about 15 hectares. Rocky Waterholes Creek Streamside Reserve and Black Dog Creek, Lilliput Streamside Reserve are the next largest. Lilliput also has Chiltern West I13 Bushland Reserve. For walks and rides, Bowser-Wagunya Preferred Route crosses Lilliput.

Getting around

Lilliput has no train station; Springhurst Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 8.4 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lilliput, so most trips start by car. By car from Lilliput, Wangaratta is about 31 minutes away and Wodonga about 41 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rutherglen War Memorial Swimming Pool in Rutherglen is about 8 minutes' drive from Lilliput. The closest library to Lilliput is Rutherglen Public Library in Rutherglen, about 8 minutes' drive. For a beach, Sandy Beach in Beechworth is about 38 minutes' drive from Lilliput. The closest skate park to Lilliput is the pump track on Victoria Street in Wahgunyah, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Lilliput is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 28 minutes' drive. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Lilliput, about 31 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Lilliput, the nearest places for kids by road are Rutherglen Public Library in Rutherglen (8 min), Apex Park Playground in Rutherglen (8 min), Rutherglen War Memorial Swimming Pool in Rutherglen (8 min), the pump track on Victoria Street in Wahgunyah (15 min), Corowa Library in Wahgunyah (17 min) and Bangerang Park in Wahgunyah (17 min).

Best for

Free days on the Bowser-Wagunya Preferred Route and in Lilliput's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Rutherglen, next to Lilliput, has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Next door to Lilliput, Chiltern has 2 playgrounds. Springhurst, next to Lilliput, has 1 playground. Chiltern Valley and Boorhaman East also border Lilliput.
