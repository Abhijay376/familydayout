Koala Bend Picnic area

Park in Smythesdale · Basic info

Koala Bend Picnic area is a park in Smythesdale, in the Golden Plains Shire Council area, good for all ages. Loader St/Glenelg Hwy (bus) is a 14-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 350 m away.
