Woady Yaloak Recreation Reserve Playground

Playground in Smythesdale · Basic info

Woady Yaloak Recreation Reserve Playground in Smythesdale is a public playground in the Golden Plains Shire area. The Ballarat-Skipton Rail Trail is about 10 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 10 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 380 m from the playground, and bus C27 stops at Loader St/Glenelg Hwy, a 6-minute walk away.
