Berrimal is in the Loddon area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Berrimal, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Berrimal. The closest to Berrimal is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 66 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Berrimal, so most trips start by car. By car from Berrimal, Bendigo is about 81 minutes away and Maryborough about 83 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn is about 20 minutes' drive from Berrimal. The closest places for kids by road from Berrimal are Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (20 min), Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (20 min), Bi-Centennial Park Playground in St Arnaud (27 min), the playground at Queen Mary Botanic Gardens in St Arnaud (28 min) and St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (28 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Berrimal.

Nearby suburbs

Wedderburn, next to Berrimal, has 1 playground and a pool. Gowar East and Fentons Creek also border Berrimal.
