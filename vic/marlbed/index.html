Marlbed in the Buloke area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Towma (Lake Marlbed) Flora and Fauna Reserve is the largest mapped park in Marlbed, at about 235 hectares. Towma Bushland Reserve and Marlbed I191 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Marlbed include Round Swamp Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Marlbed. The closest to Marlbed is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 84 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Marlbed, so most trips start by car. By car from Marlbed, Swan Hill is about 82 minutes away and Horsham about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Marlbed is Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Marlbed is Birchip Skate Park in Birchip, about 18 minutes' drive. From Marlbed, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Birchip Shire Soldiers Memorial Park in Birchip (18 min), Birchip Skate Park in Birchip (18 min), Birchip History Museum in Birchip (18 min), the playground at J.E Taylor Sunset Park in Birchip (19 min), Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock (30 min) and Woomelang Recreation Reserve Swimming Pool in Woomelang (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Marlbed.

Nearby suburbs

Kinnabulla, Karyrie, Jil Jil and Curyo also border Marlbed.
