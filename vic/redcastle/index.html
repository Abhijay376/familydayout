Redcastle in the Campaspe area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Redcastle is Redcastle Bushland Reserve, covering about 6 hectares.

Getting around

Redcastle has no train station; Goornong Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 31 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Redcastle, so most trips start by car. By car from Redcastle, Bendigo is about 57 minutes away, Seymour about 62 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Heathcote Library in Heathcote is about 22 minutes' drive from Redcastle. The closest skate park to Redcastle is Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote, about 22 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 57 minutes from Redcastle by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Redcastle are Heathcote Play Space in Heathcote (22 min), Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote (22 min), Heathcote Library in Heathcote (22 min), Mudwood Studio in Heathcote (23 min), Powder Magazine in Heathcote (24 min) and Tooborac Recreation Reserve Playground in Tooborac (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Redcastle.
