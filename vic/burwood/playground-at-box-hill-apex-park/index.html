Playground at Box Hill Apex Park

Playground in Burwood · Basic info

Playground at Box Hill Apex Park is a public playground in Burwood, in the Whitehorse City area. The Whitehorse EW6 is about 110 m away, and for food, Fusion Café is about 440 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 700 m from the playground, and buses 767 and 903 stop at Cropley Ct/Elgar Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
