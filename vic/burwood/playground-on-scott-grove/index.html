Playground on Scott Grove

Playground in Burwood · Basic info

This is a public playground in Burwood. For food, McDonald's is about 130 m away, and Playground at Burwood Reserve is another option about 390 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 160 m from the playground, and bus 903 stops at Scott Gr/Burwood Hwy , a 2-minute walk away.
