Playground at Gardiners Reserve

Playground in Burwood · Basic info

Playground at Gardiners Reserve is a public playground in Burwood, in the Monash City area. The Gardiners Creek Trail passes about 40 m away, and Playground on Ashwood Drive is another option about 110 m away. There's a public toilet about 950 m away, and bus 903 stops at Leopold St/Warrigal Rd, a 10-minute walk away.
