Playground at Fulton-Worrall Reserve

Playground in Burwood · Basic info

This is a public playground in Burwood. The Whitehorse EW6 is about 300 m away, and for food, Gotcha Fresh Tea is about 230 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 490 m from the playground, and bus 733 stops at Foundation Bvd/Middleborough Rd , a 2-minute walk away.
