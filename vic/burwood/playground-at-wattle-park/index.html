Playground at Wattle Park

Playground in Burwood · Basic info

Playground at Wattle Park in Burwood is a public playground in the Whitehorse City area. The Wattle Park Walk passes about 70 m away, and Playground on Boisdale Street is another option about 470 m away. There's a public toilet about 90 m away, and tram 70 stops at Alandale St/Riversdale Rd #59, a 4-minute walk away.
