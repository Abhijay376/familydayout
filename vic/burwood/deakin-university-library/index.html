Deakin University Library

Library in Burwood · Basic info

Deakin University Library is a library in Burwood, in the Whitehorse City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Deakin University/Holland Ave (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 53 m away.
