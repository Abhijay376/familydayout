Gardiners Reserve

Park in Burwood · Basic info

Gardiners Reserve is a park in Burwood, in the Monash City Council area, good for all ages. Evans St/Highbury Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 202 m away.
