Playground on Waratah Avenue

Playground in Burwood · Basic info

Playground on Waratah Avenue in Burwood is a public playground in the Whitehorse City area. The Whitehorse EW6 is about 60 m away, and for food, Foodrinkery is about 50 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 910 m from the playground, and bus 766 stops at Iris St/Warrigal Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
