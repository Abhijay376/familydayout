Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Creek View in the Campaspe area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Creek View. The closest to Creek View is Elmore Railway Station (V/Line), about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Creek View. By car from Creek View, Bendigo is about 53 minutes away, Echuca about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Goornong Swimming Pool in Goornong is about 25 minutes' drive from Creek View. The closest library to Creek View is Rushworth Library in Rushworth, about 33 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Lions Park in Rushworth is about 33 minutes' drive from Creek View. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Creek View, about 53 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Creek View, the nearest places for kids by road are Campaspe Run Rural Discovery Centre in Elmore (20 min), Goornong Swimming Pool in Goornong (25 min), Rushworth Museum in Rushworth (32 min), Rushworth Library in Rushworth (33 min), Rushworth Pool in Rushworth (33 min) and the skate park at Lions Park in Rushworth (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Creek View.

Nearby suburbs

Colbinabbin, next to Creek View, has 1 playground. Cornella also borders Creek View.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Creek View, without traffic.
