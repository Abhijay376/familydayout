Chandlers Creek is in the East Gippsland area, and our data lists 8 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Chandlers Creek is Coopracambra National Park, covering about 38,490 hectares. Genoa Wilderness Zone and Mount Kaye Remote and Natural Area are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Chandlers Creek include Yambulla Reference Area, Genoa River Heritage River and Jones Creek Reference Area.

Getting around

There's no train station in Chandlers Creek. The closest to Chandlers Creek is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 160 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Chandlers Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Chandlers Creek, Lakes Entrance is about 2.5 hours away and Bairnsdale about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest skate park to Chandlers Creek is the skate park at Mick Baum Park in Cann River, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Chandlers Creek are the skate park at Mick Baum Park in Cann River (32 min) and the playground at Mick Baum Park in Cann River (32 min).
