Baringhup is in the Mount Alexander area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Baringhup, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 2 public toilets in Baringhup. For walks and rides, Exploration Reef Trail crosses Baringhup.

Getting around

Baringhup has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Maryborough - Bendigo route stop in Baringhup. By car from Baringhup, Castlemaine is about 33 minutes away, Maryborough about 33 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Baringhup is Maldon Swimming Pool in Maldon, about 14 minutes' drive. For a library, Athenaeum Library in Maldon is about 14 minutes' drive from Baringhup. For a skate park, Bike Jumps in Castlemaine is about 33 minutes' drive from Baringhup. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 45 minutes' drive from Baringhup. Castlemaine is the closest bigger centre to Baringhup, about 33 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Baringhup are Athenaeum Library in Maldon (14 min), Maldon Swimming Pool in Maldon (14 min), the playground at Maldon Shire Gardens in Maldon (14 min), Maldon District Museum in Maldon (14 min), Maldon Vintage Machinery Museum in Maldon (15 min) and the playground at Market Reserve in Carisbrook (27 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Baringhup on the Exploration Reef Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Carisbrook, next to Baringhup, has 1 playground. Moolort, Joyces Creek, Bradford and Baringhup West also border Baringhup.
