Tarra-Bulga National Park

Park in Balook · Official details

Tarra-Bulga National Park is a park in Balook, in the Wellington Shire Council area, good for all ages.

Details

Toilets
yes (Parks Victoria)

partly (Parks Victoria)

Food
BYO allowed (Parks Victoria)
Seasons
Best time to see lyrebirds is winter; cool even in summer heat (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
Visitor Centre picnic area has electric barbecues; No bins: take rubbish home; Winter can be very cold and wet (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Tarra-Bulga National Park?
Tarra-Bulga National Park is in Balook, Victoria.

Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
