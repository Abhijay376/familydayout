Coldstream is in the Yarra Ranges area, and our data lists 9 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Coldstream is Margaret Lewis Reserve, covering about 18 hectares. Halley Supple Reserve and Coldstream BMX Park are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Coldstream include Glenhurst Park, Carey Court Reserve and Merton Court Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Coldstream. North Gateway Shopping Plaza and The Lodge Shopping Centre are the main shopping centres. For walks and rides, Yarra Valley Trail crosses Coldstream.

Getting around

Coldstream has no train station; Lilydale Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 4.6 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 684, 685 and 965 stop in Coldstream. By car from Coldstream, Lilydale is about 6 minutes away, Healesville about 18 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 47 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Lilydale Outdoor Pool in Lilydale is about 6 minutes' drive from Coldstream. The closest library to Coldstream is Lilydale Library in Lilydale, about 8 minutes' drive. For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 45 minutes' drive from Coldstream. For a skate park, Lilydale Skate Bowl in Lilydale is about 6 minutes' drive from Coldstream. For a cinema, Reading Cinemas in Chirnside Park is about 11 minutes' drive from Coldstream. For an indoor play centre, GAMR Laser Tag & VR in Chirnside Park is about 11 minutes' drive from Coldstream. For a zoo or wildlife park, Lyre Bird enclosure in Badger Creek is about 18 minutes' drive from Coldstream. Lilydale is the closest bigger centre to Coldstream, about 6 minutes away by car, and has 12 playgrounds, a pool and a library.

Best for

Free days on the Yarra Valley Trail and in Coldstream's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Lilydale, next to Coldstream, has 12 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Coldstream, Chirnside Park has 9 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. Next door to Coldstream, Healesville has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. Christmas Hills, next to Coldstream, has a library. Wandin North, Yarra Glen, Gruyere and Yering also border Coldstream.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Coldstream, without traffic.
