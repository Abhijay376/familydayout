Rossbridge is in the Ararat area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Rossbridge Wildlife Reserve (no hunting) is the largest mapped park in Rossbridge, at about 60 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Rossbridge. The closest to Rossbridge is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Rossbridge, so most trips start by car. By car from Rossbridge, Ararat is about 22 minutes away, Stawell about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Rossbridge is Willaura Outdoor Pool in Willaura, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest library to Rossbridge is Ararat Library in Ararat, about 22 minutes' drive. For a skate park, David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat is about 26 minutes' drive from Rossbridge. The closest cinema to Rossbridge is Astor Cinema in Ararat, about 22 minutes' drive. Ararat is the closest bigger centre to Rossbridge, about 22 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Rossbridge are Willaura Outdoor Pool in Willaura (20 min), Ararat Gallery TAMA in Ararat (22 min), Ararat Library in Ararat (22 min), Astor Cinema in Ararat (22 min), Langi Morgala Museum in Ararat (22 min) and Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat (22 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Rossbridge.

Nearby suburbs

Willaura, next to Rossbridge, has 1 playground and a pool. Next door to Rossbridge, Mininera has 1 playground.
