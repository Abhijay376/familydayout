Ninda in the Buloke area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Ninda is Bourka I156 Bushland Reserve, covering about 32 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Ninda.

Getting around

Ninda has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 74 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Ninda, so most trips start by car. By car from Ninda, Swan Hill is about 67 minutes away and Mildura about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Ninda is Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake, about 10 minutes' drive. From Ninda, the nearest places for kids by road are Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake (10 min), the playground at Apex Park in Sea Lake (10 min), the playground at Lascelles Caravan Park in Lascelles (27 min) and Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock (25 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Ninda.

Nearby suburbs

Sea Lake, next to Ninda, has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Ninda, Lascelles has 1 playground. Myall, Lake Tyrrell and Bimbourie also border Ninda.
