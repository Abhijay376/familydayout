Mount Tassie is in the Latrobe area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Mount Tassie, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mount Tassie. The closest to Mount Tassie is Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line), about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mount Tassie, so most trips start by car. By car from Mount Tassie, Traralgon is about 31 minutes away, Warragul about 72 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon is about 32 minutes' drive from Mount Tassie. The closest library to Mount Tassie is Morwell Library in Morwell, about 38 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon is about 33 minutes' drive from Mount Tassie. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 37 minutes' drive from Mount Tassie. The nearest larger centre is Traralgon, about 31 minutes from Mount Tassie by car, with 38 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Mount Tassie are the playground at Hourigan Park in Traralgon (29 min), the playground at Eric Taylor Reserve in Traralgon (29 min), Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon (32 min), Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon (33 min), Yarram Historical Society Museum in Yarram (51 min) and the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram (52 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mount Tassie.

Nearby suburbs

Callignee, Blackwarry and Balook also border Mount Tassie.
