Carranballac is in the Pyrenees area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Carranballac, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Carranballac has no train station; Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 36 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Carranballac, so most trips start by car. By car from Carranballac, Ararat is about 53 minutes away, Ballarat about 61 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Carranballac is Beaufort Pool in Beaufort, about 37 minutes' drive. The closest library to Carranballac is Lake Bolac Library in Lake Bolac, about 23 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Linton Skate Park in Linton is about 30 minutes' drive from Carranballac. Ararat is the closest bigger centre to Carranballac, about 53 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Carranballac are the playground at Simpson Park in Streatham (7 min), Lake Bolac Library in Lake Bolac (23 min), Linton Skate Park in Linton (30 min), Linton BMX park in Linton (30 min), Linton Recreation Reserve Playground in Linton (31 min) and Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum (43 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Carranballac.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Carranballac, Streatham has 1 playground. Pura Pura, Nerrin Nerrin and Lake Wongan also border Carranballac.
