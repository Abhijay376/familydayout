Mossiface is in the East Gippsland area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Mossiface Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Mossiface, at about 14 hectares. The East Gippsland Rail Trail passes through Mossiface.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mossiface. The closest to Mossiface is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mossiface, so most trips start by car. By car from Mossiface, Bairnsdale is about 23 minutes away and Lakes Entrance about 30 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale is about 23 minutes' drive from Mossiface. For a library, Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale is about 20 minutes' drive from Mossiface. The closest beach to Mossiface is 90 Mile Beach in Boole Poole, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Mossiface is the skate park at Gilsenan Reserve in Paynesville, about 35 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale is about 21 minutes' drive from Mossiface. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow is about 21 minutes' drive from Mossiface. The nearest larger centre is Bairnsdale, about 23 minutes from Mossiface by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Mossiface, the nearest places for kids by road are Bruthen Gallery in Bruthen (5 min), the playground at Howitt Park in Wy Yung (19 min), 90 Mile Beach in Boole Poole (21 min), Howitt Park in Wy Yung (20 min), Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale (20 min) and Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale (21 min).

Best for

Free days on the East Gippsland Rail Trail and in Mossiface's parks.
