Lower Moira in the Moira area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Lower Goulburn National Park is the largest mapped park in Lower Moira, at about 9,322 hectares. Lower Moira Bushland Reserve and Moira H14 Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lower Moira. The closest to Lower Moira is Echuca Railway Station (V/Line), about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lower Moira, so most trips start by car. By car from Lower Moira, Echuca is about 39 minutes away and Shepparton about 68 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Nathalia Memorial Pool in Nathalia is about 30 minutes' drive from Lower Moira. The closest library to Lower Moira is Nathalia Library in Nathalia, about 27 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Moama Echuca Community Skate Park in Echuca is about 41 minutes' drive from Lower Moira. The closest cinema to Lower Moira is Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca, about 39 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 39 minutes from Lower Moira by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Lower Moira are Barmah Forest Heritge and Education Centre in Nathalia (27 min), Nathalia Library in Nathalia (27 min), the playground at Apex Park in Nathalia (28 min), Nathalia Memorial Pool in Nathalia (30 min), Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca (39 min) and McDonald's PlayPlace in Echuca (40 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lower Moira.

Nearby suburbs

Echuca Village and Barmah also border Lower Moira.
