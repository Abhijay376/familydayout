Lance Moon Park

Playground in Yanakie · Basic info

Lance Moon Park is a public playground in Yanakie, in the South Gippsland Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 130 m away. It's easiest by car, about 1 minute from Yanakie.
