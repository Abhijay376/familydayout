Our data lists 2 playgrounds and 2 parks and reserves in Yanakie, in the South Gippsland area.

Landmarks

Corner Inlet Marine and Coastal Park is the largest mapped park in Yanakie, at about 28,567 hectares. Yanakie Flora Reserve is the next largest. Playgrounds in Yanakie include Lance Moon Park and the playground at Corner Inlet Marine and Coastal Park. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Yanakie. For walks and rides, Duck Point Walking Track crosses Yanakie.

Getting around

Yanakie has no train station; Trafalgar Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 67 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yanakie, so most trips start by car. By car from Yanakie, Leongatha is about 44 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 55 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Foster Outdoor Pool in Foster is about 17 minutes' drive from Yanakie. The closest library to Yanakie is Foster Library in Foster, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Yanakie is Cotters Beach in Wilsons Promontory, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Yanakie is the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora, about 29 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Leongatha, about 44 minutes from Yanakie by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Yanakie are Ride the Wild Goat in Fish Creek (14 min), Foster Outdoor Pool in Foster (17 min), Cotters Beach in Wilsons Promontory (22 min), Stockyard Gallery in Foster (17 min), Foster Library in Foster (17 min) and Sandy Point (Waratah Bay) in Sandy Point (24 min).

Best for

Free days on the Duck Point Walking Track and in Yanakie's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Yanakie, Foster has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Sandy Point, next to Yanakie, has 1 playground and a beach. Wilsons Promontory, next to Yanakie, has a beach. Next door to Yanakie, Fish Creek has 1 playground.
