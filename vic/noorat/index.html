Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Noorat. The Terange and Noorat Cycleway passes through Noorat.

Getting around

Noorat has no train station; Terang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 5.3 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Noorat. By car from Noorat, Warrnambool is about 46 minutes away, Colac about 54 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Noorat is Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest library to Noorat is Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake, about 15 minutes' drive. For a beach, Buttress Point in The Cove is about 44 minutes' drive from Noorat. The closest skate park to Noorat is Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake, about 15 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee is about 33 minutes' drive from Noorat. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 46 minutes from Noorat by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Noorat, the nearest places for kids by road are Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake (15 min), the playground at Tea Tree Lake Reserve in Mortlake (16 min), Market Square Playground in Mortlake (15 min), Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake (15 min), Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (20 min) and Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (33 min).

Best for

Free days on the Terange and Noorat Cycleway and in Noorat's parks.
