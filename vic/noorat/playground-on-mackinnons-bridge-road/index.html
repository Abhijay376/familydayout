Playground on Mackinnons Bridge Road

Playground in Noorat · Basic info

Playground on Mackinnons Bridge Road is a public playground in Noorat, in the Corangamite Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.0 km away. It's easiest by car, about 1 minute from Noorat.
