Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Yarrambat, in the Nillumbik area.

Landmarks

Yarrambat Park is the largest mapped park in Yarrambat, at about 32 hectares. Yarrambat War Memorial Park is the next largest. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Yarrambat. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Yarrambat for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Yarrambat. The closest to Yarrambat is Hawkstowe Railway Station (metro), about 3.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 381, 385 and 901 stop in Yarrambat. By car from Yarrambat, Broadmeadows is about 24 minutes away, Box Hill about 31 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 37 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Yarrambat is Diamond Creek Outdoor Pool in Diamond Creek, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest library to Yarrambat is Mill Park Library in Mill Park, about 9 minutes' drive. For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 35 minutes' drive from Yarrambat. The closest skate park to Yarrambat is Diamond Creek Skate Park in Diamond Creek, about 11 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Mill Park is about 10 minutes' drive from Yarrambat. The closest indoor play centre to Yarrambat is Timezone in Mill Park, about 10 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bundoora Park Farm in Bundoora is about 21 minutes' drive from Yarrambat. Broadmeadows is the closest bigger centre to Yarrambat, about 24 minutes away by car, and has 12 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Yarrambat are the playground at Jorgensen Park in Doreen (3 min), the playground at Serle Wetland Park in Doreen (4 min), ArtMasters Visual Art School in Diamond Creek (7 min), Diamond Creek Outdoor Pool in Diamond Creek (9 min), Diamond Creek Skate Park in Diamond Creek (11 min) and Aquastyle Swim School in Mill Park (9 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Yarrambat.

Nearby suburbs

Doreen, next to Yarrambat, has 32 playgrounds. Next door to Yarrambat, Diamond Creek has 23 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. South Morang, next to Yarrambat, has 26 playgrounds. Plenty, next to Yarrambat, has 5 playgrounds. Hurstbridge also borders Yarrambat.
