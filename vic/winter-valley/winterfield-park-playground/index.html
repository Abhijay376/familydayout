Winterfield Park Playground

Playground in Winter Valley · Basic info

Winterfield Park Playground is a public playground in Winter Valley, in the Ballarat City area. The Kensington Creek Trail is about 320 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 130 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 2 minutes from Winter Valley.
