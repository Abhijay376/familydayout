Winterfield Park

Park in Winter Valley · Basic info

Winterfield Park is a park in Winter Valley, in the Ballarat City Council area, good for all ages. Greenhalghs Rd/Wiltshire Lane (bus) is a 15-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 55 m away with a baby change table.
