Giant Slide

Playground in Vaughan · Basic info

Giant Slide is a public playground in Vaughan, in the Mount Alexander Shire area. The Goldfields Track passes about 20 m away, and Playground at Vaughan Springs is another option about 10 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground. It's easiest by car, about 2 minutes from Vaughan.
