Stony Hill Creek Linear Reserve

Park in Hillside · Basic info

Stony Hill Creek Linear Reserve is a park in Hillside, in the Melton City Council area, good for all ages. Sanctuary Rd/Beattys Rd (bus) is a 13-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 722 m away.
