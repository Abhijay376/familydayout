Playground at Hillside Reserve

Playground in Hillside · Basic info

Playground at Hillside Reserve in Hillside is a public playground in the Brimbank City area. Rosà is about 130 m away if you need food, and Playground on Tyson Way is another option about 200 m away. There's a public toilet about 690 m away, and bus 462 stops at Community Hub/Calder Park Dr, a 2-minute walk away.
