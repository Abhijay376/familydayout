Parkview Place Reserve

Park in Hillside · Basic info

Parkview Place Reserve is a park in Hillside, in the Melton City Council area, good for all ages. Melton Hwy/Calder Park Dr (bus) is a 10-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 885 m away.
