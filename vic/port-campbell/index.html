Port Campbell is in the Corangamite area, and our data lists 1 playground and 7 parks and reserves in the suburb. Port Campbell also has 4 beaches and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Port Campbell is Port Campbell National Park, covering about 2,402 hectares. The Arches Marine Sanctuary and Port Campbell Recreation and Camping Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Port Campbell include Port of Port Campbell, Port Campbell PV Office and Port Campbell Coastal Reserve. Our data shows 4 public toilets and 3 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Port Campbell. Places to visit in Port Campbell include Broken Head and Loch Ard Gorge. For older kids in Port Campbell there's Port Campbell Skate Park. For walks and rides, Poorpa Yanyeen Meerreeng - The Timboon to Port Campbell Trail and Port Campbell Discovery Walk cross Port Campbell. Our data lists 3 cafes and family restaurants in Port Campbell for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Port Campbell has no train station; Terang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 43 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Warrnambool - Port Campbell - Timboon via Allansford, Nullawarre, Peterborough route stop in Port Campbell. V/Line coaches stop in Port Campbell. By car from Port Campbell, Warrnambool is about 53 minutes away and Colac about 73 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Port Campbell is Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon, about 15 minutes' drive. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Port Campbell, about 53 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Teens who skate or ride, with Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell. Beach days at Broken Head and Loch Ard Gorge. Free days on the Poorpa Yanyeen Meerreeng - The Timboon to Port Campbell Trail and in Port Campbell's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Princetown, next to Port Campbell, has 1 playground and a beach. Next door to Port Campbell, Peterborough has 1 playground and a beach. Newfield, Heytesbury Lower and Cooriemungle also border Port Campbell.
