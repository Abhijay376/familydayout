Port Campbell Parks Victoria Depot

Park in Port Campbell · Basic info

Port Campbell Parks Victoria Depot is a park in Port Campbell, in the Corangamite Shire Council area, good for all ages. Surf Club/Tregea St (bus) is a 11-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 592 m away.
