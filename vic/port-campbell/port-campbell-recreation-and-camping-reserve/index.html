Port Campbell Recreation and Camping Reserve

Park in Port Campbell · Official details

Port Campbell Recreation and Camping Reserve is a park in Port Campbell, in the Corangamite Shire Council area, good for all ages. Surf Club/Tregea St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 198 m away.

Questions parents ask

Where is Port Campbell Recreation and Camping Reserve?
Port Campbell Recreation and Camping Reserve is in Port Campbell, Victoria.
Is Port Campbell Recreation and Camping Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Port Campbell Recreation and Camping Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 198 m away.
How do I get to Port Campbell Recreation and Camping Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Surf Club/Tregea St (routes Warrnambool - Port Campbell - Timboon via Allansford, Nullawarre, Peterborough), a 3-minute walk.
