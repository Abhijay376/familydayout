Port Campbell National Park

Park in Port Campbell · Basic info

Port Campbell National Park is a park in Port Campbell, in the Corangamite Shire Council area, good for all ages. London Bridge/Great Ocean Rd (coach) is a 40-minute walk away.

Where is Port Campbell National Park?
