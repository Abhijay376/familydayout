Loch Ard Gorge

Beach in Port Campbell · Basic info

Loch Ard Gorge is a beach in Port Campbell, in the Corangamite Shire Council area, good for all ages. Loch Ard Gorge/Great Ocean Rd (coach) is a 6-minute walk away.

Where is Loch Ard Gorge?
Loch Ard Gorge is in Port Campbell, Victoria.
Is Loch Ard Gorge free?
Yes. It is a public beach, free to visit.
How do I get to Loch Ard Gorge by public transport?
The closest stop is Loch Ard Gorge/Great Ocean Rd (routes Warrnambool - Melbourne via Apollo Bay & Geelong), a 6-minute walk.
