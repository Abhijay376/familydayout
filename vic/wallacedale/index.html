Wallacedale is in the Glenelg area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Wallacedale, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wallacedale. The closest to Wallacedale is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 77 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wallacedale, so most trips start by car. By car from Wallacedale, Hamilton is about 33 minutes away and Portland about 51 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Wallacedale is Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood, about 30 minutes' drive. For a library, Heywood Library in Heywood is about 30 minutes' drive from Wallacedale. For a skate park, hamilton skate park in Hamilton is about 34 minutes' drive from Wallacedale. The closest cinema to Wallacedale is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 33 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 33 minutes from Wallacedale by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Wallacedale, the nearest places for kids by road are Branxholme Rec Reserve Playground in Branxholme (13 min), Heywood Library in Heywood (30 min), the playground at Apex Park in Heywood (30 min), Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood (30 min), Hamilton History Centre in Hamilton (32 min) and Mud Gallery in Hamilton (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wallacedale.

Nearby suburbs
