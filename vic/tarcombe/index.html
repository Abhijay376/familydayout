Tarcombe in the Strathbogie area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tarcombe is Hughes Creek Flora Reserve, covering about 139 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tarcombe. The closest to Tarcombe is Avenel Railway Station (V/Line), about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tarcombe, so most trips start by car. By car from Tarcombe, Seymour is about 64 minutes away, Benalla about 89 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Tarcombe, the nearest places for kids by road are BMX Track in Caveat (48 min), Creightons Creek Recreation Reserve Playground in Creightons Creek (50 min), Longwood Recreation Reserve Playground in Longwood (46 min), Seymour Swimming Centre in Seymour (63 min), Seymour Skate Park in Seymour (64 min) and Seymour Library in Seymour (64 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tarcombe.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Tarcombe, Avenel has 1 playground. Longwood East and Highlands also border Tarcombe.
