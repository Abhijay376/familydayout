Playground at Rosehill Park

Playground in Keilor East · Official details

This is a public playground in Keilor East. Older kids have a skate park about 120 m away, and Playground at Judith Reserve is another option about 410 m away. There's a public toilet about 160 m away, and bus 469 stops at Dinah Pde/Rachelle Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
