Parkside Reserve

Park in Keilor East · Basic info

Parkside Reserve is a park in Keilor East, in the Moonee Valley City Council area, good for all ages. Sterling Dr/Norwood Dr (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1121 m away.
