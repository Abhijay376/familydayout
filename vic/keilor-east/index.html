Keilor East in the Moonee Valley area has 26 playgrounds and 24 parks and reserves in our data. Keilor East also has 2 pools and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Keilor East is Brimbank Park, covering about 373 hectares. Maribyrnong Valley Parklands and Maribyrnong Valley Parklands (COM) are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Keilor East include Brees Reserve, Rosehill Park and Judith Reserve. Playgrounds around Keilor East include Kidz City, the playground at Rosehill Park and the playground at Rosehill Park. Our data shows 16 public toilets and 9 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Keilor East. In Keilor East you'll also find Keilor East Leisure Centre and Spa. For older kids in Keilor East there's the skate park at Rosehill Park. Milleara Shopping Centre is the local shopping centre. For walks and rides, M80 Trail, Maribyrnong River Trail and Steele Creek Trail cross Keilor East. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Keilor East for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Keilor East has no train station; Albion Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 5.0 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 406, 407, 465, 469, 476 and 903 stop in Keilor East. By car from Keilor East, Sunshine is about 10 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 13 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 21 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Avondale Heights Library and Learning Centre in Avondale Heights is about 3 minutes' drive from Keilor East. The closest beach to Keilor East is Horse Arena in Abbotsford, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Keilor East is Hoyts in Maribyrnong, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Keilor East is Funtopia in Maidstone, about 7 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 26 playgrounds across Keilor East. Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at Rosehill Park in Keilor East. Free days on the M80 Trail and in Keilor East's parks.
