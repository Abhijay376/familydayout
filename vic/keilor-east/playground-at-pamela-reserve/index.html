Playground at Pamela Reserve

Playground in Keilor East · Basic info

This is a public playground in Keilor East. The Steele Creek Trail passes about 340 m away, and Playground at Brees Road Reserve is another option about 200 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.1 km away, and bus 469 stops at Arete Pl/Valley Lake Bvd, a 6-minute walk away.
