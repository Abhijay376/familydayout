Pennington Reserve

Park in Keilor East · Basic info

Pennington Reserve is a park in Keilor East, in the Moonee Valley City Council area, good for all ages. The Crossway/Milleara Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 786 m away with a baby change table.
