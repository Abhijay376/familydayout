Playground at Ashkansay Reserve

Playground in Keilor East · Basic info

Playground at Ashkansay Reserve in Keilor East is a public playground in the Moonee Valley City area. Keilor East Leisure Centre is about 540 m away, and Playground in Keilor East is another option about 410 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 630 m away, and bus 469 stops at Rachelle Rd/Noga Ave, a 1-minute walk away.
