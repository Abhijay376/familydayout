Milleara Shopping Centre

Shopping centre in Keilor East · Basic info

Milleara Shopping Centre is a shopping centre in Keilor East, in the Moonee Valley City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Milleara SC/Amis Cres (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 143 m away with a baby change table.
