Brimbank Park

Park in Keilor East · Official details

Brimbank Park is a park in Keilor East, in the Brimbank City Council area, good for all ages. Driscolls Rd/Stenson Rd (bus) is a 27-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 1044 m away and dogs are not allowed. Opening hours: Open every day from 6.00am; closes 5.30pm, or 8.00pm during daylight saving.

Where is Brimbank Park?
