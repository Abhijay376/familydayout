Steele Creek Linear Park

Park in Keilor East · Basic info

Steele Creek Linear Park is a park in Keilor East, in the Moonee Valley City Council area, good for all ages. The Avenue/Valley Lake Bvd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 767 m away with a baby change table.
