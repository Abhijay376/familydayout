Shelley Reserve

Park in Keilor East · Basic info

Shelley Reserve is a park in Keilor East, in the Moonee Valley City Council area, good for all ages. Paul Ave/Quinn Gr (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 447 m away with a baby change table.
