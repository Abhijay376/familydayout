Rosehill Park

Park in Keilor East · Official details

Rosehill Park is a park in Keilor East, in the Moonee Valley City Council area, good for all ages. Dinah Pde/Rachelle Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 83 m away.

Questions parents ask

Where is Rosehill Park?
Rosehill Park is in Keilor East, Victoria.
Is Rosehill Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Rosehill Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 83 m away.
How do I get to Rosehill Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Dinah Pde/Rachelle Rd (routes 469), a 3-minute walk.
