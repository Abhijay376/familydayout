Playground at Border Drive Reserve

Playground in Keilor East · Official details

Playground at Border Drive Reserve is a public playground in Keilor East, in the Moonee Valley City area. The M80 Trail passes about 280 m away, and Playground at Hassett Crescent Walkway is another option about 360 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.8 km away, and bus 903 stops at Keilor Park Dr, a 4-minute walk away.
