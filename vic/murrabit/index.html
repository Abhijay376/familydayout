Murrabit in the Gannawarra area has 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Murrabit is Benjeroop Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 373 hectares. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Murrabit.

Getting around

Murrabit has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Kerang - Echuca via Cohuna route stop in Murrabit. By car from Murrabit, Swan Hill is about 43 minutes away and Echuca about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Murrabit is Swimming Pool in Kerang, about 23 minutes' drive. For a library, Barham Branch Library in Koondrook is about 31 minutes' drive from Murrabit. For a skate park, Barham Skate Park in Koondrook is about 32 minutes' drive from Murrabit. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Murrabit, about 43 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Murrabit are the playground at Riverside Park in Koondrook (31 min), Barham Branch Library in Koondrook (31 min), Barham Skate Park in Koondrook (32 min), the playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV) in Koondrook (33 min), Swimming Pool in Kerang (23 min) and Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (23 min).

Best for
