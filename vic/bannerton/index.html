Bannerton is in the Swan Hill area, and our data lists 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Toltol I263 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Bannerton, at about 234 hectares. Bannerton Flora and Fauna Reserve and Toltol I40 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Bannerton include Bumbang I38 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bannerton. The closest to Bannerton is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 100 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Bannerton. By car from Bannerton, Mildura is about 1.5 hours away and Swan Hill about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang is about 35 minutes' drive from Bannerton. The closest skate park to Bannerton is Robinvale Skatepark in Robinvale, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Bannerton are the playground at Rotary Park in Robinvale (12 min), the playground at Pisasale Park in Robinvale (12 min), Rural Life Museum in Robinvale (12 min), Robinvale Skatepark in Robinvale (13 min) and Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bannerton.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Bannerton, Robinvale has 5 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Bannerton, Wemen has 1 playground. Happy Valley and Annuello also border Bannerton.
