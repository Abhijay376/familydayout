Burkes Flat is in the Loddon area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Burkes Flat is Burkes Flat Bushland Reserve, covering about 4 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Burkes Flat. The closest to Burkes Flat is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 47 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Burkes Flat, so most trips start by car. By car from Burkes Flat, Maryborough is about 56 minutes away, Bendigo about 66 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Burkes Flat is St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud, about 26 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 56 minutes from Burkes Flat by car, with a library. From Burkes Flat, the nearest places for kids by road are Bi-Centennial Park Playground in St Arnaud (25 min), the playground at Queen Mary Botanic Gardens in St Arnaud (26 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (26 min), Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (31 min), Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (31 min) and Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (29 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Burkes Flat.
