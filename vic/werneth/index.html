Werneth is in the Golden Plains area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Werneth is Cressy Streamside Reserve, covering about 8 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Werneth. The closest to Werneth is Colac Railway Station (V/Line), about 43 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Werneth, so most trips start by car. By car from Werneth, Colac is about 46 minutes away, Ballarat about 58 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Werneth is Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest library to Werneth is Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum, about 44 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Werneth is Rokewood Skate Park in Rokewood, about 22 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac is about 45 minutes' drive from Werneth. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Werneth, about 46 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Werneth are the playground at Cressy Picnic Area in Cressy (14 min), Rokewood Skate Park in Rokewood (22 min), Rokewood Recreation Reserve Playground in Rokewood (22 min), Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood (21 min), Dereel Skate Park in Dereel (29 min) and Lismore Pool in Lismore (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Werneth.

Nearby suburbs

Rokewood, next to Werneth, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Cressy, next to Werneth, has 1 playground. Pitfield, Illabarook, Duverney and Berrybank also border Werneth.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Werneth, without traffic.
