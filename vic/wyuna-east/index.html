Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Wyuna East in the Campaspe area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Wyuna East has no train station; Mooroopna Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wyuna East, so most trips start by car. By car from Wyuna East, Echuca is about 32 minutes away, Shepparton about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Community Swimming Pool in Merrigum is about 23 minutes' drive from Wyuna East. For a library, Kyabram Library And Community Hub in Kyabram is about 17 minutes' drive from Wyuna East. The closest beach to Wyuna East is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 42 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the bmx track on Racecourse Road in Kyabram is about 19 minutes' drive from Wyuna East. For a cinema, Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca is about 34 minutes' drive from Wyuna East. The closest indoor play centre to Wyuna East is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 40 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram is about 19 minutes' drive from Wyuna East. Echuca is the closest bigger centre to Wyuna East, about 32 minutes away by car, and has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Wyuna East are the playground at Drum Street Reserve in Kyabram (17 min), the playground at Edis Park in Kyabram (16 min), Kyabram Library And Community Hub in Kyabram (17 min), Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram (19 min), the bmx track on Racecourse Road in Kyabram (19 min) and Community Swimming Pool in Merrigum (23 min).
