Tempy in the Mildura area has 1 playground and 6 parks and reserves in our data. Beyond the parks, Tempy has a pool.

Landmarks

Frasers Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Tempy, at about 138 hectares. Mittyack Blue Hills Bushland Reserve and Lads Tank Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Tempy include Julians Bushland Reserve, Watseys Bushland Reserve and State Gully Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds in Tempy include the playground at Tempy Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Tempy. In Tempy you'll also find Tempy Outdoor Swimming Pool.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tempy. The closest to Tempy is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 103 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Mildura - Horsham via Hopetoun route stop in Tempy. V/Line coaches stop in Tempy. By car from Tempy, Swan Hill is about 1.5 hours away and Mildura about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest cinema to Tempy is Roxy in Ouyen, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Tempy are the playground at Patchewollock Rec Reserve in Patchewollock (23 min), Ouyen Swimming Pool in Ouyen (25 min) and Roxy in Ouyen (25 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tempy.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Tempy, Ouyen has 4 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. Next door to Tempy, Patchewollock has 1 playground. Speed, Pier Milan, Nandaly and Mittyack also border Tempy.
