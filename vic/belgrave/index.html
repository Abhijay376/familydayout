Belgrave in the Yarra Ranges area has 5 playgrounds and 7 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, Belgrave has a library, a pool and a cinema.

Landmarks

Belgrave Lake Park is the largest mapped park in Belgrave, at about 12 hectares. Borthwick Park Reserve and Bellbird Street Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Belgrave include Apex Park, Long Pockitt Bushland Reserve and Belgrave G186 Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds around Belgrave include the playground at Belgrave Lake Park and the playground at Borthwick Park Reserve. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Belgrave. In Belgrave you'll also find Cameo Belgrave, Belgrave Heated Outdoor Swimming Pool and Belgrave Library. The Ringwood - Belgrave Rail Trail, Country (ngurrak barring | RidgeWalk) and Activism (ngurrak barring | RidgeWalk) all pass through Belgrave. Our data lists 12 cafes and family restaurants in Belgrave for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Belgrave has a train station: Belgrave Railway Station (metro: Belgrave Line). Buses 663, 693, 694, 695, 697, 699 and 695F stop in Belgrave. By car from Belgrave, Dandenong is about 23 minutes away, Ringwood about 23 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 41 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a beach, Edithvale Beach in Edithvale is about 36 minutes' drive from Belgrave. The closest skate park to Belgrave is Upwey Skate Park in Upwey, about 6 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Little Rascals Play Centre in Ferntree Gully is about 9 minutes' drive from Belgrave. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Belgrave is Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale, about 30 minutes' drive.

Best for

Younger kids at Belgrave's 5 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the library and cinema in Belgrave. Free days on the Ringwood - Belgrave Rail Trail and in Belgrave's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Belgrave Heights, next to Belgrave, has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Belgrave, Tecoma has 1 playground. Selby, next to Belgrave, has 1 playground. Next door to Belgrave, Belgrave South has 1 playground. Sherbrooke also borders Belgrave.
