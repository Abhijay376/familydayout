Belgrave Lake Park

Park in Belgrave · Basic info

Belgrave Lake Park is a park in Belgrave, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Old Reservoir Rd/Belgrave - Hallam Rd (bus) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 808 m away.
