Landmarks

The biggest green space in Nichols Point is James King Park, covering about 3 hectares. Nicholls Point Reserve and Drings Hill Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Nichols Point include Wilkinson Park. Playgrounds in Nichols Point include the playground at Wilkinson Park and the playground at James King Park. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Nichols Point. The Kings Billabong Nature Walk passes through Nichols Point. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Nichols Point for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Nichols Point. The closest to Nichols Point is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 177 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nichols Point, so most trips start by car. By car from Nichols Point, Mildura is about 9 minutes away and Swan Hill about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Aquacoaster in Mildura is about 8 minutes' drive from Nichols Point. For a library, Mildura Library in Mildura is about 9 minutes' drive from Nichols Point. For a beach, Apex Beach in Mildura is about 17 minutes' drive from Nichols Point. The closest skate park to Nichols Point is Mildura Skate Park in Mildura, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Nichols Point is Deakin Cinema Complex in Mildura, about 9 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Sunraysia Tenpin in Mildura is about 7 minutes' drive from Nichols Point. For a zoo or wildlife park, Putt Putt Family Fun Centre in Mildura is about 8 minutes' drive from Nichols Point. Mildura is the closest bigger centre to Nichols Point, about 9 minutes away by car, and has 26 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Free days on the Kings Billabong Nature Walk and in Nichols Point's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Mildura, next to Nichols Point, has 26 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Nichols Point, Irymple has 3 playgrounds and a pool.
