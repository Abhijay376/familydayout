Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Koo Wee Rup North in the Cardinia area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Koo Wee Rup North has no train station; East Pakenham Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 6.6 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Koo Wee Rup North, so most trips start by car. By car from Koo Wee Rup North, Pakenham is about 17 minutes away, Cranbourne about 32 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 61 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Koo Wee Rup North is Koo Wee Rup Swimming Pool in Koo Wee Rup, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest library to Koo Wee Rup North is Pakenham Library in Pakenham, about 17 minutes' drive. For a skate park, P.B Ronald Skate Park in Pakenham is about 18 minutes' drive from Koo Wee Rup North. The closest cinema to Koo Wee Rup North is Village Cinemas in Narre Warren, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Koo Wee Rup North is Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Koo Wee Rup North is MonkeyG's in Pakenham, about 16 minutes' drive. Pakenham is the closest bigger centre to Koo Wee Rup North, about 17 minutes away by car, and has 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Koo Wee Rup North are the playground at Bayles Recreation Reserve in Bayles (11 min), MonkeyG's in Pakenham (16 min), the playground at Burke Park in Pakenham (17 min), Pakenham Library in Pakenham (17 min), P.B Ronald Skate Park in Pakenham (18 min) and Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham (17 min).
