Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Byrneside in the Greater Shepparton area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Byrneside. The closest to Byrneside is Mooroopna Railway Station (V/Line), about 17 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Bendigo Station - Shepparton Station via Kyabram route stop in Byrneside. By car from Byrneside, Shepparton is about 21 minutes away, Echuca about 56 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Community Swimming Pool in Merrigum is about 9 minutes' drive from Byrneside. For a library, Tatura Library in Tatura is about 7 minutes' drive from Byrneside. The closest beach to Byrneside is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 23 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Tatura Memorial Park in Tatura is about 8 minutes' drive from Byrneside. The closest cinema to Byrneside is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 21 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 21 minutes' drive from Byrneside. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kidstown in Mooroopna is about 19 minutes' drive from Byrneside. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 21 minutes from Byrneside by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Byrneside, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Northlinks Reserve in Tatura (8 min), Joe Ford Drive Playground in Tatura (6 min), Tatura Irrigation and Wartime Camps Museum in Tatura (6 min), Tatura Library in Tatura (7 min), the skate park at Tatura Memorial Park in Tatura (8 min) and Community Swimming Pool in Merrigum (9 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Byrneside.

Nearby suburbs

Tatura, next to Byrneside, has 8 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Merrigum, next to Byrneside, has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Cooma also borders Byrneside.
