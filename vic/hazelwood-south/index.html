Hazelwood South is in the Latrobe area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Jeeralang North Education Area is the largest mapped park in Hazelwood South, at about 146 hectares.

Getting around

Hazelwood South has no train station; Morwell Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 10 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Hazelwood South, so most trips start by car. By car from Hazelwood South, Traralgon is about 18 minutes away, Warragul about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Latrobe Leisure - Churchill in Churchill is about 8 minutes' drive from Hazelwood South. The closest library to Hazelwood South is Churchill Library in Churchill, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Hazelwood South is Churchill Skate Park in Churchill, about 9 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 15 minutes' drive from Hazelwood South. The nearest larger centre is Traralgon, about 18 minutes from Hazelwood South by car, with 38 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Hazelwood South are Latrobe Leisure - Churchill in Churchill (8 min), Glendonald Park Playground in Churchill (8 min), Churchill Skate Park in Churchill (9 min), Churchill Library in Churchill (9 min), White Parade Playground in Churchill (10 min) and Powerworks in Morwell (15 min).
