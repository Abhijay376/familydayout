Nyarrin is in the Buloke area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Nyarrin, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Nyarrin has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 80 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nyarrin, so most trips start by car. By car from Nyarrin, Swan Hill is about 76 minutes away and Mildura about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Nyarrin is Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake, about 19 minutes' drive. From Nyarrin, the nearest places for kids by road are Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake (19 min), the playground at Apex Park in Sea Lake (19 min), the playground at Tempy Recreation Reserve in Tempy (23 min) and Tempy Outdoor Swimming Pool in Tempy (23 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Nyarrin.

Nearby suburbs

Nandaly and Bimbourie also border Nyarrin.
