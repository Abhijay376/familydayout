Loch Valley in the Baw Baw area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Other parks and reserves in Loch Valley include Poplars Reserve and Checking Station. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Loch Valley. Picnic areas in Loch Valley include Checking Station and Poplars Reserve. The Upper Yarra Walking Track passes through Loch Valley.

Getting around

Loch Valley has no train station; Drouin Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 40 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Loch Valley, so most trips start by car. By car from Loch Valley, Warragul is about 46 minutes away, Healesville about 71 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest skate park to Loch Valley is Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South, about 28 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Warragul, about 46 minutes from Loch Valley by car, with 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Loch Valley are the playground at LaTrobe and Loch Rivers, Noojee Streamside Reserve in Noojee (12 min), Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South (28 min) and the playground at Blue Rock Lake West picnic area in Willow Grove (45 min).

Best for

Free days on the Upper Yarra Walking Track and in Loch Valley's parks.
