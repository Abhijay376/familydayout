Boweya is in the Benalla area, and our data lists 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Boweya is Warby Range Reference Area, covering about 148 hectares. Killawarra H103 Bushland Reserve and Boweya H102 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Boweya also has Boweya H101 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Boweya. The closest to Boweya is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Boweya, so most trips start by car. By car from Boweya, Wangaratta is about 22 minutes away and Benalla about 39 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Boweya is Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta, about 21 minutes' drive. For a library, Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta is about 22 minutes' drive from Boweya. The closest skate park to Boweya is the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta, about 26 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 22 minutes' drive from Boweya. For a zoo or wildlife park, Tunzafun in Yarrawonga is about 38 minutes' drive from Boweya. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Boweya, about 22 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Boweya, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Peechelba Reserve in Peechelba (17 min), Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta (21 min), Brien Crescent Playground in Wangaratta (22 min), Cobb & Co in Glenrowan (24 min), Glenrowan Museum in Glenrowan (24 min) and Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (22 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Boweya.

Nearby suburbs

Boweya North and Almonds also border Boweya.
