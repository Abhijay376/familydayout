Playground at Derby Street Reserve

Playground in Moonee Ponds · Basic info

Playground at Derby Street Reserve in Moonee Ponds is a public playground in the Moonee Valley City area. The Shamrock Street Popup Bike Route is about 380 m away, and Argyle Street Playground is another option about 460 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 660 m from the playground, and bus 467 stops at Norfolk St/Holmes Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
