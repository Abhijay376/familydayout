Argyle Street Playground

Playground in Moonee Ponds · Basic info

Argyle Street Playground in Moonee Ponds is a public playground in the Moonee Valley City area. For food, Darling Street Espresso is about 310 m away, and Playground at Derby Street Reserve is another option about 460 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 470 m from the playground, and bus 467 stops at Lennox St/Holmes Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
