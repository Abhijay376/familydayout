Playground at Tote Park

Playground in Moonee Ponds · Basic info

This is a public playground in Moonee Ponds. The Moonee Valley Popup Bike Lane Route is about 110 m away, and Sam Merrifield Library is about 310 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 400 m from the playground, and bus 506 stops at McNae St/Dean St, a 3-minute walk away.
