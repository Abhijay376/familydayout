Playground at Fanny Reserve

Playground in Moonee Ponds · Official details

Playground at Fanny Reserve is a public playground in Moonee Ponds, in the Moonee Valley City area. The Moonee Ponds Creek Trail passes about 30 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 50 m away. There's a public toilet about 760 m away, and bus 508 stops at Moonee Ponds PS/Wilson St, a 5-minute walk away.
