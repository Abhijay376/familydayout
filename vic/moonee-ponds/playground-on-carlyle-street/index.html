Playground at Carlyle Street Reserve

Playground in Moonee Ponds · Basic info

Playground at Carlyle Street Reserve is a public playground in Moonee Ponds, in the Moonee Valley City area. The Shamrock Street Popup Bike Route is about 300 m away, and for food, Hakata is about 290 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 460 m from the playground, and buses 469, 476, 477 and 483 stop at Buckley St/Mt Alexander Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
