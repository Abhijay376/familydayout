Turner Street Reserve

Playground in Moonee Ponds · Basic info

Turner Street Reserve is a public playground in Moonee Ponds, in the Moonee Valley City area. The Bike: Hope St / Stewart St Shimmy is about 160 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 250 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.1 km from the playground, and bus 508 stops at Juliet St/Wilson St, a 5-minute walk away.
