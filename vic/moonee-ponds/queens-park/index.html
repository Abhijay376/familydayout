Queens Park

Park in Moonee Ponds · Basic info

Queens Park is a park in Moonee Ponds, in the Moonee Valley City Council area, good for all ages. 35-Queens Park/Pascoe Vale Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 112 m away with a baby change table.
