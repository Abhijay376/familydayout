Taggerty is in the Murrindindi area, and our data lists 5 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Cathedral Range State Park is the largest mapped park in Taggerty, at about 3,599 hectares. Black Range State Forest and Black Range State Forest are the next largest. Taggerty also has Sugarloaf Saddle Day Visitor Area and Acheron River, Taggerty Streamside Reserve. Our data shows 8 public toilets in Taggerty. For walks and rides, Cathedral Range Northern Circuit, Cathedral Range Southern Circuit and Ridge Track cross Taggerty.

Getting around

Taggerty has no train station; Lilydale Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 58 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the 684 and Alexandra - Marysville via Taggerty & Buxton routes stop in Taggerty. By car from Taggerty, Healesville is about 47 minutes away, Seymour about 65 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra is about 16 minutes' drive from Taggerty. The closest library to Taggerty is Alexandra Library in Alexandra, about 15 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Gallipolli Park in Marysville is about 24 minutes' drive from Taggerty. The nearest larger centre is Healesville, about 47 minutes from Taggerty by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Taggerty are the playground at Buxton Recreation Reserve in Buxton (11 min), Alexandra Library in Alexandra (15 min), Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra (16 min), the playground at Leckie Park in Alexandra (16 min), Alexandra Timber Tramway and Museum in Alexandra (17 min) and Replica of the original police station in Marysville (23 min).

Best for

Free days on the Cathedral Range Northern Circuit and in Taggerty's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Buxton, next to Taggerty, has 2 playgrounds. Limestone and Acheron also border Taggerty.
