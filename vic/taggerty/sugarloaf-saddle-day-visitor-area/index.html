Sugarloaf Saddle Day Visitor Area

Park in Taggerty · Official details

Sugarloaf Saddle Day Visitor Area is a park in Taggerty, in the Murrindindi Shire Council area, good for all ages. The nearest public toilet is about 1 m away and dogs are not allowed.
