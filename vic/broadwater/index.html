Broadwater is in the Moyne area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Broadwater, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Broadwater. The closest to Broadwater is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 47 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Broadwater, so most trips start by car. By car from Broadwater, Hamilton is about 39 minutes away and Portland about 48 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Hawkesdale Swimming Pool in Hawkesdale is about 22 minutes' drive from Broadwater. For a library, Heywood Library in Heywood is about 33 minutes' drive from Broadwater. The closest beach to Broadwater is Eumeralla in Tyrendarra East, about 29 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Russell Clark Reserve in Port Fairy is about 28 minutes' drive from Broadwater. The closest cinema to Broadwater is Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy, about 28 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 39 minutes from Broadwater by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Broadwater are Hawkesdale Swimming Pool in Hawkesdale (22 min), Eumeralla in Tyrendarra East (29 min), the playground at Kirkstall Recreation Reserve in Kirkstall (27 min), Limestone Gallery in Port Fairy (27 min), Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy (28 min) and the skate park at Russell Clark Reserve in Port Fairy (28 min).
