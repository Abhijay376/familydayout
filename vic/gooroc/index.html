Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Gooroc in the Northern Grampians area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Gooroc has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 81 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gooroc, so most trips start by car. By car from Gooroc, Stawell is about 77 minutes away and Maryborough about 80 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Gooroc is St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud, about 15 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Donald BMX Track in Donald is about 24 minutes' drive from Gooroc. The closest cinema to Gooroc is Rex Theatre in Charlton, about 27 minutes' drive. From Gooroc, the nearest places for kids by road are Apex Park Playground in St Arnaud (14 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (15 min), Market Square Park Playground in St Arnaud (15 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (20 min), Donald BMX Track in Donald (24 min) and Rex Theatre in Charlton (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Gooroc.
