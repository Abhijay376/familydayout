Williamstown North is in the Hobsons Bay area, and our data lists 3 playgrounds and 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Williamstown North is KC White Reserve, covering about 4 hectares. RJ Long Reserve and Quarry Reserve are the next largest. Williamstown North also has Edina Street Reserve. Playgrounds in Williamstown North include Tumbles Playhouse, the playground at RJ Long Reserve and the playground at Edina Street Reserve. For walks and rides, Bike: New Port Maddock Rd shimmy crosses Williamstown North. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Williamstown North for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Williamstown North. The closest to Williamstown North is North Williamstown Railway Station (metro), about 1.3 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 415 stops in Williamstown North. By car from Williamstown North, Sunshine is about 17 minutes away, Werribee about 22 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 20 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Sweeney's Swim School in Altona is about 8 minutes' drive from Williamstown North. The closest library to Williamstown North is Williamstown Library in Williamstown, about 3 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Williamstown North is Williamstown Beach in Williamstown, about 4 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Grant Reserve in Altona is about 7 minutes' drive from Williamstown North. For a cinema, Reading Cinemas in Altona North is about 7 minutes' drive from Williamstown North. The closest indoor play centre to Williamstown North is Epic Arena in Newport, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Williamstown North is Skyline Precinct Melbourne in South Wharf, about 16 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Sunshine, about 17 minutes from Williamstown North by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Williamstown North's 3 local playgrounds. Free days on the Bike: New Port Maddock Rd shimmy and in Williamstown North's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Altona North, next to Williamstown North, has 15 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Williamstown, next to Williamstown North, has 18 playgrounds, a library and a beach. Next door to Williamstown North, Altona has 11 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Williamstown North, Newport has 15 playgrounds and a library.
