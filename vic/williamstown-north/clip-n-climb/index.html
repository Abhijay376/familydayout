Clip 'n Climb

Climbing in Williamstown North · Official details

Clip 'n Climb is a climbing in Williamstown North, in the Hobsons Bay City Council area, best for kids 5 and up and teens. Maddox Rd/Kororoit Creek Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 304 m away. Opening hours: Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat, Sun 09:00-21:00.
