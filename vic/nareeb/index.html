Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Nareeb in the Moyne area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Nareeb has no train station; Terang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 52 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nareeb, so most trips start by car. By car from Nareeb, Hamilton is about 55 minutes away and Warrnambool about 78 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Nareeb is Penshurst Swimming Pool in Penshurst, about 40 minutes' drive. The closest library to Nareeb is Lake Bolac Library in Lake Bolac, about 40 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 55 minutes from Nareeb by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Nareeb, the nearest places for kids by road are Glenthompson Rec Reserve Playground in Glenthompson (48 min), Penshurst Rec Reserve Playground in Penshurst (39 min), Penshurst Swimming Pool in Penshurst (40 min), Volcanoes Discovery Centre in Penshurst (40 min), Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake (47 min) and Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake (48 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Nareeb.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Nareeb, Penshurst has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Glenthompson, next to Nareeb, has 2 playgrounds. Chatsworth and Caramut also border Nareeb.
