Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Winton, in the Benalla area.

Landmarks

Winton Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Winton, at about 2 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Winton.

Getting around

Winton has no train station; Benalla Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 9.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Winton, so most trips start by car. By car from Winton, Benalla is about 11 minutes away, Wangaratta about 27 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Winton is Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla, about 11 minutes' drive. For a library, Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta is about 28 minutes' drive from Winton. The closest skate park to Winton is Pump Track in Benalla, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Winton is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 28 minutes' drive. Benalla is the closest bigger centre to Winton, about 11 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds and a pool. From Winton, the nearest places for kids by road are Benalla Aviation Museum in Benalla (10 min), Benalla Migrant Camp in Benalla (11 min), Pump Track in Benalla (11 min), Benalla Splash Park in Benalla (11 min), Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (11 min) and the playground at Benalla Botanical Gardens in Benalla (12 min).
