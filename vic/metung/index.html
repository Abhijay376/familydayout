Metung in the East Gippsland area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Tambo Delta - Metung Gippsland Lakes Reserve is the largest mapped park in Metung, at about 21 hectares. Metung Bushland Reserve and Shaving Point Reserve are the next largest. Metung also has A B Patterson Reserve. Our data shows 6 public toilets in Metung. For walks and rides, Wallaby Creek Walking track crosses Metung.

Getting around

Metung has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Metung, so most trips start by car. By car from Metung, Lakes Entrance is about 24 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 29 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Lakes Entrance Aquatic Centre in Lakes Entrance is about 25 minutes' drive from Metung. The closest library to Metung is Lakes Entrance Library in Lakes Entrance, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Metung is 90 Mile Beach in Boole Poole, about 2 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Metung is the skate park at Gilsenan Reserve in Paynesville, about 41 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Metung is Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale, about 27 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow is about 21 minutes' drive from Metung. Lakes Entrance is the closest bigger centre to Metung, about 24 minutes away by car, and has 3 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a beach. From Metung, the nearest places for kids by road are 90 Mile Beach in Boole Poole (2 min), the playground at Nyerimilang Heritage Park in Nyerimilang (20 min), Griffiths Sea Shell Museum in Lakes Entrance (19 min), Lakes Entrance Library in Lakes Entrance (21 min), Ninety Mile Beach in Lakes Entrance (23 min) and Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow (21 min).

Best for

Free days on the Wallaby Creek Walking track and in Metung's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Boole Poole, next to Metung, has a beach. Nyerimilang, next to Metung, has 1 playground. Johnsonville also borders Metung.
