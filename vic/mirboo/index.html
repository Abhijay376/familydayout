Mirboo is in the South Gippsland area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Mirboo Recreation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Mirboo, at about 3 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Mirboo.

Getting around

Mirboo has no train station; Trafalgar Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mirboo, so most trips start by car. By car from Mirboo, Leongatha is about 35 minutes away, Traralgon about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha is about 33 minutes' drive from Mirboo. For a library, Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North is about 13 minutes' drive from Mirboo. For a skate park, Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North in Mirboo North is about 13 minutes' drive from Mirboo. The closest cinema to Mirboo is Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha, about 35 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Leongatha, about 35 minutes from Mirboo by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Mirboo are the playground at Baromi Park in Mirboo North (13 min), Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North (13 min), Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North in Mirboo North (13 min), Boolarra Skate Park in Boolarra (22 min), the playground at Railway Park in Boolarra (22 min) and Boolarra Old School Museum in Boolarra (22 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mirboo.

Nearby suburbs

Mirboo North, next to Mirboo, has 1 playground, a library and a skate park. Dumbalk North, Dollar, Darlimurla and Boolarra South also border Mirboo.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Mirboo, without traffic.
