Playground on McMillan Street

Playground in Stratford · Basic info

Playground on McMillan Street in Stratford is a public playground in the Wellington Shire area. The Gippsland Plains Rail Trail passes about 100 m away, and Stratford Swimming Pool is about 80 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 260 m away. It's easiest by car, about 2 minutes from Stratford.
