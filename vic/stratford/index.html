Our data lists 8 playgrounds and 7 parks and reserves in Stratford, in the Wellington area. Stratford also has a pool and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Stratford is Knob Reserve, covering about 62 hectares. Stratford I12 Bushland Reserve and Unnamed P0303 Private Nature Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Stratford include Swan Park, Redbank Streamside Reserve and Stratford Historical Museum. Playgrounds around Stratford include BMX Track, Apex Park Playground and Swan Park Playground. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Stratford. Places to visit in Stratford include Stratford Swimming Pool. For older kids in Stratford there's Skate Park. For walks and rides, Gippsland Plains Rail Trail crosses Stratford. Our data lists 3 cafes and family restaurants in Stratford for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Stratford has a train station: Stratford Railway Station (V/Line: Bairnsdale - Melbourne via Sale & Traralgon). Buses on the Sale - Stratford route stop in Stratford. V/Line coaches stop in Stratford. By car from Stratford, Sale is about 15 minutes away, Bairnsdale about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Sale Library in Sale is about 16 minutes' drive from Stratford. For a cinema, Former Drive-in site in Sale is about 13 minutes' drive from Stratford.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 8 playgrounds across Stratford. Teens who skate or ride, with Skate Park in Stratford. Free days on the Gippsland Plains Rail Trail and in Stratford's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Stratford, Maffra has 6 playgrounds. Next door to Stratford, Briagolong has 2 playgrounds. Munro, Montgomery, Llowalong and Clydebank also border Stratford.
