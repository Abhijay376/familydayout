Soldiers Memorial Park Playground

Playground in Stratford · Basic info

Soldiers Memorial Park Playground is a public playground in Stratford, in the Wellington Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 340 m away, and Stratford Swimming Pool is about 540 m away. There's a public toilet about 230 m away, and coaches stop at Memorial Park/Princes Hwy, a 1-minute walk away.
