Playground on Hobson Street

Playground in Stratford · Basic info

Playground on Hobson Street is a public playground in Stratford, in the Wellington Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 70 m away, and Wa-De-Lock Cellar Door is about 250 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 280 m away, and coaches stop at Post Office/Hobson St, a 2-minute walk away.
