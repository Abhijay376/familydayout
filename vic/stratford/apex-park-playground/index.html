Apex Park Playground

Playground in Stratford · Basic info

This is a public playground in Stratford. The Gippsland Plains Rail Trail passes about 70 m away, and Stratford Swimming Pool is about 190 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground. It's easiest by car, about 2 minutes from Stratford.
