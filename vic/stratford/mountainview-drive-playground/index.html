Mountainview Drive Playground

Playground in Stratford · Basic info

Mountainview Drive Playground is a public playground in Stratford, in the Wellington Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. You'll find a public toilet about 1.2 km from the playground, and bus Sale - Stratford stops at Niel St/McFarlane St, a 7-minute walk away.
