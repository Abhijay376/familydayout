Skate Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Stratford · Basic info

Skate Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Stratford, in the Wellington Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Post Office/Hobson St (coach) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 358 m away.
