Lake Marmal is in the Loddon area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Lake Marmal Lake Reserve is the largest mapped park in Lake Marmal, at about 188 hectares.

Getting around

Lake Marmal has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 59 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lake Marmal, so most trips start by car. By car from Lake Marmal, Bendigo is about 1.5 hours away and Swan Hill about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Boort Swimming Pool in Boort is about 17 minutes' drive from Lake Marmal. The closest cinema to Lake Marmal is Rex Theatre in Charlton, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Lake Marmal are Boort Swimming Pool in Boort (17 min), Boort Court House Historical Museum in Boort (16 min), A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park in Boort (17 min), Rex Theatre in Charlton (20 min), Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (21 min) and the playground at Rotary Park in Charlton (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lake Marmal.

Nearby suburbs

Buckrabanyule and Barraport West also border Lake Marmal.
