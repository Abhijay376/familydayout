Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Springfield (Macedon Ranges) in the Macedon Ranges area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Springfield (Macedon Ranges). The closest to Springfield (Macedon Ranges) is Kilmore East Station (V/Line), about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Springfield (Macedon Ranges), so most trips start by car. By car from Springfield (Macedon Ranges), Seymour is about 49 minutes away, Kyneton about 55 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 75 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Springfield (Macedon Ranges) is Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest library to Springfield (Macedon Ranges) is Romsey Library in Romsey, about 16 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Romsey Skate Park in Romsey is about 16 minutes' drive from Springfield (Macedon Ranges). For a zoo or wildlife park, Funfields in Whittlesea is about 45 minutes' drive from Springfield (Macedon Ranges). Seymour is the closest bigger centre to Springfield (Macedon Ranges), about 49 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Springfield (Macedon Ranges), the nearest places for kids by road are Romsey Skate Park in Romsey (16 min), Romsey Library in Romsey (16 min), the playground at Moore Court Reserve in Kilmore (16 min), the playground at Hudson Park in Kilmore (16 min), Kilmore Skate Park in Kilmore (17 min) and Kilmore Library in Kilmore (17 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Springfield (Macedon Ranges).

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Springfield (Macedon Ranges), Romsey has 2 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Goldie, Forbes, Darraweit Guim and Chintin also border Springfield (Macedon Ranges).
