Keilor in the Brimbank area has 11 playgrounds and 11 parks and reserves in our data. Beyond the parks, Keilor has 2 libraries.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Keilor is Taylors Creek Linear Park, covering about 11 hectares. Caroline Chisholm Reserve and Keilor Recreation Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Keilor include Bonfield Reserve, Calder Rise Park and Patterson Gardens. Playgrounds around Keilor include the playground at Bonfield Reserve, the playground at Calder Rise Park and the playground at Patterson Gardens. Our data shows 15 public toilets and 7 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Keilor. Places to visit in Keilor include Keilor Library and Keilor Village Library. The Maribyrnong River Trail and Taylors Creek Trail all pass through Keilor. Our data lists 6 cafes and family restaurants in Keilor for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Keilor has no train station; Keilor Plains Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 3.4 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 476 stops in Keilor. By car from Keilor, Sunshine is about 11 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 13 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 24 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Keilor is 50m Pool in Keilor Downs, about 4 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Keilor is Horse Arena in Abbotsford, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Keilor is the skate park at Packard Street Reserve in Keilor Downs, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Keilor is Hoyts in Taylors Lakes, about 9 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes is about 10 minutes' drive from Keilor. For a zoo or wildlife park, Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville is about 19 minutes' drive from Keilor.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 11 playgrounds across Keilor. Rainy days at the library in Keilor. Free days on the Maribyrnong River Trail and in Keilor's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Taylors Lakes, next to Keilor, has 16 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Keilor East, next to Keilor, has 26 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Keilor Downs, next to Keilor, has 16 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Melbourne Airport, next to Keilor, has a pool. Kealba, Keilor Park, Keilor Lodge and Keilor North also border Keilor.
