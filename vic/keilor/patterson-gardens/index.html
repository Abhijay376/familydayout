Patterson Gardens

Park in Keilor · Official details

Patterson Gardens is a park in Keilor, in the Brimbank City Council area, good for all ages. Campaspe Cres/Patterson Ave (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 891 m away.

Details

Good to know

Questions parents ask

Where is Patterson Gardens?
Patterson Gardens is in Keilor, Victoria.
Is Patterson Gardens free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Patterson Gardens?
The nearest public toilet is about 891 m away.
How do I get to Patterson Gardens by public transport?
The closest stop is Campaspe Cres/Patterson Ave (routes 476), a 2-minute walk.
