Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Molesworth, in the Murrindindi area.

Landmarks

Molesworth Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Molesworth, at about 26 hectares. Cheviot Railway Tunnel Historic and Cultural Features Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Molesworth. For walks and rides, Great Victorian Rail Trail and Hume and Hovell Trail cross Molesworth.

Getting around

Molesworth has no train station; Avenel Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 41 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Molesworth. By car from Molesworth, Seymour is about 41 minutes away, Mansfield about 51 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra is about 17 minutes' drive from Molesworth. For a library, Alexandra Library in Alexandra is about 16 minutes' drive from Molesworth. The closest skate park to Molesworth is the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea, about 13 minutes' drive. Seymour is the closest bigger centre to Molesworth, about 41 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Molesworth are Yarck Recreation Reserve Playground in Yarck (9 min), the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea (13 min), the playground at Leckie Park in Alexandra (18 min), Alexandra Library in Alexandra (16 min), BMX Track in Caveat (33 min) and Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra (17 min).

Best for

Free days on the Great Victorian Rail Trail and in Molesworth's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Molesworth, Yarck has 1 playground. Caveat, next to Molesworth, has 1 playground and a skate park. Next door to Molesworth, Yea has a skate park. Limestone, Killingworth, Highlands and Cathkin also border Molesworth.
