Woorinen North is in the Swan Hill area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Woorinen North, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Woorinen North has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Woorinen North, so most trips start by car. By car from Woorinen North, Swan Hill is about 18 minutes away and Echuca about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Woorinen North is Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest library to Woorinen North is Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Woorinen North is Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South, about 14 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill is about 18 minutes' drive from Woorinen North. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Woorinen North, about 18 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Woorinen North are Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South (14 min), the playground at Nyah Heritage Park in Nyah (15 min), the playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV) in Nyah (16 min), Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill (17 min), the skate park at James Belsar Reserve in Swan Hill (18 min) and Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill (17 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Woorinen North.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Woorinen North, Woorinen South has a skate park. Beverford also borders Woorinen North.
