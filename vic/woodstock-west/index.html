Woodstock West is in the Loddon area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Woodstock H68 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Woodstock West, at about 5 hectares. Woodstock Road School Site is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Woodstock West. The closest to Woodstock West is Kangaroo Flat Railway Station (V/Line), about 25 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Woodstock West, so most trips start by car. By car from Woodstock West, Bendigo is about 35 minutes away, Maryborough about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Woodstock West is Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly, about 26 minutes' drive. For a library, Athenaeum Library in Maldon is about 28 minutes' drive from Woodstock West. The closest skate park to Woodstock West is Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk, about 35 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 35 minutes' drive from Woodstock West. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 32 minutes' drive from Woodstock West. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 41 minutes' drive from Woodstock West. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Woodstock West, about 35 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Woodstock West, the nearest places for kids by road are Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (25 min), Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (26 min), Graemar Court Playspace in Kangaroo Flat (27 min), Athenaeum Library in Maldon (28 min), Maldon Swimming Pool in Maldon (28 min) and Kangaroo Flat Library in Kangaroo Flat (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Woodstock West.
