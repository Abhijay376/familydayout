Neuarpurr is in the West Wimmera area, and our data lists 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Unnamed (No.HA1436) Heritage Agreement is the largest mapped park in Neuarpurr, at about 91 hectares. Unnamed (No.HA1303) Heritage Agreement and Neuarpur Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Neuarpurr also has Neuarpurr Former Hall Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Neuarpurr. The closest to Neuarpurr is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 177 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Neuarpurr, so most trips start by car. By car from Neuarpurr, Horsham is about 87 minutes away and Hamilton about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Neuarpurr is Goroke Swimming Centre in Goroke, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest library to Neuarpurr is West Wimmera Shire Goroke Library in Goroke, about 31 minutes' drive. From Neuarpurr, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at War Memorial Park in Apsley (26 min), Goroke Swimming Centre in Goroke (30 min), Goroke Little Desert Nature Playspace in Goroke (30 min) and West Wimmera Shire Goroke Library in Goroke (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Neuarpurr.
