Camperdown Skate Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Camperdown · Basic info

Camperdown Skate Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Camperdown, in the Corangamite Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Brooke St/Leura St (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 938 m away.
