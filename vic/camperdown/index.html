Camperdown in the Corangamite area has 3 playgrounds and 5 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, Camperdown has a skate park.

Landmarks

Lake Bullen Merri Lake Reserve is the largest mapped park in Camperdown, at about 508 hectares. Lake Gnotuk Lake Reserve and Lake Bullen Merri Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Camperdown include Apex Park and Camperdown Showgrounds. Playgrounds in Camperdown include the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve. Our data shows 14 public toilets and 2 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Camperdown. For older kids in Camperdown there's Camperdown Skate Park. The Camperdown to Timboon Rail Trail and Panorama Trail all pass through Camperdown. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Camperdown for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Camperdown has a train station: Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line: Warrnambool - Melbourne via Colac & Geelong). Buses on the Camperdown - Timboon via Cobden route stop in Camperdown. V/Line coaches stop in Camperdown. By car from Camperdown, Colac is about 37 minutes away, Warrnambool about 58 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Camperdown is Lismore Pool in Lismore, about 33 minutes' drive. For a library, Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake is about 30 minutes' drive from Camperdown. For a cinema, Colac Cinemas in Colac is about 38 minutes' drive from Camperdown. For an indoor play centre, Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac is about 38 minutes' drive from Camperdown. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 37 minutes from Camperdown by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play.

Best for

Younger kids at Camperdown's 3 local playgrounds. Teens who skate or ride, with Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown. Free days on the Camperdown to Timboon Rail Trail and in Camperdown's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Weerite, next to Camperdown, has 1 playground. Next door to Camperdown, Cobden has 1 playground. Naroghid, Koallah, Gnotuk and Chocolyn also border Camperdown.
