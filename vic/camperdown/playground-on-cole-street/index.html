Playground on Cole Street

Playground in Camperdown · Official details

This is a public playground in Camperdown. The Camperdown to Timboon Rail Trail passes about 80 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 260 m away, and coaches stop at Camperdown Station/Longmore St , a 3-minute walk away.
