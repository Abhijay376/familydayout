Waitchie is in the Swan Hill area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Waitchie is Lake Timboram Flora and Fauna Reserve, covering about 2,454 hectares. Wewin Bushland Reserve and Waitchie Flora and Fauna Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Waitchie. The closest to Waitchie is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 42 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Waitchie, so most trips start by car. By car from Waitchie, Swan Hill is about 34 minutes away and Mildura about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake is about 38 minutes' drive from Waitchie. For a library, Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill is about 35 minutes' drive from Waitchie. For a skate park, Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South is about 32 minutes' drive from Waitchie. The closest cinema to Waitchie is Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill, about 34 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 34 minutes from Waitchie by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Waitchie are Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake (38 min), the playground at Apex Park in Sea Lake (38 min), Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock (49 min), Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South (32 min), the playground at Jaycee Park in Swan Hill (35 min) and Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Waitchie.

Nearby suburbs

Polisbet, Nowie, Gowanford and Chinangin also border Waitchie.
