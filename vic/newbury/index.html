Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Newbury, in the Hepburn area.

Landmarks

Newbury Recreation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Newbury, at about 3 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Newbury. The closest to Newbury is Ballan Railway Station (V/Line), about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Newbury, so most trips start by car. By car from Newbury, Kyneton is about 30 minutes away, Daylesford about 31 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 82 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford is about 30 minutes' drive from Newbury. The closest library to Newbury is Kyneton Library in Kyneton, about 30 minutes' drive. For a beach, Canoeing in Tylden is about 24 minutes' drive from Newbury. For a skate park, Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton is about 28 minutes' drive from Newbury. Kyneton is the closest bigger centre to Newbury, about 30 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds and a library. From Newbury, the nearest places for kids by road are Little Gallery in Trentham (6 min), the playground at Quarry Street Reserve in Trentham (7 min), the playground at J P Gleeson Memorial Park in Lyonville (16 min), Canoeing in Tylden (24 min), Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton (28 min) and Kyneton Library in Kyneton (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Newbury.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Newbury, Trentham has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Newbury, Lyonville has 1 playground. Blackwood also borders Newbury.
