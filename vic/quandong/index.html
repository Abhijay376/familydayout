Quandong is on the north west edge of Wyndham. Our data has no playgrounds, parks, cafes or bus stops inside it, so this guide is about the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station or bus route in Quandong. The closest station is Wyndham Vale, on the V/Line Geelong line, about 8 kilometres from the suburb's centre point in a straight line. Little River Station, on the same line, is about 13 kilometres away. By car, Werribee is about 27 minutes away, Hoppers Crossing about 31 and the Melbourne CBD about 58 minutes, without traffic. Geelong is about an hour.

Nearby places for kids

The closest playgrounds by road are in Mambourin and Manor Lakes, about 20 to 22 minutes' drive. They include the playgrounds on Lecture Circuit, at Shillings Road Park and at Barrabool Boulevard Park in Mambourin, and Rondo Drive and Balcombe Drive playgrounds in Manor Lakes. The Manor Lakes branch of Wyndham Library Service is about 23 minutes away, and so is a larger outing at Presidents Park in Wyndham Vale, with its district playground and skate park, about 24 minutes. Werribee Outdoor Pool is about 28 minutes away, and Werribee Open Range Zoo about 36.

Best for

Families happy to drive, who can combine a playground stop in Manor Lakes or Mambourin with the library, the pool or the zoo further east.

Nearby suburbs

Manor Lakes is the closest suburb with a library, a shopping centre and a train station. Mambourin has several playgrounds close to its border. Wyndham Vale has Presidents Park. Eynesbury, Little River, Balliang and Balliang East also border Quandong.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Quandong, without traffic.
