Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Combienbar, in the East Gippsland area.

Landmarks

Errinundra River - East Branch Natural Catchment Area is the largest mapped park in Combienbar, at about 2,691 hectares. Golden Gully Machinery Site Historic Reserve is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Combienbar include Three Sisters Lookout. Picnic areas in Combienbar include Three Sisters Lookout.

Getting around

There's no train station in Combienbar. The closest to Combienbar is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 132 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Combienbar, so most trips start by car. By car from Combienbar, Lakes Entrance is about 2 hours away and Bairnsdale about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Best for
