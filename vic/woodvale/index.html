Woodvale in the Greater Bendigo area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Nerring I150 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Woodvale, at about 5 hectares. Sydney Flat Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Woodvale.

Getting around

Woodvale has no train station; Eaglehawk Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 7.6 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Woodvale, so most trips start by car. By car from Woodvale, Bendigo is about 17 minutes away, Castlemaine about 45 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Peter Krenz Leisure Centre in Eaglehawk is about 9 minutes' drive from Woodvale. The closest library to Woodvale is Bendigo Library in Bendigo, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Woodvale is McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Woodvale is Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Woodvale is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Woodvale is The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo, about 20 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 17 minutes from Woodvale by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Woodvale, the nearest places for kids by road are McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk (10 min), Peter Krenz Leisure Centre in Eaglehawk (9 min), Eaglehawk Playspace in Eaglehawk (10 min), the playground at Canterbury Park in Eaglehawk (10 min), the bmx track on Victoria Street in Eaglehawk (11 min) and Sloan Swim School in California Gully (11 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Woodvale.

Nearby suburbs

Myers Flat, Leichardt, Eaglehawk North and Campbells Forest also border Woodvale.
