Raymond Island Gippsland Lakes Reserve

Park in Raymond Island · Official details

Raymond Island Gippsland Lakes Reserve is a park in Raymond Island, in the East Gippsland Shire Council area, good for all ages.

Details

Seasons
Bike hire on the island during school holidays; ferry from Paynesville (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
Free ferry for foot passengers and bikes; small fee for cars; Koala Trail starts near the ferry arrival point (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Raymond Island Gippsland Lakes Reserve?
Raymond Island Gippsland Lakes Reserve is in Raymond Island, Victoria.
Is Raymond Island Gippsland Lakes Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
