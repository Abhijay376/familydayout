Playground on Seventh Parade

Playground in Raymond Island · Basic info

Playground on Seventh Parade in Raymond Island is a public playground in the East Gippsland Shire area. The Koala Trail is about 10 m away, and Paynesville Library is about 360 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 80 m from the playground, and coaches stop at Raymond Island Ferry Terminal/Devon Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
