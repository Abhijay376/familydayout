Oakleigh Library (City Of Monash)

Library in Oakleigh · Basic info

Oakleigh Library (City Of Monash) is a library in Oakleigh, in the Monash City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Oakleigh SC/Atherton Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 14 m away with a baby change table.
