Scotchman's Creek Linear Park

Park in Oakleigh · Basic info

Scotchman's Creek Linear Park is a park in Oakleigh, in the Monash City Council area, good for all ages. Allen St/Warrigal Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 225 m away.
