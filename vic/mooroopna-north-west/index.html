Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Mooroopna North West in the Greater Shepparton area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mooroopna North West. The closest to Mooroopna North West is Mooroopna Railway Station (V/Line), about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mooroopna North West, so most trips start by car. By car from Mooroopna North West, Shepparton is about 22 minutes away, Echuca about 53 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mooroopna North West is Community Swimming Pool in Merrigum, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest library to Mooroopna North West is Tatura Library in Tatura, about 11 minutes' drive. For a beach, Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton is about 24 minutes' drive from Mooroopna North West. For a skate park, the skate park at Tatura Memorial Park in Tatura is about 12 minutes' drive from Mooroopna North West. The closest cinema to Mooroopna North West is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Mooroopna North West is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 22 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kidstown in Mooroopna is about 20 minutes' drive from Mooroopna North West. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 22 minutes from Mooroopna North West by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Mooroopna North West, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Craigmuir Lake Park in Mooroopna (12 min), the playground at Northlinks Reserve in Tatura (11 min), Tatura Irrigation and Wartime Camps Museum in Tatura (10 min), Tatura Library in Tatura (11 min), Community Swimming Pool in Merrigum (17 min) and the skate park at Tatura Memorial Park in Tatura (12 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mooroopna North West.

Nearby suburbs

Tatura, next to Mooroopna North West, has 8 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Mooroopna North West, Undera has 1 playground. Ardmona also borders Mooroopna North West.
