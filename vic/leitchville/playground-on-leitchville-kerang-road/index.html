Playground on Leitchville-Kerang Road

Playground in Leitchville · Basic info

This is a public playground in Leitchville. Leitchville Memorial Swimming Pool is about 330 m away, and Playground on Keath Street is another option about 280 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus Kerang - Echuca via Cohuna stops at Swimming Pool/Leitchville Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
