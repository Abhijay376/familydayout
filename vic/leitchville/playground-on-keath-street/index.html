Playground on Keath Street

Playground in Leitchville · Basic info

Playground on Keath Street is a public playground in Leitchville, in the Gannawarra Shire area. Leitchville Memorial Swimming Pool is about 100 m away, and Playground on Leitchville-Kerang Road is another option about 280 m away. There's a public toilet about 160 m away, and coaches stop at King Albert Ave/Leitchville Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
