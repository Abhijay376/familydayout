Playground on Kitchener Street

Playground in Leitchville · Basic info

Playground on Kitchener Street in Leitchville is a public playground in the Gannawarra Shire area. Leitchville Memorial Swimming Pool is about 360 m away, and Playground on Leitchville South Road is another option about 330 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 380 m from the playground, and bus Kerang - Echuca via Cohuna stops at Swimming Pool/Leitchville Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
