Leitchville in the Gannawarra area has 4 playgrounds and 1 park or reserve in our data. Leitchville also has a pool.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Leitchville is Leitchville Bushland Reserve, covering about 9 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Leitchville. In Leitchville you'll also find Leitchville Memorial Swimming Pool.

Getting around

Leitchville has no train station; Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Kerang - Echuca via Cohuna route stop in Leitchville. V/Line coaches stop in Leitchville. By car from Leitchville, Echuca is about 42 minutes away and Swan Hill about 87 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Leitchville is Barham Branch Library in Koondrook, about 41 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Leitchville is Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna, about 14 minutes' drive. Echuca is the closest bigger centre to Leitchville, about 42 minutes away by car, and has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Leitchville's 4 local playgrounds.

Nearby suburbs

Pyramid Hill, next to Leitchville, has 1 playground. Horfield, Gunbower and Burkes Bridge also border Leitchville.
