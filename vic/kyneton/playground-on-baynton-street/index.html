Playground on Baynton Street

Playground in Kyneton · Basic info

Playground on Baynton Street is a public playground in Kyneton, in the Macedon Ranges Shire area. Kyneton Library is about 30 m away, and Sideshow is about 260 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and buses 1, 3 and 4 stop at High St/Mollison St, a 2-minute walk away.
