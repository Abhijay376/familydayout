Kyneton Museum

Museum or gallery in Kyneton · Official details

Kyneton Museum is a museum or gallery in Kyneton, in the Macedon Ranges Shire Council area, best for school-age kids. St Paul's Church/Powlett St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 566 m away. Opening hours: Open Friday to Sunday: 11am to 4pm; Saturday, 03 October 2026 | 11:00 AM.
