Bolton Reserve Lane Playground

Playground in Kyneton · Basic info

Bolton Reserve Lane Playground is a public playground in Kyneton, in the Macedon Ranges Shire area. Playground on Chisel Avenue is another option about 420 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 840 m from the playground, and bus 2 stops at Penzance Ct/Campaspe Dr, a 5-minute walk away.
