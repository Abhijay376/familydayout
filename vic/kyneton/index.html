Kyneton is in the Macedon Ranges area, and our data lists 10 playgrounds and 11 parks and reserves in the suburb. On top of that, Kyneton has a library, a museum or gallery and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Kyneton is Bald Hill Reserve, covering about 99 hectares. Langley I12 Bushland Reserve and Barkley Square are the next largest. Kyneton also has Hurry Reserve, Bolton Reserve and Quarry Reserve. Playgrounds in Kyneton include Jean Haynes Playground, Kyneton Community Park and Bolton Reserve Lane Playground. Our data shows 17 public toilets and 4 off-leash dog parks in Kyneton. In Kyneton you'll also find Kyneton Museum and Kyneton Library. For older kids in Kyneton there's Kyneton Velodrome. For walks and rides, Campaspe River Walk and Ridge Track cross Kyneton. Our data lists 3 cafes and family restaurants in Kyneton for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Kyneton Railway Station (V/Line: Bendigo - Melbourne via Gisborne, Echuca/Moama - Melbourne via Bendigo or Heathcote and Swan Hill - Melbourne via Bendigo) in Kyneton. Buses on the 1, 2, 3 and 4 routes stop in Kyneton. V/Line coaches stop in Kyneton. By car from Kyneton, Castlemaine is about 31 minutes away, Daylesford about 37 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 71 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Chewton Swimming Pool in Chewton is about 27 minutes' drive from Kyneton. For a beach, Canoeing in Tylden is about 13 minutes' drive from Kyneton.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 10 playgrounds across Kyneton. Rainy days at the library and museum or gallery in Kyneton. Teens who skate or ride, with Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton. Free days on the Campaspe River Walk and in Kyneton's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Kyneton, Malmsbury has 1 playground. Lauriston, next to Kyneton, has 1 playground. Pipers Creek, Pastoria, Langley and Kyneton South also border Kyneton.
