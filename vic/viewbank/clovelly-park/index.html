Clovelly Park

Park in Viewbank · Basic info

Clovelly Park is a park in Viewbank, in the Banyule City Council area, good for all ages. Grantham Rd/Lower Plenty Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 726 m away.
