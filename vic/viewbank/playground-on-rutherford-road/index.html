Playground on Rutherford Road

Playground in Viewbank · Basic info

Playground on Rutherford Road is a public playground in Viewbank, in the Banyule City area. The Plenty River Trail passes about 280 m away, and Playground at Radio Park is another option about 400 m away. There's a public toilet about 150 m away, and bus 517 stops at Moran St/Rutherford Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
