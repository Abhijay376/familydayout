Our data lists 4 playgrounds and 15 parks and reserves in St Arnaud, in the Northern Grampians area. St Arnaud also has a pool.

Landmarks

St Arnaud Regional Park is the largest mapped park in St Arnaud, at about 925 hectares. Hard Hills Nature Conservation Reserve and St Arnaud Bush Park are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in St Arnaud include Bell Rock, Wax Gardens and Pioneer Park. Playgrounds around St Arnaud include Apex Park Playground, Bi-Centennial Park Playground and Market Square Park Playground. Our data shows 9 public toilets and 2 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in St Arnaud. Picnic areas in St Arnaud include The Basin Picnic Area. In St Arnaud you'll also find St Arnaud Swimming Pool. For walks and rides, Moolerr Nature Drive, Pipe Gully Walk and Short Basin Walk cross St Arnaud.

Getting around

St Arnaud has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 65 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the St Arnaud - Stawell via Ararat and St Arnaud - Stawell via Marnoo routes stop in St Arnaud. V/Line coaches stop in St Arnaud. By car from St Arnaud, Stawell is about 63 minutes away and Maryborough about 66 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a skate park, Donald BMX Track in Donald is about 33 minutes' drive from St Arnaud. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 33 minutes' drive from St Arnaud.

Best for

Younger kids at St Arnaud's 4 local playgrounds. Free days on the Moolerr Nature Drive and in St Arnaud's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Moolerr, Carapooee West and Carapooee also border St Arnaud.
