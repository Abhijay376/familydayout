Bi-Centennial Park Playground

Playground in St Arnaud · Official details

Bi-Centennial Park Playground is a public playground in St Arnaud, in the Northern Grampians Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet about 670 m away, and coaches stop at Mobil Roadhouse/Sunraysia Hwy, a 2-minute walk away.
