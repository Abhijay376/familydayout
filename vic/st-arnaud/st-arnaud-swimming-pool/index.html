St. Arnaud Swimming Pool

Pool or water play in St Arnaud · Basic info

St. Arnaud Swimming Pool is a pool or water play in St Arnaud, in the Northern Grampians Shire Council area, good for all ages. Dundas St/Market St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 315 m away.
