King George Park

Park in St Arnaud · Basic info

King George Park is a park in St Arnaud, in the Northern Grampians Shire Council area, good for all ages. Mobil Roadhouse/Sunraysia Hwy (coach) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 44 m away.
