Our data lists 12 playgrounds and 15 parks and reserves in Pascoe Vale, in the Merri-Bek area. Pascoe Vale also has 2 pools and a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Pascoe Vale is Cole Reserve, covering about 6 hectares. K.W. Joyce Reserve and Gavin Park are the next largest. Pascoe Vale also has Tate Park, James Reserve and Hallam Reserve. Playgrounds in Pascoe Vale include Lyking Street Reserve, the playground at Tate Park and the playground at Cole Reserve. Our data shows 6 public toilets in Pascoe Vale. In Pascoe Vale you'll also find Brushworks Art Studio, Elite Swimming and Pascoe Vale Swimming Pool. For walks and rides, Glenroy link to Moonee Ponds Creek Trail and Merlynston to Pascoe Value East-West Route cross Pascoe Vale. Our data lists 17 cafes and family restaurants in Pascoe Vale for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Pascoe Vale Railway Station (metro: Craigieburn Line) in Pascoe Vale. Buses 513, 514, 527, 534, 542, 561 and 951 stop in Pascoe Vale. By car from Pascoe Vale, Broadmeadows is about 10 minutes away, Sunshine about 17 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 18 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Pascoe Vale is Little free library in Coburg North, about 4 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Pascoe Vale is Horse Arena in Abbotsford, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Pascoe Vale is Coburg Velodrome in Coburg North, about 4 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Pascoe Vale is Bofa Theatre in Strathmore, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Pascoe Vale is The Keys in Preston, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Pascoe Vale is Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville, about 13 minutes' drive.
