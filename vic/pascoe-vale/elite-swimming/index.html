Elite Swimming

Pool or water play in Pascoe Vale · Official details

Elite Swimming is a pool or water play in Pascoe Vale, in the Merri-Bek City Council area, good for all ages. Charles St/Sussex St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 470 m away.
