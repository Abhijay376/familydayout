Playground at James Reserve

Playground in Pascoe Vale · Basic info

Playground at James Reserve in Pascoe Vale is a public playground in the Merri-Bek City area. The Glenroy link to Moonee Ponds Creek Trail is about 360 m away, and Pascoe Vale Swimming Pool is about 220 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 480 m from the playground, and bus 561 stops at Ann St/Gaffney St, a 4-minute walk away.
