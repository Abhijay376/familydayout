Playground at Hallam Reserve

Playground in Pascoe Vale · Basic info

This is a public playground in Pascoe Vale. The Merlynston to Pascoe Value East-West Route is about 260 m away, and for food, Tarboosh is about 180 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 410 m from the playground, and buses 527 and 951 stop at Karadoc Ave/Derby St, a 3-minute walk away.
