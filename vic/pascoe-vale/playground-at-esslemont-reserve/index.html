Playground at Esslemont Reserve

Playground in Pascoe Vale · Basic info

Playground at Esslemont Reserve in Pascoe Vale is a public playground in the Merri-Bek City area. The Moonee Ponds Creek Trail is about 130 m away, and for food, Pizza Minded is about 240 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 420 m from the playground, and trains stop at Pascoe Vale Railway Station, a 5-minute walk away.
