H.M. Dowd Reserve

Park in Pascoe Vale · Basic info

H.M. Dowd Reserve is a park in Pascoe Vale, in the Merri-Bek City Council area, good for all ages. Perkin Ave/Rhodes Pde (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 859 m away.
