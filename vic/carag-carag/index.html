Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Carag Carag, in the Campaspe area.

Landmarks

Mansfield Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Carag Carag, at about 491 hectares. Wallenjoe Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Carag Carag. The closest to Carag Carag is Rochester Railway Station (V/Line), about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Carag Carag, so most trips start by car. By car from Carag Carag, Shepparton is about 44 minutes away, Echuca about 48 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Carag Carag is Rushworth Pool in Rushworth, about 24 minutes' drive. For a library, Rushworth Library in Rushworth is about 23 minutes' drive from Carag Carag. For a skate park, the skate park at Lions Park in Rushworth is about 23 minutes' drive from Carag Carag. The closest cinema to Carag Carag is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 44 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 44 minutes' drive from Carag Carag. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Carag Carag is Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram, about 29 minutes' drive. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Carag Carag, about 44 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Carag Carag are Rushworth Museum in Rushworth (23 min), Rushworth Library in Rushworth (23 min), Rushworth Pool in Rushworth (24 min), the skate park at Lions Park in Rushworth (23 min), Memorial Pool in Rochester (25 min) and the playground at Apex Park in Rochester (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Carag Carag.

Nearby suburbs

Rushworth, next to Carag Carag, has a pool, a library and a skate park. Burramboot also borders Carag Carag.
