Cardross is in the Mildura area, and our data lists 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Mildura I221 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Cardross, at about 12 hectares. Cardross Recreation Reserve and Mildura I223 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Cardross include Mildura I222 Bushland Reserve. For walks and rides, Former Morkalla-Redcliffs Railway crosses Cardross.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cardross. The closest to Cardross is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 175 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cardross, so most trips start by car. By car from Cardross, Mildura is about 24 minutes away and Swan Hill about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Irymple Swimming Pool in Irymple is about 17 minutes' drive from Cardross. For a library, A S Kenyon Library Red Cliffs in Red Cliffs is about 15 minutes' drive from Cardross. For a beach, Apex Beach in Mildura is about 33 minutes' drive from Cardross. For a skate park, Skatepark in Red Cliffs is about 14 minutes' drive from Cardross. For a cinema, Deakin Cinema Complex in Mildura is about 24 minutes' drive from Cardross. The closest indoor play centre to Cardross is Sunraysia Tenpin in Mildura, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Cardross is Putt Putt Family Fun Centre in Mildura, about 24 minutes' drive. Mildura is the closest bigger centre to Cardross, about 24 minutes away by car, and has 26 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Cardross are Skatepark in Red Cliffs (14 min), Woodbine Park Playground in Red Cliffs (14 min), the playground at Barclay Square in Red Cliffs (14 min), Red Cliffs Military Museum in Red Cliffs (15 min), Work Space Gallery 3496 in Red Cliffs (14 min) and A S Kenyon Library Red Cliffs in Red Cliffs (15 min).

Best for

Free days on the Former Morkalla-Redcliffs Railway and in Cardross's parks.
