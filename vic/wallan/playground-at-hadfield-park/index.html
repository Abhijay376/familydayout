Playground at Hadfield Park

Playground in Wallan · Basic info

Playground at Hadfield Park in Wallan is a public playground in the Mitchell Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 150 m away, and Wallan Library is about 250 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and coaches stop at Hadfield Park/High St, a 3-minute walk away.
