Danaher Reserve Playground

Playground in Wallan · Basic info

This is a public playground in Wallan. Wallan Library is about 460 m away, and Subway is about 410 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 370 m away, and buses Wallan Station - Wallan Central and Wallan Station via Springridge and Wallan Central (Link A) stop at Wellington Square SC/Queen St, a 7-minute walk away.
