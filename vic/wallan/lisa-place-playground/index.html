Lisa Place Playground

Playground in Wallan · Basic info

Lisa Place Playground is a public playground in Wallan, in the Mitchell Shire area. Playground on Queen Street is another option about 490 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.2 km from the playground, and buses Wallan Station - Wallan Central and Wallan Station via Springridge and Wallan Central (Link A) stop at Watson St/Dudley St, a 7-minute walk away.
