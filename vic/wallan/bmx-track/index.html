BMX Track

Skate, BMX or pump track in Wallan · Basic info

BMX Track is a skate, bmx or pump track in Wallan, in the Mitchell Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Wallan Community Centre/Windham St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 219 m away with a baby change table.
