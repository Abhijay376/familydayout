Wallan is in the Mitchell area, and our data lists 13 playgrounds and 13 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Wallan has a library and 2 skate parks.

Landmarks

Greenhill Reserve is the largest mapped park in Wallan, at about 35 hectares. Hadfield Park and Wallan Community Park are the next largest. Wallan also has Tharra Park, Lisa Reserve and Airfield Park. Playgrounds in Wallan include Lisa Place Playground, Roulston Way Playground and Danaher Reserve Playground. Our data shows 9 public toilets in Wallan. Places to visit in Wallan include Wallan Library. For older kids in Wallan there's BMX Track and Wallan Park Skate Park. Wellington Square Shopping Centre is the local shopping centre. Our data lists 17 cafes and family restaurants in Wallan for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Wallan has a train station: Wallan Railway Station (V/Line: Seymour - Melbourne via Broadmeadows and Shepparton - Melbourne via Seymour). Buses on the Wallan Station - Springridge, Wallan Station - Wallan Central, Wallan Station - Wallara Waters Shuttle (Link B) and Wallan Station via Springridge and Wallan Central (Link A) routes stop in Wallan. V/Line coaches stop in Wallan. By car from Wallan, Broadmeadows is about 32 minutes away, Seymour about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 51 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore is about 12 minutes' drive from Wallan. For a cinema, United Cinemas in Craigieburn is about 23 minutes' drive from Wallan. For an indoor play centre, Timezone in Craigieburn is about 23 minutes' drive from Wallan. For a zoo or wildlife park, Funfields in Whittlesea is about 21 minutes' drive from Wallan.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 13 playgrounds across Wallan. Rainy days at the library in Wallan. Teens who skate or ride, with BMX Track in Wallan.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Wallan, Beveridge has 4 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Wallan, Wandong has 1 playground and a skate park. Darraweit Guim and Bylands also border Wallan.
