Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Woorarra West in the South Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Woorarra West has no train station; Trafalgar Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 43 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Woorarra West, so most trips start by car. By car from Woorarra West, Leongatha is about 47 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 67 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Foster Outdoor Pool in Foster is about 26 minutes' drive from Woorarra West. For a library, Foster Library in Foster is about 25 minutes' drive from Woorarra West. For a beach, Toora Beach in Toora is about 41 minutes' drive from Woorarra West. The closest skate park to Woorarra West is the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora, about 34 minutes' drive. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Woorarra West, about 47 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Woorarra West, the nearest places for kids by road are Luminous Glass Studio in Foster (26 min), Stockyard Gallery in Foster (26 min), Foster Library in Foster (25 min), Foster Outdoor Pool in Foster (26 min), the playground at Stanley Street Playground in Toora (33 min) and Toora Pool in Toora (33 min).
