Sea Lake is in the Buloke area, and our data lists 2 playgrounds and 6 parks and reserves in the suburb. Sea Lake also has a pool.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Sea Lake is Balmers Tank Bushland Reserve, covering about 184 hectares. Burupga Bushland Reserve and Lions Park are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Sea Lake include Apex Park, Beer Garden and Sea Lake Travellers Rest. Playgrounds in Sea Lake include the playground at Apex Park. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Sea Lake. In Sea Lake you'll also find Sea Lake Swimming Pool.

Getting around

Sea Lake has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 67 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Mildura - Ouyen - Sea Lake and Swan Hill - Sea Lake via Ultima routes stop in Sea Lake. V/Line coaches stop in Sea Lake. By car from Sea Lake, Swan Hill is about 59 minutes away and Horsham about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Sea Lake, about 59 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Sea Lake, the nearest places for kids by road are Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock (17 min) and the playground at Woomelang Rec Reserve in Woomelang (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Sea Lake.

Nearby suburbs

Myall, Lake Tyrrell, Boigbeat and Banyan also border Sea Lake.
