Pigeon Bank Reserve

Park in Kangaroo Ground · Basic info

Pigeon Bank Reserve is a park in Kangaroo Ground, in the Nillumbik Shire Council area, good for all ages. Pigeon Bank Rd/Kangaroo Ground-Warrandyte Rd (bus) is a 15-minute walk away.
