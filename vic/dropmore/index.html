Dropmore in the Murrindindi area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Dropmore G45 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Dropmore, at about 35 hectares.

Getting around

Dropmore has no train station; Avenel Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dropmore, so most trips start by car. By car from Dropmore, Seymour is about 61 minutes away, Mansfield about 1.5 hours and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a skate park, BMX Track in Caveat is about 27 minutes' drive from Dropmore. From Dropmore, the nearest places for kids by road are BMX Track in Caveat (27 min), Yarck Recreation Reserve Playground in Yarck (55 min), Creightons Creek Recreation Reserve Playground in Creightons Creek (49 min), Seymour Skate Park in Seymour (60 min), Seymour Library in Seymour (60 min) and Seymour Swimming Centre in Seymour (62 min).

Best for
