Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside The Honeysuckles in the Wellington area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

The Honeysuckles has no train station; Sale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 32 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside The Honeysuckles, so most trips start by car. By car from The Honeysuckles, Sale is about 43 minutes away and Traralgon about 78 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Indoor pool in Sale is about 43 minutes' drive from The Honeysuckles. For a library, Sale Library in Sale is about 41 minutes' drive from The Honeysuckles. For a beach, 90 Mile Beach in Seaspray is about 4 minutes' drive from The Honeysuckles. The closest skate park to The Honeysuckles is Sale Skate Park in Sale, about 41 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to The Honeysuckles is Sale Cinemas in Sale, about 42 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Sale, about 43 minutes from The Honeysuckles by car, with 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From The Honeysuckles, the nearest places for kids by road are 90 Mile Beach in Seaspray (4 min), Glomar Beach in Glomar Beach (14 min), Golden Beach Community Centre Playground in Golden Beach (35 min), Historic Gunpowder Magazine in Sale (40 min), Sale Historical Museum in Sale (41 min) and Little McMillan Park Playground in Sale (41 min).
