Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Myers Flat, in the Greater Bendigo area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Myers Flat is Marong H71 Bushland Reserve, covering about 243 hectares. Marong H8A Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Myers Flat has no train station; Eaglehawk Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 3.9 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Myers Flat, so most trips start by car. By car from Myers Flat, Bendigo is about 13 minutes away, Castlemaine about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Peter Krenz Leisure Centre in Eaglehawk is about 5 minutes' drive from Myers Flat. The closest library to Myers Flat is Bendigo Library in Bendigo, about 12 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk is about 6 minutes' drive from Myers Flat. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 14 minutes' drive from Myers Flat. The closest indoor play centre to Myers Flat is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Myers Flat is The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo, about 15 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 13 minutes from Myers Flat by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Myers Flat, the nearest places for kids by road are Peter Krenz Leisure Centre in Eaglehawk (5 min), Eaglehawk Playspace in Eaglehawk (5 min), Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk (6 min), the playground at Canterbury Park in Eaglehawk (6 min), McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk (6 min) and Sloan Swim School in California Gully (7 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Myers Flat.

Nearby suburbs

Eaglehawk, next to Myers Flat, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Myers Flat, Maiden Gully has 1 playground. Marong and Leichardt also border Myers Flat.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Myers Flat, without traffic.
