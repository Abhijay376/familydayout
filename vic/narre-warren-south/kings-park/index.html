Kings Park

Park in Narre Warren South · Basic info

Kings Park is a park in Narre Warren South, in the Casey City Council area, good for all ages. Strathaird Dr/Sherwood Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 311 m away with a baby change table.
