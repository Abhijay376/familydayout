Narre Warren South in the Casey area has 18 playgrounds and 22 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, Narre Warren South has 2 pools.

Landmarks

Berwick Springs Parkland is the largest mapped park in Narre Warren South, at about 82 hectares. Strathaird Reserve and Casey Central Town Park are the next largest. Narre Warren South also has Walkway, Duck Pond and Kings Park. Playgrounds in Narre Warren South include Oak Grove Playground, Sherwood Park Playground and Lake View Drive Playground. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Narre Warren South. Places to visit in Narre Warren South include King's Swimming and Paul Sadler Swimland. Casey Central is the local shopping centre. For walks and rides, Hallam Valley Trail, Berwick Town Creek Trail and River Gum Creek Trail cross Narre Warren South. Our data lists 8 cafes and family restaurants in Narre Warren South for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Narre Warren South. The closest to Narre Warren South is Narre Warren Railway Station (metro), about 2.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 835, 841, 847, 888, 889, 892, 894, 895, 899 and 981 stop in Narre Warren South. By car from Narre Warren South, Cranbourne is about 9 minutes away, Dandenong about 14 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 37 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Narre Warren South is Narre Warren Library in Narre Warren, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Narre Warren South is Carrum Beach in Carrum, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Narre Warren South is Narre Warren Skatepark in Narre Warren, about 3 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Narre Warren South is Village Cinemas in Narre Warren, about 5 minutes' drive.
