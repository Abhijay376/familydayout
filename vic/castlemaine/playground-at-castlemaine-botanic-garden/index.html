Playground at Castlemaine Botanic Garden

Playground in Castlemaine · Basic info

Playground at Castlemaine Botanic Garden is a public playground in Castlemaine, in the Mount Alexander Shire area. Castlemaine Swimming Pool is about 500 m away, and Botanical Gardens Playspace is another option about 10 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 180 m from the playground, and bus 2 stops at Castlemaine Hospital/Cornish St, a 10-minute walk away.
