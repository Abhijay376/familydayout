Bike Jumps

Skate, BMX or pump track in Castlemaine · Basic info

Bike Jumps is a skate, bmx or pump track in Castlemaine, in the Mount Alexander Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Greenhill Ave/Wheeler St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 301 m away with a baby change table.
