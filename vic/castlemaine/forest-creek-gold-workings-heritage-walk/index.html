Forest Creek Gold Workings Heritage Walk

Park in Castlemaine · Official details

Forest Creek Gold Workings Heritage Walk is a park in Castlemaine, in the Mount Alexander Shire Council area, good for all ages. Van Heurck St/Duke St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 23 m away and dogs are allowed on a lead. Opening hours: opening_hours: 24/7.
