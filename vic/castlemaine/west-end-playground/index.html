West End Playground

Park in Castlemaine · Basic info

West End Playground is a park in Castlemaine, in the Mount Alexander Shire Council area, good for all ages. Castlemaine Railway Station (vline) is a 12-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 934 m away.
