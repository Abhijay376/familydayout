Castlemaine Velodrome

Skate, BMX or pump track in Castlemaine · Basic info

Castlemaine Velodrome is a skate, bmx or pump track in Castlemaine, in the Mount Alexander Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Railway Ave/Duke St (bus) is a 10-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1444 m away.
