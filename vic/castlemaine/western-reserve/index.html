Western Reserve

Park in Castlemaine · Basic info

Western Reserve is a park in Castlemaine, in the Mount Alexander Shire Council area, good for all ages. Greenhill Ave/Wheeler St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 360 m away with a baby change table.
