Beehive Mine Reserve

Park in Maldon · Official details

Beehive Mine Reserve is a park in Maldon, in the Mount Alexander Shire Council area, good for all ages. Francis St/High St (coach) is a 5-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 473 m away and dogs are allowed on a lead.

Questions parents ask

Where is Beehive Mine Reserve?
Beehive Mine Reserve is in Maldon, Victoria.
Is Beehive Mine Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Beehive Mine Reserve?
Yes, on a lead.
Are there toilets at Beehive Mine Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 473 m away.
How do I get to Beehive Mine Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Francis St/High St (routes Mildura - Ballarat via Swan Hill & Bendigo), a 5-minute walk.
