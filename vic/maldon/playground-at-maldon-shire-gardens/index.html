Playground at Maldon Shire Gardens

Playground in Maldon · Basic info

Playground at Maldon Shire Gardens is a public playground in Maldon, in the Mount Alexander Shire area. The Castlemaine to Maldon Trail is about 370 m away, and Athenaeum Library is about 100 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus Maryborough - Bendigo stops at Maldon Post Office/High St, a 2-minute walk away.
