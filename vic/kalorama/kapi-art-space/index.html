KAPI Art Space, Kalorama

Museum or gallery in Kalorama · Official details

KAPI Art Space is a museum or gallery in Kalorama, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, best for school-age kids. Kalorama Park Lookout/Mt Dandenong Tourist Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 424 m away. Opening hours: opening_hours: Th-Sa 11:00-17:00.
