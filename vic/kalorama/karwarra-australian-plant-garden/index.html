Karwarra Australian Plant Garden

Park in Kalorama · Basic info

Karwarra Australian Plant Garden is a park in Kalorama, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Kalorama Oval/Mt Dandenong Tourist Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 269 m away with a baby change table.
