Getting around

There's no train station in Wulgulmerang East. The closest to Wulgulmerang East is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 99 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wulgulmerang East, so most trips start by car. By car from Wulgulmerang East, Lakes Entrance is about 2 hours away and Bairnsdale about 2 hours, without traffic.
