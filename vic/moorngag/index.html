Moorngag in the Benalla area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Moorngag I66 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Moorngag, at about 75 hectares. Moorngag I65 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Moorngag. The closest to Moorngag is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Moorngag, so most trips start by car. By car from Moorngag, Benalla is about 28 minutes away, Mansfield about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Moorngag is Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla, about 28 minutes' drive. For a library, Mansfield Library in Mansfield is about 39 minutes' drive from Moorngag. For a skate park, Pump Track in Benalla is about 29 minutes' drive from Moorngag. The closest cinema to Moorngag is Swanpool Cinema in Swanpool, about 8 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Benalla, about 28 minutes from Moorngag by car, with 5 playgrounds and a pool. From Moorngag, the nearest places for kids by road are Swanpool Cinema in Swanpool (8 min), the playground at Waminda Park in Benalla (25 min), the playground at Benalla Botanical Gardens in Benalla (27 min), Benalla Art Gallery in Benalla (27 min), Benalla Costume and Pioneer Museum in Benalla (28 min) and Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (28 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Moorngag.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Moorngag, Benalla has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Swanpool, next to Moorngag, has a cinema.
