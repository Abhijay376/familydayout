Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Doctors Flat in the East Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Doctors Flat has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 59 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Doctors Flat, so most trips start by car. By car from Doctors Flat, Bairnsdale is about 81 minutes away and Lakes Entrance about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek is about 6 minutes' drive from Doctors Flat. The closest skate park to Doctors Flat is the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo, about 28 minutes' drive. From Doctors Flat, the nearest places for kids by road are Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek (6 min), Great Alpine Gallery in Swifts Creek (6 min), Petersens Art Gallery & Museum in Omeo (27 min), the playground at Omeo Historical Park & Museum in Omeo (28 min) and the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo (28 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Doctors Flat.

Nearby suburbs

Swifts Creek, next to Doctors Flat, has 1 playground and a pool. Next door to Doctors Flat, Ensay has 1 playground.
