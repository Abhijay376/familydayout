Panmure in the Moyne area has 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Panmure is Lake Munderong Lake Reserve, covering about 14 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Panmure.

Getting around

Panmure has no train station; Sherwood Park Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Panmure, so most trips start by car. By car from Panmure, Warrnambool is about 22 minutes away and Colac about 72 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool is about 25 minutes' drive from Panmure. For a library, Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool is about 24 minutes' drive from Panmure. For a beach, Buttress Point in The Cove is about 29 minutes' drive from Panmure. The closest skate park to Panmure is Warrnambool BMX Track in Warrnambool, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Panmure is Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool, about 23 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, The Bowl in Warrnambool is about 23 minutes' drive from Panmure. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Panmure is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 9 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 22 minutes from Panmure by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Panmure, the nearest places for kids by road are Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (9 min), Grieve Street Playground in Warrnambool (20 min), the playground at Bushfield Recreation Reserve in Bushfield (24 min), SCOPE Galleries in Warrnambool (22 min), Buttress Point in The Cove (29 min) and Flaxman Beach in Nullawarre (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Panmure.
