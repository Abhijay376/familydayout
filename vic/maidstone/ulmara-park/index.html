Ulmara Park

Park in Maidstone · Basic info

Ulmara Park is a park in Maidstone, in the Maribyrnong City Council area, good for all ages. Emu Rd/Hampstead Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1893 m away.
