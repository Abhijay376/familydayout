Shepherd Gardens

Park in Maidstone · Basic info

Shepherd Gardens is a park in Maidstone, in the Maribyrnong City Council area, good for all ages. Palmerston St/Essex St (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1012 m away with a baby change table.
