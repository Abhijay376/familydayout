Scovell Reserve

Park in Maidstone · Official details

Scovell Reserve is a park in Maidstone, in the Maribyrnong City Council area, good for all ages. Havelock St/Mitchell St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and dogs are allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Scovell Reserve?
Scovell Reserve is in Maidstone, Victoria.
Is Scovell Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Scovell Reserve?
Yes, dogs are allowed.
How do I get to Scovell Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Havelock St/Mitchell St (routes 406), a 4-minute walk.
