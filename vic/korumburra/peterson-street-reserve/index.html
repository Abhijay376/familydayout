Peterson Street Reserve

Park in Korumburra · Basic info

Peterson Street Reserve is a park in Korumburra, in the South Gippsland Shire Council area, good for all ages. Carinya Lodge/Carinya Cres (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 969 m away.
