Korumburra is in the South Gippsland area, and our data lists 3 playgrounds and 4 parks and reserves in the suburb. Korumburra also has a pool and a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

Coal Creek Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Korumburra, at about 18 hectares. Coal Creek Historic and Cultural Features Reserve and Coleman Park are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Korumburra include Peterson Street Reserve. Playgrounds in Korumburra include the playground at Coleman Park, the playground at Peterson Street Reserve and the playground at Coal Creek Historic and Cultural Features Reserve. Our data shows 6 public toilets in Korumburra. Places to visit in Korumburra include Coal Creek Community Park & Museum and Korumburra Outdoor Pool. For walks and rides, Great Southern Rail Trail crosses Korumburra. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Korumburra for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Korumburra. The closest to Korumburra is Warragul Railway Station (V/Line), about 31 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Korumburra Town Service - Carinya Lodge route stop in Korumburra. V/Line coaches stop in Korumburra. By car from Korumburra, Leongatha is about 15 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 35 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Leongatha Library in Leongatha is about 14 minutes' drive from Korumburra. The closest beach to Korumburra is The Caves in Inverloch, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Korumburra is Leongatha Skate Park in Leongatha, about 15 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha is about 14 minutes' drive from Korumburra. The closest indoor play centre to Korumburra is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 41 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville is about 38 minutes' drive from Korumburra. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Korumburra, about 15 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Korumburra's 3 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Korumburra. Free days on the Great Southern Rail Trail and in Korumburra's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Korumburra, Poowong has a pool and a library. Loch, next to Korumburra, has 1 playground. Bena, next to Korumburra, has 1 playground. Ranceby, Poowong East, Outtrim and Korumburra South also border Korumburra.
