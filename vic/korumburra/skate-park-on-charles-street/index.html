Skate park on Charles Street

Skate, BMX or pump track in Korumburra · Basic info

Skate park on Charles Street is a skate, bmx or pump track in Korumburra, in the South Gippsland Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Carinya Lodge/Carinya Cres (bus) is a 18-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 212 m away.
