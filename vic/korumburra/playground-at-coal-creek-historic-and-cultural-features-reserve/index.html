Playground at Coal Creek Historic and Cultural Features Reserve

Playground in Korumburra · Basic info

Playground at Coal Creek Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Korumburra is a public playground in the South Gippsland Shire area. Coal Creek Community Park & Museum is about 420 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 60 m from the playground, and coaches stop at Coal Creek Museum/South Gippsland Hwy, a 1-minute walk away.
