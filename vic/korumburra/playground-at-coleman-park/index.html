Playground at Coleman Park

Playground in Korumburra · Basic info

Playground at Coleman Park is a public playground in Korumburra, in the South Gippsland Shire area. Korumburra Outdoor Pool is about 70 m away, and Divas Tandoori Indian Restaurant is about 420 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 140 m away. It's easiest by car, about 2 minutes from Korumburra.
