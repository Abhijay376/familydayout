Landmarks

Dumbalk also has Dumbalk Memorial Park. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Dumbalk.

Getting around

Dumbalk has no train station; Trafalgar Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 36 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dumbalk, so most trips start by car. By car from Dumbalk, Leongatha is about 22 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 45 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Dumbalk is South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest library to Dumbalk is Leongatha Library in Leongatha, about 21 minutes' drive. For a beach, Toora Beach in Toora is about 39 minutes' drive from Dumbalk. The closest skate park to Dumbalk is BMX track in Leongatha, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Dumbalk is Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha, about 21 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Leongatha, about 22 minutes from Dumbalk by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Dumbalk, the nearest places for kids by road are BMX track in Leongatha (20 min), Leongatha Library in Leongatha (21 min), Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North (21 min), Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha (21 min), the playground at Baromi Park in Mirboo North (21 min) and South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha (21 min).
