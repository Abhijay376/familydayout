Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Dunneworthy in the Ararat area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Dunneworthy. The closest to Dunneworthy is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dunneworthy, so most trips start by car. By car from Dunneworthy, Ararat is about 14 minutes away, Stawell about 37 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Dunneworthy is Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat, about 14 minutes' drive. For a library, Learning Resource Centre in Ararat is about 18 minutes' drive from Dunneworthy. For a skate park, David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat is about 15 minutes' drive from Dunneworthy. The closest cinema to Dunneworthy is Astor Cinema in Ararat, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Dunneworthy is Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell, about 38 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Ararat, about 14 minutes from Dunneworthy by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Dunneworthy, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Smith Street Reserve in Ararat (13 min), David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat (15 min), Ararat Gallery TAMA in Ararat (14 min), Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat (14 min), Langi Morgala Museum in Ararat (14 min) and Learning Resource Centre in Ararat (18 min).
