Yendon is in the Moorabool area, and our data lists 1 playground in the suburb.

Landmarks

Playgrounds around Yendon include the playground at Yendon Recreation Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Yendon. The closest to Yendon is Ballarat Railway Station (V/Line), about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Yendon. By car from Yendon, Ballarat is about 19 minutes away, Bacchus Marsh about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Yendon is Buninyong Swimming Pool in Buninyong, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest library to Yendon is L Building: E J Barker Library in Mount Helen, about 9 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the bmx track at Buninyong Recreation Park in Buninyong is about 8 minutes' drive from Yendon. The closest cinema to Yendon is Victoria Theatre in Golden Point, about 17 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 17 minutes' drive from Yendon. For a zoo or wildlife park, Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East is about 17 minutes' drive from Yendon. The nearest larger centre is Ballarat, about 19 minutes from Yendon by car, with 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Yendon are the playground at Navigators Community Centre in Navigators (9 min), Buninyong Swimming Pool in Buninyong (8 min), the bmx track at Buninyong Recreation Park in Buninyong (8 min), L Building: E J Barker Library in Mount Helen (9 min), Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East (17 min) and Eureka Swimming Complex in Eureka (17 min).

Best for

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Yendon, Buninyong has 6 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Yendon, Navigators has 1 playground. Millbrook, next to Yendon, has 1 playground. Next door to Yendon, Lal Lal has 1 playground. Dunnstown, Scotsburn and Clarendon also border Yendon.
