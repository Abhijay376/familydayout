Playground on Melville Street

Playground in Numurkah · Basic info

Playground on Melville Street in Numurkah is a public playground in the Moira Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 80 m away, and Numurkah Library is about 590 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 240 m from the playground, and coaches stop at Quinn St/Mieklejohn St, a 9-minute walk away.
