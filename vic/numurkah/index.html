Numurkah is in the Moira area, and our data lists 1 playground and 5 parks and reserves in the suburb. On top of that, Numurkah has a library, a pool and a skate park.

Landmarks

Kinnairds Wetland is the largest mapped park in Numurkah, at about 89 hectares. Black Swamp (Nine Mile Creek) Wildlife Reserve (hunting) and Numurkah D59 Nature Conservation Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Numurkah include G F Harding Park and Station Street Wetlands. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Numurkah. In Numurkah you'll also find Numurkah Swimming Centre and Numurkah Library. For older kids in Numurkah there's Riverland Skate Park. For walks and rides, Red Gum Walk and Wetland Walk cross Numurkah.

Getting around

There's no train station in Numurkah. The closest to Numurkah is Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line), about 33 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Numurkah. By car from Numurkah, Shepparton is about 32 minutes away, Echuca about 69 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest beach to Numurkah is Carter Beach in Ulupna, about 39 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Numurkah is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 32 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 32 minutes' drive from Numurkah. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kidstown in Mooroopna is about 34 minutes' drive from Numurkah. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Numurkah, about 32 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Numurkah are the playground at Larsen Reserve in Tallygaroopna (16 min), the playground at Tallygaroopna Recreation Reserve in Tallygaroopna (19 min), Barmah Forest Heritge and Education Centre in Nathalia (27 min), Nathalia Library in Nathalia (27 min), Strathmerton Community Skate Park in Strathmerton (21 min) and Nathalia Memorial Pool in Nathalia (29 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the library in Numurkah. Teens who skate or ride, with Riverland Skate Park in Numurkah. Free days on the Red Gum Walk and in Numurkah's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Katunga, Kaarimba, Invergordon and Drumanure also border Numurkah.
