G F Harding Park

Park in Numurkah · Basic info

G F Harding Park is a park in Numurkah, in the Moira Shire Council area, good for all ages. Quinn St/Mieklejohn St (coach) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 353 m away.
