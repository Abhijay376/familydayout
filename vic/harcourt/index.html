Harcourt in the Mount Alexander area has 4 parks and reserves in our data. Harcourt also has a pool.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Harcourt is Walmer I139 Bushland Reserve, covering about 8 hectares. Harcourt Recreation Reserve and James Park are the next largest. Harcourt also has Stanley Park. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Harcourt. Places to visit in Harcourt include Harcourt Swimming Pool. For walks and rides, Goldfields Track and Leanganook Track cross Harcourt.

Getting around

Harcourt has no train station; Castlemaine Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 8.2 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the 3 and Maryborough - Bendigo routes stop in Harcourt. V/Line coaches stop in Harcourt. By car from Harcourt, Castlemaine is about 7 minutes away, Bendigo about 27 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

The nearest larger centre is Castlemaine, about 7 minutes from Harcourt by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library.
