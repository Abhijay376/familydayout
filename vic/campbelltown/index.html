Campbelltown is in the Hepburn area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Campbelltown, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Campbelltown. The closest to Campbelltown is Clunes Railway Station (V/Line), about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Campbelltown. By car from Campbelltown, Castlemaine is about 32 minutes away, Daylesford about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Clunes Swimming Pool in Clunes is about 29 minutes' drive from Campbelltown. For a library, Creswick Library in Creswick is about 31 minutes' drive from Campbelltown. For a skate park, Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick is about 31 minutes' drive from Campbelltown. The nearest larger centre is Castlemaine, about 32 minutes from Campbelltown by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Campbelltown are the playground at Newstead Memorial Park in Newstead (16 min), Clunes Swimming Pool in Clunes (29 min), the playground at Queens Park in Clunes (29 min), Newstead Swimming Pool in Newstead (17 min), Clunes Museum in Clunes (29 min) and The Warehouse in Clunes (29 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Campbelltown.

Nearby suburbs

Sandon, Glengower and Cotswold also border Campbelltown.
