Bangerang is in the Yarriambiack area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Bangerang I103 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Bangerang, at about 10 hectares. Bangerang I101 Bushland Reserve and Bangerang I102 Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bangerang. The closest to Bangerang is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 125 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bangerang, so most trips start by car. By car from Bangerang, Horsham is about 63 minutes away and Stawell about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem is about 25 minutes' drive from Bangerang. The closest skate park to Bangerang is Birchip Skate Park in Birchip, about 41 minutes' drive. From Bangerang, the nearest places for kids by road are Historical Centre in Warracknabeal (19 min), Lions Playground in Warracknabeal (19 min), Wheatlands Warracknabeal Agricultural Machinery Museum in Warracknabeal (20 min), the playground at Anzac Park in Warracknabeal (20 min), Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem (25 min) and Minyip Swimming Pool in Minyip (36 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bangerang.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Bangerang, Warracknabeal has 5 playgrounds. Next door to Bangerang, Brim has 1 playground. Sheep Hills, Lah, Boolite and Areegra also border Bangerang.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Bangerang, without traffic.
