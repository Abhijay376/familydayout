Our data lists 7 playgrounds and 5 parks and reserves in Aspendale Gardens, in the Kingston area.

Landmarks

Edithvale-Seaford Wetlands is the largest mapped park in Aspendale Gardens, at about 111 hectares. Yammerbrook Nature Reserve and Amott Court Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Aspendale Gardens include Dawson Court Reserve and L.L Stevenson Reserve. Playgrounds in Aspendale Gardens include the playground at Amott Court Reserve, the playground at L.L Stevenson Reserve and the playground at L.L Stevenson Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Aspendale Gardens. Aspendale Gardens Shopping Centre is the local shopping centre. The Mordialloc Freeway Trail and Mordialloc - Waterways - Braeside all pass through Aspendale Gardens. Our data lists 6 cafes and family restaurants in Aspendale Gardens for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Aspendale Gardens has no train station; Aspendale Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 1.6 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 708, 858 and 902 stop in Aspendale Gardens. By car from Aspendale Gardens, Dandenong is about 14 minutes away, Frankston about 15 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 33 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Aspendale Gardens is Grenda Aquatic Centre in Keysborough, about 8 minutes' drive. For a library, Kingston Toy Library in Mordialloc is about 6 minutes' drive from Aspendale Gardens. The closest beach to Aspendale Gardens is Edithvale Beach in Edithvale, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Aspendale Gardens is Chelsea Velodrome in Edithvale, about 4 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Aspendale Gardens is Village Cinemas in Cheltenham, about 12 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Gravity Zone Indoor Trampoline Centre in Seaford is about 10 minutes' drive from Aspendale Gardens. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Aspendale Gardens is Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale, about 19 minutes' drive.

Best for

Younger kids at Aspendale Gardens's 7 local playgrounds. Free days on the Mordialloc Freeway Trail and in Aspendale Gardens's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Keysborough, next to Aspendale Gardens, has 32 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Aspendale, next to Aspendale Gardens, has 10 playgrounds, a beach and a skate park. Next door to Aspendale Gardens, Edithvale has 3 playgrounds, a beach and a skate park. Next door to Aspendale Gardens, Chelsea Heights has 4 playgrounds. Waterways, Braeside and Bangholme also border Aspendale Gardens.
