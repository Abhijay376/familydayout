Playground on Kearney Drive

Playground in Aspendale Gardens · Basic info

Playground on Kearney Drive in Aspendale Gardens is a public playground in the Kingston City area. The Longbeach Trail passes about 180 m away, and Playground at Iluka Reserve is another option about 300 m away. There's a public toilet about 720 m away, and bus 708 stops at Bianca Dr/Wells Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
