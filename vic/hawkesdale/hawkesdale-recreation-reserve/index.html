Hawkesdale Recreation Reserve

Park in Hawkesdale · Basic info

Hawkesdale Recreation Reserve is a park in Hawkesdale, in the Moyne Shire Council area, good for all ages. Austin St/Penhurst-Warrnambool Rd (coach) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 808 m away with a baby change table.
