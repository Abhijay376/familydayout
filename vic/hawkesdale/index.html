Hawkesdale is in the Moyne area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb. On top of that, Hawkesdale has a pool.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Hawkesdale is Hawkesdale Recreation Reserve, covering about 5 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Hawkesdale. In Hawkesdale you'll also find Hawkesdale Swimming Pool.

Getting around

There's no train station in Hawkesdale. The closest to Hawkesdale is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 33 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Hawkesdale. By car from Hawkesdale, Warrnambool is about 36 minutes away and Hamilton about 49 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Hawkesdale is Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool, about 37 minutes' drive. For a beach, Reef Point Beach in Port Fairy is about 23 minutes' drive from Hawkesdale. The closest skate park to Hawkesdale is Koroit Youth Space in Koroit, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Hawkesdale is Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy, about 30 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, The Bowl in Warrnambool is about 37 minutes' drive from Hawkesdale. For a zoo or wildlife park, Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee is about 44 minutes' drive from Hawkesdale. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Hawkesdale, about 36 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Hawkesdale, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Kirkstall Recreation Reserve in Kirkstall (15 min), Koroit Youth Space in Koroit (21 min), Volcanoes Discovery Centre in Penshurst (23 min), Penshurst Botanic Gardens Playground in Penshurst (23 min), Penshurst Swimming Pool in Penshurst (23 min) and Reef Point Beach in Port Fairy (23 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Hawkesdale.

Nearby suburbs

Minjah, Minhamite, Macarthur and Gerrigerrup also border Hawkesdale.
