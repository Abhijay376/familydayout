Youarang is in the Moira area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Youarang, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Youarang. The closest to Youarang is Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line), about 39 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Youarang, so most trips start by car. By car from Youarang, Benalla is about 42 minutes away and Shepparton about 44 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Youarang is Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest library to Youarang is Numurkah Library in Numurkah, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Youarang is Fosters Beach in Cobram, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Youarang is the skate park at Federation Park in Cobram, about 25 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Cobram Cinema in Cobram is about 25 minutes' drive from Youarang. The closest indoor play centre to Youarang is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 44 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Tunzafun in Yarrawonga is about 33 minutes' drive from Youarang. The nearest larger centre is Benalla, about 42 minutes from Youarang by car, with 5 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Youarang are Katamatite Historical Society Museum in Katamatite (8 min), the playground at E.W Kilgour Reserve in Katamatite (8 min), the playground at C R Wood Park in Katamatite (9 min), Fosters Beach in Cobram (26 min), Backhouse Beach in Cobram East (25 min) and Cobram Cinema in Cobram (25 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Youarang.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Youarang, Katamatite has 2 playgrounds. Tungamah, next to Youarang, has 1 playground. Boosey also borders Youarang.
