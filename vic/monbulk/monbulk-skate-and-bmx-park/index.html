Monbulk Skate & BMX Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Monbulk · Basic info

Monbulk Skate & BMX Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Monbulk, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Emerald-Monbulk Rd/Main Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 344 m away with a baby change table.
