Sassafras Creek Nature Conservation Reserve

Park in Monbulk · Official details

Sassafras Creek Nature Conservation Reserve is a park in Monbulk, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Emerald-Monbulk Rd/Main Rd (bus) is a 20-minute walk away and dogs are allowed on a lead.

Details

Dogs
on lead (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
No bicycles, horses, camping or fires; Four picnic areas along the track: Olympic, Kays, Kensleys, Beagleys; Avoid the track on Extreme Fire Danger or high wind days (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Sassafras Creek Nature Conservation Reserve?
Sassafras Creek Nature Conservation Reserve is in Monbulk, Victoria.
Is Sassafras Creek Nature Conservation Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Sassafras Creek Nature Conservation Reserve?
Yes, on a lead.
How do I get to Sassafras Creek Nature Conservation Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Emerald-Monbulk Rd/Main Rd (routes 663, 696), a 20-minute walk.
