Bunurong Marine National Park

Park in Inverloch · Official details

Bunurong Marine National Park is a park in Inverloch, in the Bass Coast Shire Council area, good for all ages.

Details

Seasons
Hooded Plover breeds on beaches late Aug to March (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
Rockpool near low tide for most marine life; Fishing prohibited in the park (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Bunurong Marine National Park?
Bunurong Marine National Park is in Inverloch, Victoria.
Is Bunurong Marine National Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
