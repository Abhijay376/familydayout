Playground at Inverloch Coastal Reserve

Playground in Inverloch · Basic info

Playground at Inverloch Coastal Reserve in Inverloch is a public playground in the Bass Coast Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 260 m away, and Bunurong Environment Centre & Shell Museum is about 80 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 90 m from the playground, and coaches stop at Ramsay Bvd/The Esplanade, a 2-minute walk away.
