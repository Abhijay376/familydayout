Playground at Wallace Park

Playground in Inverloch · Basic info

Playground at Wallace Park is a public playground in Inverloch, in the Bass Coast Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. You'll find a public toilet about 570 m from the playground, and bus Wonthaggi - Traralgon via Leongatha stops at Kenneth St/Williams St, a 4-minute walk away.
