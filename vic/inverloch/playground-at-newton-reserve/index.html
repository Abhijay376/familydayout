Playground at Newton Reserve

Playground in Inverloch · Basic info

Playground at Newton Reserve in Inverloch is a public playground in the Bass Coast Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet about 780 m away, and coaches stop at Inverloch Police Station/Bayview Ave, a 7-minute walk away.
