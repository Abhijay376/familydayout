Monomak is in the Wellington area, and our data lists 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Ben Cruachan Natural Features and Scenic Reserve is the largest mapped park in Monomak, at about 247 hectares. Monomak also has Huggetts Lookout, Huggetts Crossing and Nicholson Rocks Picnic Area. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Monomak. Picnic areas in Monomak include Huggetts Crossing, Huggetts Lookout and Nicholson Rocks Picnic Area. The Razorback Walking Track, Avon River Walking Track and Traverse Track all pass through Monomak.

Getting around

Monomak has no train station; Stratford Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 34 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Monomak, so most trips start by car. By car from Monomak, Sale is about 81 minutes away and Traralgon about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Monomak, the nearest places for kids by road are Coongulla Community Centre Reserve Playground in Coongulla (45 min), Play Equipment in Newry (58 min), Pump Track in Heyfield (54 min), Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield (54 min), Heyfield and District Museum in Heyfield (55 min) and Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford (77 min).

Best for

Free days on the Razorback Walking Track and in Monomak's parks.
