Selwyn Park

Park in Albion · Official details

Selwyn Park is a park in Albion, in the Brimbank City Council area, good for all ages. 72 Forrest St (bus) is a 6-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 43 m away and dogs are allowed on a lead.

Questions parents ask

Where is Selwyn Park?
Selwyn Park is in Albion, Victoria.
Is Selwyn Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Selwyn Park?
Yes, on a lead.
Are there toilets at Selwyn Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 43 m away.
How do I get to Selwyn Park by public transport?
The closest stop is 72 Forrest St (routes 400, 422, 427), a 6-minute walk.
