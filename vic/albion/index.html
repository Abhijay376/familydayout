Albion in the Brimbank area has 4 playgrounds and 6 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Selwyn Park is the largest mapped park in Albion, at about 6 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Albion include Hamel Street Reserve, Bazentin Street Reserve and Coolamon Street Reserve. Playgrounds around Albion include Selwyn Park Playground, the playground at Selwyn Park and the playground at Selwyn Park. Our data shows 13 public toilets and 3 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Albion. For walks and rides, M80 Trail, Kororoit Creek Trail and Sunshine Trail cross Albion. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Albion for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Albion has a train station: Albion Railway Station (metro: Sunbury Line). Buses 215, 400, 422, 426, 427 and 456 stop in Albion. By car from Albion, Sunshine is about 4 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 19 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 24 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Sunshine Swimming Pool in Sunshine is about 5 minutes' drive from Albion. The closest library to Albion is Sunshine Library in Sunshine, about 4 minutes' drive. For a beach, Altona Dog Beach in Seaholme is about 18 minutes' drive from Albion. For a skate park, Sunshine Plaza Skatepark in Sunshine is about 5 minutes' drive from Albion. The closest cinema to Albion is Village Cinemas in Sunshine, about 4 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Zone Bowling in Braybrook is about 8 minutes' drive from Albion. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Albion is Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville, about 19 minutes' drive. Sunshine is the closest bigger centre to Albion, about 4 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Albion's 4 local playgrounds. Free days on the M80 Trail and in Albion's parks.

Nearby suburbs

St Albans, next to Albion, has 33 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Albion, Sunshine has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Albion, Sunshine West has 19 playgrounds and a skate park. Sunshine North, next to Albion, has 6 playgrounds. Ardeer also borders Albion.
