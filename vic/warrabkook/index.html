Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Warrabkook in the Moyne area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Warrabkook has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 59 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Warrabkook, so most trips start by car. By car from Warrabkook, Hamilton is about 31 minutes away and Portland about 60 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Penshurst Swimming Pool in Penshurst is about 22 minutes' drive from Warrabkook. For a library, Hamilton Library in Hamilton is about 31 minutes' drive from Warrabkook. The closest beach to Warrabkook is Eumeralla in Tyrendarra East, about 41 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Warrabkook is hamilton skate park in Hamilton, about 33 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton is about 31 minutes' drive from Warrabkook. Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Warrabkook, about 31 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Warrabkook, the nearest places for kids by road are Penshurst Botanic Gardens Playground in Penshurst (22 min), Penshurst Swimming Pool in Penshurst (22 min), Volcanoes Discovery Centre in Penshurst (22 min), Penshurst Rec Reserve Playground in Penshurst (24 min), Hawkesdale Swimming Pool in Hawkesdale (29 min) and Hamilton History Centre in Hamilton (31 min).
