Flagstaff is in the Central Goldfields area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Flagstaff, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

The Castlemaine-Maryborough Rail Trail (Under Construction) and Carrisbrook Settlers Trail all pass through Flagstaff.

Getting around

Flagstaff has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 3.2 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Flagstaff, so most trips start by car. By car from Flagstaff, Maryborough is about 4 minutes away, Castlemaine about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot is about 20 minutes' drive from Flagstaff. For a library, Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough is about 4 minutes' drive from Flagstaff. The closest skate park to Flagstaff is Bike Jumps in Castlemaine, about 40 minutes' drive. Maryborough is the closest bigger centre to Flagstaff, about 4 minutes away by car, and has a library. The closest places for kids by road from Flagstaff are the playground at Market Reserve in Carisbrook (4 min), Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (4 min), Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground in Talbot (20 min), Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (20 min), Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (28 min) and Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (28 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Flagstaff on the Castlemaine-Maryborough Rail Trail (Under Construction).

Nearby suburbs

Maryborough, next to Flagstaff, has a library. Carisbrook, next to Flagstaff, has 1 playground.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Flagstaff, without traffic.
