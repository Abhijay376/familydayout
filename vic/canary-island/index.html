Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Canary Island in the Loddon area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Canary Island. The closest to Canary Island is Kerang Railway Station (V/Line), about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Canary Island, so most trips start by car. By car from Canary Island, Swan Hill is about 78 minutes away and Bendigo about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Canary Island is Boort Swimming Pool in Boort, about 21 minutes' drive. For a library, Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang is about 33 minutes' drive from Canary Island. From Canary Island, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Lake Meran W.R in Lake Meran (19 min), A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park in Boort (20 min), Boort Court House Historical Museum in Boort (20 min), Boort Swimming Pool in Boort (21 min), Pyramid Hill & Districts Historical Museum in Pyramid Hill (34 min) and Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Canary Island.
