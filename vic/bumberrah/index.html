Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bumberrah in the East Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, East Gippsland Rail Trail crosses Bumberrah.

Getting around

Bumberrah has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bumberrah, so most trips start by car. By car from Bumberrah, Lakes Entrance is about 23 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 25 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bumberrah is Lakes Entrance Aquatic Centre in Lakes Entrance, about 24 minutes' drive. For a library, Lakes Entrance Library in Lakes Entrance is about 20 minutes' drive from Bumberrah. For a beach, 90 Mile Beach in Boole Poole is about 14 minutes' drive from Bumberrah. The closest skate park to Bumberrah is the skate park at Gilsenan Reserve in Paynesville, about 36 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale is about 23 minutes' drive from Bumberrah. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Bumberrah is Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow, about 16 minutes' drive. Lakes Entrance is the closest bigger centre to Bumberrah, about 23 minutes away by car, and has 3 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a beach. From Bumberrah, the nearest places for kids by road are Bruthen Gallery in Bruthen (13 min), 90 Mile Beach in Boole Poole (14 min), Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow (16 min), the playground at Nyerimilang Heritage Park in Nyerimilang (23 min), Griffiths Sea Shell Museum in Lakes Entrance (18 min) and Lakes Entrance Library in Lakes Entrance (20 min).
