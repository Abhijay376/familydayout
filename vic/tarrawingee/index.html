Tarrawingee in the Wangaratta area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Tarrawingee I5 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Tarrawingee, at about 11 hectares. The Murray to Mountains Rail Trail: Wangaratta – Bright passes through Tarrawingee.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tarrawingee. The closest to Tarrawingee is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tarrawingee, so most trips start by car. By car from Tarrawingee, Wangaratta is about 14 minutes away and Benalla about 39 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tarrawingee is Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta, about 15 minutes' drive. For a library, Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta is about 13 minutes' drive from Tarrawingee. The closest beach to Tarrawingee is Sandy Beach in Beechworth, about 24 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta is about 16 minutes' drive from Tarrawingee. The closest cinema to Tarrawingee is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 11 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 14 minutes from Tarrawingee by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Tarrawingee, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at John McAleese Community Park in Milawa (10 min), the playground at Apex Park in Wangaratta (11 min), Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta (11 min), Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (13 min), Eldorado Museum in Eldorado (17 min) and Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta (15 min).

Best for

Free days on the Murray to Mountains Rail Trail: Wangaratta – Bright and in Tarrawingee's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Milawa, next to Tarrawingee, has 1 playground. Next door to Tarrawingee, Eldorado has 1 playground. Londrigan, Everton Upper, Everton and East Wangaratta also border Tarrawingee.
