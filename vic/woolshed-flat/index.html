Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Woolshed Flat in the Loddon area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Woolshed Flat. The closest to Woolshed Flat is Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line), about 51 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Woolshed Flat, so most trips start by car. By car from Woolshed Flat, Bendigo is about 88 minutes away and Maryborough about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn is about 25 minutes' drive from Woolshed Flat. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 44 minutes' drive from Woolshed Flat. From Woolshed Flat, the nearest places for kids by road are Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (25 min), Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (25 min), A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park in Boort (45 min), Boort Court House Historical Museum in Boort (45 min), Boort Swimming Pool in Boort (46 min) and Rex Theatre in Charlton (44 min).

Best for
