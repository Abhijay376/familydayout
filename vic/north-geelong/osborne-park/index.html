Osborne Park

Park in North Geelong · Official details

Osborne Park is a park in North Geelong, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Swinburne St/Princes Hwy (bus) is a 5-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 270 m away and dogs are allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Osborne Park?
Osborne Park is in North Geelong, Victoria.
Is Osborne Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Osborne Park?
Yes, dogs are allowed.
Are there toilets at Osborne Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 270 m away.
How do I get to Osborne Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Swinburne St/Princes Hwy (routes 20), a 5-minute walk.
