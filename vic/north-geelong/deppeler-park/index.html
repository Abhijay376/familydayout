Deppeler Park

Park in North Geelong · Official details

Deppeler Park is a park in North Geelong, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Surrey St/Princes Hwy (bus) is a 4-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 230 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed.

Where is Deppeler Park?
Deppeler Park is in North Geelong, Victoria.
Is Deppeler Park free?

Yes, dogs are allowed.
Are there toilets at Deppeler Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 230 m away, with a baby change table.
How do I get to Deppeler Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Surrey St/Princes Hwy (routes 20), a 4-minute walk.
