Western Oval

Park in North Geelong · Official details

Western Oval is a park in North Geelong, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Geelong West Oval/Church St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 37 m away and dogs are allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Western Oval?
Western Oval is in North Geelong, Victoria.
Is Western Oval free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Western Oval?
Yes, dogs are allowed.
Are there toilets at Western Oval?
The nearest public toilet is about 37 m away.
How do I get to Western Oval by public transport?
The closest stop is Geelong West Oval/Church St (routes 22), a 2-minute walk.
