Allansford is in the Moyne area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Lake Gillear Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Allansford, at about 24 hectares. Jubilee Park is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Allansford.

Getting around

There's no train station in Allansford. The closest to Allansford is Sherwood Park Railway Station (V/Line), about 4.7 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the 9 and Warrnambool - Port Campbell - Timboon via Allansford, Nullawarre, Peterborough routes stop in Allansford. By car from Allansford, Warrnambool is about 11 minutes away and Colac about 77 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool is about 14 minutes' drive from Allansford. The closest library to Allansford is Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Allansford is Shelly Beach in Warrnambool, about 16 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Warrnambool Skate Park in Warrnambool is about 14 minutes' drive from Allansford. For a cinema, Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool is about 11 minutes' drive from Allansford. The closest indoor play centre to Allansford is The Bowl in Warrnambool, about 12 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee is about 9 minutes' drive from Allansford. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 11 minutes from Allansford by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Allansford, the nearest places for kids by road are Grieve Street Playground in Warrnambool (9 min), the playground at Brierly Park in Warrnambool (10 min), Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (9 min), SCOPE Galleries in Warrnambool (11 min), The Bowl in Warrnambool (12 min) and Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool (11 min).
