Longwood East in the Strathbogie area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Big Hill Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Longwood East, at about 62 hectares. Old Longwood Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Longwood East. The closest to Longwood East is Euroa Railway Station (V/Line), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Longwood East, so most trips start by car. By car from Longwood East, Seymour is about 35 minutes away, Benalla about 51 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa is about 16 minutes' drive from Longwood East. The closest library to Longwood East is Euroa Library in Euroa, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Longwood East is Euroa Skate Park in Euroa, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Longwood East is Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa, about 16 minutes' drive. Seymour is the closest bigger centre to Longwood East, about 35 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Longwood East are Longwood Recreation Reserve Playground in Longwood (8 min), Creightons Creek Recreation Reserve Playground in Creightons Creek (19 min), Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa (16 min), Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa (16 min), Farmers Arms Hotel Museum in Euroa (16 min) and Euroa Skate Park in Euroa (16 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Longwood East.

Nearby suburbs

Euroa, next to Longwood East, has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Longwood, next to Longwood East, has 1 playground. Next door to Longwood East, Creightons Creek has 1 playground. Tarcombe and Ruffy also border Longwood East.
