Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Germania in the Northern Grampians area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Germania. The closest to Germania is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 38 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Germania, so most trips start by car. By car from Germania, Stawell is about 14 minutes away and Ararat about 40 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell is about 13 minutes' drive from Germania. The closest library to Germania is Ararat Library in Ararat, about 40 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Germania is Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Germania is the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Germania is Astor Cinema in Ararat, about 40 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Germania is Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell, about 16 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Stawell, about 14 minutes from Germania by car, with 6 playgrounds and a pool. From Germania, the nearest places for kids by road are Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell (13 min), Stawell Skate Park in Stawell (13 min), North Park Playground in Stawell (14 min), Railway Station Art and Crafts Gallery in Stawell (15 min), Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell (16 min) and the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell (17 min).
