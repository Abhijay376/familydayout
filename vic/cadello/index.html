Cadello is in the Macedon Ranges area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Cadello is Woodend I95 Bushland Reserve, covering about 2 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cadello. The closest to Cadello is Woodend Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cadello, so most trips start by car. By car from Cadello, Kyneton is about 14 minutes away, Castlemaine about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 67 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cadello is Gisborne Aquatic Centre in Gisborne, about 22 minutes' drive. For a library, Kyneton Library in Kyneton is about 14 minutes' drive from Cadello. For a beach, Canoeing in Tylden is about 21 minutes' drive from Cadello. For a skate park, Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton is about 14 minutes' drive from Cadello. The closest cinema to Cadello is Reading Cinema in Sunbury, about 35 minutes' drive. Kyneton is the closest bigger centre to Cadello, about 14 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Cadello are Jean Haynes Playground in Kyneton (12 min), Kyneton Library in Kyneton (14 min), Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton (14 min), Kyneton Community Park in Kyneton (14 min), Kyneton Museum in Kyneton (14 min) and Canoeing in Tylden (21 min).
