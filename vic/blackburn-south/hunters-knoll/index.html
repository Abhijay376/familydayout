Hunters Knoll

Park in Blackburn South · Basic info

Hunters Knoll is a park in Blackburn South, in the Whitehorse City Council area, good for all ages. Holland Rd/Canterbury Rd (bus) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 863 m away.
