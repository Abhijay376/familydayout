Playground at Hunters Knoll

Playground in Blackburn South · Basic info

Playground at Hunters Knoll in Blackburn South is a public playground in the Whitehorse City area. Playground at Orchard Grove Reserve is another option about 520 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 840 m from the playground, and bus 703 stops at Brazeel St/Blackburn Rd, a 8-minute walk away.
