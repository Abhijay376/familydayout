Finch Place Reserve

Park in Blackburn South · Basic info

Finch Place Reserve is a park in Blackburn South, in the Whitehorse City Council area, good for all ages. Finch Pl/Blackburn Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 600 m away.
