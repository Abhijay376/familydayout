Vesper is in the Baw Baw area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Vesper, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Vesper has no train station; Warragul Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 34 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Vesper, so most trips start by car. By car from Vesper, Warragul is about 49 minutes away, Traralgon about 67 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Vesper is Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar, about 42 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South is about 30 minutes' drive from Vesper. The nearest larger centre is Warragul, about 49 minutes from Vesper by car, with 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Vesper, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at LaTrobe and Loch Rivers, Noojee Streamside Reserve in Noojee (15 min), Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South (30 min), the playground at Blue Rock Lake West picnic area in Willow Grove (29 min), Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar (42 min), Moe Skate Park in Moe (44 min) and Warragul Library in Warragul (49 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Vesper.

Nearby suburbs

Noojee, next to Vesper, has 1 playground. Fumina also borders Vesper.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Vesper, without traffic.
