Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Longlea in the Greater Bendigo area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Longlea. For walks and rides, O'Keefe Rail Trail and Okeefe Rail Trail - Longlea cross Longlea.

Getting around

There's no train station in Longlea. The closest to Longlea is Huntly Railway Station (V/Line), about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Heathcote - Bendigo via Junortoun, Axedale, Knowsley route stop in Longlea. By car from Longlea, Bendigo is about 13 minutes away, Castlemaine about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Longlea is Brennan Park Swimming Pool in Kennington, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest library to Longlea is Bendigo Library in Bendigo, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Longlea is Ewing Park Skating in Bendigo, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Longlea is Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo, about 14 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 16 minutes' drive from Longlea. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 11 minutes' drive from Longlea. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Longlea, about 13 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Longlea are McIvor Forest Estate Playspace in Junortoun (9 min), the playground at Coomoora Park in Strathfieldsaye (12 min), The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo (11 min), Ewing Park Skating in Bendigo (13 min), Specimen Cottage in Bendigo (12 min) and Bendigo Library in Bendigo (12 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Longlea on the O'Keefe Rail Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Strathfieldsaye, next to Longlea, has 6 playgrounds. Junortoun, next to Longlea, has 2 playgrounds. Axedale, next to Longlea, has 1 playground. Fosterville and Eppalock also border Longlea.
