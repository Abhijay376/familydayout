Hall Street Reserve

Park in McKinnon · Basic info

Hall Street Reserve is a park in McKinnon, in the Glen Eira City Council area, good for all ages. Jasper Rd/McKinnon Rd (bus) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 784 m away.
