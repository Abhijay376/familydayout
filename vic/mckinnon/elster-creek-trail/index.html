Elster Creek Trail

Park in McKinnon · Basic info

Elster Creek Trail is a park in McKinnon, in the Glen Eira City Council area, good for all ages. Thomas St/Union St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 647 m away.
