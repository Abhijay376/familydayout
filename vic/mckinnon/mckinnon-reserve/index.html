McKinnon Reserve

Park in McKinnon · Official details

McKinnon Reserve is a park in McKinnon, in the Glen Eira City Council area, good for all ages. McKinnon Reserve/McKinnon Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 68 m away and dogs are not allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is McKinnon Reserve?
McKinnon Reserve is in McKinnon, Victoria.
Is McKinnon Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to McKinnon Reserve?
No, dogs are not allowed.
Are there toilets at McKinnon Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 68 m away.
How do I get to McKinnon Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is McKinnon Reserve/McKinnon Rd (routes 626), a 4-minute walk.
