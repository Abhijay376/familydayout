Playground at Koonung Reserve

Playground in Blackburn North · Basic info

Playground at Koonung Reserve is a public playground in Blackburn North, in the Whitehorse City area. Playground on Polydor Place is another option about 180 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 380 m from the playground, and buses 279 and 303 stop at Blackburn High School/Springfield Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
