Playground at Hollioake Park

Playground in Cardigan · Basic info

Playground at Hollioake Park is a public playground in Cardigan, in the Ballarat City area. Older kids have a skate park about 50 m away, and Playground on Warburton Drive is another option about 560 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus 26 stops at Chase Bvd/Dyson Dr, a 6-minute walk away.
