Getting around

There's no train station in Exford. The closest to Exford is Melton Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.8 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Exford, so most trips start by car. By car from Exford, Melton is about 14 minutes away, Bacchus Marsh about 17 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 47 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Exford is Melton Swimmers in Melton South, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest library to Exford is Lerderderg Library in Bacchus Marsh, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Exford is Needles Beach in Pentland Hills, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Exford is the skate park at Rotary Park in Bacchus Marsh, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Exford is Reading Cinemas in Melton West, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Exford is Timezone in Hoppers Crossing, about 29 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Werribee Open Range Zoo in Werribee South is about 36 minutes' drive from Exford. The nearest larger centre is Melton, about 14 minutes from Exford by car, with 15 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Exford are Embleton Chase Reserve Playground in Weir Views (8 min), Goulburn Way Reserve Playground in Eynesbury (8 min), Melton Swimmers in Melton South (11 min), Australian Gliding Museum in Parwan (17 min), Bacchus Marsh Public childrens pool in Bacchus Marsh (15 min) and Lerderderg Library in Bacchus Marsh (16 min).
