Glengarry in the Wellington area has 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in our data. Glengarry also has 2 skate parks.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Glengarry is Apex Park, covering about 7 hectares. Glengarry Tennis Club is the next largest. Playgrounds around Glengarry include the playground at Apex Park. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Glengarry. For older kids in Glengarry there's Glengarry Skatepark and the skate park at Apex Park. For walks and rides, Gippsland Plains Rail Trail crosses Glengarry.

Getting around

Glengarry has no train station; Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 8.7 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Glengarry. By car from Glengarry, Traralgon is about 12 minutes away, Sale about 49 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon is about 12 minutes' drive from Glengarry. The closest library to Glengarry is Morwell Library in Morwell, about 25 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 22 minutes' drive from Glengarry. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Glengarry, about 12 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. From Glengarry, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Waite Park in Traralgon (7 min), Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon (12 min), Yallourn North Outdoor Pool in Yallourn North (18 min), Old Brown Coal Mine Museum in Yallourn North (18 min), Village 8 in Morwell (22 min) and Powerworks in Morwell (24 min).

Best for

Teens who skate or ride, with Glengarry Skatepark in Glengarry. Free days on the Gippsland Plains Rail Trail and in Glengarry's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Glengarry, Traralgon has 38 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Glengarry, Rosedale has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Traralgon East, next to Glengarry, has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Glengarry, Toongabbie has 2 playgrounds. Winnindoo, Glengarry West, Glengarry North and Flynn also border Glengarry.
