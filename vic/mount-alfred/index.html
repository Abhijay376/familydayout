Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Mount Alfred in the Towong area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mount Alfred. The closest to Mount Alfred is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 64 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Albury - Corryong via Walwa route stop in Mount Alfred. By car from Mount Alfred, Wodonga is about 81 minutes away and Wangaratta about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a beach, Pebble Beach in Walwa is about 14 minutes' drive from Mount Alfred. From Mount Alfred, the nearest places for kids by road are Pebble Beach in Walwa (14 min), The Spotted Goanna in Walwa (16 min) and Granya Museum in Granya (37 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mount Alfred.
