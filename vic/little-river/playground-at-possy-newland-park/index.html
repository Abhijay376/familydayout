Playground at Possy Newland Park

Playground in Little River · Basic info

Playground at Possy Newland Park is a playground in Little River, in the Wyndham City Council area, best for toddlers and primary-school kids. Little River Station (coach) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 8 m away.
