Skate park at Possey Newland Reserve

Skate, BMX or pump track in Little River · Basic info

Skate park at Possey Newland Reserve is a skate, bmx or pump track in Little River, in the Wyndham City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Little River Station (coach) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 194 m away.
