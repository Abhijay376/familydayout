Little River Oval Playground

Playground in Little River · Official details

Little River Oval Playground is a neighbourhood playground in Little River, in the Wyndham City area. Playground at Possy Newland Reserve is another option about 240 m away. There's a public toilet about 600 m away, and coaches stop at Little River Station , a 6-minute walk away.
