Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Modella in the Baw Baw area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Modella has no train station; Bunyip Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 10 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Modella, so most trips start by car. By car from Modella, Pakenham is about 27 minutes away, Warragul about 27 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 70 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Drouin Swimming Pool in Drouin is about 19 minutes' drive from Modella. For a library, Drouin Library in Drouin is about 19 minutes' drive from Modella. The closest skate park to Modella is Drouin Skate Park in Drouin, about 18 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 26 minutes' drive from Modella. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 26 minutes' drive from Modella. For a zoo or wildlife park, Wildlife Trail in Tynong North is about 24 minutes' drive from Modella. The nearest larger centre is Pakenham, about 27 minutes from Modella by car, with 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Modella are the playground at Longwarry Recreation Reserve in Longwarry (12 min), the playground at Bayles Recreation Reserve in Bayles (11 min), Drouin Skate Park in Drouin (18 min), Drouin Library in Drouin (19 min), Drouin Swimming Pool in Drouin (19 min) and Skate BMX Park in Garfield (19 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Modella.
