Wangie is in the Buloke area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Brimy Bill (5 Mile Lake) Wildlife Reserve (no hunting) is the largest mapped park in Wangie, at about 235 hectares. Wangie Flora and Fauna Reserve and Wangie Wildlife Reserve (no hunting) are the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wangie. The closest to Wangie is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 47 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wangie, so most trips start by car. By car from Wangie, Swan Hill is about 57 minutes away and Echuca about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Wangie is Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest library to Wangie is Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook, about 37 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 57 minutes from Wangie by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Wangie are the playground at Culgoa Memorial Park in Culgoa (18 min), the playground at Hall Corner Park in Nullawil (29 min), Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock (28 min), Quambatook Historical Centre in Quambatook (37 min), Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook (37 min) and Quambatook Memorial Pool in Quambatook (38 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wangie.

Nearby suburbs

Culgoa, next to Wangie, has 1 playground. Murnungin, Lalbert and Cokum also border Wangie.
