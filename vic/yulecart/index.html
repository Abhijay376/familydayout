Landmarks

Murndal Lake Reserve is the largest mapped park in Yulecart, at about 10 hectares. Our data shows 1 off-leash dog park in Yulecart.

Getting around

There's no train station in Yulecart. The closest to Yulecart is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 85 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yulecart, so most trips start by car. By car from Yulecart, Hamilton is about 9 minutes away and Portland about 64 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Hamilton is about 11 minutes' drive from Yulecart. For a library, Hamilton Library in Hamilton is about 9 minutes' drive from Yulecart. The closest skate park to Yulecart is hamilton skate park in Hamilton, about 11 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton is about 9 minutes' drive from Yulecart. Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Yulecart, about 9 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Yulecart are Quigley Street Playground in Hamilton (7 min), Hughan Park Playground in Hamilton (8 min), Hamilton History Centre in Hamilton (9 min), Mud Gallery in Hamilton (9 min), Hamilton Library in Hamilton (9 min) and Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton (9 min).
