Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Barwite, in the Mansfield area.

Landmarks

Cambatong Reference Area is the largest mapped park in Barwite, at about 338 hectares. The Mansfield - Mt Samaria Loop and Mansfield - Long Lane Loop all pass through Barwite.

Getting around

There's no train station in Barwite. The closest to Barwite is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 53 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Barwite, so most trips start by car. By car from Barwite, Mansfield is about 12 minutes away, Benalla about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Barwite is Mansfield Outdoor Swimming Pool in Mansfield, about 13 minutes' drive. For a library, Mansfield Library in Mansfield is about 11 minutes' drive from Barwite. The closest skate park to Barwite is the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield, about 13 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield is about 11 minutes' drive from Barwite. For a zoo or wildlife park, Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield is about 19 minutes' drive from Barwite. Mansfield is the closest bigger centre to Barwite, about 12 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Barwite are the playground at Mansfield Botanic Park in Mansfield (11 min), Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield (11 min), Mansfield Library in Mansfield (11 min), the playground at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (13 min), the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (13 min) and Mansfield Outdoor Swimming Pool in Mansfield (13 min).

Best for

Free days on the Mansfield - Mt Samaria Loop and in Barwite's parks.
