Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Jeetho in the South Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

The Great Southern Rail Trail passes through Jeetho.

Getting around

Jeetho has no train station; Longwarry Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 32 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Jeetho, so most trips start by car. By car from Jeetho, Leongatha is about 26 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 44 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Jeetho is Korumburra Outdoor Pool in Korumburra, about 13 minutes' drive. For a library, Poowong library in Poowong is about 16 minutes' drive from Jeetho. For a skate park, Nyora skate park in Nyora is about 18 minutes' drive from Jeetho. For a cinema, Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha is about 25 minutes' drive from Jeetho. The closest indoor play centre to Jeetho is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 45 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville is about 38 minutes' drive from Jeetho. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Jeetho, about 26 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Jeetho, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Sunnyside Park in Loch (10 min), the playground at Coleman Park in Korumburra (13 min), Korumburra Outdoor Pool in Korumburra (13 min), Coal Creek Community Park & Museum in Korumburra (15 min), Nyora skate park in Nyora (18 min) and Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong (16 min).

Best for
