Hollands Landing in the East Gippsland area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Hollands Landing Gippsland Lakes Reserve is the largest mapped park in Hollands Landing, at about 69 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Hollands Landing.

Getting around

Hollands Landing has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Hollands Landing, so most trips start by car. By car from Hollands Landing, Bairnsdale is about 42 minutes away and Sale about 53 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford is about 40 minutes' drive from Hollands Landing. The closest library to Hollands Landing is Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale, about 43 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Hollands Landing is Skate Park in Stratford, about 39 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale is about 43 minutes' drive from Hollands Landing. Bairnsdale is the closest bigger centre to Hollands Landing, about 42 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Hollands Landing are the playground at Amy Shand Park in Lindenow (45 min), Skate Park in Stratford (39 min), Market Reserve Playground in Stratford (39 min), Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford (40 min), Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale (42 min) and Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale (43 min).
