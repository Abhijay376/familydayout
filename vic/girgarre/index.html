Girgarre is in the Campaspe area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Girgarre, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 2 public toilets in Girgarre. The Girgarre-Stanhope Rail Trail passes through Girgarre.

Getting around

Girgarre has no train station; Rochester Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Bendigo Station - Shepparton Station via Kyabram route stop in Girgarre. V/Line coaches stop in Girgarre. By car from Girgarre, Echuca is about 39 minutes away, Shepparton about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Community Swimming Pool in Merrigum is about 24 minutes' drive from Girgarre. The closest library to Girgarre is Kyabram Library And Community Hub in Kyabram, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Girgarre is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 42 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Girgarre is the bmx track on Racecourse Road in Kyabram, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Girgarre is Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca, about 40 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Girgarre is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 40 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram is about 15 minutes' drive from Girgarre. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 39 minutes from Girgarre by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Girgarre are the playground at McCormick Reserve in Kyabram (13 min), the playground at Jaycee Park in Kyabram (13 min), Kyabram Library And Community Hub in Kyabram (14 min), the bmx track on Racecourse Road in Kyabram (15 min), Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram (15 min) and Community Swimming Pool in Merrigum (24 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Girgarre on the Girgarre-Stanhope Rail Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Kyabram, next to Girgarre, has 8 playgrounds, a library and a zoo or wildlife park. Corop, Cooma and Carag Carag also border Girgarre.
