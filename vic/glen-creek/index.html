Glen Creek is in the Alpine area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Glen Creek, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Glen Creek. The closest to Glen Creek is Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line), about 37 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glen Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Glen Creek, Wodonga is about 39 minutes away and Bright about 55 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Glen Creek is the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford, about 29 minutes' drive. For a library, Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford is about 27 minutes' drive from Glen Creek. For a beach, Sandy Beach in Beechworth is about 36 minutes' drive from Glen Creek. The closest skate park to Glen Creek is Skate Park in Yackandandah, about 15 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana is about 35 minutes' drive from Glen Creek. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 39 minutes from Glen Creek by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Glen Creek are Skate Park in Yackandandah (15 min), the playground at Sir Isaac Isaacs Park in Yackandandah (16 min), the playground at Swinburne Park in Myrtleford (27 min), Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford (27 min), Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford (27 min) and The Old School Museum in Myrtleford (28 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Glen Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Glen Creek, Yackandandah has 1 playground and a skate park. Dederang and Bruarong also border Glen Creek.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Glen Creek, without traffic.
