Narre Warren East is in the Yarra Ranges area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Narre Warren East, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Narre Warren East has no train station; Belgrave Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 5.8 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 695, 697 and 695F stop in Narre Warren East. By car from Narre Warren East, Dandenong is about 17 minutes away, Cranbourne about 22 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 38 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Narre Warren East is Belgrave Heated Outdoor Swimming Pool in Belgrave, about 8 minutes' drive. For a library, Belgrave Library in Belgrave is about 10 minutes' drive from Narre Warren East. The closest beach to Narre Warren East is Edithvale Beach in Edithvale, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Narre Warren East is the bmx track at Beaconsfield Upper Recreation Reserve in Beaconsfield Upper, about 10 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Cameo Belgrave in Belgrave is about 9 minutes' drive from Narre Warren East. For an indoor play centre, Oz Tenpin in Narre Warren is about 12 minutes' drive from Narre Warren East. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Narre Warren East is MonkeyG's in Pakenham, about 24 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Dandenong, about 17 minutes from Narre Warren East by car, with 21 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Narre Warren East are the playground at Cardinia Resv Park in Emerald (7 min), Southern Sherbrooke Historical Society in Belgrave Heights (5 min), Dalton Reserve Playground in Harkaway (6 min), Belgrave Heated Outdoor Swimming Pool in Belgrave (8 min), Janet Matthews Artist in Narre Warren North (9 min) and Belgrave Library in Belgrave (10 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Narre Warren East.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Narre Warren East, Emerald has 6 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Narre Warren East, Lysterfield has 8 playgrounds. Next door to Narre Warren East, Narre Warren North has 7 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Narre Warren East, Beaconsfield Upper has 3 playgrounds and a skate park. Harkaway and Belgrave South also border Narre Warren East.
