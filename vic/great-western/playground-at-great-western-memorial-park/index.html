Playground at Great Western Memorial Park

Playground in Great Western · Official details

Playground at Great Western Memorial Park is a public playground in Great Western, in the Northern Grampians Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and buses Ararat to Stawell via Western Hwy, Ballarat - Stawell via Ararat and St Arnaud - Stawell via Ararat stop at Cubitt St/Western Hwy, a 4-minute walk away.
