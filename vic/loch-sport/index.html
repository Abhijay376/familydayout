Loch Sport is in the Wellington area, and our data lists 1 playground and 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Loch Sport is Gippsland Lakes Coastal Park, covering about 17,802 hectares. The Lakes National Park and Charlies Street Reserve are the next largest. Loch Sport also has Point Wilson. Playgrounds around Loch Sport include Lions Park Playground. Our data shows 11 public toilets in Loch Sport. The Cygnet Track and Pelican Point Track all pass through Loch Sport. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Loch Sport for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Loch Sport has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 7 stops in Loch Sport. By car from Loch Sport, Sale is about 67 minutes away and Traralgon about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest beach to Loch Sport is Golden Beach in Golden Beach, about 42 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Loch Sport are Golden Beach Community Centre Playground in Golden Beach (38 min), Golden Beach in Golden Beach (42 min) and Flamingo Beach in Flamingo Beach (53 min).

Best for

Free days on the Cygnet Track and in Loch Sport's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Forge Creek, next to Loch Sport, has a beach. Raymond Island, next to Loch Sport, has 1 playground. Goon Nure also borders Loch Sport.
