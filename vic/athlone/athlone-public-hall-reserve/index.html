Athlone Public Hall Reserve

Park in Athlone · Basic info

Athlone Public Hall Reserve is a park in Athlone, in the Baw Baw Shire Council area, good for all ages. Athlone Primary School/Drouin Poowong Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away.
