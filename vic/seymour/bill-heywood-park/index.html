Bill Heywood Park

Park in Seymour · Basic info

Bill Heywood Park is a park in Seymour, in the Mitchell Shire Council area, good for all ages. Anzac Ave/Hume and Hovell Rd (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 798 m away.
