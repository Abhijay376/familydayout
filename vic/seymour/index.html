Seymour is in the Mitchell area, and our data lists 10 playgrounds and 16 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Seymour has a library, 2 pools and a skate park.

Landmarks

Australian Lighthorse Memorial Park is the largest mapped park in Seymour, at about 140 hectares. Seymour Bushland Reserve and Seymour Police Paddock Reserve/ Goulburn River Water Frontage are the next largest. Seymour also has Kings Park, Lions Park and Rotary Park. Playgrounds around Seymour include the playground at Kings Park, Morison Street Playground and the playground at O'Shea Park. Our data shows 15 public toilets in Seymour. For camping, there's Goulburn River - Tallarook Camping Area 2 (CLWF). In Seymour you'll also find Memorial Swimming pool, Seymour Swimming Centre and Seymour Library. For older kids in Seymour there's Seymour Skate Park. Seymour Plaza is the local shopping centre. The Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks, Seymour Multipurpose Trail and Aircrash walking track all pass through Seymour. Our data lists 10 cafes and family restaurants in Seymour for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Seymour has a train station: Seymour Railway Station (V/Line: Albury - Melbourne via Seymour, Seymour - Melbourne via Broadmeadows and Shepparton - Melbourne via Seymour). Buses 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 stop in Seymour. V/Line coaches stop in Seymour. By car from Seymour, Shepparton is about 63 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 68 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 86 minutes, without traffic.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 10 playgrounds across Seymour. Rainy days at the library in Seymour. Teens who skate or ride, with Seymour Skate Park in Seymour. Free days on the Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks and in Seymour's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Tallarook, next to Seymour, has 1 playground. Avenel, next to Seymour, has 1 playground. Mangalore and Hilldene also border Seymour.
