Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Tanwood, in the Pyrenees area.

Landmarks

Tanwood Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Tanwood, at about 15 hectares.

Getting around

Tanwood has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 33 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tanwood, so most trips start by car. By car from Tanwood, Maryborough is about 33 minutes away, Stawell about 54 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot is about 37 minutes' drive from Tanwood. The closest library to Tanwood is Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough, about 34 minutes' drive. Maryborough is the closest bigger centre to Tanwood, about 33 minutes away by car, and has a library. The closest places for kids by road from Tanwood are the playground at Avoca Lions Park in Avoca (12 min), Courthouse in Avoca (12 min), Stuart Mill Recreation Reserve Playground in Stuart Mill (25 min), Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (37 min), Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (34 min) and Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (39 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tanwood.

Nearby suburbs
