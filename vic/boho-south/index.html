Boho South is in the Strathbogie area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Boho South, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Boho South. The closest to Boho South is Violet Town Railway Station (V/Line), about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Boho South, so most trips start by car. By car from Boho South, Benalla is about 54 minutes away, Mansfield about 70 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Boho South is Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa, about 43 minutes' drive. For a library, Euroa Library in Euroa is about 43 minutes' drive from Boho South. The closest skate park to Boho South is Euroa Skate Park in Euroa, about 42 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Boho South is Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa, about 42 minutes' drive. Benalla is the closest bigger centre to Boho South, about 54 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Boho South are Strathbogie Recreation Reserve Playground in Strathbogie (15 min), Violet Town Recreation Reserve East Playground in Violet Town (33 min), Farmers Arms Hotel Museum in Euroa (42 min), Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa (42 min), Euroa Skate Park in Euroa (42 min) and Euroa Library in Euroa (43 min).
