Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Kamarooka North in the Loddon area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Kamarooka North has no train station; Dingee Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 9.3 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kamarooka North, so most trips start by car. By car from Kamarooka North, Bendigo is about 51 minutes away, Echuca about 61 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kamarooka North is Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood, about 28 minutes' drive. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Kamarooka North, about 51 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Kamarooka North, the nearest places for kids by road are Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood (28 min), the playground at R J Phelan Reserve in Mitiamo (34 min), East Loddon Historical Society in Mitiamo (34 min), Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo (34 min), Campaspe Run Rural Discovery Centre in Elmore (33 min) and the playground at Pennington Reserve in Huntly (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Kamarooka North.
