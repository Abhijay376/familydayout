Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Bolton, in the Swan Hill area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bolton is Bolton Flora and Fauna Reserve, covering about 218 hectares. Myall Bushland Reserve and Bolton Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bolton. The closest to Bolton is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 75 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bolton, so most trips start by car. By car from Bolton, Swan Hill is about 75 minutes away and Mildura about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang is about 10 minutes' drive from Bolton. The closest skate park to Bolton is Robinvale Skatepark in Robinvale, about 39 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Bolton are Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang (10 min), the playground at River Murray Reserve in Wemen (31 min), the playground at Rotary Park in Robinvale (37 min), Rural Life Museum in Robinvale (38 min) and Robinvale Skatepark in Robinvale (39 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bolton.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Bolton, Manangatang has 1 playground and a pool. Annuello also borders Bolton.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Bolton, without traffic.
