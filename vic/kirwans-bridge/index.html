Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Kirwans Bridge in the Strathbogie area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks crosses Kirwans Bridge.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kirwans Bridge. The closest to Kirwans Bridge is Nagambie Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.4 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kirwans Bridge, so most trips start by car. By car from Kirwans Bridge, Seymour is about 37 minutes away, Shepparton about 58 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kirwans Bridge is Nagambie Pool in Nagambie, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest library to Kirwans Bridge is Nagambie Library in Nagambie, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Kirwans Bridge is the skate park at Lions Park in Rushworth, about 40 minutes' drive. Seymour is the closest bigger centre to Kirwans Bridge, about 37 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Kirwans Bridge are the playground at Goulburn River Heritage River in Nagambie (15 min), Nagambie Recreation Reserve Playground in Nagambie (14 min), Nagambie Pool in Nagambie (15 min), Nagambie Historical Society in Nagambie (16 min), Nagambie Library in Nagambie (17 min) and Rushworth Library in Rushworth (39 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Kirwans Bridge on the Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks.

Nearby suburbs

Nagambie, next to Kirwans Bridge, has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Goulburn Weir, next to Kirwans Bridge, has 1 playground. Bailieston also borders Kirwans Bridge.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Kirwans Bridge, without traffic.
