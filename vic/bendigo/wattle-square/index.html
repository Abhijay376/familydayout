Wattle Square

Park in Bendigo · Basic info

Wattle Square is a park in Bendigo, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, good for all ages. Short St/High St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 685 m away with a baby change table.
