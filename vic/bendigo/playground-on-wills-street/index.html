Playground on Wills Street

Playground in Bendigo · Basic info

Playground on Wills Street in Bendigo is a public playground in the Greater Bendigo City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 730 m from the playground, and bus 64 stops at King St/Myrtle St, a 2-minute walk away.
