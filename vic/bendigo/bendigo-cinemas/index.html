Bendigo Cinemas

Cinema in Bendigo · Official details

Bendigo Cinemas is a cinema in Bendigo, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, good for school-age kids and teens. Short St/High St (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 145 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: Peak Prices apply after 5pm Thu, Fri & all day Sat, Sun, & Public Holidays & for Midnight/Advance Screenings; Off Peak prices apply all day Mon, Tue & Wed + before 5pm Thu & Fri; Available Mon-Fri before 5pm.
