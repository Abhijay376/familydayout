Bendigo Library

Library in Bendigo · Basic info

Bendigo Library is a library in Bendigo, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Bull St/Pall Mall (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 124 m away with a baby change table.
