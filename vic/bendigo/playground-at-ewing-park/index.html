Playground at Ewing Park

Playground in Bendigo · Basic info

This is a public playground in Bendigo. The Back Creek Trail passes about 220 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 210 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 220 m away, and buses 62 and 70 stop at Brougham St/Williamson St, a 1-minute walk away.
