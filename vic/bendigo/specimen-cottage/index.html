Specimen Cottage

Museum or gallery in Bendigo · Official details

Specimen Cottage is a museum or gallery in Bendigo, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, best for school-age kids. Mundy St/McCrae St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 285 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: Collection enquiries: Tuesday and Thursday 10am - 2pm; Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays from 10am to 2pm; Thursday 11am – 2pm; Saturday 11am – 2pm.
