Osbornes Flat is in the Indigo area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Osbornes Flat, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Osbornes Flat. The Beechworth to Yackandandah Rail Trail passes through Osbornes Flat.

Getting around

There's no train station in Osbornes Flat. The closest to Osbornes Flat is Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line), about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Osbornes Flat, so most trips start by car. By car from Osbornes Flat, Wodonga is about 27 minutes away and Wangaratta about 58 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Osbornes Flat is Edge Aquatics in Baranduda, about 18 minutes' drive. For a library, Upper Murray Regional Library - Wodonga in Wodonga is about 26 minutes' drive from Osbornes Flat. For a beach, Sandy Beach in Beechworth is about 26 minutes' drive from Osbornes Flat. For a skate park, Skate Park in Yackandandah is about 5 minutes' drive from Osbornes Flat. The closest cinema to Osbornes Flat is Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana, about 22 minutes' drive. Wodonga is the closest bigger centre to Osbornes Flat, about 27 minutes away by car, and has 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Osbornes Flat are the playground at Sir Isaac Isaacs Park in Yackandandah (5 min), Skate Park in Yackandandah (5 min), Skate Park in Baranduda (17 min), Mel Read Park in Baranduda (17 min), Edge Aquatics in Baranduda (18 min) and Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana (22 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Osbornes Flat on the Beechworth to Yackandandah Rail Trail.
