Playground at Pennell Reserve

Playground in Braybrook · Basic info

Playground at Pennell Reserve in Braybrook is a public playground in the Maribyrnong City area. El Jannah is about 350 m away if you need food, and Playground at Duke Street Reserve is another option about 490 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 380 m away, and buses 215, 220 and 410 stop at Braybrook College/Ballarat Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
