Kinder Smith Reserve

Park in Braybrook · Basic info

Kinder Smith Reserve is a park in Braybrook, in the Maribyrnong City Council area, good for all ages. Caroline Chisholm College/Churchill Ave (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 768 m away with a baby change table.
