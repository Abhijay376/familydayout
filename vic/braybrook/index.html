Our data lists 9 playgrounds and 10 parks and reserves in Braybrook, in the Maribyrnong area. On top of that, Braybrook has a pool and an indoor play centre.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Braybrook is Sunshine Railway Line Linear Reserve, covering about 20 hectares. Cranwell Reserve and Braybrook Park are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Braybrook include Ash Reserve, Ruby Square and Kurrong Park. Playgrounds in Braybrook include the playground at Ash Reserve, the playground at Kurrong Park and Lance Reichstein Playground. Our data shows 5 public toilets and 1 off-leash dog park in Braybrook. Places to visit in Braybrook include Zone Bowling and Paul Sadler Swimland Braybrook. Braybrook Plaza, Central West Shopping Centre and HomeCo are the main shopping centres. The Sunshine Trail and Sunshine Footscray Trail all pass through Braybrook. Our data lists 3 cafes and family restaurants in Braybrook for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Braybrook. The closest to Braybrook is Tottenham Railway Station (metro), about 1.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 215, 216, 220, 408, 410 and s3700o stop in Braybrook. By car from Braybrook, Sunshine is about 4 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 21 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 20 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Braybrook is Sunshine Library in Sunshine, about 3 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Braybrook is Altona Dog Beach in Seaholme, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Braybrook is Sunshine Plaza Skatepark in Sunshine, about 3 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Sunshine is about 4 minutes' drive from Braybrook. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Braybrook is Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville, about 15 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 9 playgrounds across Braybrook. Rainy days at the indoor play centre in Braybrook. Free days on the Sunshine Trail and in Braybrook's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Sunshine, next to Braybrook, has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Braybrook, Avondale Heights has 13 playgrounds and a library. West Footscray, next to Braybrook, has 6 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Sunshine North, next to Braybrook, has 6 playgrounds. Maidstone and Tottenham also border Braybrook.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Braybrook, without traffic.
