Zone Bowling

Indoor play in Braybrook · Official details

Zone Bowling is a indoor play in Braybrook, in the Maribyrnong City Council area, best for younger kids (check the venue's age and height rules). Central West SC/Ashley St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 180 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: Wed, Thu 09:00-21:00; Fri, Sun 09:00-22:00; Sat 09:00-23:00; Mon, Tue 10:00-21:00.
