Central West Shopping Centre

Shopping centre in Braybrook · Basic info

Central West Shopping Centre is a shopping centre in Braybrook, in the Maribyrnong City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Central West SC/Ashley St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 105 m away with a baby change table.
