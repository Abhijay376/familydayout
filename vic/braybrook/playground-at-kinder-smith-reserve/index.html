Playground at Kinder Smith Reserve

Playground in Braybrook · Basic info

This is a public playground in Braybrook. Paul Sadler Swimland Braybrook is about 80 m away, and Playground at Ash Reserve is another option about 390 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 720 m from the playground, and buses 408 and 410 stop at Caroline Chisholm College/Churchill Ave, a 5-minute walk away.
