Bundalaguah is in the Wellington area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bundalaguah, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Bundalaguah has no train station; Sale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 9.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bundalaguah, so most trips start by car. By car from Bundalaguah, Sale is about 9 minutes away, Traralgon about 45 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bundalaguah is Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest library to Bundalaguah is Sale Library in Sale, about 10 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Sale Skate Park in Sale is about 11 minutes' drive from Bundalaguah. For a cinema, Former Drive-in site in Sale is about 9 minutes' drive from Bundalaguah. Sale is the closest bigger centre to Bundalaguah, about 9 minutes away by car, and has 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Bundalaguah are Mafeking Hill Reserve Playground in Maffra (6 min), Fred Drury Reserve Playground in Maffra (6 min), Former Drive-in site in Sale (9 min), Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford (10 min), Sale Historical Museum in Sale (10 min) and Old train signals in Sale (10 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bundalaguah.

Nearby suburbs

Stratford, next to Bundalaguah, has 8 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Bundalaguah, Maffra has 6 playgrounds. Montgomery and Fulham also border Bundalaguah.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Bundalaguah, without traffic.
