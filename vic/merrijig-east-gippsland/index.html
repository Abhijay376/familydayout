Merrijig (East Gippsland) is in the East Gippsland area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Merrijig (East Gippsland) is Melwood Education Area, covering about 232 hectares.

Getting around

Merrijig (East Gippsland) has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Merrijig (East Gippsland), so most trips start by car. By car from Merrijig (East Gippsland), Bairnsdale is about 64 minutes away and Sale about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest places for kids by road from Merrijig (East Gippsland) are the playground at Amy Shand Park in Lindenow (46 min), Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale (64 min), Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale (65 min), Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale (66 min), Krowathunkoolong Keeping Place in Bairnsdale (66 min) and Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale (65 min).

Best for
