Brookville is in the East Gippsland area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Brookville, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Brookville has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 56 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Brookville, so most trips start by car. By car from Brookville, Bairnsdale is about 2 hours away and Lakes Entrance about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Brookville is Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek, about 37 minutes' drive. From Brookville, the nearest places for kids by road are Great Alpine Gallery in Swifts Creek (36 min), Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek (37 min), Petersens Art Gallery & Museum in Omeo (57 min), the playground at Omeo Historical Park & Museum in Omeo (58 min) and the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo (58 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Brookville.

Nearby suburbs

Swifts Creek, next to Brookville, has 1 playground and a pool. Ensay, next to Brookville, has 1 playground. Marthavale and Doctors Flat also border Brookville.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Brookville, without traffic.
