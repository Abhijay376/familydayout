French Island National Park

Park in French Island · Official details

French Island National Park is a park in French Island, in the Bass Coast Shire Council area, good for all ages.

Details

Phone
(03) 5257 4565 (Western Port Ferries) (Parks Victoria)
Seasons
Winter whales and penguins; spring wildflowers and migratory birds (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
Ferry from Stony Point to Tankerton Jetty takes 10 to 12 minutes; services can be cancelled at short notice; Bring insect repellent, Ross River virus on island (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is French Island National Park?
French Island National Park is in French Island, Victoria.
Is French Island National Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
