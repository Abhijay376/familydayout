Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bullarto South in the Moorabool area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bullarto South. The closest to Bullarto South is Ballan Railway Station (V/Line), about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bullarto South, so most trips start by car. By car from Bullarto South, Daylesford is about 15 minutes away, Kyneton about 37 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford is about 14 minutes' drive from Bullarto South. The closest library to Bullarto South is Daylesford Library in Daylesford, about 14 minutes' drive. For a beach, Canoeing in Tylden is about 31 minutes' drive from Bullarto South. The closest skate park to Bullarto South is the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan, about 37 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek is about 42 minutes' drive from Bullarto South. The nearest larger centre is Daylesford, about 15 minutes from Bullarto South by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Bullarto South, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at J P Gleeson Memorial Park in Lyonville (7 min), Daylesford Spa Country Railway in Daylesford (14 min), Daylesford Library in Daylesford (14 min), Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford (14 min), the playground at Quarry Street Reserve in Trentham (16 min) and Little Gallery in Trentham (16 min).

Best for
