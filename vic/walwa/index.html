Our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in Walwa, in the Towong area. Walwa also has a museum or gallery and a beach.

Landmarks

River Murray Reserve (non-PV) is the largest mapped park in Walwa, at about 184 hectares. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Walwa. Places to visit in Walwa include The Spotted Goanna and Pebble Beach.

Getting around

Walwa has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 74 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Albury - Corryong via Walwa route stop in Walwa. By car from Walwa, Wodonga is about 1.5 hours away and Wangaratta about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Walwa is Corryong Public swimming pool in Corryong, about 41 minutes' drive. For a library, Corryong Elyne Mitchell Library in Corryong is about 40 minutes' drive from Walwa. For a cinema, Corryong Cinema in Corryong is about 40 minutes' drive from Walwa. From Walwa, the nearest places for kids by road are Corryong Cinema in Corryong (40 min), Corryong Elyne Mitchell Library in Corryong (40 min) and Corryong Public swimming pool in Corryong (41 min).
