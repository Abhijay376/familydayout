Dunrobin is in the Glenelg area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Dunrobin, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Dunrobin. The closest to Dunrobin is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 135 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dunrobin, so most trips start by car. By car from Dunrobin, Hamilton is about 61 minutes away and Portland about 87 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Dunrobin is Casterton Pool in Casterton, about 10 minutes' drive. For a skate park, coleraine skate park in Coleraine is about 33 minutes' drive from Dunrobin. From Dunrobin, the nearest places for kids by road are Casterton Pool in Casterton (10 min), Gorman's art gallery in Casterton (10 min), Merino Swimming Pool in Merino (28 min), the playground at Lions Park in Merino (28 min), coleraine skate park in Coleraine (33 min) and the playground at Queens Park in Coleraine (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Dunrobin.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Dunrobin, Casterton has a pool. Next door to Dunrobin, Wando Vale has 1 playground. Corndale also borders Dunrobin.
