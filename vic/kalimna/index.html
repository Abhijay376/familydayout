Getting around

Kalimna has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 30 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 1 and 12 stop in Kalimna. V/Line coaches stop in Kalimna. By car from Kalimna, Lakes Entrance is about 8 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 33 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Lakes Entrance Aquatic Centre in Lakes Entrance is about 10 minutes' drive from Kalimna. The closest library to Kalimna is Lakes Entrance Library in Lakes Entrance, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Kalimna is Ninety Mile Beach in Lakes Entrance, about 8 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Gilsenan Reserve in Paynesville is about 45 minutes' drive from Kalimna. For a cinema, Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale is about 31 minutes' drive from Kalimna. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow is about 25 minutes' drive from Kalimna. Lakes Entrance is the closest bigger centre to Kalimna, about 8 minutes away by car, and has 3 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Kalimna are Griffiths Sea Shell Museum in Lakes Entrance (4 min), Lakes Entrance Library in Lakes Entrance (5 min), Ninety Mile Beach in Lakes Entrance (8 min), Lakes Entrance Aquatic Centre in Lakes Entrance (10 min), Bronsdon Street Park in Lakes Entrance (12 min) and 90 Mile Beach in Lakes Entrance (10 min).
