Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Clover Flat in the Southern Grampians area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Clover Flat. The closest to Clover Flat is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 118 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Clover Flat, so most trips start by car. By car from Clover Flat, Hamilton is about 40 minutes away and Portland about 87 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Casterton Pool in Casterton is about 17 minutes' drive from Clover Flat. For a library, Hamilton Library in Hamilton is about 40 minutes' drive from Clover Flat. The closest skate park to Clover Flat is coleraine skate park in Coleraine, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Clover Flat is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 40 minutes' drive. Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Clover Flat, about 40 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Clover Flat are coleraine skate park in Coleraine (12 min), the playground at Queens Park in Coleraine (13 min), Silvester Oval Playground in Coleraine (13 min), Gorman's art gallery in Casterton (16 min), Casterton Pool in Casterton (17 min) and Merino Swimming Pool in Merino (29 min).
