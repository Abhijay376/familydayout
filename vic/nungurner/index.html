Nungurner in the East Gippsland area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Nungurner Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Nungurner, at about 4 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Nungurner include Nungurner Foreshore Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Nungurner.

Getting around

There's no train station in Nungurner. The closest to Nungurner is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nungurner, so most trips start by car. By car from Nungurner, Lakes Entrance is about 23 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 36 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Lakes Entrance Aquatic Centre in Lakes Entrance is about 25 minutes' drive from Nungurner. The closest library to Nungurner is Lakes Entrance Library in Lakes Entrance, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Nungurner is 90 Mile Beach in Boole Poole, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Nungurner is Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale, about 34 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow is about 28 minutes' drive from Nungurner. The nearest larger centre is Lakes Entrance, about 23 minutes from Nungurner by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Nungurner are the playground at Nyerimilang Heritage Park in Nyerimilang (16 min), 90 Mile Beach in Boole Poole (14 min), Griffiths Sea Shell Museum in Lakes Entrance (19 min), Lakes Entrance Library in Lakes Entrance (20 min), Ninety Mile Beach in Lakes Entrance (22 min) and Lakes Entrance Aquatic Centre in Lakes Entrance (25 min).
