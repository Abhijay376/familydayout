Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bungador in the Colac Otway area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Bungador has no train station; Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bungador, so most trips start by car. By car from Bungador, Colac is about 31 minutes away, Warrnambool about 81 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon is about 45 minutes' drive from Bungador. For a library, Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac is about 34 minutes' drive from Bungador. For a skate park, the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt is about 36 minutes' drive from Bungador. For a cinema, Colac Cinemas in Colac is about 32 minutes' drive from Bungador. The closest indoor play centre to Bungador is Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac, about 32 minutes' drive. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Bungador, about 31 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Bungador, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (30 min), the playground at Western Bay Playground in Colac (31 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (32 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (32 min), Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (34 min) and the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (36 min).
