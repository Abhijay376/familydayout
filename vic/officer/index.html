Our data lists 14 playgrounds and 9 parks and reserves in Officer, in the Cardinia area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Officer is Beaconsfield Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 168 hectares. Pakenham G71 Bushland Reserve and Officer District Park are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Officer include Olio Park, Dragon Park and Hicks Reserve. Playgrounds in Officer include Parkour Station, the playground at Dragon Park and Hedgevale Drive Playground. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Officer. Arena Shopping Centre is the local shopping centre. The Cardinia Aqueduct Trail passes through Officer. Our data lists 15 cafes and family restaurants in Officer for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Officer has a train station: Officer Railway Station (metro: Pakenham Line). Buses on the 837, 925, 926 and 928 routes stop in Officer. By car from Officer, Pakenham is about 8 minutes away, Cranbourne about 18 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 43 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Officer is Pakenham Outdoor Swimming Pool in Pakenham, about 8 minutes' drive. For a library, Berwick Library in Berwick is about 8 minutes' drive from Officer. The closest beach to Officer is Carrum Beach in Carrum, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Officer is the bmx track at Kath Roberts Reserve in Beaconsfield, about 5 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Narre Warren is about 12 minutes' drive from Officer. For an indoor play centre, Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham is about 11 minutes' drive from Officer. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Officer is MonkeyG's in Pakenham, about 9 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 14 playgrounds across Officer. Free days on the Cardinia Aqueduct Trail and in Officer's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Officer, Berwick has 58 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Pakenham, next to Officer, has 46 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Clyde North, next to Officer, has 34 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Beaconsfield, next to Officer, has 8 playgrounds and a skate park. Beaconsfield Upper and Officer South also border Officer.
