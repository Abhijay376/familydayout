Playground at Comely Banks Recreation Reserve

Playground in Officer · Basic info

Playground at Comely Banks Recreation Reserve is a public playground in Officer, in the Cardinia Shire area. Flaxlilly Crescent Playground is another option about 240 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 650 m from the playground, and bus 928 stops at Bridgewood Primary School/Bridge Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
