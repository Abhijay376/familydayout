Playground on Graham Place

Playground in Box Hill · Basic info

This is a public playground in Box Hill. The Whitehorse EW2-A passes about 300 m away, and Box Hill Library is about 530 m away. There's a public toilet about 600 m away, and bus 270 stops at Whitehorse Rd/Dorking Rd, a 7-minute walk away.
