Bolton Park Playground

Playground in Box Hill · Basic info

Bolton Park Playground in Box Hill is a public playground in the Whitehorse City area. The Whitehorse EW2-A passes about 370 m away, and Playground on Hillside Crescent is another option about 530 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 450 m away, and bus 279 stops at Davey St/Middleborough Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
