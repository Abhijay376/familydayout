Box Hill Central

Shopping centre in Box Hill · Basic info

Box Hill Central is a shopping centre in Box Hill, in the Whitehorse City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Box Hill Bus Station/Station St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 483 m away with a baby change table.
