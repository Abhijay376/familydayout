Playground on Prospect Street

Playground in Box Hill · Basic info

Playground on Prospect Street is a public playground in Box Hill, in the Whitehorse City area. The Whitehorse NS2 is about 90 m away, and for food, Phoenix Kitchen is about 10 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 360 m from the playground, and tram 109 stops at Nelson Rd/Whitehorse Rd #57, a 4-minute walk away.
