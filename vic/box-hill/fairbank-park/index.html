Fairbank Park

Park in Box Hill · Basic info

Fairbank Park is a park in Box Hill, in the Whitehorse City Council area, good for all ages. Nelson Rd/Whitehorse Rd #57 (tram) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 227 m away with a baby change table.
