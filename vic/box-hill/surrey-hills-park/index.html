Surrey Hills Park

Playground in Box Hill · Basic info

Surrey Hills Park in Box Hill is a public playground in the Whitehorse City area. The Whitehorse NS2 is about 210 m away, and Aqualink Box Hill is about 230 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus 767 stops at Surrey Park/Elgar Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
