Playground at Linsley Park

Playground in Box Hill · Basic info

Playground at Linsley Park is a public playground in Box Hill, in the Whitehorse City area. The Box Hill to Ringwood Trail passes about 50 m away, and Box Hill Library is about 100 m away. There's a public toilet about 60 m away, and bus 733 stops at William St/Rutland Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
