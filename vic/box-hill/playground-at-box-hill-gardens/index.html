Playground at Box Hill Gardens

Playground in Box Hill · Basic info

Playground at Box Hill Gardens in Box Hill is a public playground in the Whitehorse City area. The Whitehorse NS2 is about 20 m away, and for food, Nambo is about 340 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 140 m from the playground, and buses 281, 293 and 302 stop at Box Hill Hospital/Nelson Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
