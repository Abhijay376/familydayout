Woolamai is in the Bass Coast area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Woolamai, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Bass Coast Rail Trail and Gippsland Odyssey Extension - Woolamai to Nyora (Proposed) cross Woolamai.

Getting around

There's no train station in Woolamai. The closest to Woolamai is Stony Point Railway Station (metro), about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Woolamai, so most trips start by car. By car from Woolamai, Wonthaggi is about 33 minutes away, Pakenham about 59 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Woolamai is Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Wonthaggi, about 31 minutes' drive. For a library, San Remo Library in San Remo is about 20 minutes' drive from Woolamai. For a beach, Shelly Beach in Kilcunda is about 19 minutes' drive from Woolamai. For a skate park, the skate park at Wonthaggi Railway Historic Reserve in Kilcunda is about 20 minutes' drive from Woolamai. For a cinema, Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi is about 31 minutes' drive from Woolamai. For a zoo or wildlife park, Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville is about 21 minutes' drive from Woolamai. Wonthaggi is the closest bigger centre to Woolamai, about 33 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Woolamai, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at George Bass Memorial Park in Bass (13 min), Art Fusion in Anderson (16 min), Shelly Beach in Kilcunda (19 min), Punchbowl in San Remo (20 min), the skate park at Wonthaggi Railway Historic Reserve in Kilcunda (20 min) and the playground at Wonthaggi Railway Historic Reserve in Kilcunda (20 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Woolamai on the Bass Coast Rail Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Kilcunda, next to Woolamai, has 1 playground, a beach and a skate park. Next door to Woolamai, Dalyston has 1 playground and a beach. Next door to Woolamai, Bass has 1 playground and a zoo or wildlife park. Glen Forbes and Anderson also border Woolamai.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Woolamai, without traffic.
