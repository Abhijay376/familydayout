Boigbeat in the Buloke area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Boigbeat is Boigbeat I158 Bushland Reserve, covering about 10 hectares.

Getting around

Boigbeat has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 64 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Boigbeat, so most trips start by car. By car from Boigbeat, Swan Hill is about 58 minutes away and Horsham about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake is about 8 minutes' drive from Boigbeat. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Boigbeat, about 58 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Boigbeat, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Apex Park in Sea Lake (8 min), Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake (8 min), Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock (9 min) and the playground at Culgoa Memorial Park in Culgoa (19 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Boigbeat.

Nearby suburbs

Sea Lake, next to Boigbeat, has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Boigbeat, Berriwillock has a pool.
