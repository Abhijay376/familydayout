Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Port Albert, in the Wellington area. On top of that, Port Albert has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Port Albert is Nooramunga Marine and Coastal Park, covering about 23,092 hectares. Alberton East Flora and Fauna Nature Conservation Reserve and Memorial Park are the next largest. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Port Albert. In Port Albert you'll also find Port Albert Maritime Museum. For walks and rides, Great Southern Rail Trail, Tarra Trail and Christopher Robinson Walking Trail cross Port Albert. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Port Albert for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Port Albert. The closest to Port Albert is Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line), about 54 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Port Albert, so most trips start by car. By car from Port Albert, Sale is about 70 minutes away, Leongatha about 72 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Port Albert is Yarram Public Pool in Yarram, about 13 minutes' drive. For a library, Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram is about 12 minutes' drive from Port Albert. For a beach, Chinaman Beach in Wilsons Promontory is about 33 minutes' drive from Port Albert. The closest skate park to Port Albert is the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Port Albert are the playground at Memorial Park in Yarram (12 min), Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram (12 min), Yarram Public Pool in Yarram (13 min), the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram (14 min), Yarram Historical Society Museum in Yarram (14 min) and Chinaman Beach in Wilsons Promontory (33 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Port Albert. Free days on the Great Southern Rail Trail and in Port Albert's parks.
