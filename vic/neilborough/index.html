Neilborough is in the Greater Bendigo area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Neilborough, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Neilborough has no train station; Raywood Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 5.0 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Neilborough, so most trips start by car. By car from Neilborough, Bendigo is about 29 minutes away, Castlemaine about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood is about 11 minutes' drive from Neilborough. For a library, Bendigo Library in Bendigo is about 28 minutes' drive from Neilborough. For a skate park, McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk is about 21 minutes' drive from Neilborough. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 30 minutes' drive from Neilborough. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 31 minutes' drive from Neilborough. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Neilborough is The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo, about 31 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 29 minutes from Neilborough by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Neilborough are Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood (11 min), McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk (21 min), Peter Krenz Leisure Centre in Eaglehawk (21 min), Eaglehawk Playspace in Eaglehawk (21 min), the playground at Canterbury Park in Eaglehawk (22 min) and the bmx track on Victoria Street in Eaglehawk (22 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Neilborough.

Nearby suburbs

Raywood, next to Neilborough, has 1 playground and a pool. Kamarooka and Huntly North also border Neilborough.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Neilborough, without traffic.
