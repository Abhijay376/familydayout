Our data lists 1 playground and 7 parks and reserves in Stuart Mill, in the Northern Grampians area.

Landmarks

Stuart Mill Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Stuart Mill, at about 2,588 hectares. Sanstrom Block Conservation Reserve and John Colahan Griffin Conservation Reserve are the next largest. Stuart Mill also has Stuart Mill Bushland Reserve, Boola Boloke Bushland Reserve and Stuart Mill Streamside Reserve. Playgrounds around Stuart Mill include Stuart Mill Recreation Reserve Playground. Our data shows 1 public toilet and 1 park or playground with a council BBQ close by in Stuart Mill.

Getting around

Stuart Mill has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 49 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Stuart Mill, so most trips start by car. By car from Stuart Mill, Maryborough is about 47 minutes away, Stawell about 67 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud is about 19 minutes' drive from Stuart Mill. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 47 minutes from Stuart Mill by car, with a library. The closest places for kids by road from Stuart Mill are Bi-Centennial Park Playground in St Arnaud (17 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (19 min) and Courthouse in Avoca (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Stuart Mill.

Nearby suburbs

Dalyenong, Carapooee West, Carapooee and Beazleys Bridge also border Stuart Mill.
