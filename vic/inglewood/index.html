Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Inglewood, in the Loddon area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Inglewood is Inglewood Bushland Reserve, covering about 154 hectares. Distillery Park and Museum and Inglewood Botanical Gardens are the next largest. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Inglewood.

Getting around

There's no train station in Inglewood. The closest to Inglewood is Raywood Railway Station (V/Line), about 30 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Bendigo - Boort via Wedderburn route stop in Inglewood. V/Line coaches stop in Inglewood. By car from Inglewood, Bendigo is about 40 minutes away, Castlemaine about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn is about 23 minutes' drive from Inglewood. For a library, Kangaroo Flat Library in Kangaroo Flat is about 38 minutes' drive from Inglewood. For a skate park, Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk is about 36 minutes' drive from Inglewood. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 41 minutes' drive from Inglewood. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 40 minutes' drive from Inglewood. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 45 minutes' drive from Inglewood. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 40 minutes from Inglewood by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Inglewood are Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (23 min), Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (23 min), Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood (32 min), Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (33 min), Westbury Boulevard Playspace in Maiden Gully (34 min) and Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk (36 min).
