Yalla-Y-Poora is in the Ararat area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Yalla-Y-Poora, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Yalla-Y-Poora has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 31 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yalla-Y-Poora, so most trips start by car. By car from Yalla-Y-Poora, Ararat is about 46 minutes away, Stawell about 73 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Yalla-Y-Poora is Ararat Library in Ararat, about 45 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Yalla-Y-Poora is Astor Cinema in Ararat, about 45 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Ararat, about 46 minutes from Yalla-Y-Poora by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Yalla-Y-Poora are the playground at Simpson Park in Streatham (22 min), Ararat Library in Ararat (45 min), Astor Cinema in Ararat (45 min), Langi Morgala Museum in Ararat (45 min), Ararat Gallery TAMA in Ararat (46 min) and Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort (53 min).
