Cocoroc is on the south west side of Wyndham. Our data has no playgrounds, cafes or bus stops inside it, so this guide is mostly about what's close by. Part of Werribee Regional Park, about 275 hectares, lies within the suburb, and the Werribee River Trail passes through.

Landmarks

Werribee Regional Park is the main green space. The Werribee River Trail links Cocoroc with Werribee and Werribee South, so it's a route for longer family bike rides rather than a place to stop and play. Our map data also shows a beach named Werribee Sewer Farm South, but we haven't listed it because we can't confirm it's open to the public.

Getting around

There's no train station or bus route in Cocoroc. Werribee Station on the metro Werribee line is about 9 kilometres from the suburb's centre point in a straight line. By car, Werribee is about 24 minutes away and the Melbourne CBD about 52 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest playgrounds by road are in Little River, at Possy Newland Reserve and Little River Oval, about 19 minutes' drive away. Werribee Outdoor Pool and Chirnside Park Playground are about 25 to 27 minutes away. Werribee Open Range Zoo is about 29 minutes' drive, and Southern Beach in Werribee South is about 31 minutes.

Best for

Longer bike rides on the Werribee River Trail. A starting point for a day that takes in Werribee, the zoo and the Werribee South beaches.

Nearby suburbs

Werribee has the most for kids nearby, with 58 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a gallery. Werribee South is where you'll find the beaches, Werribee Open Range Zoo and Werribee Park. Little River has the closest playgrounds.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Cocoroc, without traffic.
