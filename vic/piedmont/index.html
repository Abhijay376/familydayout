Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Piedmont in the Baw Baw area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Piedmont has no train station; Drouin Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Piedmont, so most trips start by car. By car from Piedmont, Warragul is about 31 minutes away, Healesville about 55 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Piedmont is Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul, about 33 minutes' drive. For a library, Warragul Library in Warragul is about 31 minutes' drive from Piedmont. For a skate park, Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South is about 12 minutes' drive from Piedmont. The closest cinema to Piedmont is Warragul Cinema in Warragul, about 31 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 32 minutes' drive from Piedmont. The nearest larger centre is Warragul, about 31 minutes from Piedmont by car, with 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Piedmont, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at LaTrobe and Loch Rivers, Noojee Streamside Reserve in Noojee (5 min), Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South (12 min), the playground at Ellen Close Linear Reserve in Warragul (30 min), Warragul Library in Warragul (31 min), Warragul Cinema in Warragul (31 min) and The Alley Warragul in Warragul (32 min).

Best for
