Mittyack is in the Mildura area, and our data lists 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Mittyack is Woornack I110 Bushland Reserve, covering about 143 hectares. Mittyack Bushland Reserve and Woornack I247 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Mittyack include Mittyack Patching Road Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mittyack. The closest to Mittyack is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 85 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mittyack, so most trips start by car. By car from Mittyack, Swan Hill is about 75 minutes away and Mildura about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Ouyen Swimming Pool in Ouyen is about 27 minutes' drive from Mittyack. The closest cinema to Mittyack is Roxy in Ouyen, about 28 minutes' drive. From Mittyack, the nearest places for kids by road are Ouyen Swimming Pool in Ouyen (27 min), Roxy in Ouyen (28 min), Jardine Park Playground in Ouyen (28 min), the playground at Ouyen Lake Park in Ouyen (30 min) and Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mittyack.

Nearby suburbs

Ouyen, next to Mittyack, has 4 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. Tempy, next to Mittyack, has 1 playground and a pool. Next door to Mittyack, Manangatang has 1 playground and a pool. Kulwin and Gerahmin also border Mittyack.
