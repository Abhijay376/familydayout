Croxton East in the Southern Grampians area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Croxton East is Lake Linlithgow and Bullrush Swamp Lake Reserve, covering about 1,189 hectares. Lake Kennedy Wildlife Reserve (hunting) and Krause Swamp Wildlife Reserve (no hunting) are the next largest. Our data shows 3 off-leash dog parks in Croxton East.

Getting around

Croxton East has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 69 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Croxton East, so most trips start by car. By car from Croxton East, Hamilton is about 24 minutes away and Warrnambool about 73 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Penshurst Swimming Pool in Penshurst is about 16 minutes' drive from Croxton East. For a library, Hamilton Library in Hamilton is about 23 minutes' drive from Croxton East. For a skate park, hamilton skate park in Hamilton is about 22 minutes' drive from Croxton East. For a cinema, Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton is about 23 minutes' drive from Croxton East. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 24 minutes from Croxton East by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Croxton East are Penshurst Botanic Gardens Playground in Penshurst (16 min), Penshurst Swimming Pool in Penshurst (16 min), Volcanoes Discovery Centre in Penshurst (17 min), Penshurst Rec Reserve Playground in Penshurst (18 min), Sir Reginald Ansett Transport Museum in Hamilton (22 min) and hamilton skate park in Hamilton (22 min).
