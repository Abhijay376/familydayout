Daisy Hill in the Central Goldfields area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Daisy Hill is Craigie State Forest, covering about 818 hectares. Daisy Hill also has Battery Dam Historic Site. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Daisy Hill. Picnic areas in Daisy Hill include Battery Dam Historic Site. The Talbot Heritage Trail, Cycle Central Goldfields and Bull Gully Rock Wells Walk all pass through Daisy Hill.

Getting around

Daisy Hill has no train station; Talbot Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 6.2 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Ballarat - Maryborough route stop in Daisy Hill. By car from Daisy Hill, Maryborough is about 9 minutes away, Ballarat about 51 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Daisy Hill is Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest library to Daisy Hill is Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough, about 10 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Alexander Park in Learmonth is about 35 minutes' drive from Daisy Hill. Maryborough is the closest bigger centre to Daisy Hill, about 9 minutes away by car, and has a library. The closest places for kids by road from Daisy Hill are Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground in Talbot (7 min), Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (8 min), Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (10 min), the playground at Market Reserve in Carisbrook (16 min), Lee Medlyn Bottle Museum in Clunes (21 min) and The Warehouse in Clunes (22 min).

Best for

Free days on the Talbot Heritage Trail and in Daisy Hill's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Talbot, next to Daisy Hill, has 1 playground and a pool. Maryborough, next to Daisy Hill, has a library. Craigie, Amherst and Adelaide Lead also border Daisy Hill.
