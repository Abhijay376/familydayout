Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Havilah in the Alpine area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Havilah has no train station; Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 57 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Havilah, so most trips start by car. By car from Havilah, Bright is about 23 minutes away and Wangaratta about 68 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Havilah is Bright Sports Centre in Bright, about 22 minutes' drive. For a library, Bright Library in Bright is about 24 minutes' drive from Havilah. For a skate park, Bright BMX Track in Bright is about 26 minutes' drive from Havilah. For a cinema, Sun Cinema Bright in Bright is about 23 minutes' drive from Havilah. The nearest larger centre is Bright, about 23 minutes from Havilah by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Havilah are the playground at Riverside Park in Porepunkah (17 min), Bright Sports Centre in Bright (22 min), the playground at Centenary Park in Bright (23 min), Bright River Pool in Bright (23 min), Bright Museum in Bright (23 min) and Sun Cinema Bright in Bright (23 min).
