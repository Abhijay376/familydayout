Peterborough is in the Corangamite area, and our data lists 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in the suburb. Peterborough also has 3 beaches.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Peterborough is Peterborough Coastal Reserve, covering about 341 hectares. Peterborough Coastal Reserve (COM) is the next largest. Playgrounds in Peterborough include the playground at Peterborough Coastal Reserve (COM). Our data shows 1 public toilet in Peterborough. Places to visit in Peterborough include Falls of Halladale and Wild Dog Cove 5.

Getting around

There's no train station in Peterborough. The closest to Peterborough is Terang Railway Station (V/Line), about 41 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Warrnambool - Port Campbell - Timboon via Allansford, Nullawarre, Peterborough route stop in Peterborough. V/Line coaches stop in Peterborough. By car from Peterborough, Warrnambool is about 45 minutes away and Colac about 81 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Peterborough is Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon, about 18 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell is about 12 minutes' drive from Peterborough. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Peterborough is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 38 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 45 minutes from Peterborough by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Peterborough, the nearest places for kids by road are Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell (12 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (18 min), the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (18 min) and Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (38 min).

Best for

Beach days at Falls of Halladale and Wild Dog Cove 5.

Nearby suburbs

Port Campbell, next to Peterborough, has 1 playground, a beach and a skate park. Next door to Peterborough, Nirranda South has a beach.
