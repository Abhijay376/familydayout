Peterborough Coastal Reserve (COM)

Park in Peterborough · Basic info

Peterborough Coastal Reserve (COM) is a park in Peterborough, in the Moyne Shire Council area, good for all ages. Peterborough General Store/Macs St (bus) is a 10-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 802 m away with a baby change table.
