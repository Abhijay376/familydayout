Playground at Peterborough Coastal Reserve (COM)

Playground in Peterborough · Basic info

This is a public playground in Peterborough. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 90 m from the playground, and bus Warrnambool - Port Campbell - Timboon via Allansford, Nullawarre, Peterborough stops at Peterborough General Store/Macs St , a 8-minute walk away.
