Our data lists 1 playground and 9 parks and reserves in Boort, in the Loddon area. On top of that, Boort has a pool and a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

Lake Boort Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Boort, at about 502 hectares. Woolshed Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting) and Lake Lyndger Wildlife Reserve (hunting) are the next largest. Boort also has Historic Park, Boort Showgrounds and Boort Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds around Boort include A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Boort. In Boort you'll also find Boort Court House Historical Museum and Boort Swimming Pool. The Federation Walk passes through Boort.

Getting around

Boort has no train station; Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 35 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Bendigo - Boort via Wedderburn route stop in Boort. V/Line coaches stop in Boort. By car from Boort, Swan Hill is about 86 minutes away and Bendigo about 87 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 38 minutes' drive from Boort. The closest places for kids by road from Boort are the playground at Lake Meran W.R in Lake Meran (27 min), Pyramid Hill & Districts Historical Museum in Pyramid Hill (35 min), Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof (44 min) and Rex Theatre in Charlton (38 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Boort. Free days on the Federation Walk and in Boort's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Minmindie, Fernihurst, Durham Ox and Catumnal also border Boort.
