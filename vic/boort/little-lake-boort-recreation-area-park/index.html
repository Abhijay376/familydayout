Little Lake Boort Recreation Area Park

Park in Boort · Basic info

Little Lake Boort Recreation Area Park is a park in Boort, in the Loddon Shire Council area, good for all ages. Boort/Godfrey St (coach) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 629 m away with a baby change table.
