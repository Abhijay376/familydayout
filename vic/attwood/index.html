Attwood in the Hume area has 3 playgrounds and 5 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Attwood is Attwood Creek Linear Reserve, covering about 7 hectares. Attwood Creek Linear Reserve is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Attwood include Attwood Creek Reserve, Attwood Creek Reserve and Tollkeepers Parade Reserve. Playgrounds around Attwood include the playground at Tollkeepers Parade Reserve. For walks and rides, Moonee Ponds Creek Trail, Attwood Creek Trail and Pipe Track cross Attwood.

Getting around

There's no train station in Attwood. The closest to Attwood is Broadmeadows Railway Station (metro), about 3.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 484 stops in Attwood. By car from Attwood, Broadmeadows is about 7 minutes away, Sunshine about 16 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 25 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Tullamarine Swim School in Tullamarine is about 6 minutes' drive from Attwood. For a library, Library in Westmeadows is about 4 minutes' drive from Attwood. The closest beach to Attwood is Horse Arena in Abbotsford, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Attwood is the skate park at Barrymore Road Recreational Reserve in Greenvale, about 4 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Attwood is Hoyts in Broadmeadows, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Attwood is Timezone in Craigieburn, about 14 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville is about 20 minutes' drive from Attwood. The nearest larger centre is Broadmeadows, about 7 minutes from Attwood by car, with 12 playgrounds, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Attwood's 3 local playgrounds. Free days on the Moonee Ponds Creek Trail and in Attwood's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Attwood, Greenvale has 21 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Attwood, Westmeadows has 10 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Attwood, Meadow Heights has 8 playgrounds. Melbourne Airport, next to Attwood, has a pool.
