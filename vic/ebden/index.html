Our data lists 3 playgrounds and 2 parks and reserves in Ebden, in the Wodonga area.

Landmarks

Ebden Reserve is the largest mapped park in Ebden, at about 3 hectares. Ludlows Reserve is the next largest. Playgrounds around Ebden include the playground at Ludlows Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Ebden. For walks and rides, High Country Rail Trail crosses Ebden.

Getting around

Ebden has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Ebden, so most trips start by car. By car from Ebden, Wodonga is about 15 minutes away and Wangaratta about 67 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, WAVES in Wodonga is about 13 minutes' drive from Ebden. For a library, Upper Murray Regional Library - Wodonga in Wodonga is about 14 minutes' drive from Ebden. The closest beach to Ebden is Finns Beach in Bandiana, about 20 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Skate Park in Baranduda is about 13 minutes' drive from Ebden. The closest cinema to Ebden is Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana, about 10 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 15 minutes from Ebden by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library.

Best for

Younger kids at Ebden's 3 local playgrounds. Free days on the High Country Rail Trail and in Ebden's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Bonegilla, next to Ebden, has 3 playgrounds. Tangambalanga, next to Ebden, has 2 playgrounds.
