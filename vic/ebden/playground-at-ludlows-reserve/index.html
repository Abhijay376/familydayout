Playground at Ludlows Reserve

Playground in Ebden · Basic info

Playground at Ludlows Reserve is a public playground in Ebden, in the Wodonga City area. The High Country Rail Trail is about 120 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 340 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 3 minutes from Ebden.
