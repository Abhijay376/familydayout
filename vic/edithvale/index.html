Our data lists 3 playgrounds and 1 park or reserve in Edithvale, in the Kingston area. On top of that, Edithvale has a beach and a skate park.

Landmarks

Edithvale also has Beeson Reserve. Playgrounds around Edithvale include the playground at Regents Park. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Edithvale. In Edithvale you'll also find Edithvale Beach. For older kids in Edithvale there's Chelsea Velodrome. The Longbeach Trail passes through Edithvale. Our data lists 8 cafes and family restaurants in Edithvale for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Edithvale has a train station: Edithvale Railway Station (metro: Frankston Line). Buses 706, 858 and 902 stop in Edithvale. By car from Edithvale, Dandenong is about 15 minutes away, Frankston about 16 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 34 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Edithvale is Grenda Aquatic Centre in Keysborough, about 8 minutes' drive. For a library, Chelsea Library in Chelsea is about 3 minutes' drive from Edithvale. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Cheltenham is about 13 minutes' drive from Edithvale. For an indoor play centre, Gravity Zone Indoor Trampoline Centre in Seaford is about 10 minutes' drive from Edithvale. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale is about 22 minutes' drive from Edithvale. Dandenong is the closest bigger centre to Edithvale, about 15 minutes away by car, and has 21 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Edithvale's 3 local playgrounds. Teens who skate or ride, with Chelsea Velodrome in Edithvale. Beach days at Edithvale Beach. Free days on the Longbeach Trail and in Edithvale's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Edithvale, Aspendale has 10 playgrounds, a beach and a skate park. Next door to Edithvale, Chelsea has 3 playgrounds, a library and a beach. Next door to Edithvale, Aspendale Gardens has 7 playgrounds. Chelsea Heights, next to Edithvale, has 4 playgrounds.
