Edithvale Beach

Beach in Edithvale · Basic info

Edithvale Beach is a beach in Edithvale, in the Kingston City Council area, good for all ages. Edithvale Station/Station St (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 495 m away.
