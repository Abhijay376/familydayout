Mount Bruno in the Benalla area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Mokoan H127 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Mount Bruno, at about 1 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Mount Bruno. For walks and rides, Pine Gully Nature Walk crosses Mount Bruno.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mount Bruno. The closest to Mount Bruno is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mount Bruno, so most trips start by car. By car from Mount Bruno, Wangaratta is about 30 minutes away and Benalla about 35 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta is about 31 minutes' drive from Mount Bruno. The closest library to Mount Bruno is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 31 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta is about 31 minutes' drive from Mount Bruno. The closest cinema to Mount Bruno is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 31 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 30 minutes from Mount Bruno by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Mount Bruno are Glenrowan Museum in Glenrowan (19 min), Cobb & Co in Glenrowan (19 min), Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta (31 min), the playground at Peechelba Reserve in Peechelba (28 min), Moore than Swimming in Wangaratta (28 min) and the playground at Dodsworth Street Reserve in Wangaratta (30 min).

Best for

Free days on the Pine Gully Nature Walk and in Mount Bruno's parks.
