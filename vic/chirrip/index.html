Chirrip is in the Buloke area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Corack East I141 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Chirrip, at about 70 hectares. Cherrip Swamp Wildlife Reserve (no hunting) is the next largest.

Getting around

Chirrip has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 87 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Chirrip, so most trips start by car. By car from Chirrip, Stawell is about 1.5 hours away and Swan Hill about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Chirrip is Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof, about 21 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Donald BMX Track in Donald is about 34 minutes' drive from Chirrip. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 35 minutes' drive from Chirrip. From Chirrip, the nearest places for kids by road are Willandra Museum in Wycheproof (19 min), the playground at Centenary Park in Wycheproof (20 min), Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof (21 min), Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem (42 min), the playground at Lake Watchem Lake Reserve in Watchem (44 min) and Donald BMX Track in Donald (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Chirrip.
