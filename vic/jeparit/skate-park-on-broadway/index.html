Skate park on Broadway

Skate, BMX or pump track in Jeparit · Basic info

Skate park on Broadway is a skate, bmx or pump track in Jeparit, in the Hindmarsh Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Roy St/Dimboola - Rainbow Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 497 m away.
