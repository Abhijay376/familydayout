Jeparit is in the Hindmarsh area, and our data lists 3 playgrounds and 11 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Jeparit has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Jeparit is Lake Hindmarsh Lake Reserve, covering about 15,339 hectares. Hindmarsh Flora Reserve and Babatchio Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Jeparit also has Jeparit Pool, Menzies Square and Ni Ni Flora Reserve. Playgrounds in Jeparit include Fitness Course and the playground at Wimmera River Heritage River. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Jeparit. Places to visit in Jeparit include Wimmera-Mallee Pioneer Museum. For walks and rides, Wimmera River Discovery Trail and Edward John Eyre Heritage River Walk cross Jeparit. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Jeparit for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Jeparit has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 152 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Horsham - Hopetoun via Dimboola route stop in Jeparit. By car from Jeparit, Horsham is about 57 minutes away and Stawell about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rainbow Pool in Rainbow is about 27 minutes' drive from Jeparit. The closest library to Jeparit is Dimboola Library in Dimboola, about 29 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Nhill Skate Park in Nhill is about 36 minutes' drive from Jeparit. The nearest larger centre is Horsham, about 57 minutes from Jeparit by car, with 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Jeparit's 3 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Jeparit. Free days on the Wimmera River Discovery Trail and in Jeparit's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Jeparit, Rainbow has 1 playground and a pool. Lorquon, Glenlee, Crymelon and Antwerp also border Jeparit.
