Jeparit Pool

Park in Jeparit · Basic info

Jeparit Pool is a park in Jeparit, in the Hindmarsh Shire Council area, good for all ages. Roy St/Dimboola - Rainbow Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 453 m away.
