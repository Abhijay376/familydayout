Playground on Broadway

Playground in Jeparit · Basic info

Playground on Broadway in Jeparit is a public playground in the Hindmarsh Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 50 m away, and Cafe 52 is about 410 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 540 m away, and bus Horsham - Hopetoun via Dimboola stops at Roy St/Dimboola - Rainbow Rd, a 7-minute walk away.
