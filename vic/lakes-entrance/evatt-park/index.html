Evatt Park

Park in Lakes Entrance · Basic info

Evatt Park is a park in Lakes Entrance, in the East Gippsland Shire Council area, good for all ages. Jemmeson St/Coates Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1093 m away.
