Playground on Marine Parade

Playground in Lakes Entrance · Basic info

Playground on Marine Parade is a public playground in Lakes Entrance, in the East Gippsland Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 100 m away, and Griffiths Sea Shell Museum is about 470 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 90 m from the playground, and bus 1 stops at Laura St/Marine Pde, a 3-minute walk away.
