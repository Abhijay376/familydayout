Bamganie is in the Golden Plains area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bamganie, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bamganie. The closest to Bamganie is Winchelsea Railway Station (V/Line), about 36 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bamganie, so most trips start by car. By car from Bamganie, Geelong is about 65 minutes away, Ballarat about 71 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Bamganie is Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn, about 43 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Bamganie is Meredith Skate Park in Meredith, about 33 minutes' drive. From Bamganie, the nearest places for kids by road are Summer Sensations in Teesdale (36 min), Shelford Recreation Reserve Playground in Shelford (37 min), Meredith Skate Park in Meredith (33 min), the skate park at Turtle Bend Park in Teesdale (39 min), Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn (43 min) and Disability beach in Steiglitz (50 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bamganie.

Nearby suburbs

Shelford, next to Bamganie, has 1 playground. Next door to Bamganie, Meredith has 1 playground and a skate park. Next door to Bamganie, Lethbridge has 1 playground.
