Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Chum Creek, in the Yarra Ranges area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Chum Creek is Paul Range State Forest, covering about 17 hectares. Maroondah Aqueduct Tunnel Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Chum Creek. The closest to Chum Creek is Lilydale Railway Station (metro), about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Chum Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Chum Creek, Healesville is about 9 minutes away, Lilydale about 30 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 70 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Chum Creek is Healesville Outdoor Pool in Healesville, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest library to Chum Creek is Yarra Plenty Regional Library in Christmas Hills, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Chum Creek is the skate park at McKenzie Reserve in Yarra Glen, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Chum Creek is The Memo, Healesville in Healesville, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Chum Creek is GAMR Laser Tag & VR in Chirnside Park, about 33 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Chum Creek is Lyre Bird enclosure in Badger Creek, about 13 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Healesville, about 9 minutes from Chum Creek by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Chum Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are Healesville Railway Playground in Healesville (8 min), Hearth Galleries in Healesville (8 min), The Memo, Healesville in Healesville (9 min), the playground at Queens Park in Healesville (9 min), Healesville Outdoor Pool in Healesville (10 min) and Toddlers Pool in Healesville (10 min).
