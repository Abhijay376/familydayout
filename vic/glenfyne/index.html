Glenfyne is in the Corangamite area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Glenfyne, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

The Camperdown to Timboon Rail Trail passes through Glenfyne.

Getting around

Glenfyne has no train station; Terang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glenfyne, so most trips start by car. By car from Glenfyne, Warrnambool is about 37 minutes away, Colac about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Glenfyne is Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon, about 16 minutes' drive. For a library, Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool is about 39 minutes' drive from Glenfyne. For a beach, Buttress Point in The Cove is about 31 minutes' drive from Glenfyne. For a skate park, the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon is about 15 minutes' drive from Glenfyne. For a cinema, Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool is about 38 minutes' drive from Glenfyne. The closest indoor play centre to Glenfyne is The Bowl in Warrnambool, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Glenfyne is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 31 minutes' drive. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Glenfyne, about 37 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Glenfyne are the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (15 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (16 min), the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve in Camperdown (25 min), Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (25 min), Buttress Point in The Cove (31 min) and Flaxman Beach in Nullawarre (33 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Glenfyne on the Camperdown to Timboon Rail Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Glenfyne, Timboon has a pool and a skate park. Cobden, next to Glenfyne, has 1 playground. Elingamite North, Elingamite, Ecklin South and Curdies River also border Glenfyne.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Glenfyne, without traffic.
