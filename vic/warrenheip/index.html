Warrenheip is in the Ballarat area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Warrenheip, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 2 public toilets in Warrenheip.

Getting around

Warrenheip has no train station; Ballarat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 6.3 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Warrenheip. By car from Warrenheip, Ballarat is about 12 minutes away, Daylesford about 35 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 84 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Warrenheip is Eureka Swimming Complex in Eureka, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest library to Warrenheip is Mechanics' Institute & Free Library in Golden Point, about 11 minutes' drive. For a beach, Lionhead Beach in Pentland Hills is about 35 minutes' drive from Warrenheip. For a skate park, the skate park at Len T Frazer Reserve in Ballarat East is about 9 minutes' drive from Warrenheip. The closest cinema to Warrenheip is Victoria Theatre in Golden Point, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Warrenheip is Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Warrenheip is Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek, about 6 minutes' drive. Ballarat is the closest bigger centre to Warrenheip, about 12 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Warrenheip are Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek (6 min), Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East (6 min), the playground at Dunnstown Recreation Reserve in Dunnstown (4 min), Eureka Swimming Complex in Eureka (7 min), Toddlers' pool in Eureka (7 min) and the playground at Navigators Community Centre in Navigators (6 min).
