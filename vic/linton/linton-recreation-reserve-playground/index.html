Linton Recreation Reserve Playground

Playground in Linton · Basic info

Linton Recreation Reserve Playground is a public playground in Linton, in the Golden Plains Shire area. The Ballarat-Skipton Rail Trail passes about 170 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 150 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and coaches stop at General Store/Glenelg Hwy, a 10-minute walk away.
