Linton BMX park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Linton · Basic info

Linton BMX park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Linton, in the Golden Plains Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. General Store/Glenelg Hwy (coach) is a 9-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 60 m away.
