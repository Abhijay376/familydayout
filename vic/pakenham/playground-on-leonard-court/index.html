Playground on Leonard Court

Playground in Pakenham · Basic info

This is a public playground in Pakenham. Older kids have a skate park about 310 m away, and Pakenham Outdoor Swimming Pool is about 380 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 890 m from the playground, and bus Cowes - Fountain Gate via Anderson stops at Atkins Rd/Princes Hwy, a 3-minute walk away.
