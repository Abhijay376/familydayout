Playground at Burke Park

Playground in Pakenham · Basic info

Playground at Burke Park in Pakenham is a public playground in the Cardinia Shire area. Pakenham Library is about 460 m away, and MonkeyG's is about 400 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 60 m from the playground, and bus Koo Wee Rup - Pakenham stops at Pakenham Station/Railway Ave , a 1-minute walk away.
