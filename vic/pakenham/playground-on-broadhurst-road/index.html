Playground on Broadhurst Road

Playground in Pakenham · Basic info

Playground on Broadhurst Road in Pakenham is a public playground in the Cardinia Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. You'll find a public toilet about 610 m from the playground, and bus 929 stops at Army Rd/Princes Hwy, a 4-minute walk away.
