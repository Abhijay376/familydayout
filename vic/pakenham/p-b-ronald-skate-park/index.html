P.B. Ronald Skate Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Pakenham · Basic info

P.B. Ronald Skate Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Pakenham, in the Cardinia Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Pakenham Rec Res/John St (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 86 m away with a baby change table.
