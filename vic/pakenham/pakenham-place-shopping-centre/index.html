Pakenham Place Shopping Centre

Shopping centre in Pakenham · Official details

Pakenham Place Shopping Centre is a shopping centre in Pakenham, in the Cardinia Shire Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Pakenham Station/Railway Ave (bus) is a 9-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 466 m away. Opening hours: Monday to Wednesday 9.00am – 5.30pm; Thursday 9:00am – 9:00pm; Friday 9:00am – 9:00pm; Saturday 9:00am – 5:00pm; Sunday & public holidays 10.00am – 5:00pm; Monday – Sunday 6:00am – 10.00PM.
