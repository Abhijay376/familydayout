McKenna Place Reserve

Park in Pakenham · Basic info

McKenna Place Reserve is a park in Pakenham, in the Cardinia Shire Council area, good for all ages. Princes Hwy/Army Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1137 m away.
