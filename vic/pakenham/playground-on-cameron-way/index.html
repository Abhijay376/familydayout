Playground on Cameron Way

Playground in Pakenham · Basic info

Playground on Cameron Way in Pakenham is a public playground in the Cardinia Shire area. Playground on Neverland Boulevard is another option about 590 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 70 m from the playground, and bus 929 stops at Barrington Dr/Cameron Way, a 3-minute walk away.
