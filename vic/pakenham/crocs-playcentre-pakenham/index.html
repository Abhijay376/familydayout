Crocs Playcentre Pakenham

Playground in Pakenham · Basic info

Crocs Playcentre Pakenham is a public playground in Pakenham, in the Cardinia Shire area. Jump Crazy Trampoline Park is another option about 60 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.0 km away. It's easiest by car, about 4 minutes from Pakenham.
