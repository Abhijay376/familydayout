Playground on Flinders Chase

Playground in Pakenham · Basic info

Playground on Flinders Chase is a public playground in Pakenham, in the Cardinia Shire area. For food, Oishi Japanese Restaurant is about 200 m away, and Playground on Clearwater Drive is another option about 440 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 300 m from the playground, and bus 925 stops at Lakeside Bvd/Waterford Rise, a 2-minute walk away.
