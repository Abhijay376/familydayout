P.B. Ronald Park

Park in Pakenham · Basic info

P.B. Ronald Park is a park in Pakenham, in the Cardinia Shire Council area, good for all ages. Pakenham Rec Res/John St (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 60 m away with a baby change table.
