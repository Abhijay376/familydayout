Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Willenabrina, in the Yarriambiack area.

Landmarks

Willenabrina I86 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Willenabrina, at about 7 hectares. Willenabrina I85 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Willenabrina has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 149 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Willenabrina, so most trips start by car. By car from Willenabrina, Horsham is about 68 minutes away and Stawell about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Willenabrina is Rainbow Pool in Rainbow, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Willenabrina are the playground at Brim Rec Reserve in Brim (17 min), the playground at Warrackanbeal Boat Ramp in Warracknabeal (22 min), Historical Centre in Warracknabeal (23 min), Wheatlands Warracknabeal Agricultural Machinery Museum in Warracknabeal (25 min) and Rainbow Pool in Rainbow (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Willenabrina.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Willenabrina, Rainbow has 1 playground and a pool. Next door to Willenabrina, Jeparit has 3 playgrounds. Next door to Willenabrina, Beulah has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Willenabrina, Brim has 1 playground. Kenmare and Crymelon also border Willenabrina.
