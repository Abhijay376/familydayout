Tourello in the Ballarat area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tourello is Tourello Streamside Reserve, covering about 8 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tourello. The closest to Tourello is Clunes Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tourello, so most trips start by car. By car from Tourello, Ballarat is about 32 minutes away, Maryborough about 42 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tourello is Clunes Swimming Pool in Clunes, about 12 minutes' drive. For a library, Creswick Library in Creswick is about 19 minutes' drive from Tourello. For a skate park, the skate park at Alexander Park in Learmonth is about 27 minutes' drive from Tourello. For a cinema, Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central is about 32 minutes' drive from Tourello. The closest indoor play centre to Tourello is Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point, about 36 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East is about 35 minutes' drive from Tourello. The nearest larger centre is Ballarat, about 32 minutes from Tourello by car, with 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Tourello are the playground at Ascot Hall Reserve in Ascot (13 min), Clunes Museum in Clunes (12 min), The Warehouse in Clunes (12 min), Clunes Swimming Pool in Clunes (12 min), the playground at Queens Park in Clunes (12 min) and the skate park at Alexander Park in Learmonth (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tourello.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Tourello, Clunes has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Ascot, next to Tourello, has 1 playground. Coghills Creek also borders Tourello.
