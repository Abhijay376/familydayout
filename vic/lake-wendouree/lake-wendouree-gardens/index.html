Lake Wendouree Gardens

Park in Lake Wendouree · Official details

Lake Wendouree Gardens is a park in Lake Wendouree, in the Ballarat City Council area, good for all ages. Botanical Gardens/Gillies St N (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 165 m away.

Questions parents ask

Where is Lake Wendouree Gardens?
Lake Wendouree Gardens is in Lake Wendouree, Victoria.
Is Lake Wendouree Gardens free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Lake Wendouree Gardens?
The nearest public toilet is about 165 m away.
How do I get to Lake Wendouree Gardens by public transport?
The closest stop is Botanical Gardens/Gillies St N (routes 10), a 6-minute walk.
