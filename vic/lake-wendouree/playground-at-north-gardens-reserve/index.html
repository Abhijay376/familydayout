Playground at North Gardens Reserve

Playground in Lake Wendouree · Official details

This is a public playground in Lake Wendouree. The Steve Moneghetti track is about 70 m away, and Playground at Lake Gardens Playground is another option about 410 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 60 m from the playground, and bus 10 stops at Lake Gardens Ave/Gillies St N, a 4-minute walk away.
