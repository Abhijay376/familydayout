Playground at Lake Wendouree Gardens

Playground in Lake Wendouree · Official details

Playground at Lake Wendouree Gardens in Lake Wendouree is a public playground in the Ballarat City area. The Steve Moneghetti track passes about 30 m away, and Ballarat Aquatic and Leisure Centre is about 340 m away. There's a public toilet about 230 m away, and bus 10 stops at Botanical Gardens/Gillies St N, a 6-minute walk away.
