Drummond Street North Reserve

Park in Lake Wendouree · Basic info

Drummond Street North Reserve is a park in Lake Wendouree, in the Ballarat City Council area, good for all ages. Mill St/Drummond St N (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 739 m away.
