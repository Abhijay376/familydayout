North Gardens Reserve

Park in Lake Wendouree · Basic info

North Gardens Reserve is a park in Lake Wendouree, in the Ballarat City Council area, good for all ages. Stirling Dr/Gillies St N (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 400 m away with a baby change table.
