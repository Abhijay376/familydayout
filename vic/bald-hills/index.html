Bald Hills is in the Ballarat area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bald Hills, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Bald Hills has no train station; Creswick Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 4.1 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bald Hills, so most trips start by car. By car from Bald Hills, Ballarat is about 16 minutes away, Daylesford about 34 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bald Hills is Infants in Creswick, about 9 minutes' drive. For a library, Creswick Library in Creswick is about 10 minutes' drive from Bald Hills. The closest skate park to Bald Hills is Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick, about 10 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central is about 16 minutes' drive from Bald Hills. The closest indoor play centre to Bald Hills is Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Bald Hills is Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East, about 19 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Ballarat, about 16 minutes from Bald Hills by car, with 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Bald Hills, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Centenary Park in Blowhard (13 min), Infants in Creswick (9 min), children in Creswick (9 min), Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick (10 min), Creswick Library in Creswick (10 min) and Apex Park Playground in Creswick (10 min).
