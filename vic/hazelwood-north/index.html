Hazelwood North is in the Latrobe area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Hazelwood North, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Hazelwood North. The closest to Hazelwood North is Morwell Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.0 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 2, 3 and 7 stop in Hazelwood North. By car from Hazelwood North, Traralgon is about 14 minutes away, Warragul about 41 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Hazelwood North is Latrobe Leisure - Churchill in Churchill, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest library to Hazelwood North is Churchill Library in Churchill, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Hazelwood North is Morwell Skate Park in Morwell, about 5 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 6 minutes' drive from Hazelwood North. The nearest larger centre is Traralgon, about 14 minutes from Hazelwood North by car, with 38 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Hazelwood North are the playground at Mathison Park in Churchill (4 min), the playground at McMillan Street Park in Morwell (5 min), Powerworks in Morwell (6 min), Village 8 in Morwell (6 min), Morwell Skate Park in Morwell (5 min) and Latrobe Leisure - Churchill in Churchill (6 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Hazelwood North.

Nearby suburbs

Traralgon, next to Hazelwood North, has 38 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Hazelwood North, Morwell has 14 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. Next door to Hazelwood North, Churchill has 9 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Koornalla, Jeeralang, Hazelwood South and Hazelwood also border Hazelwood North.
