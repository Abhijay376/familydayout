Mangalore in the Strathbogie area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Mangalore Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Mangalore, at about 79 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Mangalore. The Walking track passes through Mangalore.

Getting around

Mangalore has no train station; Avenel Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 6.9 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mangalore, so most trips start by car. By car from Mangalore, Seymour is about 13 minutes away, Shepparton about 62 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mangalore is Memorial Swimming pool in Seymour, about 12 minutes' drive. For a library, Seymour Library in Seymour is about 14 minutes' drive from Mangalore. For a skate park, Seymour Skate Park in Seymour is about 14 minutes' drive from Mangalore. The closest cinema to Mangalore is Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa, about 34 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 13 minutes from Mangalore by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Mangalore are the playground at Jubilee Park in Avenel (8 min), the playground at Rotary Park in Seymour (11 min), Memorial Swimming pool in Seymour (12 min), Seymour Swimming Centre in Seymour (14 min), Seymour Library in Seymour (14 min) and Seymour Skate Park in Seymour (14 min).

Best for

Free days on the Walking track and in Mangalore's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Seymour, next to Mangalore, has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Avenel, next to Mangalore, has 1 playground.
