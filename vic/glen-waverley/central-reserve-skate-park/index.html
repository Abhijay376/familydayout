Central Reserve Skate Park

Park in Glen Waverley · Basic info

Central Reserve Skate Park is a park in Glen Waverley, in the Monash City Council area, good for all ages. Central Reserve/Springvale Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 148 m away.
