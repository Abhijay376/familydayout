Playground on Brent Street

Playground in Glen Waverley · Basic info

Playground on Brent Street is a public playground in Glen Waverley, in the Monash City area. For food, McDonald's is about 110 m away, and Playground on High Street Road is another option about 90 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 620 m from the playground, and bus 902 stops at The Glen Shopping Centre/Springvale Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
