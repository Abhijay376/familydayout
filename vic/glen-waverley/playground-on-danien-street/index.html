Playground on Danien Street

Playground in Glen Waverley · Basic info

Playground on Danien Street in Glen Waverley is a public playground in the Monash City area. McDonald's is about 480 m away if you need food, and Playground on High Street Road is another option about 340 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 720 m away, and buses 737, 742 and 754 stop at Compton St/High Street Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
