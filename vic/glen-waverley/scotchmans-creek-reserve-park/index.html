Scotchmans Creek Reserve

Park in Glen Waverley · Basic info

Scotchmans Creek Reserve is a park in Glen Waverley, in the Monash City Council area, good for all ages. Saladin Ave/Waverley Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1270 m away with a baby change table.
