Playground on Torwood Avenue

Playground in Glen Waverley · Basic info

Playground on Torwood Avenue in Glen Waverley is a public playground in the Monash City area. The Dandenong Creek Trail passes about 220 m away, and Playground on Glen Tower Drive is another option about 470 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and buses 736 and 737 stop at Capital Ave/High Street Rd, a 7-minute walk away.
