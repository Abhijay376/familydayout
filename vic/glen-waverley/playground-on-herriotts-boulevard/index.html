Playground on Herriotts Boulevard

Playground in Glen Waverley · Basic info

This is a public playground in Glen Waverley. The Scotchmans Creek Trail is about 50 m away, and Playground on Sandgate Avenue is another option about 280 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 820 m from the playground, and buses 703 and 737 stop at Avonhurst Dr/Blackburn Rd, a 8-minute walk away.
