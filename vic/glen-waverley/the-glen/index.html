The Glen

Shopping centre in Glen Waverley · Basic info

The Glen is a shopping centre in Glen Waverley, in the Monash City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. The Glen Shopping Centre/Springvale Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 341 m away with a baby change table.
