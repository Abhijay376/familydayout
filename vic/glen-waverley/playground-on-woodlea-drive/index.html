Playground on Woodlea Drive

Playground in Glen Waverley · Basic info

Playground on Woodlea Drive is a public playground in Glen Waverley, in the Monash City area. KFC is about 280 m away if you need food, and Village Green Hotel is another option about 320 m away. There's a public toilet about 540 m away, and buses 693 and 742 stop at Woodlea Dr/Ferntree Gully Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
