Playground on McKenna Road

Playground in Glen Waverley · Basic info

Playground on McKenna Road in Glen Waverley is a public playground in the Monash City area. For food, Yunyun is about 380 m away, and Playground on Saniky Street is another option about 360 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 440 m from the playground, and buses 693 and 742 stop at Avonhurst Dr/Ferntree Gully Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
