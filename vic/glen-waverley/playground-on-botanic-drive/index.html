Playground on Botanic Drive

Playground in Glen Waverley · Basic info

Playground on Botanic Drive in Glen Waverley is a public playground in the Monash City area. The Scotchmans Creek Trail passes about 390 m away, and Playground at Central Reserve is another option about 400 m away. There's a public toilet about 700 m away, and buses 742, 850, 885 and 902 stop at Ranfurlie Dr/Springvale Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
