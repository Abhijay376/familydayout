Point Wilson in the Greater Geelong area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The Spit Wildlife Reserve is the largest mapped park in Point Wilson, at about 679 hectares.

Getting around

Point Wilson has no train station; Lara Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 10 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Point Wilson, so most trips start by car. By car from Point Wilson, Werribee is about 35 minutes away, Geelong about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 63 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, the water play at Bloom Street Park in Werribee is about 33 minutes' drive from Point Wilson. The closest library to Point Wilson is Lara Library in Lara, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Point Wilson is Werribee Sewer Farm South in Cocoroc, about 42 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Point Wilson is Lara Skate Park in Lara, about 30 minutes' drive. For a cinema, VIllage Cinemas in Hoppers Crossing is about 41 minutes' drive from Point Wilson. The closest indoor play centre to Point Wilson is General Public in Corio, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Point Wilson is Werribee Open Range Zoo in Werribee South, about 40 minutes' drive. Werribee is the closest bigger centre to Point Wilson, about 35 minutes away by car, and has 58 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Point Wilson, the nearest places for kids by road are McClelland Reserve in Lara (30 min), Esperance Reserve in Lara (30 min), Werribee Sewer Farm South in Cocoroc (42 min), Lara Skate Park in Lara (30 min), Lara Library in Lara (30 min) and Lara Heritage and History Museum in Lara (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Point Wilson.
