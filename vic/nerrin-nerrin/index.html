Nerrin Nerrin in the Ararat area has 7 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Nerrin Nerrin is Nerrin Nerrin Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 291 hectares. Salt Lake, Streatham Wildlife Reserve (hunting) and Lake Oundell Wildlife Reserve (no hunting) are the next largest. Nerrin Nerrin also has Blue Lake Wildlife Reserve (hunting), Pink Lake Wildlife Reserve (hunting) and Lake Kornong Wildlife Reserve (hunting).

Getting around

Nerrin Nerrin has no train station; Terang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 53 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nerrin Nerrin, so most trips start by car. By car from Nerrin Nerrin, Ararat is about 58 minutes away, Ballarat about 78 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Lake Bolac Library in Lake Bolac is about 29 minutes' drive from Nerrin Nerrin. Ararat is the closest bigger centre to Nerrin Nerrin, about 58 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Nerrin Nerrin, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Simpson Park in Streatham (13 min), Lake Bolac Library in Lake Bolac (29 min), Willaura Outdoor Pool in Willaura (55 min) and Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum (47 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Nerrin Nerrin.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Nerrin Nerrin, Lake Bolac has 1 playground and a library. Streatham, next to Nerrin Nerrin, has 1 playground. Pura Pura, Dundonnell and Carranballac also border Nerrin Nerrin.
