Playground at Cheel Street Reserve

Playground in Oakleigh East · Basic info

Playground at Cheel Street Reserve is a public playground in Oakleigh East, in the Monash City area. Playground at Brickmakers Park is another option about 420 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 520 m away, and buses 624 and 693 stop at Cheel St/Ferntree Gully Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
