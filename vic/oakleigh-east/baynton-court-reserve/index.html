Baynton Court Reserve

Park in Oakleigh East · Basic info

Baynton Court Reserve is a park in Oakleigh East, in the Monash City Council area, good for all ages. Macrina St/Princes Hwy (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 944 m away with a baby change table.
