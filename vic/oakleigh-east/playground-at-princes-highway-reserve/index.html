Playground at Princes Highway Reserve

Playground in Oakleigh East · Basic info

Playground at Princes Highway Reserve in Oakleigh East is a public playground in the Monash City area. The North Road Trail is about 360 m away, and Playground on Garnett Street is another option about 530 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 980 m from the playground, and buses 800, 802, 804 and 862 stop at Princes Hwy Reserve/Princes Hwy, a 5-minute walk away.
