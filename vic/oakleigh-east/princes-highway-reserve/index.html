Princes Highway Reserve

Park in Oakleigh East · Basic info

Princes Highway Reserve is a park in Oakleigh East, in the Monash City Council area, good for all ages. Princes Hwy Reserve/Princes Hwy (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 990 m away.
