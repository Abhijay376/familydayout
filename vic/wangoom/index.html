Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Wangoom in the Moyne area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Wangoom. For walks and rides, Wangoom Loop crosses Wangoom.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wangoom. The closest to Wangoom is Sherwood Park Railway Station (V/Line), about 7.2 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wangoom, so most trips start by car. By car from Wangoom, Warrnambool is about 12 minutes away and Hamilton about 81 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool is about 15 minutes' drive from Wangoom. The closest library to Wangoom is Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool, about 13 minutes' drive. For a beach, Shelly Beach in Warrnambool is about 16 minutes' drive from Wangoom. For a skate park, Warrnambool BMX Track in Warrnambool is about 15 minutes' drive from Wangoom. The closest cinema to Wangoom is Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Wangoom is The Bowl in Warrnambool, about 13 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee is about 10 minutes' drive from Wangoom. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 12 minutes from Wangoom by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Wangoom are the playground at Bushfield Recreation Reserve in Bushfield (7 min), Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (10 min), the playground at Brierly Park in Warrnambool (10 min), Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool (12 min), SCOPE Galleries in Warrnambool (12 min) and The Bowl in Warrnambool (13 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Wangoom on the Wangoom Loop.

Nearby suburbs
