Cape Woolamai in the Bass Coast area has 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in our data. Beyond the parks, Cape Woolamai has 8 beaches.

Landmarks

Woolamai Park is the largest mapped park in Cape Woolamai, at about 1 hectares. Playgrounds in Cape Woolamai include the playground at Woolamai Park. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Cape Woolamai. Places to visit in Cape Woolamai include Anzacs and Aussie Track. For walks and rides, Phillip Island Bike Path, Cape Woolamai Circuit and Cliff Track cross Cape Woolamai. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Cape Woolamai for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Cape Woolamai has no train station; Stony Point Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Cowes - Anderson - Wonthaggi and Cowes - Fountain Gate via Anderson routes stop in Cape Woolamai. V/Line coaches stop in Cape Woolamai. By car from Cape Woolamai, Wonthaggi is about 30 minutes away, Leongatha about 59 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.
