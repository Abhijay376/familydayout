Mannings Launch

Beach in Cape Woolamai · Basic info

Mannings Launch is a beach in Cape Woolamai, in the Bass Coast Shire Council area, good for all ages. Goldensands Rd/Woolamai Beach Rd (bus) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 880 m away.
