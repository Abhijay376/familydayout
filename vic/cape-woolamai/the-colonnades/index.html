The Colonnades

Beach in Cape Woolamai · Basic info

The Colonnades is a beach in Cape Woolamai, in the Bass Coast Shire Council area, good for all ages. Goldensands Rd/Woolamai Beach Rd (bus) is a 16-minute walk away.

Where is The Colonnades?
The Colonnades is in Cape Woolamai, Victoria.

Yes. It is a public beach, free to visit.
