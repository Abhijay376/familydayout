Woolamai Back Beach

Beach in Cape Woolamai · Basic info

Woolamai Back Beach is a beach in Cape Woolamai, in the Bass Coast Shire Council area, good for all ages. Bondi Ave/Vista Dr (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 41 m away.

Questions parents ask

Where is Woolamai Back Beach?
Woolamai Back Beach is in Cape Woolamai, Victoria.

Yes. It is a public beach, free to visit.

How do I get to Woolamai Back Beach by public transport?
