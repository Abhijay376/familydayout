Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Langi Kal Kal in the Pyrenees area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Langi Kal Kal has no train station; Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 9.7 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Langi Kal Kal, so most trips start by car. By car from Langi Kal Kal, Ballarat is about 41 minutes away, Ararat about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Langi Kal Kal is Beaufort Pool in Beaufort, about 16 minutes' drive. For a library, Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort is about 16 minutes' drive from Langi Kal Kal. For a skate park, the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort is about 16 minutes' drive from Langi Kal Kal. The closest cinema to Langi Kal Kal is Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central, about 42 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Langi Kal Kal is Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point, about 45 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Langi Kal Kal is Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East, about 44 minutes' drive. Ballarat is the closest bigger centre to Langi Kal Kal, about 41 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Langi Kal Kal, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (16 min), the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (16 min), the playground at Wotherspoon Reserve in Beaufort (16 min), Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort (16 min), Beaufort Pool in Beaufort (16 min) and the skate park at Alexander Park in Learmonth (35 min).
