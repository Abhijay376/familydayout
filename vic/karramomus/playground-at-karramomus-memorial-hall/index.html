Playground at Karramomus Memorial Hall

Playground in Karramomus · Official details

Playground at Karramomus Memorial Hall is a public playground in Karramomus, in the Greater Shepparton City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet right by the playground. It's easiest by car, about 1 minute from Karramomus.
