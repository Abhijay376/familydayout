Getting around

Karramomus has no train station; Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 17 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Karramomus, so most trips start by car. By car from Karramomus, Shepparton is about 30 minutes away, Benalla about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

The closest beach to Karramomus is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Karramomus is the skate park at Skate Park in Shepparton, about 28 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 30 minutes' drive from Karramomus. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 30 minutes' drive from Karramomus. For a zoo or wildlife park, Emerald Bank Leisure Land in Kialla is about 23 minutes' drive from Karramomus. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 30 minutes from Karramomus by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Karramomus, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Kialla West Reserve in Kialla West (22 min), Emerald Bank Leisure Land in Kialla (23 min), Museum of Vehicle Evolution - MOVE in Kialla (23 min), Village Cinemas in Shepparton (30 min), StarBowl in Shepparton (30 min) and Shepparton Art Museum in Shepparton (28 min).
