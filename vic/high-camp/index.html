High Camp in the Mitchell area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

High Camp Flora Reserve is the largest mapped park in High Camp, at about 11 hectares.

Getting around

High Camp has no train station; Kilmore East Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside High Camp, so most trips start by car. By car from High Camp, Seymour is about 39 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 56 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 75 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to High Camp is Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore, about 13 minutes' drive. For a library, Kilmore Library in Kilmore is about 12 minutes' drive from High Camp. The closest skate park to High Camp is Kilmore Skate Park in Kilmore, about 12 minutes' drive. Seymour is the closest bigger centre to High Camp, about 39 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From High Camp, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Pyalong Recreation Reserve in Pyalong (7 min), Kilmore Skate Park in Kilmore (12 min), Kilmore Library in Kilmore (12 min), the playground at Hudson Park in Kilmore (12 min), Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore (13 min) and Tramway Heritage Centre in Bylands (19 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from High Camp.

Nearby suburbs

Pyalong, next to High Camp, has 1 playground. Glenaroua also borders High Camp.
