Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Westbury, in the Baw Baw area.

Landmarks

Westbury Natural Features and Scenic Reserve is the largest mapped park in Westbury, at about 3 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Westbury. The closest to Westbury is Moe Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.1 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Westbury, so most trips start by car. By car from Westbury, Traralgon is about 30 minutes away, Warragul about 33 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Westbury is Diving Pool in Moe, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest library to Westbury is Morwell Library in Morwell, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Westbury is Moe Skate Park in Moe, about 6 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 21 minutes' drive from Westbury. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 32 minutes' drive from Westbury. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Westbury, about 30 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Westbury are the playground at Ferguson Street Reserve in Moe (5 min), the playground at Hennessey Street Park in Moe (5 min), Moe Skate Park in Moe (6 min), Diving Pool in Moe (7 min), Moe Outdoor Pool in Moe (8 min) and Old Brown Coal Mine Museum in Yallourn North (13 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Westbury.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Westbury, Moe has 14 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Trafalgar, next to Westbury, has 4 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Westbury, Willow Grove has 2 playgrounds. Tanjil South also borders Westbury.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Westbury, without traffic.
