Dalyenong is in the Northern Grampians area, and our data lists 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Dalyenong is Dalyenong Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 2,661 hectares. John Douglas Conservation Reserve and Dalyenong I59 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Dalyenong include Dalyenong West Nature Conservation Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Dalyenong. The closest to Dalyenong is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 42 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dalyenong, so most trips start by car. By car from Dalyenong, Maryborough is about 60 minutes away and Stawell about 82 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud is about 27 minutes' drive from Dalyenong. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 60 minutes from Dalyenong by car, with a library. From Dalyenong, the nearest places for kids by road are Stuart Mill Recreation Reserve Playground in Stuart Mill (15 min), Bi-Centennial Park Playground in St Arnaud (26 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (27 min), Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (46 min), Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (46 min) and Courthouse in Avoca (50 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Dalyenong.
