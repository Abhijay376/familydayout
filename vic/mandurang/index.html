Mandurang is in the Greater Bendigo area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Mandurang, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

The Goldfields Track and Leanganook Track all pass through Mandurang.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mandurang. The closest to Mandurang is Kangaroo Flat Railway Station (V/Line), about 4.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mandurang, so most trips start by car. By car from Mandurang, Bendigo is about 10 minutes away, Castlemaine about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mandurang is Brennan Park Swimming Pool in Kennington, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest library to Mandurang is Bendigo Library in Bendigo, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Mandurang is Ewing Park Skating in Bendigo, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Mandurang is Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Mandurang is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 9 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 14 minutes' drive from Mandurang. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Mandurang, about 10 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Mandurang, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Spring Gully Bushland Reserve in Flora Hill (6 min), Kunzea Court Playspace in Spring Gully (7 min), Ewing Park Skating in Bendigo (9 min), Brennan Park Swimming Pool in Kennington (10 min), Intagolf in Golden Gully (9 min) and Discovery Science & Technology Centre in Bendigo (10 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Mandurang on the Goldfields Track.

Nearby suburbs

Kangaroo Flat, next to Mandurang, has 13 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Mandurang, Strathfieldsaye has 6 playgrounds. Spring Gully, next to Mandurang, has 1 playground. Next door to Mandurang, Big Hill has 1 playground. Sedgwick, Mandurang South and Emu Creek also border Mandurang.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Mandurang, without traffic.
