Zeerust is in the Greater Shepparton area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Zeerust, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Zeerust. The closest to Zeerust is Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line), about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Zeerust, so most trips start by car. By car from Zeerust, Shepparton is about 17 minutes away, Benalla about 63 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.
