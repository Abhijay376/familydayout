Koroit Youth Space

Skate, BMX or pump track in Koroit · Basic info

Koroit Youth Space is a skate, bmx or pump track in Koroit, in the Moyne Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Village Green/North Rd (coach) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 62 m away.
