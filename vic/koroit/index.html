Koroit in the Moyne area has 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in our data. Beyond the parks, Koroit has a skate park.

Landmarks

Victoria Park is the largest mapped park in Koroit, at about 9 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Koroit. For older kids in Koroit there's Koroit Youth Space. For walks and rides, Port Fairy to Warrnambool Rail Trail crosses Koroit.

Getting around

There's no train station in Koroit. The closest to Koroit is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 8 stops in Koroit. V/Line coaches stop in Koroit. By car from Koroit, Warrnambool is about 17 minutes away and Hamilton about 68 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Koroit is Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool, about 20 minutes' drive. For a library, Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool is about 18 minutes' drive from Koroit. For a beach, Killarney Beach in Killarney is about 14 minutes' drive from Koroit. For a cinema, Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool is about 17 minutes' drive from Koroit. For an indoor play centre, The Bowl in Warrnambool is about 18 minutes' drive from Koroit. For a zoo or wildlife park, Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee is about 31 minutes' drive from Koroit. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 17 minutes from Koroit by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Koroit are the playground at Kirkstall Recreation Reserve in Kirkstall (7 min), Killarney Beach in Killarney (14 min), W P O'Sullivan Playground in Dennington (12 min), Reef Point Beach in Port Fairy (13 min), Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool (17 min) and Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool (18 min).

Best for

Teens who skate or ride, with Koroit Youth Space in Koroit. Free days on the Port Fairy to Warrnambool Rail Trail and in Koroit's parks.
