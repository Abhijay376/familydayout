Gooram is in the Strathbogie area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Gooram Gooram Gong I47 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Gooram, at about 51 hectares. Gooram Gooram Gong I46 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Gooram. The closest to Gooram is Euroa Railway Station (V/Line), about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gooram, so most trips start by car. By car from Gooram, Mansfield is about 44 minutes away, Benalla about 47 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa is about 11 minutes' drive from Gooram. The closest library to Gooram is Euroa Library in Euroa, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Gooram is Euroa Skate Park in Euroa, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Gooram is Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa, about 11 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Mansfield, about 44 minutes from Gooram by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Gooram, the nearest places for kids by road are Farmers Arms Hotel Museum in Euroa (11 min), Lions Park Playground in Euroa (11 min), Euroa Skate Park in Euroa (11 min), the playground at Euroa Rotary Park in Euroa (12 min), Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa (11 min) and Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa (11 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Gooram.

Nearby suburbs

Euroa, next to Gooram, has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Strathbogie, next to Gooram, has 1 playground. Next door to Gooram, Creightons Creek has 1 playground. Merton and Kelvin View also border Gooram.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Gooram, without traffic.
