Our data lists 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in Pyalong, in the Mitchell area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Pyalong is Tooborac I19 Bushland Reserve, covering about 7 hectares. Gardiners Creek Streamside Reserve is the next largest. Playgrounds in Pyalong include the playground at Pyalong Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Pyalong.

Getting around

There's no train station in Pyalong. The closest to Pyalong is Broadford Railway Station (V/Line), about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Pyalong. By car from Pyalong, Seymour is about 38 minutes away, Bendigo about 62 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 82 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore is about 20 minutes' drive from Pyalong. For a library, Kilmore Library in Kilmore is about 20 minutes' drive from Pyalong. The closest skate park to Pyalong is Kilmore Skate Park in Kilmore, about 19 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 38 minutes from Pyalong by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Pyalong are Tooborac Recreation Reserve Playground in Tooborac (10 min), Kilmore Skate Park in Kilmore (19 min), Kilmore Library in Kilmore (20 min), Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore (20 min), Mudwood Studio in Heathcote (22 min) and Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote (22 min).
