Korweinguboora is in the Moorabool area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Korweinguboora, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Goldfields Track and Great Dividing Trail cross Korweinguboora.

Getting around

Korweinguboora has no train station; Ballan Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Ballan - Hepburn via Daylesford route stop in Korweinguboora. By car from Korweinguboora, Daylesford is about 13 minutes away, Ballarat about 32 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 78 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Korweinguboora is Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford, about 12 minutes' drive. For a library, Daylesford Library in Daylesford is about 13 minutes' drive from Korweinguboora. For a beach, Lionhead Beach in Pentland Hills is about 29 minutes' drive from Korweinguboora. For a skate park, the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan is about 18 minutes' drive from Korweinguboora. For a cinema, Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central is about 32 minutes' drive from Korweinguboora. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 33 minutes' drive from Korweinguboora. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek is about 23 minutes' drive from Korweinguboora. The nearest larger centre is Daylesford, about 13 minutes from Korweinguboora by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Korweinguboora are the playground at Korweinguboora Recreation Reserve in Spargo Creek (4 min), Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford (12 min), Daylesford Library in Daylesford (13 min), Daylesford Spa Country Railway in Daylesford (15 min), the playground at Hepburn Regional Park in Daylesford (19 min) and the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan (18 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Korweinguboora on the Goldfields Track.

Nearby suburbs

Spargo Creek, next to Korweinguboora, has 1 playground. Bullarto South, Bullarto, Blakeville and Barkstead also border Korweinguboora.
