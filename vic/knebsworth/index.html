Knebsworth is in the Moyne area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Knebsworth, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Knebsworth. The closest to Knebsworth is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 69 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Knebsworth, so most trips start by car. By car from Knebsworth, Hamilton is about 30 minutes away and Portland about 46 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood is about 25 minutes' drive from Knebsworth. For a library, Heywood Library in Heywood is about 25 minutes' drive from Knebsworth. The closest skate park to Knebsworth is hamilton skate park in Hamilton, about 31 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton is about 30 minutes' drive from Knebsworth. Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Knebsworth, about 30 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Knebsworth are Heywood Library in Heywood (25 min), the playground at Apex Park in Heywood (25 min), Branxholme Rec Reserve Playground in Branxholme (25 min), Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood (25 min), Hamilton History Centre in Hamilton (29 min) and Mud Gallery in Hamilton (29 min).
