Sunshine Plaza Skatepark

Skate, BMX or pump track in Sunshine · Basic info

Sunshine Plaza Skatepark is a skate, bmx or pump track in Sunshine, in the Brimbank City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Neil St/Hertford Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 168 m away.
