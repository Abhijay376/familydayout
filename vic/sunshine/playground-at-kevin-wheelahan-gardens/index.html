Playground at Kevin Wheelahan Gardens

Playground in Sunshine · Official details

Playground at Kevin Wheelahan Gardens is a public playground in Sunshine, in the Brimbank City area. Sunshine Library is about 510 m away, and Sunshine Swimming Pool is about 270 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and buses 408 and 410 stop at K Wheelahan Gardens/Devonshire Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
