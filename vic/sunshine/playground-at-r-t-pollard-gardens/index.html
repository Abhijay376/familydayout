Playground at R T Pollard Gardens

Playground in Sunshine · Basic info

Playground at R T Pollard Gardens in Sunshine is a public playground in the Brimbank City area. The Sunshine Trail passes about 170 m away, and Karibu African Coffee Club is about 80 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 300 m away, and buses 428, 429 and 903 stop at Corio St/Hampshire Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
