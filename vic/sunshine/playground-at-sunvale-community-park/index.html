Playground at Sunvale Community Park

Playground in Sunshine · Official details

Playground at Sunvale Community Park in Sunshine is a public playground in the Brimbank City area. Older kids have a skate park about 70 m away, and Sunshine Library is about 410 m away. There's a public toilet about 100 m away, and bus 408 stops at Neil St/Hertford Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
