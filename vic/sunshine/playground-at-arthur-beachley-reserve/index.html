Playground at Arthur Beachley Reserve

Playground in Sunshine · Basic info

Playground at Arthur Beachley Reserve in Sunshine is a public playground in the Brimbank City area. The Kororoit Creek Trail is about 250 m away, and Buckingham Reserve Playground is another option about 390 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 80 m from the playground, and bus 429 stops at Arthur Beachley Reserve/Ardoyne St, a 1-minute walk away.
