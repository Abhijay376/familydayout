Tom O'Brien Park

Park in Sunshine · Official details

Tom O'Brien Park is a park in Sunshine, in the Brimbank City Council area, good for all ages. Cornwall Rd/Monash St (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 714 m away.
