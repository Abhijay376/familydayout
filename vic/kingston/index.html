Kingston in the Hepburn area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Bullarook Creek Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Kingston, at about 8 hectares.

Getting around

Kingston has no train station; Creswick Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 7.8 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kingston, so most trips start by car. By car from Kingston, Daylesford is about 23 minutes away, Ballarat about 29 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Infants in Creswick is about 11 minutes' drive from Kingston. For a library, Creswick Library in Creswick is about 11 minutes' drive from Kingston. The closest skate park to Kingston is Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick, about 11 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central is about 29 minutes' drive from Kingston. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 33 minutes' drive from Kingston. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek is about 24 minutes' drive from Kingston. Daylesford is the closest bigger centre to Kingston, about 23 minutes away by car, and has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library.
