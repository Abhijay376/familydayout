Gisborne South is in the Macedon Ranges area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Gisborne South is Gilligan Reserve, covering about 8 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet and 1 off-leash dog park in Gisborne South.

Getting around

There's no train station in Gisborne South. The closest to Gisborne South is Sunbury Railway Station (V/Line), about 8.3 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gisborne South, so most trips start by car. By car from Gisborne South, Melton is about 34 minutes away, Kyneton about 38 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 49 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Gisborne South is Sunbury Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Sunbury, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest library to Gisborne South is Sunbury Library in Sunbury, about 18 minutes' drive. For a beach, Canoeing in Tylden is about 44 minutes' drive from Gisborne South. The closest skate park to Gisborne South is Sunbury Bmx Club in Sunbury, about 17 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Reading Cinema in Sunbury is about 17 minutes' drive from Gisborne South. The closest indoor play centre to Gisborne South is Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Gisborne South is Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville, about 44 minutes' drive. Melton is the closest bigger centre to Gisborne South, about 34 minutes away by car, and has 15 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Gisborne South, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Abelia Court Reserve in Sunbury (13 min), the playground at Keith Avenue Reserve in Sunbury (14 min), Sunbury Bmx Club in Sunbury (17 min), Sunbury Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Sunbury (17 min), Reading Cinema in Sunbury (17 min) and Sunbury Library in Sunbury (18 min).
