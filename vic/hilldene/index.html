Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Hilldene, in the Mitchell area.

Landmarks

Hume International Raceway is the largest mapped park in Hilldene, at about 30 hectares. Hilldene also has Goulburn River - Seymour Camping Area 1 (CLWF). For camping, there's Goulburn River - Seymour Camping Area 1 (CLWF). The Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks and Riverwalk Trail all pass through Hilldene.

Getting around

Hilldene has no train station; Tallarook Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 7.2 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 3 stops in Hilldene. By car from Hilldene, Seymour is about 8 minutes away, Shepparton about 65 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 84 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Memorial Swimming pool in Seymour is about 7 minutes' drive from Hilldene. For a library, Seymour Library in Seymour is about 8 minutes' drive from Hilldene. The closest skate park to Hilldene is Seymour Skate Park in Seymour, about 9 minutes' drive. Seymour is the closest bigger centre to Hilldene, about 8 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Hilldene, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Rotary Park in Seymour (6 min), the playground at Kings Park in Seymour (7 min), Memorial Swimming pool in Seymour (7 min), Seymour Library in Seymour (8 min), Seymour Skate Park in Seymour (9 min) and Seymour Swimming Centre in Seymour (10 min).

Best for

Free days on the Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks and in Hilldene's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Hilldene, Seymour has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Tallarook, next to Hilldene, has 1 playground. Northwood and Glenaroua also border Hilldene.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Hilldene, without traffic.
