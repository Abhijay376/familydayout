Kardinia Park

Park in South Geelong · Basic info

Kardinia Park is a park in South Geelong, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Park Cres/Moorabool St (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 291 m away.
