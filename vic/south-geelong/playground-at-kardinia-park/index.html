Playground at Kardinia Park

Playground in South Geelong · Basic info

Playground at Kardinia Park is a public playground in South Geelong, in the Greater Geelong City area. Chilwell Library is about 480 m away, and Kardinia Aquatic Centre is about 410 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 60 m from the playground, and 5 bus routes stop at Kardinia Park/Moorabool St, a 8-minute walk away.
