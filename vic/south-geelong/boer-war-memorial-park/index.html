Boer War Memorial Park

Park in South Geelong · Basic info

Boer War Memorial Park is a park in South Geelong, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Kilgour St/Moorabool St (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 223 m away.
