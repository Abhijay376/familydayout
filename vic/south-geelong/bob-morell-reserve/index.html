Bob Morell Reserve

Park in South Geelong · Basic info

Bob Morell Reserve is a park in South Geelong, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Latrobe Bvd/Fyans St (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 106 m away.
