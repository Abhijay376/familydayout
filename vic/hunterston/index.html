Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Hunterston, in the Wellington area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Hunterston is Tarra Tarra Bushland Reserve, covering about 4 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Hunterston. The closest to Hunterston is Rosedale Railway Station (V/Line), about 47 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Hunterston, so most trips start by car. By car from Hunterston, Sale is about 55 minutes away and Traralgon about 72 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Yarram Public Pool in Yarram is about 17 minutes' drive from Hunterston. For a library, Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram is about 17 minutes' drive from Hunterston. For a beach, Woodside_Beach in Woodside Beach is about 24 minutes' drive from Hunterston. For a skate park, the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram is about 17 minutes' drive from Hunterston. The nearest larger centre is Sale, about 55 minutes from Hunterston by car, with 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Hunterston are the playground at Memorial Park in Yarram (17 min), McLoughlins Beach Playground in McLoughlins Beach (17 min), Yarram Historical Society Museum in Yarram (15 min), Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram (17 min), the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram (17 min) and Yarram Public Pool in Yarram (17 min).
