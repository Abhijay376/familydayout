Playground at Memorial Park

Playground in Broadford · Basic info

Playground at Memorial Park is a public playground in Broadford, in the Mitchell Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 380 m away, and Broadford Library is about 170 m away. There's a public toilet about 190 m away. It's easiest by car, about 1 minute from Broadford.
