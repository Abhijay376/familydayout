Our data lists 6 playgrounds and 13 parks and reserves in Broadford, in the Mitchell area. Broadford also has a library and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Broadford is Mount Piper Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 94 hectares. Broadford Recreation Reserve and Broadford Streamside Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Broadford include reserve, BMX track and Memorial Park. Playgrounds in Broadford include Govett Street Playground, Rupert Street Playground and Donaldson Drive Playground. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Broadford. In Broadford you'll also find Broadford Library. For older kids in Broadford there's Broadford Skate Park. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Broadford for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Broadford Railway Station (V/Line: Seymour - Melbourne via Broadmeadows and Shepparton - Melbourne via Seymour) in Broadford. V/Line coaches stop in Broadford. By car from Broadford, Seymour is about 21 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 52 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 71 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore is about 13 minutes' drive from Broadford. For a zoo or wildlife park, Funfields in Whittlesea is about 38 minutes' drive from Broadford.

Best for

Younger kids at Broadford's 6 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the library in Broadford. Teens who skate or ride, with Broadford Skate Park in Broadford.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Broadford, Tallarook has 1 playground. Kilmore East, next to Broadford, has 1 playground. Sugarloaf Creek, Reedy Creek, Moranding and Glenaroua also border Broadford.
