Caniambo is in the Greater Shepparton area, and our data lists 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Caniambo is Caniambo South Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 133 hectares. Caniambo Bushland Reserve and Gowangardie U46 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Caniambo include Gowangaride H62 Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Caniambo.

Getting around

There's no train station in Caniambo. The closest to Caniambo is Violet Town Railway Station (V/Line), about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Caniambo, so most trips start by car. By car from Caniambo, Shepparton is about 28 minutes away, Benalla about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Aquamoves in Shepparton is about 29 minutes' drive from Caniambo. For a library, Shepparton Library in Shepparton is about 29 minutes' drive from Caniambo. The closest beach to Caniambo is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Caniambo is the skate park at Skate Park in Shepparton, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Caniambo is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Caniambo is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Caniambo is Kidstown in Mooroopna, about 32 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 28 minutes from Caniambo by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Caniambo are the playground at O'Donnell Park in Shepparton East (20 min), the playground at Karramomus Memorial Hall in Karramomus (40 min), Village Cinemas in Shepparton (28 min), StarBowl in Shepparton (28 min), Shepparton Heritage Centre in Shepparton (29 min) and the skate park at Skate Park in Shepparton (29 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Caniambo.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Caniambo, Violet Town has 3 playgrounds. Pine Lodge, next to Caniambo, has 1 playground. Gowangardie, Earlston and Cosgrove South also border Caniambo.
