Piazza Area

Park in Echuca · Basic info

Piazza Area is a park in Echuca, in the Campaspe Shire Council area, good for all ages. Crossen St/Elizabeth St (bus) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1104 m away.
