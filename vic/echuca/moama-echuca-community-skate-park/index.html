Moama Echuca Community Skate Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Echuca · Basic info

Moama Echuca Community Skate Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Echuca, in the Campaspe Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Murray River Council/Cobb Hwy (coach) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1575 m away with a baby change table.
