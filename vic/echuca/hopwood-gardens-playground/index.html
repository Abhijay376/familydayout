Hopwood Gardens Playground

Playground in Echuca · Basic info

Hopwood Gardens Playground is a public playground in Echuca, in the Campaspe Shire area. Campaspe Regional Library is about 530 m away, and Port of Echuca Discovery Centre is about 100 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 80 m from the playground, and bus 4 stops at Dickson St/Warren St, a 1-minute walk away.
