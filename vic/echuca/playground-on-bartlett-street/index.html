Playground on Bartlett Street

Playground in Echuca · Basic info

Playground on Bartlett Street is a public playground in Echuca, in the Campaspe Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 40 m away. There's a public toilet about 2.1 km away, and coaches stop at Murray River Council/Cobb Hwy, a 3-minute walk away.
