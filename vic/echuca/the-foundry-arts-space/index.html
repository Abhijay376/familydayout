The Foundry Arts Space

Museum or gallery in Echuca · Official details

The Foundry Arts Space is a museum or gallery in Echuca, in the Campaspe Shire Council area, best for school-age kids. Dickson St/Warren St (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 191 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: Open each day 10am to 4pm (closed on Tuesdays); to 4pm weekends. Tuesday closed as usual, except school holidays..
