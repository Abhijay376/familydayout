Our data lists 8 playgrounds and 9 parks and reserves in Echuca, in the Campaspe area. On top of that, Echuca has a library, 5 museums or galleries, a cinema and a skate park.

Landmarks

Victoria Park is the largest mapped park in Echuca, at about 125 hectares. Echuca Historic and Cultural Features Reserve and Echuca Village Racecourse are the next largest. Echuca also has Piazza Area, Alton Gardens and Aquatic Reserve. Playgrounds around Echuca include McDonald's PlayPlace, the playground at Victoria P and Hopwood Gardens Playground. Our data shows 24 public toilets in Echuca. In Echuca you'll also find Customs House Gallery, Holden Museum, Echuca Paramount Cinemas and Campaspe Regional Library. For older kids in Echuca there's Moama Echuca Community Skate Park. Moama Marketplace is the local shopping centre. For walks and rides, Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks crosses Echuca. Our data lists 10 cafes and family restaurants in Echuca for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Echuca has a train station: Echuca Railway Station (V/Line: Echuca/Moama - Melbourne via Bendigo or Heathcote). Buses on the 1, 2, 3 and 4 routes stop in Echuca. V/Line coaches stop in Echuca. By car from Echuca, Shepparton is about 64 minutes away, Bendigo about 80 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Echuca is Aqua Island in Wharparilla, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Echuca is Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram, about 34 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 8 playgrounds across Echuca. Rainy days at the library, cinema and museum or gallery in Echuca. Teens who skate or ride, with Moama Echuca Community Skate Park in Echuca. Free days on the Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks and in Echuca's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Wharparilla, next to Echuca, has a pool. Echuca Village and Bamawm also border Echuca.
