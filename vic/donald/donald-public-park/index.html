Donald Public Park

Park in Donald · Basic info

Donald Public Park is a park in Donald, in the Buloke Shire Council area, good for all ages. Post Office/Sunraysia Hwy (coach) is a 9-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 278 m away with a baby change table.
