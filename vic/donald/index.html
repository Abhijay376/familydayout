Donald in the Buloke area has 2 playgrounds and 6 parks and reserves in our data. Donald also has a pool and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Donald is Lake Buloke Wildlife Management Co-operative Area, covering about 9,494 hectares. Donald Recreation Reserve and Donald I147 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Donald also has Apex Park, Donald Public Park and Donald I146 Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds in Donald include the playground at Donald Public Park. Our data shows 6 public toilets in Donald. In Donald you'll also find Donald Swimming Pool. For older kids in Donald there's Donald BMX Track.

Getting around

There's no train station in Donald. The closest to Donald is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 101 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Horsham - Donald via Murtoa and Minyip route stop in Donald. V/Line coaches stop in Donald. By car from Donald, Stawell is about 69 minutes away and Horsham about 72 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 35 minutes' drive from Donald. From Donald, the nearest places for kids by road are Guthrie Park Playground in Rich Avon West (14 min), Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem (27 min), Rex Theatre in Charlton (35 min), Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (35 min) and Willandra Museum in Wycheproof (38 min).
