Main Lead in the Pyrenees area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Main Lead is Main Lead Bushland Reserve, covering about 12 hectares.

Getting around

Main Lead has no train station; Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 5.1 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Main Lead, so most trips start by car. By car from Main Lead, Ararat is about 42 minutes away, Ballarat about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Beaufort Pool in Beaufort is about 10 minutes' drive from Main Lead. For a library, Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort is about 9 minutes' drive from Main Lead. The closest skate park to Main Lead is the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Main Lead is Astor Cinema in Ararat, about 41 minutes' drive. Ararat is the closest bigger centre to Main Lead, about 42 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Main Lead are Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort (9 min), the playground at Wotherspoon Reserve in Beaufort (9 min), the playground at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (9 min), the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (9 min), Beaufort Pool in Beaufort (10 min) and Courthouse in Avoca (36 min).

Best for
