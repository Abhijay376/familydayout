Lysterfield South in the Casey area has 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lysterfield South is Churchill National Park, covering about 272 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Lysterfield South. For walks and rides, State Mtb Course, Woodland Walk and Hug Trail cross Lysterfield South.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lysterfield South. The closest to Lysterfield South is Hallam Railway Station (metro), about 6.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lysterfield South, so most trips start by car. By car from Lysterfield South, Dandenong is about 12 minutes away, Cranbourne about 20 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 33 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lysterfield South is Doveton Pool in the Park in Doveton, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest library to Lysterfield South is Endeavour Hills Library in Endeavour Hills, about 6 minutes' drive. For a beach, Edithvale Beach in Edithvale is about 25 minutes' drive from Lysterfield South. For a skate park, Endeavour Hills Skatepark in Endeavour Hills is about 6 minutes' drive from Lysterfield South. The closest cinema to Lysterfield South is Reading Cinema in Dandenong, about 11 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Escape Reality in Rowville is about 9 minutes' drive from Lysterfield South. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Lysterfield South is Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale, about 22 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Dandenong, about 12 minutes from Lysterfield South by car, with 21 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Free days on the State Mtb Course and in Lysterfield South's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Rowville, next to Lysterfield South, has 26 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Endeavour Hills, next to Lysterfield South, has 26 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Lysterfield, next to Lysterfield South, has 8 playgrounds. Narre Warren North, next to Lysterfield South, has 7 playgrounds and a skate park.
