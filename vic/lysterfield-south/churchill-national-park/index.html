Churchill National Park

Park in Lysterfield South · Official details

Churchill National Park is a park in Lysterfield South, in the Casey City Council area, good for all ages. Girvan Cct/Shetland Dr (bus) is a 29-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 1969 m away and dogs are not allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Churchill National Park?
Churchill National Park is in Lysterfield South, Victoria, at Churchill Park Drive, Lysterfield South.
Is Churchill National Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Churchill National Park?
No, dogs are not allowed.
Are there toilets at Churchill National Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 1969 m away.
How do I get to Churchill National Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Girvan Cct/Shetland Dr (routes 843), a 29-minute walk.
