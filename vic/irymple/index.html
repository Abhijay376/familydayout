Irymple in the Mildura area has 3 playgrounds and 4 parks and reserves in our data. Beyond the parks, Irymple has a pool.

Landmarks

Kings Billabong Park is the largest mapped park in Irymple, at about 2,190 hectares. Mildura I13 Bushland Reserve and Mildura I14 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Irymple include Cardross Nature Conservation Reserve. Our data shows 7 public toilets in Irymple. Places to visit in Irymple include Irymple Swimming Pool. Our data lists 4 cafes and family restaurants in Irymple for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Irymple. The closest to Irymple is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 177 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the 100/200 route stop in Irymple. V/Line coaches stop in Irymple. By car from Irymple, Mildura is about 7 minutes away and Swan Hill about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Irymple is Mildura Library in Mildura, about 7 minutes' drive. For a beach, Apex Beach in Mildura is about 16 minutes' drive from Irymple. For a skate park, Mildura Skate Park in Mildura is about 8 minutes' drive from Irymple. The closest cinema to Irymple is Deakin Cinema Complex in Mildura, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Irymple is Sunraysia Tenpin in Mildura, about 7 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Putt Putt Family Fun Centre in Mildura is about 8 minutes' drive from Irymple. The nearest larger centre is Mildura, about 7 minutes from Irymple by car, with 26 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.
