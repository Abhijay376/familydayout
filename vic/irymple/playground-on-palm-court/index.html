Playground on Palm Court

Playground in Irymple · Basic info

Playground on Palm Court in Irymple is a public playground in the Mildura Rural City area. Irymple Swimming Pool is about 560 m away, and Angie's Pizza is about 360 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and coaches stop at Hassell St/Calder Hwy, a 5-minute walk away.
