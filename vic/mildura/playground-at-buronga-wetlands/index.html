Playground at Buronga Wetlands

Playground in Mildura · Basic info

Playground at Buronga Wetlands in Mildura is a public playground in the Mildura Rural City area. Mildura Holden Motor Museum is about 420 m away, and Playground at Police Park is another option about 310 m away.
