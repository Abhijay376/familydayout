Wirraway Drive Drainage Reserve

Park in Mildura · Basic info

Wirraway Drive Drainage Reserve is a park in Mildura, in the Mildura Rural City Council area, good for all ages. Wirraway Dr/Ellswood Cres (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1182 m away.
