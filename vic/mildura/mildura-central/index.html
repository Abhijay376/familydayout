Mildura Central

Shopping centre in Mildura · Basic info

Mildura Central is a shopping centre in Mildura, in the Mildura Rural City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. San Mateo Ave/Fifteenth St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 140 m away with a baby change table.
