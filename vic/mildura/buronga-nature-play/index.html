Buronga Nature Play

Playground in Mildura · Basic info

Buronga Nature Play is a public playground in Mildura, in the Mildura Rural City area. Water Play Park is about 500 m away, and Ornamental Lakes Park Playground is another option about 410 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.9 km away. It's easiest by car, about 6 minutes from Mildura.
