Mildura is in the Mildura area, and our data lists 26 playgrounds and 37 parks and reserves in the suburb. Mildura also has 2 libraries, 4 pools, 5 museums or galleries and a cinema.

Landmarks

Mildura I220A Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Mildura, at about 24 hectares. Mildura Sporting Precinct. and Lock Island are the next largest. Mildura also has Brodie Park, Dennis Park and Henley Park. Playgrounds in Mildura include Buronga Nature Play, Mildura Park for Play and Jaycee Park Playground. Our data shows 41 public toilets in Mildura. In Mildura you'll also find Putt Putt Family Fun Centre, Art Vault Gallery, Mildura Holden Motor Museum, Deakin Cinema Complex and Laserpunk Laserforce. For older kids in Mildura there's BMX Track and Mildura Skate Park. Mildura Central is the local shopping centre. For walks and rides, River Track, Woodlands Track and Railway Linear Reserve cross Mildura. Our data lists 38 cafes and family restaurants in Mildura for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Mildura has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 181 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the 400, 401, 500 and 501 routes stop in Mildura. V/Line coaches stop in Mildura. By car from Mildura, Swan Hill is about 3 hours away and Horsham about 4 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 26 playgrounds across Mildura. Rainy days at the library, cinema, indoor play centre and museum or gallery in Mildura. Teens who skate or ride, with BMX Track in Mildura. Beach days at Apex Beach. Free days on the River Track and in Mildura's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Mildura, Merbein has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Next door to Mildura, Irymple has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Mildura, Nichols Point has 2 playgrounds. Cabarita and Birdwoodton also border Mildura.
