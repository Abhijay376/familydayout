Laserpunk Laserforce

Indoor play in Mildura · Official details

Laserpunk Laserforce is a indoor play in Mildura, in the Mildura Rural City Council area, best for younger kids (check the venue's age and height rules). Eighth St/Deakin Ave (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 445 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: Sun-Fri 10am-8pm; Sat 5pm-8pm; Friday: 4pm - 8pm; Saturday: 11am - 8pm; Sunday: 11am - 5pm; Sun-Thu: 10am - 5pm; Friday: 10am - 8pm.
