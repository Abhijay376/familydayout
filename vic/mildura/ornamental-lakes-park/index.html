Ornamental Lakes Park

Park in Mildura · Basic info

Ornamental Lakes Park is a park in Mildura, in the Mildura Rural City Council area, good for all ages. Eighth St/Deakin Ave (bus) is a 10-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 104 m away with a baby change table.
