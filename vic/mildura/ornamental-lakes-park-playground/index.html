Ornamental Lakes Park Playground

Playground in Mildura · Basic info

Ornamental Lakes Park Playground is a public playground in Mildura, in the Mildura Rural City area. Older kids have a skate park about 240 m away, and Water Play Park is about 150 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 70 m from the playground, and buses 100/200, 211/311/312 and 250/300 stop at Eighth St/Deakin Ave, a 9-minute walk away.
