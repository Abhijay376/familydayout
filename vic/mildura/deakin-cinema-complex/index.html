Deakin Cinema Complex

Cinema in Mildura · Basic info

Deakin Cinema Complex is a cinema in Mildura, in the Mildura Rural City Council area, good for school-age kids and teens. Ninth St/Deakin Ave (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 186 m away with a baby change table.
