Jaycee Park

Park in Mildura · Basic info

Jaycee Park is a park in Mildura, in the Mildura Rural City Council area, good for all ages. Walnut Ave/Seventh St (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 247 m away with a baby change table.
