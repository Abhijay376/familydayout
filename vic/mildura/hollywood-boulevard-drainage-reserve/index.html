Hollywood Boulevard Drainage Reserve

Park in Mildura · Basic info

Hollywood Boulevard Drainage Reserve is a park in Mildura, in the Mildura Rural City Council area, good for all ages. James Cook Dr/San Mateo Ave (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 882 m away with a baby change table.
