Mildura Park for Play

Playground in Mildura · Basic info

Mildura Park for Play is a public playground in Mildura, in the Mildura Rural City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet about 180 m away, and buses 211/311/312 and 602 stop at Old Aerodrome Tennis Courts/Eleventh St, a 2-minute walk away.
