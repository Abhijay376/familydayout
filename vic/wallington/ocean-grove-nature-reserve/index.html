Ocean Grove Nature Reserve

Park in Wallington · Official details

Ocean Grove Nature Reserve is a park in Wallington, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Kingston Village Square Shopping Centre/Grubb Rd (bus) is a 14-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 4 m away and it is fenced.
