Wallington Reserve

Playground in Wallington · Basic info

Wallington Reserve is a public playground in Wallington, in the Greater Geelong City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet right by the playground. It's easiest by car, about 3 minutes from Wallington.
