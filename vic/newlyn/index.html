Getting around

There's no train station in Newlyn. The closest to Newlyn is Creswick Railway Station (V/Line), about 8.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Newlyn, so most trips start by car. By car from Newlyn, Daylesford is about 17 minutes away, Ballarat about 25 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Infants in Creswick is about 9 minutes' drive from Newlyn. For a library, Creswick Library in Creswick is about 10 minutes' drive from Newlyn. The closest skate park to Newlyn is Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick, about 10 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central is about 24 minutes' drive from Newlyn. The closest indoor play centre to Newlyn is Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point, about 26 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek is about 17 minutes' drive from Newlyn. The nearest larger centre is Daylesford, about 17 minutes from Newlyn by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Newlyn, the nearest places for kids by road are Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick (10 min), Infants in Creswick (9 min), children in Creswick (9 min), Creswick Library in Creswick (10 min), Skate Park in Creswick (10 min) and Apex Park Playground in Creswick (10 min).
