Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Macorna North, in the Gannawarra area.

Landmarks

Hird Swamp Wildlife Reserve is the largest mapped park in Macorna North, at about 465 hectares.

Getting around

Macorna North has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Macorna North, so most trips start by car. By car from Macorna North, Echuca is about 63 minutes away and Swan Hill about 70 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Macorna North is Cohuna Pool in Cohuna, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest library to Macorna North is Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang, about 23 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna is about 23 minutes' drive from Macorna North. From Macorna North, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at C L King Memorial Park in Cohuna (23 min), Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna (23 min), the playground at Cohuna Apex Park in Cohuna (24 min), Cohuna Pool in Cohuna (25 min), Leitchville Memorial Swimming Pool in Leitchville (22 min) and Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (23 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Macorna North.

Nearby suburbs

McMillans, Macorna and Kerang East also border Macorna North.
