Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Glengower, in the Hepburn area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Glengower is Tullaroop Creek Streamside Reserve, covering about 1 hectares.

Getting around

Glengower has no train station; Clunes Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glengower, so most trips start by car. By car from Glengower, Castlemaine is about 43 minutes away, Maryborough about 44 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Glengower is Clunes Swimming Pool in Clunes, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest library to Glengower is Creswick Library in Creswick, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Glengower is Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick, about 35 minutes' drive. Castlemaine is the closest bigger centre to Glengower, about 43 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Glengower, the nearest places for kids by road are Clunes Swimming Pool in Clunes (18 min), the playground at Queens Park in Clunes (18 min), Clunes Museum in Clunes (18 min), The Warehouse in Clunes (18 min), the playground at Newstead Memorial Park in Newstead (26 min) and Newstead Swimming Pool in Newstead (28 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Glengower.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Glengower, Clunes has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Campbelltown also borders Glengower.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Glengower, without traffic.
