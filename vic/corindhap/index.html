Our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in Corindhap, in the Golden Plains area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Corindhap is Corindhap Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 38 hectares. Playgrounds around Corindhap include Corindhap Recreation Reserve Playground. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Corindhap. For walks and rides, Kuruc-a-ruc trail crosses Corindhap.

Getting around

There's no train station in Corindhap. The closest to Corindhap is Ballarat Railway Station (V/Line), about 36 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Corindhap, so most trips start by car. By car from Corindhap, Ballarat is about 36 minutes away, Colac about 59 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood is about 6 minutes' drive from Corindhap. The closest library to Corindhap is Sebastopol Library in Sebastopol, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Corindhap is Rokewood Skate Park in Rokewood, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Corindhap is Showbiz Cinemas Ballarat in Smythes Creek, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Corindhap is Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point, about 37 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Corindhap is Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East, about 41 minutes' drive. Ballarat is the closest bigger centre to Corindhap, about 36 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Corindhap, the nearest places for kids by road are Rokewood Skate Park in Rokewood (6 min), Rokewood Recreation Reserve Playground in Rokewood (6 min), Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood (6 min), Dereel Skate Park in Dereel (6 min), Buninyong Swimming Pool in Buninyong (32 min) and Sebastopol Library in Sebastopol (31 min).

Best for

Free days on the Kuruc-a-ruc trail and in Corindhap's parks.

Nearby suburbs
