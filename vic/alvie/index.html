Alvie in the Colac Otway area has 1 playground and 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Alvie is Lake Werowrap Lake Reserve, covering about 37 hectares. Lake Coragulac Lake Reserve and Lake Gnalinegurk Lake Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Alvie include Red Rock Scenic Reserve. Playgrounds around Alvie include the playground at Red Rock Scenic Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Alvie.

Getting around

Alvie has no train station; Colac Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Colac - Alvie via Warrion route stop in Alvie. By car from Alvie, Colac is about 21 minutes away, Geelong about 85 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Alvie is Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Alvie is the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt, about 26 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Colac Cinemas in Colac is about 21 minutes' drive from Alvie. The closest indoor play centre to Alvie is Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac, about 22 minutes' drive. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Alvie, about 21 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Alvie are the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (20 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (21 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (22 min), Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (24 min), the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (26 min) and Birregurra Skate Park in Birregurra (38 min).
