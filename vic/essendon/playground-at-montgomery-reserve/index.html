Playground at Montgomery Reserve

Playground in Essendon · Official details

Playground at Montgomery Reserve is a public playground in Essendon, in the Moonee Valley City area. The Strand Popup Bike Route is about 70 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 110 m away. There's a public toilet about 90 m away, and bus 503 stops at Lawson St/Albion St, a 3-minute walk away.
