Lincoln Park

Park in Essendon · Official details

Lincoln Park is a park in Essendon, in the Moonee Valley City Council area, good for all ages. Leake St/Mt Alexander Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 165 m away.

Details

Good to know
Public BBQ about 110 m away (Council)

Questions parents ask

Where is Lincoln Park?
Lincoln Park is in Essendon, Victoria.
Is Lincoln Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Lincoln Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 165 m away.
How do I get to Lincoln Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Leake St/Mt Alexander Rd (routes 477, 483, 903, 959), a 4-minute walk.
