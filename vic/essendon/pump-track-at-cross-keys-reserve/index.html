Pump track at Cross Keys Reserve

Skate, BMX or pump track in Essendon · Basic info

Pump track at Cross Keys Reserve is a skate, bmx or pump track in Essendon, in the Moonee Valley City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Pascoe Vale Rd/Woodland St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 826 m away.
