Playground at Cliff Allison Park

Playground in Essendon · Official details

This is a public playground in Essendon. Windy Hill Fitness Centre is about 580 m away, and Domino's is about 470 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 760 m away, and buses 477, 483, 903 and 959 stop at Brewster St/Mt Alexander Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
