Playground at L.T. Thompson Park

Playground in Essendon · Official details

Playground at L.T. Thompson Park is a public playground in Essendon, in the Moonee Valley City area. For food, Dumplings Delight is about 110 m away, and Playground at Vin Jervis Reserve is another option about 530 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 670 m from the playground, and tram 59 stops at Service St/Keilor Rd #48, a 2-minute walk away.
