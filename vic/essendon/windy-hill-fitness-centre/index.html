Windy Hill Fitness Centre

Pool or water play in Essendon · Official details

Windy Hill Fitness Centre is a pool or water play in Essendon, in the Moonee Valley City Council area, good for all ages. Raleigh St/Napier St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 622 m away. Opening hours: Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri 05:00-21:00; Sat, Sun 07:00-16:00.
