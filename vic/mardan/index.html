Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Mardan in the South Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mardan. The closest to Mardan is Trafalgar Railway Station (V/Line), about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mardan, so most trips start by car. By car from Mardan, Leongatha is about 29 minutes away, Traralgon about 45 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mardan is South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha, about 27 minutes' drive. For a library, Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North is about 7 minutes' drive from Mardan. For a skate park, Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North in Mirboo North is about 8 minutes' drive from Mardan. For a cinema, Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha is about 29 minutes' drive from Mardan. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Mardan, about 29 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Mardan are Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North (7 min), the playground at Baromi Park in Mirboo North (7 min), Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North in Mirboo North (8 min), the playground at Mossvale Park in Berrys Creek (18 min), Boolarra Old School Museum in Boolarra (28 min) and Boolarra Skate Park in Boolarra (29 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mardan.

Nearby suburbs

Mirboo North, next to Mardan, has 1 playground, a library and a skate park. Berrys Creek, next to Mardan, has 1 playground. Koorooman, Dumbalk North, Dumbalk and Boorool also border Mardan.
