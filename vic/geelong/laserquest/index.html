Laserquest

Indoor play in Geelong · Official details

Laserquest is a indoor play in Geelong, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, best for younger kids (check the venue's age and height rules). Moorabool St/Malop St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 8 m away.
