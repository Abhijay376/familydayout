Skate park on Western Beach Road

Skate, BMX or pump track in Geelong · Basic info

Skate park on Western Beach Road is a skate, bmx or pump track in Geelong, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Deakin University Waterfront Campus/Brougham St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 150 m away.
