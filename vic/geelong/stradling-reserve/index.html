Stradling Reserve

Playground in Geelong · Basic info

Stradling Reserve is a public playground in Geelong, in the Greater Geelong City area. The Corio Bay Trail passes about 180 m away, and Tri Swimming is about 200 m away. There's a public toilet about 820 m away, and bus 20 stops at Toorak Pde/The Esplanade, a 3-minute walk away.
