Playground on Yarra Street

Playground in Geelong · Basic info

Playground on Yarra Street is a public playground in Geelong, in the Greater Geelong City area. Barwon Health Library is about 170 m away, and Museum of Play and Art is about 360 m away. There's a public toilet about 190 m away, and 7 bus routes stop at Little Myers St/Moorabool St, a 4-minute walk away.
