Playground at Pevensey Park

Playground in Geelong · Basic info

Playground at Pevensey Park in Geelong is a public playground in the Greater Geelong City area. Eastern Beach is about 340 m away, and Eastern Beach Children's Pool is about 350 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 440 m from the playground, and buses 30 and 60 stop at Fitzroy St/Ryrie St, a 5-minute walk away.
