Playground at Hopetoun Park

Playground in Geelong · Basic info

This is a public playground in Geelong. Barwon Health Library is about 570 m away, and The Local - Geelong is about 260 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 320 m away, and bus 31 stops at Fitzroy St/Kilgour St, a 2-minute walk away.
