Western Beach

Playground in Geelong · Basic info

Western Beach in Geelong is a public playground in the Greater Geelong City area. The Corio Bay Trail passes about 40 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 310 m away. There's a public toilet about 300 m away, and bus 20 stops at St Peter and Paul Catholic Church/Mercer St, a 5-minute walk away.
