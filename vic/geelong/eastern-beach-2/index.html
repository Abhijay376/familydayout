Eastern Beach

Beach in Geelong · Basic info

Eastern Beach is a beach in Geelong, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Fitzroy St/Ryrie St (bus) is a 10-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 135 m away.

Where is Eastern Beach?
Eastern Beach is in Geelong, Victoria.
