Anzac Park

Park in Haven · Basic info

Anzac Park is a park in Haven, in the Horsham Rural City Council area, good for all ages. Opp 11 Hunts Rd (bus) is a 9-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 85 m away with a baby change table.
