Haven is in the Horsham area, and our data lists 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Haven is Bungalally I48 Bushland Reserve, covering about 6 hectares. Kokoda Drive Park and Anzac Park are the next largest. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Haven.

Getting around

There's no train station in Haven. The closest to Haven is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 88 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 4 stops in Haven. By car from Haven, Horsham is about 8 minutes away and Stawell about 55 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham is about 6 minutes' drive from Haven. For a library, Dimboola Library in Dimboola is about 36 minutes' drive from Haven. For a skate park, Velodrome in Horsham is about 6 minutes' drive from Haven. The closest cinema to Haven is Centre Cinemas in Horsham, about 7 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Horsham, about 8 minutes from Haven by car, with 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Haven, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Uebergang Park in Horsham (6 min), Velodrome in Horsham (6 min), Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham (6 min), Horsham Water Play in Horsham (7 min), Centre Cinemas in Horsham (7 min) and Horsham Skate Park in Horsham (8 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Haven.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Haven, Horsham has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. Vectis, next to Haven, has 1 playground. Lower Norton and Bungalally also border Haven.
