Arawata is in the South Gippsland area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Arawata, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Arawata has no train station; Warragul Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Arawata, so most trips start by car. By car from Arawata, Leongatha is about 26 minutes away, Warragul about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Korumburra Outdoor Pool in Korumburra is about 18 minutes' drive from Arawata. The closest library to Arawata is Leongatha Library in Leongatha, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Arawata is Leongatha Skate Park in Leongatha, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Arawata is Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha, about 25 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Leongatha, about 26 minutes from Arawata by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Arawata, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Coal Creek Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Korumburra (18 min), the playground at Coleman Park in Korumburra (18 min), Korumburra Outdoor Pool in Korumburra (18 min), Coal Creek Community Park & Museum in Korumburra (19 min), Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha (25 min) and Leongatha Library in Leongatha (24 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Arawata.

Nearby suburbs

Korumburra, next to Arawata, has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Fairbank also borders Arawata.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Arawata, without traffic.
