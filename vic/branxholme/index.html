Branxholme is in the Southern Grampians area, and our data lists 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Branxholme Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Branxholme, at about 3 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Branxholme include Bushfire Place of Last Resort. Playgrounds around Branxholme include Branxholme Rec Reserve Playground. Our data shows 3 public toilets and 2 off-leash dog parks in Branxholme.

Getting around

There's no train station in Branxholme. The closest to Branxholme is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 83 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Branxholme. By car from Branxholme, Hamilton is about 22 minutes away and Portland about 49 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Branxholme is Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Hamilton, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest library to Branxholme is Hamilton Library in Hamilton, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Branxholme is hamilton skate park in Hamilton, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Branxholme is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 22 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 22 minutes from Branxholme by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Branxholme are Hughan Park Playground in Hamilton (21 min), Hamilton History Centre in Hamilton (21 min), Mud Gallery in Hamilton (21 min), Hamilton Library in Hamilton (22 min), Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton (22 min) and Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Hamilton (24 min).

Best for

Nearby suburbs

Hamilton, next to Branxholme, has 23 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Hotspur, Grassdale, Condah and Byaduk North also border Branxholme.
