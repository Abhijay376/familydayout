Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Sailors Falls in the Hepburn area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 2 public toilets in Sailors Falls. The Goldfields Track, Great Dividing Trail and Three Lost Children Walk all pass through Sailors Falls.

Getting around

Sailors Falls has no train station; Creswick Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Ballan - Hepburn via Daylesford route stop in Sailors Falls. By car from Sailors Falls, Daylesford is about 7 minutes away, Ballarat about 38 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 83 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Sailors Falls is Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford, about 7 minutes' drive. For a library, Daylesford Library in Daylesford is about 7 minutes' drive from Sailors Falls. The closest beach to Sailors Falls is Canoeing in Tylden, about 45 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick is about 26 minutes' drive from Sailors Falls. The closest cinema to Sailors Falls is Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central, about 37 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Sailors Falls is Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Sailors Falls is Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek, about 29 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Daylesford, about 7 minutes from Sailors Falls by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Sailors Falls are Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford (7 min), Daylesford Library in Daylesford (7 min), Daylesford Spa Country Railway in Daylesford (9 min), the playground at Hepburn Regional Park in Daylesford (13 min), the playground at Korweinguboora Recreation Reserve in Spargo Creek (10 min) and Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick (26 min).
