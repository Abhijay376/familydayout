Waurn Ponds Shopping Centre

Shopping centre in Grovedale · Basic info

Waurn Ponds Shopping Centre is a shopping centre in Grovedale, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Waurn Ponds SC/Princes Hwy (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 438 m away with a baby change table.
