Playground at Waurn Ponds Parkland

Playground in Grovedale · Basic info

Playground at Waurn Ponds Parkland is a public playground in Grovedale, in the Greater Geelong City area. The Waurn Ponds Trail is about 80 m away, and for food, Subway is about 400 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 280 m from the playground, and buses 40, 41 and 53 stop at Oberon Dr/Torquay Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
