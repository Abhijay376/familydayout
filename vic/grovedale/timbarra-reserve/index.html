Timbarra Reserve

Park in Grovedale · Basic info

Timbarra Reserve is a park in Grovedale, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Burdoo Dr/Pioneer Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1379 m away.
