Playground at Geelong Baseball Centre

Playground in Grovedale · Basic info

Playground at Geelong Baseball Centre is a public playground in Grovedale, in the Greater Geelong City area. The Bike: Southern Link passes about 240 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 50 m away. There's a public toilet about 100 m away, and coaches stop at Waurn Ponds Shopping Centre/Pioneer Rd , a 2-minute walk away.
