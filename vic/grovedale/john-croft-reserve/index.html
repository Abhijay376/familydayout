John Croft Reserve

Park in Grovedale · Basic info

John Croft Reserve is a park in Grovedale, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Grovedale Community Hub/Heyers Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 72 m away.
