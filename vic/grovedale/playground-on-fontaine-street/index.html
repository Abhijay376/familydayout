Playground on Fontaine Street

Playground in Grovedale · Basic info

Playground on Fontaine Street is a public playground in Grovedale, in the Greater Geelong City area. The Waurn Ponds Trail passes about 350 m away, and Playground at Timbarra Reserve is another option about 450 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.2 km away, and bus 42 stops at Burdoo Dr/Pioneer Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
