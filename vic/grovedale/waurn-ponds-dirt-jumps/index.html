Waurn Ponds Dirt Jumps

Skate, BMX or pump track in Grovedale · Basic info

Waurn Ponds Dirt Jumps is a skate, bmx or pump track in Grovedale, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Waurn Ponds Shopping Centre/Pioneer Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 303 m away.
