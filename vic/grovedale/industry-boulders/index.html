Industry Boulders

Climbing in Grovedale · Official details

Industry Boulders is a climbing in Grovedale, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, best for kids 5 and up and teens. Essington St/Bailey St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 920 m away. Opening hours: Monday: 4pm – 9:30pm; Tuesday: 12pm – 9:30pm; Wednesday: 4pm – 9:30pm; Thursday: 12pm – 9:30pm; Friday: 4pm – 9:30pm.
