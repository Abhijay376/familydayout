We Rock The Spectrum, Grovedale

Playground in Grovedale · Basic info

This is a public playground in Grovedale. Bounce Inc is about 80 m away, and for food, KFC is about 240 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 460 m from the playground, and bus 40 stops at Torquay Rd/Marshalltown Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
