Our data lists 2 playgrounds in Dookie, in the Greater Shepparton area.

Landmarks

Playground at CWA Gardens is one of the council's larger district playgrounds. Playgrounds around Dookie include the playground at Dookie Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets and 1 park or playground with a council BBQ close by in Dookie. For walks and rides, Dookie Rail Trail crosses Dookie.

Getting around

There's no train station in Dookie. The closest to Dookie is Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line), about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dookie, so most trips start by car. By car from Dookie, Shepparton is about 29 minutes away, Benalla about 37 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.
