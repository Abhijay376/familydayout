Playground at CWA Gardens

Playground in Dookie · Official details

Playground at CWA Gardens in Dookie is one of the council's larger playgrounds in the Greater Shepparton City area. The Dookie Rail Trail is about 40 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 80 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 2 minutes from Dookie.
