Grampians (Gariwerd) National Park

Park in Grampians · Official details

Grampians (Gariwerd) National Park is a park in Grampians, in the Southern Grampians Shire Council area, good for all ages. Dogs are not allowed.

Details

Dogs
not allowed (Parks Victoria)
Wheelchair access
no (Parks Victoria)
Prices
No charge to hike the Grampians Peaks Trail; the 11 hike-in campgrounds have a fee (Parks Victoria)
Seasons
Mackenzie Falls precinct closed on Extreme and Catastrophic fire danger days (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
Carry at least 4 to 6 litres of water per day on the Grampians Peaks Trail; Trail water is untreated; treat it before drinking (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Grampians (Gariwerd) National Park?
Grampians (Gariwerd) National Park is in Grampians, Victoria.
Is Grampians (Gariwerd) National Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Grampians (Gariwerd) National Park?
No, dogs are not allowed.
