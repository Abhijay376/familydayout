Grampians in the Southern Grampians area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Grampians (Gariwerd) National Park is the largest mapped park in Grampians, at about 168,295 hectares. The Sisters Reference Area is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Grampians. The Teddy Bear Gap Loop, The Fortress and The Chimney Pots Walk all pass through Grampians.

Getting around

Grampians has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 55 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Grampians, so most trips start by car. By car from Grampians, Hamilton is about 71 minutes away and Stawell about 84 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Grampians, the nearest places for kids by road are Dunkeld Memorial Park Playground in Dunkeld (44 min) and the playground at Cavendish Town Square Memorial Park in Cavendish (53 min).

Best for

Free days on the Teddy Bear Gap Loop and in Grampians's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Mafeking, Glenisla and Bellfield also border Grampians.
