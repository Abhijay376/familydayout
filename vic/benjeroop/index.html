Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Benjeroop in the Gannawarra area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Benjeroop. The closest to Benjeroop is Kerang Railway Station (V/Line), about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Benjeroop, so most trips start by car. By car from Benjeroop, Swan Hill is about 31 minutes away and Echuca about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Benjeroop is Swimming Pool in Kerang, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest library to Benjeroop is Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang, about 28 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Barham Skate Park in Koondrook is about 42 minutes' drive from Benjeroop. The closest cinema to Benjeroop is Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill, about 30 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 31 minutes from Benjeroop by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Benjeroop, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Gray Park in Lake Boga (18 min), Lake Boga Flying Boat Museum in Lake Boga (18 min), Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (28 min), Swimming Pool in Kerang (28 min), the playground at Guastalegname Park in Swan Hill (28 min) and Barham Branch Library in Koondrook (41 min).
