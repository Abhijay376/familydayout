Playground at Russell Square

Playground in Brown Hill · Official details

Playground at Russell Square in Brown Hill is a public playground in the Ballarat City area. The Goldfields Track passes about 340 m away, and Espresso District is about 380 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 510 m away, and bus 14 stops at Stawell St/Humffray St N, a 8-minute walk away.
