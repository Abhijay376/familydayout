Playground at Brown Hill Reserve

Playground in Brown Hill · Basic info

Playground at Brown Hill Reserve in Brown Hill is a public playground in the Ballarat City area. The Goldfields Track passes about 50 m away, and Brown Hill Swimming Pool is about 160 m away. There's a public toilet about 330 m away, and bus 15 stops at Brown Hill Reserve/Humffray St N, a 3-minute walk away.
