Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Turriff East in the Buloke area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Turriff East has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 91 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Turriff East, so most trips start by car. By car from Turriff East, Swan Hill is about 1.5 hours away and Mildura about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Turriff East is Tempy Outdoor Swimming Pool in Tempy, about 24 minutes' drive. From Turriff East, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Tempy Recreation Reserve in Tempy (24 min), Tempy Outdoor Swimming Pool in Tempy (24 min), the playground at Lascelles Caravan Park in Lascelles (28 min) and Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Turriff East.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Turriff East, Lascelles has 1 playground. Nyarrin and Ninda also border Turriff East.
