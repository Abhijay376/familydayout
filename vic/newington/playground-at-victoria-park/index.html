Playground at Victoria Park

Playground in Newington · Basic info

Playground at Victoria Park is a public playground in Newington, in the Ballarat City area. The Victrack passes about 40 m away, and Victoria Park Inclusive Playspace is another option about 520 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 630 m away, and bus 26 stops at Dawsons Ave/Sturt St, a 7-minute walk away.
