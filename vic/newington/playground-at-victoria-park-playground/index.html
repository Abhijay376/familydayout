Playground at Victoria Park

Playground in Newington · Official details

This is a public playground in Newington. The Victrack is about 30 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 110 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 26 stops at Victoria Park/Sturt St, a 2-minute walk away.
