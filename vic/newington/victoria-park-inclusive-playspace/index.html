Victoria Park Inclusive Playspace

Playground in Newington · Official details

This is a public playground in Newington. The Victrack passes about 40 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 260 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 160 m away, and bus 26 stops at Victoria Park/Sturt St, a 3-minute walk away.
