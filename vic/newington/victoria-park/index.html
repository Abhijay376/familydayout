Victoria Park

Park in Newington · Official details

Victoria Park is a park in Newington, in the Ballarat City Council area, good for all ages. Victoria Park/Sturt St (bus) is a 10-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 830 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Victoria Park?
Victoria Park is in Newington, Victoria.
Is Victoria Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Victoria Park?
Yes, dogs are allowed.
Are there toilets at Victoria Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 830 m away, with a baby change table.
How do I get to Victoria Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Victoria Park/Sturt St (routes 26), a 10-minute walk.
