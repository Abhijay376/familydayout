Brimboal is in the Glenelg area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Brimboal Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Brimboal, at about 9 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Brimboal. The closest to Brimboal is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 147 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Brimboal, so most trips start by car. By car from Brimboal, Hamilton is about 68 minutes away and Portland about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Brimboal is Casterton Pool in Casterton, about 29 minutes' drive. For a skate park, coleraine skate park in Coleraine is about 40 minutes' drive from Brimboal. From Brimboal, the nearest places for kids by road are Gorman's art gallery in Casterton (28 min), Casterton Pool in Casterton (29 min), Bake House Studio in Harrow (49 min), coleraine skate park in Coleraine (40 min), the playground at Queens Park in Coleraine (40 min) and Silvester Oval Playground in Coleraine (40 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Brimboal.
