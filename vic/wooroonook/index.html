Wooroonook is in the Buloke area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Wooroonook is Wooroonook Lakes (Middle and East) Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 210 hectares. Western Wooroonook Lake Reserve and Charlton West I148 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Wooroonook.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wooroonook. The closest to Wooroonook is Kerang Railway Station (V/Line), about 87 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wooroonook, so most trips start by car. By car from Wooroonook, Stawell is about 1.5 hours away and Horsham about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof is about 24 minutes' drive from Wooroonook. For a skate park, Donald BMX Track in Donald is about 25 minutes' drive from Wooroonook. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 14 minutes' drive from Wooroonook. From Wooroonook, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Gordon Park in Charlton (14 min), Rex Theatre in Charlton (14 min), Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (15 min), the playground at Rotary Park in Charlton (15 min), Willandra Museum in Wycheproof (21 min) and Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof (24 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wooroonook.

Nearby suburbs

Charlton, next to Wooroonook, has 3 playgrounds and a cinema. Corack and Chirrip also border Wooroonook.
