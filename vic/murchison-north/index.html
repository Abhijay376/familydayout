Murchison North is in the Greater Shepparton area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Murchison North, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

The Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks passes through Murchison North.

Getting around

Murchison North has no train station; Murchison East Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 7.0 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Murchison North, so most trips start by car. By car from Murchison North, Shepparton is about 25 minutes away, Seymour about 51 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Tatura Swimming complex in Tatura is about 23 minutes' drive from Murchison North. For a library, Mooroopna Library in Mooroopna is about 20 minutes' drive from Murchison North. The closest beach to Murchison North is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 28 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Tatura Memorial Park in Tatura is about 23 minutes' drive from Murchison North. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 26 minutes' drive from Murchison North. The closest indoor play centre to Murchison North is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 26 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kidstown in Mooroopna is about 23 minutes' drive from Murchison North. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Murchison North, about 25 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Murchison North, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Murchison Youth Spot in Murchison (11 min), the playground at Goulburn River Heritage River in Murchison (11 min), Mooroopna Library in Mooroopna (20 min), Tatura Library in Tatura (22 min), Kidstown in Mooroopna (23 min) and Tatura Irrigation and Wartime Camps Museum in Tatura (22 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Murchison North on the Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Murchison North, Toolamba has 2 playgrounds. Murchison, next to Murchison North, has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Murchison North, Arcadia has 1 playground. Moorilim and Dhurringile also border Murchison North.
