Narraport is in the Buloke area, and our data lists 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Narraport is Tcham Lakes Lake Reserve, covering about 98 hectares. Carapugna Bushland Reserve and Narraport Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Playgrounds around Narraport include the playground at Tcham Lakes Lake Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Narraport.

Getting around

There's no train station in Narraport. The closest to Narraport is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 87 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Narraport, so most trips start by car. By car from Narraport, Swan Hill is about 85 minutes away and Horsham about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof is about 19 minutes' drive from Narraport. The closest skate park to Narraport is Birchip Skate Park in Birchip, about 9 minutes' drive. From Narraport, the nearest places for kids by road are Birchip History Museum in Birchip (9 min), Birchip Skate Park in Birchip (9 min), the playground at Birchip Shire Soldiers Memorial Park in Birchip (9 min), Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof (19 min), Willandra Museum in Wycheproof (19 min) and Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem (24 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Narraport.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Narraport, Wycheproof has 1 playground and a pool. Next door to Narraport, Birchip has 2 playgrounds and a skate park. Morton Plains and Dumosa also border Narraport.
