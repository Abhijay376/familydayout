Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Everton, in the Wangaratta area.

Landmarks

Murmungee I75 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Everton, at about 6 hectares. Murmungee I77 Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Everton. For walks and rides, Murray to Mountains Rail Trail: Wangaratta – Bright crosses Everton.

Getting around

Everton has no train station; Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Everton. By car from Everton, Wangaratta is about 22 minutes away and Benalla about 47 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Beechworth Swimming Pool in Beechworth is about 19 minutes' drive from Everton. The closest library to Everton is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Everton is Sandy Beach in Beechworth, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Everton is the pump track on Alma Road in Beechworth, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Everton is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 19 minutes' drive. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Everton, about 22 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Everton, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at John McAleese Community Park in Milawa (14 min), the playground at Newtown Park in Beechworth (17 min), Beechworth Swimming Pool in Beechworth (19 min), Beechworth Contemporary Art Space in Beechworth (19 min), The Burke Museum in Beechworth (19 min) and Sandy Beach in Beechworth (21 min).

Best for

Free days on the Murray to Mountains Rail Trail: Wangaratta – Bright and in Everton's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Everton, Whorouly has 1 playground. Bowmans Forest also borders Everton.
