Playground on Bullumwaal Road

Playground in Wy Yung · Basic info

This is a public playground in Wy Yung. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus 3 stops at Dwyer St/Bullumwaal Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
