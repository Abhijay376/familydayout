Bullumwaal Road Reserve

Park in Wy Yung · Basic info

Bullumwaal Road Reserve is a park in Wy Yung, in the East Gippsland Shire Council area, good for all ages. Morekana Walking Track/Howitt Ave (bus) is a 13-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 42 m away.
