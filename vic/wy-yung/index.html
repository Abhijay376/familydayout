Our data lists 3 playgrounds and 2 parks and reserves in Wy Yung, in the East Gippsland area.

Landmarks

Wy Yung I18 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Wy Yung, at about 9 hectares. Bullumwaal Road Reserve is the next largest. Playgrounds in Wy Yung include Howitt Park and the playground at Howitt Park. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Wy Yung. For walks and rides, Bairnsdale LCN and Mitchell River Walk cross Wy Yung.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wy Yung. The closest to Wy Yung is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 3.1 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 3 stops in Wy Yung. By car from Wy Yung, Bairnsdale is about 5 minutes away and Lakes Entrance about 40 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Wy Yung is Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale, about 4 minutes' drive. The closest library to Wy Yung is Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale, about 4 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Wy Yung is Boyfriend Beach in Forge Creek, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Wy Yung is the skate park at Gilsenan Reserve in Paynesville, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Wy Yung is Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale, about 5 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow is about 11 minutes' drive from Wy Yung. The nearest larger centre is Bairnsdale, about 5 minutes from Wy Yung by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Wy Yung's 3 local playgrounds. Free days on the Bairnsdale LCN and in Wy Yung's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Bairnsdale, next to Wy Yung, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Eastwood, next to Wy Yung, has 1 playground. Lucknow, next to Wy Yung, has a zoo or wildlife park. Hillside, Granite Rock, Ellaswood and East Bairnsdale also border Wy Yung.
