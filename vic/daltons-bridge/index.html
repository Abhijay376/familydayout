Daltons Bridge is in the Gannawarra area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Daltons Bridge, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Daltons Bridge. The closest to Daltons Bridge is Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line), about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Daltons Bridge, so most trips start by car. By car from Daltons Bridge, Echuca is about 54 minutes away and Swan Hill about 83 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Daltons Bridge is Cohuna Pool in Cohuna, about 10 minutes' drive. For a library, Barham Branch Library in Koondrook is about 37 minutes' drive from Daltons Bridge. For a skate park, Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna is about 9 minutes' drive from Daltons Bridge. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 54 minutes from Daltons Bridge by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Daltons Bridge, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Cohuna Apex Park in Cohuna (9 min), Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna (9 min), Cohuna Pool in Cohuna (10 min), the playground at C L King Memorial Park in Cohuna (11 min), Leitchville Memorial Swimming Pool in Leitchville (16 min) and Barham Branch Library in Koondrook (37 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Daltons Bridge.
