Playground at Jubilee Park

Playground in Avenel · Basic info

Playground at Jubilee Park in Avenel is a public playground in the Strathbogie Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and trains stop at Avenel Railway Station, a 8-minute walk away.
