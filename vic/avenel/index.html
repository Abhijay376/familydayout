Avenel is in the Strathbogie area, and our data lists 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Avenel is Stewart Park, covering about 8 hectares. Jubilee Park is the next largest. Playgrounds around Avenel include the playground at Jubilee Park. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Avenel.

Getting around

Trains stop at Avenel Railway Station (V/Line: Albury - Melbourne via Seymour) in Avenel. By car from Avenel, Seymour is about 16 minutes away, Shepparton about 61 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Memorial Swimming pool in Seymour is about 15 minutes' drive from Avenel. For a library, Seymour Library in Seymour is about 17 minutes' drive from Avenel. For a skate park, Seymour Skate Park in Seymour is about 17 minutes' drive from Avenel. The closest cinema to Avenel is Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa, about 27 minutes' drive. Seymour is the closest bigger centre to Avenel, about 16 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Avenel, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Rotary Park in Seymour (15 min), Memorial Swimming pool in Seymour (15 min), Seymour Swimming Centre in Seymour (18 min), Seymour Library in Seymour (17 min), Seymour Skate Park in Seymour (17 min) and Nagambie Historical Society in Nagambie (22 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Avenel.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Avenel, Seymour has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Avenel, Longwood has 1 playground. Tarcombe, Tabilk, Mangalore and Locksley also border Avenel.
