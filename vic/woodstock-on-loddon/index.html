Woodstock On Loddon is in the Loddon area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Woodstock On Loddon, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Woodstock On Loddon has no train station; Kangaroo Flat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Woodstock On Loddon, so most trips start by car. By car from Woodstock On Loddon, Bendigo is about 27 minutes away, Castlemaine about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Sloan Swim School in California Gully is about 24 minutes' drive from Woodstock On Loddon. The closest library to Woodstock On Loddon is Kangaroo Flat Library in Kangaroo Flat, about 21 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk is about 22 minutes' drive from Woodstock On Loddon. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 27 minutes' drive from Woodstock On Loddon. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 26 minutes' drive from Woodstock On Loddon. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 32 minutes' drive from Woodstock On Loddon. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Woodstock On Loddon, about 27 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Woodstock On Loddon are Graemar Court Playspace in Kangaroo Flat (20 min), Kangaroo Flat Library in Kangaroo Flat (21 min), William Drive Playspace in Kangaroo Flat (21 min), Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk (22 min), the bmx track on Victoria Street in Eaglehawk (23 min) and Sloan Swim School in California Gully (24 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Woodstock On Loddon.

Nearby suburbs

Newbridge, next to Woodstock On Loddon, has 1 playground. Leichardt, Laanecoorie and Bridgewater also border Woodstock On Loddon.
