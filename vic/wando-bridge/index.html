Wando Bridge is in the Glenelg area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Wando River Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Wando Bridge, at about 7 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wando Bridge. The closest to Wando Bridge is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 136 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wando Bridge, so most trips start by car. By car from Wando Bridge, Hamilton is about 57 minutes away and Portland about 89 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Casterton Pool in Casterton is about 18 minutes' drive from Wando Bridge. For a skate park, coleraine skate park in Coleraine is about 29 minutes' drive from Wando Bridge. Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Wando Bridge, about 57 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Wando Bridge, the nearest places for kids by road are Gorman's art gallery in Casterton (17 min), Casterton Pool in Casterton (18 min), coleraine skate park in Coleraine (29 min), the playground at Queens Park in Coleraine (29 min), Silvester Oval Playground in Coleraine (29 min) and Merino Swimming Pool in Merino (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wando Bridge.

Nearby suburbs

Wando Vale, next to Wando Bridge, has 1 playground. Dunrobin and Brimboal also border Wando Bridge.
