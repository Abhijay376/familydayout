Hedley is in the Wellington area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Welshpool H17 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Hedley, at about 9 hectares. The Great Southern Rail Trail passes through Hedley.

Getting around

There's no train station in Hedley. The closest to Hedley is Morwell Railway Station (V/Line), about 46 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Hedley, so most trips start by car. By car from Hedley, Leongatha is about 54 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 74 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Toora Pool in Toora is about 15 minutes' drive from Hedley. For a library, Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram is about 17 minutes' drive from Hedley. For a beach, Chinaman Beach in Wilsons Promontory is about 15 minutes' drive from Hedley. The closest skate park to Hedley is the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora, about 16 minutes' drive. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Hedley, about 54 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Hedley, the nearest places for kids by road are Port Welshpool and District Maritime Museum in Port Welshpool (10 min), Chinaman Beach in Wilsons Promontory (15 min), Franklin Bank in Welshpool (15 min), Toora Pool in Toora (15 min), the playground at Stanley Street Playground in Toora (15 min) and Sagasser Park in Toora (16 min).

Best for

Free days on the Great Southern Rail Trail and in Hedley's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Hedley, Welshpool has 1 playground and a beach. Binginwarri, Alberton West and Alberton also border Hedley.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Hedley, without traffic.
