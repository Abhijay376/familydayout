Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bingo Munjie in the East Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Bingo Munjie has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 91 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bingo Munjie, so most trips start by car. By car from Bingo Munjie, Bright is about 2 hours away and Bairnsdale about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek is about 30 minutes' drive from Bingo Munjie. For a skate park, the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo is about 11 minutes' drive from Bingo Munjie. The closest places for kids by road from Bingo Munjie are Petersens Art Gallery & Museum in Omeo (10 min), the playground at Omeo Historical Park & Museum in Omeo (11 min), Omeo Historical Park & Museum in Omeo (11 min), the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo (11 min) and Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek (30 min).
