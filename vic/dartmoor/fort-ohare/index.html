Fort OHare

Park in Dartmoor · Official details

Fort OHare is a park in Dartmoor, in the Glenelg Shire Council area, good for all ages. General Store/Greenham St (coach) is a 11-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 225 m away.

Questions parents ask

Where is Fort OHare?
Fort OHare is in Dartmoor, Victoria.
Is Fort OHare free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Fort OHare?
The nearest public toilet is about 225 m away.
How do I get to Fort OHare by public transport?
The closest stop is General Store/Greenham St (routes Mount Gambier - Melbourne via Warrnambool & Geelong), a 11-minute walk.
