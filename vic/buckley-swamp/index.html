Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Buckley Swamp in the Southern Grampians area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Buckley Swamp. The closest to Buckley Swamp is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 69 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Buckley Swamp, so most trips start by car. By car from Buckley Swamp, Hamilton is about 18 minutes away and Portland about 78 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Hamilton is about 20 minutes' drive from Buckley Swamp. For a library, Hamilton Library in Hamilton is about 18 minutes' drive from Buckley Swamp. The closest skate park to Buckley Swamp is hamilton skate park in Hamilton, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Buckley Swamp is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 18 minutes' drive. Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Buckley Swamp, about 18 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Buckley Swamp are mitchell park playground in Hamilton (17 min), White Avenue Playground in Hamilton (19 min), Sir Reginald Ansett Transport Museum in Hamilton (19 min), hamilton skate park in Hamilton (19 min), Hamilton History Centre in Hamilton (18 min) and Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton (18 min).
