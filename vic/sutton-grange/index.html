Sutton Grange is in the Mount Alexander area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Sutton Grange, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Goldfields Track and Leanganook Track cross Sutton Grange.

Getting around

Sutton Grange has no train station; Castlemaine Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Sutton Grange, so most trips start by car. By car from Sutton Grange, Castlemaine is about 26 minutes away, Bendigo about 37 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Harcourt Swimming Pool in Harcourt is about 20 minutes' drive from Sutton Grange. The closest library to Sutton Grange is Castlemaine Library in Castlemaine, about 26 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Castlemaine Velodrome in Castlemaine is about 27 minutes' drive from Sutton Grange. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 37 minutes' drive from Sutton Grange. The closest indoor play centre to Sutton Grange is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Sutton Grange is The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo, about 40 minutes' drive. Castlemaine is the closest bigger centre to Sutton Grange, about 26 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Sutton Grange, the nearest places for kids by road are Harcourt Swimming Pool in Harcourt (20 min), Chewton Swimming Pool in Chewton (24 min), the playground at Sawpit Gully Reserve in Elphinstone (21 min), Metcalfe Recreation Reserve Playground in Metcalfe (29 min), Castlemaine Velodrome in Castlemaine (27 min) and the pump track on McGrath Street in Castlemaine (27 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Sutton Grange on the Goldfields Track.

Nearby suburbs

Elphinstone, next to Sutton Grange, has 1 playground. Harcourt North and Faraday also border Sutton Grange.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Sutton Grange, without traffic.
