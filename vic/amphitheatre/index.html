Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Amphitheatre, in the Pyrenees area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Amphitheatre is Ben More Bushland Reserve, covering about 137 hectares. Amphitheatre Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Amphitheatre.

Getting around

Amphitheatre has no train station; Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Ararat - Maryborough via Elmhurst, Avoca route stop in Amphitheatre. By car from Amphitheatre, Maryborough is about 33 minutes away, Ararat about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Beaufort Pool in Beaufort is about 34 minutes' drive from Amphitheatre. For a library, Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort is about 33 minutes' drive from Amphitheatre. The closest skate park to Amphitheatre is the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort, about 33 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Astor Cinema in Ararat is about 40 minutes' drive from Amphitheatre. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 33 minutes from Amphitheatre by car, with a library. From Amphitheatre, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Avoca Lions Park in Avoca (11 min), Courthouse in Avoca (12 min), the playground at Toll Bar Park in Lexton (14 min), Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort (33 min), the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (33 min) and Beaufort Pool in Beaufort (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Amphitheatre.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Amphitheatre, Lexton has 2 playgrounds. Avoca, next to Amphitheatre, has 1 playground. Lamplough, Glenpatrick, Glenlogie and Elmhurst also border Amphitheatre.
