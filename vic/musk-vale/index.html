Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Musk Vale, in the Hepburn area.

Landmarks

Other parks and reserves in Musk Vale include Wombat Creek Picnic Area. Picnic areas in Musk Vale include Wombat Creek Picnic Area. For walks and rides, Goldfields Track, Great Dividing Trail and Three Lost Children Walk cross Musk Vale.

Getting around

Musk Vale has no train station; Creswick Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Ballan - Hepburn via Daylesford route stop in Musk Vale. By car from Musk Vale, Daylesford is about 6 minutes away, Castlemaine about 37 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 85 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford is about 5 minutes' drive from Musk Vale. For a library, Daylesford Library in Daylesford is about 6 minutes' drive from Musk Vale. For a beach, Canoeing in Tylden is about 43 minutes' drive from Musk Vale. For a skate park, Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick is about 27 minutes' drive from Musk Vale. The closest cinema to Musk Vale is Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central, about 39 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Musk Vale is Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point, about 40 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Musk Vale is Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek, about 31 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Daylesford, about 6 minutes from Musk Vale by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Musk Vale, the nearest places for kids by road are Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford (5 min), Daylesford Library in Daylesford (6 min), Daylesford Spa Country Railway in Daylesford (8 min), the playground at Hepburn Regional Park in Daylesford (12 min), the playground at Korweinguboora Recreation Reserve in Spargo Creek (11 min) and Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick (27 min).

Best for

Free days on the Goldfields Track and in Musk Vale's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Musk Vale, Daylesford has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Leonards Hill and Eganstown also border Musk Vale.
