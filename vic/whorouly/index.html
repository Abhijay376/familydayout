Our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in Whorouly, in the Wangaratta area.

Landmarks

Ovens River K16 Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Whorouly, at about 6 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Whorouly.

Getting around

There's no train station in Whorouly. The closest to Whorouly is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Whorouly, so most trips start by car. By car from Whorouly, Wangaratta is about 33 minutes away and Bright about 44 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Whorouly is the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest library to Whorouly is Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford, about 17 minutes' drive. For a beach, Sandy Beach in Beechworth is about 23 minutes' drive from Whorouly. For a skate park, Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford is about 17 minutes' drive from Whorouly. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 30 minutes' drive from Whorouly. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Whorouly, about 33 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Whorouly are the playground at John McAleese Community Park in Milawa (18 min), the playground at Rotary Park in Myrtleford (15 min), The Old School Museum in Myrtleford (17 min), Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford (17 min), Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford (17 min) and the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford (18 min).
