Grieves Avenue Reserve

Playground in Indented Head · Basic info

This is a public playground in Indented Head. The Foreshore is about 300 m away, and Playground at Port Phillip Bay Coastal Reserve (COM) is another option about 350 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 250 m from the playground, and bus 60 stops at Indented Head Post Office/The Esplanade , a 6-minute walk away.
