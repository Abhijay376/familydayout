Our data lists 6 playgrounds in Indented Head, in the Greater Geelong area. Beyond the parks, Indented Head has a beach.

Landmarks

Playgrounds in Indented Head include Taylor Reserve, Anderson Reserve and Wrathhall Reserve. Our data shows 6 public toilets in Indented Head. In Indented Head you'll also find Grassy / Point George. For walks and rides, The Foreshore crosses Indented Head.

Getting around

Indented Head has no train station; Werribee Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 60 stops in Indented Head. By car from Indented Head, Geelong is about 36 minutes away, Torquay about 56 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Indented Head is Northern Bellarine Aquatic Centre in Curlewis, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest library to Indented Head is Drysdale Library in Drysdale, about 17 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Portarlington Skate Park in Portarlington is about 7 minutes' drive from Indented Head. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Geelong is about 37 minutes' drive from Indented Head. The closest indoor play centre to Indented Head is Laserquest in Geelong, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Indented Head is Adventure Park in Wallington, about 27 minutes' drive.

Best for

Younger kids at Indented Head's 6 local playgrounds. Beach days at Grassy / Point George. Free days on The Foreshore and in Indented Head's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Indented Head, Portarlington has 10 playgrounds, a beach and a skate park. St Leonards, next to Indented Head, has 9 playgrounds and a beach.
