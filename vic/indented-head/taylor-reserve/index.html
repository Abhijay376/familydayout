Taylor Reserve

Playground in Indented Head · Basic info

Taylor Reserve is a public playground in Indented Head, in the Greater Geelong City area. The Foreshore is about 10 m away, and Playground at Port Phillip Bay Coastal Reserve (COM) is another option about 410 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 60 stops at Ibbotson St/Batman Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
