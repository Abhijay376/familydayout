Our data lists 4 parks and reserves in Dingee, in the Loddon area.

Landmarks

Tang Tang Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Dingee, at about 129 hectares. Dingee Recreation Reserve and Dingee Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Dingee also has Progress Park. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Dingee.

Getting around

Dingee has a train station: Dingee Railway Station (V/Line: Swan Hill - Melbourne via Bendigo). V/Line coaches stop in Dingee. By car from Dingee, Bendigo is about 42 minutes away, Echuca about 55 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Dingee is Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo, about 14 minutes' drive. For a library, Bendigo Library in Bendigo is about 42 minutes' drive from Dingee. For a skate park, McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk is about 35 minutes' drive from Dingee. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 43 minutes' drive from Dingee. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Dingee, about 42 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Dingee are the playground at R J Phelan Reserve in Mitiamo (14 min), Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo (14 min), East Loddon Historical Society in Mitiamo (14 min), Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood (16 min), McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk (35 min) and Eaglehawk Playspace in Eaglehawk (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Dingee.

Nearby suburbs

Milloo and Kamarooka North also border Dingee.
