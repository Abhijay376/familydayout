Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Irrewillipe East, in the Colac Otway area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Irrewillipe East is Irrewillipe Bushland Reserve, covering about 26 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Irrewillipe East. The closest to Irrewillipe East is Colac Railway Station (V/Line), about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Irrewillipe East, so most trips start by car. By car from Irrewillipe East, Colac is about 23 minutes away, Geelong about 88 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Irrewillipe East is Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Irrewillipe East is the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt, about 21 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Colac Cinemas in Colac is about 23 minutes' drive from Irrewillipe East. For an indoor play centre, Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac is about 24 minutes' drive from Irrewillipe East. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 23 minutes from Irrewillipe East by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Irrewillipe East are the playground at Newcombe Estate Park in Colac (21 min), the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (21 min), the playground at Robertson Street Park in Colac (21 min), Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (22 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (23 min) and Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (24 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Irrewillipe East.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Irrewillipe East, Gellibrand has 1 playground. Barongarook West also borders Irrewillipe East.
