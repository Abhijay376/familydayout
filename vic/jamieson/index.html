Landmarks

The biggest green space in Jamieson is Jamieson River/Deep Creek Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 29 hectares. The Island and Howqua G121 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Jamieson also has Scots Mist, Burns Bridge and Quilton North. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Jamieson. Picnic areas in Jamieson include Burns Bridge, Quilton North and Scots Mist. For camping, there's Doctors Creek Reserve. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Jamieson for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Jamieson. The closest to Jamieson is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 85 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Mansfield - Woods Point via Jamieson route stop in Jamieson. By car from Jamieson, Mansfield is about 31 minutes away and Benalla about 82 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Mansfield Outdoor Swimming Pool in Mansfield is about 32 minutes' drive from Jamieson. The closest library to Jamieson is Mansfield Library in Mansfield, about 31 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield is about 31 minutes' drive from Jamieson. For a cinema, Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield is about 30 minutes' drive from Jamieson. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Jamieson is Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield, about 22 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Mansfield, about 31 minutes from Jamieson by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Jamieson, the nearest places for kids by road are Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield (22 min), Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield (30 min), Mansfield Library in Mansfield (31 min), the playground at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (31 min), the playground at Mansfield Botanic Park in Mansfield (31 min) and the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (31 min).
