Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Marnoo West in the Northern Grampians area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Marnoo West has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 65 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Marnoo West, so most trips start by car. By car from Marnoo West, Stawell is about 38 minutes away and Horsham about 60 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Marnoo West is Rupanyup Swimming Pool in Rupanyup, about 24 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Rabl Park in Murtoa is about 37 minutes' drive from Marnoo West. For a zoo or wildlife park, Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell is about 40 minutes' drive from Marnoo West. Stawell is the closest bigger centre to Marnoo West, about 38 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds and a pool. From Marnoo West, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Shanahan Park in Marnoo (16 min), Rupanyup Swimming Pool in Rupanyup (24 min), the playground at Rupanyup Memorial Park in Rupanyup (24 min), Woods' Farming and Heritage Museum in Rupanyup (24 min), Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell (36 min) and Murtoa Museum Complex in Murtoa (37 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Marnoo West.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Marnoo West, Rupanyup has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Marnoo West, Marnoo has 1 playground. Callawadda and Banyena also border Marnoo West.
