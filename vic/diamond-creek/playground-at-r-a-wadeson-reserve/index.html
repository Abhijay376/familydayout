Playground at R.A. Wadeson Reserve

Playground in Diamond Creek · Basic info

Playground at R.A. Wadeson Reserve in Diamond Creek is a public playground in the Nillumbik Shire area. Playground at Nillumbik Park is another option about 500 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 570 m from the playground, and bus 580 stops at Amaroo Ct/Broad Gully Rd, a 7-minute walk away.
