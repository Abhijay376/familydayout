Diamond Creek Regional Playspace

Playground in Diamond Creek · Basic info

Diamond Creek Regional Playspace in Diamond Creek is a public playground in the Nillumbik Shire area. The Diamond Creek Trail passes about 30 m away, and Laksa Hub is about 400 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 80 m away, and buses 343 and 580 stop at Nillumbik Park/Chute St, a 8-minute walk away.
