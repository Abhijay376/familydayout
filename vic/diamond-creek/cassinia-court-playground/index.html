Cassinia Court Playground

Playground in Diamond Creek · Basic info

Cassinia Court Playground is a public playground in Diamond Creek, in the Nillumbik Shire area. Jacaranda Drive Playground is another option about 200 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 830 m from the playground, and bus 343 stops at St Helena Marketplace/Aqueduct Rd, a 9-minute walk away.
