R.A. Wadeson Reserve

Park in Diamond Creek · Basic info

R.A. Wadeson Reserve is a park in Diamond Creek, in the Nillumbik Shire Council area, good for all ages. Amaroo Ct/Broad Gully Rd (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 580 m away with a baby change table.
