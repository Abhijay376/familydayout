Jessamber Court Playground

Playground in Diamond Creek · Basic info

Jessamber Court Playground is a public playground in Diamond Creek, in the Nillumbik Shire area. Bishop Avenue Playground is another option about 200 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 860 m from the playground, and bus 343 stops at Diamond Valley College/Main Hurstbridge Rd, a 8-minute walk away.
