Playground at Brownlow Drive Transmission Reserve

Playground in Diamond Creek · Basic info

Playground at Brownlow Drive Transmission Reserve is a public playground in Diamond Creek, in the Nillumbik Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 960 m away, and bus 381 stops at Broad Gully Rd/Black Gully Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
