Playground at Nillumbik Park

Playground in Diamond Creek · Basic info

This is a public playground in Diamond Creek. The Diamond Creek Trail passes about 100 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 350 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 640 m away, and bus 580 stops at Ryan St/Broad Gully Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
