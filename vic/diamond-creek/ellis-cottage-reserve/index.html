Ellis Cottage Reserve

Park in Diamond Creek · Basic info

Ellis Cottage Reserve is a park in Diamond Creek, in the Nillumbik Shire Council area, good for all ages. Kamarooka Dr/Main Hurstbridge Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1019 m away.
