Lorikeet Court Reserve

Park in Diamond Creek · Basic info

Lorikeet Court Reserve is a park in Diamond Creek, in the Nillumbik Shire Council area, good for all ages. Diamond Creek Cemetery/Main St (bus) is a 10-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1075 m away.
