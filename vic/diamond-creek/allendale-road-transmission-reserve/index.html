Allendale Road Transmission Reserve

Park in Diamond Creek · Basic info

Allendale Road Transmission Reserve is a park in Diamond Creek, in the Nillumbik Shire Council area, good for all ages. Leigh Ct/Parry Rd (bus) is a 10-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1715 m away.
