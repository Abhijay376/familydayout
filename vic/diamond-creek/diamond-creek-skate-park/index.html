Diamond Creek Skate Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Diamond Creek · Basic info

Diamond Creek Skate Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Diamond Creek, in the Nillumbik Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Diamond Creek Station/Station St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 131 m away.
