Carina in the Mildura area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Carina Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Carina, at about 15 hectares.

Getting around

Carina has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 225 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Carina, so most trips start by car. By car from Carina, Mildura is about 2.5 hours away and Swan Hill about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Murrayville Library in Murrayville is about 8 minutes' drive from Carina. The closest places for kids by road from Carina are Murrayville Library in Murrayville (8 min).
