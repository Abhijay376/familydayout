Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Campbells Bridge, in the Northern Grampians area.

Landmarks

Campbells Bridge Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Campbells Bridge, at about 13 hectares. Sheepwash Creek Streamside Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Campbells Bridge. The closest to Campbells Bridge is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 43 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the St Arnaud - Stawell via Marnoo route stop in Campbells Bridge. By car from Campbells Bridge, Stawell is about 14 minutes away and Ararat about 40 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Campbells Bridge is Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell, about 13 minutes' drive. For a library, Ararat Library in Ararat is about 40 minutes' drive from Campbells Bridge. For a beach, Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans is about 35 minutes' drive from Campbells Bridge. For a skate park, the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell is about 18 minutes' drive from Campbells Bridge. The closest cinema to Campbells Bridge is Astor Cinema in Ararat, about 40 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell is about 16 minutes' drive from Campbells Bridge. Stawell is the closest bigger centre to Campbells Bridge, about 14 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds and a pool. From Campbells Bridge, the nearest places for kids by road are Glenorchy Playground in Glenorchy (9 min), Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell (13 min), Stawell Skate Park in Stawell (13 min), Railway Station Art and Crafts Gallery in Stawell (15 min), Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell (16 min) and the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell (18 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Campbells Bridge.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Campbells Bridge, Stawell has 6 playgrounds, a pool and a zoo or wildlife park. Glenorchy, next to Campbells Bridge, has 1 playground. Germania, Deep Lead, Concongella and Callawadda also border Campbells Bridge.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Campbells Bridge, without traffic.
