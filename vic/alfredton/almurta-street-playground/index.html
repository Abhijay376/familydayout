Almurta Street Playground

Playground in Alfredton · Basic info

Almurta Street Playground in Alfredton is a public playground in the Ballarat City area. The Forge Pizzeria is about 170 m away if you need food, and Playground at Victory Avenue Reserve is another option about 240 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.0 km away, and bus 10 stops at Kallioota St/Sturt St, a 4-minute walk away.
