Djila-tjarriu Reserve Playground

Playground in Alfredton · Basic info

This is a public playground in Alfredton. Older kids have a skate park about 60 m away, and Playground on Donegal Drive is another option about 190 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 150 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 3 minutes from Alfredton.
