Karingga Park

Park in Alfredton · Basic info

Karingga Park is a park in Alfredton, in the Ballarat City Council area, good for all ages. Chase Bvd/Jarrah Dr (bus) is a 13-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1398 m away with a baby change table.
