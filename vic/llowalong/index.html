Llowalong is in the Wellington area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Llowalong, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Llowalong has no train station; Stratford Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 7.6 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Llowalong, so most trips start by car. By car from Llowalong, Sale is about 27 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 53 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Llowalong is Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford, about 14 minutes' drive. For a library, Sale Library in Sale is about 28 minutes' drive from Llowalong. For a skate park, Skate Park in Stratford is about 14 minutes' drive from Llowalong. The closest cinema to Llowalong is Former Drive-in site in Sale, about 25 minutes' drive. Sale is the closest bigger centre to Llowalong, about 27 minutes away by car, and has 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Llowalong, the nearest places for kids by road are Mountainview Drive Playground in Stratford (12 min), Soldiers Memorial Park Playground in Stratford (13 min), Skate Park in Stratford (14 min), Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford (14 min), Former Drive-in site in Sale (25 min) and Sale Historical Museum in Sale (28 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Llowalong.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Llowalong, Stratford has 8 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Llowalong, Maffra has 6 playgrounds. Briagolong, next to Llowalong, has 2 playgrounds. Boisdale, next to Llowalong, has 1 playground. Bushy Park also borders Llowalong.
