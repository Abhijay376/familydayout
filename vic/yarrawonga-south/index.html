Yarrawonga South is in the Moira area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Yarrawonga South, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Yarrawonga South has no train station; Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 41 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yarrawonga South, so most trips start by car. By car from Yarrawonga South, Wangaratta is about 44 minutes away and Benalla about 46 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Yarrawonga South is Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Yarrawonga South is Forges Beach No 2 in Yarrawonga, about 15 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Federation Park in Cobram is about 35 minutes' drive from Yarrawonga South. The closest cinema to Yarrawonga South is Cobram Cinema in Cobram, about 34 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Tunzafun in Yarrawonga is about 10 minutes' drive from Yarrawonga South. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 44 minutes from Yarrawonga South by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Yarrawonga South, the nearest places for kids by road are Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga (8 min), Tunzafun in Yarrawonga (10 min), Yarrawonga Mulwala Pioneer Museum in Yarrawonga (10 min), Lonsdale Reserve Playground in Yarrawonga (12 min), Mulwala Waterslide in Yarrawonga (12 min) and Swimming Pool Reserve in Yarrawonga (12 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Yarrawonga South.

Nearby suburbs

Yarrawonga, next to Yarrawonga South, has 6 playgrounds, a library and a beach. Boomahnoomoonah also borders Yarrawonga South.
