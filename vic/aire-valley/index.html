Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Aire Valley in the Colac Otway area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Aire Valley has no train station; Colac Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 39 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Aire Valley, so most trips start by car. By car from Aire Valley, Colac is about 75 minutes away and Torquay about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Apollo Bay Community Pool in Apollo Bay is about 36 minutes' drive from Aire Valley. For a beach, Shelly Beach in Apollo Bay is about 27 minutes' drive from Aire Valley. The closest cinema to Aire Valley is Mechanics Hall in Apollo Bay, about 33 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Aire Valley is Wildlife Wonders in Apollo Bay, about 28 minutes' drive. From Aire Valley, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at John H Gardner Reserve in Beech Forest (31 min), Art Reach Studio in Beech Forest (31 min), Shelly Beach in Apollo Bay (27 min), Wildlife Wonders in Apollo Bay (28 min), Mechanics Hall in Apollo Bay (33 min) and the playground at Foreshore Reserve in Apollo Bay (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Aire Valley.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Aire Valley, Apollo Bay has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. Cape Otway, next to Aire Valley, has 1 playground. Next door to Aire Valley, Beech Forest has 1 playground.
