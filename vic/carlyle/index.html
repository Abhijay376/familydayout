Carlyle is in the Indigo area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Carlyle is Carlyle H120 Bushland Reserve, covering about 4 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Carlyle. The closest to Carlyle is Springhurst Railway Station (V/Line), about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Carlyle, so most trips start by car. By car from Carlyle, Wodonga is about 35 minutes away and Wangaratta about 41 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Carlyle is Corowa Swimming Pool in Wahgunyah, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest library to Carlyle is Rutherglen Public Library in Rutherglen, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Carlyle is Finns Beach in Bandiana, about 39 minutes' drive. For a skate park, BMX Pump Track in Wahgunyah is about 7 minutes' drive from Carlyle. The closest cinema to Carlyle is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 38 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 35 minutes from Carlyle by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Carlyle are Lone Pine Park in Wahgunyah (6 min), BMX Pump Track in Wahgunyah (7 min), Rutherglen Public Library in Rutherglen (12 min), Federation Museum in Wahgunyah (8 min), RSL Park in Wahgunyah (8 min) and the pump track on Victoria Street in Wahgunyah (8 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Carlyle.

Nearby suburbs

Wahgunyah, next to Carlyle, has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Carlyle, Gooramadda has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Rutherglen, next to Carlyle, has 1 playground, a pool and a library.
