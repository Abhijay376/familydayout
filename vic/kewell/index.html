Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Kewell in the Yarriambiack area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Kewell has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 97 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kewell, so most trips start by car. By car from Kewell, Horsham is about 30 minutes away and Stawell about 66 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, the water play at Rabl Park in Murtoa is about 16 minutes' drive from Kewell. For a skate park, the skate park at Rabl Park in Murtoa is about 16 minutes' drive from Kewell. The closest cinema to Kewell is Centre Cinemas in Horsham, about 31 minutes' drive. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to Kewell, about 30 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Kewell are the playground at Rabl Park in Murtoa (15 min), the skate park at Rabl Park in Murtoa (16 min), the water play at Rabl Park in Murtoa (16 min), Murtoa Museum Complex in Murtoa (16 min), the playground at Lake Marma Park- Lions in Murtoa (16 min) and Minyip Swimming Pool in Minyip (16 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Kewell.

Nearby suburbs

Murtoa, next to Kewell, has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Kewell, Minyip has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Kewell, Longerenong has 1 playground. Murra Warra and Kellalac also border Kewell.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Kewell, without traffic.
