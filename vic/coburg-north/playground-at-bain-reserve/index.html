Playground at Bain Reserve

Playground in Coburg North · Basic info

Playground at Bain Reserve is a public playground in Coburg North, in the Merri-Bek City area. The Merlynston to Pascoe Value East-West Route is about 30 m away, and Merlo little library is about 20 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 180 m from the playground, and bus 534 stops at Merlynston Station/Merlyn St, a 1-minute walk away.
