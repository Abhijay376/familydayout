Parker Reserve

Park in Coburg North · Official details

Parker Reserve is a park in Coburg North, in the Merri-Bek City Council area, good for all ages. Anzac Ave/Sydney Rd (bus) is a 8-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 65 m away and dogs are allowed. Opening hours: opening_hours: 24/7.

Where is Parker Reserve?
