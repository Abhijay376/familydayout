Richards Reserve

Park in Coburg North · Basic info

Richards Reserve is a park in Coburg North, in the Merri-Bek City Council area, good for all ages. Mercier St/Gaffney St (bus) is a 7-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 574 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed.
