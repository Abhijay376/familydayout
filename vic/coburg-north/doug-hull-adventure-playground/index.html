Doug Hull Adventure Playground

Playground in Coburg North · Basic info

Doug Hull Adventure Playground is a public playground in Coburg North, in the Merri-Bek City area. The Merri Creek Trail is about 30 m away, and for food, Falleti's Pizza is about 490 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 290 m from the playground, and tram 19 stops at Carr St/Sydney Rd #38, a 3-minute walk away.
