Active Recreation Zone

Playground in Coburg North · Basic info

Active Recreation Zone in Coburg North is a public playground in the Merri-Bek City area. The Merlynston to Pascoe Value East-West Route is about 310 m away, and Merlo little library is about 580 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 60 m from the playground, and bus 534 stops at Athol Ave/Shorts Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
