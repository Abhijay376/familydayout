Coburg Lake Reserve

Park in Coburg North · Official details

Coburg Lake Reserve is a park in Coburg North, in the Merri-Bek City Council area, good for all ages. Murray Rd/Champ St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 161 m away and dogs are allowed. Opening hours: opening_hours: 24/7.
