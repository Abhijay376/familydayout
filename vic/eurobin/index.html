Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Eurobin, in the Alpine area.

Landmarks

Eurobin Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Eurobin, at about 2 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Eurobin.

Getting around

Eurobin has no train station; Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 59 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Eurobin, so most trips start by car. By car from Eurobin, Bright is about 14 minutes away and Wangaratta about 55 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bright Sports Centre in Bright is about 13 minutes' drive from Eurobin. For a library, Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford is about 14 minutes' drive from Eurobin. The closest beach to Eurobin is Pebble Beach in Tawonga South, about 42 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford is about 14 minutes' drive from Eurobin. The closest cinema to Eurobin is Sun Cinema Bright in Bright, about 14 minutes' drive. Bright is the closest bigger centre to Eurobin, about 14 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Eurobin, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Riverside Park in Porepunkah (8 min), Bright Sports Centre in Bright (13 min), the playground at Centenary Park in Bright (14 min), Bright River Pool in Bright (14 min), The Old School Museum in Myrtleford (13 min) and Bright Museum in Bright (14 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Eurobin.

Nearby suburbs

Porepunkah, next to Eurobin, has 1 playground. Mount Buffalo and Havilah also border Eurobin.
