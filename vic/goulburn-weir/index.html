Goulburn Weir is in the Strathbogie area, and our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Wahring Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Goulburn Weir, at about 2 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Goulburn Weir. For walks and rides, Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks crosses Goulburn Weir.

Getting around

There's no train station in Goulburn Weir. The closest to Goulburn Weir is Nagambie Railway Station (V/Line), about 7.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Goulburn Weir, so most trips start by car. By car from Goulburn Weir, Seymour is about 43 minutes away, Shepparton about 52 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Nagambie Pool in Nagambie is about 21 minutes' drive from Goulburn Weir. For a library, Nagambie Library in Nagambie is about 23 minutes' drive from Goulburn Weir. For a skate park, the skate park at Lions Park in Rushworth is about 39 minutes' drive from Goulburn Weir. For a zoo or wildlife park, Emerald Bank Leisure Land in Kialla is about 44 minutes' drive from Goulburn Weir. Seymour is the closest bigger centre to Goulburn Weir, about 43 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Goulburn Weir, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Goulburn River Heritage River in Murchison (20 min), the playground at Murchison Youth Spot in Murchison (20 min), Nagambie Pool in Nagambie (21 min), Nagambie Historical Society in Nagambie (23 min), Nagambie Library in Nagambie (23 min) and the skate park at Lions Park in Rushworth (39 min).

Best for

Free days on the Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks and in Goulburn Weir's parks.
