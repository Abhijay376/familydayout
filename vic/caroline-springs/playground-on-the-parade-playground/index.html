Playground on The Parade

Playground in Caroline Springs · Basic info

Playground on The Parade in Caroline Springs is a public playground in the Melton City area. The Kororoit Creek Trail is about 260 m away, and Caroline Springs Library is about 160 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 60 m from the playground, and bus 462 stops at Creekside College/The Crossing, a 3-minute walk away.
