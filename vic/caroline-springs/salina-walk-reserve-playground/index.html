Salina Walk Reserve Playground

Playground in Caroline Springs · Basic info

This is a public playground in Caroline Springs. The Kororoit Creek Trail passes about 180 m away, and O'Donnell Drive Reserve Playground is another option about 210 m away. There's a public toilet about 750 m away, and buses 215, 460 and 943 stop at The Grove/Caroline Springs Bvd, a 7-minute walk away.
