Playground on Becca Way

Playground in Caroline Springs · Basic info

Playground on Becca Way in Caroline Springs is a public playground in the Melton City area. The Hume Drive Trail is about 190 m away, and for food, Oceans Edge is about 240 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 670 m from the playground, and buses 460 and 943 stop at Taylors Hill Village SC/Gourlay Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
