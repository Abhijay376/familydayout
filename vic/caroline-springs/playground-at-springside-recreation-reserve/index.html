Playground at Springside Recreation Reserve

Playground in Caroline Springs · Basic info

This is a public playground in Caroline Springs. The Hume Drive Trail passes about 100 m away, and Oceans Edge is about 250 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 680 m away, and buses 461 and 464 stop at Springside PS/Hume Dr, a 2-minute walk away.
