Playground at Springlake Village Park

Playground in Caroline Springs · Basic info

Playground at Springlake Village Park in Caroline Springs is a public playground in the Melton City area. Izakaya Rin Japanese Restaurant & Bar is about 240 m away if you need food, and Millennium Gardens Playground is another option about 590 m away. There's a public toilet about 730 m away, and buses 460, 462 and 943 stop at Chisholm Dr/Caroline Springs Bvd, a 4-minute walk away.
