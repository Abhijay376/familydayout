Springside Recreation Reserve Playground

Playground in Caroline Springs · Basic info

This is a public playground in Caroline Springs. The Hume Drive Trail passes about 270 m away, and Oceans Edge is about 440 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 840 m away, and buses 460 and 943 stop at Taylors Hill Village SC/Gourlay Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
