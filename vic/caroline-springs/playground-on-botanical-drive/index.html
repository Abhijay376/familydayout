Botanical Drive Reserve Playground

Playground in Caroline Springs · Basic info

Botanical Drive Reserve Playground is a public playground in Caroline Springs, in the Melton City area. The Stony Hill Creek Trail is about 250 m away, and Parsley Street Reserve Playground is another option about 220 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.2 km from the playground, and buses 461 and 463 stop at Lawson Way/Holland Way, a 1-minute walk away.
