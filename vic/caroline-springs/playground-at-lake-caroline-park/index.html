Playground at Lake Caroline Park

Playground in Caroline Springs · Basic info

Playground at Lake Caroline Park is a public playground in Caroline Springs, in the Melton City area. For food, KFC is about 310 m away, and Lake Caroline Reserve Playground is another option about 280 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 780 m from the playground, and buses 426 and 462 stop at Lake St/Commercial Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
