Northgate Linear Park Playground

Playground in Caroline Springs · Basic info

Northgate Linear Park Playground in Caroline Springs is a public playground in the Melton City area. The Hume Drive Trail passes about 230 m away, and Pizza Hut is about 290 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 750 m away, and buses 460 and 943 stop at Heysen Pkwy/Gourlay Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
