Kinglake is in the Murrindindi area, and our data lists 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in the suburb. On top of that, Kinglake has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

Kinglake East Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Kinglake, at about 8 hectares. Kinglake also has Number Two Creek Reserve. Playgrounds in Kinglake include Bollygum Park. Our data shows 6 public toilets in Kinglake. In Kinglake you'll also find House of Bottles Museum & Tearooms. For walks and rides, Fire Access Track, Bundy Track and Shelley Harris Track cross Kinglake. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Kinglake for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Kinglake has no train station; Hurstbridge Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 384 stops in Kinglake. By car from Kinglake, Healesville is about 33 minutes away, Lilydale about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 67 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kinglake is Whittlesea Swimming Centre in Whittlesea, about 25 minutes' drive. For a library, Yarra Plenty Regional Library in Christmas Hills is about 30 minutes' drive from Kinglake. For a skate park, Hurstbridge Half-pipe in Hurstbridge is about 25 minutes' drive from Kinglake. For a cinema, The Memo, Healesville in Healesville is about 32 minutes' drive from Kinglake. For an indoor play centre, Rare Bears Indoor Play Centre in Eltham is about 41 minutes' drive from Kinglake. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Kinglake is Funfields in Whittlesea, about 28 minutes' drive. Healesville is the closest bigger centre to Kinglake, about 33 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema.

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Kinglake. Free days on the Fire Access Track and in Kinglake's parks.
