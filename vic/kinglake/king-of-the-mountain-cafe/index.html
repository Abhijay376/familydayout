King of the Mountain Cafe

Cafe or restaurant in Kinglake · Basic info

King of the Mountain Cafe is a fish and chips cafe or restaurant in Kinglake, in the Murrindindi Shire Council area, a place to eat during a day out. Kinglake Police Station/Whittlesea-Kinglake Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 148 m away with a baby change table.
