Winton North is in the Benalla area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Winton North is Winton Wetlands Reserve, covering about 8,760 hectares. For walks and rides, Benalla-Mokoan Discovery Trail, Spit Walk Loop and Flynns Road cross Winton North.

Getting around

Winton North has no train station; Benalla Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Winton North, so most trips start by car. By car from Winton North, Benalla is about 24 minutes away, Wangaratta about 31 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla is about 24 minutes' drive from Winton North. For a library, Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta is about 32 minutes' drive from Winton North. The closest skate park to Winton North is Pump Track in Benalla, about 24 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 32 minutes' drive from Winton North. The nearest larger centre is Benalla, about 24 minutes from Winton North by car, with 5 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Winton North are Cobb & Co in Glenrowan (16 min), Glenrowan Museum in Glenrowan (16 min), Pump Track in Benalla (24 min), Benalla Splash Park in Benalla (24 min), Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (24 min) and the playground at Benalla Botanical Gardens in Benalla (25 min).

Best for

Free days on the Benalla-Mokoan Discovery Trail and in Winton North's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Winton North, Benalla has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Glenrowan West and Chesney Vale also border Winton North.
