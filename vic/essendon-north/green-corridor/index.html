Green Corridor

Park in Essendon North · Basic info

Green Corridor is a park in Essendon North, in the Moonee Valley City Council area, good for all ages. Bradshaw St/Keilor Rd #51 (tram) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 701 m away.
