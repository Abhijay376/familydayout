Vin Jervis Reserve

Park in Essendon North · Basic info

Vin Jervis Reserve is a park in Essendon North, in the Moonee Valley City Council area, good for all ages. Royal Ave/Bulla Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 868 m away.
