Duchembegarra is in the Horsham area, and our data lists 6 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Duchembegarra is Lake Wyn Wyn Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 706 hectares. Olivers Lake Flora and Fauna Reserve and Grassflat Swamp Flora and Fauna Reserve are the next largest. Duchembegarra also has Connan Swamp Geological Reserve, Wimmera River - Horsham Camping Area 11 (CLWF) and Wimmera River - Horsham Camping Area 12 (CLWF). For camping, there's Wimmera River - Horsham Camping Area 11 (CLWF) and Wimmera River - Horsham Camping Area 12 (CLWF). The Desert Discovery Walk passes through Duchembegarra.

Getting around

There's no train station in Duchembegarra. The closest to Duchembegarra is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 114 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Duchembegarra, so most trips start by car. By car from Duchembegarra, Horsham is about 26 minutes away and Stawell about 80 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Duchembegarra is Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham, about 27 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk is about 14 minutes' drive from Duchembegarra. For a cinema, Centre Cinemas in Horsham is about 26 minutes' drive from Duchembegarra. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to Duchembegarra, about 26 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Duchembegarra, the nearest places for kids by road are Rope Swing in Vectis (10 min), Arapiles Climbing Museum in Natimuk (13 min), the skate park at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk (14 min), the playground at Natimuk Showgrounds Recreation Reserve in Natimuk (14 min), The Goat Gallery Natimuk in Natimuk (14 min) and Horsham Skate Park in Horsham (26 min).

Best for

Free days on the Desert Discovery Walk and in Duchembegarra's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Natimuk, next to Duchembegarra, has 4 playgrounds and a skate park. Vectis, next to Duchembegarra, has 1 playground. Nurcoung, Mitre and Little Desert also border Duchembegarra.
