Landmarks

Nangiloc Recreation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Nangiloc, at about 8 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Nangiloc.

Getting around

Nangiloc has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 146 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nangiloc, so most trips start by car. By car from Nangiloc, Mildura is about 38 minutes away and Swan Hill about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Irymple Swimming Pool in Irymple is about 31 minutes' drive from Nangiloc. The closest library to Nangiloc is A S Kenyon Library Red Cliffs in Red Cliffs, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Nangiloc is Rudd's Road Sandbar in Iraak, about 11 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Skatepark in Red Cliffs is about 24 minutes' drive from Nangiloc. The closest cinema to Nangiloc is Deakin Cinema Complex in Mildura, about 39 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Sunraysia Tenpin in Mildura is about 38 minutes' drive from Nangiloc. For a zoo or wildlife park, Putt Putt Family Fun Centre in Mildura is about 39 minutes' drive from Nangiloc. Mildura is the closest bigger centre to Nangiloc, about 38 minutes away by car, and has 26 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Nangiloc, the nearest places for kids by road are Rudd's Road Sandbar in Iraak (11 min), Ovens Park Playground in Red Cliffs (25 min), the playground at Barclay Square in Red Cliffs (23 min), Red Cliffs Military Museum in Red Cliffs (23 min), A S Kenyon Library Red Cliffs in Red Cliffs (23 min) and Work Space Gallery 3496 in Red Cliffs (23 min).
