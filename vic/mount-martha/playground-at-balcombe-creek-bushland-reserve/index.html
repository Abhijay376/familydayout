Playground at Balcombe Creek Bushland Reserve

Playground in Mount Martha · Basic info

Playground at Balcombe Creek Bushland Reserve in Mount Martha is a public playground in the Mornington Peninsula Shire area. The Balcombe Creek Estuary Boardwalk is about 10 m away, and for food, Wok on Bay is about 260 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 80 m from the playground, and bus 781 stops at Bay Rd/Esplanade, a 6-minute walk away.
