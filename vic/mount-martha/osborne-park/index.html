Osborne Park

Park in Mount Martha · Basic info

Osborne Park is a park in Mount Martha, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire Council area, good for all ages. Coolangatta Rd/Esplanade (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 414 m away.
