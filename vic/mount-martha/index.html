Mount Martha is in the Mornington Peninsula area, and our data lists 12 playgrounds and 35 parks and reserves in the suburb. On top of that, Mount Martha has a skate park.

Landmarks

The Briars Park is the largest mapped park in Mount Martha, at about 231 hectares. Mount Martha Nature Conservation Reserve and Citation Reserve are the next largest. Mount Martha also has Eco Park, Osborne Park and Fairbairn Park. Playgrounds around Mount Martha include the playground at Eco Park, the playground at Walara Reserve and the playground at Citation Reserve. Our data shows 12 public toilets in Mount Martha. For older kids in Mount Martha there's the skate park at Eco Park. For walks and rides, Mornington Peninsula Bay Trail, Harrap Creek Trail and Ker-Bur-Rer Walk cross Mount Martha. Our data lists 5 cafes and family restaurants in Mount Martha for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Mount Martha has no train station; Baxter Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 781, 784, 785 and 788 stop in Mount Martha. By car from Mount Martha, Mornington is about 9 minutes away, Frankston about 20 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 64 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mount Martha is Peninsula Aquatic Recreation Centre in Frankston, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest library to Mount Martha is Mornington Peninsula Library in Mornington, about 10 minutes' drive. For a beach, Fosters Beach in Mornington is about 6 minutes' drive from Mount Martha. The closest cinema to Mount Martha is Dromana Triple Drive-In Theatre in Dromana, about 13 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Locked In Escape Rooms in Mornington is about 11 minutes' drive from Mount Martha.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 12 playgrounds across Mount Martha. Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at Eco Park in Mount Martha. Free days on the Mornington Peninsula Bay Trail and in Mount Martha's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Mornington, next to Mount Martha, has 22 playgrounds, a library and a beach. Dromana, next to Mount Martha, has 7 playgrounds, a beach and a cinema. Next door to Mount Martha, Safety Beach has 7 playgrounds and a beach. Moorooduc, next to Mount Martha, has 3 playgrounds. Tuerong also borders Mount Martha.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Mount Martha, without traffic.
