Playground at Citation Reserve

Playground in Mount Martha · Basic info

Playground at Citation Reserve is a public playground in Mount Martha, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire area. The Balcombe Creek Estuary Boardwalk is about 210 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus 788 stops at Helena St/Nepean Hwy, a 5-minute walk away.
