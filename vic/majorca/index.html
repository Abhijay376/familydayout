Our data lists 4 parks and reserves in Majorca, in the Central Goldfields area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Majorca is Majorca Racecourse Bushland Reserve, covering about 71 hectares. Kong Meng Historic and Cultural Features Reserve and Majorca Historic and Cultural Features Reserve are the next largest. Majorca also has Victoria Park. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Majorca. For walks and rides, Cycle Central Goldfields crosses Majorca.

Getting around

Majorca has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 8.6 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Majorca, so most trips start by car. By car from Majorca, Maryborough is about 14 minutes away, Castlemaine about 48 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot is about 16 minutes' drive from Majorca. The closest library to Majorca is Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Majorca is Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick, about 42 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 14 minutes from Majorca by car, with a library. The closest places for kids by road from Majorca are the playground at Market Reserve in Carisbrook (11 min), Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (15 min), Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground in Talbot (16 min), Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (16 min), Lee Medlyn Bottle Museum in Clunes (25 min) and The Warehouse in Clunes (26 min).

Best for

Free days on the Cycle Central Goldfields and in Majorca's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Majorca, Carisbrook has 1 playground. Daisy Hill, Craigie and Cotswold also border Majorca.
