Narracan is in the Baw Baw area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Other parks and reserves in Narracan include Narracan Falls Streamside Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Narracan. The closest to Narracan is Moe Railway Station (V/Line), about 9.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Narracan, so most trips start by car. By car from Narracan, Traralgon is about 30 minutes away, Warragul about 35 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar is about 17 minutes' drive from Narracan. For a library, Morwell Library in Morwell is about 19 minutes' drive from Narracan. The closest skate park to Narracan is Moe Skate Park in Moe, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Narracan is Village 8 in Morwell, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Narracan is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 34 minutes' drive. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Narracan, about 30 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. From Narracan, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Wirrana Drive Reserve in Moe (9 min), the playground at Brindlewood Reserve in Trafalgar (16 min), Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar (17 min), Diving Pool in Moe (11 min), Moe Skate Park in Moe (12 min) and ARC Yinnar in Yinnar (22 min).
