Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Gre Gre in the Northern Grampians area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Gre Gre. The closest to Gre Gre is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 70 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gre Gre, so most trips start by car. By car from Gre Gre, Stawell is about 61 minutes away and Horsham about 77 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Gre Gre is St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud, about 24 minutes' drive. From Gre Gre, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Shanahan Park in Marnoo (22 min), the playground at Queen Mary Botanic Gardens in St Arnaud (23 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (24 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (43 min), Donald BMX Track in Donald (47 min) and Woods' Farming and Heritage Museum in Rupanyup (41 min).
