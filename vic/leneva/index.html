Leneva is in the Wodonga area, and our data lists 3 playgrounds and 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Leneva also has Brigade Park. Playgrounds around Leneva include the playground at Brigade Park.

Getting around

Leneva has no train station; Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 9.7 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the B and Albury - Wodonga - Myrtleford routes stop in Leneva. By car from Leneva, Wodonga is about 9 minutes away and Wangaratta about 58 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, WAVES in Wodonga is about 8 minutes' drive from Leneva. For a library, Upper Murray Regional Library - Wodonga in Wodonga is about 9 minutes' drive from Leneva. For a beach, Finns Beach in Bandiana is about 18 minutes' drive from Leneva. For a skate park, Skate Park in Baranduda is about 6 minutes' drive from Leneva. The closest cinema to Leneva is Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana, about 10 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 9 minutes from Leneva by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library.

Best for

Younger kids at Leneva's 3 local playgrounds.

Nearby suburbs

Wodonga, next to Leneva, has 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Leneva, Bandiana has 6 playgrounds, a beach and a cinema. Next door to Leneva, Baranduda has 8 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Yackandandah, next to Leneva, has 1 playground and a skate park. Staghorn Flat, Indigo Valley and Castle Creek also border Leneva.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Leneva, without traffic.
