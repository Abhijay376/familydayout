Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Nelse, in the East Gippsland area.

Landmarks

Bogong Remote and Natural Area is the largest mapped park in Nelse, at about 16,543 hectares. Hollands Knob Reference Area and Whiterock Creek Reference Area are the next largest. Our data shows 8 public toilets in Nelse. The Australian Alps Walking Track, Falls to Hotham Alpine Crossing and 107 Track all pass through Nelse.

Getting around

There's no train station in Nelse. The closest to Nelse is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 96 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nelse, so most trips start by car. By car from Nelse, Bright is about 69 minutes away and Wodonga about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Mt Beauty Library in Mount Beauty is about 40 minutes' drive from Nelse. The closest beach to Nelse is Pebble Beach in Tawonga South, about 44 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Mount Beauty BMX Bike Track in Mount Beauty is about 40 minutes' drive from Nelse. From Nelse, the nearest places for kids by road are Falls Creek Museum in Falls Creek (7 min), Mount Beauty BMX Bike Track in Mount Beauty (40 min), Mt Beauty Library in Mount Beauty (40 min) and Pebble Beach in Tawonga South (44 min).

Best for

Free days on the Australian Alps Walking Track and in Nelse's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Nelse, Mount Beauty has 1 playground, a library and a skate park. Next door to Nelse, Falls Creek has 1 playground. Bogong, next to Nelse, has 1 playground. Mitta Mitta, Glen Wills, Glen Valley and Bundara also border Nelse.
