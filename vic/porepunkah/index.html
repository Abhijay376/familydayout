Porepunkah in the Alpine area has 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Mount Porepunkah Scenic Reserve is the largest mapped park in Porepunkah, at about 496 hectares. Porepunkah Oval and Riverside Park are the next largest. Playgrounds around Porepunkah include the playground at Riverside Park. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Porepunkah. For walks and rides, Mt Porepunkah Loop crosses Porepunkah. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Porepunkah for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Porepunkah. The closest to Porepunkah is Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line), about 66 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Wangaratta-Bright route stop in Porepunkah. V/Line coaches stop in Porepunkah. By car from Porepunkah, Bright is about 7 minutes away and Wangaratta about 62 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bright Sports Centre in Bright is about 7 minutes' drive from Porepunkah. For a library, Bright Library in Bright is about 8 minutes' drive from Porepunkah. The closest beach to Porepunkah is Pebble Beach in Tawonga South, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Porepunkah is Bright BMX Track in Bright, about 11 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Sun Cinema Bright in Bright is about 8 minutes' drive from Porepunkah. Bright is the closest bigger centre to Porepunkah, about 7 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Porepunkah, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Centenary Park in Bright (8 min), Sun Cinema Bright in Bright (8 min), Bright Sports Centre in Bright (7 min), Bright River Pool in Bright (7 min), Bright Library in Bright (8 min) and Bright Museum in Bright (7 min).

Best for

Free days on the Mt Porepunkah Loop and in Porepunkah's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Bright, next to Porepunkah, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Eurobin and Buckland also border Porepunkah.
