Pastoria East is in the Macedon Ranges area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Pastoria East, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Pastoria East has no train station; Kyneton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 17 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Pastoria East, so most trips start by car. By car from Pastoria East, Kyneton is about 29 minutes away, Castlemaine about 56 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Pastoria East is Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore, about 37 minutes' drive. The closest library to Pastoria East is Kyneton Library in Kyneton, about 30 minutes' drive. For a beach, Canoeing in Tylden is about 41 minutes' drive from Pastoria East. The closest skate park to Pastoria East is Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton, about 30 minutes' drive. Kyneton is the closest bigger centre to Pastoria East, about 29 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds and a library. From Pastoria East, the nearest places for kids by road are Jean Haynes Playground in Kyneton (29 min), Kyneton Museum in Kyneton (29 min), Kyneton Library in Kyneton (30 min), Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton (30 min), Kyneton Community Park in Kyneton (31 min) and Canoeing in Tylden (41 min).
