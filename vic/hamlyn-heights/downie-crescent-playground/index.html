Downie Crescent Playground

Playground in Hamlyn Heights · Basic info

Downie Crescent Playground in Hamlyn Heights is a public playground in the Greater Geelong City area. Western Heights College Library is about 530 m away, and for food, Pizza Mob is about 310 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 370 m from the playground, and bus 25 stops at Acacia St/Chaucer St, a 4-minute walk away.
