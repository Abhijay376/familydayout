Skate park on Waymouth Street

Skate, BMX or pump track in Hamlyn Heights · Basic info

Skate park on Waymouth Street is a skate, bmx or pump track in Hamlyn Heights, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Vines Road Community Centre/Vines Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 103 m away.
