Tom McKean Linear Park

Park in Hamlyn Heights · Official details

Tom McKean Linear Park is a park in Hamlyn Heights, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Western Heights Secondary College/Vines Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 148 m away and dogs are allowed.
