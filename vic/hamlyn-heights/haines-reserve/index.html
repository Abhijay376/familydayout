Haines Reserve

Park in Hamlyn Heights · Basic info

Haines Reserve is a park in Hamlyn Heights, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Haines Reserve/Chaucer St (bus) is a 5-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 1100 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed.
