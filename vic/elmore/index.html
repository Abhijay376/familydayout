Elmore in the Greater Bendigo area has 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in our data. Elmore also has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Elmore is Picaninny Creek Streamside Reserve, covering about 13 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Elmore. In Elmore you'll also find Campaspe Run Rural Discovery Centre.

Getting around

Elmore has a train station: Elmore Railway Station (V/Line: Echuca/Moama - Melbourne via Bendigo or Heathcote). V/Line coaches stop in Elmore. By car from Elmore, Echuca is about 38 minutes away, Bendigo about 44 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Elmore is Memorial Pool in Rochester, about 14 minutes' drive. For a library, Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca is about 38 minutes' drive from Elmore. The closest skate park to Elmore is McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk, about 43 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Elmore is Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca, about 38 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 41 minutes' drive from Elmore. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 38 minutes from Elmore by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Elmore are Memorial Pool in Rochester (14 min), the playground at Apex Park in Rochester (16 min), Goornong Swimming Pool in Goornong (16 min), the playground at Pennington Reserve in Huntly (28 min), Bendigo Joss House Temple in North Bendigo (41 min) and The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo (41 min).
