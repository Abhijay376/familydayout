Playground on Northern Highway

Playground in Elmore · Basic info

Playground on Northern Highway is a public playground in Elmore, in the Greater Bendigo City area. Campaspe Run Rural Discovery Centre is about 100 m away. There's a public toilet about 150 m away, and coaches stop at Elmore Station/Northern Hwy , a 2-minute walk away.
