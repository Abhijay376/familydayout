Nowhere Creek is in the Pyrenees area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Nowhere Creek, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Nowhere Creek. The closest to Nowhere Creek is Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line), about 34 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nowhere Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Nowhere Creek, Ararat is about 46 minutes away, Maryborough about 60 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

Ararat is the closest bigger centre to Nowhere Creek, about 46 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Nowhere Creek are the playground at Avoca Lions Park in Avoca (38 min), Courthouse in Avoca (39 min), the playground at Toll Bar Park in Lexton (39 min), David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat (47 min), Ararat Gallery TAMA in Ararat (46 min) and Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat (46 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Nowhere Creek.
