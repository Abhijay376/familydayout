Our data lists 7 parks and reserves in Beauchamp, in the Gannawarra area.

Landmarks

Bael Bael Grassland Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Beauchamp, at about 1,777 hectares. Bael Bael Grassland (addition) Nature Conservation Reserve and Yassom Swamp Nature Conservation Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Beauchamp include Beauchamp Bushland Reserve, Lake Lookout Bushland Reserve and Korrak Korrak Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Beauchamp has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 30 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Beauchamp, so most trips start by car. By car from Beauchamp, Swan Hill is about 53 minutes away and Echuca about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 53 minutes from Beauchamp by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Beauchamp, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Gray Park in Lake Boga (39 min), Lake Boga Flying Boat Museum in Lake Boga (42 min), Quambatook Historical Centre in Quambatook (48 min), Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook (48 min), Quambatook Lions Club Playground in Quambatook (49 min) and Quambatook Memorial Pool in Quambatook (49 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Beauchamp.

Nearby suburbs

Lalbert, Kunat, Cannie and Bael Bael also border Beauchamp.
