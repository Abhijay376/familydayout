Puckapunyal is in the Mitchell area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Puckapunyal, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids. Puckapunyal does have a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

In Puckapunyal you'll also find RAAC Memorial & Army Tank Museum. For walks and rides, Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks crosses Puckapunyal.

Getting around

There's no train station in Puckapunyal. The closest to Puckapunyal is Seymour Railway Station (V/Line), about 9.1 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 3 stops in Puckapunyal. V/Line coaches stop in Puckapunyal. By car from Puckapunyal, Seymour is about 49 minutes away, Shepparton about 1.5 hours and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 49 minutes from Puckapunyal by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Puckapunyal are the playground at Rotary Park in Seymour (47 min), the playground at Kings Park in Seymour (48 min), Memorial Swimming pool in Seymour (48 min), Seymour Library in Seymour (49 min), Seymour Skate Park in Seymour (49 min) and Seymour Swimming Centre in Seymour (51 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Puckapunyal. Longer rides and walks from Puckapunyal on the Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks.

Nearby suburbs

Heathcote, next to Puckapunyal, has 1 playground, a library and a skate park. Next door to Puckapunyal, Tooborac has 1 playground. Mitchellstown, Hilldene, Graytown and Glenaroua also border Puckapunyal.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Puckapunyal, without traffic.
