Our data lists 4 parks and reserves in Meringur, in the Mildura area. Meringur also has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Meringur is Yarrara Flora and Fauna Reserve, covering about 2,263 hectares. Meringur Flora and Fauna Reserve and Karween Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Meringur include Morkala Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Meringur. Places to visit in Meringur include Millewa Pioneer Park. The Former Morkalla-Redcliffs Railway passes through Meringur.

Getting around

Meringur has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 229 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Meringur, so most trips start by car. By car from Meringur, Mildura is about 71 minutes away and Swan Hill about 3.5 hours, without traffic.
