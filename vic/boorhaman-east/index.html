Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Boorhaman East, in the Wangaratta area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Boorhaman East is Black Swamp (Black Dog Creek) Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 130 hectares. Black Swamp Nature Conservation Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Boorhaman East. The closest to Boorhaman East is Springhurst Railway Station (V/Line), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Boorhaman East, so most trips start by car. By car from Boorhaman East, Wangaratta is about 31 minutes away and Wodonga about 56 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Boorhaman East is Rutherglen War Memorial Swimming Pool in Rutherglen, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest library to Boorhaman East is Rutherglen Public Library in Rutherglen, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Boorhaman East is the pump track on Victoria Street in Wahgunyah, about 26 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 29 minutes' drive from Boorhaman East. For a zoo or wildlife park, Tunzafun in Yarrawonga is about 45 minutes' drive from Boorhaman East. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Boorhaman East, about 31 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Boorhaman East, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Peechelba Reserve in Peechelba (23 min), Apex Park Playground in Rutherglen (24 min), Rutherglen War Memorial Swimming Pool in Rutherglen (25 min), Rutherglen Public Library in Rutherglen (25 min), the pump track on Victoria Street in Wahgunyah (26 min) and Corowa Library in Wahgunyah (28 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Boorhaman East.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Boorhaman East, Springhurst has 1 playground. Boorhaman also borders Boorhaman East.
