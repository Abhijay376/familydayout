Boho is in the Strathbogie area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Boho, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Boho has no train station; Violet Town Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 7.2 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Boho, so most trips start by car. By car from Boho, Benalla is about 33 minutes away, Shepparton about 56 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Boho is Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa, about 27 minutes' drive. For a library, Euroa Library in Euroa is about 27 minutes' drive from Boho. For a skate park, Euroa Skate Park in Euroa is about 26 minutes' drive from Boho. For a cinema, Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa is about 26 minutes' drive from Boho. The nearest larger centre is Benalla, about 33 minutes from Boho by car, with 5 playgrounds and a pool. From Boho, the nearest places for kids by road are Violet Town Recreation Reserve East Playground in Violet Town (13 min), Violet Town Recreation Reserve West Playground in Violet Town (13 min), Farmers Arms Hotel Museum in Euroa (26 min), Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa (26 min), Euroa Skate Park in Euroa (26 min) and Euroa Library in Euroa (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Boho.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Boho, Violet Town has 3 playgrounds. Balmattum and Baddaginnie also border Boho.
