Playground at Savanna Parade Park

Playground in Truganina · Official details

This is a neighbourhood playground in Truganina, looked after by Wyndham City Council. The Skeleton Creek Trail is about 10 m away, and Federation Trail Playground (Park Vista Drive Park West) is another option about 490 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 820 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 12 minutes from Truganina.
