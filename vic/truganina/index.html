Our data lists 29 playgrounds and 25 parks and reserves in Truganina, in the Melton area. On top of that, Truganina has a pool.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Truganina is Mt Atkinson Nature Reserve, covering about 56 hectares. Mount Cottrell Nature Conservation Reserve and Fortitude Drive Reserve are the next largest. Playground at Mainview Boulevard Reserve is one of the council's larger district playgrounds. Other parks and reserves in Truganina include Forsyth Park, Albright Park and Durell Road Park. Other playgrounds in Truganina include Cheviot Drive Playground, Jupiter Drive Playground and Cuthbert Avenue Playground. Our data shows 11 public toilets and 3 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Truganina. In Truganina you'll also find Saltwater Swim School. Truganina Central Shopping Centre is the local shopping centre. The Federation Trail, Skeleton Creek Trail and Laverton Creek Trail (Non-contiguous) all pass through Truganina. Our data lists 7 cafes and family restaurants in Truganina for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Truganina has no train station; Tarneit Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 3.3 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 150, 151, 152, 154, 160, 400 and 140A stop in Truganina. By car from Truganina, Sunshine is about 15 minutes away, Werribee about 16 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 26 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Truganina is Julia Gillard Library in Tarneit, about 6 minutes' drive. For a beach, Altona Beach in Altona is about 19 minutes' drive from Truganina. For a skate park, the skate park at Baden Powell Drive Reserve in Tarneit is about 6 minutes' drive from Truganina. For a cinema, VIllage Cinemas in Hoppers Crossing is about 11 minutes' drive from Truganina. For an indoor play centre, Timezone in Hoppers Crossing is about 12 minutes' drive from Truganina.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 29 playgrounds across Truganina. Free days on the Federation Trail and in Truganina's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Truganina, Hoppers Crossing has 34 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. Tarneit, next to Truganina, has 37 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Aintree, next to Truganina, has 16 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Truganina, Derrimut has 7 playgrounds and a pool. Williams Landing, Rockbank, Deanside and Ravenhall also border Truganina.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Truganina, without traffic.
