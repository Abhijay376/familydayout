Jupiter Drive Park

Park in Truganina · Basic info

Jupiter Drive Park is a park in Truganina, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Custodian Way/Bolivar Esp (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 618 m away.
