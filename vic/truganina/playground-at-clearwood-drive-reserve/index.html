Playground at Clearwood Drive Reserve

Playground in Truganina · Official details

This is a neighbourhood playground in Truganina, looked after by Wyndham City Council. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet about 1.1 km away, and bus 151 stops at Stony Brook Dr/Westmeadows Lane, a 3-minute walk away.
