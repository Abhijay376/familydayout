Federation Trail Playground (Park Vista Drive Park West)

Playground in Truganina · Official details

Federation Trail Playground (Park Vista Drive Park West) in Truganina is a neighbourhood playground in the Wyndham City area. The Skeleton Creek Trail is about 80 m away, and Playground at Savanna Parade Park is another option about 490 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.1 km from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 13 minutes from Truganina.
