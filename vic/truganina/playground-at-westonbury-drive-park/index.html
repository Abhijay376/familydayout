Playground at Westonbury Drive park

Playground in Truganina · Official details

This is a small local playground in Truganina, looked after by Wyndham City Council. Forsyth Reserve Playground is another option about 370 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 700 m from the playground, and bus 151 stops at Vanderbilt Ave/Marquands Rd, a 5-minute walk away.

Features

  • Size: Small local (council data)
  • Fenced: No (council data)
  • Toilet: 700 m walk, accessible toilet, 24 hours
