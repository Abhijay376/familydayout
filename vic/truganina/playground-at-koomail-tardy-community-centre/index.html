Playground at Koomail Tardy Community Centre

Playground in Truganina · Official details

Playground at Koomail Tardy Community Centre is a playground in Truganina, in the Wyndham City Council area, best for toddlers and primary-school kids. Aphrodite Dr/Leakes St (bus) is a 12-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1823 m away.
