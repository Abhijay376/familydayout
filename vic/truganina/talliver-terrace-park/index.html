Talliver Terrace Park

Park in Truganina · Basic info

Talliver Terrace Park is a park in Truganina, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Stony Brook Dr/Westmeadows Lane (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 220 m away.
