Savanna Parade Park

Park in Truganina · Basic info

Savanna Parade Park is a park in Truganina, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Federation Bvd/Forsyth Rd (bus) is a 13-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 842 m away.
