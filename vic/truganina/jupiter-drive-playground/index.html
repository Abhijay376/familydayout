Jupiter Drive Playground

Playground in Truganina · Official details

Jupiter Drive Playground is a neighbourhood playground in Truganina, in the Wyndham City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet about 620 m away, and bus 152 stops at Custodian Way/Bolivar Esp, a 3-minute walk away.

Features

  • Size: Neighbourhood (council data)
  • Fenced: No (council data)
  • Ground: Soft fall
