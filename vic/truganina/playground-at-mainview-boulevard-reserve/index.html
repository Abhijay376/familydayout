Playground at Mainview Boulevard Reserve

Playground in Truganina · Official details

Playground at Mainview Boulevard Reserve in Truganina is one of the council's larger playgrounds in the Wyndham City area. For food, Pie Face is about 410 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 390 m from the playground, and bus 152 stops at Mainview Bvd/Leakes Rd, a 3-minute walk away.

Features

  • Size: Large, district level (council data)
  • Fenced: No (council data)
  • Toilet: 390 m walk, accessible toilet, daylight hours
  • Food nearby: Pie Face (410 m)
  • Public transport: Bus 152 at Mainview Bvd/Leakes Rd (3 min walk)
  • Drive time: 8 min from Truganina, 4 min from Williams Landing, 7 min from Tarneit
