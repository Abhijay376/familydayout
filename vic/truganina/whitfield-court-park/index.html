Whitfield Court Park

Park in Truganina · Basic info

Whitfield Court Park is a park in Truganina, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Grevillea Cres/Maple Cres (bus) is a 10-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1332 m away.
