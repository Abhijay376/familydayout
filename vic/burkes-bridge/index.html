Burkes Bridge is in the Gannawarra area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Gunbower National Park is the largest mapped park in Burkes Bridge, at about 9,331 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Burkes Bridge. The closest to Burkes Bridge is Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line), about 31 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Burkes Bridge, so most trips start by car. By car from Burkes Bridge, Echuca is about 49 minutes away and Swan Hill about 88 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Burkes Bridge is Leitchville Memorial Swimming Pool in Leitchville, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest library to Burkes Bridge is Barham Branch Library in Koondrook, about 42 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna is about 15 minutes' drive from Burkes Bridge. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 49 minutes from Burkes Bridge by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Burkes Bridge, the nearest places for kids by road are Leitchville Memorial Swimming Pool in Leitchville (11 min), Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna (15 min), the playground at Cohuna Apex Park in Cohuna (15 min), Cohuna Pool in Cohuna (16 min), the playground at C L King Memorial Park in Cohuna (16 min) and Pyramid Hill & Districts Historical Museum in Pyramid Hill (45 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Burkes Bridge.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Burkes Bridge, Leitchville has 4 playgrounds and a pool. Cohuna, next to Burkes Bridge, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park.
