Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Kaarimba, in the Moira area.

Landmarks

Kaarimba H119 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Kaarimba, at about 35 hectares. Kaarimba H25 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kaarimba. The closest to Kaarimba is Mooroopna Railway Station (V/Line), about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kaarimba, so most trips start by car. By car from Kaarimba, Shepparton is about 32 minutes away, Echuca about 53 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kaarimba is Nathalia Memorial Pool in Nathalia, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest library to Kaarimba is Nathalia Library in Nathalia, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Kaarimba is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 34 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Riverland Skate Park in Numurkah is about 28 minutes' drive from Kaarimba. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 32 minutes' drive from Kaarimba. The closest indoor play centre to Kaarimba is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Kaarimba is Kidstown in Mooroopna, about 34 minutes' drive. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Kaarimba, about 32 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Kaarimba, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Bunbartha Recreation Reserve in Bunbartha (10 min), Barmah Forest Heritge and Education Centre in Nathalia (15 min), Nathalia Library in Nathalia (15 min), the playground at Apex Park in Nathalia (16 min), Nathalia Memorial Pool in Nathalia (18 min) and Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah (28 min).
