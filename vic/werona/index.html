Werona is in the Mount Alexander area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Werona, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Werona has no train station; Creswick Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Werona, so most trips start by car. By car from Werona, Daylesford is about 29 minutes away, Castlemaine about 35 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Werona is Newstead Swimming Pool in Newstead, about 21 minutes' drive. For a library, Daylesford Library in Daylesford is about 28 minutes' drive from Werona. For a skate park, Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick is about 33 minutes' drive from Werona. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Werona is Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek, about 41 minutes' drive. Daylesford is the closest bigger centre to Werona, about 29 minutes away by car, and has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Werona are the playground at Newstead Memorial Park in Newstead (19 min), Newstead Swimming Pool in Newstead (21 min), Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford (28 min), Daylesford Library in Daylesford (28 min), Daylesford Spa Country Railway in Daylesford (30 min) and Giant Slide in Vaughan (42 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Werona.

Nearby suburbs

Franklinford and Campbelltown also border Werona.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Werona, without traffic.
