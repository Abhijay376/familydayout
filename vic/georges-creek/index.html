Georges Creek is in the Towong area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Georges Creek, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Georges Creek. The High Country Rail Trail, Tallangatta - Firebrace Loop and Tallangatta - Lake Road Loop all pass through Georges Creek.

Getting around

Georges Creek has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 33 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Georges Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Georges Creek, Wodonga is about 53 minutes away and Wangaratta about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Georges Creek is Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta, about 20 minutes' drive. For a library, Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta is about 20 minutes' drive from Georges Creek. The closest skate park to Georges Creek is Skate Park in Baranduda, about 45 minutes' drive. Wodonga is the closest bigger centre to Georges Creek, about 53 minutes away by car, and has 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Georges Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta (20 min), Tallangatta Art & Photography in Tallangatta (20 min), Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta (20 min), Granya Museum in Granya (33 min), the playground at Banksia Park in Tangambalanga (32 min) and the playground at Roy Williams Park in Bellbridge (33 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Georges Creek on the High Country Rail Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Granya and Bullioh also border Georges Creek.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Georges Creek, without traffic.
