Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Wehla, in the Loddon area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Wehla is Wehla Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 411 hectares. Kangderaar I107 Bushland Reserve and Wehla Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

Wehla has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 51 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wehla, so most trips start by car. By car from Wehla, Bendigo is about 59 minutes away, Maryborough about 61 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn is about 23 minutes' drive from Wehla. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Wehla, about 59 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Wehla are Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (23 min), Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (23 min), Bi-Centennial Park Playground in St Arnaud (26 min), the playground at Queen Mary Botanic Gardens in St Arnaud (27 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (27 min) and Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (37 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wehla.

Nearby suburbs

Cochranes Creek, Burkes Flat and Brenanah also border Wehla.
