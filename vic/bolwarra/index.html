Our data lists 4 parks and reserves in Bolwarra, in the Glenelg area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bolwarra is Bolwarra H44 Bushland Reserve, covering about 120 hectares. Bolwarra H43 Bushland Reserve and Bolwarra H45 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Bolwarra also has Bolwarra Recreation Reserve. The Great South West Walk passes through Bolwarra.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bolwarra. The closest to Bolwarra is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 76 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bolwarra, so most trips start by car. By car from Bolwarra, Portland is about 8 minutes away and Hamilton about 62 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bolwarra is Portland Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Portland, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest library to Bolwarra is Heywood Library in Heywood, about 15 minutes' drive. For a beach, Whalers Point in Portland is about 8 minutes' drive from Bolwarra. Portland is the closest bigger centre to Bolwarra, about 8 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. From Bolwarra, the nearest places for kids by road are Whalers Point in Portland (8 min), the playground at Narrawong Recreation Reserve in Narrawong (11 min), the playground at Mitchell Park in Portland (9 min), Powerhouse Motor Car Museum in Portland (10 min), Cable Tram Depot in Portland (11 min) and Portland Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Portland (11 min).

Best for

Free days on the Great South West Walk and in Bolwarra's parks.
