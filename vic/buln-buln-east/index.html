Buln Buln East is in the Baw Baw area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Buln Buln East, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Buln Buln East. The closest to Buln Buln East is Warragul Railway Station (V/Line), about 10 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Buln Buln East, so most trips start by car. By car from Buln Buln East, Warragul is about 16 minutes away, Pakenham about 45 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 89 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Buln Buln East is Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul, about 16 minutes' drive. For a library, Warragul Library in Warragul is about 17 minutes' drive from Buln Buln East. The closest skate park to Buln Buln East is Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Buln Buln East is Warragul Cinema in Warragul, about 15 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 16 minutes' drive from Buln Buln East. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Buln Buln East is Wildlife Trail in Tynong North, about 32 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Warragul, about 16 minutes from Buln Buln East by car, with 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Buln Buln East, the nearest places for kids by road are Conventry Drive Playground in Warragul (13 min), the playground at Pederson Park in Warragul (15 min), Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South (14 min), Warragul Library in Warragul (17 min), Warragul & District Historical Society Museum in Warragul (15 min) and Warragul Velodrome in Warragul (16 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Buln Buln East.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Buln Buln East, Neerim South has a skate park. Crossover and Buln Buln also border Buln Buln East.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Buln Buln East, without traffic.
