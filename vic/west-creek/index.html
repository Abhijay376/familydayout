Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside West Creek in the Bass Coast area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in West Creek. The closest to West Creek is Stony Point Railway Station (metro), about 39 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside West Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from West Creek, Wonthaggi is about 17 minutes away, Leongatha about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to West Creek is Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Wonthaggi, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest library to West Creek is Wonthaggi Principal Library in Wonthaggi, about 17 minutes' drive. For a beach, Williamsons Beach in Wonthaggi is about 24 minutes' drive from West Creek. The closest skate park to West Creek is Wonthaggi Skate Park in Wonthaggi, about 15 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi is about 16 minutes' drive from West Creek. For a zoo or wildlife park, Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville is about 31 minutes' drive from West Creek. Wonthaggi is the closest bigger centre to West Creek, about 17 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From West Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Jacobs Park in North Wonthaggi (14 min), Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Wonthaggi (15 min), Kids Playground in Wonthaggi (15 min), Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi (16 min), Wonthaggi Skate Park in Wonthaggi (15 min) and Wonthaggi Principal Library in Wonthaggi (17 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from West Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Dalyston, next to West Creek, has 1 playground and a beach. Next door to West Creek, North Wonthaggi has 1 playground. Lance Creek, Glen Alvie and Archies Creek also border West Creek.
