Bellfield (Northern Grampians) in the Northern Grampians area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Serra Range Remote and Natural Area is the largest mapped park in Bellfield (Northern Grampians), at about 11,362 hectares. Moora Valley Reference Area is the next largest. Our data shows 8 public toilets in Bellfield (Northern Grampians). For walks and rides, Grampians Peaks Trail, Mt Rosea Track and Burma Track cross Bellfield (Northern Grampians).

Getting around

There's no train station in Bellfield (Northern Grampians). The closest to Bellfield (Northern Grampians) is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 44 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Bellfield (Northern Grampians). By car from Bellfield (Northern Grampians), Stawell is about 2.5 hours away and Hamilton about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest places for kids by road from Bellfield (Northern Grampians) are MOCO Gallery in Halls Gap (129 min), Brambuk Cultural Centre in Halls Gap (132 min), the playground at Halls Gap Picnic Reserve in Halls Gap (134 min) and Halls Gap Swimming Pool in Halls Gap (134 min).

Best for

Free days on the Grampians Peaks Trail and in Bellfield (Northern Grampians)'s parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Bellfield (Northern Grampians), Halls Gap has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a zoo or wildlife park. Grampians and Glenisla also border Bellfield (Northern Grampians).
