Birchip is in the Buloke area, and our data lists 2 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in the suburb. Birchip also has a museum or gallery and a skate park.

Landmarks

Karyrie I297 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Birchip, at about 12 hectares. J.E. Taylor Sunset Park is the next largest. Birchip also has Birchip Shire Soldiers Memorial Park. Playgrounds around Birchip include the playground at J.E Taylor Sunset Park and the playground at Birchip Shire Soldiers Memorial Park. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Birchip. Places to visit in Birchip include Birchip History Museum. For older kids in Birchip there's Birchip Skate Park.

Getting around

Birchip has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 92 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Horsham - Birchip via Warracknabeal and Bendigo - Woomelang via Wedderburn, Charlton, Wycheproof, Birchip routes stop in Birchip. V/Line coaches stop in Birchip. By car from Birchip, Swan Hill is about 85 minutes away and Horsham about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem is about 17 minutes' drive from Birchip. The closest places for kids by road from Birchip are Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem (17 min), Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof (28 min) and Willandra Museum in Wycheproof (28 min).

Best for
