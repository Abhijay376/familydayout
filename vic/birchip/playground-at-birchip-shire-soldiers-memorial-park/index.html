Playground at Birchip Shire Soldiers Memorial Park

Playground in Birchip · Basic info

Playground at Birchip Shire Soldiers Memorial Park is a public playground in Birchip, in the Buloke Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 60 m away, and Birchip History Museum is about 290 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and coaches stop at Memorial Park/Cumming Ave, a 1-minute walk away.
