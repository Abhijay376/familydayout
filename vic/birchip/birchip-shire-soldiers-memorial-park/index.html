Birchip Shire Soldiers Memorial Park

Park in Birchip · Basic info

Birchip Shire Soldiers Memorial Park is a park in Birchip, in the Buloke Shire Council area, good for all ages. Memorial Park/Cumming Ave (coach) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 12 m away.
