Condah is in the Glenelg area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Condah is Annya Bushland Reserve, covering about 52 hectares. Greenhills Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Condah has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 80 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Condah. By car from Condah, Hamilton is about 29 minutes away and Portland about 39 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Condah is Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest library to Condah is Heywood Library in Heywood, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Condah is Whalers Point in Portland, about 39 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Condah is hamilton skate park in Hamilton, about 31 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton is about 29 minutes' drive from Condah. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 29 minutes from Condah by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Condah, the nearest places for kids by road are Branxholme Rec Reserve Playground in Branxholme (10 min), Heywood Library in Heywood (18 min), the playground at Apex Park in Heywood (18 min), Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood (18 min), Hamilton History Centre in Hamilton (29 min) and Mud Gallery in Hamilton (29 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Condah.

Nearby suburbs

Branxholme, next to Condah, has 1 playground. Hotspur, Grassdale and Breakaway Creek also border Condah.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Condah, without traffic.
