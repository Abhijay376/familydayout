Our data lists 3 playgrounds and 1 park or reserve in Moorooduc, in the Mornington Peninsula area. Moorooduc also has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

Moorooduc Recreation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Moorooduc, at about 4 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Moorooduc. In Moorooduc you'll also find The Studio & Co. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Moorooduc for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Moorooduc. The closest to Moorooduc is Somerville Railway Station (metro), about 6.7 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 886 stops in Moorooduc. By car from Moorooduc, Mornington is about 8 minutes away, Frankston about 10 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 53 minutes, without traffic.

The closest pool or water play area to Moorooduc is Pelican Park Recreation Centre in Hastings, about 13 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Mount Eliza Skatepark in Mount Eliza is about 5 minutes' drive from Moorooduc. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Frankston is about 13 minutes' drive from Moorooduc. The closest indoor play centre to Moorooduc is Locked In Escape Rooms in Mornington, about 5 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale is about 15 minutes' drive from Moorooduc. Mornington is the closest bigger centre to Moorooduc, about 8 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a library, indoor play and a beach.
