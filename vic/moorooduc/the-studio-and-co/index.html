The Studio & Co.

Museum or gallery in Moorooduc · Official details

The Studio & Co. is a museum or gallery in Moorooduc, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire Council area, best for school-age kids. Derril Rd/Mornington-Tyabb Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1032 m away. Opening hours: WEDS TO MON 8AM-4PM; SUN 8AM - 3PM; MON- FRI 6.30AM-1PM; SAT + SUN 7.30AM - 1PM; Saturday 10 am - 4 pm; Sunday 10 am - 3 pm.
