Getting around

Durdidwarrah has no train station; Ballan Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Durdidwarrah, so most trips start by car. By car from Durdidwarrah, Bacchus Marsh is about 49 minutes away, Geelong about 51 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Ballan Swimming Pool in Ballan is about 30 minutes' drive from Durdidwarrah. For a library, Lara Library in Lara is about 43 minutes' drive from Durdidwarrah. The closest beach to Durdidwarrah is Disability beach in Steiglitz, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Durdidwarrah is Meredith Skate Park in Meredith, about 20 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, General Public in Corio is about 44 minutes' drive from Durdidwarrah. For a zoo or wildlife park, Fairy Park in Anakie is about 15 minutes' drive from Durdidwarrah. The nearest larger centre is Bacchus Marsh, about 49 minutes from Durdidwarrah by car, with 6 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Durdidwarrah, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Beremboke Recreation Reserve in Beremboke (11 min), the playground at Mount Wallace Hall in Mount Wallace (11 min), Disability beach in Steiglitz (26 min), Fairy Park in Anakie (15 min), Meredith Skate Park in Meredith (20 min) and the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan (30 min).
