Breakaway Creek is in the Glenelg area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Breakaway Creek is Lake Condah Indigenous Protected Area, covering about 1,715 hectares. For walks and rides, Budj Bim Trails and East West Seismic Track cross Breakaway Creek.

Getting around

Breakaway Creek has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 72 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Breakaway Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Breakaway Creek, Hamilton is about 40 minutes away and Portland about 45 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood is about 24 minutes' drive from Breakaway Creek. The closest library to Breakaway Creek is Heywood Library in Heywood, about 24 minutes' drive. For a skate park, hamilton skate park in Hamilton is about 42 minutes' drive from Breakaway Creek. The closest cinema to Breakaway Creek is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 40 minutes' drive. Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Breakaway Creek, about 40 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Breakaway Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are Heywood Library in Heywood (24 min), the playground at Apex Park in Heywood (24 min), Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood (24 min), Branxholme Rec Reserve Playground in Branxholme (24 min), Fitzroy River Beach in Tyrendarra (47 min) and Bay of Whales Gallery in Narrawong (50 min).

Best for

Free days on the Budj Bim Trails and in Breakaway Creek's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Breakaway Creek, Heywood has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Macarthur, Lake Condah, Knebsworth and Condah also border Breakaway Creek.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Breakaway Creek, without traffic.
