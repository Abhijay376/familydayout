Larissa Park

Park in Ringwood · Basic info

Larissa Park is a park in Ringwood, in the Maroondah City Council area, good for all ages. Larissa Ave/Maroondah Hwy (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 330 m away with a baby change table.
