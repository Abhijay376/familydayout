Kitson Street Reserve

Park in Ringwood · Basic info

Kitson Street Reserve is a park in Ringwood, in the Maroondah City Council area, good for all ages. Aquinas College/Great Ryrie St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 789 m away.
