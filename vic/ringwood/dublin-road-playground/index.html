Dublin Road Playground

Playground in Ringwood · Basic info

Dublin Road Playground is a public playground in Ringwood, in the Maroondah City area. The Mullum Mullum Creek Trail is about 240 m away, and Aurora Art is about 600 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 340 m from the playground, and bus 670 stops at Mines Rd/Maroondah Hwy, a 1-minute walk away.
