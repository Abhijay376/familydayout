Playground on Bedford Road

Playground in Ringwood · Basic info

This is a public playground in Ringwood. The Box Hill to Ringwood Trail is about 80 m away, and Realm is about 280 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 430 m from the playground, and buses 669 and 679 stop at Wilana St/Bedford Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
