Playground at Towerhill Drive Reserve

Playground in Ringwood · Basic info

Playground at Towerhill Drive Reserve in Ringwood is a public playground in the Maroondah City area. The Ringwood North Connection is about 280 m away, and Highland Boulevard Playground is another option about 440 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 580 m from the playground, and bus 364 stops at Towerhill Dr/Wonga Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
