Playground at Sherbrook Park

Playground in Ringwood · Basic info

Playground at Sherbrook Park is a public playground in Ringwood, in the Maroondah City area. The Heatherdale Walking Route is about 20 m away, and for food, Pizzeria Express is about 430 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 670 m from the playground, and bus 742 stops at Charter St/New St, a 6-minute walk away.
