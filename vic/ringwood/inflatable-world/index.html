Inflatable World

Indoor play in Ringwood · Official details

Inflatable World is a indoor play in Ringwood, in the Maroondah City Council area, best for younger kids (check the venue's age and height rules). Reilly St/Wantirna Rd (bus) is a 9-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1275 m away. Opening hours: Sat 10:00-16:00; Sun 10:00-16:00.
