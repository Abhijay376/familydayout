Playground at Ringwood Lake Park

Playground in Ringwood · Basic info

Playground at Ringwood Lake Park is a public playground in Ringwood, in the Maroondah City area. The Ringwood - Belgrave Rail Trail is about 80 m away, and Timezone is about 560 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus 670 stops at Larissa Ave/Maroondah Hwy, a 2-minute walk away.
