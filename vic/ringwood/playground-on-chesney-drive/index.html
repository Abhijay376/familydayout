Playground on Chesney Drive

Playground in Ringwood · Basic info

Playground on Chesney Drive is a public playground in Ringwood, in the Maroondah City area. The Mullum Mullum Creek Trail passes about 60 m away, and Chiraag Indian & Nepalese Restaurant is about 420 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 880 m away, and bus 668 stops at Chesney Dr/Kalinda Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
