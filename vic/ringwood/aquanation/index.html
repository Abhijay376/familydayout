Aquanation

Pool or water play in Ringwood · Official details

Aquanation is a pool or water play in Ringwood, in the Maroondah City Council area, good for all ages. Reilly St/Wantirna Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 391 m away. Opening hours: Friday morning between 10.15am and 11.15am (excluding school and public holidays); Thursday morning between 10:30am and 11.30am - excluding school and public holidays.
