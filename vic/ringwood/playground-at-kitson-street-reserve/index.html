Playground at Kitson Street Reserve

Playground in Ringwood · Basic info

Playground at Kitson Street Reserve is a public playground in Ringwood, in the Maroondah City area. The Ringwood - Belgrave Rail Trail passes about 180 m away, and Playground on Great Ryrie Street is another option about 120 m away. There's a public toilet about 780 m away, and bus 669 stops at Aquinas College/Great Ryrie St, a 3-minute walk away.
