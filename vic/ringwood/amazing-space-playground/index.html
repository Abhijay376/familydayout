Amazing Space Playground

Playground in Ringwood · Basic info

This is a public playground in Ringwood. The Box Hill to Ringwood Trail passes about 350 m away, and Realm is about 500 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 120 m away, and bus 901 stops at Arlington St/Wantirna Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
