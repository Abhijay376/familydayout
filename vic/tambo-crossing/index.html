Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Tambo Crossing in the East Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Tambo Crossing has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 41 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tambo Crossing, so most trips start by car. By car from Tambo Crossing, Bairnsdale is about 52 minutes away and Lakes Entrance about 63 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek is about 33 minutes' drive from Tambo Crossing. Bairnsdale is the closest bigger centre to Tambo Crossing, about 52 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Tambo Crossing are Bruthen Gallery in Bruthen (28 min), Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek (33 min) and Great Alpine Gallery in Swifts Creek (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tambo Crossing.
