Our data lists 9 parks and reserves in Beeac, in the Colac Otway area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Beeac is Lough Calvert Lake Reserve, covering about 1,978 hectares. Lake Beeac Wildlife Reserve (no hunting) and Lake Cundare Wildlife Reserve (no hunting) are the next largest. Beeac also has Beeac Park, Windmill Park and Cemetery Lake Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Beeac.

Getting around

Beeac has no train station; Colac Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 17 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Beeac, so most trips start by car. By car from Beeac, Colac is about 17 minutes away, Geelong about 66 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood is about 39 minutes' drive from Beeac. For a library, Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac is about 18 minutes' drive from Beeac. For a skate park, the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt is about 22 minutes' drive from Beeac. The closest cinema to Beeac is Colac Cinemas in Colac, about 18 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac is about 17 minutes' drive from Beeac. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 17 minutes from Beeac by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Beeac, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Selwyn Brown Park in Colac (15 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (17 min), the playground at Memorial Square in Colac (17 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (18 min), Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (18 min) and the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (22 min).
