Getting around

There's no train station in Coongulla. The closest to Coongulla is Rosedale Railway Station (V/Line), about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Coongulla, so most trips start by car. By car from Coongulla, Sale is about 43 minutes away, Traralgon about 49 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Coongulla is Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield, about 13 minutes' drive. For a library, Sale Library in Sale is about 42 minutes' drive from Coongulla. The closest skate park to Coongulla is Pump Track in Heyfield, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Coongulla is Former Drive-in site in Sale, about 43 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Sale, about 43 minutes from Coongulla by car, with 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Coongulla are Graves Court - Perry Court - Wickham Court Playground in Heyfield (14 min), Pump Track in Heyfield (13 min), Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield (13 min), Heyfield and District Museum in Heyfield (14 min), Rosedale Swimming Pool in Rosedale (30 min) and Skate Park in Stratford (40 min).
