Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Upper Lurg in the Benalla area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Upper Lurg has no train station; Benalla Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Upper Lurg, so most trips start by car. By car from Upper Lurg, Benalla is about 32 minutes away and Wangaratta about 43 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Upper Lurg is Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest library to Upper Lurg is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 44 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Upper Lurg is Pump Track in Benalla, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Upper Lurg is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 44 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Benalla, about 32 minutes from Upper Lurg by car, with 5 playgrounds and a pool. From Upper Lurg, the nearest places for kids by road are Cobb & Co in Glenrowan (30 min), Glenrowan Museum in Glenrowan (30 min), Myrrhee Recreation Reserve in Myrrhee (35 min), Pump Track in Benalla (32 min), Benalla Splash Park in Benalla (32 min) and Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (32 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Upper Lurg.

Nearby suburbs

Greta South and Glenrowan West also border Upper Lurg.
