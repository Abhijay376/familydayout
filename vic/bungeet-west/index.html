Bungeet West is in the Benalla area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Bungeet H125 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Bungeet West, at about 6 hectares. Bungeet H89 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Bungeet West has no train station; Benalla Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bungeet West, so most trips start by car. By car from Bungeet West, Benalla is about 25 minutes away, Wangaratta about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bungeet West is Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest library to Bungeet West is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 42 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Pump Track in Benalla is about 26 minutes' drive from Bungeet West. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 42 minutes' drive from Bungeet West. Benalla is the closest bigger centre to Bungeet West, about 25 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds and a pool. From Bungeet West, the nearest places for kids by road are Benalla Aviation Museum in Benalla (25 min), Benalla Migrant Camp in Benalla (26 min), Pump Track in Benalla (26 min), Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (25 min), Benalla Splash Park in Benalla (25 min) and the playground at Benalla Botanical Gardens in Benalla (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bungeet West.

Nearby suburbs

Chesney Vale and Bungeet also border Bungeet West.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Bungeet West, without traffic.
