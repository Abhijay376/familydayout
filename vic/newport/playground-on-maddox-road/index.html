Playground on Maddox Road

Playground in Newport · Basic info

Playground on Maddox Road is a public playground in Newport, in the Hobsons Bay City area. The Bike: New Port Maddock Rd shimmy passes about 310 m away, and Playground at Loft Reserve is another option about 270 m away. There's a public toilet about 790 m away, and bus 432 stops at Woods St/Maddox Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
