Coronation Reserve

Park in Newport · Basic info

Coronation Reserve is a park in Newport, in the Hobsons Bay City Council area, good for all ages. Newport Park/Douglas Pde (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 519 m away.
