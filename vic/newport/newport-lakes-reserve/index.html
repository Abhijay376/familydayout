Newport Lakes Reserve

Park in Newport · Basic info

Newport Lakes Reserve is a park in Newport, in the Hobsons Bay City Council area, good for all ages. Watt St/Blackshaws Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 444 m away.
