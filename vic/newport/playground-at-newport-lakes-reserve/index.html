Playground at Newport Lakes Reserve

Playground in Newport · Official details

Playground at Newport Lakes Reserve in Newport is a public playground in the Hobsons Bay City area. Star Pizza and Pasta Cafe is about 440 m away if you need food, and Playground at Madigan, Jack Reserve is another option about 290 m away. There's a public toilet about 50 m away, and buses 471 and 947 stop at Laurie St/Mason St, a 3-minute walk away.
