Playground at Bryan Martin Oval

Playground in Newport · Basic info

Playground at Bryan Martin Oval is a public playground in Newport, in the Hobsons Bay City area. The Bike: New Port Maddock Rd shimmy is about 190 m away, and Newport Library is about 200 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 280 m from the playground, and buses 432, 471 and 947 stop at Mirls St/Mason St, a 4-minute walk away.
