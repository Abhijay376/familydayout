Armstrong Reserve

Park in Newport · Basic info

Armstrong Reserve is a park in Newport, in the Hobsons Bay City Council area, good for all ages. Paine St/Melbourne Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 385 m away.
