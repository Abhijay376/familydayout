Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside St James in the Moira area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in St James.

Getting around

There's no train station in St James. The closest to St James is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 31 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in St James. By car from St James, Benalla is about 38 minutes away and Wangaratta about 43 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to St James is Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla, about 38 minutes' drive. For a library, Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga is about 33 minutes' drive from St James. For a beach, Duffys Beach No 1 in Burramine is about 40 minutes' drive from St James. For a skate park, Pump Track in Benalla is about 39 minutes' drive from St James. The closest cinema to St James is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 43 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Tunzafun in Yarrawonga is about 35 minutes' drive from St James. The nearest larger centre is Benalla, about 38 minutes from St James by car, with 5 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from St James are the playground at Tungamah Lions Park in Tungamah (19 min), the playground at CWA Gardens in Dookie (29 min), Katamatite Historical Society Museum in Katamatite (29 min), Duffys Beach No 1 in Burramine (40 min), Pebble Beach in Burramine (42 min) and Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from St James.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to St James, Tungamah has 1 playground. Lake Rowan and Devenish also border St James.
