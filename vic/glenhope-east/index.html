Glenhope East is in the Mitchell area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Glenhope East, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Glenhope East has no train station; Kyneton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 31 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glenhope East, so most trips start by car. By car from Glenhope East, Seymour is about 44 minutes away, Kyneton about 56 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore is about 41 minutes' drive from Glenhope East. The closest library to Glenhope East is Heathcote Library in Heathcote, about 27 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote is about 27 minutes' drive from Glenhope East. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 44 minutes from Glenhope East by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Glenhope East are Tooborac Recreation Reserve Playground in Tooborac (16 min), the playground at Pyalong Recreation Reserve in Pyalong (23 min), Mudwood Studio in Heathcote (27 min), Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote (27 min), Heathcote Library in Heathcote (27 min) and Powder Magazine in Heathcote (29 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Glenhope East.

Nearby suburbs

Tooborac, next to Glenhope East, has 1 playground. Baynton also borders Glenhope East.
