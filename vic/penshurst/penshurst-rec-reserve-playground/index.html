Penshurst Rec Reserve Playground

Playground in Penshurst · Basic info

Penshurst Rec Reserve Playground is a public playground in Penshurst, in the Southern Grampians Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet right by the playground. It's easiest by car, about 2 minutes from Penshurst.
