Penshurst Recreation Reserve

Park in Penshurst · Official details

Penshurst Recreation Reserve is a park in Penshurst, in the Southern Grampians Shire Council area, good for all ages. Post Office/Hamilton Hwy (coach) is a 15-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 100 m away and dogs are allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Penshurst Recreation Reserve?
Penshurst Recreation Reserve is in Penshurst, Victoria.
Is Penshurst Recreation Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Penshurst Recreation Reserve?
Yes, dogs are allowed.
Are there toilets at Penshurst Recreation Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 100 m away.
How do I get to Penshurst Recreation Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Post Office/Hamilton Hwy (routes Portland - Casterton - Hamilton - Warrnambool - Melbourne), a 15-minute walk.
