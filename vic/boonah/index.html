Boonah in the Surf Coast area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Painkalac Creek Reference Area is the largest mapped park in Boonah, at about 297 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Boonah. The closest to Boonah is Winchelsea Railway Station (V/Line), about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Boonah, so most trips start by car. By car from Boonah, Colac is about 50 minutes away, Torquay about 63 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Lorne Sea Baths in Lorne is about 31 minutes' drive from Boonah. For a beach, Lorne Beach in Lorne is about 31 minutes' drive from Boonah. The closest skate park to Boonah is Kay Langan Skate Park in Lorne, about 31 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Lorne Theatre in Lorne is about 31 minutes' drive from Boonah. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Boonah is Live Wire Park in Lorne, about 33 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 50 minutes from Boonah by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Boonah, the nearest places for kids by road are Sunnymead Beach in Aireys Inlet (49 min), the playground at Aireys Inlet Reserve in Aireys Inlet (47 min), Aireys Inlet Skate Park in Aireys Inlet (47 min), Lorne Theatre in Lorne (31 min), Lorne Sea Baths in Lorne (31 min) and Kay Langan Skate Park in Lorne (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Boonah.

Nearby suburbs

Eastern View, next to Boonah, has a beach. Big Hill, next to Boonah, has a beach. Pennyroyal, next to Boonah, has 1 playground. Deans Marsh, Benwerrin and Bambra also border Boonah.
