Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Toora North, in the South Gippsland area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Toora North is Mount Fatigue Scenic Reserve, covering about 9 hectares. Toora H37 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Toora North. The closest to Toora North is Morwell Railway Station (V/Line), about 39 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Toora North, so most trips start by car. By car from Toora North, Leongatha is about 55 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 76 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Toora Pool in Toora is about 16 minutes' drive from Toora North. The closest library to Toora North is Foster Library in Foster, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Toora North is Toora Beach in Toora, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Toora North is the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora, about 17 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Leongatha, about 55 minutes from Toora North by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Toora North, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Stanley Street Playground in Toora (17 min), Toora Pool in Toora (16 min), the playground at Sargasser Park in Toora (17 min), the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora (17 min), Toora Beach in Toora (24 min) and Port Welshpool and District Maritime Museum in Port Welshpool (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Toora North.

Nearby suburbs

Toora, next to Toora North, has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. Hazel Park, Gunyah and Grand Ridge also border Toora North.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Toora North, without traffic.
