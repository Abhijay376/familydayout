Cato Park

Park in Stawell · Official details

Cato Park is a park in Stawell, in the Northern Grampians Shire Council area, good for all ages. Stawell Bowling Club/Napier St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 29 m away.

Details

Good to know
Public BBQ about 60 m away (Council)

Questions parents ask

Where is Cato Park?
Cato Park is in Stawell, Victoria.
Is Cato Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Cato Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 29 m away.
How do I get to Cato Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Stawell Bowling Club/Napier St (routes 1), a 3-minute walk.
