Playground at Federation Park

Playground in Stawell · Official details

Playground at Federation Park in Stawell is a public playground in the Northern Grampians Shire area. For food, Pie Face is about 410 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 1 stops at Western Hwy/Playford St, a 2-minute walk away.
