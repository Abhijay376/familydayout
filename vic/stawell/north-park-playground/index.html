North Park Playground

Playground in Stawell · Basic info

North Park Playground is a public playground in Stawell, in the Northern Grampians Shire area. Stawell Aquatic Centre is about 330 m away, and Stawell Skate Park is another option about 270 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus 2 stops at Lamont St/Ligar St, a 5-minute walk away.
