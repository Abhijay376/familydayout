Stawell Skate Park

Playground in Stawell · Basic info

This is a public playground in Stawell. Stawell Aquatic Centre is about 60 m away, and North Park Playground is another option about 270 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 350 m from the playground, and buses 2 and St Arnaud - Stawell via Marnoo stop at Newington Rd/Houston St, a 2-minute walk away.
