North Magdala Company Mine Historic Area

Park in Stawell · Basic info

North Magdala Company Mine Historic Area is a park in Stawell, in the Northern Grampians Shire Council area, good for all ages. Sumner St/Woods St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1020 m away.
