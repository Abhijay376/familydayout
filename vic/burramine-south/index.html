Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Burramine South in the Moira area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Burramine South has no train station; Benalla Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 55 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Burramine South, so most trips start by car. By car from Burramine South, Shepparton is about 53 minutes away and Wangaratta about 56 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Burramine South is Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah, about 39 minutes' drive. For a library, Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga is about 15 minutes' drive from Burramine South. The closest beach to Burramine South is Duffys Beach No 1 in Burramine, about 12 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Federation Park in Cobram is about 25 minutes' drive from Burramine South. For a cinema, Cobram Cinema in Cobram is about 25 minutes' drive from Burramine South. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Burramine South is Tunzafun in Yarrawonga, about 17 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 53 minutes from Burramine South by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Burramine South, the nearest places for kids by road are Duffys Beach No 1 in Burramine (12 min), Nevins East Beach in Burramine (15 min), the playground at Tungamah Lions Park in Tungamah (12 min), the playground at E.W Kilgour Reserve in Katamatite (14 min), Katamatite Historical Society Museum in Katamatite (14 min) and Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga (15 min).
