Anglers Rest in the East Gippsland area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Mount Battery State Forest is the largest mapped park in Anglers Rest, at about 10,896 hectares. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Anglers Rest.

Getting around

Anglers Rest has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 94 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Anglers Rest, so most trips start by car. By car from Anglers Rest, Bright is about 1.5 hours away and Wodonga about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest skate park to Anglers Rest is the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo, about 24 minutes' drive. From Anglers Rest, the nearest places for kids by road are Petersens Art Gallery & Museum in Omeo (23 min), the playground at Omeo Historical Park & Museum in Omeo (23 min), Old Omeo Courthouse in Omeo (24 min) and the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo (24 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Anglers Rest.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Anglers Rest, Omeo has 2 playgrounds and a skate park. Bingo Munjie also borders Anglers Rest.
