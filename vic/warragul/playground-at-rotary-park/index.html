Playground at Rotary Park

Playground in Warragul · Basic info

This is a public playground in Warragul. The Linear Park Arts Discovery Trail is about 30 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 330 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 150 m from the playground, and bus 81 stops at Rotary Park/Latrobe St, a 2-minute walk away.
