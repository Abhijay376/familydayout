Burke Street Park

Park in Warragul · Basic info

Burke Street Park is a park in Warragul, in the Baw Baw Shire Council area, good for all ages. Warragul Gardens Holiday Park/Burke St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 591 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: opening_hours: 24/7.
