Playground at Scenic Park

Playground in Warragul · Basic info

Playground at Scenic Park in Warragul is a public playground in the Baw Baw Shire area. The Linear Park Arts Discovery Trail passes about 20 m away, and Playground at Boronia Park is another option about 510 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.0 km away, and bus 81 stops at Affleck St/Sutton St, a 4-minute walk away.
