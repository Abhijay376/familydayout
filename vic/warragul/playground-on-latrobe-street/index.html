Playground on Latrobe Street

Playground in Warragul · Basic info

This is a public playground in Warragul. The Warragul - Drouin Two Towns Trail is about 180 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 290 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 470 m from the playground, and bus 81 stops at Logan St/Latrobe St, a 1-minute walk away.
