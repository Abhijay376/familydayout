Our data lists 22 playgrounds and 31 parks and reserves in Warragul, in the Baw Baw area. Warragul also has a library, 2 pools, a museum or gallery and a cinema.

Landmarks

Brooker Park is the largest mapped park in Warragul, at about 14 hectares. Burke Street Park and Hamilton Park are the next largest. Warragul also has Kiah Park, Civic Park and Kestle Park. Playgrounds in Warragul include Charles Street Park, the playground at Kiah Park and the playground at Civic Park. Our data shows 15 public toilets in Warragul. In Warragul you'll also find Warragul & District Historical Society Museum, Warragul Cinema, The Alley Warragul, Warragul Leisure Centre and Warragul Swimming Pool. For older kids in Warragul there's Bmx and Burke Street Pump Track. For walks and rides, Warragul - Drouin Two Towns Trail, Two Towns Trail and Linear Park Arts Discovery Trail cross Warragul. Our data lists 34 cafes and family restaurants in Warragul for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Warragul has a train station: Warragul Railway Station (V/Line: Bairnsdale - Melbourne via Sale & Traralgon and Traralgon - Melbourne via Morwell, Moe & Pakenham). Buses on the 80, 81, 82 and 83 routes stop in Warragul. V/Line coaches stop in Warragul. By car from Warragul, Pakenham is about 40 minutes away, Traralgon about 48 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 83 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a zoo or wildlife park, Wildlife Trail in Tynong North is about 26 minutes' drive from Warragul.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 22 playgrounds across Warragul. Rainy days at the library, cinema, indoor play centre and museum or gallery in Warragul. Teens who skate or ride, with Bmx in Warragul. Free days on the Warragul - Drouin Two Towns Trail and in Warragul's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Drouin, next to Warragul, has 19 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Nilma, Lillico, Drouin East and Buln Buln also border Warragul.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Warragul, without traffic.
