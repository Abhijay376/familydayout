Playground at Civic Park

Playground in Warragul · Basic info

Playground at Civic Park in Warragul is a public playground in the Baw Baw Shire area. Warragul Library is about 440 m away, and for food, Jade Chinese Cuisine is about 250 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 190 m from the playground, and bus 89 stops at Education Centre Gippsland/Smith St, a 3-minute walk away.
