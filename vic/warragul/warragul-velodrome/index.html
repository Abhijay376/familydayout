Warragul Velodrome

Skate, BMX or pump track in Warragul · Basic info

Warragul Velodrome is a skate, bmx or pump track in Warragul, in the Baw Baw Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Peace Ave/Queen St (bus) is a 10-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 244 m away.
