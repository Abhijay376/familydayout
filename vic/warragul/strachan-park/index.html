Strachan Park

Park in Warragul · Basic info

Strachan Park is a park in Warragul, in the Baw Baw Shire Council area, good for all ages. Highvale Rise/Princes Way (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 997 m away.
