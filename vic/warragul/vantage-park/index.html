Vantage Park

Park in Warragul · Basic info

Vantage Park is a park in Warragul, in the Baw Baw Shire Council area, good for all ages. Hazel Creek/King St (bus) is a 13-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1905 m away with a baby change table.
