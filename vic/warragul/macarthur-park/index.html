MacArthur Park

Park in Warragul · Basic info

MacArthur Park is a park in Warragul, in the Baw Baw Shire Council area, good for all ages. MacArthur St/Sutton St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 842 m away with a baby change table.
