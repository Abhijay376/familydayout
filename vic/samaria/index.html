Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Samaria in the Benalla area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Samaria has no train station; Benalla Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Samaria, so most trips start by car. By car from Samaria, Benalla is about 38 minutes away, Mansfield about 48 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Samaria is Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla, about 37 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Pump Track in Benalla is about 38 minutes' drive from Samaria. For a cinema, Swanpool Cinema in Swanpool is about 18 minutes' drive from Samaria. The nearest larger centre is Benalla, about 38 minutes from Samaria by car, with 5 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Samaria are Swanpool Cinema in Swanpool (18 min), the playground at Waminda Park in Benalla (34 min), The Rocket Playground in Benalla (37 min), Benalla Art Gallery in Benalla (36 min), Benalla Costume and Pioneer Museum in Benalla (37 min) and Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (37 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Samaria.
