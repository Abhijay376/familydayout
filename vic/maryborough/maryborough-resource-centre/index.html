Maryborough Resource Centre

Library in Maryborough · Basic info

Maryborough Resource Centre is a library in Maryborough, in the Central Goldfields Shire Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Maryborough Bus Interchange/Napier St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 14 m away.
