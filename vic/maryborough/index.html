Maryborough in the Central Goldfields area has 8 parks and reserves in our data. Beyond the parks, Maryborough has a library.

Landmarks

Maryborough Regional Park is the largest mapped park in Maryborough, at about 431 hectares. Bristol Hill Historic and Cultural Features Reserve and Alex Chisholm Nature Conservation Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Maryborough include Martin Reserve, Phillips Gardens and Soldiers Memorial Plot. Our data shows 14 public toilets in Maryborough. In Maryborough you'll also find Maryborough Resource Centre. The Castlemaine-Maryborough Rail Trail (Under Construction), Dunolly - Havelock Gold Rush Cycle Trail and Talbot Heritage Trail all pass through Maryborough. Our data lists 7 cafes and family restaurants in Maryborough for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line: Maryborough - Melbourne via Ballarat) in Maryborough. Buses on the 1, 2, 3 and 4 routes stop in Maryborough. V/Line coaches stop in Maryborough. By car from Maryborough, Castlemaine is about 44 minutes away, Daylesford about 60 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot is about 17 minutes' drive from Maryborough. For a skate park, BMX Track in Castlemaine is about 44 minutes' drive from Maryborough. The closest places for kids by road from Maryborough are the playground at Market Reserve in Carisbrook (7 min), Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground in Talbot (17 min), Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (17 min), Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (25 min), Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (25 min) and Courthouse in Avoca (23 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the library in Maryborough. Free days on the Castlemaine-Maryborough Rail Trail (Under Construction) and in Maryborough's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Carisbrook, next to Maryborough, has 1 playground. Havelock, Flagstaff, Daisy Hill and Craigie also border Maryborough.
