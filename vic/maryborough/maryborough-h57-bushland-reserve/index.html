Maryborough H57 Bushland Reserve

Park in Maryborough · Basic info

Maryborough H57 Bushland Reserve is a park in Maryborough, in the Central Goldfields Shire Council area, good for all ages. Spring St/Gillies St (bus) is a 11-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1899 m away.
