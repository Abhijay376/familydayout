Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Granya, in the Towong area. Granya also has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Granya is Mount Granya State Park, covering about 6,164 hectares. Granya Public Park Reserve and Granya Recreation Reserve are the next largest. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Granya. In Granya you'll also find Granya Museum. For walks and rides, Tallangatta - Firebrace Loop and Mount Granya Walking Track cross Granya.

Getting around

Granya has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 36 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Albury - Corryong via Walwa route stop in Granya. By car from Granya, Wodonga is about 49 minutes away and Wangaratta about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta is about 27 minutes' drive from Granya. The closest library to Granya is Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta, about 26 minutes' drive. Wodonga is the closest bigger centre to Granya, about 49 minutes away by car, and has 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Granya are Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta (26 min), Tallangatta Art & Photography in Tallangatta (26 min), Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta (27 min), the playground at Roy Williams Park in Bellbridge (27 min) and the playground at Banksia Park in Tangambalanga (39 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Granya. Free days on the Tallangatta - Firebrace Loop and in Granya's parks.
