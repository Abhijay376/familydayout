Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tamleugh North is Tamleugh Natural Features Reserve, covering about 2 hectares.

Getting around

Tamleugh North has no train station; Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tamleugh North, so most trips start by car. By car from Tamleugh North, Shepparton is about 33 minutes away, Benalla about 54 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.
