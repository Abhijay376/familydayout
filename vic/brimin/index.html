Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Brimin, in the Indigo area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Brimin is Murray Valley Regional Park Collendina, covering about 505 hectares. Brimin Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Brimin has no train station; Springhurst Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Brimin, so most trips start by car. By car from Brimin, Wangaratta is about 40 minutes away and Wodonga about 57 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rutherglen War Memorial Swimming Pool in Rutherglen is about 22 minutes' drive from Brimin. The closest library to Brimin is Rutherglen Public Library in Rutherglen, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Brimin is Forges Beach No 2 in Yarrawonga, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Brimin is the pump track on Victoria Street in Wahgunyah, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Brimin is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 40 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Tunzafun in Yarrawonga is about 30 minutes' drive from Brimin. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Brimin, about 40 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Brimin are the playground at Peechelba Reserve in Peechelba (22 min), Apex Park Playground in Rutherglen (22 min), Rutherglen War Memorial Swimming Pool in Rutherglen (22 min), Rutherglen Public Library in Rutherglen (22 min), the pump track on Victoria Street in Wahgunyah (23 min) and Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga (29 min).
