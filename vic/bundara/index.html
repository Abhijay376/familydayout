Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Bundara, in the East Gippsland area.

Landmarks

Bundara - Cobungra Remote and Natural Area is the largest mapped park in Bundara, at about 13,618 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Bundara. For walks and rides, Mt Cope Walking Track crosses Bundara.

Getting around

Bundara has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 96 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bundara, so most trips start by car. By car from Bundara, Bright is about 1.5 hours away and Wodonga about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Bundara, the nearest places for kids by road are Falls Creek Museum in Falls Creek (29 min).

Best for

Free days on the Mt Cope Walking Track and in Bundara's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Hotham Heights, next to Bundara, has 1 playground. Next door to Bundara, Falls Creek has 1 playground. Dinner Plain, next to Bundara, has 1 playground. Glen Valley, Cobungra and Anglers Rest also border Bundara.
