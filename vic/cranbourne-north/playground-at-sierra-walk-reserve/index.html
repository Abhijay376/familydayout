Playground at Sierra Walk Reserve

Playground in Cranbourne North · Basic info

Playground at Sierra Walk Reserve in Cranbourne North is a public playground in the Casey City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet about 770 m away, and buses 799, 841 and 981 stop at Sierra Bvd/Narre Warren-Cranbourne Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
