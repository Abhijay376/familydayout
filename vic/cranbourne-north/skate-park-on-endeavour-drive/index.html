Skate park on Endeavour Drive

Skate, BMX or pump track in Cranbourne North · Basic info

Skate park on Endeavour Drive is a skate, bmx or pump track in Cranbourne North, in the Casey City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Wentworth St/Endeavour Dr (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1146 m away.
