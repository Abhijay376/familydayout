Our data lists 12 playgrounds and 9 parks and reserves in Cranbourne North, in the Casey area. Beyond the parks, Cranbourne North has a skate park.

Landmarks

Hilltop Nature Reserve is the largest mapped park in Cranbourne North, at about 10 hectares. Endeavour Drive Reserve and Tulliallan are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Cranbourne North include Sierra Walk Reserve, Conway Street Reserve and Jessie Street Reserve. Playgrounds around Cranbourne North include Evesham Street Reserve Playground, the playground at Sierra Walk Reserve and the playground at Conway Street Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Cranbourne North. For older kids in Cranbourne North there's the pump track at Courtenay Avenue Reserve. The Bike: Thompsons Road passes through Cranbourne North. Our data lists 4 cafes and family restaurants in Cranbourne North for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cranbourne North. The closest to Cranbourne North is Cranbourne Railway Station (metro), about 2.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 799, 841, 847, 881, 888, 893, 899, 981 and 982 stop in Cranbourne North. By car from Cranbourne North, Cranbourne is about 5 minutes away, Dandenong about 16 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 40 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Paul Sadler Swimland in Narre Warren South is about 5 minutes' drive from Cranbourne North. For a library, Cranbourne Library in Cranbourne East is about 5 minutes' drive from Cranbourne North. For a beach, Carrum Beach in Carrum is about 18 minutes' drive from Cranbourne North. The closest cinema to Cranbourne North is Village Cinemas in Narre Warren, about 8 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 12 playgrounds across Cranbourne North. Teens who skate or ride, with the pump track at Courtenay Avenue Reserve in Cranbourne North. Free days on the Bike: Thompsons Road and in Cranbourne North's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Cranbourne North, Berwick has 58 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Cranbourne North, Clyde North has 34 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Cranbourne, next to Cranbourne North, has 27 playgrounds and a skate park. Cranbourne West, next to Cranbourne North, has 21 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Cranbourne East, Hampton Park, Narre Warren South and Lyndhurst also border Cranbourne North.
