Avonsleigh is in the Cardinia area, and our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Other parks and reserves in Avonsleigh include Dunstan Road Reserve. Playgrounds in Avonsleigh include the playground at Dunstan Road Reserve. The Eastern Dandenong Ranges Trail passes through Avonsleigh.

Getting around

There's no train station in Avonsleigh. The closest to Avonsleigh is Belgrave Railway Station (metro), about 10 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 695 and 695F stop in Avonsleigh. By car from Avonsleigh, Pakenham is about 25 minutes away, Dandenong about 32 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 53 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Avonsleigh is Monbulk Aquatic Centre in Monbulk, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest library to Avonsleigh is Emerald Library in Emerald, about 4 minutes' drive. For a beach, Edithvale Beach in Edithvale is about 45 minutes' drive from Avonsleigh. For a skate park, Worrell Reserve Skate Park in Emerald is about 4 minutes' drive from Avonsleigh. The closest cinema to Avonsleigh is Cameo Belgrave in Belgrave, about 18 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Little Rascals Play Centre in Ferntree Gully is about 25 minutes' drive from Avonsleigh. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Avonsleigh is MonkeyG's in Pakenham, about 27 minutes' drive. Pakenham is the closest bigger centre to Avonsleigh, about 25 minutes away by car, and has 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Avonsleigh are Emerald Library in Emerald (4 min), Worrell Reserve Skate Park in Emerald (4 min), Emerald Museum in Emerald (5 min), the playground at Puffing Billy Park in Emerald (5 min), BMX track in Emerald (5 min) and Tracey Roberts in The Patch (14 min).

Best for

Free days on the Eastern Dandenong Ranges Trail and in Avonsleigh's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Avonsleigh, Emerald has 6 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Avonsleigh, Cockatoo has 3 playgrounds and a skate park.
