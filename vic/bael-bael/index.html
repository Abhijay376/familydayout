Bael Bael is in the Gannawarra area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bael Bael is Koorangie Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 3,246 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Bael Bael.

Getting around

Bael Bael has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bael Bael, so most trips start by car. By car from Bael Bael, Swan Hill is about 50 minutes away and Echuca about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bael Bael is Swimming Pool in Kerang, about 29 minutes' drive. For a library, Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang is about 29 minutes' drive from Bael Bael. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 50 minutes from Bael Bael by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Bael Bael, the nearest places for kids by road are Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (29 min), Swimming Pool in Kerang (29 min), the playground at Gray Park in Lake Boga (37 min), Lake Boga Flying Boat Museum in Lake Boga (38 min), Quambatook Memorial Pool in Quambatook (38 min) and Quambatook Historical Centre in Quambatook (38 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bael Bael.
