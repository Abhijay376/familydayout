Main Ridge is in the Mornington Peninsula area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Main Ridge is Main Ridge Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 64 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Main Ridge. For walks and rides, Two Bays Walking Track, The Mornington Peninsula Walk and Short Circuit cross Main Ridge.

Getting around

There's no train station in Main Ridge. The closest to Main Ridge is Bittern Railway Station (metro), about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Main Ridge, so most trips start by car. By car from Main Ridge, Mornington is about 27 minutes away, Frankston about 34 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 76 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Yawa Aquatic Centre in Rosebud is about 13 minutes' drive from Main Ridge. The closest library to Main Ridge is Rosebud Library in Rosebud, about 15 minutes' drive. For a beach, Rosebud Beach in Rosebud is about 15 minutes' drive from Main Ridge. The closest skate park to Main Ridge is Rosebud Skate Park in Rosebud, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Main Ridge is Rosebud Cinemas in Rosebud, about 14 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Top Fun Shack in Rosebud is about 15 minutes' drive from Main Ridge. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Main Ridge is Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale, about 39 minutes' drive. Mornington is the closest bigger centre to Main Ridge, about 27 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a library, indoor play and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Main Ridge are Brainteaser Puzzles in Arthurs Seat (10 min), Tube Slides in Arthurs Seat (10 min), Rosebud Cinemas in Rosebud (14 min), Shipwreck'd Studio Gallery in Rosebud (14 min), Rosebud Library in Rosebud (15 min) and Rosebud Beach in Rosebud (15 min).

Best for

Free days on the Two Bays Walking Track and in Main Ridge's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Rosebud, next to Main Ridge, has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Main Ridge, Flinders has 2 playgrounds and a beach. Next door to Main Ridge, Arthurs Seat has 3 playgrounds. Shoreham, next to Main Ridge, has 2 playgrounds. Red Hill South, Red Hill and Boneo also border Main Ridge.
