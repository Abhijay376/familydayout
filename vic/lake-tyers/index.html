Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Lake Tyers in the East Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Lake Tyers has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 43 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lake Tyers, so most trips start by car. By car from Lake Tyers, Lakes Entrance is about 61 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 88 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Lake Tyers, the nearest places for kids by road are Ewings Marsh in Waygara (59 min), Lakes Entrance Aquatic Centre in Lakes Entrance (59 min), the playground at Forest Park in Orbost (57 min), the playground at Lakes Entrance - Lake Tyers Coastal Reserve (COM) in Lake Tyers Beach (66 min), Lake Tyers Beach Sand Bar in Lake Tyers Beach (67 min) and Orbost Library in Orbost (57 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lake Tyers.
