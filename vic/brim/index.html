Brim in the Yarriambiack area has 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Brim Lakeside Park is the largest mapped park in Brim, at about 15 hectares. Playgrounds in Brim include the playground at Brim Rec Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Brim.

Getting around

Brim has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 142 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Mildura - Horsham via Hopetoun route stop in Brim. V/Line coaches stop in Brim. By car from Brim, Horsham is about 61 minutes away and Stawell about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Hopetoun Swimming Pool in Hopetoun is about 34 minutes' drive from Brim. The closest places for kids by road from Brim are the playground at Beulah Lake Park in Beulah (13 min), Historical Centre in Warracknabeal (17 min), Wheatlands Warracknabeal Agricultural Machinery Museum in Warracknabeal (18 min), Hopetoun Swimming Pool in Hopetoun (34 min) and Minyip Swimming Pool in Minyip (40 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Brim.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Brim, Beulah has 2 playgrounds. Lah, Crymelon and Bangerang also border Brim.
