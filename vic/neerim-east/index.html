Neerim East in the Baw Baw area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Neerim East is Sweetwater Creek Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 1,191 hectares. Neerim Flora Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Neerim East. The closest to Neerim East is Warragul Railway Station (V/Line), about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Neerim East, so most trips start by car. By car from Neerim East, Warragul is about 38 minutes away, Pakenham about 64 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul is about 40 minutes' drive from Neerim East. For a library, Warragul Library in Warragul is about 38 minutes' drive from Neerim East. For a skate park, Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South is about 19 minutes' drive from Neerim East. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 38 minutes' drive from Neerim East. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 39 minutes' drive from Neerim East. The nearest larger centre is Warragul, about 38 minutes from Neerim East by car, with 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Neerim East, the nearest places for kids by road are Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South (19 min), the playground at Ellen Close Linear Reserve in Warragul (37 min), Charles Street Park in Warragul (37 min), Warragul Library in Warragul (38 min), Warragul Cinema in Warragul (38 min) and The Alley Warragul in Warragul (39 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Neerim East.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Neerim East, Willow Grove has 2 playgrounds. Neerim South, next to Neerim East, has a skate park. Neerim, Hill End, Fumina South and Buln Buln East also border Neerim East.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Neerim East, without traffic.
