Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Wandella, in the Gannawarra area.

Landmarks

Wandella Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Wandella, at about 980 hectares. Pelican Lake Wildlife Reserve (no hunting) is the next largest.

Getting around

Wandella has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 6.8 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wandella, so most trips start by car. By car from Wandella, Swan Hill is about 52 minutes away and Echuca about 85 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Wandella is Swimming Pool in Kerang, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest library to Wandella is Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang, about 7 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Barham Skate Park in Koondrook is about 31 minutes' drive from Wandella. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 52 minutes from Wandella by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Wandella are Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (7 min), Swimming Pool in Kerang (8 min), the playground at Lake Meran W.R in Lake Meran (20 min), the playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV) in Koondrook (29 min), Barham Branch Library in Koondrook (30 min) and Barham Skate Park in Koondrook (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wandella.

Nearby suburbs

Kerang, next to Wandella, has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Dingwall and Bael Bael also border Wandella.
