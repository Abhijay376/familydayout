Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Timor West in the Central Goldfields area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Timor West has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 10 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Timor West, so most trips start by car. By car from Timor West, Maryborough is about 16 minutes away, Castlemaine about 60 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Timor West is Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest library to Timor West is Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough, about 17 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 16 minutes from Timor West by car, with a library. From Timor West, the nearest places for kids by road are Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (17 min), Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (21 min), Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (21 min), the playground at Market Reserve in Carisbrook (23 min), Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground in Talbot (32 min) and Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (32 min).
