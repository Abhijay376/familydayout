Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Toolome in the Wellington area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Toolome has no train station; Stratford Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 40 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Toolome, so most trips start by car. By car from Toolome, Sale is about 2 hours away and Bairnsdale about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest places for kids by road from Toolome are Anzac Park Playground in Briagolong (82 min) and Boisdale Recreation Reserve Playground in Boisdale (93 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Toolome.

Nearby suburbs

Koorool and Budgee Budgee also border Toolome.
