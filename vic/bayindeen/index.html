Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Bayindeen, in the Ararat area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bayindeen is Mount Buangor State Park, covering about 2,499 hectares. Buangor Reference Area is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bayindeen. The closest to Bayindeen is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bayindeen, so most trips start by car. By car from Bayindeen, Ararat is about 29 minutes away, Stawell about 53 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat is about 29 minutes' drive from Bayindeen. The closest library to Bayindeen is Ararat Library in Ararat, about 28 minutes' drive. For a skate park, David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat is about 33 minutes' drive from Bayindeen. For a cinema, Astor Cinema in Ararat is about 28 minutes' drive from Bayindeen. The nearest larger centre is Ararat, about 29 minutes from Bayindeen by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Bayindeen, the nearest places for kids by road are Astor Cinema in Ararat (28 min), Ararat Railway Museum in Ararat (29 min), Ararat Library in Ararat (28 min), Langi Morgala Museum in Ararat (29 min), Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat (29 min) and the playground at Smith Street Reserve in Ararat (31 min).
