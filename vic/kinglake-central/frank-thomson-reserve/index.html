Frank Thomson Reserve

Park in Kinglake Central · Official details

Frank Thomson Reserve is a park in Kinglake Central, in the Murrindindi Shire Council area, good for all ages. Bald Spur Rd/Whittlesea - Kinglake Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 154 m away and dogs are not allowed.

not allowed (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Frank Thomson Reserve?
Frank Thomson Reserve is in Kinglake Central, Victoria.
Is Frank Thomson Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Frank Thomson Reserve?
No, dogs are not allowed.
Are there toilets at Frank Thomson Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 154 m away.
How do I get to Frank Thomson Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Bald Spur Rd/Whittlesea - Kinglake Rd (routes 384), a 5-minute walk.
