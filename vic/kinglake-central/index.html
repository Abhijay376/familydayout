Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Kinglake Central, in the Murrindindi area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Kinglake Central is Frank Thomson Reserve, covering about 2 hectares. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Kinglake Central. The Shepherds passes through Kinglake Central.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kinglake Central. The closest to Kinglake Central is Hurstbridge Railway Station (metro), about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 384 stops in Kinglake Central. By car from Kinglake Central, Healesville is about 37 minutes away, Lilydale about 44 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 67 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Whittlesea Swimming Centre in Whittlesea is about 22 minutes' drive from Kinglake Central. For a library, Yarra Plenty Regional Library in Christmas Hills is about 34 minutes' drive from Kinglake Central. The closest skate park to Kinglake Central is Hurstbridge Half-pipe in Hurstbridge, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Kinglake Central is The Memo, Healesville in Healesville, about 36 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Timezone in Mill Park is about 40 minutes' drive from Kinglake Central. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Kinglake Central is Funfields in Whittlesea, about 24 minutes' drive. Healesville is the closest bigger centre to Kinglake Central, about 37 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Kinglake Central, the nearest places for kids by road are Bollygum Park in Kinglake (5 min), House of Bottles Museum & Tearooms in Kinglake (6 min), the playground at Ryders Flat Reserve in Arthurs Creek (30 min), Whittlesea Swimming Centre in Whittlesea (22 min), Birdy Cove in Whittlesea (24 min) and Funfields in Whittlesea (24 min).

Best for

Free days on the Shepherds and in Kinglake Central's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Kinglake, next to Kinglake Central, has 1 playground. Next door to Kinglake Central, Glenburn has 1 playground. Flowerdale also borders Kinglake Central.
