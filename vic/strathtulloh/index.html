Strathtulloh is in the Melton area, and our data lists 7 playgrounds and 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Bridge Road Recreation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Strathtulloh, at about 25 hectares. Atherstone Adventure Park is the next largest. Playgrounds around Strathtulloh include Brasilia Street Playground, Buckingham Bvd Reserve Playground and Southlands Loop Reserve Playground. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Strathtulloh. For walks and rides, Toolern Creek Trail crosses Strathtulloh.

Getting around

There's no train station in Strathtulloh. The closest to Strathtulloh is Cobblebank Railway Station (V/Line), about 2.4 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 451 and 454 stop in Strathtulloh. V/Line coaches stop in Strathtulloh. By car from Strathtulloh, Melton is about 11 minutes away, Bacchus Marsh about 20 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 41 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Strathtulloh is Melton Swimmers in Melton South, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest library to Strathtulloh is Melton Library & Learning Hub in Melton, about 11 minutes' drive. For a beach, Lionhead Beach in Pentland Hills is about 27 minutes' drive from Strathtulloh. The closest skate park to Strathtulloh is Rockbank Skate Park in Rockbank, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Strathtulloh is Reading Cinemas in Melton West, about 11 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes is about 27 minutes' drive from Strathtulloh. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Strathtulloh is Werribee Open Range Zoo in Werribee South, about 37 minutes' drive.

Best for

Younger kids at Strathtulloh's 7 local playgrounds. Free days on the Toolern Creek Trail and in Strathtulloh's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Strathtulloh, Thornhill Park has 6 playgrounds. Next door to Strathtulloh, Weir Views has 4 playgrounds. Cobblebank, next to Strathtulloh, has 3 playgrounds.
