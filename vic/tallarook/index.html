Tallarook is in the Mitchell area, and our data lists 1 playground and 10 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tallarook is Dabyminga Creek Bushland Reserve, covering about 211 hectares. Tallarook Bushland Reserve and Tallarook Recreation Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Tallarook include CWA Park, Tassie Camp and Freemans Camp. Playgrounds in Tallarook include Link Avenue Playground. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Tallarook. Picnic areas in Tallarook include Freemans Camp and Trawool Reservoir Camp. For camping, there's Tassie Camp. The Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks and Great Victorian Rail Trail all pass through Tallarook.

Getting around

Trains stop at Tallarook Railway Station (V/Line: Seymour - Melbourne via Broadmeadows and Shepparton - Melbourne via Seymour) in Tallarook. V/Line coaches stop in Tallarook. By car from Tallarook, Seymour is about 11 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 58 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 76 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Memorial Swimming pool in Seymour is about 10 minutes' drive from Tallarook. The closest library to Tallarook is Seymour Library in Seymour, about 11 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Seymour Skate Park in Seymour is about 12 minutes' drive from Tallarook. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 11 minutes from Tallarook by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Tallarook, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Rotary Park in Seymour (9 min), Memorial Swimming pool in Seymour (10 min), Seymour Library in Seymour (11 min), Seymour Skate Park in Seymour (12 min), Broadford Skate Park in Broadford (10 min) and Seymour Swimming Centre in Seymour (13 min).

Best for

Free days on the Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks and in Tallarook's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Seymour, next to Tallarook, has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Tallarook, Broadford has 6 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Sugarloaf Creek, Strath Creek, Reedy Creek and Kerrisdale also border Tallarook.
