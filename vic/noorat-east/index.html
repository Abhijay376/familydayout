Noorat East is in the Corangamite area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Noorat East, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Noorat East. The closest to Noorat East is Terang Railway Station (V/Line), about 6.4 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Noorat East, so most trips start by car. By car from Noorat East, Warrnambool is about 47 minutes away, Colac about 51 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Noorat East is Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon, about 35 minutes' drive. For a library, Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake is about 19 minutes' drive from Noorat East. For a skate park, Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown is about 17 minutes' drive from Noorat East. For a zoo or wildlife park, Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee is about 34 minutes' drive from Noorat East. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Noorat East, about 47 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Noorat East, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve in Camperdown (22 min), Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (17 min), Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake (20 min), the playground at Tea Tree Lake Reserve in Mortlake (20 min), Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake (19 min) and Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (34 min).
