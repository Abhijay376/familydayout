Our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in Welshpool, in the South Gippsland area. Welshpool also has a beach.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Welshpool is Welshpool H16 Bushland Reserve, covering about 3 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Welshpool. In Welshpool you'll also find Franklin Bank. For walks and rides, Great Southern Rail Trail and Welshpool - Port Welshpool Pathway cross Welshpool.

Getting around

There's no train station in Welshpool. The closest to Welshpool is Morwell Railway Station (V/Line), about 48 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Welshpool. By car from Welshpool, Leongatha is about 49 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 69 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Welshpool is Toora Pool in Toora, about 10 minutes' drive. For a library, Foster Library in Foster is about 19 minutes' drive from Welshpool. The closest skate park to Welshpool is the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora, about 11 minutes' drive. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Welshpool, about 49 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Welshpool, the nearest places for kids by road are Port Welshpool and District Maritime Museum in Port Welshpool (5 min), Chinaman Beach in Wilsons Promontory (10 min), Toora Pool in Toora (10 min), the playground at Stanley Street Playground in Toora (10 min), the playground at Sargasser Park in Toora (11 min) and the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora (11 min).

Best for

Beach days at Franklin Bank. Free days on the Great Southern Rail Trail and in Welshpool's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Binginwarri and Agnes also border Welshpool.
