Ravenswood is in the Greater Bendigo area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Ravenswood is Ravenswood Bushland Reserve, covering about 49 hectares. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Ravenswood.

Getting around

There's no train station in Ravenswood. The closest to Ravenswood is Kangaroo Flat Railway Station (V/Line), about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Ravenswood, so most trips start by car. By car from Ravenswood, Castlemaine is about 20 minutes away, Bendigo about 21 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Ravenswood is Harcourt Swimming Pool in Harcourt, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest library to Ravenswood is Kangaroo Flat Library in Kangaroo Flat, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Ravenswood is Long Gully BMX Track in Long Gully, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Ravenswood is Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Ravenswood is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 18 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 26 minutes' drive from Ravenswood. Castlemaine is the closest bigger centre to Ravenswood, about 20 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Ravenswood are the playground at Jerribong Way Reserve in Kangaroo Flat (12 min), William Drive Playspace in Kangaroo Flat (14 min), Kangaroo Flat Library in Kangaroo Flat (13 min), Harcourt Swimming Pool in Harcourt (15 min), Intagolf in Golden Gully (18 min) and Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Ravenswood.

Nearby suburbs

Lockwood South, next to Ravenswood, has 1 playground. Big Hill, next to Ravenswood, has 1 playground. Mandurang South, Lockwood and Harcourt North also border Ravenswood.
