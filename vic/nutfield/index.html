Nutfield is in the Nillumbik area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Nutfield, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Nutfield has no train station; Hurstbridge Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 4.2 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nutfield, so most trips start by car. By car from Nutfield, Broadmeadows is about 31 minutes away, Ringwood about 33 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 45 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Diamond Creek Outdoor Pool in Diamond Creek is about 12 minutes' drive from Nutfield. The closest library to Nutfield is Mernda Library in Mernda, about 11 minutes' drive. For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 43 minutes' drive from Nutfield. For a skate park, Hurstbridge Skate Park in Hurstbridge is about 6 minutes' drive from Nutfield. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Mill Park is about 17 minutes' drive from Nutfield. For an indoor play centre, Timezone in Mill Park is about 17 minutes' drive from Nutfield. For a zoo or wildlife park, Funfields in Whittlesea is about 17 minutes' drive from Nutfield. Broadmeadows is the closest bigger centre to Nutfield, about 31 minutes away by car, and has 12 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Nutfield, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Ryders Flat Reserve in Arthurs Creek (3 min), the playground at Gordon & Sheila Ferguson Park in Hurstbridge (5 min), Hurstbridge Skate Park in Hurstbridge (6 min), Hurstbridge Half-pipe in Hurstbridge (6 min), Mernda Library in Mernda (11 min) and Nillumbik Historical Society in Diamond Creek (10 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Nutfield.

Nearby suburbs

Doreen, next to Nutfield, has 32 playgrounds. Hurstbridge, next to Nutfield, has 3 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Nutfield, Arthurs Creek has 1 playground.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Nutfield, without traffic.
