Powers Creek is in the West Wimmera area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Durong State Forest is the largest mapped park in Powers Creek, at about 3,062 hectares. Powers Creek School Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Powers Creek has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 146 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Powers Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Powers Creek, Hamilton is about 1.5 hours away and Horsham about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest places for kids by road from Powers Creek are the playground at Lions Park in Edenhope (29 min), Dachshund Museum & Gallery in Edenhope (29 min), West Wimmera Community Association - Red Tail Gallery in Edenhope (29 min) and Henley Park Playground in Edenhope (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Powers Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Edenhope, next to Powers Creek, has 2 playgrounds. Chetwynd also borders Powers Creek.
