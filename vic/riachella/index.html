Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Riachella in the Northern Grampians area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Riachella has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 56 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Riachella, so most trips start by car. By car from Riachella, Stawell is about 31 minutes away and Horsham about 52 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell is about 30 minutes' drive from Riachella. The closest skate park to Riachella is the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Riachella is Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell, about 33 minutes' drive. Stawell is the closest bigger centre to Riachella, about 31 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Riachella are Glenorchy Playground in Glenorchy (10 min), the playground at Lubeck Recreation Reserve in Lubeck (27 min), the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell (32 min), Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell (30 min), Rupanyup Swimming Pool in Rupanyup (30 min) and Woods' Farming and Heritage Museum in Rupanyup (30 min).
