Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Toolleen, in the Campaspe area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Toolleen is Crosbie Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 2,018 hectares. Toolleen Bushland Reserve and Tooleen Highway Park are the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Toolleen.

Getting around

Toolleen has no train station; Goornong Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Toolleen. By car from Toolleen, Bendigo is about 48 minutes away, Echuca about 60 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Goornong Swimming Pool in Goornong is about 28 minutes' drive from Toolleen. The closest library to Toolleen is Heathcote Library in Heathcote, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Toolleen is Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote, about 21 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 45 minutes' drive from Toolleen. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 48 minutes from Toolleen by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Toolleen are Heathcote Play Space in Heathcote (20 min), Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote (21 min), Heathcote Library in Heathcote (21 min), Mudwood Studio in Heathcote (21 min), Powder Magazine in Heathcote (23 min) and Goornong Swimming Pool in Goornong (28 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Toolleen.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Toolleen, Colbinabbin has 1 playground. Next door to Toolleen, Axedale has 1 playground. Muskerry, Mount Camel, Ladys Pass and Knowsley also border Toolleen.
