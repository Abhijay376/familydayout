Tottington is in the Northern Grampians area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tottington is Big Tottington Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 1,869 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tottington. The closest to Tottington is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 57 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tottington, so most trips start by car. By car from Tottington, Stawell is about 40 minutes away and Ararat about 64 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud is about 22 minutes' drive from Tottington. The nearest larger centre is Stawell, about 40 minutes from Tottington by car, with 6 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Tottington are Navarre Playground in Navarre (10 min), Stuart Mill Recreation Reserve Playground in Stuart Mill (27 min) and St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (22 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tottington.

Nearby suburbs

Gre Gre, Bolangum and Beazleys Bridge also border Tottington.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Tottington, without traffic.
