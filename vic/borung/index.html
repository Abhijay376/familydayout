Borung is in the Loddon area, and our data lists 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Borung is Unnamed P0241 Private Nature Reserve, covering about 251 hectares. Unnamed P0198 Private Nature Reserve and Borung I119 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Borung include Kinypanial Streamside Reserve.

Getting around

Borung has no train station; Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 42 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Bendigo - Boort via Wedderburn route stop in Borung. V/Line coaches stop in Borung. By car from Borung, Bendigo is about 84 minutes away and Maryborough about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Borung is Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Borung is Rex Theatre in Charlton, about 37 minutes' drive. From Borung, the nearest places for kids by road are Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (25 min), Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (25 min), A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park in Boort (26 min), Boort Court House Historical Museum in Boort (26 min), Boort Swimming Pool in Boort (27 min) and Rex Theatre in Charlton (37 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Borung.

Nearby suburbs

Kinypanial, Jarklin, Fernihurst and Bears Lagoon also border Borung.
