Lindsay in the Glenelg area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Unnamed (No.HA135) Heritage Agreement is the largest mapped park in Lindsay, at about 183 hectares. Lindsay State Forest and Kaladbro H20 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Lindsay.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lindsay. The closest to Lindsay is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 155 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lindsay, so most trips start by car. By car from Lindsay, Hamilton is about 81 minutes away and Portland about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lindsay is Casterton Pool in Casterton, about 30 minutes' drive. From Lindsay, the nearest places for kids by road are Gorman's art gallery in Casterton (30 min) and Casterton Pool in Casterton (30 min).
