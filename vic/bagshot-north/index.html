Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bagshot North in the Greater Bendigo area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Bagshot North has no train station; Huntly Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 8.4 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bagshot North, so most trips start by car. By car from Bagshot North, Bendigo is about 33 minutes away, Castlemaine about 65 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bagshot North is Goornong Swimming Pool in Goornong, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest library to Bagshot North is Bendigo Library in Bendigo, about 33 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Bagshot North is McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Bagshot North is Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo, about 34 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 37 minutes' drive from Bagshot North. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Bagshot North is The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo, about 30 minutes' drive. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Bagshot North, about 33 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Bagshot North, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Pennington Reserve in Huntly (17 min), Goornong Swimming Pool in Goornong (20 min), the playground at Epsom Village Green in Epsom (24 min), White Hills Pool in White Hills (27 min), Bendigo Joss House Temple in North Bendigo (30 min) and The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo (30 min).
