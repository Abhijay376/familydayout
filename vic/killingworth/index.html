Killingworth in the Murrindindi area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Killingworth is Goulburn River, Killingworth Rd Streamside Reserve, covering about 3 hectares. The Franklin Track passes through Killingworth.

Getting around

There's no train station in Killingworth. The closest to Killingworth is Seymour Railway Station (V/Line), about 31 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Killingworth, so most trips start by car. By car from Killingworth, Seymour is about 41 minutes away, Healesville about 64 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Killingworth is Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra, about 37 minutes' drive. The closest library to Killingworth is Alexandra Library in Alexandra, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Killingworth is the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea, about 12 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 41 minutes from Killingworth by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Killingworth, the nearest places for kids by road are the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea (12 min), TLM Moto Guzzi in Yea (32 min), Yarck Recreation Reserve Playground in Yarck (29 min), the playground at Leckie Park in Alexandra (37 min), Alexandra Library in Alexandra (36 min) and BMX Track in Caveat (53 min).

Best for

Free days on the Franklin Track and in Killingworth's parks.
