Leongatha South in the South Gippsland area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Leongatha South is Leongatha H3 Bushland Reserve, covering about 1 hectares. The Great Southern Rail Trail passes through Leongatha South.

Getting around

Leongatha South has no train station; Warragul Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 40 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Wonthaggi - Leongatha via Inverloch route stop in Leongatha South. By car from Leongatha South, Leongatha is about 7 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 26 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha is about 8 minutes' drive from Leongatha South. For a library, Leongatha Library in Leongatha is about 7 minutes' drive from Leongatha South. The closest beach to Leongatha South is The Caves in Inverloch, about 21 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Leongatha Skate Park in Leongatha is about 8 minutes' drive from Leongatha South. The closest cinema to Leongatha South is Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha, about 8 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville is about 39 minutes' drive from Leongatha South. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Leongatha South, about 7 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Leongatha South are the playground at Mary Checkley Reserve in Leongatha (6 min), Leongatha Library in Leongatha (7 min), Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha (8 min), South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha (8 min), Leongatha Skate Park in Leongatha (8 min) and Splash Pools and Stadium in Leongatha (9 min).

Best for

Free days on the Great Southern Rail Trail and in Leongatha South's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Leongatha South, Leongatha has 4 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Leongatha South, Inverloch has 4 playgrounds and a beach. Outtrim, Nerrena, Middle Tarwin and Korumburra South also border Leongatha South.
