Wunghnu in the Moira area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Wunghnu is Wunghnu Park and Recreation Reserve, covering about 8 hectares. Wunghnu also has Graham Park. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Wunghnu.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wunghnu. The closest to Wunghnu is Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line), about 25 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Wunghnu. By car from Wunghnu, Shepparton is about 24 minutes away, Benalla about 70 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Wunghnu is Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest library to Wunghnu is Numurkah Library in Numurkah, about 9 minutes' drive. For a beach, Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton is about 27 minutes' drive from Wunghnu. The closest skate park to Wunghnu is Riverland Skate Park in Numurkah, about 8 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 24 minutes' drive from Wunghnu. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 24 minutes' drive from Wunghnu. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Wunghnu is Kidstown in Mooroopna, about 27 minutes' drive. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Wunghnu, about 24 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Wunghnu are Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah (7 min), Riverland Skate Park in Numurkah (8 min), Numurkah Library in Numurkah (9 min), the playground at Larsen Reserve in Tallygaroopna (9 min), the playground at Tallygaroopna Recreation Reserve in Tallygaroopna (11 min) and Shepparton Velodrome in Shepparton (21 min).
