Seaton in the Wellington area has 5 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Seaton Education Area is the largest mapped park in Seaton, at about 118 hectares. Seaton Bushland Reserve and Glenmaggie I1 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Seaton include Glenmaggie Education Reserve and Glenmaggie I2 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Seaton has no train station; Rosedale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Seaton, so most trips start by car. By car from Seaton, Traralgon is about 43 minutes away, Sale about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Seaton is Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield, about 20 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Pump Track in Heyfield is about 20 minutes' drive from Seaton. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Seaton, about 43 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. From Seaton, the nearest places for kids by road are Apex Park Playground in Heyfield (20 min), Cricket Street Playground in Cowwarr (20 min), Heyfield and District Museum in Heyfield (20 min), Pump Track in Heyfield (20 min), Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield (20 min) and the skate park at Apex Park in Glengarry (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Seaton.

Nearby suburbs

Heyfield, next to Seaton, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Cowwarr, next to Seaton, has 1 playground. Dawson also borders Seaton.
