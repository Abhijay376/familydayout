Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Nirranda East in the Moyne area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Nirranda East. The closest to Nirranda East is Terang Railway Station (V/Line), about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nirranda East, so most trips start by car. By car from Nirranda East, Warrnambool is about 37 minutes away and Colac about 69 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Nirranda East is Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest library to Nirranda East is Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Nirranda East is Buttress Point in The Cove, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Nirranda East is the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Nirranda East is Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Nirranda East is The Bowl in Warrnambool, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Nirranda East is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 30 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 37 minutes from Nirranda East by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Nirranda East, the nearest places for kids by road are the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (15 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (15 min), Buttress Point in The Cove (19 min), Flaxman Beach in Nullawarre (21 min), the playground at Peterborough Coastal Reserve (COM) in Peterborough (27 min) and Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell (29 min).

Best for
