Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Tyntynder South in the Swan Hill area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tyntynder South. The closest to Tyntynder South is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 8.2 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tyntynder South, so most trips start by car. By car from Tyntynder South, Swan Hill is about 8 minutes away and Echuca about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tyntynder South is Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest library to Tyntynder South is Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill, about 7 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at James Belsar Reserve in Swan Hill is about 8 minutes' drive from Tyntynder South. The closest cinema to Tyntynder South is Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill, about 8 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 8 minutes from Tyntynder South by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Tyntynder South are the playground at Adams Park in Swan Hill (8 min), Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill (7 min), the skate park at James Belsar Reserve in Swan Hill (8 min), Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill (7 min), Swan Hill Outdoor Pool in Swan Hill (7 min) and the playground at Riverside Park in Swan Hill (7 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tyntynder South.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Tyntynder South, Swan Hill has 9 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Woorinen South, next to Tyntynder South, has a skate park. Murraydale, Murrawee and Beverford also border Tyntynder South.
