Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Morton Plains in the Buloke area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Morton Plains has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 103 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Morton Plains, so most trips start by car. By car from Morton Plains, Horsham is about 86 minutes away and Swan Hill about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem is about 7 minutes' drive from Morton Plains. For a skate park, Birchip Skate Park in Birchip is about 10 minutes' drive from Morton Plains. The closest places for kids by road from Morton Plains are Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem (7 min), the playground at Lake Watchem Lake Reserve in Watchem (9 min), the playground at J.E Taylor Sunset Park in Birchip (9 min), Birchip History Museum in Birchip (10 min), Birchip Skate Park in Birchip (10 min) and Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Morton Plains.

Nearby suburbs

Watchem, next to Morton Plains, has 1 playground and a pool. Next door to Morton Plains, Birchip has 2 playgrounds and a skate park. Narraport, next to Morton Plains, has 1 playground. Thalia, Corack and Birchip West also border Morton Plains.
