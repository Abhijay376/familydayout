Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Airly in the Wellington area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Airly has no train station; Stratford Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 6.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Airly, so most trips start by car. By car from Airly, Sale is about 17 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 54 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford is about 13 minutes' drive from Airly. For a library, Sale Library in Sale is about 18 minutes' drive from Airly. The closest skate park to Airly is Skate Park in Stratford, about 14 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Former Drive-in site in Sale is about 15 minutes' drive from Airly. Sale is the closest bigger centre to Airly, about 17 minutes away by car, and has 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Airly, the nearest places for kids by road are Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford (13 min), BMX Track in Stratford (13 min), Apex Park Playground in Stratford (14 min), Skate Park in Stratford (14 min), Former Drive-in site in Sale (15 min) and Sale Historical Museum in Sale (17 min).
