Getting around

There's no train station in Markwood. The closest to Markwood is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Markwood, so most trips start by car. By car from Markwood, Wangaratta is about 20 minutes away and Benalla about 42 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Markwood is Splashpark in Wangaratta, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest library to Markwood is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Markwood is Sandy Beach in Beechworth, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Markwood is the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Markwood is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 17 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 20 minutes from Markwood by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Markwood, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at John McAleese Community Park in Milawa (5 min), Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta (17 min), the playground at Apex Park in Wangaratta (18 min), Splashpark in Wangaratta (19 min), Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (19 min) and the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta (19 min).
