Corop is in the Campaspe area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Corop is Corop Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 12 hectares. Corop Bushland Reserve and Corop Cemetery B.R. are the next largest. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Corop.

Getting around

Corop has no train station; Rochester Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Corop. By car from Corop, Echuca is about 37 minutes away, Shepparton about 47 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Memorial Pool in Rochester is about 14 minutes' drive from Corop. For a library, Rushworth Library in Rushworth is about 27 minutes' drive from Corop. For a skate park, the skate park at Lions Park in Rushworth is about 27 minutes' drive from Corop. For a cinema, Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca is about 37 minutes' drive from Corop. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Corop is Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram, about 32 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 37 minutes from Corop by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Corop are Memorial Pool in Rochester (14 min), the playground at Apex Park in Rochester (15 min), Campaspe Run Rural Discovery Centre in Elmore (16 min), Rushworth Museum in Rushworth (26 min), Rushworth Library in Rushworth (27 min) and Rushworth Pool in Rushworth (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Corop.

Nearby suburbs

Carag Carag, Burramboot, Burnewang and Bonn also border Corop.
