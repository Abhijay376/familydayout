Eric Reserve

Park in Templestowe Lower · Basic info

Eric Reserve is a park in Templestowe Lower, in the Manningham City Council area, good for all ages. Swanston St/Templestowe Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 740 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed on a lead.
