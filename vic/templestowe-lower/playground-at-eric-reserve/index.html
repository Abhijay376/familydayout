Playground at Eric Reserve

Playground in Templestowe Lower · Basic info

This is a public playground in Templestowe Lower. Manningham Toy Library is about 560 m away, and Playground at Birrarrung Park is another option about 300 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 740 m from the playground, and bus 280 stops at Swanston St/Templestowe Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
