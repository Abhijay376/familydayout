Ruffey Creek Linear Park

Park in Templestowe Lower · Basic info

Ruffey Creek Linear Park is a park in Templestowe Lower, in the Manningham City Council area, good for all ages. Montpellier Cres/Williamsons Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1121 m away with a baby change table.
