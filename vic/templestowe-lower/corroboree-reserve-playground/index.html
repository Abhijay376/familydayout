Corroboree Reserve Playground

Playground in Templestowe Lower · Basic info

Corroboree Reserve Playground is a small local playground in Templestowe Lower, in the Manningham City area. Playground at Richard Reserve is another option about 440 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 910 m from the playground, and bus 282 stops at Pentlowe Ave/Templestowe Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
