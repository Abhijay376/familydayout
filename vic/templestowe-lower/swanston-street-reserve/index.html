Swanston Street Reserve

Park in Templestowe Lower · Basic info

Swanston Street Reserve is a park in Templestowe Lower, in the Manningham City Council area, good for all ages. Spring St/Pleasant Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 55 m away.
