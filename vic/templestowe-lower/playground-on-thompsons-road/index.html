Hodgson Reserve Playground

Playground in Templestowe Lower · Basic info

Hodgson Reserve Playground in Templestowe Lower is a small local playground in the Manningham City area. Playground on Jeffrey Street is another option about 20 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 300 m from the playground, and bus 905 stops at Pathway Baptist Church/Thompsons Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
