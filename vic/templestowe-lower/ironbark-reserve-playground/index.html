Ironbark Reserve Playground

Playground in Templestowe Lower · Basic info

This is a small local playground in Templestowe Lower, looked after by Manningham City Council. For food, Golden Dragon Palace is about 210 m away, and Playground at Crawford Road Reserve is another option about 390 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 570 m from the playground, and buses 281, 309 and 908 stop at Fyfe Dr/High St, a 5-minute walk away.
