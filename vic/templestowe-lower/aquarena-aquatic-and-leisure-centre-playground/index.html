Aquarena Aquatic and Leisure Centre Playground

Playground in Templestowe Lower · Basic info

Aquarena Aquatic and Leisure Centre Playground is a small local playground in Templestowe Lower, in the Manningham City area. Aquarena Aquatic and Leisure Centre is about 140 m away, and Playground at Crawford Road Reserve is another option about 90 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.4 km from the playground, and buses 279, 293 and 902 stop at Aquarena/Williamsons Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
