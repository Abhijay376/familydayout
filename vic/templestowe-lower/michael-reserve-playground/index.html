Michael Reserve Playground

Playground in Templestowe Lower · Basic info

This is a small local playground in Templestowe Lower, looked after by Manningham City Council. House of Pizza & Parma is about 330 m away if you need food, and Ted Ajani Reserve Playground is another option about 230 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 530 m away, and buses 305, 309 and 903 stop at Hotham St/Manningham Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
