Andromeda Reserve

Park in Templestowe Lower · Basic info

Andromeda Reserve is a park in Templestowe Lower, in the Manningham City Council area, good for all ages. Williamsons Rd/George St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1242 m away.
