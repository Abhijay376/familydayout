Youanmite is in the Moira area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Youanmite is Youanmite Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 100 hectares. Youanmite H37 Bushland Reserve and Youanmite H38 Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Youanmite. The closest to Youanmite is Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line), about 36 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Youanmite, so most trips start by car. By car from Youanmite, Shepparton is about 40 minutes away and Benalla about 45 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah is about 35 minutes' drive from Youanmite. The closest library to Youanmite is Numurkah Library in Numurkah, about 37 minutes' drive. For a beach, Fosters Beach in Cobram is about 28 minutes' drive from Youanmite. For a skate park, the skate park at Federation Park in Cobram is about 28 minutes' drive from Youanmite. The closest cinema to Youanmite is Cobram Cinema in Cobram, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Youanmite is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 40 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Youanmite is Kidstown in Mooroopna, about 42 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 40 minutes from Youanmite by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Youanmite, the nearest places for kids by road are Katamatite Historical Society Museum in Katamatite (11 min), the playground at E.W Kilgour Reserve in Katamatite (11 min), the playground at C R Wood Park in Katamatite (12 min), Fosters Beach in Cobram (28 min), Backhouse Beach in Cobram East (28 min) and Cobram Cinema in Cobram (28 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Youanmite.

Nearby suburbs

Katamatite, next to Youanmite, has 2 playgrounds. Katandra and Invergordon also border Youanmite.
