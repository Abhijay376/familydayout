Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Yering, in the Yarra Ranges area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Yering is Yering Bushland Reserve, covering about 104 hectares. Murrup Brarn Yarra Flats Billabongs and Spadonis Nature Conservation Reserve are the next largest. The Yarra Valley Trail, Yarra Valley Trail (Yarra Glen extension - under construction) and Spadoni's Nature Walk all pass through Yering.

Getting around

Yering has no train station; Lilydale Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 9.9 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yering, so most trips start by car. By car from Yering, Lilydale is about 13 minutes away, Healesville about 15 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 52 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Yering is Lilydale Outdoor Pool in Lilydale, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest library to Yering is Yarra Plenty Regional Library in Christmas Hills, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Yering is the skate park at McKenzie Reserve in Yarra Glen, about 3 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Yering is The Memo, Healesville in Healesville, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Yering is GAMR Laser Tag & VR in Chirnside Park, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Yering is Lyre Bird enclosure in Badger Creek, about 19 minutes' drive. Lilydale is the closest bigger centre to Yering, about 13 minutes away by car, and has 12 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Yering, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at McKenzie Reserve in Yarra Glen (3 min), the skate park at McKenzie Reserve in Yarra Glen (3 min), the playground at Hubbard Reserve in Yarra Glen (4 min), TarraWarra Museum of Art in Healesville (10 min), Eva and Marc Besen Centre in Healesville (11 min) and Yarra Plenty Regional Library in Christmas Hills (13 min).

Best for

Free days on the Yarra Valley Trail and in Yering's parks.
