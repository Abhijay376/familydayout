Broken Creek in the Benalla area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Broken Creek is Goorambat H84 Bushland Reserve, covering about 8 hectares. Broken River K24 Streamside Reserve and Goorambat H124 Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

Broken Creek has no train station; Benalla Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Broken Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Broken Creek, Benalla is about 20 minutes away, Shepparton about 47 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla is about 20 minutes' drive from Broken Creek. The closest skate park to Broken Creek is Pump Track in Benalla, about 21 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Swanpool Cinema in Swanpool is about 40 minutes' drive from Broken Creek. Benalla is the closest bigger centre to Broken Creek, about 20 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Broken Creek are Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (20 min), Benalla Splash Park in Benalla (20 min), Benalla Costume and Pioneer Museum in Benalla (20 min), Pump Track in Benalla (21 min), Benalla Art Gallery in Benalla (21 min) and The Rocket Playground in Benalla (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Broken Creek.
