Bagshot is in the Greater Bendigo area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bagshot is Bagshot Recreation Reserve, covering about 4 hectares.

Getting around

Bagshot has a train station: Huntly Railway Station (V/Line: Echuca/Moama - Melbourne via Bendigo or Heathcote). Buses on the Goornong route stop in Bagshot. By car from Bagshot, Bendigo is about 21 minutes away, Castlemaine about 53 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Goornong Swimming Pool in Goornong is about 9 minutes' drive from Bagshot. The closest library to Bagshot is Bendigo Library in Bendigo, about 21 minutes' drive. For a skate park, McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk is about 20 minutes' drive from Bagshot. The closest cinema to Bagshot is Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Bagshot is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Bagshot is The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo, about 19 minutes' drive. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Bagshot, about 21 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Bagshot, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Pennington Reserve in Huntly (5 min), the playground at Collins Park in Ascot (12 min), Goornong Swimming Pool in Goornong (9 min), White Hills Pool in White Hills (15 min), Bendigo Joss House Temple in North Bendigo (19 min) and The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo (19 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bagshot.

Nearby suburbs

Goornong, next to Bagshot, has a pool. Next door to Bagshot, Huntly has 3 playgrounds. Fosterville and Bagshot North also border Bagshot.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Bagshot, without traffic.
