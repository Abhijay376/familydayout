Wairewa is in the East Gippsland area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Wairewa, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

The East Gippsland Rail Trail passes through Wairewa.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wairewa. The closest to Wairewa is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 50 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Wairewa. By car from Wairewa, Lakes Entrance is about 40 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 67 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Lakes Entrance Aquatic Centre in Lakes Entrance is about 38 minutes' drive from Wairewa. The closest library to Wairewa is Lakes Entrance Library in Lakes Entrance, about 40 minutes' drive. For a beach, Ewings Marsh in Waygara is about 41 minutes' drive from Wairewa. Lakes Entrance is the closest bigger centre to Wairewa, about 40 minutes away by car, and has 3 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Wairewa are Ewings Marsh in Waygara (41 min), Lakes Entrance Aquatic Centre in Lakes Entrance (38 min), Lake Tyers Beach Sand Bar in Lake Tyers Beach (45 min), the playground at Lakes Entrance - Lake Tyers Coastal Reserve (COM) in Lake Tyers Beach (45 min), Lakes Entrance Library in Lakes Entrance (40 min) and Bronsdon Street Park in Lakes Entrance (43 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Wairewa on the East Gippsland Rail Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Bete Bolong also borders Wairewa.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Wairewa, without traffic.
