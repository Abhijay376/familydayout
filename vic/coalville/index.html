Coalville in the Baw Baw area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Coalville G219 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Coalville, at about 23 hectares. Coalville G218 Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Coalville also has G M Brown Reserve.

Getting around

Coalville has no train station; Moe Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 6.9 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Coalville, so most trips start by car. By car from Coalville, Traralgon is about 27 minutes away, Warragul about 34 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Diving Pool in Moe is about 8 minutes' drive from Coalville. The closest library to Coalville is Morwell Library in Morwell, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Coalville is Moe Skate Park in Moe, about 9 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 18 minutes' drive from Coalville. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 32 minutes' drive from Coalville. The nearest larger centre is Traralgon, about 27 minutes from Coalville by car, with 38 playgrounds and a pool. From Coalville, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Wirrana Drive Reserve in Moe (7 min), the playground at Smallburn Avenue Reserve in Newborough (7 min), Diving Pool in Moe (8 min), Moe Outdoor Pool in Moe (9 min), Moe Skate Park in Moe (9 min) and Old Brown Coal Mine Museum in Yallourn North (14 min).

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Coalville.
