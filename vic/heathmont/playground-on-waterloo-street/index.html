Playground on Waterloo Street

Playground in Heathmont · Basic info

Playground on Waterloo Street is a public playground in Heathmont, in the Maroondah City area. The Dandenong Creek Trail passes about 330 m away, and Playground on Blenheim Street is another option about 10 m away.
