Playground on Great Ryrie Street

Playground in Heathmont · Basic info

Playground on Great Ryrie Street is a public playground in Heathmont, in the Maroondah City area. The Ringwood - Belgrave Rail Trail is about 310 m away, and Competition Pool and Dive Pool is about 600 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 900 m from the playground, and bus 669 stops at Aquinas College/Great Ryrie St, a 4-minute walk away.
