Playground at Viviani Reserve

Playground in Heathmont · Basic info

This is a public playground in Heathmont. The Ringwood - Belgrave Rail Trail is about 230 m away, and for food, The Dish Thai is about 120 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 470 m from the playground, and bus 679 stops at Balfour Ave/Canterbury Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
