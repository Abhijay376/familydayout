Uambi Bush Reserve

Park in Heathmont · Basic info

Uambi Bush Reserve is a park in Heathmont, in the Maroondah City Council area, good for all ages. Heathmont Station/Canterbury Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 425 m away.
