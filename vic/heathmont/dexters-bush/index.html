Dexters Bush

Park in Heathmont · Basic info

Dexters Bush is a park in Heathmont, in the Maroondah City Council area, good for all ages. Heathmont Station/Canterbury Rd (bus) is a 14-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 608 m away.
