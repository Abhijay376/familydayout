Playground on Wollahra Place

Playground in Heathmont · Basic info

Playground on Wollahra Place in Heathmont is a public playground in the Maroondah City area. Villarosa Pizzeria is about 240 m away if you need food, and Playground at Wombolano Park is another option about 430 m away. There's a public toilet about 750 m away, and bus 679 stops at Aringa Ct/Canterbury Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
