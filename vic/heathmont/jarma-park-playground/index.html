Jarma Park Playground

Playground in Heathmont · Basic info

Jarma Park Playground is a public playground in Heathmont, in the Maroondah City area. Seaquest Fish & Chips is about 390 m away if you need food, and Playground on Cuthbert Street is another option about 410 m away. There's a public toilet about 990 m away, and bus 901 stops at Canterbury Rd/Wantirna Rd, a 7-minute walk away.
