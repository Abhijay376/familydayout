Heathmont is in the Maroondah area, and our data lists 16 playgrounds and 12 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Heathmont Reserve is the largest mapped park in Heathmont, at about 4 hectares. Uambi Bush Reserve and FJC Rogers Reserve are the next largest. Heathmont also has Apex Park, Jarma Park and Dexters Bush. Playgrounds around Heathmont include Jarma Park Playground, the playground at Apex Park and Dresden Avenue Playground. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Heathmont. For walks and rides, Ringwood - Belgrave Rail Trail, Bayswater-Lilydale Trail and Tarralla Creek Trail cross Heathmont. Our data lists 11 cafes and family restaurants in Heathmont for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Heathmont Railway Station (metro: Belgrave Line) in Heathmont. Buses 669 and 679 stop in Heathmont. By car from Heathmont, Ringwood is about 6 minutes away, Box Hill about 16 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 32 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Leisure Pool in Ringwood is about 4 minutes' drive from Heathmont. For a library, Realm in Ringwood is about 6 minutes' drive from Heathmont. For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 29 minutes' drive from Heathmont. For a skate park, Ringwood Skate Park in Ringwood is about 6 minutes' drive from Heathmont. The closest cinema to Heathmont is Hoyts in Ringwood, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Heathmont is Timezone in Ringwood, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Heathmont is Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale, about 23 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 16 playgrounds across Heathmont. Free days on the Ringwood - Belgrave Rail Trail and in Heathmont's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Heathmont, Ringwood has 23 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Wantirna, next to Heathmont, has 33 playgrounds. Next door to Heathmont, Bayswater has 16 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Ringwood East, next to Heathmont, has 12 playgrounds. Bayswater North also borders Heathmont.
