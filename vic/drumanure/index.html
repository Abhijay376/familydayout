Drumanure is in the Moira area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Drumanure, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Drumanure. The closest to Drumanure is Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line), about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Drumanure, so most trips start by car. By car from Drumanure, Shepparton is about 38 minutes away, Benalla about 73 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah is about 18 minutes' drive from Drumanure. For a library, Numurkah Library in Numurkah is about 20 minutes' drive from Drumanure. For a beach, Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton is about 41 minutes' drive from Drumanure. For a skate park, Riverland Skate Park in Numurkah is about 19 minutes' drive from Drumanure. The closest cinema to Drumanure is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Drumanure is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Drumanure is Kidstown in Mooroopna, about 41 minutes' drive. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Drumanure, about 38 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Drumanure are Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah (18 min), Riverland Skate Park in Numurkah (19 min), Numurkah Library in Numurkah (20 min), the playground at Katandra Rec Res Youth Spot in Katandra West (24 min), the playground at Katandra West Recreation Reserve in Katandra West (25 min) and Katamatite Historical Society Museum in Katamatite (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Drumanure.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Drumanure, Numurkah has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Tallygaroopna, next to Drumanure, has 2 playgrounds. Invergordon also borders Drumanure.
