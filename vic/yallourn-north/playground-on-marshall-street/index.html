Playground on Marshall Street

Playground in Yallourn North · Basic info

This is a public playground in Yallourn North. Yallourn North Outdoor Pool is about 560 m away, and Playground at Low Road Park is another option about 360 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 930 m from the playground, and bus 5 stops at Low Rd/East St, a 5-minute walk away.
