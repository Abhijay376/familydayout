Yallourn North in the Latrobe area has 5 playgrounds and 13 parks and reserves in our data. Beyond the parks, Yallourn North has a pool, a museum or gallery and a skate park.

Landmarks

Tyers Park is the largest mapped park in Yallourn North, at about 1,854 hectares. Narracan State Forest and Moondarra State Forest are the next largest. Yallourn North also has Lions Park, Low Road Park and North Road Park. Playgrounds in Yallourn North include the playground at Lions Park, the playground at Low Road Park and the playground at Anderson Avenue Park. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Yallourn North. Places to visit in Yallourn North include Old Brown Coal Mine Museum and Yallourn North Outdoor Pool. For older kids in Yallourn North there's Yallourn North Skate Park and BMX Track. For walks and rides, Wirilda Walking Track crosses Yallourn North.

Getting around

Yallourn North has no train station; Morwell Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 9.0 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 5 stops in Yallourn North. By car from Yallourn North, Traralgon is about 20 minutes away, Warragul about 37 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Yallourn North is Morwell Library in Morwell, about 14 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 16 minutes' drive from Yallourn North. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 36 minutes' drive from Yallourn North.

Best for

Younger kids at Yallourn North's 5 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Yallourn North. Teens who skate or ride, with Yallourn North Skate Park and BMX Track in Yallourn North. Free days on the Wirilda Walking Track and in Yallourn North's parks.
