Low Road Park

Park in Yallourn North · Basic info

Low Road Park is a park in Yallourn North, in the Latrobe City Council area, good for all ages. Low Rd/East St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 558 m away with a baby change table.
