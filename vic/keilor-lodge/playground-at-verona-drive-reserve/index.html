Playground at Verona Drive Reserve

Playground in Keilor Lodge · Official details

Playground at Verona Drive Reserve is a public playground in Keilor Lodge, in the Brimbank City area. Kore Wellness & Swim Centre is about 460 m away, and for food, Sumos Noodle & Sushi Bar is about 470 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 760 m from the playground, and bus 421 stops at Messina Cres/Verona Dr, a 1-minute walk away.
