Keilor Lodge is in the Brimbank area, and our data lists 2 playgrounds and 5 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Transmission Line Linear Reserve is the largest mapped park in Keilor Lodge, at about 3 hectares. Verona Drive Reserve and Penrhyn Court Reserve are the next largest. Keilor Lodge also has Vasa Place Reserve and Santa Monica Drive Reserve. Playgrounds around Keilor Lodge include the playground at Verona Drive Reserve and the playground at Santa Monica Drive Reserve. Our data shows 2 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Keilor Lodge.

Getting around

Keilor Lodge has no train station; Watergardens Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 2.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 421, 476 and 941 stop in Keilor Lodge. By car from Keilor Lodge, Sunshine is about 12 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 15 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 25 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Keilor Lodge is Kore Wellness & Swim Centre in Taylors Lakes, about 2 minutes' drive. For a library, Brimbank Library in Taylors Lakes is about 5 minutes' drive from Keilor Lodge. The closest beach to Keilor Lodge is Horse Arena in Abbotsford, about 28 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Packard Street Reserve in Keilor Downs is about 6 minutes' drive from Keilor Lodge. For a cinema, Hoyts in Taylors Lakes is about 5 minutes' drive from Keilor Lodge. The closest indoor play centre to Keilor Lodge is Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Keilor Lodge is Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville, about 20 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Sunshine, about 12 minutes from Keilor Lodge by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Keilor Lodge.

Nearby suburbs

Taylors Lakes, next to Keilor Lodge, has 16 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Keilor Lodge, Keilor has 11 playgrounds and a library. Keilor North, next to Keilor Lodge, has 1 playground.
