Penrhyn Court Reserve

Park in Keilor Lodge · Basic info

Penrhyn Court Reserve is a park in Keilor Lodge, in the Brimbank City Council area, good for all ages. Penrhyn Ct/Verona Dr (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1227 m away.
