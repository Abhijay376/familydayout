Peninsula Hot Springs

Pool or water play in Fingal · Official details

Peninsula Hot Springs is a pool or water play in Fingal, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire Council area, good for all ages. Sandy Rd/Tiberius Rd (bus) is a 24-minute walk away. Opening hours: Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu 07:00-23:00; Fri 07:00-02:00; Sat 05:00-02:00; Sun 05:00-23:00.
