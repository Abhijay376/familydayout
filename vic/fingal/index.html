Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Fingal, in the Mornington Peninsula area. On top of that, Fingal has 2 pools.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Fingal is Weeroona Bushland Reserve, covering about 23 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Fingal. Places to visit in Fingal include Alba Thermal Springs & Spa ARCH and Peninsula Hot Springs.

Getting around

There's no train station in Fingal. The closest to Fingal is Bittern Railway Station (metro), about 30 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Fingal, so most trips start by car. By car from Fingal, Mornington is about 31 minutes away, Frankston about 37 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 80 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Rosebud Library in Rosebud is about 14 minutes' drive from Fingal. The closest beach to Fingal is Gunnamatta Beach in Cape Schanck, about 10 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Rosebud Skate Park in Rosebud is about 12 minutes' drive from Fingal. For a cinema, Rosebud Cinemas in Rosebud is about 13 minutes' drive from Fingal. For an indoor play centre, Top Fun Shack in Rosebud is about 13 minutes' drive from Fingal. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Fingal is Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale, about 43 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Mornington, about 31 minutes from Fingal by car, with 22 playgrounds, a library, indoor play and a beach. From Fingal, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Marshall Street Reserve in Tootgarook (9 min), the playground at Port Phillip Bay Coastal Reserve (COM) in Capel Sound (9 min), Gunnamatta Beach in Cape Schanck (10 min), Number Sixteen Beach in Rye (12 min), Rosebud Skate Park in Rosebud (12 min) and Top Fun Shack in Rosebud (13 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Fingal.

Nearby suburbs

Rye, next to Fingal, has 3 playgrounds and a beach. Cape Schanck, next to Fingal, has 1 playground and a beach. Next door to Fingal, Tootgarook has 1 playground. Boneo also borders Fingal.
