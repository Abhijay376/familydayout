Landmarks

Playgrounds around Shepparton East include the playground at O'Donnell Park, the playground at WG Parker-Central Park and the playground at Central Park Tennis Courts.

Getting around

There's no train station in Shepparton East. The closest to Shepparton East is Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line), about 6.4 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Shepparton East, so most trips start by car. By car from Shepparton East, Shepparton is about 9 minutes away, Benalla about 43 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.
