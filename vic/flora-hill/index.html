Our data lists 4 playgrounds and 2 parks and reserves in Flora Hill, in the Greater Bendigo area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Flora Hill is Bushland Preserve, covering about 5 hectares. Wolstencroft Reserve is the next largest. Playgrounds around Flora Hill include Wolstencroft Playground, Somerville Street Playspace and the playground at Spring Gully Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Flora Hill. The Back Creek Trail and Spring Creek Trail all pass through Flora Hill. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Flora Hill for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Flora Hill. The closest to Flora Hill is Bendigo Railway Station (V/Line), about 2.2 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the 61, 62, 63 and 65 routes stop in Flora Hill. V/Line coaches stop in Flora Hill. By car from Flora Hill, Bendigo is about 7 minutes away, Castlemaine about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Flora Hill is Brennan Park Swimming Pool in Kennington, about 3 minutes' drive. For a library, Bendigo Library in Bendigo is about 6 minutes' drive from Flora Hill. The closest skate park to Flora Hill is Ewing Park Skating in Bendigo, about 5 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 7 minutes' drive from Flora Hill. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 6 minutes' drive from Flora Hill. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 8 minutes' drive from Flora Hill. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Flora Hill, about 7 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Flora Hill's 4 local playgrounds. Free days on the Back Creek Trail and in Flora Hill's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Bendigo, next to Flora Hill, has 4 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Kennington, next to Flora Hill, has 7 playgrounds and a pool. Strathfieldsaye, next to Flora Hill, has 6 playgrounds. Next door to Flora Hill, Spring Gully has 1 playground. Quarry Hill also borders Flora Hill.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Flora Hill, without traffic.
