Bushland Preserve

Park in Flora Hill · Basic info

Bushland Preserve is a park in Flora Hill, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, good for all ages. La Trobe University Bendigo/Keck St (bus) is a 9-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 767 m away.
