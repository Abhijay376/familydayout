Poolaijelo in the West Wimmera area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Poolaijelo is Meereek State Forest, covering about 4,018 hectares. Mageppa Bushland Reserve and Beniagh Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting) are the next largest. Poolaijelo also has Langkoop Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Poolaijelo. The closest to Poolaijelo is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 178 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Poolaijelo, so most trips start by car. By car from Poolaijelo, Hamilton is about 2 hours away and Horsham about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Poolaijelo, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at War Memorial Park in Apsley (33 min), the playground at Lions Park in Edenhope (46 min), Dachshund Museum & Gallery in Edenhope (46 min) and West Wimmera Community Association - Red Tail Gallery in Edenhope (47 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Poolaijelo.
