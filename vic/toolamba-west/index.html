Toolamba West is in the Greater Shepparton area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Toolamba West, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Toolamba West. The closest to Toolamba West is Mooroopna Railway Station (V/Line), about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Toolamba West, so most trips start by car. By car from Toolamba West, Shepparton is about 20 minutes away, Seymour about 59 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Tatura Swimming complex in Tatura is about 10 minutes' drive from Toolamba West. The closest library to Toolamba West is Tatura Library in Tatura, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Toolamba West is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 22 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Tatura Memorial Park in Tatura is about 10 minutes' drive from Toolamba West. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 21 minutes' drive from Toolamba West. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 20 minutes' drive from Toolamba West. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Toolamba West is Kidstown in Mooroopna, about 18 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 20 minutes from Toolamba West by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Toolamba West, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Hopkins Street Park in Tatura (9 min), the playground at Lions Park in Tatura (11 min), Tatura Library in Tatura (9 min), Tatura Irrigation and Wartime Camps Museum in Tatura (9 min), Tatura Swimming complex in Tatura (10 min) and the skate park at Tatura Memorial Park in Tatura (10 min).
