Chifley Park

Park in Bonshaw · Basic info

Chifley Park is a park in Bonshaw, in the Ballarat City Council area, good for all ages. Oscar Dr/Vickers St (bus) is a 9-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1541 m away.
