Mead is in the Gannawarra area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Mead, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mead. The closest to Mead is Kerang Railway Station (V/Line), about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mead, so most trips start by car. By car from Mead, Echuca is about 66 minutes away and Swan Hill about 72 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Cohuna Pool in Cohuna is about 16 minutes' drive from Mead. For a library, Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang is about 25 minutes' drive from Mead. The closest skate park to Mead is Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Mead are Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna (15 min), the playground at C L King Memorial Park in Cohuna (16 min), the playground at Cohuna Apex Park in Cohuna (15 min), Cohuna Pool in Cohuna (16 min), Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (25 min) and Swimming Pool in Kerang (25 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mead.

Nearby suburbs

Gannawarra and Cullen also border Mead.
