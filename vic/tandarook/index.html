Tandarook is in the Corangamite area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Tandarook, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Tandarook has no train station; Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tandarook, so most trips start by car. By car from Tandarook, Colac is about 35 minutes away, Warrnambool about 60 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tandarook is Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon, about 37 minutes' drive. The closest library to Tandarook is Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac, about 37 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown is about 20 minutes' drive from Tandarook. For a cinema, Colac Cinemas in Colac is about 35 minutes' drive from Tandarook. The closest indoor play centre to Tandarook is Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac, about 36 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 35 minutes from Tandarook by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Tandarook, the nearest places for kids by road are Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (20 min), the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve in Camperdown (22 min), the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (34 min), the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (37 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (37 min) and Colac Cinemas in Colac (35 min).
