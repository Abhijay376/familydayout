Landmarks

Playgrounds around Newham include the playground at Hanging Rock Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Newham. The Base walk passes through Newham.

Getting around

Newham has no train station; Woodend Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 7.9 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Lancefield - Kyneton via Newham & Carlsruhe route stop in Newham. By car from Newham, Kyneton is about 21 minutes away, Castlemaine about 47 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 66 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Newham is Gisborne Aquatic Centre in Gisborne, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest library to Newham is Kyneton Library in Kyneton, about 21 minutes' drive. For a beach, Canoeing in Tylden is about 27 minutes' drive from Newham. For a skate park, Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton is about 21 minutes' drive from Newham. The closest cinema to Newham is Reading Cinema in Sunbury, about 34 minutes' drive. Kyneton is the closest bigger centre to Newham, about 21 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds and a library. From Newham, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Stanley Park in Mount Macedon (19 min), Kyneton Library in Kyneton (21 min), Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton (21 min), Kyneton Museum in Kyneton (21 min), Romsey Library in Romsey (26 min) and Romsey Skate Park in Romsey (26 min).

Best for

Free days on the Base walk and in Newham's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Newham, Woodend has 1 playground. Lancefield, Hesket, Cobaw and Carlsruhe also border Newham.
