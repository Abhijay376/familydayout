Patho is in the Campaspe area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Patho is Patho Plains Railway Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 92 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Patho include Gunbower Creek - Patho Camping Area 1 (CLWF). For camping, there's Gunbower Creek - Patho Camping Area 1 (CLWF).

Getting around

Patho has no train station; Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Patho, so most trips start by car. By car from Patho, Echuca is about 28 minutes away and Bendigo about 88 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Patho is Leitchville Memorial Swimming Pool in Leitchville, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest library to Patho is Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca, about 26 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna is about 24 minutes' drive from Patho. The closest cinema to Patho is Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca, about 27 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 28 minutes from Patho by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Patho, the nearest places for kids by road are Leitchville Memorial Swimming Pool in Leitchville (13 min), Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna (24 min), the playground at Cohuna Apex Park in Cohuna (24 min), Cohuna Pool in Cohuna (25 min), the playground at C L King Memorial Park in Cohuna (25 min) and Holden Museum in Echuca (25 min).
