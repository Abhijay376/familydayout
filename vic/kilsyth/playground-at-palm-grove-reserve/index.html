Playground at Palm Grove Reserve

Playground in Kilsyth · Basic info

Playground at Palm Grove Reserve in Kilsyth is a public playground in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. The Liverpool Road Connection is about 240 m away, and for food, Bianco & Black is about 430 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 850 m from the playground, and bus 690 stops at Kilsyth Ave/Liverpool Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
