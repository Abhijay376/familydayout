Eastfield BMX Club

Skate, BMX or pump track in Kilsyth · Basic info

Eastfield BMX Club is a skate, bmx or pump track in Kilsyth, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Southfork Dr/Canterbury Rd (bus) is a 19-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1908 m away.
