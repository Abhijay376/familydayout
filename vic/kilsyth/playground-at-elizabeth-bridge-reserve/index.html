Playground at Elizabeth Bridge Reserve

Playground in Kilsyth · Basic info

Playground at Elizabeth Bridge Reserve is a public playground in Kilsyth, in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. The Orchard Walk passes about 10 m away, and Just Cruize in Diner is about 450 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and buses 688 and 689 stop at Hansen Park/Durham Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
