Playground on Twobar Place

Playground in Kilsyth · Basic info

Playground on Twobar Place in Kilsyth is a public playground in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. Playground at Hansford Reserve is another option about 370 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 260 m from the playground, and bus 690 stops at Kilsyth Sports Centre/Liverpool Rd, a 7-minute walk away.
