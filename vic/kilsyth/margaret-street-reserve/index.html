Margaret Street Reserve

Park in Kilsyth · Basic info

Margaret Street Reserve is a park in Kilsyth, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Amelia Ct/Hawthory Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 479 m away.
