Pembroke Secondary Reserve

Park in Kilsyth · Basic info

Pembroke Secondary Reserve is a park in Kilsyth, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Elizabeth Bridge Reserve/Durham Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 444 m away.
