Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Wildwood in the Hume area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wildwood. The closest to Wildwood is Sunbury Railway Station (metro), about 5.3 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wildwood, so most trips start by car. By car from Wildwood, Broadmeadows is about 25 minutes away, Sunshine about 32 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 41 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Wildwood is Sunbury Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Sunbury, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest library to Wildwood is Sunbury Library in Sunbury, about 12 minutes' drive. For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 44 minutes' drive from Wildwood. The closest skate park to Wildwood is Sunbury Bmx Club in Sunbury, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Wildwood is Reading Cinema in Sunbury, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Wildwood is Timezone in Craigieburn, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Wildwood is Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville, about 36 minutes' drive. Broadmeadows is the closest bigger centre to Wildwood, about 25 minutes away by car, and has 12 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Wildwood, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Turnberry Drive Reserve in Sunbury (7 min), the playground at Tulsa Drive Reserve in Sunbury (8 min), Sunbury Library in Sunbury (12 min), Sunbury Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Sunbury (12 min), Reading Cinema in Sunbury (12 min) and Sunbury Bmx Club in Sunbury (12 min).
