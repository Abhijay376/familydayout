Trawool is in the Mitchell area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Horseshoe Lagoon Flora and Fauna Reserve is the largest mapped park in Trawool, at about 46 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Trawool. The Great Victorian Rail Trail passes through Trawool.

Getting around

Trawool has no train station; Seymour Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Trawool. By car from Trawool, Seymour is about 10 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 68 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 86 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Seymour Swimming Centre in Seymour is about 11 minutes' drive from Trawool. For a library, Seymour Library in Seymour is about 9 minutes' drive from Trawool. The closest skate park to Trawool is Seymour Skate Park in Seymour, about 9 minutes' drive. Seymour is the closest bigger centre to Trawool, about 10 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Trawool, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at M O'Sullivan Reserve in Seymour (8 min), the playground at O'Shea Park in Seymour (9 min), Seymour Skate Park in Seymour (9 min), Seymour Library in Seymour (9 min), Seymour Swimming Centre in Seymour (11 min) and Memorial Swimming pool in Seymour (12 min).

Best for

Free days on the Great Victorian Rail Trail and in Trawool's parks.
