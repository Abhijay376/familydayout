Our data lists 6 parks and reserves in Nurrabiel, in the Horsham area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Nurrabiel is Connangorach Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 171 hectares. Connangorach I45 Bushland Reserve and Darragan Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting) are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Nurrabiel include Red Rock Bushland Reserve, Mount Talbot Scenic Reserve and White Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting).

Getting around

There's no train station in Nurrabiel. The closest to Nurrabiel is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 90 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nurrabiel, so most trips start by car. By car from Nurrabiel, Horsham is about 27 minutes away and Stawell about 67 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham is about 25 minutes' drive from Nurrabiel. The closest skate park to Nurrabiel is the skate park at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Nurrabiel is Centre Cinemas in Horsham, about 26 minutes' drive. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to Nurrabiel, about 27 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Nurrabiel, the nearest places for kids by road are Arapiles Climbing Museum in Natimuk (30 min), the playground at Uebergang Park in Horsham (25 min), the playground at Natimuk Showgrounds Recreation Reserve in Natimuk (31 min), the skate park at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk (30 min), The Goat Gallery Natimuk in Natimuk (31 min) and Velodrome in Horsham (25 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Nurrabiel.

Nearby suburbs

Telangatuk East, next to Nurrabiel, has 1 playground. Mockinya, Lower Norton, Clear Lake and Brimpaen also border Nurrabiel.
