Landmarks

Port Curtis Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Norval, at about 4 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Norval. The closest to Norval is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 8.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Norval, so most trips start by car. By car from Norval, Ararat is about 20 minutes away, Stawell about 38 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Norval is Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat, about 20 minutes' drive. For a library, Ararat Library in Ararat is about 20 minutes' drive from Norval. For a skate park, David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat is about 24 minutes' drive from Norval. For a cinema, Astor Cinema in Ararat is about 20 minutes' drive from Norval. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Norval is Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell, about 34 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Ararat, about 20 minutes from Norval by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Norval, the nearest places for kids by road are Ararat Gallery TAMA in Ararat (20 min), Ararat Library in Ararat (20 min), Astor Cinema in Ararat (20 min), Gum San Chinese Heritage Museum in Ararat (20 min), Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat (20 min) and Ararat Outdoor Pool in Ararat (23 min).
