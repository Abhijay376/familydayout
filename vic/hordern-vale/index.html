Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Hordern Vale in the Colac Otway area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it. Hordern Vale does have 2 beaches.

Landmarks

Our data shows 2 public toilets in Hordern Vale. Places to visit in Hordern Vale include Glenaire East and Station Beach. For walks and rides, Great Ocean Walk and Great Ocean Walk Alt Beach Route cross Hordern Vale.

Getting around

Hordern Vale has no train station; Colac Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 48 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Hordern Vale, so most trips start by car. By car from Hordern Vale, Colac is about 75 minutes away and Warrnambool about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Apollo Bay Community Pool in Apollo Bay is about 25 minutes' drive from Hordern Vale. For a cinema, Mechanics Hall in Apollo Bay is about 22 minutes' drive from Hordern Vale. For a zoo or wildlife park, Wildlife Wonders in Apollo Bay is about 17 minutes' drive from Hordern Vale. The closest places for kids by road from Hordern Vale are Wildlife Wonders in Apollo Bay (17 min), Mechanics Hall in Apollo Bay (22 min), the playground at Foreshore Reserve in Apollo Bay (23 min), Skate Bowl in Apollo Bay (23 min), Apollo Bay Community Pool in Apollo Bay (25 min) and Old Cable Station Museum in Apollo Bay (26 min).

Best for

Beach days at Glenaire East and Station Beach. Longer rides and walks from Hordern Vale on the Great Ocean Walk.
