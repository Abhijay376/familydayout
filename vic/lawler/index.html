Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Lawler in the Buloke area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lawler. The closest to Lawler is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 102 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lawler, so most trips start by car. By car from Lawler, Horsham is about 58 minutes away and Stawell about 81 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Minyip Swimming Pool in Minyip is about 18 minutes' drive from Lawler. For a skate park, Donald BMX Track in Donald is about 24 minutes' drive from Lawler. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to Lawler, about 58 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Lawler are the playground at Minyip Wetlands in Minyip (18 min), Minyip Swimming Pool in Minyip (18 min), Lions Playground in Minyip (18 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (22 min), Donald BMX Track in Donald (24 min) and Woods' Farming and Heritage Museum in Rupanyup (36 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lawler.

Nearby suburbs

Minyip, next to Lawler, has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Boolite also borders Lawler.
