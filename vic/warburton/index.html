Warburton in the Yarra Ranges area has 3 playgrounds and 5 parks and reserves in our data. Beyond the parks, Warburton has a pool.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Warburton is Yarra State Forest, covering about 25,082 hectares. Watts Creek Reference Area and Warburton Recreation Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Warburton include Story Restaurant and Warburton Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds in Warburton include Warburton Main Street Playground, the playground at Yarra River Heritage River and the playground at Warburton Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 10 public toilets in Warburton. Places to visit in Warburton include Warburton Water World. The Powelltown Tramways Railtrails, Lilydale-Warburton Rail Trail and Warburton Rail Trail all pass through Warburton. Our data lists 5 cafes and family restaurants in Warburton for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Warburton has no train station; Garfield Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 37 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 683 stops in Warburton. By car from Warburton, Healesville is about 33 minutes away, Lilydale about 41 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 82 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Street Library in Millgrove is about 8 minutes' drive from Warburton. For a skate park, the skate park at Yarra Junction Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Yarra Junction is about 15 minutes' drive from Warburton. The closest cinema to Warburton is The Memo, Healesville in Healesville, about 33 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, GAMR Laser Tag & VR in Chirnside Park is about 45 minutes' drive from Warburton. For a zoo or wildlife park, Lyre Bird enclosure in Badger Creek is about 29 minutes' drive from Warburton. Healesville is the closest bigger centre to Warburton, about 33 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Warburton's 3 local playgrounds. Free days on the Powelltown Tramways Railtrails and in Warburton's parks.

Next door to Warburton, East Warburton has 1 playground. Wesburn, Three Bridges, Fernshaw and Big Pats Creek also border Warburton.
