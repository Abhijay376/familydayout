Story Restaurant

Park in Warburton · Basic info

Story Restaurant is a park in Warburton, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Alpine Retreat Hotel/Warburton Hwy (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 228 m away.
