Warburton Main Street Playground

Playground in Warburton · Basic info

Warburton Main Street Playground in Warburton is a public playground in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. The Yarra River Walk passes about 70 m away, and All Trancio is about 40 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 70 m away, and bus 683 stops at 3403 Warburton Hwy, a 2-minute walk away.
