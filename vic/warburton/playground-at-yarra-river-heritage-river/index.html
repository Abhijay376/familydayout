Playground at Yarra River Heritage River

Playground in Warburton · Basic info

Playground at Yarra River Heritage River is a public playground in Warburton, in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. The PBN is about 40 m away, and Warburton Water World is about 30 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 110 m from the playground, and bus 683 stops at Yarra View Ret Village/Warburton Hwy, a 5-minute walk away.
