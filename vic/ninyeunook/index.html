Ninyeunook is in the Gannawarra area, and our data lists 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Mosquito Creek Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Ninyeunook, at about 180 hectares. Towaninny Nature Conservation Reserve and Ninyeunook - Lalbert Creek Nature Conservation Reserve are the next largest. Ninyeunook also has Ninyeunook Township Nature Conservation Reserve.

Getting around

Ninyeunook has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 56 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Ninyeunook, so most trips start by car. By car from Ninyeunook, Swan Hill is about 1.5 hours away and Bendigo about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Ninyeunook is Quambatook Memorial Pool in Quambatook, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest library to Ninyeunook is Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook, about 24 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 38 minutes' drive from Ninyeunook. The closest places for kids by road from Ninyeunook are Quambatook Lions Club Playground in Quambatook (24 min), Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook (24 min), Quambatook Historical Centre in Quambatook (24 min), Quambatook Memorial Pool in Quambatook (24 min), the playground at Centenary Park in Wycheproof (26 min) and Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Ninyeunook.

Nearby suburbs

Quambatook, next to Ninyeunook, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Jeruk, Bunguluke and Barraport West also border Ninyeunook.
