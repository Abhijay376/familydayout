Milawa is in the Wangaratta area, and our data lists 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Milawa is Tarrawingee Streamside Reserve, covering about 25 hectares. Milawa also has John McAleese Community Park. Playgrounds in Milawa include the playground at John McAleese Community Park. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Milawa. The Vines 2 Vines: Whitfield to Milawa, Murray to Mountains Rail Trail: Wangaratta – Milawa and Milawa Trail all pass through Milawa. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Milawa for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Milawa. The closest to Milawa is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Milawa. By car from Milawa, Wangaratta is about 17 minutes away and Benalla about 38 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Splashpark in Wangaratta is about 15 minutes' drive from Milawa. For a library, Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta is about 16 minutes' drive from Milawa. For a beach, Sandy Beach in Beechworth is about 31 minutes' drive from Milawa. The closest skate park to Milawa is the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta, about 15 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 14 minutes' drive from Milawa. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 17 minutes from Milawa by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Milawa, the nearest places for kids by road are Splashpark in Wangaratta (15 min), the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta (15 min), Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta (14 min), the playground at Apex Park in Wangaratta (15 min), Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (16 min) and Moore than Swimming in Wangaratta (16 min).

Best for

Free days on the Vines 2 Vines: Whitfield to Milawa and in Milawa's parks.

Markwood, Docker and Bobinawarrah also border Milawa.
