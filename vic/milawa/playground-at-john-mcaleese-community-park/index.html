Playground at John McAleese Community Park

Playground in Milawa · Basic info

Playground at John McAleese Community Park is a public playground in Milawa, in the Wangaratta Rural City area. The Milawa Trail is about 150 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 130 m from the playground, and coaches stop at Milawa-Bobinawarrah Rd/Snow Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
