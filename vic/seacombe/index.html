Seacombe is in the Wellington area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Seacombe, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Seacombe.

Getting around

There's no train station in Seacombe. The closest to Seacombe is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 35 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Seacombe, so most trips start by car. By car from Seacombe, Sale is about 54 minutes away and Traralgon about 89 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest beach to Seacombe is Golden Beach in Golden Beach, about 29 minutes' drive. Sale is the closest bigger centre to Seacombe, about 54 minutes away by car, and has 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Seacombe, the nearest places for kids by road are Golden Beach Community Centre Playground in Golden Beach (25 min), Lions Park Playground in Loch Sport (25 min), Golden Beach in Golden Beach (29 min), Flamingo Beach in Flamingo Beach (40 min), Historic Gunpowder Magazine in Sale (52 min) and Sale Historical Museum in Sale (52 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Seacombe.

Nearby suburbs

Loch Sport, next to Seacombe, has 1 playground. Hollands Landing, Goon Nure and Dutson Downs also border Seacombe.
