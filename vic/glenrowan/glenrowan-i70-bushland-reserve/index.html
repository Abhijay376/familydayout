Glenrowan I70 Bushland Reserve

Park in Glenrowan · Basic info

Glenrowan I70 Bushland Reserve is a park in Glenrowan, in the Wangaratta Rural City Council area, good for all ages. Ned Kelly Statue/Kate St (bus) is a 31-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 2742 m away with a baby change table.
