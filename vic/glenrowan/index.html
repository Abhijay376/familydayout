Our data lists 1 playground and 4 parks and reserves in Glenrowan, in the Wangaratta area. On top of that, Glenrowan has 4 museums or galleries.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Glenrowan is Glenrowan I70 Bushland Reserve, covering about 16 hectares. Glenrowan Spring Creek Reserve and Fosters Lake Waterhole Nature Conservation Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Glenrowan include Glenrowan I69 Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Glenrowan. Places to visit in Glenrowan include Cobb & Co and Glenrowan Art Gallery.

Getting around

Glenrowan has no train station; Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus H46 stops in Glenrowan. By car from Glenrowan, Wangaratta is about 16 minutes away, Benalla about 23 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Glenrowan is Moore than Swimming in Wangaratta, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest library to Glenrowan is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 18 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta is about 16 minutes' drive from Glenrowan. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 18 minutes' drive from Glenrowan. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 16 minutes from Glenrowan by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Glenrowan are Moore than Swimming in Wangaratta (14 min), the playground at Dodsworth Street Reserve in Wangaratta (16 min), Splashpark in Wangaratta (15 min), the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta (16 min), Brien Crescent Playground in Wangaratta (17 min) and Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (18 min).
