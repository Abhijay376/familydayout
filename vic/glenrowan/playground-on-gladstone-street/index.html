Playground on Gladstone Street

Playground in Glenrowan · Basic info

Playground on Gladstone Street is a public playground in Glenrowan, in the Wangaratta Rural City area. Cobb & Co is about 200 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus H46 stops at Ned Kelly Statue/Kate St, a 3-minute walk away.
