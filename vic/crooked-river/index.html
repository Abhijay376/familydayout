Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Crooked River in the Wellington area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Crooked River. For walks and rides, McMillans Walking Track and Moroka Junction Track cross Crooked River.

Getting around

There's no train station in Crooked River. The closest to Crooked River is Stratford Railway Station (V/Line), about 63 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Crooked River, so most trips start by car. By car from Crooked River, Bairnsdale is about 1.5 hours away and Sale about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest places for kids by road from Crooked River are Dargo Museum in Dargo (46 min) and Dargo Public Hall Playground in Dargo (46 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Crooked River on the McMillans Walking Track.
