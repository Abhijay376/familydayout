Dandenong Valley Parklands

Park in Scoresby · Official details

Dandenong Valley Parklands is a park in Scoresby, in the Knox City Council area, good for all ages. Strickland Dr/Jells Rd (bus) is a 25-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 943 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed on a lead.
