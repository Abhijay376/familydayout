Playground at Boyd Court Reserve

Playground in Scoresby · Basic info

Playground at Boyd Court Reserve is a public playground in Scoresby, in the Knox City area. The Eastern Suburbs Crit Circuit passes about 130 m away, and Playground at Lindsay Reserve is another option about 190 m away. There's a public toilet about 2.5 km away, and buses 737 and 757 stop at Redcourt Reserve/Taunton Cres, a 4-minute walk away.
