Playground at MacLean Reserve

Playground in Williamstown · Basic info

Playground at MacLean Reserve in Williamstown is a public playground in the Hobsons Bay City area. The Hobsons Bay Coastal Trail passes about 170 m away, and HMAS Castlemaine is about 450 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 550 m away, and bus 471 stops at Cole St/Nelson Pl, a 2-minute walk away.
