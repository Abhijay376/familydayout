Dennis Reserve

Park in Williamstown · Basic info

Dennis Reserve is a park in Williamstown, in the Hobsons Bay City Council area, good for all ages. Melbourne Rd/Ferguson St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 568 m away.
