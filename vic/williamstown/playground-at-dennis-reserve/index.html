Playground at Dennis Reserve

Playground in Williamstown · Basic info

Playground at Dennis Reserve in Williamstown is a public playground in the Hobsons Bay City area. Williamstown Library is about 290 m away, and for food, Hanoi Pho is about 260 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 510 m from the playground, and bus 415 stops at Melbourne Rd/Ferguson St, a 5-minute walk away.
