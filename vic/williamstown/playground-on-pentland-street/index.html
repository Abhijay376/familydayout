Playground on Pentland Street

Playground in Williamstown · Basic info

Playground on Pentland Street is a public playground in Williamstown, in the Hobsons Bay City area. Williamstown Library is about 560 m away, and Newport Railway Museum is about 440 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 330 m from the playground, and bus 472 stops at Williamstown North PS/Melbourne Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
