E. W. Jackson Playground

Playground in Williamstown · Basic info

E. W. Jackson Playground is a public playground in Williamstown, in the Hobsons Bay City area. Newport Railway Museum is about 570 m away, and Jackies Pizza is about 280 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 390 m away, and bus 472 stops at Jackson Reserve/Melbourne Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
