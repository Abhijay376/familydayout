Kennet River Coastal Reserve

Park in Kennett River · Basic info

Kennet River Coastal Reserve is a park in Kennett River, in the Colac Otway Shire Council area, good for all ages. Grey River Rd/Hawdon Ave (coach) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 621 m away.
