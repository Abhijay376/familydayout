Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lorne is Lily Pond Bushland Reserve, covering about 3 hectares. Lions Park is the next largest. Lorne also has Queens Park. Playgrounds around Lorne include the playground at Lorne - Queenscliff Coastal Reserve and the playground at Lorne - Queenscliff Coastal Reserve. Our data shows 20 public toilets in Lorne. Places to visit in Lorne include Live Wire Park, Qdos, Lorne Theatre, Lorne Sea Baths and Lorne Beach. For older kids in Lorne there's Kay Langan Skate Park. The Sheoak Falls Track, Lemonade Creek Track and Cumberland River Trail all pass through Lorne. Our data lists 8 cafes and family restaurants in Lorne for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Lorne has no train station; Birregurra Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Colac - Lorne route stop in Lorne. V/Line coaches stop in Lorne. By car from Lorne, Torquay is about 53 minutes away, Colac about 55 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.
