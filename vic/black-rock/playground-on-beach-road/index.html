Playground on Beach Road

Playground in Black Rock · Basic info

Playground on Beach Road in Black Rock is a public playground in the Bayside City area. The Bay Trail passes about 40 m away, and The Colonel's Son is about 70 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 150 m away, and buses 600, 922 and 923 stop at Balcombe Rd/Ebden Ave, a 4-minute walk away.
