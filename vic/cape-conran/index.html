Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Cape Conran in the East Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it. Cape Conran does have a beach.

Landmarks

Our data shows 14 public toilets in Cape Conran. Places to visit in Cape Conran include Sailors Grave Beach. The Cape Conran Nature Trail passes through Cape Conran.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cape Conran. The closest to Cape Conran is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 97 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cape Conran, so most trips start by car. By car from Cape Conran, Lakes Entrance is about 76 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cape Conran is Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost, about 30 minutes' drive. For a library, Orbost Library in Orbost is about 29 minutes' drive from Cape Conran. From Cape Conran, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Forest Park in Orbost (29 min), Orbost Library in Orbost (29 min), Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost (30 min), the playground at Luderick Point Memorial Park in Bemm River (35 min) and Ewings Marsh in Waygara (48 min).

Best for

Beach days at Sailors Grave Beach. Longer rides and walks from Cape Conran on the Cape Conran Nature Trail.
