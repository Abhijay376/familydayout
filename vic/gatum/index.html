Gatum is in the Southern Grampians area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Wookurkook Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Gatum, at about 10 hectares. Our data shows 1 off-leash dog park in Gatum.

Getting around

Gatum has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 90 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gatum, so most trips start by car. By car from Gatum, Hamilton is about 41 minutes away and Horsham about 83 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral is about 23 minutes' drive from Gatum. For a library, Hamilton Library in Hamilton is about 41 minutes' drive from Gatum. The closest skate park to Gatum is hamilton skate park in Hamilton, about 43 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton is about 41 minutes' drive from Gatum. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 41 minutes from Gatum by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Gatum, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Cavendish Town Square Memorial Park in Cavendish (20 min), Glendinning Street Playground in Balmoral (23 min), Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral (23 min), coleraine skate park in Coleraine (51 min), Big Woolbales & Campe Motor Museum in Hamilton (38 min) and Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Hamilton (41 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Gatum.

Nearby suburbs

Cavendish, next to Gatum, has 1 playground. Melville Forest and Gringegalgona also border Gatum.
