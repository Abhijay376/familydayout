Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Laharum, in the Horsham area.

Landmarks

Lah-arum Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Laharum, at about 6 hectares. Our data shows 6 public toilets in Laharum. For walks and rides, Grampians Peaks Trail, Mount Stapylton Loop Walk and Mt Zero Walk cross Laharum.

Getting around

Laharum has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 66 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Laharum, so most trips start by car. By car from Laharum, Horsham is about 27 minutes away and Stawell about 47 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Laharum is Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Laharum is Velodrome in Horsham, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Laharum is Centre Cinemas in Horsham, about 27 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Halls Gap Zoo in Halls Gap is about 39 minutes' drive from Laharum. The nearest larger centre is Horsham, about 27 minutes from Laharum by car, with 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Laharum, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Uebergang Park in Horsham (26 min), Velodrome in Horsham (26 min), Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham (26 min), Horsham Water Play in Horsham (27 min), Centre Cinemas in Horsham (27 min) and Horsham Skate Park in Horsham (28 min).

Best for

Free days on the Grampians Peaks Trail and in Laharum's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Drung, Dadswells Bridge, Bungalally and Brimpaen also border Laharum.
