Old Chinese Market Gardens

Park in Bridgewater · Basic info

Old Chinese Market Gardens is a park in Bridgewater, in the Loddon Shire Council area, good for all ages. Bridgewater/Calder Hwy (bus) is a 17-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1427 m away with a baby change table.
