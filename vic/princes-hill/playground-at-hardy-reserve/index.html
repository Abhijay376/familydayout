Playground at Hardy Reserve

Playground in Princes Hill · Basic info

Playground at Hardy Reserve in Princes Hill is a public playground in the Yarra City area. Carlton Library is about 550 m away, and Lucky's Pizzeria is about 480 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 540 m away, and trams 1 and 6 stop at Richardson St/Lygon St #117, a 3-minute walk away.
