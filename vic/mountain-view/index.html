Mountain View is in the Baw Baw area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Mountain View, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Mountain View has no train station; Warragul Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mountain View, so most trips start by car. By car from Mountain View, Warragul is about 33 minutes away, Leongatha about 43 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mountain View is Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest library to Mountain View is Poowong library in Poowong, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Mountain View is Drouin Skate Park in Drouin, about 28 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 32 minutes' drive from Mountain View. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 32 minutes' drive from Mountain View. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Mountain View is Gumbuya World in Tynong North, about 45 minutes' drive. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Mountain View, about 33 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Mountain View, the nearest places for kids by road are Poowong library in Poowong (21 min), Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong (21 min), the playground at Sunnyside Park in Loch (27 min), the playground at Coleman Park in Korumburra (30 min), Korumburra Outdoor Pool in Korumburra (30 min) and Drouin Skate Park in Drouin (28 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mountain View.

Nearby suburbs

Hallora, next to Mountain View, has 1 playground. Poowong East and Ferndale also border Mountain View.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Mountain View, without traffic.
