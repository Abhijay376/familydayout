Barwon Valley Park

Park in Belmont · Basic info

Barwon Valley Park is a park in Belmont, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Morris St/Mt Pleasant Rd (bus) is a 17-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 292 m away.
