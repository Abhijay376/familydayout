Playground at Glenbrae Reserve

Playground in Belmont · Basic info

Playground at Glenbrae Reserve in Belmont is a public playground in the Greater Geelong City area. The Waurn Ponds Trail is about 280 m away, and for food, Unorthodox Kitchen & Grill is about 400 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 370 m from the playground, and buses 40, 41 and 53 stop at Oberon Dr/Torquay Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
