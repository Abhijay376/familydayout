Barwon Valley Fun Park

Playground in Belmont · Basic info

Barwon Valley Fun Park in Belmont is a public playground in the Greater Geelong City area. The Barwon River Trail passes about 110 m away, and Playground at Barwon Valley Park is another option about 40 m away. There's a public toilet about 80 m away. It's easiest by car, about 3 minutes from Belmont.
