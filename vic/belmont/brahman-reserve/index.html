Brahman Reserve

Playground in Belmont · Basic info

This is a public playground in Belmont. The Waurn Ponds Trail is about 250 m away, and Leisurelink Restaurant is about 560 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.7 km from the playground, and bus 1 stops at Sirovilla Retirement Village/Princes Hwy, a 8-minute walk away.
