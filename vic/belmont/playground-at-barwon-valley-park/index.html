Playground at Barwon Valley Park

Playground in Belmont · Basic info

Playground at Barwon Valley Park is a public playground in Belmont, in the Greater Geelong City area. The Barwon River Trail passes about 80 m away, and Barwon Valley Fun Park is another option about 40 m away. There's a public toilet about 70 m away. It's easiest by car, about 4 minutes from Belmont.
