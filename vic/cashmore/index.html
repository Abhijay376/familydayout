Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Cashmore, in the Glenelg area.

Landmarks

Trewalla H49 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Cashmore, at about 9 hectares. Cashmore Recreation Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cashmore. The closest to Cashmore is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 86 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cashmore, so most trips start by car. By car from Cashmore, Portland is about 16 minutes away and Hamilton about 77 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Portland Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Portland is about 18 minutes' drive from Cashmore. For a library, Heywood Library in Heywood is about 31 minutes' drive from Cashmore. For a beach, Whalers Point in Portland is about 16 minutes' drive from Cashmore. Portland is the closest bigger centre to Cashmore, about 16 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Cashmore are the playground at Mitchell Park in Portland (15 min), Whalers Point in Portland (16 min), Powerhouse Motor Car Museum in Portland (18 min), Cable Tram Depot in Portland (19 min), Portland Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Portland (18 min) and Bridgewater Bay Surf Beach in Cape Bridgewater (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Cashmore.

Nearby suburbs
