Churchill Island Marine National Park

Park in Rhyll · Official details

Churchill Island Marine National Park is a park in Rhyll, in the Bass Coast Shire Council area, good for all ages.

Details

Seasons
Migratory birds overwinter; mangroves set seed in autumn (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
Tides vary up to three metres; keep clear of bird roosting areas; Saltmarsh and mangrove areas closed to public (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Churchill Island Marine National Park?
Churchill Island Marine National Park is in Rhyll, Victoria.
Is Churchill Island Marine National Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
