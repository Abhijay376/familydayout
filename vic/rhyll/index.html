Our data lists 2 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in Rhyll, in the Bass Coast area. Rhyll also has a zoo or wildlife park and a beach.

Landmarks

Churchill Island Marine National Park is the largest mapped park in Rhyll, at about 670 hectares. Unnamed C0293 Private Nature Reserve and Rhyll Public Park are the next largest. Playgrounds around Rhyll include the playground at Rhyll Public Park and the playground at Phillip Island Coastal Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Rhyll. In Rhyll you'll also find Koala Conservation Reserve and Smiths Beach. The Phillip Island Bike Path, Oswin Roberts Circuit and Woodland Loop all pass through Rhyll. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Rhyll for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Rhyll has no train station; Stony Point Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Cowes - Anderson - Wonthaggi and Cowes - Fountain Gate via Anderson routes stop in Rhyll. V/Line coaches stop in Rhyll. By car from Rhyll, Wonthaggi is about 40 minutes away, Leongatha about 70 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Rhyll is Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Wonthaggi, about 39 minutes' drive. For a library, Phillip Island Library in Cowes is about 9 minutes' drive from Rhyll. For a skate park, the skate park at Wonthaggi Railway Historic Reserve in Kilcunda is about 27 minutes' drive from Rhyll. For a cinema, Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi is about 39 minutes' drive from Rhyll. Wonthaggi is the closest bigger centre to Rhyll, about 40 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Beach days at Smiths Beach. Free days on the Phillip Island Bike Path and in Rhyll's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Cowes, next to Rhyll, has 6 playgrounds, a library and a beach. Smiths Beach, next to Rhyll, has 1 playground and a beach. Next door to Rhyll, Surf Beach has 1 playground. Next door to Rhyll, Sunset Strip has 1 playground. Newhaven, Sunderland Bay and Silverleaves also border Rhyll.
