Playground at Phillip Island Coastal Reserve

Playground in Rhyll · Basic info

Playground at Phillip Island Coastal Reserve is a public playground in Rhyll, in the Bass Coast Shire area. Tides of Rhyll is about 140 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 140 m away, and bus Cowes - Anderson - Wonthaggi stops at Lock Rd/Beach Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
