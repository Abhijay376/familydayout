Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Diggora in the Campaspe area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Diggora has no train station; Rochester Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Diggora, so most trips start by car. By car from Diggora, Echuca is about 30 minutes away, Bendigo about 56 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Diggora is Memorial Pool in Rochester, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest library to Diggora is Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca, about 31 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Moama Echuca Community Skate Park in Echuca is about 33 minutes' drive from Diggora. The closest cinema to Diggora is Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca, about 31 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram is about 41 minutes' drive from Diggora. Echuca is the closest bigger centre to Diggora, about 30 minutes away by car, and has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Diggora are the playground at Apex Park in Rochester (9 min), Memorial Pool in Rochester (10 min), Lockington Pool in Lockington (12 min), Campaspe Run Rural Discovery Centre in Elmore (19 min), the playground at Echuca Lions Park in Echuca (29 min) and Holden Museum in Echuca (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Diggora.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Diggora, Rochester has 1 playground and a pool. Lockington, next to Diggora, has a pool. Elmore, next to Diggora, has 1 playground. Hunter and Bamawm also border Diggora.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Diggora, without traffic.
