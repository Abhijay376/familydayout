Playground on Ford Road

Playground in Rokewood · Basic info

Playground on Ford Road in Rokewood is a public playground in the Golden Plains Shire area. The Kuruc-a-ruc trail passes about 380 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 60 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus Ballarat - Rokewood via Ross Creek stops at Health Centre/Ferrars St, a 5-minute walk away.
