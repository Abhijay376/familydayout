Rokewood Common Nature Conservation Reserve

Park in Rokewood · Basic info

Rokewood Common Nature Conservation Reserve is a park in Rokewood, in the Golden Plains Shire Council area, good for all ages. Health Centre/Ferrars St (bus) is a 22-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1971 m away.
