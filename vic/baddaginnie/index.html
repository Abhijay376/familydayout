Baddaginnie in the Benalla area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Baddaginnie Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Baddaginnie, at about 16 hectares. Baddaginnie I63 Bushland Reserve and Poison Creek Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Baddaginnie include Baddaginnie Natural Features Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Baddaginnie.

Getting around

There's no train station in Baddaginnie. The closest to Baddaginnie is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Baddaginnie, so most trips start by car. By car from Baddaginnie, Benalla is about 12 minutes away, Wangaratta about 43 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla is about 11 minutes' drive from Baddaginnie. The closest library to Baddaginnie is Euroa Library in Euroa, about 28 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Pump Track in Benalla is about 13 minutes' drive from Baddaginnie. For a cinema, Swanpool Cinema in Swanpool is about 25 minutes' drive from Baddaginnie. The nearest larger centre is Benalla, about 12 minutes from Baddaginnie by car, with 5 playgrounds and a pool. From Baddaginnie, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Waminda Park in Benalla (9 min), The Rocket Playground in Benalla (11 min), Benalla Art Gallery in Benalla (11 min), Benalla Costume and Pioneer Museum in Benalla (11 min), Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (11 min) and Pump Track in Benalla (13 min).

Nearby suburbs
