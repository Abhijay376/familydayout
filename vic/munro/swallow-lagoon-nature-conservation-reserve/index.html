Swallow Lagoon Nature Conservation Reserve

Park in Munro · Official details

Swallow Lagoon Nature Conservation Reserve is a park in Munro, in the Wellington Shire Council area, good for all ages. Dogs are not allowed.

Details

Dogs
not allowed (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
One picnic table; no bins, take rubbish home; No fires, motorbikes or horses; Bring mosquito repellent in wet weather (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Swallow Lagoon Nature Conservation Reserve?
Swallow Lagoon Nature Conservation Reserve is in Munro, Victoria.

Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.

No, dogs are not allowed.
