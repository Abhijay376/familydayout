Munro is in the Wellington area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Munro is Swallow Lagoon Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 193 hectares. Unnamed P0226 Private Nature Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Munro.

Getting around

There's no train station in Munro. The closest to Munro is Stratford Railway Station (V/Line), about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Munro, so most trips start by car. By car from Munro, Sale is about 25 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 35 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Munro is Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford, about 11 minutes' drive. For a library, Sale Library in Sale is about 26 minutes' drive from Munro. For a skate park, Skate Park in Stratford is about 10 minutes' drive from Munro. For a cinema, Former Drive-in site in Sale is about 23 minutes' drive from Munro. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Munro is Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow, about 42 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Sale, about 25 minutes from Munro by car, with 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Munro, the nearest places for kids by road are Skate Park in Stratford (10 min), Market Reserve Playground in Stratford (11 min), Soldiers Memorial Park Playground in Stratford (11 min), Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford (11 min), Former Drive-in site in Sale (23 min) and Sale Historical Museum in Sale (25 min).
