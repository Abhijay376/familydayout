Tonimbuk in the Cardinia area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tonimbuk is Diamond Creek Reference Area, covering about 343 hectares. Tonimbuk State Forest is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tonimbuk. The closest to Tonimbuk is Bunyip Railway Station (V/Line), about 10 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tonimbuk, so most trips start by car. By car from Tonimbuk, Pakenham is about 32 minutes away, Warragul about 33 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 76 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Lazy River in Tynong North is about 18 minutes' drive from Tonimbuk. For a library, Drouin Library in Drouin is about 27 minutes' drive from Tonimbuk. The closest skate park to Tonimbuk is the bmx track at Greenland Court Reserve in Garfield, about 18 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 31 minutes' drive from Tonimbuk. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 31 minutes' drive from Tonimbuk. For a zoo or wildlife park, Wildlife Trail in Tynong North is about 18 minutes' drive from Tonimbuk. Pakenham is the closest bigger centre to Tonimbuk, about 32 minutes away by car, and has 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Tonimbuk are the playground at Bunyip Sanctuary in Bunyip (18 min), the playground at Koolangarra Park in Bunyip (17 min), the bmx track at Greenland Court Reserve in Garfield (18 min), Wildlife Trail in Tynong North (18 min), Lazy River in Tynong North (18 min) and Rock Pool in Tynong North (18 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tonimbuk.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Tonimbuk, Tynong North has 1 playground, a pool and a zoo or wildlife park. Next door to Tonimbuk, Gembrook has 6 playgrounds and a skate park. Bunyip North also borders Tonimbuk.
