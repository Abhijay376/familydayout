Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Boole Poole in the East Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it. Boole Poole does have a beach.

Landmarks

Our data shows 2 public toilets in Boole Poole. Places to visit in Boole Poole include 90 Mile Beach.

Getting around

Boole Poole has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Boole Poole, so most trips start by car. By car from Boole Poole, Lakes Entrance is about 23 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 28 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Lakes Entrance Aquatic Centre in Lakes Entrance is about 24 minutes' drive from Boole Poole. For a library, Lakes Entrance Library in Lakes Entrance is about 20 minutes' drive from Boole Poole. For a skate park, the skate park at Gilsenan Reserve in Paynesville is about 40 minutes' drive from Boole Poole. For a cinema, Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale is about 26 minutes' drive from Boole Poole. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow is about 20 minutes' drive from Boole Poole. The nearest larger centre is Lakes Entrance, about 23 minutes from Boole Poole by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Boole Poole are the playground at Nyerimilang Heritage Park in Nyerimilang (19 min), Griffiths Sea Shell Museum in Lakes Entrance (18 min), Lakes Entrance Library in Lakes Entrance (20 min), Ninety Mile Beach in Lakes Entrance (22 min), Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow (20 min) and Bruthen Gallery in Bruthen (25 min).
