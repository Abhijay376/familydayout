Jerribong Way Reserve

Park in Kangaroo Flat · Basic info

Jerribong Way Reserve is a park in Kangaroo Flat, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, good for all ages. Jacks Ave/High St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1072 m away with a baby change table.
