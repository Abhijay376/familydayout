Kangaroo Flat Library

Library in Kangaroo Flat · Basic info

Kangaroo Flat Library is a library in Kangaroo Flat, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Bendigo Community Health Services/Helm St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 311 m away with a baby change table.
