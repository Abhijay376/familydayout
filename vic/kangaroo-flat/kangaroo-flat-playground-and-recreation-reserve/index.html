Kangaroo Flat Playground & Recreation Reserve

Playground in Kangaroo Flat · Basic info

Kangaroo Flat Playground & Recreation Reserve is a public playground in Kangaroo Flat, in the Greater Bendigo City area. The Bendigo Creek Trail (North) passes about 130 m away, and KFC is about 440 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 160 m away, and bus 5 stops at Cooks Reserve/High St, a 1-minute walk away.
