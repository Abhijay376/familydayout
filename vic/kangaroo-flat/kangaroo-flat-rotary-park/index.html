Kangaroo Flat Rotary Park

Park in Kangaroo Flat · Basic info

Kangaroo Flat Rotary Park is a park in Kangaroo Flat, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, good for all ages. Rotary Park/High St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 25 m away.
