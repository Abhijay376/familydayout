Lansell Square

Shopping centre in Kangaroo Flat · Basic info

Lansell Square is a shopping centre in Kangaroo Flat, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Lansell Plaza SC/High St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 326 m away with a baby change table.
