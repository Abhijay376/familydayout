Playground at Werribee Street Park

Playground in Werribee · Official details

This is a neighbourhood playground in Werribee, looked after by Wyndham City Council. The Werribee River Trail is about 170 m away, and Playground at Eldan Drive Park is another option about 500 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 2.1 km from the playground, and buses 181 and 191 stop at Werribee St North/Greaves St, a 4-minute walk away.
