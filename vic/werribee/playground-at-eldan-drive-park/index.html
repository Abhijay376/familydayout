Playground at Eldan Drive Park

Playground in Werribee · Official details

This is a neighbourhood playground in Werribee, looked after by Wyndham City Council. Werribee Recreational Reserve Playground is another option about 320 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and buses 181 and 191 stop at Quarbing St/Greaves St, a 4-minute walk away.
