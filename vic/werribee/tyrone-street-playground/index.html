Tyrone Street Playground

Playground in Werribee · Official details

Tyrone Street Playground is a neighbourhood playground in Werribee, in the Wyndham City area. The Garden Feast Cafe is about 200 m away if you need food, and Playground at Snipe Court Park is another option about 450 m away. There's a public toilet about 730 m away, and bus 153 stops at Sneydes Rd/Princes Hwy, a 3-minute walk away.
