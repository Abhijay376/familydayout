Playground at Goegan Street Park

Playground in Werribee · Official details

Playground at Goegan Street Park is a neighbourhood playground in Werribee, in the Wyndham City area. Soldiers Reserve Playground is another option about 530 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.0 km away, and bus 443 stops at Nantilla Cres/Wattle Ave, a 5-minute walk away.
