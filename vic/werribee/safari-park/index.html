Safari Park

Park in Werribee · Basic info

Safari Park is a park in Werribee, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Grandvista Bvd/Black Forest Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 802 m away with a baby change table.
