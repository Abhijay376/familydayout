Playground at Wyndham Vale South Reserve

Playground in Werribee · Official details

Playground at Wyndham Vale South Reserve is a small local playground in Werribee, in the Wyndham City area. The Lollypop Creek Trail is about 320 m away, and Playground at Wyndham Vale Reserve is another option about 480 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 660 m from the playground, and bus 192 stops at McGrath Rd/Black Forest Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
