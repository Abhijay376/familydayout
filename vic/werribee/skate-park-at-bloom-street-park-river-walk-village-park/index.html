Skate park at Bloom Street Park/River Walk Village Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Werribee · Basic info

Skate park at Bloom Street Park/River Walk Village Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Werribee, in the Wyndham City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Riparian St/Newmarket Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 47 m away.
