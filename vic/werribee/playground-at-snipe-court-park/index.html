Playground at Snipe Court Park

Playground in Werribee · Official details

Playground at Snipe Court Park in Werribee is a neighbourhood playground in the Wyndham City area. Kookaburra Ave Fish & Chips is about 160 m away if you need food, and Tyrone Street Playground is another option about 450 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.9 km away, and bus 161 stops at Rosella Ave/Kookaburra Ave, a 3-minute walk away.
