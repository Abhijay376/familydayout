Galvin Park

Park in Werribee · Official details

Galvin Park is a park in Werribee, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Galvin Park Reserve/Shaws Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 134 m away.

Good to know

Questions parents ask

Where is Galvin Park?
Galvin Park is in Werribee, Victoria.
Is Galvin Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Galvin Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 134 m away.
How do I get to Galvin Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Galvin Park Reserve/Shaws Rd (routes 166), a 2-minute walk.
