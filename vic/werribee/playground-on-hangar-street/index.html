Playground on Hangar Street

Playground in Werribee · Basic info

Playground on Hangar Street in Werribee is a public playground in the Wyndham City area. The Geelong Road Trail is about 300 m away, and Werribee Outdoor Pool is about 500 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.4 km from the playground, and bus 194 stops at Pengelly St/Farm Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
