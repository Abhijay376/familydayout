Tindale Boulevard Park

Playground in Werribee · Basic info

Tindale Boulevard Park is a public playground in Werribee, in the Wyndham City area. The Geelong Road Trail passes about 280 m away, and Playground on Winscombe Road is another option about 410 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.1 km away, and bus 194 stops at Alfred Rd/Princes Hwy, a 8-minute walk away.
