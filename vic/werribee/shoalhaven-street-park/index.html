Shoalhaven Street Park

Park in Werribee · Basic info

Shoalhaven Street Park is a park in Werribee, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Wilson Reserve/Parramatta Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 911 m away.
