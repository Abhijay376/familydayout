Playground at Safari Park

Playground in Werribee · Basic info

Playground at Safari Park in Werribee is a public playground in the Wyndham City area. KFC is about 460 m away if you need food, and Playground on Grandvista Boulevard is another option about 420 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 760 m away, and bus 194 stops at Grandvista Bvd/Black Forest Rd , a 6-minute walk away.
