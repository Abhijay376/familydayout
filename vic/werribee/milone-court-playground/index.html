Milone Court Playground

Playground in Werribee · Official details

Milone Court Playground in Werribee is a neighbourhood playground in the Wyndham City area. The Werribee River Trail is about 260 m away, and Werribee Outdoor Pool is about 540 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 790 m from the playground, and bus 443 stops at Mercy Place Nursing Home/Greaves St, a 3-minute walk away.
