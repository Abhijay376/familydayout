Coventry Drive Playground

Playground in Werribee · Official details

Coventry Drive Playground in Werribee is a neighbourhood playground in the Wyndham City area. Werribee Recreational Reserve Playground is another option about 530 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 860 m from the playground, and bus 191 stops at Edinburgh Ct/Westminster Dr, a 3-minute walk away.
