Playground at Soldiers Reserve

Playground in Werribee · Basic info

Playground at Soldiers Reserve is a playground in Werribee, in the Wyndham City Council area, best for toddlers and primary-school kids. MacKillop Catholic College/Russell St (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 180 m away.
