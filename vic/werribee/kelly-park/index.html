Kelly Park

Park in Werribee · Official details

Kelly Park is a park in Werribee, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Werribee RSL/Synnot St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 294 m away with a baby change table.

Questions parents ask

Where is Kelly Park?
Kelly Park is in Werribee, Victoria.
Is Kelly Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Kelly Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 294 m away, with a baby change table.
How do I get to Kelly Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Werribee RSL/Synnot St (routes 153, 439), a 4-minute walk.
