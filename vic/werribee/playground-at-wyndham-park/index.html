Playground at Wyndham Park

Playground in Werribee · Official details

Playground at Wyndham Park is a public playground in Werribee, in the Wyndham City area. The Werribee River Trail passes about 40 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 320 m away. There's a public toilet about 70 m away, and bus 153 stops at Wedge St/Synnot St, a 6-minute walk away.
