Playground at Heathdale Glen Orden Wetlands Reserve

Playground in Werribee · Official details

This is one of the council's larger playgrounds in Werribee, looked after by Wyndham City Council. Older kids have a skate park about 380 m away, and Cassowary Avenue Playground is another option about 590 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus 161 stops at Nightingale Dr/Kookaburra Ave, a 3-minute walk away.
