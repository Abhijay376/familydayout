Soldiers Reserve Playground

Playground in Werribee · Official details

This is a neighbourhood playground in Werribee, looked after by Wyndham City Council. For food, McDonald's is about 470 m away, and Kelly Park Playground is another option about 450 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 270 m from the playground, and bus 439 stops at Werribee Sec Col/Duncans Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
