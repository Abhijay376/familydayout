Nangiloc Crescent Playground

Playground in Werribee · Official details

This is a neighbourhood playground in Werribee, looked after by Wyndham City Council. The Federation Trail is about 340 m away, and Playground at Loyola Road Park is another option about 360 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 960 m from the playground, and bus 443 stops at Thelma Ct/Chirnside Ave, a 6-minute walk away.
