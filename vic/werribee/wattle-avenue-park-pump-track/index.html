Wattle Avenue Park pump track

Skate, BMX or pump track in Werribee · Basic info

Wattle Avenue Park pump track is a skate, bmx or pump track in Werribee, in the Wyndham City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Bowman St/Wattle Ave (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 814 m away.
