Harpley Town Centre

Shopping centre in Werribee · Basic info

Harpley Town Centre is a shopping centre in Werribee, in the Wyndham City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Rushton St/Ison Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 48 m away with a baby change table.
