Landmarks

Fresh-water Swamp, Woodside Beach Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Woodside Beach, at about 36 hectares. Playgrounds in Woodside Beach include Woodside Beach Road Playground. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Woodside Beach. Places to visit in Woodside Beach include Woodside_Beach and Woolami.

Getting around

Woodside Beach has no train station; Rosedale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 47 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Woodside Beach, so most trips start by car. By car from Woodside Beach, Sale is about 54 minutes away and Traralgon about 77 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Yarram Public Pool in Yarram is about 28 minutes' drive from Woodside Beach. For a library, Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram is about 28 minutes' drive from Woodside Beach. The closest skate park to Woodside Beach is the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram, about 28 minutes' drive. Sale is the closest bigger centre to Woodside Beach, about 54 minutes away by car, and has 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Woodside Beach, the nearest places for kids by road are McLoughlins Beach Playground in McLoughlins Beach (21 min), Yarram Historical Society Museum in Yarram (26 min), Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram (28 min), the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram (28 min), Yarram Public Pool in Yarram (28 min) and Port Albert Maritime Museum in Port Albert (40 min).
