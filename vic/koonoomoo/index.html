Landmarks

Other parks and reserves in Koonoomoo include Tocumwal swimming pool. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Koonoomoo. Places to visit in Koonoomoo include Tocumwal Library, Apex Beach and Ardley Beach. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Koonoomoo for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Koonoomoo has no train station; Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 57 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Koonoomoo. By car from Koonoomoo, Shepparton is about 58 minutes away and Benalla about 68 minutes, without traffic.

For a skate park, the skate park at Federation Park in Cobram is about 9 minutes' drive from Koonoomoo. The closest cinema to Koonoomoo is Cobram Cinema in Cobram, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Koonoomoo is Tunzafun in Yarrawonga, about 40 minutes' drive.
