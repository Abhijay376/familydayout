Nunniong is in the East Gippsland area, and our data lists 6 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Nunniong is Nunniong State Forest, covering about 33,035 hectares. Nunniong Plain Natural Features and Scenic Reserve and Nunnett Plain Natural Features and Scenic Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Nunniong include Moscow Villa, Bentley Plain and Bentley Plain Natural Features and Scenic Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Nunniong. Picnic areas in Nunniong include Bentley Plain and Moscow Villa. For walks and rides, Bentley Plain Walk and Douglas Reserve Walk cross Nunniong.

Getting around

Nunniong has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 74 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nunniong, so most trips start by car. By car from Nunniong, Bairnsdale is about 2.5 hours away and Lakes Entrance about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Nunniong, the nearest places for kids by road are Great Alpine Gallery in Swifts Creek (67 min), Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek (68 min), Petersens Art Gallery & Museum in Omeo (75 min), the playground at Omeo Historical Park & Museum in Omeo (75 min) and the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo (75 min).

Best for

Free days on the Bentley Plain Walk and in Nunniong's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Swifts Creek, next to Nunniong, has 1 playground and a pool. Next door to Nunniong, Buchan has 1 playground and a pool. Ensay, next to Nunniong, has 1 playground. Reedy Flat, Ensay North, Brumby and Bindi also border Nunniong.
