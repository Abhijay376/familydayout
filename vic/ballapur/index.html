Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Ballapur in the Buloke area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Ballapur. The closest to Ballapur is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 100 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Ballapur, so most trips start by car. By car from Ballapur, Horsham is about 85 minutes away and Swan Hill about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem is about 27 minutes' drive from Ballapur. The closest skate park to Ballapur is Birchip Skate Park in Birchip, about 12 minutes' drive. From Ballapur, the nearest places for kids by road are Birchip History Museum in Birchip (12 min), the playground at Birchip Shire Soldiers Memorial Park in Birchip (12 min), Birchip Skate Park in Birchip (12 min), the playground at J.E Taylor Sunset Park in Birchip (12 min), Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem (27 min) and Woomelang Recreation Reserve Swimming Pool in Woomelang (32 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Ballapur.
