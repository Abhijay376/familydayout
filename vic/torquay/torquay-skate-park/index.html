Torquay Skate Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Torquay · Basic info

Torquay Skate Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Torquay, in the Surf Coast Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Letham Lane/Beach Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 201 m away.
