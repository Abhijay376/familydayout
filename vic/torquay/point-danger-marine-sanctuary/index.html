Point Danger Marine Sanctuary

Park in Torquay · Official details

Point Danger Marine Sanctuary is a park in Torquay, in the Surf Coast Shire Council area, good for all ages. Price St/The Esplanade (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 343 m away.

Details

Seasons
Guided rockpool walks as part of holiday programs and Summer by the Sea in Torquay (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
Watch tides to avoid being stranded on the reef; Wear grippy shoes on rock platforms; Stay away from cliff edges (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Point Danger Marine Sanctuary?
Point Danger Marine Sanctuary is in Torquay, Victoria.
Is Point Danger Marine Sanctuary free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Point Danger Marine Sanctuary?
The nearest public toilet is about 343 m away.
How do I get to Point Danger Marine Sanctuary by public transport?
The closest stop is Price St/The Esplanade (routes 52, 53, 54), a 7-minute walk.
