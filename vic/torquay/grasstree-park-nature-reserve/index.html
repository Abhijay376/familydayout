Grasstree Park Nature Reserve

Park in Torquay · Basic info

Grasstree Park Nature Reserve is a park in Torquay, in the Surf Coast Shire Council area, good for all ages. Surf Coast Secondary College/Surf Coast Hwy (bus) is a 10-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1107 m away.
