Taylor Park

Park in Torquay · Basic info

Taylor Park is a park in Torquay, in the Surf Coast Shire Council area, good for all ages. Beach Rd/Fischer St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 386 m away.
