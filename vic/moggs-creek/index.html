Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Moggs Creek in the Surf Coast area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Moggs Creek. The Ocean View Walk and Moggs Creek Circuit Walk all pass through Moggs Creek.

Getting around

Moggs Creek has no train station; Winchelsea Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Moggs Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Moggs Creek, Torquay is about 36 minutes away, Geelong about 54 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Moggs Creek is Lorne Sea Baths in Lorne, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest library to Moggs Creek is Torquay Library in Torquay, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Moggs Creek is Fairhaven in Eastern View, about 2 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Moggs Creek is Aireys Inlet Skate Park in Aireys Inlet, about 4 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Lorne Theatre in Lorne is about 16 minutes' drive from Moggs Creek. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Moggs Creek is Live Wire Park in Lorne, about 18 minutes' drive. Torquay is the closest bigger centre to Moggs Creek, about 36 minutes away by car, and has 15 playgrounds, a library and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Moggs Creek are Fairhaven in Eastern View (2 min), the playground at Aireys Inlet Reserve in Aireys Inlet (4 min), Aireys Inlet Skate Park in Aireys Inlet (4 min), the playground at Bark Hut Reserve in Aireys Inlet (5 min), Step Beach in Aireys Inlet (5 min) and Anglesea Museum in Anglesea (17 min).
