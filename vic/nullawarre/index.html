Our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in Nullawarre, in the Moyne area. On top of that, Nullawarre has a beach.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Nullawarre is Bay of Islands Coastal Park, covering about 939 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Nullawarre. In Nullawarre you'll also find Flaxman Beach. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Nullawarre for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Nullawarre has no train station; Sherwood Park Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Warrnambool - Port Campbell - Timboon via Allansford, Nullawarre, Peterborough route stop in Nullawarre. By car from Nullawarre, Warrnambool is about 27 minutes away and Colac about 72 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool is about 28 minutes' drive from Nullawarre. For a skate park, the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon is about 18 minutes' drive from Nullawarre. For a cinema, Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool is about 27 minutes' drive from Nullawarre. For an indoor play centre, The Bowl in Warrnambool is about 28 minutes' drive from Nullawarre. For a zoo or wildlife park, Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee is about 20 minutes' drive from Nullawarre. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Nullawarre, about 27 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Nullawarre, the nearest places for kids by road are Buttress Point in The Cove (9 min), the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (18 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (18 min), the playground at Peterborough Coastal Reserve (COM) in Peterborough (19 min), Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (20 min) and Grieve Street Playground in Warrnambool (25 min).

Best for
