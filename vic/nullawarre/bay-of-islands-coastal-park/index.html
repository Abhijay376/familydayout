Bay of Islands Coastal Park

Park in Nullawarre · Official details

Bay of Islands Coastal Park is a park in Nullawarre, in the Moyne Shire Council area, good for all ages. Dogs are allowed on a lead.

Details

Dogs
on lead (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
Beaches are not patrolled and not recommended for swimming; Keep dogs away from Hooded Plover nesting area between high tide mark and dunes (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Bay of Islands Coastal Park?
Bay of Islands Coastal Park is in Nullawarre, Victoria.
Is Bay of Islands Coastal Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Bay of Islands Coastal Park?
Yes, on a lead.
