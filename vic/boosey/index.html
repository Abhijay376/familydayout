Boosey is in the Moira area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Broken-Boosey State Park is the largest mapped park in Boosey, at about 1,011 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Boosey.

Getting around

There's no train station in Boosey. The closest to Boosey is Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line), about 50 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Boosey, so most trips start by car. By car from Boosey, Shepparton is about 50 minutes away and Benalla about 60 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah is about 36 minutes' drive from Boosey. The closest library to Boosey is Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga, about 20 minutes' drive. For a beach, Backhouse Beach in Cobram East is about 17 minutes' drive from Boosey. For a skate park, the skate park at Federation Park in Cobram is about 25 minutes' drive from Boosey. The closest cinema to Boosey is Cobram Cinema in Cobram, about 24 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Tunzafun in Yarrawonga is about 22 minutes' drive from Boosey. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 50 minutes from Boosey by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Boosey are the playground at E.W Kilgour Reserve in Katamatite (11 min), Katamatite Historical Society Museum in Katamatite (11 min), the playground at C R Wood Park in Katamatite (11 min), Backhouse Beach in Cobram East (17 min), Duffys Beach No 1 in Burramine (18 min) and Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga (20 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Boosey.

Nearby suburbs

Cobram East, next to Boosey, has a beach. Next door to Boosey, Burramine has a beach. Katamatite, next to Boosey, has 2 playgrounds. Tungamah, next to Boosey, has 1 playground. Youarang, Katamatite East and Burramine South also border Boosey.
