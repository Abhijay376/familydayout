Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Leongatha North, in the South Gippsland area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Leongatha North is Carl Hamann Reserve, covering about 23 hectares.

Getting around

Leongatha North has no train station; Yarragon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Leongatha North, so most trips start by car. By car from Leongatha North, Leongatha is about 8 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha is about 6 minutes' drive from Leongatha North. The closest library to Leongatha North is Leongatha Library in Leongatha, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Leongatha North is The Caves in Inverloch, about 35 minutes' drive. For a skate park, BMX track in Leongatha is about 5 minutes' drive from Leongatha North. For a cinema, Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha is about 8 minutes' drive from Leongatha North. The nearest larger centre is Leongatha, about 8 minutes from Leongatha North by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Leongatha North, the nearest places for kids by road are BMX track in Leongatha (5 min), Leongatha Skate Park in Leongatha (6 min), South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha (6 min), Splash Pools and Stadium in Leongatha (7 min), Leongatha Library in Leongatha (7 min) and Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha (8 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Leongatha North.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Leongatha North, Leongatha has 4 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Koorooman, Fairbank and Boorool also border Leongatha North.
