Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Browns Plains, in the Indigo area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Browns Plains is Murray River K16 Streamside Reserve, covering about 17 hectares. Chiltern I91 Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Browns Plains.

Getting around

Browns Plains has no train station; Chiltern Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Browns Plains, so most trips start by car. By car from Browns Plains, Wodonga is about 22 minutes away and Wangaratta about 44 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Browns Plains is Howlong Swimming Pool in Gooramadda, about 10 minutes' drive. For a library, Howlong Library and Resource Centre in Gooramadda is about 9 minutes' drive from Browns Plains. The closest beach to Browns Plains is Finns Beach in Bandiana, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Browns Plains is the bmx track at Lowe Square in Gooramadda, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Browns Plains is Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana, about 25 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 22 minutes from Browns Plains by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Browns Plains are Howlong Library and Resource Centre in Gooramadda (9 min), Howlong Swimming Pool in Gooramadda (10 min), the bmx track at Lowe Square in Gooramadda (10 min), Lowe Square Playground in Gooramadda (10 min), Chiltern Athenaeum Museum in Chiltern (12 min) and Martin Park Playground in Chiltern (12 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Browns Plains.

Nearby suburbs

Gooramadda, next to Browns Plains, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Browns Plains, Rutherglen has 1 playground, a pool and a library.
