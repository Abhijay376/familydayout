Cullen is in the Gannawarra area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Cullen, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Cullen has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cullen, so most trips start by car. By car from Cullen, Echuca is about 58 minutes away and Swan Hill about 69 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Cohuna Pool in Cohuna is about 8 minutes' drive from Cullen. The closest library to Cullen is Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Cullen is Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna, about 7 minutes' drive. Echuca is the closest bigger centre to Cullen, about 58 minutes away by car, and has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Cullen, the nearest places for kids by road are Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna (7 min), the playground at C L King Memorial Park in Cohuna (8 min), the playground at Cohuna Apex Park in Cohuna (7 min), Cohuna Pool in Cohuna (8 min), Leitchville Memorial Swimming Pool in Leitchville (20 min) and Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (22 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Cullen.

Nearby suburbs
