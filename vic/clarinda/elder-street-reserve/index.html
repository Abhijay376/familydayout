Elder Street Reserve, Clarinda

Park in Clarinda · Basic info

Elder Street Reserve is a park in Clarinda, in the Kingston City Council area, good for all ages. Clarinda SC/Bourke Rd (bus) is a 12-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 225 m away.
