Playground at Elder Street Reserve

Playground in Clarinda · Basic info

Playground at Elder Street Reserve in Clarinda is a public playground in the Kingston City area. The Dingley Bypass Trail is about 200 m away, and Playground at Hendon Court Reserve is another option about 590 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 160 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 4 minutes from Clarinda.
