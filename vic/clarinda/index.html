Our data lists 6 playgrounds and 11 parks and reserves in Clarinda, in the Kingston area. Beyond the parks, Clarinda has a library.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Clarinda is Victory Road Reserve, covering about 31 hectares. Bald Hill Park and Elder Street Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Clarinda include Drushi Reserve, Cooinda Reserve and Tatra Close Reserve. Playgrounds in Clarinda include the playground at Bald Hill Park, the playground at Drushi Reserve and the playground at Elder Street Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Clarinda. Places to visit in Clarinda include Clarinda Library. The Dingley Bypass Trail passes through Clarinda. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Clarinda for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Clarinda. The closest to Clarinda is Clayton Railway Station (metro), about 1.7 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 631, 703, 705, 733 and 824 stop in Clarinda. By car from Clarinda, Dandenong is about 15 minutes away, Box Hill about 20 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 24 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Clarinda is Clayton Aquatics & Health Club in Clayton, about 3 minutes' drive. For a beach, Parkdale Beach in Parkdale is about 12 minutes' drive from Clarinda. For a skate park, the skate park at Namatjira Park in Clayton South is about 1 minutes' drive from Clarinda. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Clayton is about 6 minutes' drive from Clarinda. The closest indoor play centre to Clarinda is Zone Bowling in Clayton, about 6 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale is about 9 minutes' drive from Clarinda.

Best for

Younger kids at Clarinda's 6 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the library in Clarinda. Free days on the Dingley Bypass Trail and in Clarinda's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Clayton, next to Clarinda, has 7 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Clarinda, Dingley Village has 18 playgrounds and a skate park. Clayton South, next to Clarinda, has 12 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Oakleigh South, next to Clarinda, has 13 playgrounds. Heatherton also borders Clarinda.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Clarinda, without traffic.
