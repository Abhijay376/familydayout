Hazelwood is in the Latrobe area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Hazelwood, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Hazelwood has no train station; Morwell Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 6.6 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Hazelwood, so most trips start by car. By car from Hazelwood, Traralgon is about 23 minutes away, Warragul about 49 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Latrobe Leisure - Churchill in Churchill is about 12 minutes' drive from Hazelwood. For a library, Churchill Library in Churchill is about 12 minutes' drive from Hazelwood. The closest skate park to Hazelwood is Churchill Skate Park in Churchill, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Hazelwood is Village 8 in Morwell, about 14 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Traralgon, about 23 minutes from Hazelwood by car, with 38 playgrounds and a pool. From Hazelwood, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Yinnar Centenary Garden in Yinnar (8 min), ARC Yinnar in Yinnar (8 min), Railway Trolley Shed in Yinnar (9 min), Andrews Park Playground in Churchill (12 min), Latrobe Leisure - Churchill in Churchill (12 min) and Churchill Library in Churchill (12 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Hazelwood.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Hazelwood, Morwell has 14 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. Next door to Hazelwood, Churchill has 9 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Yinnar, next to Hazelwood, has 3 playgrounds. Hazelwood North and Driffield also border Hazelwood.
