Playground on Cranbourne Place

Playground in Cranbourne · Basic info

Playground on Cranbourne Place in Cranbourne is a public playground in the Casey City area. The Fish & Chip is about 60 m away if you need food, and Playground on Averne Street is another option about 520 m away. There's a public toilet about 770 m away, and buses 841, 893 and 982 stop at Camms Rd/South Gippsland Hwy, a 2-minute walk away.
