Playground at Lawson Poole Reserve

Playground in Cranbourne · Basic info

Playground at Lawson Poole Reserve in Cranbourne is a public playground in the Casey City area. The Bike: Thompsons Road passes about 270 m away, and Guzman y Gomez is about 320 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 610 m away, and bus 881 stops at Lesdon Ave/Thompsons Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
