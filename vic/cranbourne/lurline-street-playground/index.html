Lurline Street Playground

Playground in Cranbourne · Basic info

Lurline Street Playground is a public playground in Cranbourne, in the Casey City area. For food, Flake on Lurline is about 80 m away, and Playground at Mahogany Park is another option about 400 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 550 m from the playground, and buses 760, 791 and 982 stop at Lurline St/Sladen St, a 3-minute walk away.
