Kids Backyard

Playground in Cranbourne · Basic info

Kids Backyard in Cranbourne is a public playground in the Casey City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 140 m away. It's easiest by car, about 10 minutes from Cranbourne.
