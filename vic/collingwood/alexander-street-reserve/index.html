Alexander Street Reserve

Park in Collingwood · Basic info

Alexander Street Reserve is a park in Collingwood, in the Yarra City Council area, good for all ages. Truro St/Hoddle St (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 665 m away.
