Playground on Harmsworth Street

Playground in Collingwood · Basic info

Playground on Harmsworth Street is a public playground in Collingwood, in the Yarra City area. The Bike: Gipps St is about 310 m away, and Collingwood Library is about 320 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 520 m from the playground, and 12 bus routes stop at Vere St/Hoddle St, a 3-minute walk away.
