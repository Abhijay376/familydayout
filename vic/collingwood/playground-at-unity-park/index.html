Playground at Unity Park

Playground in Collingwood · Basic info

Playground at Unity Park is a public playground in Collingwood, in the Yarra City area. The Bike: Wellington St is about 30 m away, and Russian House Library is about 450 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 340 m from the playground, and buses 200 and 207 stop at Wellington St/Johnston St, a 2-minute walk away.
