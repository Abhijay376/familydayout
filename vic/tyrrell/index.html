Tyrrell is in the Buloke area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Lang Plain Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Tyrrell, at about 61 hectares. Waitchie Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tyrrell. The closest to Tyrrell is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 55 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tyrrell, so most trips start by car. By car from Tyrrell, Swan Hill is about 60 minutes away and Mildura about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tyrrell is Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake, about 17 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 60 minutes from Tyrrell by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Tyrrell, the nearest places for kids by road are Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake (17 min), the playground at Apex Park in Sea Lake (17 min), Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock (32 min) and the playground at Woomelang Rec Reserve in Woomelang (41 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tyrrell.
