Mount Macedon is in the Macedon Ranges area, and our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Stanley Park is the largest mapped park in Mount Macedon, at about 8 hectares. Playgrounds in Mount Macedon include the playground at Stanley Park. Our data shows 7 public toilets in Mount Macedon. For walks and rides, Macedon Ranges Walking Trail, Sanatorium Eco-Tourism Trail and Mt Towrong Walking Track cross Mount Macedon.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mount Macedon. The closest to Mount Macedon is Macedon Railway Station (V/Line), about 3.8 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mount Macedon, so most trips start by car. By car from Mount Macedon, Kyneton is about 27 minutes away, Bacchus Marsh about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 56 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mount Macedon is Gisborne Aquatic Centre in Gisborne, about 11 minutes' drive. For a library, Gisborne Library in Gisborne is about 11 minutes' drive from Mount Macedon. For a beach, Canoeing in Tylden is about 33 minutes' drive from Mount Macedon. For a skate park, Romsey Skate Park in Romsey is about 30 minutes' drive from Mount Macedon. For a cinema, Reading Cinema in Sunbury is about 24 minutes' drive from Mount Macedon. For an indoor play centre, Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes is about 35 minutes' drive from Mount Macedon. The nearest larger centre is Kyneton, about 27 minutes from Mount Macedon by car, with 10 playgrounds and a library. From Mount Macedon, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Ross Watt Reserve in New Gisborne (9 min), Gisborne Aquatic Centre in Gisborne (11 min), Gisborne Library in Gisborne (11 min), Old Gisborne Court House in Gisborne (12 min), Romsey Library in Romsey (30 min) and Romsey Skate Park in Romsey (30 min).

Best for

Free days on the Macedon Ranges Walking Trail and in Mount Macedon's parks.
