Playground at Sinclair Green Reserve

Playground in Derrimut · Official details

Playground at Sinclair Green Reserve is a public playground in Derrimut, in the Brimbank City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet about 840 m away, and bus 400 stops at Carew Way/Westminster Pkwy, a 1-minute walk away.
