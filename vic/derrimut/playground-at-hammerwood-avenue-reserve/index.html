Playground at Hammerwood Avenue Reserve

Playground in Derrimut · Basic info

Playground at Hammerwood Avenue Reserve is a public playground in Derrimut, in the Brimbank City area. Kingswim Derrimut is about 470 m away, and Domino's is about 320 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 500 m away, and bus 400 stops at Sheryn St/Hammerwood Ave, a 3-minute walk away.
