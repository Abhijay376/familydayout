Playground at Kenwood Green Reserve

Playground in Derrimut · Basic info

Playground at Kenwood Green Reserve in Derrimut is a public playground in the Brimbank City area. The Wellness Trail passes about 310 m away, and Playground at Balmoral Park is another option about 320 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 360 m away, and bus 400 stops at Rochester Vsta/Windsor Bvd, a 3-minute walk away.
