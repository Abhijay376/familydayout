Playground at Bothwell Green Reserve

Playground in Derrimut · Official details

Playground at Bothwell Green Reserve is a public playground in Derrimut, in the Brimbank City area. The Wellness Trail is about 370 m away, and Playground at Capesthorne Drive Reserve is another option about 480 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 860 m from the playground, and bus 400 stops at Palmer Pde/Windsor Bvd, a 7-minute walk away.
