Balmoral Park

Park in Derrimut · Basic info

Balmoral Park is a park in Derrimut, in the Brimbank City Council area, good for all ages. Hammerwood Ave/Windsor Bvd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 230 m away with a baby change table.
