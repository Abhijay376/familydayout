Derrimut in the Brimbank area has 7 playgrounds and 12 parks and reserves in our data. Derrimut also has a pool.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Derrimut is Derrimut Grassland Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 164 hectares. Gilbertsons Grassland Nature Conservation Reserve and Paramount Grassland are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Derrimut include Balmoral Park, Derrimut Reserve and Kenwood Green Reserve. Playgrounds in Derrimut include the playground at Balmoral Park, the playground at Balmoral Park and the playground at Kenwood Green Reserve. Our data shows 4 public toilets and 4 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Derrimut. Places to visit in Derrimut include Kingswim Derrimut. Derrimut Village is the local shopping centre. The M80 Trail, Laverton Creek Trail (Non-contiguous) and Wellness Trail all pass through Derrimut. Our data lists 8 cafes and family restaurants in Derrimut for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Derrimut has no train station; Deer Park Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 2.2 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 400 and 417 stop in Derrimut. By car from Derrimut, Sunshine is about 10 minutes away, Werribee about 20 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 23 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Derrimut is Brimbank Libraries Deer Park Branch in Deer Park, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Derrimut is Altona Beach in Altona, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Derrimut is the skate park at Robert Bruce Reserve in Deer Park, about 6 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Sunshine is about 11 minutes' drive from Derrimut. For an indoor play centre, Zone Bowling in Braybrook is about 14 minutes' drive from Derrimut. For a zoo or wildlife park, Skyline Precinct Melbourne in South Wharf is about 19 minutes' drive from Derrimut.

Best for

Younger kids at Derrimut's 7 local playgrounds. Free days on the M80 Trail and in Derrimut's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Truganina, next to Derrimut, has 29 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Derrimut, Deer Park has 17 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Derrimut, Sunshine West has 19 playgrounds and a skate park. Laverton North also borders Derrimut.
