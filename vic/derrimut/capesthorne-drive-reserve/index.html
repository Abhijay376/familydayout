Capesthorne Drive Reserve

Park in Derrimut · Basic info

Capesthorne Drive Reserve is a park in Derrimut, in the Brimbank City Council area, good for all ages. Lennon Pkwy/Windsor Bvd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 516 m away.
