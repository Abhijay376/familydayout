Bothwell Green Reserve

Park in Derrimut · Official details

Bothwell Green Reserve is a park in Derrimut, in the Brimbank City Council area, good for all ages. Palmer Pde/Windsor Bvd (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 881 m away.

Where is Bothwell Green Reserve?
Bothwell Green Reserve is in Derrimut, Victoria.
