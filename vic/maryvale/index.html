Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Maryvale in the Latrobe area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Maryvale has no train station; Morwell Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 7.7 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Maryvale, so most trips start by car. By car from Maryvale, Traralgon is about 14 minutes away, Warragul about 43 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Yallourn North Outdoor Pool in Yallourn North is about 6 minutes' drive from Maryvale. The closest library to Maryvale is Morwell Library in Morwell, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Maryvale is Yallourn North Skate Park and BMX Track in Yallourn North, about 7 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 9 minutes' drive from Maryvale. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Maryvale, about 14 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. From Maryvale, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Low Road Park in Yallourn North (6 min), Yallourn North Outdoor Pool in Yallourn North (6 min), Old Brown Coal Mine Museum in Yallourn North (6 min), the playground at Well Street Reserve in Morwell (8 min), Yallourn North Skate Park and BMX Track in Yallourn North (7 min) and Village 8 in Morwell (9 min).

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Maryvale, Yallourn North has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Maryvale, Yallourn has 1 playground. Tyers also borders Maryvale.
