Riggs Creek is in the Strathbogie area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Riggs Creek, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Riggs Creek has no train station; Euroa Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Riggs Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Riggs Creek, Benalla is about 39 minutes away, Shepparton about 41 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Riggs Creek is Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa, about 23 minutes' drive. For a library, Euroa Library in Euroa is about 23 minutes' drive from Riggs Creek. The closest beach to Riggs Creek is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 43 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Riggs Creek is Euroa Skate Park in Euroa, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Riggs Creek is Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Riggs Creek is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 41 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Riggs Creek is Emerald Bank Leisure Land in Kialla, about 35 minutes' drive. Benalla is the closest bigger centre to Riggs Creek, about 39 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Riggs Creek are Moglonemby Hall Playground in Moglonemby (10 min), Bicentennial Park Playground in Euroa (22 min), Euroa Library in Euroa (23 min), Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa (23 min), Euroa Skate Park in Euroa (24 min) and Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa (24 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Riggs Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Euroa, next to Riggs Creek, has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Riggs Creek, Violet Town has 3 playgrounds. Moglonemby, next to Riggs Creek, has 1 playground. Balmattum also borders Riggs Creek.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Riggs Creek, without traffic.
