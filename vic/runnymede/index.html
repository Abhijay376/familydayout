Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Runnymede, in the Campaspe area.

Landmarks

Runnymede Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Runnymede, at about 14 hectares. Runnymede Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Runnymede.

Getting around

There's no train station in Runnymede. The closest to Runnymede is Elmore Railway Station (V/Line), about 4.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Runnymede, so most trips start by car. By car from Runnymede, Echuca is about 44 minutes away, Bendigo about 48 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Runnymede is Goornong Swimming Pool in Goornong, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest library to Runnymede is Rushworth Library in Rushworth, about 40 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Runnymede is the skate park at Lions Park in Rushworth, about 40 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 44 minutes from Runnymede by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Runnymede are Campaspe Run Rural Discovery Centre in Elmore (7 min), Goornong Swimming Pool in Goornong (21 min), Memorial Pool in Rochester (21 min), the playground at Apex Park in Rochester (22 min), the playground at Pennington Reserve in Huntly (32 min) and Rushworth Museum in Rushworth (40 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Runnymede.

Nearby suburbs

Goornong, next to Runnymede, has a pool. Elmore, next to Runnymede, has 1 playground. Next door to Runnymede, Colbinabbin has 1 playground. Creek View, Corop, Burramboot and Burnewang also border Runnymede.
