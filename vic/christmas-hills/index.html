Christmas Hills is in the Nillumbik area, and our data lists 4 parks and reserves in the suburb. Christmas Hills also has a library.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Christmas Hills is Sugarloaf Reservoir Park, covering about 1,070 hectares. Warrandyte - Kinglake Nature Conservation Reserve and Yering Gorge Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Christmas Hills also has Christmas Hills Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 6 public toilets in Christmas Hills. Places to visit in Christmas Hills include Yarra Plenty Regional Library. For walks and rides, Sugarloaf Reservoir Circuit, Chris Phillips Walking Track and MarsBen Trail cross Christmas Hills.

Getting around

There's no train station in Christmas Hills. The closest to Christmas Hills is Lilydale Railway Station (metro), about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Christmas Hills, so most trips start by car. By car from Christmas Hills, Healesville is about 21 minutes away, Lilydale about 23 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 49 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Diamond Creek Outdoor Pool in Diamond Creek is about 20 minutes' drive from Christmas Hills. For a skate park, the skate park at McKenzie Reserve in Yarra Glen is about 8 minutes' drive from Christmas Hills. The closest cinema to Christmas Hills is The Memo, Healesville in Healesville, about 20 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Rare Bears Indoor Play Centre in Eltham is about 23 minutes' drive from Christmas Hills. For a zoo or wildlife park, Lyre Bird enclosure in Badger Creek is about 25 minutes' drive from Christmas Hills. Healesville is the closest bigger centre to Christmas Hills, about 21 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema.

Best for

Rainy days at the library in Christmas Hills. Free days on the Sugarloaf Reservoir Circuit and in Christmas Hills's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Chirnside Park, next to Christmas Hills, has 9 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. Wonga Park, next to Christmas Hills, has 9 playgrounds. Next door to Christmas Hills, Yarra Glen has 3 playgrounds and a skate park. Steels Creek, next to Christmas Hills, has 1 playground. St Andrews, Kinglake, Yering and Watsons Creek also border Christmas Hills.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Christmas Hills, without traffic.
