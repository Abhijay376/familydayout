Sugarloaf Reservoir Park

Park in Christmas Hills · Official details

Sugarloaf Reservoir Park is a park in Christmas Hills, in the Nillumbik Shire Council area, good for all ages. Dogs are not allowed. Opening hours: Daily 8.30am-5pm; daylight saving 8.30am-7pm.

Good to know
