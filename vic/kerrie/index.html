Landmarks

Tunnel Creek Natural Features Reserve is the largest mapped park in Kerrie, at about 7 hectares.

Getting around

Kerrie has no train station; Riddells Creek Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 9.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kerrie, so most trips start by car. By car from Kerrie, Kyneton is about 34 minutes away, Castlemaine about 60 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 78 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kerrie is Gisborne Aquatic Centre in Gisborne, about 33 minutes' drive. The closest library to Kerrie is Romsey Library in Romsey, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Kerrie is Canoeing in Tylden, about 40 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Kerrie is Romsey Skate Park in Romsey, about 18 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Reading Cinema in Sunbury is about 44 minutes' drive from Kerrie. Kyneton is the closest bigger centre to Kerrie, about 34 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Kerrie are Low Ropes Course in Riddells Creek (18 min), Romsey Library in Romsey (17 min), Romsey Skate Park in Romsey (18 min), the playground at Hanging Rock Reserve in Newham (19 min), Gisborne Aquatic Centre in Gisborne (33 min) and Gisborne Library in Gisborne (33 min).
