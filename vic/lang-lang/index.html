Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lang Lang is Adams Creek Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 418 hectares. Lang Lang Education Area and Lang Lang Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Lang Lang include Thwaites Park, Dick Jones Park and Clarks Road Reserve. Playgrounds around Lang Lang include the playground at Thwaites Park, the playground at Dick Jones Park and the playground at Clarks Road Reserve. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Lang Lang.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lang Lang. The closest to Lang Lang is Nar Nar Goon Railway Station (V/Line), about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Lang Lang. By car from Lang Lang, Pakenham is about 25 minutes away, Cranbourne about 31 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 69 minutes, without traffic.

The closest cinema to Lang Lang is Warragul Cinema in Warragul, about 45 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham is about 24 minutes' drive from Lang Lang.
