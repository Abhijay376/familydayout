Clydebank is in the Wellington area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Clydebank is Clydebank Morass Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 1,450 hectares. Clydebank Frontage Gippsland Lakes Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Clydebank. The Nature Walk passes through Clydebank.

Getting around

Clydebank has no train station; Stratford Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Clydebank, so most trips start by car. By car from Clydebank, Sale is about 25 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 55 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Clydebank is AquaEnergy in Sale, about 26 minutes' drive. For a library, Sale Library in Sale is about 26 minutes' drive from Clydebank. The closest skate park to Clydebank is Sale Skate Park in Sale, about 27 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Former Drive-in site in Sale is about 23 minutes' drive from Clydebank. The nearest larger centre is Sale, about 25 minutes from Clydebank by car, with 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Clydebank are Ibis Avenue - Pelican Court Playground in Sale (24 min), Lions Park Playground in Sale (23 min), Former Drive-in site in Sale (23 min), AquaEnergy in Sale (26 min), Indoor pool in Sale (27 min) and Roulette Cinema in East Sale (27 min).

Best for

Free days on the Nature Walk and in Clydebank's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Stratford, next to Clydebank, has 8 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Cobains and Airly also border Clydebank.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Clydebank, without traffic.
