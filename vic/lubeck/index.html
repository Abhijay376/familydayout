Lubeck is in the Yarriambiack area, and our data lists 1 playground and 6 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lubeck is Barrabool Flora and Fauna Reserve, covering about 1,036 hectares. Wimmera River, Marma Streamside Reserve and Station Creek Streamside Reserve are the next largest. Lubeck also has Warren Bushland Reserve, Gellatly Bushland Reserve and Marma I94 Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds in Lubeck include the playground at Lubeck Recreation Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lubeck. The closest to Lubeck is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 69 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lubeck, so most trips start by car. By car from Lubeck, Horsham is about 34 minutes away and Stawell about 41 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, the water play at Rabl Park in Murtoa is about 15 minutes' drive from Lubeck. For a skate park, the skate park at Rabl Park in Murtoa is about 15 minutes' drive from Lubeck. The closest cinema to Lubeck is Centre Cinemas in Horsham, about 33 minutes' drive. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to Lubeck, about 34 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Lubeck, the nearest places for kids by road are the water play at Rabl Park in Murtoa (15 min), the playground at Lake Marma Park- Lions in Murtoa (15 min), the skate park at Rabl Park in Murtoa (15 min), Murtoa Museum Complex in Murtoa (16 min), Rupanyup Swimming Pool in Rupanyup (19 min) and Woods' Farming and Heritage Museum in Rupanyup (19 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lubeck.

Nearby suburbs

Murtoa, next to Lubeck, has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Lubeck, Rupanyup has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Lubeck, Longerenong has 1 playground. St Helens Plains also borders Lubeck.
