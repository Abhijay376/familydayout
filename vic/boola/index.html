Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Boola in the Baw Baw area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

The Lyrebird and Bruntons Bridge Walk all pass through Boola.

Getting around

There's no train station in Boola. The closest to Boola is Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line), about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Boola, so most trips start by car. By car from Boola, Traralgon is about 38 minutes away, Warragul about 73 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Boola is Rawson Swimming Pool in Rawson, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest library to Boola is Morwell Library in Morwell, about 43 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Glengarry Skatepark in Glengarry is about 36 minutes' drive from Boola. The closest cinema to Boola is Village 8 in Morwell, about 45 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Traralgon, about 38 minutes from Boola by car, with 38 playgrounds and a pool. From Boola, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Croxford Park in Rawson (17 min), Rawson Swimming Pool in Rawson (18 min), the playground at Erica State Forest in Rawson (19 min), Fire station in Walhalla (27 min), Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon (38 min) and Glengarry Skatepark in Glengarry (36 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Boola on the Lyrebird.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Boola, Toongabbie has 2 playgrounds. Tyers, next to Boola, has 1 playground. Glengarry West and Glengarry North also border Boola.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Boola, without traffic.
