Bittern is in the Mornington Peninsula area, and our data lists 3 playgrounds and 12 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bittern is Warringine Park, covering about 180 hectares. Warringine Park and Warringine Park are the next largest. Bittern also has Lens Reserve, Goris Close Reserve and Haig Street Reserve. Playgrounds in Bittern include the playground at Flinders Street Reserve and the playground at Graham Myers Memorial Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Bittern. For walks and rides, Western Port Bay Trail and Ted Harris Walk cross Bittern. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Bittern for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Bittern Railway Station (metro: Stony Point Line) in Bittern. Bus 782 stops in Bittern. By car from Bittern, Frankston is about 23 minutes away, Mornington about 23 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 64 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bittern is Pelican Park Recreation Centre in Hastings, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest library to Bittern is Hastings Library in Hastings, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Bittern is Micks Beach in French Island, about 15 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Skate Ramp in Hastings is about 6 minutes' drive from Bittern. For a cinema, Dromana Triple Drive-In Theatre in Dromana is about 19 minutes' drive from Bittern. For an indoor play centre, Kidz Shed in Hastings is about 9 minutes' drive from Bittern. For a zoo or wildlife park, Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale is about 19 minutes' drive from Bittern. Frankston is the closest bigger centre to Bittern, about 23 minutes away by car, and has 27 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Bittern's 3 local playgrounds. Free days on the Western Port Bay Trail and in Bittern's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Bittern, Hastings has 11 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Crib Point, next to Bittern, has 4 playgrounds. Somers, next to Bittern, has 3 playgrounds. Next door to Bittern, Balnarring has 3 playgrounds. Tuerong and Hmas Cerberus also border Bittern.
