Playground on Frankston-Flinders Road

Playground in Bittern · Basic info

Playground on Frankston-Flinders Road is a public playground in Bittern, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire area. The Western Port Bay Trail passes about 70 m away, and Peppy's Pizza is about 50 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and trains stop at Bittern Railway Station, a 3-minute walk away.
