Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Crossley in the Moyne area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Port Fairy to Warrnambool Rail Trail crosses Crossley.

Getting around

There's no train station in Crossley. The closest to Crossley is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 8 stops in Crossley. By car from Crossley, Warrnambool is about 20 minutes away and Portland about 70 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Crossley is Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool, about 23 minutes' drive. For a library, Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool is about 21 minutes' drive from Crossley. The closest beach to Crossley is Killarney Beach in Killarney, about 8 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Koroit Youth Space in Koroit is about 6 minutes' drive from Crossley. The closest cinema to Crossley is Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Crossley is The Bowl in Warrnambool, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Crossley is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 34 minutes' drive. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Crossley, about 20 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Crossley, the nearest places for kids by road are Koroit Youth Space in Koroit (6 min), Killarney Beach in Killarney (8 min), Reef Point Beach in Port Fairy (7 min), the playground at Kirkstall Recreation Reserve in Kirkstall (9 min), Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy (14 min) and the playground at Port Fairy Coastal Reserve in Port Fairy (14 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Crossley on the Port Fairy to Warrnambool Rail Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Crossley, Killarney has a beach. Koroit, next to Crossley, has 1 playground and a skate park. Kirkstall, next to Crossley, has 1 playground. Toolong and Rosebrook also border Crossley.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Crossley, without traffic.
