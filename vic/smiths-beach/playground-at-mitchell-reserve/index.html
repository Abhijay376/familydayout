Playground at Mitchell Reserve

Playground in Smiths Beach · Basic info

Playground at Mitchell Reserve is a public playground in Smiths Beach, in the Bass Coast Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. You'll find a public toilet about 740 m from the playground, and coaches stop at Dolphin Dr/Back Beach Rd, a 8-minute walk away.
