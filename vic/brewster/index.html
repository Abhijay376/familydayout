Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Brewster in the Pyrenees area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Brewster. The closest to Brewster is Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Brewster, so most trips start by car. By car from Brewster, Ballarat is about 32 minutes away, Ararat about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Brewster is Beaufort Pool in Beaufort, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest library to Brewster is Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort, about 16 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort is about 16 minutes' drive from Brewster. The closest cinema to Brewster is Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central, about 32 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 36 minutes' drive from Brewster. For a zoo or wildlife park, Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East is about 35 minutes' drive from Brewster. Ballarat is the closest bigger centre to Brewster, about 32 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Brewster are the playground at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (16 min), the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (16 min), the playground at Wotherspoon Reserve in Beaufort (16 min), Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort (16 min), Beaufort Pool in Beaufort (17 min) and Linton BMX park in Linton (39 min).

Best for
