Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Glenbrae in the Pyrenees area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Glenbrae. The closest to Glenbrae is Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line), about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glenbrae, so most trips start by car. By car from Glenbrae, Ballarat is about 43 minutes away, Maryborough about 47 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Glenbrae is Beaufort Pool in Beaufort, about 35 minutes' drive. For a library, Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort is about 34 minutes' drive from Glenbrae. For a skate park, the skate park at Alexander Park in Learmonth is about 24 minutes' drive from Glenbrae. The closest cinema to Glenbrae is Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central, about 44 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Ballarat, about 43 minutes from Glenbrae by car, with 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Glenbrae, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Toll Bar Park in Lexton (20 min), the skate park at Alexander Park in Learmonth (24 min), the playground at Learmonth Recreation Reserve in Learmonth (25 min), the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (34 min), Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort (34 min) and Beaufort Pool in Beaufort (35 min).
