Benloch in the Macedon Ranges area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Benloch is Langley I96 Bushland Reserve, covering about 73 hectares. Langley I97 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Benloch. The closest to Benloch is Woodend Railway Station (V/Line), about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Benloch, so most trips start by car. By car from Benloch, Kyneton is about 42 minutes away, Seymour about 60 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 81 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Benloch is Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest library to Benloch is Romsey Library in Romsey, about 19 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Romsey Skate Park in Romsey is about 20 minutes' drive from Benloch. Kyneton is the closest bigger centre to Benloch, about 42 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Benloch are Romsey Library in Romsey (19 min), Romsey Skate Park in Romsey (20 min), the playground at Hanging Rock Reserve in Newham (33 min), the playground at Pyalong Recreation Reserve in Pyalong (41 min), Kyneton Museum in Kyneton (42 min) and Kyneton Library in Kyneton (42 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Benloch.

Nearby suburbs

Baynton also borders Benloch.
