Landmarks

Balmoral Crescent Reserve is the largest mapped park in Eastwood, at about 1 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Eastwood. The Bairnsdale LCN and Tulaba Track all pass through Eastwood. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Eastwood for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Eastwood. The closest to Eastwood is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 1.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 3 stops in Eastwood. By car from Eastwood, Bairnsdale is about 7 minutes away and Lakes Entrance about 36 minutes, without traffic.
