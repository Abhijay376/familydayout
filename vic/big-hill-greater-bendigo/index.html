Big Hill (Greater Bendigo) is in the Greater Bendigo area, and our data lists 1 playground in the suburb.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Big Hill (Greater Bendigo). For walks and rides, Goldfields Track, Leanganook Track and Bendigo Creek Trail (North) cross Big Hill (Greater Bendigo).

Getting around

Big Hill (Greater Bendigo) has no train station; Kangaroo Flat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 4.9 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Big Hill (Greater Bendigo), so most trips start by car. By car from Big Hill (Greater Bendigo), Bendigo is about 13 minutes away, Castlemaine about 22 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Faith Leech Aquatic Centre in Bendigo is about 14 minutes' drive from Big Hill (Greater Bendigo). For a library, Kangaroo Flat Library in Kangaroo Flat is about 6 minutes' drive from Big Hill (Greater Bendigo). For a skate park, Long Gully BMX Track in Long Gully is about 13 minutes' drive from Big Hill (Greater Bendigo). For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 13 minutes' drive from Big Hill (Greater Bendigo). The closest indoor play centre to Big Hill (Greater Bendigo) is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 10 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 18 minutes' drive from Big Hill (Greater Bendigo). Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Big Hill (Greater Bendigo), about 13 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Big Hill (Greater Bendigo), the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Jerribong Way Reserve in Kangaroo Flat (4 min), William Drive Playspace in Kangaroo Flat (6 min), Kangaroo Flat Library in Kangaroo Flat (6 min), Intagolf in Golden Gully (10 min), Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo (13 min) and Bendigo RSL Museum in Bendigo (12 min).

Best for

Free days on the Goldfields Track and in Big Hill (Greater Bendigo)'s parks.
