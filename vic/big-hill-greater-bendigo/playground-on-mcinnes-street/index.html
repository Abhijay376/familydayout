Playground on McInnes Street

Playground in Big Hill · Basic info

Playground on McInnes Street is a public playground in Big Hill, in the Greater Bendigo City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 2.8 km away. It's easiest by car, about 2 minutes from Big Hill.
