Playground at Yan Yean Reservoir Park

Playground in Yan Yean · Official details

Playground at Yan Yean Reservoir Park is a public playground in Yan Yean, in the Whittlesea City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 280 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 4 minutes from Yan Yean.
