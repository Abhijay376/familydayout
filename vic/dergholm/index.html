Dergholm in the West Wimmera area has 6 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Dergholm State Park is the largest mapped park in Dergholm, at about 10,884 hectares. Roseneath Reference Area and Dorodong State Forest are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Dergholm include Dergholm Recreation Reserve, Dergholm Streamside Reserve and Glenelg River - Dergholm Camping Area 1 (CLWF). Our data shows 1 public toilet in Dergholm. For camping, there's Glenelg River - Dergholm Camping Area 1 (CLWF). For walks and rides, Bilstons Tree Walk crosses Dergholm.

Getting around

Dergholm has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 158 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dergholm, so most trips start by car. By car from Dergholm, Hamilton is about 1.5 hours away and Portland about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Dergholm is Casterton Pool in Casterton, about 39 minutes' drive. From Dergholm, the nearest places for kids by road are Casterton Pool in Casterton (39 min) and Gorman's art gallery in Casterton (39 min).

Best for

Free days on the Bilstons Tree Walk and in Dergholm's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Dorodong and Chetwynd also border Dergholm.
