Ozenkadnook in the West Wimmera area has 5 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Morea State Forest is the largest mapped park in Ozenkadnook, at about 3,738 hectares. Waits State Forest and Bulls State Forest are the next largest. Ozenkadnook also has Pahls State Forest and Lake Carpolac Lake Reserve.

Getting around

Ozenkadnook has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 151 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Ozenkadnook, so most trips start by car. By car from Ozenkadnook, Horsham is about 73 minutes away and Hamilton about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Goroke Swimming Centre in Goroke is about 16 minutes' drive from Ozenkadnook. For a library, West Wimmera Shire Goroke Library in Goroke is about 17 minutes' drive from Ozenkadnook. From Ozenkadnook, the nearest places for kids by road are Goroke Swimming Centre in Goroke (16 min), Goroke Little Desert Nature Playspace in Goroke (17 min), West Wimmera Shire Goroke Library in Goroke (17 min), West Wimmera Community Association - Red Tail Gallery in Edenhope (20 min), Dachshund Museum & Gallery in Edenhope (20 min) and the playground at Lions Park in Edenhope (20 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Ozenkadnook.

Nearby suburbs

Goroke, next to Ozenkadnook, has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Minimay, Karnak and Bringalbert also border Ozenkadnook.
