Traralgon East is in the Latrobe area, and our data lists 2 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Robert Farmer Park is the largest mapped park in Traralgon East, at about 2 hectares. Anzac Memorial Park is the next largest. Playgrounds in Traralgon East include the playground at Robert Farmer Park and the playground at Southdown Way Reserve / Ellavale Drainage Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Traralgon East. For walks and rides, Gippsland Plains Rail Trail crosses Traralgon East. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Traralgon East for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Traralgon East has no train station; Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 4.3 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 8, 9, 44 and 45 stop in Traralgon East. V/Line coaches stop in Traralgon East. By car from Traralgon East, Traralgon is about 7 minutes away, Sale about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon is about 7 minutes' drive from Traralgon East. For a library, Morwell Library in Morwell is about 20 minutes' drive from Traralgon East. The closest skate park to Traralgon East is Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Traralgon East is Village 8 in Morwell, about 17 minutes' drive. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Traralgon East, about 7 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. From Traralgon East, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Macey Park in Traralgon (6 min), the playground at Think Big Reserve in Traralgon (6 min), Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon (7 min), Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon (7 min), Glengarry Skatepark in Glengarry (12 min) and Village 8 in Morwell (17 min).

Best for

Free days on the Gippsland Plains Rail Trail and in Traralgon East's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Traralgon, next to Traralgon East, has 38 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Traralgon East, Glengarry has 1 playground and a skate park.
