Lyre Cr Wetland Reserve Playground

Playground in Burnside · Basic info

Lyre Cr Wetland Reserve Playground is a public playground in Burnside, in the Melton City area. The Kororoit Creek Trail is about 10 m away, and Illawong Terrace Reserve Playground is another option about 390 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 2.0 km from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 4 minutes from Burnside.
