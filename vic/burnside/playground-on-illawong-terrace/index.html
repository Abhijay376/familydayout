Illawong Terrace Reserve Playground

Playground in Burnside · Basic info

Illawong Terrace Reserve Playground in Burnside is a public playground in the Melton City area. The Kororoit Creek Trail passes about 90 m away, and Playground at Isabella Williams Reserve is another option about 290 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.6 km away, and buses 426 and 456 stop at Carinya Bvd/Westwood Dr, a 6-minute walk away.
