Montmarte Boulevard Reserve Playground

Playground in Burnside · Basic info

Montmarte Boulevard Reserve Playground in Burnside is a public playground in the Melton City area. The Kororoit Creek Trail passes about 190 m away, and Pilbara Ave Reserve Playground is another option about 510 m away. There's a public toilet about 2.5 km away, and bus 426 stops at Fydler Ave/Westwood Dr, a 5-minute walk away.
