Botanic Ridge in the Casey area has 3 playgrounds and 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Homestead Park is the largest mapped park in Botanic Ridge, at about 1 hectares. Damon Green is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Botanic Ridge. Botanic Ridge Village is the local shopping centre. Our data lists 3 cafes and family restaurants in Botanic Ridge for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Botanic Ridge. The closest to Botanic Ridge is Cranbourne Railway Station (metro), about 5.8 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 792 and 795 stop in Botanic Ridge. By car from Botanic Ridge, Cranbourne is about 9 minutes away, Frankston about 15 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 45 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Botanic Ridge is JUMP! Swim Schools in Cranbourne West, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest library to Botanic Ridge is Cranbourne Library in Cranbourne East, about 10 minutes' drive. For a beach, Seaford Beach in Seaford is about 17 minutes' drive from Botanic Ridge. The closest skate park to Botanic Ridge is the pump track on Depeche Way in Cranbourne South, about 5 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Frankston is about 13 minutes' drive from Botanic Ridge. The closest indoor play centre to Botanic Ridge is Zone Bowling in Frankston, about 11 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale is about 10 minutes' drive from Botanic Ridge. Cranbourne is the closest bigger centre to Botanic Ridge, about 9 minutes away by car, and has 27 playgrounds.

Nearby suburbs

Cranbourne, next to Botanic Ridge, has 27 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Botanic Ridge, Cranbourne West has 21 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Cranbourne South, next to Botanic Ridge, has 5 playgrounds and a skate park. Junction Village, next to Botanic Ridge, has 3 playgrounds. Devon Meadows also borders Botanic Ridge.
