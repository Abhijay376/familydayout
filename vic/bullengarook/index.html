Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bullengarook is Bullengarook Recreation Reserve, covering about 10 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets and 1 off-leash dog park in Bullengarook. For walks and rides, Wombat MTB Trail One, Wombat MTB Trail Two and Through the Looking Glass cross Bullengarook.

Getting around

Bullengarook has no train station; Macedon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 7.9 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 77 stops in Bullengarook. By car from Bullengarook, Bacchus Marsh is about 22 minutes away, Melton about 31 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 55 minutes, without traffic.
