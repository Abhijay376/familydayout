Arbuckle is in the Wellington area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Mount Darling - Snowy Bluff Wilderness Zone is the largest mapped park in Arbuckle, at about 42,573 hectares. For walks and rides, McMillans Walking Track, Moroka River Track and Mount Darling Walking Track cross Arbuckle.

Getting around

There's no train station in Arbuckle. The closest to Arbuckle is Stratford Railway Station (V/Line), about 68 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Arbuckle, so most trips start by car. By car from Arbuckle, Sale is about 3 hours away and Traralgon about 3 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Free days on the McMillans Walking Track and in Arbuckle's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Crooked River, Buragwonduc and Billabong also border Arbuckle.
