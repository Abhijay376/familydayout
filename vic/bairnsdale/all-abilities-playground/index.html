All Abilities Playground

Playground in Bairnsdale · Basic info

This is a public playground in Bairnsdale. The Bairnsdale LCN is about 90 m away, and for food, Lantern Palace is about 100 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and coaches stop at Bailey St/Princes Hwy, a 7-minute walk away.
