Sun Cinema Bairnsdale

Cinema in Bairnsdale · Official details

Sun Cinema Bairnsdale is a cinema in Bairnsdale, in the East Gippsland Shire Council area, good for school-age kids and teens. Bairnsdale Railway Station/MacLeod St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 214 m away. Opening hours: Please note that all films are designated NFT (No Free Tickets) after 5pm Saturday,; No discount/group booking prices are available for sessions after 5pm Saturday or on Public Holidays..
