Deledio Reserve

Park in Dunolly · Basic info

Deledio Reserve is a park in Dunolly, in the Central Goldfields Shire Council area, good for all ages. Bull St/Broadway (coach) is a 10-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 760 m away.
