Dunolly Barkley Street Bushland Reserve

Park in Dunolly · Basic info

Dunolly Barkley Street Bushland Reserve is a park in Dunolly, in the Central Goldfields Shire Council area, good for all ages. Bull St/Broadway (coach) is a 15-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1112 m away.
