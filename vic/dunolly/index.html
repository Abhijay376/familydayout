Dunolly is in the Central Goldfields area, and our data lists 7 parks and reserves in the suburb. Dunolly also has a pool and a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

Deledio Reserve is the largest mapped park in Dunolly, at about 11 hectares. Wild Duck Dam Bushland Reserve and Dunolly Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Dunolly include Barp Bushland Reserve, Rene Fox Memorial Garden and Dunolly Barkley Street Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Dunolly. Places to visit in Dunolly include Dunolly Museum and Dunolly Swimming Pool. For walks and rides, Dunolly Cycle Tracks, Dunolly - Havelock Gold Rush Cycle Trail and Laanecoorie Cycling Track cross Dunolly. Our data lists 3 cafes and family restaurants in Dunolly for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Dunolly. The closest to Dunolly is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Dunolly. By car from Dunolly, Maryborough is about 25 minutes away, Bendigo about 53 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Dunolly is Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough, about 25 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 25 minutes from Dunolly by car, with a library. The closest places for kids by road from Dunolly are Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (25 min), the playground at Market Reserve in Carisbrook (30 min), Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground in Talbot (41 min), Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (42 min), Athenaeum Library in Maldon (38 min) and Courthouse in Avoca (31 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Dunolly. Free days on the Dunolly Cycle Tracks and in Dunolly's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Eddington, Dunluce, Bromley and Bet Bet also border Dunolly.
