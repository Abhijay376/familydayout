Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Bailieston, in the Strathbogie area.

Landmarks

Reedy Lake, Nagambie Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Bailieston, at about 1,316 hectares. Baileston Historic and Cultural Features Reserve and Baileston Bushland Reserve are the next largest. For walks and rides, Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks crosses Bailieston.

Getting around

Bailieston has no train station; Nagambie Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bailieston, so most trips start by car. By car from Bailieston, Seymour is about 40 minutes away, Shepparton about 62 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Nagambie Pool in Nagambie is about 18 minutes' drive from Bailieston. The closest library to Bailieston is Nagambie Library in Nagambie, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Bailieston is the skate park at Lions Park in Rushworth, about 23 minutes' drive. Seymour is the closest bigger centre to Bailieston, about 40 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Bailieston, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Goulburn River Heritage River in Nagambie (18 min), Nagambie Recreation Reserve Playground in Nagambie (18 min), Nagambie Pool in Nagambie (18 min), Nagambie Historical Society in Nagambie (20 min), Nagambie Library in Nagambie (20 min) and Rushworth Library in Rushworth (22 min).

Best for

Free days on the Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks and in Bailieston's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Bailieston, Nagambie has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Kirwans Bridge also borders Bailieston.
