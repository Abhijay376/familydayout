Menzies Reserve

Park in Dandenong North · Official details

Menzies Reserve is a park in Dandenong North, in the Greater Dandenong City Council area, good for all ages. Menzies Reserve/Menzies Ave (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 65 m away.

Questions parents ask

Where is Menzies Reserve?
Menzies Reserve is in Dandenong North, Victoria.
Is Menzies Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Menzies Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 65 m away.
How do I get to Menzies Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Menzies Reserve/Menzies Ave (routes 850), a 1-minute walk.
