Playground at Golding Court Reserve

Playground in Dandenong North · Basic info

Playground at Golding Court Reserve is a public playground in Dandenong North, in the Greater Dandenong City area. Big Papa's Pizza is about 450 m away if you need food, and Playground at Mckeon Reserve is another option about 340 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 840 m away, and bus 802 stops at Fletcher Rd/Loch Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
