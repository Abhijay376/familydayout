Playground at Fillmore Reserve

Playground in Dandenong North · Basic info

Playground at Fillmore Reserve in Dandenong North is a public playground in the Greater Dandenong City area. The EastLink Trail is about 100 m away, and Playground at Gately Reserve is another option about 340 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and buses 802, 804 and 850 stop at Fillmore Rd/Gladstone Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
