Playground at Blackmore-Madison Reserve

Playground in Dandenong North · Basic info

Playground at Blackmore-Madison Reserve is a public playground in Dandenong North, in the Greater Dandenong City area. Playground on Portland Street is another option about 360 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.4 km from the playground, and buses 802, 804, 850 and 862 stop at Blackmore St/Police Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
