Fraser Street Reserve

Park in Dandenong North · Basic info

Fraser Street Reserve is a park in Dandenong North, in the Greater Dandenong City Council area, good for all ages. Judith St/Stud Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 951 m away.
