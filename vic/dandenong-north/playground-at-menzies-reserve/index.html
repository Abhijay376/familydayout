Playground at Menzies Reserve

Playground in Dandenong North · Official details

Playground at Menzies Reserve is a public playground in Dandenong North, in the Greater Dandenong City area. Playground at Dandenong Day Nursery (East Side) is another option about 290 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 60 m from the playground, and bus 850 stops at Menzies Reserve/Menzies Ave, a 1-minute walk away.
