Tirhatuan Park

Park in Dandenong North · Official details

Tirhatuan Park is a park in Dandenong North, in the Greater Dandenong City Council area, good for all ages. Murray Rd/Outlook Dr (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 448 m away.
