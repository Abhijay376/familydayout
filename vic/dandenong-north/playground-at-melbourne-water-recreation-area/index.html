Playground at Melbourne Water Recreation Area

Playground in Dandenong North · Official details

Playground at Melbourne Water Recreation Area is a public playground in Dandenong North, in the Greater Dandenong City area. The Dandenong Creek Trail passes about 40 m away, and Subway is about 480 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 170 m away, and buses 862 and 901 stop at McFees Rd/Stud Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
