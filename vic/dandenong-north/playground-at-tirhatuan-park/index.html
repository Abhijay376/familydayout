Playground at Tirhatuan Park

Playground in Dandenong North · Official details

Playground at Tirhatuan Park is a public playground in Dandenong North, in the Greater Dandenong City area. The Dandenong Creek Trail is about 140 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 120 m from the playground, and bus 862 stops at Kriegel Way/Outlook Dr, a 3-minute walk away.
