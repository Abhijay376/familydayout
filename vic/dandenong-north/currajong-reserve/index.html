Currajong Reserve

Park in Dandenong North · Basic info

Currajong Reserve is a park in Dandenong North, in the Greater Dandenong City Council area, good for all ages. Frognal Dr/Bakers Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1419 m away.
