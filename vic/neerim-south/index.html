Neerim South is in the Baw Baw area, and our data lists 4 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Neerim South has a skate park.

Landmarks

Tarago River, Neerim South Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Neerim South, at about 10 hectares. Neerim G209 Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Neerim South include Latrobe Horse Visitor Area and Latrobe Trail Bike Visitor Area. Our data shows 6 public toilets in Neerim South. Picnic areas in Neerim South include Latrobe Trail Bike Visitor Area. For older kids in Neerim South there's Craig Watts Skate Park. For walks and rides, Rokeby to Neerim Trail crosses Neerim South.

Getting around

Neerim South has no train station; Warragul Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 89 stops in Neerim South. By car from Neerim South, Warragul is about 20 minutes away, Pakenham about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Neerim South is Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul, about 22 minutes' drive. For a library, Warragul Library in Warragul is about 20 minutes' drive from Neerim South. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 20 minutes' drive from Neerim South. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 21 minutes' drive from Neerim South. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Neerim South is Wildlife Trail in Tynong North, about 33 minutes' drive. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Neerim South, about 20 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Neerim South, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Ellen Close Linear Reserve in Warragul (18 min), Charles Street Park in Warragul (19 min), Warragul Library in Warragul (20 min), Warragul Cinema in Warragul (20 min), The Alley Warragul in Warragul (21 min) and Bmx in Warragul (21 min).

Best for

Teens who skate or ride, with Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South. Free days on the Rokeby to Neerim Trail and in Neerim South's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Neerim South, Rokeby has 1 playground. Gentle Annie, Crossover and Buln Buln East also border Neerim South.
