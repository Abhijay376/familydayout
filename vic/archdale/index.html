Archdale in the Central Goldfields area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Archdale is Archdale Streamside Reserve, covering about 30 hectares. Dalyenong I60 Bushland Reserve and Puzzle Flat Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

Archdale has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 33 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Archdale, so most trips start by car. By car from Archdale, Maryborough is about 47 minutes away, Bendigo about 81 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Archdale is Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly, about 29 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 47 minutes from Archdale by car, with a library. From Archdale, the nearest places for kids by road are Stuart Mill Recreation Reserve Playground in Stuart Mill (32 min), Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (29 min), Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (29 min), Bi-Centennial Park Playground in St Arnaud (42 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (44 min) and Courthouse in Avoca (41 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Archdale.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Archdale, Bealiba has 1 playground. Archdale Junction also borders Archdale.
