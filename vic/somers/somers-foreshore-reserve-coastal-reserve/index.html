Somers Foreshore Reserve Coastal Reserve

Park in Somers · Basic info

Somers Foreshore Reserve Coastal Reserve is a park in Somers, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire Council area, good for all ages. Somers Foreshore Reserve/The Boulevard (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 30 m away with a baby change table.
