Coolart Historic and Cultural Features Reserve

Park in Somers · Basic info

Coolart Historic and Cultural Features Reserve is a park in Somers, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire Council area, good for all ages. Coolart Wetlands/Lord Somers Rd (bus) is a 9-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 169 m away.
