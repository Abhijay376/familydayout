Morwell is in the Latrobe area, and our data lists 14 playgrounds and 36 parks and reserves in the suburb. On top of that, Morwell has a library, a museum or gallery, a cinema and a skate park.

Landmarks

Crinigan Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Morwell, at about 43 hectares. Waterhole Creek Reserve and Airlie Bank Reserve are the next largest. Morwell also has Morwell Park, Bulga Reserve and Alexandra Park. Playgrounds around Morwell include the playground at Berg Street Reserve, the playground at Morwell Town Common and the playground at Well Street Reserve. Our data shows 13 public toilets in Morwell. In Morwell you'll also find Powerworks, Village 8 and Morwell Library. For older kids in Morwell there's Morwell Skate Park. Mid Valley Shopping Centre is the local shopping centre. The Traralgon to Morwell shared path and Waterhole Creek Track all pass through Morwell. Our data lists 9 cafes and family restaurants in Morwell for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Morwell has a train station: Morwell Railway Station (V/Line: Bairnsdale - Melbourne via Sale & Traralgon and Traralgon - Melbourne via Morwell, Moe & Pakenham). Buses on the 1, 2, 8 and 20 routes stop in Morwell. V/Line coaches stop in Morwell. By car from Morwell, Traralgon is about 14 minutes away, Warragul about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Morwell is Latrobe Leisure - Churchill in Churchill, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Morwell is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 38 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 14 playgrounds across Morwell. Rainy days at the library, cinema and museum or gallery in Morwell. Teens who skate or ride, with Morwell Skate Park in Morwell. Free days on the Traralgon to Morwell shared path and in Morwell's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Morwell, Traralgon has 38 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Morwell, Yallourn has 1 playground. Hazelwood North, Hazelwood and Driffield also border Morwell.
