Playground at Morwell Town Common

Playground in Morwell · Basic info

Playground at Morwell Town Common in Morwell is a public playground in the Latrobe City area. The Traralgon to Morwell shared path is about 320 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 270 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 230 m from the playground, and bus 20 stops at Tarwin St/Elgin St, a 3-minute walk away.
