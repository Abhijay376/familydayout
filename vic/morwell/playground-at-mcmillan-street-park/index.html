Playground at McMillan Street Park

Playground in Morwell · Basic info

Playground at McMillan Street Park in Morwell is a public playground in the Latrobe City area. The Traralgon to Morwell shared path passes about 150 m away, and McDonald's is about 350 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 900 m away, and buses 1, 2 and 8 stop at Monash Way/Princes Dr, a 6-minute walk away.
