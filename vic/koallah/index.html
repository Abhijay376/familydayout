Koallah is in the Corangamite area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Koallah is Lake Purrumbete Wildlife Reserve (no hunting), covering about 537 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Koallah. The closest to Koallah is Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line), about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Koallah, so most trips start by car. By car from Koallah, Colac is about 32 minutes away, Warrnambool about 68 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac is about 35 minutes' drive from Koallah. The closest skate park to Koallah is Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown, about 16 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Colac Cinemas in Colac is about 33 minutes' drive from Koallah. The closest indoor play centre to Koallah is Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac, about 33 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 32 minutes from Koallah by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Koallah are Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (16 min), the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve in Camperdown (25 min), the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (31 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (33 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (33 min) and Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Koallah.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Koallah, Camperdown has 3 playgrounds and a skate park. Weerite, next to Koallah, has 1 playground. South Purrumbete and Pomborneit also border Koallah.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Koallah, without traffic.
