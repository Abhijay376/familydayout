Everton Upper is in the Wangaratta area, and our data lists 5 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Everton I4 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Everton Upper, at about 36 hectares. Murmungee I76 Bushland Reserve and Everton I78 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Everton Upper also has Everton I6 Bushland Reserve and Everton I74 Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Everton Upper. The Murray to Mountains Rail Trail: Wangaratta – Bright and Flame Trees Trail all pass through Everton Upper.

Getting around

Everton Upper has no train station; Springhurst Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 25 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Everton Upper, so most trips start by car. By car from Everton Upper, Wangaratta is about 23 minutes away and Wodonga about 44 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Beechworth Swimming Pool in Beechworth is about 11 minutes' drive from Everton Upper. The closest library to Everton Upper is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 21 minutes' drive. For a beach, Sandy Beach in Beechworth is about 13 minutes' drive from Everton Upper. The closest skate park to Everton Upper is the pump track on Alma Road in Beechworth, about 14 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 20 minutes' drive from Everton Upper. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 23 minutes from Everton Upper by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Everton Upper are the playground at Newtown Park in Beechworth (8 min), Beechworth Swimming Pool in Beechworth (11 min), Beechworth Contemporary Art Space in Beechworth (10 min), The Burke Museum in Beechworth (11 min), Sandy Beach in Beechworth (13 min) and the pump track on Alma Road in Beechworth (14 min).

Best for

Free days on the Murray to Mountains Rail Trail: Wangaratta – Bright and in Everton Upper's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Beechworth, next to Everton Upper, has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. Eldorado, next to Everton Upper, has 1 playground. Everton and Bowmans Forest also border Everton Upper.
