Playground at Meadowlink Community Park

Playground in Broadmeadows · Basic info

Playground at Meadowlink Community Park in Broadmeadows is a public playground in the Hume City area. The Meadowlink Path is about 20 m away, and Hume Global Learning Centre - Broadmeadows is about 460 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 740 m from the playground, and bus 540 stops at Broadmeadows Station/Railway Cres, a 5-minute walk away.
