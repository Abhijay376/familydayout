Playground at Ripplebrook Reserve

Playground in Broadmeadows · Basic info

This is a public playground in Broadmeadows. The Broadmeadows Valley Trail is about 40 m away, and Playground on Merricks Street is another option about 310 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 890 m from the playground, and buses 484, 901, 902 and 959 stop at Ripplebrook Dr/Johnstone St, a 5-minute walk away.
