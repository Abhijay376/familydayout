Hoyts

Cinema in Broadmeadows · Basic info

Hoyts is a cinema in Broadmeadows, in the Hume City Council area, good for school-age kids and teens. Broadmeadows Town Park/Pearcedale Pde (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 104 m away.
