Greens Creek in the Northern Grampians area has 8 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Greens Creek Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Greens Creek, at about 39 hectares. Vinelea Bushland Reserve and Greens Creek Streamside Reserve are the next largest. Greens Creek also has Malakoff Bushland Reserve, Pickfords Bushland Reserve and McSparrons Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Greens Creek has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 37 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Greens Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Greens Creek, Stawell is about 16 minutes away, Ararat about 42 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Greens Creek is Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell, about 15 minutes' drive. For a library, Ararat Library in Ararat is about 42 minutes' drive from Greens Creek. For a beach, Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans is about 37 minutes' drive from Greens Creek. For a skate park, the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell is about 20 minutes' drive from Greens Creek. The closest cinema to Greens Creek is Astor Cinema in Ararat, about 42 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell is about 18 minutes' drive from Greens Creek. The nearest larger centre is Stawell, about 16 minutes from Greens Creek by car, with 6 playgrounds and a pool. From Greens Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are Navarre Playground in Navarre (14 min), Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell (15 min), Stawell Skate Park in Stawell (16 min), Railway Station Art and Crafts Gallery in Stawell (17 min), Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell (18 min) and the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell (20 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Greens Creek.

Nearby suburbs
