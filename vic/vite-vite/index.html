Vite Vite is in the Corangamite area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Vite Vite, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Vite Vite. The closest to Vite Vite is Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line), about 39 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Vite Vite, so most trips start by car. By car from Vite Vite, Ballarat is about 78 minutes away, Colac about 79 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Vite Vite is Lismore Pool in Lismore, about 25 minutes' drive. For a library, Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum is about 17 minutes' drive from Vite Vite. From Vite Vite, the nearest places for kids by road are Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum (17 min), Lismore Pool in Lismore (25 min), the playground at Simpson Park in Streatham (37 min), Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (52 min) and the playground at Cressy Picnic Area in Cressy (47 min).
