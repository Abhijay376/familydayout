Kingswim Langwarrin

Pool or water play in Langwarrin · Official details

Kingswim Langwarrin is a pool or water play in Langwarrin, in the Frankston City Council area, good for all ages. Hornsby Dr/Southgateway (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 940 m away.
