Cranbourne-Frankston Road Linear Reserve

Playground in Langwarrin · Basic info

Cranbourne-Frankston Road Linear Reserve is a public playground in Langwarrin, in the Frankston City area. Kingswim Langwarrin is about 450 m away, and for food, The Classic Pizza House is about 80 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 600 m from the playground, and buses 789 and 791 stop at Josephine St/Cranbourne-Frankston Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
