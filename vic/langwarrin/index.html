Langwarrin in the Frankston area has 22 playgrounds and 22 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, Langwarrin has a pool, a museum or gallery and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Langwarrin is Langwarrin Flora and Fauna Reserve, covering about 215 hectares. Cruden Farm and Little Boggy Creek Reserve are the next largest. Langwarrin also has Long Reserve, Myrtle Reserve and Fernwood Reserve. Playgrounds in Langwarrin include Teenrager, Cavill Close Reserve and the playground at Long Reserve. Our data shows 11 public toilets in Langwarrin. In Langwarrin you'll also find McClelland Sculpture Park and Kingswim Langwarrin. For older kids in Langwarrin there's Langwarrin Skatepark. Langwarrin Shopping Centre and The Gateway Shopping Centre are the main shopping centres. The Peninsula Link Trail, Frankston-Baxter Trail and StringyBark Track all pass through Langwarrin. Our data lists 16 cafes and family restaurants in Langwarrin for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Langwarrin. The closest to Langwarrin is Leawarra Railway Station (metro), about 3.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 771, 776, 777, 789, 790 and 791 stop in Langwarrin. By car from Langwarrin, Frankston is about 7 minutes away, Cranbourne about 14 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 45 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Langwarrin is Frankston Library in Frankston, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Langwarrin is Frankston Beach in Frankston, about 10 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Frankston is about 5 minutes' drive from Langwarrin. The closest indoor play centre to Langwarrin is Zone Bowling in Frankston, about 4 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Langwarrin is Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale, about 14 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 22 playgrounds across Langwarrin. Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Langwarrin. Teens who skate or ride, with Langwarrin Skatepark in Langwarrin. Free days on the Peninsula Link Trail and in Langwarrin's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Frankston, next to Langwarrin, has 27 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Langwarrin, Carrum Downs has 22 playgrounds and a skate park. Frankston South, next to Langwarrin, has 15 playgrounds. Frankston North, next to Langwarrin, has 7 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Skye, Cranbourne South, Pearcedale and Langwarrin South also border Langwarrin.
