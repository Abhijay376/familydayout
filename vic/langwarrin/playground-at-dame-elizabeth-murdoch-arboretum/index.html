Playground at Dame Elizabeth Murdoch Arboretum

Playground in Langwarrin · Basic info

Playground at Dame Elizabeth Murdoch Arboretum is a public playground in Langwarrin, in the Frankston City area. Playground at Myrtle Reserve is another option about 310 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and buses 771, 789, 790 and 791 stop at Dame Elisabeth Murdoch Arboretum/Cranbourne-Frankston Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
