Playground at Myrtle Reserve

Playground in Langwarrin · Basic info

Playground at Myrtle Reserve is a public playground in Langwarrin, in the Frankston City area. The Peninsula Link Trail is about 360 m away, and Playground at Dame Elizabeth Murdoch Arboretum is another option about 310 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 560 m from the playground, and bus 777 stops at Beech St/McClelland Dr, a 3-minute walk away.
