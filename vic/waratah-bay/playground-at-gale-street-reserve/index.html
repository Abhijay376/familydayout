Playground at Gale Street Reserve

Playground in Waratah Bay · Basic info

Playground at Gale Street Reserve is a public playground in Waratah Bay, in the South Gippsland Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground. It's easiest by car, about 1 minute from Waratah Bay.
