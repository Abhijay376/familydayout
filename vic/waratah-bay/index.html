Our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in Waratah Bay, in the South Gippsland area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Waratah Bay is Gale Street Reserve, covering about 2 hectares. Playgrounds around Waratah Bay include the playground at Gale Street Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Waratah Bay.

Getting around

Waratah Bay has no train station; Yarragon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 67 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Waratah Bay, so most trips start by car. By car from Waratah Bay, Leongatha is about 50 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 61 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Foster Outdoor Pool in Foster is about 26 minutes' drive from Waratah Bay. For a library, Foster Library in Foster is about 26 minutes' drive from Waratah Bay. The closest beach to Waratah Bay is Sandy Point (Waratah Bay) in Sandy Point, about 13 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora is about 38 minutes' drive from Waratah Bay. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Waratah Bay, about 50 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Waratah Bay, the nearest places for kids by road are Sandy Point (Waratah Bay) in Sandy Point (13 min), Ride the Wild Goat in Fish Creek (21 min), Lance Moon Park in Yanakie (23 min), Foster Outdoor Pool in Foster (26 min), Stockyard Gallery in Foster (27 min) and Foster Library in Foster (26 min).
