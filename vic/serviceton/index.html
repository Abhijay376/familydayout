Serviceton in the West Wimmera area has 16 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Bangham Conservation Park is the largest mapped park in Serviceton, at about 867 hectares. Unnamed (No.HA1487) Heritage Agreement and Unnamed (No.HA1333) Heritage Agreement are the next largest. Serviceton also has Leeor I1 Bushland Reserve, Leeor I2 Bushland Reserve and Leeor I3 Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Serviceton.

Getting around

Serviceton has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 201 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Serviceton, so most trips start by car. By car from Serviceton, Horsham is about 2 hours away and Stawell about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Serviceton is Kaniva Library in Kaniva, about 23 minutes' drive. From Serviceton, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Band Park in Kaniva (22 min) and Kaniva Library in Kaniva (23 min).
