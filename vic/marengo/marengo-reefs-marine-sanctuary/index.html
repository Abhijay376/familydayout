Marengo Reefs Marine Sanctuary

Park in Marengo · Official details

Marengo Reefs Marine Sanctuary is a park in Marengo, in the Colac Otway Shire Council area, good for all ages. Harrison St/Marengo Crst (coach) is a 4-minute walk away.

Details

Good to know
Strong tidal currents when snorkelling; Do not approach Australian Fur Seals; Wear grippy shoes on rock platforms (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Marengo Reefs Marine Sanctuary?
Marengo Reefs Marine Sanctuary is in Marengo, Victoria.
Is Marengo Reefs Marine Sanctuary free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
How do I get to Marengo Reefs Marine Sanctuary by public transport?
The closest stop is Harrison St/Marengo Crst (routes Warrnambool - Melbourne via Apollo Bay & Geelong), a 4-minute walk.
