Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Bamawm, in the Campaspe area.

Landmarks

Bamawm Sports Ground is the largest mapped park in Bamawm, at about 6 hectares. Bamawm War Memorial Park is the next largest.

Getting around

Bamawm has no train station; Rochester Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bamawm, so most trips start by car. By car from Bamawm, Echuca is about 27 minutes away, Bendigo about 65 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Lockington Pool in Lockington is about 5 minutes' drive from Bamawm. For a library, Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca is about 28 minutes' drive from Bamawm. For a skate park, Moama Echuca Community Skate Park in Echuca is about 30 minutes' drive from Bamawm. For a cinema, Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca is about 28 minutes' drive from Bamawm. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 27 minutes from Bamawm by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Bamawm are Lockington Pool in Lockington (5 min), Lockington District Swimming Pool in Lockington (5 min), the playground at Apex Park in Rochester (20 min), the playground at Echuca Lions Park in Echuca (25 min), Holden Museum in Echuca (27 min) and Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca (28 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bamawm.

Nearby suburbs

Echuca, next to Bamawm, has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. Rochester, next to Bamawm, has 1 playground and a pool. Lockington, next to Bamawm, has a pool. Kotta, Echuca West, Diggora and Bamawm Extension also border Bamawm.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Bamawm, without traffic.
