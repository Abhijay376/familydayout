The Sisters is in the Moyne area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside The Sisters, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in The Sisters. The closest to The Sisters is Terang Railway Station (V/Line), about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside The Sisters, so most trips start by car. By car from The Sisters, Warrnambool is about 45 minutes away and Colac about 73 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to The Sisters is Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake, about 21 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake is about 21 minutes' drive from The Sisters. The closest zoo or wildlife park to The Sisters is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 34 minutes' drive. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to The Sisters, about 45 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From The Sisters, the nearest places for kids by road are Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake (21 min), the playground at Tea Tree Lake Reserve in Mortlake (21 min), Market Square Playground in Mortlake (21 min), Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake (21 min), Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (34 min) and Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (38 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from The Sisters.

Nearby suburbs

Mortlake, next to The Sisters, has 2 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Terang, next to The Sisters, has 1 playground. Garvoc, next to The Sisters, has 1 playground. Framlingham East, Framlingham and Ellerslie also border The Sisters.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of The Sisters, without traffic.
