Our data lists 2 playgrounds and 2 parks and reserves in Bellbridge, in the Towong area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bellbridge is Apex Park, covering about 3 hectares. Roy Williams Park is the next largest. Playgrounds around Bellbridge include the playground at Roy Williams Park. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Bellbridge.

Getting around

Bellbridge has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bellbridge, so most trips start by car. By car from Bellbridge, Wodonga is about 24 minutes away and Wangaratta about 72 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, WAVES in Wodonga is about 22 minutes' drive from Bellbridge. The closest library to Bellbridge is Albury LibraryMuseum in Gateway Island, about 21 minutes' drive. For a beach, Finns Beach in Bandiana is about 20 minutes' drive from Bellbridge. For a skate park, David Street Skate Park in Gateway Island is about 20 minutes' drive from Bellbridge. For a cinema, Regent Cinema in Gateway Island is about 20 minutes' drive from Bellbridge. Wodonga is the closest bigger centre to Bellbridge, about 24 minutes away by car, and has 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Bellbridge are The Bonegilla Migrant Experience in Bonegilla (13 min), Eastern Circuit Playground in Bonegilla (15 min), Finns Beach in Bandiana (20 min), Regent Cinema in Gateway Island (20 min), Murray Art Museum Albury (MAMA) in Gateway Island (20 min) and Albury LibraryMuseum in Gateway Island (21 min).
