Outtrim is in the South Gippsland area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Outtrim Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Outtrim, at about 9 hectares. Outtrim Cemetery Nature Conservation Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Outtrim.

Getting around

Outtrim has no train station; Warragul Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 41 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Outtrim, so most trips start by car. By car from Outtrim, Leongatha is about 15 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 24 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Korumburra Outdoor Pool in Korumburra is about 19 minutes' drive from Outtrim. For a library, Leongatha Library in Leongatha is about 16 minutes' drive from Outtrim. The closest beach to Outtrim is The Caves in Inverloch, about 21 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Leongatha Skate Park in Leongatha is about 16 minutes' drive from Outtrim. The closest cinema to Outtrim is Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Outtrim is Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville, about 30 minutes' drive. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Outtrim, about 15 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Outtrim, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Peterson Street Reserve in Korumburra (19 min), Korumburra Outdoor Pool in Korumburra (19 min), the playground at Coleman Park in Korumburra (19 min), Coal Creek Community Park & Museum in Korumburra (22 min), Bunurong Environment Centre & Shell Museum in Inverloch (15 min) and Leongatha Library in Leongatha (16 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Outtrim.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Outtrim, Inverloch has 4 playgrounds and a beach. Korumburra, next to Outtrim, has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Korumburra South, Kongwak and Jumbunna also border Outtrim.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Outtrim, without traffic.
