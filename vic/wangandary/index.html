Wangandary in the Wangaratta area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Taminick H109 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Wangandary, at about 4 hectares. Wangaratta South Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Wangandary. For walks and rides, Salisbury Falls Walk, Friends Track and Alpine Views Track cross Wangandary.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wangandary. The closest to Wangandary is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 8.1 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wangandary, so most trips start by car. By car from Wangandary, Wangaratta is about 12 minutes away and Benalla about 45 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Wangandary is Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest library to Wangandary is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 12 minutes' drive. For a beach, Sandy Beach in Beechworth is about 43 minutes' drive from Wangandary. For a skate park, the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta is about 16 minutes' drive from Wangandary. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 12 minutes' drive from Wangandary. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Wangandary, about 12 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Wangandary are Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta (11 min), the playground at Dodsworth Street Reserve in Wangaratta (12 min), Brien Crescent Playground in Wangaratta (12 min), Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (12 min), Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta (12 min) and Moore than Swimming in Wangaratta (15 min).

Best for

Free days on the Salisbury Falls Walk and in Wangandary's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Wangandary, Wangaratta has 9 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Mount Bruno and Killawarra also border Wangandary.
