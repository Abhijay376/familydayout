Getting around

Grey River has no train station; Birregurra Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 40 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Grey River, so most trips start by car. By car from Grey River, Colac is about 79 minutes away, Torquay about 79 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Grey River is Apollo Bay Community Pool in Apollo Bay, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Grey River is Kay Langan Skate Park in Lorne, about 28 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Mechanics Hall in Apollo Bay is about 21 minutes' drive from Grey River. For a zoo or wildlife park, Skate Bowl in Apollo Bay is about 21 minutes' drive from Grey River.
