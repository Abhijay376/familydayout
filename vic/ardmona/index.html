Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Ardmona, in the Greater Shepparton area.

Landmarks

Ardmona Reserve is the largest mapped park in Ardmona, at about 6 hectares. For walks and rides, Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks crosses Ardmona.

Getting around

There's no train station in Ardmona. The closest to Ardmona is Mooroopna Railway Station (V/Line), about 6.3 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Ardmona, so most trips start by car. By car from Ardmona, Shepparton is about 14 minutes away, Echuca about 53 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Ardmona is Aquamoves in Shepparton, about 14 minutes' drive. For a library, Mooroopna Library in Mooroopna is about 8 minutes' drive from Ardmona. For a beach, Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton is about 16 minutes' drive from Ardmona. For a skate park, the skate park at Tatura Memorial Park in Tatura is about 10 minutes' drive from Ardmona. The closest cinema to Ardmona is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 14 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 14 minutes' drive from Ardmona. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Ardmona is Kidstown in Mooroopna, about 12 minutes' drive. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Ardmona, about 14 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Ardmona, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Arrowsmith Crescent Reserve in Mooroopna (4 min), the playground at Gange Street Reserve in Mooroopna (5 min), Mooroopna Library in Mooroopna (8 min), Kidstown in Mooroopna (12 min), Shepparton Heritage Centre in Shepparton (13 min) and Shepparton Library in Shepparton (13 min).

Best for

Free days on the Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks and in Ardmona's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Tatura, next to Ardmona, has 8 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Ardmona, Mooroopna has 12 playgrounds, a library and a zoo or wildlife park. Mooroopna North also borders Ardmona.
