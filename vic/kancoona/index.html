Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Kancoona in the Alpine area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

The Mt Porepunkah Loop passes through Kancoona.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kancoona. The closest to Kancoona is Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line), about 52 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kancoona, so most trips start by car. By car from Kancoona, Bright is about 43 minutes away and Wodonga about 55 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford is about 26 minutes' drive from Kancoona. The closest library to Kancoona is Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Kancoona is Pebble Beach in Tawonga South, about 28 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford is about 27 minutes' drive from Kancoona. The nearest larger centre is Bright, about 43 minutes from Kancoona by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Kancoona are Pebble Beach in Tawonga South (28 min), The Old School Museum in Myrtleford (26 min), the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford (26 min), Myrtleford Swimming Pool in Myrtleford (27 min), Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford (27 min) and Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford (27 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Kancoona on the Mt Porepunkah Loop.

Nearby suburbs

Bright, next to Kancoona, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Kancoona, Porepunkah has 1 playground. Mudgegonga, Mongans Bridge, Havilah and Dederang also border Kancoona.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Kancoona, without traffic.
