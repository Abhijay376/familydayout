Allambee South is in the South Gippsland area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Allambee South, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Allambee South has no train station; Trafalgar Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 17 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Allambee South, so most trips start by car. By car from Allambee South, Leongatha is about 29 minutes away, Traralgon about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Allambee South is South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha, about 27 minutes' drive. For a library, Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North is about 12 minutes' drive from Allambee South. The closest skate park to Allambee South is Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North in Mirboo North, about 13 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha is about 28 minutes' drive from Allambee South. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Allambee South, about 29 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Allambee South are Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North (12 min), Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North in Mirboo North (13 min), the playground at Baromi Park in Mirboo North (13 min), the playground at Mossvale Park in Berrys Creek (17 min), Boolarra Old School Museum in Boolarra (34 min) and Boolarra Skate Park in Boolarra (35 min).
