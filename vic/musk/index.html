Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Musk, in the Hepburn area.

Landmarks

Musk Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Musk, at about 3 hectares. Wombat Bushland Reserve is the next largest. The Epicurean Rail Trail passes through Musk.

Getting around

Musk has no train station; Ballan Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Musk. By car from Musk, Daylesford is about 8 minutes away, Castlemaine about 35 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford is about 7 minutes' drive from Musk. For a library, Daylesford Library in Daylesford is about 7 minutes' drive from Musk. The closest beach to Musk is Canoeing in Tylden, about 31 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick is about 32 minutes' drive from Musk. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Musk is Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek, about 39 minutes' drive. Daylesford is the closest bigger centre to Musk, about 8 minutes away by car, and has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Musk, the nearest places for kids by road are Daylesford Spa Country Railway in Daylesford (6 min), Daylesford Library in Daylesford (7 min), Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford (7 min), the playground at J P Gleeson Memorial Park in Lyonville (7 min), the playground at Hepburn Regional Park in Daylesford (13 min) and Little Gallery in Trentham (16 min).

Best for

Free days on the Epicurean Rail Trail and in Musk's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Daylesford, next to Musk, has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Coomoora and Bullarto also border Musk.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Musk, without traffic.
