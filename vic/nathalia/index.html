Our data lists 1 playground and 10 parks and reserves in Nathalia, in the Moira area. On top of that, Nathalia has a library, a pool and a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

Nathalia Natural Features Reserve is the largest mapped park in Nathalia, at about 292 hectares. Skeleton Creek Streamside Reserve and Nurmurkah - Picola rail line Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Nathalia include Apex Park, Water Tower Park and Uncle Bobs Club Park. Playgrounds in Nathalia include the playground at Apex Park. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Nathalia. In Nathalia you'll also find Barmah Forest Heritge and Education Centre, Nathalia Memorial Pool and Nathalia Library. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Nathalia for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Nathalia has no train station; Mooroopna Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 40 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Nathalia. By car from Nathalia, Echuca is about 45 minutes away and Shepparton about 48 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a beach, Paradise Beach in Yalca is about 30 minutes' drive from Nathalia. For a skate park, Riverland Skate Park in Numurkah is about 28 minutes' drive from Nathalia. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Nathalia is Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram, about 39 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 45 minutes from Nathalia by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Nathalia are Numurkah Library in Numurkah (27 min), the playground at Bunbartha Recreation Reserve in Bunbartha (26 min), Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah (28 min), Riverland Skate Park in Numurkah (28 min), Paradise Beach in Yalca (30 min) and Scotts Beach in Yalca (30 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the library and museum or gallery in Nathalia.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Nathalia, Yalca has a beach. Kotupna and Kaarimba also border Nathalia.
