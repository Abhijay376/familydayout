Iguana Creek in the East Gippsland area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Iguana Creek is Calajero State Forest, covering about 7,568 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Iguana Creek. For walks and rides, Mitchell River Walking Track crosses Iguana Creek. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Iguana Creek for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Iguana Creek has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Iguana Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Iguana Creek, Bairnsdale is about 41 minutes away and Sale about 52 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale is about 41 minutes' drive from Iguana Creek. The closest library to Iguana Creek is Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale, about 42 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Skate Park in Stratford is about 39 minutes' drive from Iguana Creek. The closest cinema to Iguana Creek is Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale, about 42 minutes' drive. Bairnsdale is the closest bigger centre to Iguana Creek, about 41 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Iguana Creek are the playground at Amy Shand Park in Lindenow (23 min), Anzac Park Playground in Briagolong (44 min), Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale (41 min), Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale (42 min), Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale (43 min) and Krowathunkoolong Keeping Place in Bairnsdale (42 min).

Best for

Free days on the Mitchell River Walking Track and in Iguana Creek's parks.
