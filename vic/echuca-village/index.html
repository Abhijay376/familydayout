Echuca Village in the Campaspe area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Echuca Regional Park is the largest mapped park in Echuca Village, at about 459 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Echuca Village. The Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks passes through Echuca Village.

Getting around

There's no train station in Echuca Village. The closest to Echuca Village is Echuca Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.3 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Echuca Village, so most trips start by car. By car from Echuca Village, Echuca is about 10 minutes away and Shepparton about 66 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Aqua Island in Wharparilla is about 32 minutes' drive from Echuca Village. For a library, Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca is about 11 minutes' drive from Echuca Village. The closest skate park to Echuca Village is Moama Echuca Community Skate Park in Echuca, about 12 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca is about 11 minutes' drive from Echuca Village. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Echuca Village is Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram, about 41 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 10 minutes from Echuca Village by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Echuca Village are Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca (11 min), Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca (11 min), McDonald's PlayPlace in Echuca (11 min), The Foundry Arts Space in Echuca (11 min), Customs House Gallery in Echuca (11 min) and Hopwood Gardens Playground in Echuca (11 min).

Best for
