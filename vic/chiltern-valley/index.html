Chiltern Valley in the Indigo area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Chiltern Valley is Chiltern Valley Bushland Reserve, covering about 8 hectares. Black Dog Creek K30 Streamside Reserve is the next largest. The Bowser-Wagunya Preferred Route passes through Chiltern Valley.

Getting around

Chiltern Valley has no train station; Chiltern Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 7.4 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Chiltern Valley, so most trips start by car. By car from Chiltern Valley, Wodonga is about 36 minutes away and Wangaratta about 37 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Chiltern Valley is Rutherglen War Memorial Swimming Pool in Rutherglen, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest library to Chiltern Valley is Rutherglen Public Library in Rutherglen, about 15 minutes' drive. For a beach, Sandy Beach in Beechworth is about 33 minutes' drive from Chiltern Valley. For a skate park, the pump track on Victoria Street in Wahgunyah is about 23 minutes' drive from Chiltern Valley. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 34 minutes' drive from Chiltern Valley. Wodonga is the closest bigger centre to Chiltern Valley, about 36 minutes away by car, and has 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Chiltern Valley are Chiltern Athenaeum Museum in Chiltern (12 min), Martin Park Playground in Chiltern (12 min), Rutherglen Public Library in Rutherglen (15 min), Apex Park Playground in Rutherglen (15 min), Rutherglen War Memorial Swimming Pool in Rutherglen (16 min) and the pump track on Victoria Street in Wahgunyah (23 min).

Best for

Free days on the Bowser-Wagunya Preferred Route and in Chiltern Valley's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Rutherglen, next to Chiltern Valley, has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Chiltern, next to Chiltern Valley, has 2 playgrounds.
