Werribee Gorge State Park

Park in Pentland Hills · Official details

Werribee Gorge State Park is a park in Pentland Hills, in the Moorabool Shire Council area, good for all ages. The nearest public toilet is about 1390 m away.

Details

Toilets
yes (Parks Victoria)
Food
BYO allowed (Parks Victoria)
Seasons
Riverside tracks may flood and be impassable after heavy rain (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
Many walks are difficult; moderate fitness needed; Bring at least 3-4 litres of water per person; Riverside tracks can flood after heavy rain (Parks Victoria)
