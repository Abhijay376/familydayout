Cheshunt South is in the Wangaratta area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Cheshunt South is King Reference Area, covering about 1,018 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Cheshunt South.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cheshunt South. The closest to Cheshunt South is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 55 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cheshunt South, so most trips start by car. By car from Cheshunt South, Mansfield is about 88 minutes away and Wangaratta about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Cheshunt South, the nearest places for kids by road are Myrrhee Recreation Reserve in Myrrhee (69 min) and the playground at Apex Park in Moyhu (67 min).
