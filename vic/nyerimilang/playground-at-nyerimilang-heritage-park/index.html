Playground at Nyerimilang Heritage Park

Playground in Nyerimilang · Basic info

Playground at Nyerimilang Heritage Park is a public playground in Nyerimilang, in the East Gippsland Shire area. The Cliff top Track is about 140 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.3 km from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 2 minutes from Nyerimilang.
