Nyerimilang in the East Gippsland area has 1 playground and 7 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Nyerimilang is Nyerimilang Park Gippsland Lakes Reserve, covering about 184 hectares. Nyerimilang Heritage Park and Rigby Island Gippsland Lakes Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Nyerimilang include Baxter Island Gippsland Lakes Reserve, Fraser Island Gippsland Lakes Reserve and Flannagan Island Gippsland Lakes Reserve. Playgrounds around Nyerimilang include the playground at Nyerimilang Heritage Park. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Nyerimilang. For walks and rides, Cliff top Track crosses Nyerimilang.

Getting around

Nyerimilang has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nyerimilang, so most trips start by car. By car from Nyerimilang, Lakes Entrance is about 16 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 36 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Nyerimilang is Lakes Entrance Aquatic Centre in Lakes Entrance, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest library to Nyerimilang is Lakes Entrance Library in Lakes Entrance, about 13 minutes' drive. For a beach, Ninety Mile Beach in Lakes Entrance is about 15 minutes' drive from Nyerimilang. The closest cinema to Nyerimilang is Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Nyerimilang is Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow, about 28 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Lakes Entrance, about 16 minutes from Nyerimilang by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Nyerimilang are Griffiths Sea Shell Museum in Lakes Entrance (11 min), Lakes Entrance Library in Lakes Entrance (13 min), Ninety Mile Beach in Lakes Entrance (15 min), Lakes Entrance Aquatic Centre in Lakes Entrance (17 min), Bronsdon Street Park in Lakes Entrance (19 min) and 90 Mile Beach in Lakes Entrance (18 min).

Best for

Free days on the Cliff top Track and in Nyerimilang's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Nyerimilang, Lakes Entrance has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Nyerimilang, Boole Poole has a beach. Kalimna West and Kalimna also border Nyerimilang.
