Greta South in the Wangaratta area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Unnamed P0023 Private Nature Reserve is the largest mapped park in Greta South, at about 106 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Greta South include Greta South Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Greta South. The closest to Greta South is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Greta South, so most trips start by car. By car from Greta South, Wangaratta is about 39 minutes away and Benalla about 40 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Greta South is Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla, about 40 minutes' drive. For a library, Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta is about 40 minutes' drive from Greta South. The closest skate park to Greta South is Pump Track in Benalla, about 40 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 40 minutes' drive from Greta South. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Greta South, about 39 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Greta South are Myrrhee Recreation Reserve in Myrrhee (27 min), the playground at Apex Park in Moyhu (20 min), Cobb & Co in Glenrowan (27 min), Glenrowan Museum in Glenrowan (27 min), Pump Track in Benalla (40 min) and Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (40 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Greta South.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Greta South, Myrrhee has 1 playground. Hansonville and Greta West also border Greta South.
