Playground at Maryknoll Reserve

Playground in Maryknoll · Basic info

Playground at Maryknoll Reserve is a public playground in Maryknoll, in the Cardinia Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet right by the playground. It's easiest by car, about 2 minutes from Maryknoll.
