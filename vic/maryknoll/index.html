Maryknoll is in the Cardinia area, and our data lists 1 playground and 6 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Weatherhead Forest Reserve is the largest mapped park in Maryknoll, at about 13 hectares. Maryknoll Reserve and Wirragulla Reserve are the next largest. Maryknoll also has Koala Reserve, Triangle Reserve and St Joseph's Wildflower Reserve. Playgrounds in Maryknoll include the playground at Maryknoll Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Maryknoll.

Getting around

There's no train station in Maryknoll. The closest to Maryknoll is Nar Nar Goon Railway Station (V/Line), about 6.1 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Maryknoll, so most trips start by car. By car from Maryknoll, Pakenham is about 21 minutes away, Warragul about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 65 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Lazy River in Tynong North is about 17 minutes' drive from Maryknoll. The closest library to Maryknoll is Pakenham Library in Pakenham, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Maryknoll is the bmx track at Greenland Court Reserve in Garfield, about 19 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Narre Warren is about 34 minutes' drive from Maryknoll. The closest indoor play centre to Maryknoll is Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham, about 22 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Wildlife Trail in Tynong North is about 17 minutes' drive from Maryknoll. The nearest larger centre is Pakenham, about 21 minutes from Maryknoll by car, with 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Maryknoll are Wildlife Trail in Tynong North (17 min), Lazy River in Tynong North (17 min), Rock Pool in Tynong North (17 min), Gumbuya World in Tynong North (17 min), Typhoon Island in Tynong North (17 min) and the bmx track at Greenland Court Reserve in Garfield (19 min).
