Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Yeungroon, in the Buloke area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Yeungroon is Mount Gowar Scenic Reserve, covering about 65 hectares. Coonooer East I158 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Yeungroon. The closest to Yeungroon is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 80 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yeungroon, so most trips start by car. By car from Yeungroon, Bendigo is about 88 minutes away and Stawell about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn is about 25 minutes' drive from Yeungroon. The closest skate park to Yeungroon is Donald BMX Track in Donald, about 41 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Yeungroon is Rex Theatre in Charlton, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Yeungroon are Rex Theatre in Charlton (13 min), Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (14 min), the playground at Rotary Park in Charlton (14 min), the playground at Gordon Park in Charlton (14 min), Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (25 min) and Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (25 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Yeungroon.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Yeungroon, Charlton has 3 playgrounds and a cinema. Nine Mile, Dooboobetic, Coonooer West and Coonooer Bridge also border Yeungroon.
