Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Bulart, in the Southern Grampians area.

Landmarks

Wannon Rapids Flora Reserve is the largest mapped park in Bulart, at about 51 hectares. Nigretta Falls Flora Reserve and Wannon River (1) Streamside Reserve are the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet and 3 off-leash dog parks in Bulart.

Getting around

Bulart has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 104 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bulart, so most trips start by car. By car from Bulart, Hamilton is about 26 minutes away and Portland about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bulart is Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Hamilton, about 25 minutes' drive. For a library, Hamilton Library in Hamilton is about 26 minutes' drive from Bulart. The closest skate park to Bulart is coleraine skate park in Coleraine, about 39 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Bulart is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 26 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 26 minutes from Bulart by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Bulart, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Cavendish Town Square Memorial Park in Cavendish (13 min), Big Woolbales & Campe Motor Museum in Hamilton (23 min), Coleraine Road Playground in Hamilton (24 min), Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Hamilton (25 min), Mud Gallery in Hamilton (26 min) and Hamilton Library in Hamilton (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bulart.

Nearby suburbs

Cavendish, next to Bulart, has 1 playground. Gritjurk and Bochara also border Bulart.
