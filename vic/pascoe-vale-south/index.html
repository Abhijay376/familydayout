Pascoe Vale South in the Merri-Bek area has 8 playgrounds and 8 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Kelvin Thomson Reserve is the largest mapped park in Pascoe Vale South, at about 4 hectares. Morris Reserve and Shore Reserve are the next largest. Pascoe Vale South also has Brearley Reserve, Paul Huckin Park and York Street Park. Playgrounds around Pascoe Vale South include the playground at Morris Reserve, the playground at Brearley Reserve and Shore Reserve Playground North. Our data shows 6 public toilets in Pascoe Vale South. The Bike: DELWP passes through Pascoe Vale South. Our data lists 9 cafes and family restaurants in Pascoe Vale South for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Pascoe Vale South has no train station; Strathmore Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 1.1 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Tram 58 stops in Pascoe Vale South. Buses 510, 512, 513, 514, 527, 561, 903 and 951 stop in Pascoe Vale South. By car from Pascoe Vale South, Broadmeadows is about 13 minutes away, Sunshine about 16 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 14 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Pascoe Vale South is Elite Swimming in Pascoe Vale, about 4 minutes' drive. For a library, Campbell Turnbull Library in Brunswick West is about 2 minutes' drive from Pascoe Vale South. The closest beach to Pascoe Vale South is Horse Arena in Abbotsford, about 17 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track at Cross Keys Reserve in Essendon is about 2 minutes' drive from Pascoe Vale South. For a cinema, Bofa Theatre in Strathmore is about 3 minutes' drive from Pascoe Vale South. For an indoor play centre, The Keys in Preston is about 9 minutes' drive from Pascoe Vale South.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 8 playgrounds across Pascoe Vale South. Free days on the Bike: DELWP and in Pascoe Vale South's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Coburg, next to Pascoe Vale South, has 23 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Essendon, next to Pascoe Vale South, has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Pascoe Vale South, Pascoe Vale has 12 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Pascoe Vale South, Strathmore has 10 playgrounds and a cinema. Brunswick West also borders Pascoe Vale South.
