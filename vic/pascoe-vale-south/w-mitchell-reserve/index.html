W. Mitchell Reserve

Park in Pascoe Vale South · Basic info

W. Mitchell Reserve is a park in Pascoe Vale South, in the Merri-Bek City Council area, good for all ages. Gallipoli Pde/Reynard St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 762 m away.
