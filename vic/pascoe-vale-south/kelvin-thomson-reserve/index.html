Kelvin Thomson Reserve

Park in Pascoe Vale South · Basic info

Kelvin Thomson Reserve is a park in Pascoe Vale South, in the Merri-Bek City Council area, good for all ages. Pascoe Vale Railway Station (train) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 631 m away.
