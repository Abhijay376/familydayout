Playground at Kelvin Thomson Reserve

Playground in Pascoe Vale South · Basic info

This is a public playground in Pascoe Vale South. The Moonee Ponds Creek Trail is about 120 m away, and Playground at Tate Park is another option about 400 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 590 m from the playground, and trains stop at Pascoe Vale Railway Station, a 8-minute walk away.
