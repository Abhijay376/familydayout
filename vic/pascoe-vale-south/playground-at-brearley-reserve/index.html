Playground at Brearley Reserve

Playground in Pascoe Vale South · Basic info

Playground at Brearley Reserve is a public playground in Pascoe Vale South, in the Merri-Bek City area. The Moonee Ponds Creek Trail is about 290 m away, and Playground at Talbot Road-Wallace Crescent Reserve is another option about 450 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 340 m from the playground, and buses 513 and 514 stop at Bell St/Cumberland Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
