Purnim West is in the Moyne area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Purnim West, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Purnim West has no train station; Sherwood Park Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 9.4 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Purnim West, so most trips start by car. By car from Purnim West, Warrnambool is about 12 minutes away and Hamilton about 78 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Purnim West is Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest library to Purnim West is Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Purnim West is Shelly Beach in Warrnambool, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Purnim West is Warrnambool BMX Track in Warrnambool, about 15 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool is about 13 minutes' drive from Purnim West. For an indoor play centre, The Bowl in Warrnambool is about 13 minutes' drive from Purnim West. For a zoo or wildlife park, Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee is about 14 minutes' drive from Purnim West. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Purnim West, about 12 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Purnim West are the playground at Bushfield Recreation Reserve in Bushfield (5 min), the playground at Albert Park in Warrnambool (11 min), Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool (13 min), Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (14 min), SCOPE Galleries in Warrnambool (13 min) and The Bowl in Warrnambool (13 min).
