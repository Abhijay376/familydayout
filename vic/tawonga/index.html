Tawonga in the Alpine area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Murmungee H37 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Tawonga, at about 15 hectares. Mount Beauty Streamside Reserve and Tawonga Memorial Park are the next largest. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Tawonga. The Trappers Gap: Mitta Mitta to Mount Beauty, Quartz Ridge Track and The Staircase all pass through Tawonga.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tawonga. The closest to Tawonga is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 70 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Albury - Mt Beauty via Baranduda and Tawonga South route stop in Tawonga. By car from Tawonga, Bright is about 29 minutes away and Wodonga about 63 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bright River Pool in Bright is about 29 minutes' drive from Tawonga. For a library, Mt Beauty Library in Mount Beauty is about 10 minutes' drive from Tawonga. For a beach, Pebble Beach in Tawonga South is about 7 minutes' drive from Tawonga. For a skate park, Mount Beauty BMX Bike Track in Mount Beauty is about 10 minutes' drive from Tawonga. The closest cinema to Tawonga is Sun Cinema Bright in Bright, about 30 minutes' drive. Bright is the closest bigger centre to Tawonga, about 29 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Tawonga are Pebble Beach in Tawonga South (7 min), Mount Beauty BMX Bike Track in Mount Beauty (10 min), Mt Beauty Library in Mount Beauty (10 min), Bright River Pool in Bright (29 min), Bright Art Gallery in Bright (29 min) and Bright Splash Park in Bright (30 min).

Best for

Free days on the Trappers Gap: Mitta Mitta to Mount Beauty and in Tawonga's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Bright, next to Tawonga, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Mount Beauty, next to Tawonga, has 1 playground, a library and a skate park. Tawonga South, next to Tawonga, has a beach. Next door to Tawonga, Eskdale has a pool. Nelse, Mongans Bridge, Mitta Mitta and Germantown also border Tawonga.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Tawonga, without traffic.
