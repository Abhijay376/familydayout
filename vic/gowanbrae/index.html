Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Gowanbrae. For walks and rides, M80 Trail crosses Gowanbrae.

Getting around

Gowanbrae has no train station; Glenroy Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 2.0 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 490 stops in Gowanbrae. By car from Gowanbrae, Broadmeadows is about 10 minutes away, Sunshine about 15 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 23 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Gowanbrae is Tullamarine Swim School in Tullamarine, about 2 minutes' drive. The closest library to Gowanbrae is Tullamarine Library in Tullamarine, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Gowanbrae is Horse Arena in Abbotsford, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Gowanbrae is the skate park at Boeing Reserve in Strathmore Heights, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Gowanbrae is Village Cinemas in Airport West, about 5 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Strike Bowling Bar in Maribyrnong is about 16 minutes' drive from Gowanbrae. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Gowanbrae is Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville, about 18 minutes' drive. Broadmeadows is the closest bigger centre to Gowanbrae, about 10 minutes away by car, and has 12 playgrounds, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Gowanbrae's 3 local playgrounds. Free days on the M80 Trail and in Gowanbrae's parks.
