Playground at Blossom Grove Reserve

Playground in Knoxfield · Basic info

Playground at Blossom Grove Reserve in Knoxfield is a public playground in the Knox City area. The Burwood Highway Trail passes about 250 m away, and Playground on Cornelius Drive is another option about 260 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.0 km away, and bus 737 stops at Burwood Hwy/High Street Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
