Roy Pitman Plantation

Park in Croydon · Basic info

Roy Pitman Plantation is a park in Croydon, in the Maroondah City Council area, good for all ages. Newhaven Lane/Dorset Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 888 m away.
