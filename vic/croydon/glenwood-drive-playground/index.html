Glenwood Drive Playground

Playground in Croydon · Basic info

Glenwood Drive Playground in Croydon is a public playground in the Maroondah City area. The Mount Dandenong Road Connection passes about 220 m away, and Big Papa's Pizza is about 250 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 2.6 km away. It's easiest by car, about 3 minutes from Croydon.
