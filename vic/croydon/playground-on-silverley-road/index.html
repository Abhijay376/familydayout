Playground on Silverley Road

Playground in Croydon · Basic info

This is a public playground in Croydon. The Bayswater-Lilydale Trail is about 310 m away, and Playground on Carlyle Street is another option about 20 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.2 km from the playground, and bus 689 stops at Silverley Rd/Hull Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
