Playground on Evans Drive

Playground in Croydon · Basic info

Playground on Evans Drive in Croydon is a public playground in the Maroondah City area. The Brushy Creek Trail passes about 370 m away, and Barngeong Reserve Playground is another option about 390 m away. There's a public toilet about 800 m away. It's easiest by car, about 6 minutes from Croydon.
