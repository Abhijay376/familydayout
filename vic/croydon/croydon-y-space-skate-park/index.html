Croydon Y Space Skate Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Croydon · Basic info

Croydon Y Space Skate Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Croydon, in the Maroondah City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Civic Sq/Mt Dandenong Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 245 m away.
