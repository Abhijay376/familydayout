Vasey Concourse Reserve

Park in Croydon · Basic info

Vasey Concourse Reserve is a park in Croydon, in the Maroondah City Council area, good for all ages. 146 Mt. Dandenong Rd (bus) is a 20-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1980 m away.
