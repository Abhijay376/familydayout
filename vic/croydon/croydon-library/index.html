Croydon Library

Library in Croydon · Basic info

Croydon Library is a library in Croydon, in the Maroondah City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Arndale SC/Mt Dandenong Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 73 m away. Opening hours: opening_hours: Mo-Fr 09:00-20:00, Sa 09:00-16:00, Su 13:00-16:00.
