Eastfield Park Playground

Playground in Croydon · Basic info

This is a public playground in Croydon. Clyde Reserve Playground is another option about 300 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus 737 stops at Araluen Dr/Jesmond Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
