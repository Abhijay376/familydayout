Croydon Central

Shopping centre in Croydon · Official details

Croydon Central is a shopping centre in Croydon, in the Maroondah City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Croydon PS/Kent Ave (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 10 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: Monday - Thursday: 9:00am - 5:30pm; Friday: 9:00am - 7:00pm; Saturday: 9:00am - 5:00pm; Sunday: 10:00am - 5:00pm.
