Cheong Park

Park in Croydon · Basic info

Cheong Park is a park in Croydon, in the Maroondah City Council area, good for all ages. Eastfield Rd/Bayswater Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 44 m away.
