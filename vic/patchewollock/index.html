Patchewollock in the Yarriambiack area has 1 playground and 21 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Patchewollock is Wathe Flora and Fauna Reserve, covering about 5,996 hectares. Dattuck Reference Area and Dering Flora and Fauna Reserve are the next largest. Patchewollock also has Yarto Bushland Reserve, Hynams Bushland Reserve and Sparas Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds in Patchewollock include the playground at Patchewollock Rec Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Patchewollock.

Getting around

There's no train station in Patchewollock. The closest to Patchewollock is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 125 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Patchewollock, so most trips start by car. By car from Patchewollock, Mildura is about 2 hours away and Swan Hill about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Patchewollock is Tempy Outdoor Swimming Pool in Tempy, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest library to Patchewollock is Walpeup Library in Walpeup, about 27 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Roxy in Ouyen is about 34 minutes' drive from Patchewollock. From Patchewollock, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Tempy Recreation Reserve in Tempy (23 min), Tempy Outdoor Swimming Pool in Tempy (23 min), Walpeup Library in Walpeup (27 min), Roxy in Ouyen (34 min) and Ouyen Swimming Pool in Ouyen (34 min).

Nearby suburbs
