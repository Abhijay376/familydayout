Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside North Blackwood in the Hepburn area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

North Blackwood has no train station; Woodend Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside North Blackwood, so most trips start by car. By car from North Blackwood, Kyneton is about 36 minutes away, Daylesford about 42 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 88 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to North Blackwood is Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford, about 42 minutes' drive. For a library, Kyneton Library in Kyneton is about 36 minutes' drive from North Blackwood. The closest beach to North Blackwood is Canoeing in Tylden, about 29 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton is about 34 minutes' drive from North Blackwood. Kyneton is the closest bigger centre to North Blackwood, about 36 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds and a library. From North Blackwood, the nearest places for kids by road are Little Gallery in Trentham (18 min), the playground at Quarry Street Reserve in Trentham (19 min), the playground at J P Gleeson Memorial Park in Lyonville (27 min), Canoeing in Tylden (29 min), Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton (34 min) and Kyneton Library in Kyneton (36 min).
