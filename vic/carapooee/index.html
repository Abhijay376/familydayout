Our data lists 5 parks and reserves in Carapooee, in the Northern Grampians area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Carapooee is Carapooee I47 Bushland Reserve, covering about 11 hectares. Carapooee I46 Bushland Reserve and Carapooee Creek Streamside Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Carapooee include Carapooee H36 Bushland Reserve and Carapooee I48 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Carapooee has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 54 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Carapooee, so most trips start by car. By car from Carapooee, Maryborough is about 61 minutes away and Stawell about 70 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud is about 11 minutes' drive from Carapooee. From Carapooee, the nearest places for kids by road are Bi-Centennial Park Playground in St Arnaud (9 min), the playground at Queen Mary Botanic Gardens in St Arnaud (10 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (11 min), Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (55 min) and Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (56 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Carapooee.

Nearby suburbs

St Arnaud, next to Carapooee, has 4 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Carapooee, Stuart Mill has 1 playground. Emu, Dalyenong and Carapooee West also border Carapooee.
