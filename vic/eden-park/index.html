Eden Park is in the Whittlesea area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Eden Park, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Eden Park.

Getting around

Eden Park has no train station; Wallan Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 9.8 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Eden Park, so most trips start by car. By car from Eden Park, Broadmeadows is about 38 minutes away, Sunshine about 49 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 51 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Whittlesea Swimming Centre in Whittlesea is about 10 minutes' drive from Eden Park. For a library, Mernda Library in Mernda is about 19 minutes' drive from Eden Park. The closest skate park to Eden Park is the skate park at Mandalay Park in Beveridge, about 21 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Reading Epping Cinema in Epping is about 25 minutes' drive from Eden Park. For an indoor play centre, Timezone in Epping is about 25 minutes' drive from Eden Park. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Eden Park is Funfields in Whittlesea, about 11 minutes' drive. Broadmeadows is the closest bigger centre to Eden Park, about 38 minutes away by car, and has 12 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Eden Park, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Willow Park in Whittlesea (9 min), Fir Street Park Playground in Whittlesea (9 min), Whittlesea Swimming Centre in Whittlesea (10 min), Typhoon in Whittlesea (11 min), Funfields in Whittlesea (11 min) and the skate park at Mandalay Park in Beveridge (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Eden Park.

Nearby suburbs

Whittlesea, next to Eden Park, has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a zoo or wildlife park. Next door to Eden Park, Beveridge has 4 playgrounds and a skate park. Yan Yean, next to Eden Park, has 1 playground. Upper Plenty also borders Eden Park.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Eden Park, without traffic.
