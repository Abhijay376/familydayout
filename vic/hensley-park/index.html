Hensley Park is in the Southern Grampians area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Hensley Park, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Hensley Park.

Getting around

Hensley Park has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 94 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Hensley Park, so most trips start by car. By car from Hensley Park, Hamilton is about 17 minutes away and Portland about 84 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Hamilton is about 17 minutes' drive from Hensley Park. The closest library to Hensley Park is Hamilton Library in Hamilton, about 16 minutes' drive. For a skate park, hamilton skate park in Hamilton is about 16 minutes' drive from Hensley Park. The closest cinema to Hensley Park is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 17 minutes' drive. Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Hensley Park, about 17 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Hensley Park, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Cavendish Town Square Memorial Park in Cavendish (11 min), health station 7/8 playground in Hamilton (14 min), hamilton skate park in Hamilton (16 min), Sir Reginald Ansett Transport Museum in Hamilton (16 min), Hamilton Library in Hamilton (16 min) and Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton (17 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Hensley Park.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Hensley Park, Hamilton has 23 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Cavendish, next to Hensley Park, has 1 playground. Karabeal, Bulart and Bochara also border Hensley Park.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Hensley Park, without traffic.
