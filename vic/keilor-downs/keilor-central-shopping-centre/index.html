Keilor Central Shopping Centre

Shopping centre in Keilor Downs · Basic info

Keilor Central Shopping Centre is a shopping centre in Keilor Downs, in the Brimbank City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Keilor Central Shopping Centre/Taylors Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1 m away with a baby change table.
