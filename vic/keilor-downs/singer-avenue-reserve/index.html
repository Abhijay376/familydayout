Singer Avenue Reserve

Park in Keilor Downs · Basic info

Singer Avenue Reserve is a park in Keilor Downs, in the Brimbank City Council area, good for all ages. St Mary MacKillop PS/Sunshine Ave (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1055 m away.
