Wirilda Place Reserve

Park in Keilor Downs · Basic info

Wirilda Place Reserve is a park in Keilor Downs, in the Brimbank City Council area, good for all ages. 306 Sunshine Ave (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 479 m away.
