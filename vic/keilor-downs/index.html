Keilor Downs is in the Brimbank area, and our data lists 16 playgrounds and 19 parks and reserves in the suburb. On top of that, Keilor Downs has 4 pools and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Keilor Downs is Taylors Creek Linear Park, covering about 52 hectares. Railway Line Linear Reserve and Willys Avenue Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Keilor Downs include Antwerp Reserve, Varna Place Reserve and Dongola Road Reserve. Playgrounds around Keilor Downs include the playground at Green Gully Reserve, the playground at Dongola Road Reserve and the playground at Antwerp Drive Reserve. Our data shows 15 public toilets, 4 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by and 1 off-leash dog park in Keilor Downs. In Keilor Downs you'll also find 50m Pool and Brimbank Aquatic and Wellness Centre. For older kids in Keilor Downs there's the skate park at Packard Street Reserve. Keilor Central Shopping Centre is the local shopping centre. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Keilor Downs for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Keilor Downs. The closest to Keilor Downs is Keilor Plains Railway Station (metro), about 900 metres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 418, 419, 421, 476 and 941 stop in Keilor Downs. By car from Keilor Downs, Sunshine is about 11 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 18 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 28 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Keilor Downs is St Albans Library in St Albans, about 5 minutes' drive. For a beach, Altona Beach in Altona is about 26 minutes' drive from Keilor Downs. The closest cinema to Keilor Downs is Hoyts in Taylors Lakes, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Keilor Downs is Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes, about 9 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 16 playgrounds across Keilor Downs. Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at Packard Street Reserve in Keilor Downs.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Keilor Downs, St Albans has 33 playgrounds and a library. Taylors Lakes, next to Keilor Downs, has 16 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Keilor Downs, Sydenham has 12 playgrounds and a pool. Keilor, next to Keilor Downs, has 11 playgrounds and a library. Kealba and Delahey also border Keilor Downs.
