Playground at Dongola Road Reserve

Playground in Keilor Downs · Basic info

Playground at Dongola Road Reserve is a public playground in Keilor Downs, in the Brimbank City area. For food, Taco Bill is about 170 m away, and Playground at Tarella Drive Reserve is another option about 540 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 610 m from the playground, and buses 419 and 941 stop at Dongola Rd/Sunshine Ave, a 2-minute walk away.
