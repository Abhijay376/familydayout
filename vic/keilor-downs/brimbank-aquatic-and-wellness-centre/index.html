Brimbank Aquatic and Wellness Centre

Pool or water play in Keilor Downs · Official details

Brimbank Aquatic and Wellness Centre is a pool or water play in Keilor Downs, in the Brimbank City Council area, good for all ages. Keilor Central Shopping Centre/Taylors Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 2 m away. Opening hours: Monday - Friday 6am - 9pm; Monday - Friday 6am - 8:30pm; Monday - Friday 4pm - 7pm; Monday - Friday 9am - 7pm; Monday - Friday 6am - 9pm.
