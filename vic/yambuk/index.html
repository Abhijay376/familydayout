Yambuk is in the Moyne area, and our data lists 7 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Yambuk is Eumeralla (Yambuk) Coastal Reserve, covering about 943 hectares. Deen Maar Indigenous Protected Area and Yambuk Flora and Fauna Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Yambuk include The Crags Coastal Reserve, Yambuk Recreation Reserve and Yambuk Wetlands Nature Conservation Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Yambuk.

Getting around

There's no train station in Yambuk. The closest to Yambuk is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 37 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Yambuk. By car from Yambuk, Portland is about 43 minutes away and Warrnambool about 43 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Yambuk is Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool, about 45 minutes' drive. The closest library to Yambuk is Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool, about 44 minutes' drive. For a beach, Eumeralla in Tyrendarra East is about 16 minutes' drive from Yambuk. For a skate park, the skate park at Russell Clark Reserve in Port Fairy is about 16 minutes' drive from Yambuk. The closest cinema to Yambuk is Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Yambuk is The Bowl in Warrnambool, about 44 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Portland, about 43 minutes from Yambuk by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Yambuk are Limestone Gallery in Port Fairy (14 min), Eumeralla in Tyrendarra East (16 min), Pea Soup Beach in Port Fairy (15 min), the skate park at Russell Clark Reserve in Port Fairy (16 min), Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy (16 min) and Port Fairy Skate and Play space in Port Fairy (16 min).
