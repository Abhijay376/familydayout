Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Sugarloaf Creek in the Mitchell area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Sugarloaf Creek has no train station; Broadford Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 9.1 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Sugarloaf Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Sugarloaf Creek, Seymour is about 25 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 61 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 80 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Memorial Swimming pool in Seymour is about 24 minutes' drive from Sugarloaf Creek. For a library, Broadford Library in Broadford is about 11 minutes' drive from Sugarloaf Creek. The closest skate park to Sugarloaf Creek is Broadford Skate Park in Broadford, about 11 minutes' drive. Seymour is the closest bigger centre to Sugarloaf Creek, about 25 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Sugarloaf Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are Hamilton Street Playground in Broadford (9 min), Broadford Library in Broadford (11 min), the playground at Memorial Park in Broadford (11 min), Broadford Skate Park in Broadford (11 min), Memorial Swimming pool in Seymour (24 min) and Seymour Library in Seymour (25 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Sugarloaf Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Broadford, next to Sugarloaf Creek, has 6 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Sugarloaf Creek, Tallarook has 1 playground.
