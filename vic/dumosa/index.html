Landmarks

Getting around

Dumosa has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 73 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dumosa, so most trips start by car. By car from Dumosa, Swan Hill is about 72 minutes away and Stawell about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Dumosa is Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof, about 12 minutes' drive. For a library, Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook is about 29 minutes' drive from Dumosa. The closest skate park to Dumosa is Birchip Skate Park in Birchip, about 26 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 35 minutes' drive from Dumosa. The closest places for kids by road from Dumosa are the playground at Hall Corner Park in Nullawil (8 min), Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof (12 min), the playground at Centenary Park in Wycheproof (13 min), Willandra Museum in Wycheproof (13 min), Birchip History Museum in Birchip (25 min) and Birchip Skate Park in Birchip (26 min).
