Stewarton in the Benalla area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Stewarton Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Stewarton, at about 4 hectares.

Getting around

Stewarton has no train station; Violet Town Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Stewarton, so most trips start by car. By car from Stewarton, Benalla is about 28 minutes away, Shepparton about 38 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla is about 28 minutes' drive from Stewarton. For a library, Shepparton Library in Shepparton is about 39 minutes' drive from Stewarton. The closest beach to Stewarton is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 41 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Stewarton is Pump Track in Benalla, about 30 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 38 minutes' drive from Stewarton. The closest indoor play centre to Stewarton is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 38 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kidstown in Mooroopna is about 42 minutes' drive from Stewarton. The nearest larger centre is Benalla, about 28 minutes from Stewarton by car, with 5 playgrounds and a pool. From Stewarton, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at CWA Gardens in Dookie (24 min), the playground at Dookie Recreation Reserve in Dookie (26 min), Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (28 min), Benalla Costume and Pioneer Museum in Benalla (29 min), Pump Track in Benalla (30 min) and Benalla Art Gallery in Benalla (29 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Stewarton.

Nearby suburbs

Goomalibee, Dookie College, Devenish and Broken Creek also border Stewarton.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Stewarton, without traffic.
