Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Yielima, in the Moira area.

Landmarks

Yielima Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Yielima, at about 5 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Yielima. The closest to Yielima is Mooroopna Railway Station (V/Line), about 54 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yielima, so most trips start by car. By car from Yielima, Echuca is about 57 minutes away and Shepparton about 59 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Nathalia Memorial Pool in Nathalia is about 15 minutes' drive from Yielima. The closest library to Yielima is Nathalia Library in Nathalia, about 13 minutes' drive. For a beach, Scotts Beach in Yalca is about 18 minutes' drive from Yielima. For a skate park, Strathmerton Community Skate Park in Strathmerton is about 21 minutes' drive from Yielima. The closest cinema to Yielima is Cobram Cinema in Cobram, about 34 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 57 minutes from Yielima by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Yielima, the nearest places for kids by road are Scotts Beach in Yalca (18 min), Paradise Beach in Yalca (18 min), Barmah Forest Heritge and Education Centre in Nathalia (13 min), Nathalia Library in Nathalia (13 min), the playground at Apex Park in Nathalia (14 min) and Nathalia Memorial Pool in Nathalia (15 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Yielima.

Nearby suburbs

Nathalia, next to Yielima, has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Next door to Yielima, Yalca has a beach.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Yielima, without traffic.
