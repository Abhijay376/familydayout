Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Chintin in the Macedon Ranges area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Chintin. The closest to Chintin is Clarkefield Railway Station (V/Line), about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Chintin, so most trips start by car. By car from Chintin, Broadmeadows is about 45 minutes away, Seymour about 51 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 63 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Chintin is Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest library to Chintin is Romsey Library in Romsey, about 14 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Romsey Skate Park in Romsey is about 14 minutes' drive from Chintin. The closest cinema to Chintin is United Cinemas in Craigieburn, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Chintin is Timezone in Craigieburn, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Chintin is Funfields in Whittlesea, about 33 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Broadmeadows, about 45 minutes from Chintin by car, with 12 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Chintin are Silvertop Close Playground in Wallan (11 min), Roulston Way Playground in Wallan (11 min), Romsey Skate Park in Romsey (14 min), Romsey Library in Romsey (14 min), Wallan Library in Wallan (12 min) and BMX Track in Wallan (13 min).
