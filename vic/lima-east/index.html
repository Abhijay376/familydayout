Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Lima East, in the Benalla area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lima East is Lima South Education Area, covering about 348 hectares. Lima East also has Lima East and James Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Lima East. Picnic areas in Lima East include Lima East. For camping, there's James Reserve. The Lima East Boulders Walk and Moonee Creek Walking Track all pass through Lima East.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lima East. The closest to Lima East is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lima East, so most trips start by car. By car from Lima East, Benalla is about 36 minutes away, Mansfield about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lima East is Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla, about 35 minutes' drive. For a library, Mansfield Library in Mansfield is about 39 minutes' drive from Lima East. The closest skate park to Lima East is Pump Track in Benalla, about 37 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Swanpool Cinema in Swanpool is about 15 minutes' drive from Lima East. Benalla is the closest bigger centre to Lima East, about 36 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Lima East are Swanpool Cinema in Swanpool (15 min), the playground at Waminda Park in Benalla (33 min), The Rocket Playground in Benalla (35 min), Benalla Art Gallery in Benalla (35 min), Benalla Costume and Pioneer Museum in Benalla (35 min) and Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (35 min).

Best for

Free days on the Lima East Boulders Walk and in Lima East's parks.
