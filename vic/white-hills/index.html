White Hills in the Greater Bendigo area has 1 playground and 4 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, White Hills has a pool.

Landmarks

Bendigo Creek Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in White Hills, at about 14 hectares. Wanyarra Dum and Heywood Street Diggings Historic Reserve are the next largest. White Hills also has White Hills H91 Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 5 public toilets in White Hills. In White Hills you'll also find White Hills Pool. For walks and rides, O'Keefe Rail Trail and Bendigo Creek Trail (North) cross White Hills. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in White Hills for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in White Hills. The closest to White Hills is Epsom Railway Station (V/Line), about 2.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the 5, 50, 51 and Goornong routes stop in White Hills. By car from White Hills, Bendigo is about 7 minutes away, Castlemaine about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Bendigo Library in Bendigo is about 7 minutes' drive from White Hills. The closest skate park to White Hills is the pump track on Water Street in Bendigo, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to White Hills is Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to White Hills is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 11 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 4 minutes' drive from White Hills. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to White Hills, about 7 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Free days on the O'Keefe Rail Trail and in White Hills's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to White Hills, Bendigo has 4 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Epsom, next to White Hills, has 3 playgrounds. Ascot, next to White Hills, has 3 playgrounds. Next door to White Hills, Jackass Flat has 2 playgrounds. North Bendigo and East Bendigo also border White Hills.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of White Hills, without traffic.
