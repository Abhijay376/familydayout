Bendigo Creek Streamside Reserve

Park in White Hills · Basic info

Bendigo Creek Streamside Reserve is a park in White Hills, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, good for all ages. O'Keefe St/Finn St (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 738 m away with a baby change table.
