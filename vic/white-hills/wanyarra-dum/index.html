Wanyarra Dum

Park in White Hills · Basic info

Wanyarra Dum is a park in White Hills, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, good for all ages. Weeroona Ave/Napier St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1035 m away with a baby change table.
