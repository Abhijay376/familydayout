Playground at RF Turner Memorial Park

Playground in Golden Square · Basic info

Playground at RF Turner Memorial Park in Golden Square is a public playground in the Greater Bendigo City area. The Bendigo Creek Trail (North) is about 80 m away, and for food, Hungry Jack's is about 300 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 440 m from the playground, and bus 5 stops at Shamrock St/High St, a 2-minute walk away.
