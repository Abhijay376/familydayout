Woorarra East is in the South Gippsland area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Woorarra East, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Woorarra East has no train station; Morwell Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 42 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Woorarra East, so most trips start by car. By car from Woorarra East, Leongatha is about 52 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 72 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Toora Pool in Toora is about 13 minutes' drive from Woorarra East. For a library, Foster Library in Foster is about 22 minutes' drive from Woorarra East. For a beach, Toora Beach in Toora is about 21 minutes' drive from Woorarra East. The closest skate park to Woorarra East is the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora, about 14 minutes' drive. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Woorarra East, about 52 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Woorarra East, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Stanley Street Playground in Toora (13 min), Toora Pool in Toora (13 min), Sagasser Park in Toora (14 min), the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora (14 min), Port Welshpool and District Maritime Museum in Port Welshpool (15 min) and Toora Beach in Toora (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Woorarra East.

Nearby suburbs

Welshpool, next to Woorarra East, has 1 playground and a beach. Binginwarri also borders Woorarra East.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Woorarra East, without traffic.
