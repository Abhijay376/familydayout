Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Woorinen, in the Swan Hill area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Woorinen is Woorinen Recreation Reserve, covering about 5 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Woorinen. The closest to Woorinen is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Woorinen, so most trips start by car. By car from Woorinen, Swan Hill is about 15 minutes away and Echuca about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill is about 15 minutes' drive from Woorinen. The closest library to Woorinen is Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Woorinen is Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Woorinen is Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill, about 15 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 15 minutes from Woorinen by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Woorinen are Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South (10 min), the playground at Adams Park in Swan Hill (15 min), Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill (14 min), the skate park at James Belsar Reserve in Swan Hill (15 min), Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill (15 min) and Swan Hill Outdoor Pool in Swan Hill (15 min).

Best for
