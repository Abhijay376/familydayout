Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Port Welshpool, in the South Gippsland area. On top of that, Port Welshpool has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

Port Franklin - Port Welshpool Coastal Reserve is the largest mapped park in Port Welshpool, at about 245 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Port Welshpool. In Port Welshpool you'll also find Port Welshpool and District Maritime Museum. For walks and rides, Great Southern Rail Trail and Welshpool - Port Welshpool Pathway cross Port Welshpool.

Getting around

There's no train station in Port Welshpool. The closest to Port Welshpool is Morwell Railway Station (V/Line), about 51 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Port Welshpool, so most trips start by car. By car from Port Welshpool, Leongatha is about 53 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 73 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Port Welshpool is Toora Pool in Toora, about 13 minutes' drive. For a library, Foster Library in Foster is about 23 minutes' drive from Port Welshpool. The closest beach to Port Welshpool is Chinaman Beach in Wilsons Promontory, about 14 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora is about 14 minutes' drive from Port Welshpool. The nearest larger centre is Leongatha, about 53 minutes from Port Welshpool by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Port Welshpool are Chinaman Beach in Wilsons Promontory (14 min), Franklin Bank in Welshpool (14 min), Toora Pool in Toora (13 min), the playground at Stanley Street Playground in Toora (14 min), Sagasser Park in Toora (14 min) and the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora (14 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Port Welshpool. Free days on the Great Southern Rail Trail and in Port Welshpool's parks.
