Playground at Bakers Oval

Playground in Geelong West · Basic info

This is a public playground in Geelong West. House Iberia is about 210 m away if you need food, and Peter Lowe Reserve is another option about 570 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 80 m away, and bus 1 stops at Bakers Oval/Shannon Ave, a 1-minute walk away.
