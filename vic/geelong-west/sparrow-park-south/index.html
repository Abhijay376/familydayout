Sparrow Park South

Playground in Geelong West · Basic info

Sparrow Park South is a public playground in Geelong West, in the Greater Geelong City area. The Bike: Western Link is about 330 m away, and Geelong West Library is about 580 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 230 m from the playground, and buses 1 and 25 stop at Elizabeth St/Autumn St, a 3-minute walk away.
