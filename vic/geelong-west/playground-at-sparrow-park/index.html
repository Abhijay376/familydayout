Playground at Sparrow Park

Playground in Geelong West · Basic info

Playground at Sparrow Park in Geelong West is a public playground in the Greater Geelong City area. The Bike: Western Link is about 380 m away, and Geelong West Library is about 560 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 130 m from the playground, and buses 1 and 25 stop at Elizabeth St/Autumn St, a 4-minute walk away.
