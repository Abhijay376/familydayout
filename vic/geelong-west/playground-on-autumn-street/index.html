Playground on Autumn Street

Playground in Geelong West · Basic info

Playground on Autumn Street in Geelong West is a public playground in the Greater Geelong City area. The Bike: Western Link is about 230 m away, and Shearers Arms Gallery is about 420 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 370 m from the playground, and buses 1 and 25 stop at Shannon Ave/Autumn St, a 1-minute walk away.
