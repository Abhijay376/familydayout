Kenworthy Reserve

Park in Geelong West · Basic info

Kenworthy Reserve is a park in Geelong West, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Collins St/Pakington St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 263 m away.
