Sparrow Park East

Playground in Geelong West · Basic info

Sparrow Park East is a public playground in Geelong West, in the Greater Geelong City area. The Bike: Western Link passes about 360 m away, and Geelong West Library is about 560 m away. There's a public toilet about 140 m away, and buses 1 and 25 stop at Elizabeth St/Autumn St, a 4-minute walk away.
