Playground at Freeway Reserve

Playground in Mulgrave · Basic info

Playground at Freeway Reserve is a public playground in Mulgrave, in the Monash City area. The Wellington Road Trail is about 390 m away, and Playground on Mackellar Avenue is another option about 520 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.3 km from the playground, and bus 850 stops at Drysdale Ct/Brandon Park Dr, a 6-minute walk away.
