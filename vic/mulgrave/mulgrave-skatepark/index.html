Mulgrave Skatepark

Skate, BMX or pump track in Mulgrave · Basic info

Mulgrave Skatepark is a skate, bmx or pump track in Mulgrave, in the Monash City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Gate Seven Dr/Jacksons Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 452 m away.
