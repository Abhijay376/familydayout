Playground at Portland Reserve

Playground in Mulgrave · Basic info

Playground at Portland Reserve in Mulgrave is a public playground in the Monash City area. The EastLink Trail passes about 280 m away, and Playground on Portland Street is another option about 350 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.1 km away, and buses 802, 804, 850 and 862 stop at Blaxland Dr/Police Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
