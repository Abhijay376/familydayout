Village Green Hotel

Playground in Mulgrave · Basic info

Village Green Hotel in Mulgrave is a public playground in the Monash City area. KFC is about 120 m away if you need food, and Playground on Woodlea Drive is another option about 320 m away. There's a public toilet about 260 m away, and buses 693 and 742 stop at Springvale Rd/Ferntree Gully Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
