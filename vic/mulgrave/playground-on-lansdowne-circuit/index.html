Playground on Lansdowne Circuit

Playground in Mulgrave · Basic info

Playground on Lansdowne Circuit in Mulgrave is a public playground in the Monash City area. The Wellington Road Trail passes about 140 m away, and Playground on Rata Street is another option about 380 m away. There's a public toilet about 900 m away, and buses 802, 850, 862 and 900 stop at Waverley Park/Wellington Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
