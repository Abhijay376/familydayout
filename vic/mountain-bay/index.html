Mountain Bay is in the Mansfield area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Mountain Bay, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mountain Bay. The closest to Mountain Bay is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 72 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mountain Bay, so most trips start by car. By car from Mountain Bay, Mansfield is about 27 minutes away and Benalla about 78 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mountain Bay is Mansfield Outdoor Swimming Pool in Mansfield, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest library to Mountain Bay is Mansfield Library in Mansfield, about 27 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield is about 27 minutes' drive from Mountain Bay. The closest cinema to Mountain Bay is Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield, about 26 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield is about 18 minutes' drive from Mountain Bay. The nearest larger centre is Mansfield, about 27 minutes from Mountain Bay by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Mountain Bay are Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield (18 min), Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield (26 min), Mansfield Library in Mansfield (27 min), the playground at Mansfield Botanic Park in Mansfield (27 min), the playground at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (27 min) and the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (27 min).
