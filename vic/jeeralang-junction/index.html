Jeeralang Junction is in the Latrobe area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Yinnar Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Jeeralang Junction, at about 1 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Jeeralang Junction. For walks and rides, Stringybark Ridge Track, Fosters Gully Nature Walk and West Boundary Track cross Jeeralang Junction.

Getting around

Jeeralang Junction has no train station; Morwell Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Jeeralang Junction, so most trips start by car. By car from Jeeralang Junction, Traralgon is about 23 minutes away, Warragul about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Jeeralang Junction is Latrobe Leisure - Churchill in Churchill, about 7 minutes' drive. For a library, Churchill Library in Churchill is about 6 minutes' drive from Jeeralang Junction. The closest skate park to Jeeralang Junction is Churchill Skate Park in Churchill, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Jeeralang Junction is Village 8 in Morwell, about 14 minutes' drive. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Jeeralang Junction, about 23 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Jeeralang Junction are the playground at Reservoir Park in Churchill (6 min), Walkley Park in Churchill (6 min), Churchill Library in Churchill (6 min), Churchill Skate Park in Churchill (6 min), Latrobe Leisure - Churchill in Churchill (7 min) and ARC Yinnar in Yinnar (12 min).

Best for

Free days on the Stringybark Ridge Track and in Jeeralang Junction's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Jeeralang Junction, Churchill has 9 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Yinnar, next to Jeeralang Junction, has 3 playgrounds. Jeeralang also borders Jeeralang Junction.
