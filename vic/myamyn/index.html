Myamyn is in the Glenelg area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Myamyn, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Myamyn has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 80 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Myamyn. By car from Myamyn, Hamilton is about 34 minutes away and Portland about 34 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Myamyn is Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest library to Myamyn is Heywood Library in Heywood, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Myamyn is Whalers Point in Portland, about 34 minutes' drive. For a skate park, hamilton skate park in Hamilton is about 36 minutes' drive from Myamyn. The closest cinema to Myamyn is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 34 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 34 minutes from Myamyn by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Myamyn are Heywood Library in Heywood (13 min), the playground at Apex Park in Heywood (13 min), Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood (13 min), the playground at Heywood Youth Park in Heywood (14 min), Whalers Point in Portland (34 min) and Powerhouse Motor Car Museum in Portland (36 min).
