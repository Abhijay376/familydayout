Heyfield in the Wellington area has 2 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in our data. Beyond the parks, Heyfield has a pool, a museum or gallery and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Heyfield is Glenmaggie Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 797 hectares. Dawson Flora Reserve and Glenmaggie I4 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Playgrounds in Heyfield include Apex Park Playground and Graves Court - Perry Court - Wickham Court Playground. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Heyfield. Places to visit in Heyfield include Heyfield and District Museum and Memorial Heyfield Pool. For older kids in Heyfield there's Pump Track. For walks and rides, Gippsland Plains Rail Trail crosses Heyfield. Our data lists 4 cafes and family restaurants in Heyfield for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Heyfield. The closest to Heyfield is Rosedale Railway Station (V/Line), about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Heyfield. By car from Heyfield, Sale is about 33 minutes away, Traralgon about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

The nearest larger centre is Sale, about 33 minutes from Heyfield by car, with 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.
