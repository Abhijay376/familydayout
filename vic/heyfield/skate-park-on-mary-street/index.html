Skate park on Mary Street

Skate, BMX or pump track in Heyfield · Basic info

Skate park on Mary Street is a skate, bmx or pump track in Heyfield, in the Wellington Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Gordon St/Mary St (coach) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 309 m away with a baby change table.
