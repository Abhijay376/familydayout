Our data lists 5 parks and reserves in Toolondo, in the Horsham area.

Landmarks

Youngs State Forest is the largest mapped park in Toolondo, at about 1,824 hectares. Brooksby State Forest and Red Gum Swamp, Jallumba Wildlife Reserve (hunting) are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Toolondo include Toolondo Bushland Reserve and Wash Tomorrow (Washdamorra) Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting). Our data shows 3 public toilets in Toolondo.

Getting around

Toolondo has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 95 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Toolondo, so most trips start by car. By car from Toolondo, Horsham is about 37 minutes away and Stawell about 77 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Toolondo is Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral, about 36 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk is about 35 minutes' drive from Toolondo. The closest cinema to Toolondo is Centre Cinemas in Horsham, about 36 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Horsham, about 37 minutes from Toolondo by car, with 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Toolondo are Arapiles Climbing Museum in Natimuk (34 min), the skate park at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk (35 min), the playground at Natimuk Showgrounds Recreation Reserve in Natimuk (35 min), the playground at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk (35 min), The Goat Gallery Natimuk in Natimuk (35 min) and Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral (36 min).
