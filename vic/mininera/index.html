Getting around

Mininera has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 36 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mininera, so most trips start by car. By car from Mininera, Ararat is about 36 minutes away, Stawell about 64 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mininera is Willaura Outdoor Pool in Willaura, about 33 minutes' drive. The closest library to Mininera is Lake Bolac Library in Lake Bolac, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Mininera is David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat, about 40 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Mininera is Astor Cinema in Ararat, about 36 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Ararat, about 36 minutes from Mininera by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.
