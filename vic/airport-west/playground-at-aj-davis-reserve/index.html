Playground at AJ Davis Reserve

Playground in Airport West · Official details

Playground at AJ Davis Reserve is a public playground in Airport West, in the Moonee Valley City area. Playground at Harrington Reserve is another option about 220 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.1 km from the playground, and buses 465 and 476 stop at Roberts Rd/Fullarton Rd, a 9-minute walk away.
