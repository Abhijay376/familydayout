Weather Station Reserve

Park in Airport West · Official details

Weather Station Reserve is a park in Airport West, in the Moonee Valley City Council area, good for all ages. Grange Rd/Fullarton Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 907 m away.

Details

Good to know

Questions parents ask

Where is Weather Station Reserve?
Weather Station Reserve is in Airport West, Victoria.
Is Weather Station Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Weather Station Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 907 m away.
How do I get to Weather Station Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Grange Rd/Fullarton Rd (routes 483), a 5-minute walk.
