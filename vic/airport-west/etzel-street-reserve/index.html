Etzel Street Reserve

Park in Airport West · Basic info

Etzel Street Reserve is a park in Airport West, in the Moonee Valley City Council area, good for all ages. Thomas St/Etzel St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 113 m away.
