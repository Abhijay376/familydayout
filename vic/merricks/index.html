Landmarks

The biggest green space in Merricks is Flinders - Somers Coastal Reserve, covering about 308 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Merricks. The Red Hill Rail Trail passes through Merricks. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Merricks for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Merricks has no train station; Bittern Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 9.7 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 782 stops in Merricks. By car from Merricks, Mornington is about 25 minutes away, Frankston about 29 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 72 minutes, without traffic.
