Fairy Dell (Campaspe) is in the Campaspe area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Fairy Dell (Campaspe), so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Fairy Dell (Campaspe) has no train station; Rochester Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 5.4 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Fairy Dell (Campaspe), so most trips start by car. By car from Fairy Dell (Campaspe), Echuca is about 34 minutes away, Shepparton about 67 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Fairy Dell (Campaspe) is Memorial Pool in Rochester, about 12 minutes' drive. For a library, Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca is about 35 minutes' drive from Fairy Dell (Campaspe). For a skate park, Moama Echuca Community Skate Park in Echuca is about 37 minutes' drive from Fairy Dell (Campaspe). The closest cinema to Fairy Dell (Campaspe) is Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Fairy Dell (Campaspe) is Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram, about 36 minutes' drive. Echuca is the closest bigger centre to Fairy Dell (Campaspe), about 34 minutes away by car, and has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Fairy Dell (Campaspe) are Memorial Pool in Rochester (12 min), the playground at Apex Park in Rochester (13 min), Campaspe Run Rural Discovery Centre in Elmore (26 min), the playground at Echuca Lions Park in Echuca (33 min), Holden Museum in Echuca (34 min) and Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca (35 min).
