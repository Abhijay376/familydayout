Balwyn Community Centre Reserve

Park in Surrey Hills · Basic info

Balwyn Community Centre Reserve is a park in Surrey Hills, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Northcote Ave/Whitehorse Rd #49 (tram) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 146 m away with a baby change table.
