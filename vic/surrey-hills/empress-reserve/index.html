Empress Reserve

Park in Surrey Hills · Basic info

Empress Reserve is a park in Surrey Hills, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Guildford Rd/Union Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 689 m away with a baby change table.
