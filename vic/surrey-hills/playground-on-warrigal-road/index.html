Playground on Warrigal Road

Playground in Surrey Hills · Basic info

Playground on Warrigal Road is a public playground in Surrey Hills, in the Whitehorse City area. The Back Creek Trail is about 350 m away, and for food, Old Kingdom is about 490 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.0 km from the playground, and bus 766 stops at Thistle St/Union Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
