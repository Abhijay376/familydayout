South Surrey Park

Park in Surrey Hills · Basic info

South Surrey Park is a park in Surrey Hills, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Through Rd/Riversdale Rd #54 (tram) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 863 m away with a baby change table.
