Playground on Garfish Grove

Playground in San Remo · Basic info

Playground on Garfish Grove is a public playground in San Remo, in the Bass Coast Shire area. The Anderson-San Remo bike path is about 340 m away, and Back Beach is about 540 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.2 km from the playground, and bus Cowes - Anderson - Wonthaggi stops at Anderson St/Panorama Dr, a 5-minute walk away.
