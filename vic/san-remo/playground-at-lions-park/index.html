Playground at Lions Park

Playground in San Remo · Basic info

Playground at Lions Park in San Remo is a public playground in the Bass Coast Shire area. The Foots Lookout Track passes about 220 m away, and Back Beach is about 130 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus Cowes - Anderson - Wonthaggi stops at Shetlands Heights Rd/Back Beach Rd, a 8-minute walk away.
