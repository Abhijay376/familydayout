Our data lists 4 playgrounds and 5 parks and reserves in San Remo, in the Bass Coast area. Beyond the parks, San Remo has a library and 6 beaches.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in San Remo is San Remo Coastal Reserve, covering about 17 hectares. San Remo Coast Reserve and San Remo Recreation Reserve are the next largest. San Remo also has Lions Park and Anderson Street Reserve. Playgrounds around San Remo include the playground at Lions Park, the playground at San Remo Coastal Reserve and the playground at San Remo Coastal Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in San Remo. Places to visit in San Remo include San Remo Library, Back Beach and Bonswick Beach. The Phillip Island Bike Path, George Bass Coastal Walk and Anderson-San Remo bike path all pass through San Remo. Our data lists 6 cafes and family restaurants in San Remo for a lunch stop.

Getting around

San Remo has no train station; Stony Point Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Cowes - Anderson - Wonthaggi and Cowes - Fountain Gate via Anderson routes stop in San Remo. V/Line coaches stop in San Remo. By car from San Remo, Wonthaggi is about 25 minutes away, Leongatha about 54 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Wonthaggi is about 24 minutes' drive from San Remo. For a skate park, the skate park at Wonthaggi Railway Historic Reserve in Kilcunda is about 12 minutes' drive from San Remo. For a cinema, Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi is about 23 minutes' drive from San Remo. For a zoo or wildlife park, A Maze'N Things in Cowes is about 13 minutes' drive from San Remo.

Best for

Younger kids at San Remo's 4 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the library in San Remo. Beach days at Back Beach and Bonswick Beach. Free days on the Phillip Island Bike Path and in San Remo's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to San Remo, Kilcunda has 1 playground, a beach and a skate park. Next door to San Remo, Bass has 1 playground and a zoo or wildlife park.
