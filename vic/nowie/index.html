Nowie in the Swan Hill area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Nowie Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Nowie, at about 4 hectares.

Getting around

Nowie has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nowie, so most trips start by car. By car from Nowie, Swan Hill is about 38 minutes away and Echuca about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Nowie is Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill, about 39 minutes' drive. For a library, Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill is about 39 minutes' drive from Nowie. For a skate park, Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South is about 37 minutes' drive from Nowie. The closest cinema to Nowie is Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill, about 39 minutes' drive. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Nowie, about 38 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Nowie, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Nyah Heritage Park in Nyah (43 min), the playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV) in Nyah (44 min), Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South (37 min), Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill (39 min), Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill (39 min) and Swan Hill Outdoor Pool in Swan Hill (39 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Nowie.

Nearby suburbs
