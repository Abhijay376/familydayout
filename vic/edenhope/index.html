Edenhope in the West Wimmera area has 2 playgrounds and 20 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, Edenhope has 2 museums or galleries.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Edenhope is Caldows State Forest, covering about 353 hectares. State Forest (Unnamed) and Edenhope Aerodrome Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Edenhope also has Lions Park, Edenhope Apex Park and Edenhope Flora Reserve. Playgrounds in Edenhope include Henley Park Playground and the playground at Lions Park. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Edenhope. Places to visit in Edenhope include Dachshund Museum & Gallery and West Wimmera Community Association - Red Tail Gallery.

Getting around

Edenhope has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 149 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Horsham - Naracoorte via Natimuk, Goroke, Edenhope route stop in Edenhope. By car from Edenhope, Horsham is about 82 minutes away and Hamilton about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Goroke Swimming Centre in Goroke is about 37 minutes' drive from Edenhope. The closest library to Edenhope is West Wimmera Shire Goroke Library in Goroke, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Edenhope are Goroke Swimming Centre in Goroke (37 min) and West Wimmera Shire Goroke Library in Goroke (38 min).
