Henley Park Playground

Playground in Edenhope · Basic info

Henley Park Playground is a public playground in Edenhope, in the West Wimmera Shire area. West Wimmera Community Association - Red Tail Gallery is about 220 m away, and Playground at Lions Park is another option about 340 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus Horsham - Naracoorte via Natimuk, Goroke, Edenhope stops at Elizabeth St/Anzac Ave, a 5-minute walk away.
