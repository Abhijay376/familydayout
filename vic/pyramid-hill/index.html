Pyramid Hill is in the Loddon area, and our data lists 1 playground and 10 parks and reserves in the suburb. Pyramid Hill also has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

Mount Hope Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Pyramid Hill, at about 126 hectares. Gladfield Nature Conservation Reserve and Bullock Creek Streamside Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Pyramid Hill include Centenary Park, Blind Creek Bushland Reserve and Pyramid Hill Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Pyramid Hill. In Pyramid Hill you'll also find Pyramid Hill & Districts Historical Museum.

Getting around

Pyramid Hill has a train station: Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line: Swan Hill - Melbourne via Bendigo). V/Line coaches stop in Pyramid Hill. By car from Pyramid Hill, Echuca is about 64 minutes away and Bendigo about 76 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo is about 21 minutes' drive from Pyramid Hill. For a skate park, Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna is about 43 minutes' drive from Pyramid Hill. The closest places for kids by road from Pyramid Hill are the playground at R J Phelan Reserve in Mitiamo (21 min), East Loddon Historical Society in Mitiamo (21 min), Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo (21 min), Leitchville Memorial Swimming Pool in Leitchville (34 min), Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna (43 min) and the playground at Cohuna Apex Park in Cohuna (43 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Pyramid Hill.

Nearby suburbs

Leitchville, next to Pyramid Hill, has 4 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Pyramid Hill, Yarrawalla has 1 playground. Patho, Mologa, Mincha West and Mincha also border Pyramid Hill.
