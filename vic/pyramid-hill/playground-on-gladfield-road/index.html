Playground on Gladfield Road

Playground in Pyramid Hill · Basic info

Playground on Gladfield Road is a public playground in Pyramid Hill, in the Loddon Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.1 km from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 1 minute from Pyramid Hill.
