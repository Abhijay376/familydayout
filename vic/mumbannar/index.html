Mumbannar in the Glenelg area has 10 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Dry Creek Forest Reserve is the largest mapped park in Mumbannar, at about 765 hectares. Squatters State Forest and Snow Gum Forest Reserve are the next largest. Mumbannar also has Westgarths State Forest, David Downs State Forest and Gambles Road State Forest. Our data shows 11 public toilets in Mumbannar.

Getting around

Mumbannar has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 124 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mumbannar, so most trips start by car. By car from Mumbannar, Portland is about 59 minutes away and Hamilton about 68 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Merino Swimming Pool in Merino is about 38 minutes' drive from Mumbannar. For a library, Heywood Library in Heywood is about 38 minutes' drive from Mumbannar. Portland is the closest bigger centre to Mumbannar, about 59 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Mumbannar are Dartmoor Coach House Museum in Dartmoor (8 min), the playground at Lions Park in Merino (37 min), Merino Swimming Pool in Merino (38 min), Heywood Library in Heywood (38 min), Gorman's art gallery in Casterton (43 min) and the playground at Apex Park in Heywood (39 min).
