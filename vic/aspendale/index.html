Aspendale is in the Kingston area, and our data lists 10 playgrounds and 9 parks and reserves in the suburb. On top of that, Aspendale has a beach and a skate park.

Landmarks

Browns Reserve is the largest mapped park in Aspendale, at about 9 hectares. Regents Park and Glen Street Reserve are the next largest. Aspendale also has Iluka Reserve, Allnutt Reserve and Watkins Reserve. Playgrounds around Aspendale include the playground at Iluka Reserve, the playground at Carinya Avenue Reserve and the playground at Albany Crescent Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Aspendale. In Aspendale you'll also find Aspendale Beach. For older kids in Aspendale there's Mordialloc Skate Park. The Longbeach Trail passes through Aspendale. Our data lists 6 cafes and family restaurants in Aspendale for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Aspendale Railway Station (metro: Frankston Line) in Aspendale. Bus 706 stops in Aspendale. By car from Aspendale, Dandenong is about 18 minutes away, Frankston about 19 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 32 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Mentone Sports and Aquatic Centre in Mentone is about 8 minutes' drive from Aspendale. For a library, Chelsea Library in Chelsea is about 5 minutes' drive from Aspendale. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Cheltenham is about 11 minutes' drive from Aspendale. The closest indoor play centre to Aspendale is Gravity Zone Indoor Trampoline Centre in Seaford, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Aspendale is Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale, about 20 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 10 playgrounds across Aspendale. Teens who skate or ride, with Mordialloc Skate Park in Aspendale. Beach days at Aspendale Beach. Free days on the Longbeach Trail and in Aspendale's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Aspendale, Mordialloc has 11 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Aspendale, Edithvale has 3 playgrounds, a beach and a skate park. Aspendale Gardens, next to Aspendale, has 7 playgrounds. Braeside, next to Aspendale, has 2 playgrounds.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Aspendale, without traffic.
