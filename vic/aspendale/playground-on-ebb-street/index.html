Playground on Ebb Street

Playground in Aspendale · Basic info

Playground on Ebb Street is a public playground in Aspendale, in the Kingston City area. The Longbeach Trail is about 100 m away, and for food, Laura St Seafood is about 440 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 2.1 km from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 3 minutes from Aspendale.
