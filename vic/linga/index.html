Our data lists 7 parks and reserves in Linga, in the Mildura area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Linga is Mamemgorook I236 Bushland Reserve, covering about 432 hectares. Dunstans Flora and Fauna Reserve and Underbool I61 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Linga also has Linga Bushland Reserve, Purnya Bushland Reserve and Underbool I71 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Linga has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 171 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Linga, so most trips start by car. By car from Linga, Mildura is about 2 hours away and Swan Hill about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Underbool Swimming Pool in Underbool is about 10 minutes' drive from Linga. For a library, Walpeup Library in Walpeup is about 25 minutes' drive from Linga. From Linga, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Outen Park in Underbool (10 min), Underbool Swimming Pool in Underbool (10 min), Walpeup Library in Walpeup (25 min), the playground at Walpeup Wayside Stop in Walpeup (25 min) and Murrayville Library in Murrayville (37 min).
