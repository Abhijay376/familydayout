Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Fiskville in the Moorabool area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Fiskville has no train station; Ballan Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 8.3 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Fiskville, so most trips start by car. By car from Fiskville, Bacchus Marsh is about 31 minutes away, Daylesford about 38 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 76 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Fiskville is Ballan Swimming Pool in Ballan, about 13 minutes' drive. For a library, Lerderderg Library in Bacchus Marsh is about 31 minutes' drive from Fiskville. The closest beach to Fiskville is Needles Beach in Pentland Hills, about 40 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Fiskville is the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Fiskville is Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Fiskville is Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point, about 39 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Fairy Park in Anakie is about 32 minutes' drive from Fiskville. The nearest larger centre is Bacchus Marsh, about 31 minutes from Fiskville by car, with 6 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Fiskville are the playground at Mount Wallace Hall in Mount Wallace (8 min), the playground at McLean Recreation Reserve in Ballan (12 min), the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan (13 min), Ballan Swimming Pool in Ballan (13 min), Needles Beach in Pentland Hills (40 min) and Disability beach in Steiglitz (43 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Fiskville.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Fiskville, Ballan has 4 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Mount Wallace, next to Fiskville, has 1 playground. Bungal also borders Fiskville.
