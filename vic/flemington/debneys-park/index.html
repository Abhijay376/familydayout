Debneys Park

Park in Flemington · Official details

Debneys Park is a park in Flemington, in the Moonee Valley City Council area, good for all ages. Djerring Flemington Hub/Mt Alexander Rd #23 (tram) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 88 m away with a baby change table.

Good to know

Questions parents ask

Where is Debneys Park?
Debneys Park is in Flemington, Victoria.
Is Debneys Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Debneys Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 88 m away, with a baby change table.
How do I get to Debneys Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Djerring Flemington Hub/Mt Alexander Rd #23 (routes 59), a 2-minute walk.
