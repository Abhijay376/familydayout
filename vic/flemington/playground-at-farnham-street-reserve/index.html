Playground at Farnham Street Reserve

Playground in Flemington · Official details

Playground at Farnham Street Reserve is a public playground in Flemington, in the Moonee Valley City area. The Ascot Vale to CBD passes about 80 m away, and phat MILK is about 380 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 850 m away, and bus 959 stops at 26-Wellington St/Mt Alexander Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
