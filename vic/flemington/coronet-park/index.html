Coronet Park

Park in Flemington · Official details

Coronet Park is a park in Flemington, in the Moonee Valley City Council area, good for all ages. Edinburgh St/Ascot Vale Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 40 m away with a baby change table.

Good to know

Questions parents ask

Where is Coronet Park?
Coronet Park is in Flemington, Victoria.
Is Coronet Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Coronet Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 40 m away, with a baby change table.
How do I get to Coronet Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Edinburgh St/Ascot Vale Rd (routes 404), a 2-minute walk.
