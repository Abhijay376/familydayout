Kiewa is in the Indigo area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Kiewa, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Kiewa.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kiewa. The closest to Kiewa is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Albury - Mt Beauty via Baranduda and Tawonga South route stop in Kiewa. By car from Kiewa, Wodonga is about 19 minutes away and Wangaratta about 71 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kiewa is Edge Aquatics in Baranduda, about 10 minutes' drive. For a library, Upper Murray Regional Library - Wodonga in Wodonga is about 18 minutes' drive from Kiewa. For a beach, Finns Beach in Bandiana is about 24 minutes' drive from Kiewa. The closest skate park to Kiewa is Skate Park in Baranduda, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Kiewa is Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana, about 14 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 19 minutes from Kiewa by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Kiewa, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Banksia Park in Tangambalanga (4 min), Skate Park in Baranduda (10 min), Edge Aquatics in Baranduda (10 min), Mel Read Park in Baranduda (10 min), Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana (14 min) and Army Museum Bandiana in Bandiana (16 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Kiewa.

Next door to Kiewa, Tangambalanga has 2 playgrounds. Allans Flat also borders Kiewa.
