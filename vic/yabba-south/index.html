Yabba South is in the Moira area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Yabba South Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Yabba South, at about 30 hectares. The Dookie Rail Trail passes through Yabba South.

Getting around

Yabba South has no train station; Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yabba South, so most trips start by car. By car from Yabba South, Shepparton is about 36 minutes away, Benalla about 44 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Aquamoves in Shepparton is about 37 minutes' drive from Yabba South. The closest library to Yabba South is Shepparton Library in Shepparton, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Yabba South is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 39 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Yabba South is Shepparton Velodrome in Shepparton, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Yabba South is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 36 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 36 minutes' drive from Yabba South. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kidstown in Mooroopna is about 39 minutes' drive from Yabba South. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Yabba South, about 36 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Yabba South are the playground at CWA Gardens in Dookie (8 min), the playground at Dookie Recreation Reserve in Dookie (11 min), StarBowl in Shepparton (36 min), Village Cinemas in Shepparton (36 min), Shepparton Library in Shepparton (36 min) and Shepparton Heritage Centre in Shepparton (36 min).

Best for

Free days on the Dookie Rail Trail and in Yabba South's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Dookie, next to Yabba South, has 2 playgrounds. Cosgrove also borders Yabba South.
