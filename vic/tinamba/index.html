Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Tinamba, in the Wellington area.

Landmarks

Thomson River Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Tinamba, at about 3 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Tinamba. The Gippsland Plains Rail Trail passes through Tinamba.

Getting around

Tinamba has no train station; Stratford Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 17 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Tinamba. By car from Tinamba, Sale is about 22 minutes away, Traralgon about 47 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tinamba is Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest library to Tinamba is Sale Library in Sale, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Tinamba is Pump Track in Heyfield, about 13 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Former Drive-in site in Sale is about 22 minutes' drive from Tinamba. Sale is the closest bigger centre to Tinamba, about 22 minutes away by car, and has 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Tinamba are Macalister River Regional Park Playground in Maffra (7 min), Play Equipment in Newry (12 min), Pump Track in Heyfield (13 min), Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield (14 min), Heyfield and District Museum in Heyfield (14 min) and Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford (17 min).

Best for

Free days on the Gippsland Plains Rail Trail and in Tinamba's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Tinamba, Heyfield has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Maffra, next to Tinamba, has 6 playgrounds. Newry, next to Tinamba, has 1 playground. Riverslea, Glenmaggie and Denison also border Tinamba.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Tinamba, without traffic.
