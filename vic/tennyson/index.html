Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Tennyson, in the Campaspe area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tennyson is Milloo Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 61 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tennyson. The closest to Tennyson is Dingee Railway Station (V/Line), about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tennyson, so most trips start by car. By car from Tennyson, Echuca is about 36 minutes away, Bendigo about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Lockington District Swimming Pool in Lockington is about 12 minutes' drive from Tennyson. For a library, Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca is about 37 minutes' drive from Tennyson. For a skate park, Moama Echuca Community Skate Park in Echuca is about 39 minutes' drive from Tennyson. The closest cinema to Tennyson is Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca, about 37 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 36 minutes from Tennyson by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Tennyson, the nearest places for kids by road are Lockington District Swimming Pool in Lockington (12 min), Lockington Pool in Lockington (12 min), the playground at R J Phelan Reserve in Mitiamo (22 min), East Loddon Historical Society in Mitiamo (22 min), the playground at Apex Park in Rochester (24 min) and Campaspe Run Rural Discovery Centre in Elmore (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tennyson.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Tennyson, Mitiamo has 1 playground and a pool. Next door to Tennyson, Lockington has a pool. Milloo and Dingee also border Tennyson.
