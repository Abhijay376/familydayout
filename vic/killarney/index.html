Killarney in the Moyne area has 2 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, Killarney has a beach.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Killarney is Port Fairy Coastal Reserve, covering about 118 hectares. Yangery Coastal Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Killarney. In Killarney you'll also find Killarney Beach.

Getting around

There's no train station in Killarney. The closest to Killarney is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 8 stops in Killarney. By car from Killarney, Warrnambool is about 17 minutes away and Portland about 67 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Killarney is Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest library to Killarney is Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Killarney is Koroit Youth Space in Koroit, about 9 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy is about 11 minutes' drive from Killarney. For an indoor play centre, The Bowl in Warrnambool is about 18 minutes' drive from Killarney. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Killarney is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 33 minutes' drive. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Killarney, about 17 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Killarney are Reef Point Beach in Port Fairy (4 min), Koroit Youth Space in Koroit (9 min), the playground at Kirkstall Recreation Reserve in Kirkstall (9 min), Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy (11 min), the playground at Port Fairy Coastal Reserve in Port Fairy (11 min) and the skate park at Russell Clark Reserve in Port Fairy (12 min).

Best for

Beach days at Killarney Beach.

Nearby suburbs

Port Fairy, next to Killarney, has 2 playgrounds, a beach and a cinema.
