Moroka in the Wellington area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Pinnacles State Forest is the largest mapped park in Moroka, at about 4,174 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Moroka. For walks and rides, Moroka Gorge Walking Track crosses Moroka.

Getting around

Moroka has no train station; Stratford Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 53 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Moroka, so most trips start by car. By car from Moroka, Sale is about 3.5 hours away and Traralgon about 3.5 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Free days on the Moroka Gorge Walking Track and in Moroka's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Crooked River, Buragwonduc, Budgee Budgee and Arbuckle also border Moroka.
