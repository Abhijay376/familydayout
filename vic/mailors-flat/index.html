Getting around

Mailors Flat has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 10 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mailors Flat, so most trips start by car. By car from Mailors Flat, Warrnambool is about 12 minutes away and Hamilton about 71 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mailors Flat is Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest library to Mailors Flat is Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Mailors Flat is Shelly Beach in Warrnambool, about 14 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Koroit Youth Space in Koroit is about 10 minutes' drive from Mailors Flat. The closest cinema to Mailors Flat is Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Mailors Flat is The Bowl in Warrnambool, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Mailors Flat is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 22 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 12 minutes from Mailors Flat by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Mailors Flat, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Bushfield Recreation Reserve in Bushfield (8 min), the playground at Witham Park in Warrnambool (8 min), Koroit Youth Space in Koroit (10 min), Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool (12 min), Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool (13 min) and Warrnambool Art Gallery in Warrnambool (13 min).
