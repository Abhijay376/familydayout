New Zealand Hill Flora Reserve

Park in Foster · Basic info

New Zealand Hill Flora Reserve is a park in Foster, in the South Gippsland Shire Council area, good for all ages. Pulhams Store/Main St (coach) is a 13-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 975 m away.
