Landmarks

Trafalgar East also has Yarragon Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Trafalgar East has no train station; Trafalgar Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 4.3 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Garfield Station - Traralgon Plaza route stop in Trafalgar East. By car from Trafalgar East, Warragul is about 30 minutes away, Traralgon about 41 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar is about 12 minutes' drive from Trafalgar East. For a library, Warragul Library in Warragul is about 29 minutes' drive from Trafalgar East. For a skate park, Moe Skate Park in Moe is about 20 minutes' drive from Trafalgar East. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 28 minutes' drive from Trafalgar East. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 28 minutes' drive from Trafalgar East. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Trafalgar East, about 30 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Trafalgar East, the nearest places for kids by road are Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar (12 min), the playground at Brindlewood Reserve in Trafalgar (14 min), Yarragon Childrens Playground Reserve in Yarragon (18 min), Station Gallery in Yarragon (18 min), Moe Skate Park in Moe (20 min) and Diving Pool in Moe (22 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Trafalgar East.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Trafalgar East, Moe has 14 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Trafalgar, next to Trafalgar East, has 4 playgrounds and a pool. Moe South also borders Trafalgar East.
