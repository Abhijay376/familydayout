St Arnaud East in the Northern Grampians area has 5 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Gowar South Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in St Arnaud East, at about 24 hectares. St Arnaud I52 Bushland Reserve and St Arnaud I53 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. St Arnaud East also has St Arnaud H35 Bushland Reserve and St Arnaud I54 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in St Arnaud East. The closest to St Arnaud East is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 62 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside St Arnaud East, so most trips start by car. By car from St Arnaud East, Stawell is about 66 minutes away, Maryborough about 67 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to St Arnaud East is St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to St Arnaud East is Donald BMX Track in Donald, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to St Arnaud East is Rex Theatre in Charlton, about 37 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from St Arnaud East are Bi-Centennial Park Playground in St Arnaud (3 min), the playground at Queen Mary Botanic Gardens in St Arnaud (4 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (5 min), Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (32 min), Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (32 min) and Donald BMX Track in Donald (38 min).
