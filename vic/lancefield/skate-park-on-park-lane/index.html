Skate park on Park Lane

Skate, BMX or pump track in Lancefield · Basic info

Skate park on Park Lane is a skate, bmx or pump track in Lancefield, in the Macedon Ranges Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Lancefield Primary School/High St (bus) is a 10-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 95 m away.
