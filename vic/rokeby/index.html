Our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in Rokeby, in the Baw Baw area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Rokeby is Rokeby Flora Reserve, covering about 9 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Rokeby. For walks and rides, Rokeby to Neerim Trail, Rokeby to Crossover Rail Trail and Traverse cross Rokeby.

Getting around

There's no train station in Rokeby. The closest to Rokeby is Warragul Railway Station (V/Line), about 10 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 89 stops in Rokeby. By car from Rokeby, Warragul is about 11 minutes away, Pakenham about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 82 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul is about 13 minutes' drive from Rokeby. The closest library to Rokeby is Warragul Library in Warragul, about 11 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South is about 8 minutes' drive from Rokeby. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 11 minutes' drive from Rokeby. The closest indoor play centre to Rokeby is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 12 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Wildlife Trail in Tynong North is about 25 minutes' drive from Rokeby. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Rokeby, about 11 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Rokeby, the nearest places for kids by road are Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South (8 min), the playground at Ellen Close Linear Reserve in Warragul (10 min), Charles Street Park in Warragul (10 min), Warragul Library in Warragul (11 min), Warragul Cinema in Warragul (11 min) and The Alley Warragul in Warragul (12 min).

Best for

Free days on the Rokeby to Neerim Trail and in Rokeby's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Rokeby, Neerim South has a skate park. Buln Buln and Bravington also border Rokeby.
