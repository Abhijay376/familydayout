Drummond in the Hepburn area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Drummond is Drummond I90 Bushland Reserve, covering about 4 hectares. Drummond I89 Bushland Reserve and Burke Streamside Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

Drummond has no train station; Malmsbury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 7.1 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Drummond, so most trips start by car. By car from Drummond, Kyneton is about 15 minutes away, Daylesford about 21 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 83 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford is about 21 minutes' drive from Drummond. For a library, Kyneton Library in Kyneton is about 17 minutes' drive from Drummond. The closest beach to Drummond is Canoeing in Tylden, about 27 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton is about 17 minutes' drive from Drummond. Kyneton is the closest bigger centre to Drummond, about 15 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Drummond are the playground at Malmsbury Botanical Gardens in Malmsbury (7 min), Kyneton Museum in Kyneton (14 min), Kyneton Library in Kyneton (17 min), Kyneton Community Park in Kyneton (17 min), Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton (17 min) and Canoeing in Tylden (27 min).
