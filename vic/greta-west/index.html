Greta West is in the Wangaratta area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Greta West, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Greta West. The closest to Greta West is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Greta West, so most trips start by car. By car from Greta West, Wangaratta is about 20 minutes away, Benalla about 29 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Moore than Swimming in Wangaratta is about 18 minutes' drive from Greta West. The closest library to Greta West is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 22 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta is about 20 minutes' drive from Greta West. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 22 minutes' drive from Greta West. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Greta West, about 20 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Greta West, the nearest places for kids by road are Glenrowan Museum in Glenrowan (8 min), Cobb & Co in Glenrowan (8 min), the playground at Apex Park in Moyhu (14 min), Moore than Swimming in Wangaratta (18 min), the playground at Dodsworth Street Reserve in Wangaratta (20 min) and Splashpark in Wangaratta (19 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Greta West.

Nearby suburbs

Glenrowan, next to Greta West, has 1 playground. Greta South, Greta and Glenrowan West also border Greta West.
