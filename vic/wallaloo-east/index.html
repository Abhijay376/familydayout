Wallaloo East is in the Northern Grampians area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Wallaloo East is Willaring Streamside Reserve, covering about 30 hectares. Wallaloo Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Wallaloo East has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 55 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wallaloo East, so most trips start by car. By car from Wallaloo East, Stawell is about 31 minutes away and Ararat about 57 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell is about 30 minutes' drive from Wallaloo East. For a skate park, the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell is about 35 minutes' drive from Wallaloo East. For a zoo or wildlife park, Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell is about 33 minutes' drive from Wallaloo East. The nearest larger centre is Stawell, about 31 minutes from Wallaloo East by car, with 6 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Wallaloo East are the playground at Shanahan Park in Marnoo (13 min), Navarre Playground in Navarre (26 min), Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell (30 min), Railway Station Art and Crafts Gallery in Stawell (32 min), Rupanyup Swimming Pool in Rupanyup (32 min) and Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wallaloo East.

Nearby suburbs

Callawadda also borders Wallaloo East.
