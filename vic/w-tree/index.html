W Tree in the East Gippsland area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Snowy River Heritage River is the largest mapped park in W Tree, at about 45,959 hectares. Detarka Bushland Reserve is the next largest. The Forest walk to Tulloch Ard Lookout passes through W Tree.

Getting around

There's no train station in W Tree. The closest to W Tree is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 76 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 12 stops in W Tree. By car from W Tree, Lakes Entrance is about 80 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to W Tree is Buchan: Natural Spring in Buchan, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from W Tree are Buchan: Natural Spring in Buchan (26 min).

Best for

Free days on the Forest walk to Tulloch Ard Lookout and in W Tree's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Buchan, next to W Tree, has 1 playground and a pool. Gelantipy and Butchers Ridge also border W Tree.
