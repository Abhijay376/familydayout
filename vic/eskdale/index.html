Our data lists 5 parks and reserves in Eskdale, in the Towong area. Beyond the parks, Eskdale has a pool.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Eskdale is Mitta Mitta River K28 Streamside Reserve, covering about 19 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Eskdale include Jack Swasbricks, Sachse campground and Katie Peters Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Eskdale. For camping, there's Jack Swasbricks and Katie Peters Reserve. Places to visit in Eskdale include Eskdale Swimming Pool. For walks and rides, Mitta - Callaghan Creek Loop and Mitta - Bullhead Gap Loop cross Eskdale.

Getting around

Eskdale has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 52 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Eskdale, so most trips start by car. By car from Eskdale, Wodonga is about 53 minutes away and Wangaratta about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Eskdale is Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta, about 32 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 53 minutes from Eskdale by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Eskdale are the playground at Banksia Park in Tangambalanga (30 min), Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta (31 min), Tallangatta Art & Photography in Tallangatta (32 min), Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta (32 min) and the playground at Ludlows Reserve in Ebden (37 min).

Best for

Free days on the Mitta - Callaghan Creek Loop and in Eskdale's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Sandy Creek, Mongans Bridge, Mitta Mitta and Gundowring also border Eskdale.
