Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Tarnook in the Benalla area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tarnook. The closest to Tarnook is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tarnook, so most trips start by car. By car from Tarnook, Benalla is about 21 minutes away, Shepparton about 48 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla is about 21 minutes' drive from Tarnook. The closest library to Tarnook is Euroa Library in Euroa, about 39 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Tarnook is Pump Track in Benalla, about 22 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Swanpool Cinema in Swanpool is about 36 minutes' drive from Tarnook. The nearest larger centre is Benalla, about 21 minutes from Tarnook by car, with 5 playgrounds and a pool. From Tarnook, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Benalla Botanical Gardens in Benalla (20 min), The Rocket Playground in Benalla (20 min), Benalla Art Gallery in Benalla (20 min), Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (21 min), Benalla Costume and Pioneer Museum in Benalla (21 min) and Pump Track in Benalla (22 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tarnook.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Tarnook, Benalla has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Violet Town, next to Tarnook, has 3 playgrounds. Baddaginnie also borders Tarnook.
