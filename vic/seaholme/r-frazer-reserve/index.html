R Frazer Reserve

Park in Seaholme · Basic info

R Frazer Reserve is a park in Seaholme, in the Hobsons Bay City Council area, good for all ages. Millers Rd/Blyth St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 2 m away.
