Playground on Civic Parade

Playground in Seaholme · Basic info

Playground on Civic Parade in Seaholme is a public playground in the Hobsons Bay City area. The Kororoit Creek Trail passes about 320 m away, and Fish and Chips on Civic is about 210 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 590 m away, and buses 411 and 412 stop at Civic Pde/Millers Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
