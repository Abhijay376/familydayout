Seaholme is in the Hobsons Bay area, and our data lists 5 playgrounds and 6 parks and reserves in the suburb. On top of that, Seaholme has a beach.

Landmarks

WG Cresser Reserve is the largest mapped park in Seaholme, at about 5 hectares. HC Kim Reserve and Fell Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Seaholme include R Frazer Reserve, S G Mcintosh Reserve and Norah McIntyre Reserve. Playgrounds around Seaholme include the playground at WG Cresser Reserve, the playground at S G Mcintosh Reserve and the playground at Norah McIntyre Reserve. Our data shows 5 public toilets and 2 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Seaholme. Places to visit in Seaholme include Altona Dog Beach. The Kororoit Creek Trail and Hobsons Bay Coastal Trail all pass through Seaholme.

Getting around

Trains stop at Seaholme Railway Station (metro: Werribee Line) in Seaholme. Buses 411, 412, 415 and 903 stop in Seaholme. By car from Seaholme, Sunshine is about 16 minutes away, Werribee about 21 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 22 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Sweeney's Swim School in Altona is about 2 minutes' drive from Seaholme. The closest library to Seaholme is Altona Library in Altona, about 3 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Seaholme is the skate park at Grant Reserve in Altona, about 2 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Reading Cinemas in Altona North is about 6 minutes' drive from Seaholme. The closest indoor play centre to Seaholme is Epic Arena in Newport, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Seaholme is Skyline Precinct Melbourne in South Wharf, about 18 minutes' drive.

Best for

Younger kids at Seaholme's 5 local playgrounds. Beach days at Altona Dog Beach. Free days on the Kororoit Creek Trail and in Seaholme's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Seaholme, Altona has 11 playgrounds, a pool and a library.
