Playground at S G Mcintosh Reserve

Playground in Seaholme · Basic info

Playground at S G Mcintosh Reserve is a public playground in Seaholme, in the Hobsons Bay City area. The Hobsons Bay Coastal Trail is about 230 m away, and Altona Dog Beach is about 580 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 370 m from the playground, and trains stop at Seaholme Railway Station, a 5-minute walk away.
