Our data lists 4 parks and reserves in Tuerong, in the Mornington Peninsula area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tuerong is Devilbend Natural Features Reserve, covering about 1,005 hectares. Woods Bushland Reserve and Bittern Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Tuerong include Tubbarubba Creek Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Tuerong.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tuerong. The closest to Tuerong is Hastings Railway Station (metro), about 10 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tuerong, so most trips start by car. By car from Tuerong, Mornington is about 10 minutes away, Frankston about 14 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 57 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Pelican Park Recreation Centre in Hastings is about 17 minutes' drive from Tuerong. The closest library to Tuerong is Mornington Peninsula Library in Mornington, about 11 minutes' drive. For a beach, Fosters Beach in Mornington is about 9 minutes' drive from Tuerong. The closest skate park to Tuerong is Mornington Skate Park in Mornington, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Tuerong is Dromana Triple Drive-In Theatre in Dromana, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Tuerong is Locked In Escape Rooms in Mornington, about 6 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale is about 20 minutes' drive from Tuerong. Mornington is the closest bigger centre to Tuerong, about 10 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a library, indoor play and a beach. From Tuerong, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Harrap Road Reserve in Mount Martha (5 min), the playground at Wensleydale Drive Reserve in Mornington (6 min), The Studio & Co in Moorooduc (6 min), Locked In Escape Rooms in Mornington (6 min), Mornington Skate Park in Mornington (8 min) and Mount Eliza Skatepark in Mount Eliza (7 min).

Best for

Nearby suburbs

Hastings, next to Tuerong, has 11 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Tuerong, Dromana has 7 playgrounds, a beach and a cinema. Next door to Tuerong, Safety Beach has 7 playgrounds and a beach. Mount Martha, next to Tuerong, has 12 playgrounds and a skate park. Tyabb, Moorooduc, Bittern and Balnarring also border Tuerong.
