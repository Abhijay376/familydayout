Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Swanwater in the Northern Grampians area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Swanwater has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 84 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Swanwater, so most trips start by car. By car from Swanwater, Stawell is about 71 minutes away and Maryborough about 78 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Swanwater is St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Swanwater is Donald BMX Track in Donald, about 21 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 44 minutes' drive from Swanwater. The closest places for kids by road from Swanwater are Apex Park Playground in St Arnaud (12 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (14 min), Market Square Park Playground in St Arnaud (13 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (17 min), Donald BMX Track in Donald (21 min) and Rex Theatre in Charlton (44 min).
