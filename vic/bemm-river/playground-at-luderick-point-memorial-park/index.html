Playground at Luderick Point Memorial Park

Playground in Bemm River · Basic info

Playground at Luderick Point Memorial Park is a public playground in Bemm River, in the East Gippsland Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. You'll find a public toilet about 60 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 1 minute from Bemm River.
