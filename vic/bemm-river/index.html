Bemm River in the East Gippsland area has 2 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bemm River is Cape Conran Coastal Park, covering about 11,577 hectares. Luderick Point Memorial Park is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Bemm River include Bemm River - Bemm River Camping Area 1 (CLWF). Playgrounds in Bemm River include the playground at Luderick Point Memorial Park. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Bemm River. For camping, there's Bemm River - Bemm River Camping Area 1 (CLWF). The Overgrown passes through Bemm River.

Getting around

Bemm River has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 118 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bemm River, so most trips start by car. By car from Bemm River, Lakes Entrance is about 1.5 hours away and Bairnsdale about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest beach to Bemm River is Sailors Grave Beach in Cape Conran, about 33 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Bemm River are Sailors Grave Beach in Cape Conran (33 min).

Best for

Free days on the Overgrown and in Bemm River's parks.

Nearby suburbs
