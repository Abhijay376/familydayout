Landmarks

The biggest green space in Moyston is Jallukar Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 1,208 hectares. Moyston Flora Reserve and Rocky Point Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Moyston also has Masons Bushland Reserve, Pollands Bushland Reserve and Moyston I77 Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Moyston.

Getting around

Moyston has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Moyston. By car from Moyston, Ararat is about 14 minutes away, Stawell about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat is about 14 minutes' drive from Moyston. The closest library to Moyston is Ararat Library in Ararat, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Moyston is Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Moyston is David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Moyston is Astor Cinema in Ararat, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Moyston is Halls Gap Zoo in Halls Gap, about 23 minutes' drive. Ararat is the closest bigger centre to Moyston, about 14 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Moyston, the nearest places for kids by road are Ararat Gallery TAMA in Ararat (14 min), Ararat Library in Ararat (14 min), Astor Cinema in Ararat (14 min), Langi Morgala Museum in Ararat (14 min), Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat (14 min) and Ararat Outdoor Pool in Ararat (16 min).
