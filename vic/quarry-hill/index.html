Quarry Hill in the Greater Bendigo area has 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Solomon Gully Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Quarry Hill, at about 19 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Quarry Hill include Palmerston Square. Playgrounds in Quarry Hill include the playground at Palmerston Square. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Quarry Hill. The Goldfields Track, Leanganook Track and Back Creek Trail all pass through Quarry Hill.

Getting around

Trains stop at Bendigo Railway Station (V/Line: Bendigo - Melbourne via Gisborne, Echuca/Moama - Melbourne via Bendigo or Heathcote and Swan Hill - Melbourne via Bendigo) in Quarry Hill. Buses 63, 64 and 65 stop in Quarry Hill. By car from Quarry Hill, Bendigo is about 3 minutes away, Castlemaine about 32 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Brennan Park Swimming Pool in Kennington is about 4 minutes' drive from Quarry Hill. For a library, Bendigo Library in Bendigo is about 4 minutes' drive from Quarry Hill. For a skate park, Ewing Park Skating in Bendigo is about 3 minutes' drive from Quarry Hill. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 3 minutes' drive from Quarry Hill. The closest indoor play centre to Quarry Hill is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 2 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Quarry Hill is The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo, about 8 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 3 minutes from Quarry Hill by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Quarry Hill, the nearest places for kids by road are Somerville Street Playspace in Flora Hill (1 min), Wolstencroft Playground in Flora Hill (2 min), Intagolf in Golden Gully (2 min), Discovery Science & Technology Centre in Bendigo (3 min), Ewing Park Skating in Bendigo (3 min) and Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo (3 min).

Best for

Free days on the Goldfields Track and in Quarry Hill's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Quarry Hill, Bendigo has 4 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Kennington, next to Quarry Hill, has 7 playgrounds and a pool. Golden Square, next to Quarry Hill, has 4 playgrounds. Flora Hill, next to Quarry Hill, has 4 playgrounds. Spring Gully and Golden Gully also border Quarry Hill.
