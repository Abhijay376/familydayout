Playground at Palmerston Square

Playground in Quarry Hill · Basic info

Playground at Palmerston Square in Quarry Hill is a public playground in the Greater Bendigo City area. The Goldfields Track is about 110 m away, and Discovery Science & Technology Centre is about 310 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 260 m from the playground, and buses 63 and 65 stop at Palmerston St/Mitchell St, a 2-minute walk away.
