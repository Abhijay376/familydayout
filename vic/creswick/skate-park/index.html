Skate Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Creswick · Basic info

Skate Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Creswick, in the Hepburn Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Raglan St/Albert St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 167 m away.
