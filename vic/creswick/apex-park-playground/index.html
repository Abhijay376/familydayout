Apex Park Playground

Playground in Creswick · Basic info

This is a public playground in Creswick. The Goldfields Track passes about 190 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 320 m away. There's a public toilet about 280 m away, and bus Hepburn - Creswick via Daylesford stops at Raglan St/Albert St, a 2-minute walk away.
