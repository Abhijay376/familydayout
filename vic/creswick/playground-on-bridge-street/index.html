Playground on Bridge Street

Playground in Creswick · Basic info

Playground on Bridge Street is a public playground in Creswick, in the Hepburn Shire area. The Goldfields Track is about 370 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 400 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 60 m from the playground, and bus 30 stops at Harvey St/Clunes Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
