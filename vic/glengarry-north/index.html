Glengarry North is in the Latrobe area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Yorkys Gully State Forest is the largest mapped park in Glengarry North, at about 178 hectares. The Lyrebird passes through Glengarry North.

Getting around

There's no train station in Glengarry North. The closest to Glengarry North is Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line), about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glengarry North, so most trips start by car. By car from Glengarry North, Traralgon is about 15 minutes away, Sale about 52 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Glengarry North is Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon, about 15 minutes' drive. For a library, Morwell Library in Morwell is about 28 minutes' drive from Glengarry North. The closest skate park to Glengarry North is the skate park at Apex Park in Glengarry, about 5 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 25 minutes' drive from Glengarry North. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Glengarry North, about 15 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. From Glengarry North, the nearest places for kids by road are the skate park at Apex Park in Glengarry (5 min), Glengarry Skatepark in Glengarry (6 min), the playground at Apex Park in Glengarry (6 min), the playground at Waite Park in Traralgon (10 min), Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon (15 min) and Yallourn North Outdoor Pool in Yallourn North (21 min).

Best for

Free days on the Lyrebird and in Glengarry North's parks.
