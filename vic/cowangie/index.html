Cowangie in the Mildura area has 5 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Cowangie is Cowangie Bushland Reserve, covering about 142 hectares. Cowangie Railway Bushland Reserve and Danyo I65 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Cowangie include Pallarang Bushland Reserve and Cowangie School Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Cowangie.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cowangie. The closest to Cowangie is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 199 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Ouyen - Pinnaroo via Mallee Hwy route stop in Cowangie. By car from Cowangie, Mildura is about 2.5 hours away and Swan Hill about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cowangie is Underbool Swimming Pool in Underbool, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest library to Cowangie is Murrayville Library in Murrayville, about 17 minutes' drive. From Cowangie, the nearest places for kids by road are Murrayville Library in Murrayville (17 min), the playground at Outen Park in Underbool (31 min) and Underbool Swimming Pool in Underbool (31 min).
