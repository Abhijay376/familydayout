Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Yandoit, in the Hepburn area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Yandoit is Yandoit Recreation Reserve, covering about 20 hectares. Glamorgan Reef Bushland Reserve and Yandoit Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Yandoit. The closest to Yandoit is Castlemaine Railway Station (V/Line), about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yandoit, so most trips start by car. By car from Yandoit, Daylesford is about 20 minutes away, Castlemaine about 27 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Newstead Swimming Pool in Newstead is about 16 minutes' drive from Yandoit. For a library, Daylesford Library in Daylesford is about 20 minutes' drive from Yandoit. For a skate park, Bike Jumps in Castlemaine is about 27 minutes' drive from Yandoit. The nearest larger centre is Daylesford, about 20 minutes from Yandoit by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Yandoit, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Newstead Memorial Park in Newstead (15 min), Newstead Swimming Pool in Newstead (16 min), the playground at Hepburn Regional Park in Daylesford (18 min), Daylesford Spa Country Railway in Daylesford (19 min), Daylesford Library in Daylesford (20 min) and Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford (20 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Yandoit.
