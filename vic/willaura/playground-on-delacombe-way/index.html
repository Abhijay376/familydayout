Playground on Delacombe Way

Playground in Willaura · Basic info

Playground on Delacombe Way is a public playground in Willaura, in the Ararat Rural City area. Willaura Outdoor Pool is about 190 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 100 m from the playground, and bus Ararat - Lake Bolac via Willaura stops at Willaura Kindergarten/Willaura-Wickliffe Rd , a 4-minute walk away.
