Mooroopna is in the Greater Shepparton area, and our data lists 12 playgrounds and 8 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Mooroopna has a library and a zoo or wildlife park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Mooroopna is Gemmill Swamp Wildlife Reserve (no hunting), covering about 216 hectares. WB Ferrari Park and Norton Reserve are the next largest. KidsTown Playground, Playground at Craigmuir Lake Park and Playground at Mooroopna Recreation Reserve are among the council's larger district playgrounds. Other parks and reserves in Mooroopna include C & D Howe Park, Jack Findlay Park and Jack Findlay Park. Playgrounds around Mooroopna include the playground at Smyth Reserve, the playground at Heathmont Park and the playground at Norton Reserve. Our data shows 7 public toilets and 6 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Mooroopna. Places to visit in Mooroopna include Kidstown and Mooroopna Library. The Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks passes through Mooroopna. Our data lists 5 cafes and family restaurants in Mooroopna for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Mooroopna has a train station: Mooroopna Railway Station (V/Line: Shepparton - Melbourne via Seymour). Buses on the 1, 2, 3 and Bendigo Station - Shepparton Station via Kyabram routes stop in Mooroopna. V/Line coaches stop in Mooroopna. By car from Mooroopna, Shepparton is about 7 minutes away, Benalla about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mooroopna is Aquamoves in Shepparton, about 8 minutes' drive. For a beach, Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton is about 9 minutes' drive from Mooroopna. For a skate park, the skate park at Skate Park in Shepparton is about 8 minutes' drive from Mooroopna. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 8 minutes' drive from Mooroopna. The closest indoor play centre to Mooroopna is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 8 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 12 playgrounds across Mooroopna. Rainy days at the library in Mooroopna. Free days on the Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks and in Mooroopna's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Shepparton, next to Mooroopna, has 47 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Mooroopna, Kialla has 11 playgrounds and a zoo or wildlife park. Next door to Mooroopna, Toolamba has 2 playgrounds. Mooroopna North and Ardmona also border Mooroopna.
