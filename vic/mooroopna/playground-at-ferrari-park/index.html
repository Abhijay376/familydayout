Playground at Ferrari Park

Playground in Mooroopna · Official details

This is one of the council's biggest regional playgrounds in Mooroopna, looked after by Greater Shepparton City Council. Mooroopna Library is about 430 m away, and for food, KFC is about 200 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 280 m from the playground, and bus 2 stops at Rodney Neighbourhood Kindergarten/McLennan St, a 2-minute walk away.
