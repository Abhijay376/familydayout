Playground at Smyth Reserve

Playground in Mooroopna · Official details

Playground at Smyth Reserve in Mooroopna is a neighbourhood playground in the Greater Shepparton City area. Bill's Fish Shop is about 340 m away if you need food, and Playground at Ferrari Park is another option about 480 m away. There's a public toilet about 740 m away, and bus 2 stops at John St/Daisy St, a 5-minute walk away.
