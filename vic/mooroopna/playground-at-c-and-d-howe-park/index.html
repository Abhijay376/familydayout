Playground at C & D Howe Park

Playground in Mooroopna · Basic info

This is a neighbourhood playground in Mooroopna, looked after by Greater Shepparton City Council. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. You'll find a public toilet about 600 m from the playground, and bus 3 stops at Rosalind St/McFarlane Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
