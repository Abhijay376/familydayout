KidsTown Playground

Playground in Mooroopna · Official details

KidsTown Playground in Mooroopna is one of the council's biggest regional playgrounds in the Greater Shepparton City area. The Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks is about 270 m away, and Kidstown is about 30 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground. It's easiest by car, about 5 minutes from Mooroopna.
