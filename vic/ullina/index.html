Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Ullina in the Hepburn area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Ullina.

Getting around

Ullina has no train station; Clunes Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 9.8 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Ullina, so most trips start by car. By car from Ullina, Daylesford is about 42 minutes away, Ballarat about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Clunes Swimming Pool in Clunes is about 17 minutes' drive from Ullina. The closest library to Ullina is Creswick Library in Creswick, about 29 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick is about 29 minutes' drive from Ullina. The nearest larger centre is Daylesford, about 42 minutes from Ullina by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Ullina, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Queens Park in Clunes (17 min), Clunes Swimming Pool in Clunes (17 min), Clunes Museum in Clunes (18 min), The Warehouse in Clunes (18 min), Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick (29 min) and Infants in Creswick (29 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Ullina.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Ullina, Clunes has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Campbelltown also borders Ullina.
