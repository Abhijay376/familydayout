Bungalally is in the Horsham area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bungalally is Bungalally I52 Bushland Reserve, covering about 20 hectares. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Bungalally.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bungalally. The closest to Bungalally is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 83 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bungalally, so most trips start by car. By car from Bungalally, Horsham is about 10 minutes away and Stawell about 52 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham is about 9 minutes' drive from Bungalally. For a library, Dimboola Library in Dimboola is about 38 minutes' drive from Bungalally. For a skate park, Velodrome in Horsham is about 8 minutes' drive from Bungalally. For a cinema, Centre Cinemas in Horsham is about 9 minutes' drive from Bungalally. The nearest larger centre is Horsham, about 10 minutes from Bungalally by car, with 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Bungalally are the playground at Uebergang Park in Horsham (8 min), Velodrome in Horsham (8 min), Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham (9 min), Horsham Water Play in Horsham (10 min), Centre Cinemas in Horsham (9 min) and Horsham Skate Park in Horsham (10 min).
