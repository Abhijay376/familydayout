Landmarks

Mount Napier State Park is the largest mapped park in Mount Napier, at about 2,939 hectares. Our data shows 1 off-leash dog park in Mount Napier.

Getting around

Mount Napier has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 66 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mount Napier, so most trips start by car. By car from Mount Napier, Hamilton is about 50 minutes away and Portland about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Mount Napier, about 50 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Mount Napier, the nearest places for kids by road are Hughan Park Playground in Hamilton (49 min), Hamilton History Centre in Hamilton (49 min), Mud Gallery in Hamilton (49 min), Melville Oval Playground in Hamilton (49 min), Hamilton Library in Hamilton (50 min) and Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton (50 min).
