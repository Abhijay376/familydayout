Eversley is in the Ararat area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Eversley is Wimmera River, Eversley Streamside Reserve, covering about 22 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Eversley include Ben Nevis Picnic Area. Picnic areas in Eversley include Ben Nevis Picnic Area. For walks and rides, Borella Walk crosses Eversley.

Getting around

There's no train station in Eversley. The closest to Eversley is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Ararat - Maryborough via Elmhurst, Avoca route stop in Eversley. By car from Eversley, Ararat is about 22 minutes away, Stawell about 35 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Eversley is Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest library to Eversley is Learning Resource Centre in Ararat, about 25 minutes' drive. For a skate park, David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat is about 22 minutes' drive from Eversley. For a cinema, Astor Cinema in Ararat is about 22 minutes' drive from Eversley. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Eversley is Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell, about 37 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Ararat, about 22 minutes from Eversley by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Eversley are Community Playground in Landsborough (25 min), the playground at Smith Street Reserve in Ararat (20 min), David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat (22 min), Ararat Gallery TAMA in Ararat (21 min), Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat (21 min) and Langi Morgala Museum in Ararat (22 min).

Best for

Free days on the Borella Walk and in Eversley's parks.
