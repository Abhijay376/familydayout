Watchem is in the Buloke area, and our data lists 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in the suburb. Watchem also has a pool.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Watchem is Corack I135 Bushland Reserve, covering about 88 hectares. Lake Watchem Bushland Reserve and Lake Watchem Lake Reserve are the next largest. Playgrounds in Watchem include the playground at Lake Watchem Lake Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Watchem. Places to visit in Watchem include Watchem Swimming Pool. For walks and rides, Watchem Lake Walking Track crosses Watchem.

Getting around

Watchem has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 110 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Watchem. By car from Watchem, Horsham is about 81 minutes away and Stawell about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest skate park to Watchem is Birchip Skate Park in Birchip, about 16 minutes' drive. From Watchem, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at J.E Taylor Sunset Park in Birchip (15 min), Birchip History Museum in Birchip (16 min), Birchip Skate Park in Birchip (16 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (27 min), Donald BMX Track in Donald (30 min) and Historical Centre in Warracknabeal (37 min).

Best for

Free days on the Watchem Lake Walking Track and in Watchem's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Watchem, Donald has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Massey and Corack also border Watchem.
