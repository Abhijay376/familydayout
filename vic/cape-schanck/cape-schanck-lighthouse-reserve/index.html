Cape Schanck Lighthouse Reserve

Park in Cape Schanck · Official details

Cape Schanck Lighthouse Reserve is a park in Cape Schanck, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire Council area, good for all ages. The nearest public toilet is about 192 m away and dogs are not allowed. Opening hours: Lighthouse reserve open to pedestrians daily 6am-6pm.
