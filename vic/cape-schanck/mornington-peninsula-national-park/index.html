Mornington Peninsula National Park

Park in Cape Schanck · Official details

Mornington Peninsula National Park is a park in Cape Schanck, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire Council area, good for all ages. Dogs are not allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Mornington Peninsula National Park?
Mornington Peninsula National Park is in Cape Schanck, Victoria.
Is Mornington Peninsula National Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Mornington Peninsula National Park?
No, dogs are not allowed.
