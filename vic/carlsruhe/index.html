Landmarks

The biggest green space in Carlsruhe is Carlsruhe Streamside Reserve, covering about 6 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Carlsruhe.

Getting around

There's no train station in Carlsruhe. The closest to Carlsruhe is Kyneton Railway Station (V/Line), about 4.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Lancefield - Kyneton via Newham & Carlsruhe route stop in Carlsruhe. By car from Carlsruhe, Kyneton is about 11 minutes away, Castlemaine about 37 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 67 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Carlsruhe is Gisborne Aquatic Centre in Gisborne, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest library to Carlsruhe is Kyneton Library in Kyneton, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Carlsruhe is Canoeing in Tylden, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Carlsruhe is Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton, about 11 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Reading Cinema in Sunbury is about 35 minutes' drive from Carlsruhe. The nearest larger centre is Kyneton, about 11 minutes from Carlsruhe by car, with 10 playgrounds and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Carlsruhe are Jean Haynes Playground in Kyneton (9 min), Kyneton Library in Kyneton (11 min), Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton (11 min), Kyneton Community Park in Kyneton (11 min), Kyneton Museum in Kyneton (11 min) and Canoeing in Tylden (18 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Carlsruhe.

Nearby suburbs

Kyneton, next to Carlsruhe, has 10 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Tylden, next to Carlsruhe, has 1 playground and a beach. Next door to Carlsruhe, Newham has 1 playground. Pipers Creek, Kyneton South and Cadello also border Carlsruhe.
