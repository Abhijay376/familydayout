Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Lillico in the Baw Baw area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Lillico has no train station; Warragul Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 4.9 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lillico, so most trips start by car. By car from Lillico, Warragul is about 8 minutes away, Pakenham about 45 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 89 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lillico is Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul, about 9 minutes' drive. For a library, Warragul Library in Warragul is about 8 minutes' drive from Lillico. The closest skate park to Lillico is Warragul Velodrome in Warragul, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Lillico is Warragul Cinema in Warragul, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Lillico is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 9 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Wildlife Trail in Tynong North is about 31 minutes' drive from Lillico. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Lillico, about 8 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Lillico, the nearest places for kids by road are Conventry Drive Playground in Warragul (3 min), the playground at Ellen Close Linear Reserve in Warragul (6 min), Warragul Library in Warragul (8 min), Warragul Cinema in Warragul (8 min), Warragul & District Historical Society Museum in Warragul (8 min) and Warragul Velodrome in Warragul (8 min).
