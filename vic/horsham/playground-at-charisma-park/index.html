Playground at Charisma Park

Playground in Horsham · Basic info

Playground at Charisma Park is a public playground in Horsham, in the Horsham Rural City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 650 m away, and buses 1 and 2 stop at Winifred St/Bowler St, a 2-minute walk away.
