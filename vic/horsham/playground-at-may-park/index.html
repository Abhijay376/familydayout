Playground at May Park

Playground in Horsham · Basic info

Playground at May Park is a public playground in Horsham, in the Horsham Rural City area. Una Sosta is about 60 m away if you need food, and Playground on Wawunna Road is another option about 250 m away. There's a public toilet about 200 m away, and buses 2 and 3 stop at Park Dr/Baillie St, a 5-minute walk away.
