Horsham Skate Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Horsham · Basic info

Horsham Skate Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Horsham, in the Horsham Rural City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Shopping Square/Wilson St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 27 m away.
