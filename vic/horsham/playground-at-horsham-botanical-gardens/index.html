Playground at Horsham Botanical Gardens

Playground in Horsham · Basic info

This is a public playground in Horsham. Horsham Aquatic Centre is about 500 m away, and Horsham Water Play is another option about 210 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and buses 3 and 4 stop at Bolton St/Ocallaghans Pde, a 10-minute walk away.
