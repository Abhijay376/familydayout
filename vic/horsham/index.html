Horsham is in the Horsham area, and our data lists 13 playgrounds and 19 parks and reserves in the suburb. On top of that, Horsham has a pool, a cinema and 2 skate parks.

Landmarks

Horsham Racecourse is the largest mapped park in Horsham, at about 34 hectares. Weir Park and Southbank Wetlands are the next largest. Horsham also has May Park, Apex Island and Jardwa Park. Playgrounds in Horsham include Playland, Horsham Water Play and Apex Island Playground. Our data shows 39 public toilets in Horsham. In Horsham you'll also find Centre Cinemas and Horsham Aquatic Centre. For older kids in Horsham there's Horsham Skate Park and Velodrome. Horsham Gateway Centre and Horsham Plaza are the main shopping centres. Our data lists 11 cafes and family restaurants in Horsham for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Horsham has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 91 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the 1, 2, 3 and 4 routes stop in Horsham. V/Line coaches stop in Horsham. By car from Horsham, Stawell is about 55 minutes away and Ararat about 78 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Horsham is Dimboola Library in Dimboola, about 29 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 13 playgrounds across Horsham. Rainy days at the cinema in Horsham. Teens who skate or ride, with Horsham Skate Park in Horsham.

Nearby suburbs

Vectis, next to Horsham, has 1 playground. Haven, next to Horsham, has 1 playground.
