Horsham Aquatic Centre

Pool or water play in Horsham · Basic info

Horsham Aquatic Centre is a pool or water play in Horsham, in the Horsham Rural City Council area, good for all ages. Horsham Aquatic Centre/Urquhart St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 126 m away with a baby change table.
