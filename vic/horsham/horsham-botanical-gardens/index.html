Horsham Botanical Gardens

Park in Horsham · Basic info

Horsham Botanical Gardens is a park in Horsham, in the Horsham Rural City Council area, good for all ages. Bolton St/Ocallaghans Pde (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 54 m away with a baby change table.
