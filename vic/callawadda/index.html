Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Callawadda in the Northern Grampians area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Callawadda has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 54 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the St Arnaud - Stawell via Marnoo route stop in Callawadda. By car from Callawadda, Stawell is about 23 minutes away and Ararat about 49 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Callawadda is Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Callawadda is Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans, about 44 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell is about 26 minutes' drive from Callawadda. For a zoo or wildlife park, Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell is about 25 minutes' drive from Callawadda. Stawell is the closest bigger centre to Callawadda, about 23 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Callawadda are the playground at Shanahan Park in Marnoo (18 min), Glenorchy Playground in Glenorchy (19 min), Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell (22 min), Railway Station Art and Crafts Gallery in Stawell (24 min), Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell (25 min) and the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Callawadda.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Callawadda, Rupanyup has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Glenorchy, next to Callawadda, has 1 playground. Wal Wal, Riachella, Morrl Morrl and Marnoo West also border Callawadda.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Callawadda, without traffic.
