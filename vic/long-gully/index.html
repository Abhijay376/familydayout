Long Gully is in the Greater Bendigo area, and our data lists 4 playgrounds and 9 parks and reserves in the suburb. Long Gully also has a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Long Gully is Long Gully Recreation Reserve, covering about 8 hectares. Comet Shaft, KK Shaft, and Comet Diggings Historic and Cultural Features Reserve and Windmill Hill Historic Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Long Gully include Bundaleer Park, Derwent Drive Reserve and Casley Street Playspace. Playgrounds around Long Gully include Long Gully Splash Park, Derwent Drive Playspace and the playground at Casley Street Playspace. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Long Gully. For older kids in Long Gully there's Long Gully BMX Track. The Long Gully Trail passes through Long Gully. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Long Gully for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Long Gully. The closest to Long Gully is Eaglehawk Railway Station (V/Line), about 2.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 52, 53 and 54 stop in Long Gully. By car from Long Gully, Bendigo is about 5 minutes away, Castlemaine about 34 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Sloan Swim School in California Gully is about 2 minutes' drive from Long Gully. For a library, Bendigo Library in Bendigo is about 6 minutes' drive from Long Gully. The closest cinema to Long Gully is Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo, about 5 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 7 minutes' drive from Long Gully. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Long Gully is The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo, about 9 minutes' drive.

Best for

Younger kids at Long Gully's 4 local playgrounds. Teens who skate or ride, with Long Gully BMX Track in Long Gully. Free days on the Long Gully Trail and in Long Gully's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Long Gully, Bendigo has 4 playgrounds, a pool and a library. California Gully, next to Long Gully, has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Ironbark, next to Long Gully, has 3 playgrounds. Next door to Long Gully, North Bendigo has 1 playground. Maiden Gully and West Bendigo also border Long Gully.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Long Gully, without traffic.
