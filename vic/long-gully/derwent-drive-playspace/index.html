Derwent Drive Playspace

Playground in Long Gully · Basic info

Derwent Drive Playspace is a public playground in Long Gully, in the Greater Bendigo City area. The Long Gully Trail is about 310 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 330 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 810 m from the playground, and bus 53 stops at Energetic St/Eaglehawk Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
