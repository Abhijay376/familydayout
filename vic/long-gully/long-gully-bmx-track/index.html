Long Gully BMX Track

Skate, BMX or pump track in Long Gully · Basic info

Long Gully BMX Track is a skate, bmx or pump track in Long Gully, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Energetic St/Eaglehawk Rd (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 739 m away with a baby change table.
