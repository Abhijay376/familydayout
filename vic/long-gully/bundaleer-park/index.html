Bundaleer Park

Park in Long Gully · Basic info

Bundaleer Park is a park in Long Gully, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, good for all ages. Holdsworth Rd/Sandhurst Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1251 m away.
