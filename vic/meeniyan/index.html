Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Meeniyan, in the South Gippsland area.

Landmarks

Tanderra Park is the largest mapped park in Meeniyan, at about 1 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Meeniyan. The Great Southern Rail Trail passes through Meeniyan.

Getting around

Meeniyan has no train station; Yarragon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 42 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Meeniyan. By car from Meeniyan, Leongatha is about 15 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 35 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Meeniyan is South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha, about 15 minutes' drive. For a library, Leongatha Library in Leongatha is about 14 minutes' drive from Meeniyan. The closest beach to Meeniyan is The Caves in Inverloch, about 31 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Leongatha Skate Park in Leongatha is about 14 minutes' drive from Meeniyan. The closest cinema to Meeniyan is Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha, about 15 minutes' drive. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Meeniyan, about 15 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Meeniyan, the nearest places for kids by road are Leongatha Library in Leongatha (14 min), Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha (15 min), South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha (15 min), Leongatha Skate Park in Leongatha (14 min), the playground at Mary Checkley Reserve in Leongatha (15 min) and Splash Pools and Stadium in Leongatha (15 min).

Best for

Free days on the Great Southern Rail Trail and in Meeniyan's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Meeniyan, Buffalo has 1 playground. Middle Tarwin, Koonwarra and Dumbalk also border Meeniyan.
