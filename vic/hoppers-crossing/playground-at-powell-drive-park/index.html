Playground at Powell Drive Park

Playground in Hoppers Crossing · Official details

Playground at Powell Drive Park is a neighbourhood playground in Hoppers Crossing, in the Wyndham City area. The Federation Trail passes about 140 m away, and Michael's Fish & Chips is about 70 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 120 m away, and bus 160 stops at Hoppers Crossing Station/Old Geelong Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
