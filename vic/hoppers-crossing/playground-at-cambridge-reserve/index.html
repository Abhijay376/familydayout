Playground at Cambridge Reserve

Playground in Hoppers Crossing · Official details

Playground at Cambridge Reserve in Hoppers Crossing is a neighbourhood playground in the Wyndham City area. The Heathdale Glen Orden Trail is about 30 m away, and for food, Gloria Jean's is about 360 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 580 m from the playground, and bus 167 stops at Johnson Ave/Barber Dr, a 2-minute walk away.
