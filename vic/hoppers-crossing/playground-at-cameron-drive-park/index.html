Playground at Cameron Drive Park

Playground in Hoppers Crossing · Official details

Playground at Cameron Drive Park in Hoppers Crossing is a neighbourhood playground in the Wyndham City area. Playground at Hunter Avenue Park is another option about 250 m away. There's a public toilet about 910 m away, and buses 161 and 181 stop at Sier Ave/Cameron Dr, a 3-minute walk away.
