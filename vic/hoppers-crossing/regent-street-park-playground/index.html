Regent Street Park Playground

Playground in Hoppers Crossing · Official details

This is a neighbourhood playground in Hoppers Crossing, looked after by Wyndham City Council. Older kids have a skate park about 220 m away, and for food, Pronto Pizza & Spaghetti Bar is about 400 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 740 m from the playground, and buses 166 and 167 stop at Hampstead Dr/Heaths Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
