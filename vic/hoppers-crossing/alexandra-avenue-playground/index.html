Alexandra Avenue Playground

Playground in Hoppers Crossing · Official details

Alexandra Avenue Playground is a neighbourhood playground in Hoppers Crossing, in the Wyndham City area. Subway is about 320 m away if you need food, and Playground at St James Wood Park is another option about 270 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.3 km away, and bus 160 stops at Alexandra Ave/Morris Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
