Playground at Whitsunday Drive Park

Playground in Hoppers Crossing · Official details

Playground at Whitsunday Drive Park is a neighbourhood playground in Hoppers Crossing, in the Wyndham City area. Playground at Cameron Drive Park is another option about 410 m away. There's a public toilet about 520 m away, and bus 170 stops at Gabo St/Derrimut Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
