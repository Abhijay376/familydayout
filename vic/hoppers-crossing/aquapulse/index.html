AquaPulse

Pool or water play in Hoppers Crossing · Official details

AquaPulse is a pool or water play in Hoppers Crossing, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Aqua Pulse Leisure Centre/Derrimut Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 746 m away with a baby change table.
