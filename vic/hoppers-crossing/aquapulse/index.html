AquaPulse, Hoppers Crossing

Pool or water play in Hoppers Crossing · Official details

AquaPulse is a pool or water play in Hoppers Crossing, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Aqua Pulse Leisure Centre/Derrimut Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 746 m away with a baby change table. Questions parents ask

What are the opening hours of AquaPulse?
AquaPulse is open Mon–Thu 5:30am–9pm, Fri 5:30am–8pm, Sat 7am–6pm, Sun 8am–6pm. Hours can change on public holidays, so check before you go.
Where is AquaPulse?
AquaPulse is in Hoppers Crossing, Victoria, at 80-82 Derrimut Road, Hoppers Crossing, VIC 3029.
Are there toilets at AquaPulse?
The nearest public toilet is about 746 m away, with a baby change table.
How do I get to AquaPulse by public transport?
The closest stop is Aqua Pulse Leisure Centre/Derrimut Rd (routes 170), a 2-minute walk.
What is the phone number for AquaPulse?
Call AquaPulse on (03) 8734 5678.
Is AquaPulse wheelchair accessible?
Yes, it is wheelchair accessible.
