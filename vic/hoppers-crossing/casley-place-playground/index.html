Casley Place Playground

Playground in Hoppers Crossing · Official details

This is a neighbourhood playground in Hoppers Crossing, looked after by Wyndham City Council. For food, Subway is about 270 m away, and Alexandra Avenue Playground is another option about 310 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus 160 stops at Alexandra Ave/Morris Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
