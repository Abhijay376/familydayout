Geddes Crescent Park

Park in Hoppers Crossing · Basic info

Geddes Crescent Park is a park in Hoppers Crossing, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Geddes Cres/Heaths Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 441 m away with a baby change table.
