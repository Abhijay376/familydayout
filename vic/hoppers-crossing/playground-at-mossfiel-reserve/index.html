Playground at Mossfiel Reserve

Playground in Hoppers Crossing · Official details

This is one of the council's larger playgrounds in Hoppers Crossing, looked after by Wyndham City Council. Older kids have a skate park about 160 m away, and Regent Street Park Playground is another option about 310 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 60 m from the playground, and buses 166 and 167 stop at Hampstead Dr/Heaths Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
