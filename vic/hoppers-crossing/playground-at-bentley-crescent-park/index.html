Playground at Bentley Crescent Park

Playground in Hoppers Crossing · Official details

Playground at Bentley Crescent Park in Hoppers Crossing is a neighbourhood playground in the Wyndham City area. Timezone is about 570 m away, and for food, KFC is about 400 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 800 m from the playground, and bus 166 stops at Pacific Werribee Shopping Centre/Derrimut Rd , a 7-minute walk away.
