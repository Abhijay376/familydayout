O'Neill Avenue Playground

Playground in Hoppers Crossing · Official details

O'Neill Avenue Playground is a neighbourhood playground in Hoppers Crossing, in the Wyndham City area. Sottile's Pizza & Family Restaurant is about 500 m away if you need food, and Playground at Aitken Avenue Park is another option about 490 m away. There's a public toilet about 430 m away, and bus 160 stops at Spring Dr/Morris Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
