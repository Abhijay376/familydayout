Medina Drive Reserve

Park in Hoppers Crossing · Basic info

Medina Drive Reserve is a park in Hoppers Crossing, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Medina Dr/Wilmington Ave (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 680 m away.
