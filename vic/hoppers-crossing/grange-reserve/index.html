Grange Reserve

Park in Hoppers Crossing · Official details

Grange Reserve is a park in Hoppers Crossing, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Dowling Ave/Hogans Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 92 m away.

Questions parents ask

Where is Grange Reserve?
Grange Reserve is in Hoppers Crossing, Victoria.
Is Grange Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Grange Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 92 m away.
How do I get to Grange Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Dowling Ave/Hogans Rd (routes 167), a 3-minute walk.
