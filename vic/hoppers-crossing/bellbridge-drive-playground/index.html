Bellbridge Drive Playground

Playground in Hoppers Crossing · Official details

Bellbridge Drive Playground in Hoppers Crossing is a neighbourhood playground in the Wyndham City area. Nicklaus Drive Playground is another option about 340 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.3 km away, and bus 161 stops at Bellbridge Dr/Birchwood Bvd, a 1-minute walk away.
