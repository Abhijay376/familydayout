Playground at Fraser Street Reserve

Playground in Hoppers Crossing · Official details

Playground at Fraser Street Reserve in Hoppers Crossing is a neighbourhood playground in the Wyndham City area. Playground at Callander Crescent Park is another option about 410 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 80 m from the playground, and bus 161 stops at Stella Way/Pannam Dr, a 2-minute walk away.
