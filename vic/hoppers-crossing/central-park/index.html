Central Park

Park in Hoppers Crossing · Official details

Central Park is a park in Hoppers Crossing, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Franklin Bvd/Morris Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 165 m away.

Details

Good to know
Public BBQ about 30 m away (Council)

Questions parents ask

Where is Central Park?
Central Park is in Hoppers Crossing, Victoria.
Is Central Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Central Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 165 m away.
How do I get to Central Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Franklin Bvd/Morris Rd (routes 160, 161), a 3-minute walk.
