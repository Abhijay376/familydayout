Kiah Reserve Playground

Playground in Hoppers Crossing · Official details

This is a neighbourhood playground in Hoppers Crossing, looked after by Wyndham City Council. The Skeleton Creek Trail is about 340 m away, and Bellbridge Drive Playground is another option about 490 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus 161 stops at Grevillea Cres/Maple Cres, a 1-minute walk away.
