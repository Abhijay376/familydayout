Strathmore Crescent Playground

Playground in Hoppers Crossing · Official details

Strathmore Crescent Playground in Hoppers Crossing is a neighbourhood playground in the Wyndham City area. AquaPulse is about 450 m away, and Pronto Pizza & Spaghetti Bar is about 350 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 750 m away, and buses 166 and 167 stop at Woodville Park Dr/Heaths Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
