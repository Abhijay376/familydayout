Playground at Breton Drive Park

Playground in Hoppers Crossing · Official details

Playground at Breton Drive Park in Hoppers Crossing is a neighbourhood playground in the Wyndham City area. Bethany Road Playground is another option about 240 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.0 km away, and bus 167 stops at Clearview Court Reserve/Bethany Rd , a 4-minute walk away.
