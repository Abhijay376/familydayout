Wright Avenue Playground

Playground in Upwey · Basic info

Wright Avenue Playground in Upwey is a public playground in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. The Ringwood - Belgrave Rail Trail is about 340 m away, and Upwey / Belgrave RSL is about 230 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 730 m from the playground, and bus 699 stops at Mast Gully Rd/The Avenue, a 1-minute walk away.
