Playground on Burwood Highway

Playground in Upwey · Basic info

Playground on Burwood Highway is a public playground in Upwey, in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. The Ringwood - Belgrave Rail Trail passes about 160 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 300 m away. There's a public toilet about 440 m away, and bus 693 stops at Upwey Station/Burwood Hwy, a 2-minute walk away.
