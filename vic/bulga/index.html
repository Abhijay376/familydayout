Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Bulga, in the Swan Hill area.

Landmarks

Bulgei Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Bulga, at about 17 hectares.

Getting around

Bulga has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bulga, so most trips start by car. By car from Bulga, Swan Hill is about 35 minutes away and Echuca about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill is about 34 minutes' drive from Bulga. For a library, Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill is about 34 minutes' drive from Bulga. The closest skate park to Bulga is Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South, about 25 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill is about 35 minutes' drive from Bulga. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 35 minutes from Bulga by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Bulga are Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South (25 min), the playground at Jaycee Park in Swan Hill (37 min), the playground at Nyah Heritage Park in Nyah (32 min), Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill (34 min), the skate park at James Belsar Reserve in Swan Hill (35 min) and Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill (34 min).
