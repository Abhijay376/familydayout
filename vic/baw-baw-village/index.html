Baw Baw Village is in the Baw Baw area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Baw Baw Village, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 2 public toilets in Baw Baw Village. The Village Trail and Summit Trail all pass through Baw Baw Village.

Getting around

Baw Baw Village has no train station; Moe Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 37 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Baw Baw Village, so most trips start by car. By car from Baw Baw Village, Traralgon is about 82 minutes away, Warragul about 85 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Baw Baw Village, the nearest places for kids by road are Rawson Swimming Pool in Rawson (49 min), the playground at Erica State Forest in Rawson (50 min), the playground at Croxford Park in Rawson (50 min) and Fire station in Walhalla (62 min).
