Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Cudgewa, in the Towong area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Cudgewa is Mount Mitta Mitta Regional Park, covering about 3,934 hectares. Mount Mitta Mitta Flora Reserve and Stony Creek Streamside Reserve are the next largest. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Cudgewa.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cudgewa. The closest to Cudgewa is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 78 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Albury - Corryong via Walwa route stop in Cudgewa. By car from Cudgewa, Wodonga is about 88 minutes away and Wangaratta about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cudgewa is Corryong Public swimming pool in Corryong, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest library to Cudgewa is Corryong Elyne Mitchell Library in Corryong, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Cudgewa is Corryong Cinema in Corryong, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Cudgewa are Corryong Cinema in Corryong (12 min), Corryong Elyne Mitchell Library in Corryong (13 min), Corryong Public swimming pool in Corryong (13 min), The Spotted Goanna in Walwa (41 min) and Pebble Beach in Walwa (48 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Cudgewa.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Cudgewa, Corryong has a pool, a library and a cinema. Walwa, next to Cudgewa, has 1 playground and a beach. Pine Mountain, Nariel Valley, Lucyvale and Guys Forest also border Cudgewa.
