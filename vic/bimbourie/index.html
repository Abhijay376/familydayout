Bimbourie is in the Buloke area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bimbourie, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Bimbourie has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 72 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bimbourie, so most trips start by car. By car from Bimbourie, Swan Hill is about 73 minutes away and Mildura about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake is about 16 minutes' drive from Bimbourie. From Bimbourie, the nearest places for kids by road are Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake (16 min), the playground at Apex Park in Sea Lake (17 min), the playground at Tempy Recreation Reserve in Tempy (31 min) and Tempy Outdoor Swimming Pool in Tempy (32 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bimbourie.

Lake Tyrrell also borders Bimbourie.
