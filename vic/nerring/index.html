Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Nerring in the Pyrenees area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Nerring. The closest to Nerring is Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line), about 7.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nerring, so most trips start by car. By car from Nerring, Ballarat is about 43 minutes away, Ararat about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Nerring is Beaufort Pool in Beaufort, about 12 minutes' drive. For a library, Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort is about 12 minutes' drive from Nerring. For a skate park, the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort is about 11 minutes' drive from Nerring. Ballarat is the closest bigger centre to Nerring, about 43 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Nerring are the playground at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (11 min), the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (11 min), the playground at Wotherspoon Reserve in Beaufort (12 min), Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort (12 min), Beaufort Pool in Beaufort (12 min) and Linton BMX park in Linton (40 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Nerring.
