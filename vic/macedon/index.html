Landmarks

The biggest green space in Macedon is Centennial Park, covering about 2 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Macedon include Macedon Flora Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets and 1 off-leash dog park in Macedon.

Getting around

Trains stop at Macedon Railway Station (V/Line: Bendigo - Melbourne via Gisborne and Echuca/Moama - Melbourne via Bendigo or Heathcote) in Macedon. V/Line coaches stop in Macedon. By car from Macedon, Kyneton is about 22 minutes away, Bacchus Marsh about 38 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 54 minutes, without traffic.
