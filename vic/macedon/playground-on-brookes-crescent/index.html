Playground on Brookes Crescent

Playground in Macedon · Basic info

Playground on Brookes Crescent in Macedon is a public playground in the Macedon Ranges Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet right by the playground. It's easiest by car, about 4 minutes from Macedon.
