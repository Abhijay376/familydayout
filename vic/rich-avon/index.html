Landmarks

Laen I131 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Rich Avon, at about 5 hectares.

Getting around

Rich Avon has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 92 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Rich Avon, so most trips start by car. By car from Rich Avon, Stawell is about 60 minutes away and Horsham about 69 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Rich Avon is Donald Swimming Pool in Donald, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Rich Avon is Donald BMX Track in Donald, about 13 minutes' drive. Stawell is the closest bigger centre to Rich Avon, about 60 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Rich Avon are Guthrie Park Playground in Rich Avon West (5 min), the playground at Donald Public Park in Donald (11 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (11 min), Donald BMX Track in Donald (13 min), Rupanyup Swimming Pool in Rupanyup (34 min) and Woods' Farming and Heritage Museum in Rupanyup (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Rich Avon.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Rich Avon, Donald has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Rich Avon, Rich Avon West has 1 playground. Laen East, Laen and Cope Cope also border Rich Avon.
