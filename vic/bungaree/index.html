Bungaree is in the Moorabool area, and our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Bungaree Recreation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Bungaree, at about 7 hectares. Playgrounds in Bungaree include the playground at Bungaree Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Bungaree.

Getting around

Bungaree has no train station; Ballarat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Ballarat - Mt Egerton via Gordon route stop in Bungaree. By car from Bungaree, Ballarat is about 15 minutes away, Daylesford about 32 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 80 minutes, without traffic.
