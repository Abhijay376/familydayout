Our data lists 13 playgrounds and 2 parks and reserves in Fraser Rise, in the Melton area.

Landmarks

Stony Hill Creek Linear Reserve is the largest mapped park in Fraser Rise, at about 13 hectares. Fraser Rise also has Auden Park. Playgrounds in Fraser Rise include Lunar Way Playground, Kea St Reserve Playground and Mawson Gardens Playground. City Vista Shopping Centre is the local shopping centre. The Hume Drive Trail passes through Fraser Rise. Our data lists 3 cafes and family restaurants in Fraser Rise for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Fraser Rise has no train station; Caroline Springs Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 5.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 461, 463 and 464 stop in Fraser Rise. By car from Fraser Rise, Melton is about 18 minutes away, Sunshine about 19 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 34 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Fraser Rise is Paul Sadler Swimland in Aintree, about 8 minutes' drive. For a library, Caroline Springs Library in Caroline Springs is about 5 minutes' drive from Fraser Rise. For a beach, Altona Beach in Altona is about 29 minutes' drive from Fraser Rise. The closest skate park to Fraser Rise is the skate park at Packard Street Reserve in Keilor Downs, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Fraser Rise is Hoyts in Taylors Lakes, about 12 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes is about 13 minutes' drive from Fraser Rise. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Fraser Rise is Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville, about 32 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 13 playgrounds across Fraser Rise. Free days on the Hume Drive Trail and in Fraser Rise's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Caroline Springs, next to Fraser Rise, has 31 playgrounds and a library. Hillside, next to Fraser Rise, has 22 playgrounds. Diggers Rest, next to Fraser Rise, has 11 playgrounds. Deanside, next to Fraser Rise, has 6 playgrounds. Bonnie Brook and Calder Park also border Fraser Rise.
