Lunar Way Playground

Playground in Fraser Rise · Basic info

Lunar Way Playground in Fraser Rise is a public playground in the Melton City area. The Hume Drive Trail passes about 230 m away, and Bella Vista Café is about 390 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 1.6 km away, and bus 463 stops at Solstice Dr/City Vista Ct, a 2-minute walk away.
