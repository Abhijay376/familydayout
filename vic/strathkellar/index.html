Landmarks

The biggest green space in Strathkellar is Doling Doling Swamp Lake Reserve, covering about 34 hectares. Our data shows 1 off-leash dog park in Strathkellar.

Getting around

There's no train station in Strathkellar. The closest to Strathkellar is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 82 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Strathkellar, so most trips start by car. By car from Strathkellar, Hamilton is about 10 minutes away and Portland about 77 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Strathkellar is Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Hamilton, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest library to Strathkellar is Hamilton Library in Hamilton, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Strathkellar is hamilton skate park in Hamilton, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Strathkellar is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 10 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 10 minutes from Strathkellar by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Strathkellar are Tarrington Rec Reserve Playground in Tarrington (11 min), health station 3/4 playground in Hamilton (6 min), hamilton skate park in Hamilton (9 min), Sir Reginald Ansett Transport Museum in Hamilton (9 min), Hamilton Library in Hamilton (10 min) and Hamilton Gallery in Hamilton (10 min).

Best for

Nearby suburbs

Hamilton, next to Strathkellar, has 23 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Strathkellar, Tarrington has 1 playground. Hensley Park also borders Strathkellar.
