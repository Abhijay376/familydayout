Our data lists 34 playgrounds and 15 parks and reserves in Clyde North, in the Casey area. On top of that, Clyde North has a pool and a skate park.

Landmarks

Berwick Waters Wetlands Park is the largest mapped park in Clyde North, at about 20 hectares. Cascades on Clyde Wetlands and Ramlegh Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Clyde North include Hilltop Park, Heritage Park and Pygora Way Park. Playgrounds around Clyde North include the playground at Hilltop Park, the playground at Heritage Park and the playground at Pygora Way Park. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Clyde North. In Clyde North you'll also find King Swim. For older kids in Clyde North there's Clyde North Skatepark (Bernardins Street). For walks and rides, Bike: Thompsons Road crosses Clyde North. Our data lists 8 cafes and family restaurants in Clyde North for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Clyde North has no train station; Cranbourne Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 4.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 798, 831, 881, 888, 889, 897, 898 and 899 stop in Clyde North. By car from Clyde North, Cranbourne is about 8 minutes away, Pakenham about 20 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 45 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Clyde North is Cranbourne Library in Cranbourne East, about 6 minutes' drive. For a beach, Seaford Beach in Seaford is about 23 minutes' drive from Clyde North. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Narre Warren is about 14 minutes' drive from Clyde North. The closest indoor play centre to Clyde North is Oz Tenpin in Narre Warren, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Clyde North is Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale, about 15 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 34 playgrounds across Clyde North. Teens who skate or ride, with Clyde North Skatepark (Bernardins Street) in Clyde North. Free days on the Bike: Thompsons Road and in Clyde North's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Clyde North, Berwick has 58 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Cranbourne East, next to Clyde North, has 16 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Narre Warren South, next to Clyde North, has 18 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Clyde North, Officer has 14 playgrounds. Cranbourne North, Clyde, Cardinia and Officer South also border Clyde North.
