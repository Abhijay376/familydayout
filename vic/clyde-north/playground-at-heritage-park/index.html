Playground at Heritage Park

Playground in Clyde North · Basic info

This is a public playground in Clyde North. For food, McDonald's is about 400 m away, and Playground at Pasadena Park is another option about 540 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 50 m from the playground, and bus 897 stops at Pasedena Bvd/Berwick-Cranbourne Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
