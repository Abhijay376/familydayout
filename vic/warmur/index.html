Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Warmur, in the Buloke area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Warmur is Watchem I120 Bushland Reserve, covering about 44 hectares. Warmur I119 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Warmur has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 111 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Warmur, so most trips start by car. By car from Warmur, Horsham is about 82 minutes away and Stawell about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Warmur is Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem, about 15 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Birchip Skate Park in Birchip is about 30 minutes' drive from Warmur. From Warmur, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Lake Watchem Lake Reserve in Watchem (16 min), Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem (15 min), the playground at J.E Taylor Sunset Park in Birchip (28 min), Birchip History Museum in Birchip (29 min), Birchip Skate Park in Birchip (30 min) and Historical Centre in Warracknabeal (38 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Warmur.

Nearby suburbs

Watchem, next to Warmur, has 1 playground and a pool. Birchip West and Ballapur also border Warmur.
