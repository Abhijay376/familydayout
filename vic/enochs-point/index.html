Enochs Point in the Mansfield area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Mount Terrible Natural Features and Scenic Reserve is the largest mapped park in Enochs Point, at about 125 hectares. Enochs Point also has Enoch Point, Old Coach Camping Area and Chaffe Creek Camping Area. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Enochs Point. Picnic areas in Enochs Point include Chaffe Creek Camping Area and Enoch Point. For camping, there's Old Coach Camping Area. The Enoch Falls Walk passes through Enochs Point.

Getting around

Enochs Point has no train station; Drouin Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 82 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Enochs Point, so most trips start by car. By car from Enochs Point, Mansfield is about 1.5 hours away and Benalla about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Enochs Point, the nearest places for kids by road are Baanh ba Djila Splash Park in Eildon (80 min), the playground at W.J Moore Park in Eildon (80 min) and Eildon Swimming Pool in Eildon (80 min).

Best for

Free days on the Enoch Falls Walk and in Enochs Point's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Enochs Point, Eildon has 3 playgrounds and a pool.
