Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Pastoria, in the Macedon Ranges area.

Landmarks

Cobaw Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Pastoria, at about 1 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Pastoria. The closest to Pastoria is Kyneton Railway Station (V/Line), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Pastoria, so most trips start by car. By car from Pastoria, Kyneton is about 23 minutes away, Castlemaine about 49 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 89 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Gisborne Aquatic Centre in Gisborne is about 44 minutes' drive from Pastoria. For a library, Kyneton Library in Kyneton is about 23 minutes' drive from Pastoria. For a beach, Canoeing in Tylden is about 34 minutes' drive from Pastoria. The closest skate park to Pastoria is Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton, about 24 minutes' drive. Kyneton is the closest bigger centre to Pastoria, about 23 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds and a library. From Pastoria, the nearest places for kids by road are Jean Haynes Playground in Kyneton (23 min), Kyneton Museum in Kyneton (23 min), Kyneton Library in Kyneton (23 min), Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton (24 min), Kyneton Community Park in Kyneton (24 min) and Canoeing in Tylden (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Pastoria.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Pastoria, Kyneton has 10 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Lancefield and Benloch also border Pastoria.
