Warrak in the Ararat area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Warrak is Langi Ghiran State Park, covering about 2,969 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Warrak. The Reservoir Walk and Water Race Circuit all pass through Warrak.

Getting around

There's no train station in Warrak. The closest to Warrak is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Warrak, so most trips start by car. By car from Warrak, Ararat is about 30 minutes away, Stawell about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Warrak is Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat, about 29 minutes' drive. For a library, Learning Resource Centre in Ararat is about 33 minutes' drive from Warrak. For a skate park, David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat is about 30 minutes' drive from Warrak. For a cinema, Astor Cinema in Ararat is about 30 minutes' drive from Warrak. The nearest larger centre is Ararat, about 30 minutes from Warrak by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Warrak, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Smith Street Reserve in Ararat (28 min), David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat (30 min), Ararat Gallery TAMA in Ararat (29 min), Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat (29 min), Langi Morgala Museum in Ararat (30 min) and Learning Resource Centre in Ararat (33 min).

Best for
