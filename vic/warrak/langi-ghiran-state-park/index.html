Langi Ghiran State Park

Park in Warrak · Official details

Langi Ghiran State Park is a park in Warrak, in the Ararat Rural City Council area, good for all ages.

Details

Good to know
Reservoir and dam have deep water, supervise children, no swimming or fishing; Picnic area has tables and wood-fired BBQs (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Langi Ghiran State Park?
Langi Ghiran State Park is in Warrak, Victoria.
Is Langi Ghiran State Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
