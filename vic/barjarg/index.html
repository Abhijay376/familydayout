Landmarks

The biggest green space in Barjarg is Glen Creek Reference Area, covering about 416 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Barjarg. The closest to Barjarg is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 43 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Barjarg, so most trips start by car. By car from Barjarg, Mansfield is about 14 minutes away, Benalla about 37 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Barjarg is Mansfield Outdoor Swimming Pool in Mansfield, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest library to Barjarg is Mansfield Library in Mansfield, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Barjarg is the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield, about 15 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield is about 15 minutes' drive from Barjarg. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Barjarg is Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield, about 25 minutes' drive. Mansfield is the closest bigger centre to Barjarg, about 14 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Barjarg are the playground at Maindample Community Park in Maindample (10 min), Mansfield Library in Mansfield (14 min), Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield (15 min), the playground at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (15 min), the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (15 min) and Mansfield Outdoor Swimming Pool in Mansfield (15 min).
