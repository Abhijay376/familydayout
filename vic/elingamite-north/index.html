Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Elingamite North, in the Corangamite area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Elingamite North is Lake Elingamite Lake Reserve, covering about 373 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Elingamite North. The closest to Elingamite North is Terang Railway Station (V/Line), about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Elingamite North, so most trips start by car. By car from Elingamite North, Colac is about 45 minutes away, Warrnambool about 51 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Elingamite North is Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon, about 28 minutes' drive. For a library, Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake is about 40 minutes' drive from Elingamite North. The closest beach to Elingamite North is Buttress Point in The Cove, about 44 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Elingamite North is Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Elingamite North is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 44 minutes' drive. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Elingamite North, about 45 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Elingamite North are the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve in Camperdown (21 min), Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (20 min), the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (28 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (28 min), the playground at Tea Tree Lake Reserve in Mortlake (40 min) and Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake (40 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Elingamite North.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Elingamite North, Cobden has 1 playground. Dixie and Cobrico also border Elingamite North.
