Landmarks

Sandford also has Wannon River - Sandford Camping Area 1 (CLWF). For camping, there's Wannon River - Sandford Camping Area 1 (CLWF).

Getting around

There's no train station in Sandford. The closest to Sandford is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 125 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Sandford, so most trips start by car. By car from Sandford, Hamilton is about 51 minutes away and Portland about 73 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Sandford is Casterton Pool in Casterton, about 6 minutes' drive. For a skate park, coleraine skate park in Coleraine is about 23 minutes' drive from Sandford. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 51 minutes from Sandford by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Sandford, the nearest places for kids by road are Gorman's art gallery in Casterton (5 min), Casterton Pool in Casterton (6 min), Merino Swimming Pool in Merino (14 min), the playground at Lions Park in Merino (14 min), coleraine skate park in Coleraine (23 min) and the playground at Queens Park in Coleraine (23 min).
