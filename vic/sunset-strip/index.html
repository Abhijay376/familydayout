Sunset Strip is in the Bass Coast area, and our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Sunset Strip is Sunset Strip Reserve, covering about 1 hectares. Playgrounds around Sunset Strip include the playground at Sunset Strip Reserve. The Phillip Island Bike Path passes through Sunset Strip.

Getting around

There's no train station in Sunset Strip. The closest to Sunset Strip is Stony Point Railway Station (metro), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Sunset Strip, so most trips start by car. By car from Sunset Strip, Wonthaggi is about 36 minutes away, Leongatha about 66 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Sunset Strip is Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Wonthaggi, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest library to Sunset Strip is Phillip Island Library in Cowes, about 9 minutes' drive. For a beach, Smiths Beach in Rhyll is about 4 minutes' drive from Sunset Strip. The closest skate park to Sunset Strip is the skate park at Wonthaggi Railway Historic Reserve in Kilcunda, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Sunset Strip is Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Sunset Strip is A Maze'N Things in Cowes, about 2 minutes' drive. Wonthaggi is the closest bigger centre to Sunset Strip, about 36 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Sunset Strip are A Maze'N Things in Cowes (2 min), Koala Conservation Reserve in Rhyll (4 min), the playground at Mitchell Reserve in Smiths Beach (3 min), Smiths Beach in Rhyll (4 min), YCW in Smiths Beach (5 min) and Mingara Gallery in Cowes (6 min).

Best for

Free days on the Phillip Island Bike Path and in Sunset Strip's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Sunset Strip, Cowes has 6 playgrounds, a library and a beach. Rhyll, next to Sunset Strip, has 2 playgrounds, a beach and a zoo or wildlife park.
