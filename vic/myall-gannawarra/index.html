Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Myall (Gannawarra), in the Gannawarra area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Myall (Gannawarra) is Myall 2 Bushland Reserve, covering about 32 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Myall (Gannawarra). The closest to Myall (Gannawarra) is Kerang Railway Station (V/Line), about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Myall (Gannawarra), so most trips start by car. By car from Myall (Gannawarra), Swan Hill is about 58 minutes away and Echuca about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Myall (Gannawarra) is Swimming Pool in Kerang, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest library to Myall (Gannawarra) is Barham Branch Library in Koondrook, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Myall (Gannawarra) is Barham Skate Park in Koondrook, about 22 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 58 minutes from Myall (Gannawarra) by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Myall (Gannawarra) are the playground at Riverside Park in Koondrook (21 min), Barham Branch Library in Koondrook (21 min), Barham Skate Park in Koondrook (22 min), the playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV) in Koondrook (23 min), Swimming Pool in Kerang (25 min) and Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (25 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Myall (Gannawarra).

Nearby suburbs

Koondrook, next to Myall (Gannawarra), has 2 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Murrabit, next to Myall (Gannawarra), has 1 playground. Capels Crossing also borders Myall (Gannawarra).
