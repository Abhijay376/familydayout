Landmarks

The biggest green space in Albacutya is Lake Albacutya Park, covering about 8,334 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Albacutya.

Getting around

Albacutya has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 150 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Albacutya, so most trips start by car. By car from Albacutya, Horsham is about 2 hours away and Swan Hill about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest places for kids by road from Albacutya are the playground at Yaapeet Rec Reserve in Yaapeet (29 min), Rainbow Pool in Rainbow (46 min), the playground at Hopetoun Recreation Reserve in Hopetoun (53 min) and Hopetoun Swimming Pool in Hopetoun (53 min).
