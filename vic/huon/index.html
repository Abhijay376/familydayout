Huon in the Indigo area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Huon is Sandy Creek, Red Bluff Streamside Reserve, covering about 17 hectares. Huon Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Huon. The High Country Rail Trail passes through Huon.

Getting around

There's no train station in Huon. The closest to Huon is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Huon, so most trips start by car. By car from Huon, Wodonga is about 21 minutes away and Wangaratta about 73 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Huon is Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta, about 13 minutes' drive. For a library, Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta is about 14 minutes' drive from Huon. For a beach, Finns Beach in Bandiana is about 27 minutes' drive from Huon. The closest skate park to Huon is Skate Park in Baranduda, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Huon is Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana, about 16 minutes' drive. Wodonga is the closest bigger centre to Huon, about 21 minutes away by car, and has 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Huon are the playground at Banksia Park in Tangambalanga (7 min), the playground at Ludlows Reserve in Ebden (6 min), The Bonegilla Migrant Experience in Bonegilla (12 min), Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta (13 min), Tallangatta Art & Photography in Tallangatta (14 min) and Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta (14 min).

Best for

Free days on the High Country Rail Trail and in Huon's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Huon, Tallangatta has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Next door to Huon, Ebden has 3 playgrounds. Tangambalanga, next to Huon, has 2 playgrounds. Jarvis Creek, Charleroi and Bethanga also border Huon.
