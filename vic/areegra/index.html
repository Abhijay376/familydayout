Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Areegra in the Yarriambiack area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Areegra has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 116 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Areegra, so most trips start by car. By car from Areegra, Horsham is about 65 minutes away and Stawell about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Minyip Swimming Pool in Minyip is about 29 minutes' drive from Areegra. The closest skate park to Areegra is Donald BMX Track in Donald, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Areegra are the playground at Minyip Wetlands in Minyip (28 min), Minyip Swimming Pool in Minyip (29 min), Lions Playground in Minyip (28 min), Historical Centre in Warracknabeal (21 min), Wheatlands Warracknabeal Agricultural Machinery Museum in Warracknabeal (23 min) and Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem (32 min).
