Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Cudgee, in the Moyne area. Cudgee also has a zoo or wildlife park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Cudgee is Hopkins Falls Scenic Reserve, covering about 2 hectares. Places to visit in Cudgee include Cudgee Wildlife Park.

Getting around

Cudgee has no train station; Sherwood Park Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cudgee, so most trips start by car. By car from Cudgee, Warrnambool is about 18 minutes away and Colac about 80 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool is about 22 minutes' drive from Cudgee. For a library, Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool is about 20 minutes' drive from Cudgee. The closest beach to Cudgee is Shelly Beach in Warrnambool, about 23 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Warrnambool Skate Park in Warrnambool is about 21 minutes' drive from Cudgee. For a cinema, Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool is about 19 minutes' drive from Cudgee. For an indoor play centre, The Bowl in Warrnambool is about 19 minutes' drive from Cudgee. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Cudgee, about 18 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Cudgee, the nearest places for kids by road are Grieve Street Playground in Warrnambool (16 min), the playground at Brierly Park in Warrnambool (17 min), SCOPE Galleries in Warrnambool (18 min), The Bowl in Warrnambool (19 min), Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool (19 min) and Warrnambool Art Gallery in Warrnambool (19 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Cudgee.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Cudgee, Panmure has 1 playground. Allansford also borders Cudgee.
