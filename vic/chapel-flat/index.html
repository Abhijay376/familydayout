Chapel Flat is in the Ballarat area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Chapel Flat, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

The Goldfields Track passes through Chapel Flat.

Getting around

There's no train station in Chapel Flat. The closest to Chapel Flat is Creswick Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.7 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Chapel Flat, so most trips start by car. By car from Chapel Flat, Ballarat is about 27 minutes away, Daylesford about 38 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Chapel Flat is children in Creswick, about 15 minutes' drive. For a library, Creswick Library in Creswick is about 13 minutes' drive from Chapel Flat. For a skate park, Skate Park in Creswick is about 13 minutes' drive from Chapel Flat. The closest cinema to Chapel Flat is Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Chapel Flat is Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point, about 31 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek is about 26 minutes' drive from Chapel Flat. Ballarat is the closest bigger centre to Chapel Flat, about 27 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Chapel Flat, the nearest places for kids by road are Creswick Library in Creswick (13 min), Apex Park Playground in Creswick (14 min), Skate Park in Creswick (13 min), Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick (14 min), Temporary Library (Room 123) till Feb 2025 in Creswick (15 min) and children in Creswick (15 min).
