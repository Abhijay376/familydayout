Kunat is in the Swan Hill area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Kunat, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Kunat has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kunat, so most trips start by car. By car from Kunat, Swan Hill is about 32 minutes away and Echuca about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill is about 31 minutes' drive from Kunat. The closest library to Kunat is Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill, about 32 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Riverside Park in Swan Hill is about 31 minutes' drive from Kunat. The closest cinema to Kunat is Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill, about 31 minutes' drive. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Kunat, about 32 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Kunat are the playground at Gray Park in Lake Boga (18 min), Lake Boga Flying Boat Museum in Lake Boga (21 min), the playground at Guastalegname Park in Swan Hill (30 min), Swan Hill Regional Art Gallery in Swan Hill (31 min), the skate park at Riverside Park in Swan Hill (31 min) and Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Kunat.

Nearby suburbs

Lake Boga, next to Kunat, has 1 playground. Castle Donnington and Beauchamp also border Kunat.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Kunat, without traffic.
