Wentworth in the East Gippsland area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Livingstone Creek Natural Features and Scenic Reserve is the largest mapped park in Wentworth, at about 513 hectares.

Getting around

Wentworth has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 57 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wentworth, so most trips start by car. By car from Wentworth, Bairnsdale is about 2.5 hours away and Lakes Entrance about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Wentworth, the nearest places for kids by road are Great Alpine Gallery in Swifts Creek (74 min), Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek (75 min), Old Omeo Courthouse in Omeo (78 min), the playground at Omeo Historical Park & Museum in Omeo (78 min) and the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo (78 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wentworth.

Nearby suburbs

Swifts Creek, next to Wentworth, has 1 playground and a pool. Next door to Wentworth, Omeo has 2 playgrounds and a skate park. Dargo, next to Wentworth, has 1 playground. Brookville also borders Wentworth.
