Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Mount Glasgow, in the Central Goldfields area.

Landmarks

Mount Glasgow Scenic Reserve is the largest mapped park in Mount Glasgow, at about 8 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mount Glasgow. The closest to Mount Glasgow is Talbot Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mount Glasgow, so most trips start by car. By car from Mount Glasgow, Maryborough is about 33 minutes away, Ballarat about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot is about 17 minutes' drive from Mount Glasgow. The closest library to Mount Glasgow is Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough, about 33 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick is about 37 minutes' drive from Mount Glasgow. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 33 minutes from Mount Glasgow by car, with a library. From Mount Glasgow, the nearest places for kids by road are Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground in Talbot (17 min), Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (17 min), Lee Medlyn Bottle Museum in Clunes (19 min), The Warehouse in Clunes (20 min), Clunes Swimming Pool in Clunes (21 min) and the playground at Queens Park in Clunes (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mount Glasgow.
