Toolong is in the Moyne area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Toolong, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

The Port Fairy to Warrnambool Rail Trail passes through Toolong.

Getting around

Toolong has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Toolong, so most trips start by car. By car from Toolong, Warrnambool is about 32 minutes away and Hamilton about 66 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Toolong is Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool, about 35 minutes' drive. For a library, Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool is about 33 minutes' drive from Toolong. For a beach, Pea Soup Beach in Port Fairy is about 15 minutes' drive from Toolong. For a skate park, the skate park at Russell Clark Reserve in Port Fairy is about 13 minutes' drive from Toolong. The closest cinema to Toolong is Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy, about 13 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, The Bowl in Warrnambool is about 33 minutes' drive from Toolong. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 32 minutes from Toolong by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Toolong are Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy (13 min), Limestone Gallery in Port Fairy (13 min), the skate park at Russell Clark Reserve in Port Fairy (13 min), Port Fairy Skate and Play space in Port Fairy (14 min), Pea Soup Beach in Port Fairy (15 min) and Southcombe Beach in Port Fairy (15 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Toolong on the Port Fairy to Warrnambool Rail Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Toolong, Port Fairy has 2 playgrounds, a beach and a cinema. Next door to Toolong, Kirkstall has 1 playground. Crossley also borders Toolong.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Toolong, without traffic.
