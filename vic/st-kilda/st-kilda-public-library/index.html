St Kilda Public Library

Library in St Kilda · Basic info

St Kilda Public Library is a library in St Kilda, in the Port Phillip City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. St Kilda Town Hall/Carlisle St #36 (tram) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 19 m away with a baby change table.
