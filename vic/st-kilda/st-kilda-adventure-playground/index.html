St Kilda Adventure Playground

Playground in St Kilda · Basic info

This is a public playground in St Kilda. The St Kilda to Port Melbourne passes about 310 m away, and Art Yarramunua is about 570 m away. There's a public toilet about 720 m away, and trams 16 and 96 stop at Canterbury Rd/Fitzroy St #133, a 9-minute walk away.
