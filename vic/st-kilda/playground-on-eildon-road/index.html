Playground on Eildon Road

Playground in St Kilda · Basic info

This is a public playground in St Kilda. The St Kilda to Port Melbourne passes about 300 m away, and St Kilda Beach is about 560 m away. There's a public toilet about 420 m away, and trams 16 and 96 stop at Acland St/Fitzroy St #135, a 4-minute walk away.
