Lower Esplanade Reserve

Park in St Kilda · Basic info

Lower Esplanade Reserve is a park in St Kilda, in the Port Phillip City Council area, good for all ages. Alfred Sq/The Esplanade (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 17 m away.
