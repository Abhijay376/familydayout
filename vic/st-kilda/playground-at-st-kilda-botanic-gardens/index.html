Playground at St Kilda Botanic Gardens

Playground in St Kilda · Basic info

Playground at St Kilda Botanic Gardens is a public playground in St Kilda, in the Port Phillip City area. The Windsor to St Kilda Marina is about 50 m away, and St Kilda Public Library is about 540 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 260 m from the playground, and bus 623 stops at Dickens St/Brighton Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
