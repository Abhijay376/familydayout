Fuji Tei, St Kilda

Cafe or restaurant in St Kilda · Basic info

Fuji Tei is a japanese cafe or restaurant in St Kilda, in the Port Phillip City Council area, a place to eat during a day out. St Kilda Rd/Fitzroy St #131 (tram) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 630 m away. Opening hours: opening_hours: Mo-Su 12:00-14:30, 18:00-22:30.
