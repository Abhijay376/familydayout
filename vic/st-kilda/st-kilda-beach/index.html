St Kilda Beach

Beach in St Kilda · Basic info

St Kilda Beach is a beach in St Kilda, in the Port Phillip City Council area, good for all ages. Luna Park/The Esplanade #138 (tram) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 68 m away with a baby change table.

Where is St Kilda Beach?
St Kilda Beach is in St Kilda, Victoria.
