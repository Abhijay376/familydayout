Playground at Waterloo Reserve

Playground in St Kilda · Basic info

Playground at Waterloo Reserve in St Kilda is a public playground in the Port Phillip City area. The St Kilda Road is about 320 m away, and for food, Dear Liza is about 110 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 820 m from the playground, and buses 246, 600, 922 and 923 stop at Waterloo Cres/Barkly St, a 1-minute walk away.
