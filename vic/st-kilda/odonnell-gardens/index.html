O'Donnell Gardens

Park in St Kilda · Basic info

O'Donnell Gardens is a park in St Kilda, in the Port Phillip City Council area, good for all ages. Luna Park/The Esplanade (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 118 m away.
