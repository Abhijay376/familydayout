Playground at Renfrey Reserve

Playground in St Kilda · Basic info

This is a public playground in St Kilda. The Elwood to St Kilda is about 120 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 280 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 130 m from the playground, and bus 246 stops at Wordsworth St/Barkly St, a 3-minute walk away.
