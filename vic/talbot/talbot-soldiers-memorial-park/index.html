Talbot Soldiers Memorial Park

Park in Talbot · Basic info

Talbot Soldiers Memorial Park is a park in Talbot, in the Central Goldfields Shire Council area, good for all ages. Talbot Town Hall/Ballarat St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 94 m away.
