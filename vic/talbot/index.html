Talbot in the Central Goldfields area has 1 playground and 9 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, Talbot has a pool.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Talbot is Caralulup Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 1,379 hectares. Tunnel Hill Bushland Reserve and Amherst Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Talbot also has Talbot Bushland Reserve, Mullins Bushland Reserve and McDermid Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds in Talbot include Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Talbot. In Talbot you'll also find Talbot Outdoor Pool. The Talbot Heritage Trail passes through Talbot.

Getting around

Trains stop at Talbot Railway Station (V/Line: Maryborough - Melbourne via Ballarat) in Talbot. Buses on the Ballarat - Maryborough route stop in Talbot. V/Line coaches stop in Talbot. By car from Talbot, Maryborough is about 16 minutes away, Ballarat about 45 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Talbot is Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Talbot is Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick, about 32 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 16 minutes from Talbot by car, with a library. From Talbot, the nearest places for kids by road are Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (17 min), Lee Medlyn Bottle Museum in Clunes (15 min), The Warehouse in Clunes (16 min), Clunes Swimming Pool in Clunes (16 min), the playground at Queens Park in Clunes (16 min) and Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick (32 min).

Best for

Free days on the Talbot Heritage Trail and in Talbot's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Daisy Hill, Caralulup and Amherst also border Talbot.
