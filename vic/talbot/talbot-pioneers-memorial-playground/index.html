Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground

Playground in Talbot · Basic info

Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground in Talbot is a public playground in the Central Goldfields Shire area. The Talbot Heritage Trail passes about 100 m away, and Talbot Outdoor Pool is about 50 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus Ballarat - Maryborough stops at Talbot Town Hall/Ballarat St, a 3-minute walk away.
