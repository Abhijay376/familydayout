Getting around

There's no train station in McLoughlins Beach. The closest to McLoughlins Beach is Rosedale Railway Station (V/Line), about 51 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside McLoughlins Beach, so most trips start by car. By car from McLoughlins Beach, Sale is about 56 minutes away and Traralgon about 79 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to McLoughlins Beach is Yarram Public Pool in Yarram, about 30 minutes' drive. For a library, Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram is about 29 minutes' drive from McLoughlins Beach. The closest beach to McLoughlins Beach is Woodside_Beach in Woodside Beach, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to McLoughlins Beach is the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram, about 30 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Sale, about 56 minutes from McLoughlins Beach by car, with 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From McLoughlins Beach, the nearest places for kids by road are Woodside_Beach in Woodside Beach (21 min), Woodside Beach Road Playground in Woodside Beach (20 min), Woolami in Woodside Beach (23 min), Yarram Historical Society Museum in Yarram (28 min), Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram (29 min) and the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram (30 min).
