Merbein is in the Mildura area, and our data lists 1 playground and 7 parks and reserves in the suburb. On top of that, Merbein has a library, a pool and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Merbein is River Murray Reserve, covering about 14,662 hectares. Kenny Park and Merbein Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Merbein include Chaffey Park, Bildstien Park and Apex Civic Park. Playgrounds in Merbein include the playground at Chaffey Park. Our data shows 7 public toilets in Merbein. In Merbein you'll also find Merbein Pool and Merbein Library. For older kids in Merbein there's Merbein Skate Park. The Merbein Common Archaeological Walk, Merbein Common Walk and Catfish Billabong Walk all pass through Merbein. Our data lists 3 cafes and family restaurants in Merbein for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Merbein. The closest to Merbein is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 189 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the 250/300 and 211/311/312 routes stop in Merbein. By car from Merbein, Mildura is about 13 minutes away and Swan Hill about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest beach to Merbein is Apex Beach in Mildura, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Merbein is Deakin Cinema Complex in Mildura, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Merbein is Laserpunk Laserforce in Mildura, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Merbein is Putt Putt Family Fun Centre in Mildura, about 13 minutes' drive. Mildura is the closest bigger centre to Merbein, about 13 minutes away by car, and has 26 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Merbein, the nearest places for kids by road are BMX Track in Mildura (9 min), Mildura Park for Play in Mildura (10 min), Mildura Library in Mildura (12 min), Waves Swimming Pool in Mildura (12 min), Deakin Cinema Complex in Mildura (13 min) and Art Vault Gallery in Mildura (12 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the library in Merbein. Teens who skate or ride, with Merbein Skate Park in Merbein. Free days on the Merbein Common Archaeological Walk and in Merbein's parks.
