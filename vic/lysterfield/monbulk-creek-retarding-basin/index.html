Monbulk Creek Retarding Basin

Park in Lysterfield · Basic info

Monbulk Creek Retarding Basin is a park in Lysterfield, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Morris Rd/Glenfern Rd (bus) is a 22-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1887 m away.
