Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Cochranes Creek, in the Loddon area.

Landmarks

Moliagul Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Cochranes Creek, at about 562 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cochranes Creek. The closest to Cochranes Creek is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 41 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cochranes Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Cochranes Creek, Maryborough is about 48 minutes away, Bendigo about 59 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cochranes Creek is Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly, about 23 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 48 minutes from Cochranes Creek by car, with a library. From Cochranes Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (23 min), Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (23 min), Bi-Centennial Park Playground in St Arnaud (27 min), the playground at Queen Mary Botanic Gardens in St Arnaud (28 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (28 min) and Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Cochranes Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Bealiba, next to Cochranes Creek, has 1 playground. Logan, Emu and Burkes Flat also border Cochranes Creek.
