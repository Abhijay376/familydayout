Trida is in the South Gippsland area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Trida, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Trida. The closest to Trida is Yarragon Railway Station (V/Line), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Trida, so most trips start by car. By car from Trida, Leongatha is about 34 minutes away, Warragul about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Trida is Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest library to Trida is Warragul Library in Warragul, about 36 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Warragul Skate Park in Warragul is about 34 minutes' drive from Trida. The closest cinema to Trida is Warragul Cinema in Warragul, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Trida is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 35 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Leongatha, about 34 minutes from Trida by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Trida, the nearest places for kids by road are Yarragon Childrens Playground Reserve in Yarragon (30 min), Station Gallery in Yarragon (29 min), the playground at Mossvale Park in Berrys Creek (34 min), Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul (34 min), Warragul Skate Park in Warragul (34 min) and Warragul Leisure Centre in Warragul (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Trida.

Nearby suburbs

Hallston, Ferndale, Allambee Reserve and Allambee also border Trida.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Trida, without traffic.
