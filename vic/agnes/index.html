Agnes is in the South Gippsland area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Agnes, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

The Great Southern Rail Trail passes through Agnes.

Getting around

Agnes has no train station; Morwell Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 48 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Agnes, so most trips start by car. By car from Agnes, Leongatha is about 46 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 66 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Agnes is Toora Pool in Toora, about 7 minutes' drive. For a library, Foster Library in Foster is about 16 minutes' drive from Agnes. The closest beach to Agnes is Chinaman Beach in Wilsons Promontory, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Agnes is the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora, about 8 minutes' drive. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Agnes, about 46 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Agnes are Chinaman Beach in Wilsons Promontory (6 min), Franklin Bank in Welshpool (6 min), Toora Pool in Toora (7 min), the playground at Stanley Street Playground in Toora (7 min), the playground at Sargasser Park in Toora (8 min) and the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora (8 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Agnes on the Great Southern Rail Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Agnes, Toora has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. Next door to Agnes, Welshpool has 1 playground and a beach.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Agnes, without traffic.
