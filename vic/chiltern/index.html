Chiltern is in the Indigo area, and our data lists 2 playgrounds and 8 parks and reserves in the suburb. On top of that, Chiltern has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

Black Dog Creek Natural Features Reserve is the largest mapped park in Chiltern, at about 121 hectares. White Box Ridge Reference Area and Chiltern I87 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Chiltern include Memorial Park, Chiltern I85 Bushland Reserve and Chiltern I88 Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds around Chiltern include Martin Park Playground. Our data shows 7 public toilets in Chiltern. In Chiltern you'll also find Chiltern Athenaeum Museum. For walks and rides, Wangaratta - Eldorado Loop, Bowser-Wagunya Preferred Route and Rutherglen - Chiltern cross Chiltern.

Getting around

Chiltern has a train station: Chiltern Railway Station (V/Line: Albury - Melbourne via Seymour). Buses on the Albury - Corowa via Rutherglen and Wangaratta - Chiltern via Rutherglen routes stop in Chiltern. By car from Chiltern, Wodonga is about 26 minutes away and Wangaratta about 33 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Chiltern is Rutherglen War Memorial Swimming Pool in Rutherglen, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest library to Chiltern is Rutherglen Public Library in Rutherglen, about 21 minutes' drive. For a beach, Sandy Beach in Beechworth is about 23 minutes' drive from Chiltern. The closest skate park to Chiltern is the bmx track at Lowe Square in Gooramadda, about 20 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana is about 29 minutes' drive from Chiltern. Wodonga is the closest bigger centre to Chiltern, about 26 minutes away by car, and has 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Chiltern are Rutherglen Public Library in Rutherglen (21 min), Apex Park Playground in Rutherglen (21 min), Rutherglen War Memorial Swimming Pool in Rutherglen (21 min), Howlong Library and Resource Centre in Gooramadda (19 min), Howlong Swimming Pool in Gooramadda (20 min) and the bmx track at Lowe Square in Gooramadda (20 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Chiltern. Free days on the Wangaratta - Eldorado Loop and in Chiltern's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Beechworth, next to Chiltern, has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. Springhurst, next to Chiltern, has 1 playground. Indigo Valley, Cornishtown, Chiltern Valley and Byawatha also border Chiltern.
