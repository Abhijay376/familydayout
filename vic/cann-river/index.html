Cann River is in the East Gippsland area, and our data lists 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in the suburb. On top of that, Cann River has a skate park.

Landmarks

Cann River Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Cann River, at about 11 hectares. Mick Baum Park is the next largest. Cann River also has Cann River State Forest. Playgrounds in Cann River include the playground at Mick Baum Park. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Cann River. For older kids in Cann River there's the skate park at Mick Baum Park. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Cann River for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Cann River has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 137 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Cann River. By car from Cann River, Lakes Entrance is about 2 hours away and Bairnsdale about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest places for kids by road from Cann River are West Beach in Tamboon (50 min).

Best for

Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at Mick Baum Park in Cann River.
