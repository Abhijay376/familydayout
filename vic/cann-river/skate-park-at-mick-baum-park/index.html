Skate park at Mick Baum Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Cann River · Basic info

Skate park at Mick Baum Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Cann River, in the East Gippsland Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Cann River Police Station/Ward St (coach) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 630 m away with a baby change table.
