Playground at Mick Baum Park

Playground in Cann River · Basic info

Playground at Mick Baum Park in Cann River is a public playground in the East Gippsland Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 20 m away, and for food, Pie Face is about 250 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and coaches stop at Cann River Police Station/Ward St, a 1-minute walk away.
