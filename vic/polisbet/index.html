Polisbet is in the Swan Hill area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Polisbet is Waitchie Tank Bushland Reserve, covering about 44 hectares. Polisbet Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Polisbet has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 41 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Polisbet, so most trips start by car. By car from Polisbet, Swan Hill is about 39 minutes away and Mildura about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Polisbet is Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest library to Polisbet is Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Polisbet is Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South, about 31 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill is about 39 minutes' drive from Polisbet. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Polisbet, about 39 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Polisbet, the nearest places for kids by road are Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South (31 min), Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang (32 min), the playground at Nyah Heritage Park in Nyah (36 min), the playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV) in Nyah (37 min), Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill (38 min) and the skate park at James Belsar Reserve in Swan Hill (39 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Polisbet.
