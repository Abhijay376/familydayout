Dingwall is in the Gannawarra area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Dingwall is Lake Murphy Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 222 hectares. Great Spectacle Lake Complex Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Dingwall. The closest to Dingwall is Kerang Railway Station (V/Line), about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dingwall, so most trips start by car. By car from Dingwall, Swan Hill is about 55 minutes away and Echuca about 87 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Dingwall is Swimming Pool in Kerang, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest library to Dingwall is Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang, about 10 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Barham Skate Park in Koondrook is about 33 minutes' drive from Dingwall. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 55 minutes from Dingwall by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Dingwall, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Lake Meran W.R in Lake Meran (7 min), Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (10 min), Swimming Pool in Kerang (10 min), the playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV) in Koondrook (32 min), Barham Branch Library in Koondrook (32 min) and Boort Court House Historical Museum in Boort (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Dingwall.

Nearby suburbs

Kerang, next to Dingwall, has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Lake Meran, next to Dingwall, has 1 playground. Normanville, Meering West and Appin also border Dingwall.
