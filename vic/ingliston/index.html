Ingliston in the Moorabool area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Gorong I106 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Ingliston, at about 7 hectares. For walks and rides, Werribee Gorge Circuit Walk, Ingliston Gorge Walk and Centenary Track cross Ingliston.

Getting around

There's no train station in Ingliston. The closest to Ingliston is Ballan Railway Station (V/Line), about 7.4 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Ingliston, so most trips start by car. By car from Ingliston, Bacchus Marsh is about 24 minutes away, Melton about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 72 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Ingliston is Ballan Swimming Pool in Ballan, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest library to Ingliston is Lerderderg Library in Bacchus Marsh, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Ingliston is Needles Beach in Pentland Hills, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Ingliston is the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Ingliston is Reading Cinemas in Melton West, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Ingliston is Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek, about 40 minutes' drive. Bacchus Marsh is the closest bigger centre to Ingliston, about 24 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Ingliston are Needles Beach in Pentland Hills (12 min), the playground at Caledonian Park Recreation Reserve in Ballan (19 min), the playground at Ballan Recreation Reserve in Ballan (20 min), the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan (20 min), Ballan Swimming Pool in Ballan (20 min) and the bmx track at Bacchus Marsh Racecourse and Recreation Reserve in Maddingley (20 min).

Best for

Free days on the Werribee Gorge Circuit Walk and in Ingliston's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Ingliston, Ballan has 4 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Ingliston, Maddingley has 8 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Ingliston, Pentland Hills has a beach. Next door to Ingliston, Myrniong has 3 playgrounds. Rowsley, Glenmore and Fiskville also border Ingliston.
