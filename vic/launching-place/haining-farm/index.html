Haining Farm

Park in Launching Place · Official details

Haining Farm is a park in Launching Place, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Launching Place General Store/Warburton Hwy (bus) is a 17-minute walk away and dogs are not allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Haining Farm?
Haining Farm is in Launching Place, Victoria, at Corner of Don and Dalry Roads.
Is Haining Farm free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Haining Farm?
No, dogs are not allowed.
How do I get to Haining Farm by public transport?
The closest stop is Launching Place General Store/Warburton Hwy (routes 683), a 17-minute walk.
