Landmarks

Our data shows 2 public toilets in St Helens Plains.

Getting around

St Helens Plains has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 74 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside St Helens Plains, so most trips start by car. By car from St Helens Plains, Horsham is about 26 minutes away and Stawell about 46 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to St Helens Plains is Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham, about 25 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Velodrome in Horsham is about 24 minutes' drive from St Helens Plains. For a cinema, Centre Cinemas in Horsham is about 25 minutes' drive from St Helens Plains. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to St Helens Plains, about 26 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from St Helens Plains are the playground at Lubeck Recreation Reserve in Lubeck (14 min), the playground at Uebergang Park in Horsham (24 min), Velodrome in Horsham (24 min), Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham (25 min), Centre Cinemas in Horsham (25 min) and Horsham Skate Park in Horsham (26 min).

Nearby suburbs

Next door to St Helens Plains, Lubeck has 1 playground. Longerenong, next to St Helens Plains, has 1 playground. Drung and Dadswells Bridge also border St Helens Plains.
