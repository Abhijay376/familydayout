Myall (Buloke) in the Buloke area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Wortongie Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Myall (Buloke), at about 14 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Myall (Buloke). The closest to Myall (Buloke) is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 76 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Myall (Buloke), so most trips start by car. By car from Myall (Buloke), Swan Hill is about 71 minutes away and Horsham about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake is about 15 minutes' drive from Myall (Buloke). From Myall (Buloke), the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Apex Park in Sea Lake (15 min), Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake (15 min), the playground at Lascelles Caravan Park in Lascelles (25 min) and Woomelang Recreation Reserve Swimming Pool in Woomelang (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Myall (Buloke).

Nearby suburbs
