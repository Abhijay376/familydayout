Rokewood Junction is in the Golden Plains area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Commeralghip Flora Reserve is the largest mapped park in Rokewood Junction, at about 3 hectares.

Getting around

Rokewood Junction has no train station; Wendouree Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 36 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Rokewood Junction, so most trips start by car. By car from Rokewood Junction, Ballarat is about 37 minutes away, Colac about 63 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

The nearest larger centre is Ballarat, about 37 minutes from Rokewood Junction by car, with 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema.
