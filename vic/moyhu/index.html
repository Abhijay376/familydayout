Moyhu in the Wangaratta area has 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Moyhu is Apex Park, covering about 1 hectares. Playgrounds in Moyhu include the playground at Apex Park. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Moyhu. For walks and rides, Vines 2 Vines: Whitfield to Milawa crosses Moyhu.

Getting around

There's no train station in Moyhu. The closest to Moyhu is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 25 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the King Valley-Wangaratta route stop in Moyhu. By car from Moyhu, Wangaratta is about 24 minutes away and Benalla about 43 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Moyhu is Splashpark in Wangaratta, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest library to Moyhu is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 23 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta is about 21 minutes' drive from Moyhu. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 23 minutes' drive from Moyhu. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Moyhu, about 24 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Moyhu are the playground at John McAleese Community Park in Milawa (18 min), Splashpark in Wangaratta (20 min), the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta (21 min), Moore than Swimming in Wangaratta (22 min), Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (23 min) and Cobb & Co in Glenrowan (22 min).

Best for

Free days on the Vines 2 Vines: Whitfield to Milawa and in Moyhu's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Myrrhee, next to Moyhu, has 1 playground. Edi and Docker also border Moyhu.
