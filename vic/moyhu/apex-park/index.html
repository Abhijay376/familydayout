Apex Park

Park in Moyhu · Basic info

Apex Park is a park in Moyhu, in the Wangaratta Rural City Council area, good for all ages. Moyhu/Wangaratta - Whitfield Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 65 m away.
