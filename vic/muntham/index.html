Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Muntham in the Southern Grampians area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Muntham has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 121 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Muntham, so most trips start by car. By car from Muntham, Hamilton is about 40 minutes away and Portland about 86 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Casterton Pool in Casterton is about 15 minutes' drive from Muntham. For a library, Hamilton Library in Hamilton is about 40 minutes' drive from Muntham. The closest skate park to Muntham is coleraine skate park in Coleraine, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Muntham is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 40 minutes' drive. Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Muntham, about 40 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Muntham, the nearest places for kids by road are coleraine skate park in Coleraine (12 min), the playground at Queens Park in Coleraine (12 min), Silvester Oval Playground in Coleraine (12 min), Gorman's art gallery in Casterton (14 min), Casterton Pool in Casterton (15 min) and Merino Swimming Pool in Merino (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Muntham.

Nearby suburbs

Casterton, next to Muntham, has a pool. Coleraine, next to Muntham, has 4 playgrounds and a skate park. Clover Flat and Carapook also border Muntham.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Muntham, without traffic.
