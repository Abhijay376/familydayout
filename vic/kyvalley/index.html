Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Kyvalley in the Campaspe area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Kyvalley has no train station; Echuca Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kyvalley, so most trips start by car. By car from Kyvalley, Echuca is about 31 minutes away, Shepparton about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Community Swimming Pool in Merrigum is about 26 minutes' drive from Kyvalley. The closest library to Kyvalley is Kyabram Library And Community Hub in Kyabram, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Kyvalley is the bmx track on Racecourse Road in Kyabram, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Kyvalley is Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca, about 33 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram is about 12 minutes' drive from Kyvalley. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 31 minutes from Kyvalley by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Kyvalley, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at McCormick Reserve in Kyabram (10 min), the playground at Drum Street Reserve in Kyabram (10 min), Kyabram Library And Community Hub in Kyabram (11 min), the bmx track on Racecourse Road in Kyabram (12 min), Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram (12 min) and Community Swimming Pool in Merrigum (26 min).
