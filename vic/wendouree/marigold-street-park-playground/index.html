Marigold Street Park Playground

Playground in Wendouree · Basic info

Marigold Street Park Playground in Wendouree is a public playground in the Ballarat City area. Glory Ocean is about 200 m away if you need food, and Playground at Weeramar Park is another option about 220 m away. There's a public toilet about 400 m away, and buses 11 and 12 stop at Aster St/Gillies St N, a 3-minute walk away.
