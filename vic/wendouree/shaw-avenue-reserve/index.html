Shaw Avenue Reserve

Park in Wendouree · Basic info

Shaw Avenue Reserve is a park in Wendouree, in the Ballarat City Council area, good for all ages. Forest St/Norman St (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 670 m away with a baby change table.
