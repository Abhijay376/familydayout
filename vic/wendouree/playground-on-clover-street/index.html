Playground at Clover Street Reserve

Playground in Wendouree · Basic info

Playground at Clover Street Reserve is a public playground in Wendouree, in the Ballarat City area. Glory Ocean is about 410 m away if you need food, and Playground at McNulty Drive Reserve is another option about 570 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.0 km away, and buses 11 and 31 stop at Caravan St/Learmonth Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
