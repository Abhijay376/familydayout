Wendouree BMX Track

Skate, BMX or pump track in Wendouree · Basic info

Wendouree BMX Track is a skate, bmx or pump track in Wendouree, in the Ballarat City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Primrose St/Grevillea Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1139 m away.
