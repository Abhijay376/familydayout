Playground on Hastings Street

Playground in Wendouree · Official details

This is a public playground in Wendouree. For food, Subway is about 340 m away, and Playground at Weeramar Park is another option about 120 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 250 m from the playground, and buses 11 and 12 stop at Weeramar Park/Gillies St N, a 2-minute walk away.
