Playground at Apex Park

Playground in Altona · Official details

Playground at Apex Park is a public playground in Altona, in the Hobsons Bay City area. The Hobsons Bay Coastal Trail passes about 30 m away, and Somers Road Playground is another option about 570 m away. There's a public toilet about 70 m away, and buses 411, 412 and 415 stop at Apex Park/Queen St, a 3-minute walk away.
