Altona Library

Library in Altona · Basic info

Altona Library is a library in Altona, in the Hobsons Bay City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Pier St/Blyth St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 94 m away. Opening hours: opening_hours: Mo-Th 09:30-20:00; Fr 09:30-18:00; Sa 10:00-16:00.
