PA Burns Reserve

Park in Altona · Basic info

PA Burns Reserve is a park in Altona, in the Hobsons Bay City Council area, good for all ages. Sussex St/Millers Rd (bus) is a 15-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 139 m away.
