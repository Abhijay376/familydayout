Weaver Reserve

Park in Altona · Official details

Weaver Reserve is a park in Altona, in the Hobsons Bay City Council area, good for all ages. Pier St/Blyth St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 151 m away.

Good to know

Questions parents ask

Where is Weaver Reserve?
Weaver Reserve is in Altona, Victoria.
Is Weaver Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Weaver Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 151 m away.
How do I get to Weaver Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Pier St/Blyth St (routes 411, 412), a 4-minute walk.
