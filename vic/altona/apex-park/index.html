Apex Park

Park in Altona · Official details

Apex Park is a park in Altona, in the Hobsons Bay City Council area, good for all ages. Apex Park/Queen St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 134 m away.

Questions parents ask

Where is Apex Park?
Apex Park is in Altona, Victoria.
