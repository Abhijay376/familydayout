Playground at Ransom, G.H. Reserve

Playground in Altona · Official details

This is a public playground in Altona. The Hobsons Bay Coastal Trail is about 30 m away, and Playground at Apex Park is another option about 500 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 90 m from the playground, and buses 411, 412 and 415 stop at Queen St/Maidstone St, a 5-minute walk away.
