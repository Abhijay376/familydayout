Playground at Grant Reserve

Playground in Altona · Official details

Playground at Grant Reserve in Altona is a public playground in the Hobsons Bay City area. The Kororoit Creek Trail passes about 40 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 40 m away. There's a public toilet about 150 m away, and buses 411, 412, 415 and 903 stop at Cherry Lake Reserve/Millers Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
