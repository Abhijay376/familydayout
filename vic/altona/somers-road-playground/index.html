Somers Road Playground

Playground in Altona · Basic info

Somers Road Playground in Altona is a public playground in the Hobsons Bay City area. For food, Dragon Temple Restaurant is about 60 m away, and Playground at Apex Park is another option about 570 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 690 m from the playground, and bus 415 stops at Maidstone St/Blyth St, a 1-minute walk away.
