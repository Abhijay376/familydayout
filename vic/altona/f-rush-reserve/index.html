F. Rush Reserve

Park in Altona · Basic info

F. Rush Reserve is a park in Altona, in the Hobsons Bay City Council area, good for all ages. Ford Rd/Grieve Pde (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 4001 m away.
