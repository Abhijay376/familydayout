Logan Reserve

Park in Altona · Official details

Logan Reserve is a park in Altona, in the Hobsons Bay City Council area, good for all ages. Pier St/Blyth St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 129 m away.

Details

Good to know
Public BBQ about 60 m away (Council)

Questions parents ask

Where is Logan Reserve?
Logan Reserve is in Altona, Victoria.
Is Logan Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Logan Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 129 m away.
How do I get to Logan Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Pier St/Blyth St (routes 411, 412), a 3-minute walk.
