Goornong is in the Greater Bendigo area, and our data lists 5 parks and reserves in the suburb. On top of that, Goornong has a pool.

Landmarks

English Bridge Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Goornong, at about 37 hectares. Crabhole Creek Bushland Reserve and Goornong H102B Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Goornong include Goornong H102 Bushland Reserve and Goornong H102A Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Goornong. In Goornong you'll also find Goornong Swimming Pool.

Getting around

Goornong has a train station: Goornong Railway Station (V/Line: Echuca/Moama - Melbourne via Bendigo or Heathcote). Buses on the Goornong and Bendigo Station - Shepparton Station via Kyabram routes stop in Goornong. V/Line coaches stop in Goornong. By car from Goornong, Bendigo is about 29 minutes away, Echuca about 52 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Goornong is Bendigo Library in Bendigo, about 29 minutes' drive. For a skate park, McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk is about 28 minutes' drive from Goornong. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 30 minutes' drive from Goornong. The closest indoor play centre to Goornong is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 33 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 26 minutes' drive from Goornong. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 29 minutes from Goornong by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Goornong, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Pennington Reserve in Huntly (13 min), Campaspe Run Rural Discovery Centre in Elmore (14 min), the playground at Collins Park in Ascot (20 min), White Hills Pool in White Hills (23 min), Bendigo Joss House Temple in North Bendigo (26 min) and The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo (26 min).
