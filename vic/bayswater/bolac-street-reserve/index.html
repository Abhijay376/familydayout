Bolac Street Reserve

Park in Bayswater · Basic info

Bolac Street Reserve is a park in Bayswater, in the Knox City Council area, good for all ages. Sinclair Rd/Scoresby Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1107 m away.
