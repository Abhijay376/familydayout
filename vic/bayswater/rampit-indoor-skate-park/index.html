Rampit Indoor Skate Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Bayswater · Basic info

Rampit Indoor Skate Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Bayswater, in the Knox City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Neilson St/Scoresby Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 810 m away.
