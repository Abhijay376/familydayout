Tamar Apex Reserve

Park in Bayswater · Basic info

Tamar Apex Reserve is a park in Bayswater, in the Knox City Council area, good for all ages. Carramar Ct/Pindari Dr (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1349 m away with a baby change table.
