Mountain High Shopping Centre

Shopping centre in Bayswater · Basic info

Mountain High Shopping Centre is a shopping centre in Bayswater, in the Knox City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Bayswater SC/High St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 160 m away.
