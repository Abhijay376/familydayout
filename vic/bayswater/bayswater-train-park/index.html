Bayswater Train Park

Playground in Bayswater · Basic info

Bayswater Train Park is a public playground in Bayswater, in the Knox City area. The Ringwood - Belgrave Rail Trail is about 90 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 40 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 80 m from the playground, and buses 664, 755 and 967 stop at Marie Wallace Park/Mountain Hwy, a 2-minute walk away.
