Playground on Neal Street

Playground in Bayswater · Basic info

Playground on Neal Street in Bayswater is a public playground in the Knox City area. The Ringwood - Belgrave Rail Trail is about 10 m away, and Playground on Danielle Crescent is another option about 530 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 490 m from the playground, and bus 755 stops at Bayswater Sec Col/Orchard Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
