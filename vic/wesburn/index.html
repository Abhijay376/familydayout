Our data lists 4 parks and reserves in Wesburn, in the Yarra Ranges area.

Landmarks

Brittania Creek Cave Geological Reserve is the largest mapped park in Wesburn, at about 15 hectares. Wesburn Park and Britannia Creek G182 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Wesburn also has Britannia Creek G181 Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Wesburn. The Lilydale-Warburton Rail Trail, Warburton Rail Trail and O'Shannassy Aqueduct Trail all pass through Wesburn.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wesburn. The closest to Wesburn is Lilydale Railway Station (metro), about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 683 stops in Wesburn. By car from Wesburn, Healesville is about 24 minutes away, Lilydale about 32 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 73 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Wesburn is Warburton Water World in Warburton, about 9 minutes' drive. For a library, Street Library in Millgrove is about 2 minutes' drive from Wesburn. The closest skate park to Wesburn is the skate park at Yarra Junction Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Yarra Junction, about 6 minutes' drive. For a cinema, The Memo, Healesville in Healesville is about 24 minutes' drive from Wesburn. The closest indoor play centre to Wesburn is PlayPlace in Mooroolbark, about 38 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Lyre Bird enclosure in Badger Creek is about 20 minutes' drive from Wesburn. Healesville is the closest bigger centre to Wesburn, about 24 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema.

Best for

Free days on the Lilydale-Warburton Rail Trail and in Wesburn's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Healesville, next to Wesburn, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. Warburton, next to Wesburn, has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Yarra Junction, next to Wesburn, has 1 playground, a library and a skate park. Next door to Wesburn, Millgrove has a library. Three Bridges, Launching Place, Gladysdale and Don Valley also border Wesburn.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Wesburn, without traffic.
