Tamleugh in the Strathbogie area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tamleugh is McBurney Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 38 hectares. Tamleugh H73 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tamleugh. The closest to Tamleugh is Violet Town Railway Station (V/Line), about 9.0 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tamleugh, so most trips start by car. By car from Tamleugh, Benalla is about 34 minutes away, Shepparton about 43 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa is about 28 minutes' drive from Tamleugh. The closest library to Tamleugh is Euroa Library in Euroa, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Tamleugh is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 45 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Tamleugh is Euroa Skate Park in Euroa, about 27 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa is about 27 minutes' drive from Tamleugh. The closest indoor play centre to Tamleugh is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 43 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Emerald Bank Leisure Land in Kialla is about 37 minutes' drive from Tamleugh. The nearest larger centre is Benalla, about 34 minutes from Tamleugh by car, with 5 playgrounds and a pool. From Tamleugh, the nearest places for kids by road are Violet Town Lions Park Playground in Violet Town (10 min), Violet Town Recreation Reserve West Playground in Violet Town (11 min), Farmers Arms Hotel Museum in Euroa (27 min), Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa (27 min), Euroa Skate Park in Euroa (27 min) and Euroa Library in Euroa (28 min).

Best for
