Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Glenluce in the Mount Alexander area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Goldfields Track crosses Glenluce.

Getting around

Glenluce has no train station; Castlemaine Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glenluce, so most trips start by car. By car from Glenluce, Castlemaine is about 23 minutes away, Kyneton about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Chewton Swimming Pool in Chewton is about 21 minutes' drive from Glenluce. For a library, Castlemaine Library in Castlemaine is about 23 minutes' drive from Glenluce. For a skate park, Bike Jumps in Castlemaine is about 23 minutes' drive from Glenluce. Castlemaine is the closest bigger centre to Glenluce, about 23 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Glenluce, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Vaughan Springs in Vaughan (10 min), Giant Slide in Vaughan (10 min), Chewton Swimming Pool in Chewton (21 min), Bike Jumps in Castlemaine (23 min), BMX Track in Castlemaine (23 min) and Castlemaine CASPA in Castlemaine (24 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Glenluce on the Goldfields Track.

Nearby suburbs

Vaughan, next to Glenluce, has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Glenluce, Fryerstown has 1 playground. Glenlyon and Drummond North also border Glenluce.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Glenluce, without traffic.
