Watsonia Library

Library in Watsonia · Basic info

Watsonia Library is a library in Watsonia, in the Banyule City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Watsonia Railway Station (train) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 4 m away. Opening hours: opening_hours: Mo-Th 10:00-20:30; Fr,Sa 10:00-17:00; Su 13:00-17:00.
