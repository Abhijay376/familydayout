Playground on Ibbottson Street

Playground in Watsonia · Basic info

Playground on Ibbottson Street in Watsonia is a public playground in the Banyule City area. The Banyule LBN8 passes about 20 m away, and Watsonia Library is about 40 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and trains stop at Watsonia Railway Station, a 2-minute walk away.
