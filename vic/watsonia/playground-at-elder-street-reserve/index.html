Playground at Elder Street Reserve

Playground in Watsonia · Basic info

Playground at Elder Street Reserve is a public playground in Watsonia, in the Banyule City area. The Banyule LBN7 is about 40 m away, and for food, Village Fish & Chips is about 400 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 490 m from the playground, and bus 514 stops at Elder Street Reserve/63 Elder St, a 1-minute walk away.
