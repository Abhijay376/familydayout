Landmarks

Playgrounds around Maude include Maude Tennis Reserve Playground and Maude Recreation Reserve Playground. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Maude.

Getting around

Maude has no train station; Corio Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Maude, so most trips start by car. By car from Maude, Geelong is about 36 minutes away, Torquay about 51 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 80 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Maude is Goodfellow Aquatic Centre in Bell Post Hill, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest library to Maude is Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Maude is Disability beach in Steiglitz, about 15 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Bannockburn Skate Park in Bannockburn is about 17 minutes' drive from Maude. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Geelong is about 34 minutes' drive from Maude. The closest indoor play centre to Maude is General Public in Corio, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Maude is Fairy Park in Anakie, about 28 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Geelong, about 36 minutes from Maude by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Maude, the nearest places for kids by road are Disability beach in Steiglitz (15 min), Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn (16 min), Bannockburn Skate Park in Bannockburn (17 min), Meredith Skate Park in Meredith (17 min), Fairy Park in Anakie (28 min) and Goodfellow Aquatic Centre in Bell Post Hill (26 min).
