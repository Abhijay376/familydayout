Speed in the Yarriambiack area has 5 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Speed is Turriff Flora and Fauna Reserve, covering about 250 hectares. Speed Bushland Reserve and Dering I254 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Speed also has Speed Natural Features Reserve and Turriff East School Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Speed.

Getting around

Speed has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 102 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Mildura - Ouyen - Sea Lake and Mildura - Horsham via Hopetoun routes stop in Speed. V/Line coaches stop in Speed. By car from Speed, Swan Hill is about 1.5 hours away and Mildura about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Speed is Tempy Outdoor Swimming Pool in Tempy, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Speed is Roxy in Ouyen, about 30 minutes' drive. From Speed, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Tempy Recreation Reserve in Tempy (5 min), Tempy Outdoor Swimming Pool in Tempy (6 min), the playground at Patchewollock Rec Reserve in Patchewollock (18 min), Ouyen Swimming Pool in Ouyen (30 min) and Roxy in Ouyen (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Speed.

Nearby suburbs
