Our data lists 4 parks and reserves in Miepoll, in the Strathbogie area.

Landmarks

Seven Creeks Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Miepoll, at about 30 hectares. Gum Swamp, Miepol Wildlife Reserve (no hunting) and Karramomus Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Miepoll also has Miepoll South Natural Features Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Miepoll. The closest to Miepoll is Euroa Railway Station (V/Line), about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Shepparton - Euroa via Kialla route stop in Miepoll. By car from Miepoll, Shepparton is about 27 minutes away, Benalla about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Miepoll is Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest library to Miepoll is Euroa Library in Euroa, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Miepoll is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 29 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Euroa Skate Park in Euroa is about 17 minutes' drive from Miepoll. The closest cinema to Miepoll is Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Miepoll is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 27 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Emerald Bank Leisure Land in Kialla is about 21 minutes' drive from Miepoll. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 27 minutes from Miepoll by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Miepoll are Moglonemby Hall Playground in Moglonemby (13 min), the playground at Karramomus Memorial Hall in Karramomus (19 min), Euroa Library in Euroa (17 min), Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa (17 min), Euroa Skate Park in Euroa (17 min) and Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa (17 min).
