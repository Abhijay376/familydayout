Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Dutton Way in the Glenelg area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Dutton Way has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 75 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dutton Way, so most trips start by car. By car from Dutton Way, Portland is about 10 minutes away and Hamilton about 68 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Portland Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Portland is about 13 minutes' drive from Dutton Way. For a library, Heywood Library in Heywood is about 22 minutes' drive from Dutton Way. For a beach, Whalers Point in Portland is about 10 minutes' drive from Dutton Way. Portland is the closest bigger centre to Dutton Way, about 10 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. From Dutton Way, the nearest places for kids by road are Whalers Point in Portland (10 min), the playground at Mitchell Park in Portland (11 min), Powerhouse Motor Car Museum in Portland (12 min), the playground at Narrawong Recreation Reserve in Narrawong (10 min), Cable Tram Depot in Portland (13 min) and Portland Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Portland (13 min).
