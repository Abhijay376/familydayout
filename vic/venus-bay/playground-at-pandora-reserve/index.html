Playground at Pandora Reserve

Playground in Venus Bay · Basic info

This is a public playground in Venus Bay. The Venus Bay Tarwin Lower Bike Path is about 200 m away, and Playground at Jupiter Park is another option about 540 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 660 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 5 minutes from Venus Bay.
