Playground at Venus Bay Community Centre Reserve

Playground in Venus Bay · Basic info

Playground at Venus Bay Community Centre Reserve in Venus Bay is a public playground in the South Gippsland Shire area. The Venus Bay Tarwin Lower Bike Path is about 370 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 80 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 540 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 3 minutes from Venus Bay.
