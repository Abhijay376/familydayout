Venus Bay in the South Gippsland area has 6 playgrounds and 9 parks and reserves in our data. Venus Bay also has a skate park.

Landmarks

Anderson Inlet Coastal Reserve is the largest mapped park in Venus Bay, at about 152 hectares. Point Smythe Coastal Reserve and Van Cleef Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Venus Bay include Jupiter Park, Saturn Reserve and Pandora Reserve. Playgrounds around Venus Bay include Fishermans Road Playground, the playground at Jupiter Park and the playground at Pandora Reserve. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Venus Bay. For older kids in Venus Bay there's the skate park at Venus Bay Community Centre Reserve. The Venus Bay Tarwin Lower Bike Path, Point Smythe Walk and Anderson Inlet Walking Track all pass through Venus Bay. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Venus Bay for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Venus Bay has no train station; Warragul Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 59 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Venus Bay, so most trips start by car. By car from Venus Bay, Leongatha is about 37 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 38 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Venus Bay is South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest library to Venus Bay is Leongatha Library in Leongatha, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Venus Bay is Diggers Island South Beach in Tarwin Lower, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Venus Bay is Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha, about 37 minutes' drive.

Best for

Younger kids at Venus Bay's 6 local playgrounds. Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at Venus Bay Community Centre Reserve in Venus Bay. Free days on the Venus Bay Tarwin Lower Bike Path and in Venus Bay's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Inverloch, next to Venus Bay, has 4 playgrounds and a beach. Tarwin Lower, next to Venus Bay, has 1 playground and a beach.
