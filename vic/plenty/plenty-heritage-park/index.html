Plenty Heritage Park

Park in Plenty · Basic info

Plenty Heritage Park is a park in Plenty, in the Nillumbik Shire Council area, good for all ages. Howell Rd/Yan Yean Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 182 m away.
