Playground at Plenty War Memorial Park

Playground in Plenty · Basic info

Playground at Plenty War Memorial Park in Plenty is a public playground in the Nillumbik Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. You'll find a public toilet about 230 m from the playground, and buses 385 and 901 stop at Howell Rd/Yan Yean Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
