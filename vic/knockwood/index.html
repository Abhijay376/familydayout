Knockwood is in the Mansfield area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Upper Goulburn State Forest is the largest mapped park in Knockwood, at about 104,910 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Knockwood include Snakes Reserve and Knockwood Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Knockwood. For camping, there's Knockwood Reserve and Snakes Reserve.

Getting around

Knockwood has no train station; Moe Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 83 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Knockwood, so most trips start by car. By car from Knockwood, Mansfield is about 66 minutes away and Benalla about 2 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Knockwood.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Knockwood, Eildon has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Knockwood, Mount Buller has a cinema. Enochs Point also borders Knockwood.
