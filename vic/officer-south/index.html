Officer South is in the Cardinia area, and our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Officer South is Kaduna Park, covering about 1 hectares. Playgrounds around Officer South include the playground at Kaduna Park.

Getting around

Officer South has no train station; Cardinia Road Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 4.9 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 925 stops in Officer South. By car from Officer South, Pakenham is about 10 minutes away, Cranbourne about 20 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 47 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Officer South is Pakenham Outdoor Swimming Pool in Pakenham, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest library to Officer South is Pakenham Library in Pakenham, about 11 minutes' drive. For a beach, Seaford Beach in Seaford is about 36 minutes' drive from Officer South. The closest skate park to Officer South is P.B Ronald Skate Park in Pakenham, about 11 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Narre Warren is about 16 minutes' drive from Officer South. For an indoor play centre, Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham is about 11 minutes' drive from Officer South. For a zoo or wildlife park, MonkeyG's in Pakenham is about 10 minutes' drive from Officer South. The nearest larger centre is Pakenham, about 10 minutes from Officer South by car, with 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play. From Officer South, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Onyx Crescent Reserve in Officer (6 min), MonkeyG's in Pakenham (10 min), Historic Council Shire Offices in Pakenham (10 min), Pakenham Library in Pakenham (11 min), Pakenham Outdoor Swimming Pool in Pakenham (10 min) and P.B Ronald Skate Park in Pakenham (11 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Officer South.

Nearby suburbs

Pakenham, next to Officer South, has 46 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Officer South, Clyde North has 34 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Officer South, Officer has 14 playgrounds. Cardinia, next to Officer South, has 4 playgrounds. Pakenham South also borders Officer South.
