Our data lists 2 playgrounds and 5 parks and reserves in Tooradin, in the Casey area. Beyond the parks, Tooradin has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

Rutter Park Reserve is the largest mapped park in Tooradin, at about 15 hectares. Woodlot Lane Nature Reserve and Picnic Park are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Tooradin include Lighthouse Playground and Yannathan Nature Reserve. Playgrounds around Tooradin include the playground at Lighthouse Playground. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Tooradin. Places to visit in Tooradin include Fisherman's Cottage. Our data lists 5 cafes and family restaurants in Tooradin for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tooradin. The closest to Tooradin is Officer Railway Station (metro), about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 795 stops in Tooradin. V/Line coaches stop in Tooradin. By car from Tooradin, Cranbourne is about 15 minutes away, Pakenham about 21 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 55 minutes, without traffic.

The nearest larger centre is Cranbourne, about 15 minutes from Tooradin by car, with 27 playgrounds.
