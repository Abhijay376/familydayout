Evita Terrace Reserve Playspace

Playground in Westmeadows · Basic info

Evita Terrace Reserve Playspace is a public playground in Westmeadows, in the Hume City area. Playground on Hyton Close is another option about 330 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.7 km from the playground, and bus 477 stops at Hopetoun Ct/Mickleham Rd, a 10-minute walk away.
