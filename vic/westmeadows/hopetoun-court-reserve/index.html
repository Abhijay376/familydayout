Hopetoun Court Reserve

Park in Westmeadows · Basic info

Hopetoun Court Reserve is a park in Westmeadows, in the Hume City Council area, good for all ages. Hopetoun Ct/Mickleham Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 732 m away.
