Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Natte Yallock, in the Pyrenees area.

Landmarks

Moyreisk I70 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Natte Yallock, at about 7 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Natte Yallock.

Getting around

There's no train station in Natte Yallock. The closest to Natte Yallock is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Natte Yallock, so most trips start by car. By car from Natte Yallock, Maryborough is about 26 minutes away, Castlemaine about 70 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly is about 24 minutes' drive from Natte Yallock. The closest library to Natte Yallock is Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough, about 26 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 26 minutes from Natte Yallock by car, with a library. From Natte Yallock, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Avoca Lions Park in Avoca (21 min), Courthouse in Avoca (20 min), Stuart Mill Recreation Reserve Playground in Stuart Mill (22 min), Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (26 min), Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (23 min) and Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (24 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Natte Yallock.

Nearby suburbs
