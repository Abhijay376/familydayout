Romsey in the Macedon Ranges area has 2 playgrounds and 5 parks and reserves in our data. Romsey also has a library and a skate park.

Landmarks

Deep Creek K16 Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Romsey, at about 7 hectares. Deep Creek K17 Streamside Reserve and Bolinda Creek Streamside Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Romsey include Lions Park and Ecotherapy Park. Our data shows 6 public toilets in Romsey. Places to visit in Romsey include Romsey Library. For older kids in Romsey there's Romsey Skate Park. The Federation Trail passes through Romsey.

Getting around

Romsey has no train station; Riddells Creek Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Lancefield - Sunbury/Clarkefield via Romsey & Monegeeta and Lancefield - Gisborne via Romsey, Monegeeta & Riddells Creek routes stop in Romsey. V/Line coaches stop in Romsey. By car from Romsey, Kyneton is about 40 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 48 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 63 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Romsey is Gisborne Aquatic Centre in Gisborne, about 25 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Reading Cinema in Sunbury is about 31 minutes' drive from Romsey. For an indoor play centre, Timezone in Craigieburn is about 37 minutes' drive from Romsey. Kyneton is the closest bigger centre to Romsey, about 40 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds and a library. From Romsey, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Hanging Rock Reserve in Newham (25 min), Low Ropes Course in Riddells Creek (21 min), Gisborne Library in Gisborne (25 min), Old Gisborne Court House in Gisborne (25 min), Gisborne Aquatic Centre in Gisborne (25 min) and BMX Track in Wallan (27 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the library in Romsey. Teens who skate or ride, with Romsey Skate Park in Romsey. Free days on The Federation Trail and in Romsey's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Riddells Creek, next to Romsey, has 4 playgrounds. Lancefield, Kerrie and Hesket also border Romsey.
