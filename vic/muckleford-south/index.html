Muckleford South is in the Mount Alexander area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Muckleford South is Muckleford South Bushland Reserve, covering about 1 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Muckleford South. The closest to Muckleford South is Castlemaine Railway Station (V/Line), about 9.0 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Muckleford South, so most trips start by car. By car from Muckleford South, Castlemaine is about 10 minutes away, Maryborough about 35 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Newstead Swimming Pool in Newstead is about 6 minutes' drive from Muckleford South. For a library, Castlemaine Library in Castlemaine is about 9 minutes' drive from Muckleford South. The closest skate park to Muckleford South is Bike Jumps in Castlemaine, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Muckleford South is Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo, about 40 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 37 minutes' drive from Muckleford South. Castlemaine is the closest bigger centre to Muckleford South, about 10 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Muckleford South are Newstead Swimming Pool in Newstead (6 min), the playground at Newstead Memorial Park in Newstead (7 min), Lawrence Park Playground in Castlemaine (8 min), Bike Jumps in Castlemaine (9 min), BMX Track in Castlemaine (9 min) and Castlemaine CASPA in Castlemaine (10 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Muckleford South.
