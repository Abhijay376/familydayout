Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Dookie College, in the Greater Shepparton area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Dookie College is Oval, covering about 2 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Dookie College. The closest to Dookie College is Violet Town Railway Station (V/Line), about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dookie College, so most trips start by car. By car from Dookie College, Shepparton is about 31 minutes away, Benalla about 32 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Aquamoves in Shepparton is about 33 minutes' drive from Dookie College. For a library, Shepparton Library in Shepparton is about 33 minutes' drive from Dookie College. For a beach, Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton is about 35 minutes' drive from Dookie College. For a skate park, the skate park at Skate Park in Shepparton is about 32 minutes' drive from Dookie College. The closest cinema to Dookie College is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Dookie College is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Dookie College is Kidstown in Mooroopna, about 35 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 31 minutes from Dookie College by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Dookie College, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at CWA Gardens in Dookie (11 min), the playground at Dookie Recreation Reserve in Dookie (14 min), Village Cinemas in Shepparton (32 min), StarBowl in Shepparton (31 min), Shepparton Heritage Centre in Shepparton (32 min) and the skate park at Skate Park in Shepparton (32 min).

Best for
