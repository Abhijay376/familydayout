Lake Wongan is in the Pyrenees area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lake Wongan is Lake Wongan Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 246 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lake Wongan. The closest to Lake Wongan is Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line), about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lake Wongan, so most trips start by car. By car from Lake Wongan, Ararat is about 55 minutes away, Ballarat about 75 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Lake Bolac Library in Lake Bolac is about 26 minutes' drive from Lake Wongan. The nearest larger centre is Ararat, about 55 minutes from Lake Wongan by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Lake Wongan, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Simpson Park in Streatham (9 min), Lake Bolac Library in Lake Bolac (26 min), Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort (46 min), the playground at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (46 min), the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (47 min) and Willaura Outdoor Pool in Willaura (51 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lake Wongan.
