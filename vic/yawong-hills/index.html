Yawong Hills is in the Buloke area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Yawong Hills is Yowang Hill Geological Reserve, covering about 69 hectares. Coonooer East I157 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Yawong Hills. The closest to Yawong Hills is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 72 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yawong Hills, so most trips start by car. By car from Yawong Hills, Stawell is about 85 minutes away and Maryborough about 88 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud is about 23 minutes' drive from Yawong Hills. For a skate park, Donald BMX Track in Donald is about 44 minutes' drive from Yawong Hills. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 28 minutes' drive from Yawong Hills. The closest places for kids by road from Yawong Hills are Apex Park Playground in St Arnaud (23 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (23 min), Market Square Park Playground in St Arnaud (23 min), Rex Theatre in Charlton (28 min), Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (29 min) and Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (40 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Yawong Hills.
