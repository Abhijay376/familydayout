Wombelano is in the West Wimmera area, and our data lists 5 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Wombelano is Poyntons State Forest, covering about 195 hectares. Lake Mullancoree Wildlife Reserve (hunting) and Lake Dollanoke Lake Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Wombelano include Byrons Swamp Lake Reserve and Reillys Creek Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wombelano. The closest to Wombelano is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 122 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wombelano, so most trips start by car. By car from Wombelano, Horsham is about 62 minutes away and Hamilton about 89 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest skate park to Wombelano is the skate park at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk, about 40 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Wombelano are Harrow Discovery Centre in Harrow (21 min), Bake House Studio in Harrow (21 min), the playground at Lions Park in Edenhope (41 min), Henley Park Playground in Edenhope (42 min), Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral (47 min) and the skate park at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk (40 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wombelano.

Nearby suburbs

Edenhope, next to Wombelano, has 2 playgrounds. Charam also borders Wombelano.
