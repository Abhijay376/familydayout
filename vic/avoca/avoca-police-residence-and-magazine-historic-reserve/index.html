Avoca Police Residence and Magazine Historic Reserve

Park in Avoca · Basic info

Avoca Police Residence and Magazine Historic Reserve is a park in Avoca, in the Pyrenees Shire Council area, good for all ages. Avoca Hotel/Sunraysia Hwy (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 484 m away.
