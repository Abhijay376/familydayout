Tynong North in the Cardinia area has 1 playground in our data. Beyond the parks, Tynong North has 3 pools and 2 zoos or wildlife parks.

Landmarks

Playgrounds around Tynong North include Typhoon Island. In Tynong North you'll also find Gumbuya World, Wildlife Trail, Lazy River and Rock Pool. For walks and rides, Buttongrass Nature Walk crosses Tynong North.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tynong North. The closest to Tynong North is Tynong Railway Station (V/Line), about 3.8 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tynong North, so most trips start by car. By car from Tynong North, Pakenham is about 15 minutes away, Warragul about 32 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 59 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Tynong North is Pakenham Library in Pakenham, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Tynong North is the bmx track at Greenland Court Reserve in Garfield, about 11 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Narre Warren is about 28 minutes' drive from Tynong North. The closest indoor play centre to Tynong North is Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham, about 15 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Pakenham, about 15 minutes from Tynong North by car, with 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Tynong North are the playground at Maryknoll Reserve in Maryknoll (7 min), the bmx track at Greenland Court Reserve in Garfield (11 min), Skate BMX Park in Garfield (12 min), Pakenham Library in Pakenham (14 min), Historic Council Shire Offices in Pakenham (14 min) and Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham (15 min).

Best for

Free days on the Buttongrass Nature Walk and in Tynong North's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Garfield, next to Tynong North, has 4 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Gembrook, next to Tynong North, has 6 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Tynong North, Nar Nar Goon has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Tynong North, Maryknoll has 1 playground. Tynong, Tonimbuk, Nar Nar Goon North and Garfield North also border Tynong North.
