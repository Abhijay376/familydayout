Typhoon Island

Playground in Tynong North · Basic info

Typhoon Island in Tynong North is a public playground in the Cardinia Shire area. Lazy River is about 80 m away, and Gumbuya World is about 120 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 380 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 8 minutes from Tynong North.
