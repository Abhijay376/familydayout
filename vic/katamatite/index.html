Katamatite in the Moira area has 2 playgrounds and 4 parks and reserves in our data. Katamatite also has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

Youanmite H41 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Katamatite, at about 4 hectares. E.W. Kilgour Reserve and Lions Club of Katamatite Inc Memorial Park are the next largest. Katamatite also has C R Wood Park. Playgrounds around Katamatite include the playground at C R Wood Park and the playground at E.W Kilgour Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Katamatite. In Katamatite you'll also find Katamatite Historical Society Museum.

Getting around

Katamatite has no train station; Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 43 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Katamatite, so most trips start by car. By car from Katamatite, Shepparton is about 41 minutes away and Benalla about 51 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Katamatite is Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest library to Katamatite is Barooga Library in Cobram, about 20 minutes' drive. For a beach, Fosters Beach in Cobram is about 18 minutes' drive from Katamatite. For a skate park, the skate park at Federation Park in Cobram is about 17 minutes' drive from Katamatite. The closest cinema to Katamatite is Cobram Cinema in Cobram, about 17 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 41 minutes' drive from Katamatite. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Katamatite is Tunzafun in Yarrawonga, about 30 minutes' drive. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Katamatite, about 41 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Katamatite are Fosters Beach in Cobram (18 min), Backhouse Beach in Cobram East (17 min), Cobram Cinema in Cobram (17 min), the skate park at Federation Park in Cobram (17 min), Barooga Library in Cobram (20 min) and Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah (28 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Katamatite.

Nearby suburbs

Cobram East, next to Katamatite, has a beach. Muckatah, Katamatite East, Invergordon and Boosey also border Katamatite.
