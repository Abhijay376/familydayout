Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Terrick Terrick East, in the Campaspe area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Terrick Terrick East is Terrick Terrick National Park, covering about 6,388 hectares. Unnamed P0253 Private Nature Reserve and Mount Terrick Road Nature Conservation Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Terrick Terrick East. The closest to Terrick Terrick East is Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line), about 25 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Terrick Terrick East, so most trips start by car. By car from Terrick Terrick East, Echuca is about 34 minutes away and Bendigo about 73 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Terrick Terrick East is Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest library to Terrick Terrick East is Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca, about 33 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Terrick Terrick East is Moama Echuca Community Skate Park in Echuca, about 35 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca is about 33 minutes' drive from Terrick Terrick East. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 34 minutes from Terrick Terrick East by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Terrick Terrick East are Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo (24 min), the playground at R J Phelan Reserve in Mitiamo (24 min), East Loddon Historical Society in Mitiamo (24 min), Lockington Pool in Lockington (26 min), Holden Museum in Echuca (31 min) and the playground at Echuca Lions Park in Echuca (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Terrick Terrick East.

Nearby suburbs

Mitiamo, next to Terrick Terrick East, has 1 playground and a pool. Pine Grove, Patho, Kotta and Jungaburra also border Terrick Terrick East.
