Sunnyside Park

Park in Loch · Basic info

Sunnyside Park is a park in Loch, in the South Gippsland Shire Council area, good for all ages. Clarence St/Victoria Rd (coach) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 13 m away.

Where is Sunnyside Park?
Sunnyside Park is in Loch, Victoria.

How do I get to Sunnyside Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Clarence St/Victoria Rd (routes Yarram - Melbourne via Koo Wee Rup & Dandenong), a 3-minute walk.
