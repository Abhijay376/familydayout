Loch in the South Gippsland area has 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Loch also has Sunnyside Park. Playgrounds around Loch include the playground at Sunnyside Park. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Loch. The Great Southern Rail Trail and Gippsland Odyssey Extension - Woolamai to Nyora (Proposed) all pass through Loch.

Getting around

There's no train station in Loch. The closest to Loch is Longwarry Railway Station (V/Line), about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Loch. By car from Loch, Leongatha is about 25 minutes away, Warragul about 38 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 83 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Loch is Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong, about 7 minutes' drive. For a library, Poowong library in Poowong is about 7 minutes' drive from Loch. The closest skate park to Loch is Nyora skate park in Nyora, about 9 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha is about 24 minutes' drive from Loch. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 36 minutes' drive from Loch. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Loch is Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville, about 28 minutes' drive. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Loch, about 25 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Loch are Nyora skate park in Nyora (9 min), the playground at Nyora Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Nyora (9 min), Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong (7 min), Poowong library in Poowong (7 min), Korumburra Outdoor Pool in Korumburra (12 min) and Coal Creek Community Park & Museum in Korumburra (15 min).

Best for

Free days on the Great Southern Rail Trail and in Loch's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Loch, Poowong has a pool and a library. Korumburra, next to Loch, has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Loch, Lang Lang has 4 playgrounds. Next door to Loch, Nyora has 3 playgrounds and a skate park. Bena, Woodleigh, Krowera and Jeetho also border Loch.
