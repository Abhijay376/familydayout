Playground at Sunnyside Park

Playground in Loch · Basic info

Playground at Sunnyside Park in Loch is a public playground in the South Gippsland Shire area. The Great Southern Rail Trail passes about 120 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and coaches stop at Clarence St/Victoria Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
