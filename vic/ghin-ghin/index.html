Ghin Ghin is in the Murrindindi area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Homewood Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Ghin Ghin, at about 26 hectares. Ghin Ghin G115 Bushland Reserve and Ghin Ghin G114 Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Ghin Ghin. The closest to Ghin Ghin is Seymour Railway Station (V/Line), about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Ghin Ghin, so most trips start by car. By car from Ghin Ghin, Seymour is about 36 minutes away, Healesville about 66 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Ghin Ghin is Seymour Swimming Centre in Seymour, about 37 minutes' drive. The closest library to Ghin Ghin is Seymour Library in Seymour, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Ghin Ghin is the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea, about 14 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 36 minutes from Ghin Ghin by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Ghin Ghin are the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea (14 min), BMX Track in Caveat (41 min), TLM Moto Guzzi in Yea (34 min), Yarck Recreation Reserve Playground in Yarck (33 min), the playground at M O'Sullivan Reserve in Seymour (34 min) and Seymour Library in Seymour (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Ghin Ghin.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Ghin Ghin, Yea has a skate park. Kerrisdale, Homewood and Highlands also border Ghin Ghin.
