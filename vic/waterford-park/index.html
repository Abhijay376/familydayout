Waterford Park is in the Mitchell area, and our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Waterford Park is Waterford Park Estate Reserve, covering about 2 hectares. Playgrounds in Waterford Park include the playground at Waterford Park. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Waterford Park.

Getting around

There's no train station in Waterford Park. The closest to Waterford Park is Wandong Railway Station (V/Line), about 7.0 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Waterford Park, so most trips start by car. By car from Waterford Park, Seymour is about 30 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 45 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 63 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore is about 17 minutes' drive from Waterford Park. For a library, Broadford Library in Broadford is about 13 minutes' drive from Waterford Park. For a skate park, the skate park at Wandong and Heathcote Junction Memorial Park in Wandong is about 8 minutes' drive from Waterford Park. For a cinema, United Cinemas in Craigieburn is about 36 minutes' drive from Waterford Park. For an indoor play centre, Timezone in Craigieburn is about 36 minutes' drive from Waterford Park. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Waterford Park is Funfields in Whittlesea, about 30 minutes' drive. Seymour is the closest bigger centre to Waterford Park, about 30 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Waterford Park, the nearest places for kids by road are the skate park at Wandong and Heathcote Junction Memorial Park in Wandong (8 min), L B Davern Reserve Playground in Wandong (10 min), Broadford Library in Broadford (13 min), Broadford Skate Park in Broadford (12 min), Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore (17 min) and Wallan Library in Wallan (17 min).
