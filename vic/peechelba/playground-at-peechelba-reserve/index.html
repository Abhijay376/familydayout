Playground at Peechelba Reserve

Playground in Peechelba · Basic info

Playground at Peechelba Reserve in Peechelba is a public playground in the Wangaratta Rural City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus Wangaratta - Yarrawonga stops at Church St/Wangaratta - Yarrawonga Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
