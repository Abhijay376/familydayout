Our data lists 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in Peechelba, in the Moira area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Peechelba is Ovens River Heritage River, covering about 3,712 hectares. Peechelba H105 Bushland Reserve and Peechelba Reserve are the next largest. Playgrounds around Peechelba include the playground at Peechelba Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Peechelba.

Getting around

There's no train station in Peechelba. The closest to Peechelba is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Wangaratta - Yarrawonga route stop in Peechelba. By car from Peechelba, Wangaratta is about 18 minutes away and Benalla about 52 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta is about 17 minutes' drive from Peechelba. For a library, Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta is about 19 minutes' drive from Peechelba. For a beach, Forges Beach No 2 in Yarrawonga is about 38 minutes' drive from Peechelba. The closest skate park to Peechelba is the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta, about 23 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 18 minutes' drive from Peechelba. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Peechelba is Tunzafun in Yarrawonga, about 31 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 18 minutes from Peechelba by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Peechelba are Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta (17 min), Brien Crescent Playground in Wangaratta (18 min), Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (19 min), Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta (18 min), Moore than Swimming in Wangaratta (22 min) and the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta (23 min).
