Macarthur Court House Historic Reserve

Park in Macarthur · Basic info

Macarthur Court House Historic Reserve is a park in Macarthur, in the Moyne Shire Council area, good for all ages. Eversley St/Hamilton - Port Fairy Rd (coach) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 105 m away.
