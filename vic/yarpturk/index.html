Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Yarpturk in the Moyne area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Yarpturk. The closest to Yarpturk is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yarpturk, so most trips start by car. By car from Yarpturk, Warrnambool is about 16 minutes away and Hamilton about 75 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool is about 19 minutes' drive from Yarpturk. For a library, Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool is about 17 minutes' drive from Yarpturk. The closest beach to Yarpturk is Shelly Beach in Warrnambool, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Yarpturk is Koroit Youth Space in Koroit, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Yarpturk is Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool, about 16 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, The Bowl in Warrnambool is about 17 minutes' drive from Yarpturk. For a zoo or wildlife park, Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee is about 28 minutes' drive from Yarpturk. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 16 minutes from Yarpturk by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Yarpturk are Koroit Youth Space in Koroit (9 min), the playground at Bushfield Recreation Reserve in Bushfield (12 min), the playground at Witham Park in Warrnambool (12 min), Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool (16 min), Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool (17 min) and Warrnambool Art Gallery in Warrnambool (17 min).
