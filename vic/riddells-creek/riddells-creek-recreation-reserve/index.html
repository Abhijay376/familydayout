Riddells Creek Recreation Reserve

Park in Riddells Creek · Official details

Riddells Creek Recreation Reserve is a park in Riddells Creek, in the Macedon Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Riddells Creek Station/Hamilton St (bus) is a 10-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 292 m away and dogs are allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Riddells Creek Recreation Reserve?
Riddells Creek Recreation Reserve is in Riddells Creek, Victoria.
Is Riddells Creek Recreation Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Riddells Creek Recreation Reserve?
Yes, dogs are allowed.
Are there toilets at Riddells Creek Recreation Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 292 m away.
How do I get to Riddells Creek Recreation Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Riddells Creek Station/Hamilton St (routes Lancefield - Gisborne via Romsey, Monegeeta & Riddells Creek), a 10-minute walk.
