Playground at Koonung Creek Reserve

Playground in Balwyn North · Basic info

Playground at Koonung Creek Reserve in Balwyn North is a public playground in the Boroondara City area. The Koonung Creek Trail is about 30 m away, and Playground at Willow Bend Reserve is another option about 320 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 285 stops at Ventnor St/Wilburton Pde, a 5-minute walk away.
