Gordon Barnard Reserve

Park in Balwyn North · Basic info

Gordon Barnard Reserve is a park in Balwyn North, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Gordon Barnard Reserve/Balwyn Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 606 m away and dogs are allowed on a lead.
