Kyora Parade Reserve

Park in Balwyn North · Basic info

Kyora Parade Reserve is a park in Balwyn North, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. The Boulevard/Burke Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 825 m away with a baby change table.
