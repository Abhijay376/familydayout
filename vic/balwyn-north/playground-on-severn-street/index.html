Playground on Severn Street

Playground in Balwyn North · Basic info

Playground on Severn Street is a public playground in Balwyn North, in the Boroondara City area. For food, Saki is about 170 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 340 m from the playground, and tram 48 stops at North Balwyn Shopping Centre/Doncaster Rd #47, a 2-minute walk away.
