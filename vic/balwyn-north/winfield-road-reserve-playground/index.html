Winfield Road Reserve Playground

Playground in Balwyn North · Basic info

This is a public playground in Balwyn North. The Koonung Creek Trail is about 70 m away, and Playground on Ray Drive is another option about 520 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 990 m from the playground, and buses 207, 284 and 285 stop at Greythorn Rd/Doncaster Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
