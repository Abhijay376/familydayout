Playground at Gordon Barnard Reserve

Playground in Balwyn North · Basic info

Playground at Gordon Barnard Reserve is a public playground in Balwyn North, in the Boroondara City area. The Glass Creek Trail passes about 170 m away, and Diving pool is about 300 m away. There's a public toilet about 550 m away, and bus 285 stops at Albury Rd/Balwyn Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
