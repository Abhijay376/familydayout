Playground at Jacka Street Reserve

Playground in Balwyn North · Basic info

Playground at Jacka Street Reserve in Balwyn North is a public playground in the Boroondara City area. The Glass Creek Trail passes about 10 m away, and Boroondara Sports Complex is about 320 m away. There's a public toilet about 780 m away, and buses 302 and 304 stop at Balwyn Leisure Centre/Belmore Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
