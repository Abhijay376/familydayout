Musca Street Reserve

Park in Balwyn North · Basic info

Musca Street Reserve is a park in Balwyn North, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. The Boulevard/Burke Rd (bus) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1009 m away.
