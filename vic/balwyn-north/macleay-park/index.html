Macleay Park

Park in Balwyn North · Basic info

Macleay Park is a park in Balwyn North, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Kendari Ave/Belmore Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 13 m away with a baby change table.
