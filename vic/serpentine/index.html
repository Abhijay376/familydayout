Serpentine is in the Loddon area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Serpentine, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Serpentine.

Getting around

There's no train station in Serpentine. The closest to Serpentine is Raywood Railway Station (V/Line), about 25 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Serpentine. By car from Serpentine, Bendigo is about 43 minutes away, Castlemaine about 69 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Serpentine is Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood, about 26 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk is about 36 minutes' drive from Serpentine. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 43 minutes from Serpentine by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Serpentine are Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood (26 min), the playground at R J Phelan Reserve in Mitiamo (30 min), East Loddon Historical Society in Mitiamo (30 min), Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo (30 min), Eaglehawk Playspace in Eaglehawk (36 min) and Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk (36 min).
