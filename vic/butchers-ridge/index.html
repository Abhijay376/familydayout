Getting around

Butchers Ridge has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 84 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 12 stops in Butchers Ridge. By car from Butchers Ridge, Lakes Entrance is about 1.5 hours away and Bairnsdale about 2 hours, without traffic.
