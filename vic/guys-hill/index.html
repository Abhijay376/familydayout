Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Guys Hill in the Cardinia area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it. Guys Hill does have a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

In Guys Hill you'll also find Story Art Gallery.

Getting around

Guys Hill has no train station; Beaconsfield Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 4.2 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Guys Hill, so most trips start by car. By car from Guys Hill, Pakenham is about 16 minutes away, Cranbourne about 20 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 42 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Guys Hill is Jump Swim School Berwick in Berwick, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest library to Guys Hill is Berwick Library in Berwick, about 6 minutes' drive. For a beach, Carrum Beach in Carrum is about 33 minutes' drive from Guys Hill. For a skate park, Beaconsfield Skate and BMX Park in Beaconsfield is about 3 minutes' drive from Guys Hill. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Narre Warren is about 11 minutes' drive from Guys Hill. The closest indoor play centre to Guys Hill is Oz Tenpin in Narre Warren, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Guys Hill is MonkeyG's in Pakenham, about 17 minutes' drive. Pakenham is the closest bigger centre to Guys Hill, about 16 minutes away by car, and has 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play. From Guys Hill, the nearest places for kids by road are Beaconsfield Skate and BMX Park in Beaconsfield (3 min), the playground at Keith Ewenson Park in Beaconsfield Upper (4 min), the playground at Beaconsfield Recreational Reserve in Beaconsfield (4 min), Paperworks Gallery in Berwick (4 min), the bmx track at Beaconsfield Upper Recreation Reserve in Beaconsfield Upper (6 min) and Berwick Library in Berwick (6 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Guys Hill.

Nearby suburbs

Berwick, next to Guys Hill, has 58 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Beaconsfield, next to Guys Hill, has 8 playgrounds and a skate park. Beaconsfield Upper, next to Guys Hill, has 3 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Guys Hill, Harkaway has 1 playground.
