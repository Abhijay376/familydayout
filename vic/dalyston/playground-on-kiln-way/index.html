Playground on Kiln Way

Playground in Dalyston · Basic info

Playground on Kiln Way in Dalyston is a public playground in the Bass Coast Shire area. For food, Old Dalyston Church is about 450 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 750 m from the playground, and buses Coronet Bay - Wonthaggi via Corinella and Cowes - Anderson - Wonthaggi stop at Ozone Hotel/Bass Hwy, a 9-minute walk away.
