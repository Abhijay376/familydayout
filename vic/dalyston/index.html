Dalyston is in the Bass Coast area, and our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in the suburb. Dalyston also has a beach.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Dalyston is Kilcunda Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 4 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Dalyston. In Dalyston you'll also find Powlett River East. For walks and rides, Bass Coast Rail Trail crosses Dalyston. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Dalyston for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Dalyston has no train station; Stony Point Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 35 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Cowes - Anderson - Wonthaggi and Coronet Bay - Wonthaggi via Corinella routes stop in Dalyston. V/Line coaches stop in Dalyston. By car from Dalyston, Wonthaggi is about 8 minutes away, Leongatha about 38 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Dalyston is Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Wonthaggi, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest library to Dalyston is Wonthaggi Principal Library in Wonthaggi, about 7 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Wonthaggi Railway Historic Reserve in Kilcunda is about 6 minutes' drive from Dalyston. The closest cinema to Dalyston is Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi, about 7 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville is about 20 minutes' drive from Dalyston. The nearest larger centre is Wonthaggi, about 8 minutes from Dalyston by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Beach days at Powlett River East. Free days on the Bass Coast Rail Trail and in Dalyston's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Wonthaggi, next to Dalyston, has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Dalyston, Kilcunda has 1 playground, a beach and a skate park. South Dudley, next to Dalyston, has 1 playground. Next door to Dalyston, North Wonthaggi has 1 playground. Woolamai, West Creek and Ryanston also border Dalyston.
