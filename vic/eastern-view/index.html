Eastern View is in the Surf Coast area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Eastern View, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids. Eastern View does have a beach.

Landmarks

Places to visit in Eastern View include Fairhaven. For walks and rides, Ocean View Walk and Moggs Creek Circuit Walk cross Eastern View.

Getting around

There's no train station in Eastern View. The closest to Eastern View is Winchelsea Railway Station (V/Line), about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Eastern View. By car from Eastern View, Torquay is about 37 minutes away, Geelong about 55 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Eastern View is Lorne Sea Baths in Lorne, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest library to Eastern View is Torquay Library in Torquay, about 37 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Aireys Inlet Skate Park in Aireys Inlet is about 5 minutes' drive from Eastern View. The closest cinema to Eastern View is Lorne Theatre in Lorne, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Eastern View is Live Wire Park in Lorne, about 17 minutes' drive. Torquay is the closest bigger centre to Eastern View, about 37 minutes away by car, and has 15 playgrounds, a library and a beach. From Eastern View, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Aireys Inlet Reserve in Aireys Inlet (5 min), Aireys Inlet Skate Park in Aireys Inlet (5 min), the playground at Bark Hut Reserve in Aireys Inlet (6 min), Step Beach in Aireys Inlet (6 min), Lorne Theatre in Lorne (15 min) and Lorne Sea Baths in Lorne (15 min).

Best for

Beach days at Fairhaven. Longer rides and walks from Eastern View on the Ocean View Walk.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Eastern View, Aireys Inlet has 2 playgrounds, a beach and a skate park. Next door to Eastern View, Big Hill has a beach. Fairhaven and Boonah also border Eastern View.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Eastern View, without traffic.
