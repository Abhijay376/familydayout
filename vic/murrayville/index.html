Our data lists 7 parks and reserves in Murrayville, in the Mildura area. On top of that, Murrayville has a library.

Landmarks

Murrayville Flora Reserve is the largest mapped park in Murrayville, at about 255 hectares. Duddo Bushland Reserve and Danyo I63 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Murrayville include Pioneer Park, Danyo I64 Bushland Reserve and Duddo Wells Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Murrayville. Places to visit in Murrayville include Murrayville Library. The Crooked Mile Track passes through Murrayville. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Murrayville for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Murrayville. The closest to Murrayville is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 217 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Ouyen - Pinnaroo via Mallee Hwy route stop in Murrayville. V/Line coaches stop in Murrayville. By car from Murrayville, Mildura is about 2.5 hours away and Swan Hill about 3 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Rainy days at the library in Murrayville. Free days on the Crooked Mile Track and in Murrayville's parks.
