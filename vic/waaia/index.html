Landmarks

Our data shows 2 public toilets in Waaia.

Getting around

Waaia has no train station; Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 37 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Waaia, so most trips start by car. By car from Waaia, Shepparton is about 44 minutes away and Echuca about 60 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah is about 17 minutes' drive from Waaia. For a library, Numurkah Library in Numurkah is about 16 minutes' drive from Waaia. The closest beach to Waaia is Wasers Beach in Bearii, about 27 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Riverland Skate Park in Numurkah is about 17 minutes' drive from Waaia. For a cinema, Cobram Cinema in Cobram is about 36 minutes' drive from Waaia. The closest indoor play centre to Waaia is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 44 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 44 minutes from Waaia by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Waaia, the nearest places for kids by road are Numurkah Library in Numurkah (16 min), Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah (17 min), Riverland Skate Park in Numurkah (17 min), Barmah Forest Heritge and Education Centre in Nathalia (18 min), Nathalia Library in Nathalia (18 min) and Nathalia Memorial Pool in Nathalia (20 min).
