Bahgallah in the Glenelg area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Wilkin Flora and Fauna Reserve is the largest mapped park in Bahgallah, at about 3,233 hectares. Pieracle Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting) and Bahgallah Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Bahgallah include Glenelg River J12 Streamside Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bahgallah. The closest to Bahgallah is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 128 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bahgallah, so most trips start by car. By car from Bahgallah, Hamilton is about 64 minutes away and Portland about 85 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Casterton Pool in Casterton is about 13 minutes' drive from Bahgallah. The closest skate park to Bahgallah is coleraine skate park in Coleraine, about 36 minutes' drive. From Bahgallah, the nearest places for kids by road are Gorman's art gallery in Casterton (13 min), Casterton Pool in Casterton (13 min), Merino Swimming Pool in Merino (31 min), the playground at Lions Park in Merino (31 min), coleraine skate park in Coleraine (36 min) and the playground at Queens Park in Coleraine (36 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bahgallah.

Nearby suburbs
