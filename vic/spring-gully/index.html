Our data lists 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in Spring Gully, in the Greater Bendigo area.

Landmarks

Spring Gully Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Spring Gully, at about 13 hectares. Muldoon reserve and Spring Gully Recreation Reserve are the next largest. Playgrounds around Spring Gully include Kunzea Court Playspace. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Spring Gully. The Goldfields Track, Leanganook Track and Spring Creek Trail all pass through Spring Gully.

Getting around

Spring Gully has no train station; Bendigo Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 3.4 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 62 and 65 stop in Spring Gully. By car from Spring Gully, Bendigo is about 7 minutes away, Castlemaine about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Spring Gully is Brennan Park Swimming Pool in Kennington, about 6 minutes' drive. For a library, Bendigo Library in Bendigo is about 6 minutes' drive from Spring Gully. For a skate park, Ewing Park Skating in Bendigo is about 5 minutes' drive from Spring Gully. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 7 minutes' drive from Spring Gully. The closest indoor play centre to Spring Gully is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Spring Gully is The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo, about 10 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 7 minutes from Spring Gully by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Spring Gully, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Spring Gully Bushland Reserve in Flora Hill (2 min), Ewing Park Skating in Bendigo (5 min), Brennan Park Swimming Pool in Kennington (6 min), Intagolf in Golden Gully (6 min), Discovery Science & Technology Centre in Bendigo (6 min) and Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo (7 min).

Best for

Free days on the Goldfields Track and in Spring Gully's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Spring Gully, Kangaroo Flat has 13 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Spring Gully, Strathfieldsaye has 6 playgrounds. Flora Hill, next to Spring Gully, has 4 playgrounds. Quarry Hill, next to Spring Gully, has 1 playground. Mandurang and Golden Gully also border Spring Gully.
