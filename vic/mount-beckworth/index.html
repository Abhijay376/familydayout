Mount Beckworth in the Hepburn area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Mount Beckworth is Mount Beckworth Scenic Reserve, covering about 615 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Mount Beckworth.

Getting around

Mount Beckworth has no train station; Clunes Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 8.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mount Beckworth, so most trips start by car. By car from Mount Beckworth, Maryborough is about 39 minutes away, Ballarat about 47 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Clunes Swimming Pool in Clunes is about 28 minutes' drive from Mount Beckworth. The closest library to Mount Beckworth is Creswick Library in Creswick, about 39 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Mount Beckworth is the skate park at Alexander Park in Learmonth, about 28 minutes' drive. Maryborough is the closest bigger centre to Mount Beckworth, about 39 minutes away by car, and has a library. The closest places for kids by road from Mount Beckworth are Lee Medlyn Bottle Museum in Clunes (27 min), The Warehouse in Clunes (28 min), Clunes Swimming Pool in Clunes (28 min), the playground at Queens Park in Clunes (28 min), the playground at Ascot Hall Reserve in Ascot (29 min) and the skate park at Alexander Park in Learmonth (28 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mount Beckworth.

Nearby suburbs

Clunes, next to Mount Beckworth, has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Evansford also borders Mount Beckworth.
