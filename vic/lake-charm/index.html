Our data lists 7 parks and reserves in Lake Charm, in the Gannawarra area.

Landmarks

Cullens Lake Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Lake Charm, at about 733 hectares. Duck Lake Wildlife Reserve (hunting) and Stevenson Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting) are the next largest. Lake Charm also has Cranes Lake Bushland Reserve, Spences Lake Bushland Reserve and McMillans Lake Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Lake Charm.

Getting around

Lake Charm has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Lake Charm. By car from Lake Charm, Swan Hill is about 33 minutes away and Echuca about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Swimming Pool in Kerang is about 15 minutes' drive from Lake Charm. The closest library to Lake Charm is Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang, about 15 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Riverside Park in Swan Hill is about 32 minutes' drive from Lake Charm. The closest cinema to Lake Charm is Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill, about 32 minutes' drive. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Lake Charm, about 33 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Lake Charm, the nearest places for kids by road are Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (15 min), Swimming Pool in Kerang (15 min), the playground at Gray Park in Lake Boga (20 min), Lake Boga Flying Boat Museum in Lake Boga (21 min), the playground at Lake Meran W.R in Lake Meran (29 min) and Swan Hill Regional Art Gallery in Swan Hill (32 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lake Charm.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Lake Charm, Murrabit has 1 playground. Fairley, Capels Crossing, Benjeroop and Bael Bael also border Lake Charm.
