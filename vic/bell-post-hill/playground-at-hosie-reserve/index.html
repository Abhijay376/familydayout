Playground at Hosie Reserve

Playground in Bell Post Hill · Basic info

Playground at Hosie Reserve is a public playground in Bell Post Hill, in the Greater Geelong City area. Enrico's Pizza and Pasta is about 220 m away if you need food, and Playground at Pettit Park is another option about 120 m away. There's a public toilet about 240 m away, and bus 25 stops at Fairy St E/Rollins Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
