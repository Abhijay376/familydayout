Myers Reserve

Park in Bell Post Hill · Official details

Myers Reserve is a park in Bell Post Hill, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Rollins Rd Sth/Braund Ave (bus) is a 10-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 653 m away and dogs are allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Myers Reserve?
Myers Reserve is in Bell Post Hill, Victoria.
Is Myers Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Myers Reserve?
Yes, dogs are allowed.
Are there toilets at Myers Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 653 m away.
How do I get to Myers Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Rollins Rd Sth/Braund Ave (routes 25), a 10-minute walk.
