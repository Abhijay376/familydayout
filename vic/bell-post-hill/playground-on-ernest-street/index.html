Playground on Ernest Street

Playground in Bell Post Hill · Basic info

Playground on Ernest Street is a public playground in Bell Post Hill, in the Greater Geelong City area. Enrico's Pizza and Pasta is about 430 m away if you need food, and Playground at Hosie Reserve is another option about 220 m away. There's a public toilet about 360 m away, and bus 25 stops at Beauford Ave Sth/Braund Ave, a 5-minute walk away.
