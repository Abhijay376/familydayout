Woodhouse is in the Southern Grampians area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Woodhouse, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Woodhouse. The closest to Woodhouse is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 66 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Woodhouse, so most trips start by car. By car from Woodhouse, Hamilton is about 33 minutes away and Warrnambool about 73 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Woodhouse is Penshurst Swimming Pool in Penshurst, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest library to Woodhouse is Hamilton Library in Hamilton, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Woodhouse is hamilton skate park in Hamilton, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Woodhouse is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 32 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 33 minutes from Woodhouse by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Woodhouse are Penshurst Rec Reserve Playground in Penshurst (17 min), Penshurst Swimming Pool in Penshurst (17 min), Penshurst Botanic Gardens Playground in Penshurst (18 min), Volcanoes Discovery Centre in Penshurst (18 min), Sir Reginald Ansett Transport Museum in Hamilton (31 min) and hamilton skate park in Hamilton (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Woodhouse.

Nearby suburbs

Penshurst, next to Woodhouse, has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Glenthompson, next to Woodhouse, has 2 playgrounds. Dunkeld, next to Woodhouse, has 1 playground. Caramut also borders Woodhouse.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Woodhouse, without traffic.
