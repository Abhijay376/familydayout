Clyde in the Casey area has 11 playgrounds and 13 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, Clyde has 2 skate parks.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Clyde is Lilium Estate Reserve, covering about 18 hectares. Clyde Recreation Reserve and Orana Wetlands are the next largest. Clyde also has Oak Park, Maple Park and Pasadena Park. Playgrounds in Clyde include Big Kids Playground, Maple Park Playground and Little Kids Playground. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Clyde. For older kids in Clyde there's Maple Park Pump Track and Skate Park. Our data lists 7 cafes and family restaurants in Clyde for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Clyde has no train station; Cranbourne Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 5.8 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 795, 796, 831 and 897 stop in Clyde. V/Line coaches stop in Clyde. By car from Clyde, Cranbourne is about 8 minutes away, Pakenham about 21 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 46 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, King Swim in Clyde North is about 5 minutes' drive from Clyde. The closest library to Clyde is Cranbourne Library in Cranbourne East, about 6 minutes' drive. For a beach, Seaford Beach in Seaford is about 24 minutes' drive from Clyde. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Narre Warren is about 15 minutes' drive from Clyde. The closest indoor play centre to Clyde is Oz Tenpin in Narre Warren, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Clyde is Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale, about 13 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 11 playgrounds across Clyde. Teens who skate or ride, with Maple Park Pump Track in Clyde.

Nearby suburbs

Clyde North, next to Clyde, has 34 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Clyde, Cranbourne East has 16 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Clyde, Cardinia has 4 playgrounds. Tooradin, next to Clyde, has 2 playgrounds. Devon Meadows and Dalmore also border Clyde.
