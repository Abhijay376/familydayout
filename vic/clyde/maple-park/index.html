Maple Park

Park in Clyde · Basic info

Maple Park is a park in Clyde, in the Casey City Council area, good for all ages. Eliston District Park Playground/Waterman Dr (bus) is a 9-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1405 m away with a baby change table.
