Playground at Pasadena Park

Playground in Clyde · Basic info

This is a public playground in Clyde. For food, McDonald's is about 330 m away, and Playground at Hill Farm Park is another option about 360 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 380 m from the playground, and buses 796 and 897 stop at Clyde-Five Ways Rd/Berwick-Cranbourne Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
