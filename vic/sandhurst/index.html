Our data lists 3 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in Sandhurst, in the Frankston area.

Landmarks

Sandhurst also has Maybanke Park, Deakin Reserve and Ancient Log Park. Playgrounds in Sandhurst include Sandarra Community Playground.

Getting around

Sandhurst has no train station; Lynbrook Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 4.2 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 832 and 833 stop in Sandhurst. By car from Sandhurst, Cranbourne is about 14 minutes away, Dandenong about 15 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 40 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Sandhurst is JUMP! Swim Schools in Cranbourne West, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest library to Sandhurst is Kingston Libraries Patterson Lakes in Patterson Lakes, about 9 minutes' drive. For a beach, Carrum Beach in Carrum is about 13 minutes' drive from Sandhurst. For a skate park, Carrum Downs Skatepark in Carrum Downs is about 8 minutes' drive from Sandhurst. The closest cinema to Sandhurst is Reading Cinema in Dandenong, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Sandhurst is Gravity Zone Indoor Trampoline Centre in Seaford, about 10 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale is about 21 minutes' drive from Sandhurst. The nearest larger centre is Cranbourne, about 14 minutes from Sandhurst by car, with 27 playgrounds.

Nearby suburbs

Carrum Downs, next to Sandhurst, has 22 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Sandhurst, Skye has 7 playgrounds. Next door to Sandhurst, Lyndhurst has 7 playgrounds.
