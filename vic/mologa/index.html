Mologa in the Loddon area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Mologa is Mologa Bushland Reserve, covering about 3 hectares.

Getting around

Mologa has no train station; Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mologa, so most trips start by car. By car from Mologa, Echuca is about 60 minutes away and Bendigo about 72 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mologa is Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo, about 17 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 60 minutes from Mologa by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Mologa are the playground at R J Phelan Reserve in Mitiamo (16 min), Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo (17 min), East Loddon Historical Society in Mitiamo (17 min), Pyramid Hill & Districts Historical Museum in Pyramid Hill (17 min) and Leitchville Memorial Swimming Pool in Leitchville (50 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mologa.

Nearby suburbs

Mitiamo, next to Mologa, has 1 playground and a pool. Yarrawalla, next to Mologa, has 1 playground. Next door to Mologa, Pyramid Hill has 1 playground. Calivil also borders Mologa.
