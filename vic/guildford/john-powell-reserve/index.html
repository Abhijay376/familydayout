John Powell Reserve

Park in Guildford · Basic info

John Powell Reserve is a park in Guildford, in the Mount Alexander Shire Council area, good for all ages. Guildford General Store/Midland Hwy (coach) is a 16-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 927 m away.
