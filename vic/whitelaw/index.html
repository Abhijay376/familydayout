Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Whitelaw in the South Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Great Southern Rail Trail crosses Whitelaw.

Getting around

Whitelaw has no train station; Drouin Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 32 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Whitelaw, so most trips start by car. By car from Whitelaw, Leongatha is about 17 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 37 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Korumburra Outdoor Pool in Korumburra is about 4 minutes' drive from Whitelaw. The closest library to Whitelaw is Poowong library in Poowong, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Whitelaw is The Caves in Inverloch, about 35 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Nyora skate park in Nyora is about 15 minutes' drive from Whitelaw. For a cinema, Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha is about 16 minutes' drive from Whitelaw. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 43 minutes' drive from Whitelaw. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Whitelaw is Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville, about 35 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Leongatha, about 17 minutes from Whitelaw by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Whitelaw are the playground at Coleman Park in Korumburra (4 min), Korumburra Outdoor Pool in Korumburra (4 min), the playground at Peterson Street Reserve in Korumburra (5 min), Coal Creek Community Park & Museum in Korumburra (6 min), Nyora skate park in Nyora (15 min) and Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong (14 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Whitelaw on the Great Southern Rail Trail.
