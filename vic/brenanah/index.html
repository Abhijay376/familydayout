Brenanah is in the Loddon area, and our data lists 5 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Brenanah is Kooyoora State Park, covering about 11,459 hectares. Kooyoora Reference Area and Brenanah Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Brenanah include Sand Quarry Dam and Melville Caves Picnic Area. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Brenanah. For camping, there's Sand Quarry Dam. The Long Rock Walking Track, Eastern Walking Circuit and Southern Lookout Walking Track all pass through Brenanah.

Getting around

Brenanah has no train station; Raywood Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 45 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Brenanah, so most trips start by car. By car from Brenanah, Bendigo is about 66 minutes away, Maryborough about 75 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn is about 26 minutes' drive from Brenanah. The closest places for kids by road from Brenanah are Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (26 min), Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (26 min), Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (50 min), Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (50 min), Rex Theatre in Charlton (51 min) and the playground at Rotary Park in Charlton (51 min).

Best for

Free days on the Long Rock Walking Track and in Brenanah's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Brenanah, Wedderburn has 1 playground and a pool. Kurraca, Kingower and Glenalbyn also border Brenanah.
