Playground on The Boulevard

Playground in Ivanhoe · Basic info

Playground on The Boulevard in Ivanhoe is a public playground in the Banyule City area. The LBN1 - Watsonia to East Ivanhoe passes about 20 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 140 m away. There's a public toilet about 2.4 km away, and bus 548 stops at The Boulevard/Burke Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
