Playground at Banksia Island Reserve

Playground in Ivanhoe · Basic info

Playground at Banksia Island Reserve in Ivanhoe is a public playground in the Banyule City area. The LBN1 - Watsonia to East Ivanhoe is about 400 m away, and for food, McDonald's is about 490 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 600 m from the playground, and buses 513, 514 and 903 stop at Upper Heidelberg Rd/Bell St, a 4-minute walk away.
