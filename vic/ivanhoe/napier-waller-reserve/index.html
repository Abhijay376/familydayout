Napier Waller Reserve

Park in Ivanhoe · Basic info

Napier Waller Reserve is a park in Ivanhoe, in the Banyule City Council area, good for all ages. The Boulevard/Heidelberg Rd (bus) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1174 m away.
