Kitchener Reserve

Park in Ivanhoe · Basic info

Kitchener Reserve is a park in Ivanhoe, in the Banyule City Council area, good for all ages. Ivanhoe Station/Norman St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 158 m away.
