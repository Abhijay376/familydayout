Manns Beach is in the Wellington area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Manns Beach, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Manns Beach.

Getting around

There's no train station in Manns Beach. The closest to Manns Beach is Rosedale Railway Station (V/Line), about 54 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Manns Beach, so most trips start by car. By car from Manns Beach, Sale is about 72 minutes away and Traralgon about 89 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Yarram Public Pool in Yarram is about 29 minutes' drive from Manns Beach. For a library, Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram is about 28 minutes' drive from Manns Beach. The closest beach to Manns Beach is Woodside_Beach in Woodside Beach, about 42 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Manns Beach is the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram, about 30 minutes' drive. From Manns Beach, the nearest places for kids by road are Port Albert Maritime Museum in Port Albert (28 min), the playground at Memorial Park in Yarram (28 min), Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram (28 min), Yarram Public Pool in Yarram (29 min), the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram (30 min) and Yarram Historical Society Museum in Yarram (30 min).
