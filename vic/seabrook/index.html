Seabrook in the Hobsons Bay area has 3 playgrounds and 5 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Pipeline Reserve is the largest mapped park in Seabrook, at about 2 hectares. Homestead Run Reserve and Sandstone Point Reserve are the next largest. Seabrook also has Barry Jones Park and St Anthony Court Reserve. Playgrounds in Seabrook include the playground at Barry Jones Park, the playground at Pipeline Reserve and the playground at Homestead Run Reserve. Our data shows 3 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Seabrook. The Skeleton Creek Trail passes through Seabrook. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Seabrook for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Seabrook has no train station; Aircraft Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 1.3 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 496, 498 and 949 stop in Seabrook. By car from Seabrook, Werribee is about 11 minutes away, Sunshine about 17 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 24 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, The Canal in Point Cook is about 7 minutes' drive from Seabrook. For a library, Altona Meadows Library in Altona Meadows is about 5 minutes' drive from Seabrook. The closest beach to Seabrook is Altona Beach in Altona, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Seabrook is the skate park at Newminster Way Park in Point Cook, about 2 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Seabrook is VIllage Cinemas in Hoppers Crossing, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Seabrook is Timezone in Hoppers Crossing, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Seabrook is Werribee Open Range Zoo in Werribee South, about 14 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Werribee, about 11 minutes from Seabrook by car, with 58 playgrounds, a pool and a library.

Best for

Younger kids at Seabrook's 3 local playgrounds. Free days on the Skeleton Creek Trail and in Seabrook's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Seabrook, Point Cook has 59 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Altona Meadows, next to Seabrook, has 15 playgrounds, a library and a skate park.
