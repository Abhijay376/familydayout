Playground at Barry Jones Park

Playground in Seabrook · Official details

Playground at Barry Jones Park is a public playground in Seabrook, in the Hobsons Bay City area. The Skeleton Creek Trail is about 390 m away, and Playground at Homestead Run Reserve is another option about 210 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and buses 496 and 498 stop at Homestead Run/Point Cook Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
