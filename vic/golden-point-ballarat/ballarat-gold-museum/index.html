Ballarat Gold Museum

Museum or gallery in Golden Point · Official details

Ballarat Gold Museum is a museum or gallery in Golden Point, in the Ballarat City Council area, best for school-age kids. Sovereign Hill/Bradshaw St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 564 m away. Opening hours: Tuesday-Sunday, 10am – 5pm | Mondays: Pre-booked school groups only; Monday-Sunday 10am-5pm during spring school holidays, 19 September - 4 October.
