Jack Greville Park

Park in Golden Point · Basic info

Jack Greville Park is a park in Golden Point, in the Ballarat City Council area, good for all ages. Sovereign Hill/Main Rd (coach) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 275 m away.
