Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Mepunga East in the Moyne area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mepunga East. The closest to Mepunga East is Sherwood Park Railway Station (V/Line), about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mepunga East, so most trips start by car. By car from Mepunga East, Warrnambool is about 21 minutes away and Colac about 76 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool is about 25 minutes' drive from Mepunga East. For a library, Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool is about 23 minutes' drive from Mepunga East. For a beach, Buttress Point in The Cove is about 15 minutes' drive from Mepunga East. For a skate park, Warrnambool Skate Park in Warrnambool is about 25 minutes' drive from Mepunga East. For a cinema, Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool is about 22 minutes' drive from Mepunga East. The closest indoor play centre to Mepunga East is The Bowl in Warrnambool, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Mepunga East is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 15 minutes' drive. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Mepunga East, about 21 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Mepunga East are Buttress Point in The Cove (15 min), Flaxman Beach in Nullawarre (16 min), Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (15 min), Grieve Street Playground in Warrnambool (19 min), the playground at Brierly Park in Warrnambool (21 min) and SCOPE Galleries in Warrnambool (21 min).
