Playground at Booran Road Reserve

Playground in Glen Huntly · Basic info

This is a public playground in Glen Huntly. The Caulfield-Frankston Rail Trail is about 380 m away, and for food, Workshop Glenhuntly is about 360 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and tram 67 stops at Booran Rd/Glenhuntly Rd #58, a 2-minute walk away.
