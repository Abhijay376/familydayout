Our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in Glen Huntly, in the Glen Eira area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Glen Huntly is Booran Road Reserve, covering about 2 hectares. Playgrounds in Glen Huntly include the playground at Booran Road Reserve. Our data shows 4 public toilets and 1 off-leash dog park in Glen Huntly. For walks and rides, Rosstown Rail Trail and Caulfield-Frankston Rail Trail cross Glen Huntly. Our data lists 8 cafes and family restaurants in Glen Huntly for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Glen Huntly has a train station: Glen Huntly Railway Station (metro: Frankston Line). Tram 67 stops in Glen Huntly. Buses 623 and 624 stop in Glen Huntly. By car from Glen Huntly, Box Hill is about 19 minutes away, Dandenong about 22 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 16 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Diving Pool in Carnegie is about 3 minutes' drive from Glen Huntly. For a library, Carnegie Library in Carnegie is about 3 minutes' drive from Glen Huntly. For a beach, Elwood Beach in Elwood is about 9 minutes' drive from Glen Huntly. For a skate park, Bailey Reserve Skate Park in Bentleigh East is about 6 minutes' drive from Glen Huntly. For a cinema, Classic Cinema Elsternwick in Elsternwick is about 6 minutes' drive from Glen Huntly. The closest indoor play centre to Glen Huntly is Akira’s Arcade in Prahran, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Glen Huntly is Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale, about 7 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Box Hill, about 19 minutes from Glen Huntly by car, with 11 playgrounds, a pool and a library.

Best for

Free days on the Rosstown Rail Trail and in Glen Huntly's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Carnegie, next to Glen Huntly, has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Glen Huntly, Caulfield has a library. Next door to Glen Huntly, Ormond has 3 playgrounds. Caulfield East, next to Glen Huntly, has 2 playgrounds. Caulfield South also borders Glen Huntly.
