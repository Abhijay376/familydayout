Booran Road Reserve

Park in Glen Huntly · Official details

Booran Road Reserve is a park in Glen Huntly, in the Glen Eira City Council area, good for all ages. Booran Rd/Glenhuntly Rd #58 (tram) is a 2-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 40 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed.
