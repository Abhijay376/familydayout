White Street Reserve

Park in Cremorne · Basic info

White Street Reserve is a park in Cremorne, in the Yarra City Council area, good for all ages. Swan Street Shopping Centre/Swan St #10 (tram) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 410 m away.
