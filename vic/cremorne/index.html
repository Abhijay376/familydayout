Our data lists 3 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in Cremorne, in the Yarra area.

Landmarks

Cremorne also has White Street Reserve, Charles Evans Reserve and Stephenson Street Reserve. Playgrounds in Cremorne include the playground at White Street Reserve and the playground at Charles Evans Reserve. For walks and rides, Main Yarra Trail, Capital City Trail and Main Yarra Trail Capital City Trail cross Cremorne. Our data lists 6 cafes and family restaurants in Cremorne for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at East Richmond Railway Station (metro: Alamein Line, Belgrave Line and Glen Waverley Line) in Cremorne. Trams 70 and 78 stop in Cremorne. By car from Cremorne, Box Hill is about 20 minutes away, Sunshine about 24 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 5 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cremorne is Prahran Aquatic Centre in Prahran, about 5 minutes' drive. For a library, Richmond Library in Richmond is about 2 minutes' drive from Cremorne. The closest beach to Cremorne is Horse Arena in Abbotsford, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Cremorne is Riverslide Skate Park in Melbourne, about 4 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Cremorne is Palace Cinemas in South Yarra, about 4 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Rush Escape Game in South Yarra is about 3 minutes' drive from Cremorne. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Cremorne is Skyline Precinct Melbourne in South Wharf, about 6 minutes' drive. Box Hill is the closest bigger centre to Cremorne, about 20 minutes away by car, and has 11 playgrounds, a pool and a library.

Best for

Younger kids at Cremorne's 3 local playgrounds. Free days on the Main Yarra Trail and in Cremorne's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Richmond, next to Cremorne, has 15 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. South Yarra, next to Cremorne, has 12 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. Next door to Cremorne, Melbourne has 1 playground, a pool and a library.
