Playground at Powlett Reserve

Playground in East Melbourne · Official details

Playground at Powlett Reserve is a public playground in East Melbourne, in the Melbourne City area. The Bike: Wellington St passes about 250 m away, and East Melbourne Library and Community Centre is about 390 m away. There's a public toilet about 100 m away, and trams 109 and 12 stop at Wellington St/Victoria Pde #16, a 4-minute walk away.
