Yarra Park

Park in East Melbourne · Basic info

Yarra Park is a park in East Melbourne, in the Melbourne City Council area, good for all ages. Rowena Pde/Punt Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 260 m away.
