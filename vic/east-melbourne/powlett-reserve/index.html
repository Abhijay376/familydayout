Powlett Reserve

Park in East Melbourne · Official details

Powlett Reserve is a park in East Melbourne, in the Melbourne City Council area, good for all ages. Wellington St/Victoria Pde #16 (tram) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 97 m away.

Questions parents ask

Where is Powlett Reserve?
Powlett Reserve is in East Melbourne, Victoria.
Is Powlett Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Powlett Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 97 m away.
How do I get to Powlett Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Wellington St/Victoria Pde #16 (routes 109, 12), a 4-minute walk.
