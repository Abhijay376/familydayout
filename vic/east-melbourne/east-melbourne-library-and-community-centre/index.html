East Melbourne Library and Community Centre

Library in East Melbourne · Basic info

East Melbourne Library and Community Centre is a library in East Melbourne, in the Melbourne City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Jolimont Station-MCG/Wellington Pde #11 (tram) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 4 m away.
