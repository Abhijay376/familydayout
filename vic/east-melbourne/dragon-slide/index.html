Dragon Slide

Playground in East Melbourne · Basic info

Dragon Slide in East Melbourne is a public playground in the Melbourne City area. The Bike: Preston to CBD passes about 120 m away, and East Melbourne Library and Community Centre is about 540 m away. There's a public toilet about 160 m away, and 11 bus routes stop at Clarendon St/Victoria Pde, a 5-minute walk away.
