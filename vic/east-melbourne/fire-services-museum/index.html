Fire Services Museum

Museum or gallery in East Melbourne · Official details

Fire Services Museum is a museum or gallery in East Melbourne, in the Melbourne City Council area, best for school-age kids. St Vincents Plaza/Victoria Pde #12 (tram) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 210 m away. Opening hours: Thursdays and Fridays 9am to 3pm,; and Sundays 10am to 4pm..
