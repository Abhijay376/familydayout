Playground at Yarra Park

Playground in East Melbourne · Basic info

Playground at Yarra Park in East Melbourne is a public playground in the Melbourne City area. The MCG & surrounds is about 230 m away, and East Melbourne Library and Community Centre is about 220 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 310 m from the playground, and trams 48 and 75 stop at Jolimont Station-MCG/Wellington Pde #11, a 3-minute walk away.
