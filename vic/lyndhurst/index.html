Lyndhurst in the Greater Dandenong area has 7 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lyndhurst is Marriott Waters Recreation Reserve, covering about 21 hectares. Figtree Walk Reserve and Melington Drive Reserve are the next largest. Playgrounds around Lyndhurst include Livvi's Place and the playground at Figtree Walk Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Lyndhurst. Marriott Waters Shopping Centre is the local shopping centre. The Bike: Thompsons Road passes through Lyndhurst. Our data lists 9 cafes and family restaurants in Lyndhurst for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Lyndhurst has train stations: Lynbrook Railway Station (metro: Cranbourne Line) and Merinda Park Railway Station (metro: Cranbourne Line). Buses 863, 881, 890, 897 and 901 stop in Lyndhurst. By car from Lyndhurst, Cranbourne is about 8 minutes away, Dandenong about 13 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 38 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lyndhurst is JUMP! Swim Schools in Cranbourne West, about 5 minutes' drive. For a library, Hampton Park Library in Hampton Park is about 9 minutes' drive from Lyndhurst. The closest beach to Lyndhurst is Carrum Beach in Carrum, about 16 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track at Courtenay Avenue Reserve in Cranbourne North is about 6 minutes' drive from Lyndhurst. The closest cinema to Lyndhurst is Reading Cinema in Dandenong, about 14 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Oz Tenpin in Narre Warren is about 14 minutes' drive from Lyndhurst. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Lyndhurst is Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale, about 19 minutes' drive.

Best for

Younger kids at Lyndhurst's 7 local playgrounds. Free days on the Bike: Thompsons Road and in Lyndhurst's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Lyndhurst, Cranbourne has 27 playgrounds and a skate park. Cranbourne West, next to Lyndhurst, has 21 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Carrum Downs, next to Lyndhurst, has 22 playgrounds and a skate park. Cranbourne North, next to Lyndhurst, has 12 playgrounds and a skate park. Skye, Lynbrook, Sandhurst and Dandenong South also border Lyndhurst.
