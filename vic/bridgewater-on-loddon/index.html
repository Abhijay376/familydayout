Getting around

Bridgewater On Loddon has no train station; Raywood Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Bendigo - Boort via Wedderburn route stop in Bridgewater On Loddon. V/Line coaches stop in Bridgewater On Loddon. By car from Bridgewater On Loddon, Bendigo is about 34 minutes away, Castlemaine about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.
