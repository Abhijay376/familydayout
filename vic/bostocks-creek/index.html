Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bostocks Creek in the Corangamite area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Bostocks Creek has no train station; Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 8.1 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bostocks Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Bostocks Creek, Colac is about 42 minutes away, Warrnambool about 53 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon is about 31 minutes' drive from Bostocks Creek. The closest library to Bostocks Creek is Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum, about 43 minutes' drive. For a beach, Rutledge Creek Beach in Port Campbell is about 43 minutes' drive from Bostocks Creek. The closest skate park to Bostocks Creek is Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown, about 9 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 42 minutes from Bostocks Creek by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Bostocks Creek are Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (9 min), the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve in Camperdown (11 min), the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (30 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (31 min), the playground at Tea Tree Lake Reserve in Mortlake (39 min) and Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum (43 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bostocks Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Bostocks Creek, Camperdown has 3 playgrounds and a skate park. Cobden, next to Bostocks Creek, has 1 playground. South Purrumbete, Naroghid and Bullaharre also border Bostocks Creek.
