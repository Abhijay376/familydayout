Beremboke in the Moorabool area has 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Beremboke is Durdidwarrah Creek Reference Area, covering about 276 hectares. Beremboke Recreation Reserve is the next largest. Playgrounds in Beremboke include the playground at Beremboke Recreation Reserve. For walks and rides, Burchell Trail crosses Beremboke.

Getting around

Beremboke has no train station; Ballan Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Beremboke, so most trips start by car. By car from Beremboke, Bacchus Marsh is about 47 minutes away, Daylesford about 54 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Ballan Swimming Pool in Ballan is about 29 minutes' drive from Beremboke. The closest beach to Beremboke is Disability beach in Steiglitz, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Beremboke is Meredith Skate Park in Meredith, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Beremboke is Fairy Park in Anakie, about 26 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bacchus Marsh, about 47 minutes from Beremboke by car, with 6 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Beremboke are the playground at Mount Wallace Hall in Mount Wallace (9 min), Disability beach in Steiglitz (36 min), Fairy Park in Anakie (26 min), Meredith Skate Park in Meredith (30 min), the bmx track at Bacchus Marsh Racecourse and Recreation Reserve in Maddingley (47 min) and Ballan Swimming Pool in Ballan (29 min).

Best for

Free days on the Burchell Trail and in Beremboke's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Mount Wallace, next to Beremboke, has 1 playground. Next door to Beremboke, Balliang has 1 playground. Durdidwarrah also borders Beremboke.
