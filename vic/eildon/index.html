Our data lists 3 playgrounds and 28 parks and reserves in Eildon, in the Murrindindi area. Eildon also has a pool.

Landmarks

Big River State Forest is the largest mapped park in Eildon, at about 52,863 hectares. Lake Eildon National Park and Big River Heritage River are the next largest. Eildon also has Catford, Taponga and Petroffs. Playgrounds around Eildon include Baanh ba Djila Splash Park and the playground at W.J Moore Park. Our data shows 31 public toilets in Eildon. For camping, there's Big Bend Creek and Bobuck Ridge. In Eildon you'll also find Eildon Swimming Pool. For walks and rides, Avro Anson Memorial Track, Eildon Shared Trail and Mt Torbreck Walk cross Eildon. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Eildon for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Eildon has no train station; Euroa Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 62 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 684 and V63 stop in Eildon. By car from Eildon, Healesville is about 68 minutes away, Mansfield about 74 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Eildon is Alexandra Library in Alexandra, about 20 minutes' drive.

Best for

Younger kids at Eildon's 3 local playgrounds. Free days on the Avro Anson Memorial Track and in Eildon's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Marysville, next to Eildon, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Eildon, Jamieson has 1 playground. Rubicon, Matlock, Lake Eildon and Knockwood also border Eildon.
