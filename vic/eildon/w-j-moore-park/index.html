W.J. Moore Park

Park in Eildon · Basic info

W.J. Moore Park is a park in Eildon, in the Murrindindi Shire Council area, good for all ages. Eildon SC/Main St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 99 m away.
