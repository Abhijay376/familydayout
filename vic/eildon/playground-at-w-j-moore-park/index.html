Playground at W.J. Moore Park

Playground in Eildon · Basic info

Playground at W.J. Moore Park is a public playground in Eildon, in the Murrindindi Shire area. Eildon Swimming Pool is about 170 m away, and for food, Eildon Fish and Chips is about 130 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 60 m from the playground, and bus V63 stops at Eildon SC/Main St, a 2-minute walk away.
