Our data lists 12 playgrounds and 15 parks and reserves in Hallam, in the Casey area. On top of that, Hallam has 2 skate parks.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Hallam is Gunns Road Reserve, covering about 34 hectares. Frog Hollow Reserve and Ern Barker Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Hallam include Marson Crescent, Adam Avenue Reserve and Caron Close Reserve. Playgrounds around Hallam include Gunns Road Reserve Playground, the playground at Ern Barker Reserve and the playground at Gunns Road Reserve. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Hallam. For older kids in Hallam there's BMX Track and the pump track at Ern Barker Reserve. For walks and rides, Hallam Bypass Trail crosses Hallam. Our data lists 12 cafes and family restaurants in Hallam for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Hallam has a train station: Hallam Railway Station (metro: Pakenham Line). Buses 828, 863, 891, 893, 894, 981 and 982 stop in Hallam. By car from Hallam, Dandenong is about 10 minutes away, Cranbourne about 17 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 34 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Doveton Pool in the Park in Doveton is about 7 minutes' drive from Hallam. The closest library to Hallam is Narre Warren Library in Narre Warren, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Hallam is Edithvale Beach in Edithvale, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Hallam is Reading Cinema in Dandenong, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Hallam is Oz Tenpin in Narre Warren, about 10 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale is about 23 minutes' drive from Hallam.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 12 playgrounds across Hallam. Teens who skate or ride, with BMX Track in Hallam. Free days on the Hallam Bypass Trail and in Hallam's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Narre Warren, next to Hallam, has 30 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Endeavour Hills, next to Hallam, has 26 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Hallam, Hampton Park has 20 playgrounds and a library. Doveton, next to Hallam, has 5 playgrounds and a pool. Narre Warren North, Eumemmerring and Dandenong South also border Hallam.
