Playground at Charles Avenue Reserve

Playground in Hallam · Basic info

Playground at Charles Avenue Reserve is a public playground in Hallam, in the Casey City area. Woki Kitchen is about 180 m away if you need food, and Playground on Harmer Road is another option about 200 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.1 km away, and bus 893 stops at Harmer Rd/Princes Hwy, a 5-minute walk away.
