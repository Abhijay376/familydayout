Playground on Harmer Road

Playground in Hallam · Basic info

This is a public playground in Hallam. For food, Cup Cup And Away is about 70 m away, and Playground at Charles Avenue Reserve is another option about 200 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 940 m from the playground, and buses 828 and 981 stop at Marshall Pl/Frawley Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
