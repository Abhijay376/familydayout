Playground at Fran Street Reserve

Playground in Glenroy · Basic info

Playground at Fran Street Reserve is a public playground in Glenroy, in the Merri-Bek City area. The Moonee Ponds Creek Trail is about 60 m away, and Playground on Vanderloo Road is another option about 430 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 940 m from the playground, and bus 490 stops at Wonga Pl/Adelaide Bvd, a 5-minute walk away.
