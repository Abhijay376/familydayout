S.G. Sewell Reserve

Park in Glenroy · Basic info

S.G. Sewell Reserve is a park in Glenroy, in the Merri-Bek City Council area, good for all ages. Valencia St/Glenroy Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 16 m away.
