Bridget Shortell Reserve

Park in Glenroy · Official details

Bridget Shortell Reserve is a park in Glenroy, in the Merri-Bek City Council area, good for all ages. Wheatsheaf Rd/Plumpton Ave (bus) is a 2-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 388 m away and dogs are allowed.
