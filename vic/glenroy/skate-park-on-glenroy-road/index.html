Skate park on Glenroy Road

Skate, BMX or pump track in Glenroy · Basic info

Skate park on Glenroy Road is a skate, bmx or pump track in Glenroy, in the Merri-Bek City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Northern Golf Club/Glenroy Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 439 m away.
