LaTrobe Park

Park in Glenroy · Basic info

LaTrobe Park is a park in Glenroy, in the Merri-Bek City Council area, good for all ages. Gowrie Station/Sages Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 469 m away.
