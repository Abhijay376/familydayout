Playground at G.E. Clarke Reserve

Playground in Glenroy · Basic info

Playground at G.E. Clarke Reserve is a public playground in Glenroy, in the Merri-Bek City area. Glenroy Library is about 320 m away, and Playground at Bridget Shortell Reserve is another option about 310 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 610 m from the playground, and buses 513 and 514 stop at Kalang Rd/Plumpton Ave, a 2-minute walk away.
