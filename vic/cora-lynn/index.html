Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Cora Lynn, in the Cardinia area.

Landmarks

Cora Lynn Recreation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Cora Lynn, at about 11 hectares.

Getting around

Cora Lynn has no train station; Tynong Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 6.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cora Lynn, so most trips start by car. By car from Cora Lynn, Pakenham is about 19 minutes away, Cranbourne about 31 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 62 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cora Lynn is Garfield Swimming Pool in Garfield, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest library to Cora Lynn is Pakenham Library in Pakenham, about 19 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Skate BMX Park in Garfield is about 14 minutes' drive from Cora Lynn. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Narre Warren is about 32 minutes' drive from Cora Lynn. For an indoor play centre, Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham is about 18 minutes' drive from Cora Lynn. For a zoo or wildlife park, Wildlife Trail in Tynong North is about 19 minutes' drive from Cora Lynn. The nearest larger centre is Pakenham, about 19 minutes from Cora Lynn by car, with 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play. From Cora Lynn, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Bayles Recreation Reserve in Bayles (5 min), Skate BMX Park in Garfield (14 min), Garfield Swimming Pool in Garfield (14 min), the playground at Koolangarra Park in Bunyip (11 min), the bmx track at Greenland Court Reserve in Garfield (16 min) and Koo Wee Rup Swimming Pool in Koo Wee Rup (13 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Cora Lynn.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Cora Lynn, Garfield has 4 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Nar Nar Goon, next to Cora Lynn, has 2 playgrounds. Bayles, next to Cora Lynn, has 1 playground. Tynong, Iona and Catani also border Cora Lynn.
