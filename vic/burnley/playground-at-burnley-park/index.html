Playground at Burnley Park

Playground in Burnley · Basic info

This is a public playground in Burnley. The Richmond East-West Route is about 110 m away, and Playground at Golden Square Bicentennial Park is another option about 570 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 120 m from the playground, and tram 70 stops at Park Gr/Swan St #16, a 6-minute walk away.
