Playground at Golden Square Bicentennial Park

Playground in Burnley · Basic info

Playground at Golden Square Bicentennial Park in Burnley is a public playground in the Yarra City area. The Main Yarra Trail is about 290 m away, and for food, The Grand is about 460 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 270 m from the playground, and tram 70 stops at Stawell St/Swan St #15, a 3-minute walk away.
