Golden Square Bicentennial Park

Park in Burnley · Basic info

Golden Square Bicentennial Park is a park in Burnley, in the Yarra City Council area, good for all ages. Stawell St/Swan St #15 (tram) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 290 m away with a baby change table.
