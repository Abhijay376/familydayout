Callignee South is in the Wellington area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Callignee South, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Callignee South has no train station; Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Callignee South, so most trips start by car. By car from Callignee South, Traralgon is about 41 minutes away, Warragul about 82 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Callignee South is Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon, about 42 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Callignee South is Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon, about 44 minutes' drive. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Callignee South, about 41 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Callignee South are the playground at Hourigan Park in Traralgon (40 min), the playground at Eric Taylor Reserve in Traralgon (40 min), Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon (42 min), Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon (44 min), Powerworks in Morwell (47 min) and Village 8 in Morwell (47 min).
