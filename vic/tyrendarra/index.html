Our data lists 5 parks and reserves in Tyrendarra, in the Glenelg area. On top of that, Tyrendarra has a beach.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tyrendarra is Mt Clay State Forest, covering about 3,049 hectares. Tyrendarra Indigenous Protected Area and Narrawong Coastal Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Tyrendarra include Tyrendarra Flora Reserve and Fitzroy River Streamside Reserve. Places to visit in Tyrendarra include Fitzroy River Beach.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tyrendarra. The closest to Tyrendarra is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 63 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Tyrendarra. By car from Tyrendarra, Portland is about 22 minutes away and Hamilton about 62 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tyrendarra is Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest library to Tyrendarra is Heywood Library in Heywood, about 18 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Russell Clark Reserve in Port Fairy is about 37 minutes' drive from Tyrendarra. The closest cinema to Tyrendarra is Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy, about 37 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Portland, about 22 minutes from Tyrendarra by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Tyrendarra are Bay of Whales Gallery in Narrawong (14 min), the playground at Narrawong Recreation Reserve in Narrawong (11 min), Eumeralla in Tyrendarra East (13 min), Heywood Library in Heywood (18 min), the playground at Apex Park in Heywood (18 min) and Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood (18 min).

Best for

Beach days at Fitzroy River Beach.

Nearby suburbs

Heywood, next to Tyrendarra, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Tyrendarra East, next to Tyrendarra, has a beach. Next door to Tyrendarra, Narrawong has 1 playground. Bessiebelle also borders Tyrendarra.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Tyrendarra, without traffic.
