Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Mena Park in the Pyrenees area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Mena Park has no train station; Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mena Park, so most trips start by car. By car from Mena Park, Ballarat is about 42 minutes away, Ararat about 51 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mena Park is Beaufort Pool in Beaufort, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest library to Mena Park is Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Mena Park is the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Mena Park is Showbiz Cinemas Ballarat in Smythes Creek, about 41 minutes' drive. Ballarat is the closest bigger centre to Mena Park, about 42 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Mena Park, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (16 min), the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (17 min), the playground at Wotherspoon Reserve in Beaufort (17 min), Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort (17 min), Beaufort Pool in Beaufort (17 min) and Linton BMX park in Linton (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mena Park.
