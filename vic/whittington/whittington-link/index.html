Whittington Link

Playground in Whittington · Basic info

Whittington Link in Whittington is a public playground in the Greater Geelong City area. The Bellarine Rail Trail is about 380 m away, and Splashdown is about 490 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 860 m from the playground, and bus 30 stops at Surveyor Ct/Solar Dr, a 4-minute walk away.
