Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Billabong in the Wellington area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 2 public toilets in Billabong. For walks and rides, Bicentennial National Trail, Dry River Track and Mount Darling Walking Track cross Billabong.

Getting around

Billabong has no train station; Stratford Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 80 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Billabong, so most trips start by car. By car from Billabong, Sale is about 3 hours away and Traralgon about 3 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Billabong on the Bicentennial National Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Crooked River and Arbuckle also border Billabong.
