Kyneton South is in the Macedon Ranges area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Kyneton South is Milkingyard Creek Streamside Reserve, covering about 2 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Kyneton South.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kyneton South. The closest to Kyneton South is Kyneton Railway Station (V/Line), about 3.0 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kyneton South, so most trips start by car. By car from Kyneton South, Kyneton is about 6 minutes away, Castlemaine about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 73 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kyneton South is Gisborne Aquatic Centre in Gisborne, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest library to Kyneton South is Kyneton Library in Kyneton, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Kyneton South is Canoeing in Tylden, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Kyneton South is Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton, about 4 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Reading Cinema in Sunbury is about 40 minutes' drive from Kyneton South. Kyneton is the closest bigger centre to Kyneton South, about 6 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Kyneton South are Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton (4 min), Canoeing in Tylden (7 min), Bolton Reserve Lane Playground in Kyneton (5 min), Kyneton Community Park in Kyneton (5 min), Kyneton Library in Kyneton (6 min) and Kyneton Museum in Kyneton (6 min).
