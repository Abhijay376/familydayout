Wannon in the Southern Grampians area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Wannon Falls Scenic Reserve is the largest mapped park in Wannon, at about 54 hectares. Wannon Flora Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 2 public toilets and 2 off-leash dog parks in Wannon. The Hamilton-Coleraine Rail Trail passes through Wannon.

Getting around

Wannon has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 97 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wannon, so most trips start by car. By car from Wannon, Hamilton is about 17 minutes away and Portland about 82 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Hamilton is about 17 minutes' drive from Wannon. For a library, Hamilton Library in Hamilton is about 17 minutes' drive from Wannon. For a skate park, coleraine skate park in Coleraine is about 13 minutes' drive from Wannon. For a cinema, Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton is about 17 minutes' drive from Wannon. Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Wannon, about 17 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Wannon, the nearest places for kids by road are coleraine skate park in Coleraine (13 min), the playground at Coleraine Railway Reserve in Coleraine (14 min), the playground at Queens Park in Coleraine (13 min), Big Woolbales & Campe Motor Museum in Hamilton (14 min), Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Hamilton (17 min) and Mud Gallery in Hamilton (17 min).

Best for

Free days on the Hamilton-Coleraine Rail Trail and in Wannon's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Coleraine, next to Wannon, has 4 playgrounds and a skate park. Gritjurk, Bulart and Bochara also border Wannon.
