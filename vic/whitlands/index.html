Whitlands is in the Wangaratta area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Whitlands is Powers Lookout Scenic Reserve, covering about 1,034 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Whitlands.

Getting around

Whitlands has no train station; Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 46 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Whitlands, so most trips start by car. By car from Whitlands, Mansfield is about 42 minutes away and Wangaratta about 51 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The nearest larger centre is Mansfield, about 42 minutes from Whitlands by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Whitlands are Myrrhee Recreation Reserve in Myrrhee (15 min) and the playground at Apex Park in Moyhu (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Whitlands.
