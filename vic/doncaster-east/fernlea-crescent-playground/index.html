Fernlea Crescent Playground

Playground in Doncaster East · Basic info

Fernlea Crescent Playground is a small local playground in Doncaster East, in the Manningham City area. The Green Gully Trail is about 350 m away, and Manna Gum Playspace is another option about 590 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus 905 stops at Fernlea Cres/Blackburn Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
