St Clems Road playground

Playground in Doncaster East · Basic info

St Clems Road playground in Doncaster East is a small local playground in the Manningham City area. The St Clems Trail is about 40 m away, and Argyle Street Playground is another option about 330 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 730 m from the playground, and bus 282 stops at Tunstall Rd/St Clems Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
