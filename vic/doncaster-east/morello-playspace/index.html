Morello Playspace

Playground in Doncaster East · Basic info

Morello Playspace is a small local playground in Doncaster East, in the Manningham City area. The Andersons creek road trail passes about 300 m away, and Pie Face is about 160 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 330 m away, and buses 905 and 908 stop at The Pines SC/Reynolds Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
