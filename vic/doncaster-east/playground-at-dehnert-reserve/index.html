Playground at Dehnert Reserve

Playground in Doncaster East · Basic info

Playground at Dehnert Reserve in Doncaster East is a small local playground in the Manningham City area. Schramms Cottage is about 520 m away, and Playground at Hertford Reserve is another option about 360 m away. There's a public toilet about 460 m away, and bus 305 stops at Worthing Ave/George St, a 1-minute walk away.
