Playground at Kerry Reserve

Playground in Doncaster East · Basic info

Playground at Kerry Reserve is a small local playground in Doncaster East, in the Manningham City area. For food, Chrysanthemum House is about 480 m away, and Playground at Jocelyn Reserve is another option about 310 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 630 m from the playground, and buses 279, 295 and 318 stop at Murphy Rd/Victoria St, a 3-minute walk away.
