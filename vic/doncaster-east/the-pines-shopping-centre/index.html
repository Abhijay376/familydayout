The Pines Shopping Centre

Shopping centre in Doncaster East · Official details

The Pines Shopping Centre is a shopping centre in Doncaster East, in the Manningham City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. The Pines SC/Reynolds Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 482 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: Monday - Wednesday: 9:00am - 5:30pm; Thursday: 9:00am - 7:00pm; Friday: 9:00am - 8:00pm; Saturday: 9:00am - 5:00pm; Sunday: 10:00am - 5:00pm.
