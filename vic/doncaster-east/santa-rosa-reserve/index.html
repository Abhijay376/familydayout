Santa Rosa Reserve

Park in Doncaster East · Basic info

Santa Rosa Reserve is a park in Doncaster East, in the Manningham City Council area, good for all ages. Reynolds Rd/Springvale Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 545 m away with a baby change table.
