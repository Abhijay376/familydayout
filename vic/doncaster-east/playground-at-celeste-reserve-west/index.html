Playground at Celeste Reserve West

Playground in Doncaster East · Basic info

This is a small local playground in Doncaster East, looked after by Manningham City Council. Royal Stacks is about 190 m away if you need food, and Playground at Jocelyn Reserve is another option about 150 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 510 m away, and buses 902 and 907 stop at Dehnert St/Doncaster Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
