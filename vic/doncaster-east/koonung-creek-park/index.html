Koonung Creek Park

Park in Doncaster East · Basic info

Koonung Creek Park is a park in Doncaster East, in the Manningham City Council area, good for all ages. Renshaw St/Wetherby Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1164 m away with a baby change table.
