Rieschiecks Reserve Playground

Playground in Doncaster East · Basic info

Rieschiecks Reserve Playground is a small local playground in Doncaster East, in the Manningham City area. The Ruffey Lake Circuit passes about 150 m away, and Schramms Cottage is about 60 m away. There's a public toilet about 390 m away, and buses 279, 295 and 318 stop at Owens St/Victoria St, a 2-minute walk away.
