Manna Gum Playspace

Playground in Doncaster East · Basic info

Manna Gum Playspace is one of the council's larger playgrounds in Doncaster East, in the Manningham City area. The Mullum Mullum Trail passes about 50 m away, and Playground on Bellbird Place is another option about 240 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and buses 318, 364 and 906 stop at Deep Creek Reserve/Heidelberg-Warrandyte Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
