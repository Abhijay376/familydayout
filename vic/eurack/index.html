Eurack is in the Colac Otway area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Eurack is Warracbarunah Lake Reserve, covering about 78 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Eurack. The closest to Eurack is Birregurra Railway Station (V/Line), about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Eurack, so most trips start by car. By car from Eurack, Colac is about 24 minutes away, Geelong about 58 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood is about 42 minutes' drive from Eurack. The closest library to Eurack is Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac, about 25 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt is about 29 minutes' drive from Eurack. For a cinema, Colac Cinemas in Colac is about 25 minutes' drive from Eurack. For an indoor play centre, Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac is about 24 minutes' drive from Eurack. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Eurack, about 24 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Eurack, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Cressy Picnic Area in Cressy (18 min), the playground at Red Rock Scenic Reserve in Alvie (28 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (24 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (25 min), Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (25 min) and the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (29 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Eurack.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Eurack, without traffic.
