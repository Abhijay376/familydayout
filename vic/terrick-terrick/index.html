Terrick Terrick is in the Loddon area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Terrick Terrick Reference Area is the largest mapped park in Terrick Terrick, at about 142 hectares. Terrick Terrick North Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Terrick Terrick.

Getting around

Terrick Terrick has no train station; Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Terrick Terrick, so most trips start by car. By car from Terrick Terrick, Echuca is about 56 minutes away and Bendigo about 84 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo is about 29 minutes' drive from Terrick Terrick. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 56 minutes from Terrick Terrick by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Terrick Terrick, the nearest places for kids by road are Pyramid Hill & Districts Historical Museum in Pyramid Hill (29 min), Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo (29 min), the playground at R J Phelan Reserve in Mitiamo (29 min), East Loddon Historical Society in Mitiamo (29 min), Leitchville Memorial Swimming Pool in Leitchville (37 min) and Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna (46 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Terrick Terrick.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Terrick Terrick, Mitiamo has 1 playground and a pool. Next door to Terrick Terrick, Pyramid Hill has 1 playground. Mologa and Jungaburra also border Terrick Terrick.
