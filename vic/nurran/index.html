Our data lists 5 parks and reserves in Nurran, in the East Gippsland area.

Landmarks

Martins Creek Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Nurran, at about 6,548 hectares. Thirty-Two Mile Nature Conservation Reserve and Sardine Creek Education Area are the next largest. Nurran also has Martins Creek Scenic Reserve and Rich River Timber Workers Hut. Picnic areas in Nurran include Rich River Timber Workers Hut.

Getting around

Nurran has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 88 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nurran, so most trips start by car. By car from Nurran, Lakes Entrance is about 83 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Nurran is Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest library to Nurran is Orbost Library in Orbost, about 35 minutes' drive. From Nurran, the nearest places for kids by road are Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost (35 min), Orbost Library in Orbost (35 min) and the playground at Forest Park in Orbost (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Nurran.

Nearby suburbs

Orbost, next to Nurran, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Cabbage Tree Creek and Brodribb River also border Nurran.
