Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside King Valley in the Wangaratta area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

King Valley has no train station; Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 38 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the King Valley-Wangaratta route stop in King Valley. By car from King Valley, Wangaratta is about 36 minutes away and Benalla about 53 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to King Valley is Splashpark in Wangaratta, about 32 minutes' drive. For a library, Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta is about 35 minutes' drive from King Valley. For a skate park, the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta is about 32 minutes' drive from King Valley. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 35 minutes' drive from King Valley. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to King Valley, about 36 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From King Valley, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Apex Park in Moyhu (11 min), Myrrhee Recreation Reserve in Myrrhee (21 min), Splashpark in Wangaratta (32 min), the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta (32 min), Glenrowan Museum in Glenrowan (32 min) and Cobb & Co in Glenrowan (32 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from King Valley.

Nearby suburbs
