Brandy Creek is in the Baw Baw area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Brandy Creek, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Brandy Creek has no train station; Warragul Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 7.4 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Brandy Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Brandy Creek, Warragul is about 7 minutes away, Pakenham about 37 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 80 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul is about 10 minutes' drive from Brandy Creek. The closest library to Brandy Creek is Warragul Library in Warragul, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Brandy Creek is Bmx in Warragul, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Brandy Creek is Warragul Cinema in Warragul, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Brandy Creek is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Brandy Creek is Wildlife Trail in Tynong North, about 23 minutes' drive. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Brandy Creek, about 7 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Brandy Creek are the playground at Ellen Close Linear Reserve in Warragul (6 min), Charles Street Park in Warragul (7 min), Warragul Library in Warragul (8 min), Warragul Cinema in Warragul (8 min), The Alley Warragul in Warragul (8 min) and Bmx in Warragul (9 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Brandy Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Brandy Creek, Warragul has 22 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Bravington also borders Brandy Creek.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Brandy Creek, without traffic.
