Bonang is in the East Gippsland area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Concordia Gully Reference Area is the largest mapped park in Bonang, at about 883 hectares. Bonanza Gully Historic Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Bonang.

Getting around

Bonang has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 120 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bonang, so most trips start by car. By car from Bonang, Lakes Entrance is about 2 hours away and Bairnsdale about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bonang.

Nearby suburbs

Deddick Valley and Bendoc also border Bonang.
