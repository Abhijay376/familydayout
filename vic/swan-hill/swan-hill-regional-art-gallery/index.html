Swan Hill Regional Art Gallery

Museum or gallery in Swan Hill · Official details

Swan Hill Regional Art Gallery is a museum or gallery in Swan Hill, in the Swan Hill Rural City Council area, best for school-age kids. Art Gallery Pioneer Settlement/Marraboor St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 470 m away. Opening hours: Monday to Friday 10.00am - 5.00pm; Saturday and Sunday 10.00am - 4.00pm.
