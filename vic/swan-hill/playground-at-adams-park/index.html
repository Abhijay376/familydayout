Playground at Adams Park

Playground in Swan Hill · Basic info

Playground at Adams Park is a public playground in Swan Hill, in the Swan Hill Rural City area. Playground on Curlewis Street is another option about 240 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 660 m from the playground, and bus 1 stops at Melis Ct/Adams Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
