Swan Hill Regional Library

Library in Swan Hill · Basic info

Swan Hill Regional Library is a library in Swan Hill, in the Swan Hill Rural City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Swan Hill Regional Library/Campbell St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 27 m away. Opening hours: opening_hours: Mo-Sa 10:00-17:30.
