Slater Park

Park in Swan Hill · Basic info

Slater Park is a park in Swan Hill, in the Swan Hill Rural City Council area, good for all ages. White St/Stradbroke Ave (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 330 m away.
