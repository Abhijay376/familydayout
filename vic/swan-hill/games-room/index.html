Games Room

Playground in Swan Hill · Basic info

Games Room in Swan Hill is a public playground in the Swan Hill Rural City area. Older kids have a skate park about 180 m away, and Water play at Riverside Park is about 260 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 490 m away. It's easiest by car, about 4 minutes from Swan Hill.
