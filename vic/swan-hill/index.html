Our data lists 9 playgrounds and 20 parks and reserves in Swan Hill, in the Swan Hill area. Beyond the parks, Swan Hill has a library, 3 pools, a museum or gallery and a cinema.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Swan Hill is Barry Steggall Park, covering about 17 hectares. Riverside Park and Palaroo Park are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Swan Hill include Apex Park, Adams Park and Jaycee Park. Playgrounds around Swan Hill include Games Room, the playground at Adams Park and the playground at Jaycee Park. Our data shows 17 public toilets in Swan Hill. Places to visit in Swan Hill include Swan Hill Regional Art Gallery, Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill, Swan Hill Outdoor Pool, Swan Hill Waterslide and Swan Hill Regional Library. For older kids in Swan Hill there's the skate park at James Belsar Reserve and the skate park at Riverside Park. Our data lists 13 cafes and family restaurants in Swan Hill for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line: Swan Hill - Melbourne via Bendigo) in Swan Hill. Buses on the 1, 2, tv and South - Schools AM routes stop in Swan Hill. V/Line coaches stop in Swan Hill. By car from Swan Hill, Echuca is about 2 hours away and Bendigo about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 9 playgrounds across Swan Hill. Rainy days at the library, cinema and museum or gallery in Swan Hill. Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at James Belsar Reserve in Swan Hill.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Swan Hill, Woorinen South has a skate park. Murraydale, Murrawee, Goschen and Castle Donnington also border Swan Hill.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Swan Hill, without traffic.
