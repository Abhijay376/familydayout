Playground on Monash Drive

Playground in Swan Hill · Basic info

This is a public playground in Swan Hill. Older kids have a skate park about 180 m away, and Water play at Riverside Park is about 260 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 510 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 4 minutes from Swan Hill.
