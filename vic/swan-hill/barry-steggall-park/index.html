Barry Steggall Park

Park in Swan Hill · Basic info

Barry Steggall Park is a park in Swan Hill, in the Swan Hill Rural City Council area, good for all ages. Swan Hill TAFE/Parkside Ave (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 387 m away with a baby change table.
