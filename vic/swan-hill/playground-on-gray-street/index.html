Playground on Gray Street

Playground in Swan Hill · Basic info

Playground on Gray Street is a public playground in Swan Hill, in the Swan Hill Rural City area. Playground at Jaycee Park is another option about 530 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.1 km from the playground, and buses 2 and South - Schools AM stop at Swan Hill Primary School/Yana St, a 2-minute walk away.
