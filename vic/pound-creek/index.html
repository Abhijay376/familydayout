Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Pound Creek in the South Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Pound Creek. The closest to Pound Creek is Warragul Railway Station (V/Line), about 52 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Wonthaggi - Leongatha via Inverloch route stop in Pound Creek. By car from Pound Creek, Leongatha is about 17 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 18 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Wonthaggi is about 17 minutes' drive from Pound Creek. For a library, Wonthaggi Principal Library in Wonthaggi is about 17 minutes' drive from Pound Creek. For a beach, The Caves in Inverloch is about 13 minutes' drive from Pound Creek. For a skate park, the skate park at Venus Bay Community Centre Reserve in Venus Bay is about 17 minutes' drive from Pound Creek. For a cinema, Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi is about 17 minutes' drive from Pound Creek. For a zoo or wildlife park, Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville is about 42 minutes' drive from Pound Creek. The nearest larger centre is Leongatha, about 17 minutes from Pound Creek by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Pound Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Wallace Park in Inverloch (7 min), Bunurong Environment Centre & Shell Museum in Inverloch (7 min), the playground at Inverloch Coastal Reserve in Inverloch (7 min), The Caves in Inverloch (13 min), Eagles Nest in Inverloch (15 min) and the skate park at Venus Bay Community Centre Reserve in Venus Bay (17 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Pound Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Pound Creek, Inverloch has 4 playgrounds and a beach. Tarwin Lower, next to Pound Creek, has 1 playground and a beach.
