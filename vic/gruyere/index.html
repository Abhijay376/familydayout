Gruyere is in the Yarra Ranges area, and our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Warramate Hills Nature Conservation Area is the largest mapped park in Gruyere, at about 498 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Gruyere. The Lilydale-Warburton Rail Trail and Warburton Rail Trail all pass through Gruyere.

Getting around

There's no train station in Gruyere. The closest to Gruyere is Lilydale Railway Station (metro), about 9.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gruyere, so most trips start by car. By car from Gruyere, Lilydale is about 13 minutes away, Healesville about 24 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 54 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Seville Water Play Park in Seville is about 9 minutes' drive from Gruyere. The closest library to Gruyere is Mount Evelyn Reading Room in Mount Evelyn, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Gruyere is Lilydale Skate Bowl in Lilydale, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Gruyere is Reading Cinemas in Chirnside Park, about 17 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, GAMR Laser Tag & VR in Chirnside Park is about 17 minutes' drive from Gruyere. For a zoo or wildlife park, Lyre Bird enclosure in Badger Creek is about 24 minutes' drive from Gruyere. The nearest larger centre is Lilydale, about 13 minutes from Gruyere by car, with 12 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Gruyere, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Koonaburra Reserve in Wandin North (8 min), the playground at Greenglades Court Reserve in Wandin North (7 min), Seville Water Play Park in Seville (9 min), Mont De Lancey Museum in Wandin North (10 min), Yarra Ranges Regional Museum in Lilydale (12 min) and Lilydale Skate Bowl in Lilydale (13 min).

Best for

Free days on the Lilydale-Warburton Rail Trail and in Gruyere's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Healesville, next to Gruyere, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. Seville, next to Gruyere, has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Wandin North, next to Gruyere, has 4 playgrounds. Woori Yallock, next to Gruyere, has 1 playground. Seville East and Coldstream also border Gruyere.
