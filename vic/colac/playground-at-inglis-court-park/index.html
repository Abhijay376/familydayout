Playground at Inglis Court Park

Playground in Colac · Basic info

This is a public playground in Colac. The MBN SW3 is about 90 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 200 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 580 m from the playground, and bus 1 stops at Colac Cemetery/Hearn St, a 3-minute walk away.
