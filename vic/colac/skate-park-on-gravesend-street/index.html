Skate park on Gravesend Street

Skate, BMX or pump track in Colac · Basic info

Skate park on Gravesend Street is a skate, bmx or pump track in Colac, in the Colac Otway Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Central Reserve/Gravesend St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 145 m away.
