Colac Plaza

Shopping centre in Colac · Official details

Colac Plaza is a shopping centre in Colac, in the Colac Otway Shire Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Aldi/Hesse St (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 33 m away. Opening hours: Monday – Friday 7.00am – 9.00pm; Monday – Friday 7.00am – 6.00pm; EXCEPTIONS – GOOD FRIDAY, ANZAC DAY (9am - 1pm) AND CHRISTMAS DAY – CLOSED.
