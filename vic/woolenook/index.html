Woolenook is in the Wellington area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Woolenook, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Woolenook has no train station; Stratford Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Woolenook, so most trips start by car. By car from Woolenook, Sale is about 70 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Woolenook, the nearest places for kids by road are Anzac Park Playground in Briagolong (40 min), Boisdale Recreation Reserve Playground in Boisdale (51 min), Skate Park in Stratford (57 min), Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford (57 min), Former Drive-in site in Sale (68 min) and Sale Historical Museum in Sale (71 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Woolenook.

Nearby suburbs

Briagolong, next to Woolenook, has 2 playgrounds. Koorool also borders Woolenook.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Woolenook, without traffic.
