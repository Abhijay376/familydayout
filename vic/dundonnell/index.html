Dundonnell in the Moyne area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Dundonnell is Mount Fyans Wildlife Reserve (no hunting), covering about 45 hectares. Dundonnell Recreation Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Dundonnell. The closest to Dundonnell is Terang Railway Station (V/Line), about 40 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dundonnell, so most trips start by car. By car from Dundonnell, Ararat is about 80 minutes away, Warrnambool about 82 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum is about 38 minutes' drive from Dundonnell. The closest skate park to Dundonnell is Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake, about 41 minutes' drive. From Dundonnell, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Simpson Park in Streatham (35 min), Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum (38 min), Market Square Playground in Mortlake (40 min), Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake (41 min), Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake (41 min) and Lismore Pool in Lismore (48 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Dundonnell.

Nearby suburbs

Mortlake, next to Dundonnell, has 2 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Dundonnell, Lake Bolac has 1 playground and a library. Nerrin Nerrin and Darlington also border Dundonnell.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Dundonnell, without traffic.
