Weeaproinah is in the Colac Otway area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Weeaproinah, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Weeaproinah. The closest to Weeaproinah is Colac Railway Station (V/Line), about 33 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Weeaproinah, so most trips start by car. By car from Weeaproinah, Colac is about 42 minutes away, Geelong about 2 hours and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Weeaproinah is Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac, about 41 minutes' drive. For a beach, Wreck Beach in Gellibrand Lower is about 41 minutes' drive from Weeaproinah. The closest skate park to Weeaproinah is the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt, about 39 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Weeaproinah is Colac Cinemas in Colac, about 42 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac is about 43 minutes' drive from Weeaproinah. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 42 minutes from Weeaproinah by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Weeaproinah, the nearest places for kids by road are Art Reach Studio in Beech Forest (7 min), the playground at John H Gardner Reserve in Beech Forest (7 min), the playground at Rex Norman Park in Gellibrand (21 min), Wreck Beach in Gellibrand Lower (41 min), Station Beach in Hordern Vale (39 min) and the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (39 min).
