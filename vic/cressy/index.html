Cressy in the Colac Otway area has 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Cressy is Cressy Flora Reserve, covering about 3 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Cressy include Cressy Picnic Area. Playgrounds in Cressy include the playground at Cressy Picnic Area. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Cressy.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cressy. The closest to Cressy is Colac Railway Station (V/Line), about 35 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cressy, so most trips start by car. By car from Cressy, Colac is about 32 minutes away, Geelong about 58 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood is about 23 minutes' drive from Cressy. The closest library to Cressy is Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac, about 33 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Rokewood Skate Park in Rokewood is about 24 minutes' drive from Cressy. The closest cinema to Cressy is Colac Cinemas in Colac, about 33 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac is about 32 minutes' drive from Cressy. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Cressy, about 32 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Cressy, the nearest places for kids by road are Rokewood Recreation Reserve Playground in Rokewood (24 min), Rokewood Skate Park in Rokewood (24 min), Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood (23 min), Lismore Pool in Lismore (23 min), Dereel Skate Park in Dereel (31 min) and Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (32 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Cressy.

Nearby suburbs

Rokewood, next to Cressy, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Cundare North, Barunah Plains and Barunah Park also border Cressy.
