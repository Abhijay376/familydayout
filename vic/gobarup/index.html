Gobarup is in the Campaspe area, and our data lists 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Gobarup Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Gobarup, at about 308 hectares. Gobarup H108 Bushland Reserve and Gobarup H110 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Gobarup also has Gobarup H109 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Gobarup. The closest to Gobarup is Elmore Railway Station (V/Line), about 30 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gobarup, so most trips start by car. By car from Gobarup, Bendigo is about 72 minutes away, Echuca about 73 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Gobarup is Rushworth Pool in Rushworth, about 43 minutes' drive. For a library, Rushworth Library in Rushworth is about 43 minutes' drive from Gobarup. For a skate park, the skate park at Lions Park in Rushworth is about 43 minutes' drive from Gobarup. The closest places for kids by road from Gobarup are Rushworth Museum in Rushworth (42 min), Rushworth Library in Rushworth (43 min), Rushworth Pool in Rushworth (43 min), the skate park at Lions Park in Rushworth (43 min), Heathcote Play Space in Heathcote (36 min) and Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote (37 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Gobarup.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Gobarup, Rushworth has a pool, a library and a skate park. Next door to Gobarup, Colbinabbin has 1 playground. Moora and Cornella also border Gobarup.
