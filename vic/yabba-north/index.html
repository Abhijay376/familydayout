Yabba North is in the Moira area, and our data lists 5 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Yabba North is Dookie - Katamatite rail line Bushland Reserve, covering about 71 hectares. Yabba Yabba H39 Bushland Reserve and Yabba Yabba H35 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Yabba North include Yabba Yabba H34 Bushland Reserve and Yabba Yabba H36 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Yabba North. The closest to Yabba North is Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line), about 32 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yabba North, so most trips start by car. By car from Yabba North, Shepparton is about 47 minutes away and Benalla about 51 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest beach to Yabba North is Fosters Beach in Cobram, about 45 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Shepparton Velodrome in Shepparton is about 44 minutes' drive from Yabba North. The closest cinema to Yabba North is Cobram Cinema in Cobram, about 44 minutes' drive. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Yabba North, about 47 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Yabba North, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at CWA Gardens in Dookie (21 min), the playground at Katandra West Recreation Reserve in Katandra West (22 min), Katamatite Historical Society Museum in Katamatite (27 min), Shepparton Velodrome in Shepparton (44 min), BMX track in Shepparton (45 min) and Shepparton Library in Shepparton (46 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Yabba North.
