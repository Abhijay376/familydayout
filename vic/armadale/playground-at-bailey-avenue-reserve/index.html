Playground at Bailey Avenue Reserve

Playground in Armadale · Basic info

This is a public playground in Armadale. Bamboo is about 460 m away if you need food, and Playground at Inverness Avenue Park is another option about 290 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 320 m away, and tram 5 stops at Duncraig Ave/Wattletree Rd #44, a 2-minute walk away.
