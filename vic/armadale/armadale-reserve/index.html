Armadale Reserve

Park in Armadale · Basic info

Armadale Reserve is a park in Armadale, in the Stonnington City Council area, good for all ages. Armadale St/Wattletree Rd #41 (tram) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 434 m away.
