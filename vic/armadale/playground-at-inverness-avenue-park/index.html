Playground at Inverness Avenue Park

Playground in Armadale · Basic info

Playground at Inverness Avenue Park is a public playground in Armadale, in the Stonnington City area. Fancy Pantry is about 480 m away if you need food, and Playground at Bailey Avenue Reserve is another option about 290 m away. There's a public toilet about 250 m away, and tram 5 stops at Egerton Rd/Wattletree Rd #43, a 3-minute walk away.
