Armadale in the Stonnington area has 4 playgrounds and 6 parks and reserves in our data. Beyond the parks, Armadale has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Armadale is Toorak Park, covering about 3 hectares. Union Street Gardens and Victory Square are the next largest. Armadale also has Armadale Reserve, Bailey Avenue Reserve and Inverness Avenue Park. Playgrounds around Armadale include the playground at Victory Square, the playground at Armadale Reserve and the playground at Bailey Avenue Reserve. Our data shows 7 public toilets in Armadale. Places to visit in Armadale include Scott Livesy. Malvern Central Shopping Centre is the local shopping centre. Our data lists 6 cafes and family restaurants in Armadale for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Armadale has train stations: Armadale Railway Station (metro: Frankston Line) and Toorak Railway Station (metro: Frankston Line). Trams 5, 6, 16, 64 and 72 stop in Armadale. Buses 604 and 605 stop in Armadale. By car from Armadale, Box Hill is about 20 minutes away, Dandenong about 25 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 11 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Prahran Aquatic Centre in Prahran is about 5 minutes' drive from Armadale. For a library, Malvern Library in Malvern is about 3 minutes' drive from Armadale. For a beach, St Kilda Beach in St Kilda is about 8 minutes' drive from Armadale. For a skate park, the pump track at Windsor Siding in Windsor is about 6 minutes' drive from Armadale. The closest cinema to Armadale is The Astor Theatre in St Kilda, about 5 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Akira’s Arcade in Prahran is about 5 minutes' drive from Armadale. For a zoo or wildlife park, Luna Park in St Kilda is about 8 minutes' drive from Armadale. Box Hill is the closest bigger centre to Armadale, about 20 minutes away by car, and has 11 playgrounds, a pool and a library.

Best for

Younger kids at Armadale's 4 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Armadale.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Armadale, Prahran has 7 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Malvern, next to Armadale, has 3 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Armadale, Caulfield North has 4 playgrounds. St Kilda East, next to Armadale, has 3 playgrounds. Toorak also borders Armadale.
