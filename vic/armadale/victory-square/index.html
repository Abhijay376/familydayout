Victory Square

Park in Armadale · Basic info

Victory Square is a park in Armadale, in the Stonnington City Council area, good for all ages. Sydney St/Orrong Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 286 m away with a baby change table.
