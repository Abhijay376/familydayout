Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Waterholes in the East Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Waterholes. The closest to Waterholes is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Waterholes, so most trips start by car. By car from Waterholes, Bairnsdale is about 52 minutes away and Lakes Entrance about 79 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

Bairnsdale is the closest bigger centre to Waterholes, about 52 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Waterholes, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Howitt Park in Wy Yung (48 min), Howitt Park in Wy Yung (49 min), Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale (49 min), Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale (50 min), Krowathunkoolong Keeping Place in Bairnsdale (50 min) and Bairnsdale Museum in Bairnsdale (51 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Waterholes.
