Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Byaduk, in the Southern Grampians area.

Landmarks

Byaduk Recreation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Byaduk, at about 6 hectares. Byaduk Oval is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet and 2 off-leash dog parks in Byaduk.

Getting around

Byaduk has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 66 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Byaduk. By car from Byaduk, Hamilton is about 21 minutes away and Portland about 57 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Byaduk is Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Hamilton, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest library to Byaduk is Hamilton Library in Hamilton, about 21 minutes' drive. For a beach, Eumeralla in Tyrendarra East is about 43 minutes' drive from Byaduk. The closest skate park to Byaduk is hamilton skate park in Hamilton, about 23 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton is about 21 minutes' drive from Byaduk. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 21 minutes from Byaduk by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Byaduk are Branxholme Rec Reserve Playground in Branxholme (26 min), Hughan Park Playground in Hamilton (20 min), Hamilton History Centre in Hamilton (20 min), Mud Gallery in Hamilton (21 min), Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton (21 min) and Hamilton Library in Hamilton (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Byaduk.

Nearby suburbs

Branxholme, next to Byaduk, has 1 playground. Macarthur, Knebsworth, Gazette and Byaduk North also border Byaduk.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Byaduk, without traffic.
