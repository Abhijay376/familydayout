Byaduk Recreation Reserve

Park in Byaduk · Official details

Byaduk Recreation Reserve is a park in Byaduk, in the Southern Grampians Shire Council area, good for all ages. Pearses Rd/Hamilton - Port Fairy Rd (coach) is a 8-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 11 m away and dogs are allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Byaduk Recreation Reserve?
Byaduk Recreation Reserve is in Byaduk, Victoria.
Is Byaduk Recreation Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Byaduk Recreation Reserve?
Yes, dogs are allowed.
Are there toilets at Byaduk Recreation Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 11 m away.
How do I get to Byaduk Recreation Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Pearses Rd/Hamilton - Port Fairy Rd (routes Warrnambool - Melbourne via Ararat & Hamilton), a 8-minute walk.
