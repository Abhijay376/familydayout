Playground at Allnutt Park

Playground in Bentleigh · Official details

Playground at Allnutt Park is a public playground in Bentleigh, in the Glen Eira City area. The Elster Creek Trail passes about 20 m away, and Swaad India's Zest is about 360 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 703 stops at Campbell St/Centre Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
