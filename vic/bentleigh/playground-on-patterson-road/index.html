Playground on Patterson Road

Playground in Bentleigh · Basic info

Playground on Patterson Road is a public playground in Bentleigh, in the Glen Eira City area. The Nepean Highway Trail is about 310 m away, and for food, Caffe Crema is about 380 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus 823 stops at Vunabere Ave/Nepean Hwy, a 6-minute walk away.
