Leckie Street Reserve

Park in Bentleigh · Official details

Leckie Street Reserve is a park in Bentleigh, in the Glen Eira City Council area, good for all ages. Greek Orthodox Church/Centre Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 546 m away with a baby change table.

Where is Leckie Street Reserve?
Leckie Street Reserve is in Bentleigh, Victoria.

Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.

How do I get to Leckie Street Reserve by public transport?
