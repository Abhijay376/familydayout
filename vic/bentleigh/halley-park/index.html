Halley Park

Park in Bentleigh · Official details

Halley Park is a park in Bentleigh, in the Glen Eira City Council area, good for all ages. Halley Park/Jasper Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 73 m away and dogs are not allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Halley Park?
Halley Park is in Bentleigh, Victoria.
Is Halley Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Halley Park?
No, dogs are not allowed.
Are there toilets at Halley Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 73 m away.
How do I get to Halley Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Halley Park/Jasper Rd (routes 627), a 2-minute walk.
