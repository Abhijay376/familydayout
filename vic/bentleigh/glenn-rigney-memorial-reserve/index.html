Glenn Rigney Memorial Reserve

Park in Bentleigh · Basic info

Glenn Rigney Memorial Reserve is a park in Bentleigh, in the Glen Eira City Council area, good for all ages. Brewer Rd/Nepean Hwy (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 517 m away with a baby change table.
