Playground at Leckie Street Reserve

Playground in Bentleigh · Basic info

This is a public playground in Bentleigh. For food, Seven Stars is about 350 m away, and Playground at Hodgson Reserve is another option about 340 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 540 m from the playground, and bus 703 stops at Greek Orthodox Church/Centre Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
