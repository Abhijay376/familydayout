Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Appin South in the Loddon area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Appin South has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Appin South, so most trips start by car. By car from Appin South, Swan Hill is about 70 minutes away and Bendigo about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Appin South is Swimming Pool in Kerang, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest library to Appin South is Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Appin South are the playground at Lake Meran W.R in Lake Meran (25 min), Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (23 min), Swimming Pool in Kerang (23 min), A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park in Boort (36 min), Boort Court House Historical Museum in Boort (36 min) and Boort Swimming Pool in Boort (37 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Appin South.

Nearby suburbs

Lake Meran, next to Appin South, has 1 playground. Canary Island and Appin also border Appin South.
