Playground on Matheson Road

Playground in Forest Hill · Basic info

Playground on Matheson Road is a public playground in Forest Hill, in the Whitehorse City area. The Healesville Freeway Reserve path (under construction) is about 200 m away, and for food, NishikiAN is about 220 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 390 m from the playground, and bus 902 stops at Parkmore Rd/Springvale Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
