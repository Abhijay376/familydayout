Forest Hill Reserve

Park in Forest Hill · Basic info

Forest Hill Reserve is a park in Forest Hill, in the Whitehorse City Council area, good for all ages. Canterbury Rd/Springvale Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 776 m away.
