Zone Bowling

Indoor play in Forest Hill · Official details

Zone Bowling is a indoor play in Forest Hill, in the Whitehorse City Council area, best for younger kids (check the venue's age and height rules). Pacific Way/Mahoneys Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 544 m away with a baby change table.
