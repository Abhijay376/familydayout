Hoyts

Cinema in Forest Hill · Basic info

Hoyts is a cinema in Forest Hill, in the Whitehorse City Council area, good for school-age kids and teens. Forest Hill SC/Canterbury Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 6 m away with a baby change table.
