Playground at Jean-Lake Reserve

Playground in Forest Hill · Basic info

Playground at Jean-Lake Reserve is a public playground in Forest Hill, in the Whitehorse City area. The Whitehorse NS6 passes about 150 m away, and Zone Bowling is about 570 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 780 m away, and bus 736 stops at Ottawa Ave/Forest Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
