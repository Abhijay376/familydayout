Playground on Stevens Road

Playground in Forest Hill · Basic info

Playground on Stevens Road in Forest Hill is a public playground in the Whitehorse City area. For food, Shangri-La Inn is about 100 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus 765 stops at Brentford Square SC/Stevens Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
