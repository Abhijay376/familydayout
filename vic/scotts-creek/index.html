Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Scotts Creek in the Corangamite area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Scotts Creek.

Getting around

There's no train station in Scotts Creek. The closest to Scotts Creek is Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line), about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Camperdown - Timboon via Cobden route stop in Scotts Creek. By car from Scotts Creek, Warrnambool is about 52 minutes away, Colac about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon is about 8 minutes' drive from Scotts Creek. For a beach, Rutledge Creek Beach in Port Campbell is about 20 minutes' drive from Scotts Creek. The closest skate park to Scotts Creek is the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon, about 7 minutes' drive. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Scotts Creek, about 52 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Scotts Creek are the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (7 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (8 min), Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell (16 min), Rutledge Creek Beach in Port Campbell (20 min), Sherbrooke Estuary in Port Campbell (21 min) and the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve in Camperdown (32 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Scotts Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Timboon, next to Scotts Creek, has a pool and a skate park. Cowleys Creek and Cooriemungle also border Scotts Creek.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Scotts Creek, without traffic.
