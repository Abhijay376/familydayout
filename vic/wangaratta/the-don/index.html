The Don

Cafe or restaurant in Wangaratta · Basic info

The Don is a italian cafe or restaurant in Wangaratta, in the Wangaratta Rural City Council area, a place to eat during a day out. Wangaratta Museum/Ford St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 110 m away with a baby change table.
