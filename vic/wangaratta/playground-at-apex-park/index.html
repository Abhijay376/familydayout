Playground at Apex Park

Playground in Wangaratta · Basic info

Playground at Apex Park is a public playground in Wangaratta, in the Wangaratta Rural City area. The Wangaratta - Eldorado Loop passes about 90 m away, and Salvatore’s Pizzeria is about 280 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 110 m away, and coaches stop at Wangaratta Coach Stop/Rowan St , a 8-minute walk away.
