Batchelors Green

Park in Wangaratta · Basic info

Batchelors Green is a park in Wangaratta, in the Wangaratta Rural City Council area, good for all ages. Wangaratta Police Station/Handley St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 12 m away.

Where is Batchelors Green?
