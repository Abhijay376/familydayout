Wangaratta is in the Wangaratta area, and our data lists 9 playgrounds and 22 parks and reserves in the suburb. Wangaratta also has a library, 3 pools and a cinema.

Landmarks

Wangaratta Common Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Wangaratta, at about 77 hectares. Targoora Park and Horseshoe Lagoon Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Wangaratta also has Apex Park, Appin Park and Wonga Park. Playgrounds in Wangaratta include the playground at Apex Park, Donald Street Playground and Brien Crescent Playground. Our data shows 28 public toilets in Wangaratta. In Wangaratta you'll also find Wangaratta Cinema Centre, Moore than Swimming, Splashpark and Wangaratta Library. The Wangaratta - Eldorado Loop, Murray to Mountains Rail Trail: Wangaratta – Bright and Murray to Mountains Rail Trail: Wangaratta – Milawa all pass through Wangaratta. Our data lists 13 cafes and family restaurants in Wangaratta for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Wangaratta has a train station: Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line: Albury - Melbourne via Seymour). Buses on the 401, 402, 403 and 404 routes stop in Wangaratta. V/Line coaches stop in Wangaratta. By car from Wangaratta, Benalla is about 35 minutes away and Wodonga about 55 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a beach, Sandy Beach in Beechworth is about 35 minutes' drive from Wangaratta. The closest skate park to Wangaratta is the pump track on Alma Road in Beechworth, about 37 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 9 playgrounds across Wangaratta. Rainy days at the library and cinema in Wangaratta. Free days on the Wangaratta - Eldorado Loop and in Wangaratta's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Oxley, North Wangaratta, Laceby and East Wangaratta also border Wangaratta.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Wangaratta, without traffic.
