Playground on Mitchell Avenue

Playground in Wangaratta · Basic info

Playground on Mitchell Avenue is a public playground in Wangaratta, in the Wangaratta Rural City area. The One Mile Creek Path is about 70 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 140 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 170 m from the playground, and bus 402 stops at Hunt St/Brash Ave, a 8-minute walk away.
