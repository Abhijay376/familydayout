Reynard is in the Wellington area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Macalister Area Remote and Natural Area is the largest mapped park in Reynard, at about 33,493 hectares. East Caledonia Reference Area is the next largest. For walks and rides, Australian Alps Walking Track and Mount Reynard Walking Track cross Reynard.

Getting around

There's no train station in Reynard. The closest to Reynard is Stratford Railway Station (V/Line), about 80 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Reynard, so most trips start by car. By car from Reynard, Sale is about 2.5 hours away and Traralgon about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Free days on the Australian Alps Walking Track and in Reynard's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Reynard, Mount Buller has a cinema. Crookayan, Billabong and Arbuckle also border Reynard.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Reynard, without traffic.
