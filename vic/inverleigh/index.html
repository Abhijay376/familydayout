Inverleigh is in the Golden Plains area, and our data lists 4 playgrounds and 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Inverleigh is Inverleigh Flora Reserve, covering about 1,050 hectares. Lawsons Park is the next largest. Playgrounds around Inverleigh include the playground at Lawsons Park, Inverleigh Tennis Reserve Playground and Inverleigh Sporting Complex Playground. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Inverleigh.

Getting around

There's no train station in Inverleigh. The closest to Inverleigh is Winchelsea Railway Station (V/Line), about 17 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Geelong - Inverleigh via Fyansford route stop in Inverleigh. By car from Inverleigh, Geelong is about 29 minutes away, Torquay about 42 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 80 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Geelong Aquatic Centre in Newtown is about 26 minutes' drive from Inverleigh. For a library, Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn is about 17 minutes' drive from Inverleigh. The closest beach to Inverleigh is Eastern Beach in Geelong, about 29 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Bannockburn Skate Park in Bannockburn is about 19 minutes' drive from Inverleigh. The closest cinema to Inverleigh is Village Cinemas in Geelong, about 27 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Laserquest in Geelong is about 28 minutes' drive from Inverleigh. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Inverleigh is Adventure Park in Wallington, about 44 minutes' drive. Geelong is the closest bigger centre to Inverleigh, about 29 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Inverleigh, Bannockburn has 6 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Teesdale, next to Inverleigh, has 4 playgrounds and a skate park. Winchelsea, next to Inverleigh, has 1 playground. Next door to Inverleigh, Shelford has 1 playground. Murgheboluc, Ombersley, Hesse and Gnarwarre also border Inverleigh.
