Playground at Lawsons Park

Playground in Inverleigh · Basic info

This is a public playground in Inverleigh. Older kids have a skate park about 120 m away, and Playground on Hamilton Highway is another option about 110 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 140 m from the playground, and bus Geelong - Inverleigh via Fyansford stops at Inverleigh Public Hall/High St, a 10-minute walk away.
