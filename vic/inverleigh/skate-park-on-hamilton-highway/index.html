Skate park on Hamilton Highway

Skate, BMX or pump track in Inverleigh · Basic info

Skate park on Hamilton Highway is a skate, bmx or pump track in Inverleigh, in the Golden Plains Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Inverleigh Public Hall/High St (bus) is a 10-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 73 m away with a baby change table.
