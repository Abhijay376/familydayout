Playground on Hamilton Highway

Playground in Inverleigh · Basic info

Playground on Hamilton Highway is a public playground in Inverleigh, in the Golden Plains Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 30 m away, and Playground at Lawsons Park is another option about 110 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus Geelong - Inverleigh via Fyansford stops at Inverleigh Public Hall/High St, a 9-minute walk away.
