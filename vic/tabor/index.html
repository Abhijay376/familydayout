Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tabor is Tabor (Buckleys) Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 60 hectares. Our data shows 1 off-leash dog park in Tabor.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tabor. The closest to Tabor is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 66 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tabor, so most trips start by car. By car from Tabor, Hamilton is about 19 minutes away and Warrnambool about 67 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tabor is Penshurst Swimming Pool in Penshurst, about 10 minutes' drive. For a library, Hamilton Library in Hamilton is about 18 minutes' drive from Tabor. The closest skate park to Tabor is hamilton skate park in Hamilton, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Tabor is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 18 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 19 minutes from Tabor by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Tabor, the nearest places for kids by road are Tarrington Rec Reserve Playground in Tarrington (10 min), Penshurst Botanic Gardens Playground in Penshurst (10 min), Penshurst Swimming Pool in Penshurst (10 min), Volcanoes Discovery Centre in Penshurst (10 min), Sir Reginald Ansett Transport Museum in Hamilton (17 min) and hamilton skate park in Hamilton (17 min).
