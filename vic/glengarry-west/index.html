Glengarry West is in the Latrobe area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Glengarry West, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Glengarry West. The closest to Glengarry West is Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glengarry West, so most trips start by car. By car from Glengarry West, Traralgon is about 13 minutes away, Warragul about 49 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon is about 12 minutes' drive from Glengarry West. For a library, Morwell Library in Morwell is about 19 minutes' drive from Glengarry West. For a skate park, Glengarry Skatepark in Glengarry is about 6 minutes' drive from Glengarry West. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 20 minutes' drive from Glengarry West. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Glengarry West, about 13 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. From Glengarry West, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Tyers Community Park in Tyers (4 min), the playground at Apex Park in Glengarry (5 min), Glengarry Skatepark in Glengarry (6 min), the skate park at Apex Park in Glengarry (6 min), Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon (12 min) and Yallourn North Outdoor Pool in Yallourn North (13 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Glengarry West.

Nearby suburbs

Traralgon, next to Glengarry West, has 38 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Glengarry West, Tyers has 1 playground. Glengarry, next to Glengarry West, has 1 playground and a skate park. Boola also borders Glengarry West.
