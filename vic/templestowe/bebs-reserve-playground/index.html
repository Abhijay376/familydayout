Bebs Reserve Playground

Playground in Templestowe · Basic info

This is a small local playground in Templestowe, looked after by Manningham City Council. Playground at Fielding Way Reserve is another option about 310 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 490 m from the playground, and buses 282, 309 and 901 stop at Pettys Reserve/Reynolds Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
