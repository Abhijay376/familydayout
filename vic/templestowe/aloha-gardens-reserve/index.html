Aloha Gardens Reserve

Park in Templestowe · Basic info

Aloha Gardens Reserve is a park in Templestowe, in the Manningham City Council area, good for all ages. Pettys Reserve/Reynolds Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 493 m away with a baby change table.
