Apple Blossom Reserve Playground

Playground in Templestowe · Basic info

Apple Blossom Reserve Playground in Templestowe is a small local playground in the Manningham City area. Browning Reserve Playground is another option about 200 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.3 km from the playground, and buses 318 and 908 stop at Nedlands Ct/King St, a 5-minute walk away.
