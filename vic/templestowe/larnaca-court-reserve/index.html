Larnaca Court Reserve

Park in Templestowe · Basic info

Larnaca Court Reserve is a park in Templestowe, in the Manningham City Council area, good for all ages. Serpell PS/Tuckers Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 918 m away with a baby change table.
