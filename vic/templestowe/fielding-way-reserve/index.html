Fielding Way Reserve

Park in Templestowe · Basic info

Fielding Way Reserve is a park in Templestowe, in the Manningham City Council area, good for all ages. Aumann Dr/Reynolds Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 740 m away with a baby change table.
