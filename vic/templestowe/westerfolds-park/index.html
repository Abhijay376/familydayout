Westerfolds Park

Park in Templestowe · Official details

Westerfolds Park is a park in Templestowe, in the Manningham City Council area, good for all ages. Summerhill Rd/Fitzsimons Lane (bus) is a 12-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 630 m away.

Details

Good to know
Over 120 hectares for picnicking, cycling, walking and canoeing (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Westerfolds Park?
Westerfolds Park is in Templestowe, Victoria.
Is Westerfolds Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Westerfolds Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 630 m away.
How do I get to Westerfolds Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Summerhill Rd/Fitzsimons Lane (routes 293, 901, 902), a 12-minute walk.
