Tikalara Park

Park in Templestowe · Basic info

Tikalara Park is a park in Templestowe, in the Manningham City Council area, good for all ages. Blackburn Rd/Heidelberg-Warrandyte Rd (bus) is a 17-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1642 m away.
