Matisse Drive Reserve

Park in Templestowe · Basic info

Matisse Drive Reserve is a park in Templestowe, in the Manningham City Council area, good for all ages. Glendarragh Rd/Reynolds Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 560 m away.
