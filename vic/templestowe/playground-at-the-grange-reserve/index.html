Playground at The Grange Reserve

Playground in Templestowe · Basic info

Playground at The Grange Reserve is one of the council's larger playgrounds in Templestowe, in the Manningham City area. Noral Reserve Playground is another option about 400 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.2 km from the playground, and bus 295 stops at Peter Budge Ave/The Grange, a 2-minute walk away.
