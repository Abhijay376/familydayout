Playground at Swilk Reserve

Playground in Templestowe · Basic info

This is a public playground in Templestowe. The Ruffey Creek Trail is about 100 m away, and for food, Leelavadee is about 380 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 770 m from the playground, and buses 281, 282 and 905 stop at McLachlan St/James St, a 3-minute walk away.
