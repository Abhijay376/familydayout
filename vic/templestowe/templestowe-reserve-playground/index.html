Templestowe Reserve Playground

Playground in Templestowe · Basic info

Templestowe Reserve Playground in Templestowe is one of the council's larger playgrounds in the Manningham City area. Morecambe Reserve Playground is another option about 260 m away. There's a public toilet about 290 m away, and buses 280, 293, 901 and 902 stop at Porter St/Williamsons Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
