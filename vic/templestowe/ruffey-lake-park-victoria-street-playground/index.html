Ruffey Lake Park Victoria Street Playground

Playground in Templestowe · Basic info

Ruffey Lake Park Victoria Street Playground in Templestowe is one of the council's biggest regional playgrounds in the Manningham City area. The Ruffey Lake Circuit passes about 60 m away, and Schramms Cottage is about 170 m away. There's a public toilet about 50 m away, and buses 279, 295 and 318 stop at Owens St/Victoria St, a 3-minute walk away.
