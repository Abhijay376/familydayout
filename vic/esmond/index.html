Esmond in the Moira area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Esmond is Lower Ovens Wildlife Reserve, covering about 1,155 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Esmond. The closest to Esmond is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 34 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Esmond, so most trips start by car. By car from Esmond, Wangaratta is about 28 minutes away and Wodonga about 57 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rutherglen War Memorial Swimming Pool in Rutherglen is about 22 minutes' drive from Esmond. For a library, Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga is about 18 minutes' drive from Esmond. The closest beach to Esmond is Forges Beach No 2 in Yarrawonga, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Esmond is the pump track on Victoria Street in Wahgunyah, about 23 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 28 minutes' drive from Esmond. For a zoo or wildlife park, Tunzafun in Yarrawonga is about 20 minutes' drive from Esmond. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Esmond, about 28 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Esmond, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Peechelba Reserve in Peechelba (10 min), Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga (18 min), Tunzafun in Yarrawonga (20 min), Yarrawonga Mulwala Pioneer Museum in Yarrawonga (21 min), Lonsdale Reserve Playground in Yarrawonga (22 min) and Mulwala Waterslide in Yarrawonga (22 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Esmond.
