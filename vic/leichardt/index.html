Leichardt in the Greater Bendigo area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Leichardt Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Leichardt, at about 41 hectares.

Getting around

Leichardt has no train station; Eaglehawk Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Leichardt, so most trips start by car. By car from Leichardt, Bendigo is about 25 minutes away, Castlemaine about 41 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Leichardt is Sloan Swim School in California Gully, about 22 minutes' drive. For a library, Kangaroo Flat Library in Kangaroo Flat is about 23 minutes' drive from Leichardt. The closest skate park to Leichardt is Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk, about 20 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 25 minutes' drive from Leichardt. The closest indoor play centre to Leichardt is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Leichardt is The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo, about 30 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 25 minutes from Leichardt by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Leichardt are Westbury Boulevard Playspace in Maiden Gully (18 min), Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk (20 min), the bmx track on Victoria Street in Eaglehawk (21 min), Long Gully Splash Park in Long Gully (22 min), Sloan Swim School in California Gully (22 min) and Peter Krenz Leisure Centre in Eaglehawk (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Leichardt.

Nearby suburbs

Marong, Derby, Campbells Forest and Bridgewater also border Leichardt.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Leichardt, without traffic.
