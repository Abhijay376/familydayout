Noojee is in the Baw Baw area, and our data lists 1 playground and 11 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Noojee is Neerim State Forest, covering about 1,622 hectares. Bennie Creek Reference Area and Noojee Trestle Bridge Historic and Cultural Features Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Noojee include Noojee Trestle Bridge, Noojee Bushland Reserve and Noojee Depot Picnic Area. Playgrounds around Noojee include the playground at LaTrobe and Loch Rivers, Noojee Streamside Reserve. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Noojee. Picnic areas in Noojee include Noojee Depot Picnic Area, Noojee Trestle Bridge and Toorongo Falls Campground One. The Upper Yarra Walking Track, Noojee Rail Trail and Ralph Cornwall Walk all pass through Noojee. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Noojee for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Noojee has no train station; Warragul Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 30 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 89 stops in Noojee. By car from Noojee, Warragul is about 36 minutes away, Healesville about 60 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Noojee is Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul, about 38 minutes' drive. For a library, Warragul Library in Warragul is about 36 minutes' drive from Noojee. The closest skate park to Noojee is Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Noojee is Warragul Cinema in Warragul, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Noojee is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 37 minutes' drive. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Noojee, about 36 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Noojee are Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South (17 min), the playground at Blue Rock Lake West picnic area in Willow Grove (32 min), Warragul Library in Warragul (36 min), Warragul Cinema in Warragul (36 min), The Alley Warragul in Warragul (37 min) and Bmx in Warragul (37 min).

Best for

Free days on the Upper Yarra Walking Track and in Noojee's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Neerim North, Neerim Junction, McMahons Creek and Loch Valley also border Noojee.
