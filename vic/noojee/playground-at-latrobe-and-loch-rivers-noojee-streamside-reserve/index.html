Playground at LaTrobe and Loch Rivers, Noojee Streamside Reserve

Playground in Noojee · Basic info

Playground at LaTrobe and Loch Rivers, Noojee Streamside Reserve in Noojee is a public playground in the Baw Baw Shire area. The Ralph Cornwall Walk is about 190 m away, and for food, The Outpost Retreat Toolshed Noojee is about 200 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 89 stops at Noojee Post Office/Bennett Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
