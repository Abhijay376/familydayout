Playground on Warralily Boulevard

Playground in Armstrong Creek · Basic info

Playground on Warralily Boulevard in Armstrong Creek is a public playground in the Greater Geelong City area. The Stewarts Lane passes about 280 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 20 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 70 m away, and buses 45, 52, 53 and 54 stop at Warralily Bvd/Surf Coast Hwy , a 4-minute walk away.
