Skate park on Warralily Boulevard

Skate, BMX or pump track in Armstrong Creek · Basic info

Skate park on Warralily Boulevard is a skate, bmx or pump track in Armstrong Creek, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Warralily Bvd/Surf Coast Hwy (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 86 m away with a baby change table.
