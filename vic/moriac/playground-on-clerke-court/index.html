Playground on Clerke Court

Playground in Moriac · Basic info

This is a public playground in Moriac. Older kids have a skate park about 280 m away, and Moriac Playground is another option about 240 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 700 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 2 minutes from Moriac.
