Moriac Skate Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Moriac · Basic info

Moriac Skate Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Moriac, in the Surf Coast Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. The nearest public toilet is about 299 m away.
