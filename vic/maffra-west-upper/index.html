Landmarks

Coongulla I6 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Maffra West Upper, at about 6 hectares. Coongulla I7 Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Maffra West Upper include Pearson Point and Mount Hedrick Picnic Area. Picnic areas in Maffra West Upper include Mount Hedrick Picnic Area and Pearson Point.

Getting around

Maffra West Upper has no train station; Stratford Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Maffra West Upper, so most trips start by car. By car from Maffra West Upper, Sale is about 39 minutes away, Traralgon about 49 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Maffra West Upper is Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest library to Maffra West Upper is Sale Library in Sale, about 40 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Pump Track in Heyfield is about 14 minutes' drive from Maffra West Upper. The closest cinema to Maffra West Upper is Former Drive-in site in Sale, about 39 minutes' drive. Sale is the closest bigger centre to Maffra West Upper, about 39 minutes away by car, and has 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Maffra West Upper, the nearest places for kids by road are Coongulla Community Centre Reserve Playground in Coongulla (9 min), Play Equipment in Newry (16 min), Pump Track in Heyfield (14 min), Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield (14 min), Heyfield and District Museum in Heyfield (14 min) and Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford (34 min).
