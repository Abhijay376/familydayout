Telford is in the Moira area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Telford, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Telford has no train station; Benalla Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 49 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Telford, so most trips start by car. By car from Telford, Wangaratta is about 51 minutes away and Benalla about 53 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Telford is Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga, about 17 minutes' drive. For a beach, Duffys Beach No 1 in Burramine is about 22 minutes' drive from Telford. For a skate park, the skate park at Federation Park in Cobram is about 35 minutes' drive from Telford. For a cinema, Cobram Cinema in Cobram is about 35 minutes' drive from Telford. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Telford is Tunzafun in Yarrawonga, about 19 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 51 minutes from Telford by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Telford, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Tungamah Lions Park in Tungamah (14 min), Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga (17 min), Tunzafun in Yarrawonga (19 min), Yarrawonga Mulwala Pioneer Museum in Yarrawonga (20 min), Lonsdale Reserve Playground in Yarrawonga (21 min) and Mulwala Waterslide in Yarrawonga (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Telford.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Telford, Yarrawonga has 6 playgrounds, a library and a beach. Burramine, next to Telford, has a beach. Next door to Telford, Tungamah has 1 playground. Wilby and Burramine South also border Telford.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Telford, without traffic.
