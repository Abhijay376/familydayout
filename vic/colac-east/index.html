Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Colac East in the Colac Otway area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, MBN SW3 crosses Colac East.

Getting around

There's no train station in Colac East. The closest to Colac East is Colac Railway Station (V/Line), about 2.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Colac East, so most trips start by car. By car from Colac East, Colac is about 6 minutes away, Geelong about 61 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Colac East is Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac, about 7 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt is about 11 minutes' drive from Colac East. For a cinema, Colac Cinemas in Colac is about 7 minutes' drive from Colac East. For an indoor play centre, Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac is about 6 minutes' drive from Colac East. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Colac East, about 6 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Colac East are the playground at Selwyn Brown Park in Colac (4 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (6 min), the playground at Memorial Square in Colac (6 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (7 min), Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (7 min) and the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (11 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Colac East on the MBN SW3.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Colac East, Colac has 13 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. Elliminyt, next to Colac East, has 1 playground.
