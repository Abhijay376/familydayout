Murchison East is in the Greater Shepparton area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Murchison Lagoon Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Murchison East, at about 6 hectares. Goulburn River K49 Streamside Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Murchison East. The Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks passes through Murchison East.

Getting around

Murchison East has a train station: Murchison East Railway Station (V/Line: Shepparton - Melbourne via Seymour). V/Line coaches stop in Murchison East. By car from Murchison East, Shepparton is about 30 minutes away, Seymour about 38 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Murchison East is Rushworth Pool in Rushworth, about 24 minutes' drive. For a library, Rushworth Library in Rushworth is about 24 minutes' drive from Murchison East. The closest beach to Murchison East is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Murchison East is the skate park at Lions Park in Rushworth, about 23 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 30 minutes' drive from Murchison East. The closest indoor play centre to Murchison East is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 30 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Emerald Bank Leisure Land in Kialla is about 23 minutes' drive from Murchison East. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 30 minutes from Murchison East by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Murchison East, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Goulburn River Heritage River in Murchison (2 min), the playground at Murchison Youth Spot in Murchison (3 min), the skate park at Lions Park in Rushworth (23 min), Rushworth Museum in Rushworth (23 min), Nagambie Historical Society in Nagambie (17 min) and Rushworth Library in Rushworth (24 min).
