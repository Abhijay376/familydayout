Murchison Lagoon Wildlife Reserve (hunting)

Park in Murchison East · Basic info

Murchison Lagoon Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is a park in Murchison East, in the Greater Shepparton City Council area, good for all ages. Murchison/Stevenson St (coach) is a 85-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 7259 m away.
