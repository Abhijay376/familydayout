Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Delegate River in the East Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Delegate River Tunnel crosses Delegate River.

Getting around

Delegate River has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 135 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Delegate River, so most trips start by car. By car from Delegate River, Lakes Entrance is about 2.5 hours away and Bairnsdale about 3 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Delegate River on the Delegate River Tunnel.
