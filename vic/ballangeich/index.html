Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Ballangeich in the Moyne area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Ballangeich has no train station; Sherwood Park Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Warrnambool - Mortlake route stop in Ballangeich. By car from Ballangeich, Warrnambool is about 23 minutes away and Colac about 84 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool is about 26 minutes' drive from Ballangeich. For a library, Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake is about 19 minutes' drive from Ballangeich. The closest beach to Ballangeich is Shelly Beach in Warrnambool, about 28 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake is about 20 minutes' drive from Ballangeich. For a cinema, Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool is about 24 minutes' drive from Ballangeich. The closest indoor play centre to Ballangeich is The Bowl in Warrnambool, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Ballangeich is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 19 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 23 minutes from Ballangeich by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Ballangeich are the playground at Bushfield Recreation Reserve in Bushfield (16 min), Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (19 min), Market Square Playground in Mortlake (18 min), Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake (19 min), Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake (20 min) and Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool (24 min).

Best for
