Flying fox and slide

Playground in Marysville · Basic info

This is a public playground in Marysville. The Tree Fern Gully Track passes about 10 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 360 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 90 m away, and bus 684 stops at Falls Rd/Pack Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
