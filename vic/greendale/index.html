Our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in Greendale, in the Moorabool area.

Landmarks

Egans Reserve is the largest mapped park in Greendale, at about 2 hectares. Playgrounds in Greendale include the playground at Greendale Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Greendale. For walks and rides, Walk 5, Byers Back Track and Lerderderg Track cross Greendale.

Getting around

There's no train station in Greendale. The closest to Greendale is Ballan Railway Station (V/Line), about 7.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Greendale, so most trips start by car. By car from Greendale, Bacchus Marsh is about 17 minutes away, Melton about 26 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 61 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Ballan Swimming Pool in Ballan is about 13 minutes' drive from Greendale. For a library, Lerderderg Library in Bacchus Marsh is about 17 minutes' drive from Greendale. For a beach, Lionhead Beach in Pentland Hills is about 13 minutes' drive from Greendale. For a skate park, the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan is about 13 minutes' drive from Greendale. The closest cinema to Greendale is Reading Cinemas in Melton West, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Greendale is Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek, about 31 minutes' drive. Bacchus Marsh is the closest bigger centre to Greendale, about 17 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Greendale are the playground at Mill Park in Ballan (13 min), the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan (13 min), Ballan Swimming Pool in Ballan (13 min), Lionhead Beach in Pentland Hills (13 min), Paul Saddler Swimland in Darley (15 min) and the skate park at Rotary Park in Bacchus Marsh (17 min).

Best for

Free days on the Walk 5 and in Greendale's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Ballan, next to Greendale, has 4 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Myrniong, next to Greendale, has 3 playgrounds. Dales Creek and Blackwood also border Greendale.
