Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Major Plains in the Greater Shepparton area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Major Plains. The closest to Major Plains is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 25 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Major Plains, so most trips start by car. By car from Major Plains, Benalla is about 34 minutes away and Shepparton about 42 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla is about 34 minutes' drive from Major Plains. For a library, Shepparton Library in Shepparton is about 42 minutes' drive from Major Plains. The closest skate park to Major Plains is Pump Track in Benalla, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Major Plains is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 43 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Major Plains is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 42 minutes' drive. Benalla is the closest bigger centre to Major Plains, about 34 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Major Plains are the playground at CWA Gardens in Dookie (15 min), the playground at Dookie Recreation Reserve in Dookie (17 min), Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (34 min), Benalla Costume and Pioneer Museum in Benalla (34 min), Pump Track in Benalla (35 min) and Benalla Art Gallery in Benalla (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Major Plains.

Nearby suburbs

Dookie, next to Major Plains, has 2 playgrounds. Devenish and Boxwood also border Major Plains.
