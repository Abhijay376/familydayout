Our data lists 2 playgrounds and 1 park or reserve in Marcus Hill, in the Greater Geelong area.

Landmarks

Marcus Hill also has Bellarine I109 Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Marcus Hill. For walks and rides, Bellarine Rail Trail and Bellarine Peninsula Rail Trail cross Marcus Hill.

Getting around

There's no train station in Marcus Hill. The closest to Marcus Hill is Marshall Railway Station (V/Line), about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Marcus Hill, so most trips start by car. By car from Marcus Hill, Geelong is about 21 minutes away, Torquay about 35 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 86 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bellarine Aquatic Centre in Ocean Grove is about 5 minutes' drive from Marcus Hill. The closest library to Marcus Hill is Ocean Grove Library in Ocean Grove, about 8 minutes' drive. For a beach, Front Beach in Point Lonsdale is about 9 minutes' drive from Marcus Hill. For a skate park, the skate park at Jetty Road Reserve in Clifton Springs is about 12 minutes' drive from Marcus Hill. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Geelong is about 22 minutes' drive from Marcus Hill. For an indoor play centre, Laserquest in Geelong is about 23 minutes' drive from Marcus Hill. For a zoo or wildlife park, Adventure Park in Wallington is about 7 minutes' drive from Marcus Hill. The nearest larger centre is Geelong, about 21 minutes from Marcus Hill by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Marcus Hill, the nearest places for kids by road are Oakdene Boulevard Reserve in Ocean Grove (4 min), Bellarine Aquatic Centre in Ocean Grove (5 min), Ocean Grove Skatepark in Ocean Grove (4 min), Adventure Park in Wallington (7 min), Ocean Grove Library in Ocean Grove (8 min) and Tiny Tots in Wallington (7 min).

Best for

Free days on the Bellarine Rail Trail and in Marcus Hill's parks.
