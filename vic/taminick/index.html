Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Taminick, in the Benalla area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Taminick is Glenrowan Bushland Reserve, covering about 8 hectares. Taminick H100A Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Taminick. The Salisbury Falls Walk and Warby Heratige Falls Trail all pass through Taminick.

Getting around

There's no train station in Taminick. The closest to Taminick is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Taminick, so most trips start by car. By car from Taminick, Wangaratta is about 18 minutes away, Benalla about 30 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Taminick is Moore than Swimming in Wangaratta, about 16 minutes' drive. For a library, Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta is about 19 minutes' drive from Taminick. The closest skate park to Taminick is the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Taminick is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 19 minutes' drive. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Taminick, about 18 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Taminick are Glenrowan Museum in Glenrowan (10 min), Cobb & Co in Glenrowan (10 min), Moore than Swimming in Wangaratta (16 min), the playground at Dodsworth Street Reserve in Wangaratta (17 min), Brien Crescent Playground in Wangaratta (18 min) and Splashpark in Wangaratta (18 min).

Best for

Free days on the Salisbury Falls Walk and in Taminick's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Glenrowan, next to Taminick, has 1 playground. Thoona, Mount Bruno, Glenrowan West and Chesney Vale also border Taminick.
