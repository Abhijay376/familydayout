Jeffcott North in the Buloke area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Jeffcott Wildlife Reserve (no hunting) is the largest mapped park in Jeffcott North, at about 24 hectares. Jeffcott I144 Bushland Reserve and Banyenong Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Jeffcott North include Corack East I142 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Jeffcott North. The closest to Jeffcott North is Kerang Railway Station (V/Line), about 96 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Jeffcott North, so most trips start by car. By car from Jeffcott North, Stawell is about 83 minutes away and Horsham about 86 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Jeffcott North is Donald Swimming Pool in Donald, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Jeffcott North is Donald BMX Track in Donald, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Jeffcott North is Rex Theatre in Charlton, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Jeffcott North are Donald BMX Track in Donald (15 min), the playground at Donald Public Park in Donald (15 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (16 min), the playground at Gordon Park in Charlton (25 min), Rex Theatre in Charlton (25 min) and Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (25 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Jeffcott North.

Nearby suburbs

Donald, next to Jeffcott North, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Corack also borders Jeffcott North.
