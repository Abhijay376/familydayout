Teesdale Turtle Bend Reserve North Playground

Playground in Teesdale · Basic info

This is a public playground in Teesdale. Older kids have a skate park about 60 m away, and Playground at Turtle Bend Park is another option about 80 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground. It's easiest by car, about 1 minute from Teesdale.
