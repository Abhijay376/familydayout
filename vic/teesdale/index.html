Teesdale in the Golden Plains area has 4 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in our data. Beyond the parks, Teesdale has 2 skate parks.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Teesdale is Teesdale Recreation Reserve, covering about 32 hectares. Teesdale Sheoak Nature Conservation Reserve and Turtle Bend Park are the next largest. Playgrounds around Teesdale include Summer Sensations, the playground at Turtle Bend Park and Teesdale Recreation Reserve Playground. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Teesdale. For older kids in Teesdale there's the skate park at Turtle Bend Park and Teesdale BMX Track.

Getting around

There's no train station in Teesdale. The closest to Teesdale is Winchelsea Railway Station (V/Line), about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Teesdale, so most trips start by car. By car from Teesdale, Geelong is about 37 minutes away, Torquay about 51 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 85 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Teesdale is Goodfellow Aquatic Centre in Bell Post Hill, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest library to Teesdale is Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Teesdale is Eastern Beach in Geelong, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Teesdale is Village Cinemas in Geelong, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Teesdale is All 4 Kids Play Centre in Geelong West, about 33 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Geelong, about 37 minutes from Teesdale by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Teesdale's 4 local playgrounds. Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at Turtle Bend Park in Teesdale.

Nearby suburbs

Bannockburn, next to Teesdale, has 6 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Teesdale, Inverleigh has 4 playgrounds. Next door to Teesdale, Shelford has 1 playground. Next door to Teesdale, Lethbridge has 1 playground.
