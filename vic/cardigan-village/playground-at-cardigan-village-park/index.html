Playground at Cardigan Village Park

Playground in Cardigan Village · Official details

This is a public playground in Cardigan Village. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. You'll find a public toilet about 150 m from the playground, and coaches stop at Windermere Way/Madden Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
