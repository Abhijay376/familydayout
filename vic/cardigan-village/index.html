Cardigan Village in the Ballarat area has 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Cardigan Village Park is the largest mapped park in Cardigan Village, at about 3 hectares. Playgrounds in Cardigan Village include the playground at Cardigan Village Park. Our data shows 1 public toilet and 2 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Cardigan Village.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cardigan Village. The closest to Cardigan Village is Wendouree Railway Station (V/Line), about 10 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cardigan Village, so most trips start by car. By car from Cardigan Village, Ballarat is about 17 minutes away, Daylesford about 53 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Ballarat Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Lake Gardens is about 14 minutes' drive from Cardigan Village. For a library, Central Highlands Regional Library Corp Ballarat branch in Ballarat Central is about 19 minutes' drive from Cardigan Village. The closest skate park to Cardigan Village is Haddon Skate Park in Haddon, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Cardigan Village is Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Cardigan Village is Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Cardigan Village is Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East, about 25 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Ballarat, about 17 minutes from Cardigan Village by car, with 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Cardigan Village, the nearest places for kids by road are Sanctuary Park Playground in Lucas (9 min), Haddon Skate Park in Haddon (10 min), Haddon Tramway Preservation Association in Haddon (10 min), Hollioake Park Pump Track in Cardigan (11 min), Ballarat Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Lake Gardens (14 min) and Ballarat Tramway Museum in Lake Wendouree (15 min).
