Reedy Flat is in the East Gippsland area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Reedy Flat, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Reedy Flat has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 56 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Reedy Flat, so most trips start by car. By car from Reedy Flat, Bairnsdale is about 2 hours away and Lakes Entrance about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest places for kids by road from Reedy Flat are Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek (54 min) and Great Alpine Gallery in Swifts Creek (54 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Reedy Flat.
