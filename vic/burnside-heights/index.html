Our data lists 5 playgrounds and 2 parks and reserves in Burnside Heights, in the Melton area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Burnside Heights is Burnside Heights Recreation Reserve, covering about 23 hectares. Tenterfield Park is the next largest. Playgrounds in Burnside Heights include Inglewood Park Playground, the playground at Tenterfield Park and Arbour Bvd North Reserve Playground.

Getting around

There's no train station in Burnside Heights. The closest to Burnside Heights is Caroline Springs Railway Station (V/Line), about 3.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 418, 426 and 462 stop in Burnside Heights. By car from Burnside Heights, Sunshine is about 15 minutes away, Melton about 21 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 32 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Flippers Swim School in Sydenham is about 9 minutes' drive from Burnside Heights. For a library, Caroline Springs Library in Caroline Springs is about 4 minutes' drive from Burnside Heights. For a beach, Altona Beach in Altona is about 26 minutes' drive from Burnside Heights. The closest skate park to Burnside Heights is the skate park at Packard Street Reserve in Keilor Downs, about 8 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Hoyts in Taylors Lakes is about 11 minutes' drive from Burnside Heights. For an indoor play centre, Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes is about 11 minutes' drive from Burnside Heights. For a zoo or wildlife park, Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville is about 29 minutes' drive from Burnside Heights.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Burnside Heights, Caroline Springs has 31 playgrounds and a library. Taylors Hill, next to Burnside Heights, has 18 playgrounds. Next door to Burnside Heights, Kings Park has 10 playgrounds. Burnside, next to Burnside Heights, has 6 playgrounds. Delahey also borders Burnside Heights.
