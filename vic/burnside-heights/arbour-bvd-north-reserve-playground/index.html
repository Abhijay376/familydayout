Arbour Bvd North Reserve Playground

Playground in Burnside Heights · Basic info

Arbour Bvd North Reserve Playground is a public playground in Burnside Heights, in the Melton City area. For food, New Dragon is about 410 m away, and Playground at Cranwell Reserve is another option about 360 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 630 m from the playground, and bus 418 stops at Jacaranda Dr/Taylors Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
