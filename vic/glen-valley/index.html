Glen Valley is in the East Gippsland area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Mitta Mitta River Heritage River is the largest mapped park in Glen Valley, at about 2,347 hectares. Burnside Reference Area is the next largest. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Glen Valley. The Australian Alps Walking Track, 107 Track and Patons Road all pass through Glen Valley.

Getting around

There's no train station in Glen Valley. The closest to Glen Valley is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 100 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glen Valley, so most trips start by car. By car from Glen Valley, Bright is about 1.5 hours away and Wodonga about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a skate park, the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo is about 41 minutes' drive from Glen Valley. The closest places for kids by road from Glen Valley are Falls Creek Museum in Falls Creek (40 min), Petersens Art Gallery & Museum in Omeo (40 min), the playground at Omeo Historical Park & Museum in Omeo (40 min) and the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo (41 min).

Best for

Free days on the Australian Alps Walking Track and in Glen Valley's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Omeo, next to Glen Valley, has 2 playgrounds and a skate park. Glen Wills, Dartmouth, Bundara and Bingo Munjie also border Glen Valley.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Glen Valley, without traffic.
