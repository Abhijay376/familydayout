Three Bridges is in the Yarra Ranges area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Three Bridges is Beenak G183 B.R., covering about 4 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Three Bridges. The closest to Three Bridges is Tynong Railway Station (V/Line), about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Three Bridges, so most trips start by car. By car from Three Bridges, Healesville is about 32 minutes away, Lilydale about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 81 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Warburton Water World in Warburton is about 20 minutes' drive from Three Bridges. For a library, Yarra Junction public library in Yarra Junction is about 11 minutes' drive from Three Bridges. For a skate park, the skate park at Yarra Junction Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Yarra Junction is about 12 minutes' drive from Three Bridges. For a cinema, The Memo, Healesville in Healesville is about 32 minutes' drive from Three Bridges. The closest indoor play centre to Three Bridges is PlayPlace in Mooroolbark, about 45 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Lyre Bird enclosure in Badger Creek is about 28 minutes' drive from Three Bridges. The nearest larger centre is Healesville, about 32 minutes from Three Bridges by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Three Bridges are Yarra Junction public library in Yarra Junction (11 min), the playground at Yarra Junction Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Yarra Junction (11 min), the skate park at Yarra Junction Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Yarra Junction (12 min), Street Library in Millgrove (14 min), Warburton Main Street Playground in Warburton (18 min) and Warburton Water World in Warburton (20 min).
