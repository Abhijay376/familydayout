Pipemakers, Burndap and Footscay Park Parklands (COM)

Park in Footscray · Official details

Pipemakers, Burndap and Footscay Park Parklands (COM) is a park in Footscray, in the Maribyrnong City Council area, good for all ages. Henry Turner South Reserve/Farnsworth Ave (bus) is a 5-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 310 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed.
