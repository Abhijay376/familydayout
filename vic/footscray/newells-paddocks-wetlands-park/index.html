Newell's Paddocks Wetlands Park

Park in Footscray · Basic info

Newell's Paddocks Wetlands Park is a park in Footscray, in the Maribyrnong City Council area, good for all ages. Jamieson Ave/Ballarat Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 1041 m away and dogs are not allowed.
