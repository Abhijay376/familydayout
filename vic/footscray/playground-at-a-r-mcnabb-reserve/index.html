Playground at A R McNabb Reserve

Playground in Footscray · Basic info

This is a public playground in Footscray. Ebi Fine Foods is about 170 m away if you need food, and Lions Club of Footscray Memorial Playground is another option about 150 m away. There's a public toilet about 860 m away, and bus 220 stops at Adelaide St/Barkly St, a 2-minute walk away.
