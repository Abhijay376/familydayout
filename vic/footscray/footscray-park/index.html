Footscray Park

Park in Footscray · Official details

Footscray Park is a park in Footscray, in the Maribyrnong City Council area, good for all ages. Footscray Park/Ballarat Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 300 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Footscray Park?
Footscray Park is in Footscray, Victoria.
Is Footscray Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Footscray Park?
Yes, dogs are allowed.
Are there toilets at Footscray Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 300 m away, with a baby change table.
How do I get to Footscray Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Footscray Park/Ballarat Rd (routes 404), a 4-minute walk.
