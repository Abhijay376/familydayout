Lilardia Park

Park in Footscray · Basic info

Lilardia Park is a park in Footscray, in the Maribyrnong City Council area, good for all ages. Whitehall St/Hopkins St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 483 m away.
