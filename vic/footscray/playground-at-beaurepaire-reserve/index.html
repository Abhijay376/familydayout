Playground at Beaurepaire Reserve

Playground in Footscray · Basic info

This is a public playground in Footscray. The Sunshine Footscray Trail is about 170 m away, and West Footscray Library is about 320 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 800 m from the playground, and bus 220 stops at Summerhill Rd/Barkly St, a 3-minute walk away.
