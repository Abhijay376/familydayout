Playground at Seelaf Square

Playground in Footscray · Basic info

Playground at Seelaf Square in Footscray is a public playground in the Maribyrnong City area. The Maribyrnong River Trail is about 340 m away, and for food, The Local Cafe is about 340 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 810 m from the playground, and buses 404, 406 and 409 stop at Lynch St/Moore St, a 4-minute walk away.
