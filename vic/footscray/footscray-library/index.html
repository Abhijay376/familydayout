Footscray Library

Library in Footscray · Basic info

Footscray Library is a library in Footscray, in the Maribyrnong City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Western Region Health Centre/72 Paisley St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 48 m away.
