Playground at Miners Rest Park

Playground in Miners Rest · Basic info

Playground at Miners Rest Park in Miners Rest is a public playground in the Ballarat City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. You'll find a public toilet about 80 m from the playground, and bus 31 stops at Victoria St/Albert St, a 3-minute walk away.
