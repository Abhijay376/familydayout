Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Tongala in the Campaspe area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 2 public toilets in Tongala.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tongala. The closest to Tongala is Echuca Railway Station (V/Line), about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Tongala. By car from Tongala, Echuca is about 22 minutes away and Shepparton about 53 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Community Swimming Pool in Merrigum is about 30 minutes' drive from Tongala. The closest library to Tongala is Kyabram Library And Community Hub in Kyabram, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Tongala is the bmx track on Racecourse Road in Kyabram, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Tongala is Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca, about 23 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram is about 17 minutes' drive from Tongala. Echuca is the closest bigger centre to Tongala, about 22 minutes away by car, and has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Tongala are the playground at McCormick Reserve in Kyabram (15 min), the playground at Drum Street Reserve in Kyabram (15 min), Kyabram Library And Community Hub in Kyabram (15 min), the bmx track on Racecourse Road in Kyabram (16 min), Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram (17 min) and Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca (23 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tongala.

Nearby suburbs

Kyabram, next to Tongala, has 8 playgrounds, a library and a zoo or wildlife park. Kyvalley, Koyuga and Kanyapella also border Tongala.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Tongala, without traffic.
