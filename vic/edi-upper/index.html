Edi Upper is in the Wangaratta area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Edi Upper, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Vines 2 Vines: Whitfield to Milawa crosses Edi Upper.

Getting around

There's no train station in Edi Upper. The closest to Edi Upper is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 41 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Edi Upper, so most trips start by car. By car from Edi Upper, Wangaratta is about 42 minutes away and Benalla about 60 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Splashpark in Wangaratta is about 38 minutes' drive from Edi Upper. The closest library to Edi Upper is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 42 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Edi Upper is the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta, about 39 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Edi Upper is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 42 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 42 minutes from Edi Upper by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Edi Upper are the playground at Apex Park in Moyhu (18 min), Myrrhee Recreation Reserve in Myrrhee (31 min), Splashpark in Wangaratta (38 min), the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta (39 min), Glenrowan Museum in Glenrowan (38 min) and Cobb & Co in Glenrowan (38 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Edi Upper on the Vines 2 Vines: Whitfield to Milawa.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Edi Upper, Whitfield has 1 playground. Next door to Edi Upper, Buffalo River has 1 playground. King Valley, Edi, Dandongadale and Cheshunt also border Edi Upper.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Edi Upper, without traffic.
