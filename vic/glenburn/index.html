Glenburn in the Murrindindi area has 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Glenburn is Murrindini Bushland Reserve, covering about 7 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Glenburn include Two Hill. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Glenburn. For camping, there's Two Hill.

Getting around

Glenburn has no train station; Hurstbridge Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 31 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Glenburn. By car from Glenburn, Healesville is about 32 minutes away, Lilydale about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 79 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Glenburn is Jack Hore Memorial Pool in Healesville, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest library to Glenburn is Yarra Plenty Regional Library in Christmas Hills, about 33 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Glenburn is the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Glenburn is The Memo, Healesville in Healesville, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Glenburn is GAMR Laser Tag & VR in Chirnside Park, about 42 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Healesville Sanctuary in Badger Creek is about 36 minutes' drive from Glenburn. The nearest larger centre is Healesville, about 32 minutes from Glenburn by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Glenburn, the nearest places for kids by road are Bollygum Park in Kinglake (18 min), House of Bottles Museum & Tearooms in Kinglake (17 min), TLM Moto Guzzi in Yea (25 min), the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea (22 min), the playground at Hubbard Reserve in Yarra Glen (25 min) and the skate park at McKenzie Reserve in Yarra Glen (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Glenburn.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Glenburn, Kinglake has 1 playground. Next door to Glenburn, Yea has a skate park. Kinglake Central and Flowerdale also border Glenburn.
