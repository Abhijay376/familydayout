Kerang in the Gannawarra area has 1 playground and 7 parks and reserves in our data. Kerang also has a library and a pool.

Landmarks

Kerang Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Kerang, at about 759 hectares. Kerang Regional Park and Dry Lake B.R. are the next largest. Kerang also has Atkinson Park, North Street Public Reserve and Plumptons Wildlife Reserve (hunting). Our data shows 11 public toilets in Kerang. Places to visit in Kerang include Swimming Pool and Sir John Gorton Library. Our data lists 4 cafes and family restaurants in Kerang for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Kerang has a train station: Kerang Railway Station (V/Line: Swan Hill - Melbourne via Bendigo). Buses on the Kerang - Echuca via Cohuna route stop in Kerang. V/Line coaches stop in Kerang. By car from Kerang, Swan Hill is about 49 minutes away and Echuca about 80 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a skate park, Barham Skate Park in Koondrook is about 25 minutes' drive from Kerang. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 49 minutes from Kerang by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Kerang are the playground at Lake Meran W.R in Lake Meran (19 min), the playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV) in Koondrook (24 min), Barham Branch Library in Koondrook (24 min), Barham Skate Park in Koondrook (25 min), Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna (29 min) and Cohuna Pool in Cohuna (30 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the library in Kerang.
