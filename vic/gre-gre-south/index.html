Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Gre Gre South in the Northern Grampians area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Gre Gre South. The closest to Gre Gre South is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 67 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gre Gre South, so most trips start by car. By car from Gre Gre South, Stawell is about 50 minutes away and Horsham about 66 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud is about 25 minutes' drive from Gre Gre South. For a skate park, Donald BMX Track in Donald is about 42 minutes' drive from Gre Gre South. Stawell is the closest bigger centre to Gre Gre South, about 50 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Gre Gre South are the playground at Shanahan Park in Marnoo (11 min), the playground at Queen Mary Botanic Gardens in St Arnaud (24 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (25 min), Rupanyup Swimming Pool in Rupanyup (29 min), Woods' Farming and Heritage Museum in Rupanyup (29 min) and Donald BMX Track in Donald (42 min).

Best for
