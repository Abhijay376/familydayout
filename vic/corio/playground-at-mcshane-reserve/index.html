Playground at McShane Reserve

Playground in Corio · Basic info

Playground at McShane Reserve in Corio is a public playground in the Greater Geelong City area. For food, Subway is about 240 m away, and Playground at Howitt Reserve is another option about 310 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 480 m from the playground, and bus 23 stops at Rosewall Community Centre/Sharland Rd , a 5-minute walk away.
