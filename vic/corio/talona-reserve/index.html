Talona Reserve

Playground in Corio · Basic info

Talona Reserve is a public playground in Corio, in the Greater Geelong City area. Detroit Crescent Fish and Chips is about 490 m away if you need food, and Rotella Reserve is another option about 190 m away. There's a public toilet about 450 m away, and bus 23 stops at Kosciusko Ave/Princes Hwy, a 10-minute walk away.
