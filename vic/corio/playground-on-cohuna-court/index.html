Playground at Cohuna Reserve

Playground in Corio · Basic info

Playground at Cohuna Reserve is a public playground in Corio, in the Greater Geelong City area. Cloverdale Fish and Chips and Pizza is about 360 m away if you need food, and Landale Court Playground is another option about 340 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 800 m away, and bus 22 stops at Grange Ct/Cloverdale Dr, a 2-minute walk away.
