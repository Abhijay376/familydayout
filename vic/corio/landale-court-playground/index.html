Landale Court Playground

Playground in Corio · Basic info

Landale Court Playground in Corio is a public playground in the Greater Geelong City area. For food, Cloverdale Fish and Chips and Pizza is about 300 m away, and Playground at Cohuna Reserve is another option about 340 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 470 m from the playground, and bus 22 stops at Landale Ct/Cloverdale Dr, a 2-minute walk away.
