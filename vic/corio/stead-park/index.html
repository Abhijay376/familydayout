Stead Park

Park in Corio · Official details

Stead Park is a park in Corio, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Norlane Bowling Club/St Georges Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 57 m away and dogs are allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Stead Park?
Stead Park is in Corio, Victoria.
Is Stead Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Stead Park?
Yes, dogs are allowed.
Are there toilets at Stead Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 57 m away.
How do I get to Stead Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Norlane Bowling Club/St Georges Rd (routes 1), a 4-minute walk.
