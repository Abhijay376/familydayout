Corio Community Reserve

Park in Corio · Official details

Corio Community Reserve is a park in Corio, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Hendy St/Plantation Rd (bus) is a 8-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 724 m away and dogs are allowed.
