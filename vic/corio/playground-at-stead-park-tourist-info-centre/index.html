Playground at Stead Park-Tourist Info Centre

Playground in Corio · Basic info

Playground at Stead Park-Tourist Info Centre is a public playground in Corio, in the Greater Geelong City area. Older kids have a skate park about 40 m away, and John Drysdale Library is about 370 m away. There's a public toilet about 150 m away, and buses 20 and 22 stop at St Georges Rd/Princes Hwy, a 2-minute walk away.
