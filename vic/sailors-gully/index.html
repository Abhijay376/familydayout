Sailors Gully is in the Greater Bendigo area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Sailors Gully, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Sailors Gully. The closest to Sailors Gully is Eaglehawk Railway Station (V/Line), about 2.3 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Sailors Gully, so most trips start by car. By car from Sailors Gully, Bendigo is about 12 minutes away, Castlemaine about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Peter Krenz Leisure Centre in Eaglehawk is about 4 minutes' drive from Sailors Gully. For a library, Bendigo Library in Bendigo is about 11 minutes' drive from Sailors Gully. For a skate park, McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk is about 4 minutes' drive from Sailors Gully. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 12 minutes' drive from Sailors Gully. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 14 minutes' drive from Sailors Gully. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Sailors Gully is The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo, about 14 minutes' drive. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Sailors Gully, about 12 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Sailors Gully, the nearest places for kids by road are McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk (4 min), Peter Krenz Leisure Centre in Eaglehawk (4 min), Eaglehawk Playspace in Eaglehawk (4 min), the playground at Canterbury Park in Eaglehawk (4 min), the bmx track on Victoria Street in Eaglehawk (5 min) and Sloan Swim School in California Gully (6 min).
