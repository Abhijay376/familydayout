Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Archdale Junction in the Central Goldfields area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Archdale Junction. The closest to Archdale Junction is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Archdale Junction, so most trips start by car. By car from Archdale Junction, Maryborough is about 38 minutes away, Bendigo about 81 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Archdale Junction is Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest library to Archdale Junction is Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough, about 38 minutes' drive. Maryborough is the closest bigger centre to Archdale Junction, about 38 minutes away by car, and has a library. The closest places for kids by road from Archdale Junction are Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (29 min), Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (29 min), the playground at Avoca Lions Park in Avoca (32 min), Courthouse in Avoca (32 min), Stuart Mill Recreation Reserve Playground in Stuart Mill (35 min) and Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (38 min).
