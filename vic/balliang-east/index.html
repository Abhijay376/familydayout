Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Balliang East in the Moorabool area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Balliang East has no train station; Bacchus Marsh Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Balliang East, so most trips start by car. By car from Balliang East, Bacchus Marsh is about 15 minutes away, Melton about 24 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 54 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Balliang East is Bacchus Marsh Public childrens pool in Bacchus Marsh, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest library to Balliang East is Lerderderg Library in Bacchus Marsh, about 13 minutes' drive. For a beach, Needles Beach in Pentland Hills is about 21 minutes' drive from Balliang East. The closest skate park to Balliang East is the skate park at Rotary Park in Bacchus Marsh, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Balliang East is Reading Cinemas in Melton West, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Balliang East is Timezone in Hoppers Crossing, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Balliang East is Fairy Park in Anakie, about 28 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bacchus Marsh, about 15 minutes from Balliang East by car, with 6 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Balliang East are the playground at Balliang Recreation Reserve in Balliang (13 min), Australian Gliding Museum in Parwan (10 min), the playground at Maddingley Park in Maddingley (12 min), Bacchus Marsh Public childrens pool in Bacchus Marsh (12 min), Lerderderg Library in Bacchus Marsh (13 min) and the skate park at Rotary Park in Bacchus Marsh (14 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Balliang East.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Balliang East, Eynesbury has 6 playgrounds. Balliang, next to Balliang East, has 1 playground.
