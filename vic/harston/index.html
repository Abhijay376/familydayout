Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Harston in the Greater Shepparton area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Harston. The closest to Harston is Murchison East Railway Station (V/Line), about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Harston, so most trips start by car. By car from Harston, Shepparton is about 27 minutes away, Echuca about 65 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Harston is Tatura Swimming complex in Tatura, about 10 minutes' drive. For a library, Tatura Library in Tatura is about 9 minutes' drive from Harston. The closest beach to Harston is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 29 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Tatura Memorial Park in Tatura is about 10 minutes' drive from Harston. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 27 minutes' drive from Harston. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 27 minutes' drive from Harston. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kidstown in Mooroopna is about 25 minutes' drive from Harston. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 27 minutes from Harston by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Harston are Joe Ford Drive Playground in Tatura (8 min), Tatura Irrigation and Wartime Camps Museum in Tatura (8 min), Tatura Library in Tatura (9 min), the playground at Tatura Memorial Park in Tatura (9 min), the skate park at Tatura Memorial Park in Tatura (10 min) and Tatura Swimming complex in Tatura (10 min).
