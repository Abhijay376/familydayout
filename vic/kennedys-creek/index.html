Kennedys Creek is in the Corangamite area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Kennedys Creek, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kennedys Creek. The closest to Kennedys Creek is Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line), about 42 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kennedys Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Kennedys Creek, Colac is about 69 minutes away and Warrnambool about 86 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon is about 42 minutes' drive from Kennedys Creek. The closest beach to Kennedys Creek is Clifton Beach in Princetown, about 34 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon is about 42 minutes' drive from Kennedys Creek. From Kennedys Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are Clifton Beach in Princetown (34 min), Gibson Beach in Princetown (34 min), the playground at Rex Norman Park in Gellibrand (67 min), Art Reach Studio in Beech Forest (56 min), the playground at John H Gardner Reserve in Beech Forest (56 min) and Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (42 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Kennedys Creek.
