Mount Burnett is in the Cardinia area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Mount Burnett, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

The Kangaroo Trail passes through Mount Burnett.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mount Burnett. The closest to Mount Burnett is Pakenham Railway Station (metro), about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mount Burnett, so most trips start by car. By car from Mount Burnett, Pakenham is about 16 minutes away, Cranbourne about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 59 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Pakenham Outdoor Swimming Pool in Pakenham is about 15 minutes' drive from Mount Burnett. The closest library to Mount Burnett is Emerald Library in Emerald, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Mount Burnett is Cockatoo Skate park in Cockatoo, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Mount Burnett is Cameo Belgrave in Belgrave, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Mount Burnett is Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Mount Burnett is MonkeyG's in Pakenham, about 17 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Pakenham, about 16 minutes from Mount Burnett by car, with 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play. From Mount Burnett, the nearest places for kids by road are Cockatoo Skate park in Cockatoo (5 min), the playground at Gembrook Park in Gembrook (7 min), Gembrook Skate Park in Gembrook (7 min), the playground at JAC Russell Park in Gembrook (7 min), Emerald Museum in Emerald (11 min) and Emerald Library in Emerald (11 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Mount Burnett on the Kangaroo Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Mount Burnett, Emerald has 6 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Mount Burnett, Gembrook has 6 playgrounds and a skate park. Cockatoo, next to Mount Burnett, has 3 playgrounds and a skate park. Dewhurst also borders Mount Burnett.
