Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Talgarno, in the Towong area.

Landmarks

Wises Creek Flora Reserve is the largest mapped park in Talgarno, at about 30 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Talgarno.

Getting around

There's no train station in Talgarno. The closest to Talgarno is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Talgarno, so most trips start by car. By car from Talgarno, Wodonga is about 33 minutes away and Wangaratta about 80 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Talgarno is WAVES in Wodonga, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest library to Talgarno is Albury LibraryMuseum in Gateway Island, about 29 minutes' drive. For a beach, Finns Beach in Bandiana is about 29 minutes' drive from Talgarno. For a skate park, David Street Skate Park in Gateway Island is about 29 minutes' drive from Talgarno. The closest cinema to Talgarno is Regent Cinema in Gateway Island, about 28 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 33 minutes from Talgarno by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Talgarno, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Roy Williams Park in Bellbridge (11 min), Granya Museum in Granya (18 min), The Bonegilla Migrant Experience in Bonegilla (21 min), Eastern Circuit Playground in Bonegilla (23 min), Finns Beach in Bandiana (29 min) and Regent Cinema in Gateway Island (28 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Talgarno.

Nearby suburbs
