Getting around

There's no train station in Wurdiboluc. The closest to Wurdiboluc is Winchelsea Railway Station (V/Line), about 8.7 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wurdiboluc, so most trips start by car. By car from Wurdiboluc, Torquay is about 31 minutes away, Geelong about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.
