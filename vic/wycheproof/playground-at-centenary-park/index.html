Playground at Centenary Park

Playground in Wycheproof · Basic info

Playground at Centenary Park in Wycheproof is a public playground in the Buloke Shire area. The Wycheproof Wetland Walking trail passes about 60 m away, and Willandra Museum is about 470 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 140 m away, and coaches stop at Post Office/Calder Hwy, a 5-minute walk away.
