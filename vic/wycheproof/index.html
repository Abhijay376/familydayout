Our data lists 1 playground and 4 parks and reserves in Wycheproof, in the Buloke area. Wycheproof also has a pool and a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Wycheproof is Wycheproof Grassland Reserve, covering about 28 hectares. Wycheproof Bushland Reserve and Bunguluke I155 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Wycheproof also has Centenary Park. Playgrounds around Wycheproof include the playground at Centenary Park. Our data shows 6 public toilets in Wycheproof. Places to visit in Wycheproof include Willandra Museum and Wycheproof Swimming Pool. For walks and rides, Wycheproof Wetland Walking trail crosses Wycheproof.

Getting around

Wycheproof has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 74 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Swan Hill - Wycheproof via Lalbert and Bendigo - Woomelang via Wedderburn, Charlton, Wycheproof, Birchip routes stop in Wycheproof. V/Line coaches stop in Wycheproof. By car from Wycheproof, Swan Hill is about 84 minutes away and Stawell about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a skate park, Birchip Skate Park in Birchip is about 29 minutes' drive from Wycheproof. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 25 minutes' drive from Wycheproof. From Wycheproof, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Hall Corner Park in Nullawil (21 min), Rex Theatre in Charlton (25 min), Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (25 min), Birchip Skate Park in Birchip (29 min), Boort Swimming Pool in Boort (45 min) and Donald BMX Track in Donald (40 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Wycheproof. Free days on the Wycheproof Wetland Walking trail and in Wycheproof's parks.
