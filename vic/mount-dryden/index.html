Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Mount Dryden in the Northern Grampians area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Mount Dryden. For walks and rides, Grampians Peaks Trail and Heatherlie Quarry cross Mount Dryden.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mount Dryden. The closest to Mount Dryden is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 43 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mount Dryden, so most trips start by car. By car from Mount Dryden, Stawell is about 31 minutes away and Ararat about 55 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mount Dryden is Halls Gap Swimming Pool in Halls Gap, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Mount Dryden is Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Mount Dryden is the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Mount Dryden is Halls Gap Zoo in Halls Gap, about 22 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Stawell, about 31 minutes from Mount Dryden by car, with 6 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Mount Dryden are Halls Gap Swimming Pool in Halls Gap (21 min), the playground at Halls Gap Picnic Reserve in Halls Gap (21 min), Halls Gap Zoo in Halls Gap (22 min), Brambuk Cultural Centre in Halls Gap (25 min), MOCO Gallery in Halls Gap (25 min) and Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans (34 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Mount Dryden on the Grampians Peaks Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Mount Dryden, Halls Gap has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a zoo or wildlife park. Lake Lonsdale and Fyans Creek also border Mount Dryden.
