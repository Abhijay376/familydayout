Luxford Reserve

Park in Noble Park · Basic info

Luxford Reserve is a park in Noble Park, in the Greater Dandenong City Council area, good for all ages. Lawn Rd/Heatherton Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 465 m away.
