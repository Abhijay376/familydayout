Playground at Naomi Court Reserve

Playground in Noble Park · Basic info

Playground at Naomi Court Reserve is a public playground in Noble Park, in the Greater Dandenong City area. Playground at Rex Corrigan Reserve is another option about 600 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 880 m from the playground, and bus 824 stops at Walnut Cres/Henderson Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
