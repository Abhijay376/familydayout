Playground at Kiwanis Park

Playground in Noble Park · Basic info

Playground at Kiwanis Park in Noble Park is a public playground in the Greater Dandenong City area. The Djerring Trail passes about 320 m away, and Fab Kebab Cafe is about 450 m away if you need food. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and buses 812 and 813 stop at Goodman Dr/Chandler Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
