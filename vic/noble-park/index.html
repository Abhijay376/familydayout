Noble Park in the Greater Dandenong area has 19 playgrounds and 13 parks and reserves in our data. Noble Park also has a pool and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Noble Park is Ross Reserve, covering about 14 hectares. Noble Park Reserve and William Robert Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Noble Park include Copas Park, Bennett Reserve and Luxford Reserve. Playgrounds around Noble Park include the playground at Copas Park, the playground at Kiwanis Park and the playground at Ross Reserve. Our data shows 11 public toilets and 11 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Noble Park. Places to visit in Noble Park include Noble Park Aquatic Centre. For older kids in Noble Park there's Noble Park Skatepark. Noble Plaza is the local shopping centre. For walks and rides, EastLink Trail and Djerring Trail cross Noble Park. Our data lists 10 cafes and family restaurants in Noble Park for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Noble Park Railway Station (metro: Cranbourne Line and Pakenham Line), Sandown Park Railway Station (metro: Cranbourne Line and Pakenham Line) and Yarraman Railway Station (metro: Cranbourne Line and Pakenham Line) in Noble Park. Buses 709, 800, 811, 812, 813, 816, 824 and 979 stop in Noble Park. By car from Noble Park, Dandenong is about 6 minutes away, Ringwood about 19 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 28 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Springvale Library in Springvale is about 4 minutes' drive from Noble Park. The closest beach to Noble Park is Edithvale Beach in Edithvale, about 14 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Reading Cinema in Dandenong is about 7 minutes' drive from Noble Park. The closest indoor play centre to Noble Park is Småland in Springvale, about 7 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 19 playgrounds across Noble Park. Teens who skate or ride, with Noble Park Skatepark in Noble Park. Free days on the EastLink Trail and in Noble Park's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Keysborough, next to Noble Park, has 32 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Noble Park, Dandenong has 21 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Noble Park, Dandenong North has 24 playgrounds. Next door to Noble Park, Springvale has 16 playgrounds and a library. Noble Park North and Springvale South also border Noble Park.
