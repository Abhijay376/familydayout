Playground at Mills Reserve

Playground in Noble Park · Basic info

Playground at Mills Reserve is a public playground in Noble Park, in the Greater Dandenong City area. The Djerring Trail is about 130 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 360 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 450 m from the playground, and bus 811 stops at Ian St/Heatherton Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
