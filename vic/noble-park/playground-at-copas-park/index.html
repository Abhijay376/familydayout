Playground at Copas Park

Playground in Noble Park · Official details

This is a public playground in Noble Park. The Djerring Trail passes about 200 m away, and Noble Park Aquatic Centre is about 570 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 400 m away, and buses 709 and 816 stop at Copas Park/Noble St, a 1-minute walk away.
