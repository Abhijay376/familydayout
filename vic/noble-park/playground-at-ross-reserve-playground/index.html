Playground at Ross Reserve

Playground in Noble Park · Official details

This is a public playground in Noble Park. The Djerring Trail is about 300 m away, and Noble Park Aquatic Centre is about 580 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 370 m from the playground, and bus 811 stops at James St/Heatherton Rd, a 9-minute walk away.
