Barbara Allpress Park

Park in Newborough · Basic info

Barbara Allpress Park is a park in Newborough, in the Latrobe City Council area, good for all ages. Leith St/Murray Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 748 m away with a baby change table.
