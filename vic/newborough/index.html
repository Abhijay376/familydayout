Our data lists 11 playgrounds and 18 parks and reserves in Newborough, in the Latrobe area.

Landmarks

College Park is the largest mapped park in Newborough, at about 12 hectares. Ollerton Avenue Bushland Reserve and Moe Botanical Gardens are the next largest. Newborough also has Howitt Reserve, Newborough Square and Avon Court Reserve. Playgrounds in Newborough include the playground at Henry White Reserve, the playground at Harvey Street Reserve and the playground at Moe Botanical Gardens. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Newborough. The Moe-Yallourn Rail Trail passes through Newborough. Our data lists 7 cafes and family restaurants in Newborough for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Newborough has no train station; Moe Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 3.1 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the 1, 5, 8 and 14 routes stop in Newborough. V/Line coaches stop in Newborough. By car from Newborough, Traralgon is about 23 minutes away, Warragul about 30 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Diving Pool in Moe is about 5 minutes' drive from Newborough. For a library, Morwell Library in Morwell is about 12 minutes' drive from Newborough. For a skate park, Moe Skate Park in Moe is about 5 minutes' drive from Newborough. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 14 minutes' drive from Newborough. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 29 minutes' drive from Newborough.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 11 playgrounds across Newborough. Free days on the Moe-Yallourn Rail Trail and in Newborough's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Newborough, Moe has 14 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Newborough, Yallourn North has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Newborough, Yallourn has 1 playground. Hernes Oak also borders Newborough.
