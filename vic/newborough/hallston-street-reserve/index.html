Hallston Street Reserve

Park in Newborough · Basic info

Hallston Street Reserve is a park in Newborough, in the Latrobe City Council area, good for all ages. Old Sale Rd/John Field Dr (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1380 m away with a baby change table.
