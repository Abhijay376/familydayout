Our data lists 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in Kingsville, in the Maribyrnong area.

Landmarks

Kingsville also has Bell Reserve and Beevers Reserve. Playgrounds around Kingsville include the playground at Beevers Reserve. Our data lists 5 cafes and family restaurants in Kingsville for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Kingsville has no train station; West Footscray Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 600 metres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 411, 412, 431, 472 and 947 stop in Kingsville. By car from Kingsville, Sunshine is about 11 minutes away, Werribee about 21 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 16 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kingsville is Elite Swimming in West Footscray, about 2 minutes' drive. The closest library to Kingsville is Footscray Library in Footscray, about 4 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Kingsville is Sandridge Beach in Port Melbourne, about 10 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Sk8er Extreme Skate Park in Yarraville is about 3 minutes' drive from Kingsville. The closest cinema to Kingsville is Sun Theatre in Yarraville, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Kingsville is Dizzy in Footscray, about 4 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville is about 12 minutes' drive from Kingsville. The nearest larger centre is Sunshine, about 11 minutes from Kingsville by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Kingsville are the playground at Cruickshank Park in Yarraville (2 min), Elite Swimming in West Footscray (2 min), Sk8er Extreme Skate Park in Yarraville (3 min), Cruikshank Park BMX track in Yarraville (4 min), Footscray Library in Footscray (4 min) and Sun Theatre in Yarraville (5 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Kingsville.

Nearby suburbs

Yarraville, next to Kingsville, has 14 playgrounds, a cinema and a skate park. Next door to Kingsville, West Footscray has 6 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Seddon, next to Kingsville, has 3 playgrounds.
