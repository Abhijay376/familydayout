Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Buln Buln in the Baw Baw area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Buln Buln. The closest to Buln Buln is Warragul Railway Station (V/Line), about 7.2 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 89 stops in Buln Buln. By car from Buln Buln, Warragul is about 9 minutes away, Pakenham about 38 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 82 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Buln Buln is Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest library to Buln Buln is Warragul Library in Warragul, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Buln Buln is Bmx in Warragul, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Buln Buln is Warragul Cinema in Warragul, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Buln Buln is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Buln Buln is Wildlife Trail in Tynong North, about 25 minutes' drive. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Buln Buln, about 9 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Buln Buln, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Ellen Close Linear Reserve in Warragul (8 min), Charles Street Park in Warragul (8 min), Warragul Library in Warragul (9 min), Warragul Cinema in Warragul (9 min), The Alley Warragul in Warragul (10 min) and Bmx in Warragul (10 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Buln Buln.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Buln Buln, Warragul has 22 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Buln Buln, Rokeby has 1 playground. Crossover, Buln Buln East, Bravington and Brandy Creek also border Buln Buln.
