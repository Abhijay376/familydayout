Mooralla is in the Southern Grampians area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Beear State Forest is the largest mapped park in Mooralla, at about 1,784 hectares. Victoria Lagoon Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet and 2 off-leash dog parks in Mooralla.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mooralla. The closest to Mooralla is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 73 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mooralla, so most trips start by car. By car from Mooralla, Hamilton is about 37 minutes away and Horsham about 69 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mooralla is Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest library to Mooralla is Hamilton Library in Hamilton, about 37 minutes' drive. For a skate park, hamilton skate park in Hamilton is about 39 minutes' drive from Mooralla. The closest cinema to Mooralla is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 37 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 37 minutes from Mooralla by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Mooralla are the playground at Cavendish Town Square Memorial Park in Cavendish (16 min), Glendinning Street Playground in Balmoral (36 min), Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral (36 min), Big Woolbales & Campe Motor Museum in Hamilton (35 min), Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Hamilton (37 min) and Mud Gallery in Hamilton (37 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mooralla.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Mooralla, Cavendish has 1 playground. Glenisla and Gatum also border Mooralla.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Mooralla, without traffic.
