Shelley is in the Towong area, and our data lists 5 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Mountain Creek Education Area is the largest mapped park in Shelley, at about 749 hectares. Lucyvale Creek Reference Area and Pheasant Creek Flora Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Shelley include Avondale Gardens and Berringama Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Shelley. Picnic areas in Shelley include Avondale Gardens. For walks and rides, High Country Rail Trail crosses Shelley.

Getting around

Shelley has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 57 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Shelley, so most trips start by car. By car from Shelley, Wodonga is about 67 minutes away and Wangaratta about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta is about 33 minutes' drive from Shelley. For a library, Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta is about 33 minutes' drive from Shelley. For a beach, Pebble Beach in Walwa is about 35 minutes' drive from Shelley. The closest cinema to Shelley is Corryong Cinema in Corryong, about 34 minutes' drive. From Shelley, the nearest places for kids by road are The Spotted Goanna in Walwa (29 min), Granya Museum in Granya (35 min), Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta (33 min), Pebble Beach in Walwa (35 min), Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta (33 min) and Corryong Cinema in Corryong (34 min).

Best for

Free days on the High Country Rail Trail and in Shelley's parks.
