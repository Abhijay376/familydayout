Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Strathmerton, in the Moira area. Beyond the parks, Strathmerton has a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Strathmerton is Brian Pinnuck Memorial Park / Lions Park, covering about 1 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Strathmerton. For older kids in Strathmerton there's Strathmerton Community Skate Park.

Getting around

Strathmerton has no train station; Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 51 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Strathmerton. By car from Strathmerton, Shepparton is about 47 minutes away and Benalla about 73 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Strathmerton is Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest library to Strathmerton is Barooga Library in Cobram, about 18 minutes' drive. For a beach, Carter Beach in Ulupna is about 28 minutes' drive from Strathmerton. For a cinema, Cobram Cinema in Cobram is about 14 minutes' drive from Strathmerton. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Strathmerton, about 47 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Strathmerton, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Federation Park in Cobram (14 min), the skate park at Federation Park in Cobram (14 min), Cobram Cinema in Cobram (14 min), Carter Beach in Ulupna (28 min), Labbett Beach in Ulupna (29 min) and the playground at Cobram Regional Park in Cobram (17 min).

Best for

Teens who skate or ride, with Strathmerton Community Skate Park in Strathmerton.

Nearby suburbs

Koonoomoo, next to Strathmerton, has 1 playground, a library and a beach. Next door to Strathmerton, Yalca has a beach. Ulupna, next to Strathmerton, has a beach. Next door to Strathmerton, Mywee has a beach. Bearii, Yarroweyah and Katunga also border Strathmerton.
