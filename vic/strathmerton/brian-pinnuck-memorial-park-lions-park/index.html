Brian Pinnuck Memorial Park / Lions Park

Park in Strathmerton · Basic info

Brian Pinnuck Memorial Park / Lions Park is a park in Strathmerton, in the Moira Shire Council area, good for all ages. Maguire St/Murray Valley Hwy (coach) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 85 m away.
