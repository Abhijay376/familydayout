Strathmerton Community Skate Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Strathmerton · Basic info

Strathmerton Community Skate Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Strathmerton, in the Moira Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Maguire St/Murray Valley Hwy (coach) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 63 m away.
