Getting around

There's no train station in Skenes Creek. The closest to Skenes Creek is Birregurra Railway Station (V/Line), about 44 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Colac - Marengo via Apollo Bay route stop in Skenes Creek. V/Line coaches stop in Skenes Creek. By car from Skenes Creek, Colac is about 65 minutes away, Torquay about 1.5 hours and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Skenes Creek is Apollo Bay Community Pool in Apollo Bay, about 8 minutes' drive. For a beach, White Crest in Wongarra is about 7 minutes' drive from Skenes Creek. The closest skate park to Skenes Creek is the skate park at Forrest Common in Forrest, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Skenes Creek is Mechanics Hall in Apollo Bay, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Skenes Creek is Skate Bowl in Apollo Bay, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Skenes Creek are Old Cable Station Museum in Apollo Bay (4 min), Skate Bowl in Apollo Bay (8 min), the playground at Foreshore Reserve in Apollo Bay (8 min), Mechanics Hall in Apollo Bay (9 min), Apollo Bay Community Pool in Apollo Bay (8 min) and White Crest in Wongarra (7 min).
