Kilmore East in the Mitchell area has 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Kilmore East is Kilmore East Bushland Reserve, covering about 4 hectares. Nanny Creek Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Kilmore East.

Getting around

Kilmore East has a train station: Kilmore East Station (V/Line: Seymour - Melbourne via Broadmeadows and Shepparton - Melbourne via Seymour). Buses on the Kilmore Town Service (Link Bus) route stop in Kilmore East. V/Line coaches stop in Kilmore East. By car from Kilmore East, Seymour is about 38 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 69 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kilmore East is Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore, about 7 minutes' drive. For a library, Kilmore Library in Kilmore is about 6 minutes' drive from Kilmore East. For a skate park, Kilmore Skate Park in Kilmore is about 6 minutes' drive from Kilmore East. The closest cinema to Kilmore East is United Cinemas in Craigieburn, about 41 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Kilmore East is Timezone in Craigieburn, about 41 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Funfields in Whittlesea is about 38 minutes' drive from Kilmore East. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 38 minutes from Kilmore East by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Kilmore East, the nearest places for kids by road are Kilmore Skate Park in Kilmore (6 min), Kilmore Library in Kilmore (6 min), the playground at Hudson Park in Kilmore (6 min), Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore (7 min), Link Reserve Playground in Kilmore (9 min) and Tramway Heritage Centre in Bylands (13 min).
