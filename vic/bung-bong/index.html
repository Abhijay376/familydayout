Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Bung Bong, in the Central Goldfields area.

Landmarks

Bung Bong Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Bung Bong, at about 421 hectares. Bung Bong Streamside Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bung Bong. The closest to Bung Bong is Talbot Railway Station (V/Line), about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bung Bong, so most trips start by car. By car from Bung Bong, Maryborough is about 14 minutes away, Castlemaine about 58 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot is about 19 minutes' drive from Bung Bong. The closest library to Bung Bong is Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough, about 14 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Alexander Park in Learmonth is about 44 minutes' drive from Bung Bong. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 14 minutes from Bung Bong by car, with a library. The closest places for kids by road from Bung Bong are the playground at Avoca Lions Park in Avoca (9 min), Courthouse in Avoca (9 min), Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground in Talbot (19 min), Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (19 min), Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (14 min) and Lee Medlyn Bottle Museum in Clunes (32 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bung Bong.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Bung Bong, Avoca has 1 playground. Lamplough, Homebush, Amherst and Adelaide Lead also border Bung Bong.
