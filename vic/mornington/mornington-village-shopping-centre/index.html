Mornington Village Shopping Centre

Shopping centre in Mornington · Basic info

Mornington Village Shopping Centre is a shopping centre in Mornington, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. The Bays Hospital/Main St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 325 m away.
