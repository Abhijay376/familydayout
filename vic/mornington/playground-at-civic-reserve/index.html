Playground at Civic Reserve

Playground in Mornington · Basic info

Playground at Civic Reserve is a public playground in Mornington, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire area. The Harrap Creek Trail passes about 210 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 50 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 785 stops at Maxwell St/Dunns Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
