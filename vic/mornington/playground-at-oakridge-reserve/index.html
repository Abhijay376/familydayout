Playground at Oakridge Reserve

Playground in Mornington · Basic info

Playground at Oakridge Reserve is a public playground in Mornington, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.5 km away, and bus 784 stops at Exford Dr/Bungowe Rd, a 9-minute walk away.
