Our data lists 6 parks and reserves in Selwyn, in the Alpine area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Selwyn is Alpine National Park, covering about 663,156 hectares. Wongungurra River headwaters Natural Catchment Area and Blue Rag Creek Natural Catchment Area are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Selwyn include The Bend, Basalt Knob and Blue Rag Reference Area. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Selwyn. Picnic areas in Selwyn include Basalt Knob. For camping, there's The Bend. For walks and rides, Australian Alps Walking Track crosses Selwyn.

Getting around

There's no train station in Selwyn. The closest to Selwyn is Stratford Railway Station (V/Line), about 100 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Selwyn, so most trips start by car. By car from Selwyn, Bright is about 2.5 hours away and Wangaratta about 3 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Free days on the Australian Alps Walking Track and in Selwyn's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Harrietville, next to Selwyn, has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Selwyn, Hotham Heights has 1 playground. Buckland and Abbeyard also border Selwyn.
