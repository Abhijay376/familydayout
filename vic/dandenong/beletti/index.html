Beletti, Dandenong

Cafe or restaurant in Dandenong · Basic info

Beletti is a cafe or restaurant in Dandenong, in the Greater Dandenong City Council area, a place to eat during a day out. Walker St/Lonsdale St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 262 m away with a baby change table.
