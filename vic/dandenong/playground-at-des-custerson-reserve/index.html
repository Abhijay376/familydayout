Playground at Des Custerson Reserve

Playground in Dandenong · Official details

This is a public playground in Dandenong. The Dandenong Creek Trail is about 100 m away, and Playground at Dandenong Creek Reserve is another option about 290 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 680 m from the playground, and buses 857 and 890 stop at Noon St/Morwell Ave, a 3-minute walk away.
