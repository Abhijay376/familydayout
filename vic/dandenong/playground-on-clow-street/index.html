Playground at Robert Booth Reserve-(South)

Playground in Dandenong · Basic info

Playground at Robert Booth Reserve-(South) is a public playground in Dandenong, in the Greater Dandenong City area. The Dandenong Creek Trail is about 180 m away, and for food, Mix Grill and Pizza is about 210 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 180 m from the playground, and buses 843, 845 and 982 stop at Robert Booth Reserve/Clow St, a 3-minute walk away.
