Playground at Apex Park

Playground in Dandenong · Basic info

Playground at Apex Park in Dandenong is a public playground in the Greater Dandenong City area. The Herbert St Connection to Dandenong Creek Trail passes about 290 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 560 m away, and buses 802, 804, 811 and 861 stop at Dandenong Hospital/Cleeland St, a 2-minute walk away.
