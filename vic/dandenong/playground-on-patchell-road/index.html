Playground at Norine Cox Reserve

Playground in Dandenong · Official details

Playground at Norine Cox Reserve is a public playground in Dandenong, in the Greater Dandenong City area. The Dandenong South Trail passes about 260 m away, and Playground at Des Custerson Reserve is another option about 520 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 70 m away, and buses 857 and 890 stop at Kirkham Rd/Morwell Ave, a 4-minute walk away.
