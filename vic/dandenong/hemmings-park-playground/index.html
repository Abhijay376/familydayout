Hemmings Park Playground

Playground in Dandenong · Official details

Hemmings Park Playground is a public playground in Dandenong, in the Greater Dandenong City area. The Herbert St Connection to Dandenong Creek Trail is about 330 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 60 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus 800 stops at Princes Hwy/Potter St, a 4-minute walk away.
