Gardiner Reserve

Park in Dandenong · Basic info

Gardiner Reserve is a park in Dandenong, in the Greater Dandenong City Council area, good for all ages. Rylands Rd/Benga Ave (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 763 m away.
