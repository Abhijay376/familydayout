Dandenong Park

Park in Dandenong · Official details

Dandenong Park is a park in Dandenong, in the Greater Dandenong City Council area, good for all ages. St Marys PS/McCrae St (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 210 m away with a baby change table.

Questions parents ask

Where is Dandenong Park?
Dandenong Park is in Dandenong, Victoria.
Is Dandenong Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Dandenong Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 210 m away, with a baby change table.
How do I get to Dandenong Park by public transport?
The closest stop is St Marys PS/McCrae St (routes 828, 844, 892, 893), a 5-minute walk.
