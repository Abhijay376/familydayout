Des Custerson Reserve

Park in Dandenong · Official details

Des Custerson Reserve is a park in Dandenong, in the Greater Dandenong City Council area, good for all ages. Noon St/Morwell Ave (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 713 m away with a baby change table.
