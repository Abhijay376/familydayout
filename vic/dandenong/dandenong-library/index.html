Dandenong Library

Library in Dandenong · Basic info

Dandenong Library is a library in Dandenong, in the Greater Dandenong City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Walker St/Lonsdale St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 20 m away with a baby change table.
