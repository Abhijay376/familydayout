Playground at Dandenong Creek Reserve

Playground in Dandenong · Official details

Playground at Dandenong Creek Reserve is a public playground in Dandenong, in the Greater Dandenong City area. The Dandenong Creek Trail is about 50 m away, and Playground at Keneally Street (66-80) Reserve is another option about 100 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 710 m from the playground, and bus 857 stops at Francis St/Dalgety St, a 1-minute walk away.
