Playground at Fotheringham Reserve (North) - (Neighbourhood)

Playground in Dandenong · Official details

Playground at Fotheringham Reserve (North) - (Neighbourhood) is a public playground in Dandenong, in the Greater Dandenong City area. The EastLink Trail is about 70 m away, and Playground at Fotheringham Reserve (South) - (Local) is another option about 440 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 800 stops at King George Pde/Princes Hwy, a 7-minute walk away.
