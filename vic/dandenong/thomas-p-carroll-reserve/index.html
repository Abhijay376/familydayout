Thomas P Carroll Reserve

Park in Dandenong · Basic info

Thomas P Carroll Reserve is a park in Dandenong, in the Greater Dandenong City Council area, good for all ages. Thomas Carroll Reserve/Box St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1260 m away.
