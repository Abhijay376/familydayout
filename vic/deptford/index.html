Deptford in the East Gippsland area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Deptford also has Deptford and Stantons Place. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Deptford. Picnic areas in Deptford include Deptford and Stantons Place. For walks and rides, Slaughter Walking Track and Tubal Cain Walk cross Deptford.

Getting around

Deptford has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Deptford, so most trips start by car. By car from Deptford, Bairnsdale is about 63 minutes away and Lakes Entrance about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest places for kids by road from Deptford are the playground at Howitt Park in Wy Yung (59 min), Howitt Park in Wy Yung (60 min), Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale (60 min), Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale (61 min), Krowathunkoolong Keeping Place in Bairnsdale (61 min) and Bairnsdale Museum in Bairnsdale (62 min).

Best for

Free days on the Slaughter Walking Track and in Deptford's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Marthavale, Fairy Dell, Double Bridges and Bullumwaal also border Deptford.
