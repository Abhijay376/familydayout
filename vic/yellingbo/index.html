Our data lists 5 parks and reserves in Yellingbo, in the Yarra Ranges area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Yellingbo is Yellingbo Nature Conservation Area, covering about 607 hectares. Sheep Station Creek Nature Conservation Area and Yellingbo Nature Conservation Area - LR Nature Conservation Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Yellingbo include Nangana Bushland Reserve and Shepherd Creek Natural Features Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Yellingbo. For walks and rides, Manna Gum Track, Tomahawk Creek Track and Possum Track cross Yellingbo.

Getting around

Yellingbo has no train station; Lilydale Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yellingbo, so most trips start by car. By car from Yellingbo, Lilydale is about 20 minutes away, Healesville about 21 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 61 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Yellingbo is Seville Water Play Park in Seville, about 7 minutes' drive. For a library, Yarra Junction public library in Yarra Junction is about 14 minutes' drive from Yellingbo. For a skate park, the skate park at Yarra Junction Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Yarra Junction is about 14 minutes' drive from Yellingbo. For a cinema, The Memo, Healesville in Healesville is about 21 minutes' drive from Yellingbo. The closest indoor play centre to Yellingbo is PlayPlace in Mooroolbark, about 26 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Lyre Bird enclosure in Badger Creek is about 17 minutes' drive from Yellingbo. Lilydale is the closest bigger centre to Yellingbo, about 20 minutes away by car, and has 12 playgrounds, a pool and a library.

Best for

Free days on the Manna Gum Track and in Yellingbo's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Seville, next to Yellingbo, has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Yellingbo, Gembrook has 6 playgrounds and a skate park. Woori Yallock, next to Yellingbo, has 1 playground. Next door to Yellingbo, Silvan has 1 playground. Seville East, Nangana, Macclesfield and Hoddles Creek also border Yellingbo.
