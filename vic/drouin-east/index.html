Drouin East is in the Baw Baw area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Drouin East, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

The Warragul - Drouin Two Towns Trail and Two Towns Trail all pass through Drouin East.

Getting around

There's no train station in Drouin East. The closest to Drouin East is Drouin Railway Station (V/Line), about 3.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Drouin East, so most trips start by car. By car from Drouin East, Warragul is about 10 minutes away, Pakenham about 37 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 81 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Drouin East is Drouin Swimming Pool in Drouin, about 8 minutes' drive. For a library, Drouin Library in Drouin is about 8 minutes' drive from Drouin East. The closest skate park to Drouin East is Drouin Skate Park in Drouin, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Drouin East is Warragul Cinema in Warragul, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Drouin East is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 8 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Wildlife Trail in Tynong North is about 24 minutes' drive from Drouin East. The nearest larger centre is Warragul, about 10 minutes from Drouin East by car, with 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Drouin East are Harmon Drive Playground in Drouin (6 min), the playground at L G Boeyen Reserve in Drouin (6 min), Drouin Skate Park in Drouin (7 min), Drouin Swimming Pool in Drouin (8 min), Drouin Library in Drouin (8 min) and Bmx in Warragul (8 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Drouin East on the Warragul - Drouin Two Towns Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Warragul, next to Drouin East, has 22 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Drouin, next to Drouin East, has 19 playgrounds, a pool and a library.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Drouin East, without traffic.
