Ultima East is in the Swan Hill area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Ultima East, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Ultima East. The closest to Ultima East is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Ultima East, so most trips start by car. By car from Ultima East, Swan Hill is about 26 minutes away and Echuca about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Ultima East is Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest library to Ultima East is Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Ultima East is Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South, about 24 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill is about 26 minutes' drive from Ultima East. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Ultima East, about 26 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Ultima East are Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South (24 min), the playground at Jaycee Park in Swan Hill (27 min), Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill (26 min), Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill (26 min), Swan Hill Outdoor Pool in Swan Hill (26 min) and the playground at Riverside Park in Swan Hill (27 min).
