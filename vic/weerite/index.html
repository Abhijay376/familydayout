Weerite is in the Corangamite area, and our data lists 1 playground in the suburb.

Getting around

There's no train station in Weerite. The closest to Weerite is Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line), about 8.2 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Weerite, so most trips start by car. By car from Weerite, Colac is about 30 minutes away, Warrnambool about 65 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Weerite is Lismore Pool in Lismore, about 39 minutes' drive. For a library, Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac is about 32 minutes' drive from Weerite. The closest skate park to Weerite is Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Weerite is Colac Cinemas in Colac, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Weerite is Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac, about 30 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 30 minutes from Weerite by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Weerite, the nearest places for kids by road are Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (9 min), the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve in Camperdown (17 min), the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (28 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (30 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (30 min) and Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (32 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Weerite.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Weerite, Camperdown has 3 playgrounds and a skate park. Koallah, Kariah and Chocolyn also border Weerite.
