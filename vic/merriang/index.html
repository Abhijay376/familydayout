Merriang is in the Alpine area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Merriang, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Merriang. The closest to Merriang is Chiltern Railway Station (V/Line), about 45 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Merriang, so most trips start by car. By car from Merriang, Bright is about 32 minutes away and Wangaratta about 46 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Merriang is the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest library to Merriang is Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Merriang is Sandy Beach in Beechworth, about 32 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford is about 6 minutes' drive from Merriang. For a cinema, Sun Cinema Bright in Bright is about 32 minutes' drive from Merriang. Bright is the closest bigger centre to Merriang, about 32 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Merriang are the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford (6 min), Myrtleford Swimming Pool in Myrtleford (7 min), The Old School Museum in Myrtleford (6 min), Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford (6 min), Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford (6 min) and Cundy Park Playground in Myrtleford (6 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Merriang.

Nearby suburbs

Myrtleford, next to Merriang, has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Merriang, Buffalo River has 1 playground. Merriang South and Gapsted also border Merriang.
