Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Ellinbank in the Baw Baw area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Ellinbank. The closest to Ellinbank is Warragul Railway Station (V/Line), about 8.7 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Ellinbank, so most trips start by car. By car from Ellinbank, Warragul is about 14 minutes away, Pakenham about 48 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul is about 12 minutes' drive from Ellinbank. For a library, Warragul Library in Warragul is about 14 minutes' drive from Ellinbank. For a skate park, Warragul Skate Park in Warragul is about 12 minutes' drive from Ellinbank. The closest cinema to Ellinbank is Warragul Cinema in Warragul, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Ellinbank is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Ellinbank is Wildlife Trail in Tynong North, about 34 minutes' drive. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Ellinbank, about 14 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Ellinbank are Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul (12 min), Warragul Skate Park in Warragul (12 min), the playground at Burke Street Park in Warragul (12 min), Warragul Leisure Centre in Warragul (12 min), Burke Street Pump Track in Warragul (13 min) and Warragul Cinema in Warragul (12 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Ellinbank.

Nearby suburbs

Gainsborough, Cloverlea and Bona Vista also border Ellinbank.
