Willangie is in the Buloke area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Bellevue Tank Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Willangie, at about 112 hectares.

Getting around

Willangie has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 80 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Willangie, so most trips start by car. By car from Willangie, Swan Hill is about 76 minutes away and Horsham about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Woomelang Recreation Reserve Swimming Pool in Woomelang is about 11 minutes' drive from Willangie. The closest skate park to Willangie is Birchip Skate Park in Birchip, about 30 minutes' drive. From Willangie, the nearest places for kids by road are Woomelang Recreation Reserve Swimming Pool in Woomelang (11 min), the playground at Woomelang Rec Reserve in Woomelang (11 min), the playground at High way Park Woomelang in Woomelang (11 min), Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock (25 min), Birchip Skate Park in Birchip (30 min) and Birchip History Museum in Birchip (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Willangie.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Willangie, Woomelang has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Berriwillock, next to Willangie, has a pool. Marlbed and Banyan also border Willangie.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Willangie, without traffic.
