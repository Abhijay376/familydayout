Our data lists 2 playgrounds and 4 parks and reserves in Rupanyup, in the Yarriambiack area. Beyond the parks, Rupanyup has a pool and a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Rupanyup is Coorong Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 54 hectares. Mutton Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting) and Rupanyup I112 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Rupanyup include Rupanyup Memorial Park. Playgrounds in Rupanyup include the playground at Rupanyup Memorial Park and the playground at Rupanyup Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Rupanyup. In Rupanyup you'll also find Woods' Farming and Heritage Museum and Rupanyup Swimming Pool.

Getting around

There's no train station in Rupanyup. The closest to Rupanyup is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 77 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Murtoa to Rupanyup shuttle service and Horsham - Donald via Murtoa and Minyip routes stop in Rupanyup. V/Line coaches stop in Rupanyup. By car from Rupanyup, Horsham is about 38 minutes away and Stawell about 49 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a skate park, the skate park at Rabl Park in Murtoa is about 14 minutes' drive from Rupanyup. The closest cinema to Rupanyup is Centre Cinemas in Horsham, about 39 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Horsham, about 38 minutes from Rupanyup by car, with 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Rupanyup are Murtoa Museum Complex in Murtoa (14 min), the water play at Rabl Park in Murtoa (14 min), the skate park at Rabl Park in Murtoa (14 min), Velodrome in Horsham (38 min) and Centre Cinemas in Horsham (39 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Rupanyup.

Nearby suburbs

Murtoa, next to Rupanyup, has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Minyip, next to Rupanyup, has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Rich Avon West, next to Rupanyup, has 1 playground. Next door to Rupanyup, Lubeck has 1 playground. Wal Wal, Marnoo West, Callawadda and Banyena also border Rupanyup.
