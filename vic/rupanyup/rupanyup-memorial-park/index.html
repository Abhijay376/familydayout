Rupanyup Memorial Park

Park in Rupanyup · Basic info

Rupanyup Memorial Park is a park in Rupanyup, in the Yarriambiack Shire Council area, good for all ages. Walter St/Cromie St (coach) is a 11-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 73 m away.
