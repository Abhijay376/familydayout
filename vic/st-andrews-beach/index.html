Getting around

There's no train station in St Andrews Beach. The closest to St Andrews Beach is Bittern Railway Station (metro), about 31 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 786 stops in St Andrews Beach. By car from St Andrews Beach, Mornington is about 34 minutes away, Frankston about 41 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 83 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Peninsula Hot Springs in Fingal is about 8 minutes' drive from St Andrews Beach. For a library, Rosebud Library in Rosebud is about 17 minutes' drive from St Andrews Beach. The closest beach to St Andrews Beach is Gunnamatta Beach in Cape Schanck, about 10 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Rosebud Skate Park in Rosebud is about 15 minutes' drive from St Andrews Beach. The closest cinema to St Andrews Beach is Rosebud Cinemas in Rosebud, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to St Andrews Beach is Top Fun Shack in Rosebud, about 16 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Mornington, about 34 minutes from St Andrews Beach by car, with 22 playgrounds, a library, indoor play and a beach. From St Andrews Beach, the nearest places for kids by road are Peninsula Hot Springs in Fingal (8 min), Gunnamatta Beach in Cape Schanck (10 min), Number Sixteen Beach in Rye (12 min), the playground at Port Phillip Bay Coastal Reserve (COM) in Rye (12 min), Alba Thermal Springs & Spa ARCH in Fingal (11 min) and the playground at Moonah Waren Bushland Reserve in Rye (14 min).
