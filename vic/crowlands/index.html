Crowlands in the Pyrenees area has 5 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Glendhu Historic and Cultural Features Reserve is the largest mapped park in Crowlands, at about 43 hectares. Crowlands Natural Features Reserve and Astons Scour Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Crowlands also has Crowlands Recreation Reserve and Landsborough H30 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Crowlands. The closest to Crowlands is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the St Arnaud - Stawell via Ararat route stop in Crowlands. By car from Crowlands, Ararat is about 21 minutes away, Stawell about 30 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Crowlands is Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat, about 21 minutes' drive. For a library, Learning Resource Centre in Ararat is about 25 minutes' drive from Crowlands. For a skate park, David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat is about 22 minutes' drive from Crowlands. For a cinema, Astor Cinema in Ararat is about 22 minutes' drive from Crowlands. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Crowlands is Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell, about 31 minutes' drive. Ararat is the closest bigger centre to Crowlands, about 21 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Crowlands, the nearest places for kids by road are Community Playground in Landsborough (20 min), the playground at Smith Street Reserve in Ararat (20 min), David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat (22 min), Ararat Gallery TAMA in Ararat (21 min), Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat (21 min) and Langi Morgala Museum in Ararat (22 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Crowlands.

Nearby suburbs

Landsborough, next to Crowlands, has 1 playground. Joel South, Glenlofty, Eversley and Elmhurst also border Crowlands.
