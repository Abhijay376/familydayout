Seaspray is in the Wellington area, and our data lists 1 playground in the suburb. Seaspray also has a beach.

Landmarks

Our data shows 2 public toilets in Seaspray. In Seaspray you'll also find 90 Mile Beach.

Getting around

Seaspray has no train station; Sale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 32 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Sale - Seaspray via Longford route stop in Seaspray. By car from Seaspray, Sale is about 36 minutes away and Traralgon about 71 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Seaspray is Indoor pool in Sale, about 36 minutes' drive. For a library, Sale Library in Sale is about 35 minutes' drive from Seaspray. The closest skate park to Seaspray is Sale Skate Park in Sale, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Seaspray is Sale Cinemas in Sale, about 36 minutes' drive. Sale is the closest bigger centre to Seaspray, about 36 minutes away by car, and has 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Seaspray are 90 Mile Beach Marine National Park in Giffard (15 min), Golden Beach Community Centre Playground in Golden Beach (40 min), Historic Gunpowder Magazine in Sale (34 min), Sale Historical Museum in Sale (34 min), Little McMillan Park Playground in Sale (35 min) and Sale Skate Park in Sale (35 min).
