Getting around

Byaduk North has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 71 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Byaduk North. By car from Byaduk North, Hamilton is about 16 minutes away and Portland about 60 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Byaduk North is Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Hamilton, about 18 minutes' drive. For a library, Hamilton Library in Hamilton is about 16 minutes' drive from Byaduk North. The closest skate park to Byaduk North is hamilton skate park in Hamilton, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Byaduk North is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 16 minutes' drive. Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Byaduk North, about 16 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Byaduk North are Branxholme Rec Reserve Playground in Branxholme (21 min), Hughan Park Playground in Hamilton (15 min), Hamilton History Centre in Hamilton (15 min), Mud Gallery in Hamilton (16 min), Hamilton Library in Hamilton (16 min) and Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton (16 min).
