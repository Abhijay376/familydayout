Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lindenow South is Saplings Morass Flora and Fauna Reserve, covering about 10 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Lindenow South.

Getting around

Lindenow South has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 17 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Lindenow South. By car from Lindenow South, Bairnsdale is about 21 minutes away and Sale about 44 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lindenow South is Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest library to Lindenow South is Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale, about 22 minutes' drive. For a beach, Boyfriend Beach in Forge Creek is about 34 minutes' drive from Lindenow South. The closest skate park to Lindenow South is Skate Park in Stratford, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Lindenow South is Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Lindenow South is Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow, about 28 minutes' drive. Bairnsdale is the closest bigger centre to Lindenow South, about 21 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Lindenow South are the playground at Amy Shand Park in Lindenow (4 min), Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale (21 min), Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale (22 min), Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale (23 min), Krowathunkoolong Keeping Place in Bairnsdale (22 min) and Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale (22 min).
