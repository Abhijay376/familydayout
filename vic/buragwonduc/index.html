Buragwonduc in the Wellington area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Carey State Forest is the largest mapped park in Buragwonduc, at about 13,852 hectares. The McMillans Walking Track, Wellington River Walking Track and Riggall Spur Track all pass through Buragwonduc.

Getting around

Buragwonduc has no train station; Stratford Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 62 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Buragwonduc, so most trips start by car. By car from Buragwonduc, Sale is about 2.5 hours away and Traralgon about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Free days on the McMillans Walking Track and in Buragwonduc's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Licola, Crookayan and Arbuckle also border Buragwonduc.
