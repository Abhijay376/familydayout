Market Square Playground

Playground in Mortlake · Basic info

Market Square Playground is a public playground in Mortlake, in the Moyne Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 350 m away, and Mortlake Community Centre is about 230 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 100 m away, and coaches stop at Webster St/Dunlop St, a 4-minute walk away.
