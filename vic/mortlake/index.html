Mortlake is in the Moyne area, and our data lists 2 playgrounds and 7 parks and reserves in the suburb. Mortlake also has a library and a skate park.

Landmarks

Mortlake Common Flora Reserve is the largest mapped park in Mortlake, at about 304 hectares. Mortlake N17 Lake Reserve and Mortlake N16 Lake Reserve are the next largest. Mortlake also has Market Square, Centenary Grove and Red Cross Square. Playgrounds around Mortlake include Market Square Playground and the playground at Tea Tree Lake Reserve. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Mortlake. Places to visit in Mortlake include Mortlake Community Centre. For older kids in Mortlake there's Mortlake Skate Park.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mortlake. The closest to Mortlake is Terang Railway Station (V/Line), about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Warrnambool - Mortlake route stop in Mortlake. V/Line coaches stop in Mortlake. By car from Mortlake, Warrnambool is about 41 minutes away, Colac about 67 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a cinema, Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool is about 42 minutes' drive from Mortlake. For a zoo or wildlife park, Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee is about 37 minutes' drive from Mortlake. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Mortlake, about 41 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Mortlake, the nearest places for kids by road are Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (32 min), Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum (33 min), Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (37 min) and Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool (42 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the library in Mortlake. Teens who skate or ride, with Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake.
