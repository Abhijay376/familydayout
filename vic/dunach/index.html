Dunach in the Central Goldfields area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Mount Greenock Geological Reserve is the largest mapped park in Dunach, at about 142 hectares.

Getting around

Dunach has no train station; Talbot Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 5.6 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Ballarat - Maryborough route stop in Dunach. By car from Dunach, Maryborough is about 22 minutes away, Ballarat about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Dunach is Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest library to Dunach is Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Dunach is Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick, about 26 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central is about 39 minutes' drive from Dunach. The closest indoor play centre to Dunach is Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point, about 43 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Dunach is Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East, about 42 minutes' drive. Maryborough is the closest bigger centre to Dunach, about 22 minutes away by car, and has a library. From Dunach, the nearest places for kids by road are Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground in Talbot (6 min), Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (6 min), Lee Medlyn Bottle Museum in Clunes (9 min), The Warehouse in Clunes (9 min), Clunes Swimming Pool in Clunes (10 min) and the playground at Queens Park in Clunes (10 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Dunach.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Dunach, Clunes has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Talbot, next to Dunach, has 1 playground and a pool. Mount Glasgow, Mount Cameron and Evansford also border Dunach.
