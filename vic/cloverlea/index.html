Cloverlea is in the Baw Baw area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Cloverlea, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Cloverlea has no train station; Yarragon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 6.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cloverlea, so most trips start by car. By car from Cloverlea, Warragul is about 16 minutes away, Traralgon about 45 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cloverlea is Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest library to Cloverlea is Warragul Library in Warragul, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Cloverlea is Warragul Skate Park in Warragul, about 15 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 15 minutes' drive from Cloverlea. The closest indoor play centre to Cloverlea is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Cloverlea is Wildlife Trail in Tynong North, about 35 minutes' drive. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Cloverlea, about 16 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Cloverlea, the nearest places for kids by road are Yarragon Childrens Playground Reserve in Yarragon (10 min), Station Gallery in Yarragon (9 min), the playground at Pederson Park in Warragul (16 min), Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul (14 min), Warragul Skate Park in Warragul (15 min) and Warragul Leisure Centre in Warragul (15 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Cloverlea.

Nearby suburbs

Yarragon, next to Cloverlea, has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Cloverlea, Darnum has 2 playgrounds. Gainsborough, Ellinbank and Bona Vista also border Cloverlea.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Cloverlea, without traffic.
