Our data lists 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in Coimadai, in the Moorabool area.

Landmarks

Merrimu Reservoir Picnic Area is the largest mapped park in Coimadai, at about 4 hectares. Coimadai Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Playgrounds in Coimadai include the playground at Merrimu Reservoir Picnic Area. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Coimadai. For walks and rides, Lerderderg Gorge Track crosses Coimadai.

Getting around

Coimadai has no train station; Bacchus Marsh Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 9.1 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Coimadai, so most trips start by car. By car from Coimadai, Bacchus Marsh is about 10 minutes away, Melton about 19 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 54 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Coimadai is Paul Saddler Swimland in Darley, about 7 minutes' drive. For a library, Lerderderg Library in Bacchus Marsh is about 10 minutes' drive from Coimadai. For a beach, Lionhead Beach in Pentland Hills is about 17 minutes' drive from Coimadai. For a skate park, the bmx track on Dundas Street in Darley is about 7 minutes' drive from Coimadai. For a cinema, Reading Cinemas in Melton West is about 18 minutes' drive from Coimadai. For an indoor play centre, Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes is about 37 minutes' drive from Coimadai. The nearest larger centre is Bacchus Marsh, about 10 minutes from Coimadai by car, with 6 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Coimadai, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Federation Park Recreation Reserve in Darley (6 min), the bmx track on Dundas Street in Darley (7 min), Paul Saddler Swimland in Darley (7 min), the skate park at Rotary Park in Bacchus Marsh (10 min), Lerderderg Library in Bacchus Marsh (10 min) and Bacchus Marsh Public childrens pool in Bacchus Marsh (10 min).

Best for

Free days on the Lerderderg Gorge Track and in Coimadai's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Coimadai, Darley has 13 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Coimadai, Gisborne has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Coimadai, Toolern Vale has 1 playground. Long Forest, Lerderderg and Bullengarook also border Coimadai.
