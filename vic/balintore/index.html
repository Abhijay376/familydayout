Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Balintore in the Colac Otway area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Balintore has no train station; Colac Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 7.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Balintore, so most trips start by car. By car from Balintore, Colac is about 23 minutes away, Geelong about 85 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Balintore is Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac, about 25 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt is about 27 minutes' drive from Balintore. The closest cinema to Balintore is Colac Cinemas in Colac, about 23 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac is about 24 minutes' drive from Balintore. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Balintore, about 23 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Balintore, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Red Rock Scenic Reserve in Alvie (18 min), the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (21 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (23 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (24 min), Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (25 min) and the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Balintore.

Nearby suburbs

Colac, next to Balintore, has 13 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. Coragulac also borders Balintore.
