Playground at Ardeer Community Park

Playground in Ardeer · Official details

Playground at Ardeer Community Park in Ardeer is a public playground in the Brimbank City area. The Sunshine Ardeer is about 70 m away, and Collenso Street Reserve Playground is another option about 500 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 530 m from the playground, and buses 400, 422 and 427 stop at Yallourn St/Forrest St, a 1-minute walk away.
