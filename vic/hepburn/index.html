Hepburn is in the Hepburn area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Hepburn, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Hepburn. For walks and rides, Goldfields Track crosses Hepburn.

Getting around

There's no train station in Hepburn. The closest to Hepburn is Castlemaine Railway Station (V/Line), about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Daylesford - Hepburn Springs, Ballan - Hepburn via Daylesford and Hepburn - Creswick via Daylesford routes stop in Hepburn. By car from Hepburn, Daylesford is about 6 minutes away, Castlemaine about 32 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford is about 6 minutes' drive from Hepburn. The closest library to Hepburn is Daylesford Library in Daylesford, about 6 minutes' drive. For a beach, Canoeing in Tylden is about 43 minutes' drive from Hepburn. For a skate park, Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick is about 30 minutes' drive from Hepburn. For a cinema, Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central is about 45 minutes' drive from Hepburn. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek is about 38 minutes' drive from Hepburn. Daylesford is the closest bigger centre to Hepburn, about 6 minutes away by car, and has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Hepburn are the playground at Hepburn Regional Park in Daylesford (3 min), Daylesford Library in Daylesford (6 min), Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford (6 min), Daylesford Spa Country Railway in Daylesford (8 min), the playground at J P Gleeson Memorial Park in Lyonville (20 min) and Little Gallery in Trentham (29 min).
