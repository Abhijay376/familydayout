Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Kenmare in the Hindmarsh area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Kenmare has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 141 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kenmare, so most trips start by car. By car from Kenmare, Horsham is about 85 minutes away and Swan Hill about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kenmare is Rainbow Pool in Rainbow, about 15 minutes' drive. From Kenmare, the nearest places for kids by road are Rainbow Pool in Rainbow (15 min), the playground at Beulah Lions Park in Beulah (20 min), the playground at Beulah Lake Park in Beulah (21 min), Hopetoun Swimming Pool in Hopetoun (27 min) and Wimmera-Mallee Pioneer Museum in Jeparit (33 min).

Best for
