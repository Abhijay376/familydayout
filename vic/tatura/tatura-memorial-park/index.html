Tatura Memorial Park

Park in Tatura · Official details

Tatura Memorial Park is a park in Tatura, in the Greater Shepparton City Council area, good for all ages. Hogan St/Fraser St (bus) is a 9-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 110 m away with a baby change table.

Questions parents ask

Where is Tatura Memorial Park?
Tatura Memorial Park is in Tatura, Victoria.
Is Tatura Memorial Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Tatura Memorial Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 110 m away, with a baby change table.
How do I get to Tatura Memorial Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Hogan St/Fraser St (routes Bendigo Station - Shepparton Station via Kyabram), a 9-minute walk.
