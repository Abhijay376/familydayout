Playground at Hopkins Street Park

Playground in Tatura · Basic info

This is a neighbourhood playground in Tatura, looked after by Greater Shepparton City Council. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet about 940 m away. It's easiest by car, about 2 minutes from Tatura.
