Playground at Lions Park

Playground in Tatura · Basic info

Playground at Lions Park in Tatura is a neighbourhood playground in the Greater Shepparton City area. Golden Wheel Chinese Restaurant is about 470 m away if you need food, and Playground on Hughes Street is another option about 250 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 870 m away. It's easiest by car, about 4 minutes from Tatura.
