Playground on Hughes Street

Playground in Tatura · Basic info

This is a public playground in Tatura. Older kids have a skate park about 240 m away, and Tatura Swimming complex is about 590 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 360 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 2 minutes from Tatura.
