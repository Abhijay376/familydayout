Tatura in the Greater Shepparton area has 8 playgrounds and 6 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, Tatura has a library, a pool, a museum or gallery and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tatura is Cussen Park, covering about 32 hectares. Cussen Park and Lions Park are the next largest. Playground at Tatura Memorial Park, Playground at Northlinks Reserve and Joe Ford Drive Playground are among the council's larger district playgrounds. Other parks and reserves in Tatura include Tatura Memorial Park, Wilma Wilson Gardens and Hopkins Street Reserve. Other playgrounds in Tatura include the playground at Lions Park and the playground at Hopkins Street Park. Our data shows 8 public toilets and 3 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Tatura. Places to visit in Tatura include Tatura Irrigation and Wartime Camps Museum, Tatura Swimming complex and Tatura Library. For older kids in Tatura there's the skate park at Tatura Memorial Park. Our data lists 4 cafes and family restaurants in Tatura for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tatura. The closest to Tatura is Mooroopna Railway Station (V/Line), about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Bendigo Station - Shepparton Station via Kyabram route stop in Tatura. V/Line coaches stop in Tatura. By car from Tatura, Shepparton is about 21 minutes away, Echuca about 60 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a beach, Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton is about 23 minutes' drive from Tatura. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 21 minutes' drive from Tatura. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 21 minutes' drive from Tatura. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kidstown in Mooroopna is about 19 minutes' drive from Tatura.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 8 playgrounds across Tatura. Rainy days at the library and museum or gallery in Tatura. Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at Tatura Memorial Park in Tatura.

Nearby suburbs

Merrigum, next to Tatura, has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Mooroopna North West, Harston, Gillieston and Dhurringile also border Tatura.
