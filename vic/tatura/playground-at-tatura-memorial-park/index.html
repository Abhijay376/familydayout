Playground at Tatura Memorial Park

Playground in Tatura · Official details

Playground at Tatura Memorial Park is one of the council's larger playgrounds in Tatura, in the Greater Shepparton City area. Older kids have a skate park about 100 m away, and Tatura Swimming complex is about 410 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus Bendigo Station - Shepparton Station via Kyabram stops at Hogan St/Fraser St, a 8-minute walk away.
