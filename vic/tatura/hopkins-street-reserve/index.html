Hopkins Street Reserve

Park in Tatura · Basic info

Hopkins Street Reserve is a park in Tatura, in the Greater Shepparton City Council area, good for all ages. Hogan St/Fraser St (bus) is a 20-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 939 m away.
