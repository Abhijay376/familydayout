Kevington in the Mansfield area has 15 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Kevington is Williams Creek Natural Catchment Area, covering about 1,012 hectares. Kevington also has Pines, Miners Hut and The Rapids. Our data shows 13 public toilets in Kevington. Picnic areas in Kevington include Bains Bridge, Miners Hut and Mount Terrible Hut. For camping, there's Blue Hole Reserve and Bulldog Flat. The Star of West Goldmine Track and Star Of West Goldmine Walk all pass through Kevington.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kevington. The closest to Kevington is Moe Railway Station (V/Line), about 91 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Mansfield - Woods Point via Jamieson route stop in Kevington. By car from Kevington, Mansfield is about 40 minutes away and Benalla about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Mansfield Outdoor Swimming Pool in Mansfield is about 41 minutes' drive from Kevington. The closest library to Kevington is Mansfield Library in Mansfield, about 39 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Kevington is the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield, about 40 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield is about 39 minutes' drive from Kevington. For a zoo or wildlife park, Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield is about 31 minutes' drive from Kevington. Mansfield is the closest bigger centre to Kevington, about 40 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Kevington, the nearest places for kids by road are Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield (31 min), Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield (39 min), Mansfield Library in Mansfield (39 min), the playground at Mansfield Botanic Park in Mansfield (40 min), the playground at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (40 min) and the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (40 min).
