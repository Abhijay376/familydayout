Gobur is in the Murrindindi area, and our data lists 7 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Gobur is Gobur Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 415 hectares. Unnamed P0310 Private Nature Reserve and Gobur Recreation Reserve are the next largest. Gobur also has Gobur G48 Bushland Reserve, Gobur G102 Bushland Reserve and Merton G103 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Gobur has no train station; Euroa Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 30 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gobur, so most trips start by car. By car from Gobur, Mansfield is about 51 minutes away, Seymour about 65 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra is about 31 minutes' drive from Gobur. For a library, Alexandra Library in Alexandra is about 30 minutes' drive from Gobur. For a skate park, BMX Track in Caveat is about 37 minutes' drive from Gobur. The nearest larger centre is Mansfield, about 51 minutes from Gobur by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Gobur, the nearest places for kids by road are Yarck Recreation Reserve Playground in Yarck (15 min), Creightons Creek Recreation Reserve Playground in Creightons Creek (31 min), BMX Track in Caveat (37 min), Alexandra Library in Alexandra (30 min), Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra (31 min) and Alexandra Timber Tramway and Museum in Alexandra (32 min).
