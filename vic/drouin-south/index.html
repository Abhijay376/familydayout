Drouin South is in the Baw Baw area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Drouin South, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Drouin South has no train station; Drouin Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 4.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Warragul Station - Poowong East via Drouin, Ripplebrook route stop in Drouin South. By car from Drouin South, Warragul is about 15 minutes away, Pakenham about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 80 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Drouin South is Drouin Swimming Pool in Drouin, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest library to Drouin South is Drouin Library in Drouin, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Drouin South is Drouin Skate Park in Drouin, about 6 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 14 minutes' drive from Drouin South. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 13 minutes' drive from Drouin South. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Drouin South is Wildlife Trail in Tynong North, about 24 minutes' drive. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Drouin South, about 15 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Drouin South are Drouin Skate Park in Drouin (6 min), Drouin Library in Drouin (7 min), the playground at Civic Park in Drouin (7 min), Water Play in Drouin (7 min), Drouin Swimming Pool in Drouin (7 min) and Bmx in Warragul (14 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Drouin South.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Drouin South, Drouin has 19 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Drouin South, Longwarry has 3 playgrounds. Hallora, next to Drouin South, has 1 playground. Lardner and Athlone also border Drouin South.
