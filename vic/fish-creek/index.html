Our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in Fish Creek, in the South Gippsland area. Fish Creek also has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

Doomburrin B.R is the largest mapped park in Fish Creek, at about 2 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Fish Creek. Places to visit in Fish Creek include Ride the Wild Goat. For walks and rides, Great Southern Rail Trail and Hoddle Mountain Trail cross Fish Creek.

Getting around

There's no train station in Fish Creek. The closest to Fish Creek is Yarragon Railway Station (V/Line), about 55 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Fish Creek. By car from Fish Creek, Leongatha is about 29 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 41 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Fish Creek is Foster Outdoor Pool in Foster, about 11 minutes' drive. For a library, Foster Library in Foster is about 11 minutes' drive from Fish Creek. For a beach, Sandy Point (Waratah Bay) in Sandy Point is about 23 minutes' drive from Fish Creek. The closest skate park to Fish Creek is the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Fish Creek is Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha, about 29 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Leongatha, about 29 minutes from Fish Creek by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Fish Creek are Foster Outdoor Pool in Foster (11 min), Stockyard Gallery in Foster (11 min), Foster Library in Foster (11 min), Lance Moon Park in Yanakie (15 min), the playground at Gale Street Reserve in Waratah Bay (20 min) and Sandy Point (Waratah Bay) in Sandy Point (23 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Fish Creek. Free days on the Great Southern Rail Trail and in Fish Creek's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Foster, next to Fish Creek, has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Tarwin Lower, next to Fish Creek, has 1 playground and a beach. Sandy Point, next to Fish Creek, has 1 playground and a beach. Yanakie, next to Fish Creek, has 2 playgrounds. Waratah Bay, Buffalo, Walkerville and Foster North also border Fish Creek.
