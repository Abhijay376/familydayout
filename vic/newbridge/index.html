Our data lists 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in Newbridge, in the Loddon area.

Landmarks

Newbridge Recreation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Newbridge, at about 19 hectares. Tarnagulla I103 Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Newbridge.

Getting around

There's no train station in Newbridge. The closest to Newbridge is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 37 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Newbridge. By car from Newbridge, Bendigo is about 34 minutes away, Maryborough about 42 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Newbridge is Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly, about 20 minutes' drive. For a library, Kangaroo Flat Library in Kangaroo Flat is about 29 minutes' drive from Newbridge. The closest skate park to Newbridge is Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk, about 29 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 34 minutes' drive from Newbridge. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 34 minutes' drive from Newbridge. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Newbridge is The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo, about 39 minutes' drive. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Newbridge, about 34 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Newbridge are Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (20 min), Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (20 min), Westbury Boulevard Playspace in Maiden Gully (27 min), Graemar Court Playspace in Kangaroo Flat (29 min), Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk (29 min) and Kangaroo Flat Library in Kangaroo Flat (29 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Newbridge.

Nearby suburbs

Tarnagulla, next to Newbridge, has 1 playground. Laanecoorie, Bridgewater and Arnold also border Newbridge.
