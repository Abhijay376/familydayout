Newbridge Recreation Reserve

Park in Newbridge · Basic info

Newbridge Recreation Reserve is a park in Newbridge, in the Loddon Shire Council area, good for all ages. Newbridge General Store/Wimmera Hwy (coach) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 607 m away with a baby change table.
