Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Mincha West, in the Gannawarra area.

Landmarks

Rowland Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Mincha West, at about 140 hectares. Flannery Nature Conservation Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Mincha West has no train station; Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mincha West, so most trips start by car. By car from Mincha West, Echuca is about 80 minutes away and Swan Hill about 83 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mincha West is Leitchville Memorial Swimming Pool in Leitchville, about 43 minutes' drive. The closest library to Mincha West is Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang, about 36 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna is about 44 minutes' drive from Mincha West. From Mincha West, the nearest places for kids by road are Pyramid Hill & Districts Historical Museum in Pyramid Hill (16 min), the playground at C L King Memorial Park in Cohuna (43 min), Leitchville Memorial Swimming Pool in Leitchville (43 min), Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (36 min), Swimming Pool in Kerang (36 min) and the playground at Lake Meran W.R in Lake Meran (49 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mincha West.

Nearby suburbs

Leitchville, next to Mincha West, has 4 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Mincha West, Pyramid Hill has 1 playground. Macorna North, Macorna and Horfield also border Mincha West.
