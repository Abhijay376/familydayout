Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Mount Cameron in the Central Goldfields area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Mount Cameron has no train station; Clunes Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mount Cameron, so most trips start by car. By car from Mount Cameron, Maryborough is about 30 minutes away, Ballarat about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mount Cameron is Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot, about 19 minutes' drive. For a library, Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough is about 31 minutes' drive from Mount Cameron. For a skate park, Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick is about 44 minutes' drive from Mount Cameron. Maryborough is the closest bigger centre to Mount Cameron, about 30 minutes away by car, and has a library. From Mount Cameron, the nearest places for kids by road are Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground in Talbot (19 min), Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (19 min), Clunes Swimming Pool in Clunes (27 min), the playground at Queens Park in Clunes (27 min), Clunes Museum in Clunes (27 min) and The Warehouse in Clunes (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mount Cameron.

Nearby suburbs

Clunes, next to Mount Cameron, has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Glengower, Dunach and Cotswold also border Mount Cameron.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Mount Cameron, without traffic.
