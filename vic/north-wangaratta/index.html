North Wangaratta is in the Wangaratta area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in North Wangaratta is Reedy Creek K14 Streamside Reserve, covering about 17 hectares. Carraragarmungee B.R. is the next largest. Our data shows 3 public toilets in North Wangaratta. The Wangaratta - Eldorado Loop, Murray to Mountains Rail Trail: Wangaratta – Bright and Bowser-Wagunya Preferred Route all pass through North Wangaratta.

Getting around

There's no train station in North Wangaratta. The closest to North Wangaratta is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 3.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside North Wangaratta, so most trips start by car. By car from North Wangaratta, Wangaratta is about 7 minutes away and Benalla about 39 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to North Wangaratta is Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest library to North Wangaratta is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest beach to North Wangaratta is Sandy Beach in Beechworth, about 33 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to North Wangaratta is the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to North Wangaratta is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 4 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 7 minutes from North Wangaratta by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from North Wangaratta are the playground at Apex Park in Wangaratta (4 min), Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta (4 min), the playground at King George V Gardens in Wangaratta (4 min), Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (6 min), Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta (8 min) and the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta (8 min).

Best for

Free days on the Wangaratta - Eldorado Loop and in North Wangaratta's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to North Wangaratta, Wangaratta has 9 playgrounds, a pool and a library. East Wangaratta, Dockers Plains, Byawatha and Bowser also border North Wangaratta.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of North Wangaratta, without traffic.
