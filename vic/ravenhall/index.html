Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Ravenhall, in the Melton area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Ravenhall is Ravenhall Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 118 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Ravenhall. For walks and rides, Wellness Trail crosses Ravenhall. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Ravenhall for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Caroline Springs Railway Station (V/Line: Ararat - Melbourne via Ballarat, Ballarat/Wendouree - Melbourne via Melton and Maryborough - Melbourne via Ballarat) in Ravenhall. Buses 400, 460 and 462 stop in Ravenhall. V/Line coaches stop in Ravenhall. By car from Ravenhall, Sunshine is about 11 minutes away, Melton about 16 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 27 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Kingswim Derrimut in Derrimut is about 7 minutes' drive from Ravenhall. For a library, Caroline Springs Library in Caroline Springs is about 5 minutes' drive from Ravenhall. The closest beach to Ravenhall is Altona Beach in Altona, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Ravenhall is the skate park at Robert Bruce Reserve in Deer Park, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Ravenhall is Village Cinemas in Sunshine, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Ravenhall is Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes, about 16 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville is about 27 minutes' drive from Ravenhall. Sunshine is the closest bigger centre to Ravenhall, about 11 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Ravenhall are the playground at Springlake Village Park in Caroline Springs (2 min), Chisholm Park Wetlands Playground in Caroline Springs (2 min), Caroline Springs Library in Caroline Springs (5 min), Kingswim Derrimut in Derrimut (7 min), the skate park at Robert Bruce Reserve in Deer Park (7 min) and Brimbank Libraries Deer Park Branch in Deer Park (8 min).

Best for

Free days on the Wellness Trail and in Ravenhall's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Ravenhall, Caroline Springs has 31 playgrounds and a library. Truganina, next to Ravenhall, has 29 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Ravenhall, Deer Park has 17 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Derrimut, next to Ravenhall, has 7 playgrounds and a pool. Deanside and Burnside also border Ravenhall.
