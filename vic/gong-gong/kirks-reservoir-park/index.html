Kirks Reservoir Park

Park in Gong Gong · Basic info

Kirks Reservoir Park is a park in Gong Gong, in the Ballarat City Council area, good for all ages. Daylesford Rd/Cypress Rd (bus) is a 21-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 71 m away.
