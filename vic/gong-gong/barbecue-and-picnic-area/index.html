Barbecue and Picnic Area

Park in Gong Gong · Basic info

Barbecue and Picnic Area is a park in Gong Gong, in the Ballarat City Council area, good for all ages. Daylesford Rd/Cypress Rd (bus) is a 18-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 13 m away with a baby change table.
