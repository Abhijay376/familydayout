Getting around

There's no train station in Hallora. The closest to Hallora is Drouin Railway Station (V/Line), about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Hallora, so most trips start by car. By car from Hallora, Warragul is about 23 minutes away, Leongatha about 42 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Hallora is Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong, about 16 minutes' drive. For a library, Poowong library in Poowong is about 16 minutes' drive from Hallora. The closest skate park to Hallora is Drouin Skate Park in Drouin, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Hallora is Warragul Cinema in Warragul, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Hallora is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Hallora is Wildlife Trail in Tynong North, about 36 minutes' drive. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Hallora, about 23 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Hallora are Poowong library in Poowong (16 min), Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong (16 min), Drouin Skate Park in Drouin (18 min), Drouin Library in Drouin (18 min), the playground at Civic Park in Drouin (18 min) and Water Play in Drouin (18 min).
