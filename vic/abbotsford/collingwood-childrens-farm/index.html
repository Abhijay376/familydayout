Collingwood Children's Farm

Zoo, farm or wildlife park in Abbotsford · Official details

Collingwood Children's Farm is a zoo, farm or wildlife park in Abbotsford, in the Yarra City Council area, good for all ages. Clarke St/Johnston St (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 33 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: Monday to Friday: 9:30am to 3:30pm – last entry 3:00pm; Saturday and Sunday: 9:30am to 4:30pm – last entry 4:00pm.
