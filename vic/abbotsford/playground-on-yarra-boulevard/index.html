Playground on Yarra Boulevard

Playground in Abbotsford · Basic info

Playground on Yarra Boulevard in Abbotsford is a public playground in the Yarra City area. The Main Yarra Trail passes about 20 m away, and Horse Arena is about 190 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 170 m away, and buses 200, 207 and 241 stop at Clarke St/Johnston St, a 6-minute walk away.
