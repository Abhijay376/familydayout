Collingwood Town Hall Reserve

Park in Abbotsford · Basic info

Collingwood Town Hall Reserve is a park in Abbotsford, in the Yarra City Council area, good for all ages. Vere St/Hoddle St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 217 m away with a baby change table.
