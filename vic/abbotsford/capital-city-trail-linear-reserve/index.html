Capital City Trail Linear Reserve

Park in Abbotsford · Basic info

Capital City Trail Linear Reserve is a park in Abbotsford, in the Yarra City Council area, good for all ages. Clarke St/Johnston St (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 644 m away with a baby change table.
