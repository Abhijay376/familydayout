Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Bete Bolong, in the East Gippsland area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bete Bolong is Wombat Creek Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 688 hectares. Bete Bolong also has Hectors Campground. For camping, there's Hectors Campground .

Getting around

Bete Bolong has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 69 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bete Bolong, so most trips start by car. By car from Bete Bolong, Lakes Entrance is about 60 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 86 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost is about 17 minutes' drive from Bete Bolong. For a library, Orbost Library in Orbost is about 16 minutes' drive from Bete Bolong. The closest beach to Bete Bolong is Ewings Marsh in Waygara, about 32 minutes' drive. Lakes Entrance is the closest bigger centre to Bete Bolong, about 60 minutes away by car, and has 3 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a beach. From Bete Bolong, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Forest Park in Orbost (16 min), Orbost Library in Orbost (16 min), Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost (17 min), Ewings Marsh in Waygara (32 min), Sailors Grave Beach in Cape Conran (44 min) and Buchan: Natural Spring in Buchan (71 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bete Bolong.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Bete Bolong, Waygara has a beach. Newmerella, Jarrahmond, Buchan South and Bete Bolong North also border Bete Bolong.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Bete Bolong, without traffic.
