Landmarks

The biggest green space in Callignee is Traralgon South Flora and Fauna Reserve, covering about 848 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Callignee.

Getting around

Callignee has no train station; Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Callignee, so most trips start by car. By car from Callignee, Traralgon is about 25 minutes away, Warragul about 66 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Callignee is Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest library to Callignee is Morwell Library in Morwell, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Callignee is Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Callignee is Village 8 in Morwell, about 31 minutes' drive. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Callignee, about 25 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Callignee are the playground at Hourigan Park in Traralgon (24 min), the playground at Eric Taylor Reserve in Traralgon (24 min), Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon (26 min), Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon (28 min), Powerworks in Morwell (31 min) and Village 8 in Morwell (31 min).
