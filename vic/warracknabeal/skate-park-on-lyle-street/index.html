Skate park on Lyle Street

Skate, BMX or pump track in Warracknabeal · Basic info

Skate park on Lyle Street is a skate, bmx or pump track in Warracknabeal, in the Yarriambiack Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Scott St/Lyle St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 429 m away.
