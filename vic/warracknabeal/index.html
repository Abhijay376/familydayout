Our data lists 5 playgrounds and 8 parks and reserves in Warracknabeal, in the Yarriambiack area. On top of that, Warracknabeal has 2 museums or galleries.

Landmarks

Warracknabeal Rifle Range Flora Reserve is the largest mapped park in Warracknabeal, at about 71 hectares. Warracknabeal Streamside Reserve and Rotary Park are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Warracknabeal include Apex Park, Roma Ross Reserve and Thomas Leck Reserve. Playgrounds around Warracknabeal include Lions Playground, the playground at Apex Park and the playground at Anzac Park. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Warracknabeal. In Warracknabeal you'll also find Historical Centre and Wheatlands Warracknabeal Agricultural Machinery Museum. The Yarriambiack Creek Walk passes through Warracknabeal. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Warracknabeal for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Warracknabeal. The closest to Warracknabeal is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 124 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Mildura - Horsham via Hopetoun and Horsham - Birchip via Warracknabeal routes stop in Warracknabeal. V/Line coaches stop in Warracknabeal. By car from Warracknabeal, Horsham is about 46 minutes away and Stawell about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Warracknabeal is Minyip Swimming Pool in Minyip, about 25 minutes' drive. For a library, Dimboola Library in Dimboola is about 32 minutes' drive from Warracknabeal.

Best for

Younger kids at Warracknabeal's 5 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Warracknabeal. Free days on the Yarriambiack Creek Walk and in Warracknabeal's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Crymelon, Cannum and Bangerang also border Warracknabeal.
