Playground on Campbell Street

Playground in Warracknabeal · Basic info

Playground on Campbell Street is a public playground in Warracknabeal, in the Yarriambiack Shire area. The Yarriambiack Creek Walk passes about 130 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 330 m away. There's a public toilet about 440 m away, and bus Mildura - Horsham via Hopetoun stops at Scott St/Lyle St, a 7-minute walk away.
