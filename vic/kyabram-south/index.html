Kyabram South is in the Greater Shepparton area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Kyabram South, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Kyabram South has no train station; Murchison East Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 30 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kyabram South, so most trips start by car. By car from Kyabram South, Shepparton is about 38 minutes away, Echuca about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kyabram South is Community Swimming Pool in Merrigum, about 12 minutes' drive. For a library, Kyabram Library And Community Hub in Kyabram is about 18 minutes' drive from Kyabram South. The closest beach to Kyabram South is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 40 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the bmx track on Racecourse Road in Kyabram is about 19 minutes' drive from Kyabram South. The closest cinema to Kyabram South is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 39 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 38 minutes' drive from Kyabram South. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Kyabram South is Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram, about 19 minutes' drive. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Kyabram South, about 38 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Kyabram South, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Merrigum Lions Park in Merrigum (12 min), Community Swimming Pool in Merrigum (12 min), the playground at Judd Park in Merrigum (12 min), Kyabram Library And Community Hub in Kyabram (18 min), the bmx track on Racecourse Road in Kyabram (19 min) and Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram (19 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Kyabram South.
