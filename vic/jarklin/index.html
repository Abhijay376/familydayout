Landmarks

Other parks and reserves in Jarklin include Serpentine Creek - Fernihurst Camping Area 1 (CLWF). For camping, there's Serpentine Creek - Fernihurst Camping Area 1 (CLWF).

Getting around

There's no train station in Jarklin. The closest to Jarklin is Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line), about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Jarklin, so most trips start by car. By car from Jarklin, Bendigo is about 55 minutes away, Echuca about 66 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo is about 23 minutes' drive from Jarklin. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Jarklin, about 55 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Jarklin, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at R J Phelan Reserve in Mitiamo (23 min), East Loddon Historical Society in Mitiamo (23 min), Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo (23 min), Pyramid Hill & Districts Historical Museum in Pyramid Hill (26 min), A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park in Boort (30 min) and Boort Swimming Pool in Boort (31 min).

Best for
