Montpelier Reserve

Park in Lower Plenty · Basic info

Montpelier Reserve is a park in Lower Plenty, in the Banyule City Council area, good for all ages. Omar St/Parker St (bus) is a 20-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1454 m away with a baby change table.
