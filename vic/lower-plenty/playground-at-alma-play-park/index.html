Playground at Alma Play Park

Playground in Lower Plenty · Basic info

Playground at Alma Play Park is a public playground in Lower Plenty, in the Banyule City area. The Plenty River Trail passes about 310 m away, and Suwan is about 260 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 300 m away, and buses 513 and 901 stop at Para Rd/Main Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
