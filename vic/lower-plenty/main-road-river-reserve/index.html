Main Road River Reserve

Park in Lower Plenty · Basic info

Main Road River Reserve is a park in Lower Plenty, in the Banyule City Council area, good for all ages. Heidelberg Golf Club/Main Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 686 m away.
