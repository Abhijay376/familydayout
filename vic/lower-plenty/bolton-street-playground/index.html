Bolton Street Playground

Playground in Lower Plenty · Basic info

This is a public playground in Lower Plenty. The Fitzsimons Lane Trail passes about 80 m away, and Chicken on Charcoal is about 200 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 510 m away, and buses 293, 513 and 901 stop at Bolton St/Main Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
