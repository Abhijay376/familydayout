Playground at Cheverton Valley Park

Playground in Lower Plenty · Basic info

Playground at Cheverton Valley Park in Lower Plenty is a public playground in the Banyule City area. For food, Suwan is about 490 m away, and Playground on Main Road is another option about 240 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 760 m from the playground, and buses 513 and 901 stop at Cheverton Rd/Main Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
