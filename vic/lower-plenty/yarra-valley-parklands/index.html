Yarra Valley Parklands

Park in Lower Plenty · Basic info

Yarra Valley Parklands is a park in Lower Plenty, in the Banyule City Council area, good for all ages. Foote St/Thompsons Rd (bus) is a 26-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1724 m away with a baby change table.
