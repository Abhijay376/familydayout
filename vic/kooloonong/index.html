Our data lists 4 parks and reserves in Kooloonong, in the Swan Hill area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Kooloonong is Menzies Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 377 hectares. Kodoonong Bushland Reserve and Yungera Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Kooloonong include Bower Tank Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Kooloonong has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 63 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kooloonong, so most trips start by car. By car from Kooloonong, Swan Hill is about 61 minutes away and Mildura about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a skate park, the bmx track at Piangil Memorial Park in Piangil is about 28 minutes' drive from Kooloonong. From Kooloonong, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Boundary Bend Memorial Park in Boundary Bend (29 min), the playground at Mensforth Park in Piangil (27 min), the bmx track at Piangil Memorial Park in Piangil (28 min) and Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang (49 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Kooloonong.

Nearby suburbs

Boundary Bend, next to Kooloonong, has 1 playground. Lake Powell, Kenley, Bolton and Annuello also border Kooloonong.
