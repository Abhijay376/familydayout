Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Minmindie in the Loddon area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Minmindie has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 38 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Minmindie, so most trips start by car. By car from Minmindie, Swan Hill is about 77 minutes away and Bendigo about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Boort Swimming Pool in Boort is about 8 minutes' drive from Minmindie. The closest library to Minmindie is Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang, about 32 minutes' drive. From Minmindie, the nearest places for kids by road are A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park in Boort (7 min), Boort Court House Historical Museum in Boort (7 min), Boort Swimming Pool in Boort (8 min), the playground at Lake Meran W.R in Lake Meran (18 min), Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook (59 min) and Quambatook Historical Centre in Quambatook (59 min).
