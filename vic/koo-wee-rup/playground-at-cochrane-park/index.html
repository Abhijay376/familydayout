Playground at Cochrane Park

Playground in Koo Wee Rup · Basic info

This is a public playground in Koo Wee Rup. The Koo Wee Rup Rail Trail is about 100 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 30 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 120 m from the playground, and buses Cowes - Fountain Gate via Anderson and Koo Wee Rup - Pakenham stop at Koo Wee Rup Bus Interchange/Rossiter Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
