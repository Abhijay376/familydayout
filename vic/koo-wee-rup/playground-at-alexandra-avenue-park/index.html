Playground at Alexandra Avenue Park

Playground in Koo Wee Rup · Basic info

This is a public playground in Koo Wee Rup. The Koo Wee Rup Rail Trail passes about 120 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 350 m away. There's a public toilet about 70 m away, and buses Cowes - Fountain Gate via Anderson and Koo Wee Rup - Pakenham stop at Koo Wee Rup Bus Interchange/Rossiter Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
