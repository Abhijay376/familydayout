Our data lists 5 playgrounds and 4 parks and reserves in Koo Wee Rup, in the Cardinia area. Koo Wee Rup also has a pool and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Koo Wee Rup is Koo Wee Rup Recreation Reserve, covering about 9 hectares. Cochrane Park and Lions Park are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Koo Wee Rup include Alexandra Avenue Park. Playgrounds in Koo Wee Rup include the playground at Cochrane Park and the playground at Alexandra Avenue Park. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Koo Wee Rup. In Koo Wee Rup you'll also find Koo Wee Rup Swimming Pool. For older kids in Koo Wee Rup there's the skate park at Cochrane Park. For walks and rides, Koo Wee Rup Rail Trail crosses Koo Wee Rup.

Getting around

There's no train station in Koo Wee Rup. The closest to Koo Wee Rup is East Pakenham Railway Station (metro), about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Koo Wee Rup - Pakenham and Cowes - Fountain Gate via Anderson routes stop in Koo Wee Rup. V/Line coaches stop in Koo Wee Rup. By car from Koo Wee Rup, Pakenham is about 15 minutes away, Cranbourne about 24 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 58 minutes, without traffic.
