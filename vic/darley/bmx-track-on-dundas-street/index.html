BMX track on Dundas Street

Skate, BMX or pump track in Darley · Basic info

BMX track on Dundas Street is a skate, bmx or pump track in Darley, in the Moorabool Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Grey St/Nelson St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 238 m away with a baby change table.
