Torrumbarry is in the Campaspe area, and our data lists 5 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Torrumbarry is Baillieu Lagoon Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 246 hectares. Welton Nature Conservation Reserve and Murphy Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting) are the next largest. Torrumbarry also has Turrumberry North Bushland Reserve and McIntyre Rd Grassland Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Torrumbarry. The closest to Torrumbarry is Echuca Railway Station (V/Line), about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Kerang - Echuca via Cohuna route stop in Torrumbarry. V/Line coaches stop in Torrumbarry. By car from Torrumbarry, Echuca is about 22 minutes away and Bendigo about 83 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Leitchville Memorial Swimming Pool in Leitchville is about 19 minutes' drive from Torrumbarry. For a library, Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca is about 21 minutes' drive from Torrumbarry. The closest skate park to Torrumbarry is Moama Echuca Community Skate Park in Echuca, about 23 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca is about 21 minutes' drive from Torrumbarry. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 22 minutes from Torrumbarry by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Torrumbarry, the nearest places for kids by road are Holden Museum in Echuca (20 min), the playground at Echuca Lions Park in Echuca (21 min), Hopwood Gardens Playground in Echuca (20 min), Leitchville Memorial Swimming Pool in Leitchville (19 min), Port of Echuca Discovery Centre in Echuca (20 min) and Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca (21 min).
