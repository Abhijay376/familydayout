Landmarks

Nar-Nar-Goon G76 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Nar Nar Goon North, at about 8 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Nar Nar Goon North.

Getting around

There's no train station in Nar Nar Goon North. The closest to Nar Nar Goon North is Nar Nar Goon Railway Station (V/Line), about 7.1 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nar Nar Goon North, so most trips start by car. By car from Nar Nar Goon North, Pakenham is about 13 minutes away, Cranbourne about 34 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 59 minutes, without traffic.
