Ninety Mile Beach Marine National Park

Park in Giffard · Official details

Ninety Mile Beach Marine National Park is a park in Giffard, in the Wellington Shire Council area, good for all ages.

Details

Good to know
Some points not passable at high tide; Strong currents and undertows when swimming (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Ninety Mile Beach Marine National Park?
Ninety Mile Beach Marine National Park is in Giffard, Victoria.
Is Ninety Mile Beach Marine National Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
