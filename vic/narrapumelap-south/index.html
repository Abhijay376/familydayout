Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Narrapumelap South in the Ararat area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Narrapumelap South has no train station; Terang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 59 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Narrapumelap South, so most trips start by car. By car from Narrapumelap South, Ararat is about 73 minutes away and Hamilton about 77 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Narrapumelap South is Willaura Outdoor Pool in Willaura, about 45 minutes' drive. For a library, Lake Bolac Library in Lake Bolac is about 30 minutes' drive from Narrapumelap South. From Narrapumelap South, the nearest places for kids by road are Lake Bolac Library in Lake Bolac (30 min), Glenthompson Lions Park Playground in Glenthompson (35 min), Willaura Outdoor Pool in Willaura (45 min) and Glenthompson Rec Reserve Playground in Glenthompson (37 min).
