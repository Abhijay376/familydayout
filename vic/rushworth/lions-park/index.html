Lions Park

Park in Rushworth · Basic info

Lions Park is a park in Rushworth, in the Campaspe Shire Council area, good for all ages. School St/Moora Rd (coach) is a 12-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 738 m away with a baby change table.
