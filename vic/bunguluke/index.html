Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Bunguluke, in the Buloke area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bunguluke is Bunguluke I156 Bushland Reserve, covering about 1 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bunguluke. The closest to Bunguluke is Kerang Railway Station (V/Line), about 60 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bunguluke, so most trips start by car. By car from Bunguluke, Swan Hill is about 1.5 hours away and Bendigo about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bunguluke is Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest library to Bunguluke is Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook, about 34 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 28 minutes' drive from Bunguluke. The closest places for kids by road from Bunguluke are the playground at Centenary Park in Wycheproof (16 min), Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof (17 min), Willandra Museum in Wycheproof (17 min), Quambatook Lions Club Playground in Quambatook (34 min), Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook (34 min) and Quambatook Historical Centre in Quambatook (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bunguluke.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Bunguluke, Wycheproof has 1 playground and a pool. Jeruk, Glenloth East and Glenloth also border Bunguluke.
