Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Warragul South in the Baw Baw area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Warragul South has no train station; Warragul Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 6.4 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Warragul South, so most trips start by car. By car from Warragul South, Warragul is about 10 minutes away, Pakenham about 44 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 87 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Warragul South is Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul, about 8 minutes' drive. For a library, Warragul Library in Warragul is about 10 minutes' drive from Warragul South. The closest skate park to Warragul South is Warragul Skate Park in Warragul, about 8 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 8 minutes' drive from Warragul South. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 9 minutes' drive from Warragul South. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Warragul South is Wildlife Trail in Tynong North, about 30 minutes' drive. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Warragul South, about 10 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Warragul South, the nearest places for kids by road are Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul (8 min), Warragul Skate Park in Warragul (8 min), the playground at Burke Street Park in Warragul (8 min), Warragul Leisure Centre in Warragul (8 min), Burke Street Pump Track in Warragul (9 min) and Warragul Cinema in Warragul (8 min).

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Warragul South, Warragul has 22 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Warragul South, Drouin has 19 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Lardner, Ellinbank and Bona Vista also border Warragul South.
