Landmarks

The biggest green space in Waubra is Waubra Recreation Reserve, covering about 6 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Waubra.

Getting around

There's no train station in Waubra. The closest to Waubra is Clunes Railway Station (V/Line), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Ballarat - Maryborough route stop in Waubra. By car from Waubra, Ballarat is about 28 minutes away, Maryborough about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot is about 21 minutes' drive from Waubra. The closest library to Waubra is Central Highlands Regional Library Corp Ballarat branch in Ballarat Central, about 27 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Alexander Park in Learmonth is about 9 minutes' drive from Waubra. For a cinema, Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central is about 28 minutes' drive from Waubra. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 32 minutes' drive from Waubra. For a zoo or wildlife park, Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East is about 31 minutes' drive from Waubra. The nearest larger centre is Ballarat, about 28 minutes from Waubra by car, with 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Waubra are the skate park at Alexander Park in Learmonth (9 min), the playground at Learmonth Recreation Reserve in Learmonth (9 min), the playground at Lake Learmonth Lake Reserve in Learmonth (12 min), Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (21 min), Wendouree BMX Track in Wendouree (23 min) and Friends of the Anson Air Museum in Mitchell Park (25 min).
