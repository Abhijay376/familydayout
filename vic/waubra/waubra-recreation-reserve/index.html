Waubra Recreation Reserve

Park in Waubra · Basic info

Waubra Recreation Reserve is a park in Waubra, in the Pyrenees Shire Council area, good for all ages. Hall St/Sunraysia Hwy (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 198 m away.
