Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Homerton, in the Glenelg area.

Landmarks

Kurtonitj Indigenous Protected Area is the largest mapped park in Homerton, at about 367 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Homerton. The closest to Homerton is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 69 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Homerton, so most trips start by car. By car from Homerton, Portland is about 29 minutes away and Hamilton about 56 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Homerton is Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood, about 10 minutes' drive. For a library, Heywood Library in Heywood is about 10 minutes' drive from Homerton. The closest beach to Homerton is Fitzroy River Beach in Tyrendarra, about 18 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Portland, about 29 minutes from Homerton by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. From Homerton, the nearest places for kids by road are Heywood Library in Heywood (10 min), the playground at Apex Park in Heywood (10 min), Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood (10 min), the playground at Heywood Youth Park in Heywood (11 min), Fitzroy River Beach in Tyrendarra (18 min) and Bay of Whales Gallery in Narrawong (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Homerton.

Nearby suburbs

Heywood, next to Homerton, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Tyrendarra, next to Homerton, has a beach.
