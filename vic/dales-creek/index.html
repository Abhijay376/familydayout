Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Dales Creek in the Moorabool area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Dales Creek. The closest to Dales Creek is Ballan Railway Station (V/Line), about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dales Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Dales Creek, Bacchus Marsh is about 23 minutes away, Melton about 32 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 67 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Ballan Swimming Pool in Ballan is about 19 minutes' drive from Dales Creek. For a library, Lerderderg Library in Bacchus Marsh is about 23 minutes' drive from Dales Creek. For a beach, Lionhead Beach in Pentland Hills is about 19 minutes' drive from Dales Creek. For a skate park, the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan is about 19 minutes' drive from Dales Creek. For a cinema, Reading Cinemas in Melton West is about 31 minutes' drive from Dales Creek. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek is about 36 minutes' drive from Dales Creek. Bacchus Marsh is the closest bigger centre to Dales Creek, about 23 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Dales Creek are the playground at Greendale Recreation Reserve in Greendale (6 min), the playground at Pykes Creek Reserve in Myrniong (15 min), Little Gallery in Trentham (21 min), the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan (19 min), Lionhead Beach in Pentland Hills (19 min) and Ballan Swimming Pool in Ballan (19 min).
