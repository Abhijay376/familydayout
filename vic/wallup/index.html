Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Wallup, in the Yarriambiack area.

Landmarks

Barrett Flora and Fauna Reserve is the largest mapped park in Wallup, at about 224 hectares.

Getting around

Wallup has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 122 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wallup, so most trips start by car. By car from Wallup, Horsham is about 43 minutes away and Stawell about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Wallup is Dimboola Swimming Pool in Dimboola, about 17 minutes' drive. For a library, Dimboola Library in Dimboola is about 17 minutes' drive from Wallup. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to Wallup, about 43 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Wallup are the playground at Apex Park in Warracknabeal (14 min), the playground at Anzac Park in Warracknabeal (16 min), Historical Centre in Warracknabeal (16 min), Wheatlands Warracknabeal Agricultural Machinery Museum in Warracknabeal (18 min), Dimboola Swimming Pool in Dimboola (17 min) and Dimboola Library in Dimboola (17 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wallup.

Nearby suburbs

Dimboola, next to Wallup, has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Warracknabeal, next to Wallup, has 5 playgrounds. Cannum also borders Wallup.
