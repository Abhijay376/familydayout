Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Vasey, in the Southern Grampians area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Vasey is Claude Austin State Forest, covering about 2,660 hectares. Dundas Range Scenic Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 2 off-leash dog parks in Vasey.

Getting around

Vasey has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 95 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Vasey, so most trips start by car. By car from Vasey, Hamilton is about 56 minutes away and Horsham about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral is about 21 minutes' drive from Vasey. For a skate park, coleraine skate park in Coleraine is about 39 minutes' drive from Vasey. Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Vasey, about 56 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Vasey, the nearest places for kids by road are Glendinning Street Playground in Balmoral (21 min), Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral (21 min), the playground at Cavendish Town Square Memorial Park in Cavendish (34 min), coleraine skate park in Coleraine (39 min), Harrow Discovery Centre in Harrow (43 min) and Bake House Studio in Harrow (43 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Vasey.

Nearby suburbs

Balmoral, next to Vasey, has 1 playground and a pool. Englefield also borders Vasey.
