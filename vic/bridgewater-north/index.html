Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bridgewater North is Bridgewater Bushland Reserve, covering about 10 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bridgewater North. The closest to Bridgewater North is Raywood Railway Station (V/Line), about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bridgewater North, so most trips start by car. By car from Bridgewater North, Bendigo is about 35 minutes away, Castlemaine about 58 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bridgewater North is Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest library to Bridgewater North is Bendigo Library in Bendigo, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Bridgewater North is Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Bridgewater North is Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Bridgewater North is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 37 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Bridgewater North is The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo, about 37 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 35 minutes from Bridgewater North by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Bridgewater North are Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood (17 min), Peter Krenz Leisure Centre in Eaglehawk (27 min), Eaglehawk Playspace in Eaglehawk (27 min), Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk (27 min), the playground at Canterbury Park in Eaglehawk (27 min) and McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk (28 min).

Best for
