Playground on Fern Road

Playground in Upper Ferntree Gully · Basic info

Playground on Fern Road in Upper Ferntree Gully is a public playground in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. Playground at Talaskia Reserve is another option about 520 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 750 m from the playground, and bus 732 stops at Chandler House/16 Albert St, a 8-minute walk away.
