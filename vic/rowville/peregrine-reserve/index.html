Peregrine Reserve

Park in Rowville · Basic info

Peregrine Reserve is a park in Rowville, in the Knox City Council area, good for all ages. Peregrine Reserve/Dandelion Dr (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 966 m away.
