Rowville Community Playground

Playground in Rowville · Basic info

This is a public playground in Rowville. Rowville Library is about 250 m away, and Paul Sadler Swimland is about 600 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 90 m from the playground, and buses 681 and 691 stop at Simon Ave/Bridgewater Way, a 3-minute walk away.
