Playground at Lakeside Reserve

Playground in Rowville · Basic info

Playground at Lakeside Reserve is a public playground in Rowville, in the Knox City area. The Kelletts Road Trail is about 260 m away, and for food, Kebab Stop is about 410 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 560 m from the playground, and buses 681, 682 and 901 stop at Lakeside Bvd/Stud Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
