Playground on Turramurra Drive

Playground in Rowville · Basic info

Playground on Turramurra Drive in Rowville is a public playground in the Knox City area. The Wellington Road Trail passes about 180 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 20 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 920 m away, and bus 681 stops at McKay Rd/Taylors Lane, a 2-minute walk away.
