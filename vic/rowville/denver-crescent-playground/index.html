Denver Crescent Playground

Playground in Rowville · Basic info

Denver Crescent Playground is a public playground in Rowville, in the Knox City area. Mr. Biryani is about 460 m away if you need food, and Playground at Luton Court Playground is another option about 350 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.1 km away, and 6 bus routes stop at Stud Park SC/Stud Rd, a 7-minute walk away.
