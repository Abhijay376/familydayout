Our data lists 4 parks and reserves in Panitya, in the Mildura area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Panitya is Manya Bushland Reserve, covering about 851 hectares. Manya Flora Reserve and Panitya Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Panitya also has Berrook. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Panitya. For camping, there's Berrook. For walks and rides, Berrook Walk crosses Panitya.

Getting around

There's no train station in Panitya. The closest to Panitya is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 233 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Panitya, so most trips start by car. By car from Panitya, Mildura is about 3 hours away and Swan Hill about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Murrayville Library in Murrayville is about 15 minutes' drive from Panitya. The closest places for kids by road from Panitya are Murrayville Library in Murrayville (15 min).

Best for

Free days on the Berrook Walk and in Panitya's parks.
