BMX track on Houdini Drive

Skate, BMX or pump track in Diggers Rest · Basic info

BMX track on Houdini Drive is a skate, bmx or pump track in Diggers Rest, in the Melton City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Plumpton Rd/Houdini Dr (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 136 m away.
