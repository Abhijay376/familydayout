Splendour Cct Reserve Playground

Playground in Diggers Rest · Basic info

Splendour Cct Reserve Playground is a public playground in Diggers Rest, in the Melton City area. The Vineyard Road is about 310 m away, and Bouquet Cct Reserve Playground is another option about 340 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 880 m from the playground, and bus 475 stops at Paddock St/Madisons Ave , a 4-minute walk away.
