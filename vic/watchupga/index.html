Watchupga in the Buloke area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Watchupga I185 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Watchupga, at about 11 hectares. Watchupga I294 B.R is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Watchupga. The closest to Watchupga is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 89 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Watchupga, so most trips start by car. By car from Watchupga, Swan Hill is about 83 minutes away and Horsham about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Watchupga is Woomelang Recreation Reserve Swimming Pool in Woomelang, about 11 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Birchip Skate Park in Birchip is about 29 minutes' drive from Watchupga. The closest places for kids by road from Watchupga are the playground at Woomelang Rec Reserve in Woomelang (10 min), Woomelang Recreation Reserve Swimming Pool in Woomelang (11 min), the playground at High way Park Woomelang in Woomelang (10 min), Birchip History Museum in Birchip (28 min), Birchip Skate Park in Birchip (29 min) and Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock (32 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Watchupga.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Watchupga, Hopetoun has 4 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Watchupga, Woomelang has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Beulah, next to Watchupga, has 2 playgrounds. Marlbed and Curyo also border Watchupga.
