Landmarks

Other parks and reserves in Gunbower include Ghow Swamp Picnic Area. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Gunbower.

Getting around

Gunbower has no train station; Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 25 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Kerang - Echuca via Cohuna route stop in Gunbower. V/Line coaches stop in Gunbower. By car from Gunbower, Echuca is about 35 minutes away and Swan Hill about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Leitchville Memorial Swimming Pool in Leitchville is about 7 minutes' drive from Gunbower. For a library, Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca is about 33 minutes' drive from Gunbower. For a skate park, Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna is about 18 minutes' drive from Gunbower. The closest cinema to Gunbower is Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca, about 33 minutes' drive. Echuca is the closest bigger centre to Gunbower, about 35 minutes away by car, and has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Gunbower, the nearest places for kids by road are Leitchville Memorial Swimming Pool in Leitchville (7 min), Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna (18 min), the playground at Cohuna Apex Park in Cohuna (18 min), Cohuna Pool in Cohuna (19 min), the playground at C L King Memorial Park in Cohuna (19 min) and Pyramid Hill & Districts Historical Museum in Pyramid Hill (39 min).
