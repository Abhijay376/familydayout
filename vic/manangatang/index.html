Manangatang is in the Swan Hill area, and our data lists 1 playground and 11 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Manangatang has a pool.

Landmarks

Manangatang (Lulla) Flora and Fauna Reserve is the largest mapped park in Manangatang, at about 199 hectares. Kulwyne Swamp Bushland Reserve and Kookoombo West Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Manangatang also has Manangatang Memorial Park, Manangatang Bushland Reserve and Millers Tank Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Manangatang. In Manangatang you'll also find Manangatang Swimming Pool.

Getting around

There's no train station in Manangatang. The closest to Manangatang is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 70 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Manangatang. By car from Manangatang, Swan Hill is about 66 minutes away and Mildura about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a skate park, the bmx track at Piangil Memorial Park in Piangil is about 32 minutes' drive from Manangatang. From Manangatang, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Piangil Memorial Park in Piangil (32 min), the bmx track at Piangil Memorial Park in Piangil (32 min) and the playground at Mensforth Park in Piangil (37 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Manangatang.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Manangatang, Piangil has 2 playgrounds and a skate park. Mittyack, Kulwin, Gerahmin and Cocamba also border Manangatang.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Manangatang, without traffic.
