Stirling is in the East Gippsland area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Stirling, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Stirling has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 44 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Stirling, so most trips start by car. By car from Stirling, Bairnsdale is about 79 minutes away and Lakes Entrance about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Stirling is Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek, about 43 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Stirling are Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek (43 min), Great Alpine Gallery in Swifts Creek (44 min) and Bruthen Gallery in Bruthen (55 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Stirling.

Nearby suburbs

Ensay, next to Stirling, has 1 playground. Brookville also borders Stirling.
