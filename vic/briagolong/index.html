Briagolong is in the Wellington area, and our data lists 2 playgrounds and 14 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Briagolong Redgum Reserve is the largest mapped park in Briagolong, at about 117 hectares. Mount Moornapa Flora Reserve and George Creek Streamside Reserve are the next largest. Briagolong also has Froam, Anzac Park and Carneys Camp. Playgrounds in Briagolong include Anzac Park Playground. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Briagolong. Picnic areas in Briagolong include Blue Pool Campground, Blue Pool Day Visitor Area and Froam. For camping, there's Carneys Camp. The Track Ninety Six, Freestone Creek Walking Track and Red Gums Walk South Block all pass through Briagolong.

Getting around

There's no train station in Briagolong. The closest to Briagolong is Stratford Railway Station (V/Line), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Briagolong, so most trips start by car. By car from Briagolong, Sale is about 30 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 56 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Briagolong is Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest library to Briagolong is Sale Library in Sale, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Briagolong is Skate Park in Stratford, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Briagolong is Former Drive-in site in Sale, about 28 minutes' drive. Sale is the closest bigger centre to Briagolong, about 30 minutes away by car, and has 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Briagolong, the nearest places for kids by road are Boisdale Recreation Reserve Playground in Boisdale (11 min), Skate Park in Stratford (17 min), Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford (17 min), Former Drive-in site in Sale (28 min), Sale Historical Museum in Sale (31 min) and Old train signals in Sale (31 min).

Best for

Free days on the Track Ninety Six and in Briagolong's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Briagolong, Stratford has 8 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Moornapa, Llowalong and Bushy Park also border Briagolong.
