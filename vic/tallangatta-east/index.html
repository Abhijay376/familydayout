Tallangatta East is in the Towong area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Tallangatta East, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, High Country Rail Trail, Tallangatta - Firebrace Loop and Tallangatta - Lake Road Loop cross Tallangatta East.

Getting around

Tallangatta East has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 33 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tallangatta East, so most trips start by car. By car from Tallangatta East, Wodonga is about 41 minutes away and Wangaratta about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta is about 7 minutes' drive from Tallangatta East. The closest library to Tallangatta East is Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Tallangatta East is Skate Park in Baranduda, about 33 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana is about 35 minutes' drive from Tallangatta East. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 41 minutes from Tallangatta East by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Tallangatta East, the nearest places for kids by road are Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta (7 min), Tallangatta Art & Photography in Tallangatta (7 min), Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta (7 min), Granya Museum in Granya (21 min), the playground at Banksia Park in Tangambalanga (19 min) and the playground at Ludlows Reserve in Ebden (25 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Tallangatta East on the High Country Rail Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Tallangatta East, Tallangatta has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Georges Creek and Bullioh also border Tallangatta East.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Tallangatta East, without traffic.
