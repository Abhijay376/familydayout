Our data lists 14 playgrounds and 14 parks and reserves in Tullamarine, in the Hume area. Beyond the parks, Tullamarine has a library and a pool.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tullamarine is Leo Dineen Reserve, covering about 5 hectares. Melrose Drive Reserve and Steele Creek Linear Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Tullamarine include Camphill Park, Andlon Reserve and Fisher Grove Reserve. Playgrounds in Tullamarine include the playground at Camphill Park, the playground at Andlon Reserve and the playground at Leo Dineen Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Tullamarine. In Tullamarine you'll also find Tullamarine Swim School and Tullamarine Library. The M80 Trail passes through Tullamarine. Our data lists 12 cafes and family restaurants in Tullamarine for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Tullamarine has no train station; Jacana Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 2.7 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 477, 478, 479, 482, 490, 902 and 959 stop in Tullamarine. By car from Tullamarine, Broadmeadows is about 9 minutes away, Sunshine about 15 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 24 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 26 minutes' drive from Tullamarine. The closest skate park to Tullamarine is the skate park at Boeing Reserve in Strathmore Heights, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Tullamarine is Village Cinemas in Airport West, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Tullamarine is Strike Bowling Bar in Maribyrnong, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Tullamarine is Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville, about 19 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 14 playgrounds across Tullamarine. Rainy days at the library in Tullamarine. Free days on the M80 Trail and in Tullamarine's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Airport West, next to Tullamarine, has 13 playgrounds and a cinema. Next door to Tullamarine, Westmeadows has 10 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Tullamarine, Gladstone Park has 9 playgrounds and a library. Melbourne Airport, next to Tullamarine, has a pool. Keilor Park, Gowanbrae and Strathmore Heights also border Tullamarine.
