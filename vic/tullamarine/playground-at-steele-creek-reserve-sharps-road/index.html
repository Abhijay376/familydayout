Playground at Steele Creek Reserve (Sharps Road)

Playground in Tullamarine · Basic info

Playground at Steele Creek Reserve (Sharps Road) is a public playground in Tullamarine, in the Hume City area. Tullamarine Library is about 350 m away, and Playground at Leo Dineen Reserve is another option about 310 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus 482 stops at Coonamar St/Sharps Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
