S. F. Hedger Reserve

Park in Tullamarine · Basic info

S. F. Hedger Reserve is a park in Tullamarine, in the Hume City Council area, good for all ages. Sharps Rd/Broadmeadows Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1819 m away.
