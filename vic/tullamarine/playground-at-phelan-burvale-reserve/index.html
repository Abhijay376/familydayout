Playground at Phelan - Burvale Reserve

Playground in Tullamarine · Basic info

This is a public playground in Tullamarine. Tullamarine Library is about 550 m away, and Playground on Catherine Avenue is another option about 250 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 970 m away, and buses 478 and 479 stop at Trade Park Reserve/Melrose Dr, a 5-minute walk away.
