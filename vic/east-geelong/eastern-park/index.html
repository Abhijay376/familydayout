Eastern Park

Park in East Geelong · Basic info

Eastern Park is a park in East Geelong, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Geelong Botanic Gardens/Ryrie St (bus) is a 13-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 78 m away.
