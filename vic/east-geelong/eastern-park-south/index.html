Eastern Park South

Playground in East Geelong · Basic info

Eastern Park South is a public playground in East Geelong, in the Greater Geelong City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 60 stops at Holt Rd/Ryrie St, a 3-minute walk away.
