Hattah is in the Mildura area, and our data lists 6 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Hattah - Kulkyne National Park is the largest mapped park in Hattah, at about 49,995 hectares. Murray - Kulkyne Park and Kia Reference Area are the next largest. Hattah also has Gayfield Bushland Reserve, Chalka Creek Reference Area and Tarpaulin Bend Reference Area. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Hattah. For walks and rides, Camel Pad Track and Hattah Nature Walk cross Hattah.

Getting around

Hattah has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 134 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Hattah. By car from Hattah, Mildura is about 53 minutes away and Swan Hill about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Hattah is Ouyen Swimming Pool in Ouyen, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Hattah is Roxy in Ouyen, about 27 minutes' drive. Mildura is the closest bigger centre to Hattah, about 53 minutes away by car, and has 26 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Hattah are Jardine Park Playground in Ouyen (26 min), the playground at Ouyen Lake Park in Ouyen (27 min), Ouyen Swimming Pool in Ouyen (26 min) and Roxy in Ouyen (27 min).

Best for

Free days on the Camel Pad Track and in Hattah's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Hattah, Ouyen has 4 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. Colignan and Carwarp also border Hattah.
