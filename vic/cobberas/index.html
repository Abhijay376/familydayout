Cobberas is in the East Gippsland area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Cobberas is Cobberas Wilderness Zone, covering about 9,913 hectares. Forest Hill Reference Area is the next largest. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Cobberas. The Australian Alps Walking Track passes through Cobberas.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cobberas. The closest to Cobberas is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 113 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cobberas, so most trips start by car. By car from Cobberas, Lakes Entrance is about 3.5 hours away and Bright about 3.5 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Free days on the Australian Alps Walking Track and in Cobberas's parks.

Benambra also borders Cobberas.
