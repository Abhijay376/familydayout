Landmarks

The biggest green space in Kinypanial is Kinypanial South Bushland Reserve, covering about 1 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kinypanial. The closest to Kinypanial is Dingee Railway Station (V/Line), about 36 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kinypanial, so most trips start by car. By car from Kinypanial, Bendigo is about 85 minutes away and Echuca about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn is about 39 minutes' drive from Kinypanial. From Kinypanial, the nearest places for kids by road are Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (39 min), Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (39 min), A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park in Boort (48 min), Boort Court House Historical Museum in Boort (48 min) and Boort Swimming Pool in Boort (49 min).
