Langley in the Mount Alexander area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Turpins Falls Scenic Reserve is the largest mapped park in Langley, at about 6 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Langley. The closest to Langley is Kyneton Railway Station (V/Line), about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Langley, so most trips start by car. By car from Langley, Kyneton is about 14 minutes away, Castlemaine about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 80 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Chewton Swimming Pool in Chewton is about 36 minutes' drive from Langley. The closest library to Langley is Kyneton Library in Kyneton, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Langley is Canoeing in Tylden, about 25 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton is about 15 minutes' drive from Langley. The nearest larger centre is Kyneton, about 14 minutes from Langley by car, with 10 playgrounds and a library. From Langley, the nearest places for kids by road are Jean Haynes Playground in Kyneton (14 min), Kyneton Museum in Kyneton (14 min), Kyneton Library in Kyneton (14 min), Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton (15 min), Kyneton Community Park in Kyneton (15 min) and Canoeing in Tylden (25 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Langley.

Nearby suburbs

Kyneton, next to Langley, has 10 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Edgecombe and Barfold also border Langley.
