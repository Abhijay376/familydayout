Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Henty in the Glenelg area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Henty has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 117 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Henty, so most trips start by car. By car from Henty, Hamilton is about 46 minutes away and Portland about 66 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Merino Swimming Pool in Merino is about 7 minutes' drive from Henty. The closest skate park to Henty is coleraine skate park in Coleraine, about 29 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 46 minutes from Henty by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Henty, the nearest places for kids by road are Merino Swimming Pool in Merino (7 min), the playground at Lions Park in Merino (6 min), Gorman's art gallery in Casterton (12 min), Casterton Pool in Casterton (13 min), coleraine skate park in Coleraine (29 min) and the playground at Queens Park in Coleraine (29 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Henty.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Henty, Merino has 1 playground and a pool. Clover Flat also borders Henty.
