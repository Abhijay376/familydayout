Warncoort in the Colac Otway area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Warncoort is Lake Burn Lake Reserve, covering about 71 hectares. Lake Ayrey Wildlife Reserve (no hunting) is the next largest.

Getting around

Warncoort has no train station; Birregurra Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 5.8 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Warncoort, so most trips start by car. By car from Warncoort, Colac is about 16 minutes away, Geelong about 53 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Lorne Sea Baths in Lorne is about 45 minutes' drive from Warncoort. The closest library to Warncoort is Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Warncoort is Lorne Beach in Lorne, about 45 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Birregurra Skate Park in Birregurra is about 9 minutes' drive from Warncoort. The closest cinema to Warncoort is Colac Cinemas in Colac, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Warncoort is Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac, about 15 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 16 minutes from Warncoort by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Warncoort, the nearest places for kids by road are Birregurra Skate Park in Birregurra (9 min), the playground at Selwyn Brown Park in Colac (14 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (15 min), the playground at Memorial Square in Colac (15 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (16 min) and Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (16 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Warncoort.
