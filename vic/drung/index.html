Drung is in the Horsham area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Green Lake Recreation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Drung, at about 6 hectares.

Getting around

Drung has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 85 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Drung, so most trips start by car. By car from Drung, Horsham is about 10 minutes away and Stawell about 49 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Drung is Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham, about 8 minutes' drive. For a library, Dimboola Library in Dimboola is about 38 minutes' drive from Drung. For a skate park, Velodrome in Horsham is about 8 minutes' drive from Drung. For a cinema, Centre Cinemas in Horsham is about 9 minutes' drive from Drung. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to Drung, about 10 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Drung, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Uebergang Park in Horsham (7 min), Velodrome in Horsham (8 min), Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham (8 min), Horsham Water Play in Horsham (10 min), Centre Cinemas in Horsham (9 min) and Horsham Skate Park in Horsham (10 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Drung.

Nearby suburbs

Longerenong, next to Drung, has 1 playground. Dooen and Bungalally also border Drung.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Drung, without traffic.
