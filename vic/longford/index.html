Longford in the Wellington area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Longford is Holey Plains State Park, covering about 10,745 hectares. Longford Natural Features Reserve and Holey Hill Campground are the next largest. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Longford. The Long Swamp Waling Track passes through Longford.

Getting around

Longford has no train station; Sale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 7.3 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the 7 and Sale - Seaspray via Longford routes stop in Longford. By car from Longford, Sale is about 8 minutes away, Traralgon about 43 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Indoor pool in Sale is about 8 minutes' drive from Longford. The closest library to Longford is Sale Library in Sale, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Longford is 90 Mile Beach in Seaspray, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Longford is Sale Skate Park in Sale, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Longford is Sale Cinemas in Sale, about 7 minutes' drive. Sale is the closest bigger centre to Longford, about 8 minutes away by car, and has 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Longford, the nearest places for kids by road are Historic Gunpowder Magazine in Sale (6 min), Sale Historical Museum in Sale (6 min), Little McMillan Park Playground in Sale (6 min), Sale Skate Park in Sale (7 min), Sale Library in Sale (6 min) and Interactive Play Space in Sale (7 min).

Best for

Free days on the Long Swamp Waling Track and in Longford's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Sale, next to Longford, has 14 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Rosedale, next to Longford, has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Seaspray, next to Longford, has 1 playground and a beach. Glomar Beach, next to Longford, has a beach. Flamingo Beach, Willung, The Honeysuckles and The Heart also border Longford.
