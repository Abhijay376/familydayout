Kurraca West is in the Loddon area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Kurraca West, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kurraca West. The closest to Kurraca West is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 62 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kurraca West, so most trips start by car. By car from Kurraca West, Maryborough is about 70 minutes away, Bendigo about 71 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn is about 11 minutes' drive from Kurraca West. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 36 minutes' drive from Kurraca West. From Kurraca West, the nearest places for kids by road are Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (11 min), Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (11 min), Bi-Centennial Park Playground in St Arnaud (29 min), the playground at Queen Mary Botanic Gardens in St Arnaud (30 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (30 min) and Rex Theatre in Charlton (36 min).
