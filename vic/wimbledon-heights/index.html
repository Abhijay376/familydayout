Wimbledon Heights is in the Bass Coast area, and our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Wimbledon Heights is Wimbledon Heights Reserve, covering about 2 hectares. Playgrounds around Wimbledon Heights include the playground at Wimbledon Heights Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wimbledon Heights. The closest to Wimbledon Heights is Stony Point Railway Station (metro), about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wimbledon Heights, so most trips start by car. By car from Wimbledon Heights, Wonthaggi is about 41 minutes away, Leongatha about 70 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Wonthaggi is about 39 minutes' drive from Wimbledon Heights. The closest library to Wimbledon Heights is Phillip Island Library in Cowes, about 6 minutes' drive. For a beach, YCW in Smiths Beach is about 7 minutes' drive from Wimbledon Heights. For a skate park, the skate park at Wonthaggi Railway Historic Reserve in Kilcunda is about 28 minutes' drive from Wimbledon Heights. The closest cinema to Wimbledon Heights is Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi, about 39 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Phillip Island Wildlife Park in Cowes is about 4 minutes' drive from Wimbledon Heights. The nearest larger centre is Wonthaggi, about 41 minutes from Wimbledon Heights by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Wimbledon Heights, the nearest places for kids by road are Phillip Island Wildlife Park in Cowes (4 min), Mingara Gallery in Cowes (4 min), A Maze'N Things in Cowes (5 min), Pyramid Rock Gallery in Cowes (6 min), the playground at Mitchell Reserve in Smiths Beach (5 min) and Phillip Island Library in Cowes (6 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wimbledon Heights.
