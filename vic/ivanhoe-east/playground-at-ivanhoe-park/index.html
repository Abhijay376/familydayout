Playground at Ivanhoe Park

Playground in Ivanhoe East · Basic info

Playground at Ivanhoe Park in Ivanhoe East is a public playground in the Banyule City area. The LBN1 - Watsonia to East Ivanhoe passes about 60 m away, and Junior School Playground is another option about 530 m away. There's a public toilet about 220 m away, and buses 546 and 548 stop at Ivanhoe Park/Lower Heidelberg Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
