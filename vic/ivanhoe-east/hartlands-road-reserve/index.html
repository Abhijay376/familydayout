Hartlands Road Reserve

Park in Ivanhoe East · Basic info

Hartlands Road Reserve is a park in Ivanhoe East, in the Banyule City Council area, good for all ages. Hartlands Rd/Lower Heidelberg Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 951 m away.
