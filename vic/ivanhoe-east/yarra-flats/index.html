Yarra Flats

Park in Ivanhoe East · Basic info

Yarra Flats is a park in Ivanhoe East, in the Banyule City Council area, good for all ages. Carlsberg Rd/Lower Heidelberg Rd (bus) is a 18-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1594 m away.
