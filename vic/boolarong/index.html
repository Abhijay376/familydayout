Boolarong in the South Gippsland area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Boolarong is Little Franklin River Scenic Reserve, covering about 38 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Boolarong. The closest to Boolarong is Morwell Railway Station (V/Line), about 39 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Boolarong, so most trips start by car. By car from Boolarong, Leongatha is about 60 minutes away, Traralgon about 79 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Foster Outdoor Pool in Foster is about 39 minutes' drive from Boolarong. For a library, Foster Library in Foster is about 37 minutes' drive from Boolarong. For a skate park, the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora is about 43 minutes' drive from Boolarong. The nearest larger centre is Leongatha, about 60 minutes from Boolarong by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Boolarong are Luminous Glass Studio in Foster (38 min), Stockyard Gallery in Foster (38 min), Foster Library in Foster (37 min), Foster Outdoor Pool in Foster (39 min), the playground at Stanley Street Playground in Toora (43 min) and Toora Pool in Toora (43 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Boolarong.

Nearby suburbs
