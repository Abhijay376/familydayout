Playground at Windella Avenue Reserve

Playground in Kew East · Basic info

Playground at Windella Avenue Reserve in Kew East is a public playground in the Boroondara City area. The Anniversary Trail is about 130 m away, and for food, Vicky's Restaurant is about 100 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 300 m from the playground, and buses 302 and 304 stop at High St/Valerie St, a 2-minute walk away.
