Playground on Willow Grove

Playground in Kew East · Basic info

Playground on Willow Grove in Kew East is a public playground in the Boroondara City area. The Main Yarra Trail passes about 90 m away, and Playground at Hyde Park is another option about 530 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and buses 200, 207 and 305 stop at Belford Rd/Kilby Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
