Hays Paddock Playground

Playground in Kew East · Basic info

Hays Paddock Playground is a public playground in Kew East, in the Boroondara City area. The Main Yarra Trail passes about 210 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and buses 200, 207 and 305 stop at Leason St/Kilby Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
