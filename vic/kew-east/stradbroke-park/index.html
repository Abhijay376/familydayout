Stradbroke Park

Park in Kew East · Basic info

Stradbroke Park is a park in Kew East, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Lawrence St/Burke Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 510 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed on a lead.
