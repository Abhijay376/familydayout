Warranwood is in the Maroondah area, and our data lists 5 playgrounds and 5 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Warranwood Reserve is the largest mapped park in Warranwood, at about 8 hectares. Yanggai Barring Reserve and O'Neill Way Reserve are the next largest. Warranwood also has Wellington Park and Little John Reserve. Playgrounds in Warranwood include Eden Valley Playground, the playground at Wellington Park and the playground at Warranwood Reserve. For walks and rides, Croydon Hills Connection and Ringwood North Connection cross Warranwood. Our data lists 4 cafes and family restaurants in Warranwood for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Warranwood has no train station; Ringwood East Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 4.2 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 364 and 668 stop in Warranwood. By car from Warranwood, Ringwood is about 7 minutes away, Lilydale about 13 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 34 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Croydon Memorial Pool in Croydon is about 8 minutes' drive from Warranwood. For a library, Realm in Ringwood is about 8 minutes' drive from Warranwood. The closest beach to Warranwood is Horse Arena in Abbotsford, about 32 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Bike Jumps in Croydon Hills is about 5 minutes' drive from Warranwood. For a cinema, Hoyts in Ringwood is about 7 minutes' drive from Warranwood. For an indoor play centre, Timezone in Ringwood is about 7 minutes' drive from Warranwood. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Warranwood is Bundoora Park Farm in Bundoora, about 37 minutes' drive.

Best for

Younger kids at Warranwood's 5 local playgrounds. Free days on the Croydon Hills Connection and in Warranwood's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Warranwood, Ringwood has 23 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Warranwood, Ringwood North has 15 playgrounds. Next door to Warranwood, Wonga Park has 9 playgrounds. Croydon Hills, next to Warranwood, has 6 playgrounds and a skate park. Warrandyte South also borders Warranwood.
