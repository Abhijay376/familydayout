Playground at Warranwood Reserve

Playground in Warranwood · Basic info

Playground at Warranwood Reserve is a public playground in Warranwood, in the Maroondah City area. The Croydon Hills Connection is about 310 m away, and Eden Valley Playground is another option about 550 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus 668 stops at Berrywood Walk/Bemboka Rd, a 7-minute walk away.
