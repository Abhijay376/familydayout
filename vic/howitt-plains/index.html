Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Howitt Plains in the Wellington area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 3 public toilets in Howitt Plains. The Australian Alps Walking Track, Howqua Feeder Track and Bicentennial National Trail all pass through Howitt Plains.

Getting around

There's no train station in Howitt Plains. The closest to Howitt Plains is Stratford Railway Station (V/Line), about 92 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Howitt Plains, so most trips start by car. By car from Howitt Plains, Sale is about 3.5 hours away and Traralgon about 3.5 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Howitt Plains on the Australian Alps Walking Track.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Howitt Plains, Mount Buller has a cinema. Billabong also borders Howitt Plains.
