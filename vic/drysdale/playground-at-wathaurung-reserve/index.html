Playground at Wathaurung Reserve

Playground in Drysdale · Basic info

Playground at Wathaurung Reserve in Drysdale is a public playground in the Greater Geelong City area. Drysdale Library is about 530 m away, and Wathaurong Reserve is another option about 70 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 60 stops at Wathaurung Reserve/Collins St, a 3-minute walk away.
