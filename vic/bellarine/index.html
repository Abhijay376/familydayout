Bellarine is in the Greater Geelong area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bellarine, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bellarine. The closest to Bellarine is Little River Railway Station (V/Line), about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 60 stops in Bellarine. By car from Bellarine, Geelong is about 26 minutes away, Torquay about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bellarine is Northern Bellarine Aquatic Centre in Curlewis, about 11 minutes' drive. For a library, Drysdale Library in Drysdale is about 7 minutes' drive from Bellarine. The closest beach to Bellarine is Grassy Point West Beach in Portarlington, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Bellarine is Portarlington Skate Park in Portarlington, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Bellarine is Village Cinemas in Geelong, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Bellarine is Laserquest in Geelong, about 28 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Adventure Park in Wallington is about 17 minutes' drive from Bellarine. The nearest larger centre is Geelong, about 26 minutes from Bellarine by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Bellarine, the nearest places for kids by road are Hawthorn Reserve in Portarlington (3 min), the playground at Evandale Reserve in Portarlington (3 min), Portarlington Skate Park in Portarlington (5 min), Grassy Point West Beach in Portarlington (7 min), Drysdale Library in Drysdale (7 min) and Old Court House Museum in Drysdale (7 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bellarine.

Nearby suburbs

Portarlington, next to Bellarine, has 10 playgrounds, a beach and a skate park. Next door to Bellarine, Drysdale has 5 playgrounds and a library.
