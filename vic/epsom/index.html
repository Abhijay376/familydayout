Epsom is in the Greater Bendigo area, and our data lists 3 playgrounds and 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Epsom is Epsom Village Green, covering about 1 hectares. Playgrounds around Epsom include the playground at Epsom Village Green. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Epsom. Village Epsom Shopping Centre is the local shopping centre. For walks and rides, Bendigo Creek Trail (North) crosses Epsom. Our data lists 5 cafes and family restaurants in Epsom for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Epsom Railway Station (V/Line: Bendigo - Melbourne via Gisborne and Echuca/Moama - Melbourne via Bendigo or Heathcote) in Epsom. Buses on the 5, 50, 51 and Goornong routes stop in Epsom. V/Line coaches stop in Epsom. By car from Epsom, Bendigo is about 10 minutes away, Castlemaine about 42 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, White Hills Pool in White Hills is about 4 minutes' drive from Epsom. The closest library to Epsom is Bendigo Library in Bendigo, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Epsom is McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk, about 8 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 11 minutes' drive from Epsom. The closest indoor play centre to Epsom is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 14 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 7 minutes' drive from Epsom. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 10 minutes from Epsom by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Epsom's 3 local playgrounds. Free days on the Bendigo Creek Trail (North) and in Epsom's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Epsom, Eaglehawk has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Epsom, White Hills has 1 playground and a pool. Next door to Epsom, Huntly has 3 playgrounds. Next door to Epsom, Ascot has 3 playgrounds. Jackass Flat also borders Epsom.
