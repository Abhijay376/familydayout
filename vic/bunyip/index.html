Bunyip in the Cardinia area has 2 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bunyip is Bunyip Sanctuary, covering about 55 hectares. Koolangarra Park is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Bunyip include Bunyip G212 Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds around Bunyip include the playground at Bunyip Sanctuary and the playground at Koolangarra Park. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Bunyip.

Getting around

Bunyip has a train station: Bunyip Railway Station (V/Line: Bairnsdale - Melbourne via Sale & Traralgon and Traralgon - Melbourne via Morwell, Moe & Pakenham). Buses on the Garfield Station - Traralgon Plaza route stop in Bunyip. V/Line coaches stop in Bunyip. By car from Bunyip, Pakenham is about 21 minutes away, Warragul about 23 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 66 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bunyip is Garfield Swimming Pool in Garfield, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest library to Bunyip is Drouin Library in Drouin, about 16 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Skate BMX Park in Garfield is about 5 minutes' drive from Bunyip. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 21 minutes' drive from Bunyip. The closest indoor play centre to Bunyip is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Bunyip is Wildlife Trail in Tynong North, about 9 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Pakenham, about 21 minutes from Bunyip by car, with 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play. From Bunyip, the nearest places for kids by road are Skate BMX Park in Garfield (5 min), Garfield Swimming Pool in Garfield (5 min), the bmx track at Greenland Court Reserve in Garfield (6 min), Wildlife Trail in Tynong North (9 min), Rock Pool in Tynong North (9 min) and Gumbuya World in Tynong North (9 min).

Nearby suburbs

Garfield, next to Bunyip, has 4 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Longwarry, next to Bunyip, has 3 playgrounds. Garfield North and Bunyip North also border Bunyip.
