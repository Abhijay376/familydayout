Hume Reserve

Park in Bell Park · Official details

Hume Reserve is a park in Bell Park, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Holy Family Primary School/Separation St (bus) is a 5-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 88 m away and dogs are allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Hume Reserve?
Hume Reserve is in Bell Park, Victoria.
Is Hume Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Hume Reserve?
Yes, dogs are allowed.
Are there toilets at Hume Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 88 m away.
How do I get to Hume Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Holy Family Primary School/Separation St (routes 24), a 5-minute walk.
