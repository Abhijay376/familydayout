Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Buangor in the Ararat area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 2 public toilets in Buangor. The Cave Hill Circuit passes through Buangor.

Getting around

There's no train station in Buangor. The closest to Buangor is Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line), about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Buangor. By car from Buangor, Ararat is about 22 minutes away, Stawell about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Buangor is Beaufort Pool in Beaufort, about 19 minutes' drive. For a library, Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort is about 18 minutes' drive from Buangor. The closest skate park to Buangor is the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort, about 19 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Astor Cinema in Ararat is about 21 minutes' drive from Buangor. The nearest larger centre is Ararat, about 22 minutes from Buangor by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Buangor, the nearest places for kids by road are Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort (18 min), the playground at Wotherspoon Reserve in Beaufort (19 min), the playground at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (19 min), the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (19 min), Beaufort Pool in Beaufort (19 min) and Astor Cinema in Ararat (21 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Buangor on the Cave Hill Circuit.
