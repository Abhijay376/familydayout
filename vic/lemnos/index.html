Lemnos in the Greater Shepparton area has 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lemnos is Lemnos Recreation Reserve, covering about 5 hectares. Playgrounds in Lemnos include the playground at Lemnos Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 2 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Lemnos.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lemnos. The closest to Lemnos is Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.8 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lemnos, so most trips start by car. By car from Lemnos, Shepparton is about 9 minutes away, Benalla about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Aquamoves in Shepparton is about 11 minutes' drive from Lemnos. The closest library to Lemnos is Shepparton Library in Shepparton, about 9 minutes' drive. For a beach, Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton is about 13 minutes' drive from Lemnos. The closest skate park to Lemnos is Shepparton Velodrome in Shepparton, about 10 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 9 minutes' drive from Lemnos. The closest indoor play centre to Lemnos is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Lemnos is Kidstown in Mooroopna, about 12 minutes' drive. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Lemnos, about 9 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Lemnos are the playground at Bailey Park in Shepparton (7 min), StarBowl in Shepparton (9 min), Village Cinemas in Shepparton (9 min), Shepparton Library in Shepparton (9 min), Shepparton Heritage Centre in Shepparton (10 min) and Shepparton Velodrome in Shepparton (10 min).
