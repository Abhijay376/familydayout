Landmarks

Crinoline Creek Reference Area is the largest mapped park in Carlisle River, at about 324 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Carlisle River.

Getting around

There's no train station in Carlisle River. The closest to Carlisle River is Colac Railway Station (V/Line), about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Carlisle River, so most trips start by car. By car from Carlisle River, Colac is about 44 minutes away, Warrnambool about 2 hours and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac is about 43 minutes' drive from Carlisle River. The closest skate park to Carlisle River is the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt, about 42 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Carlisle River is Colac Cinemas in Colac, about 43 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Carlisle River is Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac, about 44 minutes' drive. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Carlisle River, about 44 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Carlisle River are the playground at Rex Norman Park in Gellibrand (41 min), Art Reach Studio in Beech Forest (57 min), the playground at John H Gardner Reserve in Beech Forest (57 min), the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (42 min), Clifton Beach in Princetown (55 min) and Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (43 min).
