Eaglehawk in the Greater Bendigo area has 2 playgrounds and 4 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, Eaglehawk has a pool and 2 skate parks.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Eaglehawk is Canterbury Park, covering about 18 hectares. Gladeville Park and Sandhurst H90 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Eaglehawk also has Scout Hall Reserve. Playgrounds in Eaglehawk include Eaglehawk Playspace and the playground at Canterbury Park. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Eaglehawk. Places to visit in Eaglehawk include Peter Krenz Leisure Centre. For older kids in Eaglehawk there's Bendigo BMX Club and McKern Skate Park. The Mulga Bill Bicycle Trail and Lake Neangar Trail all pass through Eaglehawk. Our data lists 3 cafes and family restaurants in Eaglehawk for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Eaglehawk has a train station: Eaglehawk Railway Station (V/Line: Bendigo - Melbourne via Gisborne and Swan Hill - Melbourne via Bendigo). Buses 51, 52 and 53 stop in Eaglehawk. V/Line coaches stop in Eaglehawk. By car from Eaglehawk, Bendigo is about 9 minutes away, Castlemaine about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Bendigo Library in Bendigo is about 8 minutes' drive from Eaglehawk. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 9 minutes' drive from Eaglehawk. The closest indoor play centre to Eaglehawk is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 11 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 11 minutes' drive from Eaglehawk. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 9 minutes from Eaglehawk by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Eaglehawk are Sloan Swim School in California Gully (3 min), Bendigo Steam Engine Museum in North Bendigo (7 min), Golden Dragon Museum in Bendigo (8 min), Bendigo Library in Bendigo (8 min), Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo (9 min) and Intagolf in Golden Gully (11 min).

Best for

Teens who skate or ride, with Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk. Free days on the Mulga Bill Bicycle Trail and in Eaglehawk's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Eaglehawk, California Gully has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Eaglehawk, Huntly has 3 playgrounds. Next door to Eaglehawk, Epsom has 3 playgrounds. Jackass Flat, next to Eaglehawk, has 2 playgrounds. Maiden Gully, Whipstick, Sailors Gully and Myers Flat also border Eaglehawk.
