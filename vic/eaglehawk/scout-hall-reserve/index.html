Scout Hall Reserve

Park in Eaglehawk · Basic info

Scout Hall Reserve is a park in Eaglehawk, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, good for all ages. Lake Neangar/Simpsons Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 463 m away.
