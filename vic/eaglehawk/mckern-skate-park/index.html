McKern Skate Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Eaglehawk · Basic info

McKern Skate Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Eaglehawk, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Eaglehawk Bowling Club/Simpsons Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 136 m away.
