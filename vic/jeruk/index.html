Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Jeruk in the Buloke area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Jeruk. The closest to Jeruk is Kerang Railway Station (V/Line), about 59 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Jeruk, so most trips start by car. By car from Jeruk, Swan Hill is about 1.5 hours away and Bendigo about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof is about 20 minutes' drive from Jeruk. For a library, Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook is about 30 minutes' drive from Jeruk. The closest cinema to Jeruk is Rex Theatre in Charlton, about 32 minutes' drive. From Jeruk, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Centenary Park in Wycheproof (20 min), Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof (20 min), Willandra Museum in Wycheproof (20 min), Quambatook Lions Club Playground in Quambatook (30 min), Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook (30 min) and Quambatook Historical Centre in Quambatook (31 min).
