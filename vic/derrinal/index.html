Derrinal in the Greater Bendigo area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

One Eye Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Derrinal, at about 2 hectares. For walks and rides, O'Keefe Rail Trail crosses Derrinal.

Getting around

Derrinal has no train station; Goornong Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 31 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Heathcote - Bendigo via Junortoun, Axedale, Knowsley route stop in Derrinal. By car from Derrinal, Bendigo is about 33 minutes away, Seymour about 48 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Derrinal is Goornong Swimming Pool in Goornong, about 43 minutes' drive. The closest library to Derrinal is Heathcote Library in Heathcote, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Derrinal is Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote, about 7 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 33 minutes' drive from Derrinal. The closest indoor play centre to Derrinal is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 36 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 31 minutes' drive from Derrinal. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Derrinal, about 33 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Derrinal, the nearest places for kids by road are Heathcote Play Space in Heathcote (7 min), Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote (7 min), Heathcote Library in Heathcote (7 min), Mudwood Studio in Heathcote (8 min), Powder Magazine in Heathcote (9 min) and Tooborac Recreation Reserve Playground in Tooborac (20 min).

Best for

Free days on the O'Keefe Rail Trail and in Derrinal's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Derrinal, Heathcote has 1 playground, a library and a skate park. Knowsley also borders Derrinal.
