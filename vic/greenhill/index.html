Landmarks

Green Hill Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Greenhill, at about 5 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Greenhill. The closest to Greenhill is Malmsbury Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.4 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Greenhill, so most trips start by car. By car from Greenhill, Kyneton is about 14 minutes away, Castlemaine about 29 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 80 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Greenhill is Chewton Swimming Pool in Chewton, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest library to Greenhill is Kyneton Library in Kyneton, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Greenhill is Canoeing in Tylden, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Greenhill is Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton, about 15 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Kyneton, about 14 minutes from Greenhill by car, with 10 playgrounds and a library. From Greenhill, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Malmsbury Botanical Gardens in Malmsbury (8 min), Kyneton Library in Kyneton (15 min), Metcalfe Recreation Reserve Playground in Metcalfe (15 min), Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton (15 min), Kyneton Museum in Kyneton (14 min) and Canoeing in Tylden (25 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Greenhill.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Greenhill, Kyneton has 10 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Greenhill, Malmsbury has 1 playground. Edgecombe also borders Greenhill.
