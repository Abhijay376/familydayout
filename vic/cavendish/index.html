Our data lists 1 playground and 6 parks and reserves in Cavendish, in the Southern Grampians area.

Landmarks

Cavendish Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Cavendish, at about 24 hectares. Gatum Gatum Flora Reserve and Geerak Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Cavendish include State Forest, Ron Lewis Park and Cavendish Town Square Memorial Park. Playgrounds in Cavendish include the playground at Cavendish Town Square Memorial Park. Our data shows 3 public toilets and 6 off-leash dog parks in Cavendish.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cavendish. The closest to Cavendish is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 84 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cavendish, so most trips start by car. By car from Cavendish, Hamilton is about 22 minutes away and Horsham about 82 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cavendish is Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Hamilton, about 22 minutes' drive. For a library, Hamilton Library in Hamilton is about 22 minutes' drive from Cavendish. For a skate park, hamilton skate park in Hamilton is about 24 minutes' drive from Cavendish. The closest cinema to Cavendish is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 22 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 22 minutes from Cavendish by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Cavendish are Big Woolbales & Campe Motor Museum in Hamilton (20 min), Coleraine Road Playground in Hamilton (20 min), Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Hamilton (22 min), Mud Gallery in Hamilton (22 min), Hamilton Library in Hamilton (22 min) and Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton (22 min).
