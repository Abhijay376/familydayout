Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bindi in the East Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Bindi has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 81 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bindi, so most trips start by car. By car from Bindi, Bairnsdale is about 2 hours away and Lakes Entrance about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek is about 33 minutes' drive from Bindi. For a skate park, the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo is about 40 minutes' drive from Bindi. The closest places for kids by road from Bindi are Great Alpine Gallery in Swifts Creek (32 min), Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek (33 min), Petersens Art Gallery & Museum in Omeo (40 min), the playground at Omeo Historical Park & Museum in Omeo (40 min) and the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo (40 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bindi.

Nearby suburbs

Swifts Creek, next to Bindi, has 1 playground and a pool. Next door to Bindi, Omeo has 2 playgrounds and a skate park. Brumby and Benambra also border Bindi.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Bindi, without traffic.
