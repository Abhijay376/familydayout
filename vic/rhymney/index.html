Rhymney is in the Ararat area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Rhymney is Rhymney Reef Bushland Reserve, covering about 2 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Rhymney. The closest to Rhymney is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 17 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Rhymney, so most trips start by car. By car from Rhymney, Stawell is about 37 minutes away and Ararat about 38 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell is about 37 minutes' drive from Rhymney. The closest library to Rhymney is Ararat Library in Ararat, about 38 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell is about 36 minutes' drive from Rhymney. For a cinema, Astor Cinema in Ararat is about 38 minutes' drive from Rhymney. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Rhymney is Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell, about 33 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Stawell, about 37 minutes from Rhymney by car, with 6 playgrounds and a pool. From Rhymney, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Great Western Memorial Park in Great Western (24 min), Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell (33 min), the playground at Taylors Gully Park in Stawell (36 min), the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell (36 min), Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell (37 min) and Railway Station Art and Crafts Gallery in Stawell (37 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Rhymney.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Rhymney, Great Western has 1 playground. Black Range also borders Rhymney.
