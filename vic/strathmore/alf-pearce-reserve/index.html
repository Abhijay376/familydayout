Alf Pearce Reserve

Park in Strathmore · Official details

Alf Pearce Reserve is a park in Strathmore, in the Moonee Valley City Council area, good for all ages. Lloyd St/Carnarvon Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 873 m away.

Questions parents ask

Where is Alf Pearce Reserve?
Alf Pearce Reserve is in Strathmore, Victoria.
Is Alf Pearce Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Alf Pearce Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 873 m away.
How do I get to Alf Pearce Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Lloyd St/Carnarvon Rd (routes 469), a 3-minute walk.
