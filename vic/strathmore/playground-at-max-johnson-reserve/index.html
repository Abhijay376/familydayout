Playground at Max Johnson Reserve

Playground in Strathmore · Official details

Playground at Max Johnson Reserve in Strathmore is a public playground in the Moonee Valley City area. The Moonee Ponds Creek Trail is about 330 m away, and Playground in Strathmore is another option about 110 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 430 m from the playground, and bus 469 stops at Loeman St/Napier St, a 3-minute walk away.
