Max Johnson Reserve

Park in Strathmore · Official details

Max Johnson Reserve is a park in Strathmore, in the Moonee Valley City Council area, good for all ages. Loeman St/Napier St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 460 m away.

Questions parents ask

Where is Max Johnson Reserve?
Max Johnson Reserve is in Strathmore, Victoria.
Is Max Johnson Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Max Johnson Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 460 m away.
How do I get to Max Johnson Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Loeman St/Napier St (routes 469), a 3-minute walk.
