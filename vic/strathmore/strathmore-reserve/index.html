Strathmore Reserve

Park in Strathmore · Basic info

Strathmore Reserve is a park in Strathmore, in the Moonee Valley City Council area, good for all ages. Strathaird St/Mascoma St (bus) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 903 m away.
