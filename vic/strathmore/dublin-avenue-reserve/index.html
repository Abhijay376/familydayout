Dublin Avenue Reserve

Park in Strathmore · Basic info

Dublin Avenue Reserve is a park in Strathmore, in the Moonee Valley City Council area, good for all ages. Lloyd St/Carnarvon Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1003 m away.
