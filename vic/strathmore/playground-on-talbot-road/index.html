Playground at Talbot Road-Wallace Crescent Reserve

Playground in Strathmore · Basic info

This is a public playground in Strathmore. The Moonee Ponds Creek Trail is about 30 m away, and for food, Marlen's Warehouse is about 340 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.1 km from the playground, and bus 512 stops at Pascoe Vale Rd/Woodland St, a 6-minute walk away.
