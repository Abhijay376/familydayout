Goongerah in the East Gippsland area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Goongerah is Errinundra National Park, covering about 43,161 hectares. Brodribb River Headwaters Natural Catchment Area and Brodribb Remote and Natural Area are the next largest. Goongerah also has Goongerah. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Goongerah. Picnic areas in Goongerah include Goongerah. The Greens Road passes through Goongerah.

Getting around

Goongerah has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 110 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Goongerah, so most trips start by car. By car from Goongerah, Lakes Entrance is about 2 hours away and Bairnsdale about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Free days on the Greens Road and in Goongerah's parks.
