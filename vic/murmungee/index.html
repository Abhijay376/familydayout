Murmungee is in the Wangaratta area, and our data lists 5 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Cunningham Gully Historic Reserve is the largest mapped park in Murmungee, at about 36 hectares. Murmungee I81 Bushland Reserve and Murmungee I37 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Murmungee include Murmungee Lookout and Murmungee Scenic Reserve. Picnic areas in Murmungee include Murmungee Lookout. For walks and rides, Burgoigee mining race crosses Murmungee.

Getting around

Murmungee has no train station; Chiltern Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 33 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Murmungee, so most trips start by car. By car from Murmungee, Wangaratta is about 36 minutes away and Bright about 41 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Beechworth Swimming Pool in Beechworth is about 12 minutes' drive from Murmungee. The closest library to Murmungee is Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford, about 15 minutes' drive. For a beach, Sandy Beach in Beechworth is about 13 minutes' drive from Murmungee. For a skate park, the pump track on Alma Road in Beechworth is about 15 minutes' drive from Murmungee. The closest cinema to Murmungee is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 33 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 36 minutes from Murmungee by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Murmungee are Beechworth Galleries in Beechworth (11 min), Beechworth Swimming Pool in Beechworth (12 min), Beechworth Contemporary Art Space in Beechworth (12 min), the playground at Newtown Park in Beechworth (12 min), Sandy Beach in Beechworth (13 min) and the pump track on Alma Road in Beechworth (15 min).

Best for

Free days on the Burgoigee mining race and in Murmungee's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Murmungee, Beechworth has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. Bowmans Forest also borders Murmungee.
