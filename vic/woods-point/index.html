Woods Point is in the Mansfield area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Other parks and reserves in Woods Point include Jack Scotts and Connor Plain. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Woods Point. Picnic areas in Woods Point include Jack Scotts. For camping, there's Connor Plain. For walks and rides, Australian Alps Walking Track, McMillans Walking Track and Knockwood Trail Pt 2 (approx way) cross Woods Point.

Getting around

Woods Point has no train station; Moe Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 67 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Mansfield - Woods Point via Jamieson route stop in Woods Point. By car from Woods Point, Mansfield is about 1.5 hours away and Healesville about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Free days on the Australian Alps Walking Track and in Woods Point's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Woods Point, Mount Buller has a cinema. Licola, Jericho and Gaffneys Creek also border Woods Point.
