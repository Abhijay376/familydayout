Echuca West is in the Campaspe area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Piper Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Echuca West, at about 13 hectares. Echuca West Bushland Reserve and Wharparilla Recreation Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Echuca West. The closest to Echuca West is Echuca Railway Station (V/Line), about 8.1 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Echuca West, so most trips start by car. By car from Echuca West, Echuca is about 11 minutes away, Shepparton about 73 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Echuca West is Lockington Pool in Lockington, about 19 minutes' drive. For a library, Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca is about 11 minutes' drive from Echuca West. For a skate park, Moama Echuca Community Skate Park in Echuca is about 13 minutes' drive from Echuca West. The closest cinema to Echuca West is Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca, about 11 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram is about 44 minutes' drive from Echuca West. Echuca is the closest bigger centre to Echuca West, about 11 minutes away by car, and has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Echuca West are the playground at Echuca Lions Park in Echuca (10 min), Holden Museum in Echuca (10 min), Hopwood Gardens Playground in Echuca (10 min), Port of Echuca Discovery Centre in Echuca (10 min), Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca (11 min) and Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca (11 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Echuca West.
