Sedgwick is in the Greater Bendigo area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Sedgwick is Sedgwick State Forest, covering about 1,212 hectares. Sedgwick Bushland Reserve is the next largest. For walks and rides, Goldfields Track, Leanganook Track and Cavagna Road MTB track cross Sedgwick.

Getting around

There's no train station in Sedgwick. The closest to Sedgwick is Kangaroo Flat Railway Station (V/Line), about 10 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Sedgwick, so most trips start by car. By car from Sedgwick, Bendigo is about 21 minutes away, Castlemaine about 32 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Brennan Park Swimming Pool in Kennington is about 21 minutes' drive from Sedgwick. The closest library to Sedgwick is Kangaroo Flat Library in Kangaroo Flat, about 21 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Ewing Park Skating in Bendigo is about 20 minutes' drive from Sedgwick. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 21 minutes' drive from Sedgwick. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 20 minutes' drive from Sedgwick. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Sedgwick is The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo, about 25 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 21 minutes from Sedgwick by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Sedgwick, the nearest places for kids by road are Clydebank Court Playspace in Strathfieldsaye (16 min), the playground at Coomoora Park in Strathfieldsaye (16 min), Ewing Park Skating in Bendigo (20 min), Brennan Park Swimming Pool in Kennington (21 min), Intagolf in Golden Gully (20 min) and Discovery Science & Technology Centre in Bendigo (20 min).

Best for

Free days on the Goldfields Track and in Sedgwick's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Harcourt North, Emu Creek and Axe Creek also border Sedgwick.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Sedgwick, without traffic.
