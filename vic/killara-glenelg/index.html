Our data lists 5 parks and reserves in Killara (Glenelg), in the Glenelg area.

Landmarks

Weecurra State Forest is the largest mapped park in Killara (Glenelg), at about 7,546 hectares. Killara State Forest and Stokes River (5) Streamside Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Killara (Glenelg) include Mocamboro Flora Reserve and Barrys Swamp Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Killara (Glenelg). The closest to Killara (Glenelg) is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 117 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Killara (Glenelg), so most trips start by car. By car from Killara (Glenelg), Hamilton is about 70 minutes away and Portland about 82 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Merino Swimming Pool in Merino is about 32 minutes' drive from Killara (Glenelg). The closest places for kids by road from Killara (Glenelg) are the playground at Lions Park in Merino (31 min), Merino Swimming Pool in Merino (32 min), Gorman's art gallery in Casterton (40 min), Casterton Pool in Casterton (41 min), Dartmoor Coach House Museum in Dartmoor (41 min) and the playground at Peter Francis Points Arboretum in Coleraine (59 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Killara (Glenelg).
