Kanya is in the Northern Grampians area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Kanya is Kanya Bushland Reserve, covering about 21 hectares.

Getting around

Kanya has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 56 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kanya, so most trips start by car. By car from Kanya, Stawell is about 41 minutes away and Ararat about 67 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud is about 40 minutes' drive from Kanya. The closest skate park to Kanya is the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell, about 45 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell is about 43 minutes' drive from Kanya. The nearest larger centre is Stawell, about 41 minutes from Kanya by car, with 6 playgrounds and a pool. From Kanya, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Shanahan Park in Marnoo (23 min), Navarre Playground in Navarre (33 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (40 min), Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell (41 min), Railway Station Art and Crafts Gallery in Stawell (42 min) and Woods' Farming and Heritage Museum in Rupanyup (42 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Kanya.
