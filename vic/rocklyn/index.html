Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Rocklyn in the Hepburn area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

The Goldfields Track and Great Dividing Trail all pass through Rocklyn.

Getting around

Rocklyn has no train station; Creswick Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Rocklyn, so most trips start by car. By car from Rocklyn, Daylesford is about 24 minutes away, Ballarat about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Rocklyn is Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford, about 24 minutes' drive. For a library, Daylesford Library in Daylesford is about 24 minutes' drive from Rocklyn. For a skate park, Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick is about 24 minutes' drive from Rocklyn. The closest cinema to Rocklyn is Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central, about 38 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 39 minutes' drive from Rocklyn. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek is about 30 minutes' drive from Rocklyn. Daylesford is the closest bigger centre to Rocklyn, about 24 minutes away by car, and has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Rocklyn are the playground at Bullarook Recreation Reserve in Bullarook (23 min), the playground at Korweinguboora Recreation Reserve in Spargo Creek (29 min), Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford (24 min), Daylesford Library in Daylesford (24 min), Daylesford Spa Country Railway in Daylesford (26 min) and Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick (24 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Rocklyn on the Goldfields Track.

Nearby suburbs

Leonards Hill, Langdons Hill, Korweinguboora and Dean also border Rocklyn.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Rocklyn, without traffic.
