Invermay Park in the Ballarat area has 2 playgrounds and 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Invermay Park is Rizal Park, covering about 1 hectares. Invermay Park also has Jack Toohey Park. Playgrounds around Invermay Park include the playground at Rizal Park and the playground at Jack Toohey Park. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Invermay Park.

Getting around

Invermay Park has no train station; Ballarat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 3.4 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 10 and 13 stop in Invermay Park. By car from Invermay Park, Ballarat is about 6 minutes away, Daylesford about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, the water play at Midlands Reserve in Ballarat North is about 2 minutes' drive from Invermay Park. For a library, Central Highlands Regional Library Corp Ballarat branch in Ballarat Central is about 5 minutes' drive from Invermay Park. For a skate park, the skate park at Len T Frazer Reserve in Ballarat East is about 8 minutes' drive from Invermay Park. The closest cinema to Invermay Park is Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central, about 6 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 10 minutes' drive from Invermay Park. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Invermay Park is Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East, about 12 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Ballarat, about 6 minutes from Invermay Park by car, with 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Invermay Park are the water play at Midlands Reserve in Ballarat North (2 min), Central Highlands Regional Library Corp Ballarat branch in Ballarat Central (5 min), Black Hill Swimming Pool in Black Hill (6 min), Ballarat Fine Art Gallery in Ballarat Central (6 min), Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central (6 min) and Golden City Steamer Museum in Lake Wendouree (6 min).
