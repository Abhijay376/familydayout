Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Marraweeney, in the Strathbogie area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Marraweeney is Marraweeny I54 Bushland Reserve, covering about 87 hectares. Marraweeny I55 Bushland Reserve and Boho Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Marraweeney. The closest to Marraweeney is Violet Town Railway Station (V/Line), about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Marraweeney, so most trips start by car. By car from Marraweeney, Benalla is about 43 minutes away, Shepparton about 65 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa is about 35 minutes' drive from Marraweeney. For a library, Euroa Library in Euroa is about 36 minutes' drive from Marraweeney. The closest skate park to Marraweeney is Euroa Skate Park in Euroa, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Marraweeney is Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa, about 35 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Benalla, about 43 minutes from Marraweeney by car, with 5 playgrounds and a pool. From Marraweeney, the nearest places for kids by road are Violet Town Recreation Reserve East Playground in Violet Town (22 min), Violet Town Recreation Reserve West Playground in Violet Town (22 min), Farmers Arms Hotel Museum in Euroa (34 min), Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa (35 min), Euroa Skate Park in Euroa (35 min) and Euroa Library in Euroa (36 min).
