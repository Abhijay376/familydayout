Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tabberabbera is Sinnotts, covering about 64 hectares.

Getting around

Tabberabbera has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 38 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tabberabbera, so most trips start by car. By car from Tabberabbera, Bairnsdale is about 1.5 hours away and Sale about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Tabberabbera, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Amy Shand Park in Lindenow (76 min).
