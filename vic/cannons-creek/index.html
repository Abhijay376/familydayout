Landmarks

North Western Port Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Cannons Creek, at about 2,022 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Cannons Creek.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cannons Creek. The closest to Cannons Creek is Cranbourne Railway Station (metro), about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 795 stops in Cannons Creek. By car from Cannons Creek, Cranbourne is about 15 minutes away, Frankston about 20 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 55 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cannons Creek is King Swim in Clyde North, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest library to Cannons Creek is Cranbourne Library in Cranbourne East, about 14 minutes' drive. For a beach, Frankston Beach in Frankston is about 24 minutes' drive from Cannons Creek. The closest skate park to Cannons Creek is Pearcedale BMX Track in Pearcedale, about 10 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Frankston is about 21 minutes' drive from Cannons Creek. The closest indoor play centre to Cannons Creek is Kidz Shed in Hastings, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Cannons Creek is Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale, about 7 minutes' drive. Cranbourne is the closest bigger centre to Cannons Creek, about 15 minutes away by car, and has 27 playgrounds. From Cannons Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Blind Bight Foreshore Reserve in Blind Bight (7 min), the playground at Blind Bight Bushland Reserve in Blind Bight (8 min), Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale (7 min), Pearcedale BMX Track in Pearcedale (10 min), Fisherman's Cottage in Tooradin (9 min) and Skate Park in Clyde (12 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Cannons Creek.

Nearby suburbs
