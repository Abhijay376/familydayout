Cathkin is in the Murrindindi area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Cathkin, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Cathkin. For walks and rides, Great Victorian Rail Trail crosses Cathkin.

Getting around

Cathkin has no train station; Euroa Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 44 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cathkin, so most trips start by car. By car from Cathkin, Seymour is about 45 minutes away, Mansfield about 48 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cathkin is Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest library to Cathkin is Alexandra Library in Alexandra, about 13 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea is about 16 minutes' drive from Cathkin. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 45 minutes from Cathkin by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Cathkin are Yarck Recreation Reserve Playground in Yarck (6 min), Alexandra Library in Alexandra (13 min), the playground at Leckie Park in Alexandra (14 min), Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra (14 min), Alexandra Timber Tramway and Museum in Alexandra (15 min) and the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea (16 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Cathkin on the Great Victorian Rail Trail.
