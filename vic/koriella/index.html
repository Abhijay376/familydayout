Koriella is in the Murrindindi area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Koriella, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 2 public toilets in Koriella. For walks and rides, Great Victorian Rail Trail crosses Koriella.

Getting around

There's no train station in Koriella. The closest to Koriella is Euroa Railway Station (V/Line), about 46 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Koriella, so most trips start by car. By car from Koriella, Mansfield is about 52 minutes away, Seymour about 52 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra is about 8 minutes' drive from Koriella. For a library, Alexandra Library in Alexandra is about 7 minutes' drive from Koriella. The closest skate park to Koriella is the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea, about 24 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Mansfield, about 52 minutes from Koriella by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Koriella are Alexandra Library in Alexandra (7 min), the playground at Leckie Park in Alexandra (8 min), Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra (8 min), Alexandra Timber Tramway and Museum in Alexandra (9 min), Yarck Recreation Reserve Playground in Yarck (10 min) and the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea (24 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Koriella on the Great Victorian Rail Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Koriella, Alexandra has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Yarck, next to Koriella, has 1 playground. Cathkin also borders Koriella.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Koriella, without traffic.
