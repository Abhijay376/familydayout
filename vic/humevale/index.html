Landmarks

Yan Yean (north) Reference Area is the largest mapped park in Humevale, at about 80 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Humevale. The closest to Humevale is Mernda Railway Station (metro), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 384 stops in Humevale. By car from Humevale, Broadmeadows is about 43 minutes away, Sunshine about 54 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 56 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Humevale is Whittlesea Swimming Centre in Whittlesea, about 10 minutes' drive. For a library, Mernda Library in Mernda is about 21 minutes' drive from Humevale. The closest skate park to Humevale is Epping Skate Park in Epping, about 30 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Mill Park is about 29 minutes' drive from Humevale. The closest indoor play centre to Humevale is Timezone in Mill Park, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Humevale is Funfields in Whittlesea, about 13 minutes' drive. Broadmeadows is the closest bigger centre to Humevale, about 43 minutes away by car, and has 12 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Humevale are Whittlesea Swimming Centre in Whittlesea (10 min), Church Street Park Playground in Whittlesea (10 min), Fir Street Park Playground in Whittlesea (11 min), Birdy Cove in Whittlesea (13 min), Funfields in Whittlesea (13 min) and Mernda Library in Mernda (21 min).
