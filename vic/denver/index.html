Denver in the Hepburn area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Denver Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Denver, at about 2 hectares. Denver I10 Bushland Reserve and Denver I87 Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Denver. The closest to Denver is Malmsbury Railway Station (V/Line), about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Denver, so most trips start by car. By car from Denver, Daylesford is about 18 minutes away, Kyneton about 20 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 88 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Denver is Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford, about 17 minutes' drive. For a library, Daylesford Library in Daylesford is about 17 minutes' drive from Denver. For a beach, Canoeing in Tylden is about 27 minutes' drive from Denver. The closest skate park to Denver is Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton, about 21 minutes' drive. Daylesford is the closest bigger centre to Denver, about 18 minutes away by car, and has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Denver, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Malmsbury Botanical Gardens in Malmsbury (11 min), Daylesford Spa Country Railway in Daylesford (17 min), Kyneton Museum in Kyneton (19 min), Daylesford Library in Daylesford (17 min), Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford (17 min) and Kyneton Library in Kyneton (21 min).
