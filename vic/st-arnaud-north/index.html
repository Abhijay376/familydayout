Landmarks

Darkbonee I50 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in St Arnaud North, at about 25 hectares.

Getting around

St Arnaud North has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 71 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside St Arnaud North, so most trips start by car. By car from St Arnaud North, Stawell is about 68 minutes away and Maryborough about 71 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to St Arnaud North is St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud, about 7 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Donald BMX Track in Donald is about 28 minutes' drive from St Arnaud North. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 38 minutes' drive from St Arnaud North. The closest places for kids by road from St Arnaud North are Apex Park Playground in St Arnaud (5 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (7 min), Market Square Park Playground in St Arnaud (6 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (24 min), Donald BMX Track in Donald (28 min) and Rex Theatre in Charlton (38 min).
