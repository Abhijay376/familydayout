Pranjip in the Strathbogie area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Pranjip Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Pranjip, at about 5 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Pranjip. The closest to Pranjip is Murchison East Railway Station (V/Line), about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Pranjip, so most trips start by car. By car from Pranjip, Shepparton is about 45 minutes away, Seymour about 54 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Pranjip is Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest library to Pranjip is Euroa Library in Euroa, about 31 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Euroa Skate Park in Euroa is about 32 minutes' drive from Pranjip. The closest cinema to Pranjip is Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Pranjip is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 45 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Emerald Bank Leisure Land in Kialla is about 37 minutes' drive from Pranjip. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 45 minutes from Pranjip by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Pranjip, the nearest places for kids by road are Longwood Recreation Reserve Playground in Longwood (22 min), Bicentennial Park Playground in Euroa (30 min), Euroa Library in Euroa (31 min), Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa (31 min), Euroa Skate Park in Euroa (32 min) and Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa (32 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Pranjip.

Nearby suburbs

Nagambie, next to Pranjip, has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Longwood, next to Pranjip, has 1 playground.
