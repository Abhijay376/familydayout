Wycheproof South is in the Buloke area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Wycheproof South, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wycheproof South. The closest to Wycheproof South is Kerang Railway Station (V/Line), about 80 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wycheproof South, so most trips start by car. By car from Wycheproof South, Swan Hill is about 1.5 hours away and Stawell about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Wycheproof South is Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof, about 9 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Donald BMX Track in Donald is about 31 minutes' drive from Wycheproof South. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 22 minutes' drive from Wycheproof South. From Wycheproof South, the nearest places for kids by road are Willandra Museum in Wycheproof (7 min), the playground at Centenary Park in Wycheproof (8 min), Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof (9 min), the playground at Gordon Park in Charlton (22 min), Rex Theatre in Charlton (22 min) and Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (22 min).
