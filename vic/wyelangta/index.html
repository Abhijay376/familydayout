Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Wyelangta in the Colac Otway area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

The Little Aire Falls Walk and Triplet Falls Loop all pass through Wyelangta.

Getting around

Wyelangta has no train station; Colac Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 37 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wyelangta, so most trips start by car. By car from Wyelangta, Colac is about 48 minutes away, Warrnambool about 1.5 hours and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest beach to Wyelangta is Wreck Beach in Gellibrand Lower, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Wyelangta is the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt, about 45 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Wyelangta is Wildlife Wonders in Apollo Bay, about 44 minutes' drive. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Wyelangta, about 48 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Wyelangta are Art Reach Studio in Beech Forest (14 min), the playground at John H Gardner Reserve in Beech Forest (14 min), the playground at Rex Norman Park in Gellibrand (28 min), Wreck Beach in Gellibrand Lower (34 min), Glenaire East in Hordern Vale (32 min) and the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (45 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Wyelangta on the Little Aire Falls Walk.

Nearby suburbs

Carlisle River, next to Wyelangta, has 1 playground. Beech Forest, next to Wyelangta, has 1 playground. Johanna, Glenaire and Ferguson also border Wyelangta.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Wyelangta, without traffic.
