Gil Gil in the Buloke area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Lake Jil Jil Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Gil Gil, at about 10 hectares.

Getting around

Gil Gil has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 107 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gil Gil, so most trips start by car. By car from Gil Gil, Stawell is about 78 minutes away and Horsham about 82 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Donald Swimming Pool in Donald is about 11 minutes' drive from Gil Gil. For a skate park, Donald BMX Track in Donald is about 10 minutes' drive from Gil Gil. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 40 minutes' drive from Gil Gil. From Gil Gil, the nearest places for kids by road are Donald BMX Track in Donald (10 min), the playground at Donald Public Park in Donald (11 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (11 min), Guthrie Park Playground in Rich Avon West (24 min), Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem (36 min) and Rex Theatre in Charlton (40 min).
