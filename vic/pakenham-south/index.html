Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Pakenham South in the Cardinia area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Pakenham South. The closest to Pakenham South is East Pakenham Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.1 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Pakenham South, so most trips start by car. By car from Pakenham South, Pakenham is about 11 minutes away, Cranbourne about 27 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 55 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Pakenham South is Pakenham Outdoor Swimming Pool in Pakenham, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest library to Pakenham South is Pakenham Library in Pakenham, about 11 minutes' drive. For a beach, Seaford Beach in Seaford is about 43 minutes' drive from Pakenham South. The closest skate park to Pakenham South is P.B Ronald Skate Park in Pakenham, about 12 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Narre Warren is about 24 minutes' drive from Pakenham South. For an indoor play centre, Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham is about 11 minutes' drive from Pakenham South. For a zoo or wildlife park, MonkeyG's in Pakenham is about 9 minutes' drive from Pakenham South. Pakenham is the closest bigger centre to Pakenham South, about 11 minutes away by car, and has 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play. From Pakenham South, the nearest places for kids by road are MonkeyG's in Pakenham (9 min), the playground at Burke Park in Pakenham (10 min), Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham (11 min), Crocs Playcentre Pakenham in Pakenham (11 min), Pakenham Library in Pakenham (11 min) and P.B Ronald Skate Park in Pakenham (12 min).
