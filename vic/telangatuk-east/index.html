Our data lists 1 playground and 5 parks and reserves in Telangatuk East, in the Horsham area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Telangatuk East is Black Range State Park, covering about 11,731 hectares. Telangatuk State Forest and Daahl Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Telangatuk East include Telangatuk I42 Bushland Reserve and Connangorach I44 Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Telangatuk East. For walks and rides, Rock Escarpment Discovery Walk crosses Telangatuk East.

Getting around

There's no train station in Telangatuk East. The closest to Telangatuk East is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 88 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Telangatuk East, so most trips start by car. By car from Telangatuk East, Horsham is about 62 minutes away and Stawell about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral is about 37 minutes' drive from Telangatuk East. The closest places for kids by road from Telangatuk East are Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral (37 min), Glendinning Street Playground in Balmoral (38 min), Harrow Discovery Centre in Harrow (59 min), Bake House Studio in Harrow (59 min), the playground at Natimuk Showgrounds Recreation Reserve in Natimuk (62 min) and the skate park at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk (61 min).

Best for

Free days on the Rock Escarpment Discovery Walk and in Telangatuk East's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Telangatuk East, Balmoral has 1 playground and a pool. Kanagulk, Cherrypool and Brimpaen also border Telangatuk East.
