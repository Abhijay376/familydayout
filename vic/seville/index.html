Seville is in the Yarra Ranges area, and our data lists 2 playgrounds and 7 parks and reserves in the suburb. On top of that, Seville has a pool.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Seville is Warburton Highway Reserve, covering about 1 hectares. Seville also has Valley Reserve, Amanda Court Reserve and Belair Close Reserve. Playgrounds around Seville include Seville Pool Playground and the playground at Valley Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Seville. Places to visit in Seville include Seville Water Play Park. For walks and rides, Lilydale-Warburton Rail Trail and Warburton Rail Trail cross Seville. Our data lists 4 cafes and family restaurants in Seville for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Seville. The closest to Seville is Lilydale Railway Station (metro), about 10 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 683 and 965 stop in Seville. By car from Seville, Lilydale is about 13 minutes away, Healesville about 24 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 54 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Seville is Mount Evelyn Reading Room in Mount Evelyn, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Seville is Mount Evelyn Skate Park in Mount Evelyn, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Seville is Reading Cinemas in Chirnside Park, about 18 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, PlayPlace in Mooroolbark is about 19 minutes' drive from Seville. For a zoo or wildlife park, Lyre Bird enclosure in Badger Creek is about 20 minutes' drive from Seville. The nearest larger centre is Lilydale, about 13 minutes from Seville by car, with 12 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Seville are Mont De Lancey Museum in Wandin North (7 min), Mount Evelyn Skate Park in Mount Evelyn (10 min), the pump track at Lilydale-Warburton in Mount Evelyn (10 min), Mount Evelyn Reading Room in Mount Evelyn (10 min), Yarra Ranges Regional Museum in Lilydale (13 min) and Lilydale Outdoor Pool in Lilydale (13 min).

Best for

Free days on the Lilydale-Warburton Rail Trail and in Seville's parks.
