Seville Pool Playground

Playground in Seville · Basic info

This is a public playground in Seville. Seville Water Play Park is about 70 m away, and Meat & Dumpling is about 150 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and buses 683 and 965 stop at Seville War Memorial/Warburton Hwy, a 2-minute walk away.
