Berrybank is in the Corangamite area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Berrybank, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Berrybank.

Getting around

There's no train station in Berrybank. The closest to Berrybank is Colac Railway Station (V/Line), about 41 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Berrybank, so most trips start by car. By car from Berrybank, Colac is about 45 minutes away, Ballarat about 64 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Berrybank is Lismore Pool in Lismore, about 11 minutes' drive. For a library, Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum is about 21 minutes' drive from Berrybank. For a skate park, Rokewood Skate Park in Rokewood is about 35 minutes' drive from Berrybank. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Berrybank, about 45 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Berrybank, the nearest places for kids by road are Lismore Pool in Lismore (11 min), the playground at Cressy Picnic Area in Cressy (13 min), Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum (21 min), Rokewood Recreation Reserve Playground in Rokewood (35 min), Rokewood Skate Park in Rokewood (35 min) and Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Berrybank.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Berrybank, Lismore has a pool. Mount Bute, Foxhow and Duverney also border Berrybank.
