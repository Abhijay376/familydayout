Moutajup is in the Southern Grampians area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Moutajup, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Moutajup has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 83 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Moutajup, so most trips start by car. By car from Moutajup, Hamilton is about 18 minutes away and Ararat about 69 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Moutajup is Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Hamilton, about 19 minutes' drive. For a library, Hamilton Library in Hamilton is about 18 minutes' drive from Moutajup. The closest skate park to Moutajup is hamilton skate park in Hamilton, about 17 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton is about 18 minutes' drive from Moutajup. Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Moutajup, about 18 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Moutajup are Dunkeld Memorial Park Playground in Dunkeld (9 min), health station 3/4 playground in Hamilton (14 min), hamilton skate park in Hamilton (17 min), Sir Reginald Ansett Transport Museum in Hamilton (17 min), Hamilton Gallery in Hamilton (18 min) and Hamilton Library in Hamilton (18 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Moutajup.

Nearby suburbs

Dunkeld, next to Moutajup, has 1 playground. Hensley Park and Croxton East also border Moutajup.
