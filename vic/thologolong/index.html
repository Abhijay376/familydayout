Getting around

There's no train station in Thologolong. The closest to Thologolong is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 43 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Thologolong, so most trips start by car. By car from Thologolong, Wodonga is about 60 minutes away and Wangaratta about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Thologolong is Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta, about 41 minutes' drive. The closest library to Thologolong is Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta, about 40 minutes' drive. For a beach, Pebble Beach in Walwa is about 36 minutes' drive from Thologolong. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 60 minutes from Thologolong by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Thologolong, the nearest places for kids by road are Granya Museum in Granya (15 min), Pebble Beach in Walwa (36 min), The Spotted Goanna in Walwa (38 min), Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta (40 min), the playground at Roy Williams Park in Bellbridge (38 min) and Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta (41 min).
