Our data lists 8 parks and reserves in Moornapa, in the Wellington area.

Landmarks

Freestone / Wurrundyan'garla State Forest is the largest mapped park in Moornapa, at about 20,975 hectares. Moornapa State Forest and Stockdale State Forest are the next largest. Moornapa also has Lloyds Knob, Shadeys Place and Johnstons Flat. Picnic areas in Moornapa include Johnstons Flat, Lees Creek Camping Area and Lees Creek Picnic Area. For walks and rides, Track Ninety Six, Alistair Fielding Walk and Alister Fielding Memorial Walk cross Moornapa.

Getting around

Moornapa has no train station; Stratford Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 34 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Moornapa, so most trips start by car. By car from Moornapa, Bairnsdale is about 80 minutes away and Sale about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Moornapa, the nearest places for kids by road are Anzac Park Playground in Briagolong (62 min), Boisdale Recreation Reserve Playground in Boisdale (72 min) and Dargo Museum in Dargo (57 min).

Best for

Free days on the Track Ninety Six and in Moornapa's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Briagolong, next to Moornapa, has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Moornapa, Dargo has 1 playground. Stockdale, Glenaladale, Fernbank and Cobbannah also border Moornapa.
