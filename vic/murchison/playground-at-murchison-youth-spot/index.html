Playground at Murchison Youth Spot

Playground in Murchison · Basic info

This is a neighbourhood playground in Murchison, looked after by Greater Shepparton City Council. The Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks passes about 20 m away, and Playground at Goulburn River Heritage River is another option about 180 m away. There's a public toilet about 80 m away, and coaches stop at Murchison/Stevenson St, a 2-minute walk away.
