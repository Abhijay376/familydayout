Playground at Goulburn River Heritage River

Playground in Murchison · Official details

Playground at Goulburn River Heritage River in Murchison is one of the council's larger playgrounds in the Greater Shepparton City area. The Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks is about 30 m away, and Playground at Murchison Youth Spot is another option about 180 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 210 m from the playground, and coaches stop at Murchison/Stevenson St, a 1-minute walk away.
