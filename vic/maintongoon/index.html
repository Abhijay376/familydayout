Maintongoon is in the Murrindindi area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Maintongoon is Maintongoon G105 Bushland Reserve, covering about 751 hectares. Maintongoon G106 Bushland Reserve and Maintongoon G107 Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Maintongoon. The closest to Maintongoon is Euroa Railway Station (V/Line), about 46 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Maintongoon, so most trips start by car. By car from Maintongoon, Mansfield is about 70 minutes away, Seymour about 74 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra is about 22 minutes' drive from Maintongoon. For a library, Alexandra Library in Alexandra is about 21 minutes' drive from Maintongoon. The closest places for kids by road from Maintongoon are Alexandra Timber Tramway and Museum in Alexandra (21 min), the playground at Leckie Park in Alexandra (21 min), Alexandra Library in Alexandra (21 min), Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra (22 min), Yarck Recreation Reserve Playground in Yarck (32 min) and Eildon Swimming Pool in Eildon (41 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Maintongoon.

Nearby suburbs

Alexandra, next to Maintongoon, has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Bonnie Doon, next to Maintongoon, has 1 playground. Devils River also borders Maintongoon.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Maintongoon, without traffic.
