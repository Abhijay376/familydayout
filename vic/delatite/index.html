Delatite is in the Mansfield area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Delatite, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

The Mansfield - Merrijig passes through Delatite.

Getting around

There's no train station in Delatite. The closest to Delatite is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 68 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Delatite, so most trips start by car. By car from Delatite, Mansfield is about 20 minutes away, Benalla about 71 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Mansfield Outdoor Swimming Pool in Mansfield is about 21 minutes' drive from Delatite. The closest library to Delatite is Mansfield Library in Mansfield, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Delatite is the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Delatite is Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield, about 19 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield is about 13 minutes' drive from Delatite. Mansfield is the closest bigger centre to Delatite, about 20 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Delatite, the nearest places for kids by road are Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield (13 min), Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield (19 min), Mansfield Library in Mansfield (19 min), the playground at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (20 min), the playground at Mansfield Botanic Park in Mansfield (20 min) and the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (20 min).
