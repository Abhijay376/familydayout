Edgecombe is in the Macedon Ranges area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Edgecombe, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Edgecombe. The closest to Edgecombe is Kyneton Railway Station (V/Line), about 8.3 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Edgecombe, so most trips start by car. By car from Edgecombe, Kyneton is about 10 minutes away, Castlemaine about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 76 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Chewton Swimming Pool in Chewton is about 32 minutes' drive from Edgecombe. For a library, Kyneton Library in Kyneton is about 10 minutes' drive from Edgecombe. For a beach, Canoeing in Tylden is about 21 minutes' drive from Edgecombe. The closest skate park to Edgecombe is Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton, about 11 minutes' drive. Kyneton is the closest bigger centre to Edgecombe, about 10 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Edgecombe are Jean Haynes Playground in Kyneton (10 min), Kyneton Museum in Kyneton (10 min), Kyneton Library in Kyneton (10 min), Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton (11 min), Kyneton Community Park in Kyneton (11 min) and Canoeing in Tylden (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Edgecombe.

Nearby suburbs

Kyneton, next to Edgecombe, has 10 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Malmsbury, next to Edgecombe, has 1 playground.
