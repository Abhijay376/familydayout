Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Strathewen.

Getting around

Strathewen has no train station; Hurstbridge Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Strathewen, so most trips start by car. By car from Strathewen, Healesville is about 45 minutes away, Ringwood about 45 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 60 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Diamond Creek Outdoor Pool in Diamond Creek is about 27 minutes' drive from Strathewen. For a library, Yarra Plenty Regional Library in Christmas Hills is about 25 minutes' drive from Strathewen. The closest skate park to Strathewen is Hurstbridge Half-pipe in Hurstbridge, about 19 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Mill Park is about 33 minutes' drive from Strathewen. The closest indoor play centre to Strathewen is Timezone in Mill Park, about 32 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Funfields in Whittlesea is about 31 minutes' drive from Strathewen. Healesville is the closest bigger centre to Strathewen, about 45 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Strathewen, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Ryders Flat Reserve in Arthurs Creek (13 min), Childrens Playground in St Andrews (12 min), House of Bottles Museum & Tearooms in Kinglake (22 min), Hurstbridge Half-pipe in Hurstbridge (19 min), Hurstbridge Skate Park in Hurstbridge (20 min) and Yarra Plenty Regional Library in Christmas Hills (25 min).
