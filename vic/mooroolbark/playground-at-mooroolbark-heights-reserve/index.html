Playground at Mooroolbark Heights Reserve

Playground in Mooroolbark · Basic info

Playground at Mooroolbark Heights Reserve in Mooroolbark is a public playground in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. The Brushy Creek Linear Trail is about 210 m away, and for food, Cardigan Pizza is about 380 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 600 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 3 minutes from Mooroolbark.
