Playground on Carronvale Road

Playground in Mooroolbark · Basic info

Playground on Carronvale Road in Mooroolbark is a public playground in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. The Bayswater-Lilydale Trail is about 80 m away, and for food, Hull Road Fish & Chips is about 360 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus 680 stops at Mooroolbark Rd/Hull Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
