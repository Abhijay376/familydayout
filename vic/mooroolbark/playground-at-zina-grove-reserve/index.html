Playground at Zina Grove Reserve

Playground in Mooroolbark · Basic info

Playground at Zina Grove Reserve in Mooroolbark is a public playground in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. The Brushy Creek Linear Trail passes about 10 m away, and Playground at Mooroolbark Heights Reserve is another option about 360 m away. There's a public toilet about 690 m away, and bus 689 stops at Regina St/Cambridge Rd, a 10-minute walk away.
