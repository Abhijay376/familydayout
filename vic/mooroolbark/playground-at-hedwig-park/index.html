Playground at Hedwig Park

Playground in Mooroolbark · Basic info

Playground at Hedwig Park in Mooroolbark is a public playground in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. The Green Spine Shared Path passes about 280 m away, and GAMR Laser Tag & VR is about 560 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.8 km away, and buses 664 and 675 stop at Batterbee Dr/Manchester Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
