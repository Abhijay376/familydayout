Hookey Park

Park in Mooroolbark · Basic info

Hookey Park is a park in Mooroolbark, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Mooroolbark Station/Brice Ave (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 114 m away.
