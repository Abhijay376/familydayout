Playground on Summerhill Park Drive

Playground in Mooroolbark · Basic info

Playground on Summerhill Park Drive is a public playground in Mooroolbark, in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. The Bayswater-Lilydale Trail is about 260 m away, and Playground on Carronvale Road is another option about 560 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus 680 stops at Summerhill Park Dr/Hull Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
