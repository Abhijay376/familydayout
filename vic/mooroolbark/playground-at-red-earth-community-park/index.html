Playground at Red Earth Community Park

Playground in Mooroolbark · Basic info

This is a public playground in Mooroolbark. The Brushy Creek Linear Trail passes about 30 m away, and Mooroolbark Library is about 440 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus 680 stops at Mooroolbark Community Centre/Brice Ave, a 1-minute walk away.
