Bimbadeen Gardens

Park in Mooroolbark · Basic info

Bimbadeen Gardens is a park in Mooroolbark, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Mooroolbark Rd/Hull Rd (bus) is a 8-minute walk away.
