Yinnar in the Latrobe area has 3 playgrounds and 2 parks and reserves in our data. Yinnar also has 2 museums or galleries.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Yinnar is Boolarra Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 13 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Yinnar include Yinnar Centenary Garden. Playgrounds in Yinnar include the playground at Yinnar Centenary Garden. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Yinnar. Places to visit in Yinnar include ARC Yinnar and Railway Trolley Shed.

Getting around

Yinnar has no train station; Morwell Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 4 stops in Yinnar. By car from Yinnar, Traralgon is about 28 minutes away, Leongatha about 45 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Yinnar is Latrobe Leisure - Churchill in Churchill, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest library to Yinnar is Churchill Library in Churchill, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Yinnar is Boolarra Skate Park in Boolarra, about 10 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 19 minutes' drive from Yinnar. The nearest larger centre is Traralgon, about 28 minutes from Yinnar by car, with 38 playgrounds and a pool.

Best for

Younger kids at Yinnar's 3 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Yinnar.

Nearby suburbs

Churchill, next to Yinnar, has 9 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Boolarra, next to Yinnar, has 1 playground and a skate park. Hazelwood, Driffield and Delburn also border Yinnar.
