Playground on Wicks Street

Playground in Yinnar · Basic info

Playground on Wicks Street in Yinnar is a public playground in the Latrobe City area. Older kids have a skate park about 160 m away, and ARC Yinnar is about 210 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 260 m away, and bus 4 stops at 60 Main St, a 5-minute walk away.
