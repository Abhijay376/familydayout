Skate park on Main Street

Skate, BMX or pump track in Yinnar · Basic info

Skate park on Main Street is a skate, bmx or pump track in Yinnar, in the Latrobe City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. 60 Main St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 128 m away.
