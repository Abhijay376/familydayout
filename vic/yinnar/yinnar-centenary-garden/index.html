Yinnar Centenary Garden

Park in Yinnar · Basic info

Yinnar Centenary Garden is a park in Yinnar, in the Latrobe City Council area, good for all ages. 60 Main St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 61 m away with a baby change table.
