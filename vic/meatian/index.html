Meatian in the Swan Hill area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Meatian is White Gate Bushland Reserve, covering about 9 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Meatian. The closest to Meatian is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 33 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Meatian, so most trips start by car. By car from Meatian, Swan Hill is about 47 minutes away and Echuca about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Meatian is Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock, about 44 minutes' drive. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Meatian, about 47 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Meatian are the playground at Gray Park in Lake Boga (39 min), Lake Boga Flying Boat Museum in Lake Boga (41 min), the playground at Guastalegname Park in Swan Hill (45 min), Swan Hill Regional Art Gallery in Swan Hill (46 min), the skate park at Riverside Park in Swan Hill (47 min) and Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill (47 min).
