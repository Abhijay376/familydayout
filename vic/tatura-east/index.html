Tatura East is in the Greater Shepparton area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Tatura East, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Tatura East has no train station; Mooroopna Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 7.0 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tatura East, so most trips start by car. By car from Tatura East, Shepparton is about 18 minutes away, Echuca about 62 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Tatura Swimming complex in Tatura is about 11 minutes' drive from Tatura East. For a library, Tatura Library in Tatura is about 12 minutes' drive from Tatura East. The closest beach to Tatura East is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 20 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Tatura Memorial Park in Tatura is about 10 minutes' drive from Tatura East. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 18 minutes' drive from Tatura East. The closest indoor play centre to Tatura East is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 18 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kidstown in Mooroopna is about 16 minutes' drive from Tatura East. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Tatura East, about 18 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Tatura East, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Lions Park in Tatura (10 min), the playground at Tatura Memorial Park in Tatura (10 min), the skate park at Tatura Memorial Park in Tatura (10 min), Tatura Swimming complex in Tatura (11 min), Tatura Irrigation and Wartime Camps Museum in Tatura (11 min) and Tatura Library in Tatura (12 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tatura East.

Nearby suburbs

Tatura, next to Tatura East, has 8 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Tatura East, Mooroopna has 12 playgrounds, a library and a zoo or wildlife park. Next door to Tatura East, Toolamba has 2 playgrounds. Ardmona also borders Tatura East.
