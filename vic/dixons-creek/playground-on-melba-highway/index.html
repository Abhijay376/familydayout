Playground on Melba Highway

Playground in Dixons Creek · Basic info

Playground on Melba Highway in Dixons Creek is a public playground in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet right by the playground. It's easiest by car, about 1 minute from Dixons Creek.
