Grahamvale is in the Greater Shepparton area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Grahamvale, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Grahamvale has no train station; Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 3.2 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Grahamvale, so most trips start by car. By car from Grahamvale, Shepparton is about 6 minutes away, Benalla about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Grahamvale is Aquamoves in Shepparton, about 8 minutes' drive. For a library, Shepparton Library in Shepparton is about 6 minutes' drive from Grahamvale. The closest beach to Grahamvale is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 10 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Shepparton Velodrome in Shepparton is about 7 minutes' drive from Grahamvale. The closest cinema to Grahamvale is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Grahamvale is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Grahamvale is Kidstown in Mooroopna, about 10 minutes' drive. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Grahamvale, about 6 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Grahamvale are the playground at Bailey Park in Shepparton (4 min), the playground at W.S James Park in Shepparton (4 min), StarBowl in Shepparton (6 min), Village Cinemas in Shepparton (7 min), Shepparton Library in Shepparton (6 min) and Shepparton Heritage Centre in Shepparton (7 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Grahamvale.

Nearby suburbs

Shepparton, next to Grahamvale, has 47 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Shepparton North, next to Grahamvale, has 4 playgrounds. Next door to Grahamvale, Shepparton East has 3 playgrounds. Congupna, next to Grahamvale, has 2 playgrounds. Lemnos also borders Grahamvale.
