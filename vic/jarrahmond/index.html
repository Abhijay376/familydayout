Jarrahmond is in the East Gippsland area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Other parks and reserves in Jarrahmond include Woods Point and Woods Point Overflow Campground. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Jarrahmond. Picnic areas in Jarrahmond include Woods Point. For camping, there's Woods Point Overflow Campground .

Getting around

There's no train station in Jarrahmond. The closest to Jarrahmond is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 70 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Jarrahmond, so most trips start by car. By car from Jarrahmond, Lakes Entrance is about 60 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 87 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Jarrahmond is Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost, about 13 minutes' drive. For a library, Orbost Library in Orbost is about 13 minutes' drive from Jarrahmond. The closest beach to Jarrahmond is Ewings Marsh in Waygara, about 32 minutes' drive. Lakes Entrance is the closest bigger centre to Jarrahmond, about 60 minutes away by car, and has 3 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Jarrahmond are Orbost Library in Orbost (13 min), the playground at Forest Park in Orbost (13 min), Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost (13 min), Ewings Marsh in Waygara (32 min) and Sailors Grave Beach in Cape Conran (41 min).

Best for
