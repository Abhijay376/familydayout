Schooner Bay Drive Reserve

Park in Patterson Lakes · Basic info

Schooner Bay Drive Reserve is a park in Patterson Lakes, in the Kingston City Council area, good for all ages. Patterson Lakes Ret Village/McLeod Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 520 m away with a baby change table.
