John Lindsay Reserve

Park in Patterson Lakes · Basic info

John Lindsay Reserve is a park in Patterson Lakes, in the Kingston City Council area, good for all ages. Seafarer Ct/Palm Beach Dr (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 912 m away.
