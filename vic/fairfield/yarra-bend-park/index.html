Yarra Bend Park

Park in Fairfield · Official details

Yarra Bend Park is a park in Fairfield, in the Yarra City Council area, good for all ages. Melbourne Polytechnic/Heidelberg Rd (bus) is a 14-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 60 m away and dogs are allowed on a lead.

Details

Dogs
on lead (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
Up to 4 dogs allowed; Dogs not allowed on golf courses, playgrounds or Galatea Point; Canoe launch ramps at Lower Loop and Bellbird Picnic Areas (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Yarra Bend Park?
Yarra Bend Park is in Fairfield, Victoria.
Is Yarra Bend Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Yarra Bend Park?
Yes, on a lead.
Are there toilets at Yarra Bend Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 60 m away.
How do I get to Yarra Bend Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Melbourne Polytechnic/Heidelberg Rd (routes 546), a 14-minute walk.
