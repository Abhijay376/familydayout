Playground on Heidelberg Road

Playground in Fairfield · Basic info

Playground on Heidelberg Road is a public playground in Fairfield, in the Yarra City area. The Bike: Heidelberg Road Link is about 30 m away, and for food, Fairfield Boathouse is about 100 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 100 m from the playground, and bus 546 stops at Panther Pl/Heidelberg Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
