Our data lists 1 park or reserve in McKenzie Hill, in the Mount Alexander area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in McKenzie Hill is Castlemaine H80 Bushland Reserve, covering about 5 hectares. The Castlemaine to Maldon Trail passes through McKenzie Hill.

Getting around

There's no train station in McKenzie Hill. The closest to McKenzie Hill is Castlemaine Railway Station (V/Line), about 2.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 4 stops in McKenzie Hill. By car from McKenzie Hill, Castlemaine is about 4 minutes away, Daylesford about 33 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Castlemaine Swimming Pool in Castlemaine is about 6 minutes' drive from McKenzie Hill. The closest library to McKenzie Hill is Castlemaine Library in Castlemaine, about 4 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to McKenzie Hill is Bike Jumps in Castlemaine, about 4 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 37 minutes' drive from McKenzie Hill. The closest indoor play centre to McKenzie Hill is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 34 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 42 minutes' drive from McKenzie Hill. The nearest larger centre is Castlemaine, about 4 minutes from McKenzie Hill by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From McKenzie Hill, the nearest places for kids by road are Lawrence Park Playground in Castlemaine (3 min), the playground at Norwood Hill Progress Association Recreation Reserve in Castlemaine (4 min), Bike Jumps in Castlemaine (4 min), BMX Track in Castlemaine (4 min), Castlemaine CASPA in Castlemaine (5 min) and Castlemaine Library in Castlemaine (4 min).

Best for

Free days on the Castlemaine to Maldon Trail and in McKenzie Hill's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Castlemaine, next to McKenzie Hill, has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Campbells Creek, next to McKenzie Hill, has 3 playgrounds. Muckleford, next to McKenzie Hill, has 1 playground.
