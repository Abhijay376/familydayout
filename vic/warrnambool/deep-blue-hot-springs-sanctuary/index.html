Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary

Pool or water play in Warrnambool · Basic info

Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary is a pool or water play in Warrnambool, in the Warrnambool City Council area, good for all ages. Port Warrnambool Village/Pertobe Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 400 m away.
