Playground on Botanic Road

Playground in Warrnambool · Basic info

Playground on Botanic Road is a public playground in Warrnambool, in the Warrnambool City area. The Russell Creek Trail is about 230 m away, and Playground on Queens Road is another option about 230 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 240 m from the playground, and bus 2 stops at St Joseph's PS/Botanic Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
