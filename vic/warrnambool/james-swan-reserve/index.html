James Swan Reserve

Park in Warrnambool · Basic info

James Swan Reserve is a park in Warrnambool, in the Warrnambool City Council area, good for all ages. Lava Street Interchange/Lava St (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 135 m away with a baby change table.
