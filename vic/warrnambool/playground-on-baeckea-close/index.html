Playground on Baeckea Close

Playground in Warrnambool · Basic info

Playground on Baeckea Close is a public playground in Warrnambool, in the Warrnambool City area. Boost Juice is about 490 m away if you need food, and Playground at Brierly Park is another option about 460 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.3 km away, and buses 2 and 3 stop at McKiernan Rd/Dales Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
