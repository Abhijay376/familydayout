Playground on Queens Road

Playground in Warrnambool · Basic info

Playground on Queens Road is a public playground in Warrnambool, in the Warrnambool City area. The Russell Creek Trail is about 190 m away, and Playground on Botanic Road is another option about 230 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 2 stops at St Joseph's PS/Botanic Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
