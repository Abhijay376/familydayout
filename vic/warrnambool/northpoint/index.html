Northpoint

Shopping centre in Warrnambool · Basic info

Northpoint is a shopping centre in Warrnambool, in the Warrnambool City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Northpoint Shopping Centre/Mortlake Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 14 m away with a baby change table.
