Warrnambool Bluehole Reserve and Revegetation Area

Park in Warrnambool · Basic info

Warrnambool Bluehole Reserve and Revegetation Area is a park in Warrnambool, in the Warrnambool City Council area, good for all ages. Hopkins Rd/Marfell Rd (bus) is a 9-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 143 m away.
