Our data lists 45 playgrounds and 28 parks and reserves in Warrnambool, in the Warrnambool area. On top of that, Warrnambool has a library, a pool, 2 museums or galleries and a cinema.

Landmarks

Thunder Point Coastal Reserve is the largest mapped park in Warrnambool, at about 155 hectares. Foreshore Trust Grounds and E Johnson Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Warrnambool include Albert Park, Civic Green and Rotary Park. Playgrounds in Warrnambool include Maze, Adventure Playground and Grieve Street Playground. Our data shows 34 public toilets in Warrnambool. In Warrnambool you'll also find SCOPE Galleries, Warrnambool Art Gallery, Capitol Cinema, The Bowl and Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary. For older kids in Warrnambool there's Warrnambool BMX Track and Warrnambool Skate Park. Gateway Plaza, Homemaker Centre and Northpoint are the main shopping centres. For walks and rides, Port Fairy to Warrnambool Rail Trail, MBN SW5 and Wangoom Loop cross Warrnambool. Our data lists 32 cafes and family restaurants in Warrnambool for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Sherwood Park Railway Station (V/Line: Warrnambool - Melbourne via Colac & Geelong) and Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line: Warrnambool - Melbourne via Colac & Geelong) in Warrnambool. Buses on the 1, 2, 3 and 4 routes stop in Warrnambool. V/Line coaches stop in Warrnambool. By car from Warrnambool, Hamilton is about 83 minutes away and Portland about 84 minutes, without traffic.
