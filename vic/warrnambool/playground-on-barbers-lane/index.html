Playground on Barbers Lane

Playground in Warrnambool · Basic info

Playground on Barbers Lane is a public playground in Warrnambool, in the Warrnambool City area. The Russell Creek Trail is about 140 m away, and Playground on Botanic Road is another option about 400 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 790 m from the playground, and bus 2 stops at Emmanuel College/Ardlie St, a 5-minute walk away.
