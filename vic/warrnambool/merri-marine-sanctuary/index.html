Merri Marine Sanctuary

Park in Warrnambool · Official details

Merri Marine Sanctuary is a park in Warrnambool, in the Warrnambool City Council area, good for all ages. Port Warrnambool Village/Pertobe Rd (bus) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 310 m away.

Details

Water play
yes (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
Middle Island is closed to the public; Guided rockpool walks run in holiday programs; Wear shoes that grip on rock platforms (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Merri Marine Sanctuary?
Merri Marine Sanctuary is in Warrnambool, Victoria.
Is Merri Marine Sanctuary free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Merri Marine Sanctuary?
The nearest public toilet is about 310 m away.
How do I get to Merri Marine Sanctuary by public transport?
The closest stop is Port Warrnambool Village/Pertobe Rd (routes 5), a 8-minute walk.
