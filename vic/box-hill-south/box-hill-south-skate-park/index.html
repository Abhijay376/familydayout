Box Hill South Skate Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Box Hill South · Basic info

Box Hill South Skate Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Box Hill South, in the Whitehorse City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Canterbury Rd/Middleborough Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 18 m away.
