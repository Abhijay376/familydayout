The Artists' Park

Park in Box Hill South · Basic info

The Artists' Park is a park in Box Hill South, in the Whitehorse City Council area, good for all ages. Prince St/Haig St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1044 m away.
