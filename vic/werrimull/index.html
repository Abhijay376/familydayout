Werrimull is in the Mildura area, and our data lists 5 parks and reserves in the suburb. Werrimull also has a library.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Werrimull is Mallanbool Flora and Fauna Reserve, covering about 516 hectares. Werrimull Bushland Reserve and Bambill Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Werrimull include Millewa East Community and Pioneer Forest. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Werrimull. Places to visit in Werrimull include Mildura Mobile Library stops here. The Former Morkalla-Redcliffs Railway passes through Werrimull.

Getting around

There's no train station in Werrimull. The closest to Werrimull is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 209 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Werrimull, so most trips start by car. By car from Werrimull, Mildura is about 54 minutes away and Swan Hill about 3.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The nearest larger centre is Mildura, about 54 minutes from Werrimull by car, with 26 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Werrimull, the nearest places for kids by road are Millewa Pioneer Park in Meringur (19 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the library in Werrimull. Free days on the Former Morkalla-Redcliffs Railway and in Werrimull's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Werrimull, Wargan has 6 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Werrimull, Cullulleraine has 1 playground. Merrinee and Meringur also border Werrimull.
