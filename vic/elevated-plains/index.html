Elevated Plains is in the Hepburn area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Elevated Plains, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Goldfields Track and Dry Diggings Track cross Elevated Plains.

Getting around

There's no train station in Elevated Plains. The closest to Elevated Plains is Castlemaine Railway Station (V/Line), about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Elevated Plains, so most trips start by car. By car from Elevated Plains, Daylesford is about 9 minutes away, Castlemaine about 35 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Elevated Plains is Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest library to Elevated Plains is Daylesford Library in Daylesford, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Elevated Plains is Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick, about 33 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek is about 41 minutes' drive from Elevated Plains. Daylesford is the closest bigger centre to Elevated Plains, about 9 minutes away by car, and has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Elevated Plains, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Hepburn Regional Park in Daylesford (6 min), Daylesford Library in Daylesford (9 min), Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford (9 min), Daylesford Spa Country Railway in Daylesford (11 min), the playground at J P Gleeson Memorial Park in Lyonville (23 min) and Newstead Swimming Pool in Newstead (29 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Elevated Plains on the Goldfields Track.

Nearby suburbs

Dry Diggings and Basalt also border Elevated Plains.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Elevated Plains, without traffic.
