Hexham is in the Moyne area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Hexham is Cobra Killuc Wildlife Reserve (no hunting), covering about 638 hectares. Hexham Water Supply Reserve and Hexham School Historic Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Hexham. The closest to Hexham is Terang Railway Station (V/Line), about 33 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Hexham. By car from Hexham, Warrnambool is about 51 minutes away and Hamilton about 58 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Penshurst Swimming Pool in Penshurst is about 33 minutes' drive from Hexham. The closest library to Hexham is Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Hexham is Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake, about 14 minutes' drive. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Hexham, about 51 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Hexham, the nearest places for kids by road are Market Square Playground in Mortlake (13 min), Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake (13 min), Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake (14 min), the playground at Tea Tree Lake Reserve in Mortlake (14 min), Penshurst Swimming Pool in Penshurst (33 min) and Volcanoes Discovery Centre in Penshurst (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Hexham.

Nearby suburbs

Mortlake, next to Hexham, has 2 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Ellerslie, Chatsworth and Caramut also border Hexham.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Hexham, without traffic.
