Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Carboor in the Wangaratta area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Carboor.

Getting around

Carboor has no train station; Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 33 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Carboor, so most trips start by car. By car from Carboor, Wangaratta is about 39 minutes away and Benalla about 60 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford is about 42 minutes' drive from Carboor. The closest library to Carboor is Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford, about 42 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Carboor is Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford, about 42 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Carboor is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 36 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 39 minutes from Carboor by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Carboor, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at John McAleese Community Park in Milawa (24 min), the playground at Apex Park in Moyhu (34 min), The Old School Museum in Myrtleford (42 min), Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford (42 min), Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford (42 min) and the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford (42 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Carboor.

Nearby suburbs

Buffalo River, next to Carboor, has 1 playground. Dandongadale and Bobinawarrah also border Carboor.
