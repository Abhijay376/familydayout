Our data lists 6 parks and reserves in Digby, in the Glenelg area.

Landmarks

Digby H5 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Digby, at about 20 hectares. Digby H31 Bushland Reserve and Digby H32 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Digby include Digby Memorial Park, Digby Springs Reserve and Digby H30 Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Digby.

Getting around

There's no train station in Digby. The closest to Digby is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 105 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Digby, so most trips start by car. By car from Digby, Hamilton is about 40 minutes away and Portland about 52 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Digby is Merino Swimming Pool in Merino, about 9 minutes' drive. For a library, Heywood Library in Heywood is about 31 minutes' drive from Digby. For a skate park, coleraine skate park in Coleraine is about 37 minutes' drive from Digby. The closest cinema to Digby is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 40 minutes' drive. Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Digby, about 40 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Digby, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Lions Park in Merino (8 min), Merino Swimming Pool in Merino (9 min), Dartmoor Coach House Museum in Dartmoor (24 min), Gorman's art gallery in Casterton (26 min), Casterton Pool in Casterton (27 min) and Branxholme Rec Reserve Playground in Branxholme (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Digby.

Nearby suburbs

Merino, next to Digby, has 1 playground and a pool. Grassdale and Dartmoor also border Digby.
