Jack River is in the Wellington area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Jack River, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Jack River has no train station; Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 39 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Jack River, so most trips start by car. By car from Jack River, Sale is about 64 minutes away and Traralgon about 69 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Yarram Public Pool in Yarram is about 9 minutes' drive from Jack River. The closest library to Jack River is Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Jack River is Woodside_Beach in Woodside Beach, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Jack River is the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram, about 9 minutes' drive. From Jack River, the nearest places for kids by road are Yarram Historical Society Museum in Yarram (8 min), the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram (9 min), Yarram Public Pool in Yarram (9 min), Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram (9 min), the playground at Memorial Park in Yarram (10 min) and Port Albert Maritime Museum in Port Albert (21 min).
