Dennington in the Warrnambool area has 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Dennington is Belfast Coastal Reserve, covering about 1,680 hectares. Dennington Recreation Reserve and GG Payne Reserve are the next largest. Playgrounds around Dennington include W P O'Sullivan Playground. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Dennington. The Port Fairy to Warrnambool Rail Trail, Walking and Bridle Path and Thunder Pt to Levys Pt (beach) all pass through Dennington.

Getting around

Dennington has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 4.3 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 1 and 8 stop in Dennington. By car from Dennington, Warrnambool is about 7 minutes away and Hamilton about 79 minutes, without traffic.

The closest skate park to Dennington is Warrnambool Skate Park in Warrnambool, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Dennington is Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Dennington is The Bowl in Warrnambool, about 8 minutes' drive.
