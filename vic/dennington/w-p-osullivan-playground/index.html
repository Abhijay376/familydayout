W. P. O'Sullivan Playground

Playground in Dennington · Basic info

W. P. O'Sullivan Playground is a public playground in Dennington, in the Warrnambool City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. You'll find a public toilet about 870 m from the playground, and buses 1 and 8 stop at Hood St/Raglan Pde, a 1-minute walk away.
