Steiglitz in the Golden Plains area has 1 park or reserve in our data. Steiglitz also has a beach.

Landmarks

Steiglitz Historic Park is the largest mapped park in Steiglitz, at about 430 hectares. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Steiglitz. Places to visit in Steiglitz include Disability beach. For walks and rides, Burchell Trail, Link Track (Burchell Trail) and Nugget Track cross Steiglitz.

Getting around

There's no train station in Steiglitz. The closest to Steiglitz is Ballan Railway Station (V/Line), about 30 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Steiglitz, so most trips start by car. By car from Steiglitz, Geelong is about 47 minutes away, Ballarat about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Steiglitz is Goodfellow Aquatic Centre in Bell Post Hill, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest library to Steiglitz is Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn, about 27 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Meredith Skate Park in Meredith is about 12 minutes' drive from Steiglitz. For an indoor play centre, General Public in Corio is about 39 minutes' drive from Steiglitz. For a zoo or wildlife park, Fairy Park in Anakie is about 21 minutes' drive from Steiglitz. Geelong is the closest bigger centre to Steiglitz, about 47 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Steiglitz are Anakie Reserve Playground in Anakie (21 min), Maude Tennis Reserve Playground in Maude (11 min), Meredith Skate Park in Meredith (12 min), Fairy Park in Anakie (21 min), Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn (27 min) and Bannockburn Skate Park in Bannockburn (28 min).

Best for

Beach days at Disability beach. Free days on the Burchell Trail and in Steiglitz's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Maude, next to Steiglitz, has 2 playgrounds. Meredith, next to Steiglitz, has 1 playground and a skate park. Next door to Steiglitz, Anakie has 1 playground and a zoo or wildlife park. She Oaks and Durdidwarrah also border Steiglitz.
