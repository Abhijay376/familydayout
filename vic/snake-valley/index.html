Snake Valley in the Pyrenees area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Carngham Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Snake Valley, at about 6 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Snake Valley.

Getting around

Snake Valley has no train station; Wendouree Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus C27 stops in Snake Valley. By car from Snake Valley, Ballarat is about 34 minutes away, Ararat about 73 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Ballarat Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Lake Gardens is about 32 minutes' drive from Snake Valley. The closest library to Snake Valley is Sebastopol Library in Sebastopol, about 29 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Linton BMX park in Linton is about 12 minutes' drive from Snake Valley. The closest cinema to Snake Valley is Showbiz Cinemas Ballarat in Smythes Creek, about 26 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 34 minutes' drive from Snake Valley. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Snake Valley is Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East, about 39 minutes' drive. Ballarat is the closest bigger centre to Snake Valley, about 34 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Snake Valley, the nearest places for kids by road are Linton BMX park in Linton (12 min), Linton Skate Park in Linton (12 min), Linton Recreation Reserve Playground in Linton (13 min), Woady Yaloak Recreation Reserve Playground in Smythesdale (15 min), Haddon Tramway Preservation Association in Haddon (23 min) and Showbiz Cinemas Ballarat in Smythes Creek (26 min).
