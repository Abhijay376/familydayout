Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Lake Rowan, in the Moira area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lake Rowan is Rowan Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 377 hectares. Lake Rowan Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lake Rowan. The closest to Lake Rowan is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 31 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lake Rowan, so most trips start by car. By car from Lake Rowan, Benalla is about 30 minutes away and Wangaratta about 34 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lake Rowan is Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla, about 30 minutes' drive. For a library, Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga is about 23 minutes' drive from Lake Rowan. For a beach, Forges Beach No 2 in Yarrawonga is about 30 minutes' drive from Lake Rowan. For a skate park, Pump Track in Benalla is about 31 minutes' drive from Lake Rowan. The closest cinema to Lake Rowan is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 34 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Tunzafun in Yarrawonga is about 25 minutes' drive from Lake Rowan. Benalla is the closest bigger centre to Lake Rowan, about 30 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds and a pool. From Lake Rowan, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Tungamah Lions Park in Tungamah (17 min), Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga (23 min), Tunzafun in Yarrawonga (25 min), Yarrawonga Mulwala Pioneer Museum in Yarrawonga (26 min), Lonsdale Reserve Playground in Yarrawonga (27 min) and Mulwala Waterslide in Yarrawonga (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lake Rowan.

Nearby suburbs

Bungeet and Almonds also border Lake Rowan.
