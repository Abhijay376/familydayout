Bungeet is in the Benalla area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bungeet, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Bungeet has no train station; Benalla Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bungeet, so most trips start by car. By car from Bungeet, Wangaratta is about 32 minutes away and Benalla about 34 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bungeet is Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta, about 31 minutes' drive. For a library, Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta is about 32 minutes' drive from Bungeet. The closest beach to Bungeet is Forges Beach No 2 in Yarrawonga, about 43 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Bungeet is the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Bungeet is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Bungeet is Tunzafun in Yarrawonga, about 37 minutes' drive. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Bungeet, about 32 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Bungeet are the playground at Peechelba Reserve in Peechelba (26 min), Glenrowan Museum in Glenrowan (27 min), Cobb & Co in Glenrowan (27 min), the playground at Tungamah Lions Park in Tungamah (29 min), Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta (31 min) and Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (32 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bungeet.
