Douglas Reserve

Park in Brunswick East · Basic info

Douglas Reserve is a park in Brunswick East, in the Merri-Bek City Council area, good for all ages. Weston St/Lygon St #121 (tram) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 465 m away.
