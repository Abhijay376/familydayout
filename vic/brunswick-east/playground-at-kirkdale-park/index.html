Playground at Kirkdale Park

Playground in Brunswick East · Official details

This is a public playground in Brunswick East. The Merri Creek Trail is about 30 m away, and Ok Violet is about 370 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 450 m from the playground, and bus 508 stops at Roberts St/Blyth St, a 5-minute walk away.
