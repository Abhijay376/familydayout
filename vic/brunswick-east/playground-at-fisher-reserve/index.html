Playground at Fisher Reserve

Playground in Brunswick East · Basic info

Playground at Fisher Reserve in Brunswick East is a public playground in the Merri-Bek City area. The East Brunswick Shimmy is about 340 m away, and 1 John Street Little Library is about 590 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 500 m from the playground, and tram 96 stops at Glenlyon Rd/Nicholson St #21, a 2-minute walk away.
