Curlewis is in the Greater Geelong area, and our data lists 3 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Curlewis has a pool.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Curlewis is Drysdale Sports Precinct, covering about 16 hectares. Percy Cherry Park and Brompton Avenue Reserve are the next largest. Playgrounds in Curlewis include St Thomas Playground 1, the playground at Adrian Mannix Reserve and the playground at Brompton Avenue Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Curlewis. Places to visit in Curlewis include Northern Bellarine Aquatic Centre. For walks and rides, Bellarine Rail Trail crosses Curlewis. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Curlewis for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Curlewis has no train station; South Geelong Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 60 and 61 stop in Curlewis. By car from Curlewis, Geelong is about 15 minutes away, Torquay about 35 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 79 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Curlewis is Leopold Library in Leopold, about 6 minutes' drive. For a beach, Grand Scenic in Leopold is about 7 minutes' drive from Curlewis. For a skate park, the skate park at Jetty Road Reserve in Clifton Springs is about 8 minutes' drive from Curlewis. The closest cinema to Curlewis is Village Cinemas in Geelong, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Curlewis is Laserquest in Geelong, about 16 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Adventure Park in Wallington is about 5 minutes' drive from Curlewis. Geelong is the closest bigger centre to Curlewis, about 15 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Curlewis's 3 local playgrounds. Free days on the Bellarine Rail Trail and in Curlewis's parks.
