Playground at Memorial Park

Playground in Box Hill North · Basic info

Playground at Memorial Park in Box Hill North is a public playground in the Whitehorse City area. The Bushy Creek Trail is about 60 m away, and for food, Persian Halal Kebabery is about 450 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.1 km from the playground, and bus 903 stops at Paisley St/Station St, a 2-minute walk away.
