Playground on Trainor Street

Playground in Box Hill North · Basic info

Playground on Trainor Street in Box Hill North is a public playground in the Whitehorse City area. The Whitehorse NS2 passes about 200 m away, and Pizza Works is about 170 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 930 m away, and buses 281, 293 and 302 stop at Woodhouse Gr/Elgar Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
