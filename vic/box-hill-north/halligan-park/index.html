Halligan Park

Park in Box Hill North · Basic info

Halligan Park is a park in Box Hill North, in the Whitehorse City Council area, good for all ages. Tyne St/Station St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 616 m away with a baby change table.
