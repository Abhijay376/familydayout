Playground at Bushy Creek Parklands

Playground in Box Hill North · Basic info

This is a public playground in Box Hill North. The Bushy Creek Trail passes about 10 m away, and Playground on Killara Street is another option about 220 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 400 m away, and bus 270 stops at Wimmera St/Dorking Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
