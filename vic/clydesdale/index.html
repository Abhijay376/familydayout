Our data lists 4 parks and reserves in Clydesdale, in the Hepburn area.

Landmarks

Rise and Shine Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Clydesdale, at about 113 hectares. Pickpocket Diggings Historic and Cultural Features Reserve and Clydesdale Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Clydesdale include Yandoit K47 Streamside Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Clydesdale. The closest to Clydesdale is Castlemaine Railway Station (V/Line), about 17 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Clydesdale, so most trips start by car. By car from Clydesdale, Daylesford is about 23 minutes away, Castlemaine about 25 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Newstead Swimming Pool in Newstead is about 10 minutes' drive from Clydesdale. The closest library to Clydesdale is Athenaeum Library in Maldon, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Clydesdale is Bike Jumps in Castlemaine, about 25 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Daylesford, about 23 minutes from Clydesdale by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Clydesdale, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Newstead Memorial Park in Newstead (9 min), Newstead Swimming Pool in Newstead (10 min), Giant Slide in Vaughan (28 min), Daylesford Spa Country Railway in Daylesford (21 min), Maldon District Museum in Maldon (25 min) and Athenaeum Library in Maldon (25 min).
