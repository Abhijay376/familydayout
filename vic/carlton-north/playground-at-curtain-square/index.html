Playground at Curtain Square

Playground in Carlton North · Basic info

Playground at Curtain Square in Carlton North is a public playground in the Yarra City area. The Fairfield to CBD is about 120 m away, and Carlton Library is about 110 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and buses 250 and 251 stop at Curtain Square/Rathdowne St, a 1-minute walk away.
