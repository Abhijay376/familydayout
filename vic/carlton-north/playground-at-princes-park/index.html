Playground at Princes Park

Playground in Carlton North · Official details

Playground at Princes Park in Carlton North is a public playground in the Melbourne City area. The City via Peel Street is about 280 m away, and for food, Moja Duck Restaurant & Bar is about 420 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 140 m from the playground, and bus 505 stops at Princes Park/Royal Pde, a 5-minute walk away.
