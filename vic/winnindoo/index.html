Winnindoo is in the Wellington area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Winnindoo, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Winnindoo has no train station; Rosedale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Winnindoo, so most trips start by car. By car from Winnindoo, Sale is about 30 minutes away, Traralgon about 32 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Winnindoo is Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest library to Winnindoo is Sale Library in Sale, about 28 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Pump Track in Heyfield is about 6 minutes' drive from Winnindoo. The closest cinema to Winnindoo is Sale Cinemas in Sale, about 29 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Sale, about 30 minutes from Winnindoo by car, with 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Winnindoo are Apex Park Playground in Heyfield (6 min), Heyfield and District Museum in Heyfield (6 min), Pump Track in Heyfield (6 min), Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield (6 min), Cricket Street Playground in Cowwarr (6 min) and Rosedale Swimming Pool in Rosedale (12 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Winnindoo.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Winnindoo, Rosedale has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Winnindoo, Toongabbie has 2 playgrounds. Glengarry, next to Winnindoo, has 1 playground and a skate park. Next door to Winnindoo, Cowwarr has 1 playground. Nambrok and Denison also border Winnindoo.
