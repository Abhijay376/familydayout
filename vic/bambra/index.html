Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Bambra, in the Surf Coast area.

Landmarks

Bambra Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Bambra, at about 38 hectares. Retreat Creek Streamside Reserve is the next largest. Bambra also has Paddys Swamp Trail Bike Visitor Area. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Bambra. Picnic areas in Bambra include Paddys Swamp Trail Bike Visitor Area.

Getting around

Bambra has no train station; Winchelsea Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bambra, so most trips start by car. By car from Bambra, Colac is about 42 minutes away, Torquay about 44 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bambra is Lorne Sea Baths in Lorne, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest library to Bambra is Torquay Library in Torquay, about 43 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Bambra is Birregurra Skate Park in Birregurra, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Bambra is Lorne Theatre in Lorne, about 28 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac is about 41 minutes' drive from Bambra. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Bambra is Live Wire Park in Lorne, about 31 minutes' drive. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Bambra, about 42 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Bambra are Sunnymead Beach in Aireys Inlet (47 min), Red Rocks Beach in Aireys Inlet (48 min), the playground at Modewarre Recreation Reserve in Modewarre (24 min), Birregurra Skate Park in Birregurra (24 min), the bmx track at Newling Reserve in Moriac (31 min) and Moriac Playground in Moriac (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bambra.
