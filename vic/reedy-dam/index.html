Reedy Dam is in the Buloke area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Reedy Dam, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Reedy Dam. The closest to Reedy Dam is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 108 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Reedy Dam, so most trips start by car. By car from Reedy Dam, Horsham is about 84 minutes away and Swan Hill about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Reedy Dam is Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem, about 35 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Birchip Skate Park in Birchip is about 20 minutes' drive from Reedy Dam. From Reedy Dam, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Beulah Lions Park in Beulah (19 min), the playground at Beulah Lake Park in Beulah (20 min), Birchip History Museum in Birchip (20 min), Birchip Skate Park in Birchip (20 min), Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem (35 min) and Hopetoun Swimming Pool in Hopetoun (39 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Reedy Dam.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Reedy Dam, Beulah has 2 playgrounds. Brim, next to Reedy Dam, has 1 playground. Ballapur also borders Reedy Dam.
