Winlaton is in the Swan Hill area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Winlaton Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Winlaton, at about 86 hectares. Lake Kelly Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Winlaton has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Winlaton, so most trips start by car. By car from Winlaton, Swan Hill is about 26 minutes away and Echuca about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill is about 26 minutes' drive from Winlaton. The closest library to Winlaton is Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill, about 26 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Riverside Park in Swan Hill is about 26 minutes' drive from Winlaton. For a cinema, Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill is about 26 minutes' drive from Winlaton. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Winlaton, about 26 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Winlaton, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Gray Park in Lake Boga (13 min), Lake Boga Flying Boat Museum in Lake Boga (14 min), the playground at Guastalegname Park in Swan Hill (24 min), Swan Hill Regional Art Gallery in Swan Hill (25 min), the skate park at Riverside Park in Swan Hill (26 min) and Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Winlaton.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Winlaton, Lake Boga has 1 playground. Fish Point and Benjeroop also border Winlaton.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Winlaton, without traffic.
