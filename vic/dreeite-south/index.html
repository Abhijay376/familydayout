Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Dreeite South in the Colac Otway area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Dreeite South. The closest to Dreeite South is Colac Railway Station (V/Line), about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dreeite South, so most trips start by car. By car from Dreeite South, Colac is about 39 minutes away, Geelong about 1.5 hours and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac is about 42 minutes' drive from Dreeite South. For a skate park, the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt is about 44 minutes' drive from Dreeite South. For a cinema, Colac Cinemas in Colac is about 39 minutes' drive from Dreeite South. For an indoor play centre, Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac is about 40 minutes' drive from Dreeite South. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Dreeite South, about 39 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Dreeite South are the playground at Red Rock Scenic Reserve in Alvie (19 min), the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (38 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (39 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (40 min), Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (42 min) and the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (44 min).
