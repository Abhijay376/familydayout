Boisdale is in the Wellington area, and our data lists 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Boisdale also has Dermodys, The Channels and Green Hill Lookout. Playgrounds around Boisdale include Boisdale Recreation Reserve Playground. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Boisdale. Picnic areas in Boisdale include Green Hill Lookout and The Channels. For camping, there's Dermodys. For walks and rides, Avon River Walking Track, The Channel Loop Walking Track and Green Hills Walking Track cross Boisdale.

Getting around

Boisdale has no train station; Stratford Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Boisdale, so most trips start by car. By car from Boisdale, Sale is about 25 minutes away and Traralgon about 61 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Boisdale is Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford, about 21 minutes' drive. For a library, Sale Library in Sale is about 26 minutes' drive from Boisdale. The closest skate park to Boisdale is Skate Park in Stratford, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Boisdale is Former Drive-in site in Sale, about 25 minutes' drive. Sale is the closest bigger centre to Boisdale, about 25 minutes away by car, and has 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Boisdale, the nearest places for kids by road are Play Equipment in Newry (13 min), Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford (21 min), Skate Park in Stratford (22 min), Former Drive-in site in Sale (25 min), Sale Historical Museum in Sale (26 min) and Old train signals in Sale (26 min).

Best for

Free days on the Avon River Walking Track and in Boisdale's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Boisdale, Maffra has 6 playgrounds. Newry, next to Boisdale, has 1 playground. Llowalong, Koorool and Bushy Park also border Boisdale.
