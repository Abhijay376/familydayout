Landmarks

Kangaroo Dam Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Chinangin, at about 8 hectares.

Getting around

Chinangin has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 38 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Chinangin, so most trips start by car. By car from Chinangin, Swan Hill is about 33 minutes away and Echuca about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Chinangin is Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest library to Chinangin is Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill, about 33 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Chinangin is Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Chinangin is Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill, about 33 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 33 minutes from Chinangin by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Chinangin are the playground at Culgoa Memorial Park in Culgoa (21 min), Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock (21 min), Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South (31 min), the playground at Jaycee Park in Swan Hill (34 min), Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill (33 min) and Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill (33 min).
