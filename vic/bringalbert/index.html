Bringalbert in the West Wimmera area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bringalbert is Tallageira Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 3,751 hectares. Dumbopperty Swamp and Lake Bringalbart Lake Reserve and Morea I4 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Bringalbert include Boiler Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting). Our data shows 1 public toilet in Bringalbert.

Getting around

Bringalbert has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 166 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bringalbert, so most trips start by car. By car from Bringalbert, Horsham is about 84 minutes away and Hamilton about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Goroke Swimming Centre in Goroke is about 27 minutes' drive from Bringalbert. For a library, West Wimmera Shire Goroke Library in Goroke is about 28 minutes' drive from Bringalbert. From Bringalbert, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at War Memorial Park in Apsley (13 min), Goroke Swimming Centre in Goroke (27 min), Goroke Little Desert Nature Playspace in Goroke (27 min), West Wimmera Shire Goroke Library in Goroke (28 min), Dachshund Museum & Gallery in Edenhope (29 min) and West Wimmera Community Association - Red Tail Gallery in Edenhope (30 min).

Best for
