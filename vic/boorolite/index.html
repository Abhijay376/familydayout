Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Boorolite, in the Mansfield area.

Landmarks

Merrijig Memorial Park is the largest mapped park in Boorolite, at about 1 hectares. For walks and rides, Mansfield - Merrijig crosses Boorolite.

Getting around

There's no train station in Boorolite. The closest to Boorolite is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 70 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Boorolite, so most trips start by car. By car from Boorolite, Mansfield is about 26 minutes away and Benalla about 77 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Mansfield Outdoor Swimming Pool in Mansfield is about 27 minutes' drive from Boorolite. The closest library to Boorolite is Mansfield Library in Mansfield, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Boorolite is the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield, about 26 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield is about 25 minutes' drive from Boorolite. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Boorolite is Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield, about 18 minutes' drive. Mansfield is the closest bigger centre to Boorolite, about 26 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Boorolite, the nearest places for kids by road are Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield (18 min), Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield (25 min), Mansfield Library in Mansfield (26 min), the playground at Mansfield Botanic Park in Mansfield (26 min), the playground at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (26 min) and the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (26 min).

Best for

Free days on the Mansfield - Merrijig and in Boorolite's parks.
