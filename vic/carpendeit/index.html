Carpendeit is in the Corangamite area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Carpendeit, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Carpendeit. The closest to Carpendeit is Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line), about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Carpendeit, so most trips start by car. By car from Carpendeit, Colac is about 33 minutes away, Warrnambool about 67 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Carpendeit is Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon, about 44 minutes' drive. For a library, Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac is about 36 minutes' drive from Carpendeit. For a skate park, Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown is about 30 minutes' drive from Carpendeit. For a cinema, Colac Cinemas in Colac is about 34 minutes' drive from Carpendeit. For an indoor play centre, Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac is about 34 minutes' drive from Carpendeit. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Carpendeit, about 33 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Carpendeit, the nearest places for kids by road are Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (30 min), the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve in Camperdown (32 min), the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (32 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (34 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (34 min) and Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (36 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Carpendeit.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Carpendeit, Cobden has 1 playground. Jancourt East and Bungador also border Carpendeit.
