Langi Logan is in the Ararat area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Langi Logan, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Langi Logan. The closest to Langi Logan is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Langi Logan, so most trips start by car. By car from Langi Logan, Ararat is about 12 minutes away, Stawell about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat is about 12 minutes' drive from Langi Logan. The closest library to Langi Logan is Ararat Library in Ararat, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Langi Logan is David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Langi Logan is Astor Cinema in Ararat, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Langi Logan is Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell, about 36 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Ararat, about 12 minutes from Langi Logan by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Langi Logan are Ararat Library in Ararat (11 min), Astor Cinema in Ararat (11 min), Langi Morgala Museum in Ararat (11 min), Ararat Gallery TAMA in Ararat (12 min), Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat (12 min) and Ararat Outdoor Pool in Ararat (15 min).
