Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Barnadown, in the Greater Bendigo area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Barnadown is Campaspe River K41 Streamside Reserve, covering about 16 hectares.

Getting around

Barnadown has no train station; Goornong Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 4.9 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Barnadown, so most trips start by car. By car from Barnadown, Bendigo is about 27 minutes away, Echuca about 58 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Barnadown is Goornong Swimming Pool in Goornong, about 9 minutes' drive. For a library, Bendigo Library in Bendigo is about 27 minutes' drive from Barnadown. For a skate park, McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk is about 27 minutes' drive from Barnadown. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 28 minutes' drive from Barnadown. The closest indoor play centre to Barnadown is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 31 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 24 minutes' drive from Barnadown. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 27 minutes from Barnadown by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Barnadown are Goornong Swimming Pool in Goornong (9 min), Campaspe Run Rural Discovery Centre in Elmore (20 min), the playground at Pennington Reserve in Huntly (17 min), the playground at Collins Park in Ascot (17 min), White Hills Pool in White Hills (21 min) and Bendigo Joss House Temple in North Bendigo (24 min).
