Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Mystic Park, in the Gannawarra area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Mystic Park is Lake Tutchewop Reserve, covering about 974 hectares. Mystic Park Bushland Reserve and Tutchewop Wildlife Reserve (hunting) are the next largest. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Mystic Park.

Getting around

Mystic Park has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mystic Park, so most trips start by car. By car from Mystic Park, Swan Hill is about 33 minutes away and Echuca about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mystic Park is Swimming Pool in Kerang, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest library to Mystic Park is Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Mystic Park is the skate park at Riverside Park in Swan Hill, about 32 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill is about 32 minutes' drive from Mystic Park. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 33 minutes from Mystic Park by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Mystic Park, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Gray Park in Lake Boga (20 min), Lake Boga Flying Boat Museum in Lake Boga (20 min), the playground at Guastalegname Park in Swan Hill (30 min), Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (29 min), Swimming Pool in Kerang (29 min) and Swan Hill Regional Art Gallery in Swan Hill (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mystic Park.

Nearby suburbs

Benjeroop, Beauchamp and Bael Bael also border Mystic Park.
