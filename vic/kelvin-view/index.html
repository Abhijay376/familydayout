Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Kelvin View, in the Strathbogie area.

Landmarks

Mount Wombat-Garden Range Flora and Fauna Reserve is the largest mapped park in Kelvin View, at about 1,423 hectares. Wondoomarook I53 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kelvin View. The closest to Kelvin View is Euroa Railway Station (V/Line), about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kelvin View, so most trips start by car. By car from Kelvin View, Benalla is about 57 minutes away, Seymour about 64 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kelvin View is Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa, about 23 minutes' drive. For a library, Euroa Library in Euroa is about 23 minutes' drive from Kelvin View. The closest skate park to Kelvin View is Euroa Skate Park in Euroa, about 22 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa is about 22 minutes' drive from Kelvin View. The nearest larger centre is Benalla, about 57 minutes from Kelvin View by car, with 5 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Kelvin View are Strathbogie Recreation Reserve Playground in Strathbogie (16 min), Farmers Arms Hotel Museum in Euroa (22 min), Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa (22 min), Euroa Skate Park in Euroa (22 min), the playground at Euroa Rotary Park in Euroa (22 min) and Euroa Library in Euroa (23 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Kelvin View.

Nearby suburbs

Euroa, next to Kelvin View, has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Strathbogie, next to Kelvin View, has 1 playground. Gooram also borders Kelvin View.
