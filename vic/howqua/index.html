Landmarks

Howqua also has Running Creek. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Howqua. For camping, there's Running Creek. The Howqua Feeder Walking Track, Howqua River Walking Track and Lower Howqua Feeder Walking Track all pass through Howqua.

Getting around

There's no train station in Howqua. The closest to Howqua is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 77 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Howqua, so most trips start by car. By car from Howqua, Mansfield is about 22 minutes away, Benalla about 73 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.
