Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Glenmore in the Moorabool area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

The Aspera Track passes through Glenmore.

Getting around

There's no train station in Glenmore. The closest to Glenmore is Bacchus Marsh Railway Station (V/Line), about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glenmore, so most trips start by car. By car from Glenmore, Bacchus Marsh is about 31 minutes away, Melton about 44 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 79 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bacchus Marsh Public childrens pool in Bacchus Marsh is about 29 minutes' drive from Glenmore. The closest library to Glenmore is Lerderderg Library in Bacchus Marsh, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Glenmore is Needles Beach in Pentland Hills, about 33 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Glenmore is the bmx track at Bacchus Marsh Racecourse and Recreation Reserve in Maddingley, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Glenmore is Reading Cinemas in Melton West, about 42 minutes' drive. Bacchus Marsh is the closest bigger centre to Glenmore, about 31 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Glenmore are the playground at Mount Wallace Hall in Mount Wallace (25 min), the bmx track at Bacchus Marsh Racecourse and Recreation Reserve in Maddingley (25 min), Australian Gliding Museum in Parwan (35 min), the playground at Guy Place Recreation Reserve in Maddingley (26 min), Bacchus Marsh Public childrens pool in Bacchus Marsh (29 min) and the skate park at Rotary Park in Bacchus Marsh (31 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Glenmore on the Aspera Track.

Nearby suburbs

Mount Wallace, next to Glenmore, has 1 playground. Balliang, next to Glenmore, has 1 playground. Fiskville also borders Glenmore.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Glenmore, without traffic.
