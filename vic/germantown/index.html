Germantown is in the Alpine area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Tawonga Gap Scenic Reserve is the largest mapped park in Germantown, at about 42 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Germantown. The closest to Germantown is Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line), about 73 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Germantown, so most trips start by car. By car from Germantown, Bright is about 11 minutes away and Wangaratta about 79 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bright River Pool in Bright is about 11 minutes' drive from Germantown. For a library, Bright Library in Bright is about 12 minutes' drive from Germantown. The closest beach to Germantown is Pebble Beach in Tawonga South, about 17 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Bright BMX Track in Bright is about 13 minutes' drive from Germantown. The closest cinema to Germantown is Sun Cinema Bright in Bright, about 12 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bright, about 11 minutes from Germantown by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Germantown, the nearest places for kids by road are Bright River Pool in Bright (11 min), Bright Art Gallery in Bright (11 min), Bright Splash Park in Bright (11 min), Sun Cinema Bright in Bright (12 min), the playground at Centenary Park in Bright (12 min) and Bright Library in Bright (12 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Germantown.

Nearby suburbs

Bright, next to Germantown, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Germantown, Tawonga South has a beach.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Germantown, without traffic.
