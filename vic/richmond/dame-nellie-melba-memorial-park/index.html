Dame Nellie Melba Memorial Park

Park in Richmond · Basic info

Dame Nellie Melba Memorial Park is a park in Richmond, in the Yarra City Council area, good for all ages. Coppin St/Bridge Rd #20 (tram) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 583 m away.
