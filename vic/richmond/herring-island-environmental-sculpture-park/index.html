Herring Island Environmental Sculpture Park

Park in Richmond · Official details

Herring Island Environmental Sculpture Park is a park in Richmond, in the Yarra City Council area, good for all ages. Como Park/Alexandra Ave (bus) is a 5-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 24 m away and dogs are not allowed.
