O'Connell Reserve

Park in Richmond · Basic info

O'Connell Reserve is a park in Richmond, in the Yarra City Council area, good for all ages. Yarra Bvd/Bridge Rd #22 (tram) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 240 m away.
