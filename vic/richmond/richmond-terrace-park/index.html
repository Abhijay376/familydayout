Richmond Terrace Park

Park in Richmond · Basic info

Richmond Terrace Park is a park in Richmond, in the Yarra City Council area, good for all ages. St Ignatius Church/Church St #59 (tram) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 312 m away.
