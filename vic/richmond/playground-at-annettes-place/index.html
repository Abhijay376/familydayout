Playground at Annettes Place

Playground in Richmond · Basic info

Playground at Annettes Place is a public playground in Richmond, in the Yarra City area. The Main Yarra Trail passes about 40 m away, and Småland is about 370 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 520 m away, and tram 109 stops at River Bvd/Victoria St #25, a 5-minute walk away.
