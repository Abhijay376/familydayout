Lennox and Butler Reserve

Park in Richmond · Basic info

Lennox and Butler Reserve is a park in Richmond, in the Yarra City Council area, good for all ages. Lennox St/Victoria St #20 (tram) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 227 m away with a baby change table.
