Alexander Reserve

Park in Richmond · Basic info

Alexander Reserve is a park in Richmond, in the Yarra City Council area, good for all ages. Abinger St/Church St #60 (tram) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 291 m away.
