Playground at Williams Reserve

Playground in Richmond · Basic info

This is a public playground in Richmond. The Main Yarra Trail passes about 90 m away, and Småland is about 150 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 230 m away, and trams 109 and 12 stop at Burnley St/Victoria St #24, a 1-minute walk away.
