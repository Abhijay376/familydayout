Playground at Barkly Gardens

Playground in Richmond · Basic info

Playground at Barkly Gardens in Richmond is a public playground in the Yarra City area. The Main Yarra Trail passes about 340 m away, and Richmond Library is about 480 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and tram 70 stops at Coppin St/Swan St #12, a 5-minute walk away.
