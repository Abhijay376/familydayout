Citizens Park

Park in Richmond · Basic info

Citizens Park is a park in Richmond, in the Yarra City Council area, good for all ages. Highett St/Church St #62 (tram) is a 2-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 86 m away and dogs are allowed.
