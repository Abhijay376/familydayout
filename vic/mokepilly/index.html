Mokepilly is in the Northern Grampians area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Mokepilly, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Grampians Rail Trail crosses Mokepilly.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mokepilly. The closest to Mokepilly is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 35 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mokepilly, so most trips start by car. By car from Mokepilly, Stawell is about 14 minutes away and Ararat about 37 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Halls Gap Swimming Pool in Halls Gap is about 11 minutes' drive from Mokepilly. For a library, Ararat Library in Ararat is about 37 minutes' drive from Mokepilly. For a beach, Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans is about 12 minutes' drive from Mokepilly. For a skate park, the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell is about 13 minutes' drive from Mokepilly. For a cinema, Astor Cinema in Ararat is about 37 minutes' drive from Mokepilly. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Mokepilly is Halls Gap Zoo in Halls Gap, about 10 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Stawell, about 14 minutes from Mokepilly by car, with 6 playgrounds and a pool. From Mokepilly, the nearest places for kids by road are Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans (12 min), Halls Gap Swimming Pool in Halls Gap (11 min), the playground at Halls Gap Picnic Reserve in Halls Gap (11 min), Halls Gap Zoo in Halls Gap (10 min), the playground at Federation Park in Stawell (10 min) and Brambuk Cultural Centre in Halls Gap (15 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Mokepilly on the Grampians Rail Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Mokepilly, Stawell has 6 playgrounds, a pool and a zoo or wildlife park. Next door to Mokepilly, Lake Fyans has a beach. Fyans Creek and Bellellen also border Mokepilly.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Mokepilly, without traffic.
