Lions Playground

Playground in Minyip · Basic info

Lions Playground in Minyip is a public playground in the Yarriambiack Shire area. Minyip Swimming Pool is about 540 m away, and Playground at Minyip Wetlands is another option about 490 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 120 m from the playground, and coaches stop at Main St/Church St, a 2-minute walk away.
