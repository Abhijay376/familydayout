Minyip is in the Yarriambiack area, and our data lists 2 playgrounds and 6 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Minyip has a pool.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Minyip is Minyip Bushland Reserve, covering about 19 hectares. Burrereo Bushland Reserve and Laen I130 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Minyip also has Laen Bushland Reserve, Nullan I109 Bushland Reserve and Gregson Park Recreation Reserve. Playgrounds in Minyip include Lions Playground and the playground at Minyip Wetlands. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Minyip. Places to visit in Minyip include Minyip Swimming Pool.

Getting around

Minyip has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 96 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Horsham - Donald via Murtoa and Minyip route stop in Minyip. V/Line coaches stop in Minyip. By car from Minyip, Horsham is about 41 minutes away and Stawell about 68 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest skate park to Minyip is the skate park at Rabl Park in Murtoa, about 21 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Horsham, about 41 minutes from Minyip by car, with 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Minyip are Woods' Farming and Heritage Museum in Rupanyup (19 min), Rupanyup Swimming Pool in Rupanyup (21 min), the skate park at Rabl Park in Murtoa (21 min), Murtoa Museum Complex in Murtoa (21 min) and Donald BMX Track in Donald (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Minyip.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Minyip, Murtoa has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Rupanyup, next to Minyip, has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Minyip, Rich Avon West has 1 playground. Lawler, Laen North, Laen and Kewell also border Minyip.
