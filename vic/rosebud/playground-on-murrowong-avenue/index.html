Playground on Murrowong Avenue

Playground in Rosebud · Basic info

Playground on Murrowong Avenue in Rosebud is a public playground in the Mornington Peninsula Shire area. Rostentine is about 400 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 670 m away, and bus 787 stops at The Cresent/Warranilla Ave, a 2-minute walk away.
