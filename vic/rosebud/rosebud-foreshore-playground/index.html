Rosebud Foreshore Playground

Playground in Rosebud · Basic info

Rosebud Foreshore Playground is a public playground in Rosebud, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire area. The Mornington Peninsula Bay Trail passes about 30 m away, and Rosebud Beach is about 460 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and buses 788 and 887 stop at Jetty Rd/Point Nepean Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
