Our data lists 10 playgrounds and 25 parks and reserves in Rosebud, in the Mornington Peninsula area. Rosebud also has a library, a pool, a museum or gallery and a cinema.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Rosebud is Rosebud Public Park and Recreation Reserve Natural Features Reserve, covering about 65 hectares. Seamists Drive Reserve and Peninsula Sands Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Rosebud also has Lawson Park, Civic Reserve and Buckingham Park. Playgrounds in Rosebud include Rotary Park Playground, Village Green Playground and Rosebud Foreshore Playground. Our data shows 17 public toilets in Rosebud. Places to visit in Rosebud include Shipwreck'd Studio Gallery, Rosebud Cinemas, Top Fun Shack, Yawa Aquatic Centre and Rosebud Library. For older kids in Rosebud there's Rosebud Skate Park. The Two Bays Walking Track, The Mornington Peninsula Walk and Mornington Peninsula Bay Trail all pass through Rosebud. Our data lists 23 cafes and family restaurants in Rosebud for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Rosebud has no train station; Bittern Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 787, 788 and 887 stop in Rosebud. By car from Rosebud, Mornington is about 20 minutes away, Frankston about 26 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 69 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a zoo or wildlife park, Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale is about 32 minutes' drive from Rosebud.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 10 playgrounds across Rosebud. Rainy days at the library, cinema, indoor play centre and museum or gallery in Rosebud. Teens who skate or ride, with Rosebud Skate Park in Rosebud. Beach days at Rosebud Beach. Free days on the Two Bays Walking Track and in Rosebud's parks.

Nearby suburbs

McCrae, next to Rosebud, has 1 playground and a beach. Arthurs Seat, next to Rosebud, has 3 playgrounds. Capel Sound, next to Rosebud, has 2 playgrounds. Boneo also borders Rosebud.
