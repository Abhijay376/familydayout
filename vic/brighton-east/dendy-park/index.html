Dendy Park

Park in Brighton East · Basic info

Dendy Park is a park in Brighton East, in the Bayside City Council area, good for all ages. Dendy St/Nepean Hwy (bus) is a 5-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 316 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed.
