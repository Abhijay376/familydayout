Old Dairy Reserve

Park in Brighton East · Basic info

Old Dairy Reserve is a park in Brighton East, in the Bayside City Council area, good for all ages. Grant St/Hampton St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 686 m away.
