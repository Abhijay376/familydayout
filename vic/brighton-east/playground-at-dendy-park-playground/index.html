Playground at Dendy Park

Playground in Brighton East · Basic info

Playground at Dendy Park is a public playground in Brighton East, in the Bayside City area. The Nepean Highway Trail is about 230 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 100 m from the playground, and bus 823 stops at Dendy St/Nepean Hwy, a 5-minute walk away.
