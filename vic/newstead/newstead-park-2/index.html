Newstead Park

Park in Newstead · Basic info

Newstead Park is a park in Newstead, in the Mount Alexander Shire Council area, good for all ages. Panmure St/Pyrenees Hwy (coach) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 152 m away.
