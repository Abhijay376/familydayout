Playground at Newstead Memorial Park

Playground in Newstead · Basic info

Playground at Newstead Memorial Park is a public playground in Newstead, in the Mount Alexander Shire area. The Castlemaine-Maryborough Rail Trail (Under Construction) passes about 60 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 60 m away, and coaches stop at Railway Hotel/Creswick - Newstead Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
