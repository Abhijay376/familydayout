Wensleydale is in the Surf Coast area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Wensleydale is Wensleydale Bushland Reserve, covering about 12 hectares. Wensleydale also has Hammonds Road North and Tanners Road Camp Site. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Wensleydale. For camping, there's Hammonds Road North and Tanners Road Camp Site.

Getting around

Wensleydale has no train station; Winchelsea Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wensleydale, so most trips start by car. By car from Wensleydale, Torquay is about 44 minutes away, Geelong about 48 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Leisurelink Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Grovedale is about 40 minutes' drive from Wensleydale. The closest library to Wensleydale is Torquay Library in Torquay, about 44 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Wensleydale is Sunnymead Beach in Aireys Inlet, about 41 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Wensleydale is Aireys Inlet Skate Park in Aireys Inlet, about 42 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Reading Cinemas in Grovedale is about 41 minutes' drive from Wensleydale. The closest indoor play centre to Wensleydale is Leisurelink Restaurant in Highton, about 42 minutes' drive. Torquay is the closest bigger centre to Wensleydale, about 44 minutes away by car, and has 15 playgrounds, a library and a beach. From Wensleydale, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Modewarre Recreation Reserve in Modewarre (24 min), Sunnymead Beach in Aireys Inlet (41 min), the playground at Aireys Inlet Reserve in Aireys Inlet (42 min), Aireys Inlet Skate Park in Aireys Inlet (42 min), Smelly Beach in Aireys Inlet (42 min) and Moriac Skate Park in Moriac (31 min).

Best for

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Wensleydale, Anglesea has 3 playgrounds, a beach and a skate park. Aireys Inlet, next to Wensleydale, has 2 playgrounds, a beach and a skate park. Next door to Wensleydale, Eastern View has a beach. Winchelsea South, Gherang, Boonah and Bambra also border Wensleydale.
