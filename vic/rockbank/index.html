Our data lists 7 playgrounds and 1 park or reserve in Rockbank, in the Melton area. On top of that, Rockbank has a skate park.

Landmarks

Ian Cowie Recreation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Rockbank, at about 4 hectares. Playgrounds in Rockbank include Clay Cr Reserve Playground, Sedate Dr Reserve Playground and Honeydew Way Reserve Playground. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Rockbank. For older kids in Rockbank there's Rockbank Skate Park.

Getting around

Rockbank has no train station; Rockbank Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 400 metres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 456, 943 and 140A stop in Rockbank. By car from Rockbank, Melton is about 9 minutes away, Bacchus Marsh about 18 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 34 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Rockbank is Paul Sadler Swimland in Aintree, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest library to Rockbank is Melton Library & Learning Hub in Melton, about 9 minutes' drive. For a beach, Altona Beach in Altona is about 28 minutes' drive from Rockbank. The closest cinema to Rockbank is Reading Cinemas in Melton West, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Rockbank is Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Rockbank is Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville, about 34 minutes' drive.

Best for

Younger kids at Rockbank's 7 local playgrounds. Teens who skate or ride, with Rockbank Skate Park in Rockbank.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Rockbank, Truganina has 29 playgrounds and a pool. Aintree, next to Rockbank, has 16 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Rockbank, Thornhill Park has 6 playgrounds. Deanside, next to Rockbank, has 6 playgrounds. Mount Cottrell also borders Rockbank.
