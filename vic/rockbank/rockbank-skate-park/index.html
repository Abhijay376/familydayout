Rockbank Skate Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Rockbank · Basic info

Rockbank Skate Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Rockbank, in the Melton City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Rockbank Caravan Park/Westcott Pde (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 98 m away.
