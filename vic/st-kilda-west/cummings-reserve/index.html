Cummings Reserve

Park in St Kilda West · Basic info

Cummings Reserve is a park in St Kilda West, in the Port Phillip City Council area, good for all ages. Fraser St/Park St #140 (tram) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 267 m away.
