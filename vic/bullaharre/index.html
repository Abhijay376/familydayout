Bullaharre is in the Corangamite area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bullaharre, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bullaharre. The closest to Bullaharre is Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line), about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bullaharre, so most trips start by car. By car from Bullaharre, Colac is about 33 minutes away, Warrnambool about 54 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon is about 31 minutes' drive from Bullaharre. For a library, Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac is about 36 minutes' drive from Bullaharre. For a skate park, Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown is about 17 minutes' drive from Bullaharre. The closest cinema to Bullaharre is Colac Cinemas in Colac, about 34 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac is about 34 minutes' drive from Bullaharre. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Bullaharre, about 33 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Bullaharre, the nearest places for kids by road are Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (17 min), the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve in Camperdown (19 min), the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (31 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (31 min), the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (32 min) and Colac Cinemas in Colac (34 min).
