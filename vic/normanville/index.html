Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Normanville in the Gannawarra area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Normanville has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 17 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Normanville, so most trips start by car. By car from Normanville, Swan Hill is about 70 minutes away and Echuca about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Normanville is Swimming Pool in Kerang, about 26 minutes' drive. For a library, Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang is about 26 minutes' drive from Normanville. The closest places for kids by road from Normanville are the playground at Lake Meran W.R in Lake Meran (23 min), Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (26 min), Swimming Pool in Kerang (26 min), Quambatook Memorial Pool in Quambatook (35 min), Quambatook Historical Centre in Quambatook (35 min) and Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Normanville.
