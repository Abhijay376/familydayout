Drumborg is in the Glenelg area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Drumborg is Cobboboonee National Park, covering about 18,530 hectares. Drumborg Bushland Reserve and Lake Sinclair Wildlife Reserve (hunting) are the next largest. For walks and rides, Great South West Walk crosses Drumborg.

Getting around

Drumborg has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 85 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Drumborg, so most trips start by car. By car from Drumborg, Portland is about 28 minutes away and Hamilton about 51 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Drumborg is Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest library to Drumborg is Heywood Library in Heywood, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Drumborg is Whalers Point in Portland, about 28 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Portland, about 28 minutes from Drumborg by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Drumborg are Heywood Library in Heywood (7 min), the playground at Apex Park in Heywood (7 min), Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood (7 min), the playground at Heywood Youth Park in Heywood (7 min), Whalers Point in Portland (28 min) and Powerhouse Motor Car Museum in Portland (30 min).

Best for

Free days on the Great South West Walk and in Drumborg's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Heywood, next to Drumborg, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Hotspur, Greenwald, Gorae West and Gorae also border Drumborg.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Drumborg, without traffic.
