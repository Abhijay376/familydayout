Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Victoria Point in the Southern Grampians area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Victoria Point. The closest to Victoria Point is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 70 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Victoria Point, so most trips start by car. By car from Victoria Point, Hamilton is about 41 minutes away and Ararat about 85 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Hamilton is about 42 minutes' drive from Victoria Point. The closest library to Victoria Point is Hamilton Library in Hamilton, about 41 minutes' drive. For a skate park, hamilton skate park in Hamilton is about 40 minutes' drive from Victoria Point. The closest cinema to Victoria Point is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 41 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 41 minutes from Victoria Point by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Victoria Point are the playground at Cavendish Town Square Memorial Park in Cavendish (21 min), Dunkeld Memorial Park Playground in Dunkeld (25 min), hamilton skate park in Hamilton (40 min), Sir Reginald Ansett Transport Museum in Hamilton (40 min), Hamilton Library in Hamilton (41 min) and Hamilton Gallery in Hamilton (41 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Victoria Point.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Victoria Point, Cavendish has 1 playground. Grampians also borders Victoria Point.
