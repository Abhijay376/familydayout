Lake Lonsdale in the Northern Grampians area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lake Lonsdale is Lonsdale Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 691 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Lake Lonsdale. For walks and rides, Grampians Rail Trail crosses Lake Lonsdale.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lake Lonsdale. The closest to Lake Lonsdale is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 41 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lake Lonsdale, so most trips start by car. By car from Lake Lonsdale, Stawell is about 24 minutes away and Ararat about 47 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lake Lonsdale is Halls Gap Swimming Pool in Halls Gap, about 19 minutes' drive. For a beach, Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans is about 29 minutes' drive from Lake Lonsdale. The closest skate park to Lake Lonsdale is the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell, about 24 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Halls Gap Zoo in Halls Gap is about 19 minutes' drive from Lake Lonsdale. The nearest larger centre is Stawell, about 24 minutes from Lake Lonsdale by car, with 6 playgrounds and a pool. From Lake Lonsdale, the nearest places for kids by road are Halls Gap Swimming Pool in Halls Gap (19 min), the playground at Halls Gap Picnic Reserve in Halls Gap (20 min), Halls Gap Zoo in Halls Gap (19 min), Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans (29 min), Brambuk Cultural Centre in Halls Gap (23 min) and the playground at Federation Park in Stawell (21 min).

Best for

Free days on the Grampians Rail Trail and in Lake Lonsdale's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Fyans Creek and Deep Lead also border Lake Lonsdale.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Lake Lonsdale, without traffic.
