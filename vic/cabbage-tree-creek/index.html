Our data lists 4 parks and reserves in Cabbage Tree Creek, in the East Gippsland area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Cabbage Tree Creek is Cabbage Tree Creek Flora Reserve, covering about 1,701 hectares. Arte River Flora Reserve is the next largest. Cabbage Tree Creek also has Little Cabbage Tree Creek and Glen Arte Timbers Workers Hut. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Cabbage Tree Creek. Picnic areas in Cabbage Tree Creek include Glen Arte Timbers Workers Hut and Little Cabbage Tree Creek. The Falls Creek Memorial and Little Cabbage Tree Creek Falls all pass through Cabbage Tree Creek.

Getting around

Cabbage Tree Creek has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 97 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Cabbage Tree Creek. By car from Cabbage Tree Creek, Lakes Entrance is about 67 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cabbage Tree Creek is Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest library to Cabbage Tree Creek is Orbost Library in Orbost, about 21 minutes' drive. For a beach, Sailors Grave Beach in Cape Conran is about 16 minutes' drive from Cabbage Tree Creek. The closest skate park to Cabbage Tree Creek is the skate park at Mick Baum Park in Cann River, about 38 minutes' drive. From Cabbage Tree Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are Sailors Grave Beach in Cape Conran (16 min), Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost (20 min), Orbost Library in Orbost (21 min), the playground at Forest Park in Orbost (21 min), the playground at Luderick Point Memorial Park in Bemm River (24 min) and Ewings Marsh in Waygara (39 min).

Best for

Free days on the Falls Creek Memorial and in Cabbage Tree Creek's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Marlo, next to Cabbage Tree Creek, has 1 playground. Next door to Cabbage Tree Creek, Bellbird Creek has 1 playground. Errinundra, Club Terrace and Brodribb River also border Cabbage Tree Creek.
