Banyena is in the Northern Grampians area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Banyena is Matthews Bushland Reserve, covering about 9 hectares. Marnoo Bushland Reserve and Banyena I134 Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

Banyena has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 79 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Banyena, so most trips start by car. By car from Banyena, Stawell is about 52 minutes away and Horsham about 55 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Banyena is Rupanyup Swimming Pool in Rupanyup, about 19 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Donald BMX Track in Donald is about 27 minutes' drive from Banyena. The nearest larger centre is Stawell, about 52 minutes from Banyena by car, with 6 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Banyena are Guthrie Park Playground in Rich Avon West (11 min), the playground at Shanahan Park in Marnoo (12 min), Rupanyup Swimming Pool in Rupanyup (19 min), Woods' Farming and Heritage Museum in Rupanyup (19 min), Minyip Swimming Pool in Minyip (21 min) and Donald BMX Track in Donald (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Banyena.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Banyena, Rupanyup has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Rich Avon West, next to Banyena, has 1 playground. Next door to Banyena, Marnoo has 1 playground. Marnoo West, Grays Bridge and Avon Plains also border Banyena.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Banyena, without traffic.
