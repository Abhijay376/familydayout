Our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in Keilor North, in the Brimbank area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Keilor North is yaluk barring park, covering about 251 hectares. Playgrounds around Keilor North include the playground at yaluk barring park. Our data shows 10 public toilets in Keilor North. The Escarpment Walk passes through Keilor North. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Keilor North for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Keilor North has no train station; Watergardens Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 2.1 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 483 stops in Keilor North. By car from Keilor North, Sunshine is about 16 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 17 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 28 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Keilor North is Flippers Swim School in Sydenham, about 5 minutes' drive. For a library, Brimbank Library in Taylors Lakes is about 5 minutes' drive from Keilor North. For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 30 minutes' drive from Keilor North. The closest skate park to Keilor North is the skate park at Packard Street Reserve in Keilor Downs, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Keilor North is Hoyts in Taylors Lakes, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Keilor North is Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes, about 6 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville is about 22 minutes' drive from Keilor North. The nearest larger centre is Sunshine, about 16 minutes from Keilor North by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Free days on the Escarpment Walk and in Keilor North's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Taylors Lakes, next to Keilor North, has 16 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Keilor, next to Keilor North, has 11 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Keilor North, Diggers Rest has 11 playgrounds. Melbourne Airport, next to Keilor North, has a pool. Keilor Lodge, Calder Park and Bulla also border Keilor North.
