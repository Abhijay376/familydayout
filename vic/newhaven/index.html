Landmarks

Other parks and reserves in Newhaven include Graydens Reserve. Playgrounds in Newhaven include the playground at Graydens Reserve. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Newhaven. In Newhaven you'll also find National Vietnam Veterans Museum. For walks and rides, Phillip Island Bike Path crosses Newhaven. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Newhaven for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Newhaven has no train station; Stony Point Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Cowes - Anderson - Wonthaggi and Cowes - Fountain Gate via Anderson routes stop in Newhaven. V/Line coaches stop in Newhaven. By car from Newhaven, Wonthaggi is about 27 minutes away, Leongatha about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a cinema, Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi is about 26 minutes' drive from Newhaven. The nearest larger centre is Wonthaggi, about 27 minutes from Newhaven by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.
