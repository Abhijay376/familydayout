Laen East in the Buloke area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Laen East is Purcells Tank Bushland Reserve, covering about 2 hectares.

Getting around

Laen East has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 100 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Laen East, so most trips start by car. By car from Laen East, Horsham is about 65 minutes away and Stawell about 71 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Donald Swimming Pool in Donald is about 11 minutes' drive from Laen East. For a skate park, Donald BMX Track in Donald is about 14 minutes' drive from Laen East. From Laen East, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Donald Public Park in Donald (12 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (11 min), Donald BMX Track in Donald (14 min), Guthrie Park Playground in Rich Avon West (16 min) and Minyip Swimming Pool in Minyip (25 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Laen East.
