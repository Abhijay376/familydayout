Spring Hill in the Macedon Ranges area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Spring Hill is Coliban I8 Bushland Reserve, covering about 3 hectares. Coliban I9 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Spring Hill. The closest to Spring Hill is Malmsbury Railway Station (V/Line), about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Spring Hill, so most trips start by car. By car from Spring Hill, Daylesford is about 24 minutes away, Kyneton about 26 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 83 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Spring Hill is Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford, about 24 minutes' drive. For a library, Kyneton Library in Kyneton is about 26 minutes' drive from Spring Hill. For a beach, Canoeing in Tylden is about 20 minutes' drive from Spring Hill. The closest skate park to Spring Hill is Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton, about 24 minutes' drive. Daylesford is the closest bigger centre to Spring Hill, about 24 minutes away by car, and has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Spring Hill are Canoeing in Tylden (20 min), the playground at Quarry Street Reserve in Trentham (18 min), Little Gallery in Trentham (17 min), the playground at J P Gleeson Memorial Park in Lyonville (18 min), Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton (24 min) and Kyneton Library in Kyneton (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Spring Hill.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Spring Hill, Kyneton has 10 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Tylden, next to Spring Hill, has 1 playground and a beach. Next door to Spring Hill, Trentham has 2 playgrounds. Lauriston, next to Spring Hill, has 1 playground. Little Hampton, Kyneton South, Glenlyon and Denver also border Spring Hill.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Spring Hill, without traffic.
