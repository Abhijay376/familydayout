Ledcourt is in the Northern Grampians area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Ledcourt is Howards Bushland Reserve, covering about 12 hectares. Ledcourt I57 Bushland Reserve is the next largest. For walks and rides, Grampians Peaks Trail and Heatherlie Quarry cross Ledcourt.

Getting around

There's no train station in Ledcourt. The closest to Ledcourt is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 51 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Ledcourt, so most trips start by car. By car from Ledcourt, Stawell is about 32 minutes away and Horsham about 44 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Ledcourt is Halls Gap Swimming Pool in Halls Gap, about 41 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell is about 31 minutes' drive from Ledcourt. For a cinema, Centre Cinemas in Horsham is about 43 minutes' drive from Ledcourt. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Ledcourt is Halls Gap Zoo in Halls Gap, about 45 minutes' drive. Stawell is the closest bigger centre to Ledcourt, about 32 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Ledcourt are Glenorchy Playground in Glenorchy (28 min), Halls Gap Swimming Pool in Halls Gap (41 min), the playground at Halls Gap Picnic Reserve in Halls Gap (42 min), Brambuk Cultural Centre in Halls Gap (45 min), Halls Gap Zoo in Halls Gap (45 min) and MOCO Gallery in Halls Gap (46 min).

Best for

Free days on the Grampians Peaks Trail and in Ledcourt's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Glenorchy, next to Ledcourt, has 1 playground. Lake Lonsdale, Deep Lead and Dadswells Bridge also border Ledcourt.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Ledcourt, without traffic.
