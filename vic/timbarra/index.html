Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Timbarra, in the East Gippsland area.

Landmarks

Mount Elizabeth Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Timbarra, at about 5,232 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Timbarra include Bark Sheds. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Timbarra. Picnic areas in Timbarra include Bark Sheds.

Getting around

Timbarra has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 48 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Timbarra, so most trips start by car. By car from Timbarra, Bairnsdale is about 62 minutes away and Lakes Entrance about 73 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek is about 45 minutes' drive from Timbarra. The closest places for kids by road from Timbarra are Bruthen Gallery in Bruthen (39 min), Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek (45 min) and Great Alpine Gallery in Swifts Creek (46 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Timbarra.

Nearby suburbs

Buchan, next to Timbarra, has 1 playground and a pool. Ensay, next to Timbarra, has 1 playground. Double Bridges and Buchan South also border Timbarra.
