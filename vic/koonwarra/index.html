Koonwarra in the South Gippsland area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Koonwarra Fish Beds Geological Reserve is the largest mapped park in Koonwarra, at about 26 hectares. Koonwarra Flora Reserve and Koonwarra Memorial Park are the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Koonwarra. For walks and rides, Great Southern Rail Trail crosses Koonwarra.

Getting around

Koonwarra has no train station; Yarragon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 39 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Koonwarra. By car from Koonwarra, Leongatha is about 9 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 29 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Koonwarra is South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha, about 9 minutes' drive. For a library, Leongatha Library in Leongatha is about 8 minutes' drive from Koonwarra. For a beach, The Caves in Inverloch is about 25 minutes' drive from Koonwarra. The closest skate park to Koonwarra is Leongatha Skate Park in Leongatha, about 9 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha is about 9 minutes' drive from Koonwarra. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Koonwarra, about 9 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Koonwarra are Leongatha Library in Leongatha (8 min), Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha (9 min), South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha (9 min), Leongatha Skate Park in Leongatha (9 min), the playground at Mary Checkley Reserve in Leongatha (9 min) and Splash Pools and Stadium in Leongatha (10 min).

Best for

Free days on the Great Southern Rail Trail and in Koonwarra's parks.
