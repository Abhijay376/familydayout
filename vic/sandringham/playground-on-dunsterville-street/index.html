Playground on Dunsterville Street

Playground in Sandringham · Basic info

Playground on Dunsterville Street in Sandringham is a public playground in the Bayside City area. The Bike: Linacre St passes about 320 m away, and Coffee Shop At Raw is about 400 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 940 m away, and bus 828 stops at Alfreda St/Highett Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
