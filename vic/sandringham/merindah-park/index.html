Merindah Park

Park in Sandringham · Basic info

Merindah Park is a park in Sandringham, in the Bayside City Council area, good for all ages. Aberdeen Rd/Bay Rd (bus) is a 10-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 532 m away and dogs are allowed.
