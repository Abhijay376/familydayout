John Batman Reserve

Park in Sandringham · Basic info

John Batman Reserve is a park in Sandringham, in the Bayside City Council area, good for all ages. John Batman Gardens/Bay Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 1258 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed on a lead.
