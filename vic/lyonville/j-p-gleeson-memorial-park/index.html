J. P. Gleeson Memorial Park

Park in Lyonville · Basic info

J. P. Gleeson Memorial Park is a park in Lyonville, in the Hepburn Shire Council area, good for all ages. Main St/Daylesford-Trentham Rd (coach) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 86 m away.
