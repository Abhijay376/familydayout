Lyonville in the Hepburn area has 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Other parks and reserves in Lyonville include Lyonville Mineral Springs and J P Gleeson Memorial Park. Playgrounds in Lyonville include the playground at J P Gleeson Memorial Park. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Lyonville. Picnic areas in Lyonville include Lyonville Mineral Springs. The Domino Rail Trail and Domino Trail Alternate all pass through Lyonville.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lyonville. The closest to Lyonville is Ballan Railway Station (V/Line), about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Lyonville. By car from Lyonville, Daylesford is about 15 minutes away, Kyneton about 30 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 87 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford is about 15 minutes' drive from Lyonville. The closest library to Lyonville is Daylesford Library in Daylesford, about 14 minutes' drive. For a beach, Canoeing in Tylden is about 23 minutes' drive from Lyonville. For a skate park, Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton is about 28 minutes' drive from Lyonville. Daylesford is the closest bigger centre to Lyonville, about 15 minutes away by car, and has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Lyonville are the playground at Quarry Street Reserve in Trentham (9 min), Little Gallery in Trentham (9 min), Daylesford Spa Country Railway in Daylesford (14 min), Daylesford Library in Daylesford (14 min), Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford (15 min) and Canoeing in Tylden (23 min).

Best for
