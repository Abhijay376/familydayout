Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Gerrigerrup in the Moyne area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Gerrigerrup has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 53 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gerrigerrup, so most trips start by car. By car from Gerrigerrup, Hamilton is about 40 minutes away and Warrnambool about 54 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Hawkesdale Swimming Pool in Hawkesdale is about 19 minutes' drive from Gerrigerrup. For a library, Hamilton Library in Hamilton is about 40 minutes' drive from Gerrigerrup. The closest skate park to Gerrigerrup is hamilton skate park in Hamilton, about 41 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Gerrigerrup is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 40 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 40 minutes from Gerrigerrup by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Gerrigerrup are Hawkesdale Swimming Pool in Hawkesdale (19 min), Penshurst Botanic Gardens Playground in Penshurst (22 min), Penshurst Swimming Pool in Penshurst (22 min), Volcanoes Discovery Centre in Penshurst (22 min), Penshurst Rec Reserve Playground in Penshurst (24 min) and Sir Reginald Ansett Transport Museum in Hamilton (41 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Gerrigerrup.

Nearby suburbs

Penshurst, next to Gerrigerrup, has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Gerrigerrup, Hawkesdale has a pool. Macarthur and Gazette also border Gerrigerrup.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Gerrigerrup, without traffic.
