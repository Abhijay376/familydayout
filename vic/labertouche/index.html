Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Labertouche, in the Baw Baw area.

Landmarks

Jindivick G77 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Labertouche, at about 58 hectares. Labertouche also has Forest Road Horse Visitor Area.

Getting around

There's no train station in Labertouche. The closest to Labertouche is Longwarry Railway Station (V/Line), about 7.8 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Labertouche, so most trips start by car. By car from Labertouche, Warragul is about 23 minutes away, Pakenham about 33 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 77 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Drouin Swimming Pool in Drouin is about 17 minutes' drive from Labertouche. The closest library to Labertouche is Drouin Library in Drouin, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Labertouche is Drouin Skate Park in Drouin, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Labertouche is Warragul Cinema in Warragul, about 21 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 21 minutes' drive from Labertouche. For a zoo or wildlife park, Wildlife Trail in Tynong North is about 19 minutes' drive from Labertouche. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Labertouche, about 23 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Labertouche, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Longwarry Recreation Reserve in Longwarry (13 min), the playground at Bunyip Sanctuary in Bunyip (15 min), Drouin Library in Drouin (16 min), Drouin Swimming Pool in Drouin (17 min), Drouin Skate Park in Drouin (17 min) and the bmx track at Greenland Court Reserve in Garfield (18 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Labertouche.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Labertouche, Gembrook has 6 playgrounds and a skate park. Gentle Annie, Drouin West and Bunyip North also border Labertouche.
