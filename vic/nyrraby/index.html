Nyrraby is in the Swan Hill area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Nyrraby, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Nyrraby has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 32 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nyrraby, so most trips start by car. By car from Nyrraby, Swan Hill is about 36 minutes away and Mildura about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Nyrraby is Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest library to Nyrraby is Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Nyrraby is Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Nyrraby is Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill, about 36 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 36 minutes from Nyrraby by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Nyrraby are the playground at Nyah Heritage Park in Nyah (21 min), the playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV) in Nyah (22 min), Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South (28 min), the bmx track at Piangil Memorial Park in Piangil (32 min), Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill (35 min) and Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Nyrraby.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Nyrraby, Nyah West has 1 playground. Pira and Nowie also border Nyrraby.
