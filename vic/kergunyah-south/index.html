Kergunyah South is in the Alpine area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Kergunyah South, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Kergunyah South has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 39 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Albury - Mt Beauty via Baranduda and Tawonga South route stop in Kergunyah South. By car from Kergunyah South, Wodonga is about 35 minutes away and Bright about 57 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kergunyah South is Edge Aquatics in Baranduda, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest library to Kergunyah South is Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford, about 40 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Kergunyah South is Pebble Beach in Tawonga South, about 35 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Skate Park in Yackandandah is about 28 minutes' drive from Kergunyah South. For a cinema, Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana is about 30 minutes' drive from Kergunyah South. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 35 minutes from Kergunyah South by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Kergunyah South, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Banksia Park in Tangambalanga (20 min), Skate Park in Yackandandah (28 min), the playground at Sir Isaac Isaacs Park in Yackandandah (28 min), Skate Park in Baranduda (26 min), Edge Aquatics in Baranduda (26 min) and Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Kergunyah South.
