Licola is in the Wellington area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Licola, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 2 public toilets in Licola. The Wellington River Walking Track and Mount Margaret Walking Track all pass through Licola.

Getting around

Licola has no train station; Rosedale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 60 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Licola, so most trips start by car. By car from Licola, Sale is about 88 minutes away and Traralgon about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Licola on the Wellington River Walking Track.

Nearby suburbs

Licola North, Glenmaggie, Glenfalloch and Crookayan also border Licola.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Licola, without traffic.
