Breamlea is in the Greater Geelong area, and our data lists 2 playgrounds in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Breamlea has 2 beaches.

Landmarks

Playgrounds in Breamlea include Breamlea Recreation Reserve and the playground at Lorne - Queenscliff Coastal Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Breamlea. Places to visit in Breamlea include Breamlea Beach and Buckleys (Stingray) Bay.

Getting around

There's no train station in Breamlea. The closest to Breamlea is Marshall Railway Station (V/Line), about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Breamlea, so most trips start by car. By car from Breamlea, Torquay is about 15 minutes away, Geelong about 23 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 85 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Salt Water Swim Schools in Mount Duneed is about 17 minutes' drive from Breamlea. The closest library to Breamlea is Torquay Library in Torquay, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Breamlea is the bmx track at Kalkarra Park in Mount Duneed, about 12 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Reading Cinemas in Grovedale is about 21 minutes' drive from Breamlea. For an indoor play centre, Bounce Inc in Grovedale is about 19 minutes' drive from Breamlea. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Breamlea is Tiger Moth World in Torquay, about 7 minutes' drive. Torquay is the closest bigger centre to Breamlea, about 15 minutes away by car, and has 15 playgrounds, a library and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Breamlea are Tiger Moth World in Torquay (7 min), the bmx track at Kalkarra Park in Mount Duneed (12 min), Barwon Heads Skatepark in Barwon Heads (14 min), Torquay Library in Torquay (14 min), Australian National Surfing Museum in Torquay (15 min) and Biyal-an Armstrong Creek Library in Armstrong Creek (15 min).
