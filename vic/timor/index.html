Timor is in the Central Goldfields area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Timor Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Timor, at about 776 hectares. Timor Historic and Cultural Features Reserve is the next largest. For walks and rides, Dunolly - Havelock Gold Rush Cycle Trail crosses Timor.

Getting around

Timor has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 8.8 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Timor, so most trips start by car. By car from Timor, Maryborough is about 13 minutes away, Castlemaine about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Timor is Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest library to Timor is Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough, about 14 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 13 minutes from Timor by car, with a library. The closest places for kids by road from Timor are Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (14 min), Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (20 min), Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (20 min), the playground at Market Reserve in Carisbrook (20 min), Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground in Talbot (29 min) and Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (30 min).

Best for

Free days on the Dunolly - Havelock Gold Rush Cycle Trail and in Timor's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Maryborough, next to Timor, has a library. Bet Bet also borders Timor.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Timor, without traffic.
