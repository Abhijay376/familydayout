Victoria Valley in the Southern Grampians area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Victoria Valley is Bryan Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 727 hectares. Freshwater Lake Reserve and Bullawin Flora Reserve are the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet and 4 off-leash dog parks in Victoria Valley. For walks and rides, Grampians Peaks Trail, Mount Sturgeon Track and Mt Abrupt Walking Track cross Victoria Valley.

Getting around

There's no train station in Victoria Valley. The closest to Victoria Valley is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 61 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Victoria Valley, so most trips start by car. By car from Victoria Valley, Hamilton is about 38 minutes away and Ararat about 71 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Victoria Valley is Penshurst Swimming Pool in Penshurst, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest library to Victoria Valley is Hamilton Library in Hamilton, about 37 minutes' drive. For a skate park, hamilton skate park in Hamilton is about 36 minutes' drive from Victoria Valley. For a cinema, Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton is about 37 minutes' drive from Victoria Valley. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 38 minutes from Victoria Valley by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Victoria Valley, the nearest places for kids by road are Dunkeld Memorial Park Playground in Dunkeld (11 min), the playground at Cavendish Town Square Memorial Park in Cavendish (32 min), Penshurst Swimming Pool in Penshurst (34 min), Volcanoes Discovery Centre in Penshurst (34 min), Sir Reginald Ansett Transport Museum in Hamilton (36 min) and hamilton skate park in Hamilton (36 min).

Best for

Free days on the Grampians Peaks Trail and in Victoria Valley's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Victoria Valley, Dunkeld has 1 playground. Grampians and Bornes Hill also border Victoria Valley.
