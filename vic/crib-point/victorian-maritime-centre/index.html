Victorian Maritime Centre

Museum or gallery in Crib Point · Official details

Victorian Maritime Centre is a museum or gallery in Crib Point, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire Council area, best for school-age kids. The nearest public toilet is about 213 m away. Opening hours: Saturday 10:00am-3:00pm; Sunday 10:00am-3:00pm.
