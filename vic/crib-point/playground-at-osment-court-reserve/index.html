Playground at Osment Court Reserve

Playground in Crib Point · Basic info

Playground at Osment Court Reserve is a public playground in Crib Point, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. You'll find a public toilet about 900 m from the playground, and bus 782 stops at Lorimer St/Stony Point Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
