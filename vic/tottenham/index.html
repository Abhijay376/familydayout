Tottenham is in the Maribyrnong area, and our data lists 1 playground in the suburb.

Getting around

Tottenham has no train station; Tottenham Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 900 metres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tottenham, so most trips start by car. By car from Tottenham, Sunshine is about 7 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 24 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 18 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Elite Swimming in West Footscray is about 3 minutes' drive from Tottenham. The closest library to Tottenham is West Footscray Library in West Footscray, about 5 minutes' drive. For a beach, Altona Dog Beach in Seaholme is about 14 minutes' drive from Tottenham. For a skate park, Johnson Reserve Skate Park in West Footscray is about 4 minutes' drive from Tottenham. The closest cinema to Tottenham is Village Cinemas in Sunshine, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Tottenham is Zone Bowling in Braybrook, about 3 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Tottenham is Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville, about 15 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Sunshine, about 7 minutes from Tottenham by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Tottenham, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at John McDonald Reserve in West Footscray (2 min), Zone Bowling in Braybrook (3 min), the playground at Gaudion Reserve in West Footscray (3 min), Elite Swimming in West Footscray (3 min), West Footscray Library in West Footscray (5 min) and Johnson Reserve Skate Park in West Footscray (4 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tottenham.

Nearby suburbs

Sunshine, next to Tottenham, has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Tottenham, Yarraville has 14 playgrounds, a cinema and a skate park. Next door to Tottenham, West Footscray has 6 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Tottenham, Braybrook has 9 playgrounds and a pool. Brooklyn also borders Tottenham.
