Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Homewood in the Murrindindi area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Homewood. For walks and rides, Great Victorian Rail Trail crosses Homewood.

Getting around

Homewood has no train station; Tallarook Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Homewood, so most trips start by car. By car from Homewood, Seymour is about 23 minutes away, Healesville about 61 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Homewood is Seymour Swimming Centre in Seymour, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest library to Homewood is Seymour Library in Seymour, about 22 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea is about 9 minutes' drive from Homewood. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 23 minutes from Homewood by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Homewood, the nearest places for kids by road are the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea (9 min), TLM Moto Guzzi in Yea (29 min), the playground at M O'Sullivan Reserve in Seymour (22 min), the playground at O'Shea Park in Seymour (22 min), Seymour Skate Park in Seymour (22 min) and Seymour Library in Seymour (22 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Homewood on the Great Victorian Rail Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Homewood, Yea has a skate park. Flowerdale also borders Homewood.
