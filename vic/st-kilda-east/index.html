Our data lists 3 playgrounds and 4 parks and reserves in St Kilda East, in the Port Phillip area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in St Kilda East is Alma Park West, covering about 5 hectares. Alma Park East and Greenmeadows Gardens are the next largest. St Kilda East also has Te-Arai Reserve. Playgrounds in St Kilda East include the playground at Alma Park East. Our data shows 2 public toilets in St Kilda East. The Windsor to St Kilda Marina passes through St Kilda East. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in St Kilda East for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in St Kilda East. The closest to St Kilda East is Balaclava Railway Station (metro), about 900 metres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Trams 3, 5, 16, 64 and 78 stop in St Kilda East. Buses 603, 604 and 623 stop in St Kilda East. By car from St Kilda East, Box Hill is about 23 minutes away, Dandenong about 26 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 11 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to St Kilda East is St Kilda Sea Baths in St Kilda, about 5 minutes' drive. For a library, St Kilda Public Library in St Kilda is about 2 minutes' drive from St Kilda East. For a beach, St Kilda Beach in St Kilda is about 4 minutes' drive from St Kilda East. For a skate park, the pump track at Windsor Siding in Windsor is about 4 minutes' drive from St Kilda East. The closest cinema to St Kilda East is Classic Cinema Elsternwick in Elsternwick, about 3 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to St Kilda East is Akira’s Arcade in Prahran, about 4 minutes' drive.

Best for

Younger kids at St Kilda East's 3 local playgrounds. Free days on the Windsor to St Kilda Marina and in St Kilda East's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to St Kilda East, St Kilda has 9 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to St Kilda East, Prahran has 7 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Elsternwick, next to St Kilda East, has 3 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. Windsor, next to St Kilda East, has 4 playgrounds and a skate park. Caulfield North, Armadale, Ripponlea and Balaclava also border St Kilda East.
