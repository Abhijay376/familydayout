Greenmeadows Gardens

Park in St Kilda East · Official details

Greenmeadows Gardens is a park in St Kilda East, in the Glen Eira City Council area, good for all ages. Allan Rd/Balaclava Rd #42 (tram) is a 5-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 122 m away and dogs are allowed on a lead.

Questions parents ask

Where is Greenmeadows Gardens?
Greenmeadows Gardens is in St Kilda East, Victoria.
Is Greenmeadows Gardens free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Greenmeadows Gardens?
Yes, on a lead.
Are there toilets at Greenmeadows Gardens?
The nearest public toilet is about 122 m away.
How do I get to Greenmeadows Gardens by public transport?
The closest stop is Allan Rd/Balaclava Rd #42 (routes 16, 3), a 5-minute walk.
