Tootgarook in the Mornington Peninsula area has 1 playground and 5 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Mathis Avenue Reserve is the largest mapped park in Tootgarook, at about 2 hectares. Marshall Street Reserve and Quinns Park are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Tootgarook include Romney Park and Kevin Street Reserve. Playgrounds in Tootgarook include the playground at Marshall Street Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Tootgarook. For walks and rides, Mornington Peninsula Bay Trail crosses Tootgarook.

Getting around

Tootgarook has no train station; Bittern Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 787 and 788 stop in Tootgarook. By car from Tootgarook, Mornington is about 29 minutes away, Frankston about 35 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 78 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tootgarook is Alba Thermal Springs & Spa ARCH in Fingal, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest library to Tootgarook is Rosebud Library in Rosebud, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Tootgarook is Rosebud Beach in Rosebud, about 9 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Rosebud Skate Park in Rosebud is about 7 minutes' drive from Tootgarook. For a cinema, Rosebud Cinemas in Rosebud is about 9 minutes' drive from Tootgarook. For an indoor play centre, Top Fun Shack in Rosebud is about 9 minutes' drive from Tootgarook. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Tootgarook is Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale, about 41 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Mornington, about 29 minutes from Tootgarook by car, with 22 playgrounds, a library, indoor play and a beach. From Tootgarook, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Port Phillip Bay Coastal Reserve (COM) in Capel Sound (4 min), Rosebud Skate Park in Rosebud (7 min), Alba Thermal Springs & Spa ARCH in Fingal (6 min), Yawa Aquatic Centre in Rosebud (8 min), Top Fun Shack in Rosebud (9 min) and Rosebud Beach in Rosebud (9 min).
