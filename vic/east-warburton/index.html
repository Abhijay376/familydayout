Our data lists 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in East Warburton, in the Yarra Ranges area.

Landmarks

Yarra Tributaries Forest Reserve is the largest mapped park in East Warburton, at about 13,364 hectares. Mississippi No. 1 Mill Historic and Cultural Features Reserve is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in East Warburton include Brahams Trail Bike Visitor Area. Our data shows 3 public toilets in East Warburton. The Powelltown Tramways Railtrails, O'Shannassy Aqueduct Trail and Walk into history Trail all pass through East Warburton.

Getting around

East Warburton has no train station; Garfield Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 39 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 683 stops in East Warburton. By car from East Warburton, Healesville is about 36 minutes away, Lilydale about 44 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 85 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Warburton Water World in Warburton is about 5 minutes' drive from East Warburton. For a library, Street Library in Millgrove is about 11 minutes' drive from East Warburton. The closest skate park to East Warburton is the skate park at Yarra Junction Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Yarra Junction, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to East Warburton is The Memo, Healesville in Healesville, about 36 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Lyre Bird enclosure in Badger Creek is about 33 minutes' drive from East Warburton. Healesville is the closest bigger centre to East Warburton, about 36 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from East Warburton are the playground at Yarra River Heritage River in Warburton (4 min), Warburton Water World in Warburton (5 min), the playground at Warburton Recreation Reserve in Warburton (6 min), Street Library in Millgrove (11 min), Yarra Junction public library in Yarra Junction (18 min) and the skate park at Yarra Junction Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Yarra Junction (18 min).

Best for

Free days on the Powelltown Tramways Railtrails and in East Warburton's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to East Warburton, Warburton has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Marysville, next to East Warburton, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Powelltown, next to East Warburton, has 1 playground. McMahons Creek, Fernshaw, Big Pats Creek and Ada also border East Warburton.
