Getting around

Rich Avon East has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 84 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Rich Avon East, so most trips start by car. By car from Rich Avon East, Stawell is about 52 minutes away and Horsham about 62 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Donald Swimming Pool in Donald is about 18 minutes' drive from Rich Avon East. For a skate park, Donald BMX Track in Donald is about 20 minutes' drive from Rich Avon East. The nearest larger centre is Stawell, about 52 minutes from Rich Avon East by car, with 6 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Rich Avon East are Guthrie Park Playground in Rich Avon West (4 min), the playground at Shanahan Park in Marnoo (13 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (18 min), Donald BMX Track in Donald (20 min), Rupanyup Swimming Pool in Rupanyup (26 min) and Woods' Farming and Heritage Museum in Rupanyup (26 min).
