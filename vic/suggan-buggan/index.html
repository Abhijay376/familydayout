Suggan Buggan is in the East Gippsland area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Suggan Buggan is Suggan Buggan Remote and Natural Area, covering about 17,978 hectares. Suggan Buggan and Berrima Rivers Heritage River is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Suggan Buggan. For walks and rides, Mt Stradbroke Walking Track crosses Suggan Buggan.

Getting around

There's no train station in Suggan Buggan. The closest to Suggan Buggan is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 115 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Suggan Buggan, so most trips start by car. By car from Suggan Buggan, Lakes Entrance is about 2.5 hours away and Bairnsdale about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Free days on the Mt Stradbroke Walking Track and in Suggan Buggan's parks.
