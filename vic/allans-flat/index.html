Allans Flat in the Indigo area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Allans Flat is Allans Flat Bushland Reserve, covering about 3 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Allans Flat.

Getting around

There's no train station in Allans Flat. The closest to Allans Flat is Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line), about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Allans Flat, so most trips start by car. By car from Allans Flat, Wodonga is about 22 minutes away and Wangaratta about 61 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Allans Flat is Edge Aquatics in Baranduda, about 13 minutes' drive. For a library, Upper Murray Regional Library - Wodonga in Wodonga is about 21 minutes' drive from Allans Flat. For a beach, Finns Beach in Bandiana is about 27 minutes' drive from Allans Flat. For a skate park, Skate Park in Yackandandah is about 8 minutes' drive from Allans Flat. The closest cinema to Allans Flat is Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana, about 17 minutes' drive. Wodonga is the closest bigger centre to Allans Flat, about 22 minutes away by car, and has 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Allans Flat are the playground at Sir Isaac Isaacs Park in Yackandandah (8 min), Skate Park in Yackandandah (8 min), Skate Park in Baranduda (12 min), Edge Aquatics in Baranduda (13 min), Mel Read Park in Baranduda (12 min) and Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana (17 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Allans Flat.

Nearby suburbs

Yackandandah, next to Allans Flat, has 1 playground and a skate park. Kergunyah also borders Allans Flat.
