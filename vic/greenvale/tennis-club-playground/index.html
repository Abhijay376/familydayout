Tennis Club Playground

Playground in Greenvale · Basic info

Tennis Club Playground in Greenvale is a public playground in the Hume City area. Playground at Greenvale Recreation Reserve is another option about 200 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 300 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 2 minutes from Greenvale.
