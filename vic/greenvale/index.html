Greenvale in the Hume area has 21 playgrounds and 32 parks and reserves in our data. Greenvale also has a skate park.

Landmarks

Woodlands Historic Park is the largest mapped park in Greenvale, at about 813 hectares. Back Paddock and Greenvale Recreation Reserve are the next largest. Greenvale also has Napoli Park, Greenhaven Park and Providence Park. Playgrounds around Greenvale include Tennis Club Playground, Kirkham Drive Playground and the playground at Napoli Park. Our data shows 15 public toilets in Greenvale. For older kids in Greenvale there's the skate park at Barrymore Road Recreational Reserve. Greenvale Shopping Centre is the local shopping centre. For walks and rides, Moonee Ponds Creek Trail, Attwood Creek Trail and Greenvale Creek Trail cross Greenvale. Our data lists 5 cafes and family restaurants in Greenvale for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Greenvale. The closest to Greenvale is Coolaroo Railway Station (metro), about 5.8 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 484, 541 and 543 stop in Greenvale. By car from Greenvale, Broadmeadows is about 12 minutes away, Sunshine about 20 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 30 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Greenvale is Splash Aqua Park and Leisure Centre in Craigieburn, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest library to Greenvale is Library in Westmeadows, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Greenvale is Horse Arena in Abbotsford, about 32 minutes' drive. For a cinema, United Cinemas in Craigieburn is about 11 minutes' drive from Greenvale. The closest indoor play centre to Greenvale is Timezone in Craigieburn, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Greenvale is Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville, about 24 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 21 playgrounds across Greenvale. Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at Barrymore Road Recreational Reserve in Greenvale. Free days on the Moonee Ponds Creek Trail and in Greenvale's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Craigieburn, next to Greenvale, has 54 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Roxburgh Park, next to Greenvale, has 14 playgrounds and a skate park. Meadow Heights, next to Greenvale, has 8 playgrounds. Next door to Greenvale, Melbourne Airport has a pool. Attwood, Yuroke, Oaklands Junction and Bulla also border Greenvale.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Greenvale, without traffic.
