Greenvale Reservoir Park

Park in Greenvale · Official details

Greenvale Reservoir Park is a park in Greenvale, in the Hume City Council area, good for all ages. Napoli Park/Napoli Cct (bus) is a 19-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 1071 m away and dogs are allowed on a lead. Opening hours: opening_hours: 24/7.
