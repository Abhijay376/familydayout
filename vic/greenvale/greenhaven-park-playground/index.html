Greenhaven Park Playground

Playground in Greenvale · Basic info

This is a public playground in Greenvale. The Attwood Creek Trail passes about 310 m away, and Playground on Providence Road is another option about 410 m away. There's a public toilet about 870 m away. It's easiest by car, about 5 minutes from Greenvale.
