Skate park at Barrymore Road Recreational Reserve

Skate, BMX or pump track in Greenvale · Basic info

Skate park at Barrymore Road Recreational Reserve is a skate, bmx or pump track in Greenvale, in the Hume City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Elgin Rd/Barrymore Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1071 m away with a baby change table.
