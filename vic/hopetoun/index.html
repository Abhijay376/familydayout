Our data lists 4 playgrounds and 7 parks and reserves in Hopetoun, in the Yarriambiack area. Hopetoun also has a pool.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Hopetoun is Lake Coorong Reserve, covering about 761 hectares. Dattuck Bushland Reserve and Cambacanya Flora and Fauna Reserve are the next largest. Hopetoun also has Goyura Bushland Reserve, Chiprick I177 Bushland Reserve and Wilhelmina Natural Features Reserve. Playgrounds in Hopetoun include the playground at Hopetoun Recreation Reserve, Hopetoun Caravan Park &Tennis Court Play Ground and Laury&Jenny Poulton and Family Memorial Playground. Our data shows 6 public toilets in Hopetoun. In Hopetoun you'll also find Hopetoun Swimming Pool.

Getting around

There's no train station in Hopetoun. The closest to Hopetoun is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 117 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Mildura - Horsham via Hopetoun and Horsham - Hopetoun via Dimboola routes stop in Hopetoun. V/Line coaches stop in Hopetoun. By car from Hopetoun, Horsham is about 1.5 hours away and Swan Hill about 1.5 hours, without traffic.
