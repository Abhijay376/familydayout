Laury&Jenny Poulton and Family Memorial Playground

Playground in Hopetoun · Basic info

Laury&Jenny Poulton and Family Memorial Playground in Hopetoun is a public playground in the Yarriambiack Shire area. Hopetoun Swimming Pool is about 70 m away, and Hopetoun Caravan Park &Tennis Court Play Ground is another option about 180 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus Mildura - Horsham via Hopetoun stops at Austin St/Henty Hwy, a 3-minute walk away.
