Glenisla in the Southern Grampians area has 8 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Glenisla is Victoria Range Remote and Natural Area, covering about 14,183 hectares. Woohlpooer State Forest and Beear Reference Area are the next largest. Glenisla also has Henrys Campsite, Fergusons Campsite and Glenisla State Forest. Our data shows 4 public toilets and 7 off-leash dog parks in Glenisla. Picnic areas in Glenisla include Fergusons Campsite and Henrys Campsite. For walks and rides, The Fortress, Hut Creek Track and Manja Shelter Track cross Glenisla.

Getting around

Glenisla has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 66 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glenisla, so most trips start by car. By car from Glenisla, Horsham is about 50 minutes away and Hamilton about 53 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The nearest larger centre is Horsham, about 50 minutes from Glenisla by car, with 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Glenisla, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Cavendish Town Square Memorial Park in Cavendish (31 min).

Best for

Free days on The Fortress and in Glenisla's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Cherrypool and Bellfield also border Glenisla.
