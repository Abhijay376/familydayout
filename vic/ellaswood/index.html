Ellaswood is in the East Gippsland area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Ellaswood, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Ellaswood has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 5.4 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Ellaswood, so most trips start by car. By car from Ellaswood, Bairnsdale is about 10 minutes away and Lakes Entrance about 44 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale is about 9 minutes' drive from Ellaswood. The closest library to Ellaswood is Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Ellaswood is Boyfriend Beach in Forge Creek, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Ellaswood is the skate park at Gilsenan Reserve in Paynesville, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Ellaswood is Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Ellaswood is Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow, about 15 minutes' drive. Bairnsdale is the closest bigger centre to Ellaswood, about 10 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Ellaswood, the nearest places for kids by road are Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale (9 min), Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale (9 min), Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale (10 min), Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale (9 min), Krowathunkoolong Keeping Place in Bairnsdale (11 min) and All Abilities Playground in Bairnsdale (11 min).
