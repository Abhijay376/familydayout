Paradise is in the Northern Grampians area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Morrl Morrl Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Paradise, at about 1,982 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Paradise. The closest to Paradise is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 52 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the St Arnaud - Stawell via Ararat route stop in Paradise. By car from Paradise, Stawell is about 37 minutes away and Ararat about 60 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud is about 26 minutes' drive from Paradise. The closest skate park to Paradise is the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell, about 40 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Paradise is Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell, about 38 minutes' drive. Stawell is the closest bigger centre to Paradise, about 37 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Paradise are Navarre Playground in Navarre (7 min), Community Playground in Landsborough (20 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (26 min), Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell (36 min), Railway Station Art and Crafts Gallery in Stawell (38 min) and Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell (38 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Paradise.

Nearby suburbs

Navarre, next to Paradise, has 1 playground. Next door to Paradise, Marnoo has 1 playground. Tottington, Morrl Morrl, Kanya and Greens Creek also border Paradise.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Paradise, without traffic.
