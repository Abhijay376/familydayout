Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Beenak, in the Yarra Ranges area.

Landmarks

Kurth Kiln Regional Park is the largest mapped park in Beenak, at about 3,452 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Beenak. For walks and rides, Tomahawk Creek Track and Thornton Walking Track cross Beenak.

Getting around

Beenak has no train station; Nar Nar Goon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Beenak, so most trips start by car. By car from Beenak, Healesville is about 37 minutes away, Pakenham about 38 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 81 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Beenak is Seville Water Play Park in Seville, about 28 minutes' drive. For a library, Yarra Junction public library in Yarra Junction is about 23 minutes' drive from Beenak. For a skate park, Gembrook Skate Park in Gembrook is about 17 minutes' drive from Beenak. For a cinema, The Memo, Healesville in Healesville is about 37 minutes' drive from Beenak. The closest indoor play centre to Beenak is Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham, about 39 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Beenak is Lyre Bird enclosure in Badger Creek, about 34 minutes' drive. Healesville is the closest bigger centre to Beenak, about 37 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Beenak, the nearest places for kids by road are Flying Fox Platform in Gembrook (11 min), Low Ropes Course in Gembrook (12 min), Gembrook Skate Park in Gembrook (17 min), Cockatoo Skate park in Cockatoo (25 min), Yarra Junction public library in Yarra Junction (23 min) and Street Library in Millgrove (28 min).

Best for

Free days on the Tomahawk Creek Track and in Beenak's parks.
