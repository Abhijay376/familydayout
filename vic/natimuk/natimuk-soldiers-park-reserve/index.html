Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve

Park in Natimuk · Basic info

Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve is a park in Natimuk, in the Horsham Rural City Council area, good for all ages. Lake Ave/Main St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 594 m away.
