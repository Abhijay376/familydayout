Playground at Natimuk Showgrounds Recreation Reserve

Playground in Natimuk · Basic info

Playground at Natimuk Showgrounds Recreation Reserve is a public playground in Natimuk, in the Horsham Rural City area. The Arapiles Big Sky bike trail is about 390 m away, and Arapiles Climbing Museum is about 380 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 940 m from the playground, and bus Horsham - Naracoorte via Natimuk, Goroke, Edenhope stops at Lake Ave/Main St , a 9-minute walk away.
