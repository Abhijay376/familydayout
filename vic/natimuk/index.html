Our data lists 4 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in Natimuk, in the Horsham area. On top of that, Natimuk has 2 museums or galleries and a skate park.

Landmarks

Lake Natimuk and Natimuk Creek Lake Reserve is the largest mapped park in Natimuk, at about 395 hectares. Natimuk Showgrounds Recreation Reserve and Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve are the next largest. Playgrounds around Natimuk include the playground at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve, the playground at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve and the playground at Natimuk Showgrounds Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Natimuk. In Natimuk you'll also find Arapiles Climbing Museum and The Goat Gallery Natimuk. For older kids in Natimuk there's the skate park at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve. The Arapiles Big Sky bike trail passes through Natimuk.

Getting around

Natimuk has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 107 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Horsham - Naracoorte via Natimuk, Goroke, Edenhope route stop in Natimuk. By car from Natimuk, Horsham is about 23 minutes away and Stawell about 75 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Natimuk is Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest library to Natimuk is West Wimmera Shire Goroke Library in Goroke, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Natimuk is Centre Cinemas in Horsham, about 22 minutes' drive. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to Natimuk, about 23 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Natimuk's 4 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Natimuk. Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk. Free days on the Arapiles Big Sky bike trail and in Natimuk's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Natimuk, Vectis has 1 playground. Noradjuha, Mitre, Lower Norton and Duchembegarra also border Natimuk.
