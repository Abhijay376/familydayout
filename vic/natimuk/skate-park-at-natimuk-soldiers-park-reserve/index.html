Skate park at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve

Skate, BMX or pump track in Natimuk · Basic info

Skate park at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve is a skate, bmx or pump track in Natimuk, in the Horsham Rural City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Lake Ave/Main St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 365 m away.
