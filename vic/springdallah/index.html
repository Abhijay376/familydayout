Springdallah is in the Golden Plains area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Springdallah, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Springdallah has no train station; Wendouree Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Springdallah, so most trips start by car. By car from Springdallah, Ballarat is about 32 minutes away, Colac about 71 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood is about 21 minutes' drive from Springdallah. The closest library to Springdallah is Sebastopol Library in Sebastopol, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Springdallah is Smythesdale Skate Park in Smythesdale, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Springdallah is Showbiz Cinemas Ballarat in Smythes Creek, about 24 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 32 minutes' drive from Springdallah. For a zoo or wildlife park, Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East is about 37 minutes' drive from Springdallah. The nearest larger centre is Ballarat, about 32 minutes from Springdallah by car, with 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Springdallah are Cape Clear & District Lions Park Playground in Cape Clear (4 min), the playground at Berringa Recreation Reserve in Berringa (11 min), Smythesdale Skate Park in Smythesdale (13 min), the bmx track on Smythesdale - Snake Valley Road in Smythesdale (14 min), Haddon Tramway Preservation Association in Haddon (21 min) and Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood (21 min).
