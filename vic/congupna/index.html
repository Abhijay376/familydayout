Our data lists 2 playgrounds and 2 parks and reserves in Congupna, in the Greater Shepparton area.

Landmarks

Congupna H50 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Congupna, at about 8 hectares. Congupna H49 Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Playgrounds in Congupna include the playground at Farrell Park and the playground at Congupna Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet and 1 park or playground with a council BBQ close by in Congupna.

Getting around

Congupna has no train station; Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 9.4 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Congupna. By car from Congupna, Shepparton is about 12 minutes away, Benalla about 58 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest cinema to Congupna is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 12 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 12 minutes' drive from Congupna. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Congupna is Kidstown in Mooroopna, about 15 minutes' drive. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Congupna, about 12 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Congupna, the nearest places for kids by road are Shepparton Velodrome in Shepparton (9 min), BMX track in Shepparton (10 min), Shepparton Library in Shepparton (11 min), Village Cinemas in Shepparton (12 min), Shepparton Heritage Centre in Shepparton (12 min) and StarBowl in Shepparton (12 min).
