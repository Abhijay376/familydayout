Playground at Congupna Recreation Reserve

Playground in Congupna · Official details

Playground at Congupna Recreation Reserve in Congupna is a public playground in the Greater Shepparton City area. Playground at Farrell Park is another option about 270 m away. There's a public toilet about 70 m away, and coaches stop at Farrell Park/3591 Katamatite-Shepparton Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
