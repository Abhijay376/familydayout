Playground at Campbells Creek Recreation Reserve

Playground in Campbells Creek · Basic info

Playground at Campbells Creek Recreation Reserve in Campbells Creek is a public playground in the Mount Alexander Shire area. The Castlemaine-Maryborough Rail Trail (Under Construction) passes about 270 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 1 stops at Winkelmann Dr/Station St, a 6-minute walk away.
