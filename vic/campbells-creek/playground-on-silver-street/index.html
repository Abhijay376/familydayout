Playground on Silver Street

Playground in Campbells Creek · Basic info

This is a public playground in Campbells Creek. The Campbells Creek Trail is about 20 m away, and Playground on Cemetery Road is another option about 250 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 1 stops at Campbells Creek Bowling Club/Main Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
