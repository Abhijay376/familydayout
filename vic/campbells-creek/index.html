Campbells Creek in the Mount Alexander area has 3 playgrounds and 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Campbells Creek is Guildford I132 Bushland Reserve, covering about 28 hectares. Guildford I131 Bushland Reserve and Campbells Creek Recreation Reserve are the next largest. Campbells Creek also has Lindsay Park. Playgrounds in Campbells Creek include the playground at Campbells Creek Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Campbells Creek. The Goldfields Track, Castlemaine-Maryborough Rail Trail (Under Construction) and Campbells Creek Trail all pass through Campbells Creek.

Getting around

Campbells Creek has no train station; Castlemaine Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 2.8 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 1 stops in Campbells Creek. By car from Campbells Creek, Castlemaine is about 6 minutes away, Daylesford about 30 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Castlemaine Swimming Pool in Castlemaine is about 8 minutes' drive from Campbells Creek. For a library, Castlemaine Library in Castlemaine is about 5 minutes' drive from Campbells Creek. The closest skate park to Campbells Creek is Bike Jumps in Castlemaine, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Campbells Creek is Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Campbells Creek is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 35 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 43 minutes' drive from Campbells Creek. Castlemaine is the closest bigger centre to Campbells Creek, about 6 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library.

Best for

Younger kids at Campbells Creek's 3 local playgrounds. Free days on the Goldfields Track and in Campbells Creek's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Castlemaine, next to Campbells Creek, has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Chewton, next to Campbells Creek, has a pool. Next door to Campbells Creek, Vaughan has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Campbells Creek, Muckleford has 1 playground. Fryerstown, Yapeen and McKenzie Hill also border Campbells Creek.
