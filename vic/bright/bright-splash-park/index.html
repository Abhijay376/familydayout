Bright Splash Park

Pool or water play in Bright · Basic info

Bright Splash Park is a pool or water play in Bright, in the Alpine Shire Council area, good for all ages. Alpine Hotel/Anderson St (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 145 m away with a baby change table.
