Playground on Cobden Street

Playground in Bright · Basic info

Playground on Cobden Street is a public playground in Bright, in the Alpine Shire area. The Mt Porepunkah Loop is about 60 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 360 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 840 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 3 minutes from Bright.
