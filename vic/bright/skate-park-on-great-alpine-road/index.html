Skate park on Great Alpine Road

Skate, BMX or pump track in Bright · Basic info

Skate park on Great Alpine Road is a skate, bmx or pump track in Bright, in the Alpine Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Alpine Hotel/Anderson St (bus) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 392 m away with a baby change table.
