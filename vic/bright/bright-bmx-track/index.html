Bright BMX Track

Skate, BMX or pump track in Bright · Basic info

Bright BMX Track is a skate, bmx or pump track in Bright, in the Alpine Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Alpine Hotel/Anderson St (bus) is a 20-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 452 m away.
