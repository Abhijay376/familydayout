Playground at Centenary Park

Playground in Bright · Basic info

This is a public playground in Bright. The Mt Porepunkah Loop is about 70 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 210 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 160 m from the playground, and coaches stop at Alpine Hotel/Anderson St, a 5-minute walk away.
