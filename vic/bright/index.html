Our data lists 2 playgrounds and 15 parks and reserves in Bright, in the Alpine area. On top of that, Bright has a library, 3 pools, 2 museums or galleries and a cinema.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bright is Ovens River J15 Streamside Reserve, covering about 88 hectares. Wandiligong Education Area and Ovens River J20 Streamside Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Bright include Jacks, Jenveys and Apex Park. Playgrounds in Bright include the playground at Centenary Park. Our data shows 6 public toilets in Bright. Picnic areas in Bright include Bakers Gully Reservoirs, Jacks and Jenveys. In Bright you'll also find Bright Art Gallery, Bright Museum, Sun Cinema Bright, Bright River Pool and Bright Splash Park. For older kids in Bright there's Bright BMX Track. For walks and rides, Mt Porepunkah Loop, Bright - Mount Beauty and Great Valley Trail cross Bright. Our data lists 13 cafes and family restaurants in Bright for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bright. The closest to Bright is Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line), about 69 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Wangaratta-Bright and Omeo - Bright via Hotham Heights routes stop in Bright. V/Line coaches stop in Bright. By car from Bright, Wangaratta is about 69 minutes away and Wodonga about 83 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a beach, Pebble Beach in Tawonga South is about 28 minutes' drive from Bright. From Bright, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Riverside Park in Porepunkah (8 min), Pebble Beach in Tawonga South (28 min), Mount Beauty BMX Bike Track in Mount Beauty (31 min) and Mt Beauty Library in Mount Beauty (31 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the library, cinema and museum or gallery in Bright. Teens who skate or ride, with Bright BMX Track in Bright. Free days on the Mt Porepunkah Loop and in Bright's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Bright, Porepunkah has 1 playground. Germantown, Freeburgh, Coral Bank and Buckland also border Bright.
