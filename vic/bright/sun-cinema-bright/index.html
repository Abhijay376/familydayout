Sun Cinema Bright

Cinema in Bright · Official details

Sun Cinema Bright is a cinema in Bright, in the Alpine Shire Council area, good for school-age kids and teens. Alpine Hotel/Anderson St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 140 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: Ticket prices (Wed- Sun, Public Holidays and St. Valentine’s Day, excluding after 5pm Saturday); Please note that all films are designated NFT (No Free Tickets) after 5pm Saturday,; Thursday – Sunday (not available after 5pm Saturday); Monday – Wednesday (not available after 5pm Tuesday).
