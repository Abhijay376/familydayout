Dowling Street Playground

Playground in Kealba · Basic info

Dowling Street Playground is a public playground in Kealba, in the Brimbank City area. The Maribyrnong River Trail passes about 310 m away, and Playground at Driscolls Road Reserve is another option about 300 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.1 km away, and bus 421 stops at Dowling St/Driscolls Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
