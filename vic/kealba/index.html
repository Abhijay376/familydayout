Kealba in the Brimbank area has 5 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Bellara Crescent Reserve is the largest mapped park in Kealba, at about 2 hectares. Harefield Crescent Reserve is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Kealba include Driscolls Road Reserve. Playgrounds in Kealba include Dowling Street Playground, the playground at Driscolls Road Reserve and the playground at Bundeena Avenue Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets and 2 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Kealba. The M80 Trail and Maribyrnong River Trail all pass through Kealba. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Kealba for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kealba. The closest to Kealba is St Albans Railway Station (St Albans) (metro), about 2.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 419, 421 and 941 stop in Kealba. By car from Kealba, Sunshine is about 9 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 15 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 25 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, 50m Pool in Keilor Downs is about 2 minutes' drive from Kealba. The closest library to Kealba is Keilor Library in Keilor, about 3 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Kealba is Altona Beach in Altona, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Kealba is the skate park at Packard Street Reserve in Keilor Downs, about 3 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Sunshine is about 10 minutes' drive from Kealba. For an indoor play centre, Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes is about 9 minutes' drive from Kealba. For a zoo or wildlife park, Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville is about 20 minutes' drive from Kealba.

Best for

Younger kids at Kealba's 5 local playgrounds. Free days on the M80 Trail and in Kealba's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Kealba, St Albans has 33 playgrounds and a library. Keilor East, next to Kealba, has 26 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Keilor Downs, next to Kealba, has 16 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Keilor, next to Kealba, has 11 playgrounds and a library. Sunshine North also borders Kealba.
