Carwarp is in the Mildura area, and our data lists 12 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Nurnurnemal Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Carwarp, at about 251 hectares. Nowingi Bushland Reserve and Carwarp Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Carwarp include Boonoonar Bushland Reserve, Ginquam I4 Bushland Reserve and Ginquam I5 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Carwarp. The closest to Carwarp is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 159 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Carwarp, so most trips start by car. By car from Carwarp, Mildura is about 29 minutes away and Swan Hill about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Carwarp is Irymple Swimming Pool in Irymple, about 22 minutes' drive. For a library, A S Kenyon Library Red Cliffs in Red Cliffs is about 14 minutes' drive from Carwarp. The closest beach to Carwarp is Rudd's Road Sandbar in Iraak, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Carwarp is Skatepark in Red Cliffs, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Carwarp is Deakin Cinema Complex in Mildura, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Carwarp is Sunraysia Tenpin in Mildura, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Carwarp is Putt Putt Family Fun Centre in Mildura, about 30 minutes' drive. Mildura is the closest bigger centre to Carwarp, about 29 minutes away by car, and has 26 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Carwarp, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Barclay Square in Red Cliffs (14 min), Woodbine Park Playground in Red Cliffs (14 min), Red Cliffs Military Museum in Red Cliffs (14 min), A S Kenyon Library Red Cliffs in Red Cliffs (14 min), Work Space Gallery 3496 in Red Cliffs (14 min) and Skatepark in Red Cliffs (15 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Carwarp.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Carwarp, Red Cliffs has 6 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Iraak, next to Carwarp, has a beach. Merrinee, Koorlong, Hattah and Colignan also border Carwarp.
