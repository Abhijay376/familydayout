Landmarks

The biggest green space in Glenlogie is Albion Sawmill Historic and Cultural Features Reserve, covering about 1 hectares.

Getting around

Glenlogie has no train station; Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glenlogie, so most trips start by car. By car from Glenlogie, Ararat is about 38 minutes away, Maryborough about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Glenlogie is Beaufort Pool in Beaufort, about 39 minutes' drive. The closest library to Glenlogie is Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Glenlogie is the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort, about 39 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Glenlogie is Astor Cinema in Ararat, about 38 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Ararat, about 38 minutes from Glenlogie by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Glenlogie, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Avoca Lions Park in Avoca (28 min), Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort (38 min), the playground at Wotherspoon Reserve in Beaufort (38 min), Courthouse in Avoca (29 min), the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (39 min) and Beaufort Pool in Beaufort (39 min).
