L.W. Williams Reserve

Park in Preston · Basic info

L.W. Williams Reserve is a park in Preston, in the Darebin City Council area, good for all ages. Spring St/Regent St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 807 m away.
