Playground at Asling Reserve

Playground in Preston · Basic info

Playground at Asling Reserve in Preston is a public playground in the Darebin City area. The Great Western Shimmy is about 170 m away, and for food, Luigi Boyz is about 160 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 620 m from the playground, and bus 527 stops at Reidford Ave/Murray Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
