Playground at Greenbelt Reserve

Playground in Preston · Basic info

Playground at Greenbelt Reserve in Preston is a public playground in the Darebin City area. The Local Access Route 3 passes about 100 m away, and Jamaica Blue is about 430 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 730 m away, and bus 567 stops at Seston St/Tyler St, a 2-minute walk away.
