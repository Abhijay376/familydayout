The Keys

Indoor play in Preston · Official details

The Keys is a indoor play in Preston, in the Darebin City Council area, best for younger kids (check the venue's age and height rules). Osborne Gr/Plenty Rd #44 (tram) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 535 m away.
