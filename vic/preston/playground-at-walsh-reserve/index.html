Playground at Walsh Reserve

Playground in Preston · Basic info

Playground at Walsh Reserve in Preston is a public playground in the Darebin City area. The Great Eastern Cycleway passes about 110 m away, and Pizza and Kebabs on Bell is about 360 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 280 m away, and bus 251 stops at Mornane St/Victoria St, a 2-minute walk away.
