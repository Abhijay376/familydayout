A H Capp Reserve

Park in Preston · Basic info

A H Capp Reserve is a park in Preston, in the Darebin City Council area, good for all ages. Harding St/Nicholson St #133 (tram) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 310 m away.
