Playground on Herbert Street

Playground in Preston · Basic info

Playground on Herbert Street is a public playground in Preston, in the Darebin City area. The St Georges Road Trail is about 170 m away, and C is about 250 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 340 m from the playground, and bus 903 stops at Melbourne Polytechnic/St Georges Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
