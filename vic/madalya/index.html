Madalya is in the Wellington area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Madalya, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Madalya has no train station; Morwell Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Madalya, so most trips start by car. By car from Madalya, Traralgon is about 79 minutes away and Sale about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Madalya, the nearest places for kids by road are Yarram Historical Society Museum in Yarram (46 min), the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram (46 min), Yarram Public Pool in Yarram (47 min), Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram (46 min), the playground at Memorial Park in Yarram (47 min) and Boolarra Skate Park in Boolarra (65 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Madalya.

Nearby suburbs

Grand Ridge, Devon North and Balook also border Madalya.

Getting there
