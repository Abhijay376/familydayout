Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Stonyford, in the Corangamite area.

Landmarks

Bungador Stoney Rises Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Stonyford, at about 16 hectares.

Getting around

Stonyford has no train station; Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Stonyford, so most trips start by car. By car from Stonyford, Colac is about 19 minutes away, Warrnambool about 68 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Stonyford is Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac, about 22 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown is about 19 minutes' drive from Stonyford. For a cinema, Colac Cinemas in Colac is about 20 minutes' drive from Stonyford. For an indoor play centre, Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac is about 20 minutes' drive from Stonyford. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Stonyford, about 19 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Stonyford, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (18 min), the playground at Western Bay Playground in Colac (19 min), Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (19 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (20 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (20 min) and Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (22 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Stonyford.

Nearby suburbs

Pirron Yallock and Carpendeit also border Stonyford.
