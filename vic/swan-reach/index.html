Swan Reach is in the East Gippsland area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Bumberrah Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Swan Reach, at about 2 hectares. Johnsonville Boat Ramp is the next largest. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Swan Reach.

Getting around

Swan Reach has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 12 stops in Swan Reach. V/Line coaches stop in Swan Reach. By car from Swan Reach, Lakes Entrance is about 17 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 23 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Lakes Entrance Aquatic Centre in Lakes Entrance is about 18 minutes' drive from Swan Reach. The closest library to Swan Reach is Lakes Entrance Library in Lakes Entrance, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Swan Reach is 90 Mile Beach in Boole Poole, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Swan Reach is the skate park at Gilsenan Reserve in Paynesville, about 34 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale is about 21 minutes' drive from Swan Reach. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Swan Reach is Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow, about 14 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Lakes Entrance, about 17 minutes from Swan Reach by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Swan Reach are 90 Mile Beach in Boole Poole (9 min), the playground at Nyerimilang Heritage Park in Nyerimilang (17 min), Griffiths Sea Shell Museum in Lakes Entrance (12 min), Lakes Entrance Library in Lakes Entrance (14 min), Ninety Mile Beach in Lakes Entrance (16 min) and Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow (14 min).
