Halls Gap Fitness Park

Park in Halls Gap · Basic info

Halls Gap Fitness Park is a park in Halls Gap, in the Northern Grampians Shire Council area, good for all ages. Lakeside Caravan Park/Tymna Dr (coach) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 569 m away.
