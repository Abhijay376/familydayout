Halls Gap Picnic Reserve

Park in Halls Gap · Official details

Halls Gap Picnic Reserve is a park in Halls Gap, in the Northern Grampians Shire Council area, good for all ages. Information Centre/Grampians Rd (coach) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 82 m away with a baby change table.

Good to know

Questions parents ask

Where is Halls Gap Picnic Reserve?
Halls Gap Picnic Reserve is in Halls Gap, Victoria.
Is Halls Gap Picnic Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Halls Gap Picnic Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 82 m away, with a baby change table.
How do I get to Halls Gap Picnic Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Information Centre/Grampians Rd (routes Halls Gap - Melbourne via Stawell & Ballarat), a 2-minute walk.
