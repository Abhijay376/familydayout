Brambuk - The National Park and Cultural Centre Park

Park in Halls Gap · Official details

Brambuk - The National Park and Cultural Centre Park is a park in Halls Gap, in the Northern Grampians Shire Council area, good for all ages. Brambuk the National Park and Cultural Centre/Grampians Rd (coach) is a 3-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 208 m away and dogs are not allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Brambuk - The National Park and Cultural Centre Park?
Brambuk - The National Park and Cultural Centre Park is in Halls Gap, Victoria.
Is Brambuk - The National Park and Cultural Centre Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Brambuk - The National Park and Cultural Centre Park?
No, dogs are not allowed.
Are there toilets at Brambuk - The National Park and Cultural Centre Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 208 m away.
How do I get to Brambuk - The National Park and Cultural Centre Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Brambuk the National Park and Cultural Centre/Grampians Rd (routes Halls Gap - Melbourne via Stawell & Ballarat), a 3-minute walk.
