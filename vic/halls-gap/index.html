Halls Gap in the Northern Grampians area has 3 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in our data. Halls Gap also has a pool, 2 museums or galleries and a zoo or wildlife park.

Landmarks

Brambuk - The National Park and Cultural Centre Park is the largest mapped park in Halls Gap, at about 2 hectares. Halls Gap Picnic Reserve is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Halls Gap include Halls Gap Fitness Park. Playgrounds in Halls Gap include the playground at Halls Gap Picnic Reserve. Our data shows 12 public toilets and 2 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Halls Gap. In Halls Gap you'll also find Halls Gap Zoo, Brambuk Cultural Centre, MOCO Gallery and Halls Gap Swimming Pool. The Grampians Peaks Trail, Boronia Trail and Halls Gap Bike Path (Ararat Rd to Brambuk) all pass through Halls Gap. Our data lists 5 cafes and family restaurants in Halls Gap for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Halls Gap. The closest to Halls Gap is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 40 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Halls Gap. By car from Halls Gap, Stawell is about 25 minutes away and Ararat about 42 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Ararat Library in Ararat is about 41 minutes' drive from Halls Gap. The closest beach to Halls Gap is Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Halls Gap is the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell, about 25 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Astor Cinema in Ararat is about 41 minutes' drive from Halls Gap. The nearest larger centre is Stawell, about 25 minutes from Halls Gap by car, with 6 playgrounds and a pool.

Best for

Younger kids at Halls Gap's 3 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Halls Gap. Free days on the Grampians Peaks Trail and in Halls Gap's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Halls Gap, Lake Fyans has a beach. Fyans Creek and Bellfield also border Halls Gap.
