Landmarks

Calivil Recreation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Calivil, at about 10 hectares.

Getting around

Calivil has no train station; Dingee Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Calivil, so most trips start by car. By car from Calivil, Bendigo is about 60 minutes away, Echuca about 61 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Calivil is Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo, about 18 minutes' drive. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Calivil, about 60 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Calivil, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at R J Phelan Reserve in Mitiamo (18 min), Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo (18 min), East Loddon Historical Society in Mitiamo (18 min), Pyramid Hill & Districts Historical Museum in Pyramid Hill (24 min), Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood (34 min) and A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park in Boort (40 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Calivil.

Nearby suburbs

Mitiamo, next to Calivil, has 1 playground and a pool. Yarrawalla, next to Calivil, has 1 playground. Mologa, Jarklin and Bears Lagoon also border Calivil.
