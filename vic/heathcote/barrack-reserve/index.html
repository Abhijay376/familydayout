Barrack Reserve

Park in Heathcote · Basic info

Barrack Reserve is a park in Heathcote, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, good for all ages. Heathcote Lions Park/Northern Hwy (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 460 m away with a baby change table.
