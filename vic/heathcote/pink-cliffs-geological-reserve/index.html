Pink Cliffs Geological Reserve

Park in Heathcote · Basic info

Pink Cliffs Geological Reserve is a park in Heathcote, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, good for all ages. Jennings St/Northern Hwy (coach) is a 15-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1268 m away with a baby change table.
