Heathcote Play Space

Playground in Heathcote · Basic info

Heathcote Play Space is a public playground in Heathcote, in the Greater Bendigo City area. The McIvor Creek Trail is about 200 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 280 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 290 m from the playground, and bus Heathcote - Bendigo via Junortoun, Axedale, Knowsley stops at Heathcote Lions Park/Northern Hwy, a 1-minute walk away.
