Swan Marsh is in the Colac Otway area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Swan Marsh, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Swan Marsh.

Getting around

Swan Marsh has no train station; Colac Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Swan Marsh, so most trips start by car. By car from Swan Marsh, Colac is about 21 minutes away, Warrnambool about 77 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac is about 24 minutes' drive from Swan Marsh. For a skate park, the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt is about 26 minutes' drive from Swan Marsh. For a cinema, Colac Cinemas in Colac is about 22 minutes' drive from Swan Marsh. For an indoor play centre, Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac is about 22 minutes' drive from Swan Marsh. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 21 minutes from Swan Marsh by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Swan Marsh are the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (20 min), the playground at Western Bay Playground in Colac (20 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (22 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (22 min), Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (24 min) and the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (26 min).
