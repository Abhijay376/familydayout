Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Vervale in the Cardinia area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Vervale. The closest to Vervale is Garfield Railway Station (V/Line), about 4.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Vervale, so most trips start by car. By car from Vervale, Pakenham is about 22 minutes away, Warragul about 29 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 66 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Vervale is Garfield Swimming Pool in Garfield, about 9 minutes' drive. For a library, Drouin Library in Drouin is about 21 minutes' drive from Vervale. For a skate park, Skate BMX Park in Garfield is about 8 minutes' drive from Vervale. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 27 minutes' drive from Vervale. The closest indoor play centre to Vervale is Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham, about 22 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Wildlife Trail in Tynong North is about 13 minutes' drive from Vervale. Pakenham is the closest bigger centre to Vervale, about 22 minutes away by car, and has 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Vervale are Skate BMX Park in Garfield (8 min), Garfield Swimming Pool in Garfield (9 min), the playground at Koolangarra Park in Bunyip (6 min), the playground at Greenland Court Reserve in Garfield (10 min), the bmx track at Greenland Court Reserve in Garfield (10 min) and Wildlife Trail in Tynong North (13 min).
