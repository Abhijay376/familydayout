Playground on Raymond McMahon Boulevard

Playground in Endeavour Hills · Basic info

Playground on Raymond McMahon Boulevard is a public playground in Endeavour Hills, in the Casey City area. Older kids have a skate park about 40 m away, and Endeavour Hills Library is about 60 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 250 m from the playground, and bus 861 stops at Raymond McMahon Medical Centre/Raymond McMahon Bvd, a 3-minute walk away.
