Endeavour Hills in the Casey area has 26 playgrounds and 21 parks and reserves in our data. Endeavour Hills also has a library and 2 skate parks.

Landmarks

Dandenong Police Paddocks Reserve is the largest mapped park in Endeavour Hills, at about 424 hectares. Sydney Pargeter Recreation Area and Joan Phillips Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Endeavour Hills include Reema Reserve, Horse Agistment and Clifton Way Park. Playgrounds around Endeavour Hills include the playground at Reema Reserve, the playground at Horse Agistment and the playground at Clifton Way Park. Our data shows 7 public toilets and 1 park or playground with a council BBQ close by in Endeavour Hills. In Endeavour Hills you'll also find Endeavour Hills Library. For older kids in Endeavour Hills there's BMX Track and Endeavour Hills Skatepark. Endeavour Hills Shopping Centre and The Terrace Shopping Centre are the main shopping centres. The Hallam Bypass Trail, Woodland Walk and Heatherton Road Trail all pass through Endeavour Hills. Our data lists 12 cafes and family restaurants in Endeavour Hills for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Endeavour Hills. The closest to Endeavour Hills is Hallam Railway Station (metro), about 4.8 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 842, 843, 845, 861, 863 and 982 stop in Endeavour Hills. By car from Endeavour Hills, Dandenong is about 8 minutes away, Cranbourne about 18 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 30 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Doveton Pool in the Park in Doveton is about 6 minutes' drive from Endeavour Hills. The closest beach to Endeavour Hills is Edithvale Beach in Edithvale, about 22 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 26 playgrounds across Endeavour Hills. Rainy days at the library in Endeavour Hills. Teens who skate or ride, with BMX Track in Endeavour Hills. Free days on the Hallam Bypass Trail and in Endeavour Hills's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Endeavour Hills, Rowville has 26 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Endeavour Hills, Dandenong North has 24 playgrounds. Hallam, next to Endeavour Hills, has 12 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Endeavour Hills, Doveton has 5 playgrounds and a pool. Narre Warren North, Eumemmerring and Lysterfield South also border Endeavour Hills.
