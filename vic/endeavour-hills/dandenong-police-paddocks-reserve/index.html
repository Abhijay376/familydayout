Dandenong Police Paddocks Reserve

Park in Endeavour Hills · Official details

Dandenong Police Paddocks Reserve is a park in Endeavour Hills, in the Casey City Council area, good for all ages. Power Rd/Kennington Park Dr (bus) is a 21-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 830 m away and dogs are allowed on a lead.

Questions parents ask

Where is Dandenong Police Paddocks Reserve?
Dandenong Police Paddocks Reserve is in Endeavour Hills, Victoria, at Brady Road (via Nerre Nerre Warren picnic area).
Is Dandenong Police Paddocks Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Dandenong Police Paddocks Reserve?
Yes, on a lead.
Are there toilets at Dandenong Police Paddocks Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 830 m away.
How do I get to Dandenong Police Paddocks Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Power Rd/Kennington Park Dr (routes 845), a 21-minute walk.
