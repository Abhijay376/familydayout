James Alexander Reserve Playground

Playground in Endeavour Hills · Basic info

James Alexander Reserve Playground in Endeavour Hills is a public playground in the Casey City area. Playground at Sydney Parkinson Reserve is another option about 510 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 790 m from the playground, and buses 845 and 982 stop at Hunter Rise/Matthew Flinders Ave, a 5-minute walk away.
