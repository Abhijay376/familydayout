Playground at Rod Lavin Reserve

Playground in Endeavour Hills · Basic info

Playground at Rod Lavin Reserve in Endeavour Hills is a public playground in the Casey City area. The Hallam Bypass Trail passes about 310 m away, and Gunns Road Reserve Playground is another option about 580 m away. There's a public toilet about 780 m away, and bus 861 stops at Preston Ave/Robert Molyneux Ave, a 5-minute walk away.
