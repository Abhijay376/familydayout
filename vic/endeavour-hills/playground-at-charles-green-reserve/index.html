Playground at Charles Green Reserve

Playground in Endeavour Hills · Basic info

Playground at Charles Green Reserve is a public playground in Endeavour Hills, in the Casey City area. Playground on Kidds Road is another option about 370 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 844 stops at Sheoak St/Kidds Rd, a 9-minute walk away.
