Playground at Aldridge Street Reserve

Playground in Endeavour Hills · Basic info

Playground at Aldridge Street Reserve is a public playground in Endeavour Hills, in the Casey City area. The Heatherton Road Trail passes about 220 m away, and Playground on Cormorant Close is another option about 270 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and buses 845 and 982 stop at Aldridge St/Power Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
