Playground at Police Paddocks (Softball)

Playground in Endeavour Hills · Official details

Playground at Police Paddocks (Softball) in Endeavour Hills is a public playground in the Greater Dandenong City area. The Woodland Walk is about 370 m away, and Playground at Dandenong Police Paddocks Reserve is another option about 360 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 60 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 9 minutes from Endeavour Hills.
