Myrniong is in the Moorabool area, and our data lists 3 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Myrniong is Lerderderg River Heritage River, covering about 5,167 hectares. Ah Kows Gully Reference Area and Myrniong Recreation Reserve are the next largest. Playgrounds around Myrniong include the playground at Pykes Creek Reserve and the playground at Myrniong Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Myrniong. The Lerderderg Gorge Track, Long Point Track and Lower Chadwick Track all pass through Myrniong.

Getting around

Myrniong has no train station; Ballan Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 10 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Myrniong, so most trips start by car. By car from Myrniong, Bacchus Marsh is about 12 minutes away, Melton about 21 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 56 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Myrniong is Ballan Swimming Pool in Ballan, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest library to Myrniong is Lerderderg Library in Bacchus Marsh, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Myrniong is Lionhead Beach in Pentland Hills, about 7 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan is about 11 minutes' drive from Myrniong. For a cinema, Reading Cinemas in Melton West is about 19 minutes' drive from Myrniong. For an indoor play centre, Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes is about 39 minutes' drive from Myrniong. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek is about 28 minutes' drive from Myrniong. The nearest larger centre is Bacchus Marsh, about 12 minutes from Myrniong by car, with 6 playgrounds, a pool and a library.

Best for

Younger kids at Myrniong's 3 local playgrounds. Free days on the Lerderderg Gorge Track and in Myrniong's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Myrniong, Darley has 13 playgrounds and a pool. Ballan, next to Myrniong, has 4 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Pentland Hills, next to Myrniong, has a beach. Greendale, next to Myrniong, has 1 playground. Lerderderg, Korobeit and Ingliston also border Myrniong.
