Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Willaura North in the Ararat area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Willaura North has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Willaura North, so most trips start by car. By car from Willaura North, Ararat is about 20 minutes away, Stawell about 42 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Willaura Outdoor Pool in Willaura is about 16 minutes' drive from Willaura North. The closest library to Willaura North is Ararat Library in Ararat, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Willaura North is Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Willaura North is David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat, about 25 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Astor Cinema in Ararat is about 20 minutes' drive from Willaura North. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Willaura North is Halls Gap Zoo in Halls Gap, about 29 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Ararat, about 20 minutes from Willaura North by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Willaura North, the nearest places for kids by road are Willaura Outdoor Pool in Willaura (16 min), Ararat Gallery TAMA in Ararat (20 min), Ararat Library in Ararat (20 min), Astor Cinema in Ararat (20 min), Langi Morgala Museum in Ararat (20 min) and Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat (20 min).
