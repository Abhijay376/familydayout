Allambee Reserve is in the Baw Baw area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Allambee Reserve, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Allambee Reserve has no train station; Yarragon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 10 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Allambee Reserve, so most trips start by car. By car from Allambee Reserve, Warragul is about 36 minutes away, Leongatha about 59 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar is about 30 minutes' drive from Allambee Reserve. The closest library to Allambee Reserve is Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North, about 44 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Allambee Reserve is Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North in Mirboo North, about 44 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 35 minutes' drive from Allambee Reserve. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 35 minutes' drive from Allambee Reserve. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Allambee Reserve, about 36 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Allambee Reserve, the nearest places for kids by road are Yarragon Childrens Playground Reserve in Yarragon (24 min), Station Gallery in Yarragon (25 min), Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar (30 min), Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North (44 min), Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North in Mirboo North (44 min) and the playground at Baromi Park in Mirboo North (44 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Allambee Reserve.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Allambee Reserve, Mirboo North has 1 playground, a library and a skate park. Hallston, Childers, Allambee South and Allambee also border Allambee Reserve.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Allambee Reserve, without traffic.
