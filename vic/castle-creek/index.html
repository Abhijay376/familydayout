Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Castle Creek in the Wodonga area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Castle Creek. The closest to Castle Creek is Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line), about 9.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Castle Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Castle Creek, Wodonga is about 22 minutes away and Wangaratta about 74 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, WAVES in Wodonga is about 21 minutes' drive from Castle Creek. The closest library to Castle Creek is Upper Murray Regional Library - Wodonga in Wodonga, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Castle Creek is Finns Beach in Bandiana, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Castle Creek is James Scott Memorial Skate Park in Wodonga, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Castle Creek is Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana, about 23 minutes' drive. Wodonga is the closest bigger centre to Castle Creek, about 22 minutes away by car, and has 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Castle Creek are the playground at Ewart Brothers Reserve in Wodonga (17 min), the playground at Daniel Bayne Park in Wodonga (18 min), WAVES in Wodonga (21 min), James Scott Memorial Skate Park in Wodonga (21 min), Art Space Wodonga in Wodonga (21 min) and Upper Murray Regional Library - Wodonga in Wodonga (22 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Castle Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Wodonga, next to Castle Creek, has 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Castle Creek, Leneva has 3 playgrounds. Next door to Castle Creek, Huon Creek has 1 playground.
