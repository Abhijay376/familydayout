Elaine is in the Moorabool area, and our data lists 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Elaine is Elaine Recreational Reserve, covering about 3 hectares. Mount Doran Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Elaine also has Hunts Bridge Picnic And Camping Area. Playgrounds around Elaine include the playground at Elaine Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Elaine. Picnic areas in Elaine include Hunts Bridge Picnic And Camping Area.

Getting around

Elaine has no train station; Ballan Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Elaine. By car from Elaine, Ballarat is about 31 minutes away, Geelong about 51 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.
