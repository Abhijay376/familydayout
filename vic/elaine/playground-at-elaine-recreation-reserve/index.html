Playground at Elaine Recreation Reserve

Playground in Elaine · Basic info

Playground at Elaine Recreation Reserve is a small local playground in Elaine, in the Moorabool Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and coaches stop at General Store/Midland Hwy, a 9-minute walk away.
