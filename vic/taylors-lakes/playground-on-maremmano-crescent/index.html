Playground on Maremmano Crescent

Playground in Taylors Lakes · Basic info

Playground on Maremmano Crescent in Taylors Lakes is a public playground in the Brimbank City area. Red Rooster is about 360 m away if you need food, and Playground at Transmission Line Linear Reserve is another option about 400 m away. There's a public toilet about 570 m away, and bus 421 stops at Wentworth Dr/McCubbin Dr, a 2-minute walk away.
