Playground at Taylors Lakes Linear Park

Playground in Taylors Lakes · Official details

Playground at Taylors Lakes Linear Park is a public playground in Taylors Lakes, in the Brimbank City area. The Taylors Creek Trail is about 10 m away, and Kore Wellness & Swim Centre is about 430 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and buses 419 and 476 stop at Taylors Lakes Sec Col/Parmelia Dr, a 6-minute walk away.
