Junglemania

Playground in Taylors Lakes · Basic info

Junglemania in Taylors Lakes is a public playground in the Brimbank City area. The Taylors Creek Trail passes about 250 m away, and Kore Wellness & Swim Centre is about 40 m away. There's a public toilet about 350 m away, and buses 421 and 941 stop at Verona Dr/Melton Hwy, a 4-minute walk away.
