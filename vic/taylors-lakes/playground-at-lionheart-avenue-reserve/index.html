Playground at Lionheart Avenue Reserve

Playground in Taylors Lakes · Official details

Playground at Lionheart Avenue Reserve in Taylors Lakes is a public playground in the Brimbank City area. Playground at Solent Crescent Reserve is another option about 460 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 80 m from the playground, and bus 421 stops at Cocoparra Cres/Chichester Dr, a 4-minute walk away.
