Playground at Kingston Park

Playground in Ocean Grove · Basic info

Playground at Kingston Park is a public playground in Ocean Grove, in the Greater Geelong City area. Bellarine Aquatic Centre is about 450 m away, and The Mex is about 310 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 56 stops at Shell Rd/Grubb Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
