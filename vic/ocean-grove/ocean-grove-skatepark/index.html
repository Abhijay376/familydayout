Ocean Grove Skatepark

Playground in Ocean Grove · Basic info

Ocean Grove Skatepark is a public playground in Ocean Grove, in the Greater Geelong City area. Bellarine Aquatic Centre is about 110 m away, and The Mex is about 340 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 240 m away, and bus 56 stops at Bellarine Secondary College Ocean Grove Campus/Shell Rd , a 4-minute walk away.
