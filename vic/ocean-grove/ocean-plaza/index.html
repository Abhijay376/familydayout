Ocean Plaza

Shopping centre in Ocean Grove · Basic info

Ocean Plaza is a shopping centre in Ocean Grove, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Ocean Grove Shops/The Terrace (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 62 m away with a baby change table.
