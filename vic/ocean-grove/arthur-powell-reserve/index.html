Arthur Powell Reserve

Park in Ocean Grove · Basic info

Arthur Powell Reserve is a park in Ocean Grove, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. The Avenue/Eggleston St (bus) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 92 m away.
