Lookout Reserve playground

Playground in Ocean Grove · Basic info

Lookout Reserve playground is a public playground in Ocean Grove, in the Greater Geelong City area. Ocean Grove Library is about 470 m away, and for food, Chang Noi Thai is about 280 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 330 m from the playground, and bus 55 stops at Ocean Grove Shops/The Terrace, a 6-minute walk away.
