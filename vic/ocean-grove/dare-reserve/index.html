Dare Reserve

Playground in Ocean Grove · Basic info

This is a public playground in Ocean Grove. Ocean Grove Library is about 370 m away, and for food, Ming Terrace is about 270 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 490 m from the playground, and bus 55 stops at Lelean St/Wallington Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
