Cobains in the Wellington area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Lake Kakydra Gippsland Lakes Reserve is the largest mapped park in Cobains, at about 175 hectares. Lake Melanydra Gippsland Lakes Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Cobains has no train station; Sale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 8.0 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cobains, so most trips start by car. By car from Cobains, Sale is about 17 minutes away and Traralgon about 57 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cobains is AquaEnergy in Sale, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest library to Cobains is Sale Library in Sale, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Cobains is Sale Skate Park in Sale, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Cobains is Former Drive-in site in Sale, about 14 minutes' drive. Sale is the closest bigger centre to Cobains, about 17 minutes away by car, and has 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Cobains are Ibis Avenue - Pelican Court Playground in Sale (14 min), Lions Park Playground in Sale (14 min), Former Drive-in site in Sale (14 min), AquaEnergy in Sale (16 min), Indoor pool in Sale (17 min) and Roulette Cinema in East Sale (16 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Cobains.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Cobains, Sale has 14 playgrounds, a pool and a library. East Sale, next to Cobains, has a cinema. Montgomery, Clydebank and Airly also border Cobains.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Cobains, without traffic.
