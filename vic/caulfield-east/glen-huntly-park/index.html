Glen Huntly Park

Park in Caulfield East · Official details

Glen Huntly Park is a park in Caulfield East, in the Glen Eira City Council area, good for all ages. Roseberry Gr/Neerim Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 66 m away and dogs are not allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Glen Huntly Park?
Glen Huntly Park is in Caulfield East, Victoria.
Is Glen Huntly Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Glen Huntly Park?
No, dogs are not allowed.
Are there toilets at Glen Huntly Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 66 m away.
How do I get to Glen Huntly Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Roseberry Gr/Neerim Rd (routes 623, 624), a 1-minute walk.
