The Wedge

Park in Caulfield East · Basic info

The Wedge is a park in Caulfield East, in the Glen Eira City Council area, good for all ages. Wilgra Cres/Booran Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 657 m away and dogs are allowed.
