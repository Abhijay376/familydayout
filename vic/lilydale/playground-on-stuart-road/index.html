Playground on Stuart Road

Playground in Lilydale · Basic info

Playground on Stuart Road is a public playground in Lilydale, in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.0 km away, and buses 670, 679 and 683 stop at Albert Hill Rd/Maroondah Hwy, a 8-minute walk away.
