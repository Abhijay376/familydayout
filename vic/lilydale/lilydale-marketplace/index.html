Lilydale Marketplace

Shopping centre in Lilydale · Basic info

Lilydale Marketplace is a shopping centre in Lilydale, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Lilydale Marketplace/Hutchinson St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 140 m away with a baby change table.
