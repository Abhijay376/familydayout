Playground at Melba Park

Playground in Lilydale · Basic info

Playground at Melba Park in Lilydale is a public playground in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. The Bayswater-Lilydale Trail passes about 170 m away, and Lilydale Outdoor Pool is about 120 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 100 m away, and 7 bus routes stop at Castella St/Maroondah Hwy, a 4-minute walk away.
