Lillydale Lake Playground

Playground in Lilydale · Basic info

This is a public playground in Lilydale. The Bayswater-Lilydale Trail is about 50 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 180 m from the playground, and buses 663, 680 and 965 stop at Lillydale Lake Entrance/Swansea Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
