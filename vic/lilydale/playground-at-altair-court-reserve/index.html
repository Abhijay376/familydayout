Playground at Altair Court Reserve

Playground in Lilydale · Basic info

Playground at Altair Court Reserve in Lilydale is a public playground in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. You'll find a public toilet about 1.4 km from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 4 minutes from Lilydale.
