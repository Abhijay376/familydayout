Lilydale Skate Bowl

Skate, BMX or pump track in Lilydale · Basic info

Lilydale Skate Bowl is a skate, bmx or pump track in Lilydale, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Cobden Cres/Anderson St (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 452 m away.
