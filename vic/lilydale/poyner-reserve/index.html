Poyner Reserve

Park in Lilydale · Basic info

Poyner Reserve is a park in Lilydale, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Poyner Ave/Cave Hill Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1100 m away with a baby change table.
