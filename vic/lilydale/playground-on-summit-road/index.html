Playground on Summit Road

Playground in Lilydale · Basic info

Playground on Summit Road in Lilydale is a public playground in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. The Lilydale-Warburton Rail Trail is about 50 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 710 m from the playground, and buses 683, 684, 685 and 965 stop at North Rd/Maroondah Hwy, a 6-minute walk away.
