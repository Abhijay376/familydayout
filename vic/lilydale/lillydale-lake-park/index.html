Lillydale Lake Park

Park in Lilydale · Basic info

Lillydale Lake Park is a park in Lilydale, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Lillydale Lake Entrance/Swansea Rd (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 308 m away with a baby change table.
