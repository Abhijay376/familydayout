Bunbartha in the Greater Shepparton area has 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Goulburn River Heritage River is the largest mapped park in Bunbartha, at about 16,599 hectares. Loch Garry Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the next largest. Playgrounds around Bunbartha include the playground at Bunbartha Recreation Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bunbartha. The closest to Bunbartha is Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line), about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Bunbartha. By car from Bunbartha, Shepparton is about 22 minutes away, Echuca about 63 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bunbartha is Aquamoves in Shepparton, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest library to Bunbartha is Shepparton Library in Shepparton, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Bunbartha is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Bunbartha is Shepparton Velodrome in Shepparton, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Bunbartha is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 22 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 22 minutes' drive from Bunbartha. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Bunbartha is Kidstown in Mooroopna, about 25 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 22 minutes from Bunbartha by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Bunbartha, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Larsen Reserve in Tallygaroopna (15 min), Shepparton Velodrome in Shepparton (20 min), BMX track in Shepparton (20 min), Shepparton Library in Shepparton (21 min), Village Cinemas in Shepparton (22 min) and Shepparton Heritage Centre in Shepparton (22 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bunbartha.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Bunbartha, Shepparton North has 4 playgrounds. Tallygaroopna, next to Bunbartha, has 2 playgrounds. Undera, next to Bunbartha, has 1 playground. Mundoona, Kaarimba and Coomboona also border Bunbartha.
