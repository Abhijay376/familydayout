Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Ombersley, in the Colac Otway area.

Landmarks

Ombersley Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Ombersley, at about 6 hectares.

Getting around

Ombersley has no train station; Winchelsea Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Ombersley, so most trips start by car. By car from Ombersley, Colac is about 40 minutes away, Geelong about 48 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Leisurelink Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Grovedale is about 40 minutes' drive from Ombersley. The closest library to Ombersley is Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn, about 43 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Ombersley is Moriac Skate Park in Moriac, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Ombersley is Reading Cinemas in Grovedale, about 41 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac is about 39 minutes' drive from Ombersley. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Ombersley, about 40 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Ombersley are Inverleigh Tennis Reserve Playground in Inverleigh (26 min), Inverleigh Sporting Complex Playground in Inverleigh (27 min), Moriac Skate Park in Moriac (38 min), the bmx track at Newling Reserve in Moriac (38 min), Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn (43 min) and Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (39 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Ombersley.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Ombersley, Inverleigh has 4 playgrounds. Next door to Ombersley, Winchelsea has 1 playground. Warncoort, Irrewarra, Hesse and Eurack also border Ombersley.
