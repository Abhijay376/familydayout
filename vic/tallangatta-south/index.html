Tallangatta South is in the Towong area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Wagra Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Tallangatta South, at about 162 hectares. Noorongong Bushland Reserve and Mitta Mitta River K26 Streamside Reserve are the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Tallangatta South. For walks and rides, Mitta - Callaghan Creek Loop and Mitta - Bullhead Gap Loop cross Tallangatta South.

Getting around

Tallangatta South has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 46 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tallangatta South, so most trips start by car. By car from Tallangatta South, Wodonga is about 50 minutes away and Wangaratta about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tallangatta South is Eskdale Swimming Pool in Eskdale, about 15 minutes' drive. For a library, Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta is about 24 minutes' drive from Tallangatta South. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 50 minutes from Tallangatta South by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Tallangatta South are Eskdale Swimming Pool in Eskdale (15 min), Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta (24 min), Tallangatta Art & Photography in Tallangatta (24 min), Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta (24 min), the playground at Banksia Park in Tangambalanga (28 min) and the playground at Ludlows Reserve in Ebden (35 min).

Best for

Free days on the Mitta - Callaghan Creek Loop and in Tallangatta South's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Tallangatta South, Tallangatta has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Next door to Tallangatta South, Eskdale has a pool. Tallandoon, Mitta Mitta and Charleroi also border Tallangatta South.
