Shane Crescent Reserve

Park in Croydon South · Basic info

Shane Crescent Reserve is a park in Croydon South, in the Maroondah City Council area, good for all ages. Highton St/Railway Ave (bus) is a 8-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 698 m away and dogs are allowed.
