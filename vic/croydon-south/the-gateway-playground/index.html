The Gateway Playground

Playground in Croydon South · Basic info

The Gateway Playground is a public playground in Croydon South, in the Maroondah City area. The Dorset Road Connection is about 210 m away, and for food, Dorset Heights Fish & Chips is about 350 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 740 m from the playground, and bus 737 stops at The Gateway/Dorset Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
