Woodland Park

Park in Croydon South · Basic info

Woodland Park is a park in Croydon South, in the Maroondah City Council area, good for all ages. The Gateway/Dorset Rd (bus) is a 12-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 825 m away.
