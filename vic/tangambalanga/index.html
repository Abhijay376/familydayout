Tangambalanga is in the Indigo area, and our data lists 2 playgrounds and 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tangambalanga is Beethang I100 Bushland Reserve, covering about 2 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Tangambalanga include Banksia Park. Playgrounds around Tangambalanga include the playground at Banksia Park. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Tangambalanga.

Getting around

Tangambalanga has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Albury - Mt Beauty via Baranduda and Tawonga South route stop in Tangambalanga. By car from Tangambalanga, Wodonga is about 21 minutes away and Wangaratta about 73 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tangambalanga is Edge Aquatics in Baranduda, about 13 minutes' drive. For a library, Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta is about 15 minutes' drive from Tangambalanga. The closest beach to Tangambalanga is Finns Beach in Bandiana, about 26 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Skate Park in Baranduda is about 12 minutes' drive from Tangambalanga. The closest cinema to Tangambalanga is Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana, about 16 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 21 minutes from Tangambalanga by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Tangambalanga are the playground at Ludlows Reserve in Ebden (14 min), Skate Park in Baranduda (12 min), Edge Aquatics in Baranduda (13 min), Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta (14 min), Tallangatta Art & Photography in Tallangatta (15 min) and Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta (15 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tangambalanga.

Nearby suburbs

Ebden, next to Tangambalanga, has 3 playgrounds. Next door to Tangambalanga, Bonegilla has 3 playgrounds. Kiewa, Huon and Gundowring also border Tangambalanga.
