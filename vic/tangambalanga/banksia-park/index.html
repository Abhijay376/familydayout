Banksia Park

Park in Tangambalanga · Basic info

Banksia Park is a park in Tangambalanga, in the Indigo Shire Council area, good for all ages. Tangambalanga General Store/Kiewa East Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 712 m away.

Questions parents ask

Where is Banksia Park?
Banksia Park is in Tangambalanga, Victoria.

How do I get to Banksia Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Tangambalanga General Store/Kiewa East Rd (routes Albury - Mt Beauty via Baranduda and Tawonga South), a 6-minute walk.
