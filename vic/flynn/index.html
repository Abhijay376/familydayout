Flynn is in the Latrobe area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Flynn, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Flynn. The closest to Flynn is Rosedale Railway Station (V/Line), about 7.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Flynn, so most trips start by car. By car from Flynn, Traralgon is about 15 minutes away, Sale about 31 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rosedale Swimming Pool in Rosedale is about 7 minutes' drive from Flynn. For a library, Morwell Library in Morwell is about 28 minutes' drive from Flynn. For a skate park, Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon is about 15 minutes' drive from Flynn. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 25 minutes' drive from Flynn. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Flynn, about 15 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Flynn are Queen Street Reserve Playground in Rosedale (7 min), Prince Street Reserve Playground in Rosedale (7 min), Rosedale Swimming Pool in Rosedale (7 min), Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon (15 min), Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon (15 min) and Glengarry Skatepark in Glengarry (19 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Flynn.

Nearby suburbs

Rosedale, next to Flynn, has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Flynn, Traralgon East has 2 playgrounds. Gormandale, next to Flynn, has 1 playground. Glengarry, next to Flynn, has 1 playground and a skate park. Willung, Loy Yang, Hiamdale and Flynns Creek also border Flynn.
