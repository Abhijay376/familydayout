Hayes Park

Park in Thornbury · Basic info

Hayes Park is a park in Thornbury, in the Darebin City Council area, good for all ages. Wilmoth St/Mansfield St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1046 m away with a baby change table.
