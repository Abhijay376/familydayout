Playground at Clyde Jones Reserve

Playground in Thornbury · Basic info

Playground at Clyde Jones Reserve in Thornbury is a public playground in the Darebin City area. The Great Eastern Cycleway is about 30 m away, and for food, Rat The Cafe is about 300 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.1 km from the playground, and buses 251 and 552 stop at Clyde St/Victoria Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
