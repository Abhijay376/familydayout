Playground on Raleigh Street

Playground in Thornbury · Basic info

Playground on Raleigh Street is a public playground in Thornbury, in the Darebin City area. The South Morang Line Shimmy is about 270 m away, and for food, Le Cafe Flo is about 90 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 60 m from the playground, and bus 510 stops at High St/Clarendon St, a 1-minute walk away.
