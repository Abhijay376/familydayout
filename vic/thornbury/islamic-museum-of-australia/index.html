Islamic Museum of Australia

Museum or gallery in Thornbury · Official details

Islamic Museum of Australia is a museum or gallery in Thornbury, in the Darebin City Council area, best for school-age kids. Merri Business Park/Normanby Ave (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 610 m away. Opening hours: Open Mon-Sat, 10am to 4pm; Open Mon-Sat, 10am to 4pm; Monday - Saturday, 10am - 4pm.
