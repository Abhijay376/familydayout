Mayer Park

Park in Thornbury · Basic info

Mayer Park is a park in Thornbury, in the Darebin City Council area, good for all ages. Mayer Park/Normanby Ave (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 232 m away.
