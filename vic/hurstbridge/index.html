Our data lists 3 playgrounds and 11 parks and reserves in Hurstbridge, in the Nillumbik area. Hurstbridge also has 2 skate parks.

Landmarks

Hurstbridge Memorial Park is the largest mapped park in Hurstbridge, at about 18 hectares. Fergusons Paddock and Temple Ridge Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Hurstbridge include Jaspers Park, Anzac Memorial Park and Dunstan Drive Reserve. Playgrounds around Hurstbridge include the playground at Anzac Memorial Park, the playground at Hurstbridge Memorial Park and the playground at Gordon & Sheila Ferguson Park. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Hurstbridge. For older kids in Hurstbridge there's Hurstbridge Half-pipe and Hurstbridge Skate Park. The Diamond Creek Trail passes through Hurstbridge. Our data lists 5 cafes and family restaurants in Hurstbridge for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Hurstbridge has a train station: Hurstbridge Railway Station (metro: Hurstbridge Line). Bus 343 stops in Hurstbridge. By car from Hurstbridge, Ringwood is about 28 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 29 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 42 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Hurstbridge is Diamond Creek Outdoor Pool in Diamond Creek, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest library to Hurstbridge is Diamond Valley Library in Greensborough, about 13 minutes' drive. For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 39 minutes' drive from Hurstbridge. The closest cinema to Hurstbridge is Hoyts in Greensborough, about 17 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Rare Bears Indoor Play Centre in Eltham is about 17 minutes' drive from Hurstbridge. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Hurstbridge is Bundoora Park Farm in Bundoora, about 26 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Ringwood, about 28 minutes from Hurstbridge by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Hurstbridge's 3 local playgrounds. Teens who skate or ride, with Hurstbridge Half-pipe in Hurstbridge. Free days on the Diamond Creek Trail and in Hurstbridge's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Hurstbridge, Doreen has 32 playgrounds. Diamond Creek, next to Hurstbridge, has 23 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Wattle Glen, next to Hurstbridge, has 2 playgrounds. Panton Hill, Nutfield, Kangaroo Ground and Cottles Bridge also border Hurstbridge.
