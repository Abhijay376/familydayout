Temple Ridge Bushland Reserve

Park in Hurstbridge · Basic info

Temple Ridge Bushland Reserve is a park in Hurstbridge, in the Nillumbik Shire Council area, good for all ages. Silvan Rd/Heidelberg-Kinglake Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away.
