Playground at Hurstbridge Memorial Park

Playground in Hurstbridge · Basic info

Playground at Hurstbridge Memorial Park in Hurstbridge is a public playground in the Nillumbik Shire area. The Diamond Creek Trail passes about 30 m away, and Playground at Gordon & Sheila Ferguson Park is another option about 450 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and trains stop at Hurstbridge Railway Station, a 5-minute walk away.
