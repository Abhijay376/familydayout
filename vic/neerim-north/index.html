Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Neerim North, in the Baw Baw area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Neerim North is Neerim G208 Bushland Reserve, covering about 1 hectares.

Getting around

Neerim North has no train station; Warragul Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Neerim North, so most trips start by car. By car from Neerim North, Warragul is about 36 minutes away, Pakenham about 62 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Neerim North is Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest library to Neerim North is Warragul Library in Warragul, about 36 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South is about 17 minutes' drive from Neerim North. The closest cinema to Neerim North is Warragul Cinema in Warragul, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Neerim North is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 37 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Warragul, about 36 minutes from Neerim North by car, with 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Neerim North, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at LaTrobe and Loch Rivers, Noojee Streamside Reserve in Noojee (15 min), Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South (17 min), the playground at Ellen Close Linear Reserve in Warragul (35 min), Warragul Library in Warragul (36 min), Warragul Cinema in Warragul (36 min) and The Alley Warragul in Warragul (37 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Neerim North.

Nearby suburbs

Noojee, next to Neerim North, has 1 playground. Fumina South and Fumina also border Neerim North.
