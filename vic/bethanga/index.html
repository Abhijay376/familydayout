Bethanga in the Towong area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Bethanga Historic Area is the largest mapped park in Bethanga, at about 286 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Bethanga. For walks and rides, Tallangatta - Lake Road Loop crosses Bethanga.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bethanga. The closest to Bethanga is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bethanga, so most trips start by car. By car from Bethanga, Wodonga is about 32 minutes away and Wangaratta about 79 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, WAVES in Wodonga is about 29 minutes' drive from Bethanga. For a library, Albury LibraryMuseum in Gateway Island is about 28 minutes' drive from Bethanga. The closest beach to Bethanga is Finns Beach in Bandiana, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Bethanga is David Street Skate Park in Gateway Island, about 27 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Regent Cinema in Gateway Island is about 27 minutes' drive from Bethanga. Wodonga is the closest bigger centre to Bethanga, about 32 minutes away by car, and has 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Bethanga, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Roy Williams Park in Bellbridge (8 min), The Bonegilla Migrant Experience in Bonegilla (20 min), Eastern Circuit Playground in Bonegilla (22 min), Finns Beach in Bandiana (27 min), Regent Cinema in Gateway Island (27 min) and Murray Art Museum Albury (MAMA) in Gateway Island (27 min).

Best for

Free days on the Tallangatta - Lake Road Loop and in Bethanga's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Tallangatta, next to Bethanga, has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Ebden, next to Bethanga, has 3 playgrounds. Bellbridge, next to Bethanga, has 2 playgrounds. Jarvis Creek and Huon also border Bethanga.
