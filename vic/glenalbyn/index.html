Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Glenalbyn, in the Loddon area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Glenalbyn is Mount Korong Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 464 hectares. Korong Bushland Reserve is the next largest. The Mt Korong Walking track passes through Glenalbyn.

Getting around

Glenalbyn has no train station; Raywood Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 39 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glenalbyn, so most trips start by car. By car from Glenalbyn, Bendigo is about 54 minutes away, Castlemaine about 71 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Glenalbyn is Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Glenalbyn is Rex Theatre in Charlton, about 41 minutes' drive. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Glenalbyn, about 54 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Glenalbyn are Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (16 min), Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (16 min), Rex Theatre in Charlton (41 min), Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (41 min), the playground at Rotary Park in Charlton (42 min) and the playground at Gordon Park in Charlton (42 min).

Best for

Free days on the Mt Korong Walking track and in Glenalbyn's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Glenalbyn, Wedderburn has 1 playground and a pool. Fiery Flat and Brenanah also border Glenalbyn.
