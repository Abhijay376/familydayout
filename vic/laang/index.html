Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Laang in the Moyne area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Laang has no train station; Terang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Laang, so most trips start by car. By car from Laang, Warrnambool is about 31 minutes away and Colac about 69 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon is about 28 minutes' drive from Laang. For a library, Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool is about 32 minutes' drive from Laang. The closest beach to Laang is Buttress Point in The Cove, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Laang is the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon, about 28 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool is about 31 minutes' drive from Laang. The closest indoor play centre to Laang is The Bowl in Warrnambool, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Laang is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 17 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 31 minutes from Laang by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Laang are Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (17 min), Buttress Point in The Cove (34 min), Flaxman Beach in Nullawarre (36 min), the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (28 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (28 min) and Grieve Street Playground in Warrnambool (29 min).
