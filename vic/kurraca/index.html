Kurraca is in the Loddon area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Kurraca, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Kurraca has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 60 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kurraca, so most trips start by car. By car from Kurraca, Bendigo is about 64 minutes away, Maryborough about 66 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kurraca is Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn, about 9 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 35 minutes' drive from Kurraca. The closest places for kids by road from Kurraca are Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (9 min), Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (9 min), Bi-Centennial Park Playground in St Arnaud (35 min), the playground at Queen Mary Botanic Gardens in St Arnaud (36 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (37 min) and Rex Theatre in Charlton (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Kurraca.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Kurraca, Wedderburn has 1 playground and a pool. Brenanah also borders Kurraca.
