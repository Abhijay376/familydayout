Fernshaw in the Yarra Ranges area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Yarra Ranges National Park is the largest mapped park in Fernshaw, at about 77,229 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Fernshaw. For walks and rides, Morley Walking Track and Mount Juliet Walking Track cross Fernshaw.

Getting around

Fernshaw has no train station; Lilydale Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Fernshaw, so most trips start by car. By car from Fernshaw, Healesville is about 13 minutes away, Lilydale about 35 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 76 minutes, without traffic.
