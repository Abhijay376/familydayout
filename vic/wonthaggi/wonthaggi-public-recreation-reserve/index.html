Wonthaggi Public Recreation Reserve

Park in Wonthaggi · Basic info

Wonthaggi Public Recreation Reserve is a park in Wonthaggi, in the Bass Coast Shire Council area, good for all ages. Broome Cres/Brown St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 308 m away.
