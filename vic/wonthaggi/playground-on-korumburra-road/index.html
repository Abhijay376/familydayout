Playground on Korumburra Road

Playground in Wonthaggi · Basic info

This is a public playground in Wonthaggi. The MBN passes about 70 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 120 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 60 m away, and bus North stops at Wonthaggi North Oval/Korumburra Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
