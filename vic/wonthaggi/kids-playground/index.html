Kids Playground

Playground in Wonthaggi · Basic info

This is a public playground in Wonthaggi. The MBN passes about 40 m away, and The Hicksborough General Store and Café is about 430 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 400 m away, and coaches stop at White Rd/McKenzie St, a 1-minute walk away.
