Playground at Rodney Court Reserve

Playground in Wonthaggi · Basic info

Playground at Rodney Court Reserve is a public playground in Wonthaggi, in the Bass Coast Shire area. The MBN is about 100 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 330 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 620 m from the playground, and bus North stops at Wonthaggi YMCA/Wentworth Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
