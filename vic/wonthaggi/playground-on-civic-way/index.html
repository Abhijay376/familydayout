Playground on Civic Way

Playground in Wonthaggi · Basic info

Playground on Civic Way in Wonthaggi is a public playground in the Bass Coast Shire area. The MBN passes about 310 m away, and Playground on McGibbonys Road is another option about 420 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus North stops at Storey St/Korumburra Rd, a 10-minute walk away.
