Playground on Korumburra Road

Playground in Wonthaggi · Basic info

This is a public playground in Wonthaggi. The MBN is about 70 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 350 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 530 m from the playground, and bus North stops at Elizabeth St/Dowling St, a 3-minute walk away.
