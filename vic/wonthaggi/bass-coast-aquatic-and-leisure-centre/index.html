Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre

Pool or water play in Wonthaggi · Basic info

Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre is a pool or water play in Wonthaggi, in the Bass Coast Shire Council area, good for all ages. Wonthaggi YMCA/Wentworth Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 345 m away with a baby change table.
