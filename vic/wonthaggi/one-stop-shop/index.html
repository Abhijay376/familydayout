One Stop Shop, Wonthaggi

Cafe or restaurant in Wonthaggi · Basic info

One Stop Shop is a fish and chips cafe or restaurant in Wonthaggi, in the Bass Coast Shire Council area, a place to eat during a day out. McLeod St/Graham St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 306 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: opening_hours: Mo-Fr 06:00-21:00; Sa 07:00-21:00; Su 08:00-21:00.
