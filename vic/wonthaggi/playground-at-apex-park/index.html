Playground at Apex Park

Playground in Wonthaggi · Basic info

This is a public playground in Wonthaggi. The Bass Coast Rail Trail is about 30 m away, and Wonthaggi Principal Library is about 170 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 130 m from the playground, and bus North stops at Korumburra Rd/McKenzie St, a 4-minute walk away.
