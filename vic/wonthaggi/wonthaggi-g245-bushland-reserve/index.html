Wonthaggi G245 Bushland Reserve

Park in Wonthaggi · Basic info

Wonthaggi G245 Bushland Reserve is a park in Wonthaggi, in the Bass Coast Shire Council area, good for all ages. Honeys Rd/Cape Paterson Rd (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1911 m away.
