Navarre Playground

Playground in Navarre · Official details

This is a public playground in Navarre. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus St Arnaud - Stawell via Ararat stops at General Store/Ararat - St Arnaud Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
