Navarre in the Northern Grampians area has 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Navarre Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Navarre, at about 9 hectares. Navarre Recreation Reserve and Navarre Nature Conservation Reserve are the next largest. Playgrounds in Navarre include Navarre Playground. Our data shows 2 public toilets and 1 park or playground with a council BBQ close by in Navarre.

Getting around

Navarre has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 45 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the St Arnaud - Stawell via Ararat route stop in Navarre. By car from Navarre, Stawell is about 30 minutes away, Ararat about 54 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Navarre is Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell, about 30 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell is about 34 minutes' drive from Navarre. For a zoo or wildlife park, Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell is about 32 minutes' drive from Navarre. The nearest larger centre is Stawell, about 30 minutes from Navarre by car, with 6 playgrounds and a pool. From Navarre, the nearest places for kids by road are Community Playground in Landsborough (14 min), Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell (30 min), Railway Station Art and Crafts Gallery in Stawell (31 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (33 min), Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell (32 min) and the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Navarre.

Nearby suburbs

Barkly, next to Navarre, has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Navarre, Landsborough has 1 playground. Tulkara, Paradise and Greens Creek also border Navarre.
