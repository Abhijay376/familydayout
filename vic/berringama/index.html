Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Berringama, in the Towong area.

Landmarks

Beetoomba Creek Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Berringama, at about 3 hectares. Berringama also has Beetoomba Trestle Bridge Historic Reserve and Trestle Bridge, Cudgewa Creek Historic Reserve.

Getting around

Berringama has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 67 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Berringama, so most trips start by car. By car from Berringama, Wodonga is about 75 minutes away and Wangaratta about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Corryong Public swimming pool in Corryong is about 22 minutes' drive from Berringama. For a library, Corryong Elyne Mitchell Library in Corryong is about 22 minutes' drive from Berringama. For a beach, Pebble Beach in Walwa is about 40 minutes' drive from Berringama. For a cinema, Corryong Cinema in Corryong is about 22 minutes' drive from Berringama. The closest places for kids by road from Berringama are Corryong Cinema in Corryong (22 min), Corryong Public swimming pool in Corryong (22 min), Corryong Elyne Mitchell Library in Corryong (22 min), The Spotted Goanna in Walwa (34 min) and Pebble Beach in Walwa (40 min).
