Genoa is in the East Gippsland area, and our data lists 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Double Creek Natural Catchment Area is the largest mapped park in Genoa, at about 1,583 hectares. Wallagaraugh River Streamside Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Genoa. For walks and rides, Genoa Peak Lookout Track crosses Genoa.

Getting around

There's no train station in Genoa. The closest to Genoa is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 178 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Mallacoota - Genoa via Gipsy Point route stop in Genoa. V/Line coaches stop in Genoa. By car from Genoa, Lakes Entrance is about 2.5 hours away and Bairnsdale about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest beach to Genoa is Davis Creek Beach in Mallacoota, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Genoa is the skate park at Mick Baum Park in Cann River, about 37 minutes' drive. From Genoa, the nearest places for kids by road are Davis Creek Beach in Mallacoota (22 min), Betka Beach in Mallacoota (22 min), WWII Bunker Museum in Mallacoota (24 min), the playground at Mick Baum Park in Cann River (36 min) and the skate park at Mick Baum Park in Cann River (37 min).

Best for

Free days on the Genoa Peak Lookout Track and in Genoa's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Genoa, Wingan River has a beach. Next door to Genoa, Mallacoota has a beach. Noorinbee North, Maramingo Creek, Gipsy Point and Chandlers Creek also border Genoa.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Genoa, without traffic.
