Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Oxley Flats in the Wangaratta area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Oxley Flats. The closest to Oxley Flats is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 9.2 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Oxley Flats, so most trips start by car. By car from Oxley Flats, Wangaratta is about 11 minutes away and Benalla about 43 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Oxley Flats is Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta, about 12 minutes' drive. For a library, Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta is about 10 minutes' drive from Oxley Flats. The closest beach to Oxley Flats is Sandy Beach in Beechworth, about 31 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta is about 13 minutes' drive from Oxley Flats. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 8 minutes' drive from Oxley Flats. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Oxley Flats, about 11 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Oxley Flats are the playground at John McAleese Community Park in Milawa (6 min), Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta (8 min), the playground at Apex Park in Wangaratta (9 min), Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (10 min), Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta (12 min) and the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta (13 min).

Best for

Nearby suburbs
