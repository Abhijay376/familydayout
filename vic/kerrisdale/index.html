Kerrisdale in the Murrindindi area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Wyndham Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Kerrisdale, at about 12 hectares. For walks and rides, Great Victorian Rail Trail crosses Kerrisdale.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kerrisdale. The closest to Kerrisdale is Tallarook Railway Station (V/Line), about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kerrisdale, so most trips start by car. By car from Kerrisdale, Seymour is about 16 minutes away, Healesville about 68 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kerrisdale is Seymour Swimming Centre in Seymour, about 17 minutes' drive. For a library, Seymour Library in Seymour is about 15 minutes' drive from Kerrisdale. The closest skate park to Kerrisdale is Seymour Skate Park in Seymour, about 15 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 16 minutes from Kerrisdale by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Kerrisdale are the playground at M O'Sullivan Reserve in Seymour (14 min), the playground at O'Shea Park in Seymour (14 min), Seymour Skate Park in Seymour (15 min), Seymour Library in Seymour (15 min), the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea (16 min) and Seymour Swimming Centre in Seymour (17 min).

Best for

Free days on the Great Victorian Rail Trail and in Kerrisdale's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Tallarook, next to Kerrisdale, has 1 playground. Ghin Ghin also borders Kerrisdale.
