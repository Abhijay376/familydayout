Dallas is in the Hume area, and our data lists 5 playgrounds and 7 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Dallas is Laura Douglas Reserve, covering about 7 hectares. Gibb Reserve and Kevin De Laine Reserve are the next largest. Dallas also has J Culpin Reserve, Jim Stewart Reserve and Blair Street Reserve. Playgrounds in Dallas include the playground at Gibb Reserve, the playground at Jim Stewart Reserve and the playground at Kevin De Laine Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Dallas. Dallas Shopping Centre is the local shopping centre. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Dallas for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Dallas. The closest to Dallas is Upfield Railway Station (metro), about 600 metres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 532 and 540 stop in Dallas. By car from Dallas, Broadmeadows is about 5 minutes away, Sunshine about 20 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 27 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Dallas is Fawkner Leisure Centre in Fawkner, about 9 minutes' drive. For a library, Hume Global Learning Centre - Broadmeadows in Broadmeadows is about 6 minutes' drive from Dallas. For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 29 minutes' drive from Dallas. For a skate park, the bmx track in Campbellfield in Campbellfield is about 8 minutes' drive from Dallas. The closest cinema to Dallas is Hoyts in Broadmeadows, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Dallas is MOPA in Thomastown, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Dallas is Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville, about 22 minutes' drive.

Best for

Younger kids at Dallas's 5 local playgrounds.

Nearby suburbs

Broadmeadows, next to Dallas, has 12 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. Next door to Dallas, Campbellfield has 6 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Dallas, Coolaroo has 2 playgrounds.
