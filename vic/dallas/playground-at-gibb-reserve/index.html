Playground at Gibb Reserve

Playground in Dallas · Basic info

Playground at Gibb Reserve is a public playground in Dallas, in the Hume City area. Playground at Kevin De Laine Reserve is another option about 500 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 660 m from the playground, and buses 532 and 540 stop at Corinella Cres/Blair St, a 2-minute walk away.
