Blair Street Reserve

Park in Dallas · Basic info

Blair Street Reserve is a park in Dallas, in the Hume City Council area, good for all ages. Eltham St/Blair St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 417 m away with a baby change table.
