Drysdale/Clifton Spring Skate

Playground in Clifton Springs · Basic info

This is a public playground in Clifton Springs. Older kids have a skate park about 10 m away, and Jetty Road Reserve is another option about 70 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 61 stops at Jetty Road Reserve/Jetty Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
