Almerta Reserve

Park in Clifton Springs · Basic info

Almerta Reserve is a park in Clifton Springs, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Bukatilla Ave/Bay Shore Ave (bus) is a 10-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1133 m away.
