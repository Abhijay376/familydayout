Skate park at Jetty Road Reserve

Skate, BMX or pump track in Clifton Springs · Basic info

Skate park at Jetty Road Reserve is a skate, bmx or pump track in Clifton Springs, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Jetty Road Reserve/Jetty Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 14 m away.
