Clifton Springs in the Greater Geelong area has 11 playgrounds and 7 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, Clifton Springs has a skate park.

Landmarks

Beacon Point Reserve is the largest mapped park in Clifton Springs, at about 12 hectares. Adrian Mannix Reserve and The Dell are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Clifton Springs include Almerta Reserve, Central Reserve and Bullimah Reserve. Playgrounds around Clifton Springs include Country Club, Malcolm Reserve and Jetty Road Reserve. Our data shows 4 public toilets and 2 off-leash dog parks in Clifton Springs. For older kids in Clifton Springs there's the skate park at Jetty Road Reserve.

Getting around

Clifton Springs has no train station; Lara Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 61 stops in Clifton Springs. By car from Clifton Springs, Geelong is about 25 minutes away, Torquay about 45 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 89 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Clifton Springs is Northern Bellarine Aquatic Centre in Curlewis, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest library to Clifton Springs is Drysdale Library in Drysdale, about 4 minutes' drive. For a beach, Grassy Point West Beach in Portarlington is about 14 minutes' drive from Clifton Springs. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Geelong is about 25 minutes' drive from Clifton Springs. The closest indoor play centre to Clifton Springs is Laserquest in Geelong, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Clifton Springs is Adventure Park in Wallington, about 16 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 11 playgrounds across Clifton Springs. Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at Jetty Road Reserve in Clifton Springs.

Nearby suburbs

Drysdale, next to Clifton Springs, has 5 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Clifton Springs, Curlewis has 3 playgrounds and a pool.
