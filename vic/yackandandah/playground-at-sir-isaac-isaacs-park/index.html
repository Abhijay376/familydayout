Playground at Sir Isaac Isaacs Park

Playground in Yackandandah · Basic info

Playground at Sir Isaac Isaacs Park is a public playground in Yackandandah, in the Indigo Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 330 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 7 stops at Yackandandah Health/Isaacs Ave, a 1-minute walk away.
