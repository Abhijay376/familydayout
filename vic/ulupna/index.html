Ulupna in the Moira area has 1 park or reserve in our data. On top of that, Ulupna has 10 beaches.

Landmarks

Tocumwal Regional Park is the largest mapped park in Ulupna, at about 219 hectares. Places to visit in Ulupna include Carter Beach and Doctors Beach.

Getting around

Ulupna has no train station; Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 60 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Ulupna, so most trips start by car. By car from Ulupna, Shepparton is about 63 minutes away and Echuca about 89 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Ulupna is Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest library to Ulupna is Tocumwal Library in Koonoomoo, about 42 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Strathmerton Community Skate Park in Strathmerton is about 25 minutes' drive from Ulupna. For a cinema, Cobram Cinema in Cobram is about 37 minutes' drive from Ulupna. From Ulupna, the nearest places for kids by road are Strathmerton Community Skate Park in Strathmerton (25 min), Tocumwal Library in Koonoomoo (42 min), Numurkah Library in Numurkah (35 min), the playground at Federation Park in Cobram (37 min), the skate park at Federation Park in Cobram (37 min) and Cobram Cinema in Cobram (37 min).

Best for

Beach days at Carter Beach and Doctors Beach.

Nearby suburbs

Mywee, next to Ulupna, has a beach. Next door to Ulupna, Strathmerton has a skate park.
