Garfield North in the Cardinia area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Garfield North is Mount Cannibal Flora and Fauna Reserve, covering about 53 hectares. Cannibal Creek Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Garfield North. For walks and rides, Mount Cannibal Walking Track crosses Garfield North.

Getting around

There's no train station in Garfield North. The closest to Garfield North is Garfield Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.4 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Garfield North, so most trips start by car. By car from Garfield North, Pakenham is about 26 minutes away, Warragul about 33 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 70 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wave Pool in Tynong North is about 12 minutes' drive from Garfield North. For a library, Pakenham Library in Pakenham is about 25 minutes' drive from Garfield North. For a skate park, the bmx track at Greenland Court Reserve in Garfield is about 12 minutes' drive from Garfield North. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 31 minutes' drive from Garfield North. The closest indoor play centre to Garfield North is Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Garfield North is Wildlife Trail in Tynong North, about 12 minutes' drive. Pakenham is the closest bigger centre to Garfield North, about 26 minutes away by car, and has 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Garfield North are Wave Pool in Tynong North (12 min), the playground at Barker Road Reserve in Garfield (11 min), Wildlife Trail in Tynong North (12 min), Rock Pool in Tynong North (12 min), Gumbuya World in Tynong North (12 min) and Typhoon Island in Tynong North (12 min).

Best for

Free days on the Mount Cannibal Walking Track and in Garfield North's parks.

Nearby suburbs
