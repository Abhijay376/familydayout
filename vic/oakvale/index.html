Oakvale is in the Gannawarra area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Oakvale is Griffith Lagoon Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 66 hectares. Quambatook Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Oakvale has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 38 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Oakvale, so most trips start by car. By car from Oakvale, Swan Hill is about 87 minutes away and Echuca about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Oakvale is Quambatook Memorial Pool in Quambatook, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest library to Oakvale is Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook, about 17 minutes' drive. From Oakvale, the nearest places for kids by road are Quambatook Lions Club Playground in Quambatook (17 min), Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook (17 min), Quambatook Historical Centre in Quambatook (17 min), Quambatook Memorial Pool in Quambatook (17 min), Boort Court House Historical Museum in Boort (43 min) and A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park in Boort (44 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Oakvale.
