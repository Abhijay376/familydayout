Playground on Marine Drive

Playground in Safety Beach · Basic info

Playground on Marine Drive in Safety Beach is a public playground in the Mornington Peninsula Shire area. The Mornington Peninsula Bay Trail passes about 10 m away, and Safety Beach is about 40 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and buses 781 and 788 stop at Safety Beach Sailing Club/Marine Dr, a 4-minute walk away.
