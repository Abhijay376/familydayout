Goroke Little Desert Nature Playspace

Playground in Goroke · Basic info

Goroke Little Desert Nature Playspace is a public playground in Goroke, in the West Wimmera Shire area. West Wimmera Shire Goroke Library is about 540 m away, and Goroke Swimming Centre is about 270 m away. There's a public toilet about 200 m away, and bus Horsham - Naracoorte via Natimuk, Goroke, Edenhope stops at Station St/Main St, a 6-minute walk away.
