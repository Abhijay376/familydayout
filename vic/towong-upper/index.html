Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Towong Upper in the Towong area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Towong Upper has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 100 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Towong Upper, so most trips start by car. By car from Towong Upper, Wodonga is about 2 hours away and Wangaratta about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Towong Upper is Corryong Public swimming pool in Corryong, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest library to Towong Upper is Corryong Elyne Mitchell Library in Corryong, about 19 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Corryong Cinema in Corryong is about 19 minutes' drive from Towong Upper. From Towong Upper, the nearest places for kids by road are Corryong Cinema in Corryong (19 min), Corryong Elyne Mitchell Library in Corryong (19 min), Corryong Public swimming pool in Corryong (20 min) and The Spotted Goanna in Walwa (46 min).
