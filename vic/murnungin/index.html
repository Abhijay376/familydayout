Murnungin is in the Swan Hill area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Murnungin, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Murnungin has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 41 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Murnungin, so most trips start by car. By car from Murnungin, Swan Hill is about 52 minutes away and Echuca about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Murnungin is Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock, about 32 minutes' drive. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Murnungin, about 52 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Murnungin, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Culgoa Memorial Park in Culgoa (31 min), Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock (32 min), the playground at Gray Park in Lake Boga (48 min), Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake (44 min) and Lake Boga Flying Boat Museum in Lake Boga (50 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Murnungin.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Murnungin, Culgoa has 1 playground. Meatian, Lalbert and Chinangin also border Murnungin.
