Mount Duneed in the Greater Geelong area has 12 playgrounds and 8 parks and reserves in our data. Mount Duneed also has a pool and 2 skate parks.

Landmarks

Armstrong Greenway is the largest mapped park in Mount Duneed, at about 5 hectares. Rice Nature Reserve and Bunjils Nest are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Mount Duneed include Nova Park, Kalkarra Park and McCubbin Drive Reserve. Playgrounds around Mount Duneed include Mt Duneed Hall, the playground at Nova Park and the playground at Bunjils Nest. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Mount Duneed. Places to visit in Mount Duneed include Salt Water Swim Schools. For older kids in Mount Duneed there's the bmx track at Kalkarra Park and the skate park at Bunjils Nest. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Mount Duneed for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Waurn Ponds Railway Station (V/Line: Geelong - Melbourne and Warrnambool - Melbourne via Colac & Geelong) in Mount Duneed. Buses 45, 52, 53 and 54 stop in Mount Duneed. V/Line coaches stop in Mount Duneed. By car from Mount Duneed, Torquay is about 9 minutes away, Geelong about 15 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 77 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Mount Duneed is Biyal-an Armstrong Creek Library in Armstrong Creek, about 4 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Mount Duneed is Fishermans Beach in Torquay, about 10 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Reading Cinemas in Grovedale is about 9 minutes' drive from Mount Duneed. The closest indoor play centre to Mount Duneed is Bounce Inc in Grovedale, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Mount Duneed is Tiger Moth World in Torquay, about 7 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 12 playgrounds across Mount Duneed. Teens who skate or ride, with the bmx track at Kalkarra Park in Mount Duneed.
