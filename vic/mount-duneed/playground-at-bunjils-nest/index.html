Playground at Bunjils Nest

Playground in Mount Duneed · Basic info

Playground at Bunjils Nest is a public playground in Mount Duneed, in the Greater Geelong City area. Older kids have a skate park about 100 m away, and Salt Water Swim Schools is about 90 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus 45 stops at Poa Banyul Community Hub/Unity Dr , a 1-minute walk away.
