Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Caralulup in the Central Goldfields area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Caralulup. The closest to Caralulup is Talbot Railway Station (V/Line), about 6.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Caralulup, so most trips start by car. By car from Caralulup, Maryborough is about 21 minutes away, Ballarat about 42 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Caralulup is Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot, about 6 minutes' drive. For a library, Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough is about 21 minutes' drive from Caralulup. For a skate park, the skate park at Alexander Park in Learmonth is about 23 minutes' drive from Caralulup. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 21 minutes from Caralulup by car, with a library. The closest places for kids by road from Caralulup are Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground in Talbot (6 min), Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (6 min), the playground at Toll Bar Park in Lexton (12 min), Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (21 min), Lee Medlyn Bottle Museum in Clunes (19 min) and The Warehouse in Clunes (20 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Caralulup.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Caralulup, Talbot has 1 playground and a pool. Burnbank also borders Caralulup.
