Playground on North Road

Playground in Brighton · Basic info

Playground on North Road in Brighton is a public playground in the Bayside City area. The Bay Trail is about 10 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 50 m from the playground, and buses 600, 922 and 923 stop at North Rd/St Kilda St, a 5-minute walk away.
