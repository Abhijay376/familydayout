Playground at Elsternwick Park

Playground in Brighton · Basic info

Playground at Elsternwick Park is a public playground in Brighton, in the Bayside City area. The Elster Creek Trail passes about 130 m away, and Baked in Elwood Pizza & Pasta is about 300 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 320 m away, and bus 246 stops at St Kilda St/Glen Huntly Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
