Elsternwick Park

Park in Brighton · Basic info

Elsternwick Park is a park in Brighton, in the Bayside City Council area, good for all ages. St Kilda St/Bent Ave (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 477 m away with a baby change table.
