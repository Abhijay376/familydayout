Whyte Street Reserve playground

Playground in Brighton · Basic info

Whyte Street Reserve playground is a public playground in Brighton, in the Bayside City area. For food, Brighton Were Street Fish Supply is about 340 m away, and Hanby Street Reserve Playground is another option about 240 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and buses 811 and 812 stop at Whyte St/Dendy St, a 5-minute walk away.
