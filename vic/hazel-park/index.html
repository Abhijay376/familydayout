Hazel Park in the South Gippsland area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Toora H41 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Hazel Park, at about 19 hectares. Agnes Falls Scenic Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Hazel Park.

Getting around

Hazel Park has no train station; Morwell Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 43 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Hazel Park, so most trips start by car. By car from Hazel Park, Leongatha is about 51 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 71 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Toora Pool in Toora is about 12 minutes' drive from Hazel Park. For a library, Foster Library in Foster is about 21 minutes' drive from Hazel Park. For a beach, Toora Beach in Toora is about 20 minutes' drive from Hazel Park. The closest skate park to Hazel Park is the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora, about 13 minutes' drive. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Hazel Park, about 51 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Hazel Park are the playground at Stanley Street Playground in Toora (12 min), Toora Pool in Toora (12 min), Sagasser Park in Toora (13 min), the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora (13 min), Toora Beach in Toora (20 min) and Port Welshpool and District Maritime Museum in Port Welshpool (22 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Hazel Park.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Hazel Park, Toora has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. Welshpool, next to Hazel Park, has 1 playground and a beach. Agnes also borders Hazel Park.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Hazel Park, without traffic.
