Our data lists 4 playgrounds in Moolap, in the Greater Geelong area. Beyond the parks, Moolap has a pool.

Landmarks

Playgrounds around Moolap include Moolap Reserve, S Swim-Creche and Grinter Reserve. Our data shows 7 public toilets in Moolap. In Moolap you'll also find Splashdown. For walks and rides, Bellarine Rail Trail crosses Moolap. Our data lists 3 cafes and family restaurants in Moolap for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Moolap has no train station; South Geelong Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 5.8 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 30, 31, 32, 56, 60 and 61 stop in Moolap. By car from Moolap, Geelong is about 7 minutes away, Torquay about 26 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 72 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Moolap is Newcomb Library in Newcomb, about 3 minutes' drive. For a beach, Eastern Beach in Geelong is about 8 minutes' drive from Moolap. The closest skate park to Moolap is John Hansen Memorial Skate Park in Leopold, about 4 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Geelong is about 9 minutes' drive from Moolap. The closest indoor play centre to Moolap is Laserquest in Geelong, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Moolap is Adventure Park in Wallington, about 10 minutes' drive. Geelong is the closest bigger centre to Moolap, about 7 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Moolap's 4 local playgrounds. Free days on the Bellarine Rail Trail and in Moolap's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Leopold, next to Moolap, has 18 playgrounds, a library and a beach. Next door to Moolap, Newcomb has 14 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Moolap, Armstrong Creek has 8 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Moolap, St Albans Park has 6 playgrounds. Connewarre, Whittington and Charlemont also border Moolap.
