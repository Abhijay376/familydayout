Colbrook is in the Moorabool area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Colbrook, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Colbrook. The closest to Colbrook is Ballan Railway Station (V/Line), about 8.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Colbrook, so most trips start by car. By car from Colbrook, Bacchus Marsh is about 33 minutes away, Daylesford about 33 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 77 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Ballan Swimming Pool in Ballan is about 13 minutes' drive from Colbrook. The closest library to Colbrook is Daylesford Library in Daylesford, about 33 minutes' drive. For a beach, Lionhead Beach in Pentland Hills is about 29 minutes' drive from Colbrook. The closest skate park to Colbrook is the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan, about 13 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central is about 44 minutes' drive from Colbrook. The closest indoor play centre to Colbrook is Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point, about 45 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek is about 35 minutes' drive from Colbrook. Bacchus Marsh is the closest bigger centre to Colbrook, about 33 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Colbrook, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Mill Park in Ballan (13 min), the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan (13 min), Ballan Swimming Pool in Ballan (13 min), the playground at McLean Recreation Reserve in Ballan (14 min), Lionhead Beach in Pentland Hills (29 min) and Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford (33 min).
