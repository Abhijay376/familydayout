North Wonthaggi is in the Bass Coast area, and our data lists 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in North Wonthaggi is Sussex Court Park, covering about 1 hectares. Other parks and reserves in North Wonthaggi include Jacobs Park. Playgrounds around North Wonthaggi include the playground at Jacobs Park. The MBN passes through North Wonthaggi. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in North Wonthaggi for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in North Wonthaggi. The closest to North Wonthaggi is Stony Point Railway Station (metro), about 41 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the North, Dudley and Cowes - Anderson - Wonthaggi routes stop in North Wonthaggi. V/Line coaches stop in North Wonthaggi. By car from North Wonthaggi, Wonthaggi is about 5 minutes away, Leongatha about 34 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to North Wonthaggi is Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Wonthaggi, about 3 minutes' drive. For a library, Wonthaggi Principal Library in Wonthaggi is about 4 minutes' drive from North Wonthaggi. The closest beach to North Wonthaggi is Baxters Beach in Wonthaggi, about 13 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Wonthaggi Skate Park in Wonthaggi is about 3 minutes' drive from North Wonthaggi. For a cinema, Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi is about 4 minutes' drive from North Wonthaggi. For a zoo or wildlife park, Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville is about 28 minutes' drive from North Wonthaggi. The nearest larger centre is Wonthaggi, about 5 minutes from North Wonthaggi by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Free days on the MBN and in North Wonthaggi's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Wonthaggi, next to North Wonthaggi, has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to North Wonthaggi, Dalyston has 1 playground and a beach. South Dudley, next to North Wonthaggi, has 1 playground. St Clair also borders North Wonthaggi.
