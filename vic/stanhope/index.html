Stanhope is in the Campaspe area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Stanhope, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Stanhope.

Getting around

There's no train station in Stanhope. The closest to Stanhope is Rochester Railway Station (V/Line), about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Bendigo Station - Shepparton Station via Kyabram route stop in Stanhope. V/Line coaches stop in Stanhope. By car from Stanhope, Shepparton is about 35 minutes away, Echuca about 43 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rushworth Pool in Rushworth is about 16 minutes' drive from Stanhope. The closest library to Stanhope is Rushworth Library in Rushworth, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Stanhope is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 37 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Stanhope is the skate park at Lions Park in Rushworth, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Stanhope is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 35 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 35 minutes' drive from Stanhope. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Stanhope is Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram, about 20 minutes' drive. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Stanhope, about 35 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Stanhope are Rushworth Museum in Rushworth (15 min), Rushworth Library in Rushworth (15 min), Rushworth Pool in Rushworth (16 min), the skate park at Lions Park in Rushworth (15 min), the playground at Judd Park in Merrigum (20 min) and Community Swimming Pool in Merrigum (20 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Stanhope.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Stanhope, Rushworth has a pool, a library and a skate park. Cooma, Carag Carag and Burramboot also border Stanhope.
