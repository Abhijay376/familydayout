Landmarks

The biggest green space in Carapooee West is Stoney Creek Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 594 hectares.

Getting around

Carapooee West has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 55 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Carapooee West, so most trips start by car. By car from Carapooee West, Maryborough is about 55 minutes away and Stawell about 69 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Carapooee West is St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud, about 12 minutes' drive. Maryborough is the closest bigger centre to Carapooee West, about 55 minutes away by car, and has a library. From Carapooee West, the nearest places for kids by road are Stuart Mill Recreation Reserve Playground in Stuart Mill (8 min), Bi-Centennial Park Playground in St Arnaud (11 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (12 min) and Courthouse in Avoca (43 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Carapooee West.

Nearby suburbs

St Arnaud, next to Carapooee West, has 4 playgrounds and a pool. Stuart Mill, next to Carapooee West, has 1 playground. Carapooee and Beazleys Bridge also border Carapooee West.
