Woowookarung Regional Park

Park in Mount Clear · Official details

Woowookarung Regional Park is a park in Mount Clear, in the Ballarat City Council area, good for all ages. Park Lane/Vincent Dr (bus) is a 23-minute walk away and dogs are allowed on a lead.

Questions parents ask

Where is Woowookarung Regional Park?
Woowookarung Regional Park is in Mount Clear, Victoria.
Is Woowookarung Regional Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Woowookarung Regional Park?
Yes, on a lead.
How do I get to Woowookarung Regional Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Park Lane/Vincent Dr (routes 22), a 23-minute walk.
