Playground on Dallas Avenue

Playground in Mount Clear · Basic info

Playground on Dallas Avenue in Mount Clear is a public playground in the Ballarat City area. The Goldfields Track is about 270 m away, and Playground at Canadian Creek Reserve is another option about 310 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and buses 21 and 22 stop at Olympic Ave/Geelong Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
