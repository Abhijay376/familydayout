Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Nilma in the Baw Baw area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Nilma has no train station; Warragul Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 3.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Garfield Station - Traralgon Plaza route stop in Nilma. V/Line coaches stop in Nilma. By car from Nilma, Warragul is about 7 minutes away, Pakenham about 41 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 84 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul is about 6 minutes' drive from Nilma. For a library, Warragul Library in Warragul is about 6 minutes' drive from Nilma. For a skate park, Warragul Velodrome in Warragul is about 5 minutes' drive from Nilma. The closest cinema to Nilma is Warragul Cinema in Warragul, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Nilma is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 5 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Wildlife Trail in Tynong North is about 27 minutes' drive from Nilma. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Nilma, about 7 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Nilma, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Pederson Park in Warragul (5 min), Warragul & District Historical Society Museum in Warragul (5 min), Warragul Velodrome in Warragul (5 min), Warragul Cinema in Warragul (5 min), Burke Street Pump Track in Warragul (5 min) and The Alley Warragul in Warragul (5 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Nilma.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Nilma, Warragul has 22 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Darnum, next to Nilma, has 2 playgrounds.
