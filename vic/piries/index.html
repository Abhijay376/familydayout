Piries is in the Mansfield area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Piries, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

The Mansfield - Merrijig passes through Piries.

Getting around

There's no train station in Piries. The closest to Piries is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 68 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Mansfield - Woods Point via Jamieson route stop in Piries. By car from Piries, Mansfield is about 11 minutes away, Benalla about 62 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Mansfield Outdoor Swimming Pool in Mansfield is about 12 minutes' drive from Piries. For a library, Mansfield Library in Mansfield is about 11 minutes' drive from Piries. The closest skate park to Piries is the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Piries is Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield, about 11 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield is about 3 minutes' drive from Piries. Mansfield is the closest bigger centre to Piries, about 11 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Piries, the nearest places for kids by road are Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield (3 min), Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield (11 min), Mansfield Library in Mansfield (11 min), the playground at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (11 min), the playground at Mansfield Botanic Park in Mansfield (11 min) and the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (11 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Piries on the Mansfield - Merrijig.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Piries, Mansfield has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Howqua Inlet, Howqua, Goughs Bay and Delatite also border Piries.
