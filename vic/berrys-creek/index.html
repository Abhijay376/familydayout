Our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in Berrys Creek, in the South Gippsland area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Berrys Creek is Mossvale Park, covering about 7 hectares. Playgrounds in Berrys Creek include the playground at Mossvale Park. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Berrys Creek.

Getting around

There's no train station in Berrys Creek. The closest to Berrys Creek is Yarragon Railway Station (V/Line), about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Berrys Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Berrys Creek, Leongatha is about 15 minutes away, Traralgon about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Berrys Creek is South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha, about 13 minutes' drive. For a library, Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North is about 8 minutes' drive from Berrys Creek. For a beach, The Caves in Inverloch is about 41 minutes' drive from Berrys Creek. For a skate park, Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North in Mirboo North is about 9 minutes' drive from Berrys Creek. The closest cinema to Berrys Creek is Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha, about 15 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Leongatha, about 15 minutes from Berrys Creek by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Berrys Creek are Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North (8 min), Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North in Mirboo North (9 min), the playground at Baromi Park in Mirboo North (9 min), BMX track in Leongatha (12 min), South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha (13 min) and Splash Pools and Stadium in Leongatha (14 min).
