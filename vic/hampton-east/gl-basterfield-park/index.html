GL Basterfield Park

Park in Hampton East · Basic info

GL Basterfield Park is a park in Hampton East, in the Bayside City Council area, good for all ages. Dane Rd/Spring Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 128 m away with a baby change table.
