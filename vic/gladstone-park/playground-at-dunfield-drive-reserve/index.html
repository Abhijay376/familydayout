Playground at Dunfield Drive Reserve

Playground in Gladstone Park · Basic info

Playground at Dunfield Drive Reserve in Gladstone Park is a public playground in the Hume City area. The Bike: Low gradient access to Western Ring Road Trail passes about 130 m away, and Gladstone Park Library is about 600 m away. There's a public toilet about 700 m away, and bus 477 stops at Claredale Ave/Carrick Dr, a 3-minute walk away.
