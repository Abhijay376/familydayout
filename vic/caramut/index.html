Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Caramut, in the Moyne area.

Landmarks

Caramut Recreation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Caramut, at about 5 hectares. Caramut Conservation Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Caramut.

Getting around

Caramut has no train station; Sherwood Park Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 47 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Caramut. By car from Caramut, Hamilton is about 46 minutes away and Warrnambool about 48 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Caramut is Penshurst Swimming Pool in Penshurst, about 20 minutes' drive. For a library, Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake is about 26 minutes' drive from Caramut. The closest skate park to Caramut is Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake, about 27 minutes' drive. Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Caramut, about 46 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Caramut are Penshurst Rec Reserve Playground in Penshurst (19 min), Penshurst Swimming Pool in Penshurst (20 min), Penshurst Botanic Gardens Playground in Penshurst (20 min), Volcanoes Discovery Centre in Penshurst (20 min), Hawkesdale Swimming Pool in Hawkesdale (28 min) and Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Caramut.

Nearby suburbs

Penshurst, next to Caramut, has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Minjah, Minhamite, Hexham and Chatsworth also border Caramut.
