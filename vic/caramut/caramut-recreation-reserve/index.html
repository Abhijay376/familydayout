Caramut Recreation Reserve

Park in Caramut · Basic info

Caramut Recreation Reserve is a park in Caramut, in the Moyne Shire Council area, good for all ages. Post Office/Hamilton Hwy (coach) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 707 m away.
