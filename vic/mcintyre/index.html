McIntyre in the Loddon area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

McIntyre Historic and Cultural Features Reserve is the largest mapped park in McIntyre, at about 36 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in McIntyre. The closest to McIntyre is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 41 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside McIntyre, so most trips start by car. By car from McIntyre, Maryborough is about 50 minutes away, Bendigo about 51 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to McIntyre is Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly, about 26 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 50 minutes from McIntyre by car, with a library. The closest places for kids by road from McIntyre are Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (25 min), Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (26 min), Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (25 min), Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (25 min), Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (50 min) and the playground at Market Reserve in Carisbrook (55 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from McIntyre.
