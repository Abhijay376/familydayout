Walkerville South is in the South Gippsland area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Walkerville Coastal Reserve is the largest mapped park in Walkerville South, at about 49 hectares.

Getting around

Walkerville South has no train station; Yarragon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 73 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Walkerville South, so most trips start by car. By car from Walkerville South, Leongatha is about 56 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a beach, Diggers Island South Beach in Tarwin Lower is about 1 minutes' drive from Walkerville South. For a skate park, the skate park at Venus Bay Community Centre Reserve in Venus Bay is about 36 minutes' drive from Walkerville South. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Walkerville South, about 56 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Walkerville South are Diggers Island South Beach in Tarwin Lower (1 min), the playground at Walkerville Coastal Reserve in Walkerville North (22 min), Cape Liptrap in Tarwin Lower (28 min), the playground at RV Fisher Reserve in Tarwin Lower (28 min), Ride the Wild Goat in Fish Creek (42 min) and the skate park at Venus Bay Community Centre Reserve in Venus Bay (36 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Walkerville South.

Nearby suburbs
