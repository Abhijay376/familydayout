Bearii is in the Moira area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bearii, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids. Bearii does have 2 beaches.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Bearii. In Bearii you'll also find Top End Beach and Wasers Beach.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bearii. The closest to Bearii is Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line), about 54 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bearii, so most trips start by car. By car from Bearii, Shepparton is about 58 minutes away and Echuca about 72 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Nathalia Memorial Pool in Nathalia is about 30 minutes' drive from Bearii. For a library, Nathalia Library in Nathalia is about 28 minutes' drive from Bearii. For a skate park, Strathmerton Community Skate Park in Strathmerton is about 20 minutes' drive from Bearii. The closest cinema to Bearii is Cobram Cinema in Cobram, about 33 minutes' drive. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Bearii, about 58 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Bearii, the nearest places for kids by road are Strathmerton Community Skate Park in Strathmerton (20 min), Barmah Forest Heritge and Education Centre in Nathalia (28 min), Nathalia Library in Nathalia (28 min), the playground at Apex Park in Nathalia (28 min), Nathalia Memorial Pool in Nathalia (30 min) and Numurkah Library in Numurkah (31 min).

Best for

Beach days at Top End Beach and Wasers Beach.

Nearby suburbs

Yalca, next to Bearii, has a beach. Strathmerton, next to Bearii, has a skate park.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Bearii, without traffic.
