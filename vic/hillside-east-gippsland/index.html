Hillside (East Gippsland) is in the East Gippsland area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Hillside (East Gippsland), so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Hillside (East Gippsland). The closest to Hillside (East Gippsland) is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 10 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Hillside (East Gippsland), so most trips start by car. By car from Hillside (East Gippsland), Bairnsdale is about 16 minutes away and Lakes Entrance about 52 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Hillside (East Gippsland) is Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest library to Hillside (East Gippsland) is Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale, about 17 minutes' drive. For a beach, Boyfriend Beach in Forge Creek is about 29 minutes' drive from Hillside (East Gippsland). For a skate park, the skate park at Gilsenan Reserve in Paynesville is about 28 minutes' drive from Hillside (East Gippsland). The closest cinema to Hillside (East Gippsland) is Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Hillside (East Gippsland) is Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow, about 23 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bairnsdale, about 16 minutes from Hillside (East Gippsland) by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Hillside (East Gippsland), the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Amy Shand Park in Lindenow (14 min), Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale (15 min), Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale (16 min), Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale (17 min), Krowathunkoolong Keeping Place in Bairnsdale (17 min) and Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale (17 min).

Best for

Nearby suburbs

Bairnsdale, next to Hillside (East Gippsland), has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Wy Yung, next to Hillside (East Gippsland), has 3 playgrounds. Next door to Hillside (East Gippsland), Lindenow has 1 playground. Goon Nure and Calulu also border Hillside (East Gippsland).
