Landmarks

Ellerslie Memorial Park & Grassland is the largest mapped park in Ellerslie, at about 8 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Ellerslie.

Getting around

There's no train station in Ellerslie. The closest to Ellerslie is Terang Railway Station (V/Line), about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Warrnambool - Mortlake route stop in Ellerslie. By car from Ellerslie, Warrnambool is about 31 minutes away and Colac about 78 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Ellerslie is Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest library to Ellerslie is Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake, about 12 minutes' drive. For a beach, Shelly Beach in Warrnambool is about 35 minutes' drive from Ellerslie. For a skate park, Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake is about 13 minutes' drive from Ellerslie. For a cinema, Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool is about 31 minutes' drive from Ellerslie. For an indoor play centre, The Bowl in Warrnambool is about 31 minutes' drive from Ellerslie. For a zoo or wildlife park, Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee is about 27 minutes' drive from Ellerslie. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 31 minutes from Ellerslie by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Ellerslie are Market Square Playground in Mortlake (11 min), Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake (12 min), Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake (13 min), the playground at Tea Tree Lake Reserve in Mortlake (13 min), Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (27 min) and Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Ellerslie.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Ellerslie, Mortlake has 2 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Framlingham East and Ballangeich also border Ellerslie.
