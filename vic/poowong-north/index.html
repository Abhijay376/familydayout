Poowong North is in the South Gippsland area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Poowong North is Lang Lang River, Topiram Streamside Reserve, covering about 6 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Poowong North. The closest to Poowong North is Drouin Railway Station (V/Line), about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Warragul Station - Poowong East via Drouin, Ripplebrook route stop in Poowong North. By car from Poowong North, Warragul is about 24 minutes away, Leongatha about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong is about 7 minutes' drive from Poowong North. For a library, Poowong library in Poowong is about 7 minutes' drive from Poowong North. The closest skate park to Poowong North is Nyora skate park in Nyora, about 16 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 23 minutes' drive from Poowong North. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 23 minutes' drive from Poowong North. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Poowong North is Wildlife Trail in Tynong North, about 36 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Warragul, about 24 minutes from Poowong North by car, with 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Poowong North are Poowong library in Poowong (7 min), Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong (7 min), the playground at Sunnyside Park in Loch (13 min), Nyora skate park in Nyora (16 min), the playground at Nyora Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Nyora (16 min) and Drouin Skate Park in Drouin (19 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Poowong North.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Poowong North, Poowong has a pool and a library. Nyora, next to Poowong North, has 3 playgrounds and a skate park. Hallora, next to Poowong North, has 1 playground. Heath Hill and Athlone also border Poowong North.
