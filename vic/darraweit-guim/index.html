Darraweit Guim is in the Macedon Ranges area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Darraweit Guim is Boyds Creek Streamside Reserve, covering about 1 hectares. Darraweit Streamside Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Darraweit Guim.

Getting around

Darraweit Guim has no train station; Wallan Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 10.0 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Darraweit Guim, so most trips start by car. By car from Darraweit Guim, Broadmeadows is about 43 minutes away, Seymour about 49 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 61 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore is about 18 minutes' drive from Darraweit Guim. For a library, Wallan Library in Wallan is about 11 minutes' drive from Darraweit Guim. For a skate park, BMX Track in Wallan is about 12 minutes' drive from Darraweit Guim. For a cinema, United Cinemas in Craigieburn is about 32 minutes' drive from Darraweit Guim. For an indoor play centre, Timezone in Craigieburn is about 32 minutes' drive from Darraweit Guim. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Darraweit Guim is Funfields in Whittlesea, about 31 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Broadmeadows, about 43 minutes from Darraweit Guim by car, with 12 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Darraweit Guim, the nearest places for kids by road are Silvertop Close Playground in Wallan (9 min), Roulston Way Playground in Wallan (9 min), Wallan Library in Wallan (11 min), BMX Track in Wallan (12 min), Wallan Park Skate Park in Wallan (12 min) and Tramway Heritage Centre in Bylands (14 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Darraweit Guim.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Darraweit Guim, Wallan has 13 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Beveridge, next to Darraweit Guim, has 4 playgrounds and a skate park. Chintin, Bylands and Bolinda also border Darraweit Guim.
