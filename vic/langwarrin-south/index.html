Landmarks

Robinsons Road Reserve is the largest mapped park in Langwarrin South, at about 1 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Langwarrin South. For walks and rides, Peninsula Link Trail and Frankston-Baxter Trail cross Langwarrin South.

Getting around

Langwarrin South has no train station; Baxter Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 1.9 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 776 stops in Langwarrin South. By car from Langwarrin South, Frankston is about 8 minutes away, Cranbourne about 17 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 49 minutes, without traffic.
