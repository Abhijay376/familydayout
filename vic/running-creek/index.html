Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Running Creek in the Alpine area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Running Creek. The closest to Running Creek is Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line), about 50 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Albury - Mt Beauty via Baranduda and Tawonga South route stop in Running Creek. By car from Running Creek, Bright is about 44 minutes away and Wodonga about 48 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Running Creek is the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford, about 34 minutes' drive. For a library, Mt Beauty Library in Mount Beauty is about 25 minutes' drive from Running Creek. For a beach, Pebble Beach in Tawonga South is about 22 minutes' drive from Running Creek. The closest skate park to Running Creek is Mount Beauty BMX Bike Track in Mount Beauty, about 25 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Sun Cinema Bright in Bright is about 45 minutes' drive from Running Creek. Bright is the closest bigger centre to Running Creek, about 44 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Running Creek are Pebble Beach in Tawonga South (22 min), Mt Beauty Library in Mount Beauty (25 min), Mount Beauty BMX Bike Track in Mount Beauty (25 min), Skate Park in Yackandandah (34 min), the playground at Sir Isaac Isaacs Park in Yackandandah (34 min) and The Old School Museum in Myrtleford (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Running Creek.
