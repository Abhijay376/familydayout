Tarrone is in the Moyne area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Tarrone, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Tarrone has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 30 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tarrone, so most trips start by car. By car from Tarrone, Warrnambool is about 38 minutes away and Hamilton about 61 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tarrone is Hawkesdale Swimming Pool in Hawkesdale, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest library to Tarrone is Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool, about 39 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Tarrone is Pea Soup Beach in Port Fairy, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Tarrone is the skate park at Russell Clark Reserve in Port Fairy, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Tarrone is Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Tarrone is The Bowl in Warrnambool, about 39 minutes' drive. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Tarrone, about 38 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Tarrone are the playground at Kirkstall Recreation Reserve in Kirkstall (17 min), Limestone Gallery in Port Fairy (21 min), Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy (21 min), the skate park at Russell Clark Reserve in Port Fairy (22 min), Port Fairy Skate and Play space in Port Fairy (22 min) and Pea Soup Beach in Port Fairy (23 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tarrone.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Tarrone, Port Fairy has 2 playgrounds, a beach and a cinema. Kirkstall, next to Tarrone, has 1 playground. Toolong and Orford also border Tarrone.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Tarrone, without traffic.
