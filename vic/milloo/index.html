Milloo is in the Campaspe area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Milloo, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Milloo. The closest to Milloo is Dingee Railway Station (V/Line), about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Milloo, so most trips start by car. By car from Milloo, Echuca is about 41 minutes away, Bendigo about 47 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Lockington Pool in Lockington is about 16 minutes' drive from Milloo. Echuca is the closest bigger centre to Milloo, about 41 minutes away by car, and has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Milloo, the nearest places for kids by road are Lockington Pool in Lockington (16 min), Lockington District Swimming Pool in Lockington (16 min), the playground at R J Phelan Reserve in Mitiamo (20 min), East Loddon Historical Society in Mitiamo (20 min), the playground at Apex Park in Rochester (27 min) and Campaspe Run Rural Discovery Centre in Elmore (28 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Milloo.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Milloo, Lockington has a pool. Drummartin, Dingee and Diggora also border Milloo.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Milloo, without traffic.
