Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Devenish.

Getting around

Devenish has no train station; Benalla Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 25 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Devenish. By car from Devenish, Benalla is about 30 minutes away and Shepparton about 47 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla is about 29 minutes' drive from Devenish. For a library, Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga is about 40 minutes' drive from Devenish. For a skate park, Pump Track in Benalla is about 31 minutes' drive from Devenish. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Devenish is Tunzafun in Yarrawonga, about 42 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Benalla, about 30 minutes from Devenish by car, with 5 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Devenish are the playground at Tungamah Lions Park in Tungamah (30 min), the playground at CWA Gardens in Dookie (20 min), Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (29 min), Benalla Costume and Pioneer Museum in Benalla (30 min), Pump Track in Benalla (31 min) and Benalla Art Gallery in Benalla (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Devenish.

Nearby suburbs

Lake Rowan, Goorambat, Bungeet West and Boxwood also border Devenish.
