Alberton is in the Wellington area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Jack Crew Memorial Picnic Area (Albert River Reserve) is the largest mapped park in Alberton, at about 4 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Alberton. The Great Southern Rail Trail and Tarra Trail all pass through Alberton.

Getting around

Alberton has no train station; Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 47 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Alberton. By car from Alberton, Sale is about 63 minutes away, Leongatha about 67 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Yarram Public Pool in Yarram is about 6 minutes' drive from Alberton. The closest library to Alberton is Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram, about 5 minutes' drive. For a beach, Chinaman Beach in Wilsons Promontory is about 28 minutes' drive from Alberton. The closest skate park to Alberton is the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Alberton are the playground at Memorial Park in Yarram (5 min), Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram (5 min), Yarram Public Pool in Yarram (6 min), the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram (7 min), Yarram Historical Society Museum in Yarram (7 min) and Port Albert Maritime Museum in Port Albert (8 min).

Best for

Free days on the Great Southern Rail Trail and in Alberton's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Yarram, next to Alberton, has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Hedley, Gelliondale and Alberton West also border Alberton.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Alberton, without traffic.
