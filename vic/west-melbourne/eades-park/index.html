Eades Park

Park in West Melbourne · Basic info

Eades Park is a park in West Melbourne, in the Melbourne City Council area, good for all ages. King St/Dudley St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 311 m away with a baby change table.
