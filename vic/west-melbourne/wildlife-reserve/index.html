Park in West Melbourne · Basic info

Wildlife Reserve is a park in West Melbourne, in the Melbourne City Council area, good for all ages. Kensington Rd/Dynon Rd (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1168 m away.
