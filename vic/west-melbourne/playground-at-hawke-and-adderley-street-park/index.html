Playground at Hawke and Adderley Street Park

Playground in West Melbourne · Official details

Playground at Hawke and Adderley Street Park is a public playground in West Melbourne, in the Melbourne City area. The Royal Park to Docklands passes about 50 m away, and Embassy Cafe is about 280 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 510 m away, and trains stop at North Melbourne Railway Station, a 3-minute walk away.
