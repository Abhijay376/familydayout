Getting around

Winchelsea South has no train station; Winchelsea Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Winchelsea South, so most trips start by car. By car from Winchelsea South, Colac is about 38 minutes away, Torquay about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Winchelsea South is Lorne Sea Baths in Lorne, about 33 minutes' drive. The closest library to Winchelsea South is Torquay Library in Torquay, about 39 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Winchelsea South is Lorne Beach in Lorne, about 33 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Birregurra Skate Park in Birregurra is about 20 minutes' drive from Winchelsea South. For a cinema, Lorne Theatre in Lorne is about 32 minutes' drive from Winchelsea South. The closest indoor play centre to Winchelsea South is Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac, about 38 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Live Wire Park in Lorne is about 34 minutes' drive from Winchelsea South. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Winchelsea South, about 38 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Winchelsea South are the playground at Modewarre Recreation Reserve in Modewarre (20 min), Birregurra Skate Park in Birregurra (20 min), Moriac Playground in Moriac (27 min), Moriac Skate Park in Moriac (27 min), Lorne Theatre in Lorne (32 min) and Lorne Sea Baths in Lorne (33 min).
