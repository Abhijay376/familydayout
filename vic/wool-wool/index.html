Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Wool Wool in the Corangamite area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wool Wool. The closest to Wool Wool is Colac Railway Station (V/Line), about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wool Wool, so most trips start by car. By car from Wool Wool, Colac is about 43 minutes away, Geelong about 2 hours and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest cinema to Wool Wool is Colac Cinemas in Colac, about 43 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Wool Wool is Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac, about 44 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 43 minutes from Wool Wool by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Wool Wool, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Red Rock Scenic Reserve in Alvie (23 min), the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (42 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (43 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (44 min), Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (46 min) and the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (47 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wool Wool.

Nearby suburbs

Alvie, next to Wool Wool, has 1 playground. Dreeite South, Dreeite and Corunnun also border Wool Wool.
