Chetwynd in the West Wimmera area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Brimboal State Forest is the largest mapped park in Chetwynd, at about 6,677 hectares. Ganoo Ganoo Bushland Reserve and Steep Bank Creek Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Chetwynd also has Bourkes Bridge Streamside Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Chetwynd.

Getting around

There's no train station in Chetwynd. The closest to Chetwynd is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 135 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Chetwynd, so most trips start by car. By car from Chetwynd, Hamilton is about 82 minutes away and Portland about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Chetwynd is Casterton Pool in Casterton, about 43 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Chetwynd are Bake House Studio in Harrow (35 min), Harrow Discovery Centre in Harrow (36 min), the playground at Lions Park in Edenhope (37 min), Henley Park Playground in Edenhope (38 min), Casterton Pool in Casterton (43 min) and Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral (58 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Chetwynd.

Nearby suburbs

Kadnook, Dergholm, Culla and Connewirricoo also border Chetwynd.
