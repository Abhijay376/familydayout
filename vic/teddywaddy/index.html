Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Teddywaddy, in the Buloke area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Teddywaddy is Charlton West I150 Bushland Reserve, covering about 35 hectares. Teddywaddy I153 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Teddywaddy has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 73 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Teddywaddy. By car from Teddywaddy, Bendigo is about 1.5 hours away and Swan Hill about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Teddywaddy is Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Teddywaddy is Rex Theatre in Charlton, about 8 minutes' drive. From Teddywaddy, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Gordon Park in Charlton (8 min), Rex Theatre in Charlton (8 min), Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (8 min), the playground at Rotary Park in Charlton (8 min), Willandra Museum in Wycheproof (15 min) and Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof (17 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Teddywaddy.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Teddywaddy, Charlton has 3 playgrounds and a cinema. Next door to Teddywaddy, Wycheproof has 1 playground and a pool. Glenloth also borders Teddywaddy.
