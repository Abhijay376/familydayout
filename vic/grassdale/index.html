Grassdale is in the Glenelg area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Grassdale is Winyayung State Forest, covering about 1,384 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Grassdale. The closest to Grassdale is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 98 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Grassdale, so most trips start by car. By car from Grassdale, Hamilton is about 31 minutes away and Portland about 55 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Grassdale is Merino Swimming Pool in Merino, about 14 minutes' drive. For a library, Hamilton Library in Hamilton is about 31 minutes' drive from Grassdale. The closest skate park to Grassdale is coleraine skate park in Coleraine, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Grassdale is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 31 minutes' drive. Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Grassdale, about 31 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Grassdale are the playground at Lions Park in Merino (13 min), Merino Swimming Pool in Merino (14 min), Branxholme Rec Reserve Playground in Branxholme (24 min), coleraine skate park in Coleraine (28 min), Hamilton History Centre in Hamilton (30 min) and Mud Gallery in Hamilton (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Grassdale.

Nearby suburbs

Merino, next to Grassdale, has 1 playground and a pool. Next door to Grassdale, Branxholme has 1 playground. Hotspur, Digby and Condah also border Grassdale.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Grassdale, without traffic.
