Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Dooboobetic in the Buloke area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Dooboobetic has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 89 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dooboobetic, so most trips start by car. By car from Dooboobetic, Stawell is about 83 minutes away and Maryborough about 87 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Dooboobetic is Donald Swimming Pool in Donald, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Dooboobetic is Donald BMX Track in Donald, about 22 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 19 minutes' drive from Dooboobetic. The closest places for kids by road from Dooboobetic are Donald BMX Track in Donald (22 min), the playground at Gordon Park in Charlton (19 min), Rex Theatre in Charlton (19 min), Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (19 min), the playground at Rotary Park in Charlton (19 min) and Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (23 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Dooboobetic.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Dooboobetic, Charlton has 3 playgrounds and a cinema. Donald, next to Dooboobetic, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Coonooer West also borders Dooboobetic.
