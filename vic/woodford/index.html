Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Woodford, in the Warrnambool area.

Landmarks

Jellie Reserve is the largest mapped park in Woodford, at about 10 hectares. Jubilee Park / Wurrumbit Birrng Yaar and Mechanics Institute Park are the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Woodford.

Getting around

Woodford has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 7.7 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Woodford, so most trips start by car. By car from Woodford, Warrnambool is about 11 minutes away and Hamilton about 74 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool is about 14 minutes' drive from Woodford. The closest library to Woodford is Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Woodford is Shelly Beach in Warrnambool, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Woodford is Koroit Youth Space in Koroit, about 11 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool is about 11 minutes' drive from Woodford. For an indoor play centre, The Bowl in Warrnambool is about 12 minutes' drive from Woodford. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Woodford is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 21 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 11 minutes from Woodford by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Woodford are the playground at Bushfield Recreation Reserve in Bushfield (5 min), the playground at Witham Park in Warrnambool (7 min), Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool (11 min), Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool (12 min), Warrnambool Art Gallery in Warrnambool (12 min) and The Bowl in Warrnambool (12 min).
