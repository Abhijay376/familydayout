Getting around

There's no train station in Biggara. The closest to Biggara is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 101 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Biggara, so most trips start by car. By car from Biggara, Wodonga is about 2.5 hours away and Wangaratta about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Corryong Elyne Mitchell Library in Corryong is about 44 minutes' drive from Biggara. The closest cinema to Biggara is Corryong Cinema in Corryong, about 44 minutes' drive. From Biggara, the nearest places for kids by road are Corryong Cinema in Corryong (44 min), Corryong Elyne Mitchell Library in Corryong (44 min) and Corryong Public swimming pool in Corryong (46 min).
