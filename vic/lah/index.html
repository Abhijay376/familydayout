Lah is in the Yarriambiack area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Lah, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Lah has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 136 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lah, so most trips start by car. By car from Lah, Horsham is about 56 minutes away and Stawell about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Minyip Swimming Pool in Minyip is about 35 minutes' drive from Lah. The nearest larger centre is Horsham, about 56 minutes from Lah by car, with 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Lah, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Brim Rec Reserve in Brim (7 min), Historical Centre in Warracknabeal (12 min), Lions Playground in Warracknabeal (12 min), Wheatlands Warracknabeal Agricultural Machinery Museum in Warracknabeal (13 min), Minyip Swimming Pool in Minyip (35 min) and Hopetoun Swimming Pool in Hopetoun (38 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lah.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Lah, Warracknabeal has 5 playgrounds. Brim, next to Lah, has 1 playground.
