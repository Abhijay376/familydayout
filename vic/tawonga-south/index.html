Tawonga South is in the Alpine area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Tawonga South, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids. Tawonga South does have a beach.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Tawonga South. In Tawonga South you'll also find Pebble Beach. The Bright - Mount Beauty and Kiewa River Trail all pass through Tawonga South.

Getting around

Tawonga South has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 74 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Albury - Mt Beauty via Baranduda and Tawonga South route stop in Tawonga South. V/Line coaches stop in Tawonga South. By car from Tawonga South, Bright is about 26 minutes away and Wodonga about 68 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bright River Pool in Bright is about 26 minutes' drive from Tawonga South. The closest library to Tawonga South is Mt Beauty Library in Mount Beauty, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Tawonga South is Mount Beauty BMX Bike Track in Mount Beauty, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Tawonga South is Sun Cinema Bright in Bright, about 27 minutes' drive. Bright is the closest bigger centre to Tawonga South, about 26 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Tawonga South, the nearest places for kids by road are Mount Beauty BMX Bike Track in Mount Beauty (5 min), Mt Beauty Library in Mount Beauty (5 min), Bright River Pool in Bright (26 min), Bright Art Gallery in Bright (26 min), Bright Splash Park in Bright (27 min) and Sun Cinema Bright in Bright (27 min).

Best for

Beach days at Pebble Beach. Longer rides and walks from Tawonga South on the Bright - Mount Beauty.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Tawonga South, Mount Beauty has 1 playground, a library and a skate park. Next door to Tawonga South, Hotham Heights has 1 playground. Next door to Tawonga South, Falls Creek has 1 playground. Next door to Tawonga South, Bogong has 1 playground. Tawonga, Smoko, Germantown and Freeburgh also border Tawonga South.
