Upton Hill is in the Strathbogie area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Wallaby Gully (Upton Hill) Flora Reserve is the largest mapped park in Upton Hill, at about 131 hectares. Monea South I42 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Upton Hill has no train station; Avenel Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Upton Hill, so most trips start by car. By car from Upton Hill, Seymour is about 47 minutes away, Benalla about 68 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa is about 34 minutes' drive from Upton Hill. For a library, Euroa Library in Euroa is about 35 minutes' drive from Upton Hill. For a skate park, Euroa Skate Park in Euroa is about 34 minutes' drive from Upton Hill. For a cinema, Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa is about 34 minutes' drive from Upton Hill. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 47 minutes from Upton Hill by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Upton Hill, the nearest places for kids by road are Longwood Recreation Reserve Playground in Longwood (26 min), Creightons Creek Recreation Reserve Playground in Creightons Creek (43 min), Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa (34 min), Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa (34 min), Farmers Arms Hotel Museum in Euroa (34 min) and Euroa Skate Park in Euroa (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Upton Hill.
