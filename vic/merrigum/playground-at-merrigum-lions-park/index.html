Playground at Merrigum Lions Park

Playground in Merrigum · Official details

Playground at Merrigum Lions Park is a public playground in Merrigum, in the Greater Shepparton City area. Community Swimming Pool is about 270 m away, and Playground at Judd Park is another option about 330 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 280 m away, and bus Bendigo Station - Shepparton Station via Kyabram stops at Caltex Service Station/Waverley Ave, a 6-minute walk away.
