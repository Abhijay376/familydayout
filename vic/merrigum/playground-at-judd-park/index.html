Playground at Judd Park

Playground in Merrigum · Official details

Playground at Judd Park in Merrigum is a neighbourhood playground in the Greater Shepparton City area. Community Swimming Pool is about 60 m away, and Playground at Merrigum Lions Park is another option about 330 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 70 m from the playground, and bus Bendigo Station - Shepparton Station via Kyabram stops at Caltex Service Station/Waverley Ave, a 2-minute walk away.
