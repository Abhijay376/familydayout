Goldie is in the Macedon Ranges area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Goldie Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Goldie, at about 8 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Goldie. The closest to Goldie is Kilmore East Station (V/Line), about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Goldie, so most trips start by car. By car from Goldie, Kyneton is about 53 minutes away, Seymour about 55 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 85 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Goldie is Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest library to Goldie is Kilmore Library in Kilmore, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Goldie is Kilmore Skate Park in Kilmore, about 23 minutes' drive. Kyneton is the closest bigger centre to Goldie, about 53 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds and a library. From Goldie, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Moore Court Reserve in Kilmore (22 min), the playground at Hudson Park in Kilmore (22 min), Kilmore Skate Park in Kilmore (23 min), Kilmore Library in Kilmore (23 min), Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore (23 min) and Romsey Library in Romsey (25 min).
