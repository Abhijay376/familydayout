Our data lists 4 parks and reserves in Towong, in the Towong area.

Landmarks

Lighthouse Crossing, Murray River Reserve is the largest mapped park in Towong, at about 9 hectares. Corryong Creek K35 Streamside Reserve and Towong Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Towong include Mount Elliot. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Towong. Picnic areas in Towong include Mount Elliot.

Getting around

Towong has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 96 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Albury - Corryong via Walwa route stop in Towong. By car from Towong, Wodonga is about 2 hours away and Wangaratta about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Corryong Public swimming pool in Corryong is about 12 minutes' drive from Towong. The closest library to Towong is Corryong Elyne Mitchell Library in Corryong, about 11 minutes' drive. For a beach, Pebble Beach in Walwa is about 35 minutes' drive from Towong. The closest cinema to Towong is Corryong Cinema in Corryong, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Towong are Corryong Cinema in Corryong (11 min), Corryong Elyne Mitchell Library in Corryong (11 min), Corryong Public swimming pool in Corryong (12 min), The Spotted Goanna in Walwa (29 min) and Pebble Beach in Walwa (35 min).
