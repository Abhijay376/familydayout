Greta is in the Wangaratta area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Greta, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Greta has no train station; Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Greta, so most trips start by car. By car from Greta, Wangaratta is about 25 minutes away and Benalla about 33 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Greta is Moore than Swimming in Wangaratta, about 22 minutes' drive. For a library, Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta is about 26 minutes' drive from Greta. For a skate park, the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta is about 24 minutes' drive from Greta. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 26 minutes' drive from Greta. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Greta, about 25 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Greta are the playground at Apex Park in Moyhu (9 min), Glenrowan Museum in Glenrowan (12 min), Cobb & Co in Glenrowan (12 min), Moore than Swimming in Wangaratta (22 min), the playground at Dodsworth Street Reserve in Wangaratta (24 min) and Splashpark in Wangaratta (23 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Greta.

Nearby suburbs

Moyhu, next to Greta, has 1 playground. Docker also borders Greta.
