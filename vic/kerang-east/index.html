Kerang East in the Gannawarra area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Kerang East is Johnson Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 721 hectares. Salter Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kerang East. The closest to Kerang East is Kerang Railway Station (V/Line), about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kerang East, so most trips start by car. By car from Kerang East, Swan Hill is about 58 minutes away and Echuca about 66 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Swimming Pool in Kerang is about 12 minutes' drive from Kerang East. The closest library to Kerang East is Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang, about 11 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna is about 15 minutes' drive from Kerang East. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Kerang East, about 58 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Kerang East are Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (11 min), Swimming Pool in Kerang (12 min), Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna (15 min), the playground at C L King Memorial Park in Cohuna (16 min), the playground at Cohuna Apex Park in Cohuna (15 min) and Cohuna Pool in Cohuna (16 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Kerang East.

Nearby suburbs

Kerang, next to Kerang East, has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Koroop also borders Kerang East.
