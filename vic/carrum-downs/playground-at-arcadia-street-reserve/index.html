Playground at Arcadia Street Reserve

Playground in Carrum Downs · Basic info

Playground at Arcadia Street Reserve is a public playground in Carrum Downs, in the Frankston City area. Hong's Asian Kitchen is about 230 m away if you need food, and Playground at Allied Reserve is another option about 430 m away. There's a public toilet about 480 m away, and bus 832 stops at Loretta St/Gamble Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
