Playground at Carrum Downs Reserve

Playground in Carrum Downs · Basic info

Playground at Carrum Downs Reserve is a public playground in Carrum Downs, in the Frankston City area. Playground on Polaris Court is another option about 390 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 110 m from the playground, and buses 832 and 833 stop at Carrum Downs Reserve/Wedge Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
