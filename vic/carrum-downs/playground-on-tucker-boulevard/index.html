Playground on Tucker Boulevard

Playground in Carrum Downs · Basic info

Playground on Tucker Boulevard is a public playground in Carrum Downs, in the Frankston City area. Guzman y Gomez is about 460 m away if you need food, and Playground at Lavender Hills Reserve is another option about 290 m away. There's a public toilet about 600 m away, and buses 778 and 901 stop at Tuxen Ave/Frankston-Dandenong Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
