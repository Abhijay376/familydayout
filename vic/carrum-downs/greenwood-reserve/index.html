Greenwood Reserve

Park in Carrum Downs · Basic info

Greenwood Reserve is a park in Carrum Downs, in the Frankston City Council area, good for all ages. Thornbill Dr/Lyrebird Dr (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 992 m away with a baby change table.
