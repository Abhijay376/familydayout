Bayside Rock Climbing

Climbing in Carrum Downs · Official details

Bayside Rock Climbing is a climbing in Carrum Downs, in the Frankston City Council area, best for kids 5 and up and teens. Tuxen Ave/Frankston-Dandenong Rd (bus) is a 14-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1485 m away.
