The Pines Flora and Fauna Reserve

Park in Carrum Downs · Official details

The Pines Flora and Fauna Reserve is a park in Carrum Downs, in the Frankston City Council area, good for all ages. Flinders Christian Community College/Ballarto Rd (bus) is a 11-minute walk away and dogs are allowed on a lead.

Questions parents ask

Where is The Pines Flora and Fauna Reserve?
The Pines Flora and Fauna Reserve is in Carrum Downs, Victoria.
Is The Pines Flora and Fauna Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to The Pines Flora and Fauna Reserve?
Yes, on a lead.
How do I get to The Pines Flora and Fauna Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Flinders Christian Community College/Ballarto Rd (routes 760, 832), a 11-minute walk.
