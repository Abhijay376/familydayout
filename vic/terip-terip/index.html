Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Terip Terip in the Murrindindi area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Terip Terip. The closest to Terip Terip is Euroa Railway Station (V/Line), about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Terip Terip, so most trips start by car. By car from Terip Terip, Mansfield is about 67 minutes away, Seymour about 73 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest skate park to Terip Terip is BMX Track in Caveat, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Terip Terip are BMX Track in Caveat (21 min), Creightons Creek Recreation Reserve Playground in Creightons Creek (22 min), Yarck Recreation Reserve Playground in Yarck (31 min), Farmers Arms Hotel Museum in Euroa (47 min), Euroa Skate Park in Euroa (48 min) and Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa (47 min).
