Barossa Avenue Reserve

Park in Vermont South · Basic info

Barossa Avenue Reserve is a park in Vermont South, in the Whitehorse City Council area, good for all ages. Morack Rd/Burwood Hwy (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 425 m away.
