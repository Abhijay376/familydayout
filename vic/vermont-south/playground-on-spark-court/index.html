Playground on Spark Court

Playground in Vermont South · Basic info

This is a public playground in Vermont South. The Healesville Freeway Reserve path (under construction) is about 370 m away, and Playground on Yalita Road is another option about 200 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 860 m from the playground, and buses 736 and 742 stop at Hanover Reserve/Hanover Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
