Playground at Terrara Park

Playground in Vermont South · Basic info

Playground at Terrara Park in Vermont South is a public playground in the Whitehorse City area. The Bellbird Track passes about 290 m away, and Playground at Barossa Avenue Reserve is another option about 360 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 120 m away, and bus 742 stops at Bellbird Dell Reserve/George Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
