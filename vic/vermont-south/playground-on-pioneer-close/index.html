Playground on Pioneer Close

Playground in Vermont South · Basic info

Playground on Pioneer Close in Vermont South is a public playground in the Whitehorse City area. The Dandenong Creek Trail passes about 120 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 390 m away. There's a public toilet about 500 m away, and buses 732, 736 and 967 stop at Morack Rd/Burwood Hwy, a 7-minute walk away.
