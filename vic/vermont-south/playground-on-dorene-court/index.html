Playground on Dorene Court

Playground in Vermont South · Basic info

Playground on Dorene Court in Vermont South is a public playground in the Whitehorse City area. The Healesville Freeway Reserve path (under construction) passes about 240 m away, and Playground on Oxford Grove is another option about 270 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.2 km away, and bus 765 stops at Michael Ct/Hampshire Rd, a 8-minute walk away.
