Tyrol Park

Park in Vermont South · Basic info

Tyrol Park is a park in Vermont South, in the Whitehorse City Council area, good for all ages. Vermont Ret Village/Burwood Hwy (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 721 m away.
