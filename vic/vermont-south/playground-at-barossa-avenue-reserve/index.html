Playground at Barossa Avenue Reserve

Playground in Vermont South · Basic info

Playground at Barossa Avenue Reserve is a public playground in Vermont South, in the Whitehorse City area. The Whitehorse NS10 is about 220 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 370 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 400 m from the playground, and buses 732, 736 and 967 stop at Morack Rd/Burwood Hwy, a 4-minute walk away.
