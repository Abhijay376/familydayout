Vermont South in the Whitehorse area has 20 playgrounds and 13 parks and reserves in our data. Vermont South also has a library.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Vermont South is Terrara Park, covering about 8 hectares. Billabong Park and ngarrak nakorang wilam park are the next largest. Vermont South also has Tyrol Park, Dalroy Bend Reserve and Wynne Court Reserve. Playgrounds around Vermont South include Wynne Court Playground, the playground at Tyrol Park and Winswood Close Playground. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Vermont South. Places to visit in Vermont South include Vermont South Library. Vermont South Shopping Centre is the local shopping centre. For walks and rides, Dandenong Creek Trail, Whitehorse NS10 and Healesville Freeway Reserve path (under construction) cross Vermont South. Our data lists 15 cafes and family restaurants in Vermont South for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Vermont South has no train station; Nunawading Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 3.4 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Tram 75 stops in Vermont South. Buses 732, 736, 742, 765, 902 and 967 stop in Vermont South. By car from Vermont South, Ringwood is about 10 minutes away, Box Hill about 12 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 25 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Aqualink Nunawading in Forest Hill is about 5 minutes' drive from Vermont South. The closest beach to Vermont South is Horse Arena in Abbotsford, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Vermont South is Blackburn Velodrome in Burwood East, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Vermont South is Hoyts in Forest Hill, about 6 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Zone Bowling in Forest Hill is about 5 minutes' drive from Vermont South.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 20 playgrounds across Vermont South. Rainy days at the library in Vermont South. Free days on the Dandenong Creek Trail and in Vermont South's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Vermont South, Glen Waverley has 35 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Wantirna South, next to Vermont South, has 25 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Wantirna, next to Vermont South, has 33 playgrounds. Burwood East, next to Vermont South, has 17 playgrounds, a cinema and a skate park. Forest Hill and Vermont also border Vermont South.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Vermont South, without traffic.
