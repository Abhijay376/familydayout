Winnap in the Glenelg area has 6 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Winnap is Stokes River (3) Streamside Reserve, covering about 430 hectares. Winnap State Forest and Wataepoolan State Forest are the next largest. Winnap also has Stokes River (1) Streamside Reserve, Lake Crawford Wildlife Reserve (hunting) and Burgess Swamp Wildlife Reserve (no hunting).

Getting around

Winnap has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 113 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Winnap, so most trips start by car. By car from Winnap, Portland is about 50 minutes away and Hamilton about 63 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Winnap is Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood, about 29 minutes' drive. For a library, Heywood Library in Heywood is about 29 minutes' drive from Winnap. Portland is the closest bigger centre to Winnap, about 50 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. From Winnap, the nearest places for kids by road are Dartmoor Coach House Museum in Dartmoor (6 min), Heywood Library in Heywood (29 min), the playground at Apex Park in Heywood (30 min), Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood (29 min), the playground at Heywood Youth Park in Heywood (30 min) and Merino Swimming Pool in Merino (33 min).
