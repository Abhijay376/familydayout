Mudgegonga is in the Alpine area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Mudgegonga is Mount Stanley Scenic Reserve, covering about 2,751 hectares.

Getting around

Mudgegonga has no train station; Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 43 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Albury - Wodonga - Myrtleford route stop in Mudgegonga. By car from Mudgegonga, Bright is about 41 minutes away and Wodonga about 43 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mudgegonga is the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest library to Mudgegonga is Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Mudgegonga is Sandy Beach in Beechworth, about 37 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Mudgegonga is Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Mudgegonga is Sun Cinema Bright in Bright, about 41 minutes' drive. Bright is the closest bigger centre to Mudgegonga, about 41 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Mudgegonga are the playground at Swinburne Park in Myrtleford (13 min), Cundy Park Playground in Myrtleford (13 min), Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford (13 min), Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford (13 min), The Old School Museum in Myrtleford (14 min) and the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford (15 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mudgegonga.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Mudgegonga, Myrtleford has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Glen Creek, Gapsted, Dederang and Bruarong also border Mudgegonga.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Mudgegonga, without traffic.
