Gundowring is in the Indigo area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Gundowring Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Gundowring, at about 5 hectares. Kergunyah Streamside Reserve and Gundowring Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Gundowring. The closest to Gundowring is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 40 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gundowring, so most trips start by car. By car from Gundowring, Wodonga is about 40 minutes away and Bright about 62 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Gundowring is Edge Aquatics in Baranduda, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest library to Gundowring is Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford, about 45 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Gundowring is Pebble Beach in Tawonga South, about 40 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Gundowring is Skate Park in Yackandandah, about 33 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana is about 35 minutes' drive from Gundowring. Wodonga is the closest bigger centre to Gundowring, about 40 minutes away by car, and has 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Gundowring are the playground at Banksia Park in Tangambalanga (26 min), Skate Park in Yackandandah (33 min), the playground at Sir Isaac Isaacs Park in Yackandandah (33 min), Skate Park in Baranduda (31 min), Edge Aquatics in Baranduda (32 min) and Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Gundowring.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Gundowring, Eskdale has a pool. Next door to Gundowring, Tangambalanga has 2 playgrounds. Kiewa, Kergunyah South and Kergunyah also border Gundowring.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Gundowring, without traffic.
