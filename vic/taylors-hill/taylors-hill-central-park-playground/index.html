Taylors Hill Central Park Playground

Playground in Taylors Hill · Basic info

Taylors Hill Central Park Playground in Taylors Hill is a public playground in the Melton City area. The Hume Drive Trail passes about 330 m away, and Playground at Homestead Park is another option about 400 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.2 km away, and bus 462 stops at Homestead Park/Calder Park Dr, a 4-minute walk away.
