Jindabyne/Maple Walkway

Park in Taylors Hill · Basic info

Jindabyne/Maple Walkway is a park in Taylors Hill, in the Melton City Council area, good for all ages. Evergreen Ct/Hume Dr (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1018 m away.
