Kirribilli Park Playground

Playground in Taylors Hill · Basic info

This is a public playground in Taylors Hill. Centennial Park Playground is another option about 320 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 940 m from the playground, and buses 460 and 943 stop at Kirribilli Bvd/Gourlay Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
