Taylors Hill Sports Park Playground

Playground in Taylors Hill · Basic info

Taylors Hill Sports Park Playground is a public playground in Taylors Hill, in the Melton City area. The Hume Drive Trail is about 350 m away, and for food, Wat The Pho is about 480 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 730 m from the playground, and buses 460 and 943 stop at Taylors Hill Bvd/Gourlay Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
