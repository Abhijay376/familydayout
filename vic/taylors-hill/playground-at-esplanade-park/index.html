Playground at Esplanade Park

Playground in Taylors Hill · Basic info

Playground at Esplanade Park is a public playground in Taylors Hill, in the Melton City area. Kirribilli Park Playground is another option about 480 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.3 km away, and buses 460 and 943 stop at Woodbury Ave/Gourlay Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
