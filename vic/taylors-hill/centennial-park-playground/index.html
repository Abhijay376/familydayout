Centennial Park Playground

Playground in Taylors Hill · Basic info

Centennial Park Playground is a public playground in Taylors Hill, in the Melton City area. The Hume Drive Trail is about 280 m away, and for food, Wat The Pho is about 270 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 610 m from the playground, and buses 461 and 464 stop at Vaucluse Bvd/Hume Dr, a 4-minute walk away.
