Playground at Jimin Reserve

Playground in Taylors Hill · Basic info

Playground at Jimin Reserve in Taylors Hill is a public playground in the Melton City area. The Hume Drive Trail is about 140 m away, and Bloomsbury Drive Reserve Playground is another option about 320 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and buses 461 and 464 stop at Calder Park Dr/Hume Dr, a 3-minute walk away.
