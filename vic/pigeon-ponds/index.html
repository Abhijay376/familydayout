Pigeon Ponds in the Southern Grampians area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Pigeon Ponds is Pigeon Ponds Recreation Reserve, covering about 9 hectares. Tarrayoukyan Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet and 2 off-leash dog parks in Pigeon Ponds.

Getting around

Pigeon Ponds has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 113 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Pigeon Ponds, so most trips start by car. By car from Pigeon Ponds, Hamilton is about 57 minutes away and Horsham about 89 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral is about 19 minutes' drive from Pigeon Ponds. The closest skate park to Pigeon Ponds is coleraine skate park in Coleraine, about 29 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 57 minutes from Pigeon Ponds by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Pigeon Ponds, the nearest places for kids by road are Harrow Discovery Centre in Harrow (14 min), Bake House Studio in Harrow (14 min), Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral (19 min), Glendinning Street Playground in Balmoral (19 min), coleraine skate park in Coleraine (29 min) and the playground at Queens Park in Coleraine (29 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Pigeon Ponds.

Nearby suburbs

Balmoral, next to Pigeon Ponds, has 1 playground and a pool. Culla, Coojar and Brit Brit also border Pigeon Ponds.
