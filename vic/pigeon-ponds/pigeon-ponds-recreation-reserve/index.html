Pigeon Ponds Recreation Reserve

Park in Pigeon Ponds · Official details

Pigeon Ponds Recreation Reserve is a park in Pigeon Ponds, in the Southern Grampians Shire Council area, good for all ages. The nearest public toilet is about 807 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed.
