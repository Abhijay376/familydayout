Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Churchill Island, in the Bass Coast area.

Landmarks

Churchill Island Heritage Farm is the largest mapped park in Churchill Island, at about 4 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Churchill Island. The closest to Churchill Island is Stony Point Railway Station (metro), about 17 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Churchill Island, so most trips start by car. By car from Churchill Island, Wonthaggi is about 36 minutes away, Leongatha about 66 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Wonthaggi is about 35 minutes' drive from Churchill Island. For a library, San Remo Library in San Remo is about 12 minutes' drive from Churchill Island. For a beach, The Colonnades in Cape Woolamai is about 13 minutes' drive from Churchill Island. The closest skate park to Churchill Island is the skate park at Wonthaggi Railway Historic Reserve in Kilcunda, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Churchill Island is Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi, about 35 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, A Maze'N Things in Cowes is about 18 minutes' drive from Churchill Island. The nearest larger centre is Wonthaggi, about 36 minutes from Churchill Island by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Churchill Island, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Graydens Reserve in Newhaven (10 min), the playground at Woolamai Park in Cape Woolamai (11 min), National Vietnam Veterans Museum in Newhaven (11 min), The Colonnades in Cape Woolamai (13 min), Mannings Launch in Cape Woolamai (12 min) and San Remo Library in San Remo (12 min).
