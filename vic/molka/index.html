Molka is in the Strathbogie area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Little Branjee Creek Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Molka, at about 5 hectares. Molka H67 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Molka. The closest to Molka is Euroa Railway Station (V/Line), about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Molka, so most trips start by car. By car from Molka, Shepparton is about 46 minutes away, Benalla about 56 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa is about 20 minutes' drive from Molka. The closest library to Molka is Euroa Library in Euroa, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Molka is Euroa Skate Park in Euroa, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Molka is Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa, about 20 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Emerald Bank Leisure Land in Kialla is about 38 minutes' drive from Molka. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 46 minutes from Molka by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Molka, the nearest places for kids by road are Bicentennial Park Playground in Euroa (18 min), Euroa Library in Euroa (20 min), Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa (20 min), the playground at Euroa Rotary Park in Euroa (20 min), Euroa Skate Park in Euroa (20 min) and Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa (20 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Molka.

Nearby suburbs

Euroa, next to Molka, has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Longwood, next to Molka, has 1 playground. Moorilim, Miepoll and Arcadia South also border Molka.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Molka, without traffic.
