Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Pomborneit North, in the Corangamite area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Pomborneit North is Lake Corangamite Lake Reserve, covering about 24,180 hectares. Pomborneit North Nature Conservation Reserve and Purrumbete North Lake Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

Pomborneit North has no train station; Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Pomborneit North, so most trips start by car. By car from Pomborneit North, Colac is about 29 minutes away, Warrnambool about 73 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac is about 31 minutes' drive from Pomborneit North. For a skate park, Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown is about 17 minutes' drive from Pomborneit North. For a cinema, Colac Cinemas in Colac is about 29 minutes' drive from Pomborneit North. For an indoor play centre, Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac is about 30 minutes' drive from Pomborneit North. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 29 minutes from Pomborneit North by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Pomborneit North are Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (17 min), the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve in Camperdown (26 min), the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (27 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (29 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (30 min) and Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Pomborneit North.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Pomborneit North, Weerite has 1 playground. Leslie Manor and Dreeite also border Pomborneit North.
