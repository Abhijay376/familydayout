Glenfalloch is in the Wellington area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Glenfalloch, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Glenfalloch. The closest to Glenfalloch is Rosedale Railway Station (V/Line), about 51 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glenfalloch, so most trips start by car. By car from Glenfalloch, Sale is about 74 minutes away and Traralgon about 76 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield is about 41 minutes' drive from Glenfalloch. The closest skate park to Glenfalloch is Pump Track in Heyfield, about 41 minutes' drive. From Glenfalloch, the nearest places for kids by road are Pump Track in Heyfield (41 min), Apex Park Playground in Heyfield (41 min), Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield (41 min), Heyfield and District Museum in Heyfield (42 min) and Graves Court - Perry Court - Wickham Court Playground in Heyfield (42 min).
