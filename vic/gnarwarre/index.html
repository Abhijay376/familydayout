Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Gnarwarre in the Surf Coast area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Gnarwarre. The closest to Gnarwarre is Waurn Ponds Railway Station (V/Line), about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gnarwarre, so most trips start by car. By car from Gnarwarre, Geelong is about 28 minutes away, Torquay about 35 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 82 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Gnarwarre is Leisurelink Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Grovedale, about 22 minutes' drive. For a library, Highton Library in Highton is about 21 minutes' drive from Gnarwarre. The closest beach to Gnarwarre is Eastern Beach in Geelong, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Gnarwarre is Moriac Skate Park in Moriac, about 21 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Reading Cinemas in Grovedale is about 23 minutes' drive from Gnarwarre. The closest indoor play centre to Gnarwarre is Leisurelink Restaurant in Highton, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Gnarwarre is Tiger Moth World in Torquay, about 33 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Geelong, about 28 minutes from Gnarwarre by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Gnarwarre are the playground at Modewarre Recreation Reserve in Modewarre (20 min), the playground at Mount Moriac Recreation Reserve in Mount Moriac (15 min), Moriac Skate Park in Moriac (21 min), the bmx track at Newling Reserve in Moriac (21 min), Highton Library in Highton (21 min) and Reading Cinemas in Grovedale (23 min).
