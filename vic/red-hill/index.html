Red Hill in the Mornington Peninsula area has 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Bald Hill Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Red Hill, at about 23 hectares. Kangerong Nature Conservation Reserve and Red Hill South Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Red Hill. For walks and rides, Red Hill Rail Trail and Wonga cross Red Hill. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Red Hill for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Red Hill has no train station; Bittern Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Red Hill, so most trips start by car. By car from Red Hill, Mornington is about 16 minutes away, Frankston about 23 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 65 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Yawa Aquatic Centre in Rosebud is about 14 minutes' drive from Red Hill. The closest library to Red Hill is Rosebud Library in Rosebud, about 14 minutes' drive. For a beach, Dromana Beach in Dromana is about 8 minutes' drive from Red Hill. For a skate park, Pump Track in Dromana is about 3 minutes' drive from Red Hill. For a cinema, Dromana Triple Drive-In Theatre in Dromana is about 5 minutes' drive from Red Hill. The closest indoor play centre to Red Hill is Top Fun Shack in Rosebud, about 14 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale is about 28 minutes' drive from Red Hill. Mornington is the closest bigger centre to Red Hill, about 16 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a library, indoor play and a beach.

Best for

Free days on the Red Hill Rail Trail and in Red Hill's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Dromana, next to Red Hill, has 7 playgrounds, a beach and a cinema. Arthurs Seat, next to Red Hill, has 3 playgrounds. Next door to Red Hill, Red Hill South has 1 playground. Main Ridge also borders Red Hill.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Red Hill, without traffic.
