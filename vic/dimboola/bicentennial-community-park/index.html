Bicentennial Community Park

Park in Dimboola · Basic info

Bicentennial Community Park is a park in Dimboola, in the Hindmarsh Shire Council area, good for all ages. Car Park Rear Toilet Block/Lloyd St (coach) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 110 m away with a baby change table.
