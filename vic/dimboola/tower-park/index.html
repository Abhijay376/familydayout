Tower Park

Park in Dimboola · Basic info

Tower Park is a park in Dimboola, in the Hindmarsh Shire Council area, good for all ages. Car Park Rear Toilet Block/Lloyd St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 70 m away with a baby change table.
