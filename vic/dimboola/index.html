Our data lists 1 playground and 13 parks and reserves in Dimboola, in the Hindmarsh area. On top of that, Dimboola has a library and a pool.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Dimboola is Snape Reserve, covering about 758 hectares. Wimmera River Heritage Area (future addition) Park and Loch Iel (Pink Lake) Lake Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Dimboola include Tower Park, Nine Creeks Reserve and Dimboola Flora Reserve. Our data shows 13 public toilets in Dimboola. In Dimboola you'll also find Dimboola Swimming Pool and Dimboola Library. For walks and rides, Wimmera River Discovery Trail crosses Dimboola.

Getting around

Dimboola has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 123 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Horsham - Kaniva via Dimboola and Horsham - Hopetoun via Dimboola routes stop in Dimboola. V/Line coaches stop in Dimboola. By car from Dimboola, Horsham is about 30 minutes away and Stawell about 84 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a skate park, Horsham Skate Park in Horsham is about 30 minutes' drive from Dimboola. For a cinema, Centre Cinemas in Horsham is about 30 minutes' drive from Dimboola. The nearest larger centre is Horsham, about 30 minutes from Dimboola by car, with 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Dimboola are the playground at Elbourne Park in Horsham (28 min), Playland in Horsham (29 min), Horsham Skate Park in Horsham (30 min), Centre Cinemas in Horsham (30 min), Wimmera-Mallee Pioneer Museum in Jeparit (29 min) and Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham (31 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the library in Dimboola. Free days on the Wimmera River Discovery Trail and in Dimboola's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Little Desert, Kalkee, Gerang Gerung and Cannum also border Dimboola.
