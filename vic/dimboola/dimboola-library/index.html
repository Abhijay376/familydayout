Dimboola Library

Library in Dimboola · Basic info

Dimboola Library is a library in Dimboola, in the Hindmarsh Shire Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Car Park Rear Toilet Block/Lloyd St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 40 m away with a baby change table.
