BMX track at H. D. Graham Reserve

Skate, BMX or pump track in Altona Meadows · Basic info

BMX track at H. D. Graham Reserve is a skate, bmx or pump track in Altona Meadows, in the Hobsons Bay City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Altona Sports Centre/Queen St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 242 m away.
