J. J. Stewart Reserve

Park in Altona Meadows · Basic info

J. J. Stewart Reserve is a park in Altona Meadows, in the Hobsons Bay City Council area, good for all ages. Kurrajong Dr/Victoria St (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 638 m away.
