Point Cook Coastal Park

Park in Altona Meadows · Official details

Point Cook Coastal Park is a park in Altona Meadows, in the Hobsons Bay City Council area, good for all ages. Altona Green Park/Victoria St (bus) is a 8-minute walk away and dogs are not allowed. Opening hours: April to September 8:00am-5:00pm; October to March 8:00am-7:00pm.

Questions parents ask

Where is Point Cook Coastal Park?
Point Cook Coastal Park is in Altona Meadows, Victoria.
Is Point Cook Coastal Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Point Cook Coastal Park?
No, dogs are not allowed.
How do I get to Point Cook Coastal Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Altona Green Park/Victoria St (routes 411, 412), a 8-minute walk.
