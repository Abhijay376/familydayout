Playground at Carinza Reserve

Playground in Altona Meadows · Official details

Playground at Carinza Reserve is a public playground in Altona Meadows, in the Hobsons Bay City area. The Skeleton Creek Trail passes about 110 m away, and Domino's is about 420 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 770 m away, and buses 496, 498 and 949 stop at Carinza Ave/Point Cook Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
