Playground at Bruce Comben Reserve

Playground in Altona Meadows · Basic info

Playground at Bruce Comben Reserve is a public playground in Altona Meadows, in the Hobsons Bay City area. The Skeleton Creek Trail is about 10 m away, and for food, McDonald's is about 340 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 440 m from the playground, and buses 496 and 498 stop at Carinza Ave/Point Cook Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
