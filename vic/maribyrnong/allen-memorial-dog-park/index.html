Allen Memorial Dog Park

Park in Maribyrnong · Basic info

Allen Memorial Dog Park is a park in Maribyrnong, in the Maribyrnong City Council area, good for all ages. Van Ness Ave/Raleigh Rd #43 (tram) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 588 m away.
