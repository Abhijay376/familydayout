Boulevard Plaza

Park in Maribyrnong · Basic info

Boulevard Plaza is a park in Maribyrnong, in the Maribyrnong City Council area, good for all ages. Bracken Ave/Edgewater Bvd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 944 m away.
