Playground at Village Green

Playground in Maribyrnong · Basic info

Playground at Village Green in Maribyrnong is a public playground in the Maribyrnong City area. The Maribyrnong River Trail is about 260 m away, and Funtopia is about 550 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 990 m from the playground, and buses 406 and 407 stop at Central Park Ave/Cordite Ave, a 5-minute walk away.
