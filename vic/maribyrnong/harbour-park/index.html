Harbour Park

Park in Maribyrnong · Basic info

Harbour Park is a park in Maribyrnong, in the Maribyrnong City Council area, good for all ages. Cormorant Pl/Cumberland Dr (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1022 m away.
