Paynesville Maritime Museum

Museum or gallery in Paynesville · Official details

Paynesville Maritime Museum is a museum or gallery in Paynesville, in the East Gippsland Shire Council area, best for school-age kids. Raymond Island Ferry Terminal/Devon Rd (coach) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 255 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: Sunday 14th June 10am - 12noon; Sunday 12th July 10am - 12noon; Sunday 9th August 10am - 12noon.
