Belgrave Heights is in the Yarra Ranges area, and our data lists 2 playgrounds and 2 parks and reserves in the suburb. Belgrave Heights also has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

Birdsland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Belgrave Heights, at about 91 hectares. Monbulk Creek, Belgrave Heights Streamside Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Belgrave Heights. Places to visit in Belgrave Heights include Southern Sherbrooke Historical Society. For walks and rides, Country (ngurrak barring | RidgeWalk) crosses Belgrave Heights.

Getting around

Belgrave Heights has no train station; Belgrave Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 1.9 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 697 stops in Belgrave Heights. By car from Belgrave Heights, Dandenong is about 21 minutes away, Cranbourne about 25 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 41 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Belgrave Heated Outdoor Swimming Pool in Belgrave is about 4 minutes' drive from Belgrave Heights. For a library, Belgrave Library in Belgrave is about 5 minutes' drive from Belgrave Heights. For a beach, Edithvale Beach in Edithvale is about 34 minutes' drive from Belgrave Heights. For a skate park, Upwey Skate Park in Upwey is about 8 minutes' drive from Belgrave Heights. The closest cinema to Belgrave Heights is Cameo Belgrave in Belgrave, about 5 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Little Rascals Play Centre in Ferntree Gully is about 11 minutes' drive from Belgrave Heights. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale is about 30 minutes' drive from Belgrave Heights. Dandenong is the closest bigger centre to Belgrave Heights, about 21 minutes away by car, and has 21 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Belgrave Heights. Free days on the Country (ngurrak barring | RidgeWalk) and in Belgrave Heights's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Belgrave Heights, Belgrave has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Belgrave Heights, Lysterfield has 8 playgrounds. Next door to Belgrave Heights, Tecoma has 1 playground. Next door to Belgrave Heights, Belgrave South has 1 playground.
