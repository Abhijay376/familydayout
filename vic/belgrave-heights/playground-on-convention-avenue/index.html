Playground on Convention Avenue

Playground in Belgrave Heights · Basic info

Playground on Convention Avenue is a public playground in Belgrave Heights, in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. Southern Sherbrooke Historical Society is about 450 m away, and Playground on Colby Drive is another option about 490 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 770 m from the playground, and bus 697 stops at The Crescent/Colby Dr, a 8-minute walk away.
