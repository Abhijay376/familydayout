Moora is in the Campaspe area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Moora, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Moora has no train station; Nagambie Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Moora, so most trips start by car. By car from Moora, Shepparton is about 48 minutes away, Echuca about 61 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Moora is Rushworth Pool in Rushworth, about 7 minutes' drive. For a library, Rushworth Library in Rushworth is about 7 minutes' drive from Moora. The closest skate park to Moora is the skate park at Lions Park in Rushworth, about 7 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram is about 37 minutes' drive from Moora. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 48 minutes from Moora by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Moora are Rushworth Museum in Rushworth (6 min), Rushworth Library in Rushworth (7 min), Rushworth Pool in Rushworth (7 min), the skate park at Lions Park in Rushworth (7 min), the playground at Murchison Youth Spot in Murchison (27 min) and the playground at Goulburn River Heritage River in Murchison (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Moora.

Nearby suburbs

Rushworth, next to Moora, has a pool, a library and a skate park. Colbinabbin, next to Moora, has 1 playground.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Moora, without traffic.
