Bengworden in the East Gippsland area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bengworden is Backwater Morass Gippsland Lakes Reserve, covering about 552 hectares. Bengworden Nature Conservation Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bengworden. The closest to Bengworden is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bengworden, so most trips start by car. By car from Bengworden, Bairnsdale is about 21 minutes away and Sale about 45 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale is about 20 minutes' drive from Bengworden. The closest library to Bengworden is Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Bengworden is Boyfriend Beach in Forge Creek, about 30 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Gilsenan Reserve in Paynesville is about 29 minutes' drive from Bengworden. For a cinema, Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale is about 21 minutes' drive from Bengworden. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Bengworden is Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow, about 28 minutes' drive. Bairnsdale is the closest bigger centre to Bengworden, about 21 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Bengworden are Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale (20 min), Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale (21 min), Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale (22 min), Krowathunkoolong Keeping Place in Bairnsdale (22 min), Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale (22 min) and Bairnsdale Museum in Bairnsdale (22 min).
