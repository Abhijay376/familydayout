Tesbury is in the Corangamite area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Tesbury, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tesbury. The closest to Tesbury is Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line), about 7.7 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tesbury, so most trips start by car. By car from Tesbury, Colac is about 48 minutes away, Warrnambool about 72 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest skate park to Tesbury is Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown, about 21 minutes' drive. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Tesbury, about 48 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Tesbury are Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (21 min), the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve in Camperdown (24 min), the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (49 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (49 min), the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (47 min) and Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum (53 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tesbury.

Nearby suburbs

Camperdown, next to Tesbury, has 3 playgrounds and a skate park.
