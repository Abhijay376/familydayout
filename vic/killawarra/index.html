Killawarra is in the Wangaratta area, and our data lists 7 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Killawarra is Warby-Ovens National Park, covering about 14,710 hectares. Killawarra Reference Area and Peechelba H104 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Killawarra also has Wangaratta H8A Bushland Reserve, Killawarra H106 Bushland Reserve and Killawarra H107 Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Killawarra. For walks and rides, Springtime Flower Walk (South) and Springtime Flower Walk (North) cross Killawarra.

Getting around

Killawarra has no train station; Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Wangaratta - Yarrawonga route stop in Killawarra. By car from Killawarra, Wangaratta is about 14 minutes away and Benalla about 47 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Killawarra is Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta, about 13 minutes' drive. For a library, Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta is about 14 minutes' drive from Killawarra. The closest beach to Killawarra is Forges Beach No 2 in Yarrawonga, about 42 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Killawarra is the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Killawarra is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 14 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Tunzafun in Yarrawonga is about 35 minutes' drive from Killawarra. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 14 minutes from Killawarra by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Killawarra, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Peechelba Reserve in Peechelba (5 min), Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta (13 min), Brien Crescent Playground in Wangaratta (13 min), Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (14 min), Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta (14 min) and Moore than Swimming in Wangaratta (17 min).

Best for

Free days on the Springtime Flower Walk (South) and in Killawarra's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Killawarra, Peechelba has 1 playground. North Wangaratta, Mount Bruno, Dockers Plains and Boweya North also border Killawarra.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Killawarra, without traffic.
