Warby-Ovens National Park

Park in Killawarra · Official details

Warby-Ovens National Park is a park in Killawarra, in the Wangaratta Rural City Council area, good for all ages. The nearest public toilet is about 717 m away.

Details

Seasons
Summer: waterfalls dry up and walking can be uncomfortably hot; spring wildflowers (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
No walking tracks along the Ovens River: GPS or map and compass recommended; Unpowered camping; no fees or bookings; bring firewood and water (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Warby-Ovens National Park is in Killawarra, Victoria.

Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
