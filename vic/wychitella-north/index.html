Landmarks

Wychitella North Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Wychitella North, at about 40 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wychitella North. The closest to Wychitella North is Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line), about 48 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wychitella North, so most trips start by car. By car from Wychitella North, Bendigo is about 1.5 hours away and Maryborough about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Boort Swimming Pool in Boort is about 34 minutes' drive from Wychitella North. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 26 minutes' drive from Wychitella North. From Wychitella North, the nearest places for kids by road are Rex Theatre in Charlton (26 min), Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (26 min), the playground at Rotary Park in Charlton (26 min), the playground at Gordon Park in Charlton (26 min), Boort Swimming Pool in Boort (34 min) and Boort Court House Historical Museum in Boort (33 min).
