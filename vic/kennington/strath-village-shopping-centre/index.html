Strath Village Shopping Centre

Shopping centre in Kennington · Basic info

Strath Village Shopping Centre is a shopping centre in Kennington, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Strath Village SC/Edwards Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 323 m away with a baby change table.
