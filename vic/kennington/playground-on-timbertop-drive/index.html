Playground on Timbertop Drive

Playground in Kennington · Basic info

Playground on Timbertop Drive in Kennington is a public playground in the Greater Bendigo City area. Kennington Kebab House is about 400 m away if you need food, and Playground on Summit Drive is another option about 320 m away. There's a public toilet about 470 m away, and bus Heathcote - Bendigo via Junortoun, Axedale, Knowsley stops at Strath-Haven Community/Condon St, a 7-minute walk away.
