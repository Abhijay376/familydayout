Brennan Park

Park in Kennington · Basic info

Brennan Park is a park in Kennington, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, good for all ages. La Trobe University Bendigo/Ellis St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 478 m away.
