Landmarks

Fumina South Education Area is the largest mapped park in Fumina South, at about 127 hectares.

Getting around

Fumina South has no train station; Trafalgar Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Fumina South, so most trips start by car. By car from Fumina South, Warragul is about 42 minutes away, Traralgon about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar is about 25 minutes' drive from Fumina South. The closest library to Fumina South is Morwell Library in Morwell, about 39 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Fumina South is Moe Skate Park in Moe, about 26 minutes' drive. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Fumina South, about 42 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Fumina South are the playground at Blue Rock Lake West picnic area in Willow Grove (11 min), the playground at LaTrobe and Loch Rivers, Noojee Streamside Reserve in Noojee (22 min), Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar (25 min), Moe Skate Park in Moe (26 min), Diving Pool in Moe (27 min) and Old Brown Coal Mine Museum in Yallourn North (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Fumina South.
