Koondrook Historic and Cultural Features Reserve

Park in Koondrook · Basic info

Koondrook Historic and Cultural Features Reserve is a park in Koondrook, in the Gannawarra Shire Council area, good for all ages. Keene St/Main St (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 614 m away.
