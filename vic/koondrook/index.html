Koondrook in the Gannawarra area has 2 playgrounds and 8 parks and reserves in our data. Beyond the parks, Koondrook has a library and a skate park.

Landmarks

Barham Lakes Complex (Closed) is the largest mapped park in Koondrook, at about 40 hectares. Pioneer Park and Federation Botanic Reserve are the next largest. Koondrook also has Ti Tree, Twin Bridges and Riverside Park. Playgrounds around Koondrook include the playground at Riverside Park and the playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV). Our data shows 3 public toilets in Koondrook. Picnic areas in Koondrook include Ti Tree and Twin Bridges. In Koondrook you'll also find Barham Branch Library. For older kids in Koondrook there's Barham Skate Park. For walks and rides, Koondrook Red Gum Forest Walk - Turtle Loop and Koondrook Red Gum Forest Walk - Eagle Loop cross Koondrook.

Getting around

There's no train station in Koondrook. The closest to Koondrook is Kerang Railway Station (V/Line), about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Kerang - Echuca via Cohuna route stop in Koondrook. V/Line coaches stop in Koondrook. By car from Koondrook, Swan Hill is about 68 minutes away and Echuca about 77 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Koondrook is Cohuna Pool in Cohuna, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Koondrook are Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna (26 min), Cohuna Pool in Cohuna (28 min), Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (21 min) and Swimming Pool in Kerang (21 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the library in Koondrook. Teens who skate or ride, with Barham Skate Park in Koondrook. Free days on the Koondrook Red Gum Forest Walk - Turtle Loop and in Koondrook's parks.

Nearby suburbs
