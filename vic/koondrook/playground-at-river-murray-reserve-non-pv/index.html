Playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV)

Playground in Koondrook · Basic info

Playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV) is a public playground in Koondrook, in the Gannawarra Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet about 450 m away, and bus Kerang - Echuca via Cohuna stops at Keene St/Main St, a 4-minute walk away.
