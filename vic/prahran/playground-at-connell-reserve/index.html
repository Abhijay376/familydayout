Playground at Connell Reserve

Playground in Prahran · Basic info

Playground at Connell Reserve in Prahran is a public playground in the Stonnington City area. Braille and Talking Book Library is about 370 m away, and Hungry Jack's is about 340 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 410 m away, and bus 246 stops at Greville St/Punt Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
