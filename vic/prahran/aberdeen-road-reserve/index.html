Aberdeen Road Reserve

Park in Prahran · Basic info

Aberdeen Road Reserve is a park in Prahran, in the Stonnington City Council area, good for all ages. Williams Rd/High St #35 (tram) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 372 m away.
