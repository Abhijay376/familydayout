Playground at Grattan Gardens

Playground in Prahran · Basic info

This is a public playground in Prahran. Prahran Square Library is about 100 m away, and Prahran Aquatic Centre is about 410 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 120 m from the playground, and tram 72 stops at Prahran Market/Commercial Rd #30, a 2-minute walk away.
