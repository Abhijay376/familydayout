Lumley Park

Park in Prahran · Basic info

Lumley Park is a park in Prahran, in the Stonnington City Council area, good for all ages. Wrexham Rd/Williams Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 332 m away.
