Playground at Orrong Park

Playground in Prahran · Basic info

Playground at Orrong Park is a public playground in Prahran, in the Stonnington City area. The Mathoura Rd, Chatsworth Rd is about 270 m away, and for food, Morning Market is about 320 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 100 m from the playground, and bus 604 stops at Toorak Station/Orrong Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
