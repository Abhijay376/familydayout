Gowanford is in the Swan Hill area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Gowanford, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Gowanford. The closest to Gowanford is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 35 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gowanford, so most trips start by car. By car from Gowanford, Swan Hill is about 38 minutes away and Echuca about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Gowanford is Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill, about 38 minutes' drive. For a library, Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill is about 39 minutes' drive from Gowanford. The closest skate park to Gowanford is Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South, about 36 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill is about 38 minutes' drive from Gowanford. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Gowanford, about 38 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Gowanford are Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South (36 min), the playground at Jaycee Park in Swan Hill (39 min), Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill (38 min), Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill (38 min), Swan Hill Outdoor Pool in Swan Hill (38 min) and the playground at Riverside Park in Swan Hill (38 min).
