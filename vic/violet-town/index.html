Our data lists 3 playgrounds and 11 parks and reserves in Violet Town, in the Strathbogie area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Violet Town is Balmattum Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 220 hectares. Jubilee Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting) and Violet State Forest are the next largest. Violet Town also has Lions Park, Violet Town Sports Complex and Violet Town Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds in Violet Town include Violet Town Lions Park Playground, Violet Town Recreation Reserve East Playground and Violet Town Recreation Reserve West Playground. Our data shows 7 public toilets in Violet Town.

Getting around

Violet Town has a train station: Violet Town Railway Station (V/Line: Albury - Melbourne via Seymour). By car from Violet Town, Benalla is about 25 minutes away, Shepparton about 45 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Violet Town is Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest library to Violet Town is Euroa Library in Euroa, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Violet Town is Euroa Skate Park in Euroa, about 18 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa is about 18 minutes' drive from Violet Town. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Violet Town is Emerald Bank Leisure Land in Kialla, about 38 minutes' drive. Benalla is the closest bigger centre to Violet Town, about 25 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds and a pool.

Best for

Younger kids at Violet Town's 3 local playgrounds.

Nearby suburbs

Riggs Creek, Earlston, Caniambo and Boho also border Violet Town.
