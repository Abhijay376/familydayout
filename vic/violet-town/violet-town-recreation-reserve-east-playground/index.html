Violet Town Recreation Reserve East Playground

Playground in Violet Town · Basic info

Violet Town Recreation Reserve East Playground in Violet Town is a public playground in the Strathbogie Shire area. Violet Town Recreation Reserve West Playground is another option about 240 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 60 m away, and trains stop at Violet Town Railway Station, a 9-minute walk away.
