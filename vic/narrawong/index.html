Landmarks

Narrawong Recreation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Narrawong, at about 4 hectares. Playgrounds around Narrawong include the playground at Narrawong Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Narrawong. Places to visit in Narrawong include Bay of Whales Gallery.

Getting around

Narrawong has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 69 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Narrawong. By car from Narrawong, Portland is about 14 minutes away and Hamilton about 69 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Narrawong is Portland Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Portland, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest library to Narrawong is Heywood Library in Heywood, about 24 minutes' drive. For a beach, Fitzroy River Beach in Tyrendarra is about 18 minutes' drive from Narrawong. Portland is the closest bigger centre to Narrawong, about 14 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Narrawong are Fitzroy River Beach in Tyrendarra (18 min), Whalers Point in Portland (14 min), the playground at Mitchell Park in Portland (15 min), Powerhouse Motor Car Museum in Portland (16 min), Portland Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Portland (17 min) and Heywood Library in Heywood (24 min).
