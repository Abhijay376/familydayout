Playground at Narrawong Recreation Reserve

Playground in Narrawong · Basic info

Playground at Narrawong Recreation Reserve is a public playground in Narrawong, in the Glenelg Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet about 70 m away, and coaches stop at Narrawong Store/Princes Hwy, a 8-minute walk away.
