Playground on Tanner Street

Playground in Breakwater · Basic info

This is a public playground in Breakwater. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 40 stops at Tanner St/Leather St, a 5-minute walk away.
