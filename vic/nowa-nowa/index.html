Nowa Nowa is in the East Gippsland area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Other parks and reserves in Nowa Nowa include Cossticks Weir and Nowa Nowa MTB Trailhead. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Nowa Nowa. Picnic areas in Nowa Nowa include Cossticks Weir and Nowa Nowa MTB Trailhead. For walks and rides, East Gippsland Rail Trail, 4 Scat Track and 3 Skull Track cross Nowa Nowa.

Getting around

Nowa Nowa has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 42 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 12 stops in Nowa Nowa. V/Line coaches stop in Nowa Nowa. By car from Nowa Nowa, Lakes Entrance is about 20 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 47 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Lakes Entrance Aquatic Centre in Lakes Entrance is about 17 minutes' drive from Nowa Nowa. For a library, Lakes Entrance Library in Lakes Entrance is about 20 minutes' drive from Nowa Nowa. The closest beach to Nowa Nowa is Lake Tyers Beach Sand Bar in Lake Tyers Beach, about 25 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Lakes Entrance, about 20 minutes from Nowa Nowa by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Nowa Nowa are Lakes Entrance Aquatic Centre in Lakes Entrance (17 min), Lake Tyers Beach Sand Bar in Lake Tyers Beach (25 min), the playground at Lakes Entrance - Lake Tyers Coastal Reserve (COM) in Lake Tyers Beach (24 min), 90 Mile Beach in Lakes Entrance (18 min), Lakes Entrance Library in Lakes Entrance (20 min) and Bronsdon Street Park in Lakes Entrance (22 min).

Best for
