Budgerum East in the Gannawarra area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Lake Gilmour Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Budgerum East, at about 50 hectares.

Getting around

Budgerum East has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Budgerum East, so most trips start by car. By car from Budgerum East, Swan Hill is about 64 minutes away and Echuca about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Quambatook Memorial Pool in Quambatook is about 12 minutes' drive from Budgerum East. The closest library to Budgerum East is Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Budgerum East are Quambatook Memorial Pool in Quambatook (12 min), Quambatook Historical Centre in Quambatook (11 min), Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook (12 min), Quambatook Lions Club Playground in Quambatook (12 min), Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (20 min) and Swimming Pool in Kerang (20 min).

Best for
