Nalinga is in the Greater Shepparton area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Nalinga, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Nalinga has no train station; Violet Town Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 25 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nalinga, so most trips start by car. By car from Nalinga, Benalla is about 25 minutes away, Shepparton about 25 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Aquamoves in Shepparton is about 27 minutes' drive from Nalinga. For a library, Shepparton Library in Shepparton is about 27 minutes' drive from Nalinga. The closest beach to Nalinga is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Nalinga is the skate park at Skate Park in Shepparton, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Nalinga is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Nalinga is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Nalinga is Kidstown in Mooroopna, about 29 minutes' drive. Benalla is the closest bigger centre to Nalinga, about 25 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds and a pool. From Nalinga, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at CWA Gardens in Dookie (14 min), the playground at Dookie Recreation Reserve in Dookie (16 min), Village Cinemas in Shepparton (26 min), StarBowl in Shepparton (25 min), Shepparton Heritage Centre in Shepparton (26 min) and the skate park at Skate Park in Shepparton (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Nalinga.
