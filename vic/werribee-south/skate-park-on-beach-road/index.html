Skate park on Beach Road

Skate, BMX or pump track in Werribee South · Basic info

Skate park on Beach Road is a skate, bmx or pump track in Werribee South, in the Wyndham City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. O'Connors Rd/Beach Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 55 m away.
