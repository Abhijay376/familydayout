Werribee Park

Park in Werribee South · Official details

Werribee Park is a park in Werribee South, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Werribee Park Mansion/Main Dr (bus) is a 2-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 325 m away and dogs are not allowed. Opening hours: Mon-Sun and public holidays 10am-4pm.

Questions parents ask

Where is Werribee Park?
