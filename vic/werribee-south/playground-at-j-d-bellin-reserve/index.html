Playground at J D Bellin Reserve

Playground in Werribee South · Official details

Playground at J D Bellin Reserve in Werribee South is one of the council's larger playgrounds in the Wyndham City area. The Wyndham Bay Trail is about 30 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 400 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 140 m from the playground, and bus 439 stops at Finch Rd/Beach Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
