Skate park on John Street

Skate, BMX or pump track in Wahgunyah · Basic info

Skate park on John Street is a skate, bmx or pump track in Wahgunyah, in the Indigo Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Sanger St/River St (coach) is a 9-minute walk away.
