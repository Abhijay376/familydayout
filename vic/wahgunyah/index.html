Wahgunyah in the Indigo area has 10 playgrounds and 10 parks and reserves in our data. Wahgunyah also has a library, a pool, a museum or gallery and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Wahgunyah is Corowa Common, covering about 81 hectares. Whitehead Street Wetlands and Carlyle H8A Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Wahgunyah include Lions Park, Morris Park and Rowers Park. Playgrounds around Wahgunyah include RSL Park, Lions Park and Morris Park. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Wahgunyah. In Wahgunyah you'll also find Federation Museum, Corowa Swimming Pool and Corowa Library. For older kids in Wahgunyah there's BMX Pump Track. For walks and rides, Murray to Mountains Rail Trail: Everton – Beechworth and Murray to Mountains Rail Trail: Wahgunyah – Rutherglen cross Wahgunyah. Our data lists 4 cafes and family restaurants in Wahgunyah for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Wahgunyah has no train station; Springhurst Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Albury - Corowa via Rutherglen route stop in Wahgunyah. V/Line coaches stop in Wahgunyah. By car from Wahgunyah, Wangaratta is about 37 minutes away and Wodonga about 42 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 35 minutes' drive from Wahgunyah.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 10 playgrounds across Wahgunyah. Rainy days at the library and museum or gallery in Wahgunyah. Teens who skate or ride, with BMX Pump Track in Wahgunyah. Free days on the Murray to Mountains Rail Trail: Everton – Beechworth and in Wahgunyah's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Rutherglen, next to Wahgunyah, has 1 playground, a pool and a library.
