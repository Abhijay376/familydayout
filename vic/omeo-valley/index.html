Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Omeo Valley, in the East Gippsland area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Omeo Valley is Hinnomunjie Bridge Recreation Area, covering about 4 hectares. Omeo Valley also has Hinnomunjie. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Omeo Valley. Picnic areas in Omeo Valley include Hinnomunjie.

Getting around

There's no train station in Omeo Valley. The closest to Omeo Valley is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 98 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Omeo Valley, so most trips start by car. By car from Omeo Valley, Bright is about 2 hours away and Bairnsdale about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Omeo Valley is Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek, about 38 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo is about 18 minutes' drive from Omeo Valley. The closest places for kids by road from Omeo Valley are Petersens Art Gallery & Museum in Omeo (18 min), the playground at Omeo Historical Park & Museum in Omeo (18 min), Old Omeo Courthouse in Omeo (18 min), the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo (18 min) and Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek (38 min).
