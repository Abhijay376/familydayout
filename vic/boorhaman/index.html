Boorhaman is in the Wangaratta area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Boorhaman, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Boorhaman. The closest to Boorhaman is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Boorhaman, so most trips start by car. By car from Boorhaman, Wangaratta is about 31 minutes away and Benalla about 62 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Boorhaman is Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta, about 32 minutes' drive. For a library, Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta is about 30 minutes' drive from Boorhaman. For a skate park, the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta is about 33 minutes' drive from Boorhaman. The closest cinema to Boorhaman is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Boorhaman is Tunzafun in Yarrawonga, about 42 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 31 minutes from Boorhaman by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Boorhaman are the playground at Peechelba Reserve in Peechelba (16 min), the playground at Apex Park in Wangaratta (28 min), Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta (28 min), Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (30 min), Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta (32 min) and the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Boorhaman.

Nearby suburbs

Springhurst, next to Boorhaman, has 1 playground. Boralma and Boorhaman East also border Boorhaman.
