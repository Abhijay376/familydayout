Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Moe South in the Latrobe area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Moe South has no train station; Moe Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 4.8 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Moe South, so most trips start by car. By car from Moe South, Traralgon is about 28 minutes away, Warragul about 33 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Moe South is Diving Pool in Moe, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest library to Moe South is Morwell Library in Morwell, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Moe South is Moe Skate Park in Moe, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Moe South is Village 8 in Morwell, about 19 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 32 minutes' drive from Moe South. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Moe South, about 28 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Moe South are the playground at Wirrana Drive Reserve in Moe (4 min), the playground at Scorpio Reserve in Moe (5 min), Diving Pool in Moe (6 min), Moe Outdoor Pool in Moe (6 min), Moe Skate Park in Moe (7 min) and Old Brown Coal Mine Museum in Yallourn North (15 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Moe South.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Moe South, Moe has 14 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Hernes Oak and Coalville also border Moe South.
