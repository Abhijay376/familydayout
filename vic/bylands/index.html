Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bylands in the Mitchell area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it. Bylands does have a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

Places to visit in Bylands include Tramway Heritage Centre.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bylands. The closest to Bylands is Kilmore East Station (V/Line), about 6.8 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bylands, so most trips start by car. By car from Bylands, Broadmeadows is about 39 minutes away, Seymour about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 57 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bylands is Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest library to Bylands is Kilmore Library in Kilmore, about 6 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Kilmore Skate Park in Kilmore is about 6 minutes' drive from Bylands. For a cinema, United Cinemas in Craigieburn is about 29 minutes' drive from Bylands. The closest indoor play centre to Bylands is Timezone in Craigieburn, about 29 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Funfields in Whittlesea is about 27 minutes' drive from Bylands. Broadmeadows is the closest bigger centre to Bylands, about 39 minutes away by car, and has 12 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Bylands, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Elms Estate Reserve in Kilmore (6 min), the playground at Vacan Block - The Elms in Kilmore (6 min), Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore (6 min), Kilmore Skate Park in Kilmore (6 min), Kilmore Library in Kilmore (6 min) and Wallan Library in Wallan (7 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Bylands.

Nearby suburbs

Wallan, next to Bylands, has 13 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Kilmore, next to Bylands, has 8 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Wandong, next to Bylands, has 1 playground and a skate park. Forbes and Darraweit Guim also border Bylands.
