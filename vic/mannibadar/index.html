Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Mannibadar in the Golden Plains area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Mannibadar has no train station; Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 39 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mannibadar, so most trips start by car. By car from Mannibadar, Ballarat is about 47 minutes away, Ararat about 71 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood is about 23 minutes' drive from Mannibadar. The closest library to Mannibadar is Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort, about 37 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Mannibadar is Linton Skate Park in Linton, about 18 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Showbiz Cinemas Ballarat in Smythes Creek is about 39 minutes' drive from Mannibadar. The nearest larger centre is Ballarat, about 47 minutes from Mannibadar by car, with 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Mannibadar are Linton Skate Park in Linton (18 min), Linton BMX park in Linton (18 min), Linton Recreation Reserve Playground in Linton (19 min), Cape Clear & District Lions Park Playground in Cape Clear (12 min), Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood (23 min) and Lismore Pool in Lismore (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mannibadar.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Mannibadar, Linton has 1 playground and a skate park. Cape Clear, next to Mannibadar, has 1 playground. Skipton, Pittong, Pitfield and Happy Valley also border Mannibadar.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Mannibadar, without traffic.
