Thoona is in the Benalla area, and our data lists 5 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Thoona Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Thoona, at about 10 hectares. Natamatite Creek Bushland Reserve and Bungeet Creek Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Thoona include Mokoan H90 Bushland Reserve and Mokoan H100 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Thoona has no train station; Benalla Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Thoona, so most trips start by car. By car from Thoona, Wangaratta is about 32 minutes away and Benalla about 37 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta is about 34 minutes' drive from Thoona. The closest library to Thoona is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 34 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta is about 34 minutes' drive from Thoona. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 34 minutes' drive from Thoona. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Thoona, about 32 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Thoona are Glenrowan Museum in Glenrowan (22 min), Cobb & Co in Glenrowan (22 min), Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta (34 min), the playground at Peechelba Reserve in Peechelba (30 min), Moore than Swimming in Wangaratta (31 min) and the playground at Dodsworth Street Reserve in Wangaratta (32 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Thoona.

Nearby suburbs

Bungeet and Boweya also border Thoona.
