Our data lists 5 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in Eumemmerring, in the Casey area. Beyond the parks, Eumemmerring has a skate park.

Landmarks

Waratah Reserve is the largest mapped park in Eumemmerring, at about 14 hectares. Olive Road Reserve is the next largest. Eumemmerring also has Mystic Court Reserve. Playgrounds around Eumemmerring include the playground at Waratah Reserve, the playground at Waratah Reserve and the playground at Mystic Court Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Eumemmerring. For older kids in Eumemmerring there's the skate park at Waratah Reserve. The Hallam Bypass Trail and Eumemmerring Creek Trail all pass through Eumemmerring. Our data lists 5 cafes and family restaurants in Eumemmerring for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Eumemmerring. The closest to Eumemmerring is Hallam Railway Station (metro), about 3.0 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 828, 844, 893 and 981 stop in Eumemmerring. By car from Eumemmerring, Dandenong is about 7 minutes away, Cranbourne about 14 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 31 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Eumemmerring is Doveton Pool in the Park in Doveton, about 4 minutes' drive. The closest library to Eumemmerring is Dandenong Library in Dandenong, about 6 minutes' drive. For a beach, Edithvale Beach in Edithvale is about 18 minutes' drive from Eumemmerring. The closest cinema to Eumemmerring is Reading Cinema in Dandenong, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Eumemmerring is Oz Tenpin in Narre Warren, about 9 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale is about 20 minutes' drive from Eumemmerring.

Best for

Younger kids at Eumemmerring's 5 local playgrounds. Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at Waratah Reserve in Eumemmerring. Free days on the Hallam Bypass Trail and in Eumemmerring's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Eumemmerring, Endeavour Hills has 26 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Eumemmerring, Hallam has 12 playgrounds and a skate park. Doveton, next to Eumemmerring, has 5 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Eumemmerring, Dandenong South has 1 playground.
