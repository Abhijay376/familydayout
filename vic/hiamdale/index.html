Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Hiamdale, in the Wellington area.

Landmarks

Merrimans Creek Flora Reserve is the largest mapped park in Hiamdale, at about 67 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Hiamdale. The closest to Hiamdale is Rosedale Railway Station (V/Line), about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Hiamdale, so most trips start by car. By car from Hiamdale, Traralgon is about 33 minutes away, Sale about 42 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rosedale Swimming Pool in Rosedale is about 19 minutes' drive from Hiamdale. The closest library to Hiamdale is Morwell Library in Morwell, about 39 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon is about 35 minutes' drive from Hiamdale. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 38 minutes' drive from Hiamdale. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Hiamdale, about 33 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. From Hiamdale, the nearest places for kids by road are Edward Crooke Park Playground in Rosedale (18 min), Rosedale Swimming Pool in Rosedale (19 min), Prince Street Reserve Playground in Rosedale (19 min), Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon (33 min), Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon (35 min) and Powerworks in Morwell (38 min).

Best for
