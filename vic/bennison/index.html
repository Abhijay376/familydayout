Bennison in the South Gippsland area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bennison is Bennison Flora and Fauna Reserve, covering about 2 hectares. The Great Southern Rail Trail passes through Bennison.

Getting around

Bennison has no train station; Morwell Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 49 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bennison, so most trips start by car. By car from Bennison, Leongatha is about 39 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 59 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Toora Pool in Toora is about 11 minutes' drive from Bennison. For a library, Foster Library in Foster is about 9 minutes' drive from Bennison. The closest beach to Bennison is Toora Beach in Toora, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Bennison is the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora, about 10 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha is about 39 minutes' drive from Bennison. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Bennison, about 39 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Bennison are the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora (10 min), the playground at Stanley Street Playground in Toora (10 min), the playground at Sargasser Park in Toora (10 min), Toora Pool in Toora (11 min), Luminous Glass Studio in Foster (9 min) and Stockyard Gallery in Foster (9 min).

Best for

Free days on the Great Southern Rail Trail and in Bennison's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Bennison, Toora has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. Foster, next to Bennison, has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Bennison, without traffic.
