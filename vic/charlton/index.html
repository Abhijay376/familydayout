Our data lists 3 playgrounds and 10 parks and reserves in Charlton, in the Buloke area. Beyond the parks, Charlton has a museum or gallery and a cinema.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Charlton is Mount Doboobetic Bushland Reserve, covering about 348 hectares. Charlton East I160 Bushland Reserve and Howells Hill Scenic Reserve are the next largest. Charlton also has Lions Park, Gordon Park and Rotary Park. Playgrounds around Charlton include the playground at Gordon Park and the playground at Rotary Park. Our data shows 9 public toilets in Charlton. For camping, there's Avoca River - Charlton Camping Area 6 (CLWF). Places to visit in Charlton include Golden Grains Museum and Rex Theatre. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Charlton for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Charlton. The closest to Charlton is Kerang Railway Station (V/Line), about 79 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Bendigo - Woomelang via Wedderburn, Charlton, Wycheproof, Birchip route stop in Charlton. V/Line coaches stop in Charlton. By car from Charlton, Bendigo is about 89 minutes away and Stawell about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Charlton is Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Charlton is Donald BMX Track in Donald, about 35 minutes' drive.

Best for

Younger kids at Charlton's 3 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the cinema and museum or gallery in Charlton.
