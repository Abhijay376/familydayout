Elliot Gardens

Park in Charlton · Basic info

Elliot Gardens is a park in Charlton, in the Buloke Shire Council area, good for all ages. Travellers Rest/John Curtin Dr (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 198 m away.
