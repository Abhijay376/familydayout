Hopkins River, Wickliffe Streamside Reserve

Park in Wickliffe · Basic info

Hopkins River, Wickliffe Streamside Reserve is a park in Wickliffe, in the Ararat Rural City Council area, good for all ages. CFA Station/Glenelg Hwy (bus) is a 45-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 3755 m away.
