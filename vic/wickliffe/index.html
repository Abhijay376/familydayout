Wickliffe in the Ararat area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Hopkins River, Wickliffe Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Wickliffe, at about 26 hectares. Wickliffe Recreation Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Wickliffe.

Getting around

Wickliffe has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 49 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Ararat - Lake Bolac via Willaura route stop in Wickliffe. V/Line coaches stop in Wickliffe. By car from Wickliffe, Ararat is about 52 minutes away and Hamilton about 56 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Willaura Outdoor Pool in Willaura is about 24 minutes' drive from Wickliffe. The closest library to Wickliffe is Lake Bolac Library in Lake Bolac, about 10 minutes' drive. Ararat is the closest bigger centre to Wickliffe, about 52 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Wickliffe, the nearest places for kids by road are Lake Bolac Library in Lake Bolac (10 min), Glenthompson Lions Park Playground in Glenthompson (14 min), Willaura Outdoor Pool in Willaura (24 min) and Glenthompson Rec Reserve Playground in Glenthompson (16 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wickliffe.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Wickliffe, Willaura has 1 playground and a pool. Lake Bolac, next to Wickliffe, has 1 playground and a library. Next door to Wickliffe, Glenthompson has 2 playgrounds. Narrapumelap South and Chatsworth also border Wickliffe.
