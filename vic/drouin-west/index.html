Drouin West in the Baw Baw area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Drouin West is Tarago River, Robin Hood Streamside Reserve, covering about 14 hectares. Whiskey Creek Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Drouin West. The closest to Drouin West is Drouin Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Drouin West, so most trips start by car. By car from Drouin West, Warragul is about 12 minutes away, Pakenham about 32 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 76 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Drouin West is Drouin Swimming Pool in Drouin, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest library to Drouin West is Drouin Library in Drouin, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Drouin West is Drouin Skate Park in Drouin, about 7 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 12 minutes' drive from Drouin West. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 13 minutes' drive from Drouin West. For a zoo or wildlife park, Wildlife Trail in Tynong North is about 19 minutes' drive from Drouin West. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Drouin West, about 12 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Drouin West are Waddell Park Playground in Drouin (4 min), Hearn Street Park Playground in Drouin (5 min), Drouin Library in Drouin (7 min), Drouin Swimming Pool in Drouin (7 min), Drouin Skate Park in Drouin (7 min) and Warragul Library in Warragul (12 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Drouin West.

Nearby suburbs

Drouin, next to Drouin West, has 19 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Drouin West, Longwarry has 3 playgrounds. Rokeby, next to Drouin West, has 1 playground. Labertouche, Jindivick, Drouin East and Bravington also border Drouin West.
