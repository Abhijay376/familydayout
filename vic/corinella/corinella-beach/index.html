Corinella Beach

Beach in Corinella · Basic info

Corinella Beach is a beach in Corinella, in the Bass Coast Shire Council area, good for all ages. Albon St/The Esplanade (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 140 m away.

This page has basic info from the map: name, location and type. We have not checked the details yet. Know this place? Tell us.

Questions parents ask

Where is Corinella Beach?
Corinella Beach is in Corinella, Victoria.
Is Corinella Beach free?
Yes. It is a public beach, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Corinella Beach?
The nearest public toilet is about 140 m away.
How do I get to Corinella Beach by public transport?
The closest stop is Albon St/The Esplanade (routes Coronet Bay - Grantville via Corinella, Coronet Bay - Wonthaggi via Corinella), a 6-minute walk.
