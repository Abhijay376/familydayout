Gooramadda is in the Indigo area, and our data lists 2 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Gooramadda has a library, a pool and a skate park.

Landmarks

Mount Ochtertyre Scenic Reserve is the largest mapped park in Gooramadda, at about 26 hectares. Lowe Square and Murray River K15 Streamside Reserve are the next largest. Playgrounds in Gooramadda include Lowe Square Playground. Places to visit in Gooramadda include Howlong Swimming Pool and Howlong Library and Resource Centre. For older kids in Gooramadda there's the bmx track at Lowe Square.

Getting around

Gooramadda has no train station; Chiltern Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gooramadda, so most trips start by car. By car from Gooramadda, Wodonga is about 30 minutes away and Wangaratta about 44 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest beach to Gooramadda is Finns Beach in Bandiana, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Gooramadda is Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana, about 33 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 30 minutes from Gooramadda by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Gooramadda, the nearest places for kids by road are Rutherglen Public Library in Rutherglen (9 min), Apex Park Playground in Rutherglen (9 min), Rutherglen War Memorial Swimming Pool in Rutherglen (10 min), BMX Pump Track in Wahgunyah (13 min), Federation Museum in Wahgunyah (13 min) and Chiltern Athenaeum Museum in Chiltern (20 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the library in Gooramadda. Teens who skate or ride, with the bmx track at Lowe Square in Gooramadda.

Nearby suburbs

Rutherglen, next to Gooramadda, has 1 playground, a pool and a library.
