BMX track at Lowe Square

Skate, BMX or pump track in Gooramadda · Basic info

BMX track at Lowe Square is a skate, bmx or pump track in Gooramadda, in the Indigo Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Howlong Public School/Hawkins St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away.
