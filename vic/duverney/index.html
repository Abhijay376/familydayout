Duverney is in the Corangamite area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Duverney is Lake Rosine Wildlife Reserve (no hunting), covering about 176 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Duverney. The closest to Duverney is Colac Railway Station (V/Line), about 37 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Duverney, so most trips start by car. By car from Duverney, Colac is about 39 minutes away, Geelong about 64 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Lismore Pool in Lismore is about 19 minutes' drive from Duverney. The closest library to Duverney is Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum, about 28 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Rokewood Skate Park in Rokewood is about 29 minutes' drive from Duverney. The closest cinema to Duverney is Colac Cinemas in Colac, about 40 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac is about 39 minutes' drive from Duverney. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 39 minutes from Duverney by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Duverney, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Cressy Picnic Area in Cressy (8 min), Lismore Pool in Lismore (19 min), Rokewood Skate Park in Rokewood (29 min), Rokewood Recreation Reserve Playground in Rokewood (29 min), Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood (29 min) and Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum (28 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Duverney.

Nearby suburbs

Cressy, next to Duverney, has 1 playground. Berrybank also borders Duverney.
