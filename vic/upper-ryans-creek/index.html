Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Upper Ryans Creek in the Benalla area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Upper Ryans Creek has no train station; Benalla Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Upper Ryans Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Upper Ryans Creek, Benalla is about 34 minutes away and Wangaratta about 46 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Upper Ryans Creek is Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Upper Ryans Creek is Pump Track in Benalla, about 34 minutes' drive. Benalla is the closest bigger centre to Upper Ryans Creek, about 34 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds and a pool. From Upper Ryans Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are Myrrhee Recreation Reserve in Myrrhee (29 min), Benalla Aviation Museum in Benalla (33 min), Benalla Migrant Camp in Benalla (33 min), the playground at Apex Park in Moyhu (26 min), Pump Track in Benalla (34 min) and Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Upper Ryans Creek.
