East Sale is in the Wellington area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb. On top of that, East Sale has a cinema.

Landmarks

Other parks and reserves in East Sale include Yarning Circle. In East Sale you'll also find Roulette Cinema.

Getting around

There's no train station in East Sale. The closest to East Sale is Sale Railway Station (V/Line), about 6.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside East Sale, so most trips start by car. By car from East Sale, Sale is about 8 minutes away, Traralgon about 52 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to East Sale is AquaEnergy in Sale, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest library to East Sale is Sale Library in Sale, about 9 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Sale Skate Park in Sale is about 10 minutes' drive from East Sale. The nearest larger centre is Sale, about 8 minutes from East Sale by car, with 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From East Sale, the nearest places for kids by road are Ibis Avenue - Pelican Court Playground in Sale (5 min), Cooper Court - Ruff Street Playground in Sale (6 min), AquaEnergy in Sale (8 min), Indoor pool in Sale (8 min), Former Drive-in site in Sale (9 min) and Sale Historical Museum in Sale (9 min).
