Landmarks

The biggest green space in Toongabbie is Boola Boola State Forest, covering about 21,612 hectares. Eaglehawk Creek Reference Area and Toongabbie Creek Water Frontage are the next largest. Toongabbie also has Toongabbie Bushland Reserve and Toongabbie Mechanics Institute Historic Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Toongabbie. The Gippsland Plains Rail Trail passes through Toongabbie. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Toongabbie for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Toongabbie. The closest to Toongabbie is Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line), about 17 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Toongabbie. By car from Toongabbie, Traralgon is about 18 minutes away, Sale about 45 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Toongabbie is Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon, about 18 minutes' drive. For a library, Morwell Library in Morwell is about 30 minutes' drive from Toongabbie. The closest skate park to Toongabbie is the skate park at Apex Park in Glengarry, about 8 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 28 minutes' drive from Toongabbie. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Toongabbie, about 18 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. From Toongabbie, the nearest places for kids by road are the skate park at Apex Park in Glengarry (8 min), Glengarry Skatepark in Glengarry (8 min), the playground at Apex Park in Glengarry (9 min), Cricket Street Playground in Cowwarr (10 min), Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon (18 min) and Rosedale Swimming Pool in Rosedale (18 min).

Best for

Free days on the Gippsland Plains Rail Trail and in Toongabbie's parks.
