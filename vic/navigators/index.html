Our data lists 1 playground in Navigators, in the Moorabool area.

Landmarks

Playgrounds in Navigators include the playground at Navigators Community Centre. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Navigators. The Boundary Walk passes through Navigators.

Getting around

There's no train station in Navigators. The closest to Navigators is Ballarat Railway Station (V/Line), about 8.8 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Navigators. By car from Navigators, Ballarat is about 14 minutes away, Daylesford about 41 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Eureka Swimming Complex in Eureka is about 9 minutes' drive from Navigators. For a library, L Building: E J Barker Library in Mount Helen is about 12 minutes' drive from Navigators. For a skate park, the bmx track at Canadian Creek Reserve in Mount Clear is about 12 minutes' drive from Navigators. For a cinema, Victoria Theatre in Golden Point is about 13 minutes' drive from Navigators. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 14 minutes' drive from Navigators. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Navigators is Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East, about 9 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Ballarat, about 14 minutes from Navigators by car, with 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Navigators are the playground at Yendon Recreation Reserve in Yendon (8 min), L Building: E J Barker Library in Mount Helen (12 min), Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East (9 min), Eureka Swimming Complex in Eureka (9 min), Toddlers' pool in Eureka (9 min) and the bmx track at Canadian Creek Reserve in Mount Clear (12 min).

Best for

Free days on the Boundary Walk and in Navigators's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Navigators, Buninyong has 6 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Mount Helen, next to Navigators, has 3 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Navigators, Mount Clear has 6 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Navigators, Canadian has 6 playgrounds. Yendon, Dunnstown and Warrenheip also border Navigators.
