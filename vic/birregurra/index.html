Birregurra in the Colac Otway area has 2 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, Birregurra has a skate park.

Landmarks

Yeo Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Birregurra, at about 6 hectares. Birregurra Park is the next largest. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Birregurra. For older kids in Birregurra there's Birregurra Skate Park.

Getting around

Birregurra has a train station: Birregurra Railway Station (V/Line: Warrnambool - Melbourne via Colac & Geelong). Buses on the Colac - Lorne and Colac - Marengo via Apollo Bay routes stop in Birregurra. V/Line coaches stop in Birregurra. By car from Birregurra, Colac is about 19 minutes away, Torquay about 51 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac is about 19 minutes' drive from Birregurra. The closest beach to Birregurra is Lorne Beach in Lorne, about 37 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Colac Cinemas in Colac is about 19 minutes' drive from Birregurra. For an indoor play centre, Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac is about 18 minutes' drive from Birregurra. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Birregurra is Live Wire Park in Lorne, about 38 minutes' drive. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Birregurra, about 19 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Birregurra, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Selwyn Brown Park in Colac (17 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (18 min), the playground at Memorial Square in Colac (18 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (19 min), Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (19 min) and the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (24 min).
