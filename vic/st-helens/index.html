St Helens is in the Moyne area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in St Helens is St Helens Flora Reserve, covering about 31 hectares.

Getting around

St Helens has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 38 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside St Helens, so most trips start by car. By car from St Helens, Portland is about 42 minutes away and Warrnambool about 44 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Hawkesdale Swimming Pool in Hawkesdale is about 37 minutes' drive from St Helens. For a library, Heywood Library in Heywood is about 38 minutes' drive from St Helens. The closest beach to St Helens is Eumeralla in Tyrendarra East, about 16 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Russell Clark Reserve in Port Fairy is about 23 minutes' drive from St Helens. For a cinema, Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy is about 23 minutes' drive from St Helens. The nearest larger centre is Portland, about 42 minutes from St Helens by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. From St Helens, the nearest places for kids by road are Eumeralla in Tyrendarra East (16 min), Limestone Gallery in Port Fairy (21 min), Pea Soup Beach in Port Fairy (22 min), the skate park at Russell Clark Reserve in Port Fairy (23 min), Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy (23 min) and Port Fairy Skate and Play space in Port Fairy (23 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from St Helens.
