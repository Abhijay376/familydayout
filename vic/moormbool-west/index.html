Moormbool West is in the Strathbogie area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Costerfield State Forest is the largest mapped park in Moormbool West, at about 1,570 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Moormbool West. The closest to Moormbool West is Nagambie Railway Station (V/Line), about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Moormbool West, so most trips start by car. By car from Moormbool West, Seymour is about 49 minutes away, Shepparton about 71 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Nagambie Pool in Nagambie is about 27 minutes' drive from Moormbool West. The closest library to Moormbool West is Nagambie Library in Nagambie, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Moormbool West is Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote, about 33 minutes' drive. Seymour is the closest bigger centre to Moormbool West, about 49 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Moormbool West, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Goulburn River Heritage River in Nagambie (27 min), Nagambie Recreation Reserve Playground in Nagambie (27 min), Nagambie Pool in Nagambie (27 min), Nagambie Historical Society in Nagambie (29 min), Nagambie Library in Nagambie (29 min) and Mudwood Studio in Heathcote (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Moormbool West.
