Cliff Avenue Playspace

Playground in Strathdale · Basic info

Cliff Avenue Playspace in Strathdale is a public playground in the Greater Bendigo City area. The Grassy Flat Creek Trail is about 240 m away, and Playground on East Lloyd Street is another option about 500 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 880 m from the playground, and bus 60 stops at Ellesmere Tce/Beischer St, a 6-minute walk away.
