Playground at Boronia Park

Playground in Boronia · Basic info

Playground at Boronia Park is a public playground in Boronia, in the Knox City area. The Knox City Council Informal Bike Route passes about 140 m away, and Boronia Library is about 100 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 753 stops at Dorset Rd/Park Cres, a 1-minute walk away.
