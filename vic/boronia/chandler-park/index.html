Chandler Park

Park in Boronia · Basic info

Chandler Park is a park in Boronia, in the Knox City Council area, good for all ages. Chandler Park/Chandler Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 642 m away with a baby change table.
