Colchester Park Playground

Playground in Boronia · Basic info

Colchester Park Playground is a public playground in Boronia, in the Knox City area. The Basin Trail is about 120 m away, and Mountain Thai is about 460 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 720 m away, and buses 690 and 755 stop at Colchester Park/Beresford Dr, a 1-minute walk away.
