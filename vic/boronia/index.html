Boronia in the Knox area has 29 playgrounds and 29 parks and reserves in our data. Beyond the parks, Boronia has 2 libraries, a pool, a cinema and an indoor play centre.

Landmarks

Miller Park is the largest mapped park in Boronia, at about 6 hectares. Tormore Reserve and Boronia Park are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Boronia include Public BBQ, Chandler Park and Blamey Reserve. Playgrounds around Boronia include Cobbitty Court Reserve, Timor Court Playground and Herbert/Cardiff Reserve. Our data shows 13 public toilets in Boronia. Places to visit in Boronia include Metro Cinemas, Zone Bowling, Knox Leisureworks, Boronia Library and Miller's Homestead. Boronia Mall Shopping Centre is the local shopping centre. The Ringwood - Belgrave Rail Trail, Knox City Council Informal Bike Route and Blind Creek Trail all pass through Boronia. Our data lists 23 cafes and family restaurants in Boronia for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Boronia Railway Station (metro: Belgrave Line) in Boronia. Buses 690, 691, 737, 745, 753, 755 and 967 stop in Boronia. By car from Boronia, Ringwood is about 13 minutes away, Lilydale about 18 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 34 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a beach, Edithvale Beach in Edithvale is about 30 minutes' drive from Boronia. The closest skate park to Boronia is Rampit Indoor Skate Park in Bayswater, about 6 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale is about 24 minutes' drive from Boronia.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 29 playgrounds across Boronia. Rainy days at the library, cinema and indoor play centre in Boronia. Free days on the Ringwood - Belgrave Rail Trail and in Boronia's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Ferntree Gully, next to Boronia, has 33 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Wantirna South, next to Boronia, has 25 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Bayswater, next to Boronia, has 16 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Knoxfield, next to Boronia, has 11 playgrounds and a skate park. Kilsyth, Bayswater North, The Basin and Kilsyth South also border Boronia.
