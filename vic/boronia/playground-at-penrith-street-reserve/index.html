Playground at Penrith Street Reserve

Playground in Boronia · Basic info

Playground at Penrith Street Reserve is a public playground in Boronia, in the Knox City area. Knox Leisureworks is about 110 m away, and Playground on Grevillea Avenue is another option about 110 m away. There's a public toilet about 500 m away, and buses 737, 745 and 753 stop at Tormore Rd/Boronia Rd, a 9-minute walk away.
