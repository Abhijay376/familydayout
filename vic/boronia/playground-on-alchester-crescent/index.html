Playground on Alchester Crescent

Playground in Boronia · Basic info

Playground on Alchester Crescent in Boronia is a public playground in the Knox City area. The Basin Trail is about 160 m away, and Alchester Noodle Bar is about 30 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 70 m away, and bus 690 stops at Mountain Hwy/Albert Ave, a 2-minute walk away.
