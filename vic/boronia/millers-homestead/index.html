Miller's Homestead

Library in Boronia · Basic info

Miller's Homestead is a library in Boronia, in the Knox City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Mountain Hwy/Beresford Dr (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 880 m away with a baby change table.
