Boronia Mall Shopping Centre

Shopping centre in Boronia · Basic info

Boronia Mall Shopping Centre is a shopping centre in Boronia, in the Knox City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Boronia Mall/Chandler Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 49 m away with a baby change table.
