Axe Creek is in the Greater Bendigo area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Axe Creek, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Axe Creek has no train station; Bendigo Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Axe Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Axe Creek, Bendigo is about 21 minutes away, Castlemaine about 44 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Axe Creek is Brennan Park Swimming Pool in Kennington, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest library to Axe Creek is Bendigo Library in Bendigo, about 20 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Ewing Park Skating in Bendigo is about 19 minutes' drive from Axe Creek. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 21 minutes' drive from Axe Creek. The closest indoor play centre to Axe Creek is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Axe Creek is The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo, about 21 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 21 minutes from Axe Creek by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Axe Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Coomoora Park in Strathfieldsaye (9 min), Clydebank Court Playspace in Strathfieldsaye (10 min), Brennan Park Swimming Pool in Kennington (18 min), Ewing Park Skating in Bendigo (19 min), Discovery Science & Technology Centre in Bendigo (20 min) and Bendigo Library in Bendigo (20 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Axe Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Axe Creek, Strathfieldsaye has 6 playgrounds. Emu Creek also borders Axe Creek.
