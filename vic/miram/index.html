Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Miram in the West Wimmera area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Miram. The closest to Miram is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 176 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Miram, so most trips start by car. By car from Miram, Horsham is about 1.5 hours away and Stawell about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Miram is Kaniva Library in Kaniva, about 16 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Nhill Skate Park in Nhill is about 31 minutes' drive from Miram. The closest places for kids by road from Miram are the playground at Band Park in Kaniva (16 min), Kaniva Library in Kaniva (16 min), Nhill Playground in Nhill (31 min), Nhill Skate Park in Nhill (31 min), Wagonin in Nhill (31 min) and Gallery Central in Nhill (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Miram.

Nearby suburbs

Nhill, next to Miram, has 3 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Miram, Kaniva has 1 playground and a library.
