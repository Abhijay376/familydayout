Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Nintingbool in the Golden Plains area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

The Ballarat-Skipton Rail Trail passes through Nintingbool.

Getting around

Nintingbool has no train station; Wendouree Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nintingbool, so most trips start by car. By car from Nintingbool, Ballarat is about 15 minutes away, Daylesford about 54 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Nintingbool is Ballarat Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Lake Gardens, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest library to Nintingbool is Sebastopol Library in Sebastopol, about 12 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Haddon Skate Park in Haddon is about 5 minutes' drive from Nintingbool. The closest cinema to Nintingbool is Showbiz Cinemas Ballarat in Smythes Creek, about 7 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 16 minutes' drive from Nintingbool. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Nintingbool is Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East, about 21 minutes' drive. Ballarat is the closest bigger centre to Nintingbool, about 15 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Nintingbool, the nearest places for kids by road are Haddon Recreation Reserve Playground in Haddon (4 min), Haddon Lions Park Playground in Haddon (5 min), Haddon Skate Park in Haddon (5 min), Haddon Tramway Preservation Association in Haddon (5 min), Smythesdale Skate Park in Smythesdale (4 min) and Showbiz Cinemas Ballarat in Smythes Creek (7 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Nintingbool on the Ballarat-Skipton Rail Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Smythes Creek, next to Nintingbool, has a cinema. Haddon, next to Nintingbool, has 3 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Nintingbool, Smythesdale has 2 playgrounds and a skate park.
