Rosebrook is in the Moyne area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Rosebrook, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Rosebrook. The closest to Rosebrook is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 8 stops in Rosebrook. By car from Rosebrook, Warrnambool is about 23 minutes away and Portland about 62 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool is about 25 minutes' drive from Rosebrook. For a library, Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool is about 24 minutes' drive from Rosebrook. The closest beach to Rosebrook is Reef Point Beach in Port Fairy, about 3 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Russell Clark Reserve in Port Fairy is about 7 minutes' drive from Rosebrook. The closest cinema to Rosebrook is Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy, about 6 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, The Bowl in Warrnambool is about 24 minutes' drive from Rosebrook. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Rosebrook is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 39 minutes' drive. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Rosebrook, about 23 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Rosebrook, the nearest places for kids by road are Reef Point Beach in Port Fairy (3 min), Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy (6 min), Limestone Gallery in Port Fairy (6 min), the skate park at Russell Clark Reserve in Port Fairy (7 min), Port Fairy Skate and Play space in Port Fairy (7 min) and Pea Soup Beach in Port Fairy (8 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Rosebrook.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Rosebrook, Port Fairy has 2 playgrounds, a beach and a cinema. Killarney, next to Rosebrook, has a beach.
