Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Wingeel in the Colac Otway area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wingeel. The closest to Wingeel is Winchelsea Railway Station (V/Line), about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wingeel, so most trips start by car. By car from Wingeel, Geelong is about 42 minutes away, Colac about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood is about 36 minutes' drive from Wingeel. For a library, Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn is about 31 minutes' drive from Wingeel. The closest beach to Wingeel is Eastern Beach in Geelong, about 43 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Wingeel is the skate park at Turtle Bend Park in Teesdale, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Wingeel is Village Cinemas in Geelong, about 41 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Wingeel is Laserquest in Geelong, about 42 minutes' drive. Geelong is the closest bigger centre to Wingeel, about 42 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Wingeel are Inverleigh Tennis Reserve Playground in Inverleigh (14 min), Inverleigh Sporting Complex Playground in Inverleigh (16 min), the skate park at Turtle Bend Park in Teesdale (29 min), Teesdale BMX Track in Teesdale (30 min), Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn (31 min) and Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood (36 min).
