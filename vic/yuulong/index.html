Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Yuulong in the Colac Otway area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

The Great Ocean Walk and Milanesia Beach to Ryans Den all pass through Yuulong.

Getting around

There's no train station in Yuulong. The closest to Yuulong is Colac Railway Station (V/Line), about 49 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yuulong, so most trips start by car. By car from Yuulong, Colac is about 64 minutes away and Warrnambool about 88 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a beach, Wreck Beach in Gellibrand Lower is about 18 minutes' drive from Yuulong. The closest skate park to Yuulong is Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell, about 36 minutes' drive. From Yuulong, the nearest places for kids by road are Wreck Beach in Gellibrand Lower (18 min), Gellibrand East Beach in Gellibrand Lower (26 min), Art Reach Studio in Beech Forest (30 min), the playground at John H Gardner Reserve in Beech Forest (30 min), Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell (36 min) and the playground at Rex Norman Park in Gellibrand (44 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Yuulong on the Great Ocean Walk.

Nearby suburbs

Gellibrand Lower, next to Yuulong, has a beach. Chapple Vale also borders Yuulong.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Yuulong, without traffic.
