Killara (Wodonga) is in the Wodonga area, and our data lists 2 playgrounds and 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Henrika Kuljurgies Reserve is the largest mapped park in Killara (Wodonga), at about 3 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Killara (Wodonga) include Schorobura Park. Playgrounds around Killara (Wodonga) include the playground at Henrika Kuljurgies Reserve. The Kiewa Trail passes through Killara (Wodonga).

Getting around

Killara (Wodonga) has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 6.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Killara (Wodonga), so most trips start by car. By car from Killara (Wodonga), Wodonga is about 10 minutes away and Wangaratta about 62 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Killara (Wodonga) is WAVES in Wodonga, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest library to Killara (Wodonga) is Upper Murray Regional Library - Wodonga in Wodonga, about 9 minutes' drive. For a beach, Finns Beach in Bandiana is about 15 minutes' drive from Killara (Wodonga). The closest skate park to Killara (Wodonga) is Skate Park in Baranduda, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Killara (Wodonga) is Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana, about 5 minutes' drive. Wodonga is the closest bigger centre to Killara (Wodonga), about 10 minutes away by car, and has 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Killara (Wodonga) are the playground at Henrika Kuljurgies Reserve in Killara (3 min), Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana (5 min), Army Museum Bandiana in Bandiana (7 min), the playground at Kosovar Park in Bandiana (7 min), WAVES in Wodonga (8 min) and Skate Park in Baranduda (8 min).

Best for

Free days on the Kiewa Trail and in Killara (Wodonga)'s parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Killara (Wodonga), Bandiana has 6 playgrounds, a beach and a cinema. Bonegilla, next to Killara (Wodonga), has 3 playgrounds.
