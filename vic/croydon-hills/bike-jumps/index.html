Bike Jumps

Skate, BMX or pump track in Croydon Hills · Basic info

Bike Jumps is a skate, bmx or pump track in Croydon Hills, in the Maroondah City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. James Milne Dr/Yarra Rd (bus) is a 7-minute walk away.
