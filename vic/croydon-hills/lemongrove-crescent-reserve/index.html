Lemongrove Crescent Reserve

Park in Croydon Hills · Basic info

Lemongrove Crescent Reserve is a park in Croydon Hills, in the Maroondah City Council area, good for all ages. Plymouth Rd/Narr-maen Dr (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 768 m away with a baby change table.
