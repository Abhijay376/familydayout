Candlebark Walk Reserve Playground

Playground in Croydon Hills · Basic info

Candlebark Walk Reserve Playground in Croydon Hills is a public playground in the Maroondah City area. The Croydon Hills Connection passes about 100 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 370 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus 672 stops at Mckerral Pl/Yarra Rd, a 7-minute walk away.
