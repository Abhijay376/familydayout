Deep Lead in the Northern Grampians area has 13 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Deep Lead is Deep Lead Nature Conservation Reserve (No. 1), covering about 1,169 hectares. Timmins Hill Bushland Reserve and Nuggety Hill Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Deep Lead include Bunyip Bushland Reserve, Repper Bushland Reserve and Illawarra H9 Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Deep Lead.

Getting around

Deep Lead has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 35 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Deep Lead, so most trips start by car. By car from Deep Lead, Stawell is about 9 minutes away and Ararat about 32 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Deep Lead is Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest library to Deep Lead is Ararat Library in Ararat, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Deep Lead is Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Deep Lead is the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell, about 8 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Astor Cinema in Ararat is about 32 minutes' drive from Deep Lead. For a zoo or wildlife park, Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell is about 11 minutes' drive from Deep Lead. The nearest larger centre is Stawell, about 9 minutes from Deep Lead by car, with 6 playgrounds and a pool. From Deep Lead, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Federation Park in Stawell (5 min), North Park Playground in Stawell (7 min), Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell (7 min), the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell (8 min), Railway Station Art and Crafts Gallery in Stawell (9 min) and Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell (11 min).

Best for

Nearby suburbs

Stawell, next to Deep Lead, has 6 playgrounds, a pool and a zoo or wildlife park. Glenorchy, next to Deep Lead, has 1 playground. Illawarra, Germania and Campbells Bridge also border Deep Lead.
