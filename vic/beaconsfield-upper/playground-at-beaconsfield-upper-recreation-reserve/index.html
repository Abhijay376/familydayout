Playground at Beaconsfield Upper Recreation Reserve

Playground in Beaconsfield Upper · Basic info

Playground at Beaconsfield Upper Recreation Reserve is a public playground in Beaconsfield Upper, in the Cardinia Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 110 m away, and Playground at Sutherland Park Recreation Reserve is another option about 440 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km. It's easiest by car, about 2 minutes from Beaconsfield Upper.
