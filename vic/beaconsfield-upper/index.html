Beaconsfield Upper is in the Cardinia area, and our data lists 3 playgrounds and 9 parks and reserves in the suburb. On top of that, Beaconsfield Upper has a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Beaconsfield Upper is Upper Beaconsfield Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 35 hectares. Beaconsfield Upper Recreation Reserve and Gembrook G66 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Beaconsfield Upper include Harris Reserve, Keith Ewenson Park and Charing Cross Reserve. Playgrounds in Beaconsfield Upper include the playground at Keith Ewenson Park, the playground at Sutherland Park Recreation Reserve and the playground at Beaconsfield Upper Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Beaconsfield Upper. For older kids in Beaconsfield Upper there's the bmx track at Beaconsfield Upper Recreation Reserve. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Beaconsfield Upper for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Beaconsfield Upper has no train station; Officer Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 6.7 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Beaconsfield Upper, so most trips start by car. By car from Beaconsfield Upper, Pakenham is about 15 minutes away, Cranbourne about 24 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 46 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Jump Swim School Berwick in Berwick is about 11 minutes' drive from Beaconsfield Upper. The closest library to Beaconsfield Upper is Berwick Library in Berwick, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Beaconsfield Upper is Carrum Beach in Carrum, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Beaconsfield Upper is Village Cinemas in Narre Warren, about 15 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Oz Tenpin in Narre Warren is about 13 minutes' drive from Beaconsfield Upper.

Best for

Younger kids at Beaconsfield Upper's 3 local playgrounds. Teens who skate or ride, with the bmx track at Beaconsfield Upper Recreation Reserve in Beaconsfield Upper.

Harkaway, Pakenham Upper, Narre Warren East and Guys Hill also border Beaconsfield Upper.
