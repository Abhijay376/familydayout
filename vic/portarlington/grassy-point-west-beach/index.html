Grassy Point West Beach

Beach in Portarlington · Basic info

Grassy Point West Beach is a beach in Portarlington, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Oxley St/The Esplanade (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1263 m away.
