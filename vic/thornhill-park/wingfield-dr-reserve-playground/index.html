Wingfield Dr Reserve Playground

Playground in Thornhill Park · Basic info

Wingfield Dr Reserve Playground in Thornhill Park is a public playground in the Melton City area. Playground on Gilfillan Circuit is another option about 300 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.3 km from the playground, and bus 451 stops at Central Square Shopping Centre/Maya Ave, a 8-minute walk away.
