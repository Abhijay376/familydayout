Thornhill Park is in the Melton area, and our data lists 6 playgrounds in the suburb.

Landmarks

Playgrounds around Thornhill Park include Arklay Rd Reserve Playground, Parklea Way Reserve Playground and Wingfield Dr Reserve Playground.

Getting around

Thornhill Park has a train station: Rockbank Railway Station (V/Line: Ararat - Melbourne via Ballarat, Ballarat/Wendouree - Melbourne via Melton and Maryborough - Melbourne via Ballarat). Buses 444, 451, 464, 943 and 140A stop in Thornhill Park. V/Line coaches stop in Thornhill Park. By car from Thornhill Park, Melton is about 7 minutes away, Bacchus Marsh about 17 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 40 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Thornhill Park is Melton Swimmers in Melton South, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest library to Thornhill Park is Melton Library & Learning Hub in Melton, about 7 minutes' drive. For a beach, Lionhead Beach in Pentland Hills is about 23 minutes' drive from Thornhill Park. For a skate park, the skate park at Cambrian Way Reserve in Melton West is about 11 minutes' drive from Thornhill Park. For a cinema, Reading Cinemas in Melton West is about 8 minutes' drive from Thornhill Park. For an indoor play centre, Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes is about 23 minutes' drive from Thornhill Park. For a zoo or wildlife park, Werribee Open Range Zoo in Werribee South is about 39 minutes' drive from Thornhill Park.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Thornhill Park, Melton has 15 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Thornhill Park, Aintree has 16 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Thornhill Park, Strathtulloh has 7 playgrounds. Rockbank, next to Thornhill Park, has 7 playgrounds and a skate park. Cobblebank, Mount Cottrell and Grangefields also border Thornhill Park.
