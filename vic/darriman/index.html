Our data lists 7 parks and reserves in Darriman, in the Wellington area.

Landmarks

McLoughlins Beach - Seaspray Coastal Reserve is the largest mapped park in Darriman, at about 1,735 hectares. Mullungdung Flora and Fauna Reserve and Woodside H28 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Darriman include Mullungdung Reference Area, Darriman H33 Bushland Reserve and Warrigal Creek Streamside Reserve. The Bullockys Grave Walk passes through Darriman.

Getting around

There's no train station in Darriman. The closest to Darriman is Rosedale Railway Station (V/Line), about 35 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Darriman, so most trips start by car. By car from Darriman, Sale is about 35 minutes away and Traralgon about 70 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Darriman is Yarram Public Pool in Yarram, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest library to Darriman is Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Darriman is Woodside_Beach in Woodside Beach, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Darriman is the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram, about 24 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Sale Cinemas in Sale is about 34 minutes' drive from Darriman. The nearest larger centre is Sale, about 35 minutes from Darriman by car, with 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Darriman, the nearest places for kids by road are Woodside_Beach in Woodside Beach (19 min), Woodside Beach Road Playground in Woodside Beach (19 min), Woolami in Woodside Beach (22 min), McLoughlins Beach Playground in McLoughlins Beach (21 min), Yarram Historical Society Museum in Yarram (22 min) and Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram (23 min).

Best for

Free days on the Bullockys Grave Walk and in Darriman's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Giffard, next to Darriman, has a beach. Carrajung South also borders Darriman.
