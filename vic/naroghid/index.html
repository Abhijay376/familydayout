Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Naroghid in the Corangamite area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

The Camperdown to Timboon Rail Trail passes through Naroghid.

Getting around

There's no train station in Naroghid. The closest to Naroghid is Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line), about 8.7 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Naroghid, so most trips start by car. By car from Naroghid, Colac is about 51 minutes away, Warrnambool about 61 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon is about 38 minutes' drive from Naroghid. The closest library to Naroghid is Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake, about 38 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown is about 14 minutes' drive from Naroghid. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Naroghid, about 51 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Naroghid, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve in Camperdown (9 min), Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (14 min), the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (38 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (38 min), the playground at Tea Tree Lake Reserve in Mortlake (38 min) and Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake (38 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Naroghid on the Camperdown to Timboon Rail Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Naroghid, Camperdown has 3 playgrounds and a skate park. Cobden, next to Naroghid, has 1 playground. Bostocks Creek and Boorcan also border Naroghid.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Naroghid, without traffic.
