Getting around

There's no train station in Ocean Grange. The closest to Ocean Grange is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Ocean Grange, so most trips start by car. By car from Ocean Grange, Sale is about 1.5 hours away and Traralgon about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest places for kids by road from Ocean Grange are Lions Park Playground in Loch Sport (37 min) and Golden Beach Community Centre Playground in Golden Beach (71 min).
