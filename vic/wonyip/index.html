Wonyip is in the Wellington area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Wonyip, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Wonyip has no train station; Morwell Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 36 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wonyip, so most trips start by car. By car from Wonyip, Leongatha is about 66 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 86 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Toora Pool in Toora is about 27 minutes' drive from Wonyip. The closest library to Wonyip is Foster Library in Foster, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Wonyip is Toora Beach in Toora, about 35 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora is about 28 minutes' drive from Wonyip. From Wonyip, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Stanley Street Playground in Toora (27 min), Toora Pool in Toora (27 min), the playground at Sargasser Park in Toora (27 min), the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora (28 min), Toora Beach in Toora (35 min) and Port Welshpool and District Maritime Museum in Port Welshpool (33 min).
