Purnim in the Moyne area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Purnim is Purnim Recreation Reserve, covering about 6 hectares. The Wangoom Loop passes through Purnim.

Getting around

There's no train station in Purnim. The closest to Purnim is Sherwood Park Railway Station (V/Line), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Warrnambool - Mortlake route stop in Purnim. By car from Purnim, Warrnambool is about 17 minutes away and Hamilton about 83 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool is about 20 minutes' drive from Purnim. The closest library to Purnim is Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Purnim is Shelly Beach in Warrnambool, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Purnim is Warrnambool BMX Track in Warrnambool, about 20 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool is about 18 minutes' drive from Purnim. The closest indoor play centre to Purnim is The Bowl in Warrnambool, about 18 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee is about 13 minutes' drive from Purnim. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Purnim, about 17 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Purnim are the playground at Bushfield Recreation Reserve in Bushfield (10 min), Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (13 min), the playground at Albert Park in Warrnambool (16 min), Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool (18 min), SCOPE Galleries in Warrnambool (18 min) and The Bowl in Warrnambool (18 min).

Best for

Free days on the Wangoom Loop and in Purnim's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Purnim, Panmure has 1 playground. Framlingham, Cudgee and Ballangeich also border Purnim.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Purnim, without traffic.
