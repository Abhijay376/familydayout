Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Ensay North in the East Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Ensay North has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 58 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Ensay North, so most trips start by car. By car from Ensay North, Bairnsdale is about 85 minutes away and Lakes Entrance about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek is about 25 minutes' drive from Ensay North. From Ensay North, the nearest places for kids by road are Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek (25 min), Great Alpine Gallery in Swifts Creek (25 min), Petersens Art Gallery & Museum in Omeo (46 min), the playground at Omeo Historical Park & Museum in Omeo (47 min) and the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo (47 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Ensay North.
