Pirron Yallock in the Colac Otway area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Floating Islands Flora and Fauna Reserve is the largest mapped park in Pirron Yallock, at about 83 hectares. Unnamed P0353 Private Nature Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Pirron Yallock has no train station; Colac Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Pirron Yallock, so most trips start by car. By car from Pirron Yallock, Colac is about 12 minutes away, Warrnambool about 75 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac is about 15 minutes' drive from Pirron Yallock. For a skate park, the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt is about 17 minutes' drive from Pirron Yallock. For a cinema, Colac Cinemas in Colac is about 13 minutes' drive from Pirron Yallock. The closest indoor play centre to Pirron Yallock is Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac, about 13 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 12 minutes from Pirron Yallock by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Pirron Yallock, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (11 min), the playground at Western Bay Playground in Colac (12 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (13 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (13 min), Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (15 min) and the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (17 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Pirron Yallock.
