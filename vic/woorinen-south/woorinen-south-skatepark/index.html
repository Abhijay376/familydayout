Woorinen South Skatepark

Skate, BMX or pump track in Woorinen South · Basic info

Woorinen South Skatepark is a skate, bmx or pump track in Woorinen South, in the Swan Hill Rural City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Palmer Street/Station St (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 29 m away.
