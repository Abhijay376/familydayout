Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Maramingo Creek, in the East Gippsland area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Maramingo Creek is Maramingo Creek Flora Reserve, covering about 535 hectares. Unnamed P0069 Private Nature Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Maramingo Creek. The closest to Maramingo Creek is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 183 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Maramingo Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Maramingo Creek, Lakes Entrance is about 2.5 hours away and Bairnsdale about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a beach, Davis Creek Beach in Mallacoota is about 27 minutes' drive from Maramingo Creek. The closest places for kids by road from Maramingo Creek are Davis Creek Beach in Mallacoota (27 min), Betka Beach in Mallacoota (27 min) and WWII Bunker Museum in Mallacoota (29 min).
