Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Winslow, in the Moyne area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Winslow is Lake Cartcarrong Lake Reserve, covering about 27 hectares. Winslow Recreation Reserve and War Memorial Park are the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Winslow. The closest to Winslow is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Winslow, so most trips start by car. By car from Winslow, Warrnambool is about 18 minutes away and Hamilton about 66 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Winslow is Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest library to Winslow is Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Winslow is Shelly Beach in Warrnambool, about 19 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Koroit Youth Space in Koroit is about 13 minutes' drive from Winslow. For a cinema, Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool is about 17 minutes' drive from Winslow. For an indoor play centre, The Bowl in Warrnambool is about 19 minutes' drive from Winslow. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Winslow is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 26 minutes' drive. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Winslow, about 18 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Winslow, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Bushfield Recreation Reserve in Bushfield (14 min), Koroit Youth Space in Koroit (13 min), the playground at Witham Park in Warrnambool (13 min), Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool (17 min), Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool (19 min) and Warrnambool Art Gallery in Warrnambool (19 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Winslow.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Winslow, Koroit has 1 playground and a skate park. Mailors Flat and Grassmere also border Winslow.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Winslow, without traffic.
