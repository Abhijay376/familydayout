Playground on Village Drive

Playground in Dingley Village · Basic info

Playground on Village Drive in Dingley Village is a public playground in the Kingston City area. The Mordialloc Freeway Trail passes about 260 m away, and Playground on Balcombe Place is another option about 440 m away. There's a public toilet about 170 m away, and bus 812 stops at Chadwick Reserve/Howard Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
