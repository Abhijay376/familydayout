Dingley Village in the Kingston area has 18 playgrounds and 11 parks and reserves in our data. Dingley Village also has a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Dingley Village is Dingley Reserve, covering about 6 hectares. Higgins Close Reserve and Wheatland Crescent Reserve are the next largest. Dingley Village also has Swallow Reserve, Coughlan Reserve and The Grange Reserve. Playgrounds in Dingley Village include the playground at Coughlan Reserve, the playground at The Grange Reserve and the playground at Gumley Court Reserve. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Dingley Village. For older kids in Dingley Village there's Dingley Skate Park. The Mordialloc Freeway Trail, Dingley Bypass Trail and Mordialloc - Waterways - Braeside all pass through Dingley Village. Our data lists 5 cafes and family restaurants in Dingley Village for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Dingley Village. The closest to Dingley Village is Westall Railway Station (metro), about 4.3 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 705, 811, 812, 828 and 902 stop in Dingley Village. By car from Dingley Village, Dandenong is about 12 minutes away, Frankston about 19 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 28 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Dingley Village is Grenda Aquatic Centre in Keysborough, about 6 minutes' drive. For a library, MacGregor Library in Keysborough is about 6 minutes' drive from Dingley Village. The closest beach to Dingley Village is Mordialloc Beach in Parkdale, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Dingley Village is Village Cinemas in Cheltenham, about 9 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Småland in Springvale is about 8 minutes' drive from Dingley Village. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale is about 13 minutes' drive from Dingley Village.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 18 playgrounds across Dingley Village. Teens who skate or ride, with Dingley Skate Park in Dingley Village. Free days on the Mordialloc Freeway Trail and in Dingley Village's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Keysborough, next to Dingley Village, has 32 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Clayton South, next to Dingley Village, has 12 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Mordialloc, next to Dingley Village, has 11 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Dingley Village, Clarinda has 6 playgrounds and a library. Heatherton, Springvale South, Braeside and Moorabbin Airport also border Dingley Village.
