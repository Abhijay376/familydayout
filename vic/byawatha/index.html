Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Byawatha, in the Wangaratta area.

Landmarks

Byawatha Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Byawatha, at about 2 hectares. For walks and rides, Wangaratta - Eldorado Loop, Murray to Mountains Rail Trail: Wangaratta – Bright and Bowser-Wagunya Preferred Route cross Byawatha.

Getting around

Byawatha has no train station; Springhurst Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Byawatha, so most trips start by car. By car from Byawatha, Wangaratta is about 28 minutes away and Wodonga about 53 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Byawatha is Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest library to Byawatha is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 26 minutes' drive. For a beach, Sandy Beach in Beechworth is about 40 minutes' drive from Byawatha. The closest skate park to Byawatha is the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta, about 29 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 25 minutes' drive from Byawatha. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 28 minutes from Byawatha by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Byawatha are the playground at Gunhouse Park Reserve in Eldorado (12 min), Eldorado Museum in Eldorado (13 min), the playground at Apex Park in Wangaratta (24 min), Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta (25 min), Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (26 min) and Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta (29 min).

Best for

Free days on the Wangaratta - Eldorado Loop and in Byawatha's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Beechworth, next to Byawatha, has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. Chiltern, next to Byawatha, has 2 playgrounds. Springhurst, next to Byawatha, has 1 playground. Eldorado, next to Byawatha, has 1 playground. North Wangaratta, Londrigan, Dockers Plains and Bowser also border Byawatha.
