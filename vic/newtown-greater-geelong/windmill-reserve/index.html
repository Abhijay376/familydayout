Windmill Reserve

Playground in Newtown · Basic info

Windmill Reserve in Newtown is a public playground in the Greater Geelong City area. The Barwon River Trail is about 270 m away, and Buckingham Reserve is another option about 500 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 210 m from the playground, and bus 24 stops at Camden Rd/Ardlui Dr, a 9-minute walk away.
