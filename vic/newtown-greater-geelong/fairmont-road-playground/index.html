Fairmont Road Playground

Playground in Newtown · Basic info

Fairmont Road Playground in Newtown is a public playground in the Greater Geelong City area. The Hill, Mountian bike park Newtown is about 390 m away, and Subway is about 340 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 1.2 km away, and bus 24 stops at Harcourt St/Camden Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
