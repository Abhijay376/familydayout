(J.F.) Frier Reserve

Park in Newtown · Official details

(J.F.) Frier Reserve is a park in Newtown, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Frier Reserve/West Fyans St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 182 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed.
