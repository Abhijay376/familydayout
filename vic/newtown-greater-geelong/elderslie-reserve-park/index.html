Elderslie Reserve

Park in Newtown · Official details

Elderslie Reserve is a park in Newtown, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Raith Tce/Autumn St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 344 m away and dogs are allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Elderslie Reserve?
Elderslie Reserve is in Newtown, Victoria, at 40 Read Street, Newtown, VIC 3220.
