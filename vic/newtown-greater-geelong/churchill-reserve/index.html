Churchill Reserve

Playground in Newtown · Basic info

Churchill Reserve is a public playground in Newtown, in the Greater Geelong City area. The Bike: Western Link is about 230 m away, and Shearers Arms Gallery is about 320 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 280 m from the playground, and bus 24 stops at Silk St/Aberdeen, a 1-minute walk away.
