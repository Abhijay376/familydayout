Arden Avenue Reserve

Playground in Leopold · Basic info

Arden Avenue Reserve in Leopold is a public playground in the Greater Geelong City area. Leopold Library is about 220 m away, and Silvio's On Dorothy St is about 420 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 330 m away, and bus 32 stops at Kensington Hill Retirement Village/Kensington Rd , a 4-minute walk away.
