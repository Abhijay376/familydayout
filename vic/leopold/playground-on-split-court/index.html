Playground on Split Court

Playground in Leopold · Basic info

This is a public playground in Leopold. Older kids have a skate park about 40 m away, and for food, Subway is about 160 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 160 m from the playground, and buses 56 and 61 stop at Leopold Memorial Park/Bellarine Hwy, a 2-minute walk away.
