Playground at Gateway Sanctuary

Playground in Leopold · Basic info

Playground at Gateway Sanctuary in Leopold is a public playground in the Greater Geelong City area. Older kids have a skate park about 310 m away, and for food, Luka Pizza Bar is about 170 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 70 m from the playground, and bus 32 stops at Gateway Sanctuary/Melaluka Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
