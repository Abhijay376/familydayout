Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Liparoo in the Swan Hill area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Liparoo. The closest to Liparoo is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 111 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Liparoo, so most trips start by car. By car from Liparoo, Mildura is about 72 minutes away and Swan Hill about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest skate park to Liparoo is Robinvale Skatepark in Robinvale, about 34 minutes' drive. From Liparoo, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at River Murray Reserve in Wemen (7 min), the playground at Rotary Park in Robinvale (32 min), Rural Life Museum in Robinvale (33 min) and Robinvale Skatepark in Robinvale (34 min).
