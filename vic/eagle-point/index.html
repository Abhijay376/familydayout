Eagle Point in the East Gippsland area has 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Eagle Point Gippsland Lakes Reserve is the largest mapped park in Eagle Point, at about 55 hectares. Eagle Point Flora & Fauna Reserve and Eagle Point Gippsland Lakes Reserve are the next largest. Playgrounds around Eagle Point include the playground at Eagle Point Gippsland Lakes Reserve. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Eagle Point. For walks and rides, Paynesville - Eagle Point Foreshore Track crosses Eagle Point.

Getting around

Eagle Point has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 8.0 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Eagle Point, so most trips start by car. By car from Eagle Point, Bairnsdale is about 14 minutes away and Lakes Entrance about 44 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale is about 13 minutes' drive from Eagle Point. The closest library to Eagle Point is Paynesville Library in Paynesville, about 11 minutes' drive. For a beach, Boyfriend Beach in Forge Creek is about 12 minutes' drive from Eagle Point. For a skate park, the skate park at Gilsenan Reserve in Paynesville is about 11 minutes' drive from Eagle Point. For a cinema, Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale is about 11 minutes' drive from Eagle Point. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow is about 15 minutes' drive from Eagle Point. The nearest larger centre is Bairnsdale, about 14 minutes from Eagle Point by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Free days on the Paynesville - Eagle Point Foreshore Track and in Eagle Point's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Eagle Point, Bairnsdale has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Paynesville, next to Eagle Point, has 4 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Eagle Point, Forge Creek has a beach. Boole Poole, next to Eagle Point, has a beach. Newlands Arm and East Bairnsdale also border Eagle Point.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Eagle Point, without traffic.
