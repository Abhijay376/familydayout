Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Giffard West in the Wellington area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Giffard West has no train station; Sale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 30 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Giffard West, so most trips start by car. By car from Giffard West, Sale is about 27 minutes away and Traralgon about 62 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Giffard West is Indoor pool in Sale, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest library to Giffard West is Sale Library in Sale, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Giffard West is 90 Mile Beach Marine National Park in Giffard, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Giffard West is Sale Skate Park in Sale, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Giffard West is Sale Cinemas in Sale, about 27 minutes' drive. Sale is the closest bigger centre to Giffard West, about 27 minutes away by car, and has 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Giffard West are 90 Mile Beach Marine National Park in Giffard (34 min), Woodside_Beach in Woodside Beach (27 min), Woodside Beach Road Playground in Woodside Beach (27 min), Historic Gunpowder Magazine in Sale (25 min), McLoughlins Beach Playground in McLoughlins Beach (29 min) and Sale Historical Museum in Sale (25 min).
