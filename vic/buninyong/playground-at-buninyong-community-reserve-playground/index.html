Playground at Buninyong Community Reserve

Playground in Buninyong · Basic info

This is a public playground in Buninyong. The Australian National Road Cycling Championships Course passes about 190 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 330 m away. There's a public toilet about 650 m away, and bus 21 stops at De Soza Park/Warrenheip St, a 8-minute walk away.
