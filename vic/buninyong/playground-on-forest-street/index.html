Playground on Forest Street

Playground in Buninyong · Official details

Playground on Forest Street in Buninyong is a public playground in the Ballarat City area. The Australian National Road Cycling Championships Course is about 60 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 160 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 400 m from the playground, and bus 21 stops at Midland Hwy/Warrenheip St, a 5-minute walk away.
