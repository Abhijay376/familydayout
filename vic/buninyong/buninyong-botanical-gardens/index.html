Buninyong Botanical Gardens

Park in Buninyong · Basic info

Buninyong Botanical Gardens is a park in Buninyong, in the Ballarat City Council area, good for all ages. Yuille St/Warrenheip St (bus) is a 9-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 361 m away.
