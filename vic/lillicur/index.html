Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Lillicur, in the Central Goldfields area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lillicur is Talbot Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 165 hectares. Dulapwhang Waterholes Streamside Reserve and Amherst Reef Geological Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

Lillicur has no train station; Talbot Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lillicur, so most trips start by car. By car from Lillicur, Maryborough is about 35 minutes away, Ballarat about 54 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lillicur is Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot, about 28 minutes' drive. For a library, Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough is about 36 minutes' drive from Lillicur. For a skate park, the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort is about 43 minutes' drive from Lillicur. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 35 minutes from Lillicur by car, with a library. The closest places for kids by road from Lillicur are Courthouse in Avoca (13 min), the playground at Avoca Lions Park in Avoca (14 min), the playground at Toll Bar Park in Lexton (15 min), Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (28 min), Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (36 min) and Lee Medlyn Bottle Museum in Clunes (41 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lillicur.

Nearby suburbs

Talbot, next to Lillicur, has 1 playground and a pool. Next door to Lillicur, Lexton has 2 playgrounds. Burnbank, Bung Bong and Amherst also border Lillicur.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Lillicur, without traffic.
