Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Winnambool, in the Swan Hill area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Winnambool is Winnambool Bushland Reserve, covering about 11 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Winnambool. The closest to Winnambool is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 86 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Winnambool, so most trips start by car. By car from Winnambool, Swan Hill is about 86 minutes away and Mildura about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Winnambool is Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang, about 21 minutes' drive. From Winnambool, the nearest places for kids by road are Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang (21 min), the playground at River Murray Reserve in Wemen (43 min), Ouyen Swimming Pool in Ouyen (47 min), Roxy in Ouyen (47 min) and Jardine Park Playground in Ouyen (48 min).
