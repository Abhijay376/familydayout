Playground on Peryman Street

Playground in Pearcedale · Basic info

Playground on Peryman Street in Pearcedale is a public playground in the Casey City area. Older kids have a skate park about 260 m away, and Playground on Baxter - Tooradin Road is another option about 150 m away. There's a public toilet about 310 m away, and bus 776 stops at Pearcedale Village SC/Baxter-Tooradin Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
