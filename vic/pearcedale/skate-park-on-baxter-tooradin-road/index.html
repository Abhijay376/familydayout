Skate park on Baxter - Tooradin Road

Skate, BMX or pump track in Pearcedale · Basic info

Skate park on Baxter - Tooradin Road is a skate, bmx or pump track in Pearcedale, in the Casey City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Middle Rd/Baxter-Tooradin Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 498 m away.
