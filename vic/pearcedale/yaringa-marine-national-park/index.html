Yaringa Marine National Park

Park in Pearcedale · Official details

Yaringa Marine National Park is a park in Pearcedale, in the Casey City Council area, good for all ages.

Details

Good to know
Only accessible by boat; Small boats can be hired at Yaringa Boat Harbour; Large tidal variations up to three metres (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Yaringa Marine National Park?
Yaringa Marine National Park is in Pearcedale, Victoria.
Is Yaringa Marine National Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
