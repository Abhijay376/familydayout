Our data lists 4 playgrounds and 5 parks and reserves in Pearcedale, in the Casey area. Pearcedale also has a zoo or wildlife park and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Pearcedale is Yaringa Marine National Park, covering about 803 hectares. Tyabb Bushland Reserve and Colley Street Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Pearcedale also has Eric Court Reserve and Bayliss Court Reserve. Playgrounds in Pearcedale include the playground at Eric Court Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Pearcedale. Places to visit in Pearcedale include Moonlit Sanctuary. For older kids in Pearcedale there's Pearcedale BMX Track.

Getting around

There's no train station in Pearcedale. The closest to Pearcedale is Somerville Railway Station (metro), about 5.8 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 776 and 792 stop in Pearcedale. By car from Pearcedale, Frankston is about 12 minutes away, Cranbourne about 16 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 49 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Kingswim Langwarrin in Langwarrin is about 10 minutes' drive from Pearcedale. For a library, Frankston Library in Frankston is about 14 minutes' drive from Pearcedale. For a beach, Frankston Beach in Frankston is about 16 minutes' drive from Pearcedale. The closest cinema to Pearcedale is Village Cinemas in Frankston, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Pearcedale is Zone Bowling in Frankston, about 12 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Frankston, about 12 minutes from Pearcedale by car, with 27 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Pearcedale's 4 local playgrounds. Teens who skate or ride, with Pearcedale BMX Track in Pearcedale.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Pearcedale, Langwarrin has 22 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Somerville, next to Pearcedale, has 13 playgrounds and a skate park. Baxter, next to Pearcedale, has 6 playgrounds. Next door to Pearcedale, Cranbourne South has 5 playgrounds and a skate park. Langwarrin South, Devon Meadows and Cannons Creek also border Pearcedale.
