Fyans Creek in the Northern Grampians area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Fyans Creek is Fyans Creek Bushland Reserve, covering about 68 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Fyans Creek. The closest to Fyans Creek is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 40 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Fyans Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Fyans Creek, Stawell is about 23 minutes away and Ararat about 47 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Halls Gap Swimming Pool in Halls Gap is about 14 minutes' drive from Fyans Creek. For a beach, Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans is about 25 minutes' drive from Fyans Creek. The closest skate park to Fyans Creek is the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell, about 23 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Halls Gap Zoo in Halls Gap is about 14 minutes' drive from Fyans Creek. Stawell is the closest bigger centre to Fyans Creek, about 23 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds and a pool. From Fyans Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are Halls Gap Swimming Pool in Halls Gap (14 min), the playground at Halls Gap Picnic Reserve in Halls Gap (15 min), Halls Gap Zoo in Halls Gap (14 min), Brambuk Cultural Centre in Halls Gap (18 min), Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans (25 min) and MOCO Gallery in Halls Gap (19 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Fyans Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Fyans Creek, Halls Gap has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a zoo or wildlife park. Lake Fyans, next to Fyans Creek, has a beach. Lake Lonsdale also borders Fyans Creek.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Fyans Creek, without traffic.
