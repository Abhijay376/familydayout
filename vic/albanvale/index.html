Albanvale is in the Brimbank area, and our data lists 4 playgrounds and 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Albanvale is Evergreen Avenue Reserve, covering about 3 hectares. Playgrounds around Albanvale include the playground at Evergreen Avenue Reserve, the playground at Albanvale Community Centre and the playground at Bloomingdale/Witchwood/Woodland Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Albanvale.

Getting around

Albanvale has no train station; Deer Park Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 3.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 420, 422, 423 and 424 stop in Albanvale. By car from Albanvale, Sunshine is about 12 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 21 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 30 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Albanvale is Warm Water in Keilor Downs, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest library to Albanvale is Brimbank Libraries Deer Park Branch in Deer Park, about 4 minutes' drive. For a beach, Altona Beach in Altona is about 25 minutes' drive from Albanvale. For a skate park, the skate park at Robert Bruce Reserve in Deer Park is about 4 minutes' drive from Albanvale. The closest cinema to Albanvale is Hoyts in Taylors Lakes, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Albanvale is Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes, about 10 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville is about 27 minutes' drive from Albanvale. The nearest larger centre is Sunshine, about 12 minutes from Albanvale by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Albanvale's 4 local playgrounds.

Nearby suburbs

St Albans, next to Albanvale, has 33 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Albanvale, Deer Park has 17 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Cairnlea, next to Albanvale, has 12 playgrounds. Kings Park, next to Albanvale, has 10 playgrounds. Burnside also borders Albanvale.
