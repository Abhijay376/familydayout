Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Ryanston in the Bass Coast area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Ryanston has no train station; Stony Point Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 37 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Ryanston, so most trips start by car. By car from Ryanston, Wonthaggi is about 14 minutes away, Leongatha about 44 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Ryanston is Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Wonthaggi, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest library to Ryanston is Wonthaggi Principal Library in Wonthaggi, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Ryanston is Williamsons Beach in Wonthaggi, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Ryanston is Wonthaggi Skate Park in Wonthaggi, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Ryanston is Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Ryanston is Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville, about 29 minutes' drive. Wonthaggi is the closest bigger centre to Ryanston, about 14 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Ryanston, the nearest places for kids by road are Kids Playground in Wonthaggi (12 min), the playground at South Dudley Reserve in South Dudley (13 min), Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Wonthaggi (13 min), Bass Coast Arts Society in Wonthaggi (14 min), Wonthaggi Skate Park in Wonthaggi (13 min) and Wonthaggi & District Historical Museum in Wonthaggi (14 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Ryanston.

Nearby suburbs

Dalyston, next to Ryanston, has 1 playground and a beach. Glen Alvie and Almurta also border Ryanston.
