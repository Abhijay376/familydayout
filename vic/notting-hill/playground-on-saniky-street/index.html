Playground on Saniky Street

Playground in Notting Hill · Basic info

Playground on Saniky Street is a public playground in Notting Hill, in the Monash City area. Yunyun is about 270 m away if you need food, and Playground on Samada Street is another option about 180 m away. There's a public toilet about 480 m away, and buses 693 and 742 stop at Blackburn Rd/Ferntree Gully Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
