Jumbunna is in the South Gippsland area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Jumbunna, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Jumbunna has no train station; Drouin Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 37 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Jumbunna, so most trips start by car. By car from Jumbunna, Leongatha is about 21 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 28 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Korumburra Outdoor Pool in Korumburra is about 7 minutes' drive from Jumbunna. For a library, Leongatha Library in Leongatha is about 20 minutes' drive from Jumbunna. For a beach, The Caves in Inverloch is about 26 minutes' drive from Jumbunna. The closest skate park to Jumbunna is Leongatha Skate Park in Leongatha, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Jumbunna is Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha, about 20 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville is about 34 minutes' drive from Jumbunna. The nearest larger centre is Leongatha, about 21 minutes from Jumbunna by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Jumbunna are the playground at Peterson Street Reserve in Korumburra (7 min), Korumburra Outdoor Pool in Korumburra (7 min), the playground at Coleman Park in Korumburra (7 min), Coal Creek Community Park & Museum in Korumburra (11 min), Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha (20 min) and Leongatha Library in Leongatha (20 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Jumbunna.

Nearby suburbs

Korumburra, next to Jumbunna, has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Bena, next to Jumbunna, has 1 playground. Kongwak also borders Jumbunna.
