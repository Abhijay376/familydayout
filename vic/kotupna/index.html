Kotupna is in the Moira area, and our data lists 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Wakiti Creek Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Kotupna, at about 312 hectares. Deep Creek Streamside Reserve and Kotupna Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Kotupna include Kotupna School Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Kotupna has no train station; Mooroopna Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 34 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kotupna, so most trips start by car. By car from Kotupna, Echuca is about 34 minutes away, Shepparton about 45 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kotupna is Nathalia Memorial Pool in Nathalia, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest library to Kotupna is Nathalia Library in Nathalia, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Kotupna is Scotts Beach in Yalca, about 39 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the bmx track on Racecourse Road in Kyabram is about 28 minutes' drive from Kotupna. For a cinema, Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca is about 35 minutes' drive from Kotupna. The closest indoor play centre to Kotupna is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 45 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Kotupna is Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram, about 28 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 34 minutes from Kotupna by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Kotupna are Barmah Forest Heritge and Education Centre in Nathalia (10 min), Nathalia Library in Nathalia (10 min), the playground at Apex Park in Nathalia (11 min), Nathalia Memorial Pool in Nathalia (12 min), the playground at Undera Youth Spot in Undera (24 min) and Kyabram Library And Community Hub in Kyabram (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Kotupna.

Nearby suburbs

Nathalia, next to Kotupna, has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Undera, next to Kotupna, has 1 playground. St Germains, Picola, Lower Moira and Kaarimba also border Kotupna.
