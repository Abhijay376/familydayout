Moorabbin Airport Playground

Playground in Moorabbin Airport · Basic info

This is a public playground in Moorabbin Airport. Australian National Aviation Museum is about 290 m away. There's a public toilet about 130 m away, and buses 811 and 812 stop at Bundora Pde/Lower Dandenong Rd, a 9-minute walk away.
