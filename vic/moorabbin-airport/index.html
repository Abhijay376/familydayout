Moorabbin Airport in the Kingston area has 1 playground in our data. On top of that, Moorabbin Airport has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

Playgrounds in Moorabbin Airport include Moorabbin Airport Playground. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Moorabbin Airport. Places to visit in Moorabbin Airport include Australian National Aviation Museum. DFO Moorabbin, Direct Factory Outlets and Kingston Central Plaza are the main shopping centres. Our data lists 9 cafes and family restaurants in Moorabbin Airport for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Moorabbin Airport. The closest to Moorabbin Airport is Parkdale Railway Station (metro), about 2.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 811, 812 and 828 stop in Moorabbin Airport. By car from Moorabbin Airport, Dandenong is about 15 minutes away, Frankston about 21 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 29 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Moorabbin Airport is Mentone Sports and Aquatic Centre in Mentone, about 7 minutes' drive. For a library, Parkdale Library in Parkdale is about 6 minutes' drive from Moorabbin Airport. For a beach, Mordialloc Beach in Parkdale is about 7 minutes' drive from Moorabbin Airport. For a skate park, Mordialloc Skate Park in Aspendale is about 7 minutes' drive from Moorabbin Airport. The closest cinema to Moorabbin Airport is Village Cinemas in Cheltenham, about 7 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Småland in Springvale is about 13 minutes' drive from Moorabbin Airport. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale is about 15 minutes' drive from Moorabbin Airport. Dandenong is the closest bigger centre to Moorabbin Airport, about 15 minutes away by car, and has 21 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.
