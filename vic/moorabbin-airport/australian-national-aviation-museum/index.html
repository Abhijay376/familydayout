Australian National Aviation Museum

Museum or gallery in Moorabbin Airport · Basic info

Australian National Aviation Museum is a museum or gallery in Moorabbin Airport, in the Kingston City Council area, best for school-age kids. DFO/Centre Dandenong Rd (bus) is a 14-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 330 m away.
