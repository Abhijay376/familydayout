Our data lists 19 parks and reserves in Mount Buller, in the Mansfield area. On top of that, Mount Buller has a museum or gallery, a cinema and an indoor play centre.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Mount Buller is Barkly River State Forest, covering about 27,578 hectares. The Governors Remote and Natural Area and Howqua River Heritage River are the next largest. Mount Buller also has Gallows, Axe Track and Fergusons. Our data shows 16 public toilets in Mount Buller. Picnic areas in Mount Buller include Circuit Road, Craigs Hut and Razorback Hut. For camping, there's Axe Track and Fergusons. In Mount Buller you'll also find National Alpine Museum of Australia, Mt Buller Cinema and Buller Air Zone. The Australian Alps Walking Track, McMillans Walking Track and Merrijig - Two Rivers Loop all pass through Mount Buller. Our data lists 3 cafes and family restaurants in Mount Buller for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Mount Buller has no train station; Benalla Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 79 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mount Buller, so most trips start by car. By car from Mount Buller, Mansfield is about 49 minutes away and Benalla about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The nearest larger centre is Mansfield, about 49 minutes from Mount Buller by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Mount Buller are Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield (48 min), Mansfield Library in Mansfield (49 min), the playground at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (49 min), the playground at Mansfield Botanic Park in Mansfield (49 min), the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (49 min) and Mansfield Outdoor Swimming Pool in Mansfield (50 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the cinema, indoor play centre and museum or gallery in Mount Buller. Free days on the Australian Alps Walking Track and in Mount Buller's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Tolmie, next to Mount Buller, has 1 playground. Next door to Mount Buller, Jamieson has 1 playground. Reynard, Mirimbah, Merrijig and Licola North also border Mount Buller.
