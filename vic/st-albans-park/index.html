St Albans Park is in the Greater Geelong area, and our data lists 6 playgrounds and 6 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Aldershot Reserve is the largest mapped park in St Albans Park, at about 5 hectares. Paramount Crescent Reserve and Ottoman Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in St Albans Park include Aitken Park, Stan Swain Park and Ellstone Reserve. Playgrounds around St Albans Park include the playground at Aitken Park, the playground at Ottoman Reserve and the playground at Stan Swain Park.

Getting around

There's no train station in St Albans Park. The closest to St Albans Park is Marshall Railway Station (V/Line), about 3.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 30, 31 and 40 stop in St Albans Park. By car from St Albans Park, Geelong is about 7 minutes away, Torquay about 24 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 72 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to St Albans Park is Splashdown in Moolap, about 3 minutes' drive. For a library, Newcomb Library in Newcomb is about 3 minutes' drive from St Albans Park. The closest beach to St Albans Park is Eastern Beach in Geelong, about 8 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the bmx track in Moolap in Moolap is about 3 minutes' drive from St Albans Park. The closest cinema to St Albans Park is The Pivotonian Cinema in South Geelong, about 8 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Laserquest in Geelong is about 10 minutes' drive from St Albans Park. For a zoo or wildlife park, Adventure Park in Wallington is about 14 minutes' drive from St Albans Park.

Best for

Younger kids at St Albans Park's 6 local playgrounds.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to St Albans Park, Moolap has 4 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to St Albans Park, Whittington has 5 playgrounds. Next door to St Albans Park, Thomson has 3 playgrounds. Next door to St Albans Park, Charlemont has 3 playgrounds. Breakwater also borders St Albans Park.
