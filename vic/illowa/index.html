Illowa in the Moyne area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Illowa is Warrnambool - Port Fairy Coastal Reserve, covering about 379 hectares. Belfast (Addition) Coastal Reserve and Belfast (Addition) Coastal Reserve are the next largest. The Port Fairy to Warrnambool Rail Trail and Walking and Bridle Path all pass through Illowa.

Getting around

Illowa has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 8.7 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 8 stops in Illowa. By car from Illowa, Warrnambool is about 12 minutes away and Portland about 75 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool is about 15 minutes' drive from Illowa. The closest library to Illowa is Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Illowa is Killarney Beach in Killarney, about 13 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Koroit Youth Space in Koroit is about 9 minutes' drive from Illowa. For a cinema, Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool is about 12 minutes' drive from Illowa. The closest indoor play centre to Illowa is The Bowl in Warrnambool, about 13 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee is about 29 minutes' drive from Illowa. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 12 minutes from Illowa by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Illowa are W P O'Sullivan Playground in Dennington (6 min), Koroit Youth Space in Koroit (9 min), the playground at Victoria Park in Warrnambool (9 min), Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool (12 min), Killarney Beach in Killarney (13 min) and Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool (13 min).

Best for

Free days on the Port Fairy to Warrnambool Rail Trail and in Illowa's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Illowa, Koroit has 1 playground and a skate park. Dennington, next to Illowa, has 1 playground. Southern Cross also borders Illowa.
