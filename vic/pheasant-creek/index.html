Landmarks

Kinglake G129 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Pheasant Creek, at about 2 hectares.

Getting around

Pheasant Creek has no train station; Hurstbridge Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 384 stops in Pheasant Creek. By car from Pheasant Creek, Healesville is about 40 minutes away, Lilydale about 47 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 63 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Pheasant Creek is Whittlesea Swimming Centre in Whittlesea, about 17 minutes' drive. For a library, Mernda Library in Mernda is about 28 minutes' drive from Pheasant Creek. The closest skate park to Pheasant Creek is Hurstbridge Half-pipe in Hurstbridge, about 32 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Mill Park is about 36 minutes' drive from Pheasant Creek. The closest indoor play centre to Pheasant Creek is Timezone in Mill Park, about 35 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Funfields in Whittlesea is about 20 minutes' drive from Pheasant Creek. Healesville is the closest bigger centre to Pheasant Creek, about 40 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Pheasant Creek are Bollygum Park in Kinglake (8 min), House of Bottles Museum & Tearooms in Kinglake (9 min), Whittlesea Swimming Centre in Whittlesea (17 min), Church Street Park Playground in Whittlesea (17 min), Birdy Cove in Whittlesea (20 min) and Funfields in Whittlesea (20 min).
