Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Betley in the Central Goldfields area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Dunolly Cycle Tracks, Dunolly - Havelock Gold Rush Cycle Trail and Betley Cycle Track cross Betley.

Getting around

Betley has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Betley, so most trips start by car. By car from Betley, Maryborough is about 21 minutes away, Bendigo about 52 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly is about 13 minutes' drive from Betley. For a library, Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough is about 21 minutes' drive from Betley. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 21 minutes from Betley by car, with a library. From Betley, the nearest places for kids by road are Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (12 min), Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (13 min), Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (21 min), the playground at Market Reserve in Carisbrook (25 min), Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground in Talbot (37 min) and Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (37 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Betley on the Dunolly Cycle Tracks.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Betley, without traffic.
