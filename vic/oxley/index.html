Oxley is in the Wangaratta area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Oxley is Oxley Bushland Reserve, covering about 7 hectares. Oxley Streamside Reserve is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Oxley include Oxley Memorial Park. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Oxley. The Murray to Mountains Rail Trail: Wangaratta – Bright, Murray to Mountains Rail Trail: Wangaratta – Milawa and Milawa Trail all pass through Oxley.

Getting around

There's no train station in Oxley. The closest to Oxley is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Oxley. By car from Oxley, Wangaratta is about 14 minutes away and Benalla about 33 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Splashpark in Wangaratta is about 11 minutes' drive from Oxley. For a library, Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta is about 14 minutes' drive from Oxley. For a beach, Sandy Beach in Beechworth is about 35 minutes' drive from Oxley. The closest skate park to Oxley is the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta, about 11 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 14 minutes' drive from Oxley. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Oxley, about 14 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Oxley, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at John McAleese Community Park in Milawa (5 min), Splashpark in Wangaratta (11 min), the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta (11 min), Moore than Swimming in Wangaratta (12 min), the playground at Batchelors Green in Wangaratta (13 min) and Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (14 min).

Best for

Free days on the Murray to Mountains Rail Trail: Wangaratta – Bright and in Oxley's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Oxley, Wangaratta has 9 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Oxley, Milawa has 1 playground. Docker also borders Oxley.
