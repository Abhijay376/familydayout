Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Carngham in the Pyrenees area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Carngham has no train station; Wendouree Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Carngham, so most trips start by car. By car from Carngham, Ballarat is about 29 minutes away, Ararat about 65 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Carngham is Ballarat Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Lake Gardens, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest library to Carngham is Sebastopol Library in Sebastopol, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Carngham is Linton BMX park in Linton, about 19 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Showbiz Cinemas Ballarat in Smythes Creek is about 27 minutes' drive from Carngham. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 32 minutes' drive from Carngham. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Carngham is Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East, about 35 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Ballarat, about 29 minutes from Carngham by car, with 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Carngham are Linton BMX park in Linton (19 min), Linton Skate Park in Linton (19 min), Linton Recreation Reserve Playground in Linton (20 min), Woady Yaloak Recreation Reserve Playground in Smythesdale (18 min), Haddon Tramway Preservation Association in Haddon (18 min) and Showbiz Cinemas Ballarat in Smythes Creek (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Carngham.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Carngham, Haddon has 3 playgrounds and a skate park. Burrumbeet, Brewster and Bo Peep also border Carngham.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Carngham, without traffic.
