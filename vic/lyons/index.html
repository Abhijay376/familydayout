Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Lyons, in the Glenelg area.

Landmarks

Lyons H37 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Lyons, at about 42 hectares. Lyons H12 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Lyons has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 98 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lyons, so most trips start by car. By car from Lyons, Portland is about 38 minutes away and Hamilton about 61 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood is about 17 minutes' drive from Lyons. The closest library to Lyons is Heywood Library in Heywood, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Lyons is Whalers Point in Portland, about 38 minutes' drive. Portland is the closest bigger centre to Lyons, about 38 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Lyons are Dartmoor Coach House Museum in Dartmoor (17 min), Heywood Library in Heywood (17 min), the playground at Apex Park in Heywood (17 min), Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood (17 min), the playground at Heywood Youth Park in Heywood (18 min) and Whalers Point in Portland (38 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lyons.
