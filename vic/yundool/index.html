Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Yundool, in the Moira area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Yundool is Youarang Natural Features Reserve, covering about 217 hectares.

Getting around

Yundool has no train station; Benalla Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 35 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yundool, so most trips start by car. By car from Yundool, Benalla is about 32 minutes away and Shepparton about 49 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Yundool is Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest library to Yundool is Numurkah Library in Numurkah, about 44 minutes' drive. For a beach, Fosters Beach in Cobram is about 36 minutes' drive from Yundool. The closest skate park to Yundool is Pump Track in Benalla, about 33 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Cobram Cinema in Cobram is about 36 minutes' drive from Yundool. The nearest larger centre is Benalla, about 32 minutes from Yundool by car, with 5 playgrounds and a pool. From Yundool, the nearest places for kids by road are Katamatite Historical Society Museum in Katamatite (19 min), the playground at E.W Kilgour Reserve in Katamatite (19 min), the playground at C R Wood Park in Katamatite (19 min), Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (31 min), Benalla Costume and Pioneer Museum in Benalla (32 min) and Pump Track in Benalla (33 min).

Best for
