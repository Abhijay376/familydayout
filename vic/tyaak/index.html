Tyaak in the Mitchell area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tyaak is The Round House Conservation Reserve, covering about 84 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tyaak. The closest to Tyaak is Broadford Railway Station (V/Line), about 8.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tyaak, so most trips start by car. By car from Tyaak, Seymour is about 29 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 56 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 75 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore is about 23 minutes' drive from Tyaak. For a library, Broadford Library in Broadford is about 11 minutes' drive from Tyaak. For a skate park, Broadford Skate Park in Broadford is about 9 minutes' drive from Tyaak. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 29 minutes from Tyaak by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Tyaak are Rupert Street Playground in Broadford (9 min), Broadford Skate Park in Broadford (9 min), Sutherland Street Playground in Broadford (10 min), Broadford Library in Broadford (11 min), Kilmore Skate Park in Kilmore (22 min) and Kilmore Library in Kilmore (23 min).

Best for
