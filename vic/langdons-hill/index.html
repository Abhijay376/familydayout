Langdons Hill is in the Hepburn area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Langdons Hill, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Langdons Hill has no train station; Creswick Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Langdons Hill, so most trips start by car. By car from Langdons Hill, Daylesford is about 24 minutes away, Ballarat about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Langdons Hill is Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest library to Langdons Hill is Daylesford Library in Daylesford, about 24 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick is about 25 minutes' drive from Langdons Hill. The closest cinema to Langdons Hill is Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central, about 40 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 41 minutes' drive from Langdons Hill. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek is about 33 minutes' drive from Langdons Hill. The nearest larger centre is Daylesford, about 24 minutes from Langdons Hill by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Langdons Hill, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Korweinguboora Recreation Reserve in Spargo Creek (21 min), Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford (24 min), the playground at Bullarook Recreation Reserve in Bullarook (28 min), Daylesford Spa Country Railway in Daylesford (27 min), Daylesford Library in Daylesford (24 min) and Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick (25 min).
