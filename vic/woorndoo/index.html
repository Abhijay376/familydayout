Woorndoo in the Moyne area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Lake Eyang Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Woorndoo, at about 130 hectares. Towanway N6 Lake Reserve and Towanway N5 Lake Reserve are the next largest. Woorndoo also has Woorndoo Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Woorndoo.

Getting around

Woorndoo has no train station; Terang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 40 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Woorndoo, so most trips start by car. By car from Woorndoo, Warrnambool is about 59 minutes away and Ararat about 61 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Willaura Outdoor Pool in Willaura is about 45 minutes' drive from Woorndoo. The closest library to Woorndoo is Lake Bolac Library in Lake Bolac, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Woorndoo is Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake, about 19 minutes' drive. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Woorndoo, about 59 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Woorndoo, the nearest places for kids by road are Lake Bolac Library in Lake Bolac (17 min), Market Square Playground in Mortlake (18 min), Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake (19 min), Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake (19 min), the playground at Tea Tree Lake Reserve in Mortlake (19 min) and Willaura Outdoor Pool in Willaura (45 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Woorndoo.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Woorndoo, Mortlake has 2 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Woorndoo, Lake Bolac has 1 playground and a library. Dundonnell and Chatsworth also border Woorndoo.
