Our data lists 1 playground and 5 parks and reserves in Springhurst, in the Wangaratta area.

Landmarks

Barambogie I12 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Springhurst, at about 65 hectares. Barambogie I11 Bushland Reserve and Carraragarmungee I10 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Springhurst include Barambogie I71 Bushland Reserve and Bontherambo H119 Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Springhurst. The Wangaratta - Eldorado Loop and Bowser-Wagunya Preferred Route all pass through Springhurst.

Getting around

Trains stop at Springhurst Railway Station (V/Line: Albury - Melbourne via Seymour) in Springhurst. Buses on the Wangaratta - Yarrawonga and Wangaratta - Chiltern via Rutherglen routes stop in Springhurst. V/Line coaches stop in Springhurst. By car from Springhurst, Wangaratta is about 25 minutes away and Wodonga about 35 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Springhurst is Rutherglen War Memorial Swimming Pool in Rutherglen, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest library to Springhurst is Rutherglen Public Library in Rutherglen, about 15 minutes' drive. For a beach, Sandy Beach in Beechworth is about 32 minutes' drive from Springhurst. For a skate park, the pump track on Victoria Street in Wahgunyah is about 22 minutes' drive from Springhurst. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 23 minutes' drive from Springhurst. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 25 minutes from Springhurst by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Springhurst, the nearest places for kids by road are Martin Park Playground in Chiltern (13 min), Chiltern Athenaeum Museum in Chiltern (13 min), Rutherglen Public Library in Rutherglen (15 min), Apex Park Playground in Rutherglen (15 min), Rutherglen War Memorial Swimming Pool in Rutherglen (16 min) and Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta (23 min).

Best for

Free days on the Wangaratta - Eldorado Loop and in Springhurst's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Chiltern, next to Springhurst, has 2 playgrounds. Boralma, Boorhaman East and Boorhaman also border Springhurst.
