Playground at Kirwana Place Reserve

Playground in Montmorency · Basic info

Playground at Kirwana Place Reserve is a public playground in Montmorency, in the Banyule City area. The Bolton Street Bike Path is about 230 m away, and Playground at Sackville Reserve is another option about 220 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.3 km from the playground, and buses 293, 513 and 901 stop at Cressy St/Main Rd, a 10-minute walk away.
