Our data lists 10 playgrounds and 24 parks and reserves in Montmorency, in the Banyule area.

Landmarks

Montmorency Park is the largest mapped park in Montmorency, at about 10 hectares. Petrie Park and Rattray Reserve are the next largest. Montmorency also has Wooded Way, Belmont Reserve and Sylvan Play Park. Playgrounds around Montmorency include the playground at Petrie Park, the playground at Montmorency Park and the playground at Sylvan Play Park. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Montmorency. The Plenty River Trail, Banyule LBN8 and Bolton Street Bike Path all pass through Montmorency. Our data lists 6 cafes and family restaurants in Montmorency for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Montmorency has a train station: Montmorency Railway Station (metro: Hurstbridge Line). Buses 293, 513, 514, 901 and 902 stop in Montmorency. By car from Montmorency, Box Hill is about 18 minutes away, Ringwood about 22 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 28 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, WaterMarc in Greensborough is about 5 minutes' drive from Montmorency. The closest library to Montmorency is Eltham Library in Eltham, about 6 minutes' drive. For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 26 minutes' drive from Montmorency. For a skate park, Eltham Skate Park in Eltham is about 4 minutes' drive from Montmorency. The closest cinema to Montmorency is Hoyts in Greensborough, about 6 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Rare Bears Indoor Play Centre in Eltham is about 4 minutes' drive from Montmorency. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bundoora Park Farm in Bundoora is about 18 minutes' drive from Montmorency.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 10 playgrounds across Montmorency. Free days on the Plenty River Trail and in Montmorency's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Greensborough, next to Montmorency, has 35 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Eltham, next to Montmorency, has 15 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Montmorency, Yallambie has 6 playgrounds. Lower Plenty, next to Montmorency, has 6 playgrounds. Briar Hill also borders Montmorency.
