Goomalibee in the Benalla area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Goomalibee is Goomalibee H83 Bushland Reserve, covering about 6 hectares. Goomalibee H81 Bushland Reserve and Goomalibee Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Goomalibee include Goomalibee H82 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Goomalibee. The closest to Goomalibee is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Goomalibee, so most trips start by car. By car from Goomalibee, Benalla is about 17 minutes away, Shepparton about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Goomalibee is Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla, about 17 minutes' drive. For a library, Shepparton Library in Shepparton is about 40 minutes' drive from Goomalibee. The closest beach to Goomalibee is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 42 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Pump Track in Benalla is about 18 minutes' drive from Goomalibee. For a cinema, Swanpool Cinema in Swanpool is about 37 minutes' drive from Goomalibee. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 39 minutes' drive from Goomalibee. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kidstown in Mooroopna is about 43 minutes' drive from Goomalibee. The nearest larger centre is Benalla, about 17 minutes from Goomalibee by car, with 5 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Goomalibee are Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (17 min), Benalla Splash Park in Benalla (17 min), Benalla Costume and Pioneer Museum in Benalla (17 min), Pump Track in Benalla (18 min), Benalla Art Gallery in Benalla (18 min) and the playground at Benalla Botanical Gardens in Benalla (18 min).
