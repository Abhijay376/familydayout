Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Jericho, in the Baw Baw area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Jericho is Thomson River Forest Reserve, covering about 40,365 hectares. Jericho also has Jericho Cemetery Historic and Cultural Features Reserve and Red Jacket Cemetery Historic and Cultural Features Reserve. For walks and rides, Australian Alps Walking Track, McMillans Walking Track and Red Jacket Relics Access Track cross Jericho.

Getting around

Jericho has no train station; Moe Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 60 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Jericho, so most trips start by car. By car from Jericho, Mansfield is about 2 hours away and Traralgon about 2 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Free days on the Australian Alps Walking Track and in Jericho's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Matlock, Cambarville and Baw Baw also border Jericho.
