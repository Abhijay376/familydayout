Point Addis Marine National Park

Park in Bells Beach · Official details

Point Addis Marine National Park is a park in Bells Beach, in the Surf Coast Shire Council area, good for all ages.

Details

Seasons
Whale spotting May to September; summer weekend car parks can get crowded (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
Tides: some points not passable at high tide; Wear grippy shoes on rock platforms; Eastern end of Addiscott Beach is an optional clothing beach (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Point Addis Marine National Park?
Point Addis Marine National Park is in Bells Beach, Victoria.
Is Point Addis Marine National Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
