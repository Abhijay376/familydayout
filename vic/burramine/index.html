Burramine is in the Moira area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb. Burramine also has 17 beaches.

Landmarks

Murray Valley National Park Boomanoomana is the largest mapped park in Burramine, at about 610 hectares. Murray Valley Regional Park Mulwala and Big Reedy Lagoon Wildlife Reserve (hunting) are the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Burramine. In Burramine you'll also find Bourkes Beach No 1 and Bourkes Beach No 2. The Murray River Adventure Trail passes through Burramine. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Burramine for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Burramine has no train station; Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 54 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Burramine. By car from Burramine, Wangaratta is about 54 minutes away and Benalla about 58 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga is about 9 minutes' drive from Burramine. The closest skate park to Burramine is the skate park at Federation Park in Cobram, about 22 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Cobram Cinema in Cobram is about 21 minutes' drive from Burramine. For a zoo or wildlife park, Tunzafun in Yarrawonga is about 11 minutes' drive from Burramine. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Burramine, about 54 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Burramine, the nearest places for kids by road are Forges Beach No 2 in Yarrawonga (6 min), Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga (9 min), Tunzafun in Yarrawonga (11 min), Yarrawonga Mulwala Pioneer Museum in Yarrawonga (12 min), Lonsdale Reserve Playground in Yarrawonga (13 min) and Mulwala Waterslide in Yarrawonga (13 min).

Best for

Beach days at Bourkes Beach No. 1 and Bourkes Beach No. 2. Free days on the Murray River Adventure Trail and in Burramine's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Burramine, Yarrawonga has 6 playgrounds, a library and a beach.
