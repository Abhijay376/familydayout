Dorodong is in the West Wimmera area, and our data lists 7 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Kanawinka State Forest is the largest mapped park in Dorodong, at about 2,178 hectares. Kanawinka Flora Reserve and State Forest (Unnamed) are the next largest. Dorodong also has Grassy Swamp Bushland Reserve, Kanawinka H16 Bushland Reserve and Dorodong Nature Conservation Reserve.

Getting around

Dorodong has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 170 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dorodong, so most trips start by car. By car from Dorodong, Hamilton is about 2 hours away and Portland about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Dorodong, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at War Memorial Park in Apsley (71 min), Casterton Pool in Casterton (60 min) and Gorman's art gallery in Casterton (60 min).
