Playground on Towong Street

Playground in Tallangatta · Basic info

This is a public playground in Tallangatta. The High Country Rail Trail is about 240 m away, and Tallangatta Library is about 140 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 100 m from the playground, and bus Albury - Tallangatta via Bonegilla stops at Tallangatta Information Centre/Towong St, a 2-minute walk away.
