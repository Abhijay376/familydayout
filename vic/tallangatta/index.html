Tallangatta is in the Towong area, and our data lists 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in the suburb. Tallangatta also has a library, a pool and a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

Beethang I29 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Tallangatta, at about 5 hectares. Memorial Park is the next largest. Our data shows 8 public toilets in Tallangatta. Places to visit in Tallangatta include Tallangatta Art & Photography, Tallangatta Swimming Pool and Tallangatta Library. For walks and rides, High Country Rail Trail, Tallangatta - Firebrace Loop and Tallangatta - Lake Road Loop cross Tallangatta. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Tallangatta for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Tallangatta has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Albury - Tallangatta via Bonegilla route stop in Tallangatta. By car from Tallangatta, Wodonga is about 35 minutes away and Wangaratta about 87 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest beach to Tallangatta is Finns Beach in Bandiana, about 40 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Skate Park in Baranduda is about 27 minutes' drive from Tallangatta. For a cinema, Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana is about 30 minutes' drive from Tallangatta. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 35 minutes from Tallangatta by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Tallangatta are the playground at Banksia Park in Tangambalanga (14 min), the playground at Ludlows Reserve in Ebden (20 min), The Bonegilla Migrant Experience in Bonegilla (26 min), Skate Park in Baranduda (27 min), Edge Aquatics in Baranduda (28 min) and Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana (30 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the library and museum or gallery in Tallangatta. Free days on the High Country Rail Trail and in Tallangatta's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Huon, Charleroi and Bethanga also border Tallangatta.
