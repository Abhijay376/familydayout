Knights

Cafe or restaurant in Tallangatta · Basic info

Knights is a fish and chips cafe or restaurant in Tallangatta, in the Towong Shire Council area, a place to eat during a day out. Tallangatta Information Centre/Towong St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 96 m away with a baby change table.
