North Warrandyte in the Nillumbik area has 1 playground and 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Professors Hill Reserve is the largest mapped park in North Warrandyte, at about 4 hectares. Other parks and reserves in North Warrandyte include Koornong, Normans Reserve and Cooks Weerona Reserve. Playgrounds around North Warrandyte include the playground at Cooks Weerona Reserve. For walks and rides, Glynns Track crosses North Warrandyte.

Getting around

North Warrandyte has no train station; Eltham Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 5.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 578 and 579 stop in North Warrandyte. By car from North Warrandyte, Ringwood is about 16 minutes away, Lilydale about 23 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 37 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to North Warrandyte is Streamline Swim School in Eltham, about 11 minutes' drive. For a library, Warrandyte Library in Warrandyte is about 4 minutes' drive from North Warrandyte. For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 35 minutes' drive from North Warrandyte. The closest skate park to North Warrandyte is Warrandyte Skate Park in Warrandyte, about 7 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Hoyts in Ringwood is about 17 minutes' drive from North Warrandyte. For an indoor play centre, Rare Bears Indoor Play Centre in Eltham is about 13 minutes' drive from North Warrandyte. The closest zoo or wildlife park to North Warrandyte is Bundoora Park Farm in Bundoora, about 31 minutes' drive. Ringwood is the closest bigger centre to North Warrandyte, about 16 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from North Warrandyte are Federation Playspace in Warrandyte (4 min), Warrandyte Library in Warrandyte (4 min), Warrandyte Historical Society in Warrandyte (4 min), Stonehouse Gallery in Warrandyte (4 min), Warrandyte Skate Park in Warrandyte (7 min) and Warrandyte high school library in Warrandyte (10 min).
