Skipton Court House Historic Reserve

Park in Skipton · Basic info

Skipton Court House Historic Reserve is a park in Skipton, in the Corangamite Shire Council area, good for all ages. BP Roadhouse/Glenelg Hwy (coach) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 305 m away.
