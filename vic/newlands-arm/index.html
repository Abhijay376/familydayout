Landmarks

The biggest green space in Newlands Arm is Simpson Park, covering about 14 hectares. Playgrounds around Newlands Arm include the playground at Steel Bay - Newland Backwater Gippsland Lakes Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Newlands Arm.

Getting around

There's no train station in Newlands Arm. The closest to Newlands Arm is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Newlands Arm, so most trips start by car. By car from Newlands Arm, Bairnsdale is about 23 minutes away and Lakes Entrance about 54 minutes, without traffic.

The nearest larger centre is Bairnsdale, about 23 minutes from Newlands Arm by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Newlands Arm, the nearest places for kids by road are Boyfriend Beach in Forge Creek (10 min), Elbow Point in Forge Creek (13 min), the playground at Eagle Point Gippsland Lakes Reserve in Eagle Point (17 min), Paynesville Library in Paynesville (19 min), Paynesville Maritime Museum in Paynesville (19 min) and the skate park at Gilsenan Reserve in Paynesville (19 min).
