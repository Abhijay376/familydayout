Olinda Precinct Nature Playscape

Playground in Olinda · Official details

Olinda Precinct Nature Playscape in Olinda is a public playground in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. Olinda Pool is about 320 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 110 m away, and bus 696 stops at Olinda Golf Course/Olinda-Monbulk Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
