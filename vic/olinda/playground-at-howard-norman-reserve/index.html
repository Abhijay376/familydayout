Playground at Howard Norman Reserve

Playground in Olinda · Basic info

Playground at Howard Norman Reserve is a public playground in Olinda, in the Yarra Ranges Shire area. The Community (ngurrak barring | RidgeWalk) is about 30 m away, and for food, White Ginger is about 100 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 880 m from the playground, and bus 688 stops at Ridge Rd/Mt Dandenong Tourist Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
