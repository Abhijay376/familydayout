Howard Norman Reserve

Park in Olinda · Basic info

Howard Norman Reserve is a park in Olinda, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Ridge Rd/Mt Dandenong Tourist Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 829 m away with a baby change table.
