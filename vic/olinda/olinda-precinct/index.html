Olinda Precinct

Park in Olinda · Official details

Olinda Precinct is a park in Olinda, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Olinda Hall/Olinda-Monbulk Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 411 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed on a lead. Opening hours: opening_hours: 24/7.
