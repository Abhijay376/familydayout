R J Hamer Arboretum

Park in Olinda · Official details

R J Hamer Arboretum is a park in Olinda, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Olinda Golf Course/Olinda-Monbulk Rd (bus) is a 18-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 1020 m away and dogs are allowed on a lead. Opening hours: Daylight hours, seven days a week, except days of Extreme Fire Danger Rating or severe weather.
