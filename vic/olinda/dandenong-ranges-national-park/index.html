Dandenong Ranges National Park

Park in Olinda · Official details

Dandenong Ranges National Park is a park in Olinda, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Dogs are not allowed. Opening hours: Open every day including weekends and public holidays; closed on days of Extreme Fire Danger Rating or above.
