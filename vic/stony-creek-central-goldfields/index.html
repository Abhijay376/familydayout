Stony Creek (Central Goldfields) is in the Central Goldfields area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Stony Creek (Central Goldfields), so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Stony Creek (Central Goldfields) has no train station; Talbot Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 6.0 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Stony Creek (Central Goldfields), so most trips start by car. By car from Stony Creek (Central Goldfields), Maryborough is about 25 minutes away, Ballarat about 48 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot is about 10 minutes' drive from Stony Creek (Central Goldfields). For a library, Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough is about 26 minutes' drive from Stony Creek (Central Goldfields). The closest skate park to Stony Creek (Central Goldfields) is Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick, about 35 minutes' drive. Maryborough is the closest bigger centre to Stony Creek (Central Goldfields), about 25 minutes away by car, and has a library. The closest places for kids by road from Stony Creek (Central Goldfields) are Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground in Talbot (9 min), Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (10 min), Lee Medlyn Bottle Museum in Clunes (17 min), The Warehouse in Clunes (18 min), Clunes Swimming Pool in Clunes (19 min) and the playground at Queens Park in Clunes (19 min).
