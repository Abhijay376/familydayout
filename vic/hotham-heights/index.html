Hotham Heights is in the Alpine area, and our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Boiler Plain Reference Area is the largest mapped park in Hotham Heights, at about 867 hectares. Playgrounds around Hotham Heights include Little Higginbotham Park. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Hotham Heights. JACKFROST is the local shopping centre. For walks and rides, Australian Alps Walking Track, McMillans Walking Track and Falls to Hotham Alpine Crossing cross Hotham Heights. Our data lists 3 cafes and family restaurants in Hotham Heights for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Hotham Heights has no train station; Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 101 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Omeo - Bright via Hotham Heights route stop in Hotham Heights. By car from Hotham Heights, Bright is about 50 minutes away and Wangaratta about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The nearest larger centre is Bright, about 50 minutes from Hotham Heights by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Hotham Heights are Harrietville Museum in Harrietville (27 min) and the playground at Tavare Park in Harrietville (28 min).

Best for

Free days on the Australian Alps Walking Track and in Hotham Heights's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Tawonga South, next to Hotham Heights, has a beach. Next door to Hotham Heights, Harrietville has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Hotham Heights, Falls Creek has 1 playground. Dinner Plain, next to Hotham Heights, has 1 playground. Dargo, Bogong, Wongungarra and Smoko also border Hotham Heights.
