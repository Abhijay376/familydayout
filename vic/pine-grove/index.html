Pine Grove in the Campaspe area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Pannoobamawm Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Pine Grove, at about 6 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Pine Grove. The closest to Pine Grove is Dingee Railway Station (V/Line), about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Pine Grove, so most trips start by car. By car from Pine Grove, Echuca is about 35 minutes away, Bendigo about 61 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Lockington Pool in Lockington is about 19 minutes' drive from Pine Grove. For a library, Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca is about 36 minutes' drive from Pine Grove. For a skate park, Moama Echuca Community Skate Park in Echuca is about 38 minutes' drive from Pine Grove. The closest cinema to Pine Grove is Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca, about 35 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 35 minutes from Pine Grove by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Pine Grove, the nearest places for kids by road are Lockington Pool in Lockington (19 min), Lockington District Swimming Pool in Lockington (19 min), the playground at R J Phelan Reserve in Mitiamo (17 min), East Loddon Historical Society in Mitiamo (17 min), the playground at Echuca Lions Park in Echuca (33 min) and Holden Museum in Echuca (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Pine Grove.

Nearby suburbs

Mitiamo, next to Pine Grove, has 1 playground and a pool. Lockington, next to Pine Grove, has a pool. Kotta also borders Pine Grove.
