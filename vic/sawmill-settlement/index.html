Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Sawmill Settlement in the Mansfield area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

The Merrijig - Two Rivers Loop passes through Sawmill Settlement.

Getting around

Sawmill Settlement has no train station; Benalla Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 71 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Sawmill Settlement, so most trips start by car. By car from Sawmill Settlement, Mansfield is about 26 minutes away and Benalla about 77 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Mansfield Outdoor Swimming Pool in Mansfield is about 26 minutes' drive from Sawmill Settlement. For a library, Mansfield Library in Mansfield is about 25 minutes' drive from Sawmill Settlement. The closest skate park to Sawmill Settlement is the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield, about 26 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Mt Buller Cinema in Mount Buller is about 23 minutes' drive from Sawmill Settlement. For an indoor play centre, Buller Air Zone in Mount Buller is about 23 minutes' drive from Sawmill Settlement. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Sawmill Settlement is Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield, about 28 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Mansfield, about 26 minutes from Sawmill Settlement by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Sawmill Settlement, the nearest places for kids by road are Mt Buller Cinema in Mount Buller (23 min), National Alpine Museum of Australia in Mount Buller (23 min), Buller Air Zone in Mount Buller (23 min), Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield (25 min), Mansfield Library in Mansfield (25 min) and the playground at Mansfield Botanic Park in Mansfield (26 min).
