Wedderburn is in the Loddon area, and our data lists 1 playground and 12 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Wedderburn has a pool and a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

Wychitella Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Wedderburn, at about 6,894 hectares. Korong Vale Reference Area and Kurraca I111 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Wedderburn include Ted's Garden, Mickeys Historic Site and Hard Hill Tourist Reserve. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Wedderburn. Picnic areas in Wedderburn include Mickeys Historic Site. In Wedderburn you'll also find Coach House Gallery & Museum and Wedderburn Swimming Pool.

Getting around

Wedderburn has no train station; Raywood Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 54 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Bendigo - Boort via Wedderburn and Bendigo - Woomelang via Wedderburn, Charlton, Wycheproof, Birchip routes stop in Wedderburn. V/Line coaches stop in Wedderburn. By car from Wedderburn, Bendigo is about 64 minutes away, Maryborough about 76 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 25 minutes' drive from Wedderburn. From Wedderburn, the nearest places for kids by road are Rex Theatre in Charlton (25 min), Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (25 min), the playground at Rotary Park in Charlton (26 min), the playground at Gordon Park in Charlton (26 min) and St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (37 min).
