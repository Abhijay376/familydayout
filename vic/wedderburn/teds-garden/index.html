Ted's Garden

Park in Wedderburn · Basic info

Ted's Garden is a park in Wedderburn, in the Loddon Shire Council area, good for all ages. Reef St/Wilson St (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 216 m away.
