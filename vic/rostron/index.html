Rostron is in the Northern Grampians area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Rostron Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Rostron, at about 2 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Rostron. The closest to Rostron is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 61 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Rostron, so most trips start by car. By car from Rostron, Stawell is about 51 minutes away and Maryborough about 64 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Rostron is St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud, about 18 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Stawell, about 51 minutes from Rostron by car, with 6 playgrounds and a pool. From Rostron, the nearest places for kids by road are Stuart Mill Recreation Reserve Playground in Stuart Mill (17 min), the playground at Queen Mary Botanic Gardens in St Arnaud (17 min) and St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (18 min).

Best for
