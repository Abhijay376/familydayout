Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Pine Mountain, in the Towong area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Pine Mountain is Pine Mountain Reference Area, covering about 481 hectares. Neils Bend, Murray River Reserve is the next largest. For walks and rides, Pine Mountain Walking Track crosses Pine Mountain.

Getting around

There's no train station in Pine Mountain. The closest to Pine Mountain is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 85 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Pine Mountain, so most trips start by car. By car from Pine Mountain, Wodonga is about 2 hours away and Wangaratta about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Corryong Public swimming pool in Corryong is about 35 minutes' drive from Pine Mountain. The closest library to Pine Mountain is Corryong Elyne Mitchell Library in Corryong, about 33 minutes' drive. For a beach, Pebble Beach in Walwa is about 22 minutes' drive from Pine Mountain. The closest cinema to Pine Mountain is Corryong Cinema in Corryong, about 33 minutes' drive. From Pine Mountain, the nearest places for kids by road are The Spotted Goanna in Walwa (16 min), Pebble Beach in Walwa (22 min), Corryong Cinema in Corryong (33 min), Corryong Elyne Mitchell Library in Corryong (33 min) and Corryong Public swimming pool in Corryong (35 min).

Best for

Free days on the Pine Mountain Walking Track and in Pine Mountain's parks.
