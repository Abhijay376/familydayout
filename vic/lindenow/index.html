Landmarks

Lindenow also has Amy Shand Park. Playgrounds around Lindenow include the playground at Amy Shand Park. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Lindenow.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lindenow. The closest to Lindenow is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lindenow, so most trips start by car. By car from Lindenow, Bairnsdale is about 18 minutes away and Sale about 48 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lindenow is Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale, about 18 minutes' drive. For a library, Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale is about 19 minutes' drive from Lindenow. For a beach, Boyfriend Beach in Forge Creek is about 34 minutes' drive from Lindenow. For a skate park, the skate park at Gilsenan Reserve in Paynesville is about 33 minutes' drive from Lindenow. For a cinema, Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale is about 19 minutes' drive from Lindenow. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Lindenow is Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow, about 25 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bairnsdale, about 18 minutes from Lindenow by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Lindenow, the nearest places for kids by road are Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale (18 min), Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale (19 min), Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale (20 min), Krowathunkoolong Keeping Place in Bairnsdale (20 min), Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale (19 min) and Bairnsdale Museum in Bairnsdale (20 min).
