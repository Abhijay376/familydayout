Gunyah is in the South Gippsland area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Gunyah, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Gunyah. The closest to Gunyah is Morwell Railway Station (V/Line), about 32 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gunyah, so most trips start by car. By car from Gunyah, Leongatha is about 60 minutes away, Traralgon about 67 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Toora Pool in Toora is about 45 minutes' drive from Gunyah. For a library, Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North is about 38 minutes' drive from Gunyah. The closest skate park to Gunyah is Boolarra Skate Park in Boolarra, about 31 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Leongatha, about 60 minutes from Gunyah by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Gunyah are Boolarra Skate Park in Boolarra (31 min), Boolarra BMX track in Boolarra (32 min), the playground at Railway Park in Boolarra (32 min), Boolarra Old School Museum in Boolarra (32 min), the playground at Stanley Street Playground in Toora (45 min) and Toora Pool in Toora (45 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Gunyah.

Nearby suburbs

Grand Ridge, Boolarra South and Boolarong also border Gunyah.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Gunyah, without traffic.
