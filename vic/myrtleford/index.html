Myrtleford is in the Alpine area, and our data lists 3 playgrounds and 19 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Myrtleford has a library, 2 pools, a museum or gallery and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Myrtleford is Reform Hill State Forest, covering about 98 hectares. Myrtleford Show Grounds and Jubilee Park are the next largest. Myrtleford also has Apex Park, Cundy Park and Rotary Park. Playgrounds in Myrtleford include Cundy Park Playground, the playground at Rotary Park and the playground at Swinburne Park. Our data shows 7 public toilets in Myrtleford. Picnic areas in Myrtleford include Reform Hill Gold Battery and Reform Hill Lookout. Places to visit in Myrtleford include The Old School Museum, Myrtleford Swimming Pool, the water play at Jubilee Park and Myrtleford Library. For older kids in Myrtleford there's Myrtleford Skate Park. The Myrtleford - Gapsted Loop, Myrtleford Hinterland Loop and Reform Hill Lookout Loop all pass through Myrtleford. Our data lists 8 cafes and family restaurants in Myrtleford for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Myrtleford. The closest to Myrtleford is Chiltern Railway Station (V/Line), about 46 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Wangaratta-Bright, Wangaratta - Myrtleford and Albury - Wodonga - Myrtleford routes stop in Myrtleford. V/Line coaches stop in Myrtleford. By car from Myrtleford, Bright is about 28 minutes away and Wangaratta about 42 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a beach, Sandy Beach in Beechworth is about 27 minutes' drive from Myrtleford. The closest cinema to Myrtleford is Sun Cinema Bright in Bright, about 28 minutes' drive. Bright is the closest bigger centre to Myrtleford, about 28 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Myrtleford's 3 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the library and museum or gallery in Myrtleford. Teens who skate or ride, with Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford. Free days on the Myrtleford - Gapsted Loop and in Myrtleford's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Buffalo River, next to Myrtleford, has 1 playground. Mudgegonga, Merriang, Gapsted and Barwidgee also border Myrtleford.
