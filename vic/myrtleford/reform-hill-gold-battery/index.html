Reform Hill Gold Battery

Park in Myrtleford · Official details

Reform Hill Gold Battery is a park in Myrtleford, in the Alpine Shire Council area, good for all ages. Post Office/Myrtle St (bus) is a 12-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 891 m away.
