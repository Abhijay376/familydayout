Buffalo Boulders

Climbing in Myrtleford · Official details

Buffalo Boulders is a climbing in Myrtleford, in the Alpine Shire Council area, best for kids 5 and up and teens. Post Office/Myrtle St (coach) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 680 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: Monday 12pm-8pm; Tuesday 12pm - 8pm; Wednesday 12pm - 8pm; Thursday 12pm - 8pm; Friday 12pm - 8pm; Saturday 10am - 6pm; Sunday. 10am - 6pm.
