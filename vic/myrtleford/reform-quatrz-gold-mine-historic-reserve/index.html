Reform Quatrz Gold Mine Historic Reserve

Park in Myrtleford · Basic info

Reform Quatrz Gold Mine Historic Reserve is a park in Myrtleford, in the Alpine Shire Council area, good for all ages. Post Office/Myrtle St (bus) is a 15-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1167 m away.
