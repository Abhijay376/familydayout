Cundy Park Playground

Playground in Myrtleford · Basic info

This is a public playground in Myrtleford. The Myrtleford Hinterland Loop passes about 40 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 50 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and buses Albury - Wodonga - Myrtleford, Wangaratta - Myrtleford and Wangaratta-Bright stop at Post Office/Myrtle St, a 9-minute walk away.
