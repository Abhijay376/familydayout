Getting around

There's no train station in Landsborough West. The closest to Landsborough West is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 32 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Landsborough West, so most trips start by car. By car from Landsborough West, Stawell is about 27 minutes away, Ararat about 43 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Landsborough West is Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell, about 27 minutes' drive. For a library, Ararat Library in Ararat is about 43 minutes' drive from Landsborough West. For a skate park, the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell is about 31 minutes' drive from Landsborough West. The closest cinema to Landsborough West is Astor Cinema in Ararat, about 43 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Landsborough West is Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell, about 28 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Stawell, about 27 minutes from Landsborough West by car, with 6 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Landsborough West are Community Playground in Landsborough (13 min), Navarre Playground in Navarre (25 min), Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell (27 min), Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell (28 min), Railway Station Art and Crafts Gallery in Stawell (28 min) and the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell (31 min).
