Tarranyurk is in the Hindmarsh area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Tarranyurk, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Wimmera River Discovery Trail crosses Tarranyurk.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tarranyurk. The closest to Tarranyurk is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 144 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Horsham - Hopetoun via Dimboola route stop in Tarranyurk. By car from Tarranyurk, Horsham is about 50 minutes away and Stawell about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Dimboola Swimming Pool in Dimboola is about 22 minutes' drive from Tarranyurk. The closest library to Tarranyurk is Dimboola Library in Dimboola, about 22 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Horsham, about 50 minutes from Tarranyurk by car, with 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Tarranyurk, the nearest places for kids by road are Wimmera-Mallee Pioneer Museum in Jeparit (7 min), Fitness Course in Jeparit (9 min), the playground at Wimmera River Heritage River in Jeparit (9 min), Dimboola Library in Dimboola (22 min), Dimboola Swimming Pool in Dimboola (22 min) and Historical Centre in Warracknabeal (38 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Tarranyurk on the Wimmera River Discovery Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Dimboola, next to Tarranyurk, has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Next door to Tarranyurk, Jeparit has 3 playgrounds. Aubrey and Antwerp also border Tarranyurk.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Tarranyurk, without traffic.
