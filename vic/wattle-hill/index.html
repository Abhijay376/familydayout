Wattle Hill is in the Corangamite area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Wattle Hill, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Great Ocean Walk, Milanesia Beach to Ryans Den and Ryans Den to Moonlight Head cross Wattle Hill.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wattle Hill. The closest to Wattle Hill is Colac Railway Station (V/Line), about 53 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wattle Hill, so most trips start by car. By car from Wattle Hill, Colac is about 72 minutes away and Warrnambool about 83 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest beach to Wattle Hill is Wreck Beach in Gellibrand Lower, about 10 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell is about 31 minutes' drive from Wattle Hill. The closest places for kids by road from Wattle Hill are Wreck Beach in Gellibrand Lower (10 min), Gellibrand East Beach in Gellibrand Lower (21 min), Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell (31 min), Art Reach Studio in Beech Forest (37 min), the playground at John H Gardner Reserve in Beech Forest (37 min) and the playground at Peterborough Coastal Reserve (COM) in Peterborough (42 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Wattle Hill on the Great Ocean Walk.

Nearby suburbs

Gellibrand Lower, next to Wattle Hill, has a beach.
