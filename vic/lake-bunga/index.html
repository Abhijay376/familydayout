Lake Bunga is in the East Gippsland area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Lake Bunga, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lake Bunga. The closest to Lake Bunga is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 36 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lake Bunga, so most trips start by car. By car from Lake Bunga, Lakes Entrance is about 7 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 42 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lake Bunga is Lakes Entrance Aquatic Centre in Lakes Entrance, about 5 minutes' drive. For a library, Lakes Entrance Library in Lakes Entrance is about 7 minutes' drive from Lake Bunga. For a beach, 90 Mile Beach in Lakes Entrance is about 5 minutes' drive from Lake Bunga. For a cinema, Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale is about 40 minutes' drive from Lake Bunga. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow is about 34 minutes' drive from Lake Bunga. The nearest larger centre is Lakes Entrance, about 7 minutes from Lake Bunga by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Lake Bunga are Lakes Entrance Aquatic Centre in Lakes Entrance (5 min), 90 Mile Beach in Lakes Entrance (5 min), Ninety Mile Beach in Lakes Entrance (6 min), Lakes Entrance Library in Lakes Entrance (7 min), Bronsdon Street Park in Lakes Entrance (10 min) and Griffiths Sea Shell Museum in Lakes Entrance (8 min).
