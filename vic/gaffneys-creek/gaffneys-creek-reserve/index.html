Gaffneys Creek Reserve

Park in Gaffneys Creek · Official details

Gaffneys Creek Reserve is a park in Gaffneys Creek, in the Mansfield Shire Council area, good for all ages. Gaffneys Creek Community Centre/Mansfield - Woods Point Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 2 m away.

Questions parents ask

Where is Gaffneys Creek Reserve?
Gaffneys Creek Reserve is in Gaffneys Creek, Victoria.
Is Gaffneys Creek Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Gaffneys Creek Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 2 m away.
How do I get to Gaffneys Creek Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Gaffneys Creek Community Centre/Mansfield - Woods Point Rd (routes Mansfield - Woods Point via Jamieson), a 6-minute walk.
