Gaffneys Creek in the Mansfield area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Gaffneys Creek also has Gaffneys Creek Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Gaffneys Creek. Picnic areas in Gaffneys Creek include Gaffneys Creek Reserve. The Australian Alps Walking Track and McMillans Walking Track all pass through Gaffneys Creek.

Getting around

There's no train station in Gaffneys Creek. The closest to Gaffneys Creek is Moe Railway Station (V/Line), about 79 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Mansfield - Woods Point via Jamieson route stop in Gaffneys Creek. By car from Gaffneys Creek, Mansfield is about 78 minutes away and Benalla about 2 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Free days on the Australian Alps Walking Track and in Gaffneys Creek's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Eildon, next to Gaffneys Creek, has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Gaffneys Creek, Mount Buller has a cinema. Knockwood also borders Gaffneys Creek.
