Allan Avenue Park

Park in South Morang · Basic info

Allan Avenue Park is a park in South Morang, in the Whittlesea City Council area, good for all ages. Plenty Rd/Development Bvd (bus) is a 9-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 684 m away with a baby change table.
