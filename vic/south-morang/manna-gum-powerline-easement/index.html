Manna Gum Powerline Easement

Park in South Morang · Basic info

Manna Gum Powerline Easement is a park in South Morang, in the Whittlesea City Council area, good for all ages. Riverside Dr/Gorge Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 847 m away.
