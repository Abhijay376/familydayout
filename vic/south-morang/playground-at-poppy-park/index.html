Playground at Poppy Park

Playground in South Morang · Basic info

Playground at Poppy Park is a public playground in South Morang, in the Whittlesea City area. The South Morang - Mernda Trail is about 20 m away, and Playground at Red Oaks Park is another option about 570 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 520 m from the playground, and buses 386 and 387 stop at Hillcroft Park/Cuckoo St, a 4-minute walk away.
