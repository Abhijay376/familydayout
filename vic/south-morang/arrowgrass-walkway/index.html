Arrowgrass Walkway

Park in South Morang · Basic info

Arrowgrass Walkway is a park in South Morang, in the Whittlesea City Council area, good for all ages. Arrowgrass St/McArthurs Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 413 m away with a baby change table.
