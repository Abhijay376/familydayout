Nighthawk Park Playground

Playground in South Morang · Basic info

Nighthawk Park Playground is a public playground in South Morang, in the Whittlesea City area. The South Morang - Mernda Trail passes about 280 m away, and Butcher Bird Park Playground is another option about 230 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.1 km away, and bus 383 stops at Nighthawk Bvd/Grange Dr, a 3-minute walk away.
