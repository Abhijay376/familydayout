Riverside Reserve Playground

Playground in South Morang · Basic info

Riverside Reserve Playground is a public playground in South Morang, in the Whittlesea City area. For food, Chungsan Chinese Restaurant is about 500 m away, and Playground at Reid Park is another option about 460 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 901 stops at Riverside Dr/Gorge Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
