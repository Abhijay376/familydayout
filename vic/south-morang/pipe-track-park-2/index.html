Pipe Track Park

Park in South Morang · Basic info

Pipe Track Park is a park in South Morang, in the Whittlesea City Council area, good for all ages. Oleander Dr/McDonalds Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 554 m away and dogs are allowed on a lead.
