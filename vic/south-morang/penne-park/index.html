Penne Park

Park in South Morang · Basic info

Penne Park is a park in South Morang, in the Whittlesea City Council area, good for all ages. Grange Dr/The Lakes Bvd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 984 m away with a baby change table.
