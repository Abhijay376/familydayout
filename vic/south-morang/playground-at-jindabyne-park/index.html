Playground at Jindabyne Park

Playground in South Morang · Basic info

This is a public playground in South Morang. The South Morang - Mernda Trail passes about 360 m away, and Nighthawk Park Playground is another option about 370 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.1 km away, and bus 383 stops at Kinkora Cres/Grange Dr, a 3-minute walk away.
