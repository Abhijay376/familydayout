Comata Park

Park in South Morang · Basic info

Comata Park is a park in South Morang, in the Whittlesea City Council area, good for all ages. Triandra Dr/Gordons Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 696 m away with a baby change table.
