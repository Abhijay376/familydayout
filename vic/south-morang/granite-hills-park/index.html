Granite Hills Park

Park in South Morang · Basic info

Granite Hills Park is a park in South Morang, in the Whittlesea City Council area, good for all ages. Gordons Rd/The Lakes Bvd (bus) is a 17-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 5689 m away.
