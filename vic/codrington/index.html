Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Codrington in the Moyne area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Codrington. The closest to Codrington is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 47 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Codrington, so most trips start by car. By car from Codrington, Portland is about 35 minutes away and Warrnambool about 52 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Heywood Swimming Pool in Heywood is about 30 minutes' drive from Codrington. The closest library to Codrington is Heywood Library in Heywood, about 30 minutes' drive. For a beach, Eumeralla in Tyrendarra East is about 8 minutes' drive from Codrington. For a skate park, the skate park at Russell Clark Reserve in Port Fairy is about 25 minutes' drive from Codrington. For a cinema, Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy is about 25 minutes' drive from Codrington. Portland is the closest bigger centre to Codrington, about 35 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Codrington are Eumeralla in Tyrendarra East (8 min), Fitzroy River Beach in Tyrendarra (19 min), Bay of Whales Gallery in Narrawong (27 min), the playground at Narrawong Recreation Reserve in Narrawong (24 min), Limestone Gallery in Port Fairy (24 min) and the skate park at Russell Clark Reserve in Port Fairy (25 min).
