Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Whorouly South in the Wangaratta area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Whorouly South has no train station; Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 34 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Whorouly South, so most trips start by car. By car from Whorouly South, Wangaratta is about 40 minutes away and Bright about 47 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Whorouly South is the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford, about 21 minutes' drive. For a library, Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford is about 21 minutes' drive from Whorouly South. The closest beach to Whorouly South is Sandy Beach in Beechworth, about 33 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Whorouly South is Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Whorouly South is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 37 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 40 minutes from Whorouly South by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Whorouly South are the playground at Rotary Park in Myrtleford (19 min), The Old School Museum in Myrtleford (21 min), Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford (21 min), Cundy Park Playground in Myrtleford (21 min), Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford (21 min) and the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Whorouly South.

Nearby suburbs

Whorouly, next to Whorouly South, has 1 playground. Next door to Whorouly South, Buffalo River has 1 playground. Merriang, Carboor and Bobinawarrah also border Whorouly South.
