Yeungroon East is in the Buloke area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Yeungroon East, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Yeungroon East has no train station; Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 71 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yeungroon East, so most trips start by car. By car from Yeungroon East, Bendigo is about 84 minutes away and Stawell about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Yeungroon East is Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn, about 21 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Donald BMX Track in Donald is about 40 minutes' drive from Yeungroon East. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 12 minutes' drive from Yeungroon East. From Yeungroon East, the nearest places for kids by road are Rex Theatre in Charlton (12 min), Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (13 min), the playground at Rotary Park in Charlton (13 min), the playground at Gordon Park in Charlton (13 min), Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (21 min) and Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Yeungroon East.
