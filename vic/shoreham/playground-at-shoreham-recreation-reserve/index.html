Playground at Shoreham Recreation Reserve

Playground in Shoreham · Basic info

Playground at Shoreham Recreation Reserve in Shoreham is a public playground in the Mornington Peninsula Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. You'll find a public toilet about 250 m from the playground, and bus 782 stops at Prout Webb Rd/Byrnes Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
