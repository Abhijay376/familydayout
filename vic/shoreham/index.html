Our data lists 2 playgrounds and 4 parks and reserves in Shoreham, in the Mornington Peninsula area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Shoreham is Buxton Bushland Reserve, covering about 6 hectares. Flinders Natural Features Reserve and Shoreham Recreation Reserve are the next largest. Shoreham also has Pine Grove Reserve. Playgrounds around Shoreham include the playground at Pine Grove Reserve and the playground at Shoreham Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Shoreham.

Getting around

Shoreham has no train station; Bittern Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 782 stops in Shoreham. By car from Shoreham, Mornington is about 29 minutes away, Frankston about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 78 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Shoreham is Yawa Aquatic Centre in Rosebud, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest library to Shoreham is Rosebud Library in Rosebud, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Shoreham is Cairns Bay in Flinders, about 12 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Pump Track in Dromana is about 16 minutes' drive from Shoreham. The closest cinema to Shoreham is Dromana Triple Drive-In Theatre in Dromana, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Shoreham is Top Fun Shack in Rosebud, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Shoreham is Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale, about 35 minutes' drive. Mornington is the closest bigger centre to Shoreham, about 29 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a library, indoor play and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Shoreham are Cairns Bay in Flinders (12 min), Pump Track in Dromana (16 min), Dromana Triple Drive-In Theatre in Dromana (18 min), Dromana and District Historical Society Museum in Dromana (20 min), Rosebud Cinemas in Rosebud (25 min) and Dromana Beach in Dromana (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Shoreham.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Shoreham, Flinders has 2 playgrounds and a beach. Point Leo, next to Shoreham, has 2 playgrounds. Red Hill South, next to Shoreham, has 1 playground. Main Ridge also borders Shoreham.
