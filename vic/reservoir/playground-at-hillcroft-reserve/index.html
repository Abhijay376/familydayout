Playground at Hillcroft Reserve

Playground in Reservoir · Basic info

Playground at Hillcroft Reserve in Reservoir is a public playground in the Darebin City area. Misty's 1950's Diner is about 330 m away if you need food, and Playground at J.E. Moore Park is another option about 430 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.0 km away, and bus 553 stops at Wattlebrae St/Henty St, a 4-minute walk away.
