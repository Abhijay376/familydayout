Playground at Fyfe Street Reserve

Playground in Reservoir · Basic info

This is a public playground in Reservoir. The St Georges Road Trail is about 280 m away, and Playground at Frank Allen Arthur Dunstan Reserve is another option about 580 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 830 m from the playground, and bus 556 stops at Purinuan Rd/Cheddar Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
