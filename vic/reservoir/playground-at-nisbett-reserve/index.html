Playground at Nisbett Reserve

Playground in Reservoir · Basic info

Playground at Nisbett Reserve is a public playground in Reservoir, in the Darebin City area. For food, Flamed Charcoal is about 300 m away, and Playground at Progress Park is another option about 150 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 540 m from the playground, and bus 567 stops at Progress Park/Chaleyer St, a 2-minute walk away.
