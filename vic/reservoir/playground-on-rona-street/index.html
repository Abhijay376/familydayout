Playground on Rona Street

Playground in Reservoir · Basic info

Playground on Rona Street is a public playground in Reservoir, in the Darebin City area. The Great Western Shimmy passes about 90 m away, and Manara Pizza House is about 440 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 580 m away, and bus 553 stops at Crispe St/Haig St, a 3-minute walk away.
