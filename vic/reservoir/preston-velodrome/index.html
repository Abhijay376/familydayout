Preston Velodrome

Skate, BMX or pump track in Reservoir · Basic info

Preston Velodrome is a skate, bmx or pump track in Reservoir, in the Darebin City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Fordham Rd/Broadway (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 244 m away with a baby change table.
