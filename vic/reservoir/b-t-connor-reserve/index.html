B.T. Connor Reserve

Park in Reservoir · Basic info

B.T. Connor Reserve is a park in Reservoir, in the Darebin City Council area, good for all ages. Radford Rd/Broadhurst Ave (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 261 m away.
