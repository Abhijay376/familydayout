Skate park at Edwardes Lake Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Reservoir · Basic info

Skate park at Edwardes Lake Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Reservoir, in the Darebin City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Gill St/Banbury Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 698 m away with a baby change table.
