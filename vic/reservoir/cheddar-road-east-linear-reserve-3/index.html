Cheddar Road East Linear Reserve

Park in Reservoir · Basic info

Cheddar Road East Linear Reserve is a park in Reservoir, in the Darebin City Council area, good for all ages. Miranda Rd/Cheddar Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 640 m away.
