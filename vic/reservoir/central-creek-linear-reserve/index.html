Central Creek Linear Reserve

Park in Reservoir · Basic info

Central Creek Linear Reserve is a park in Reservoir, in the Darebin City Council area, good for all ages. Lockton Ave/Davidson St (bus) is a 9-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1089 m away.
