Playground at Darebin Creek Reserve

Playground in Reservoir · Basic info

Playground at Darebin Creek Reserve in Reservoir is a public playground in the Darebin City area. The Darebin Creek Trail passes about 40 m away, and Bella Nina Pizza and Pasta is about 310 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 400 m away, and bus 552 stops at Clements Gr/Boldrewood Pde, a 4-minute walk away.
