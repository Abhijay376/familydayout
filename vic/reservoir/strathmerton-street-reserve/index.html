Strathmerton Street Reserve

Park in Reservoir · Basic info

Strathmerton Street Reserve is a park in Reservoir, in the Darebin City Council area, good for all ages. Arch Gibson Reserve/Boldrewood Pde (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 459 m away with a baby change table.
