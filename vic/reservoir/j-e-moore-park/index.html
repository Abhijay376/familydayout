J.E. Moore Park

Park in Reservoir · Basic info

J.E. Moore Park is a park in Reservoir, in the Darebin City Council area, good for all ages. Griffiths St/Edwardes St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 377 m away.
