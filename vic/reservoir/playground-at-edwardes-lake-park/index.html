Playground at Edwardes Lake Park

Playground in Reservoir · Basic info

This is a public playground in Reservoir. The Reservoir - Merri Creek LCN passes about 280 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 80 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 620 m away, and bus 553 stops at Gill St/Banbury Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
