Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Moranding, in the Mitchell area.

Landmarks

Moranding I22 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Moranding, at about 3 hectares. Moranding I23 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Moranding. The closest to Moranding is Kilmore East Station (V/Line), about 7.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Moranding, so most trips start by car. By car from Moranding, Seymour is about 33 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 51 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 70 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore is about 7 minutes' drive from Moranding. The closest library to Moranding is Kilmore Library in Kilmore, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Moranding is Kilmore Skate Park in Kilmore, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Moranding is United Cinemas in Craigieburn, about 42 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Moranding is Timezone in Craigieburn, about 41 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Moranding is Funfields in Whittlesea, about 38 minutes' drive. Seymour is the closest bigger centre to Moranding, about 33 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Moranding are Kilmore Skate Park in Kilmore (7 min), Kilmore Library in Kilmore (7 min), the playground at Hudson Park in Kilmore (7 min), Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore (7 min), the playground at Moore Court Reserve in Kilmore (9 min) and Tramway Heritage Centre in Bylands (14 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Moranding.

Nearby suburbs

Kilmore, next to Moranding, has 8 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Broadford, next to Moranding, has 6 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Moranding, Kilmore East has 1 playground. High Camp and Glenaroua also border Moranding.
