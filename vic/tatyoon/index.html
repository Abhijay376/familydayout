Tatyoon is in the Ararat area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tatyoon is Fiery Creek Streamside Reserve, covering about 36 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tatyoon. The closest to Tatyoon is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tatyoon, so most trips start by car. By car from Tatyoon, Ararat is about 24 minutes away, Stawell about 52 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tatyoon is Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat, about 24 minutes' drive. For a library, Ararat Library in Ararat is about 23 minutes' drive from Tatyoon. For a skate park, David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat is about 28 minutes' drive from Tatyoon. The closest cinema to Tatyoon is Astor Cinema in Ararat, about 23 minutes' drive. Ararat is the closest bigger centre to Tatyoon, about 24 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Tatyoon are the playground at Simpson Park in Streatham (26 min), Ararat Library in Ararat (23 min), Astor Cinema in Ararat (23 min), Langi Morgala Museum in Ararat (23 min), Ararat Gallery TAMA in Ararat (24 min) and Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat (24 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tatyoon.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Tatyoon, Streatham has 1 playground. Mininera, next to Tatyoon, has 1 playground. Maroona, Langi Logan and Ballyrogan also border Tatyoon.
