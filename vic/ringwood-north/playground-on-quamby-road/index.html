Playground on Quamby Road

Playground in Ringwood North · Basic info

Playground on Quamby Road is a public playground in Ringwood North, in the Maroondah City area. For food, Little John Pizza and Pasta is about 190 m away, and Playground in Ringwood North is another option about 520 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 900 m from the playground, and bus 364 stops at Quamby Rd/Old Warrandyte Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
