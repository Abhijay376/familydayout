Playground on Aurum Crescent

Playground in Ringwood North · Basic info

This is a public playground in Ringwood North. The Ringwood Heights Walking Route is about 50 m away, and for food, Paradise Fish and Chips is about 470 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.3 km from the playground, and bus 370 stops at Craig Rd/Glenvale Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
