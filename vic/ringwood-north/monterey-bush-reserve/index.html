Monterey Bush Reserve

Park in Ringwood North · Basic info

Monterey Bush Reserve is a park in Ringwood North, in the Maroondah City Council area, good for all ages. North Ringwood Community House/Tortice Dr (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 658 m away with a baby change table.
