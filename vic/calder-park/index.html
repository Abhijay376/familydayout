Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Calder Park in the Brimbank area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Calder Park.

Getting around

Calder Park has no train station; Watergardens Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 4.0 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Calder Park, so most trips start by car. By car from Calder Park, Sunshine is about 18 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 19 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 29 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Flippers Swim School in Sydenham is about 7 minutes' drive from Calder Park. The closest library to Calder Park is Brimbank Library in Taylors Lakes, about 8 minutes' drive. For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 32 minutes' drive from Calder Park. The closest skate park to Calder Park is the bmx track on Houdini Drive in Diggers Rest, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Calder Park is Hoyts in Taylors Lakes, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Calder Park is Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes, about 8 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville is about 24 minutes' drive from Calder Park. Sunshine is the closest bigger centre to Calder Park, about 18 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Calder Park are the playground at Salamander Drive Reserve in Taylors Lakes (5 min), the playground at yaluk barring park in Keilor North (10 min), Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes (8 min), Hoyts in Taylors Lakes (7 min), Brimbank Library in Taylors Lakes (8 min) and Flippers Swim School in Sydenham (7 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Calder Park.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Calder Park, Taylors Lakes has 16 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Calder Park, Hillside has 22 playgrounds. Next door to Calder Park, Sydenham has 12 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Calder Park, Fraser Rise has 13 playgrounds. Diggers Rest and Keilor North also border Calder Park.
