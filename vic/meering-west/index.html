Meering West in the Gannawarra area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Meering West is Meering West Bushland Reserve, covering about 20 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Meering West. The closest to Meering West is Kerang Railway Station (V/Line), about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Meering West, so most trips start by car. By car from Meering West, Swan Hill is about 77 minutes away and Echuca about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Swimming Pool in Kerang is about 32 minutes' drive from Meering West. The closest library to Meering West is Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang, about 32 minutes' drive. From Meering West, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Lake Meran W.R in Lake Meran (17 min), Quambatook Memorial Pool in Quambatook (48 min), Quambatook Lions Club Playground in Quambatook (48 min), Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook (48 min), Quambatook Historical Centre in Quambatook (49 min) and Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (32 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Meering West.

Nearby suburbs

Quambatook, next to Meering West, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Lake Meran, next to Meering West, has 1 playground. Leaghur, Gredgwin, Dingwall and Barraport also border Meering West.
