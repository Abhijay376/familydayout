Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Murra Warra in the Horsham area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Murra Warra has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 113 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Murra Warra, so most trips start by car. By car from Murra Warra, Horsham is about 34 minutes away and Stawell about 89 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Dimboola Swimming Pool in Dimboola is about 21 minutes' drive from Murra Warra. For a library, Dimboola Library in Dimboola is about 21 minutes' drive from Murra Warra. The closest skate park to Murra Warra is Velodrome in Horsham, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Murra Warra is Centre Cinemas in Horsham, about 35 minutes' drive. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to Murra Warra, about 34 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Murra Warra, the nearest places for kids by road are Dimboola Swimming Pool in Dimboola (21 min), Dimboola Library in Dimboola (21 min), the playground at Apex Park in Warracknabeal (26 min), the playground at Anzac Park in Warracknabeal (28 min), Historical Centre in Warracknabeal (28 min) and Centre Cinemas in Horsham (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Murra Warra.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Murra Warra, Dimboola has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Longerenong, next to Murra Warra, has 1 playground. Kewell, Kellalac and Kalkee also border Murra Warra.
