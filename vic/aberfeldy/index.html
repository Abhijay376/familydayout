Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Aberfeldy in the Baw Baw area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Aberfeldy. The closest to Aberfeldy is Moe Railway Station (V/Line), about 54 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Aberfeldy, so most trips start by car. By car from Aberfeldy, Traralgon is about 84 minutes away and Warragul about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Aberfeldy, the nearest places for kids by road are Rawson Swimming Pool in Rawson (51 min), the playground at Erica State Forest in Rawson (52 min) and the playground at Croxford Park in Rawson (52 min).
