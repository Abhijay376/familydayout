Landmarks

Joel Joel Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Joel Joel, at about 251 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Joel Joel. The closest to Joel Joel is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 31 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Joel Joel, so most trips start by car. By car from Joel Joel, Stawell is about 19 minutes away, Ararat about 35 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Joel Joel is Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell, about 18 minutes' drive. For a library, Learning Resource Centre in Ararat is about 39 minutes' drive from Joel Joel. For a beach, Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans is about 40 minutes' drive from Joel Joel. The closest skate park to Joel Joel is the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell, about 23 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Astor Cinema in Ararat is about 36 minutes' drive from Joel Joel. For a zoo or wildlife park, Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell is about 20 minutes' drive from Joel Joel. Stawell is the closest bigger centre to Joel Joel, about 19 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds and a pool. From Joel Joel, the nearest places for kids by road are Community Playground in Landsborough (18 min), Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell (18 min), Stawell Skate Park in Stawell (19 min), Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell (20 min), Railway Station Art and Crafts Gallery in Stawell (20 min) and the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell (23 min).
