Boneo is in the Mornington Peninsula area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Boneo is Peninsula Gardens Bushland Reserve, covering about 44 hectares. Boneo Recreation Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Boneo. The Two Bays Walking Track and The Mornington Peninsula Walk all pass through Boneo. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Boneo for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Boneo has no train station; Bittern Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Boneo, so most trips start by car. By car from Boneo, Mornington is about 26 minutes away, Frankston about 32 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 75 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Boneo is Yawa Aquatic Centre in Rosebud, about 5 minutes' drive. For a library, Rosebud Library in Rosebud is about 9 minutes' drive from Boneo. The closest beach to Boneo is Gunnamatta Beach in Cape Schanck, about 7 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Rosebud Skate Park in Rosebud is about 7 minutes' drive from Boneo. For a cinema, Rosebud Cinemas in Rosebud is about 9 minutes' drive from Boneo. The closest indoor play centre to Boneo is Top Fun Shack in Rosebud, about 8 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale is about 38 minutes' drive from Boneo. The nearest larger centre is Mornington, about 26 minutes from Boneo by car, with 22 playgrounds, a library, indoor play and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Boneo are Yawa Aquatic Centre in Rosebud (5 min), Alba Thermal Springs & Spa ARCH in Fingal (6 min), Rotary Park Playground in Rosebud (6 min), the playground at Olympic Park Recreation Reserve in Rosebud (7 min), Rosebud Skate Park in Rosebud (7 min) and Gunnamatta Beach in Cape Schanck (7 min).

Best for

Free days on the Two Bays Walking Track and in Boneo's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Rosebud, next to Boneo, has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Flinders, next to Boneo, has 2 playgrounds and a beach. Cape Schanck, next to Boneo, has 1 playground and a beach. Next door to Boneo, Fingal has a pool. Capel Sound, Tootgarook and Main Ridge also border Boneo.
