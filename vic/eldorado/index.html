Eldorado in the Wangaratta area has 1 playground and 6 parks and reserves in our data. Beyond the parks, Eldorado has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

Eldorado Historic and Cultural Features Reserve is the largest mapped park in Eldorado, at about 190 hectares. Centennial Park and Tarrawingee I9 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Eldorado include Gunhouse Park Reserve, Eldorado I73 Bushland Reserve and Tarrawingee I8 Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds in Eldorado include the playground at Gunhouse Park Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Eldorado. In Eldorado you'll also find Eldorado Museum. For walks and rides, Wangaratta - Eldorado Loop and Reids Walk cross Eldorado.

Getting around

Eldorado has no train station; Springhurst Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Eldorado, so most trips start by car. By car from Eldorado, Wangaratta is about 25 minutes away and Benalla about 52 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Eldorado is Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest library to Eldorado is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Eldorado is Sandy Beach in Beechworth, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Eldorado is the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta, about 26 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 22 minutes' drive from Eldorado. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 25 minutes from Eldorado by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Eldorado are the playground at Apex Park in Wangaratta (22 min), Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta (22 min), Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (23 min), Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta (26 min), the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta (26 min) and Splashpark in Wangaratta (27 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Eldorado. Free days on the Wangaratta - Eldorado Loop and in Eldorado's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Beechworth, next to Eldorado, has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. Byawatha also borders Eldorado.
