Playground at Gunhouse Park Reserve

Playground in Eldorado · Basic info

Playground at Gunhouse Park Reserve is a public playground in Eldorado, in the Wangaratta Rural City area. The Wangaratta - Eldorado Loop passes about 20 m away, and Eldorado Museum is about 230 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground. It's easiest by car, about 1 minute from Eldorado.
