Katandra is in the Greater Shepparton area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Katandra, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Katandra. The closest to Katandra is Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line), about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Katandra, so most trips start by car. By car from Katandra, Shepparton is about 36 minutes away, Benalla about 65 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Katandra is Aquamoves in Shepparton, about 37 minutes' drive. The closest library to Katandra is Shepparton Library in Shepparton, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Katandra is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Katandra is Shepparton Velodrome in Shepparton, about 33 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 36 minutes' drive from Katandra. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 36 minutes' drive from Katandra. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kidstown in Mooroopna is about 38 minutes' drive from Katandra. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 36 minutes from Katandra by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Katandra, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Katandra West Recreation Reserve in Katandra West (11 min), the playground at Katandra Rec Res Youth Spot in Katandra West (11 min), Shepparton Velodrome in Shepparton (33 min), BMX track in Shepparton (34 min), Shepparton Library in Shepparton (35 min) and Village Cinemas in Shepparton (36 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Katandra.

Nearby suburbs

Katandra West, next to Katandra, has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Katandra, Dookie has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Katandra, Pine Lodge has 1 playground. Yabba South, Yabba North, Invergordon and Cosgrove also border Katandra.
