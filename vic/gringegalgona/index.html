Gringegalgona is in the Southern Grampians area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Gringegalgona, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Gringegalgona has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 101 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gringegalgona, so most trips start by car. By car from Gringegalgona, Hamilton is about 55 minutes away and Horsham about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Gringegalgona is Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Gringegalgona is coleraine skate park in Coleraine, about 27 minutes' drive. Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Gringegalgona, about 55 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Gringegalgona, the nearest places for kids by road are Glendinning Street Playground in Balmoral (25 min), Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral (26 min), coleraine skate park in Coleraine (27 min), the playground at Queens Park in Coleraine (27 min), Harrow Discovery Centre in Harrow (32 min) and Bake House Studio in Harrow (32 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Gringegalgona.
