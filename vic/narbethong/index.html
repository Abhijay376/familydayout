Our data lists 4 parks and reserves in Narbethong, in the Murrindindi area.

Landmarks

Acheron River, Granton Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Narbethong, at about 6 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Narbethong include Dindi Mill, Walindjerri and Walindjerri Camp. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Narbethong. Picnic areas in Narbethong include Walindjerri. For camping, there's Dindi Mill and Walindjerri Camp. For walks and rides, Morley Walking Track, Narbethong Downhill Trail and Redwoods Walk cross Narbethong.

Getting around

Narbethong has no train station; Lilydale Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 35 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 684 stops in Narbethong. By car from Narbethong, Healesville is about 22 minutes away, Lilydale about 45 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 86 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Narbethong is Marysville Outdoor Swimming Pool in Marysville, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest library to Narbethong is Yarra Junction public library in Yarra Junction, about 42 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Narbethong is the skate park at Gallipolli Park in Marysville, about 11 minutes' drive. For a cinema, The Memo, Healesville in Healesville is about 21 minutes' drive from Narbethong. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Narbethong is Healesville Sanctuary in Badger Creek, about 26 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Healesville, about 22 minutes from Narbethong by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Narbethong, the nearest places for kids by road are Replica of the original police station in Marysville (10 min), the playground at Gallipolli Park in Marysville (10 min), Flying fox and slide in Marysville (10 min), Marysville Outdoor Swimming Pool in Marysville (10 min), the skate park at Gallipolli Park in Marysville (11 min) and Bruno's Art and Sculpture Garden in Marysville (12 min).

Best for

Free days on the Morley Walking Track and in Narbethong's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Marysville, next to Narbethong, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Narbethong, Buxton has 2 playgrounds. East Warburton, next to Narbethong, has 1 playground. Murrindindi and Fernshaw also border Narbethong.
