Woodfield is in the Mansfield area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Woodfield Trestle Bridge Historic and Cultural Features Reserve is the largest mapped park in Woodfield, at about 5 hectares. The Great Victorian Rail Trail passes through Woodfield.

Getting around

Woodfield has no train station; Euroa Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 36 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Woodfield. By car from Woodfield, Mansfield is about 23 minutes away, Benalla about 58 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Mansfield Outdoor Swimming Pool in Mansfield is about 23 minutes' drive from Woodfield. The closest library to Woodfield is Mansfield Library in Mansfield, about 23 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield is about 24 minutes' drive from Woodfield. The closest cinema to Woodfield is Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Woodfield is Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield, about 33 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Mansfield, about 23 minutes from Woodfield by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Woodfield, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Maindample Community Park in Maindample (12 min), Yarck Recreation Reserve Playground in Yarck (20 min), Mansfield Outdoor Swimming Pool in Mansfield (23 min), Mansfield Library in Mansfield (23 min), Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield (23 min) and the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (24 min).

Best for

Free days on the Great Victorian Rail Trail and in Woodfield's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Bonnie Doon, next to Woodfield, has 1 playground. Fawcett and Ancona also border Woodfield.
