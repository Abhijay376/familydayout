Marshall in the Greater Geelong area has 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Marshall is Brearley Reserve, covering about 11 hectares. Marshall Reserve is the next largest. Playgrounds in Marshall include the playground at Marshall Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets and 1 off-leash dog park in Marshall. The Waurn Ponds Trail and Karndorr Track all pass through Marshall.

Getting around

Marshall has a train station: Marshall Railway Station (V/Line: Geelong - Melbourne and Warrnambool - Melbourne via Colac & Geelong). Buses 40, 52, 54 and 55 stop in Marshall. V/Line coaches stop in Marshall. By car from Marshall, Geelong is about 9 minutes away, Torquay about 18 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 71 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Leisurelink Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Grovedale is about 6 minutes' drive from Marshall. For a library, Belmont Library in Belmont is about 5 minutes' drive from Marshall. The closest beach to Marshall is Eastern Beach in Geelong, about 11 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the bmx track in Belmont in Belmont is about 4 minutes' drive from Marshall. For a cinema, Reading Cinemas in Grovedale is about 6 minutes' drive from Marshall. For an indoor play centre, Bounce Inc in Grovedale is about 3 minutes' drive from Marshall. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Marshall is Tiger Moth World in Torquay, about 15 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Geelong, about 9 minutes from Marshall by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Marshall are We Rock The Spectrum in Grovedale (2 min), Bounce Inc in Grovedale (3 min), the bmx track in Belmont in Belmont (4 min), The Geelong Criterium Track in Belmont (6 min), Belmont Library in Belmont (5 min) and Verandah in Belmont (6 min).

Best for

Free days on the Waurn Ponds Trail and in Marshall's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Marshall, Belmont has 27 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Marshall, Grovedale has 12 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Charlemont, next to Marshall, has 3 playgrounds.
