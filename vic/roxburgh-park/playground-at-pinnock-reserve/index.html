Playground at Pinnock Reserve

Playground in Roxburgh Park · Basic info

Playground at Pinnock Reserve is a public playground in Roxburgh Park, in the Hume City area. The Broadmeadows Valley Trail is about 320 m away, and Playground at Kirwan Park is another option about 340 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.2 km from the playground, and buses 541 and 953 stop at Pinnock Ave/Roxburgh Park Dr, a 2-minute walk away.
