Murchison Square

Park in Roxburgh Park · Basic info

Murchison Square is a park in Roxburgh Park, in the Hume City Council area, good for all ages. David Munroe Dr/Thomas Brunton Pde (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 482 m away with a baby change table.
