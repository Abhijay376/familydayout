Adams-Grenville Walkway

Park in Roxburgh Park · Basic info

Adams-Grenville Walkway is a park in Roxburgh Park, in the Hume City Council area, good for all ages. Westbury Pkwy/James Mirams Dr (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 669 m away with a baby change table.
