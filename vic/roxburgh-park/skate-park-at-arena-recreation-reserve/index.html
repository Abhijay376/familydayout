Skate park at Arena Recreation Reserve

Skate, BMX or pump track in Roxburgh Park · Basic info

Skate park at Arena Recreation Reserve is a skate, bmx or pump track in Roxburgh Park, in the Hume City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Katsura Ret/James Mirams Dr (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 270 m away with a baby change table.
