Roxburgh Village Shopping Centre

Shopping centre in Roxburgh Park · Basic info

Roxburgh Village Shopping Centre is a shopping centre in Roxburgh Park, in the Hume City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Roxburgh Park SC (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 156 m away with a baby change table.
