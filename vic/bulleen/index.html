Our data lists 16 playgrounds and 21 parks and reserves in Bulleen, in the Manningham area. Bulleen also has a library and a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

Bulleen Park is the largest mapped park in Bulleen, at about 31 hectares. Banksia Park and Carey Sports Complex are the next largest. Playground at Bulleen Park, Playground at Grant Olsen Reserve and Ronald Reserve Playground are among the council's larger district playgrounds. Other parks and reserves in Bulleen include Vista Reserve, Lilian Reserve and Koonung Reserve. Playgrounds around Bulleen include Dumossa Reserve Playground, the playground at Banksia Park and Stanley Reserve Playground. Our data shows 8 public toilets in Bulleen. Places to visit in Bulleen include Heide Museum of Modern Art and Bulleen Library. Bulleen Plaza is the local shopping centre. Our data lists 10 cafes and family restaurants in Bulleen for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bulleen. The closest to Bulleen is Heidelberg Railway Station (metro), about 3.0 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 200, 280, 282, 305, 309, 903 and 905 stop in Bulleen. By car from Bulleen, Box Hill is about 12 minutes away, Ringwood about 17 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 17 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Aquarena Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Templestowe Lower is about 6 minutes' drive from Bulleen. For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 15 minutes' drive from Bulleen. The closest skate park to Bulleen is the bmx track at Malahang Reserve in Heidelberg West, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Bulleen is Village Cinemas in Doncaster, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Bulleen is BOUNCEinc in Blackburn North, about 9 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bundoora Park Farm in Bundoora is about 18 minutes' drive from Bulleen.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 16 playgrounds across Bulleen. Rainy days at the library and museum or gallery in Bulleen.

Nearby suburbs

Doncaster, next to Bulleen, has 37 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. Next door to Bulleen, Templestowe Lower has 25 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Balwyn North, next to Bulleen, has 14 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Viewbank, next to Bulleen, has 8 playgrounds. Ivanhoe East, Eaglemont and Heidelberg also border Bulleen.
