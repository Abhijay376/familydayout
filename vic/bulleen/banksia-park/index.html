Banksia Park

Park in Bulleen · Official details

Banksia Park is a park in Bulleen, in the Manningham City Council area, good for all ages. Bulleen Rd/Manningham Rd (bus) is a 10-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 283 m away and dogs are allowed on a lead.

Questions parents ask

Where is Banksia Park?
Banksia Park is in Bulleen, Victoria.
Is Banksia Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Banksia Park?
Yes, on a lead.
Are there toilets at Banksia Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 283 m away.
How do I get to Banksia Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Bulleen Rd/Manningham Rd (routes 903), a 10-minute walk.
