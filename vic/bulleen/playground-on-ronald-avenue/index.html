Ronald Reserve Playground

Playground in Bulleen · Basic info

Ronald Reserve Playground is one of the council's larger playgrounds in Bulleen, in the Manningham City area. Bulleen Library is about 240 m away, and for food, Bulleen Sushi Bar is about 270 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 490 m from the playground, and buses 280 and 903 stop at Ronald Ave/Manningham Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
