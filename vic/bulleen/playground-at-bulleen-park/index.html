Playground at Bulleen Park

Playground in Bulleen · Basic info

This is one of the council's larger playgrounds in Bulleen, looked after by Manningham City Council. The Main Yarra Trail is about 200 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 940 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 6 minutes from Bulleen.
