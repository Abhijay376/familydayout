Heide Museum of Modern Art

Museum or gallery in Bulleen · Official details

Heide Museum of Modern Art is a museum or gallery in Bulleen, in the Manningham City Council area, best for school-age kids. Yarra Valley Bvd/Rosemont Dr (bus) is a 9-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 328 m away. Opening hours: Tu,We,Th,Fr 10:00-16:00; Sa,Su 10:00-17:00; Mo Closed.
