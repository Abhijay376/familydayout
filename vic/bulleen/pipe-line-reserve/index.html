Pipe Line Reserve

Park in Bulleen · Basic info

Pipe Line Reserve is a park in Bulleen, in the Manningham City Council area, good for all ages. Thompsons Heights Shops/Thompsons Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 950 m away.
