Playground at Harold Street Reserve

Playground in Bulleen · Basic info

This is a small local playground in Bulleen, looked after by Manningham City Council. Playground at Riverview Terrace Reserve is another option about 280 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 780 m from the playground, and buses 200, 305, 309 and 905 stop at Millicent Ave/Thompsons Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
