Playground at Morris Williams Reserve

Playground in Bulleen · Basic info

Playground at Morris Williams Reserve is one of the council's larger playgrounds in Bulleen, in the Manningham City area. Manorom Thai is about 480 m away if you need food, and Playground at Lilian Reserve is another option about 560 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 630 m away, and bus 280 stops at Marcellin Rd/Golden Way, a 1-minute walk away.
