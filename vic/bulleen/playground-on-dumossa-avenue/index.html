Dumossa Reserve Playground

Playground in Bulleen · Basic info

Dumossa Reserve Playground in Bulleen is a small local playground in the Manningham City area. Bulleen Library is about 480 m away, and for food, Hungry Jack's is about 330 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 820 m from the playground, and buses 280 and 903 stop at Helene St/Manningham Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
