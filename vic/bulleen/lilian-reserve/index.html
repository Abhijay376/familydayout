Lilian Reserve

Park in Bulleen · Basic info

Lilian Reserve is a park in Bulleen, in the Manningham City Council area, good for all ages. Lincoln Dr/Manningham Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 415 m away with a baby change table.
