Strathdownie in the Glenelg area has 15 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Kerr Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Strathdownie, at about 290 hectares. Kaladbro H3 Bushland Reserve and Doughboy State Forest are the next largest. Strathdownie also has Werrikoo B.R, Rennick State Forest and Ardno Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Strathdownie. The closest to Strathdownie is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 138 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Strathdownie, so most trips start by car. By car from Strathdownie, Hamilton is about 76 minutes away and Portland about 83 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Strathdownie is Casterton Pool in Casterton, about 25 minutes' drive. From Strathdownie, the nearest places for kids by road are Gorman's art gallery in Casterton (24 min), Casterton Pool in Casterton (25 min) and Dartmoor Coach House Museum in Dartmoor (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Strathdownie.
