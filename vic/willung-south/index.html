Willung South in the Wellington area has 5 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Stradbroke Flora and Fauna Reserve is the largest mapped park in Willung South, at about 3,451 hectares. Willung South Bushland Reserve and Toms Cap Scenic Reserve are the next largest. Willung South also has Powers Hill State Forest and Willung Bushland Reserve. The Bullockys Grave Walk passes through Willung South.

Getting around

There's no train station in Willung South. The closest to Willung South is Rosedale Railway Station (V/Line), about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Yarram - Traralgon via Gormandale route stop in Willung South. By car from Willung South, Traralgon is about 31 minutes away, Sale about 55 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rosedale Swimming Pool in Rosedale is about 31 minutes' drive from Willung South. The closest library to Willung South is Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram, about 33 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Willung South is Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon, about 34 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 37 minutes' drive from Willung South. The nearest larger centre is Traralgon, about 31 minutes from Willung South by car, with 38 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Willung South are the playground at Hourigan Park in Traralgon (29 min), Edward Crooke Park Playground in Rosedale (31 min), Rosedale Swimming Pool in Rosedale (31 min), Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon (32 min), Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon (34 min) and Yarram Historical Society Museum in Yarram (31 min).

Best for

Free days on the Bullockys Grave Walk and in Willung South's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Gormandale, next to Willung South, has 1 playground. Darriman, Carrajung South, Carrajung Lower and Carrajung also border Willung South.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Willung South, without traffic.
