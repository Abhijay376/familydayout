Gipsy Point is in the East Gippsland area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Gipsy Point, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 3 public toilets in Gipsy Point.

Getting around

Gipsy Point has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 185 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Mallacoota - Genoa via Gipsy Point route stop in Gipsy Point. V/Line coaches stop in Gipsy Point. By car from Gipsy Point, Lakes Entrance is about 2.5 hours away and Bairnsdale about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest beach to Gipsy Point is Davis Creek Beach in Mallacoota, about 18 minutes' drive. From Gipsy Point, the nearest places for kids by road are Davis Creek Beach in Mallacoota (18 min), Betka Beach in Mallacoota (18 min) and WWII Bunker Museum in Mallacoota (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Gipsy Point.

Nearby suburbs

Mallacoota, next to Gipsy Point, has a beach. Genoa, next to Gipsy Point, has 1 playground.
