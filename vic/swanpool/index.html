Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Swanpool, in the Benalla area. On top of that, Swanpool has a cinema.

Landmarks

Samaria Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Swanpool, at about 8 hectares. Broken River K13 Streamside Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Swanpool. In Swanpool you'll also find Swanpool Cinema.

Getting around

Swanpool has no train station; Benalla Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Swanpool, so most trips start by car. By car from Swanpool, Benalla is about 21 minutes away, Mansfield about 30 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla is about 20 minutes' drive from Swanpool. For a library, Mansfield Library in Mansfield is about 31 minutes' drive from Swanpool. The closest skate park to Swanpool is Pump Track in Benalla, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Swanpool is Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield, about 41 minutes' drive. Benalla is the closest bigger centre to Swanpool, about 21 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Swanpool are the playground at Waminda Park in Benalla (18 min), the playground at Benalla Botanical Gardens in Benalla (20 min), Benalla Art Gallery in Benalla (20 min), Benalla Costume and Pioneer Museum in Benalla (20 min), Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (20 min) and Pump Track in Benalla (22 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the cinema in Swanpool.

Nearby suburbs

Benalla, next to Swanpool, has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Lima South, Lima East and Lima also border Swanpool.
