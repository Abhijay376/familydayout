Cornella is in the Campaspe area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Cornella, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cornella. The closest to Cornella is Elmore Railway Station (V/Line), about 25 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cornella, so most trips start by car. By car from Cornella, Echuca is about 58 minutes away, Bendigo about 59 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cornella is Rushworth Pool in Rushworth, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest library to Cornella is Heathcote Library in Heathcote, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Cornella is Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote, about 24 minutes' drive. Echuca is the closest bigger centre to Cornella, about 58 minutes away by car, and has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Cornella are Heathcote Play Space in Heathcote (23 min), Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote (24 min), Heathcote Library in Heathcote (24 min), Mudwood Studio in Heathcote (24 min), Powder Magazine in Heathcote (26 min) and Rushworth Library in Rushworth (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Cornella.

Nearby suburbs

Colbinabbin, next to Cornella, has 1 playground. Creek View also borders Cornella.
