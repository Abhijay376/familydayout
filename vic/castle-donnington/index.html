Castle Donnington is in the Swan Hill area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Castle Donnington, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Castle Donnington. The closest to Castle Donnington is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 8.1 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Castle Donnington, so most trips start by car. By car from Castle Donnington, Swan Hill is about 9 minutes away and Echuca about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Castle Donnington is Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest library to Castle Donnington is Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Castle Donnington is the skate park at Riverside Park in Swan Hill, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Castle Donnington is Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill, about 8 minutes' drive. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Castle Donnington, about 9 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Castle Donnington are Lake Boga Flying Boat Museum in Lake Boga (5 min), the playground at Guastalegname Park in Swan Hill (6 min), the playground at Gray Park in Lake Boga (7 min), Swan Hill Regional Art Gallery in Swan Hill (7 min), the skate park at Riverside Park in Swan Hill (8 min) and Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill (8 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Castle Donnington.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Castle Donnington, Swan Hill has 9 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Castle Donnington, Lake Boga has 1 playground. Goschen also borders Castle Donnington.
