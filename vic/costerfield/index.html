Costerfield is in the Greater Bendigo area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Costerfield Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Costerfield, at about 17 hectares. Costerfield South Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Costerfield.

Getting around

There's no train station in Costerfield. The closest to Costerfield is Seymour Railway Station (V/Line), about 35 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Costerfield, so most trips start by car. By car from Costerfield, Seymour is about 46 minutes away, Bendigo about 52 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Nagambie Pool in Nagambie is about 28 minutes' drive from Costerfield. The closest library to Costerfield is Heathcote Library in Heathcote, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Costerfield is Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote, about 12 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 46 minutes from Costerfield by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Costerfield are Mudwood Studio in Heathcote (12 min), Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote (12 min), Heathcote Library in Heathcote (12 min), Heathcote Play Space in Heathcote (12 min), Powder Magazine in Heathcote (14 min) and Tooborac Recreation Reserve Playground in Tooborac (18 min).
