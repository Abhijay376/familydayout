Bonbeach Beach

Beach in Bonbeach · Basic info

Bonbeach Beach is a beach in Bonbeach, in the Kingston City Council area, good for all ages. Bonbeach Railway Station (train) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 723 m away.
