Bonbeach in the Kingston area has 3 playgrounds and 4 parks and reserves in our data. Bonbeach also has a beach.

Landmarks

Bonbeach also has Mary Bell Park, Mascot Avenue Reserve and Mernda Avenue Reserve. Playgrounds in Bonbeach include the playground at Mernda Avenue Reserve and the playground at Scotch Parade Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Bonbeach. Places to visit in Bonbeach include Bonbeach Beach. The Bike: Carrum - Frankston and Longbeach Trail all pass through Bonbeach.

Getting around

Trains stop at Bonbeach Railway Station (metro: Frankston Line) in Bonbeach. Bus 857 stops in Bonbeach. By car from Bonbeach, Frankston is about 14 minutes away, Dandenong about 19 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 38 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Grenda Aquatic Centre in Keysborough is about 13 minutes' drive from Bonbeach. The closest library to Bonbeach is Chelsea Library in Chelsea, about 2 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Chelsea Skate Park in Chelsea is about 4 minutes' drive from Bonbeach. For a cinema, Hoyts in Frankston is about 14 minutes' drive from Bonbeach. The closest indoor play centre to Bonbeach is Gravity Zone Indoor Trampoline Centre in Seaford, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Bonbeach is Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale, about 26 minutes' drive. Frankston is the closest bigger centre to Bonbeach, about 14 minutes away by car, and has 27 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Bonbeach's 3 local playgrounds. Beach days at Bonbeach Beach. Free days on the Bike: Carrum - Frankston and in Bonbeach's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Chelsea, next to Bonbeach, has 3 playgrounds, a library and a beach. Next door to Bonbeach, Patterson Lakes has 7 playgrounds and a library. Carrum, next to Bonbeach, has 2 playgrounds and a beach. Chelsea Heights, next to Bonbeach, has 4 playgrounds.
