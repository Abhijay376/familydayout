Grassmere in the Moyne area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Grassmere is Grassmere Recreation Reserve, covering about 1 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Grassmere. The closest to Grassmere is Sherwood Park Railway Station (V/Line), about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Grassmere, so most trips start by car. By car from Grassmere, Warrnambool is about 13 minutes away and Hamilton about 74 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Grassmere is Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest library to Grassmere is Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool, about 14 minutes' drive. For a beach, Shelly Beach in Warrnambool is about 17 minutes' drive from Grassmere. The closest skate park to Grassmere is Warrnambool Skate Park in Warrnambool, about 16 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool is about 13 minutes' drive from Grassmere. For an indoor play centre, The Bowl in Warrnambool is about 14 minutes' drive from Grassmere. For a zoo or wildlife park, Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee is about 17 minutes' drive from Grassmere. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Grassmere, about 13 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Grassmere are the playground at Bushfield Recreation Reserve in Bushfield (6 min), the playground at Albert Park in Warrnambool (12 min), Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool (13 min), SCOPE Galleries in Warrnambool (14 min), The Bowl in Warrnambool (14 min) and Warrnambool Art Gallery in Warrnambool (14 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Grassmere.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Grassmere, Bushfield has 1 playground. Wangoom, Purnim West, Purnim and Mailors Flat also border Grassmere.
