Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Kilmany in the Wellington area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Kilmany has no train station; Rosedale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kilmany, so most trips start by car. By car from Kilmany, Sale is about 15 minutes away, Traralgon about 33 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kilmany is Rosedale Swimming Pool in Rosedale, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest library to Kilmany is Sale Library in Sale, about 12 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Sale Skate Park in Sale is about 12 minutes' drive from Kilmany. The closest cinema to Kilmany is Sale Cinemas in Sale, about 13 minutes' drive. Sale is the closest bigger centre to Kilmany, about 15 minutes away by car, and has 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Kilmany, the nearest places for kids by road are Wurruk Playground in Wurruk (10 min), Sale Skate Park in Sale (12 min), Sale Library in Sale (12 min), Gippsland Art Gallery in Sale (12 min), Old train signals in Sale (13 min) and Rosedale Swimming Pool in Rosedale (12 min).
