Landmarks

South Frederick The Great Historic and Cultural Features Reserve is the largest mapped park in Sebastian, at about 5 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Sebastian.

Getting around

There's no train station in Sebastian. The closest to Sebastian is Raywood Railway Station (V/Line), about 7.0 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Sebastian. By car from Sebastian, Bendigo is about 24 minutes away, Castlemaine about 53 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood is about 10 minutes' drive from Sebastian. For a library, Bendigo Library in Bendigo is about 24 minutes' drive from Sebastian. For a skate park, McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk is about 17 minutes' drive from Sebastian. The closest cinema to Sebastian is Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Sebastian is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 26 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 27 minutes' drive from Sebastian. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 24 minutes from Sebastian by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Sebastian, the nearest places for kids by road are Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood (10 min), McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk (17 min), Peter Krenz Leisure Centre in Eaglehawk (17 min), Eaglehawk Playspace in Eaglehawk (17 min), the playground at Canterbury Park in Eaglehawk (17 min) and the bmx track on Victoria Street in Eaglehawk (18 min).
