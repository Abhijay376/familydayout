Segarta Reserve

Park in Ferntree Gully · Basic info

Segarta Reserve is a park in Ferntree Gully, in the Knox City Council area, good for all ages. Faith St/Mountain Gate Dr (bus) is a 9-minute walk away and dogs are allowed on a lead. Opening hours: opening_hours: 24/7.
