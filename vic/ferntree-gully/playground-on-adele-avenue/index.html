Playground on Adele Avenue

Playground in Ferntree Gully · Basic info

This is a public playground in Ferntree Gully. The Knox City Council Informal Bike Route passes about 50 m away, and Break 40 is about 60 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 140 m away, and bus 755 stops at Mountain Gate SC/Mountain Gate Dr, a 1-minute walk away.
