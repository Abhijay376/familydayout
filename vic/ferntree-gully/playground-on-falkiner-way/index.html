Playground on Falkiner Way

Playground in Ferntree Gully · Basic info

Playground on Falkiner Way in Ferntree Gully is a public playground in the Knox City area. For food, 24 Hour Kebab is about 150 m away, and Playground on Sylvia Street is another option about 450 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 650 m from the playground, and buses 693 and 732 stop at Dorset Rd/Burwood Hwy, a 1-minute walk away.
