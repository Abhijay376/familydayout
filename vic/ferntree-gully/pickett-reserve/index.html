Pickett Reserve

Park in Ferntree Gully · Basic info

Pickett Reserve is a park in Ferntree Gully, in the Knox City Council area, good for all ages. Burwood Hwy/Commercial Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 319 m away.
