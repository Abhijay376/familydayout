Playground on Adele Avenue

Playground in Ferntree Gully · Basic info

Playground on Adele Avenue in Ferntree Gully is a public playground in the Knox City area. The Knox City Council Informal Bike Route is about 220 m away, and for food, Mountain Gate Indian and Nepalese Restaurant is about 450 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 780 m from the playground, and bus 693 stops at Ashton Rd/Ferntree Gully Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
