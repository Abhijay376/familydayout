Playground at Windermere Reserve

Playground in Ferntree Gully · Basic info

This is a public playground in Ferntree Gully. The Scoresby Road Trail is about 330 m away, and Playground on Paddock Court is another option about 280 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 930 m from the playground, and bus 755 stops at Windermere Reserve/Windermere Dr, a 1-minute walk away.
