Playground at Kent Park

Playground in Ferntree Gully · Basic info

Playground at Kent Park is a public playground in Ferntree Gully, in the Knox City area. The Knox City Council Informal Bike Route is about 390 m away, and Playground on Yarana Street is another option about 540 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 980 m from the playground, and buses 753 and 755 stop at Sandgate Bvd/Scoresby Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
