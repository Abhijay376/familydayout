Playground on Dobson Street

Playground in Ferntree Gully · Basic info

Playground on Dobson Street is a public playground in Ferntree Gully, in the Knox City area. The Knox City Council Informal Bike Route passes about 100 m away, and McDonald's is about 380 m away if you need food. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and buses 732 and 967 stop at Hayward Rd/Burwood Hwy, a 6-minute walk away.
