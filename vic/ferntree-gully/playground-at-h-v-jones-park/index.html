Playground at H.V. Jones Park

Playground in Ferntree Gully · Basic info

Playground at H.V. Jones Park in Ferntree Gully is a public playground in the Knox City area. The Ferny Creek Trail is about 280 m away, and Playground at Natasha Reserve is another option about 380 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.1 km from the playground, and bus 755 stops at Ashton Rd/Mountain Gate Dr, a 3-minute walk away.
