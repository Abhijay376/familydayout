Playground on Victoria Street

Playground in Ferntree Gully · Basic info

Playground on Victoria Street in Ferntree Gully is a public playground in the Knox City area. The Ringwood - Belgrave Rail Trail passes about 240 m away, and Station Street Fish and Chips is about 200 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 470 m away, and buses 691 and 693 stop at Alpine St/Station St, a 4-minute walk away.
