Stockton Avenue Reserve

Park in Ferntree Gully · Basic info

Stockton Avenue Reserve is a park in Ferntree Gully, in the Knox City Council area, good for all ages. Ferntree Gully Cemetery/Forest Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 511 m away with a baby change table.
