Playground at Agora Reserve

Playground in Ferntree Gully · Basic info

Playground at Agora Reserve is a public playground in Ferntree Gully, in the Knox City area. The Blind Creek Trail is about 100 m away, and Playground on Stirling Street is another option about 190 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 870 m from the playground, and bus 753 stops at Narcissus Ave/Springfield Rd, a 8-minute walk away.
