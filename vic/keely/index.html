Keely is in the Gannawarra area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Keely, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Keely has no train station; Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 25 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Keely, so most trips start by car. By car from Keely, Echuca is about 51 minutes away and Swan Hill about 82 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Keely is Cohuna Pool in Cohuna, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest library to Keely is Barham Branch Library in Koondrook, about 36 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna is about 9 minutes' drive from Keely. Echuca is the closest bigger centre to Keely, about 51 minutes away by car, and has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Keely are Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna (9 min), the playground at Cohuna Apex Park in Cohuna (9 min), Cohuna Pool in Cohuna (10 min), the playground at C L King Memorial Park in Cohuna (10 min), Leitchville Memorial Swimming Pool in Leitchville (13 min) and Barham Branch Library in Koondrook (36 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Keely.

Nearby suburbs

Leitchville, next to Keely, has 4 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Keely, Cohuna has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Daltons Bridge also borders Keely.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Keely, without traffic.
