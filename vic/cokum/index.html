Cokum is in the Buloke area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Cokum is Cokam Bushland Reserve, covering about 16 hectares.

Getting around

Cokum has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 53 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cokum, so most trips start by car. By car from Cokum, Swan Hill is about 58 minutes away and Echuca about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Quambatook Memorial Pool in Quambatook is about 31 minutes' drive from Cokum. For a library, Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook is about 31 minutes' drive from Cokum. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Cokum, about 58 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Cokum are the playground at Hall Corner Park in Nullawil (19 min), the playground at Culgoa Memorial Park in Culgoa (20 min), Quambatook Historical Centre in Quambatook (30 min), Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook (31 min), Quambatook Memorial Pool in Quambatook (31 min) and Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Cokum.

Nearby suburbs

Culgoa, next to Cokum, has 1 playground. Kalpienung also borders Cokum.
