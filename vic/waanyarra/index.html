Waanyarra is in the Loddon area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Waanyarra Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Waanyarra, at about 2,864 hectares. Waanyarra also has Waanyarra Recreation Site. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Waanyarra. Picnic areas in Waanyarra include Waanyarra Recreation Site. For walks and rides, Dunolly Cycle Tracks, Laanecoorie Cycling Track and Waanyarra Cycle Track cross Waanyarra.

Getting around

Waanyarra has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Waanyarra, so most trips start by car. By car from Waanyarra, Maryborough is about 32 minutes away, Bendigo about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Waanyarra is Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest library to Waanyarra is Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Waanyarra is Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk, about 41 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 32 minutes from Waanyarra by car, with a library. From Waanyarra, the nearest places for kids by road are Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (7 min), Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (8 min), Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (32 min), the playground at Market Reserve in Carisbrook (37 min), Athenaeum Library in Maldon (34 min) and Maldon Swimming Pool in Maldon (35 min).

Best for

Free days on the Dunolly Cycle Tracks and in Waanyarra's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Waanyarra, Dunolly has a pool. Tarnagulla, next to Waanyarra, has 1 playground. Next door to Waanyarra, Newbridge has 1 playground. Laanecoorie and Eddington also border Waanyarra.
