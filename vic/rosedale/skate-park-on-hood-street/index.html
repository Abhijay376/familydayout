Skate park on Hood Street

Skate, BMX or pump track in Rosedale · Basic info

Skate park on Hood Street is a skate, bmx or pump track in Rosedale, in the Wellington Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Latrobe St/Princes Hwy (coach) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 304 m away.
