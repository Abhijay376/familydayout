Queen Street Reserve Playground

Playground in Rosedale · Basic info

This is a public playground in Rosedale. Older kids have a skate park about 320 m away, and Rosedale Swimming Pool is about 410 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 350 m from the playground, and coaches stop at Latrobe St/Princes Hwy, a 1-minute walk away.
