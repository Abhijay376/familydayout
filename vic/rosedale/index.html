Rosedale is in the Wellington area, and our data lists 3 playgrounds and 2 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Rosedale has a pool.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Rosedale is Rosedale Bushland Reserve, covering about 17 hectares. Willow Park is the next largest. Playgrounds in Rosedale include Edward Crooke Park Playground, Queen Street Reserve Playground and Prince Street Reserve Playground. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Rosedale. Places to visit in Rosedale include Rosedale Swimming Pool. For walks and rides, Harrier's Swamp Walking Track crosses Rosedale. Our data lists 9 cafes and family restaurants in Rosedale for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Rosedale has a train station: Rosedale Railway Station (V/Line: Bairnsdale - Melbourne via Sale & Traralgon). V/Line coaches stop in Rosedale. By car from Rosedale, Traralgon is about 21 minutes away, Sale about 25 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Rosedale is Sale Library in Sale, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Rosedale is Pump Track in Heyfield, about 18 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Sale Cinemas in Sale is about 23 minutes' drive from Rosedale. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Rosedale, about 21 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool.

Best for

Younger kids at Rosedale's 3 local playgrounds. Free days on the Harrier's Swamp Walking Track and in Rosedale's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Rosedale, Glengarry has 1 playground and a skate park. Nambrok, Longford, Kilmany and Flynn also border Rosedale.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Rosedale, without traffic.
