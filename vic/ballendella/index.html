Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Ballendella in the Campaspe area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Ballendella. The closest to Ballendella is Rochester Railway Station (V/Line), about 8.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Ballendella, so most trips start by car. By car from Ballendella, Echuca is about 22 minutes away, Bendigo about 66 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Memorial Pool in Rochester is about 10 minutes' drive from Ballendella. For a library, Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca is about 23 minutes' drive from Ballendella. The closest skate park to Ballendella is Moama Echuca Community Skate Park in Echuca, about 25 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca is about 23 minutes' drive from Ballendella. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram is about 41 minutes' drive from Ballendella. Echuca is the closest bigger centre to Ballendella, about 22 minutes away by car, and has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Ballendella are the playground at Apex Park in Rochester (10 min), Memorial Pool in Rochester (10 min), Lockington Pool in Lockington (12 min), the playground at Echuca Lions Park in Echuca (20 min), Holden Museum in Echuca (22 min) and Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca (23 min).
