Playground at Bush Reserve

Playground in Coburg · Basic info

This is a public playground in Coburg. The West Brunswick Shimmy passes about 170 m away, and Playground at Soudan Reserve is another option about 290 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and buses 513, 514, 561 and 903 stop at Linsey St/Bell St, a 3-minute walk away.
