Playground at Gandolfo Gardens

Playground in Coburg · Basic info

Playground at Gandolfo Gardens in Coburg is a public playground in the Merri-Bek City area. The Upfield Bike Path is about 30 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 230 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 230 m from the playground, and trains stop at Moreland Railway Station, a 1-minute walk away.
