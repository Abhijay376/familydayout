Playground on Tanderum Drive

Playground in Coburg · Basic info

This is a public playground in Coburg. The L3 to Pentridge Link is about 90 m away, and Coburg Olympic Pool is about 220 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 560 m from the playground, and bus 526 stops at Coburg Swimming Pool/Murray Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
