Playground at McKay Street Reserve

Playground in Coburg · Basic info

Playground at McKay Street Reserve in Coburg is a public playground in the Merri-Bek City area. The Upfield Shared Path passes about 90 m away, and Kirrip biik Park Free mini library is about 280 m away. There's a public toilet about 370 m away, and buses 526 and 530 stop at Bell St/McKay St, a 2-minute walk away.
