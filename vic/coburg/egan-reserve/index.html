Egan Reserve

Park in Coburg · Basic info

Egan Reserve is a park in Coburg, in the Merri-Bek City Council area, good for all ages. The Avenue/Nicholson St #130 (tram) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 920 m away.
