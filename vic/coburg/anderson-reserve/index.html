Anderson Reserve

Park in Coburg · Official details

Anderson Reserve is a park in Coburg, in the Merri-Bek City Council area, good for all ages. John Fawkner Private Hospital/Moreland Rd (bus) is a 9-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 612 m away and dogs are allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Anderson Reserve?
Anderson Reserve is in Coburg, Victoria.
Is Anderson Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Anderson Reserve?
Yes, dogs are allowed.
Are there toilets at Anderson Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 612 m away.
How do I get to Anderson Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is John Fawkner Private Hospital/Moreland Rd (routes 510), a 9-minute walk.
