Playground at Kirrip biik Park

Playground in Coburg · Basic info

Playground at Kirrip biik Park in Coburg is a public playground in the Merri-Bek City area. The Upfield Bike Path is about 100 m away, and Kirrip biik Park Free mini library is about 20 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 670 m from the playground, and bus 527 stops at O'Hea St/Main St, a 3-minute walk away.
