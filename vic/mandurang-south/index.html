Mandurang South in the Greater Bendigo area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Sandhurst Reference Area is the largest mapped park in Mandurang South, at about 452 hectares. For walks and rides, Goldfields Track and Leanganook Track cross Mandurang South.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mandurang South. The closest to Mandurang South is Kangaroo Flat Railway Station (V/Line), about 8.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mandurang South, so most trips start by car. By car from Mandurang South, Bendigo is about 20 minutes away, Castlemaine about 42 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Brennan Park Swimming Pool in Kennington is about 19 minutes' drive from Mandurang South. For a library, Kangaroo Flat Library in Kangaroo Flat is about 18 minutes' drive from Mandurang South. The closest skate park to Mandurang South is Ewing Park Skating in Bendigo, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Mandurang South is Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo, about 19 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 18 minutes' drive from Mandurang South. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 23 minutes' drive from Mandurang South. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Mandurang South, about 20 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Mandurang South, the nearest places for kids by road are Kangaroo Flat Library in Kangaroo Flat (18 min), the playground at Spring Gully Bushland Reserve in Flora Hill (15 min), Kangaroo Flat Playground & Recreation Reserve in Kangaroo Flat (18 min), Ewing Park Skating in Bendigo (18 min), Brennan Park Swimming Pool in Kennington (19 min) and Intagolf in Golden Gully (18 min).

Best for

Free days on the Goldfields Track and in Mandurang South's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Mandurang South, Big Hill has 1 playground. Harcourt North also borders Mandurang South.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Mandurang South, without traffic.
