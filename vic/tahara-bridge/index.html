Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Tahara Bridge, in the Glenelg area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tahara Bridge is Wannon River (2) Streamside Reserve, covering about 3 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tahara Bridge. The closest to Tahara Bridge is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 106 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tahara Bridge, so most trips start by car. By car from Tahara Bridge, Hamilton is about 36 minutes away and Portland about 65 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Merino Swimming Pool in Merino is about 17 minutes' drive from Tahara Bridge. For a library, Hamilton Library in Hamilton is about 36 minutes' drive from Tahara Bridge. The closest skate park to Tahara Bridge is coleraine skate park in Coleraine, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Tahara Bridge is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 36 minutes' drive. Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Tahara Bridge, about 36 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Tahara Bridge are the playground at Lions Park in Merino (16 min), the playground at Peter Francis Points Arboretum in Coleraine (13 min), Merino Swimming Pool in Merino (17 min), coleraine skate park in Coleraine (14 min), Gorman's art gallery in Casterton (33 min) and Casterton Pool in Casterton (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tahara Bridge.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Tahara Bridge, Merino has 1 playground and a pool. Next door to Tahara Bridge, Coleraine has 4 playgrounds and a skate park. Tahara and Paschendale also border Tahara Bridge.
