Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Myola in the Campaspe area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Myola. The closest to Myola is Goornong Railway Station (V/Line), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Myola, so most trips start by car. By car from Myola, Bendigo is about 45 minutes away, Echuca about 52 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Myola is Goornong Swimming Pool in Goornong, about 20 minutes' drive. For a library, Heathcote Library in Heathcote is about 26 minutes' drive from Myola. For a skate park, Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote is about 26 minutes' drive from Myola. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 42 minutes' drive from Myola. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Myola, about 45 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Myola are Campaspe Run Rural Discovery Centre in Elmore (14 min), Goornong Swimming Pool in Goornong (20 min), the playground at Collins Park in Ascot (34 min), Heathcote Play Space in Heathcote (25 min), Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote (26 min) and Heathcote Library in Heathcote (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Myola.

Nearby suburbs

Goornong, next to Myola, has a pool. Creek View also borders Myola.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Myola, without traffic.
