Landmarks

Wongarra Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Skenes Creek North, at about 1 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Skenes Creek North. The closest to Skenes Creek North is Birregurra Railway Station (V/Line), about 40 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Skenes Creek North, so most trips start by car. By car from Skenes Creek North, Colac is about 57 minutes away, Torquay about 1.5 hours and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Skenes Creek North is Apollo Bay Community Pool in Apollo Bay, about 14 minutes' drive. For a beach, White Crest in Wongarra is about 15 minutes' drive from Skenes Creek North. For a skate park, the skate park at Forrest Common in Forrest is about 24 minutes' drive from Skenes Creek North. The closest cinema to Skenes Creek North is Mechanics Hall in Apollo Bay, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Skenes Creek North is Skate Bowl in Apollo Bay, about 14 minutes' drive. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Skenes Creek North, about 57 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Skenes Creek North, the nearest places for kids by road are Old Cable Station Museum in Apollo Bay (10 min), Skate Bowl in Apollo Bay (14 min), the playground at Foreshore Reserve in Apollo Bay (14 min), Mechanics Hall in Apollo Bay (15 min), Apollo Bay Community Pool in Apollo Bay (14 min) and White Crest in Wongarra (15 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Skenes Creek North.

Nearby suburbs

Apollo Bay, next to Skenes Creek North, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a beach. Next door to Skenes Creek North, Wongarra has a beach.
