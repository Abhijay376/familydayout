Swifts Creek is in the East Gippsland area, and our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in the suburb. On top of that, Swifts Creek has a pool and a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Swifts Creek is Cassilis Historic Area, covering about 4,355 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Swifts Creek. Places to visit in Swifts Creek include Great Alpine Gallery and Swifts Creek Memorial Pool.

Getting around

Swifts Creek has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 63 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 14 stops in Swifts Creek. By car from Swifts Creek, Bairnsdale is about 85 minutes away and Lakes Entrance about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a skate park, the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo is about 23 minutes' drive from Swifts Creek. The closest places for kids by road from Swifts Creek are Petersens Art Gallery & Museum in Omeo (22 min), the playground at Omeo Historical Park & Museum in Omeo (22 min) and the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo (23 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Swifts Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Swifts Creek, Omeo has 2 playgrounds and a skate park. Ensay, next to Swifts Creek, has 1 playground. Nunniong, Ensay North, Doctors Flat and Cassilis also border Swifts Creek.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Swifts Creek, without traffic.
