Landmarks

Wellsford also has Gunyah Picnic Area. Picnic areas in Wellsford include Gunyah Picnic Area. For walks and rides, O'Keefe Rail Trail and Doder Track cross Wellsford.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wellsford. The closest to Wellsford is Huntly Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wellsford, so most trips start by car. By car from Wellsford, Bendigo is about 21 minutes away, Castlemaine about 53 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, White Hills Pool in White Hills is about 15 minutes' drive from Wellsford. For a library, Bendigo Library in Bendigo is about 21 minutes' drive from Wellsford. The closest skate park to Wellsford is the pump track on Water Street in Bendigo, about 22 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 22 minutes' drive from Wellsford. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 25 minutes' drive from Wellsford. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 18 minutes' drive from Wellsford. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Wellsford, about 21 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Wellsford are the playground at Collins Park in Ascot (13 min), the playground at Epsom Village Green in Epsom (14 min), White Hills Pool in White Hills (15 min), Bendigo Joss House Temple in North Bendigo (18 min), The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo (18 min) and Bendigo Steam Engine Museum in North Bendigo (20 min).

Best for

Free days on the O'Keefe Rail Trail and in Wellsford's parks.
