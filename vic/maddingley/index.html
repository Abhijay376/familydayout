Maddingley is in the Moorabool area, and our data lists 8 playgrounds and 6 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Maddingley has a skate park.

Landmarks

Bacchus Marsh Racecourse and Recreation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Maddingley, at about 80 hectares. Maddingley Park and Kel Shields Flora Park are the next largest. Playground at Maddingley Park and Playground at Sarino Park Recreation Reserve are among the council's larger district playgrounds. Other parks and reserves in Maddingley include McCrae Park, Essence Park and Stonehill Park. Other playgrounds in Maddingley include McCrae playground, Essence Playground and Stonehill Playground. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Maddingley. For older kids in Maddingley there's the bmx track at Bacchus Marsh Racecourse and Recreation Reserve. For walks and rides, Golf Course Footpath and Aqualink East cross Maddingley.

Getting around

Trains stop at Bacchus Marsh Railway Station (V/Line: Ararat - Melbourne via Ballarat, Ballarat/Wendouree - Melbourne via Melton and Maryborough - Melbourne via Ballarat) in Maddingley. Buses 433 and 434 stop in Maddingley. V/Line coaches stop in Maddingley. By car from Maddingley, Bacchus Marsh is about 5 minutes away, Melton about 18 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 53 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bacchus Marsh Public childrens pool in Bacchus Marsh is about 3 minutes' drive from Maddingley. The closest library to Maddingley is Lerderderg Library in Bacchus Marsh, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Maddingley is Needles Beach in Pentland Hills, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Maddingley is Reading Cinemas in Melton West, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Maddingley is Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes, about 36 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Fairy Park in Anakie is about 42 minutes' drive from Maddingley.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 8 playgrounds across Maddingley. Teens who skate or ride, with the bmx track at Bacchus Marsh Racecourse and Recreation Reserve in Maddingley. Free days on the Golf Course Footpath and in Maddingley's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Bacchus Marsh, next to Maddingley, has 6 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Maddingley, Pentland Hills has a beach. Ingliston also borders Maddingley.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Maddingley, without traffic.
