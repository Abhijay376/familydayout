McCrae playground

Playground in Maddingley · Basic info

This is a small local playground in Maddingley, looked after by Moorabool Shire Council. Bacchus Marsh Public childrens pool is about 570 m away. There's a public toilet about 610 m away, and bus 433 stops at McCrae St/Griffith St, a 2-minute walk away.
