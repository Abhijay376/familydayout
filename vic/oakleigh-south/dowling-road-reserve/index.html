Dowling Road Reserve

Park in Oakleigh South · Basic info

Dowling Road Reserve is a park in Oakleigh South, in the Kingston City Council area, good for all ages. Tatiara Ave/Clarinda Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1150 m away with a baby change table.
