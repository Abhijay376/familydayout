Playground at W.A. Scammell Reserve

Playground in Oakleigh South · Basic info

Playground at W.A. Scammell Reserve in Oakleigh South is a public playground in the Monash City area. Playground on Wilson Street is another option about 520 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 320 m from the playground, and bus 630 stops at South Oakleigh Bowling Club/North Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
