Playground at Davies Reserve

Playground in Oakleigh South · Basic info

Playground at Davies Reserve in Oakleigh South is a public playground in the Monash City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. You'll find a public toilet about 60 m from the playground, and bus 704 stops at Valley St/Coora Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
