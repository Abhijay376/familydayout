Davies Reserve

Park in Oakleigh South · Basic info

Davies Reserve is a park in Oakleigh South, in the Monash City Council area, good for all ages. Coombs Ave/Legon Rd (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 329 m away.
