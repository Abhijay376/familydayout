Talbot Reserve

Park in Oakleigh South · Basic info

Talbot Reserve is a park in Oakleigh South, in the Monash City Council area, good for all ages. Elder St/Centre Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 196 m away.
