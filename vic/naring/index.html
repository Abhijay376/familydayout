Naring is in the Moira area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Naring is Unnamed P0066 Private Nature Reserve, covering about 18 hectares. Naringaningalook H33 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Naring. The closest to Naring is Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line), about 38 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Naring, so most trips start by car. By car from Naring, Shepparton is about 47 minutes away and Benalla about 70 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah is about 17 minutes' drive from Naring. For a library, Numurkah Library in Numurkah is about 16 minutes' drive from Naring. The closest beach to Naring is Fosters Beach in Cobram, about 38 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Riverland Skate Park in Numurkah is about 17 minutes' drive from Naring. For a cinema, Cobram Cinema in Cobram is about 34 minutes' drive from Naring. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 47 minutes from Naring by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Naring, the nearest places for kids by road are Numurkah Library in Numurkah (16 min), Riverland Skate Park in Numurkah (17 min), Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah (17 min), the playground at C R Wood Park in Katamatite (22 min), the playground at E.W Kilgour Reserve in Katamatite (22 min) and Katamatite Historical Society Museum in Katamatite (22 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Naring.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Naring, Numurkah has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Next door to Naring, Katamatite has 2 playgrounds. Invergordon also borders Naring.
