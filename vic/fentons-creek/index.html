Fentons Creek in the Loddon area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Berrimal Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Fentons Creek, at about 7 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Fentons Creek. The closest to Fentons Creek is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 59 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Fentons Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Fentons Creek, Maryborough is about 64 minutes away, Bendigo about 74 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Fentons Creek is Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Fentons Creek is Rex Theatre in Charlton, about 40 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Fentons Creek are Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (14 min), Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (14 min), Bi-Centennial Park Playground in St Arnaud (23 min), the playground at Queen Mary Botanic Gardens in St Arnaud (24 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (24 min) and Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (39 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Fentons Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Gowar East, Burkes Flat and Berrimal also border Fentons Creek.
