Rex Corrigan Reserve

Park in Keysborough · Official details

Rex Corrigan Reserve is a park in Keysborough, in the Greater Dandenong City Council area, good for all ages. Walnut Cres/Henderson Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 738 m away.
