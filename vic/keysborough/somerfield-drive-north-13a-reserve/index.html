Somerfield Drive North(13A) Reserve

Playground in Keysborough · Official details

Somerfield Drive North(13A) Reserve in Keysborough is a public playground in the Greater Dandenong City area. Playground - Pirate Park is another option about 350 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 450 m from the playground, and bus 816 stops at Somerfield Dr/Perry Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
