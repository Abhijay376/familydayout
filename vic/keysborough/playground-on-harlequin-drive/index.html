Playground at Ascot Reserve - Harlequin Drive

Playground in Keysborough · Official details

Playground at Ascot Reserve - Harlequin Drive is a public playground in Keysborough, in the Greater Dandenong City area. The Dandenong South Trail passes about 280 m away, and Rowley Allan Reserve Playground is another option about 540 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 650 m away, and bus 816 stops at Tulip Way/Church Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
