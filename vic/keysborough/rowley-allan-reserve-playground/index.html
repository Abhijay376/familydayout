Rowley Allan Reserve Playground

Playground in Keysborough · Official details

Rowley Allan Reserve Playground in Keysborough is a public playground in the Greater Dandenong City area. The Dandenong South Trail is about 260 m away, and Playground at Ascot Reserve - Harlequin Drive is another option about 540 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 460 m from the playground, and buses 828 and 979 stop at Garden Ct/Cheltenham Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
