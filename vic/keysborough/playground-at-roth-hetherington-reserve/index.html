Playground at Roth Hetherington Reserve

Playground in Keysborough · Official details

This is a public playground in Keysborough. Canton Court Chinese is about 340 m away if you need food, and Playground at Rex Corrigan Reserve is another option about 420 m away. There's a public toilet about 60 m away, and buses 812, 813 and 824 stop at Yagoona Ave/Kingsclere Ave, a 4-minute walk away.
