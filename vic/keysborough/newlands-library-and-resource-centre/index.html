Newlands Library & Resource Centre

Library in Keysborough · Basic info

Newlands Library & Resource Centre is a library in Keysborough, in the Greater Dandenong City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Haileybury Keysborough Campus/Springvale Rd (bus) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1853 m away with a baby change table.
