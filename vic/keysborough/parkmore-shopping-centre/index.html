Parkmore Shopping Centre

Shopping centre in Keysborough · Official details

Parkmore Shopping Centre is a shopping centre in Keysborough, in the Greater Dandenong City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Parkmore Shopping Centre/Kingsclere Ave (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 276 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: - TK Maxx opens Thursday 13 August - 9am; - Aldi opens Wednesday 19 August at 8am; - Rebel Sport opens Thursday 20 August 9am.
