Playground at Tatterson Park

Playground in Keysborough · Official details

Playground at Tatterson Park in Keysborough is a public playground in the Greater Dandenong City area. The Dandenong South Trail passes about 320 m away, and Ian Tatterson Park Playgorund is another option about 10 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 130 m away. It's easiest by car, about 5 minutes from Keysborough.
