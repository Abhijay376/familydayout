Playground - Pirate Park

Playground in Keysborough · Official details

Playground - Pirate Park is a public playground in Keysborough, in the Greater Dandenong City area. Somerfield Drive North(13A) Reserve is another option about 350 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 80 m from the playground, and bus 709 stops at Westwood Bvd/Perry Rd, a 7-minute walk away.
