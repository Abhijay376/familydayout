Playground on Carribean Drive

Playground in Keysborough · Basic info

Playground on Carribean Drive is a public playground in Keysborough, in the Greater Dandenong City area. Playground at Bilbungra Drive Reserve is another option about 240 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 150 m from the playground, and buses 812 and 979 stop at Resurrection Church/Corrigan Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
