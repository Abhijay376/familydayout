Playground at Donnici Reserve

Playground in Keysborough · Official details

Playground at Donnici Reserve in Keysborough is a public playground in the Greater Dandenong City area. Pencil Park Playground - Chapel Road (152) Reserve is another option about 400 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 560 m from the playground, and bus 816 stops at Westwood Bvd/Chapel Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
