N-essentials

Natural skincare and DIY ingredients in Keysborough · Details from the business

N-essentials is a Keysborough supplier of essential oils, carrier oils, raw butters and DIY skincare and soap-making ingredients. You can order online for delivery or pick up from Southpark Close with click and collect on weekdays.

It suits families who like making things at home: bath bombs on a wet weekend, lip balm and body butter with teenagers, or a simple massage oil for tired parents after a long day out. Most ingredients come in small sizes for home use as well as bulk sizes for soap and skincare makers.

Click and collect is the easy option if you live in the south-east: pick-up is in Keysborough, next to Braeside and Dandenong South.

What they sell

  • Essential oils: Cedarwood, rosemary, bergamot, lemongrass, ginger and more.
  • Carrier oils: Apricot kernel, argan, castor, grapeseed, sesame, sunflower and more, for diluting essential oils and making balms.
  • Body butters: Raw cocoa, shea and mango butters for lip balms and whipped body butter.

Ideas using their products

  • After a big day out: an easy wind-down for the whole family
  • Rainy-day project: fizzy bath bombs with the kids
  • Make lip balm, body butter and a charcoal face mask with your teen
