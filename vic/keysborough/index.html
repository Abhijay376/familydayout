Our data lists 32 playgrounds and 16 parks and reserves in Keysborough, in the Greater Dandenong area. Keysborough also has 3 libraries and a pool.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Keysborough is Tatterson Park, covering about 66 hectares. Maurie Jarvis Woodland and Frederick Wachter Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Keysborough include Greens, Donnici Reserve and Conservation Area. Playgrounds in Keysborough include 152 Chapel Road Reserve, Junior School Playground and Playground - Pirate Park. Our data shows 14 public toilets, 21 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by and 2 off-leash dog parks in Keysborough. Places to visit in Keysborough include Grenda Aquatic Centre, Bhai Vir Singh Library and MacGregor Library. HomeCo, Keysborough South Shopping Centre and Parkmore Shopping Centre are the main shopping centres. For walks and rides, EastLink Trail and Dandenong South Trail cross Keysborough. Our data lists 25 cafes and family restaurants in Keysborough for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Keysborough. The closest to Keysborough is Yarraman Railway Station (metro), about 2.1 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 709, 812, 813, 816, 824, 828, 902 and 979 stop in Keysborough. By car from Keysborough, Dandenong is about 7 minutes away, Cranbourne about 19 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 30 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a beach, Mordialloc Beach in Parkdale is about 13 minutes' drive from Keysborough. For a skate park, Noble Park Skatepark in Noble Park is about 5 minutes' drive from Keysborough. For a cinema, Reading Cinema in Dandenong is about 8 minutes' drive from Keysborough. For an indoor play centre, Småland in Springvale is about 11 minutes' drive from Keysborough. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale is about 18 minutes' drive from Keysborough.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 32 playgrounds across Keysborough. Rainy days at the library in Keysborough. Free days on the EastLink Trail and in Keysborough's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Dandenong, next to Keysborough, has 21 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Keysborough, Noble Park has 19 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Dingley Village, next to Keysborough, has 18 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Keysborough, Aspendale Gardens has 7 playgrounds. Springvale South, Waterways, Braeside and Dandenong South also border Keysborough.
