Playground at Coomoora Road (28-30) Reserve

Playground in Keysborough · Basic info

Playground at Coomoora Road (28-30) Reserve is a public playground in Keysborough, in the Greater Dandenong City area. The Dandenong South Trail passes about 230 m away, and Playground at Markham Court Reserve is another option about 500 m away. There's a public toilet about 210 m away, and bus 902 stops at Coomoora Rd/Springvale Rd, a 7-minute walk away.
