Playground at Keysborough Community Park

Playground in Keysborough · Official details

Playground at Keysborough Community Park in Keysborough is a public playground in the Greater Dandenong City area. The Dandenong South Trail is about 210 m away, and Playground on Carribean Drive is another option about 300 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 310 m from the playground, and buses 812 and 828 stop at Beauvorno Ave/Cheltenham Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
