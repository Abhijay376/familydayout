Kaniva in the West Wimmera area has 1 playground and 25 parks and reserves in our data. Kaniva also has a library.

Landmarks

Peechember Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Kaniva, at about 246 hectares. Yanipy I24 Bushland Reserve and Mirampiram I38 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Kaniva include Band Park, Mirampiram Flora Reserve and Kaniva Recreation Reserve. Playgrounds around Kaniva include the playground at Band Park. Our data shows 7 public toilets in Kaniva. In Kaniva you'll also find Kaniva Library.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kaniva. The closest to Kaniva is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 182 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Horsham - Kaniva via Dimboola route stop in Kaniva. V/Line coaches stop in Kaniva. By car from Kaniva, Horsham is about 1.5 hours away and Stawell about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest skate park to Kaniva is Nhill Skate Park in Nhill, about 32 minutes' drive. From Kaniva, the nearest places for kids by road are Nhill Playground in Nhill (32 min), Nhill Skate Park in Nhill (32 min), Wagonin in Nhill (32 min), Gallery Central in Nhill (32 min) and Nhill Library in Nhill (33 min).

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Kaniva, Nhill has 3 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Miram, Minimay, Lillimur and Lawloit also border Kaniva.
