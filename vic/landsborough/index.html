Landmarks

The biggest green space in Landsborough is Landsborough Hill Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 1,077 hectares. Malakoff Lead, Landsborough Historic and Cultural Features Reserve and Landsborough North Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Landsborough also has Shays Flat Bushland Reserve, Wattle Creek Bushland Reserve and Landsborough Recreation Reserve. Playgrounds around Landsborough include Community Playground. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Landsborough.

Getting around

There's no train station in Landsborough. The closest to Landsborough is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 35 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the St Arnaud - Stawell via Ararat route stop in Landsborough. By car from Landsborough, Stawell is about 36 minutes away, Ararat about 41 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell is about 36 minutes' drive from Landsborough. For a library, Learning Resource Centre in Ararat is about 44 minutes' drive from Landsborough. For a skate park, the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell is about 40 minutes' drive from Landsborough. For a cinema, Astor Cinema in Ararat is about 41 minutes' drive from Landsborough. For a zoo or wildlife park, Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell is about 38 minutes' drive from Landsborough. Stawell is the closest bigger centre to Landsborough, about 36 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds and a pool. From Landsborough, the nearest places for kids by road are Navarre Playground in Navarre (13 min), Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell (36 min), Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell (38 min), Railway Station Art and Crafts Gallery in Stawell (37 min), the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell (40 min) and David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat (42 min).
