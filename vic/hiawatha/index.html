Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Hiawatha in the Wellington area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Hiawatha. The closest to Hiawatha is Morwell Railway Station (V/Line), about 32 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Hiawatha, so most trips start by car. By car from Hiawatha, Leongatha is about 1.5 hours away and Sale about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Hiawatha is Yarram Public Pool in Yarram, about 44 minutes' drive. The closest library to Hiawatha is Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram, about 43 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Hiawatha is the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram, about 43 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Hiawatha are Yarram Historical Society Museum in Yarram (43 min), the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram (43 min), Yarram Public Pool in Yarram (44 min), Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram (43 min), the playground at Memorial Park in Yarram (44 min) and Port Welshpool and District Maritime Museum in Port Welshpool (49 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Hiawatha.
