Playground on Sydare Avenue

Playground in Malvern East · Basic info

Playground on Sydare Avenue in Malvern East is a public playground in the Stonnington City area. The Urban Forest Trail is about 230 m away, and Playground on The Rialto is another option about 460 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 960 m from the playground, and bus 612 stops at The Rialto/Waverley Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
