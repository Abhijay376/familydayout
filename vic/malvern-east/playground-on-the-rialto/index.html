Playground on The Rialto

Playground in Malvern East · Basic info

Playground on The Rialto is a public playground in Malvern East, in the Stonnington City area. The Scotchmans Creek Trail is about 30 m away, and for food, Cilantro Hearth's Inheritance is about 410 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 380 m from the playground, and bus 612 stops at Oravel Ave/Waverley Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
