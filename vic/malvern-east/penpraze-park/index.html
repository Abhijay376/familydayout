Penpraze Park

Park in Malvern East · Basic info

Penpraze Park is a park in Malvern East, in the Stonnington City Council area, good for all ages. Findon St/Tooronga Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 319 m away.
