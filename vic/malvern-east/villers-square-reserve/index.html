Villers Square Reserve

Park in Malvern East · Basic info

Villers Square Reserve is a park in Malvern East, in the Stonnington City Council area, good for all ages. Karma Ave/Princes Hwy (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 680 m away.
