Playground at Percy Treyvaud Memorial Park

Playground in Malvern East · Basic info

Playground at Percy Treyvaud Memorial Park is a public playground in Malvern East, in the Stonnington City area. The Scotchmans Creek Trail is about 340 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 310 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 280 m from the playground, and bus 612 stops at Fenwick St/Chadstone Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
