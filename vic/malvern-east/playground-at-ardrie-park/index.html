Playground at Ardrie Park

Playground in Malvern East · Basic info

Playground at Ardrie Park in Malvern East is a public playground in the Stonnington City area. Tuan Kitchen is about 460 m away if you need food, and Playground at East Caulfield Reserve is another option about 500 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and tram 3 stops at Macgregor St/Waverley Rd #62, a 4-minute walk away.
