Playground at Darling Park

Playground in Malvern East · Basic info

Playground at Darling Park is a public playground in Malvern East, in the Stonnington City area. The Gardiners Creek Trail passes about 100 m away, and Playground at Dorothy Laver Reserve East is another option about 380 m away. There's a public toilet about 540 m away, and trains stop at Darling Railway Station, a 3-minute walk away.
