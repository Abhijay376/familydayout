Playground at Ambrose Avenue Reserve

Playground in Malvern East · Basic info

Playground at Ambrose Avenue Reserve is a public playground in Malvern East, in the Stonnington City area. The Waverley Rail Trail passes about 40 m away, and Phoenix Park Library is about 540 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 980 m away, and trains stop at Holmesglen Railway Station, a 8-minute walk away.
