Hedgeley Dene Gardens

Park in Malvern East · Basic info

Hedgeley Dene Gardens is a park in Malvern East, in the Stonnington City Council area, good for all ages. Ferncroft Ave/Wattletree Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 209 m away.
