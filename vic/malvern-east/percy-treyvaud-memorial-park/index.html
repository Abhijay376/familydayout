Percy Treyvaud Memorial Park

Park in Malvern East · Basic info

Percy Treyvaud Memorial Park is a park in Malvern East, in the Stonnington City Council area, good for all ages. Abbotsford Ave/Chadstone Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 23 m away.
