Dairy Park, Malvern East

Park in Malvern East · Basic info

Dairy Park is a park in Malvern East, in the Stonnington City Council area, good for all ages. Paxton St/Darling Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 826 m away.
