Queenscliff is in the Queenscliffe area, and our data lists 3 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Queenscliff has 2 museums or galleries and a beach.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Queenscliff is Queenscliff Natural Features Reserve, covering about 4 hectares. Queenscliff also has Hodgetts Reserve and Springhill Reserve. Our data shows 10 public toilets in Queenscliff. In Queenscliff you'll also find Historical Museum, Queenscliffe Maritime Museum and Queenscliff - Pier Beach. For walks and rides, Bellarine Rail Trail and Lovers Walk cross Queenscliff. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Queenscliff for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Queenscliff. The closest to Queenscliff is Marshall Railway Station (V/Line), about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 56 stops in Queenscliff. By car from Queenscliff, Geelong is about 31 minutes away, Torquay about 44 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Queenscliff is Bellarine Aquatic Centre in Ocean Grove, about 14 minutes' drive. For a library, Ocean Grove Library in Ocean Grove is about 16 minutes' drive from Queenscliff. For a skate park, the pump track in Marcus Hill in Marcus Hill is about 12 minutes' drive from Queenscliff. The closest cinema to Queenscliff is Atheneum Cinema in Sorrento, about 45 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Laserquest in Geelong is about 33 minutes' drive from Queenscliff. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Queenscliff is Adventure Park in Wallington, about 17 minutes' drive. Geelong is the closest bigger centre to Queenscliff, about 31 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Queenscliff's 3 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Queenscliff. Beach days at Queenscliff - Pier Beach. Free days on the Bellarine Rail Trail and in Queenscliff's parks.
