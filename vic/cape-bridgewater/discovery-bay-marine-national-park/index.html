Discovery Bay Marine National Park

Park in Cape Bridgewater · Official details

Discovery Bay Marine National Park is a park in Cape Bridgewater, in the Glenelg Shire Council area, good for all ages.

Details

Seasons
Hooded Plover breeds on beaches late Aug to March (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
Some points not passable at high tide; Whites Beach is the most accessible part (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Discovery Bay Marine National Park?
Discovery Bay Marine National Park is in Cape Bridgewater, Victoria.
Is Discovery Bay Marine National Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
