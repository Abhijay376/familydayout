Our data lists 5 playgrounds and 10 parks and reserves in Doveton, in the Casey area. Doveton also has a pool.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Doveton is Eumemmerring Park, covering about 20 hectares. Betula Reserve and Power Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Doveton include Rowan Reserve, Chestnut Reserve and Ray Giles Reserve. Playgrounds in Doveton include the playground at Power Reserve, the playground at Rowan Reserve and the playground at Chestnut Reserve. Our data shows 6 public toilets in Doveton. Places to visit in Doveton include Doveton Pool in the Park. The Dandenong Creek Trail, Hallam Bypass Trail and Heatherton Road Trail all pass through Doveton. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Doveton for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Doveton. The closest to Doveton is Dandenong Railway Station (metro), about 2.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 828, 843, 844, 845, 893, 981 and 982 stop in Doveton. By car from Doveton, Dandenong is about 4 minutes away, Cranbourne about 15 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 30 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Doveton is Dandenong Library in Dandenong, about 4 minutes' drive. For a beach, Edithvale Beach in Edithvale is about 17 minutes' drive from Doveton. The closest skate park to Doveton is the skate park at Waratah Reserve in Eumemmerring, about 2 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Doveton is Reading Cinema in Dandenong, about 3 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Doveton is Escape Reality in Rowville, about 10 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale is about 19 minutes' drive from Doveton.

Best for

Younger kids at Doveton's 5 local playgrounds. Free days on the Dandenong Creek Trail and in Doveton's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Doveton, Dandenong has 21 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Endeavour Hills, next to Doveton, has 26 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Dandenong North, next to Doveton, has 24 playgrounds. Hallam, next to Doveton, has 12 playgrounds and a skate park. Eumemmerring and Dandenong South also border Doveton.
