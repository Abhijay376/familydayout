Playground at Kevin Adlard Reserve

Playground in Doveton · Basic info

Playground at Kevin Adlard Reserve in Doveton is a public playground in the Casey City area. The Hallam Bypass Trail passes about 70 m away, and G'Day Chef is about 400 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 340 m away, and bus 844 stops at Kevin Adlard Reserve/Laurel Ave, a 5-minute walk away.
