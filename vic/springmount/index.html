Springmount is in the Hepburn area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Springmount, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Goldfields Track crosses Springmount.

Getting around

There's no train station in Springmount. The closest to Springmount is Creswick Railway Station (V/Line), about 4.7 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Springmount, so most trips start by car. By car from Springmount, Daylesford is about 20 minutes away, Ballarat about 23 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Infants in Creswick is about 5 minutes' drive from Springmount. The closest library to Springmount is Creswick Library in Creswick, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Springmount is Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Springmount is Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central, about 23 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 26 minutes' drive from Springmount. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek is about 18 minutes' drive from Springmount. Daylesford is the closest bigger centre to Springmount, about 20 minutes away by car, and has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Springmount, the nearest places for kids by road are Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick (5 min), Infants in Creswick (5 min), (Diving tower, restricted) in Creswick (5 min), Creswick Library in Creswick (5 min), Skate Park in Creswick (5 min) and Apex Park Playground in Creswick (5 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Springmount on the Goldfields Track.

Nearby suburbs

Creswick, next to Springmount, has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Broomfield also borders Springmount.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Springmount, without traffic.
