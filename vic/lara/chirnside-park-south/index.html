Chirnside Park South

Playground in Lara · Basic info

This is a public playground in Lara. For food, Six Ways Fish & Chips is about 480 m away, and Allister Reserve is another option about 550 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 530 m from the playground, and bus 12 stops at Chirnside Park/Patullos Ave, a 4-minute walk away.
