Allister Reserve

Playground in Lara · Basic info

Allister Reserve is a public playground in Lara, in the Greater Geelong City area. Norseman Reserve is another option about 250 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.2 km from the playground, and bus 12 stops at Kees Rd/Benjamin Dr, a 5-minute walk away.
