Bank Reserve

Playground in Lara · Basic info

Bank Reserve is a public playground in Lara, in the Greater Geelong City area. The Hovell Creek Trail is about 220 m away, and for food, Tuptim Thai is about 320 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 510 m from the playground, and bus 10 stops at Peak St/Station Lake Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
