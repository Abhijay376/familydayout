Lara Library

Library in Lara · Basic info

Lara Library is a library in Lara, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. The Centreway/Waverley Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 13 m away with a baby change table.
