Our data lists 1 playground in Dinner Plain, in the Alpine area.

Landmarks

Our data shows 4 public toilets in Dinner Plain. For walks and rides, Brabralung Trail, Dungey Track and Dungey Track (overgrown) cross Dinner Plain.

Getting around

Dinner Plain has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 96 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Omeo - Bright via Hotham Heights route stop in Dinner Plain. By car from Dinner Plain, Bright is about 62 minutes away and Wangaratta about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a skate park, the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo is about 44 minutes' drive from Dinner Plain. From Dinner Plain, the nearest places for kids by road are Little Higginbotham Park in Hotham Heights (13 min), Harrietville Museum in Harrietville (40 min), the playground at Tavare Park in Harrietville (40 min), Omeo Historical Park & Museum in Omeo (44 min) and the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo (44 min).

Best for

Free days on the Brabralung Trail and in Dinner Plain's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Dinner Plain, Hotham Heights has 1 playground. Dargo, next to Dinner Plain, has 1 playground.
