Landmarks

The biggest green space in Warrandyte South is Colman Park, covering about 6 hectares. Playgrounds in Warrandyte South include Colman Park Playground. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Warrandyte South. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Warrandyte South for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Warrandyte South. The closest to Warrandyte South is Ringwood East Railway Station (metro), about 5.4 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 364 stops in Warrandyte South. By car from Warrandyte South, Ringwood is about 8 minutes away, Lilydale about 15 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 34 minutes, without traffic.
