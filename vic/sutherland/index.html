Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Sutherland, in the Northern Grampians area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Sutherland is Swanwater Bushland Reserve, covering about 15 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Sutherland. The closest to Sutherland is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 76 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Sutherland, so most trips start by car. By car from Sutherland, Stawell is about 72 minutes away and Maryborough about 76 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Sutherland is St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud, about 11 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Donald BMX Track in Donald is about 30 minutes' drive from Sutherland. The closest cinema to Sutherland is Rex Theatre in Charlton, about 39 minutes' drive. From Sutherland, the nearest places for kids by road are Apex Park Playground in St Arnaud (10 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (11 min), Market Square Park Playground in St Arnaud (11 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (26 min), Donald BMX Track in Donald (30 min) and Rex Theatre in Charlton (39 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Sutherland.

Nearby suburbs

St Arnaud, next to Sutherland, has 4 playgrounds and a pool. Gooroc and Cope Cope also border Sutherland.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Sutherland, without traffic.
