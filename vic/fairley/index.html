Fairley is in the Gannawarra area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Lake Elizabeth Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Fairley, at about 121 hectares.

Getting around

Fairley has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 9.4 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Fairley, so most trips start by car. By car from Fairley, Swan Hill is about 44 minutes away and Echuca about 88 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Fairley is Swimming Pool in Kerang, about 11 minutes' drive. For a library, Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang is about 11 minutes' drive from Fairley. For a skate park, Barham Skate Park in Koondrook is about 34 minutes' drive from Fairley. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Fairley, about 44 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Fairley are Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (11 min), Swimming Pool in Kerang (11 min), the playground at Lake Meran W.R in Lake Meran (25 min), the playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV) in Koondrook (33 min), Barham Branch Library in Koondrook (33 min) and Lake Boga Flying Boat Museum in Lake Boga (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Fairley.

Nearby suburbs

Kerang, next to Fairley, has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Bael Bael also borders Fairley.
