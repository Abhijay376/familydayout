Alfred Nicholas Memorial Gardens

Park in Sherbrooke · Official details

Alfred Nicholas Memorial Gardens is a park in Sherbrooke, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Nobles Lane/Sherbrooke Rd (bus) is a 9-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 47 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed on a lead. Opening hours: Daylight hours, seven days, except Extreme Fire Danger Rating or severe weather days.
