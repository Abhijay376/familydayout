George Tindale Memorial Gardens

Park in Sherbrooke · Official details

George Tindale Memorial Gardens is a park in Sherbrooke, in the Yarra Ranges Shire Council area, good for all ages. Braeside Ave/Sherbrooke Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 1 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed on a lead. Opening hours: Daylight hours, seven days, except Extreme Fire Danger or severe weather days.
