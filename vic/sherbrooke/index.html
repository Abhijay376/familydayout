Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Sherbrooke, in the Yarra Ranges area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Sherbrooke is Alfred Nicholas Memorial Gardens, covering about 13 hectares. George Tindale Memorial Gardens is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Sherbrooke include O'Donohue Picnic Ground. Our data shows 8 public toilets in Sherbrooke. For walks and rides, Country (ngurrak barring | RidgeWalk), Activism (ngurrak barring | RidgeWalk) and Dandenong Ranges Tourist Track cross Sherbrooke. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Sherbrooke for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Sherbrooke. The closest to Sherbrooke is Belgrave Railway Station (metro), about 3.3 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 663 and 694 stop in Sherbrooke. By car from Sherbrooke, Ringwood is about 26 minutes away, Lilydale about 27 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 44 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Monbulk Aquatic Centre in Monbulk is about 7 minutes' drive from Sherbrooke. The closest library to Sherbrooke is Belgrave Library in Belgrave, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Sherbrooke is Edithvale Beach in Edithvale, about 39 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Sherbrooke is Monbulk Skate & BMX Park in Monbulk, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Sherbrooke is Cameo Belgrave in Belgrave, about 6 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Little Rascals Play Centre in Ferntree Gully is about 11 minutes' drive from Sherbrooke. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Sherbrooke is Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale, about 33 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Ringwood, about 26 minutes from Sherbrooke by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Free days on the Country (ngurrak barring | RidgeWalk) and in Sherbrooke's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Sherbrooke, Belgrave has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Sherbrooke, Olinda has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Sherbrooke, Sassafras has 3 playgrounds. Next door to Sherbrooke, Tecoma has 1 playground. Selby, Kallista and Ferny Creek also border Sherbrooke.
