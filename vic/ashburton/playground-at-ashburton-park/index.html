Playground at Ashburton Park

Playground in Ashburton · Basic info

Playground at Ashburton Park in Ashburton is a public playground in the Boroondara City area. Ashburton Pool and Recreation Centre is about 490 m away, and Oak Room is about 360 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 620 m away, and bus 734 stops at Stocks Ave/High St, a 4-minute walk away.
