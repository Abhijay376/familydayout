Alamein Community Garden Playground

Playground in Ashburton · Basic info

Alamein Community Garden Playground in Ashburton is a public playground in the Boroondara City area. The Anniversary Trail is about 100 m away, and Ashburton Pool and Recreation Centre is about 410 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 640 m from the playground, and trains stop at Alamein Railway Station, a 1-minute walk away.
