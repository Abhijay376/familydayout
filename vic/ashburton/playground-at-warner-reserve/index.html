Playground at Warner Reserve

Playground in Ashburton · Basic info

Playground at Warner Reserve in Ashburton is a public playground in the Boroondara City area. The Anniversary Trail is about 400 m away, and Ashburton Pool and Recreation Centre is about 80 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 760 m from the playground, and bus 734 stops at Samarinda Ave/High St, a 5-minute walk away.
