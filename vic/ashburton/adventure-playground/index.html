Adventure Playground

Playground in Ashburton · Basic info

Adventure Playground in Ashburton is a public playground in the Boroondara City area. The Gardiners Creek Trail passes about 20 m away, and Ashburton Pool and Recreation Centre is about 510 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 130 m away, and bus 903 stops at CooRondo Home/Warrigal Rd, a 9-minute walk away.
