Corunnun is in the Colac Otway area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Corunnun is Lake Purdiguluc Lake Reserve, covering about 88 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Corunnun. The closest to Corunnun is Colac Railway Station (V/Line), about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Colac - Alvie via Warrion route stop in Corunnun. By car from Corunnun, Colac is about 19 minutes away, Geelong about 84 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Corunnun is Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Corunnun is the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Corunnun is Colac Cinemas in Colac, about 20 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac is about 20 minutes' drive from Corunnun. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Corunnun, about 19 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Corunnun, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Red Rock Scenic Reserve in Alvie (15 min), the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (18 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (20 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (20 min), Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (22 min) and the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (24 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Corunnun.

Nearby suburbs

Alvie, next to Corunnun, has 1 playground. Larpent, Cororooke and Coragulac also border Corunnun.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Corunnun, without traffic.
