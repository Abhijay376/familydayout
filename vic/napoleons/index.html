Napoleons is in the Golden Plains area, and our data lists 2 playgrounds and 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Yarrowee Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Napoleons, at about 2 hectares. Playgrounds around Napoleons include Napoleons Recreation Reserve Playground. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Napoleons.

Getting around

Napoleons has no train station; Ballarat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Ballarat - Rokewood via Ross Creek route stop in Napoleons. By car from Napoleons, Ballarat is about 16 minutes away, Daylesford about 55 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Buninyong Swimming Pool in Buninyong is about 12 minutes' drive from Napoleons. For a library, Sebastopol Library in Sebastopol is about 11 minutes' drive from Napoleons. The closest skate park to Napoleons is Enfield Skate Park in Enfield, about 7 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Showbiz Cinemas Ballarat in Smythes Creek is about 15 minutes' drive from Napoleons. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 17 minutes' drive from Napoleons. For a zoo or wildlife park, Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East is about 22 minutes' drive from Napoleons. The nearest larger centre is Ballarat, about 16 minutes from Napoleons by car, with 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Napoleons are Enfield Skate Park in Enfield (7 min), Enfield Harrison Reserve Playground in Enfield (7 min), Buninyong Swimming Pool in Buninyong (12 min), the bmx track at Buninyong Recreation Park in Buninyong (12 min), Sebastopol Library in Sebastopol (11 min) and L Building: E J Barker Library in Mount Helen (17 min).

Best for
