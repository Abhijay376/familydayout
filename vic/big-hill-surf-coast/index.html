Big Hill (Surf Coast) is in the Surf Coast area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Big Hill (Surf Coast), so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids. Big Hill (Surf Coast) does have a beach.

Landmarks

In Big Hill (Surf Coast) you'll also find Reedy Creek Beach.

Getting around

There's no train station in Big Hill (Surf Coast). The closest to Big Hill (Surf Coast) is Winchelsea Railway Station (V/Line), about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Big Hill (Surf Coast), so most trips start by car. By car from Big Hill (Surf Coast), Torquay is about 45 minutes away, Colac about 60 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Lorne Sea Baths in Lorne is about 10 minutes' drive from Big Hill (Surf Coast). For a skate park, Kay Langan Skate Park in Lorne is about 10 minutes' drive from Big Hill (Surf Coast). For a cinema, Lorne Theatre in Lorne is about 10 minutes' drive from Big Hill (Surf Coast). For a zoo or wildlife park, Live Wire Park in Lorne is about 12 minutes' drive from Big Hill (Surf Coast). Torquay is the closest bigger centre to Big Hill (Surf Coast), about 45 minutes away by car, and has 15 playgrounds, a library and a beach. From Big Hill (Surf Coast), the nearest places for kids by road are Reedy Creek Beach in Big Hill (5 min), Fairhaven in Eastern View (8 min), Lorne Theatre in Lorne (10 min), Lorne Sea Baths in Lorne (10 min), Kay Langan Skate Park in Lorne (10 min) and the playground at Lorne - Queenscliff Coastal Reserve in Lorne (10 min).

Best for
