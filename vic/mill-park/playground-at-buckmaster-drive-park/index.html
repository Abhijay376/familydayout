Playground at Buckmaster Drive Park

Playground in Mill Park · Basic info

Playground at Buckmaster Drive Park is a public playground in Mill Park, in the Whittlesea City area. Playground at Statesman Crescent Park is another option about 460 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 710 m from the playground, and buses 386 and 387 stop at Childs Rd/Plenty Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
