Mill Park Recreation Reserve

Park in Mill Park · Basic info

Mill Park Recreation Reserve is a park in Mill Park, in the Whittlesea City Council area, good for all ages. Border Dr/Manning Clark Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 574 m away.
