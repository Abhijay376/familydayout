Playground at Coventry Park

Playground in Mill Park · Basic info

This is a public playground in Mill Park. Aquastyle Swim School is about 320 m away, and for food, RAPT Korean Chicken & Burger is about 260 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 300 m from the playground, and buses 382 and 570 stop at Plenty Rd/McKimmies Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
