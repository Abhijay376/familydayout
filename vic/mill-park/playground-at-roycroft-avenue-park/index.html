Playground at Roycroft Avenue Park

Playground in Mill Park · Basic info

Playground at Roycroft Avenue Park in Mill Park is a public playground in the Whittlesea City area. The Yan Yean Pipe Track is about 40 m away, and for food, Amalfi Pizza & Pasta is about 470 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 910 m from the playground, and bus 564 stops at Wenden Rd/Roycroft Ave, a 1-minute walk away.
