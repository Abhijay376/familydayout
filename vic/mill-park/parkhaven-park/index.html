Parkhaven Park

Park in Mill Park · Basic info

Parkhaven Park is a park in Mill Park, in the Whittlesea City Council area, good for all ages. University Dr/Development Bvd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 489 m away with a baby change table.
