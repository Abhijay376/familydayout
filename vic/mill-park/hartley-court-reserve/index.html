Hartley Court Reserve

Park in Mill Park · Basic info

Hartley Court Reserve is a park in Mill Park, in the Whittlesea City Council area, good for all ages. Prince Of Wales Ave/Childs Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1210 m away with a baby change table.
