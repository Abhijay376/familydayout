Playground at Doctor Harry Jenkins Reserve

Playground in Mill Park · Basic info

Playground at Doctor Harry Jenkins Reserve in Mill Park is a public playground in the Whittlesea City area. Aquastyle Swim School is about 440 m away, and for food, Italia Pizza is about 460 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 620 m from the playground, and bus 566 stops at Dr Harry Jenkins Reserve/Childs Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
