Playground on Blamey Avenue

Playground in Mill Park · Basic info

Playground on Blamey Avenue in Mill Park is a public playground in the Whittlesea City area. Aquastyle Swim School is about 30 m away, and for food, RAPT Korean Chicken & Burger is about 80 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 120 m from the playground, and buses 383, 386, 387 and 566 stop at Mayfield Dr/Plenty Rd, a 7-minute walk away.
