Playground at Redleap Reserve

Playground in Mill Park · Basic info

Playground at Redleap Reserve in Mill Park is a public playground in the Whittlesea City area. The Yan Yean Pipe Track passes about 350 m away, and The Golden Horseshoe Fish and Chips is about 430 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 490 m away, and bus 564 stops at Moorhead Dr/Redleap Ave, a 2-minute walk away.
