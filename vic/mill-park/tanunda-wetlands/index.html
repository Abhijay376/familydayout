Tanunda Wetlands

Park in Mill Park · Basic info

Tanunda Wetlands is a park in Mill Park, in the Whittlesea City Council area, good for all ages. Janefield Dr/Ormond Bvd (bus) is a 23-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1215 m away with a baby change table.
