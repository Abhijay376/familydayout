Playground at Hinkler Park

Playground in Mill Park · Basic info

Playground at Hinkler Park is a public playground in Mill Park, in the Whittlesea City area. The Yan Yean Pipe Track passes about 30 m away, and Playground at Redleap Reserve is another option about 440 m away. There's a public toilet about 960 m away, and bus 564 stops at Hurlstone Cres/Redleap Ave, a 4-minute walk away.
