Playground at Mill Park Recreation Reserve

Playground in Mill Park · Basic info

This is a public playground in Mill Park. The South Morang Rail Trail is about 330 m away, and Mill Park Leisure Centre is about 440 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 550 m from the playground, and bus 569 stops at Romano Ave/Manning Clark Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
