Clonbinane is in the Mitchell area, and our data lists 11 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Kinglake National Park is the largest mapped park in Clonbinane, at about 23,212 hectares. Mount Disappointment State Forest and Wandong Regional Park are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Clonbinane include Regular Camp, Andersons Garden and Strath Creek Falls. Our data shows 6 public toilets in Clonbinane. Picnic areas in Clonbinane include Andersons Garden, Number One Camp Horseyards and Regular Camp. The Sunday Creek Walk, Sunday Creek Reservoir Walk and Strath Creek Falls Walk all pass through Clonbinane.

Getting around

Clonbinane has no train station; Wandong Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 6.4 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Clonbinane, so most trips start by car. By car from Clonbinane, Seymour is about 39 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 55 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 73 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore is about 27 minutes' drive from Clonbinane. For a library, Broadford Library in Broadford is about 23 minutes' drive from Clonbinane. The closest skate park to Clonbinane is the skate park at Wandong and Heathcote Junction Memorial Park in Wandong, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Clonbinane is United Cinemas in Craigieburn, about 45 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Timezone in Craigieburn is about 45 minutes' drive from Clonbinane. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Clonbinane is Funfields in Whittlesea, about 40 minutes' drive. Seymour is the closest bigger centre to Clonbinane, about 39 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library.

Best for

Free days on the Sunday Creek Walk and in Clonbinane's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Kilmore, next to Clonbinane, has 8 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Clonbinane, Broadford has 6 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Clonbinane, Whittlesea has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a zoo or wildlife park. Next door to Clonbinane, Waterford Park has 1 playground. Wandong, Kilmore East, Upper Plenty and Sunday Creek also border Clonbinane.
