Cambrian way playground

Playground in Melton West · Basic info

Cambrian way playground is a public playground in Melton West, in the Melton City area. Older kids have a skate park about 20 m away, and Shalimar Place Reserve Playground is another option about 390 m away. There's a public toilet about 70 m away, and bus 457 stops at Cambrian Way Preschool/54 Cambrian Way, a 2-minute walk away.
