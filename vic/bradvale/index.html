Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bradvale in the Corangamite area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bradvale. The closest to Bradvale is Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line), about 41 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bradvale, so most trips start by car. By car from Bradvale, Ballarat is about 61 minutes away, Ararat about 75 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bradvale is Lismore Pool in Lismore, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest library to Bradvale is Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum, about 35 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Linton Skate Park in Linton is about 31 minutes' drive from Bradvale. From Bradvale, the nearest places for kids by road are Lismore Pool in Lismore (31 min), Cape Clear & District Lions Park Playground in Cape Clear (27 min), Linton Skate Park in Linton (31 min), Linton BMX park in Linton (31 min), Linton Recreation Reserve Playground in Linton (31 min) and Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bradvale.
