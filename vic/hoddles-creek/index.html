Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Hoddles Creek, in the Yarra Ranges area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Hoddles Creek is Beenak Nature Conservation Area, covering about 126 hectares. Lusatia Park Road Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Hoddles Creek has no train station; Nar Nar Goon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Hoddles Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Hoddles Creek, Healesville is about 25 minutes away, Lilydale about 29 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 70 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Seville Water Play Park in Seville is about 16 minutes' drive from Hoddles Creek. For a library, Yarra Junction public library in Yarra Junction is about 11 minutes' drive from Hoddles Creek. The closest skate park to Hoddles Creek is the skate park at Yarra Junction Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Yarra Junction, about 11 minutes' drive. For a cinema, The Memo, Healesville in Healesville is about 25 minutes' drive from Hoddles Creek. The closest indoor play centre to Hoddles Creek is PlayPlace in Mooroolbark, about 35 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Lyre Bird enclosure in Badger Creek is about 21 minutes' drive from Hoddles Creek. The nearest larger centre is Healesville, about 25 minutes from Hoddles Creek by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Hoddles Creek are the playground at Yarra Junction Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Yarra Junction (11 min), Yarra Junction public library in Yarra Junction (11 min), the skate park at Yarra Junction Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Yarra Junction (11 min), the playground at Symes Road Park in Woori Yallock (10 min), Street Library in Millgrove (16 min) and Seville Water Play Park in Seville (16 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Hoddles Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Hoddles Creek, Woori Yallock has 1 playground. Gladysdale and Beenak also border Hoddles Creek.
