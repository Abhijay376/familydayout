Yallambie is in the Banyule area, and our data lists 6 playgrounds and 20 parks and reserves in the suburb. On top of that, Yallambie has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Yallambie is Yallambie Park, covering about 4 hectares. Coleen Reserve and Arthur Streeton Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Yallambie include Red Gum Reserve, Barron Way Reserve and Streamline Reserve. Playgrounds in Yallambie include the playground at Coleen Reserve, the playground at Yallambie Park and the playground at Yallambie Park. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Yallambie. Places to visit in Yallambie include Royal Australian Corps of Signals Museum. For walks and rides, Plenty River Trail, Banyule LBN7 and Banyule LBN6 cross Yallambie. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Yallambie for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Yallambie. The closest to Yallambie is Montmorency Railway Station (metro), about 2.0 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 513, 514 and 517 stop in Yallambie. By car from Yallambie, Box Hill is about 18 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 22 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 24 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Yarra Plenty Indoor Pool in Greensborough is about 3 minutes' drive from Yallambie. The closest library to Yallambie is Rosanna Library in Rosanna, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Yallambie is Horse Arena in Abbotsford, about 22 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Greensborough Skate Park in Greensborough is about 6 minutes' drive from Yallambie. For a cinema, Hoyts in Greensborough is about 6 minutes' drive from Yallambie. The closest indoor play centre to Yallambie is Rare Bears Indoor Play Centre in Eltham, about 7 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bundoora Park Farm in Bundoora is about 15 minutes' drive from Yallambie.

Best for

Younger kids at Yallambie's 6 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Yallambie. Free days on the Plenty River Trail and in Yallambie's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Greensborough, next to Yallambie, has 35 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Rosanna, next to Yallambie, has 9 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Yallambie, Montmorency has 10 playgrounds. Viewbank, next to Yallambie, has 8 playgrounds. Macleod and Lower Plenty also border Yallambie.
