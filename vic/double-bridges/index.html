Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Double Bridges, in the East Gippsland area.

Landmarks

Tambo River Reference Area is the largest mapped park in Double Bridges, at about 482 hectares.

Getting around

Double Bridges has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 37 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Double Bridges, so most trips start by car. By car from Double Bridges, Bairnsdale is about 41 minutes away and Lakes Entrance about 52 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Double Bridges is Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale, about 41 minutes' drive. For a library, Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale is about 39 minutes' drive from Double Bridges. The closest beach to Double Bridges is 90 Mile Beach in Boole Poole, about 44 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Double Bridges is Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale, about 39 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Double Bridges is Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow, about 39 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bairnsdale, about 41 minutes from Double Bridges by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Double Bridges, the nearest places for kids by road are Bruthen Gallery in Bruthen (17 min), the playground at Howitt Park in Wy Yung (37 min), Howitt Park in Wy Yung (38 min), Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale (39 min), Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale (39 min) and Krowathunkoolong Keeping Place in Bairnsdale (40 min).
