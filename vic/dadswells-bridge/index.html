Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Dadswells Bridge in the Horsham area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Dadswells Bridge. The Grampians Peaks Trail passes through Dadswells Bridge.

Getting around

There's no train station in Dadswells Bridge. The closest to Dadswells Bridge is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 55 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Dadswells Bridge. By car from Dadswells Bridge, Stawell is about 25 minutes away and Horsham about 30 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell is about 23 minutes' drive from Dadswells Bridge. For a beach, Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans is about 42 minutes' drive from Dadswells Bridge. The closest skate park to Dadswells Bridge is the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell, about 25 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Centre Cinemas in Horsham is about 30 minutes' drive from Dadswells Bridge. For a zoo or wildlife park, Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell is about 27 minutes' drive from Dadswells Bridge. The nearest larger centre is Stawell, about 25 minutes from Dadswells Bridge by car, with 6 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Dadswells Bridge are Glenorchy Playground in Glenorchy (21 min), the playground at Federation Park in Stawell (22 min), Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell (23 min), the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell (25 min), Railway Station Art and Crafts Gallery in Stawell (25 min) and Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell (27 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Dadswells Bridge on the Grampians Peaks Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Glenorchy, next to Dadswells Bridge, has 1 playground. Roses Gap, Ledcourt and Laharum also border Dadswells Bridge.
