The Heart in the Wellington area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in The Heart is Heart Morass Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 375 hectares. Andrew Bay - Grebe Bay Gippsland Lakes Reserve and Sale Camping Reserve Gippsland Lakes Reserve are the next largest. The Heart also has The Waterhole Gippsland Lakes Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in The Heart. The closest to The Heart is Sale Railway Station (V/Line), about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside The Heart, so most trips start by car. By car from The Heart, Sale is about 18 minutes away and Traralgon about 60 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, AquaEnergy in Sale is about 16 minutes' drive from The Heart. The closest library to The Heart is Sale Library in Sale, about 18 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Sale Skate Park in Sale is about 19 minutes' drive from The Heart. The closest cinema to The Heart is Roulette Cinema in East Sale, about 13 minutes' drive. Sale is the closest bigger centre to The Heart, about 18 minutes away by car, and has 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from The Heart are Roulette Cinema in East Sale (13 min), Patten Street - Woodburne Drive Playground in Sale (15 min), Helen Crescent to Inglis Street Playground in Sale (15 min), AquaEnergy in Sale (16 min), Indoor pool in Sale (17 min) and Sale Historical Museum in Sale (18 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from The Heart.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to The Heart, Sale has 14 playgrounds, a pool and a library. East Sale, next to The Heart, has a cinema. Dutson, Cobains and Clydebank also border The Heart.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of The Heart, without traffic.
