Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Fulham in the Wellington area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Fulham. The closest to Fulham is Sale Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.8 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Fulham, so most trips start by car. By car from Fulham, Sale is about 9 minutes away, Traralgon about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Indoor pool in Sale is about 9 minutes' drive from Fulham. For a library, Sale Library in Sale is about 7 minutes' drive from Fulham. For a beach, 90 Mile Beach in Seaspray is about 44 minutes' drive from Fulham. The closest skate park to Fulham is Sale Skate Park in Sale, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Fulham is Sale Cinemas in Sale, about 7 minutes' drive. Sale is the closest bigger centre to Fulham, about 9 minutes away by car, and has 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Fulham, the nearest places for kids by road are Wurruk Playground in Wurruk (5 min), Sale Skate Park in Sale (7 min), Gippsland Art Gallery in Sale (7 min), Sale Library in Sale (7 min), Old train signals in Sale (7 min) and Sale Cinemas in Sale (7 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Fulham.

Nearby suburbs

Wurruk, next to Fulham, has 1 playground. Myrtlebank, Kilmany, Denison and Bundalaguah also border Fulham.
