Playground at Frederick Reserve

Playground in Yarraville · Basic info

Playground at Frederick Reserve is a public playground in Yarraville, in the Maribyrnong City area. The Westgate Trail is about 160 m away, and for food, Godfather’s Pizza is about 150 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 300 m from the playground, and bus 409 stops at Little Hyde St/Hyde St, a 3-minute walk away.
