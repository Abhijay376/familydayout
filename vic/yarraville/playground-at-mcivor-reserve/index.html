Playground at McIvor Reserve

Playground in Yarraville · Basic info

This is a public playground in Yarraville. The Federation Trail is about 360 m away, and Playground on Wembley Avenue is another option about 220 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.2 km from the playground, and bus 432 stops at Wembley Ave/Benbow St, a 4-minute walk away.
