McNish Reserve

Park in Yarraville · Official details

McNish Reserve is a park in Yarraville, in the Maribyrnong City Council area, good for all ages. Drew St/Freame St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 289 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is McNish Reserve?
McNish Reserve is in Yarraville, Victoria.
Is McNish Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to McNish Reserve?
Yes, dogs are allowed.
Are there toilets at McNish Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 289 m away, with a baby change table.
How do I get to McNish Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Drew St/Freame St (routes 431), a 4-minute walk.
