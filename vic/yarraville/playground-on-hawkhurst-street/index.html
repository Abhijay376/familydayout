Playground on Hawkhurst Street

Playground in Yarraville · Basic info

Playground on Hawkhurst Street in Yarraville is a public playground in the Maribyrnong City area. Older kids have a skate park about 330 m away, and Playground on Wembley Avenue is another option about 60 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 710 m from the playground, and bus 431 stops at Francis St/Freame St, a 3-minute walk away.
