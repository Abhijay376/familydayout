McIvor Reserve

Park in Yarraville · Official details

McIvor Reserve is a park in Yarraville, in the Maribyrnong City Council area, good for all ages. Bromyard St/Francis St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 1108 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is McIvor Reserve?
McIvor Reserve is in Yarraville, Victoria.
Is McIvor Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to McIvor Reserve?
Yes, dogs are allowed.
Are there toilets at McIvor Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 1108 m away, with a baby change table.
How do I get to McIvor Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Bromyard St/Francis St (routes 431), a 4-minute walk.
