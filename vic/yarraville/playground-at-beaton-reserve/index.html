Playground at Beaton Reserve

Playground in Yarraville · Basic info

This is a public playground in Yarraville. Older kids have a skate park about 240 m away, and Subway is about 310 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 450 m away, and buses 409 and 432 stop at Simpson St/Fehon St, a 2-minute walk away.
