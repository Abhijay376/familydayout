Playground at Sandford Grove Reserve

Playground in Yarraville · Basic info

Playground at Sandford Grove Reserve in Yarraville is a public playground in the Maribyrnong City area. The Westgate Trail passes about 120 m away, and Boma Coffee is about 110 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 480 m away, and bus 409 stops at 204 Hyde St, a 3-minute walk away.
