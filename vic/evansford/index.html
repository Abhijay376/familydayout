Evansford is in the Pyrenees area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Evansford Community Centre is the largest mapped park in Evansford, at about 1 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Evansford. The closest to Evansford is Talbot Railway Station (V/Line), about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Evansford, so most trips start by car. By car from Evansford, Maryborough is about 26 minutes away, Ballarat about 37 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot is about 11 minutes' drive from Evansford. The closest library to Evansford is Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Evansford is the skate park at Alexander Park in Learmonth, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Evansford is Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central, about 37 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 41 minutes' drive from Evansford. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Evansford is Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East, about 39 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 26 minutes from Evansford by car, with a library. The closest places for kids by road from Evansford are the playground at Toll Bar Park in Lexton (13 min), Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground in Talbot (11 min), Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (11 min), the skate park at Alexander Park in Learmonth (18 min), Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (27 min) and Lee Medlyn Bottle Museum in Clunes (24 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Evansford.

Nearby suburbs

Clunes, next to Evansford, has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Dunach, Caralulup and Burnbank also border Evansford.
