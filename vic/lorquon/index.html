Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Lorquon, in the Hindmarsh area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lorquon is Lorquon I64 Bushland Reserve, covering about 7 hectares. Lorquon I63 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lorquon. The closest to Lorquon is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 164 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lorquon, so most trips start by car. By car from Lorquon, Horsham is about 85 minutes away and Stawell about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Lorquon is Nhill Library in Nhill, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Lorquon is Nhill Skate Park in Nhill, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Lorquon are the playground at Wimmera River Heritage River in Jeparit (37 min), Fitness Course in Jeparit (38 min), Wimmera-Mallee Pioneer Museum in Jeparit (38 min), Nhill Library in Nhill (29 min), Gallery Central in Nhill (29 min) and Nhill Skate Park in Nhill (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lorquon.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Lorquon, Nhill has 3 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Lorquon, Jeparit has 3 playgrounds.
