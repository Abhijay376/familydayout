Playground on Girdlestone Street

Playground in Ararat · Basic info

Playground on Girdlestone Street is a public playground in Ararat, in the Ararat Rural City area. Ararat Library is about 520 m away, and Ararat Outdoor Pool is about 100 m away. There's a public toilet about 110 m away, and buses 1 and St Arnaud - Stawell via Ararat stop at Ararat Hospital/Girdlestone St, a 4-minute walk away.
