Murrindal is in the East Gippsland area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Murrindal and Lilly Pilly Cave Reserve is the largest mapped park in Murrindal, at about 95 hectares. The Pyramids Cave Reserve and Anticline Cave Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Murrindal. The closest to Murrindal is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 69 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Murrindal, so most trips start by car. By car from Murrindal, Lakes Entrance is about 72 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 87 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Buchan: Natural Spring in Buchan is about 18 minutes' drive from Murrindal. From Murrindal, the nearest places for kids by road are Buchan: Natural Spring in Buchan (18 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Murrindal.
