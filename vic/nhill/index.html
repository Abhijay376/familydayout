Nhill is in the Hindmarsh area, and our data lists 3 playgrounds and 23 parks and reserves in the suburb. On top of that, Nhill has a library, 2 museums or galleries and a skate park.

Landmarks

Unnamed P0203 Private Nature Reserve is the largest mapped park in Nhill, at about 308 hectares. Boyeo Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting) and Little Desert Nature Lodge are the next largest. Nhill also has Goldsworthy Park, Rotary Plantation and Boyeo Flora Reserve. Playgrounds in Nhill include Nhill Playground and the playground at Goldsworthy Park. Our data shows 15 public toilets in Nhill. In Nhill you'll also find Gallery Central, Wagonin and Nhill Library. For older kids in Nhill there's Nhill Skate Park. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Nhill for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Nhill. The closest to Nhill is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 155 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Horsham - Kaniva via Dimboola route stop in Nhill. V/Line coaches stop in Nhill. By car from Nhill, Horsham is about 59 minutes away and Stawell about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Dimboola Swimming Pool in Dimboola is about 31 minutes' drive from Nhill. The nearest larger centre is Horsham, about 59 minutes from Nhill by car, with 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Nhill's 3 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the library and museum or gallery in Nhill. Teens who skate or ride, with Nhill Skate Park in Nhill.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Nhill, Kaniva has 1 playground and a library. Lorquon, Little Desert, Lawloit and Kiata also border Nhill.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Nhill, without traffic.
