Bridge Creek in the Mansfield area has 8 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Mount Samaria State Park is the largest mapped park in Bridge Creek, at about 7,487 hectares. Hat Hill Flora and Fauna Reserve and Blue Range Reference Area are the next largest. Bridge Creek also has Blue Range Creek, Toombullup State Forest and Toombullup State Forest. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Bridge Creek. Picnic areas in Bridge Creek include Blue Range Creek. The Mansfield - Mt Samaria Loop, Mount Samaria Track and Wileman Track all pass through Bridge Creek.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bridge Creek. The closest to Bridge Creek is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 48 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bridge Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Bridge Creek, Mansfield is about 12 minutes away, Benalla about 56 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

The closest pool or water play area to Bridge Creek is Mansfield Outdoor Swimming Pool in Mansfield, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest library to Bridge Creek is Mansfield Library in Mansfield, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Bridge Creek is the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Bridge Creek is Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Bridge Creek is Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield, about 22 minutes' drive. Mansfield is the closest bigger centre to Bridge Creek, about 12 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Bridge Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Mansfield Botanic Park in Mansfield (11 min), Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield (12 min), Mansfield Library in Mansfield (12 min), the playground at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (13 min), the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (13 min) and Mansfield Outdoor Swimming Pool in Mansfield (13 min).

Best for
