Edwards Reserve

Park in South Kingsville · Official details

Edwards Reserve is a park in South Kingsville, in the Hobsons Bay City Council area, good for all ages. Edwards Reserve/Brunel St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 246 m away.

Questions parents ask

Where is Edwards Reserve?
Edwards Reserve is in South Kingsville, Victoria.
Is Edwards Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Edwards Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 246 m away.
How do I get to Edwards Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Edwards Reserve/Brunel St (routes 432), a 4-minute walk.
