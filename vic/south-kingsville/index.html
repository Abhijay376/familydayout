South Kingsville is in the Hobsons Bay area, and our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in South Kingsville is Edwards Reserve, covering about 3 hectares. Playgrounds in South Kingsville include the playground at Edwards Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet and 2 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in South Kingsville. Our data lists 3 cafes and family restaurants in South Kingsville for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in South Kingsville. The closest to South Kingsville is Spotswood Railway Station (metro), about 1.3 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 432 stops in South Kingsville. By car from South Kingsville, Sunshine is about 14 minutes away, Werribee about 23 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 15 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Paul Sadler Swimland Westgate in Altona North is about 6 minutes' drive from South Kingsville. The closest library to South Kingsville is Newport Library in Newport, about 4 minutes' drive. The closest beach to South Kingsville is Williamstown Beach in Williamstown, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to South Kingsville is Sk8er Extreme Skate Park in Yarraville, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to South Kingsville is Sun Theatre in Yarraville, about 6 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Epic Arena in Newport is about 4 minutes' drive from South Kingsville. The closest zoo or wildlife park to South Kingsville is Skyline Precinct Melbourne in South Wharf, about 11 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Sunshine, about 14 minutes from South Kingsville by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from South Kingsville are the playground at Ducrow Reserve in Newport (2 min), Angela Sparks Gallery in Spotswood (4 min), Industrial Art Sign Co in Spotswood (4 min), Epic Arena in Newport (4 min), Newport Library in Newport (4 min) and Sk8er Extreme Skate Park in Yarraville (5 min).
