Wartook is in the Northern Grampians area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Wartook, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 3 public toilets in Wartook. For walks and rides, Grampians Peaks Trail crosses Wartook.

Getting around

Wartook has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 60 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wartook, so most trips start by car. By car from Wartook, Horsham is about 35 minutes away and Stawell about 45 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Halls Gap Swimming Pool in Halls Gap is about 26 minutes' drive from Wartook. For a skate park, Velodrome in Horsham is about 33 minutes' drive from Wartook. For a cinema, Centre Cinemas in Horsham is about 34 minutes' drive from Wartook. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Wartook is Halls Gap Zoo in Halls Gap, about 31 minutes' drive. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to Wartook, about 35 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Wartook are Halls Gap Swimming Pool in Halls Gap (26 min), the playground at Halls Gap Picnic Reserve in Halls Gap (26 min), Brambuk Cultural Centre in Halls Gap (30 min), Halls Gap Zoo in Halls Gap (31 min), MOCO Gallery in Halls Gap (29 min) and Glenorchy Playground in Glenorchy (41 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Wartook on the Grampians Peaks Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Laharum, Dadswells Bridge and Brimpaen also border Wartook.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Wartook, without traffic.
