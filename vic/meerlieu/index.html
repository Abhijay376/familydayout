Our data lists 6 parks and reserves in Meerlieu, in the Wellington area.

Landmarks

Victoria Lagoon Gippsland Lakes Reserve is the largest mapped park in Meerlieu, at about 452 hectares. Salt Lake - Backwater Morass Gippsland Lakes Reserve and Red Morass Gippsland Lakes Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Meerlieu include Morley Swamp Gippsland Lakes Reserve, Roseneath Peninsula (1) Gippsland Lakes Reserve and Roseneath Peninsula (2) Gippsland Lakes Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Meerlieu.

Getting around

There's no train station in Meerlieu. The closest to Meerlieu is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Meerlieu, so most trips start by car. By car from Meerlieu, Bairnsdale is about 28 minutes away and Sale about 38 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford is about 25 minutes' drive from Meerlieu. The closest library to Meerlieu is Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale, about 29 minutes' drive. For a beach, Boyfriend Beach in Forge Creek is about 38 minutes' drive from Meerlieu. The closest skate park to Meerlieu is Skate Park in Stratford, about 24 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale is about 29 minutes' drive from Meerlieu. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow is about 35 minutes' drive from Meerlieu. Bairnsdale is the closest bigger centre to Meerlieu, about 28 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Meerlieu, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Amy Shand Park in Lindenow (31 min), Skate Park in Stratford (24 min), Market Reserve Playground in Stratford (25 min), Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford (25 min), Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale (28 min) and Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale (29 min).
