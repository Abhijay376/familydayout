Clayton Plaza

Shopping centre in Clayton · Basic info

Clayton Plaza is a shopping centre in Clayton, in the Monash City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Clayton SC/Clayton Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 37 m away with a baby change table.
