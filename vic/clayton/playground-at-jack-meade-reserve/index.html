Playground at Jack Meade Reserve

Playground in Clayton · Basic info

Playground at Jack Meade Reserve in Clayton is a public playground in the Monash City area. The Djerring Trail passes about 40 m away, and Shinwari Inn is about 490 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 590 m away, and 5 bus routes stop at Clayton Station/Clayton Rd, a 7-minute walk away.
