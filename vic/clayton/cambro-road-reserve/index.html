Cambro Road Reserve

Park in Clayton · Basic info

Cambro Road Reserve is a park in Clayton, in the Monash City Council area, good for all ages. Centre for Nanofabrication/Wellington Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 739 m away.
