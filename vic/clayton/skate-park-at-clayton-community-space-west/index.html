Skate park at Clayton Community Space West

Skate, BMX or pump track in Clayton · Basic info

Skate park at Clayton Community Space West is a skate, bmx or pump track in Clayton, in the Monash City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Clayton Railway Station (train) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 160 m away.
