Playground at Evelyn Street Reserve

Playground in Clayton · Basic info

Playground at Evelyn Street Reserve is a public playground in Clayton, in the Monash City area. The Djerring Trail to Monash University is about 320 m away, and Zone Bowling is about 590 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.0 km from the playground, and bus 800 stops at Evelyn St/Princes Hwy, a 4-minute walk away.
