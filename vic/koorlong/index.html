Koorlong in the Mildura area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Koorlong Education Area is the largest mapped park in Koorlong, at about 659 hectares. Benetook Bushland Reserve is the next largest. For walks and rides, Former Morkalla-Redcliffs Railway crosses Koorlong.

Getting around

Koorlong has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 180 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Koorlong, so most trips start by car. By car from Koorlong, Mildura is about 19 minutes away and Swan Hill about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Splash Park in Mildura is about 15 minutes' drive from Koorlong. The closest library to Koorlong is Mildura Library in Mildura, about 18 minutes' drive. For a beach, Apex Beach in Mildura is about 27 minutes' drive from Koorlong. The closest skate park to Koorlong is Mildura Skate Park in Mildura, about 19 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Deakin Cinema Complex in Mildura is about 19 minutes' drive from Koorlong. For an indoor play centre, Laserpunk Laserforce in Mildura is about 19 minutes' drive from Koorlong. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Koorlong is Putt Putt Family Fun Centre in Mildura, about 19 minutes' drive. Mildura is the closest bigger centre to Koorlong, about 19 minutes away by car, and has 26 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Koorlong, the nearest places for kids by road are The RAAF Air Museum in Mildura (15 min), the playground at Hollywood Boulevard Drainage Reserve in Mildura (13 min), the playground at The Grange - Park in Mildura (14 min), Splash Park in Mildura (15 min), Irymple Swimming Pool in Irymple (17 min) and Mildura Library in Mildura (18 min).

Best for

Free days on the Former Morkalla-Redcliffs Railway and in Koorlong's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Mildura, next to Koorlong, has 26 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Red Cliffs, next to Koorlong, has 6 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Koorlong, Irymple has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Merbein South, Carwarp, Cardross and Birdwoodton also border Koorlong.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Koorlong, without traffic.
