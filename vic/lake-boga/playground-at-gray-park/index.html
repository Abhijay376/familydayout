Playground at Gray Park

Playground in Lake Boga · Basic info

This is a public playground in Lake Boga. Older kids have a skate park about 380 m away, and for food, Leila's Cafe and Newsagency is about 260 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 70 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 1 minute from Lake Boga.
