Skate park on Cemetery Road

Skate, BMX or pump track in Lake Boga · Basic info

Skate park on Cemetery Road is a skate, bmx or pump track in Lake Boga, in the Swan Hill Rural City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Murray Valley Hwy (coach) is a 11-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 308 m away.
