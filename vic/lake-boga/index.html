Lake Boga in the Swan Hill area has 1 playground and 5 parks and reserves in our data. Lake Boga also has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lake Boga is Lake Mannaor Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 84 hectares. Lake Boga Lake Reserve and Lake Boga Foreshore Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Lake Boga include Gray Park and Lake Boga Community Park. Playgrounds in Lake Boga include the playground at Gray Park. Our data shows 8 public toilets in Lake Boga. In Lake Boga you'll also find Lake Boga Flying Boat Museum. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Lake Boga for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Lake Boga has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Swan Hill - Sea Lake via Ultima route stop in Lake Boga. V/Line coaches stop in Lake Boga. By car from Lake Boga, Swan Hill is about 15 minutes away and Echuca about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lake Boga is Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest library to Lake Boga is Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Lake Boga is the skate park at Riverside Park in Swan Hill, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Lake Boga is Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill, about 14 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 15 minutes from Lake Boga by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Lake Boga, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Guastalegname Park in Swan Hill (13 min), Swan Hill Regional Art Gallery in Swan Hill (13 min), the skate park at Riverside Park in Swan Hill (14 min), Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill (14 min), Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill (14 min) and Swan Hill Outdoor Pool in Swan Hill (14 min).
