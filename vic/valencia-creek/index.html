Valencia Creek in the Wellington area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Valencia Creek is Briagolong Bushland Reserve, covering about 10 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Valencia Creek include Valencia Creek Picnic Area. Picnic areas in Valencia Creek include Valencia Creek Picnic Area.

Getting around

Valencia Creek has no train station; Stratford Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Valencia Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Valencia Creek, Sale is about 35 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 71 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford is about 30 minutes' drive from Valencia Creek. The closest library to Valencia Creek is Sale Library in Sale, about 36 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Skate Park in Stratford is about 32 minutes' drive from Valencia Creek. The closest cinema to Valencia Creek is Former Drive-in site in Sale, about 35 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Sale, about 35 minutes from Valencia Creek by car, with 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Valencia Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are Boisdale Recreation Reserve Playground in Boisdale (10 min), Anzac Park Playground in Briagolong (18 min), Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford (30 min), Skate Park in Stratford (32 min), Former Drive-in site in Sale (35 min) and Sale Historical Museum in Sale (36 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Valencia Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Briagolong, next to Valencia Creek, has 2 playgrounds. Boisdale, next to Valencia Creek, has 1 playground. Bushy Park also borders Valencia Creek.
