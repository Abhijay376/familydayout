Anderson Park

Park in Hawthorn East · Basic info

Anderson Park is a park in Hawthorn East, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Constance St/Tooronga Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 154 m away with a baby change table.
