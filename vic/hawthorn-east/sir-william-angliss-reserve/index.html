Sir William Angliss Reserve

Park in Hawthorn East · Basic info

Sir William Angliss Reserve is a park in Hawthorn East, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Rathmines Rd/Burke Rd #66 (tram) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 100 m away.
