Gardiners Creek Reserve

Park in Hawthorn East · Basic info

Gardiners Creek Reserve is a park in Hawthorn East, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Tooronga SC/Tooronga Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 876 m away.
