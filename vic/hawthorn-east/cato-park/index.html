Cato Park

Park in Hawthorn East · Basic info

Cato Park is a park in Hawthorn East, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Buley St/Tooronga Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 554 m away with a baby change table.
