Playground at Victoria Road Reserve

Playground in Hawthorn East · Basic info

This is a public playground in Hawthorn East. For food, Pizza Art is about 370 m away, and Playground at Central Gardens is another option about 420 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 170 m from the playground, and bus 624 stops at Liddiard Rd/Auburn Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
