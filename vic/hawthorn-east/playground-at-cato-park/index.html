Playground at Cato Park

Playground in Hawthorn East · Basic info

Playground at Cato Park is a public playground in Hawthorn East, in the Boroondara City area. Hall St. Provider is about 340 m away if you need food, and Playground at Anderson Park is another option about 470 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 550 m away, and bus 624 stops at Buley St/Tooronga Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
