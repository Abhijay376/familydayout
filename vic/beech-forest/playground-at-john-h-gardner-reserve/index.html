Playground at John H Gardner Reserve

Playground in Beech Forest · Basic info

Playground at John H Gardner Reserve in Beech Forest is a public playground in the Colac Otway Shire area. The Old Beechy Rail Trail is about 20 m away, and Art Reach Studio is about 240 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground. It's easiest by car, about 1 minute from Beech Forest.
