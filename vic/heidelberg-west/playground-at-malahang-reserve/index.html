Playground at Malahang Reserve

Playground in Heidelberg West · Basic info

Playground at Malahang Reserve is a public playground in Heidelberg West, in the Banyule City area. The Banyule LBN3 passes about 60 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 70 m away. There's a public toilet about 80 m away, and buses 250, 350, 549 and 903 stop at Malahang Reserve/Oriel Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
