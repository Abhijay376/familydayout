BMX track at Malahang Reserve

Skate, BMX or pump track in Heidelberg West · Basic info

BMX track at Malahang Reserve is a skate, bmx or pump track in Heidelberg West, in the Banyule City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Malahang Reserve/Oriel Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 279 m away.
