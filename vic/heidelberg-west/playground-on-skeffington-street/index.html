Playground on Skeffington Street

Playground in Heidelberg West · Basic info

Playground on Skeffington Street is a public playground in Heidelberg West, in the Banyule City area. The Banyule LBN3 is about 130 m away, and for food, Kebabs On Bell is about 240 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 380 m from the playground, and buses 513 and 514 stop at Oriel Rd/Bell St, a 2-minute walk away.
