Western Port Intertidal Coastal Reserve

Park in Grantville · Basic info

Western Port Intertidal Coastal Reserve is a park in Grantville, in the Bass Coast Shire Council area, good for all ages. Grantville-Glen Alvie Rd/Bass Hwy (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 586 m away.
