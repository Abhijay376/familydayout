Playground on Pier Road

Playground in Grantville · Basic info

Playground on Pier Road in Grantville is a public playground in the Bass Coast Shire area. Euro Bites Greek Eatery is about 150 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and coaches stop at Pier Rd/Bass Hwy, a 1-minute walk away.
