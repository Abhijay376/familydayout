Our data lists 1 playground and 5 parks and reserves in Grantville, in the Bass Coast area. Beyond the parks, Grantville has a zoo or wildlife park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Grantville is Western Port Intertidal Coastal Reserve, covering about 450 hectares. Grantville Bushland Reserve and Western Port Foreshore Reserve Grantville Coastal Reserve (COM) are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Grantville include Corinella Bushland Reserve and Colbert Creek Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Grantville. Places to visit in Grantville include Maru Koala & Animal Park. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Grantville for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Grantville has no train station; Stony Point Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Cowes - Fountain Gate via Anderson and Coronet Bay - Grantville via Corinella routes stop in Grantville. V/Line coaches stop in Grantville. By car from Grantville, Wonthaggi is about 30 minutes away, Pakenham about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 82 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Grantville is Koo Wee Rup Swimming Pool in Koo Wee Rup, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest library to Grantville is San Remo Library in San Remo, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Grantville is Corinella Beach in Corinella, about 16 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Wonthaggi Railway Historic Reserve in Kilcunda is about 19 minutes' drive from Grantville. For a cinema, Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi is about 29 minutes' drive from Grantville. The closest indoor play centre to Grantville is Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham, about 38 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wonthaggi, about 30 minutes from Grantville by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Grantville.

Nearby suburbs

Corinella, next to Grantville, has 1 playground and a beach. Next door to Grantville, Pioneer Bay has 1 playground. Kernot, Glen Forbes, Almurta and Adams Estate also border Grantville.
