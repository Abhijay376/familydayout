Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Barramunga, in the Colac Otway area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Barramunga is Olangolah Creek Reference Area, covering about 121 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Barramunga include Stevensons Falls Campground and Stevensons Falls Picnic Area. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Barramunga. Picnic areas in Barramunga include Stevensons Falls Picnic Area. For camping, there's Stevensons Falls Campground. The Red Carpet Trail Middle Trail, Barlidjaru Trail and Baridjaru all pass through Barramunga.

Getting around

Barramunga has no train station; Colac Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Colac - Marengo via Apollo Bay route stop in Barramunga. By car from Barramunga, Colac is about 41 minutes away, Torquay about 75 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Barramunga is Apollo Bay Community Pool in Apollo Bay, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest library to Barramunga is Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac, about 40 minutes' drive. For a beach, White Crest in Wongarra is about 31 minutes' drive from Barramunga. For a skate park, the skate park at Forrest Common in Forrest is about 7 minutes' drive from Barramunga. The closest cinema to Barramunga is Mechanics Hall in Apollo Bay, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Barramunga is Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac, about 40 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Barramunga is Skate Bowl in Apollo Bay, about 31 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 41 minutes from Barramunga by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play.

Best for
