Mirimbah in the Mansfield area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Mirimbah is Mount Russell Education Area, covering about 211 hectares. Mirimbah also has Carters Mill Camping Area. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Mirimbah. For camping, there's Carters Mill Camping Area. The Merrijig - Two Rivers Loop, Mirimbah Loop and Klingsporn Bridle Track all pass through Mirimbah.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mirimbah. The closest to Mirimbah is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 73 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mirimbah, so most trips start by car. By car from Mirimbah, Mansfield is about 28 minutes away and Benalla about 79 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mirimbah is Mansfield Outdoor Swimming Pool in Mansfield, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest library to Mirimbah is Mansfield Library in Mansfield, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Mirimbah is the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Mirimbah is Mt Buller Cinema in Mount Buller, about 20 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Buller Air Zone in Mount Buller is about 20 minutes' drive from Mirimbah. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Mirimbah is Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield, about 30 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Mansfield, about 28 minutes from Mirimbah by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Mirimbah, the nearest places for kids by road are Mt Buller Cinema in Mount Buller (20 min), National Alpine Museum of Australia in Mount Buller (20 min), Buller Air Zone in Mount Buller (20 min), Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield (27 min), Mansfield Library in Mansfield (28 min) and the playground at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (28 min).
