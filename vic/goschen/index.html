Goschen in the Swan Hill area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Goschen Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Goschen, at about 20 hectares. Magpie Tank Bushland Reserve and Company Dam Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Goschen also has Clear Tank Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Goschen has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Goschen, so most trips start by car. By car from Goschen, Swan Hill is about 22 minutes away and Echuca about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Goschen is Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill, about 21 minutes' drive. For a library, Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill is about 22 minutes' drive from Goschen. The closest skate park to Goschen is the skate park at Riverside Park in Swan Hill, about 21 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill is about 21 minutes' drive from Goschen. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Goschen, about 22 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Goschen, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Gray Park in Lake Boga (13 min), Lake Boga Flying Boat Museum in Lake Boga (15 min), the playground at Guastalegname Park in Swan Hill (19 min), Swan Hill Regional Art Gallery in Swan Hill (20 min), the skate park at Riverside Park in Swan Hill (21 min) and Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Goschen.

Nearby suburbs

Swan Hill, next to Goschen, has 9 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Meatian, Lalbert, Kunat and Castle Donnington also border Goschen.
