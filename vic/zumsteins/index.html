Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Zumsteins in the Northern Grampians area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 11 public toilets in Zumsteins. For walks and rides, Mackenzie River Walk, Mackenzie Falls Lookout Walk and Mackenzie Falls Lookout cross Zumsteins.

Getting around

There's no train station in Zumsteins. The closest to Zumsteins is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 53 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Zumsteins, so most trips start by car. By car from Zumsteins, Horsham is about 42 minutes away and Stawell about 42 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Zumsteins is Halls Gap Swimming Pool in Halls Gap, about 19 minutes' drive. For a beach, Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans is about 39 minutes' drive from Zumsteins. The closest skate park to Zumsteins is the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell, about 41 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Halls Gap Zoo in Halls Gap is about 25 minutes' drive from Zumsteins. The nearest larger centre is Horsham, about 42 minutes from Zumsteins by car, with 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Zumsteins are Halls Gap Swimming Pool in Halls Gap (19 min), the playground at Halls Gap Picnic Reserve in Halls Gap (20 min), Brambuk Cultural Centre in Halls Gap (23 min), Halls Gap Zoo in Halls Gap (25 min), MOCO Gallery in Halls Gap (23 min) and Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans (39 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Zumsteins on the Mackenzie River Walk.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Zumsteins, Halls Gap has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a zoo or wildlife park. Cherrypool, Brimpaen and Bellfield also border Zumsteins.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Zumsteins, without traffic.
