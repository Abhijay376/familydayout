Glenlee is in the Hindmarsh area, and our data lists 5 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Glenlee is Glenlee Flora and Fauna Reserve, covering about 554 hectares. Big One Dam Bushland Reserve and Glenlee Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Glenlee also has Woorak Bushland Reserve and Ni Ni Wildlife Reserve (hunting).

Getting around

There's no train station in Glenlee. The closest to Glenlee is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 149 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glenlee, so most trips start by car. By car from Glenlee, Horsham is about 60 minutes away and Stawell about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Dimboola Swimming Pool in Dimboola is about 33 minutes' drive from Glenlee. For a library, Nhill Library in Nhill is about 24 minutes' drive from Glenlee. For a skate park, Nhill Skate Park in Nhill is about 25 minutes' drive from Glenlee. The nearest larger centre is Horsham, about 60 minutes from Glenlee by car, with 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Glenlee are the playground at Wimmera River Heritage River in Jeparit (23 min), Fitness Course in Jeparit (24 min), Nhill Library in Nhill (24 min), Wimmera-Mallee Pioneer Museum in Jeparit (24 min), Gallery Central in Nhill (24 min) and Nhill Skate Park in Nhill (25 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Glenlee.

Nearby suburbs

Nhill, next to Glenlee, has 3 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Jeparit, next to Glenlee, has 3 playgrounds. Gerang Gerung and Antwerp also border Glenlee.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Glenlee, without traffic.
