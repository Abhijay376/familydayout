Playground at Laura Court Reserve

Playground in Bayswater North · Basic info

Playground at Laura Court Reserve is a public playground in Bayswater North, in the Maroondah City area. The Dandenong Creek Trail is about 60 m away, and Playground at Canterbury Gardens is another option about 460 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 730 m from the playground, and bus 690 stops at Toolimerin Ave/Allambanan Dr, a 5-minute walk away.
