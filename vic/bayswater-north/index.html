Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bayswater North is Glen Park, covering about 4 hectares. Canterbury Gardens and Laura Court Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Bayswater North include Pipe Reserve, Pipe Reserve and Pipe Reserve. Playgrounds around Bayswater North include the playground at Glen Park, the playground at Canterbury Gardens and the playground at Laura Court Reserve. Our data shows 7 public toilets in Bayswater North. Canterbury Gardens Shopping Centre is the local shopping centre. The Dandenong Creek Trail, Bayswater-Lilydale Trail and Dorset Road Connection all pass through Bayswater North. Our data lists 10 cafes and family restaurants in Bayswater North for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bayswater North. The closest to Bayswater North is Bayswater Railway Station (metro), about 1.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 664, 679, 690, 737 and 967 stop in Bayswater North. By car from Bayswater North, Ringwood is about 8 minutes away, Lilydale about 15 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 35 minutes, without traffic.

For a pool or water play area, Croydon Memorial Pool in Croydon is about 6 minutes' drive from Bayswater North. The closest library to Bayswater North is Bayswater Library in Bayswater, about 4 minutes' drive. For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 33 minutes' drive from Bayswater North. For a skate park, Bayswater Ledges in Bayswater is about 3 minutes' drive from Bayswater North. The closest cinema to Bayswater North is Metro Cinemas in Boronia, about 6 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Lollipop's Playland & Cafe in Croydon is about 7 minutes' drive from Bayswater North. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Bayswater North is Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale, about 26 minutes' drive.
