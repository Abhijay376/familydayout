Laura Court Reserve

Park in Bayswater North · Basic info

Laura Court Reserve is a park in Bayswater North, in the Maroondah City Council area, good for all ages. Toolimerin Ave/Allambanan Dr (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 726 m away.
