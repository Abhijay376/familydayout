Playground at Glen Park

Playground in Bayswater North · Basic info

Playground at Glen Park is a public playground in Bayswater North, in the Maroondah City area. The Dandenong Creek Trail passes about 200 m away, and Bánh Mì Joint is about 390 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 200 m away, and bus 664 stops at Glen Park/Glen Park Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
