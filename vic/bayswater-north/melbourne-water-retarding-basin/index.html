Melbourne Water Retarding Basin

Park in Bayswater North · Basic info

Melbourne Water Retarding Basin is a park in Bayswater North, in the Maroondah City Council area, good for all ages. 62 Burdekin Ave (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 597 m away.
