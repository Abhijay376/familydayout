Apex Park Playground

Playground in Rutherglen · Basic info

Apex Park Playground in Rutherglen is a public playground in the Indigo Shire area. Rutherglen Public Library is about 230 m away, and Rutherglen War Memorial Swimming Pool is about 190 m away. There's a public toilet about 200 m away, and bus Albury - Corowa via Rutherglen stops at High St/Murray St, a 3-minute walk away.
