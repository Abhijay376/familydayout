Playground at Pipe Track Linear Reserve

Playground in Mitcham · Basic info

Playground at Pipe Track Linear Reserve is a public playground in Mitcham, in the Whitehorse City area. The Pipe Track passes about 10 m away, and Sapporo Japanese Restaurant is about 480 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 700 m away, and bus 740 stops at Clive St/Garden Ave, a 3-minute walk away.
