Antonio Park Playground

Playground in Mitcham · Basic info

Antonio Park Playground in Mitcham is a public playground in the Whitehorse City area. The Box Hill to Ringwood Trail passes about 300 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 901 stops at Deep Creek Rd/Whitehorse Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
