Cobham Road Playground

Playground in Mitcham · Basic info

This is a public playground in Mitcham. The Whitehorse NS10 is about 250 m away, and Hillcrest Reserve Playground is another option about 480 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.2 km from the playground, and buses 303 and 907 stop at Chippewa Ave/Mitcham Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
