Kerry Barreiro ​Swim School

Pool or water play in Mitcham · Official details

Kerry Barreiro ​Swim School is a pool or water play in Mitcham, in the Whitehorse City Council area, good for all ages. Trenham Ct/Highland Ave (bus) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 114 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: Monday – 1pm; Tuesday – 12pm, 12.30pm and 1pm.
