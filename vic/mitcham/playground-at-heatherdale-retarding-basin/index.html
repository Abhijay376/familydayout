Playground at Heatherdale Retarding Basin

Playground in Mitcham · Basic info

Playground at Heatherdale Retarding Basin is a public playground in Mitcham, in the Whitehorse City area. The Whitehorse NS10 is about 30 m away, and Playground at Berry Avenue Reserve is another option about 460 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 280 m from the playground, and bus 740 stops at Ferris Ave/Glenburnie Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
