Redland Drive Reserve

Park in Mitcham · Basic info

Redland Drive Reserve is a park in Mitcham, in the Whitehorse City Council area, good for all ages. Premier Ave/Mitcham Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 223 m away.
