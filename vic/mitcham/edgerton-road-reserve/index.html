Edgerton Road Reserve

Park in Mitcham · Basic info

Edgerton Road Reserve is a park in Mitcham, in the Whitehorse City Council area, good for all ages. Irene Cres/Wattle Valley Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 506 m away.
