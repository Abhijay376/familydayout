Schwerkolt Cottage & Museum Complex

Museum or gallery in Mitcham · Official details

Schwerkolt Cottage & Museum Complex is a museum or gallery in Mitcham, in the Whitehorse City Council area, best for school-age kids. Irene Cres/Wattle Valley Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 226 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: Saturday 1pm – 4pm; Sunday 1pm – 4pm.
