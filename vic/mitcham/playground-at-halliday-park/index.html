Playground at Halliday Park

Playground in Mitcham · Basic info

Playground at Halliday Park in Mitcham is a public playground in the Whitehorse City area. Cobham Road Playground is another option about 520 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and buses 270 and 907 stop at Mitcham Bowls Club/Mitcham Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
