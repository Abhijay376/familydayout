Our data lists 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in Streatham, in the Ararat area.

Landmarks

Salt Lake, Wongan Wildlife Reserve (no hunting) is the largest mapped park in Streatham, at about 24 hectares. Streatham Streamside Reserve is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Streatham include Simpson Park. Playgrounds in Streatham include the playground at Simpson Park. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Streatham.

Getting around

There's no train station in Streatham. The closest to Streatham is Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line), about 40 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Streatham. By car from Streatham, Ararat is about 46 minutes away, Ballarat about 68 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Willaura Outdoor Pool in Willaura is about 43 minutes' drive from Streatham. The closest library to Streatham is Lake Bolac Library in Lake Bolac, about 17 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Linton Skate Park in Linton is about 38 minutes' drive from Streatham. Ararat is the closest bigger centre to Streatham, about 46 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Streatham, the nearest places for kids by road are Lake Bolac Library in Lake Bolac (17 min), Willaura Outdoor Pool in Willaura (43 min), Linton Skate Park in Linton (38 min), Linton BMX park in Linton (38 min), Linton Recreation Reserve Playground in Linton (39 min) and Beaufort Pool in Beaufort (40 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Streatham.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Streatham, Mininera has 1 playground. Stoneleigh, Nerrin Nerrin, Lake Wongan and Cross Roads also border Streatham.
