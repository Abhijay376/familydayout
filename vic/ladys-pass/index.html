Ladys Pass is in the Greater Bendigo area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Ladys Pass, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Ladys Pass. The closest to Ladys Pass is Goornong Railway Station (V/Line), about 30 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Ladys Pass, so most trips start by car. By car from Ladys Pass, Bendigo is about 44 minutes away, Seymour about 50 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Goornong Swimming Pool in Goornong is about 36 minutes' drive from Ladys Pass. For a library, Heathcote Library in Heathcote is about 9 minutes' drive from Ladys Pass. The closest skate park to Ladys Pass is Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote, about 9 minutes' drive. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Ladys Pass, about 44 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Ladys Pass, the nearest places for kids by road are Heathcote Play Space in Heathcote (9 min), Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote (9 min), Heathcote Library in Heathcote (9 min), Mudwood Studio in Heathcote (10 min), Powder Magazine in Heathcote (11 min) and Tooborac Recreation Reserve Playground in Tooborac (22 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Ladys Pass.

Nearby suburbs

Heathcote, next to Ladys Pass, has 1 playground, a library and a skate park. Derrinal also borders Ladys Pass.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Ladys Pass, without traffic.
