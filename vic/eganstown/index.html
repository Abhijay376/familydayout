Eganstown is in the Hepburn area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Eganstown is Deep Creek, Eganstown Streamside Reserve, covering about 27 hectares. Eganstown Streamside Reserve is the next largest. The Goldfields Track and Three Lost Children Walk all pass through Eganstown.

Getting around

There's no train station in Eganstown. The closest to Eganstown is Creswick Railway Station (V/Line), about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Hepburn - Creswick via Daylesford route stop in Eganstown. By car from Eganstown, Daylesford is about 9 minutes away, Ballarat about 34 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Eganstown is Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford, about 8 minutes' drive. For a library, Daylesford Library in Daylesford is about 8 minutes' drive from Eganstown. For a skate park, Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick is about 18 minutes' drive from Eganstown. The closest cinema to Eganstown is Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central, about 33 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 34 minutes' drive from Eganstown. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Eganstown is Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek, about 26 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Daylesford, about 9 minutes from Eganstown by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Eganstown, the nearest places for kids by road are Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford (8 min), Daylesford Library in Daylesford (8 min), Daylesford Spa Country Railway in Daylesford (11 min), the playground at Hepburn Regional Park in Daylesford (15 min), Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick (18 min) and Infants in Creswick (18 min).

Best for

Free days on the Goldfields Track and in Eganstown's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Eganstown, Daylesford has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Kooroocheang and Blampied also border Eganstown.
