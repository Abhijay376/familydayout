Nareen is in the Southern Grampians area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Nareen, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Nareen.

Getting around

Nareen has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 138 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nareen, so most trips start by car. By car from Nareen, Hamilton is about 53 minutes away and Portland about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Nareen is Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral, about 33 minutes' drive. For a skate park, coleraine skate park in Coleraine is about 26 minutes' drive from Nareen. Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Nareen, about 53 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Nareen, the nearest places for kids by road are Bake House Studio in Harrow (25 min), Harrow Discovery Centre in Harrow (25 min), coleraine skate park in Coleraine (26 min), the playground at Queens Park in Coleraine (26 min), Silvester Oval Playground in Coleraine (26 min) and Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Nareen.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Nareen, Wando Vale has 1 playground. Coojar, Chetwynd, Carapook and Brimboal also border Nareen.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Nareen, without traffic.
