Getting around

There's no train station in Moonambel. The closest to Moonambel is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 38 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Moonambel, so most trips start by car. By car from Moonambel, Maryborough is about 37 minutes away, Stawell about 49 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot is about 41 minutes' drive from Moonambel. The closest library to Moonambel is Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough, about 38 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 37 minutes from Moonambel by car, with a library. From Moonambel, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Avoca Lions Park in Avoca (16 min), Courthouse in Avoca (16 min), Navarre Playground in Navarre (19 min), Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (41 min), Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (38 min) and St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (44 min).
