Hansonville is in the Wangaratta area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Myrrhee H3 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Hansonville, at about 5 hectares.

Getting around

Hansonville has no train station; Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Hansonville, so most trips start by car. By car from Hansonville, Wangaratta is about 32 minutes away and Benalla about 41 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Moore than Swimming in Wangaratta is about 29 minutes' drive from Hansonville. For a library, Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta is about 33 minutes' drive from Hansonville. The closest skate park to Hansonville is the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Hansonville is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 33 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 32 minutes from Hansonville by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Hansonville are the playground at Apex Park in Moyhu (9 min), Cobb & Co in Glenrowan (19 min), Glenrowan Museum in Glenrowan (19 min), Myrrhee Recreation Reserve in Myrrhee (26 min), Moore than Swimming in Wangaratta (29 min) and Splashpark in Wangaratta (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Hansonville.

Nearby suburbs

Myrrhee, next to Hansonville, has 1 playground. Next door to Hansonville, Moyhu has 1 playground. Greta also borders Hansonville.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Hansonville, without traffic.
