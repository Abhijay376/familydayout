Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Glenloth East in the Buloke area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Glenloth East has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 56 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glenloth East, so most trips start by car. By car from Glenloth East, Swan Hill is about 1.5 hours away and Bendigo about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof is about 21 minutes' drive from Glenloth East. For a library, Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook is about 33 minutes' drive from Glenloth East. The closest cinema to Glenloth East is Rex Theatre in Charlton, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Glenloth East are the playground at Centenary Park in Wycheproof (20 min), Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof (21 min), Willandra Museum in Wycheproof (21 min), Quambatook Lions Club Playground in Quambatook (32 min), Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook (33 min) and Quambatook Historical Centre in Quambatook (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Glenloth East.
