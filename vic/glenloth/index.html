Glenloth in the Buloke area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Shingle Hill Highway Park is the largest mapped park in Glenloth, at about 29 hectares.

Getting around

Glenloth has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 70 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glenloth, so most trips start by car. By car from Glenloth, Swan Hill is about 1.5 hours away and Bendigo about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof is about 18 minutes' drive from Glenloth. The closest cinema to Glenloth is Rex Theatre in Charlton, about 12 minutes' drive. From Glenloth, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Gordon Park in Charlton (12 min), Rex Theatre in Charlton (12 min), Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (12 min), the playground at Rotary Park in Charlton (13 min), Willandra Museum in Wycheproof (16 min) and Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof (18 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Glenloth.
