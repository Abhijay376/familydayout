Playground at Brooklyn Reserve

Playground in Brooklyn · Official details

This is a public playground in Brooklyn. The Federation Trail passes about 150 m away, and Parotta Station is about 360 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and buses 411, 412, 903 and 947 stop at Cypress Ave/Millers Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
