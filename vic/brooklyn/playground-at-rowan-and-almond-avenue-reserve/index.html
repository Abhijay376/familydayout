Playground at Rowan & Almond Avenue Reserve

Playground in Brooklyn · Basic info

Playground at Rowan & Almond Avenue Reserve is a public playground in Brooklyn, in the Hobsons Bay City area. The Federation Trail is about 200 m away, and Playground at D. N. Duane Reserve is another option about 380 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 740 m from the playground, and bus 414 stops at Conifer Ave/Geelong Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
