Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Cobaw, in the Macedon Ranges area.

Landmarks

Twin Bridges Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Cobaw, at about 9 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cobaw. The closest to Cobaw is Woodend Railway Station (V/Line), about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cobaw, so most trips start by car. By car from Cobaw, Kyneton is about 29 minutes away, Castlemaine about 54 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 76 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cobaw is Gisborne Aquatic Centre in Gisborne, about 31 minutes' drive. For a library, Romsey Library in Romsey is about 26 minutes' drive from Cobaw. For a beach, Canoeing in Tylden is about 35 minutes' drive from Cobaw. For a skate park, Romsey Skate Park in Romsey is about 27 minutes' drive from Cobaw. For a cinema, Reading Cinema in Sunbury is about 44 minutes' drive from Cobaw. Kyneton is the closest bigger centre to Cobaw, about 29 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds and a library. From Cobaw, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Hanging Rock Reserve in Newham (18 min), Romsey Library in Romsey (26 min), Jean Haynes Playground in Kyneton (26 min), Romsey Skate Park in Romsey (27 min), Kyneton Library in Kyneton (28 min) and Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton (28 min).

Best for
