East Wangaratta in the Wangaratta area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in East Wangaratta is East Wangaratta Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 169 hectares.

Getting around

East Wangaratta has no train station; Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 6.8 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside East Wangaratta, so most trips start by car. By car from East Wangaratta, Wangaratta is about 9 minutes away and Benalla about 34 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to East Wangaratta is Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest library to East Wangaratta is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 8 minutes' drive. For a beach, Sandy Beach in Beechworth is about 27 minutes' drive from East Wangaratta. For a skate park, the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta is about 11 minutes' drive from East Wangaratta. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 6 minutes' drive from East Wangaratta. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to East Wangaratta, about 9 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from East Wangaratta are the playground at Apex Park in Wangaratta (6 min), Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta (6 min), the playground at King George V Gardens in Wangaratta (6 min), Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (8 min), Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta (10 min) and the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta (11 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from East Wangaratta.

Nearby suburbs

Wangaratta, next to East Wangaratta, has 9 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Londrigan also borders East Wangaratta.

Getting there
