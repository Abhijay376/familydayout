Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Brit Brit in the Southern Grampians area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Brit Brit has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 122 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Brit Brit, so most trips start by car. By car from Brit Brit, Hamilton is about 47 minutes away and Portland about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Brit Brit is Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Brit Brit is coleraine skate park in Coleraine, about 19 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 47 minutes from Brit Brit by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Brit Brit are coleraine skate park in Coleraine (19 min), the playground at Queens Park in Coleraine (19 min), Silvester Oval Playground in Coleraine (19 min), Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral (27 min), Harrow Discovery Centre in Harrow (33 min) and Bake House Studio in Harrow (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Brit Brit.

Englefield and Coojar also border Brit Brit.
