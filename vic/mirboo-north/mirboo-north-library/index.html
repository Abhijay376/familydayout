Mirboo North Library

Library in Mirboo North · Basic info

Mirboo North Library is a library in Mirboo North, in the South Gippsland Shire Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Brennan St/Ridgway St (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 64 m away with a baby change table.
