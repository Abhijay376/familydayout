Landmarks

The biggest green space in Mirboo North is Baromi Park, covering about 6 hectares. Mirboo North H13 Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Mirboo North include Dickies Hill Picnic Reserve and Allambee East H12 Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds in Mirboo North include the playground at Baromi Park. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Mirboo North. In Mirboo North you'll also find Mirboo North Library. For older kids in Mirboo North there's Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North. For walks and rides, Grand Ridge Rail Trail and Lyrebird Forest Walk cross Mirboo North. Our data lists 5 cafes and family restaurants in Mirboo North for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Mirboo North has no train station; Trafalgar Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Wonthaggi - Traralgon via Leongatha route stop in Mirboo North. By car from Mirboo North, Leongatha is about 23 minutes away, Traralgon about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mirboo North is South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha, about 21 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha is about 22 minutes' drive from Mirboo North. The nearest larger centre is Leongatha, about 23 minutes from Mirboo North by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Mirboo North are the playground at Mossvale Park in Berrys Creek (11 min), Boolarra Old School Museum in Boolarra (22 min), Boolarra Skate Park in Boolarra (23 min), Railway Trolley Shed in Yinnar (23 min), South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha (21 min) and Splash Pools and Stadium in Leongatha (22 min).
