Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North

Skate, BMX or pump track in Mirboo North · Basic info

Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North is a skate, bmx or pump track in Mirboo North, in the South Gippsland Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Brennan St/Ridgway St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 216 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: opening_hours: 24/7.
