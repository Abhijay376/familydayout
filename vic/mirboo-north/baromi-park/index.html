Baromi Park

Park in Mirboo North · Basic info

Baromi Park is a park in Mirboo North, in the South Gippsland Shire Council area, good for all ages. Brennan St/Ridgway St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 186 m away with a baby change table.
