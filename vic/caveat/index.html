Caveat is in the Murrindindi area, and our data lists 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Caveat has a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Caveat is Dropmore G46 Bushland Reserve, covering about 125 hectares. Caveat Nature Conservation Reserve and Dropmore G101 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. For older kids in Caveat there's BMX Track.

Getting around

There's no train station in Caveat. The closest to Caveat is Avenel Railway Station (V/Line), about 33 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Caveat, so most trips start by car. By car from Caveat, Seymour is about 62 minutes away, Mansfield about 75 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Caveat is Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra, about 41 minutes' drive. The closest library to Caveat is Alexandra Library in Alexandra, about 40 minutes' drive. From Caveat, the nearest places for kids by road are Yarck Recreation Reserve Playground in Yarck (33 min), Creightons Creek Recreation Reserve Playground in Creightons Creek (42 min), the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea (38 min), Alexandra Library in Alexandra (40 min), Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra (41 min) and Alexandra Timber Tramway and Museum in Alexandra (42 min).

Best for

Teens who skate or ride, with BMX Track in Caveat.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Caveat, Yarck has 1 playground. Highlands and Dropmore also border Caveat.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Caveat, without traffic.
