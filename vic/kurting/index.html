Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Kurting, in the Loddon area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Kurting is Glenalbyn Bushland Reserve, covering about 17 hectares. Kurting Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kurting. The closest to Kurting is Raywood Railway Station (V/Line), about 35 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kurting, so most trips start by car. By car from Kurting, Bendigo is about 46 minutes away, Castlemaine about 62 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn is about 18 minutes' drive from Kurting. The closest skate park to Kurting is Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk, about 41 minutes' drive. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Kurting, about 46 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Kurting, the nearest places for kids by road are Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (18 min), Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (18 min), Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood (37 min), Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (38 min), Westbury Boulevard Playspace in Maiden Gully (39 min) and Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk (41 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Kurting.
