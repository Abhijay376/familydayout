Our data lists 4 parks and reserves in Teal Point, in the Gannawarra area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Teal Point is McDonald Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 215 hectares. Gannawarra Red Gum Swamp Nature Conservation Reserve and Harts Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting) are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Teal Point include Murrabit Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Teal Point has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Teal Point, so most trips start by car. By car from Teal Point, Swan Hill is about 61 minutes away and Echuca about 77 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Teal Point is Swimming Pool in Kerang, about 14 minutes' drive. For a library, Barham Branch Library in Koondrook is about 8 minutes' drive from Teal Point. For a skate park, Barham Skate Park in Koondrook is about 9 minutes' drive from Teal Point. The closest places for kids by road from Teal Point are the playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV) in Koondrook (7 min), the playground at Riverside Park in Koondrook (8 min), Barham Branch Library in Koondrook (8 min), Barham Skate Park in Koondrook (9 min), Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (14 min) and Swimming Pool in Kerang (14 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Teal Point.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Teal Point, Kerang has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Next door to Teal Point, Koondrook has 2 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Koroop and Gannawarra also border Teal Point.
