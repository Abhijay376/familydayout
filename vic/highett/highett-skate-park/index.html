Highett Skate Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Highett · Basic info

Highett Skate Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Highett, in the Bayside City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Telford St/Highett Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 245 m away with a baby change table.
