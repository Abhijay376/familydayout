Playground at Train Street Park

Playground in Highett · Basic info

Playground at Train Street Park in Highett is a public playground in the Bayside City area. Highett Library is about 340 m away, and Sushi Pedia is about 260 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 360 m away, and bus 708 stops at Dart St/Worthing Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
