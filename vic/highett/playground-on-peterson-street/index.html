Playground on Peterson Street

Playground in Highett · Basic info

Playground on Peterson Street is a public playground in Highett, in the Bayside City area. The Bike: Linacre St is about 330 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 160 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 140 m from the playground, and bus 828 stops at Telford St/Highett Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
