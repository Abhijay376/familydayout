Playground on Livingston Street

Playground in Highett · Basic info

Playground on Livingston Street is a public playground in Highett, in the Bayside City area. Highett Library is about 530 m away, and Sushi Pedia is about 400 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 640 m away, and bus 708 stops at Livingston St/Worthing Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
