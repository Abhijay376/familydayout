Ashwood Avenue Park

Park in Highett · Basic info

Ashwood Avenue Park is a park in Highett, in the Bayside City Council area, good for all ages. Sandringham College/Bluff Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 801 m away with a baby change table.
