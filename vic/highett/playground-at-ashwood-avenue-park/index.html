Playground at Ashwood Avenue Park

Playground in Highett · Basic info

Playground at Ashwood Avenue Park in Highett is a public playground in the Bayside City area. For food, Hampton Fish & Chips is about 460 m away, and Playground on Marchant Street is another option about 430 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 780 m from the playground, and bus 825 stops at Sandringham College/Bluff Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
