Playground at Sir William Fry Reserve

Playground in Highett · Basic info

Playground at Sir William Fry Reserve is a public playground in Highett, in the Kingston City area. Older kids have a skate park about 240 m away, and Krishna is about 440 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and buses 708 and 823 stop at Remington Dr/Nepean Hwy, a 4-minute walk away.
