McFarlane Court Reserve

Park in Highett · Basic info

McFarlane Court Reserve is a park in Highett, in the Kingston City Council area, good for all ages. Wickham Rd/Nepean Hwy (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 568 m away with a baby change table.
