Playground at Bonnie Doon Park

Playground in Gateway Island · Basic info

This is a public playground in Gateway Island. The West Albury Trail passes about 190 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 280 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus NSW901 stops at Hakea Pl/Kurrajong Cres, a 2-minute walk away.
