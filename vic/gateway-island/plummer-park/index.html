Plummer Park

Park in Gateway Island · Basic info

Plummer Park is a park in Gateway Island, in the Wodonga City Council area, good for all ages. Panmure St/Olive St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1543 m away with a baby change table.
