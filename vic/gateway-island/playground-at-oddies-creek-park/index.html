Playground at Oddies Creek Park

Playground in Gateway Island · Basic info

Playground at Oddies Creek Park in Gateway Island is a public playground in the Wodonga City area. The Hume and Hovell Walking Track passes about 80 m away, and Playground at Noreuil Park is another option about 390 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 940 m away, and buses 150, 160 and AW stop at Ebden St/Wodonga Pl, a 5-minute walk away.
