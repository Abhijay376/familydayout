Gateway Island in the Wodonga area has 11 playgrounds and 14 parks and reserves in our data. Beyond the parks, Gateway Island has a library, a museum or gallery, a cinema and 2 skate parks.

Landmarks

Monument Hill Parklands is the largest mapped park in Gateway Island, at about 18 hectares. Noreuil Park and Bonnie Doon Park are the next largest. Gateway Island also has Uiver Park, Padman Park and QEII Square. Playgrounds in Gateway Island include the playground at Uiver Park, the playground at Noreuil Park and the playground at Plummer Park. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Gateway Island. Places to visit in Gateway Island include Murray Art Museum Albury (MAMA), Regent Cinema and Albury LibraryMuseum. For older kids in Gateway Island there's David Street Skate Park and Skate Park. Myer Centrepoint Albury and West End Plaza are the main shopping centres. For walks and rides, Crossing Place Trail (Gravel), Lincoln Causeway Path and Gateway Island Trail cross Gateway Island. Our data lists 30 cafes and family restaurants in Gateway Island for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Gateway Island has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 2.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses AW, 150 and 160 stop in Gateway Island. By car from Gateway Island, Wodonga is about 7 minutes away and Wangaratta about 55 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wodonga Sports & Leisure Centre in West Wodonga is about 9 minutes' drive from Gateway Island. The closest beach to Gateway Island is Finns Beach in Bandiana, about 9 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 11 playgrounds across Gateway Island. Rainy days at the library, cinema and museum or gallery in Gateway Island. Teens who skate or ride, with David Street Skate Park in Gateway Island. Free days on the Crossing Place Trail (Gravel) and in Gateway Island's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Gateway Island, Wodonga has 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library.
