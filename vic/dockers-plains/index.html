Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Dockers Plains in the Wangaratta area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Dockers Plains. The closest to Dockers Plains is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 9.4 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dockers Plains, so most trips start by car. By car from Dockers Plains, Wangaratta is about 13 minutes away and Benalla about 43 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta is about 14 minutes' drive from Dockers Plains. The closest library to Dockers Plains is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 12 minutes' drive. For a beach, Sandy Beach in Beechworth is about 38 minutes' drive from Dockers Plains. The closest skate park to Dockers Plains is the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Dockers Plains is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 10 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 13 minutes from Dockers Plains by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Dockers Plains are the playground at Apex Park in Wangaratta (10 min), Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta (10 min), the playground at King George V Gardens in Wangaratta (11 min), Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (12 min), Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta (14 min) and the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta (15 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Dockers Plains.
