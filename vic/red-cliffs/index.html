Red Cliffs in the Mildura area has 6 playgrounds and 14 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, Red Cliffs has a library, 2 museums or galleries and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Red Cliffs is Lambert Island Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 1,294 hectares. Yatpool I6 Bushland Reserve and Mildura I15 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Red Cliffs include Ovens Park, Barclay Square and Red Cliffs Peace Walk. Playgrounds around Red Cliffs include Ovens Park Playground, Quandong Park Playground and Woodbine Park Playground. Our data shows 10 public toilets in Red Cliffs. Places to visit in Red Cliffs include Red Cliffs Military Museum, Work Space Gallery 3496 and A S Kenyon Library Red Cliffs. For older kids in Red Cliffs there's Skatepark. For walks and rides, Former Morkalla-Redcliffs Railway crosses Red Cliffs. Our data lists 5 cafes and family restaurants in Red Cliffs for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Red Cliffs has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 170 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the 100/200 and Mildura - Horsham via Hopetoun routes stop in Red Cliffs. V/Line coaches stop in Red Cliffs. By car from Red Cliffs, Mildura is about 17 minutes away and Swan Hill about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Irymple Swimming Pool in Irymple is about 9 minutes' drive from Red Cliffs. The closest beach to Red Cliffs is Apex Beach in Mildura, about 26 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Deakin Cinema Complex in Mildura is about 17 minutes' drive from Red Cliffs. The closest indoor play centre to Red Cliffs is Sunraysia Tenpin in Mildura, about 16 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Putt Putt Family Fun Centre in Mildura is about 17 minutes' drive from Red Cliffs.

Best for

Younger kids at Red Cliffs's 6 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the library and museum or gallery in Red Cliffs. Teens who skate or ride, with Skatepark in Red Cliffs. Free days on the Former Morkalla-Redcliffs Railway and in Red Cliffs's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Irymple, next to Red Cliffs, has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Red Cliffs, Iraak has a beach. Cardross also borders Red Cliffs.
