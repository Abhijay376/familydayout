Playground at Barclay Square

Playground in Red Cliffs · Basic info

Playground at Barclay Square is a public playground in Red Cliffs, in the Mildura Rural City area. A S Kenyon Library Red Cliffs is about 110 m away, and Red Cliffs Military Museum is about 90 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 60 m from the playground, and coaches stop at Red Cliffs Post Office/Indi Ave , a 1-minute walk away.
