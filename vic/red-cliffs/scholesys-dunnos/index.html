Scholesy's (Dunno's), Red Cliffs

Cafe or restaurant in Red Cliffs · Basic info

Scholesy's (Dunno's) is a cafe or restaurant in Red Cliffs, in the Mildura Rural City Council area, a place to eat during a day out. Barclay Square Park/Indi Ave (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 165 m away with a baby change table.
