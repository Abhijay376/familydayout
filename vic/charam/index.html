Charam in the West Wimmera area has 10 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Charam is Konnepra State Forest, covering about 3,147 hectares. Arnolds State Forest and Richardsons State Forest are the next largest. Charam also has State Forest (Unnamed), Charam Bushland Reserve and Murranbool Swamp Lake Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Charam. The closest to Charam is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 131 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Charam, so most trips start by car. By car from Charam, Horsham is about 75 minutes away and Hamilton about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Charam, the nearest places for kids by road are West Wimmera Community Association - Red Tail Gallery in Edenhope (28 min), Dachshund Museum & Gallery in Edenhope (28 min), the playground at Lions Park in Edenhope (28 min), Henley Park Playground in Edenhope (29 min), West Wimmera Shire Goroke Library in Goroke (51 min) and Goroke Swimming Centre in Goroke (51 min).
