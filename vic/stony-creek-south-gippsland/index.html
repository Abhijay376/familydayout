Stony Creek (South Gippsland) is in the South Gippsland area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Stony Creek (South Gippsland) is Stony Creek Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 1 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Stony Creek (South Gippsland). For walks and rides, Great Southern Rail Trail crosses Stony Creek (South Gippsland).

Getting around

Stony Creek (South Gippsland) has no train station; Yarragon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 43 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Stony Creek (South Gippsland), so most trips start by car. By car from Stony Creek (South Gippsland), Leongatha is about 20 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.
