Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bullarto in the Hepburn area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bullarto. The closest to Bullarto is Ballan Railway Station (V/Line), about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Bullarto. By car from Bullarto, Daylesford is about 12 minutes away, Kyneton about 34 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bullarto is Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford, about 12 minutes' drive. For a library, Daylesford Library in Daylesford is about 12 minutes' drive from Bullarto. The closest beach to Bullarto is Canoeing in Tylden, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Bullarto is Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Bullarto is Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek, about 44 minutes' drive. Daylesford is the closest bigger centre to Bullarto, about 12 minutes away by car, and has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Bullarto, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at J P Gleeson Memorial Park in Lyonville (5 min), Daylesford Spa Country Railway in Daylesford (11 min), Daylesford Library in Daylesford (12 min), Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford (12 min), the playground at Quarry Street Reserve in Trentham (14 min) and Little Gallery in Trentham (14 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bullarto.

Nearby suburbs

Lyonville, next to Bullarto, has 1 playground. Korweinguboora and Bullarto South also border Bullarto.

Getting there
