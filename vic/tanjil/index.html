Tanjil in the Baw Baw area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Erica State Forest is the largest mapped park in Tanjil, at about 13,323 hectares. Bull Beef Creek Nature Conservation Reserve and Pretty Creek Reference Area are the next largest. For walks and rides, Pitmans Creek Track crosses Tanjil.

Getting around

Tanjil has no train station; Moe Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 17 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tanjil, so most trips start by car. By car from Tanjil, Traralgon is about 61 minutes away, Warragul about 68 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tanjil is Rawson Swimming Pool in Rawson, about 37 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Tanjil is Moe Skate Park in Moe, about 40 minutes' drive. From Tanjil, the nearest places for kids by road are Rawson Swimming Pool in Rawson (37 min), the playground at Erica State Forest in Rawson (38 min), the playground at Croxford Park in Rawson (37 min), Moe Skate Park in Moe (40 min), Diving Pool in Moe (41 min) and Old Brown Coal Mine Museum in Yallourn North (42 min).

Best for

Free days on the Pitmans Creek Track and in Tanjil's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Tanjil, Willow Grove has 2 playgrounds. Hill End, Fumina South, Fumina and Caringal also border Tanjil.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Tanjil, without traffic.
