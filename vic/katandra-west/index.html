Katandra West is in the Greater Shepparton area, and our data lists 2 playgrounds and 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Katandra West is Inglis Bushland Reserve, covering about 20 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Katandra West include Katandra Football Netball Club. Playgrounds around Katandra West include the playground at Katandra Rec Res Youth Spot and the playground at Katandra West Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet and 2 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Katandra West.

Getting around

There's no train station in Katandra West. The closest to Katandra West is Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line), about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Katandra West, so most trips start by car. By car from Katandra West, Shepparton is about 30 minutes away, Benalla about 69 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.
