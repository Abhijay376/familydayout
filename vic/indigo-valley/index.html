Indigo Valley in the Indigo area has 7 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Chiltern-Mt Pilot National Park is the largest mapped park in Indigo Valley, at about 21,672 hectares. Pilot Range Reference Area and Woorragee North I18 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Indigo Valley also has Woorragee I93 Bushland Reserve, Indigo Creek Streamside Reserve and Woorragee North I92 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Indigo Valley has no train station; Chiltern Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 10 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Indigo Valley, so most trips start by car. By car from Indigo Valley, Wodonga is about 29 minutes away and Wangaratta about 47 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wodonga Sports & Leisure Centre in West Wodonga is about 27 minutes' drive from Indigo Valley. The closest library to Indigo Valley is Howlong Library and Resource Centre in Gooramadda, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Indigo Valley is Finns Beach in Bandiana, about 32 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the bmx track on Silva Drive in West Wodonga is about 25 minutes' drive from Indigo Valley. The closest cinema to Indigo Valley is Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana, about 31 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 29 minutes from Indigo Valley by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Indigo Valley are Chiltern Athenaeum Museum in Chiltern (15 min), Martin Park Playground in Chiltern (16 min), the playground at Max Beck Park in West Wodonga (22 min), the bmx track on Silva Drive in West Wodonga (25 min), Skate Park in Yackandandah (28 min) and Wodonga Sports & Leisure Centre in West Wodonga (27 min).

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Indigo Valley, West Wodonga has 20 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Indigo Valley, Leneva has 3 playgrounds. Chiltern, next to Indigo Valley, has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Indigo Valley, Yackandandah has 1 playground and a skate park. Huon Creek, Wooragee, Castle Creek and Barnawartha North also border Indigo Valley.
