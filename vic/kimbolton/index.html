Kimbolton in the Greater Bendigo area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Kimbolton is Kimbolton State Forest, covering about 1,921 hectares. Eppalock Education Area is the next largest.

Getting around

Kimbolton has no train station; Bendigo Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kimbolton, so most trips start by car. By car from Kimbolton, Bendigo is about 35 minutes away, Kyneton about 48 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kimbolton is Brennan Park Swimming Pool in Kennington, about 32 minutes' drive. For a library, Bendigo Library in Bendigo is about 34 minutes' drive from Kimbolton. The closest skate park to Kimbolton is Ewing Park Skating in Bendigo, about 33 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 35 minutes' drive from Kimbolton. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 35 minutes' drive from Kimbolton. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 35 minutes' drive from Kimbolton. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Kimbolton, about 35 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Kimbolton, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Coomoora Park in Strathfieldsaye (23 min), Clydebank Court Playspace in Strathfieldsaye (24 min), Brennan Park Swimming Pool in Kennington (32 min), Ewing Park Skating in Bendigo (33 min), Discovery Science & Technology Centre in Bendigo (34 min) and Bendigo Library in Bendigo (34 min).
