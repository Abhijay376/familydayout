Wahring is in the Strathbogie area, and our data lists 5 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Creightons Creek Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Wahring, at about 30 hectares. Bunganail Bushland Reserve and Noorilim Estate are the next largest. Wahring also has Dargalong Streamside Reserve and Goulburn River Heritage River. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Wahring.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wahring. The closest to Wahring is Nagambie Railway Station (V/Line), about 9.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wahring, so most trips start by car. By car from Wahring, Seymour is about 31 minutes away, Shepparton about 37 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Wahring is Nagambie Pool in Nagambie, about 13 minutes' drive. For a library, Nagambie Library in Nagambie is about 11 minutes' drive from Wahring. For a beach, Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton is about 38 minutes' drive from Wahring. For a skate park, the skate park at Lions Park in Rushworth is about 34 minutes' drive from Wahring. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 37 minutes' drive from Wahring. The closest indoor play centre to Wahring is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 37 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Emerald Bank Leisure Land in Kialla is about 30 minutes' drive from Wahring. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 31 minutes from Wahring by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Wahring, the nearest places for kids by road are Nagambie Historical Society in Nagambie (11 min), Nagambie Library in Nagambie (11 min), River Street Playground in Nagambie (11 min), Blayney Reserve Playground in Nagambie (13 min), Nagambie Pool in Nagambie (13 min) and the skate park at Lions Park in Rushworth (34 min).

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Wahring, Nagambie has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Murchison, next to Wahring, has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Wahring, Goulburn Weir has 1 playground. Murchison East, Moorilim, Molka and Kirwans Bridge also border Wahring.
