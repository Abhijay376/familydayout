Mount Bute in the Corangamite area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Mount Bute Recreation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Mount Bute, at about 1 hectares.

Getting around

Mount Bute has no train station; Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 45 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mount Bute, so most trips start by car. By car from Mount Bute, Ballarat is about 62 minutes away, Colac about 67 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mount Bute is Lismore Pool in Lismore, about 20 minutes' drive. For a library, Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum is about 29 minutes' drive from Mount Bute. The closest skate park to Mount Bute is Linton Skate Park in Linton, about 44 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Mount Bute are Lismore Pool in Lismore (20 min), Cape Clear & District Lions Park Playground in Cape Clear (27 min), Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum (29 min), the playground at Berringa Recreation Reserve in Berringa (40 min), Linton Skate Park in Linton (44 min) and Linton BMX park in Linton (44 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mount Bute.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Mount Bute, Lismore has a pool. Berrybank also borders Mount Bute.
