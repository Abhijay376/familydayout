Middle Tarwin is in the South Gippsland area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Middle Tarwin is Drumdlemara H8 Bushland Reserve, covering about 32 hectares.

Getting around

Middle Tarwin has no train station; Yarragon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 53 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Middle Tarwin, so most trips start by car. By car from Middle Tarwin, Leongatha is about 25 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 29 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Middle Tarwin is South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha, about 24 minutes' drive. For a library, Leongatha Library in Leongatha is about 24 minutes' drive from Middle Tarwin. The closest beach to Middle Tarwin is The Caves in Inverloch, about 24 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Venus Bay Community Centre Reserve in Venus Bay is about 14 minutes' drive from Middle Tarwin. For a cinema, Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha is about 24 minutes' drive from Middle Tarwin. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Middle Tarwin, about 25 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Middle Tarwin, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at RV Fisher Reserve in Tarwin Lower (7 min), the playground at Jupiter Park in Venus Bay (13 min), the skate park at Venus Bay Community Centre Reserve in Venus Bay (14 min), Ride the Wild Goat in Fish Creek (12 min), Bunurong Environment Centre & Shell Museum in Inverloch (18 min) and Leongatha Library in Leongatha (24 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Middle Tarwin.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Middle Tarwin, Tarwin Lower has 1 playground and a beach. Buffalo, next to Middle Tarwin, has 1 playground. Meeniyan and Leongatha South also border Middle Tarwin.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Middle Tarwin, without traffic.
