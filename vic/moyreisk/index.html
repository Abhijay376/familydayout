Landmarks

Moyreisk I69 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Moyreisk, at about 11 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Moyreisk. The closest to Moyreisk is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 36 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Moyreisk, so most trips start by car. By car from Moyreisk, Maryborough is about 38 minutes away, Stawell about 67 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Moyreisk is St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud, about 33 minutes' drive. For a library, Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough is about 38 minutes' drive from Moyreisk. Maryborough is the closest bigger centre to Moyreisk, about 38 minutes away by car, and has a library. From Moyreisk, the nearest places for kids by road are Stuart Mill Recreation Reserve Playground in Stuart Mill (15 min), the playground at Avoca Lions Park in Avoca (28 min), Courthouse in Avoca (27 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (33 min), Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (38 min) and Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (35 min).
