Moorabbin in the Kingston area has 5 playgrounds and 2 parks and reserves in our data. Moorabbin also has a museum or gallery and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Moorabbin is GR Bricker Reserve, covering about 3 hectares. Moorabbin also has Horscroft Place Pocket Park. Playgrounds around Moorabbin include the playground at GR Bricker Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Moorabbin. Places to visit in Moorabbin include Twisted Science. For older kids in Moorabbin there's St Kilda Cycling Club Capella Crescent Criterium Circuit. Our data lists 5 cafes and family restaurants in Moorabbin for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Moorabbin has a train station: Moorabbin Railway Station (metro: Frankston Line). Buses 627, 767, 811, 812, 822, 823, 824, 825 and 903 stop in Moorabbin. By car from Moorabbin, Dandenong is about 21 minutes away, Box Hill about 25 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 21 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Waves Leisure Centre in Highett is about 5 minutes' drive from Moorabbin. For a library, Highett Library in Highett is about 3 minutes' drive from Moorabbin. For a beach, Brighton Beach in Brighton is about 7 minutes' drive from Moorabbin. The closest cinema to Moorabbin is Village Cinemas in Cheltenham, about 5 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Zone Bowling in Clayton is about 15 minutes' drive from Moorabbin. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Moorabbin is Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale, about 11 minutes' drive.
