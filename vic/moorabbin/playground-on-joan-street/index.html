Playground on Joan Street

Playground in Moorabbin · Basic info

This is a public playground in Moorabbin. Twisted Science is about 420 m away, and Playground at GR Bricker Reserve is another option about 270 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 60 m from the playground, and bus 822 stops at G R Bricker Reserve/Rowans Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
