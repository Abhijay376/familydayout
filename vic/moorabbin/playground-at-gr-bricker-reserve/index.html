Playground at GR Bricker Reserve

Playground in Moorabbin · Basic info

Playground at GR Bricker Reserve in Moorabbin is a public playground in the Kingston City area. Twisted Science is about 300 m away, and Playground on Joan Street is another option about 270 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus 822 stops at G R Bricker Reserve/Rowans Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
