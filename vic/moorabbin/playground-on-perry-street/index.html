Playground on Perry Street

Playground in Moorabbin · Basic info

Playground on Perry Street is a public playground in Moorabbin, in the Kingston City area. Naked Japan is about 480 m away if you need food, and Playground on Dane Road is another option about 420 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.1 km away, and bus 823 stops at Schofield St/Nepean Hwy, a 5-minute walk away.
