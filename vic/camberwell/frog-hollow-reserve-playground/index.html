Frog Hollow Reserve Playground

Playground in Camberwell · Basic info

Frog Hollow Reserve Playground is a public playground in Camberwell, in the Boroondara City area. The Anniversary Trail passes about 20 m away, and Pizza Republica Camberwell is about 500 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 170 m away, and trains stop at Willison Railway Station, a 6-minute walk away.
