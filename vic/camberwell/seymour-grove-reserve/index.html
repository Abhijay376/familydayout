Seymour Grove Reserve

Park in Camberwell · Basic info

Seymour Grove Reserve is a park in Camberwell, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Seymour Gr/Camberwell Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 402 m away with a baby change table.
