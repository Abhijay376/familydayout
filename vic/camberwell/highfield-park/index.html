Highfield Park

Park in Camberwell · Basic info

Highfield Park is a park in Camberwell, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Highfield Rd/Riversdale Rd #51 (tram) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 29 m away with a baby change table.
