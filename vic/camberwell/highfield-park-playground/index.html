Highfield Park Playground

Playground in Camberwell · Basic info

This is a public playground in Camberwell. For food, Mimi's Pizza is about 230 m away, and Playground on Prospect Hill Road is another option about 550 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 240 m from the playground, and tram 70 stops at Highfield Rd/Riversdale Rd #51, a 2-minute walk away.
