Coopers Reserve

Park in Camberwell · Basic info

Coopers Reserve is a park in Camberwell, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Norman St/Through Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 83 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed. Opening hours: opening_hours: 24/7.
