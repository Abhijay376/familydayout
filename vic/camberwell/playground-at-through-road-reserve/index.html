Playground at Through Road Reserve

Playground in Camberwell · Basic info

Playground at Through Road Reserve is a public playground in Camberwell, in the Boroondara City area. The Back Creek Trail passes about 240 m away, and Lynden Park Playground is another option about 460 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 680 m away, and bus 766 stops at Through Rd/Joffre St, a 1-minute walk away.
