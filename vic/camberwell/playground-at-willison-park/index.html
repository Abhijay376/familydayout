Playground at Willison Park

Playground in Camberwell · Basic info

Playground at Willison Park is a public playground in Camberwell, in the Boroondara City area. The Anniversary Trail passes about 110 m away, and Pizza Republica Camberwell is about 300 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 320 m away, and tram 75 stops at Orrong Cres/Camberwell Rd #48, a 5-minute walk away.
