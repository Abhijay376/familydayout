Playground at Fordham Gardens

Playground in Camberwell · Basic info

Playground at Fordham Gardens is a public playground in Camberwell, in the Boroondara City area. The Anniversary Trail is about 200 m away, and for food, Camberwell Pizza is about 450 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 740 m from the playground, and tram 75 stops at Wilson Gr/Camberwell Rd #49, a 3-minute walk away.
