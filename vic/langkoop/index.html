Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Langkoop, in the West Wimmera area.

Landmarks

Meereek Flora Reserve is the largest mapped park in Langkoop, at about 282 hectares. Lignum Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting) and Meereek Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

Langkoop has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 170 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Langkoop, so most trips start by car. By car from Langkoop, Horsham is about 2 hours away and Hamilton about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest places for kids by road from Langkoop are the playground at War Memorial Park in Apsley (18 min), the playground at Lions Park in Edenhope (30 min), Dachshund Museum & Gallery in Edenhope (31 min) and West Wimmera Community Association - Red Tail Gallery in Edenhope (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Langkoop.

Nearby suburbs

Edenhope, next to Langkoop, has 2 playgrounds. Apsley, next to Langkoop, has 1 playground.
