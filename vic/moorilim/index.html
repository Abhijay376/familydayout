Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Moorilim in the Strathbogie area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Moorilim. The closest to Moorilim is Murchison East Railway Station (V/Line), about 4.8 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Moorilim, so most trips start by car. By car from Moorilim, Shepparton is about 27 minutes away, Seymour about 42 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rushworth Pool in Rushworth is about 29 minutes' drive from Moorilim. The closest library to Moorilim is Nagambie Library in Nagambie, about 22 minutes' drive. For a beach, Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton is about 28 minutes' drive from Moorilim. For a skate park, the skate park at Lions Park in Rushworth is about 27 minutes' drive from Moorilim. The closest cinema to Moorilim is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Moorilim is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Moorilim is Emerald Bank Leisure Land in Kialla, about 20 minutes' drive. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Moorilim, about 27 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Moorilim are the playground at Goulburn River Heritage River in Murchison (7 min), the playground at Murchison Youth Spot in Murchison (7 min), Emerald Bank Leisure Land in Kialla (20 min), Museum of Vehicle Evolution - MOVE in Kialla (19 min), the skate park at Lions Park in Rushworth (27 min) and Nagambie Historical Society in Nagambie (22 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Moorilim.
