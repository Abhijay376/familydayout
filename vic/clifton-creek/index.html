Clifton Creek is in the East Gippsland area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Wy Yung I19 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Clifton Creek, at about 23 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Clifton Creek. The closest to Clifton Creek is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Clifton Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Clifton Creek, Bairnsdale is about 25 minutes away and Lakes Entrance about 52 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale is about 24 minutes' drive from Clifton Creek. The closest library to Clifton Creek is Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Clifton Creek is Boyfriend Beach in Forge Creek, about 37 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Gilsenan Reserve in Paynesville is about 36 minutes' drive from Clifton Creek. The closest cinema to Clifton Creek is Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale, about 23 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow is about 23 minutes' drive from Clifton Creek. The nearest larger centre is Bairnsdale, about 25 minutes from Clifton Creek by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Clifton Creek are the playground at Howitt Park in Wy Yung (21 min), Howitt Park in Wy Yung (21 min), Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale (22 min), Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale (23 min), Krowathunkoolong Keeping Place in Bairnsdale (23 min) and Bairnsdale Museum in Bairnsdale (24 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Clifton Creek.
