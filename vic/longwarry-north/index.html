Landmarks

The biggest green space in Longwarry North is Tarago River Picnic Point SSR, covering about 3 hectares. Tarago River, Picnic Point Streamside Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Longwarry North. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Longwarry North for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Longwarry North has no train station; Longwarry Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 3.6 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Longwarry North, so most trips start by car. By car from Longwarry North, Warragul is about 16 minutes away, Pakenham about 26 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 70 minutes, without traffic.
