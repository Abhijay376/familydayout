Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Yandoit Hills in the Mount Alexander area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Yandoit Hills has no train station; Castlemaine Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yandoit Hills, so most trips start by car. By car from Yandoit Hills, Daylesford is about 27 minutes away, Castlemaine about 34 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Newstead Swimming Pool in Newstead is about 22 minutes' drive from Yandoit Hills. For a library, Daylesford Library in Daylesford is about 27 minutes' drive from Yandoit Hills. The closest skate park to Yandoit Hills is BMX Track in Castlemaine, about 33 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Daylesford, about 27 minutes from Yandoit Hills by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Yandoit Hills are the playground at Newstead Memorial Park in Newstead (20 min), Newstead Swimming Pool in Newstead (22 min), the playground at Hepburn Regional Park in Daylesford (25 min), Daylesford Spa Country Railway in Daylesford (26 min), Daylesford Library in Daylesford (27 min) and Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Yandoit Hills.
