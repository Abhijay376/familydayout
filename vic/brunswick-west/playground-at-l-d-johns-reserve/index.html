Playground at L.D. Johns Reserve

Playground in Brunswick West · Basic info

Playground at L.D. Johns Reserve in Brunswick West is a public playground in the Merri-Bek City area. The Bike: DELWP is about 240 m away, and for food, Kebablaki is about 470 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 580 m from the playground, and bus 510 stops at McLean St/Moreland Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
