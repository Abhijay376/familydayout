Landmarks

The biggest green space in Benwerrin is Aquila Creek Reference Area, covering about 266 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Benwerrin.

Getting around

Benwerrin has no train station; Birregurra Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Benwerrin, so most trips start by car. By car from Benwerrin, Colac is about 42 minutes away, Torquay about 59 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

The closest skate park to Benwerrin is Kay Langan Skate Park in Lorne, about 11 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Lorne Theatre in Lorne is about 11 minutes' drive from Benwerrin. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Benwerrin, about 42 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play.
