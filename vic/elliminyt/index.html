Elliminyt in the Colac Otway area has 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Harris Road Swamp Reserve is the largest mapped park in Elliminyt, at about 2 hectares. Joiner Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Elliminyt. For walks and rides, Old Beechy Rail Trail and MBN SW3 cross Elliminyt.

Getting around

Elliminyt has no train station; Colac Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 2.0 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 1 stops in Elliminyt. By car from Elliminyt, Colac is about 4 minutes away, Geelong about 68 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac is about 3 minutes' drive from Elliminyt. For a skate park, Birregurra Skate Park in Birregurra is about 21 minutes' drive from Elliminyt. For a cinema, Colac Cinemas in Colac is about 3 minutes' drive from Elliminyt. The closest indoor play centre to Elliminyt is Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac, about 4 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 4 minutes from Elliminyt by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Elliminyt, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Inglis Court Park in Colac (2 min), Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (3 min), the playground at Lawrence Court Park in Colac (4 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (3 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (4 min) and Birregurra Skate Park in Birregurra (21 min).

Best for

Free days on the Old Beechy Rail Trail and in Elliminyt's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Elliminyt, Colac has 13 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. Next door to Elliminyt, Colac West has 1 playground. Colac East, Barongarook West and Barongarook also border Elliminyt.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Elliminyt, without traffic.
