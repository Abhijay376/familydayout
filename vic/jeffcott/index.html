Jeffcott is in the Buloke area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Mount Jeffcott Flora and Fauna Reserve is the largest mapped park in Jeffcott, at about 265 hectares. Jeffcott I145 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Jeffcott has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 96 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Jeffcott, so most trips start by car. By car from Jeffcott, Stawell is about 82 minutes away and Horsham about 85 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Donald Swimming Pool in Donald is about 15 minutes' drive from Jeffcott. For a skate park, Donald BMX Track in Donald is about 14 minutes' drive from Jeffcott. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 26 minutes' drive from Jeffcott. The closest places for kids by road from Jeffcott are Donald BMX Track in Donald (14 min), the playground at Donald Public Park in Donald (14 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (15 min), the playground at Gordon Park in Charlton (26 min), Rex Theatre in Charlton (26 min) and Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Jeffcott.

Nearby suburbs

Charlton, next to Jeffcott, has 3 playgrounds and a cinema. Next door to Jeffcott, Donald has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Gil Gil and Dooboobetic also border Jeffcott.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Jeffcott, without traffic.
