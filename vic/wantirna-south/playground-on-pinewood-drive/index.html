Playground on Pinewood Drive

Playground in Wantirna South · Basic info

Playground on Pinewood Drive is a public playground in Wantirna South, in the Knox City area. The Blind Creek Trail is about 60 m away, and Knox Library – Ngarrgoo is about 40 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 750 m from the playground, and bus 737 stops at Knox City Shopping Centre , a 3-minute walk away.
