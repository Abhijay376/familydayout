Knox Library – Ngarrgoo

Library in Wantirna South · Basic info

Knox Library – Ngarrgoo is a library in Wantirna South, in the Knox City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Knox City Shopping Centre (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 689 m away with a baby change table.
