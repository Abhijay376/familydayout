Norfolk Avenue Playground

Playground in Wantirna South · Basic info

Norfolk Avenue Playground in Wantirna South is a public playground in the Knox City area. The Stud Road Trail is about 370 m away, and Playground on Tamar Street is another option about 600 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 870 m from the playground, and buses 664 and 901 stop at Boronia Rd/Stud Rd, a 7-minute walk away.
