Playground at Lewis Park

Playground in Wantirna South · Basic info

This is a public playground in Wantirna South. The Blind Creek Trail is about 60 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 210 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 350 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 6 minutes from Wantirna South.
