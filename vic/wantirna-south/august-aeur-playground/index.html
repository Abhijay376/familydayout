August Aeur Playground

Playground in Wantirna South · Basic info

August Aeur Playground is a public playground in Wantirna South, in the Knox City area. The Stud Road Trail is about 130 m away, and for food, Studfield Fish & Chips is about 60 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 230 m from the playground, and bus 737 stops at Stud Rd/Coleman Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
