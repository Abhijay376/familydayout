Shepherds Bush

Park in Wantirna South · Basic info

Shepherds Bush is a park in Wantirna South, in the Knox City Council area, good for all ages. 1103 High Street Rd (bus) is a 12-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 711 m away.
