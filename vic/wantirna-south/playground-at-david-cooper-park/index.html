Playground at David Cooper Park

Playground in Wantirna South · Basic info

Playground at David Cooper Park is a public playground in Wantirna South, in the Knox City area. The Blind Creek Trail passes about 190 m away, and Playground at Finley Reserve is another option about 440 m away. There's a public toilet about 910 m away, and bus 757 stops at Caitlyn Ct/Jenola Pde, a 2-minute walk away.
