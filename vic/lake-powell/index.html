Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Lake Powell, in the Swan Hill area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lake Powell is Proposed Murray River Park (part), covering about 21,775 hectares. Lakes Powell and Carpul Wildlife Reserve (no hunting) is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lake Powell. The closest to Lake Powell is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 92 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lake Powell, so most trips start by car. By car from Lake Powell, Swan Hill is about 88 minutes away and Mildura about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a skate park, Robinvale Skatepark in Robinvale is about 18 minutes' drive from Lake Powell. From Lake Powell, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Pisasale Park in Robinvale (17 min), the playground at Rotary Park in Robinvale (17 min), Rural Life Museum in Robinvale (17 min) and Robinvale Skatepark in Robinvale (18 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lake Powell.

Nearby suburbs

Boundary Bend, next to Lake Powell, has 1 playground. Bannerton and Annuello also border Lake Powell.
