Lucyvale in the Towong area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lucyvale is Cudgewa Creek Reference Area, covering about 824 hectares. Cudgewa Creek Streamside Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Lucyvale has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 67 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lucyvale, so most trips start by car. By car from Lucyvale, Wodonga is about 84 minutes away and Wangaratta about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lucyvale is Corryong Public swimming pool in Corryong, about 27 minutes' drive. For a library, Corryong Elyne Mitchell Library in Corryong is about 27 minutes' drive from Lucyvale. The closest cinema to Lucyvale is Corryong Cinema in Corryong, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Lucyvale are Corryong Cinema in Corryong (27 min), Corryong Elyne Mitchell Library in Corryong (27 min) and Corryong Public swimming pool in Corryong (27 min).
