Pimpinio in the Horsham area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

West Wail Flora and Fauna Reserve is the largest mapped park in Pimpinio, at about 589 hectares. Wail Bushland Reserve and Verandah Swamp Wildlife Reserve (no hunting) are the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Pimpinio. The closest to Pimpinio is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 106 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Horsham - Kaniva via Dimboola and Horsham - Hopetoun via Dimboola routes stop in Pimpinio. V/Line coaches stop in Pimpinio. By car from Pimpinio, Horsham is about 15 minutes away and Stawell about 69 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Pimpinio is Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest library to Pimpinio is Dimboola Library in Dimboola, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Pimpinio is Horsham Skate Park in Horsham, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Pimpinio is Centre Cinemas in Horsham, about 16 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Horsham, about 15 minutes from Pimpinio by car, with 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Pimpinio are the playground at Elbourne Park in Horsham (13 min), Playland in Horsham (14 min), Horsham Skate Park in Horsham (16 min), Centre Cinemas in Horsham (16 min), Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham (16 min) and Velodrome in Horsham (16 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Pimpinio.

Nearby suburbs

Natimuk, next to Pimpinio, has 4 playgrounds and a skate park. Vectis, next to Pimpinio, has 1 playground. Duchembegarra and Dooen also border Pimpinio.
