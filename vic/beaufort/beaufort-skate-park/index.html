Beaufort Skate Park

Park in Beaufort · Basic info

Beaufort Skate Park is a park in Beaufort, in the Pyrenees Shire Council area, good for all ages. Beaufort Station/Pratt St (coach) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 133 m away with a baby change table.
