Michael O'Connor Memorial Park

Park in Beaufort · Basic info

Michael O'Connor Memorial Park is a park in Beaufort, in the Pyrenees Shire Council area, good for all ages. Havelock St/Neill St (bus) is a 14-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1348 m away with a baby change table.
