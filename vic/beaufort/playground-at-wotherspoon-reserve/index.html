Playground at Wotherspoon Reserve

Playground in Beaufort · Basic info

Playground at Wotherspoon Reserve in Beaufort is a public playground in the Pyrenees Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 360 m away, and Beaufort Community Resource Centre is about 170 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus Ballarat - Stawell via Ararat stops at Havelock St/Neill St, a 3-minute walk away.
