Beaufort Community Resource Centre

Library in Beaufort · Basic info

Beaufort Community Resource Centre is a library in Beaufort, in the Pyrenees Shire Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. Havelock St/Neill St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 200 m away with a baby change table.
