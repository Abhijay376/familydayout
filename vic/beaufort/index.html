Beaufort in the Pyrenees area has 3 playgrounds and 8 parks and reserves in our data. Beyond the parks, Beaufort has a library, a pool and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Beaufort is Snowgums Bushland Reserve, covering about 27 hectares. Goldfields Recreation Reserve and Beaufort South Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Beaufort also has Beaufort Skate Park, Wotherspoon Reserve and Camp Hill Picnic Area. Playgrounds around Beaufort include the playground at Beaufort Skate Park and the playground at Wotherspoon Reserve. Our data shows 7 public toilets in Beaufort. In Beaufort you'll also find Beaufort Pool and Beaufort Community Resource Centre. For older kids in Beaufort there's the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park. Our data lists 3 cafes and family restaurants in Beaufort for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Beaufort has a train station: Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line: Ararat - Melbourne via Ballarat). Buses on the Ballarat - Stawell via Ararat route stop in Beaufort. V/Line coaches stop in Beaufort. By car from Beaufort, Ararat is about 35 minutes away, Ballarat about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a cinema, Astor Cinema in Ararat is about 34 minutes' drive from Beaufort. The nearest larger centre is Ararat, about 35 minutes from Beaufort by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Beaufort's 3 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the library in Beaufort. Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort.

Nearby suburbs

Nerring, Middle Creek, Main Lead and Lake Goldsmith also border Beaufort.
