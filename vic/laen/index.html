Laen is in the Buloke area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Laen, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Laen has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 94 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Laen, so most trips start by car. By car from Laen, Horsham is about 68 minutes away and Stawell about 71 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Donald Swimming Pool in Donald is about 24 minutes' drive from Laen. For a skate park, Donald BMX Track in Donald is about 26 minutes' drive from Laen. The closest places for kids by road from Laen are Guthrie Park Playground in Rich Avon West (16 min), the playground at Donald Public Park in Donald (24 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (24 min), Donald BMX Track in Donald (26 min), Minyip Swimming Pool in Minyip (28 min) and Woods' Farming and Heritage Museum in Rupanyup (44 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Laen.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Laen, Minyip has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Laen, Rich Avon West has 1 playground.
