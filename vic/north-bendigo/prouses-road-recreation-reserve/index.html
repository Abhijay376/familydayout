Prouses Road Recreation Reserve

Park in North Bendigo · Basic info

Prouses Road Recreation Reserve is a park in North Bendigo, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, good for all ages. Kalianna Special School/Nolan St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 602 m away.
