North Bendigo in the Greater Bendigo area has 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in our data. Beyond the parks, North Bendigo has 2 museums or galleries.

Landmarks

California Gully Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in North Bendigo, at about 48 hectares. Comet Hill Natural Features Reserve and Prouses Road Recreation Reserve are the next largest. Our data shows 2 public toilets in North Bendigo. In North Bendigo you'll also find Bendigo Joss House Temple and Bendigo Steam Engine Museum. For walks and rides, Bendigo Creek Trail (North) and Long Gully Trail cross North Bendigo.

Getting around

North Bendigo has no train station; Bendigo Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 2.4 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 50, 51 and 52 stop in North Bendigo. By car from North Bendigo, Bendigo is about 5 minutes away, Castlemaine about 37 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Faith Leech Aquatic Centre in Bendigo is about 4 minutes' drive from North Bendigo. For a library, Bendigo Library in Bendigo is about 4 minutes' drive from North Bendigo. The closest skate park to North Bendigo is the pump track on Water Street in Bendigo, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to North Bendigo is Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo, about 6 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 8 minutes' drive from North Bendigo. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 6 minutes' drive from North Bendigo. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to North Bendigo, about 5 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in North Bendigo. Free days on the Bendigo Creek Trail (North) and in North Bendigo's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Bendigo, next to North Bendigo, has 4 playgrounds, a pool and a library. California Gully, next to North Bendigo, has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to North Bendigo, White Hills has 1 playground and a pool. Long Gully, next to North Bendigo, has 4 playgrounds and a skate park. Jackass Flat also borders North Bendigo.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of North Bendigo, without traffic.
