Alexandra Gardens

Park in Melbourne · Basic info

Alexandra Gardens is a park in Melbourne, in the Melbourne City Council area, good for all ages. Arts Precinct/St Kilda Rd #14 (tram) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 178 m away with a baby change table.
