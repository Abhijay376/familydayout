RMIT Swanston Library

Library in Melbourne · Basic info

RMIT Swanston Library is a library in Melbourne, in the Melbourne City Council area, best for little ones and primary-school kids. RMIT University/Swanston St #7 (tram) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 184 m away with a baby change table.
