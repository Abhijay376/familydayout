ACMI-X

Cinema in Melbourne · Official details

ACMI-X is a cinema in Melbourne, in the Melbourne City Council area, good for school-age kids and teens. Federation Square/Swanston St #13 (tram) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 106 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: Mon–Fri 10am – 2.30pm; Mon – Fri 8.30am – 4.30pm*; Sat & Sun 10am – 4.30pm*.
