ACMI

Museum or gallery in Melbourne · Official details

ACMI is a museum or gallery in Melbourne, in the Melbourne City Council area, best for school-age kids. Swanston St/Flinders St #5 (tram) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 282 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: Mon–Fri 10am – 2.30pm; Mon – Fri 8.30am – 4.30pm*; Sat & Sun 10am – 4.30pm*.
