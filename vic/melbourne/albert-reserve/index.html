Albert Reserve

Park in Melbourne · Basic info

Albert Reserve is a park in Melbourne, in the Port Phillip City Council area, good for all ages. Commercial Rd/St Kilda Rd #25 (tram) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 579 m away.
