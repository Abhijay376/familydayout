Queen Victoria Gardens

Park in Melbourne · Basic info

Queen Victoria Gardens is a park in Melbourne, in the Melbourne City Council area, good for all ages. Arts Precinct/St Kilda Rd #14 (tram) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 160 m away.
