ArtPlay Playground

Playground in Melbourne · Basic info

ArtPlay Playground in Melbourne is a public playground in the Melbourne City area. The Main Yarra Trail is about 40 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 300 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and trams 35, 70 and 75 stop at Russell St/Flinders St #6, a 4-minute walk away.
