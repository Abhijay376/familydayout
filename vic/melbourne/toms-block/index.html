Tom's Block

Park in Melbourne · Basic info

Tom's Block is a park in Melbourne, in the Melbourne City Council area, good for all ages. Grant St-Police Memorial/St Kilda Rd #17 (tram) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 310 m away.
