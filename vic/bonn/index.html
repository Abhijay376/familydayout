Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Bonn, in the Campaspe area.

Landmarks

Bonn Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Bonn, at about 7 hectares.

Getting around

Bonn has no train station; Rochester Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 5.0 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bonn, so most trips start by car. By car from Bonn, Echuca is about 30 minutes away, Bendigo about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bonn is Memorial Pool in Rochester, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest library to Bonn is Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Bonn is Moama Echuca Community Skate Park in Echuca, about 33 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Bonn is Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca, about 30 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram is about 36 minutes' drive from Bonn. Echuca is the closest bigger centre to Bonn, about 30 minutes away by car, and has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Bonn are Memorial Pool in Rochester (7 min), the playground at Apex Park in Rochester (8 min), Campaspe Run Rural Discovery Centre in Elmore (15 min), Lockington Pool in Lockington (26 min), the playground at Echuca Lions Park in Echuca (28 min) and Holden Museum in Echuca (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bonn.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Bonn, Rochester has 1 playground and a pool. Elmore, next to Bonn, has 1 playground. Burnewang also borders Bonn.
