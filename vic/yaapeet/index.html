Our data lists 2 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in Yaapeet, in the Yarriambiack area.

Landmarks

Paradise Flora and Fauna Reserve is the largest mapped park in Yaapeet, at about 2,368 hectares. Yaapeet Bushland Reserve and Yaapeet Recreation Reserve are the next largest. Playgrounds in Yaapeet include the playground at Yaapeet Rec Reserve. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Yaapeet. The Desert Walk, Tyakil Nature Walk and Frews Plain Track all pass through Yaapeet.

Getting around

There's no train station in Yaapeet. The closest to Yaapeet is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 145 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Horsham - Hopetoun via Dimboola route stop in Yaapeet. By car from Yaapeet, Horsham is about 1.5 hours away and Swan Hill about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rainbow Pool in Rainbow is about 18 minutes' drive from Yaapeet. The closest places for kids by road from Yaapeet are Rainbow Pool in Rainbow (18 min), the playground at Hopetoun Recreation Reserve in Hopetoun (26 min) and Hopetoun Swimming Pool in Hopetoun (25 min).

Best for

Free days on the Desert Walk and in Yaapeet's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Yaapeet, Hopetoun has 4 playgrounds and a pool. Rainbow, next to Yaapeet, has 1 playground and a pool. Next door to Yaapeet, Patchewollock has 1 playground. Albacutya also borders Yaapeet.
