Playground on Woods Street

Playground in Yaapeet · Basic info

Playground on Woods Street is a public playground in Yaapeet, in the Yarriambiack Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. You'll find a public toilet about 460 m from the playground, and bus Horsham - Hopetoun via Dimboola stops at Woods St/Byrne St, a 7-minute walk away.
