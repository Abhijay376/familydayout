Mount Moriac in the Surf Coast area has 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Mount Moriac Recreation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Mount Moriac, at about 7 hectares. Playgrounds around Mount Moriac include the playground at Mount Moriac Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Mount Moriac.

Getting around

Mount Moriac has no train station; Waurn Ponds Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mount Moriac, so most trips start by car. By car from Mount Moriac, Geelong is about 20 minutes away, Torquay about 24 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 78 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mount Moriac is Leisurelink Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Grovedale, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest library to Mount Moriac is Waurn Ponds Library in Grovedale, about 13 minutes' drive. For a beach, Eastern Beach in Geelong is about 23 minutes' drive from Mount Moriac. For a skate park, Moriac Skate Park in Moriac is about 9 minutes' drive from Mount Moriac. For a cinema, Reading Cinemas in Grovedale is about 13 minutes' drive from Mount Moriac. The closest indoor play centre to Mount Moriac is Leisurelink Restaurant in Highton, about 14 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Tiger Moth World in Torquay is about 23 minutes' drive from Mount Moriac. Geelong is the closest bigger centre to Mount Moriac, about 20 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Mount Moriac are Moriac Skate Park in Moriac (9 min), Moriac Playground in Moriac (9 min), the bmx track at Newling Reserve in Moriac (9 min), Reading Cinemas in Grovedale (13 min), Leisurelink Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Grovedale (12 min) and Waurn Ponds Library in Grovedale (13 min).

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Mount Moriac, Mount Duneed has 12 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Mount Moriac, Waurn Ponds has 5 playgrounds and a library. Moriac, next to Mount Moriac, has 2 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Mount Moriac, Freshwater Creek has 2 playgrounds. Modewarre, Gnarwarre, Buckley and Barrabool also border Mount Moriac.
