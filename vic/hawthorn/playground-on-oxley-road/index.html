Playground on Oxley Road

Playground in Hawthorn · Basic info

Playground on Oxley Road is a public playground in Hawthorn, in the Boroondara City area. Hawthorn Library is about 60 m away, and Hawthorn Aquatic and Leisure Centre is about 480 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and tram 16 stops at Manningtree Rd/Glenferrie Rd #72, a 2-minute walk away.
