Dean Avenue Reserve

Park in Hawthorn · Basic info

Dean Avenue Reserve is a park in Hawthorn, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Oxley Rd/Auburn Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 494 m away.
