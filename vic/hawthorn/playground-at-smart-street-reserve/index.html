Playground at Smart Street Reserve

Playground in Hawthorn · Basic info

Playground at Smart Street Reserve in Hawthorn is a public playground in the Boroondara City area. The Elgin St Hawthorn Local Cycle Route is about 120 m away, and Playground at Fashoda Street Reserve is another option about 300 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 560 m from the playground, and tram 48 stops at Brook St/Church St #25, a 2-minute walk away.
