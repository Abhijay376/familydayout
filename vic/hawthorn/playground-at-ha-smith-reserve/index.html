Playground at HA Smith Reserve

Playground in Hawthorn · Basic info

This is a public playground in Hawthorn. The Gardiners Creek Trail passes about 50 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 320 m away. There's a public toilet about 400 m away, and tram 16 stops at Gardiner Rd/Glenferrie Rd #67, a 4-minute walk away.
