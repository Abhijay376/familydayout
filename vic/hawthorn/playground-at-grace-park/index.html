Playground at Grace Park

Playground in Hawthorn · Basic info

Playground at Grace Park is a public playground in Hawthorn, in the Boroondara City area. The Hawthorn-Kew Rail Trail is about 300 m away, and Hawthorn Aquatic and Leisure Centre is about 520 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 710 m from the playground, and bus 609 stops at Grace Park/Power St, a 3-minute walk away.
