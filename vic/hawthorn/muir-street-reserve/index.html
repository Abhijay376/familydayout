Muir Street Reserve

Park in Hawthorn · Basic info

Muir Street Reserve is a park in Hawthorn, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. River Bvd/Victoria St #25 (tram) is a 6-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 584 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed on a lead.
