Fashoda Street Reserve

Park in Hawthorn · Basic info

Fashoda Street Reserve is a park in Hawthorn, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Grace Park/Power St (bus) is a 6-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 490 m away and dogs are allowed on a lead.
