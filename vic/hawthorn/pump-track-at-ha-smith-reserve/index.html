Pump track at HA Smith Reserve

Skate, BMX or pump track in Hawthorn · Basic info

Pump track at HA Smith Reserve is a skate, bmx or pump track in Hawthorn, in the Boroondara City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Airedale Ave/Auburn Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 281 m away.
