Scotsburn Street Reserve

Park in Hawthorn · Basic info

Scotsburn Street Reserve is a park in Hawthorn, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Callantina Rd/Glenferrie Rd #68 (tram) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1036 m away.
