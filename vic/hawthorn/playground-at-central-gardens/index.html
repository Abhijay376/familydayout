Playground at Central Gardens

Playground in Hawthorn · Basic info

Playground at Central Gardens in Hawthorn is a public playground in the Boroondara City area. Swinburne Library is about 320 m away, and Gallery Elysium is about 360 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 80 m from the playground, and bus 624 stops at Auburn Station/Auburn Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
