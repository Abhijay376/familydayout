Playground at Fairview Park

Playground in Hawthorn · Basic info

Playground at Fairview Park is a public playground in Hawthorn, in the Boroondara City area. The Main Yarra Trail passes about 100 m away, and Burnley Library Building (914) is about 450 m away. There's a public toilet about 340 m away, and trams 70 and 75 stop at Power St/Riversdale Rd #29, a 4-minute walk away.
