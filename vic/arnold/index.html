Arnold in the Loddon area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Tarnagulla I105 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Arnold, at about 6 hectares. Arnold Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Arnold. The closest to Arnold is Raywood Railway Station (V/Line), about 35 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Arnold, so most trips start by car. By car from Arnold, Bendigo is about 40 minutes away, Maryborough about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Arnold is Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest library to Arnold is Kangaroo Flat Library in Kangaroo Flat, about 36 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Bendigo BMX Club in Eaglehawk is about 36 minutes' drive from Arnold. The closest cinema to Arnold is Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo, about 41 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 40 minutes' drive from Arnold. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Arnold is The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo, about 45 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 40 minutes from Arnold by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Arnold, the nearest places for kids by road are Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (21 min), Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (21 min), Westbury Boulevard Playspace in Maiden Gully (34 min), Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (34 min), Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (34 min) and Graemar Court Playspace in Kangaroo Flat (36 min).

Best for
