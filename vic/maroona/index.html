Maroona is in the Ararat area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Maroona Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Maroona, at about 11 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Maroona. The closest to Maroona is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Ararat - Lake Bolac via Willaura route stop in Maroona. By car from Maroona, Ararat is about 18 minutes away, Stawell about 45 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Maroona is Willaura Outdoor Pool in Willaura, about 16 minutes' drive. For a library, Ararat Library in Ararat is about 17 minutes' drive from Maroona. For a skate park, David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat is about 22 minutes' drive from Maroona. The closest cinema to Maroona is Astor Cinema in Ararat, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Maroona is Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell, about 41 minutes' drive. Ararat is the closest bigger centre to Maroona, about 18 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Maroona are Willaura Outdoor Pool in Willaura (16 min), Ararat Gallery TAMA in Ararat (17 min), Ararat Library in Ararat (17 min), Astor Cinema in Ararat (17 min), Langi Morgala Museum in Ararat (17 min) and Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat (17 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Maroona.

Nearby suburbs

Willaura, next to Maroona, has 1 playground and a pool. Rocky Point, Moyston, Langi Logan and Denicull Creek also border Maroona.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Maroona, without traffic.
