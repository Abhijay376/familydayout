Dunluce is in the Central Goldfields area, and our data lists 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Erskines Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Dunluce, at about 30 hectares. Wisemans Hill Bushland Reserve and Jennings Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Dunluce include Erskines No.2 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Dunluce. The closest to Dunluce is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dunluce, so most trips start by car. By car from Dunluce, Maryborough is about 36 minutes away, Bendigo about 73 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Dunluce is Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest library to Dunluce is Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough, about 36 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 36 minutes from Dunluce by car, with a library. From Dunluce, the nearest places for kids by road are Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (21 min), Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (21 min), the playground at Avoca Lions Park in Avoca (31 min), Courthouse in Avoca (31 min), Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (36 min) and the playground at Market Reserve in Carisbrook (43 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Dunluce.

Nearby suburbs

Dunolly, next to Dunluce, has a pool. Bealiba, next to Dunluce, has 1 playground. Natte Yallock, Mount Hooghly, Goldsborough and Archdale Junction also border Dunluce.
