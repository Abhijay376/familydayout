Whitlam Place

Park in Fitzroy · Basic info

Whitlam Place is a park in Fitzroy, in the Yarra City Council area, good for all ages. St David St/Brunswick St #15 (tram) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 47 m away with a baby change table.
