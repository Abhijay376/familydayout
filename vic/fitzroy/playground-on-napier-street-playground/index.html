Playground on Napier Street

Playground in Fitzroy · Basic info

Playground on Napier Street is a public playground in Fitzroy, in the Yarra City area. The Fairfield to CBD is about 70 m away, and Fitzroy Library is about 360 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 400 m from the playground, and tram 86 stops at Napier St/Gertrude St #14, a 2-minute walk away.
