Playground at Condell Reserve

Playground in Fitzroy · Basic info

This is a public playground in Fitzroy. The Fairfield to CBD is about 90 m away, and Fitzroy Library is about 130 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 180 m from the playground, and tram 86 stops at Charles St/Smith St #17, a 4-minute walk away.
