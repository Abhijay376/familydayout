Condell Reserve, Fitzroy

Park in Fitzroy · Basic info

Condell Reserve is a park in Fitzroy, in the Yarra City Council area, good for all ages. King William St/Brunswick St #14 (tram) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 136 m away with a baby change table.
