Playground at Garryowen Park

Playground in Fitzroy · Basic info

Playground at Garryowen Park in Fitzroy is a public playground in the Yarra City area. The Fairfield to CBD is about 280 m away, and Fitzroy Swimming Pool is about 400 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 410 m from the playground, and tram 96 stops at Rose St/Nicholson St #14, a 2-minute walk away.
