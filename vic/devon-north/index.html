Devon North in the Wellington area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Devon Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Devon North, at about 18 hectares. Tarra River Streamside Reserve and Jack River Streamside Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Devon North. The closest to Devon North is Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line), about 37 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Devon North, so most trips start by car. By car from Devon North, Traralgon is about 60 minutes away, Sale about 63 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Yarram Public Pool in Yarram is about 8 minutes' drive from Devon North. For a library, Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram is about 8 minutes' drive from Devon North. For a beach, Woodside_Beach in Woodside Beach is about 32 minutes' drive from Devon North. For a skate park, the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram is about 8 minutes' drive from Devon North. The nearest larger centre is Traralgon, about 60 minutes from Devon North by car, with 38 playgrounds and a pool. From Devon North, the nearest places for kids by road are Yarram Historical Society Museum in Yarram (7 min), the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram (8 min), Yarram Public Pool in Yarram (8 min), Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram (8 min), the playground at Memorial Park in Yarram (8 min) and Port Albert Maritime Museum in Port Albert (20 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Devon North.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Devon North, Yarram has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Macks Creek, Jack River, Calrossie and Alberton West also border Devon North.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Devon North, without traffic.
