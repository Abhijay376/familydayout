Playground at Tallygaroopna Recreation Reserve

Playground in Tallygaroopna · Basic info

Playground at Tallygaroopna Recreation Reserve in Tallygaroopna is a public playground in the Greater Shepparton City area. Playground at Larsen Reserve is another option about 320 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 530 m from the playground, and coaches stop at Victoria Rd/Goulburn Valley Hwy, a 10-minute walk away.
