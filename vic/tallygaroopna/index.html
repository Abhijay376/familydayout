Tallygaroopna is in the Greater Shepparton area, and our data lists 2 playgrounds in the suburb.

Landmarks

Playgrounds around Tallygaroopna include the playground at Larsen Reserve and the playground at Tallygaroopna Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet and 1 park or playground with a council BBQ close by in Tallygaroopna.

Getting around

Tallygaroopna has no train station; Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Tallygaroopna. By car from Tallygaroopna, Shepparton is about 16 minutes away, Benalla about 62 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Tallygaroopna, about 16 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.
