Playground at Larsen Reserve

Playground in Tallygaroopna · Official details

This is a neighbourhood playground in Tallygaroopna, looked after by Greater Shepparton City Council. Playground at Tallygaroopna Recreation Reserve is another option about 320 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 70 m away, and coaches stop at Victoria Rd/Goulburn Valley Hwy, a 4-minute walk away.
