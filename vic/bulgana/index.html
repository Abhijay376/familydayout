Bulgana is in the Northern Grampians area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bulgana, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Bulgana has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bulgana, so most trips start by car. By car from Bulgana, Stawell is about 28 minutes away, Ararat about 38 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell is about 27 minutes' drive from Bulgana. For a library, Learning Resource Centre in Ararat is about 42 minutes' drive from Bulgana. For a skate park, the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell is about 31 minutes' drive from Bulgana. For a cinema, Astor Cinema in Ararat is about 38 minutes' drive from Bulgana. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Bulgana is Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell, about 27 minutes' drive. Stawell is the closest bigger centre to Bulgana, about 28 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Bulgana are the playground at Great Western Memorial Park in Great Western (27 min), Gladstone Park Playground in Stawell (26 min), Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell (27 min), Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell (27 min), Railway Station Art and Crafts Gallery in Stawell (29 min) and the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bulgana.

Nearby suburbs

Great Western, next to Bulgana, has 1 playground. Concongella also borders Bulgana.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Bulgana, without traffic.
