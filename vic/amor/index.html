Amor is in the Baw Baw area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Amor, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

The Australian Alps Walking Track, Poverty Point Walk and Poverty Gully Walking Track all pass through Amor.

Getting around

There's no train station in Amor. The closest to Amor is Moe Railway Station (V/Line), about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Amor, so most trips start by car. By car from Amor, Traralgon is about 37 minutes away, Warragul about 58 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rawson Swimming Pool in Rawson is about 4 minutes' drive from Amor. For a library, Morwell Library in Morwell is about 41 minutes' drive from Amor. The closest skate park to Amor is Moe Skate Park in Moe, about 31 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 43 minutes' drive from Amor. The nearest larger centre is Traralgon, about 37 minutes from Amor by car, with 38 playgrounds and a pool. From Amor, the nearest places for kids by road are Rawson Swimming Pool in Rawson (4 min), the playground at Erica State Forest in Rawson (5 min), the playground at Croxford Park in Rawson (4 min), Fire station in Walhalla (16 min), Moe Skate Park in Moe (31 min) and Diving Pool in Moe (31 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Amor on the Australian Alps Walking Track.

Nearby suburbs

Rawson, next to Amor, has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Walhalla, next to Amor, has 1 playground. Erica, next to Amor, has 1 playground. Baw Baw also borders Amor.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Amor, without traffic.
