Playground at Alex Nelson Reserve

Playground in Springvale South · Official details

Playground at Alex Nelson Reserve in Springvale South is a public playground in the Greater Dandenong City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet about 610 m away, and buses 813 and 979 stop at Parker St/Athol Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
