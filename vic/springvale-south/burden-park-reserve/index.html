Burden Park Reserve

Park in Springvale South · Official details

Burden Park Reserve is a park in Springvale South, in the Greater Dandenong City Council area, good for all ages. Heatherton Rd/Springvale Rd (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 130 m away with a baby change table.

Questions parents ask

Where is Burden Park Reserve?
Burden Park Reserve is in Springvale South, Victoria.
Is Burden Park Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Burden Park Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 130 m away, with a baby change table.
How do I get to Burden Park Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Heatherton Rd/Springvale Rd (routes 813, 902), a 7-minute walk.
