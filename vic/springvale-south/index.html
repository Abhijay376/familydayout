Springvale South is in the Greater Dandenong area, and our data lists 6 playgrounds and 8 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Springvale South is Spring Valley Reserve, covering about 32 hectares. Burden Park Reserve and Alex Nelson Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Springvale South include Corio Reserve, Darren Reserve and Martin Street Reserve. Playgrounds around Springvale South include Burden Park Playground, the playground at Corio Reserve and the playground at Alex Nelson Reserve. Our data shows 6 public toilets, 9 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by and 1 off-leash dog park in Springvale South. Springvale South Shopping Village is the local shopping centre. For walks and rides, Dandenong South Trail, Dingley Bypass Trail and Bike: Westall - Braeside cross Springvale South. Our data lists 3 cafes and family restaurants in Springvale South for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Springvale South. The closest to Springvale South is Sandown Park Railway Station (metro), about 2.3 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 811, 813, 814, 824, 902 and 979 stop in Springvale South. By car from Springvale South, Dandenong is about 10 minutes away, Frankston about 20 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 30 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Springvale South is Noble Park Aquatic Centre in Noble Park, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest library to Springvale South is Springvale Library in Springvale, about 4 minutes' drive. For a beach, Edithvale Beach in Edithvale is about 11 minutes' drive from Springvale South. The closest skate park to Springvale South is Dingley Skate Park in Dingley Village, about 4 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Reading Cinema in Dandenong is about 11 minutes' drive from Springvale South.

Best for

Younger kids at Springvale South's 6 local playgrounds. Free days on the Dandenong South Trail and in Springvale South's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Springvale South, Keysborough has 32 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Springvale South, Noble Park has 19 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Springvale, next to Springvale South, has 16 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Springvale South, Dingley Village has 18 playgrounds and a skate park. Clayton South also borders Springvale South.
