Darren Reserve

Park in Springvale South · Basic info

Darren Reserve is a park in Springvale South, in the Greater Dandenong City Council area, good for all ages. Gwent St/Paterson Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 803 m away.
