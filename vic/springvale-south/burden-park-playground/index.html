Burden Park Playground

Playground in Springvale South · Official details

Burden Park Playground in Springvale South is a public playground in the Greater Dandenong City area. For food, McDonald's is about 230 m away, and Reconfigure Polygon - Playground - Amersham Avenue Reserve is another option about 580 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 90 m from the playground, and buses 813 and 902 stop at Heatherton Rd/Springvale Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
