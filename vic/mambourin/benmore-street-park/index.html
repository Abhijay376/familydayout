Benmore Street Park

Park in Mambourin · Basic info

Benmore Street Park is a park in Mambourin, in the Wyndham City Council area, good for all ages. Gardenvale Ave/Ison Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1241 m away.
