Undera in the Greater Shepparton area has 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Undera Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Undera, at about 1 hectares. Playgrounds around Undera include the playground at Undera Youth Spot. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Undera. For walks and rides, Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks crosses Undera.

Getting around

There's no train station in Undera. The closest to Undera is Mooroopna Railway Station (V/Line), about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Undera, so most trips start by car. By car from Undera, Shepparton is about 24 minutes away, Echuca about 43 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Undera is Community Swimming Pool in Merrigum, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest library to Undera is Tatura Library in Tatura, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Undera is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Undera is the skate park at Tatura Memorial Park in Tatura, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Undera is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 24 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 24 minutes' drive from Undera. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Undera is Kyabram Fauna Park in Kyabram, about 26 minutes' drive. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Undera, about 24 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Undera are the playground at Craigmuir Lake Park in Mooroopna (14 min), Tatura Irrigation and Wartime Camps Museum in Tatura (17 min), Tatura Library in Tatura (18 min), Mooroopna Library in Mooroopna (18 min), Community Swimming Pool in Merrigum (23 min) and the skate park at Tatura Memorial Park in Tatura (19 min).

Best for

Free days on the Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks and in Undera's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Bunbartha, next to Undera, has 1 playground. Lancaster, Kotupna, Kaarimba and Gillieston also border Undera.
