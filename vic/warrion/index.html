Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Warrion in the Colac Otway area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Warrion.

Getting around

There's no train station in Warrion. The closest to Warrion is Colac Railway Station (V/Line), about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Colac - Alvie via Warrion route stop in Warrion. By car from Warrion, Colac is about 21 minutes away, Geelong about 73 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac is about 24 minutes' drive from Warrion. For a skate park, the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt is about 26 minutes' drive from Warrion. For a cinema, Colac Cinemas in Colac is about 22 minutes' drive from Warrion. The closest indoor play centre to Warrion is Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac, about 22 minutes' drive. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Warrion, about 21 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Warrion, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Red Rock Scenic Reserve in Alvie (13 min), the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (20 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (22 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (22 min), Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (24 min) and the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Warrion.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Warrion, Alvie has 1 playground. Coragulac, Beeac and Balintore also border Warrion.
