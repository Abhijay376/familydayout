Waygara is in the East Gippsland area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb. On top of that, Waygara has a beach.

Landmarks

Ewing Morass Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Waygara, at about 6,820 hectares. In Waygara you'll also find Ewings Marsh. The East Gippsland Rail Trail passes through Waygara.

Getting around

There's no train station in Waygara. The closest to Waygara is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 62 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Waygara. By car from Waygara, Lakes Entrance is about 41 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 67 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Waygara is Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost, about 17 minutes' drive. For a library, Orbost Library in Orbost is about 16 minutes' drive from Waygara. Lakes Entrance is the closest bigger centre to Waygara, about 41 minutes away by car, and has 3 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a beach. The closest places for kids by road from Waygara are the playground at Forest Park in Orbost (16 min), Orbost Library in Orbost (16 min), Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost (17 min), Lakes Entrance Aquatic Centre in Lakes Entrance (38 min), the playground at Lakes Entrance - Lake Tyers Coastal Reserve (COM) in Lake Tyers Beach (45 min) and Lake Tyers Beach Sand Bar in Lake Tyers Beach (46 min).

Best for

Beach days at Ewings Marsh. Free days on the East Gippsland Rail Trail and in Waygara's parks.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Waygara, without traffic.
