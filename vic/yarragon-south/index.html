Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Yarragon South, in the Baw Baw area.

Landmarks

Warragul Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Yarragon South, at about 6 hectares.

Getting around

Yarragon South has no train station; Yarragon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 3.9 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yarragon South, so most trips start by car. By car from Yarragon South, Warragul is about 23 minutes away, Traralgon about 44 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Yarragon South is Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest library to Yarragon South is Warragul Library in Warragul, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Yarragon South is Warragul Skate Park in Warragul, about 21 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 21 minutes' drive from Yarragon South. The closest indoor play centre to Yarragon South is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Yarragon South is Wildlife Trail in Tynong North, about 42 minutes' drive. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Yarragon South, about 23 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Yarragon South are Yarragon Childrens Playground Reserve in Yarragon (11 min), Station Gallery in Yarragon (11 min), Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar (14 min), the playground at Brindlewood Reserve in Trafalgar (16 min), Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul (21 min) and Warragul Skate Park in Warragul (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Yarragon South.

Nearby suburbs

Trafalgar, next to Yarragon South, has 4 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Yarragon South, Yarragon has 2 playgrounds. Cloverlea, Childers, Allambee Reserve and Allambee also border Yarragon South.
