Towan in the Swan Hill area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Towan is Towan Bushland Reserve, covering about 44 hectares. Yarraby Flora Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Towan. The closest to Towan is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 36 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Towan, so most trips start by car. By car from Towan, Swan Hill is about 41 minutes away and Mildura about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Towan is Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill, about 40 minutes' drive. For a library, Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill is about 40 minutes' drive from Towan. For a skate park, the bmx track at Piangil Memorial Park in Piangil is about 20 minutes' drive from Towan. For a cinema, Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill is about 41 minutes' drive from Towan. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Towan, about 41 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Towan, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV) in Nyah (18 min), the playground at Nyah Heritage Park in Nyah (18 min), the bmx track at Piangil Memorial Park in Piangil (20 min), Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South (38 min), Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill (40 min) and Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill (40 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Towan.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Towan, Piangil has 2 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Towan, Nyah West has 1 playground. Miralie also borders Towan.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Towan, without traffic.
