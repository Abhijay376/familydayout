Krowera is in the Bass Coast area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Krowera, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Krowera.

Getting around

There's no train station in Krowera. The closest to Krowera is Longwarry Railway Station (V/Line), about 37 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Krowera, so most trips start by car. By car from Krowera, Wonthaggi is about 29 minutes away, Leongatha about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong is about 22 minutes' drive from Krowera. The closest library to Krowera is Poowong library in Poowong, about 22 minutes' drive. For a beach, Baxters Beach in Wonthaggi is about 36 minutes' drive from Krowera. For a skate park, Nyora skate park in Nyora is about 23 minutes' drive from Krowera. The closest cinema to Krowera is Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi, about 28 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville is about 24 minutes' drive from Krowera. The nearest larger centre is Wonthaggi, about 29 minutes from Krowera by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Krowera are the playground at Sunnyside Park in Loch (15 min), Nyora skate park in Nyora (23 min), the playground at Nyora Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Nyora (23 min), Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong (22 min), Poowong library in Poowong (22 min) and Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville (24 min).
