Ravenswood South is in the Mount Alexander area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Ravenswood South, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Ravenswood South. The closest to Ravenswood South is Castlemaine Railway Station (V/Line), about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Ravenswood South, so most trips start by car. By car from Ravenswood South, Castlemaine is about 11 minutes away, Bendigo about 22 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Ravenswood South is Harcourt Swimming Pool in Harcourt, about 6 minutes' drive. For a library, Castlemaine Library in Castlemaine is about 11 minutes' drive from Ravenswood South. For a skate park, BMX Track in Castlemaine is about 12 minutes' drive from Ravenswood South. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 22 minutes' drive from Ravenswood South. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 19 minutes' drive from Ravenswood South. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Ravenswood South is The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo, about 28 minutes' drive. Castlemaine is the closest bigger centre to Ravenswood South, about 11 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Ravenswood South, the nearest places for kids by road are Harcourt Swimming Pool in Harcourt (6 min), Botanical Gardens Playspace in Castlemaine (12 min), the playground at Castlemaine Botanic Garden in Castlemaine (12 min), Castlemaine Swimming Pool in Castlemaine (12 min), Castlemaine Library in Castlemaine (11 min) and Castlemaine CASPA in Castlemaine (12 min).
