Smoko is in the Alpine area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Smoko is Smoko Streamside Reserve, covering about 30 hectares. Smoko also has Smoko. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Smoko. Picnic areas in Smoko include Smoko. The Bright - Mount Beauty and Great Valley Trail all pass through Smoko.

Getting around

Smoko has no train station; Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 82 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Smoko, so most trips start by car. By car from Smoko, Bright is about 15 minutes away and Wangaratta about 82 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Smoko is Bright Splash Park in Bright, about 14 minutes' drive. For a library, Bright Library in Bright is about 15 minutes' drive from Smoko. The closest beach to Smoko is Pebble Beach in Tawonga South, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Smoko is Bright BMX Track in Bright, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Smoko is Sun Cinema Bright in Bright, about 14 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bright, about 15 minutes from Smoko by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Smoko are the playground at Tavare Park in Harrietville (8 min), Harrietville Museum in Harrietville (8 min), Bright Art Gallery in Bright (13 min), Bright Splash Park in Bright (14 min), Sun Cinema Bright in Bright (14 min) and the playground at Centenary Park in Bright (15 min).

Best for

Free days on the Bright - Mount Beauty and in Smoko's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Smoko, Tawonga South has a beach. Next door to Smoko, Harrietville has 2 playgrounds. Hotham Heights, next to Smoko, has 1 playground. Freeburgh and Buckland also border Smoko.
