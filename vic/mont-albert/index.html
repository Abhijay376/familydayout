Mont Albert in the Whitehorse area has 6 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Mont Albert is Kingsley Gardens, covering about 3 hectares. Mont Albert also has Windsor Reserve and Lorne Parade Reserve. Playgrounds around Mont Albert include the playground at Windsor Reserve, the playground at Kingsley Gardens and the playground at Lorne Parade Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Mont Albert. For walks and rides, Whitehorse EW4 and Whitehorse NS1 cross Mont Albert. Our data lists 4 cafes and family restaurants in Mont Albert for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Union Railway Station (metro: Belgrave Line and Lilydale Line) in Mont Albert. Tram 109 stops in Mont Albert. Buses 284, 766 and 767 stop in Mont Albert. By car from Mont Albert, Box Hill is about 3 minutes away, Ringwood about 14 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 19 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Aqualink Box Hill in Box Hill is about 3 minutes' drive from Mont Albert. The closest library to Mont Albert is Box Hill Library in Box Hill, about 3 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Mont Albert is Horse Arena in Abbotsford, about 15 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Box Hill South Skate Park in Box Hill South is about 5 minutes' drive from Mont Albert. The closest cinema to Mont Albert is Palace Cinemas in Balwyn, about 4 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, BOUNCEinc in Blackburn North is about 7 minutes' drive from Mont Albert. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Mont Albert is Collingwood Children's Farm in Abbotsford, about 15 minutes' drive.

Best for

Younger kids at Mont Albert's 6 local playgrounds. Free days on the Whitehorse EW4 and in Mont Albert's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Mont Albert, Box Hill has 11 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Balwyn, next to Mont Albert, has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. Surrey Hills, next to Mont Albert, has 11 playgrounds. Box Hill North, next to Mont Albert, has 11 playgrounds. Mont Albert North also borders Mont Albert.
