Playground at Kingsley Gardens

Playground in Mont Albert · Basic info

Playground at Kingsley Gardens is a public playground in Mont Albert, in the Whitehorse City area. Chinese Restaurant Land Kitchen is about 480 m away if you need food, and Playground on Serpentine Street is another option about 480 m away. There's a public toilet about 980 m away, and bus 284 stops at Nangnak Lane/Whitehorse Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
