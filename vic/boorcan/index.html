Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Boorcan in the Corangamite area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Boorcan. The closest to Boorcan is Terang Railway Station (V/Line), about 9.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Boorcan, so most trips start by car. By car from Boorcan, Colac is about 47 minutes away, Warrnambool about 48 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon is about 36 minutes' drive from Boorcan. For a library, Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake is about 22 minutes' drive from Boorcan. For a skate park, Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown is about 12 minutes' drive from Boorcan. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Boorcan is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 34 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 47 minutes from Boorcan by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Boorcan, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve in Camperdown (18 min), Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (12 min), Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake (22 min), the playground at Tea Tree Lake Reserve in Mortlake (22 min), Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake (22 min) and Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Boorcan.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Boorcan, Terang has 1 playground. Glenormiston South, Glenormiston North, Dixie and Cobrico also border Boorcan.
