Drummartin in the Greater Bendigo area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Drummartin is Drummartin Bushland Reserve, covering about 1 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Drummartin. The closest to Drummartin is Goornong Railway Station (V/Line), about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Drummartin, so most trips start by car. By car from Drummartin, Bendigo is about 39 minutes away, Echuca about 49 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood is about 22 minutes' drive from Drummartin. The closest library to Drummartin is Bendigo Library in Bendigo, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Drummartin is McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Drummartin is Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo, about 40 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Drummartin is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 42 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Drummartin is The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo, about 36 minutes' drive. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Drummartin, about 39 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Drummartin, the nearest places for kids by road are Campaspe Run Rural Discovery Centre in Elmore (15 min), Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood (22 min), the playground at Pennington Reserve in Huntly (22 min), Lockington Pool in Lockington (25 min), the playground at Epsom Village Green in Epsom (30 min) and Bendigo Joss House Temple in North Bendigo (36 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Drummartin.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Drummartin, Elmore has 1 playground. Hunter also borders Drummartin.
