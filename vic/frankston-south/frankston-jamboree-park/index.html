Frankston Jamboree Park

Park in Frankston South · Basic info

Frankston Jamboree Park is a park in Frankston South, in the Frankston City Council area, good for all ages. The Range/Kars St (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 984 m away.
