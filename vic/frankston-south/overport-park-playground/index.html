Overport Park

Playground in Frankston South · Basic info

Overport Park in Frankston South is a public playground in the Frankston City area. The Kookaburra Bush Circuit is about 390 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and buses 772 and 773 stop at Overton Park/157 Overport Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
