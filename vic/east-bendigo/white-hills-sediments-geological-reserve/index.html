White Hills Sediments Geological Reserve

Park in East Bendigo · Basic info

White Hills Sediments Geological Reserve is a park in East Bendigo, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, good for all ages. Grace St/Napier St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away.
