The Zone Fun Park

Zoo, farm or wildlife park in East Bendigo · Official details

The Zone Fun Park is a zoo, farm or wildlife park in East Bendigo, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, good for all ages. Strickland Rd/Murphy St (bus) is a 11-minute walk away. Opening hours: Come in to The Zone at 8:30am every Saturday to learn some beginner skating tips and tricks.; On Wednesday the 28th of October we are open 10:30am-5:00pm and have an amazing all day pass!; This deal runs from 3:30pm to 7:30pm every Tuesday!!.
