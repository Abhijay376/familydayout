Ormond in the Glen Eira area has 3 playgrounds and 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Ormond is E.E. Gunn Reserve, covering about 6 hectares. Joyce Park is the next largest. Playgrounds in Ormond include the playground at Joyce Park, the playground at E.E Gunn Reserve and the playground at E.E Gunn Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Ormond. The Rosstown Rail Trail and Caulfield-Frankston Rail Trail all pass through Ormond. Our data lists 10 cafes and family restaurants in Ormond for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Ormond Railway Station (metro: Frankston Line) in Ormond. Buses 625, 626 and 630 stop in Ormond. By car from Ormond, Box Hill is about 21 minutes away, Dandenong about 22 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 19 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Diving Pool in Carnegie is about 4 minutes' drive from Ormond. The closest library to Ormond is Bentleigh Library in Bentleigh, about 4 minutes' drive. For a beach, Sandown Street Beach in Brighton is about 8 minutes' drive from Ormond. For a skate park, Bailey Reserve Skate Park in Bentleigh East is about 4 minutes' drive from Ormond. For a cinema, Palace Brighton Bay in Brighton is about 7 minutes' drive from Ormond. For an indoor play centre, Zone Bowling in Clayton is about 12 minutes' drive from Ormond. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Ormond is Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale, about 8 minutes' drive. Box Hill is the closest bigger centre to Ormond, about 21 minutes away by car, and has 11 playgrounds, a pool and a library.

Best for

Younger kids at Ormond's 3 local playgrounds. Free days on the Rosstown Rail Trail and in Ormond's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Ormond, Bentleigh East has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Brighton East, next to Ormond, has 7 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Ormond, Carnegie has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. McKinnon, next to Ormond, has 3 playgrounds. Glen Huntly and Caulfield South also border Ormond.
