Joyce Park

Park in Ormond · Official details

Joyce Park is a park in Ormond, in the Glen Eira City Council area, good for all ages. Tyrone St/North Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 65 m away.

Questions parents ask

Where is Joyce Park?
Joyce Park is in Ormond, Victoria.
Is Joyce Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Joyce Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 65 m away.
How do I get to Joyce Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Tyrone St/North Rd (routes 630), a 5-minute walk.
