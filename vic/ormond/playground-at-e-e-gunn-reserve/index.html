Playground at E.E. Gunn Reserve

Playground in Ormond · Basic info

Playground at E.E. Gunn Reserve is a public playground in Ormond, in the Glen Eira City area. The Rosstown Rail Trail passes about 180 m away, and Mr Brightside is about 290 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 150 m away, and bus 625 stops at Booran Rd/Eumeralla Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
