Birdwoodton is in the Mildura area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Birdwoodton, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Birdwoodton has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 187 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the 250/300 route stop in Birdwoodton. By car from Birdwoodton, Mildura is about 13 minutes away and Swan Hill about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Merbein Pool in Merbein is about 4 minutes' drive from Birdwoodton. For a library, Merbein Library in Merbein is about 3 minutes' drive from Birdwoodton. For a beach, Apex Beach in Mildura is about 21 minutes' drive from Birdwoodton. The closest skate park to Birdwoodton is Merbein Skate Park in Merbein, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Birdwoodton is Deakin Cinema Complex in Mildura, about 13 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Laserpunk Laserforce in Mildura is about 13 minutes' drive from Birdwoodton. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Birdwoodton is Putt Putt Family Fun Centre in Mildura, about 13 minutes' drive. Mildura is the closest bigger centre to Birdwoodton, about 13 minutes away by car, and has 26 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Birdwoodton, the nearest places for kids by road are Merbein Library in Merbein (3 min), Merbein Pool in Merbein (4 min), the playground at Chaffey Park in Merbein (6 min), Merbein Skate Park in Merbein (7 min), the playground at Hollywood Boulevard Drainage Reserve in Mildura (9 min) and The RAAF Air Museum in Mildura (10 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Birdwoodton.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Birdwoodton, Mildura has 26 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Merbein, next to Birdwoodton, has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Cabarita also borders Birdwoodton.
