Waterways in the Kingston area has 3 playgrounds and 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Waterways is Braeside Linear Reserve, covering about 1 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Waterways. For walks and rides, Mordialloc Freeway Trail and Mordialloc - Waterways - Braeside cross Waterways.

Getting around

There's no train station in Waterways. The closest to Waterways is Aspendale Railway Station (metro), about 2.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 709 and 902 stop in Waterways. By car from Waterways, Dandenong is about 12 minutes away, Frankston about 15 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 34 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Grenda Aquatic Centre in Keysborough is about 6 minutes' drive from Waterways. For a library, Newlands Library & Resource Centre in Keysborough is about 5 minutes' drive from Waterways. The closest beach to Waterways is Edithvale Beach in Edithvale, about 6 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Chelsea Velodrome in Edithvale is about 5 minutes' drive from Waterways. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Cheltenham is about 14 minutes' drive from Waterways. For an indoor play centre, Gravity Zone Indoor Trampoline Centre in Seaford is about 10 minutes' drive from Waterways. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Waterways is Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale, about 20 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Dandenong, about 12 minutes from Waterways by car, with 21 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Waterways's 3 local playgrounds. Free days on the Mordialloc Freeway Trail and in Waterways's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Keysborough, next to Waterways, has 32 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Aspendale Gardens, next to Waterways, has 7 playgrounds. Braeside, next to Waterways, has 2 playgrounds. Bangholme, next to Waterways, has 1 playground.
