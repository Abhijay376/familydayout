Braeside Linear Reserve

Park in Waterways · Basic info

Braeside Linear Reserve is a park in Waterways, in the Kingston City Council area, good for all ages. Governor Rd/Burdekin Bvd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 669 m away.
