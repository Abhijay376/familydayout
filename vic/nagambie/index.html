Nagambie is in the Strathbogie area, and our data lists 5 playgrounds and 2 parks and reserves in the suburb. Nagambie also has a library, a pool and a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Nagambie is Monea North Bushland Reserve, covering about 6 hectares. Glass Square Reserve is the next largest. Playgrounds in Nagambie include Buckley Park Playground, River Street Playground and Blayney Reserve Playground. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Nagambie. In Nagambie you'll also find Nagambie Historical Society, Nagambie Pool and Nagambie Library. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Nagambie for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Nagambie has a train station: Nagambie Railway Station (V/Line: Shepparton - Melbourne via Seymour). V/Line coaches stop in Nagambie. By car from Nagambie, Seymour is about 24 minutes away, Shepparton about 44 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a skate park, Seymour Skate Park in Seymour is about 25 minutes' drive from Nagambie. For a cinema, Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa is about 44 minutes' drive from Nagambie. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Nagambie is Emerald Bank Leisure Land in Kialla, about 37 minutes' drive.

Best for

Younger kids at Nagambie's 5 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the library and museum or gallery in Nagambie.

Nearby suburbs

Longwood, next to Nagambie, has 1 playground. Mitchellstown, Locksley, Kirwans Bridge and Bailieston also border Nagambie.
