Mackie Road Reserve

Park in Bentleigh East · Official details

Mackie Road Reserve is a park in Bentleigh East, in the Glen Eira City Council area, good for all ages. Mackie Rd Reserve/Mackie Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 50 m away and dogs are allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Mackie Road Reserve?
Mackie Road Reserve is in Bentleigh East, Victoria.
Is Mackie Road Reserve free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Mackie Road Reserve?
Yes, dogs are allowed.
Are there toilets at Mackie Road Reserve?
The nearest public toilet is about 50 m away.
How do I get to Mackie Road Reserve by public transport?
The closest stop is Mackie Rd Reserve/Mackie Rd (routes 701, 767), a 2-minute walk.
