Centenary Park

Park in Bentleigh East · Official details

Centenary Park is a park in Bentleigh East, in the Glen Eira City Council area, good for all ages. St Bede's College/Bignell Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 209 m away with a baby change table and dogs are not allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Centenary Park?
Centenary Park is in Bentleigh East, Victoria.
Is Centenary Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Centenary Park?
No, dogs are not allowed.
Are there toilets at Centenary Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 209 m away, with a baby change table.
How do I get to Centenary Park by public transport?
The closest stop is St Bede's College/Bignell Rd (routes 767), a 2-minute walk.
