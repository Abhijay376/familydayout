Orange Street Park

Park in Bentleigh East · Basic info

Orange Street Park is a park in Bentleigh East, in the Glen Eira City Council area, good for all ages. Brooks St/Mackie Rd (bus) is a 7-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 709 m away and dogs are allowed on a lead.
