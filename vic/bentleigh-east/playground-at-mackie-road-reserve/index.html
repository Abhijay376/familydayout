Playground at Mackie Road Reserve

Playground in Bentleigh East · Official details

This is a public playground in Bentleigh East. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet about 70 m away, and buses 701 and 767 stop at Mackie Rd Reserve/Mackie Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
