Playground at Bailey Reserve

Playground in Bentleigh East · Basic info

This is a public playground in Bentleigh East. Older kids have a skate park about 220 m away, and Glen Eira Sports and Aquatic Centre is about 310 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 240 m from the playground, and bus 822 stops at Gardeners Rd/Quinns Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
