Cormick Street Reserve

Park in Bentleigh East · Basic info

Cormick Street Reserve is a park in Bentleigh East, in the Glen Eira City Council area, good for all ages. Centre Rd/Mackie Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 630 m away and dogs are allowed on a lead.
