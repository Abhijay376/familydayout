Playground on Colin Street

Playground in Bentleigh East · Basic info

Playground on Colin Street in Bentleigh East is a public playground in the Glen Eira City area. For food, Pizza Hut is about 440 m away, and Playground at Leckie Street Reserve is another option about 490 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 630 m from the playground, and bus 627 stops at Neville St/Tucker Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
