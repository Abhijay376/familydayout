Playground on Kalimna Street

Playground in Bentleigh East · Basic info

Playground on Kalimna Street is a public playground in Bentleigh East, in the Glen Eira City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 822 stops at King George VI Memorial Reserve/East Boundary Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
