Playground on Vine Court

Playground in Bentleigh East · Basic info

Playground on Vine Court in Bentleigh East is a public playground in the Glen Eira City area. Older kids have a skate park about 380 m away, and Glen Eira Sports and Aquatic Centre is about 380 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 880 m away, and bus 627 stops at Glen Eira Sports and Aquatic Centre/East Boundary Rd, a 10-minute walk away.
