Nina Reserve

Park in Bentleigh East · Basic info

Nina Reserve is a park in Bentleigh East, in the Glen Eira City Council area, good for all ages. Nina Ct/South Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 899 m away.
