Annie and Arthur Abrahams Reserve

Park in Bentleigh East · Official details

Annie and Arthur Abrahams Reserve is a park in Bentleigh East, in the Glen Eira City Council area, good for all ages. Baker St/North Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 526 m away and dogs are allowed on a lead.
