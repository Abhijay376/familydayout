Bailey Reserve Skate Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Bentleigh East · Official details

Bailey Reserve Skate Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Bentleigh East, in the Glen Eira City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Glen Eira Sports and Aquatic Centre/East Boundary Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 114 m away and dogs are not allowed.
