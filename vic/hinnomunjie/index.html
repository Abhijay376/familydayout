Hinnomunjie is in the East Gippsland area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Hinnomunjie, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Hinnomunjie. The closest to Hinnomunjie is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 91 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Hinnomunjie, so most trips start by car. By car from Hinnomunjie, Bright is about 2 hours away and Bairnsdale about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Hinnomunjie is Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Hinnomunjie is the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo, about 12 minutes' drive. From Hinnomunjie, the nearest places for kids by road are Petersens Art Gallery & Museum in Omeo (11 min), the playground at Omeo Historical Park & Museum in Omeo (11 min), Old Omeo Courthouse in Omeo (12 min), the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo (12 min) and Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Hinnomunjie.

Nearby suburbs
