Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Tatong, in the Benalla area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tatong is Ryan Creek Reference Area, covering about 327 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Tatong include Tatong Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Tatong has no train station; Benalla Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tatong, so most trips start by car. By car from Tatong, Benalla is about 30 minutes away and Mansfield about 54 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla is about 30 minutes' drive from Tatong. The closest skate park to Tatong is Pump Track in Benalla, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Tatong is Swanpool Cinema in Swanpool, about 24 minutes' drive. Benalla is the closest bigger centre to Tatong, about 30 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Tatong are Swanpool Cinema in Swanpool (24 min), Benalla Aviation Museum in Benalla (29 min), Benalla Migrant Camp in Benalla (30 min), Pump Track in Benalla (30 min), Benalla Splash Park in Benalla (30 min) and Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tatong.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Tatong, Benalla has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Tolmie, next to Tatong, has 1 playground. Myrrhee, next to Tatong, has 1 playground. Samaria, Moorngag, Molyullah and Greta South also border Tatong.
