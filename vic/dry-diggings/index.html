Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Dry Diggings in the Hepburn area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Goldfields Track and Dry Diggings Track cross Dry Diggings.

Getting around

There's no train station in Dry Diggings. The closest to Dry Diggings is Castlemaine Railway Station (V/Line), about 25 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dry Diggings, so most trips start by car. By car from Dry Diggings, Daylesford is about 9 minutes away, Castlemaine about 26 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Dry Diggings is Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford, about 9 minutes' drive. For a library, Daylesford Library in Daylesford is about 9 minutes' drive from Dry Diggings. For a beach, Canoeing in Tylden is about 43 minutes' drive from Dry Diggings. For a skate park, Bike Jumps in Castlemaine is about 25 minutes' drive from Dry Diggings. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Dry Diggings is Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek, about 41 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Daylesford, about 9 minutes from Dry Diggings by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Dry Diggings, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Hepburn Regional Park in Daylesford (7 min), Daylesford Spa Country Railway in Daylesford (8 min), Daylesford Library in Daylesford (9 min), Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford (9 min), the playground at J P Gleeson Memorial Park in Lyonville (19 min) and Newstead Swimming Pool in Newstead (26 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Dry Diggings on the Goldfields Track.

Nearby suburbs

Daylesford, next to Dry Diggings, has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Elevated Plains also borders Dry Diggings.
