Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Drummond North, in the Hepburn area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Drummond North is Fryers Ridge Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 2,022 hectares. For walks and rides, Coliban Main Channel Walking Trail crosses Drummond North.

Getting around

Drummond North has no train station; Malmsbury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 4.7 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Drummond North, so most trips start by car. By car from Drummond North, Kyneton is about 22 minutes away, Daylesford about 32 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 87 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Drummond North is Chewton Swimming Pool in Chewton, about 29 minutes' drive. For a library, Kyneton Library in Kyneton is about 23 minutes' drive from Drummond North. The closest beach to Drummond North is Canoeing in Tylden, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Drummond North is Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton, about 23 minutes' drive. Kyneton is the closest bigger centre to Drummond North, about 22 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Drummond North are the playground at Malmsbury Botanical Gardens in Malmsbury (11 min), the playground at Taradale Mineral Springs Reserve in Taradale (18 min), Kyneton Museum in Kyneton (21 min), Kyneton Library in Kyneton (23 min), Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton (23 min) and Chewton Swimming Pool in Chewton (29 min).

Best for

Free days on the Coliban Main Channel Walking Trail and in Drummond North's parks.

Nearby suburbs
