Gazette is in the Southern Grampians area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Gazette, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Gazette. The closest to Gazette is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 59 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gazette, so most trips start by car. By car from Gazette, Hamilton is about 32 minutes away and Warrnambool about 65 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Gazette is Penshurst Swimming Pool in Penshurst, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest library to Gazette is Hamilton Library in Hamilton, about 31 minutes' drive. For a skate park, hamilton skate park in Hamilton is about 30 minutes' drive from Gazette. The closest cinema to Gazette is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 31 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 32 minutes from Gazette by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Gazette, the nearest places for kids by road are Penshurst Botanic Gardens Playground in Penshurst (11 min), Penshurst Swimming Pool in Penshurst (11 min), Volcanoes Discovery Centre in Penshurst (11 min), Penshurst Rec Reserve Playground in Penshurst (13 min), Sir Reginald Ansett Transport Museum in Hamilton (30 min) and hamilton skate park in Hamilton (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Gazette.

Nearby suburbs

Penshurst, next to Gazette, has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Mount Napier, Gerrigerrup, Byaduk and Buckley Swamp also border Gazette.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Gazette, without traffic.
