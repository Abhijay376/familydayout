Boralma in the Wangaratta area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Boralma is Carraragarmungee H111 Bushland Reserve, covering about 14 hectares. Bontherambo H112 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Boralma. The closest to Boralma is Springhurst Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.7 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Boralma, so most trips start by car. By car from Boralma, Wangaratta is about 22 minutes away and Wodonga about 42 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Boralma is Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta, about 23 minutes' drive. For a library, Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta is about 20 minutes' drive from Boralma. For a beach, Sandy Beach in Beechworth is about 39 minutes' drive from Boralma. The closest skate park to Boralma is the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta, about 23 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 19 minutes' drive from Boralma. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Boralma, about 22 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Boralma, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Apex Park in Wangaratta (18 min), Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta (19 min), the playground at King George V Gardens in Wangaratta (19 min), Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (20 min), Eldorado Museum in Eldorado (23 min) and Chiltern Athenaeum Museum in Chiltern (19 min).
