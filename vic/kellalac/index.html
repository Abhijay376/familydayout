Kellalac in the Yarriambiack area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Kellalac Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Kellalac, at about 30 hectares.

Getting around

Kellalac has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 111 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kellalac, so most trips start by car. By car from Kellalac, Horsham is about 33 minutes away and Stawell about 80 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kellalac is Minyip Swimming Pool in Minyip, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest library to Kellalac is Dimboola Library in Dimboola, about 36 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Rabl Park in Murtoa is about 30 minutes' drive from Kellalac. For a cinema, Centre Cinemas in Horsham is about 34 minutes' drive from Kellalac. The nearest larger centre is Horsham, about 33 minutes from Kellalac by car, with 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Kellalac are Wheatlands Warracknabeal Agricultural Machinery Museum in Warracknabeal (11 min), the playground at Anzac Park in Warracknabeal (12 min), Historical Centre in Warracknabeal (14 min), the playground at Apex Park in Warracknabeal (13 min), Minyip Swimming Pool in Minyip (28 min) and the skate park at Rabl Park in Murtoa (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Kellalac.
