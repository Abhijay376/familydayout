Curyo in the Buloke area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Curyo I188 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Curyo, at about 17 hectares. Curyo I295 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Curyo. The closest to Curyo is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 90 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Curyo, so most trips start by car. By car from Curyo, Swan Hill is about 87 minutes away and Horsham about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Curyo is Woomelang Recreation Reserve Swimming Pool in Woomelang, about 21 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Birchip Skate Park in Birchip is about 21 minutes' drive from Curyo. The closest places for kids by road from Curyo are the playground at Woomelang Rec Reserve in Woomelang (20 min), Birchip History Museum in Birchip (21 min), the playground at Birchip Shire Soldiers Memorial Park in Birchip (20 min), Birchip Skate Park in Birchip (21 min), Woomelang Recreation Reserve Swimming Pool in Woomelang (21 min) and Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock (35 min).
