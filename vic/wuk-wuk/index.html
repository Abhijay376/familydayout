Wuk Wuk is in the East Gippsland area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Wuk Wuk, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Wuk Wuk has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wuk Wuk, so most trips start by car. By car from Wuk Wuk, Bairnsdale is about 26 minutes away and Sale about 53 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Wuk Wuk is Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest library to Wuk Wuk is Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Wuk Wuk is Boyfriend Beach in Forge Creek, about 42 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Gilsenan Reserve in Paynesville is about 41 minutes' drive from Wuk Wuk. For a cinema, Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale is about 27 minutes' drive from Wuk Wuk. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Wuk Wuk is Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow, about 33 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bairnsdale, about 26 minutes from Wuk Wuk by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Wuk Wuk, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Amy Shand Park in Lindenow (8 min), Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale (26 min), Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale (27 min), Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale (28 min), Krowathunkoolong Keeping Place in Bairnsdale (28 min) and Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wuk Wuk.

Nearby suburbs

Lindenow, next to Wuk Wuk, has 1 playground. Iguana Creek, Flaggy Creek and Calulu also border Wuk Wuk.
