Tavare Park

Park in Harrietville · Basic info

Tavare Park is a park in Harrietville, in the Alpine Shire Council area, good for all ages. Pioneer Park/Great Alpine Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 411 m away.
