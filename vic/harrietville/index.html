Our data lists 2 playgrounds and 4 parks and reserves in Harrietville, in the Alpine area. Harrietville also has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Harrietville is Tavare Park, covering about 1 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Harrietville include Pioneer Park, Tronoh Dredge Reserve and Clarrie Keating Memorial Park. Playgrounds around Harrietville include the playground at Tavare Park. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Harrietville. In Harrietville you'll also find Harrietville Museum. For walks and rides, Great Valley Trail, Bon Accord Walking Track and Northwest Spur Track cross Harrietville. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Harrietville for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Harrietville has no train station; Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 89 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Omeo - Bright via Hotham Heights route stop in Harrietville. By car from Harrietville, Bright is about 25 minutes away and Wangaratta about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bright Splash Park in Bright is about 24 minutes' drive from Harrietville. The closest library to Harrietville is Bright Library in Bright, about 25 minutes' drive. For a beach, Pebble Beach in Tawonga South is about 42 minutes' drive from Harrietville. The closest skate park to Harrietville is Bright BMX Track in Bright, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Harrietville is Sun Cinema Bright in Bright, about 24 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bright, about 25 minutes from Harrietville by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Harrietville, the nearest places for kids by road are Bright Art Gallery in Bright (24 min), Bright Splash Park in Bright (24 min), Sun Cinema Bright in Bright (24 min), the playground at Centenary Park in Bright (25 min), Bright Library in Bright (25 min) and Bright BMX Track in Bright (26 min).
