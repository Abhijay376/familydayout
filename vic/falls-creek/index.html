Landmarks

Our data shows 9 public toilets in Falls Creek. Places to visit in Falls Creek include Falls Creek Museum. The Australian Alps Walking Track, Falls to Hotham Alpine Crossing and Grey Hills Track all pass through Falls Creek. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Falls Creek for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Falls Creek has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 92 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Falls Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Falls Creek, Bright is about 62 minutes away and Wodonga about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Mt Beauty Library in Mount Beauty is about 34 minutes' drive from Falls Creek. The closest beach to Falls Creek is Pebble Beach in Tawonga South, about 37 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Mount Beauty BMX Bike Track in Mount Beauty is about 34 minutes' drive from Falls Creek. The closest places for kids by road from Falls Creek are Mount Beauty BMX Bike Track in Mount Beauty (34 min), Mt Beauty Library in Mount Beauty (34 min) and Pebble Beach in Tawonga South (37 min).
