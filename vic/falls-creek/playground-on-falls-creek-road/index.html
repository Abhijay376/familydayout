Playground on Falls Creek Road

Playground in Falls Creek · Basic info

Playground on Falls Creek Road is a public playground in Falls Creek, in the Alpine Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 60 m away, and Alpine Pepper Cafe is about 480 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 840 m away. It's easiest by car, about 2 minutes from Falls Creek.
