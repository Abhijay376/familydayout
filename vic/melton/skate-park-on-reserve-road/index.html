Skate park on Reserve Road

Skate, BMX or pump track in Melton · Basic info

Skate park on Reserve Road is a skate, bmx or pump track in Melton, in the Melton City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. McKenzie St/Smith St (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 512 m away.
