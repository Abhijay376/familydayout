Boolarra Skate Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Boolarra · Basic info

Boolarra Skate Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Boolarra, in the Latrobe City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Boolarra Terminus/Tarwin St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 208 m away with a baby change table.
