Playground at Railway Park

Playground in Boolarra · Basic info

Playground at Railway Park in Boolarra is a public playground in the Latrobe City area. The Grand Ridge Rail Trail is about 20 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 180 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus 4 stops at Boolarra Terminus/Tarwin St, a 1-minute walk away.
