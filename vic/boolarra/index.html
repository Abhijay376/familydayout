Our data lists 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in Boolarra, in the Latrobe area. Boolarra also has a museum or gallery and 2 skate parks.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Boolarra is Railway Park, covering about 1 hectares. Boolarra also has Old Mill Site and Boolarra Memorial Park. Playgrounds in Boolarra include the playground at Railway Park. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Boolarra. Picnic areas in Boolarra include Old Mill Site. In Boolarra you'll also find Boolarra Old School Museum. For older kids in Boolarra there's Boolarra BMX track and Boolarra Skate Park. For walks and rides, Grand Ridge Rail Trail crosses Boolarra.

Getting around

There's no train station in Boolarra. The closest to Boolarra is Morwell Railway Station (V/Line), about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 4 stops in Boolarra. By car from Boolarra, Traralgon is about 38 minutes away, Leongatha about 44 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.
