The Patch in the Yarra Ranges area has 1 playground in our data. The Patch also has a museum or gallery.

Landmarks

Places to visit in The Patch include Tracey Roberts.

Getting around

The Patch has no train station; Belgrave Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 3.8 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 663 stops in The Patch. By car from The Patch, Lilydale is about 24 minutes away, Ringwood about 29 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 47 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to The Patch is Monbulk Aquatic Centre in Monbulk, about 4 minutes' drive. For a library, Belgrave Library in Belgrave is about 8 minutes' drive from The Patch. For a beach, Edithvale Beach in Edithvale is about 42 minutes' drive from The Patch. For a skate park, Monbulk Skate & BMX Park in Monbulk is about 5 minutes' drive from The Patch. The closest cinema to The Patch is Cameo Belgrave in Belgrave, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to The Patch is Little Rascals Play Centre in Ferntree Gully, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to The Patch is Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale, about 36 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Lilydale, about 24 minutes from The Patch by car, with 12 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from The Patch are Monbulk Aquatic Centre in Monbulk (4 min), the playground at Baynes Park in Monbulk (4 min), Monbulk Skate & BMX Park in Monbulk (5 min), the playground at Burns Way Reserve in Monbulk (7 min), Belgrave Library in Belgrave (8 min) and Cameo Belgrave in Belgrave (8 min).
