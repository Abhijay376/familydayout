Skate park at Forrest Common

Skate, BMX or pump track in Forrest · Basic info

Skate park at Forrest Common is a skate, bmx or pump track in Forrest, in the Colac Otway Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Forrest General Store/Birregurra - Forrest Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 114 m away.
