Playground at Forrest Common

Playground in Forrest · Basic info

Playground at Forrest Common in Forrest is a public playground in the Colac Otway Shire area. The Forrest Loop Trail is about 30 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 10 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 130 m from the playground, and bus Colac - Marengo via Apollo Bay stops at Forrest General Store/Birregurra - Forrest Rd, a 7-minute walk away.
