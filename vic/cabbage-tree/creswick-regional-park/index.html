Creswick Regional Park

Park in Cabbage Tree · Official details

Creswick Regional Park is a park in Cabbage Tree, in the Hepburn Shire Council area, good for all ages. Dogs are allowed on a lead.

Details

Dogs
on lead (Parks Victoria)
Seasons
Wildflowers in spring (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
Bring your own drinking water; Stay on tracks, deep mine shafts; Bikes not allowed on walking tracks or in Le Gerche Gully (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Creswick Regional Park?
Creswick Regional Park is in Cabbage Tree, Victoria.
Is Creswick Regional Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Creswick Regional Park?
Yes, on a lead.
