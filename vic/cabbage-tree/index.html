Landmarks

The biggest green space in Cabbage Tree is Creswick Regional Park, covering about 1,263 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Cabbage Tree. The Goldfields Track passes through Cabbage Tree.

Getting around

Cabbage Tree has no train station; Creswick Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 4.4 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cabbage Tree, so most trips start by car. By car from Cabbage Tree, Ballarat is about 23 minutes away, Daylesford about 35 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.
