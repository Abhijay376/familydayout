Watsonia North in the Banyule area has 5 playgrounds and 8 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Binnak Park is the largest mapped park in Watsonia North, at about 13 hectares. Watsonia North also has Kalista Park, Hakea Street Reserve and Lawson Street Reserve. Playgrounds in Watsonia North include Binnak Park Playground, Binnak Park Playground and the playground at Binnak Park. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Watsonia North. For walks and rides, Banyule LBN7 and Banyule LBN10 cross Watsonia North.

Getting around

Watsonia North has no train station; Watsonia Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 1.8 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 566 and 902 stop in Watsonia North. By car from Watsonia North, Broadmeadows is about 19 minutes away, Box Hill about 27 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 29 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Watsonia North is WaterMarc in Greensborough, about 7 minutes' drive. For a library, Watsonia Library in Watsonia is about 6 minutes' drive from Watsonia North. The closest beach to Watsonia North is Horse Arena in Abbotsford, about 27 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Norris Bank Skate Park in Bundoora is about 7 minutes' drive from Watsonia North. For a cinema, Hoyts in Greensborough is about 8 minutes' drive from Watsonia North. The closest indoor play centre to Watsonia North is Greensborough Lanes in Bundoora, about 4 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Watsonia North is Bundoora Park Farm in Bundoora, about 12 minutes' drive.

Best for

Younger kids at Watsonia North's 5 local playgrounds. Free days on the Banyule LBN7 and in Watsonia North's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Watsonia North, Greensborough has 35 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Bundoora, next to Watsonia North, has 41 playgrounds, a library and a zoo or wildlife park. Watsonia, next to Watsonia North, has 6 playgrounds and a library.
