Binnak Park

Park in Watsonia North · Basic info

Binnak Park is a park in Watsonia North, in the Banyule City Council area, good for all ages. Cameron Pde/Macorna St (bus) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 76 m away.
