Bayles in the Cardinia area has 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bayles is Bayles Recreation Reserve, covering about 14 hectares. Playgrounds around Bayles include the playground at Bayles Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Bayles.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bayles. The closest to Bayles is Nar Nar Goon Railway Station (V/Line), about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bayles, so most trips start by car. By car from Bayles, Pakenham is about 16 minutes away, Cranbourne about 29 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 60 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Koo Wee Rup Swimming Pool in Koo Wee Rup is about 8 minutes' drive from Bayles. The closest library to Bayles is Pakenham Library in Pakenham, about 16 minutes' drive. For a beach, Carrum Beach in Carrum is about 45 minutes' drive from Bayles. For a skate park, the skate park at Cochrane Park in Koo Wee Rup is about 8 minutes' drive from Bayles. The closest cinema to Bayles is Village Cinemas in Narre Warren, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Bayles is Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Bayles is MonkeyG's in Pakenham, about 15 minutes' drive. Pakenham is the closest bigger centre to Bayles, about 16 minutes away by car, and has 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Bayles are the playground at Alexandra Avenue Park in Koo Wee Rup (7 min), the skate park at Cochrane Park in Koo Wee Rup (8 min), Koo Wee Rup Swimming Pool in Koo Wee Rup (8 min), Skate BMX Park in Garfield (16 min), Garfield Swimming Pool in Garfield (17 min) and MonkeyG's in Pakenham (15 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bayles.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Bayles, Koo Wee Rup has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Nar Nar Goon, next to Bayles, has 2 playgrounds. Cora Lynn and Catani also border Bayles.
