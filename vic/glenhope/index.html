Glenhope in the Mitchell area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Glenhope Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Glenhope, at about 5 hectares. Barfold Gorge Geological Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Glenhope. The closest to Glenhope is Kyneton Railway Station (V/Line), about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glenhope, so most trips start by car. By car from Glenhope, Kyneton is about 36 minutes away, Bendigo about 60 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Glenhope is Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore, about 38 minutes' drive. For a library, Kyneton Library in Kyneton is about 36 minutes' drive from Glenhope. The closest skate park to Glenhope is Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton, about 37 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Kyneton, about 36 minutes from Glenhope by car, with 10 playgrounds and a library. From Glenhope, the nearest places for kids by road are Jean Haynes Playground in Kyneton (35 min), Kyneton Museum in Kyneton (36 min), Kyneton Library in Kyneton (36 min), Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton (37 min), Kyneton Community Park in Kyneton (37 min) and Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote (23 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Glenhope.

Nearby suburbs

Baynton and Barfold also border Glenhope.
