Cundare North is in the Corangamite area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Cundare North is Cundare Pool (Lake Martin) Lake Reserve, covering about 2,846 hectares.

Getting around

Cundare North has no train station; Colac Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cundare North, so most trips start by car. By car from Cundare North, Colac is about 44 minutes away, Geelong about 88 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cundare North is Lismore Pool in Lismore, about 44 minutes' drive. The closest library to Cundare North is Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac, about 44 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Colac Cinemas in Colac is about 44 minutes' drive from Cundare North. For an indoor play centre, Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac is about 43 minutes' drive from Cundare North. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 44 minutes from Cundare North by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Cundare North are the playground at Cressy Picnic Area in Cressy (33 min), the playground at Red Rock Scenic Reserve in Alvie (48 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (43 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (44 min), Lismore Pool in Lismore (44 min) and Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (44 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Cundare North.

Nearby suburbs

Cressy, next to Cundare North, has 1 playground. Duverney, Dreeite and Cundare also border Cundare North.
