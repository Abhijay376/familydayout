Bolangum in the Northern Grampians area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Mount Bolangum Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Bolangum, at about 2,700 hectares.

Getting around

Bolangum has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 63 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bolangum, so most trips start by car. By car from Bolangum, Stawell is about 53 minutes away and Horsham about 75 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bolangum is Rupanyup Swimming Pool in Rupanyup, about 38 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Stawell, about 53 minutes from Bolangum by car, with 6 playgrounds and a pool. From Bolangum, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Shanahan Park in Marnoo (19 min), Guthrie Park Playground in Rich Avon West (35 min), Rupanyup Swimming Pool in Rupanyup (38 min), Woods' Farming and Heritage Museum in Rupanyup (38 min) and St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (43 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bolangum.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Bolangum, Marnoo has 1 playground. Gre Gre South also borders Bolangum.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Bolangum, without traffic.
