Modewarre in the Surf Coast area has 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Brown Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Modewarre, at about 37 hectares. Modewarre Recreation Reserve is the next largest. Playgrounds in Modewarre include the playground at Modewarre Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Modewarre.

Getting around

There's no train station in Modewarre. The closest to Modewarre is Winchelsea Railway Station (V/Line), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Modewarre, so most trips start by car. By car from Modewarre, Torquay is about 23 minutes away, Geelong about 28 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 86 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Modewarre is Leisurelink Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Grovedale, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest library to Modewarre is Waurn Ponds Library in Grovedale, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Modewarre is Eumeralla in Anglesea, about 22 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Moriac Skate Park in Moriac is about 7 minutes' drive from Modewarre. For a cinema, Reading Cinemas in Grovedale is about 21 minutes' drive from Modewarre. The closest indoor play centre to Modewarre is Leisurelink Restaurant in Highton, about 22 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Tiger Moth World in Torquay is about 25 minutes' drive from Modewarre. Torquay is the closest bigger centre to Modewarre, about 23 minutes away by car, and has 15 playgrounds, a library and a beach. From Modewarre, the nearest places for kids by road are Moriac Playground in Moriac (7 min), Moriac Skate Park in Moriac (7 min), the bmx track at Newling Reserve in Moriac (7 min), Reading Cinemas in Grovedale (21 min), Leisurelink Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Grovedale (20 min) and Waurn Ponds Library in Grovedale (20 min).
