Murrabit West is in the Gannawarra area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Murrabit West, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Murrabit West has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Murrabit West, so most trips start by car. By car from Murrabit West, Swan Hill is about 43 minutes away and Echuca about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Swimming Pool in Kerang is about 29 minutes' drive from Murrabit West. For a library, Barham Branch Library in Koondrook is about 37 minutes' drive from Murrabit West. For a skate park, Barham Skate Park in Koondrook is about 38 minutes' drive from Murrabit West. For a cinema, Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill is about 42 minutes' drive from Murrabit West. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Murrabit West, about 43 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Murrabit West, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Riverside Park in Koondrook (37 min), Barham Branch Library in Koondrook (37 min), Barham Skate Park in Koondrook (38 min), the playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV) in Koondrook (39 min), Swimming Pool in Kerang (29 min) and Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (29 min).
