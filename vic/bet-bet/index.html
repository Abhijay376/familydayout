Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Bet Bet, in the Central Goldfields area.

Landmarks

Bet Bet Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Bet Bet, at about 4 hectares. For walks and rides, Dunolly - Havelock Gold Rush Cycle Trail crosses Bet Bet.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bet Bet. The closest to Bet Bet is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bet Bet, so most trips start by car. By car from Bet Bet, Maryborough is about 16 minutes away, Bendigo about 58 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bet Bet is Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest library to Bet Bet is Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough, about 16 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 16 minutes from Bet Bet by car, with a library. The closest places for kids by road from Bet Bet are Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (8 min), Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (9 min), Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (16 min), the playground at Market Reserve in Carisbrook (21 min), Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground in Talbot (33 min) and Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (33 min).

Best for

Free days on the Dunolly - Havelock Gold Rush Cycle Trail and in Bet Bet's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Dunolly, next to Bet Bet, has a pool. Havelock, Bromley and Betley also border Bet Bet.
