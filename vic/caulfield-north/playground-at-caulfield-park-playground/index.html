Playground at Caulfield Park

Playground in Caulfield North · Basic info

Playground at Caulfield Park in Caulfield North is a public playground in the Glen Eira City area. Nostralis is about 260 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 230 m away, and tram 3 stops at Caulfield Park Bowling Club/Balaclava Rd #52, a 2-minute walk away.
