Memorial Park

Park in Caulfield North · Official details

Memorial Park is a park in Caulfield North, in the Glen Eira City Council area, good for all ages. Dorgan St/Kooyong Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 889 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed on a lead.

Questions parents ask

Where is Memorial Park?
Memorial Park is in Caulfield North, Victoria.
Is Memorial Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Memorial Park?
Yes, on a lead.
Are there toilets at Memorial Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 889 m away, with a baby change table.
How do I get to Memorial Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Dorgan St/Kooyong Rd (routes 605), a 2-minute walk.
