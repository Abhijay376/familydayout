Playground at Memorial Park

Playground in Caulfield North · Basic info

Playground at Memorial Park is a public playground in Caulfield North, in the Glen Eira City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 880 m away, and bus 605 stops at Dorgan St/Kooyong Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
