Our data lists 4 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in Caulfield North, in the Glen Eira area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Caulfield North is Caulfield Park, covering about 25 hectares. Caulfield North also has Eskdale Park and Memorial Park. Playgrounds in Caulfield North include the playground at Memorial Park, the playground at Caulfield Park and the playground at Caulfield Park. Our data shows 5 public toilets and 1 off-leash dog park in Caulfield North. Our data lists 9 cafes and family restaurants in Caulfield North for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Caulfield North. The closest to Caulfield North is Malvern Railway Station (metro), about 800 metres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Trams 3, 5, 16 and 64 stop in Caulfield North. Buses 604, 605, 623 and 624 stop in Caulfield North. By car from Caulfield North, Box Hill is about 20 minutes away, Dandenong about 23 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 13 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Caulfield North is Diving Pool in Carnegie, about 7 minutes' drive. For a library, Caulfield Library in Caulfield is about 1 minutes' drive from Caulfield North. For a beach, St Kilda Beach in St Kilda is about 7 minutes' drive from Caulfield North. For a skate park, the pump track at Windsor Siding in Windsor is about 6 minutes' drive from Caulfield North. For a cinema, Classic Cinema Elsternwick in Elsternwick is about 5 minutes' drive from Caulfield North. For an indoor play centre, Akira’s Arcade in Prahran is about 6 minutes' drive from Caulfield North. For a zoo or wildlife park, Luna Park in St Kilda is about 6 minutes' drive from Caulfield North. The nearest larger centre is Box Hill, about 20 minutes from Caulfield North by car, with 11 playgrounds, a pool and a library.
