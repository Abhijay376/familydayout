Landmarks

Arthurs Seat State Park is the largest mapped park in Arthurs Seat, at about 566 hectares. Playgrounds around Arthurs Seat include Tube Slides, Brainteaser Puzzles and the playground at Arthurs Seat State Park. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Arthurs Seat. The Two Bays Walking Track, The Mornington Peninsula Walk and Two Bays Walking Trail all pass through Arthurs Seat. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Arthurs Seat for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Arthurs Seat. The closest to Arthurs Seat is Bittern Railway Station (metro), about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Arthurs Seat, so most trips start by car. By car from Arthurs Seat, Mornington is about 22 minutes away, Frankston about 28 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 71 minutes, without traffic.
