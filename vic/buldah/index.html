Buldah is in the East Gippsland area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Buldah is Tennyson Creek Flora Reserve, covering about 494 hectares. Little Bog Creek Flora and Fauna Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Buldah. The closest to Buldah is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 148 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Buldah, so most trips start by car. By car from Buldah, Lakes Entrance is about 2.5 hours away and Bairnsdale about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest places for kids by road from Buldah are the skate park at Mick Baum Park in Cann River (55 min) and the playground at Mick Baum Park in Cann River (55 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Buldah.
