Laanecoorie is in the Loddon area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Laanecoorie is Waanyarra Bushland Reserve, covering about 18 hectares. Janevale Monier Bridge Historic and Cultural Features Reserve is the next largest. Laanecoorie also has Loddon River - Laanecoorie Camping Area 1 (CLWF). Our data shows 2 public toilets in Laanecoorie. For camping, there's Loddon River - Laanecoorie Camping Area 1 (CLWF). The Dunolly Cycle Tracks and Laanecoorie Cycling Track all pass through Laanecoorie.

Getting around

There's no train station in Laanecoorie. The closest to Laanecoorie is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Laanecoorie, so most trips start by car. By car from Laanecoorie, Maryborough is about 34 minutes away, Bendigo about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly is about 18 minutes' drive from Laanecoorie. The closest library to Laanecoorie is Athenaeum Library in Maldon, about 25 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track on Water Street in Bendigo is about 40 minutes' drive from Laanecoorie. The closest cinema to Laanecoorie is Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo, about 39 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 36 minutes' drive from Laanecoorie. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 44 minutes' drive from Laanecoorie. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 34 minutes from Laanecoorie by car, with a library. From Laanecoorie, the nearest places for kids by road are Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (18 min), Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (18 min), Athenaeum Library in Maldon (25 min), Maldon Swimming Pool in Maldon (26 min), the playground at Maldon Shire Gardens in Maldon (26 min) and Maldon District Museum in Maldon (26 min).

Best for

Free days on the Dunolly Cycle Tracks and in Laanecoorie's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Newbridge, next to Laanecoorie, has 1 playground. Eddington and Eastville also border Laanecoorie.
