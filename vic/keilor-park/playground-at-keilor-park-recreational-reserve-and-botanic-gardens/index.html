Playground at Keilor Park Recreational Reserve and Botanic Gardens

Playground in Keilor Park · Official details

Playground at Keilor Park Recreational Reserve and Botanic Gardens is a public playground in Keilor Park, in the Brimbank City area. For food, Ella James Cafe is about 340 m away, and Playground at Thea Court Reserve is another option about 550 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 482 stops at Assembly Dr/Tullamarine Park Rd, a 8-minute walk away.
