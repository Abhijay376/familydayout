Our data lists 3 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in Keilor Park, in the Brimbank area.

Landmarks

Keilor Park Recreational Reserve and Botanic Gardens is the largest mapped park in Keilor Park, at about 53 hectares. Thea Court Reserve is the next largest. Keilor Park also has Collinson Street Reserve. Playgrounds in Keilor Park include the playground at Thea Court Reserve, the playground at Collinson Street Reserve and the playground at Keilor Park Recreational Reserve and Botanic Gardens. Our data shows 18 public toilets and 3 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Keilor Park. The M80 Trail passes through Keilor Park. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Keilor Park for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Keilor Park has no train station; Ginifer Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 5.8 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 465 and 476 stop in Keilor Park. By car from Keilor Park, Broadmeadows is about 12 minutes away, Sunshine about 12 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 22 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Keilor East Leisure Centre in Keilor East is about 5 minutes' drive from Keilor Park. The closest library to Keilor Park is Keilor Village Library in Keilor, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Keilor Park is Horse Arena in Abbotsford, about 25 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Rosehill Park in Keilor East is about 7 minutes' drive from Keilor Park. The closest cinema to Keilor Park is Village Cinemas in Airport West, about 8 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Funtopia in Maidstone is about 12 minutes' drive from Keilor Park. For a zoo or wildlife park, Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville is about 17 minutes' drive from Keilor Park.

Best for

Younger kids at Keilor Park's 3 local playgrounds. Free days on the M80 Trail and in Keilor Park's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Keilor East, next to Keilor Park, has 26 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Keilor Park, Tullamarine has 14 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Airport West, next to Keilor Park, has 13 playgrounds and a cinema. Next door to Keilor Park, Keilor has 11 playgrounds and a library. Melbourne Airport also borders Keilor Park.
