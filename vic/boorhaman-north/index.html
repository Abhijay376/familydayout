Boorhaman North is in the Wangaratta area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Boorhaman North, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Boorhaman North. The closest to Boorhaman North is Springhurst Railway Station (V/Line), about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Boorhaman North, so most trips start by car. By car from Boorhaman North, Wangaratta is about 44 minutes away and Wodonga about 62 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rutherglen War Memorial Swimming Pool in Rutherglen is about 27 minutes' drive from Boorhaman North. For a library, Rutherglen Public Library in Rutherglen is about 27 minutes' drive from Boorhaman North. The closest beach to Boorhaman North is Forges Beach No 2 in Yarrawonga, about 41 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track on Victoria Street in Wahgunyah is about 28 minutes' drive from Boorhaman North. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 44 minutes' drive from Boorhaman North. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Boorhaman North is Tunzafun in Yarrawonga, about 34 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wangaratta, about 44 minutes from Boorhaman North by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Boorhaman North are the playground at Peechelba Reserve in Peechelba (26 min), Apex Park Playground in Rutherglen (26 min), Rutherglen War Memorial Swimming Pool in Rutherglen (27 min), Rutherglen Public Library in Rutherglen (27 min), the pump track on Victoria Street in Wahgunyah (28 min) and Corowa Library in Wahgunyah (30 min).

Best for

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Boorhaman North, Peechelba has 1 playground. Bundalong South and Brimin also border Boorhaman North.
