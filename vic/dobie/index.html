Dobie is in the Ararat area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Dobie, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Dobie has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 8.3 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dobie, so most trips start by car. By car from Dobie, Ararat is about 9 minutes away, Stawell about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat is about 8 minutes' drive from Dobie. For a library, Ararat Library in Ararat is about 8 minutes' drive from Dobie. For a skate park, David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat is about 12 minutes' drive from Dobie. The closest cinema to Dobie is Astor Cinema in Ararat, about 8 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell is about 32 minutes' drive from Dobie. The nearest larger centre is Ararat, about 9 minutes from Dobie by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Dobie are Astor Cinema in Ararat (8 min), Ararat Railway Museum in Ararat (8 min), Ararat Library in Ararat (8 min), Langi Morgala Museum in Ararat (8 min), Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat (8 min) and the playground at Smith Street Reserve in Ararat (10 min).
