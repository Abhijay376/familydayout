Wallace in the Moorabool area has 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Wallace is Wallace Recreation Reserve, covering about 6 hectares. Playgrounds in Wallace include the playground at Wallace Recreation Reserve.

Getting around

Wallace has no train station; Ballan Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 17 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Ballarat - Mt Egerton via Gordon route stop in Wallace. By car from Wallace, Ballarat is about 19 minutes away, Daylesford about 30 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 75 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Brown Hill Swimming Pool in Brown Hill is about 13 minutes' drive from Wallace. The closest library to Wallace is Central Highlands Regional Library Corp Ballarat branch in Ballarat Central, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Wallace is Lionhead Beach in Pentland Hills, about 27 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Len T Frazer Reserve in Ballarat East is about 16 minutes' drive from Wallace. For a cinema, Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central is about 18 minutes' drive from Wallace. The closest indoor play centre to Wallace is Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Wallace is Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek, about 9 minutes' drive. Ballarat is the closest bigger centre to Wallace, about 19 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Wallace are the playground at Millbrook Community Centre in Millbrook (10 min), Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek (9 min), Brown Hill Swimming Pool in Brown Hill (13 min), Eureka Swimming Complex in Eureka (15 min), Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East (15 min) and the skate park at Len T Frazer Reserve in Ballarat East (16 min).
