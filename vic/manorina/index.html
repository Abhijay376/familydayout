Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Manorina in the East Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Manorina. The closest to Manorina is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 104 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Manorina. By car from Manorina, Lakes Entrance is about 74 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Manorina is Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost, about 27 minutes' drive. For a library, Orbost Library in Orbost is about 28 minutes' drive from Manorina. For a beach, Sailors Grave Beach in Cape Conran is about 17 minutes' drive from Manorina. For a skate park, the skate park at Mick Baum Park in Cann River is about 32 minutes' drive from Manorina. From Manorina, the nearest places for kids by road are Sailors Grave Beach in Cape Conran (17 min), the playground at Luderick Point Memorial Park in Bemm River (17 min), Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost (27 min), Orbost Library in Orbost (28 min), the playground at Forest Park in Orbost (28 min) and the skate park at Mick Baum Park in Cann River (32 min).
