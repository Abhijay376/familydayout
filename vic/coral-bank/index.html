Coral Bank in the Alpine area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Mullagong I36 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Coral Bank, at about 37 hectares.

Getting around

Coral Bank has no train station; Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 58 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Coral Bank, so most trips start by car. By car from Coral Bank, Bright is about 38 minutes away and Wodonga about 54 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bright River Pool in Bright is about 38 minutes' drive from Coral Bank. The closest library to Coral Bank is Mt Beauty Library in Mount Beauty, about 18 minutes' drive. For a beach, Pebble Beach in Tawonga South is about 16 minutes' drive from Coral Bank. For a skate park, Mount Beauty BMX Bike Track in Mount Beauty is about 18 minutes' drive from Coral Bank. For a cinema, Sun Cinema Bright in Bright is about 39 minutes' drive from Coral Bank. Bright is the closest bigger centre to Coral Bank, about 38 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Coral Bank, the nearest places for kids by road are Pebble Beach in Tawonga South (16 min), Mount Beauty BMX Bike Track in Mount Beauty (18 min), Mt Beauty Library in Mount Beauty (18 min), Bright River Pool in Bright (38 min), Bright Art Gallery in Bright (38 min) and Bright Splash Park in Bright (39 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Coral Bank.

Nearby suburbs

Bright, next to Coral Bank, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library.
