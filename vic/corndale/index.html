Corndale in the Glenelg area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Corndale is Nangeela State Forest, covering about 5,150 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Corndale include Long Lead Picnic Area. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Corndale. Picnic areas in Corndale include Long Lead Picnic Area.

Getting around

There's no train station in Corndale. The closest to Corndale is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 143 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Corndale, so most trips start by car. By car from Corndale, Hamilton is about 69 minutes away and Portland about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Corndale is Casterton Pool in Casterton, about 18 minutes' drive. For a skate park, coleraine skate park in Coleraine is about 41 minutes' drive from Corndale. The closest places for kids by road from Corndale are Gorman's art gallery in Casterton (18 min), Casterton Pool in Casterton (18 min), Merino Swimming Pool in Merino (36 min), the playground at Lions Park in Merino (36 min), coleraine skate park in Coleraine (41 min) and the playground at Queens Park in Coleraine (41 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Corndale.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Corndale, Casterton has a pool. Lake Mundi and Dunrobin also border Corndale.
