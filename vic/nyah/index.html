Our data lists 2 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in Nyah, in the Swan Hill area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Nyah is Nyah-Vinifera Park, covering about 1,372 hectares. Nyah Bushland Reserve and Nyah Heritage Park are the next largest. Playgrounds around Nyah include the playground at Nyah Heritage Park and the playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV). Our data shows 5 public toilets in Nyah.

Getting around

There's no train station in Nyah. The closest to Nyah is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 25 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus tv stops in Nyah. V/Line coaches stop in Nyah. By car from Nyah, Swan Hill is about 24 minutes away and Echuca about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill is about 23 minutes' drive from Nyah. For a library, Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill is about 22 minutes' drive from Nyah. The closest skate park to Nyah is Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South, about 21 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill is about 24 minutes' drive from Nyah. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Nyah, about 24 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Nyah are Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South (21 min), the bmx track at Piangil Memorial Park in Piangil (14 min), Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill (22 min), Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill (23 min), Swan Hill Outdoor Pool in Swan Hill (23 min) and Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill (24 min).
