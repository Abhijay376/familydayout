Nyah Heritage Park

Park in Nyah · Basic info

Nyah Heritage Park is a park in Nyah, in the Swan Hill Rural City Council area, good for all ages. McAlpine Rd/Murray Valley Hwy (coach) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 271 m away.
