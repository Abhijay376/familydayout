Native play space

Playground in Euroa · Basic info

Native play space is a public playground in Euroa, in the Strathbogie Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet right by the playground. It's easiest by car, about 5 minutes from Euroa.
