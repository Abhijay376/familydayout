Our data lists 5 playgrounds and 9 parks and reserves in Euroa, in the Strathbogie area. Euroa also has a library, a pool, a museum or gallery and a cinema.

Landmarks

Balmattum Hill Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Euroa, at about 77 hectares. Castle Creek Bushland Reserve and Euroa Arboretum are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Euroa include Lions Park, Telegraph Park and Euroa Rotary Park. Playgrounds in Euroa include Native play space, Lions Park Playground and Bicentennial Park Playground. Our data shows 8 public toilets in Euroa. Places to visit in Euroa include Farmers Arms Hotel Museum, Euroa Community Cinema, Euroa Swimming Pool and Euroa Library. For older kids in Euroa there's Euroa Skate Park. For walks and rides, Apex Walk Trail, Balmattum Hill Scout Route and Balmattum Hill Walking Track cross Euroa. Our data lists 8 cafes and family restaurants in Euroa for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Euroa Railway Station (V/Line: Albury - Melbourne via Seymour) in Euroa. Buses on the Shepparton - Euroa via Kialla route stop in Euroa. V/Line coaches stop in Euroa. By car from Euroa, Benalla is about 36 minutes away, Seymour about 42 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 43 minutes' drive from Euroa. For a zoo or wildlife park, Emerald Bank Leisure Land in Kialla is about 36 minutes' drive from Euroa.

Best for

Younger kids at Euroa's 5 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the library, cinema and museum or gallery in Euroa. Teens who skate or ride, with Euroa Skate Park in Euroa. Free days on the Apex Walk Trail and in Euroa's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Euroa, Moglonemby has 1 playground. Longwood, next to Euroa, has 1 playground. Creightons Creek, next to Euroa, has 1 playground. Riggs Creek, Molka, Miepoll and Longwood East also border Euroa.
