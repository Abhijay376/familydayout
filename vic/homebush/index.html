Homebush in the Pyrenees area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Homebush is Lower Homebush Bushland Reserve, covering about 40 hectares. Glenmona H40 Bushland Reserve and Homebush Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Homebush also has Lower Homebush Historic and Cultural Features Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Homebush.

Getting around

There's no train station in Homebush. The closest to Homebush is Talbot Railway Station (V/Line), about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Homebush, so most trips start by car. By car from Homebush, Maryborough is about 27 minutes away, Ararat about 63 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Homebush is Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest library to Homebush is Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough, about 27 minutes' drive. Maryborough is the closest bigger centre to Homebush, about 27 minutes away by car, and has a library. From Homebush, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Avoca Lions Park in Avoca (13 min), Courthouse in Avoca (12 min), Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (27 min), Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground in Talbot (34 min), Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (34 min) and Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (28 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Homebush.
