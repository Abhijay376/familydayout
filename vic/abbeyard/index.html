Our data lists 4 parks and reserves in Abbeyard, in the Alpine area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Abbeyard is Razor - Viking Wilderness Zone, covering about 15,573 hectares. Dandongadale (part in Alpine NP) Remote and Natural Area is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Abbeyard include Mclver and Abbeyards. Picnic areas in Abbeyard include Abbeyards and Mclver. For walks and rides, Australian Alps Walking Track and Selwyn Track cross Abbeyard.

Getting around

There's no train station in Abbeyard. The closest to Abbeyard is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 73 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Abbeyard, so most trips start by car. By car from Abbeyard, Bright is about 1.5 hours away and Wangaratta about 2 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Free days on the Australian Alps Walking Track and in Abbeyard's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Dandongadale and Buckland also border Abbeyard.
