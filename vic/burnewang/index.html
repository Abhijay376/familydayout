Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Burnewang, in the Campaspe area.

Landmarks

Elmore Events Centre is the largest mapped park in Burnewang, at about 19 hectares. Aysons Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Burnewang.

Getting around

Burnewang has no train station; Elmore Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 4.2 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Burnewang, so most trips start by car. By car from Burnewang, Echuca is about 40 minutes away, Bendigo about 47 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Memorial Pool in Rochester is about 17 minutes' drive from Burnewang. The closest library to Burnewang is Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca, about 41 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Moama Echuca Community Skate Park in Echuca is about 43 minutes' drive from Burnewang. For a cinema, Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca is about 41 minutes' drive from Burnewang. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 44 minutes' drive from Burnewang. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 40 minutes from Burnewang by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Burnewang are Campaspe Run Rural Discovery Centre in Elmore (5 min), Memorial Pool in Rochester (17 min), the playground at Apex Park in Rochester (18 min), Goornong Swimming Pool in Goornong (19 min), the playground at Pennington Reserve in Huntly (30 min) and Holden Museum in Echuca (40 min).
