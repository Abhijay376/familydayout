Skate park on Station Street

Skate, BMX or pump track in Cobden · Basic info

Skate park on Station Street is a skate, bmx or pump track in Cobden, in the Corangamite Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. McKenzie St/Curdie St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 841 m away.
