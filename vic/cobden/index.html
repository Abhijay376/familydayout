Cobden in the Corangamite area has 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Cobden Botanic Gardens is the largest mapped park in Cobden, at about 3 hectares. Our data shows 9 public toilets and 2 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Cobden. The Camperdown to Timboon Rail Trail passes through Cobden.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cobden. The closest to Cobden is Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line), about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Camperdown - Timboon via Cobden route stop in Cobden. By car from Cobden, Colac is about 40 minutes away, Warrnambool about 48 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cobden is Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest library to Cobden is Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake, about 41 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Cobden is Rutledge Creek Beach in Port Campbell, about 38 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown is about 15 minutes' drive from Cobden. The closest cinema to Cobden is Colac Cinemas in Colac, about 40 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Cobden is Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac, about 41 minutes' drive. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Cobden, about 40 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Cobden, the nearest places for kids by road are Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (15 min), the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve in Camperdown (17 min), the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (25 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (25 min), Rutledge Creek Beach in Port Campbell (38 min) and Sherbrooke Estuary in Port Campbell (38 min).

Best for

Free days on the Camperdown to Timboon Rail Trail and in Cobden's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Cobden, Camperdown has 3 playgrounds and a skate park. Jancourt, Glenfyne, Elingamite North and Elingamite also border Cobden.
