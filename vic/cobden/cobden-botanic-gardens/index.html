Cobden Botanic Gardens

Park in Cobden · Official details

Cobden Botanic Gardens is a park in Cobden, in the Corangamite Shire Council area, good for all ages. Cobden Post Office/Curdie St (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 146 m away.

Details

Good to know
Public BBQ about 80 m away (Council)

Questions parents ask

Where is Cobden Botanic Gardens?
Cobden Botanic Gardens is in Cobden, Victoria.
Is Cobden Botanic Gardens free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Cobden Botanic Gardens?
The nearest public toilet is about 146 m away.
How do I get to Cobden Botanic Gardens by public transport?
The closest stop is Cobden Post Office/Curdie St (routes Camperdown - Timboon via Cobden), a 5-minute walk.
