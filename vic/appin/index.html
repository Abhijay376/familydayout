Appin is in the Gannawarra area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Appin, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Appin. The closest to Appin is Kerang Railway Station (V/Line), about 17 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Appin, so most trips start by car. By car from Appin, Swan Hill is about 64 minutes away and Echuca about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Appin is Swimming Pool in Kerang, about 19 minutes' drive. For a library, Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang is about 19 minutes' drive from Appin. The closest skate park to Appin is Barham Skate Park in Koondrook, about 42 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Appin are the playground at Lake Meran W.R in Lake Meran (16 min), Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (19 min), Swimming Pool in Kerang (19 min), Pyramid Hill & Districts Historical Museum in Pyramid Hill (50 min), A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park in Boort (38 min) and Boort Court House Historical Museum in Boort (39 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Appin.

Nearby suburbs
