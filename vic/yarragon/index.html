Getting around

Trains stop at Yarragon Railway Station (V/Line: Bairnsdale - Melbourne via Sale & Traralgon and Traralgon - Melbourne via Morwell, Moe & Pakenham) in Yarragon. Buses on the Garfield Station - Traralgon Plaza route stop in Yarragon. V/Line coaches stop in Yarragon. By car from Yarragon, Warragul is about 13 minutes away, Traralgon about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 89 minutes, without traffic.
