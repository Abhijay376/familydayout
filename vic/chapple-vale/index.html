Chapple Vale is in the Colac Otway area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Chapple Vale, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Chapple Vale has no train station; Colac Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 39 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Chapple Vale, so most trips start by car. By car from Chapple Vale, Colac is about 61 minutes away and Warrnambool about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest places for kids by road from Chapple Vale are Clifton Beach in Princetown (46 min), Gibson Beach in Princetown (47 min), Art Reach Studio in Beech Forest (40 min), the playground at John H Gardner Reserve in Beech Forest (40 min), the playground at Rex Norman Park in Gellibrand (54 min) and the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (59 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Chapple Vale.

Nearby suburbs

Princetown, next to Chapple Vale, has 1 playground and a beach. Gellibrand Lower, next to Chapple Vale, has a beach. Next door to Chapple Vale, Carlisle River has 1 playground. Simpson, Lavers Hill and Kennedys Creek also border Chapple Vale.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Chapple Vale, without traffic.
