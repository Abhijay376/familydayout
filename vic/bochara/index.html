Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Bochara, in the Southern Grampians area.

Landmarks

Nigretta Falls Scenic Reserve is the largest mapped park in Bochara, at about 9 hectares. Bochara Natural Features Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet and 2 off-leash dog parks in Bochara. The Hamilton-Coleraine Rail Trail passes through Bochara.

Getting around

Bochara has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 91 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bochara, so most trips start by car. By car from Bochara, Hamilton is about 11 minutes away and Portland about 76 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Hamilton is about 10 minutes' drive from Bochara. For a library, Hamilton Library in Hamilton is about 11 minutes' drive from Bochara. The closest skate park to Bochara is hamilton skate park in Hamilton, about 13 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton is about 11 minutes' drive from Bochara. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 11 minutes from Bochara by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Bochara, the nearest places for kids by road are Big Woolbales & Campe Motor Museum in Hamilton (8 min), Coleraine Road Playground in Hamilton (8 min), Quigley Street Playground in Hamilton (9 min), Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Hamilton (10 min), Mud Gallery in Hamilton (11 min) and Hamilton Library in Hamilton (11 min).

Best for

Free days on the Hamilton-Coleraine Rail Trail and in Bochara's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Bochara, Hamilton has 23 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Hensley Park and Bulart also border Bochara.
