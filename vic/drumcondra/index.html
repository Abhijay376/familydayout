Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Drumcondra, in the Greater Geelong area.

Landmarks

Other parks and reserves in Drumcondra include Lunan Park. For walks and rides, Corio Bay Trail and Bob McGovan Path cross Drumcondra.

Getting around

There's no train station in Drumcondra. The closest to Drumcondra is North Geelong Railway Station (V/Line), about 1.1 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 20 stops in Drumcondra. By car from Drumcondra, Geelong is about 6 minutes away, Torquay about 25 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 62 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Tri Swimming in Geelong West is about 2 minutes' drive from Drumcondra. For a library, Geelong West Library in Geelong West is about 2 minutes' drive from Drumcondra. The closest beach to Drumcondra is Eastern Beach in Geelong, about 4 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Drumcondra is the pump track at Western Oval in North Geelong, about 2 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Geelong is about 4 minutes' drive from Drumcondra. For an indoor play centre, All 4 Kids Play Centre in Geelong West is about 1 minutes' drive from Drumcondra. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Drumcondra is Adventure Park in Wallington, about 22 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Geelong, about 6 minutes from Drumcondra by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Drumcondra are Stradling Reserve in Geelong (1 min), All 4 Kids Play Centre in Geelong West (1 min), Geelong Community Adventure Playground in Rippleside (2 min), Tri Swimming in Geelong West (2 min), the pump track at Western Oval in North Geelong (2 min) and Geelong West Library in Geelong West (2 min).

Best for

Free days on the Corio Bay Trail and in Drumcondra's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Geelong, next to Drumcondra, has 9 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Geelong West, next to Drumcondra, has 8 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Drumcondra, Rippleside has 3 playgrounds. North Geelong, next to Drumcondra, has 3 playgrounds and a skate park.
