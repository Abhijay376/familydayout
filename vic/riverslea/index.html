Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Riverslea in the Wellington area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Riverslea has no train station; Stratford Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Riverslea, so most trips start by car. By car from Riverslea, Sale is about 28 minutes away and Traralgon about 60 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Riverslea is Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest library to Riverslea is Sale Library in Sale, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Riverslea is Pump Track in Heyfield, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Riverslea is Former Drive-in site in Sale, about 28 minutes' drive. Sale is the closest bigger centre to Riverslea, about 28 minutes away by car, and has 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Riverslea are Macalister River Regional Park Playground in Maffra (13 min), Victoria Park Playground in Maffra (15 min), Pump Track in Heyfield (26 min), Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield (27 min), Heyfield and District Museum in Heyfield (27 min) and Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford (23 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Riverslea.

Nearby suburbs

Maffra, next to Riverslea, has 6 playgrounds. Bundalaguah also borders Riverslea.
