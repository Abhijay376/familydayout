Crymelon in the Yarriambiack area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Crymelon is Morep Dam Bushland Reserve, covering about 18 hectares. Crymelon Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Crymelon. The closest to Crymelon is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 139 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Crymelon, so most trips start by car. By car from Crymelon, Horsham is about 60 minutes away and Stawell about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Crymelon is Dimboola Swimming Pool in Dimboola, about 37 minutes' drive. The closest library to Crymelon is Dimboola Library in Dimboola, about 37 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Horsham, about 60 minutes from Crymelon by car, with 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Crymelon are the playground at Warrackanbeal Boat Ramp in Warracknabeal (14 min), Historical Centre in Warracknabeal (15 min), Lions Playground in Warracknabeal (15 min), Wheatlands Warracknabeal Agricultural Machinery Museum in Warracknabeal (17 min), Dimboola Swimming Pool in Dimboola (37 min) and Dimboola Library in Dimboola (37 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Crymelon.

Nearby suburbs

Warracknabeal, next to Crymelon, has 5 playgrounds. Jeparit, next to Crymelon, has 3 playgrounds. Next door to Crymelon, Brim has 1 playground. Tarranyurk, Lah, Cannum and Aubrey also border Crymelon.
