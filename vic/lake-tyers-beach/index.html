Landmarks

Lakes Entrance - Lake Tyers Coastal Reserve is the largest mapped park in Lake Tyers Beach, at about 165 hectares. Playgrounds in Lake Tyers Beach include the playground at Lakes Entrance - Lake Tyers Coastal Reserve (COM). Our data shows 6 public toilets in Lake Tyers Beach. Places to visit in Lake Tyers Beach include Lake Tyers Beach Sand Bar.

Getting around

Lake Tyers Beach has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 40 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Lake Tyers Beach. By car from Lake Tyers Beach, Lakes Entrance is about 18 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 52 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lake Tyers Beach is Lakes Entrance Aquatic Centre in Lakes Entrance, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest library to Lake Tyers Beach is Lakes Entrance Library in Lakes Entrance, about 18 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow is about 44 minutes' drive from Lake Tyers Beach. The nearest larger centre is Lakes Entrance, about 18 minutes from Lake Tyers Beach by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a beach. From Lake Tyers Beach, the nearest places for kids by road are Lakes Entrance Aquatic Centre in Lakes Entrance (15 min), 90 Mile Beach in Lakes Entrance (16 min), Lakes Entrance Library in Lakes Entrance (18 min), Bronsdon Street Park in Lakes Entrance (21 min), Griffiths Sea Shell Museum in Lakes Entrance (19 min) and Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow (44 min).
