Landmarks

The biggest green space in Korong Vale is Korong Vale South Bushland Reserve, covering about 4 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Korong Vale.

Getting around

Korong Vale has no train station; Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 50 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Bendigo - Boort via Wedderburn route stop in Korong Vale. V/Line coaches stop in Korong Vale. By car from Korong Vale, Bendigo is about 76 minutes away, Maryborough about 89 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Korong Vale is Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn, about 15 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 41 minutes' drive from Korong Vale. The closest places for kids by road from Korong Vale are Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (15 min), Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (15 min), A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park in Boort (36 min), Boort Court House Historical Museum in Boort (37 min), Boort Swimming Pool in Boort (37 min) and Rex Theatre in Charlton (41 min).

Best for

Nearby suburbs
