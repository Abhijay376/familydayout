Cannum is in the Yarriambiack area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Cannum, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Cannum has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 124 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cannum, so most trips start by car. By car from Cannum, Horsham is about 49 minutes away and Stawell about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Dimboola Swimming Pool in Dimboola is about 24 minutes' drive from Cannum. The closest library to Cannum is Dimboola Library in Dimboola, about 24 minutes' drive. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to Cannum, about 49 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Cannum are the playground at Apex Park in Warracknabeal (8 min), the playground at Anzac Park in Warracknabeal (10 min), Historical Centre in Warracknabeal (10 min), Wheatlands Warracknabeal Agricultural Machinery Museum in Warracknabeal (12 min), Dimboola Swimming Pool in Dimboola (24 min) and Dimboola Library in Dimboola (24 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Cannum.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Cannum, Dimboola has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Warracknabeal, next to Cannum, has 5 playgrounds. Kellalac, Crymelon and Aubrey also border Cannum.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Cannum, without traffic.
