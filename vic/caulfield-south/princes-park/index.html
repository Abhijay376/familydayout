Princes Park

Park in Caulfield South · Official details

Princes Park is a park in Caulfield South, in the Glen Eira City Council area, good for all ages. Princes Park/Dover St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 50 m away and dogs are not allowed.

Questions parents ask

Where is Princes Park?
Princes Park is in Caulfield South, Victoria.
Is Princes Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Princes Park?
No, dogs are not allowed.
Are there toilets at Princes Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 50 m away.
How do I get to Princes Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Princes Park/Dover St (routes 625), a 3-minute walk.
