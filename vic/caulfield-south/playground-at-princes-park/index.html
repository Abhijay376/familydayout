Playground at Princes Park

Playground in Caulfield South · Basic info

Playground at Princes Park is a public playground in Caulfield South, in the Glen Eira City area. The Rosstown Rail Trail passes about 230 m away. There's a public toilet about 70 m away, and tram 64 stops at Princes Park/Hawthorn Rd #59, a 3-minute walk away.
