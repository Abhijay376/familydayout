Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Upper Gundowring in the Alpine area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Upper Gundowring has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 46 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Upper Gundowring, so most trips start by car. By car from Upper Gundowring, Wodonga is about 49 minutes away and Bright about 57 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Edge Aquatics in Baranduda is about 40 minutes' drive from Upper Gundowring. For a library, Mt Beauty Library in Mount Beauty is about 38 minutes' drive from Upper Gundowring. For a beach, Pebble Beach in Tawonga South is about 35 minutes' drive from Upper Gundowring. The closest skate park to Upper Gundowring is Mount Beauty BMX Bike Track in Mount Beauty, about 38 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Upper Gundowring is Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana, about 43 minutes' drive. Wodonga is the closest bigger centre to Upper Gundowring, about 49 minutes away by car, and has 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Upper Gundowring, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Banksia Park in Tangambalanga (34 min), Pebble Beach in Tawonga South (35 min), Mount Beauty BMX Bike Track in Mount Beauty (38 min), Mt Beauty Library in Mount Beauty (38 min), Skate Park in Yackandandah (40 min) and the playground at Sir Isaac Isaacs Park in Yackandandah (41 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Upper Gundowring.

Nearby suburbs

Eskdale, next to Upper Gundowring, has a pool. Gundowring and Dederang also border Upper Gundowring.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Upper Gundowring, without traffic.
