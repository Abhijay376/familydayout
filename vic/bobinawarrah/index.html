Bobinawarrah is in the Wangaratta area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bobinawarrah, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

The Vines 2 Vines: Whitfield to Milawa passes through Bobinawarrah.

Getting around

Bobinawarrah has no train station; Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 25 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bobinawarrah, so most trips start by car. By car from Bobinawarrah, Wangaratta is about 27 minutes away and Benalla about 48 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford is about 30 minutes' drive from Bobinawarrah. The closest library to Bobinawarrah is Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Bobinawarrah is Sandy Beach in Beechworth, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Bobinawarrah is Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford, about 30 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 24 minutes' drive from Bobinawarrah. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Bobinawarrah, about 27 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Bobinawarrah are the playground at John McAleese Community Park in Milawa (12 min), the playground at Apex Park in Moyhu (25 min), The Old School Museum in Myrtleford (30 min), Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford (30 min), Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford (30 min) and the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford (30 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Bobinawarrah on the Vines 2 Vines: Whitfield to Milawa.

Nearby suburbs

Whorouly, next to Bobinawarrah, has 1 playground. Next door to Bobinawarrah, Milawa has 1 playground. Markwood and Carboor also border Bobinawarrah.
