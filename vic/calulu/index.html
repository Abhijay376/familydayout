Calulu is in the East Gippsland area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Calulu, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Calulu. The closest to Calulu is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 10 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Calulu, so most trips start by car. By car from Calulu, Bairnsdale is about 18 minutes away and Lakes Entrance about 53 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale is about 17 minutes' drive from Calulu. The closest library to Calulu is Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Calulu is Boyfriend Beach in Forge Creek, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Calulu is the skate park at Gilsenan Reserve in Paynesville, about 34 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale is about 18 minutes' drive from Calulu. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow is about 24 minutes' drive from Calulu. Bairnsdale is the closest bigger centre to Calulu, about 18 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Calulu are the playground at Amy Shand Park in Lindenow (14 min), Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale (17 min), Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale (17 min), Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale (19 min), Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale (18 min) and Krowathunkoolong Keeping Place in Bairnsdale (19 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Calulu.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Calulu, Wy Yung has 3 playgrounds. Next door to Calulu, Lindenow has 1 playground. Melwood, Hillside, Flaggy Creek and Ellaswood also border Calulu.
