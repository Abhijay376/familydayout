Lake Eildon is in the Mansfield area, and our data lists 45 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lake Eildon is Lake Eildon NP (VPC addition), covering about 6 hectares. Lake Eildon also has Dunns, Kirbys and Leo’s. Our data shows 24 public toilets in Lake Eildon. For camping, there's Acacia and Bee Tree. For walks and rides, Mansfield - Goughs Bay crosses Lake Eildon.

Getting around

Lake Eildon has no train station; Euroa Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 54 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lake Eildon, so most trips start by car. By car from Lake Eildon, Mansfield is about 1.5 hours away and Benalla about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Lake Eildon, the nearest places for kids by road are Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield (88 min).

Best for

Free days on the Mansfield - Goughs Bay and in Lake Eildon's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Lake Eildon, Eildon has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Lake Eildon, Jamieson has 1 playground. Next door to Lake Eildon, Bonnie Doon has 1 playground. Piries, Mountain Bay, Howqua Inlet and Howes Creek also border Lake Eildon.
