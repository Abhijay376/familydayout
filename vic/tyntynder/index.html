Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Tyntynder in the Swan Hill area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Tyntynder. The closest to Tyntynder is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tyntynder, so most trips start by car. By car from Tyntynder, Swan Hill is about 16 minutes away and Echuca about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill is about 15 minutes' drive from Tyntynder. For a library, Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill is about 15 minutes' drive from Tyntynder. The closest skate park to Tyntynder is Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South, about 21 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill is about 16 minutes' drive from Tyntynder. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Tyntynder, about 16 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Tyntynder are Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South (21 min), the playground at Adams Park in Swan Hill (16 min), Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill (15 min), the skate park at James Belsar Reserve in Swan Hill (16 min), Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill (15 min) and Swan Hill Outdoor Pool in Swan Hill (16 min).
