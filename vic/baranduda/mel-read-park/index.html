Mel Read Park

Playground in Baranduda · Basic info

Mel Read Park in Baranduda is a public playground in the Wodonga City area. The Bananduda Bike Path passes about 290 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 320 m away. There's a public toilet about 420 m away, and bus B stops at Baranduda Community Centre/Sage Ct, a 5-minute walk away.
