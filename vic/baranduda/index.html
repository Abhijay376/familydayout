Baranduda in the Wodonga area has 8 playgrounds and 1 park or reserve in our data. On top of that, Baranduda has a pool and a skate park.

Landmarks

Baranduda Reserve is the largest mapped park in Baranduda, at about 5 hectares. Playgrounds around Baranduda include Bidgood Park, Mel Read Park and the playground at Baranduda Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Baranduda. Places to visit in Baranduda include Edge Aquatics. For older kids in Baranduda there's Skate Park. The High Country Rail Trail and Bananduda Bike Path all pass through Baranduda. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Baranduda for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Baranduda. The closest to Baranduda is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the 7, B, Albury - Wodonga - Myrtleford and Albury - Mt Beauty via Baranduda and Tawonga South routes stop in Baranduda. By car from Baranduda, Wodonga is about 11 minutes away and Wangaratta about 63 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Upper Murray Regional Library - Wodonga in Wodonga is about 10 minutes' drive from Baranduda. The closest beach to Baranduda is Finns Beach in Bandiana, about 16 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana is about 6 minutes' drive from Baranduda.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 8 playgrounds across Baranduda. Teens who skate or ride, with Skate Park in Baranduda. Free days on the High Country Rail Trail and in Baranduda's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Baranduda, Bandiana has 6 playgrounds, a beach and a cinema. Next door to Baranduda, Leneva has 3 playgrounds. Bonegilla, next to Baranduda, has 3 playgrounds.
