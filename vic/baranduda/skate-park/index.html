Skate Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Baranduda · Basic info

Skate Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Baranduda, in the Wodonga City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Baranduda Community Centre/Sage Ct (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 107 m away.
