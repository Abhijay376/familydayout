Bidgood Park

Playground in Baranduda · Basic info

Bidgood Park is a public playground in Baranduda, in the Wodonga City area. Edge Aquatics is about 390 m away, and Playground on Lorage Street is another option about 200 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 460 m from the playground, and bus B stops at Baranduda Primary School/7 Verbeena St, a 3-minute walk away.
