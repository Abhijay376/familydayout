Splash Pools and Stadium

Pool or water play in Leongatha · Basic info

Splash Pools and Stadium is a pool or water play in Leongatha, in the South Gippsland Shire Council area, good for all ages. Leongatha Railway Station/Long St (bus) is a 10-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 419 m away.
