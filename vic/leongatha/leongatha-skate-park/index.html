Leongatha Skate Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Leongatha · Basic info

Leongatha Skate Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Leongatha, in the South Gippsland Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Leongatha Railway Station/Long St (bus) is a 13-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 41 m away.
