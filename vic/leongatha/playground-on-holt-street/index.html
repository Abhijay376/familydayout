Playground on Holt Street

Playground in Leongatha · Basic info

Playground on Holt Street in Leongatha is a public playground in the South Gippsland Shire area. The Great Southern Rail Trail is about 40 m away, and for food, Punjabi Pantry Leongatha is about 220 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 80 m from the playground, and coaches stop at Leongatha Railway Station/Long St, a 7-minute walk away.
