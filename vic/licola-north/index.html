Licola North in the Wellington area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Other parks and reserves in Licola North include Rumpffs and Barkley Bridge. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Licola North. For camping, there's Barkley Bridge and Rumpffs. For walks and rides, McMillans Walking Track and Middle Ridge Track cross Licola North.

Getting around

Licola North has no train station; Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 77 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Licola North, so most trips start by car. By car from Licola North, Sale is about 2.5 hours away and Traralgon about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Free days on the McMillans Walking Track and in Licola North's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Licola North, Mount Buller has a cinema. Crookayan also borders Licola North.
