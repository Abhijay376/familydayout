Bendoc is in the East Gippsland area, and our data lists 12 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bendoc is Musket Creek Reference Area, covering about 642 hectares. The Gap Scenic Reserve and Bidwell Education Area are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Bendoc include Wilsons Hut, Jungle King Mine and Delegate River Tunnel. Our data shows 6 public toilets in Bendoc. Picnic areas in Bendoc include Delegate River Tunnel, Jungle King Mine and Queensborough Campground. The Delegate River Tunnel passes through Bendoc.

Getting around

Bendoc has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 134 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bendoc, so most trips start by car. By car from Bendoc, Lakes Entrance is about 2.5 hours away and Bairnsdale about 3 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Free days on the Delegate River Tunnel and in Bendoc's parks.
