Lynbrook in the Casey area has 6 playgrounds and 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Cranbourne Wetlands Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Lynbrook, at about 25 hectares. Banjo Paterson Park and Tower Hill Park are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Lynbrook include Mackellar Park. Playgrounds around Lynbrook include the playground at Mackellar Park, the playground at Tower Hill Park and the playground at Banjo Paterson Park. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Lynbrook. Lynbrook Village is the local shopping centre. For walks and rides, Lynbrook Creek Trail crosses Lynbrook. Our data lists 4 cafes and family restaurants in Lynbrook for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Lynbrook has no train station; Lynbrook Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 900 metres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 863, 891, 892, 893, 895 and 982 stop in Lynbrook. By car from Lynbrook, Cranbourne is about 8 minutes away, Dandenong about 10 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 35 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lynbrook is Paul Sadler Swimland in Narre Warren South, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest library to Lynbrook is Hampton Park Library in Hampton Park, about 5 minutes' drive. For a beach, Carrum Beach in Carrum is about 18 minutes' drive from Lynbrook. The closest skate park to Lynbrook is the pump track at Courtenay Avenue Reserve in Cranbourne North, about 5 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Narre Warren is about 11 minutes' drive from Lynbrook. For an indoor play centre, Oz Tenpin in Narre Warren is about 10 minutes' drive from Lynbrook. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Lynbrook is Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale, about 21 minutes' drive.

Best for

Younger kids at Lynbrook's 6 local playgrounds. Free days on the Lynbrook Creek Trail and in Lynbrook's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Hampton Park, next to Lynbrook, has 20 playgrounds and a library. Cranbourne North, next to Lynbrook, has 12 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Lynbrook, Lyndhurst has 7 playgrounds. Next door to Lynbrook, Dandenong South has 1 playground.
