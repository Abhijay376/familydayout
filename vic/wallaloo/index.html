Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Wallaloo in the Northern Grampians area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wallaloo. The closest to Wallaloo is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 60 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wallaloo, so most trips start by car. By car from Wallaloo, Stawell is about 38 minutes away and Ararat about 65 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Wallaloo is Rupanyup Swimming Pool in Rupanyup, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Wallaloo is the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell, about 42 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Wallaloo is Caspers Tourist Park in Stawell, about 41 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Stawell, about 38 minutes from Wallaloo by car, with 6 playgrounds and a pool. From Wallaloo, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Shanahan Park in Marnoo (10 min), Guthrie Park Playground in Rich Avon West (26 min), Rupanyup Swimming Pool in Rupanyup (29 min), Woods' Farming and Heritage Museum in Rupanyup (29 min), Stawell Aquatic Centre in Stawell (37 min) and Railway Station Art and Crafts Gallery in Stawell (39 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wallaloo.

Nearby suburbs

Marnoo, next to Wallaloo, has 1 playground. Callawadda also borders Wallaloo.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Wallaloo, without traffic.
