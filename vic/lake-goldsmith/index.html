Lake Goldsmith is in the Pyrenees area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lake Goldsmith is Lake Goldsmith Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 924 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lake Goldsmith. The closest to Lake Goldsmith is Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lake Goldsmith, so most trips start by car. By car from Lake Goldsmith, Ararat is about 46 minutes away, Ballarat about 49 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lake Goldsmith is Beaufort Pool in Beaufort, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest library to Lake Goldsmith is Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Lake Goldsmith is the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort, about 12 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Ararat, about 46 minutes from Lake Goldsmith by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Lake Goldsmith are the playground at Wotherspoon Reserve in Beaufort (12 min), Beaufort Pool in Beaufort (12 min), Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort (12 min), the playground at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (12 min), the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (12 min) and Linton Skate Park in Linton (28 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lake Goldsmith.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Lake Goldsmith, Beaufort has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Mena Park also borders Lake Goldsmith.
