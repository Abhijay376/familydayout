Reedy Creek is in the Mitchell area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Reedy Creek, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Reedy Creek. The closest to Reedy Creek is Broadford Railway Station (V/Line), about 9.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Reedy Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Reedy Creek, Seymour is about 41 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 68 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 87 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore is about 35 minutes' drive from Reedy Creek. For a library, Broadford Library in Broadford is about 23 minutes' drive from Reedy Creek. The closest skate park to Reedy Creek is Broadford Skate Park in Broadford, about 21 minutes' drive. Seymour is the closest bigger centre to Reedy Creek, about 41 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Reedy Creek are Rupert Street Playground in Broadford (21 min), Broadford Skate Park in Broadford (21 min), Sutherland Street Playground in Broadford (22 min), Broadford Library in Broadford (23 min), Kilmore Skate Park in Kilmore (34 min) and Kilmore Library in Kilmore (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Reedy Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Reedy Creek, Broadford has 6 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Reedy Creek, Tallarook has 1 playground. Clonbinane also borders Reedy Creek.
