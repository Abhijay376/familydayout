Playground on Hockley Avenue

Playground in Mickleham · Basic info

Playground on Hockley Avenue is a public playground in Mickleham, in the Hume City area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.1 km away, and bus 525 stops at Salcey Way Playground/The Woods Bvd, a 1-minute walk away.
