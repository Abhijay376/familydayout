Merrifield City Shopping Centre

Shopping centre in Mickleham · Basic info

Merrifield City Shopping Centre is a shopping centre in Mickleham, in the Hume City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Merrifield Bvd/Donnybrook Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 103 m away with a baby change table.
