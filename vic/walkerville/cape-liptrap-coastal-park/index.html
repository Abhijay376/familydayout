Cape Liptrap Coastal Park

Park in Walkerville · Official details

Cape Liptrap Coastal Park is a park in Walkerville, in the South Gippsland Shire Council area, good for all ages.

Details

Seasons
Venus Bay No. 1 Beach patrolled by surf lifesavers in summer (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
Strong currents and rips along Venus Bay beaches; Bear Gully beach is unpatrolled, not recommended for swimming (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Cape Liptrap Coastal Park?
Cape Liptrap Coastal Park is in Walkerville, Victoria.
Is Cape Liptrap Coastal Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
