Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Jumbuk in the Latrobe area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Jumbuk. The closest to Jumbuk is Morwell Railway Station (V/Line), about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Jumbuk, so most trips start by car. By car from Jumbuk, Traralgon is about 39 minutes away, Warragul about 65 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Latrobe Leisure - Churchill in Churchill is about 22 minutes' drive from Jumbuk. For a library, Churchill Library in Churchill is about 21 minutes' drive from Jumbuk. For a skate park, Churchill Skate Park in Churchill is about 22 minutes' drive from Jumbuk. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 30 minutes' drive from Jumbuk. The nearest larger centre is Traralgon, about 39 minutes from Jumbuk by car, with 38 playgrounds and a pool. From Jumbuk, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Reservoir Park in Churchill (21 min), Walkley Park in Churchill (22 min), Churchill Library in Churchill (21 min), Churchill Skate Park in Churchill (22 min), Latrobe Leisure - Churchill in Churchill (22 min) and ARC Yinnar in Yinnar (25 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Jumbuk.
