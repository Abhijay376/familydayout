Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Durham Ox in the Loddon area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Durham Ox has no train station; Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Durham Ox. By car from Durham Ox, Bendigo is about 67 minutes away, Echuca about 69 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Durham Ox is Boort Swimming Pool in Boort, about 18 minutes' drive. For a library, Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang is about 35 minutes' drive from Durham Ox. From Durham Ox, the nearest places for kids by road are A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park in Boort (17 min), Pyramid Hill & Districts Historical Museum in Pyramid Hill (16 min), Boort Court House Historical Museum in Boort (17 min), Boort Swimming Pool in Boort (18 min), the playground at R J Phelan Reserve in Mitiamo (26 min) and Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Durham Ox.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Durham Ox, Boort has 1 playground and a pool. Next door to Durham Ox, Yarrawalla has 1 playground. Pyramid Hill, next to Durham Ox, has 1 playground. Loddon Vale, Jarklin, Gladfield and Fernihurst also border Durham Ox.
