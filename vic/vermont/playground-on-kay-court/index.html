Playground on Kay Court

Playground in Vermont · Basic info

Playground on Kay Court in Vermont is a public playground in the Whitehorse City area. The Whitehorse NS10 passes about 100 m away, and Scott Street Playground is another option about 580 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and buses 738 and 742 stop at Lusk Dr/Boronia Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
