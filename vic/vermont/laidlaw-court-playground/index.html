Laidlaw Court Playground

Playground in Vermont · Basic info

Laidlaw Court Playground in Vermont is a public playground in the Whitehorse City area. The Whitehorse NS10 passes about 150 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 380 m away. There's a public toilet about 580 m away, and bus 740 stops at Canterbury Rd/Purches St, a 4-minute walk away.
