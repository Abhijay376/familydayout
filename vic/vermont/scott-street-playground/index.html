Scott Street Playground

Playground in Vermont · Basic info

This is a public playground in Vermont. The Whitehorse NS10 is about 340 m away, and Playground on Kay Court is another option about 580 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.1 km from the playground, and bus 740 stops at Scott St/Churinga Ave, a 4-minute walk away.
