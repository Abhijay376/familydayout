Culbara Drive Playground

Playground in Vermont · Basic info

Culbara Drive Playground is a public playground in Vermont, in the Whitehorse City area. The Dandenong Creek Trail;EastLink Trail passes about 230 m away, and Playground on Rowan Street is another option about 460 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.1 km away, and buses 740 and 742 stop at Beddoe Rd/Centre Rd, a 7-minute walk away.
