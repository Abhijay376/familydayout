Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Kithbrook, in the Strathbogie area.

Landmarks

Marraweeny I57 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Kithbrook, at about 22 hectares.

Getting around

Kithbrook has no train station; Violet Town Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kithbrook, so most trips start by car. By car from Kithbrook, Benalla is about 61 minutes away, Mansfield about 62 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Kithbrook is Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa, about 36 minutes' drive. For a library, Euroa Library in Euroa is about 36 minutes' drive from Kithbrook. For a skate park, Euroa Skate Park in Euroa is about 35 minutes' drive from Kithbrook. The closest cinema to Kithbrook is Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Kithbrook are Strathbogie Recreation Reserve Playground in Strathbogie (5 min), Farmers Arms Hotel Museum in Euroa (35 min), Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa (35 min), Euroa Skate Park in Euroa (35 min), the playground at Euroa Rotary Park in Euroa (35 min) and Euroa Library in Euroa (36 min).
