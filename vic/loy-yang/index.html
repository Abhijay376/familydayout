Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Loy Yang in the Latrobe area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Loy Yang has no train station; Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 6.8 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Loy Yang, so most trips start by car. By car from Loy Yang, Traralgon is about 14 minutes away, Warragul about 55 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon is about 15 minutes' drive from Loy Yang. For a library, Morwell Library in Morwell is about 21 minutes' drive from Loy Yang. For a skate park, Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon is about 17 minutes' drive from Loy Yang. The closest cinema to Loy Yang is Village 8 in Morwell, about 20 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Traralgon, about 14 minutes from Loy Yang by car, with 38 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Loy Yang are the playground at Hourigan Park in Traralgon (12 min), the playground at Eric Taylor Reserve in Traralgon (13 min), Gippsland Regional Aquatic Centre in Traralgon (15 min), Traralgon Skate Park in Traralgon (17 min), Powerworks in Morwell (20 min) and Village 8 in Morwell (20 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Loy Yang.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Loy Yang, Traralgon has 38 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Traralgon East, next to Loy Yang, has 2 playgrounds. Flynn and Callignee also border Loy Yang.
