Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Lake Wellington, in the Wellington area.

Landmarks

The Dardenelles Gippsland Lakes Reserve is the largest mapped park in Lake Wellington, at about 303 hectares. Poddy Bay Gippsland Lakes Reserve and Tucker Swamp Gippsland Lakes Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lake Wellington. The closest to Lake Wellington is Sale Railway Station (V/Line), about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lake Wellington, so most trips start by car. By car from Lake Wellington, Sale is about 34 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 64 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, AquaEnergy in Sale is about 36 minutes' drive from Lake Wellington. The closest library to Lake Wellington is Sale Library in Sale, about 35 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Sale Skate Park in Sale is about 36 minutes' drive from Lake Wellington. For a cinema, Former Drive-in site in Sale is about 32 minutes' drive from Lake Wellington. The nearest larger centre is Sale, about 34 minutes from Lake Wellington by car, with 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Lake Wellington are Ibis Avenue - Pelican Court Playground in Sale (33 min), Lions Park Playground in Sale (33 min), Former Drive-in site in Sale (32 min), AquaEnergy in Sale (36 min), Indoor pool in Sale (36 min) and Roulette Cinema in East Sale (36 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lake Wellington.

Nearby suburbs

Dutson Downs, Dutson and Clydebank also border Lake Wellington.
