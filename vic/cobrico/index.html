Cobrico is in the Corangamite area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Cobrico Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Cobrico, at about 13 hectares. Cobrico Recreation Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cobrico. The closest to Cobrico is Terang Railway Station (V/Line), about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cobrico, so most trips start by car. By car from Cobrico, Colac is about 47 minutes away, Warrnambool about 48 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon is about 30 minutes' drive from Cobrico. For a library, Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake is about 33 minutes' drive from Cobrico. The closest beach to Cobrico is Buttress Point in The Cove, about 42 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown is about 22 minutes' drive from Cobrico. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Cobrico is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 40 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 47 minutes from Cobrico by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Cobrico, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve in Camperdown (22 min), Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (22 min), the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (29 min), the playground at Tea Tree Lake Reserve in Mortlake (34 min), Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake (33 min) and Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (30 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Cobrico.

Nearby suburbs

Cobden, next to Cobrico, has 1 playground. Boorcan also borders Cobrico.
