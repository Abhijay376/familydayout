Connewarre in the Greater Geelong area has 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, Connewarre has 2 beaches.

Landmarks

Lake Connewarre Wildlife Reserve is the largest mapped park in Connewarre, at about 3,692 hectares. Breamlea Flora and Fauna Reserve and Conewarre K48 Streamside Reserve are the next largest. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Connewarre. Places to visit in Connewarre include Bancoora Beach and Thirteenth Beach. For walks and rides, Barwon Heads Trail crosses Connewarre.

Getting around

There's no train station in Connewarre. The closest to Connewarre is Marshall Railway Station (V/Line), about 8.1 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 55 stops in Connewarre. By car from Connewarre, Torquay is about 14 minutes away, Geelong about 16 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 79 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Salt Water Swim Schools in Mount Duneed is about 10 minutes' drive from Connewarre. The closest library to Connewarre is Biyal-an Armstrong Creek Library in Armstrong Creek, about 9 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the bmx track at Kalkarra Park in Mount Duneed is about 5 minutes' drive from Connewarre. For a cinema, Reading Cinemas in Grovedale is about 14 minutes' drive from Connewarre. The closest indoor play centre to Connewarre is Bounce Inc in Grovedale, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Connewarre is Tiger Moth World in Torquay, about 6 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Torquay, about 14 minutes from Connewarre by car, with 15 playgrounds, a library and a beach.

Best for

Beach days at Bancoora Beach and Thirteenth Beach. Free days on the Barwon Heads Trail and in Connewarre's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Leopold, next to Connewarre, has 18 playgrounds, a library and a beach. Next door to Connewarre, Torquay has 15 playgrounds, a library and a beach. Next door to Connewarre, Mount Duneed has 12 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Armstrong Creek, next to Connewarre, has 8 playgrounds and a library. Moolap, Breamlea, Wallington and Barwon Heads also border Connewarre.
