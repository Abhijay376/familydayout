Landmarks

Cerberus Rugby Field is the largest mapped park in Hmas Cerberus, at about 5 hectares. The Western Port Bay Trail passes through Hmas Cerberus.

Getting around

There's no train station in Hmas Cerberus. The closest to Hmas Cerberus is Crib Point Railway Station (metro), about 500 metres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 782 stops in Hmas Cerberus. By car from Hmas Cerberus, Mornington is about 29 minutes away, Frankston about 30 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 67 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Pelican Park Recreation Centre in Hastings is about 11 minutes' drive from Hmas Cerberus. The closest library to Hmas Cerberus is Hastings Library in Hastings, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Hmas Cerberus is Micks Beach in French Island, about 18 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Skate Ramp in Hastings is about 11 minutes' drive from Hmas Cerberus. For a cinema, Dromana Triple Drive-In Theatre in Dromana is about 23 minutes' drive from Hmas Cerberus. The closest indoor play centre to Hmas Cerberus is Kidz Shed in Hastings, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Hmas Cerberus is Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale, about 22 minutes' drive. Mornington is the closest bigger centre to Hmas Cerberus, about 29 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a library, indoor play and a beach. From Hmas Cerberus, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Arthur W Peterson Reserve in Crib Point (2 min), the playground at Crib Point Recreation Reserve in Crib Point (4 min), Victorian Maritime Centre in Crib Point (4 min), Hastings Museum in Hastings (10 min), Hastings Library in Hastings (11 min) and Pelican Park Recreation Centre in Hastings (11 min).

Best for
