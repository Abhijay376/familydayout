Craigieburn Centennial Park

Park in Craigieburn · Basic info

Craigieburn Centennial Park is a park in Craigieburn, in the Hume City Council area, good for all ages. Craigieburn Bicentennial Park/Craigieburn Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 28 m away.
