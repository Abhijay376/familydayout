Playground at Golden Sun Moth Park

Playground in Craigieburn · Basic info

Playground at Golden Sun Moth Park is a public playground in Craigieburn, in the Hume City area. The Malcolm Creek Trail is about 160 m away, and for food, Nando's is about 470 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 810 m from the playground, and bus 529 stops at Malcolm Creek Park/Grand Bvd, a 3-minute walk away.
