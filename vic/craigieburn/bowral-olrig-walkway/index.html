Bowral-Olrig Walkway

Park in Craigieburn · Basic info

Bowral-Olrig Walkway is a park in Craigieburn, in the Hume City Council area, good for all ages. Explorers Pl/Grand Bvd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1209 m away with a baby change table.
