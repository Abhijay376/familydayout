Playground on Essex Way

Playground in Craigieburn · Basic info

Playground on Essex Way is a public playground in Craigieburn, in the Hume City area. Playground on Windrock Avenue is another option about 200 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 390 m from the playground, and bus 953 stops at Mount Ridley College/Windrock Ave, a 6-minute walk away.
