Playground on Yarcombe Crescent

Playground in Craigieburn · Basic info

This is a public playground in Craigieburn. The Broadmeadows Valley Trail is about 340 m away, and Playground at Pembroke Crescent Reserve is another option about 540 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 800 m from the playground, and buses 544 and 953 stop at Hothlyn Dr/Bridgewater Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
