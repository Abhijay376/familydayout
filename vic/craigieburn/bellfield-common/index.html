Bellfield Common

Park in Craigieburn · Basic info

Bellfield Common is a park in Craigieburn, in the Hume City Council area, good for all ages. Highlands Retirement Village/Waterview Bvd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 475 m away with a baby change table.
