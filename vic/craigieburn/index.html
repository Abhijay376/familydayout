Craigieburn in the Hume area has 54 playgrounds and 73 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, Craigieburn has a library, a pool, a cinema and an indoor play centre.

Landmarks

Future Conservation Area 27 - Summerhill Road (West), Wollert is the largest mapped park in Craigieburn, at about 26 hectares. Golden Sun Moth Park and Malcolm Creek Linear Park are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Craigieburn include Anzac Park, Olrig Field and Newbury Park. Playgrounds in Craigieburn include Volcano Park, the playground at Anzac Park and the playground at Olrig Field. Our data shows 31 public toilets in Craigieburn. Places to visit in Craigieburn include United Cinemas, Timezone, Splash Aqua Park and Leisure Centre and Craigieburn Library. For older kids in Craigieburn there's the skate park at Aston Reserve and the skate park at D.S Aitken Reserve. Craigieburn Central, Craigieburn Junction and Craigieburn Plaza are the main shopping centres. The Galada Tamboore Trail, Galada Tamboore Pathway and Broadmeadows Valley Trail all pass through Craigieburn. Our data lists 22 cafes and family restaurants in Craigieburn for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Craigieburn has a train station: Craigieburn Railway Station (metro: Craigieburn Line, Seymour - Melbourne via Broadmeadows and Shepparton - Melbourne via Seymour). 13 bus routes stop in Craigieburn, including 390, 501, 511, 525, 528 and 529. V/Line coaches stop in Craigieburn. By car from Craigieburn, Broadmeadows is about 18 minutes away, Sunshine about 29 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 37 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a beach, Horse Arena in Abbotsford is about 37 minutes' drive from Craigieburn. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Craigieburn is Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville, about 32 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 54 playgrounds across Craigieburn. Rainy days at the library, cinema and indoor play centre in Craigieburn. Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at Aston Reserve in Craigieburn. Free days on the Galada Tamboore Trail and in Craigieburn's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Epping, next to Craigieburn, has 43 playgrounds, a cinema and a skate park. Greenvale, next to Craigieburn, has 21 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Craigieburn, Roxburgh Park has 14 playgrounds and a skate park. Wollert, next to Craigieburn, has 12 playgrounds. Donnybrook, Mickleham, Kalkallo and Yuroke also border Craigieburn.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Craigieburn, without traffic.
