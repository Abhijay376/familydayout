Playground at Pembroke Crescent Reserve

Playground in Craigieburn · Basic info

Playground at Pembroke Crescent Reserve in Craigieburn is a public playground in the Hume City area. The Broadmeadows Valley Trail is about 140 m away, and Playground on Yarcombe Crescent is another option about 540 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.2 km from the playground, and bus 528 stops at Ramsbury St/Newlyn Dr, a 5-minute walk away.
