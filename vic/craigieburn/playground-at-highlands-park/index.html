Playground at Highlands Park

Playground in Craigieburn · Basic info

Playground at Highlands Park in Craigieburn is a public playground in the Hume City area. The Aitken Creek Trail passes about 290 m away, and Craigieburn Library is about 400 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 50 m away, and buses 529 and 543 stop at Highlands Retirement Village/Waterview Bvd, a 4-minute walk away.
