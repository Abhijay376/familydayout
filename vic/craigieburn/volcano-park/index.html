Volcano Park

Playground in Craigieburn · Basic info

This is a public playground in Craigieburn. The Aitken Creek Trail is about 250 m away, and Playground on Elevation Boulevard is another option about 260 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 620 m from the playground, and bus 528 stops at Waterview Bvd/Elevation Bvd, a 4-minute walk away.
