Playground at Olrig Field

Playground in Craigieburn · Basic info

Playground at Olrig Field in Craigieburn is a public playground in the Hume City area. Playground at Hamilton Hume Reserve is another option about 360 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 920 m from the playground, and buses 533 and 953 stop at Windrock Ave/Grand Bvd, a 1-minute walk away.
