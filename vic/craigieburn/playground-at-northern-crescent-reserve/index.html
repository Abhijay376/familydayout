Playground at Northern Crescent Reserve

Playground in Craigieburn · Basic info

Playground at Northern Crescent Reserve is a public playground in Craigieburn, in the Hume City area. The Malcolm Creek Trail passes about 160 m away, and Playground on Field Street is another option about 440 m away. There's a public toilet about 480 m away, and buses 533 and 953 stop at Kingswood Dr/Hanson Rd, a 8-minute walk away.
