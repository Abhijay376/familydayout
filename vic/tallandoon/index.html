Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Tallandoon, in the Towong area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tallandoon is Mitta Mitta River K27 Streamside Reserve, covering about 17 hectares. Tallandoon also has Mitta Mitta River - Tallandoon Camping Area 1a (CLWF). For camping, there's Mitta Mitta River - Tallandoon Camping Area 1a (CLWF).

Getting around

There's no train station in Tallandoon. The closest to Tallandoon is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 46 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tallandoon, so most trips start by car. By car from Tallandoon, Wodonga is about 47 minutes away and Wangaratta about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tallandoon is Eskdale Swimming Pool in Eskdale, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest library to Tallandoon is Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Tallandoon is Skate Park in Baranduda, about 38 minutes' drive. Wodonga is the closest bigger centre to Tallandoon, about 47 minutes away by car, and has 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Tallandoon, the nearest places for kids by road are Eskdale Swimming Pool in Eskdale (6 min), the playground at Banksia Park in Tangambalanga (25 min), Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta (26 min), Tallangatta Art & Photography in Tallangatta (26 min), Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta (26 min) and the playground at Ludlows Reserve in Ebden (31 min).
