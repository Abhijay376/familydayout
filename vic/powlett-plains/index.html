Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Powlett Plains, in the Loddon area.

Landmarks

Inglewood Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Powlett Plains, at about 1,608 hectares. Serpentine Weir Streamside Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Powlett Plains. The closest to Powlett Plains is Raywood Railway Station (V/Line), about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Powlett Plains, so most trips start by car. By car from Powlett Plains, Bendigo is about 67 minutes away, Castlemaine about 84 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn is about 44 minutes' drive from Powlett Plains. The closest places for kids by road from Powlett Plains are Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (45 min), Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (44 min) and Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood (49 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Powlett Plains.

Nearby suburbs

Glenalbyn and Fiery Flat also border Powlett Plains.
