Yinnar South is in the Latrobe area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Yinnar South is Morwell National Park, covering about 565 hectares. Budgeree Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Yinnar South. For walks and rides, Stringybark Ridge Track, Fosters Gully Nature Walk and West Boundary Track cross Yinnar South.

Getting around

Yinnar South has no train station; Morwell Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Yinnar South, so most trips start by car. By car from Yinnar South, Traralgon is about 37 minutes away, Leongatha about 60 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Latrobe Leisure - Churchill in Churchill is about 20 minutes' drive from Yinnar South. For a library, Churchill Library in Churchill is about 19 minutes' drive from Yinnar South. The closest skate park to Yinnar South is Churchill Skate Park in Churchill, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Yinnar South is Village 8 in Morwell, about 28 minutes' drive. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Yinnar South, about 37 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Yinnar South are ARC Yinnar in Yinnar (15 min), the playground at Yinnar Centenary Garden in Yinnar (15 min), Railway Trolley Shed in Yinnar (15 min), the playground at Reservoir Park in Churchill (19 min), Churchill Library in Churchill (19 min) and Churchill Skate Park in Churchill (20 min).

Best for

Free days on the Stringybark Ridge Track and in Yinnar South's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Yinnar, next to Yinnar South, has 3 playgrounds. Boolarra, next to Yinnar South, has 1 playground and a skate park. Budgeree also borders Yinnar South.
