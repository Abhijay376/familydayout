Gipsy Village Park

Park in Hampton · Basic info

Gipsy Village Park is a park in Hampton, in the Bayside City Council area, good for all ages. Nelson St/Highett Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 656 m away.
