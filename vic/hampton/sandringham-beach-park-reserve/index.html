Sandringham Beach Park Reserve

Park in Hampton · Basic info

Sandringham Beach Park Reserve is a park in Hampton, in the Bayside City Council area, good for all ages. Jetty Rd/Beach Rd (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 136 m away.
