Sandringham Harbour Beach

Beach in Hampton · Basic info

Sandringham Harbour Beach is a beach in Hampton, in the Bayside City Council area, good for all ages. Triangle Gardens/Hampton St (bus) is a 8-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 330 m away and dogs are allowed.
