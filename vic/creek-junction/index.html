Creek Junction in the Benalla area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Toorour Reference Area is the largest mapped park in Creek Junction, at about 461 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Creek Junction include Rocky Ned Camp and Rocky Ned Camp 2. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Creek Junction. Picnic areas in Creek Junction include Rocky Ned Camp. For camping, there's Rocky Ned Camp 2. The Rocky Ned Falls Walk, Rocky Ned and Lima Falls all pass through Creek Junction.

Getting around

Creek Junction has no train station; Violet Town Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Creek Junction, so most trips start by car. By car from Creek Junction, Benalla is about 60 minutes away, Mansfield about 65 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa is about 45 minutes' drive from Creek Junction. The closest library to Creek Junction is Euroa Library in Euroa, about 45 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Euroa Skate Park in Euroa is about 44 minutes' drive from Creek Junction. The closest cinema to Creek Junction is Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa, about 44 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Benalla, about 60 minutes from Creek Junction by car, with 5 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Creek Junction are Strathbogie Recreation Reserve Playground in Strathbogie (17 min), Violet Town Recreation Reserve East Playground in Violet Town (39 min), Swanpool Cinema in Swanpool (62 min), Farmers Arms Hotel Museum in Euroa (44 min), Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa (44 min) and Euroa Skate Park in Euroa (44 min).

Best for

Free days on the Rocky Ned Falls Walk and in Creek Junction's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Strathbogie, next to Creek Junction, has 1 playground. Next door to Creek Junction, Bonnie Doon has 1 playground. Lima East, Lima, Kithbrook and Boho South also border Creek Junction.
