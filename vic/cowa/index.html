Cowa is in the Wellington area, and our data lists 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Grant Historic Area is the largest mapped park in Cowa, at about 7,408 hectares. Thirteen Mile Spur Reference Area is the next largest. Cowa also has Ollies Jump Up and Two Mile Creek. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Cowa. Picnic areas in Cowa include Ollies Jump Up and Two Mile Creek. For walks and rides, Eldorado Spur Walking Track crosses Cowa.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cowa. The closest to Cowa is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 57 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cowa, so most trips start by car. By car from Cowa, Bairnsdale is about 81 minutes away and Sale about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest places for kids by road from Cowa are Dargo Public Hall Playground in Dargo (8 min) and Dargo Museum in Dargo (9 min).

Best for
