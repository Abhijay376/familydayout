K.P. Hardiman Reserve

Park in Kingsbury · Basic info

K.P. Hardiman Reserve is a park in Kingsbury, in the Darebin City Council area, good for all ages. Kyneton Ave/Winter Cres (bus) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 593 m away.
