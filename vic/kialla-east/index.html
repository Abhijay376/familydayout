Kialla East in the Greater Shepparton area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Broken River, Keadys Bridge Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Kialla East, at about 12 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Kialla East. The closest to Kialla East is Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line), about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Kialla East, so most trips start by car. By car from Kialla East, Shepparton is about 18 minutes away, Benalla about 48 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Aquamoves in Shepparton is about 20 minutes' drive from Kialla East. The closest library to Kialla East is Shepparton Library in Shepparton, about 20 minutes' drive. For a beach, Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton is about 22 minutes' drive from Kialla East. For a skate park, the skate park at Skate Park in Shepparton is about 19 minutes' drive from Kialla East. The closest cinema to Kialla East is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 19 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 19 minutes' drive from Kialla East. For a zoo or wildlife park, Emerald Bank Leisure Land in Kialla is about 21 minutes' drive from Kialla East. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 18 minutes from Kialla East by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Kialla East are the playground at O'Donnell Park in Shepparton East (11 min), the playground at Karramomus Memorial Hall in Karramomus (17 min), Emerald Bank Leisure Land in Kialla (21 min), Museum of Vehicle Evolution - MOVE in Kialla (21 min), Village Cinemas in Shepparton (19 min) and StarBowl in Shepparton (19 min).

Best for
