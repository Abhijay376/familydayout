Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lindsay Point is Berribee Homestead Historic Reserve, covering about 1 hectares.

Getting around

Lindsay Point has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 273 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lindsay Point, so most trips start by car. By car from Lindsay Point, Mildura is about 3.5 hours away and Swan Hill about 6 hours, without traffic.

Best for
