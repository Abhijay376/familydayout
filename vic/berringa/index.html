Berringa is in the Golden Plains area, and our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Berringa Recreation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Berringa, at about 2 hectares. Playgrounds in Berringa include the playground at Berringa Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Berringa. The Rainbow Bird Trail passes through Berringa.

Getting around

There's no train station in Berringa. The closest to Berringa is Wendouree Railway Station (V/Line), about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Berringa, so most trips start by car. By car from Berringa, Ballarat is about 33 minutes away, Daylesford about 72 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood is about 28 minutes' drive from Berringa. The closest library to Berringa is Sebastopol Library in Sebastopol, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Berringa is Dereel Skate Park in Dereel, about 19 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Showbiz Cinemas Ballarat in Smythes Creek is about 26 minutes' drive from Berringa. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 34 minutes' drive from Berringa. For a zoo or wildlife park, Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East is about 39 minutes' drive from Berringa. Ballarat is the closest bigger centre to Berringa, about 33 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Berringa, the nearest places for kids by road are Cape Clear & District Lions Park Playground in Cape Clear (14 min), Dereel Skate Park in Dereel (19 min), Smythesdale Skate Park in Smythesdale (15 min), Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood (28 min), Haddon Tramway Preservation Association in Haddon (23 min) and Showbiz Cinemas Ballarat in Smythes Creek (26 min).

Best for

Free days on the Rainbow Bird Trail and in Berringa's parks.

Nearby suburbs
