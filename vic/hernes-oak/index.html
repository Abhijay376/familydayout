Hernes Oak in the Latrobe area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Outlook Way / Arwon Drive Reserve is the largest mapped park in Hernes Oak, at about 8 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Hernes Oak. The closest to Hernes Oak is Moe Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Hernes Oak, so most trips start by car. By car from Hernes Oak, Traralgon is about 25 minutes away, Warragul about 32 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Hernes Oak is Diving Pool in Moe, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest library to Hernes Oak is Morwell Library in Morwell, about 14 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Moe Skate Park in Moe is about 7 minutes' drive from Hernes Oak. The closest cinema to Hernes Oak is Village 8 in Morwell, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Hernes Oak is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 31 minutes' drive. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Hernes Oak, about 25 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. From Hernes Oak, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Wirrana Drive Reserve in Moe (5 min), the playground at Smallburn Avenue Reserve in Newborough (6 min), Diving Pool in Moe (6 min), Moe Outdoor Pool in Moe (7 min), Moe Skate Park in Moe (7 min) and Morwell Skate Park in Morwell (13 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Hernes Oak.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Hernes Oak, Morwell has 14 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. Moe, next to Hernes Oak, has 14 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Hernes Oak, Newborough has 11 playgrounds. Next door to Hernes Oak, Yallourn has 1 playground. Moe South, Hazelwood, Driffield and Coalville also border Hernes Oak.
