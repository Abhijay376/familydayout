Barongarook is in the Colac Otway area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Barongarook, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

The Old Beechy Rail Trail passes through Barongarook.

Getting around

Barongarook has no train station; Colac Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 9.2 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Barongarook, so most trips start by car. By car from Barongarook, Colac is about 14 minutes away, Geelong about 78 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac is about 13 minutes' drive from Barongarook. For a skate park, the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt is about 10 minutes' drive from Barongarook. For a cinema, Colac Cinemas in Colac is about 13 minutes' drive from Barongarook. The closest indoor play centre to Barongarook is Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac, about 14 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 14 minutes from Barongarook by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Barongarook are the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (10 min), the playground at Inglis Court Park in Colac (12 min), Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (13 min), the playground at Lawrence Court Park in Colac (13 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (13 min) and Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (14 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Barongarook on the Old Beechy Rail Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Elliminyt, next to Barongarook, has 1 playground. Gerangamete and Barongarook West also border Barongarook.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Barongarook, without traffic.
