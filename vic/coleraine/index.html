Coleraine is in the Southern Grampians area, and our data lists 4 playgrounds and 6 parks and reserves in the suburb. Coleraine also has a skate park.

Landmarks

Queens Park is the largest mapped park in Coleraine, at about 56 hectares. Peter Francis Points Arboretum and Coleraine Railway Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Coleraine include East Park, West Park and Coleraine Court House Historic Reserve. Playgrounds in Coleraine include the playground at Queens Park, Silvester Oval Playground and the playground at Coleraine Railway Reserve. Our data shows 8 public toilets and 6 off-leash dog parks in Coleraine. For older kids in Coleraine there's coleraine skate park. The Hamilton-Coleraine Rail Trail passes through Coleraine.

Getting around

Coleraine has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 111 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Coleraine. By car from Coleraine, Hamilton is about 29 minutes away and Portland about 75 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Merino Swimming Pool in Merino is about 30 minutes' drive from Coleraine. The closest library to Coleraine is Hamilton Library in Hamilton, about 29 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton is about 29 minutes' drive from Coleraine. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 29 minutes from Coleraine by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Coleraine's 4 local playgrounds. Teens who skate or ride, with coleraine skate park in Coleraine. Free days on the Hamilton-Coleraine Rail Trail and in Coleraine's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Paschendale, Muntham, Konongwootong and Hilgay also border Coleraine.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Coleraine, without traffic.
