Playground at Queens Park

Playground in Coleraine · Basic info

This is a public playground in Coleraine. Older kids have a skate park about 60 m away, and Silvester Oval Playground is another option about 330 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and coaches stop at Whyte St/Henty St, a 2-minute walk away.
