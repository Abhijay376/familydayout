Coleraine Court House Historic Reserve

Park in Coleraine · Official details

Coleraine Court House Historic Reserve is a park in Coleraine, in the Southern Grampians Shire Council area, good for all ages. Whyte St/Henty St (coach) is a 3-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 101 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed.
