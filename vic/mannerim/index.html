Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Mannerim in the Greater Geelong area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it. Mannerim does have 2 museums or galleries.

Landmarks

Places to visit in Mannerim include Little Tin Local Gallery and Tin Liz Gallery. The Bellarine Rail Trail and Bellarine Peninsula Rail Trail all pass through Mannerim.

Getting around

Mannerim has no train station; Marshall Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mannerim, so most trips start by car. By car from Mannerim, Geelong is about 25 minutes away, Torquay about 39 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Northern Bellarine Aquatic Centre in Curlewis is about 10 minutes' drive from Mannerim. The closest library to Mannerim is Drysdale Library in Drysdale, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Mannerim is Front Beach in Point Lonsdale, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Mannerim is the pump track in Marcus Hill in Marcus Hill, about 6 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Geelong is about 26 minutes' drive from Mannerim. The closest indoor play centre to Mannerim is Laserquest in Geelong, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Mannerim is Adventure Park in Wallington, about 11 minutes' drive. Geelong is the closest bigger centre to Mannerim, about 25 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Mannerim. Longer rides and walks from Mannerim on the Bellarine Rail Trail.

Nearby suburbs

St Leonards, next to Mannerim, has 9 playgrounds and a beach. Point Lonsdale, next to Mannerim, has 7 playgrounds and a beach. Next door to Mannerim, Drysdale has 5 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Mannerim, Wallington has 1 playground, a pool and a zoo or wildlife park. Marcus Hill and Swan Bay also border Mannerim.
