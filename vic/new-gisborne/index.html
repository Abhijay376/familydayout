Getting around

Buses on the 73, 74, 77 and Lancefield - Gisborne via Romsey, Monegeeta & Riddells Creek routes stop in New Gisborne. V/Line coaches stop in New Gisborne. By car from New Gisborne, Kyneton is about 26 minutes away, Bacchus Marsh about 33 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 49 minutes, without traffic.
