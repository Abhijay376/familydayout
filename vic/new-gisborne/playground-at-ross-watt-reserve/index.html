Playground at Ross Watt Reserve

Playground in New Gisborne · Basic info

Playground at Ross Watt Reserve in New Gisborne is a public playground in the Macedon Ranges Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet about 460 m away, and bus 74 stops at Station Rd/Chessy Park Dr , a 2-minute walk away.
