Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Koroop in the Gannawarra area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Koroop. The closest to Koroop is Kerang Railway Station (V/Line), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Koroop, so most trips start by car. By car from Koroop, Swan Hill is about 68 minutes away and Echuca about 75 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Koroop is Swimming Pool in Kerang, about 21 minutes' drive. For a library, Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang is about 21 minutes' drive from Koroop. For a skate park, Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna is about 24 minutes' drive from Koroop. The closest places for kids by road from Koroop are Sir John Gorton Library in Kerang (21 min), Swimming Pool in Kerang (21 min), the playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV) in Koondrook (27 min), Cohuna Skate Park in Cohuna (24 min), the playground at Riverside Park in Koondrook (27 min) and Barham Branch Library in Koondrook (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Koroop.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Koroop, Kerang has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Kerang East and Gannawarra also border Koroop.
