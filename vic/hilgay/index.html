Hilgay is in the Southern Grampians area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Hilgay, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Hilgay. The closest to Hilgay is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 111 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Hilgay, so most trips start by car. By car from Hilgay, Hamilton is about 39 minutes away and Portland about 81 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Merino Swimming Pool in Merino is about 36 minutes' drive from Hilgay. For a library, Hamilton Library in Hamilton is about 39 minutes' drive from Hilgay. The closest skate park to Hilgay is coleraine skate park in Coleraine, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Hilgay is Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton, about 39 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 39 minutes from Hilgay by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Hilgay, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Peter Francis Points Arboretum in Coleraine (8 min), the playground at Coleraine Railway Reserve in Coleraine (11 min), coleraine skate park in Coleraine (11 min), Merino Swimming Pool in Merino (36 min), Gorman's art gallery in Casterton (34 min) and Casterton Pool in Casterton (34 min).
