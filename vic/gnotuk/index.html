Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Gnotuk in the Corangamite area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Gnotuk has no train station; Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 4.8 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gnotuk, so most trips start by car. By car from Gnotuk, Colac is about 42 minutes away, Warrnambool about 54 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Gnotuk is Lismore Pool in Lismore, about 33 minutes' drive. For a library, Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake is about 26 minutes' drive from Gnotuk. For a skate park, Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown is about 7 minutes' drive from Gnotuk. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Gnotuk is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 41 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 42 minutes from Gnotuk by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Gnotuk, the nearest places for kids by road are Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (7 min), the playground at Lake Bullen Merri Reserve in Camperdown (14 min), Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake (27 min), the playground at Tea Tree Lake Reserve in Mortlake (27 min), Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake (26 min) and Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Gnotuk.

Nearby suburbs

Camperdown, next to Gnotuk, has 3 playgrounds and a skate park. Bookaar also borders Gnotuk.
