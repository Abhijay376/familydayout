Playground on Bolivar Street

Playground in Terang · Basic info

Playground on Bolivar Street in Terang is a public playground in the Corangamite Shire area. The Lions Club Walking Track is about 380 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 310 m from the playground, and coaches stop at Terang Station/Swanston St , a 7-minute walk away.
