Terang in the Corangamite area has 1 playground and 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Lake Keilambete Lake Reserve is the largest mapped park in Terang, at about 268 hectares. Lake Terang Lake Reserve and Mary Bradshaw Avenue are the next largest. Terang also has Apex Park. Our data shows 6 public toilets and 2 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Terang. The Terange and Noorat Cycleway and Lions Club Walking Track all pass through Terang.

Getting around

Terang has a train station: Terang Railway Station (V/Line: Warrnambool - Melbourne via Colac & Geelong). V/Line coaches stop in Terang. By car from Terang, Warrnambool is about 40 minutes away, Colac about 56 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon is about 31 minutes' drive from Terang. For a library, Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake is about 20 minutes' drive from Terang. For a beach, Buttress Point in The Cove is about 38 minutes' drive from Terang. For a skate park, Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake is about 21 minutes' drive from Terang. The closest cinema to Terang is Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool, about 40 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Terang is The Bowl in Warrnambool, about 41 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee is about 27 minutes' drive from Terang. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 40 minutes from Terang by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Terang are Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake (21 min), the playground at Tea Tree Lake Reserve in Mortlake (21 min), Market Square Playground in Mortlake (20 min), Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake (20 min), Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (21 min) and Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (27 min).

Best for
