Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside York Plains in the Northern Grampians area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

York Plains has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 77 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside York Plains, so most trips start by car. By car from York Plains, Stawell is about 55 minutes away and Horsham about 66 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Donald Swimming Pool in Donald is about 29 minutes' drive from York Plains. For a skate park, Donald BMX Track in Donald is about 32 minutes' drive from York Plains. Stawell is the closest bigger centre to York Plains, about 55 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from York Plains are the playground at Shanahan Park in Marnoo (16 min), Guthrie Park Playground in Rich Avon West (16 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (29 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (29 min), Donald BMX Track in Donald (32 min) and Woods' Farming and Heritage Museum in Rupanyup (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from York Plains.
