Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Massey in the Buloke area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Massey has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 118 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Massey, so most trips start by car. By car from Massey, Horsham is about 77 minutes away and Stawell about 86 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem is about 8 minutes' drive from Massey. For a skate park, Donald BMX Track in Donald is about 22 minutes' drive from Massey. From Massey, the nearest places for kids by road are Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem (8 min), the playground at Lake Watchem Lake Reserve in Watchem (11 min), the playground at Donald Public Park in Donald (20 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (20 min), Donald BMX Track in Donald (22 min) and Birchip History Museum in Birchip (23 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Massey.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Massey, Donald has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Watchem, next to Massey, has 1 playground and a pool.
