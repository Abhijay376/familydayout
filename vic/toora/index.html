Our data lists 3 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in Toora, in the South Gippsland area. On top of that, Toora has a pool, a beach and a skate park.

Landmarks

Franklin River Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Toora, at about 26 hectares. Sargasser Park is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Toora include Stanley Street Playground. Playgrounds in Toora include Sagasser Park, the playground at Sargasser Park and the playground at Stanley Street Playground. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Toora. Places to visit in Toora include Toora Pool and Toora Beach. For older kids in Toora there's the skate park at Sargasser Park. For walks and rides, Great Southern Rail Trail crosses Toora.

Getting around

Toora has no train station; Morwell Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 48 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Toora. By car from Toora, Leongatha is about 41 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 61 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Toora is Foster Library in Foster, about 11 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha is about 41 minutes' drive from Toora. The nearest larger centre is Leongatha, about 41 minutes from Toora by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Toora's 3 local playgrounds. Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at Sargasser Park in Toora. Beach days at Toora Beach. Free days on the Great Southern Rail Trail and in Toora's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Foster, next to Toora, has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Mount Best, Hazel Park, Bennison and Agnes also border Toora.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Toora, without traffic.
