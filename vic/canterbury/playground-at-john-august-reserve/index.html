Playground at John August Reserve

Playground in Canterbury · Basic info

Playground at John August Reserve in Canterbury is a public playground in the Boroondara City area. Balwyn Library is about 450 m away, and McDonald's is about 450 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 500 m away, and bus 285 stops at Mont Albert Rd/Rochester Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
