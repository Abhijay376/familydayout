Canterbury Gardens

Park in Canterbury · Basic info

Canterbury Gardens is a park in Canterbury, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Canterbury Station/Rochester Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 70 m away.
