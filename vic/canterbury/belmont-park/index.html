Belmont Park

Park in Canterbury · Basic info

Belmont Park is a park in Canterbury, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Hardwicke St/Whitehorse Rd #45 (tram) is a 7-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 639 m away and dogs are allowed on a lead.
