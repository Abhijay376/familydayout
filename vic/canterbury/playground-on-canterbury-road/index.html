Playground on Canterbury Road

Playground in Canterbury · Basic info

Playground on Canterbury Road in Canterbury is a public playground in the Boroondara City area. The Anniversary Trail passes about 30 m away, and Playground on Broadway is another option about 540 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.1 km away, and bus 285 stops at Gwenda Ave/Canterbury Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
