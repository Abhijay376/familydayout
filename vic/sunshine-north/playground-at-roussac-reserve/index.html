Playground at Roussac Reserve

Playground in Sunshine North · Official details

This is a public playground in Sunshine North. The M80 Trail is about 260 m away, and Playground at Dumosa/Magnolia Street Access Way is another option about 540 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 760 m from the playground, and buses 903 and 941 stop at Munro Ave/McIntyre Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
