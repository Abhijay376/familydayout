Playground at Dempster Park

Playground in Sunshine North · Official details

Playground at Dempster Park is a public playground in Sunshine North, in the Brimbank City area. Red Rooster is about 360 m away if you need food, and Playground at Suffolk Road Reserve is another option about 410 m away. There's a public toilet about 170 m away, and buses 215 and 220 stop at Westmoreland Rd/Ballarat Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
