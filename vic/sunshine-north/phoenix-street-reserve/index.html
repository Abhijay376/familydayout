Phoenix Street Reserve

Park in Sunshine North · Basic info

Phoenix Street Reserve is a park in Sunshine North, in the Brimbank City Council area, good for all ages. Harvester Rd/Ballarat Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 512 m away.
