Sunshine North in the Brimbank area has 6 playgrounds and 8 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Sunshine North is Knights Stadium , covering about 6 hectares. Dempster Park and Duke Street Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Sunshine North include Lloyd Reserve, Roussac Reserve and Suffolk Road Reserve. Playgrounds in Sunshine North include the playground at Dempster Park, the playground at Lloyd Reserve and the playground at Roussac Reserve. Our data shows 11 public toilets and 6 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Sunshine North. The M80 Trail passes through Sunshine North. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Sunshine North for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Sunshine North has no train station; Albion Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 3.2 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 215, 220, 408, 903 and 941 stop in Sunshine North. By car from Sunshine North, Sunshine is about 6 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 19 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 24 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Sunshine North is Paul Sadler Swimland Braybrook in Braybrook, about 6 minutes' drive. For a library, Sunshine Library in Sunshine is about 7 minutes' drive from Sunshine North. The closest beach to Sunshine North is Altona Dog Beach in Seaholme, about 20 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Sunshine Plaza Skatepark in Sunshine is about 6 minutes' drive from Sunshine North. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Sunshine is about 7 minutes' drive from Sunshine North. The closest indoor play centre to Sunshine North is Zone Bowling in Braybrook, about 8 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville is about 19 minutes' drive from Sunshine North.

Best for

Younger kids at Sunshine North's 6 local playgrounds. Free days on the M80 Trail and in Sunshine North's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Sunshine North, St Albans has 33 playgrounds and a library. Keilor East, next to Sunshine North, has 26 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Sunshine, next to Sunshine North, has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Sunshine North, Avondale Heights has 13 playgrounds and a library. Braybrook, Kealba and Albion also border Sunshine North.
