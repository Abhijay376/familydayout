Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Arnold West in the Loddon area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Arnold West. The closest to Arnold West is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 44 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Arnold West, so most trips start by car. By car from Arnold West, Bendigo is about 52 minutes away, Maryborough about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly is about 32 minutes' drive from Arnold West. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Arnold West, about 52 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Arnold West are Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (32 min), Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (32 min), Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (33 min) and Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (33 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Arnold West.
