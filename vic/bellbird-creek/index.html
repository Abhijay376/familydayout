Getting around

Bellbird Creek has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 107 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bellbird Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Bellbird Creek, Lakes Entrance is about 76 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bellbird Creek is Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest library to Bellbird Creek is Orbost Library in Orbost, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Bellbird Creek is Sailors Grave Beach in Cape Conran, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Bellbird Creek is the skate park at Mick Baum Park in Cann River, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Bellbird Creek are Sailors Grave Beach in Cape Conran (20 min), the playground at Luderick Point Memorial Park in Bemm River (20 min), the playground at Mick Baum Park in Cann River (29 min), the skate park at Mick Baum Park in Cann River (29 min), Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost (30 min) and Orbost Library in Orbost (31 min).
