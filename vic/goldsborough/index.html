Goldsborough is in the Central Goldfields area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Goldsborough is Goldsborough Historic and Cultural Features Reserve, covering about 13 hectares. For walks and rides, Dunolly Cycle Tracks, Bealiba Cycle Track and Moliagul Cycle Track cross Goldsborough.

Getting around

Goldsborough has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Goldsborough, so most trips start by car. By car from Goldsborough, Maryborough is about 36 minutes away, Bendigo about 63 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly is about 11 minutes' drive from Goldsborough. The closest library to Goldsborough is Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough, about 36 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 36 minutes from Goldsborough by car, with a library. From Goldsborough, the nearest places for kids by road are Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (11 min), Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (11 min), Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (36 min), the playground at Market Reserve in Carisbrook (41 min), Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground in Talbot (52 min) and Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (52 min).

Best for

Free days on the Dunolly Cycle Tracks and in Goldsborough's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Dunolly, next to Goldsborough, has a pool. Next door to Goldsborough, Bealiba has 1 playground. Dunluce also borders Goldsborough.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Goldsborough, without traffic.
