Buffalo River is in the Alpine area, and our data lists 1 playground in the suburb.

Landmarks

Our data shows 2 public toilets in Buffalo River. For walks and rides, Whitfield - Lake Buffalo crosses Buffalo River.

Getting around

There's no train station in Buffalo River. The closest to Buffalo River is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 45 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Buffalo River, so most trips start by car. By car from Buffalo River, Bright is about 38 minutes away and Wangaratta about 52 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford is about 11 minutes' drive from Buffalo River. The closest library to Buffalo River is Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford, about 12 minutes' drive. For a beach, Sandy Beach in Beechworth is about 37 minutes' drive from Buffalo River. The closest skate park to Buffalo River is Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Buffalo River is Sun Cinema Bright in Bright, about 38 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bright, about 38 minutes from Buffalo River by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Buffalo River are the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford (11 min), Myrtleford Swimming Pool in Myrtleford (12 min), The Old School Museum in Myrtleford (11 min), Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford (12 min), Cundy Park Playground in Myrtleford (12 min) and Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford (12 min).

Best for

Free days on the Whitfield - Lake Buffalo and in Buffalo River's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Buffalo River, Myrtleford has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Merriang South, Merriang, Edi Upper and Dandongadale also border Buffalo River.
