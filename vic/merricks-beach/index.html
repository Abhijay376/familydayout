Getting around

There's no train station in Merricks Beach. The closest to Merricks Beach is Bittern Railway Station (metro), about 9.2 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Merricks Beach, so most trips start by car. By car from Merricks Beach, Mornington is about 26 minutes away, Frankston about 30 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 73 minutes, without traffic.
