Bill Carroll Reserve

Park in Merricks Beach · Basic info

Bill Carroll Reserve is a park in Merricks Beach, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire Council area, good for all ages. Merricks Beach Rd/Frankston-Flinders Rd (bus) is a 24-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 948 m away.
