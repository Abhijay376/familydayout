Shepparton in the Greater Shepparton area has 47 playgrounds and 16 parks and reserves in our data. Shepparton also has a library, a pool, 2 museums or galleries and a cinema.

Landmarks

Chas Johnson Park is the largest mapped park in Shepparton, at about 5 hectares. Vibert Reserve and Skate Park are the next largest. Playground at Shepparton Regional Park, Playground at Skate Park and Playground at Deakin Park are among the council's larger district playgrounds. Other parks and reserves in Shepparton include BAILEY PARK, Harlow Park and Monash Park. Other playgrounds in Shepparton include McDonald's PlayPlace, SAM Nature Playground and Harlow Park Playground. Our data shows 20 public toilets and 12 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Shepparton. In Shepparton you'll also find Shepparton Art Museum, Shepparton Heritage Centre, Village Cinemas, StarBowl and Aquamoves. For older kids in Shepparton there's BMX track and Shepparton Velodrome. Centre Plaza and Shepparton Marketplace are the main shopping centres. The Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks, Broken River Path and Shepparton Lake Bike Track all pass through Shepparton. Our data lists 22 cafes and family restaurants in Shepparton for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line: Shepparton - Melbourne via Seymour) in Shepparton. Buses on the 1, 2, 3 and 4 routes stop in Shepparton. V/Line coaches stop in Shepparton. By car from Shepparton, Benalla is about 50 minutes away, Seymour about 63 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest zoo or wildlife park to Shepparton is Kidstown in Mooroopna, about 4 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 47 playgrounds across Shepparton. Rainy days at the library, cinema, indoor play centre and museum or gallery in Shepparton. Teens who skate or ride, with BMX track in Shepparton. Beach days at Canoe Club Boat Launch Site. Free days on the Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks and in Shepparton's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Mooroopna, next to Shepparton, has 12 playgrounds, a library and a zoo or wildlife park. Next door to Shepparton, Kialla has 11 playgrounds and a zoo or wildlife park. Next door to Shepparton, Shepparton North has 4 playgrounds. Shepparton East, next to Shepparton, has 3 playgrounds. Orrvale, Mooroopna North and Grahamvale also border Shepparton.
