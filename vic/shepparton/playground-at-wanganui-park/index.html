Playground at Wanganui Park

Playground in Shepparton · Official details

This is one of the council's larger playgrounds in Shepparton, looked after by Greater Shepparton City Council. Older kids have a skate park about 330 m away, and Playground at Roche Reserve is another option about 330 m away. There's no public toilet listed within 1 km, and bus 2 stops at Glory Way Ct/Parkside Dr , a 2-minute walk away.
