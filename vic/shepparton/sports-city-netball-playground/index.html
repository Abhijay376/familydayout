Sports City Netball Playground

Playground in Shepparton · Official details

Sports City Netball Playground is a public playground in Shepparton, in the Greater Shepparton City area. Mustafas Kebabs is about 320 m away if you need food, and Playground at Morrison Park is another option about 410 m away. There's a public toilet about 430 m away, and bus 4 stops at Cameron Ave/Numurkah Rd, a 7-minute walk away.
