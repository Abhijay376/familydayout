Playground at Wauchope Park

Playground in Shepparton · Basic info

This is a neighbourhood playground in Shepparton, looked after by Greater Shepparton City Council. Canoe Club Boat Launch Site is about 510 m away, and for food, Mustafas Kebabs is about 380 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.4 km from the playground, and bus 8 stops at Longstaff St/Abernethy St, a 2-minute walk away.
