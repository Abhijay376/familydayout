SAM Nature Playground

Playground in Shepparton · Official details

SAM Nature Playground is a public playground in Shepparton, in the Greater Shepparton City area. The Shepparton Lake Bike Track is about 100 m away, and Canoe Club Boat Launch Site is about 240 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 90 m from the playground, and buses 5, 6 and 8 stop at Hayes St/Maude St, a 8-minute walk away.
