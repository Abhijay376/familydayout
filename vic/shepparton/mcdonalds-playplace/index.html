McDonald's PlayPlace

Playground in Shepparton · Basic info

McDonald's PlayPlace is a public playground in Shepparton, in the Greater Shepparton City area. The Broken River Path is about 380 m away, and for food, McDonald's is about 10 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.5 km from the playground, and bus 8 stops at Goulburn Valley Media Centre/Wyndham St, a 4-minute walk away.
