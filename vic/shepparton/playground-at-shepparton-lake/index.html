Playground at Shepparton Lake

Playground in Shepparton · Official details

Playground at Shepparton Lake in Shepparton is one of the council's larger playgrounds in the Greater Shepparton City area. The Shepparton Lake Bike Track passes about 20 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 280 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 9 stops at Aquamoves/Tom Collins Dr, a 8-minute walk away.
