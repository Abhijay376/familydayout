Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Strath Creek, in the Murrindindi area.

Landmarks

Kerrisdale Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Strath Creek, at about 10 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Strath Creek.

Getting around

Strath Creek has no train station; Broadford Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Strath Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Strath Creek, Seymour is about 35 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 67 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 85 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Seymour Swimming Centre in Seymour is about 36 minutes' drive from Strath Creek. The closest library to Strath Creek is Broadford Library in Broadford, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Strath Creek is Broadford Skate Park in Broadford, about 20 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 35 minutes from Strath Creek by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Strath Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are Rupert Street Playground in Broadford (19 min), Broadford Skate Park in Broadford (20 min), Sutherland Street Playground in Broadford (20 min), Broadford Library in Broadford (21 min), the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea (33 min) and Seymour Library in Seymour (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Strath Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Strath Creek, Tallarook has 1 playground. Flowerdale and Clonbinane also border Strath Creek.
