Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Dreeite, in the Colac Otway area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Dreeite is Dreeite Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 97 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Dreeite. The closest to Dreeite is Colac Railway Station (V/Line), about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Dreeite, so most trips start by car. By car from Dreeite, Colac is about 39 minutes away, Geelong about 88 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Dreeite is Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac, about 42 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Dreeite is the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt, about 44 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Dreeite is Colac Cinemas in Colac, about 40 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Dreeite is Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac, about 40 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 39 minutes from Dreeite by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Dreeite are the playground at Red Rock Scenic Reserve in Alvie (20 min), the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (38 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (40 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (40 min), Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (42 min) and the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (44 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Dreeite.
