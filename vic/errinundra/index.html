Errinundra is in the East Gippsland area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Errinundra is Big River Reference Area, covering about 390 hectares. For walks and rides, Greens Road crosses Errinundra.

Getting around

Errinundra has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 109 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Errinundra, so most trips start by car. By car from Errinundra, Lakes Entrance is about 2.5 hours away and Bairnsdale about 3 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Free days on the Greens Road and in Errinundra's parks.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Errinundra, without traffic.
