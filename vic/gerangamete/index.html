Gerangamete in the Colac Otway area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Otway Forest Park is the largest mapped park in Gerangamete, at about 39,555 hectares.

Getting around

Gerangamete has no train station; Colac Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gerangamete, so most trips start by car. By car from Gerangamete, Colac is about 24 minutes away, Torquay about 77 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Gerangamete is Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Gerangamete is the skate park at Forrest Common in Forrest, about 11 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Colac Cinemas in Colac is about 23 minutes' drive from Gerangamete. The closest indoor play centre to Gerangamete is Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac, about 23 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 24 minutes from Gerangamete by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Gerangamete, the nearest places for kids by road are the skate park at Forrest Common in Forrest (11 min), the playground at Forrest Common in Forrest (11 min), the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (24 min), the playground at Newcombe Estate Park in Colac (28 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (23 min) and Colac Cinemas in Colac (23 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Gerangamete.

Nearby suburbs

Forrest, next to Gerangamete, has 1 playground and a skate park. Birregurra, Barwon Downs and Barongarook also border Gerangamete.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Gerangamete, without traffic.
