First Surf Beach

Beach in Cape Paterson · Basic info

First Surf Beach is a beach in Cape Paterson, in the Bass Coast Shire Council area, good for all ages. Marine St/Spray St (bus) is a 12-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 347 m away with a baby change table.

Where is First Surf Beach?
