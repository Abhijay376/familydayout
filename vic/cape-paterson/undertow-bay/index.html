Undertow Bay

Beach in Cape Paterson · Basic info

Undertow Bay is a beach in Cape Paterson, in the Bass Coast Shire Council area, good for all ages. Surf Beach Rd/Cape Paterson Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 549 m away.
