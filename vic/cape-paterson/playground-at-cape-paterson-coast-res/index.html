Playground at Cape Paterson Coast Res

Playground in Cape Paterson · Basic info

Playground at Cape Paterson Coast Res is a public playground in Cape Paterson, in the Bass Coast Shire area. The Bay is about 190 m away, and Cape Paterson Rock Pool is about 120 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus Wonthaggi Town Service (Cape Paterson) stops at Surf Beach Rd/Cape Paterson Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
