Landmarks

Howes Creek also has Blue Gum Flat, Magazine Gully and Blue Gum Flat 2. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Howes Creek. For camping, there's Blue Gum Flat and Blue Gum Flat 2. For walks and rides, Mansfield - Goughs Bay crosses Howes Creek.

Getting around

Howes Creek has no train station; Violet Town Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 62 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Howes Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Howes Creek, Mansfield is about 19 minutes away, Benalla about 69 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.
