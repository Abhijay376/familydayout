Yea is in the Murrindindi area, and our data lists 8 parks and reserves in the suburb. Yea also has a museum or gallery and a skate park.

Landmarks

Yea River, Yea Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Yea, at about 46 hectares. Yea G117 Bushland Reserve and Perts Streamside Reserve are the next largest. Yea also has Apex Park, Yea G54 Bushland Reserve and Yea G116 Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 7 public toilets in Yea. Places to visit in Yea include TLM Moto Guzzi. For older kids in Yea there's the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res. For walks and rides, Great Victorian Rail Trail, Franklin Track and Constituition Hill Walking track cross Yea. Our data lists 6 cafes and family restaurants in Yea for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Yea. The closest to Yea is Tallarook Railway Station (V/Line), about 32 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Yea. By car from Yea, Seymour is about 31 minutes away, Healesville about 55 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra is about 30 minutes' drive from Yea. For a library, Alexandra Library in Alexandra is about 29 minutes' drive from Yea. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 31 minutes from Yea by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Yea, the nearest places for kids by road are Yarck Recreation Reserve Playground in Yarck (22 min), the playground at Leckie Park in Alexandra (30 min), Alexandra Library in Alexandra (29 min), BMX Track in Caveat (46 min), Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra (30 min) and Alexandra Timber Tramway and Museum in Alexandra (31 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Yea. Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea. Free days on the Great Victorian Rail Trail and in Yea's parks.

Nearby suburbs
