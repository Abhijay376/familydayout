Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Jindivick, in the Baw Baw area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Jindivick is Jindivick G78 Bushland Reserve, covering about 4 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Jindivick. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Jindivick for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Jindivick. The closest to Jindivick is Drouin Railway Station (V/Line), about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Jindivick, so most trips start by car. By car from Jindivick, Warragul is about 24 minutes away, Pakenham about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Jindivick is Drouin Swimming Pool in Drouin, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest library to Jindivick is Drouin Library in Drouin, about 24 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South is about 12 minutes' drive from Jindivick. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 24 minutes' drive from Jindivick. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 25 minutes' drive from Jindivick. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Jindivick is Wildlife Trail in Tynong North, about 33 minutes' drive. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Jindivick, about 24 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Jindivick, the nearest places for kids by road are Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South (12 min), Waddell Park Playground in Drouin (21 min), Hearn Street Park Playground in Drouin (22 min), Drouin Library in Drouin (24 min), Drouin Swimming Pool in Drouin (24 min) and Drouin Skate Park in Drouin (24 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Jindivick.

Nearby suburbs

Rokeby, next to Jindivick, has 1 playground. Next door to Jindivick, Neerim South has a skate park. Drouin West also borders Jindivick.
