Wombat Creek is in the East Gippsland area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Wombat Creek, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Wombat Creek. For walks and rides, East Gippsland Rail Trail crosses Wombat Creek.

Getting around

Wombat Creek has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 58 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wombat Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Wombat Creek, Lakes Entrance is about 34 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 61 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost is about 16 minutes' drive from Wombat Creek. For a library, Orbost Library in Orbost is about 15 minutes' drive from Wombat Creek. The closest beach to Wombat Creek is Ewings Marsh in Waygara, about 17 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Lakes Entrance, about 34 minutes from Wombat Creek by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a beach. From Wombat Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are Ewings Marsh in Waygara (17 min), the playground at Forest Park in Orbost (15 min), Orbost Library in Orbost (15 min), Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost (16 min), Lakes Entrance Aquatic Centre in Lakes Entrance (31 min) and the playground at Lakes Entrance - Lake Tyers Coastal Reserve (COM) in Lake Tyers Beach (38 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Wombat Creek on the East Gippsland Rail Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Waygara, next to Wombat Creek, has a beach.
