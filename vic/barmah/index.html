Barmah is in the Moira area, and our data lists 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Barmah is Top Island Reference Area, covering about 153 hectares. Dharnya Centre and Moira Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Barmah include Terry Tinkler Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Barmah.

Getting around

Barmah has no train station; Echuca Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Barmah 8 route stop in Barmah. V/Line coaches stop in Barmah. By car from Barmah, Echuca is about 50 minutes away and Shepparton about 71 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Barmah is Nathalia Memorial Pool in Nathalia, about 27 minutes' drive. For a library, Nathalia Library in Nathalia is about 25 minutes' drive from Barmah. The closest beach to Barmah is Scotts Beach in Yalca, about 41 minutes' drive. Echuca is the closest bigger centre to Barmah, about 50 minutes away by car, and has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Barmah, the nearest places for kids by road are Moama Echuca Community Skate Park in Echuca (52 min), Barmah Forest Heritge and Education Centre in Nathalia (25 min), Nathalia Library in Nathalia (25 min), the playground at Apex Park in Nathalia (26 min), Nathalia Memorial Pool in Nathalia (27 min) and Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca (51 min).
