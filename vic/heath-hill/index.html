Heath Hill is in the Cardinia area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Heath Hill, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Heath Hill. The closest to Heath Hill is Bunyip Railway Station (V/Line), about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Heath Hill, so most trips start by car. By car from Heath Hill, Pakenham is about 31 minutes away, Warragul about 33 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 75 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong is about 15 minutes' drive from Heath Hill. The closest library to Heath Hill is Poowong library in Poowong, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Heath Hill is Corinella Beach in Corinella, about 41 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Heath Hill is Nyora skate park in Nyora, about 13 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 31 minutes' drive from Heath Hill. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 31 minutes' drive from Heath Hill. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Heath Hill is Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville, about 29 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Pakenham, about 31 minutes from Heath Hill by car, with 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Heath Hill are the playground at James Street Reserve in Lang Lang (13 min), the playground at Dick Jones Park in Lang Lang (13 min), Nyora skate park in Nyora (13 min), Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong (15 min), Poowong library in Poowong (16 min) and Drouin Skate Park in Drouin (24 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Heath Hill.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Heath Hill, Nyora has 3 playgrounds and a skate park. Modella, Lang Lang East, Catani and Athlone also border Heath Hill.
