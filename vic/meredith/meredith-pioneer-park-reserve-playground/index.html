Meredith Pioneer Park Reserve Playground

Playground in Meredith · Basic info

Meredith Pioneer Park Reserve Playground in Meredith is a public playground in the Golden Plains Shire area. Older kids have a skate park about 180 m away. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and coaches stop at Lawler St/Midland Hwy, a 6-minute walk away.
