Meredith in the Golden Plains area has 1 playground and 10 parks and reserves in our data. Beyond the parks, Meredith has a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Meredith is Meredith Education Area, covering about 285 hectares. Cargerie I42 Bushland Reserve and Moreep I44 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Meredith include Coopers Bridge, Moreep Reserve and Meredith Pioneer Park. Playgrounds around Meredith include Meredith Pioneer Park Reserve Playground. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Meredith. Picnic areas in Meredith include Coopers Bridge and Moreep Reserve. For older kids in Meredith there's Meredith Skate Park.

Getting around

Meredith has no train station; Ballan Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 30 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Meredith. By car from Meredith, Ballarat is about 39 minutes away, Geelong about 43 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Ballarat is the closest bigger centre to Meredith, about 39 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Meredith are the playground at Elaine Recreation Reserve in Elaine (8 min), Disability beach in Steiglitz (17 min), Fairy Park in Anakie (32 min), Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn (21 min), Bannockburn Skate Park in Bannockburn (22 min) and Buninyong Swimming Pool in Buninyong (24 min).
