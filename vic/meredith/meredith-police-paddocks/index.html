Meredith Police Paddocks

Park in Meredith · Basic info

Meredith Police Paddocks is a park in Meredith, in the Golden Plains Shire Council area, good for all ages. Lawler St/Midland Hwy (coach) is a 13-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1200 m away.
