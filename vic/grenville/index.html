Landmarks

Grenville Recreation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Grenville, at about 3 hectares. Playgrounds around Grenville include the playground at Grenville Recreation Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Grenville. The closest to Grenville is Ballarat Railway Station (V/Line), about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Grenville, so most trips start by car. By car from Grenville, Ballarat is about 38 minutes away, Daylesford about 74 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Buninyong Swimming Pool in Buninyong is about 24 minutes' drive from Grenville. The closest library to Grenville is L Building: E J Barker Library in Mount Helen, about 29 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Enfield Skate Park in Enfield is about 27 minutes' drive from Grenville. The closest cinema to Grenville is Victoria Theatre in Golden Point, about 35 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 35 minutes' drive from Grenville. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Grenville is Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East, about 39 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Ballarat, about 38 minutes from Grenville by car, with 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Grenville, the nearest places for kids by road are Garibaldi Playground in Garibaldi (12 min), Enfield Skate Park in Enfield (27 min), Buninyong Swimming Pool in Buninyong (24 min), the bmx track at Buninyong Recreation Park in Buninyong (24 min), L Building: E J Barker Library in Mount Helen (29 min) and Ballaarat Observatory in Mount Pleasant (34 min).
