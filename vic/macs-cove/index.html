Macs Cove is in the Mansfield area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Macs Cove, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Macs Cove has no train station; Benalla Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 75 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Mansfield - Woods Point via Jamieson route stop in Macs Cove. By car from Macs Cove, Mansfield is about 19 minutes away, Benalla about 70 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Mansfield Outdoor Swimming Pool in Mansfield is about 20 minutes' drive from Macs Cove. For a library, Mansfield Library in Mansfield is about 19 minutes' drive from Macs Cove. For a skate park, the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield is about 19 minutes' drive from Macs Cove. For a cinema, Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield is about 18 minutes' drive from Macs Cove. For a zoo or wildlife park, Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield is about 10 minutes' drive from Macs Cove. The nearest larger centre is Mansfield, about 19 minutes from Macs Cove by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Macs Cove, the nearest places for kids by road are Mansfield Zoo in Mansfield (10 min), Mansfield Armchair Cinema in Mansfield (18 min), Mansfield Library in Mansfield (19 min), the playground at Mansfield Botanic Park in Mansfield (19 min), the playground at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (19 min) and the skate park at Mansfield Recreation Reserve in Mansfield (19 min).
