Huon Creek is in the Wodonga area, and our data lists 1 playground in the suburb.

Getting around

Huon Creek has no train station; Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 6.1 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Huon Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Huon Creek, Wodonga is about 10 minutes away and Wangaratta about 61 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, WAVES in Wodonga is about 8 minutes' drive from Huon Creek. The closest library to Huon Creek is Upper Murray Regional Library - Wodonga in Wodonga, about 9 minutes' drive. For a beach, Finns Beach in Bandiana is about 18 minutes' drive from Huon Creek. For a skate park, James Scott Memorial Skate Park in Wodonga is about 9 minutes' drive from Huon Creek. For a cinema, Bandiana Area Cinema in Bandiana is about 10 minutes' drive from Huon Creek. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 10 minutes from Huon Creek by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Huon Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Veronica Egan Park in Wodonga (4 min), the playground at Daniel Bayne Park in Wodonga (5 min), WAVES in Wodonga (8 min), James Scott Memorial Skate Park in Wodonga (9 min), Art Space Wodonga in Wodonga (9 min) and Wodonga Sports & Leisure Centre in West Wodonga (10 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Huon Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Wodonga, next to Huon Creek, has 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. West Wodonga, next to Huon Creek, has 20 playgrounds and a pool.
