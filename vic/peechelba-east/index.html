Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Peechelba East in the Wangaratta area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Peechelba East has no train station; Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Peechelba East, so most trips start by car. By car from Peechelba East, Wangaratta is about 27 minutes away and Benalla about 60 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Peechelba East is Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest library to Peechelba East is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Peechelba East is Forges Beach No 2 in Yarrawonga, about 41 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Peechelba East is the pump track on Victoria Street in Wahgunyah, about 32 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 26 minutes' drive from Peechelba East. For a zoo or wildlife park, Tunzafun in Yarrawonga is about 34 minutes' drive from Peechelba East. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Peechelba East, about 27 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Peechelba East are the playground at Peechelba Reserve in Peechelba (8 min), Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta (25 min), Brien Crescent Playground in Wangaratta (26 min), Rutherglen War Memorial Swimming Pool in Rutherglen (31 min), Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (27 min) and Rutherglen Public Library in Rutherglen (31 min).
