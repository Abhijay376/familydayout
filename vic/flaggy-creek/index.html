Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Flaggy Creek in the East Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Flaggy Creek. The closest to Flaggy Creek is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Flaggy Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Flaggy Creek, Bairnsdale is about 32 minutes away and Sale about 63 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale is about 32 minutes' drive from Flaggy Creek. For a library, Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale is about 32 minutes' drive from Flaggy Creek. For a cinema, Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale is about 32 minutes' drive from Flaggy Creek. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow is about 38 minutes' drive from Flaggy Creek. Bairnsdale is the closest bigger centre to Flaggy Creek, about 32 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Flaggy Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Amy Shand Park in Lindenow (19 min), Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale (32 min), Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale (32 min), Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale (32 min), Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale (33 min) and Krowathunkoolong Keeping Place in Bairnsdale (33 min).
