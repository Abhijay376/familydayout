Reefton is in the Yarra Ranges area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Reefton, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Reefton. The Upper Yarra Walking Track and Doctor's Creek Walking Track all pass through Reefton.

Getting around

Reefton has no train station; Longwarry Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 49 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Reefton, so most trips start by car. By car from Reefton, Healesville is about 49 minutes away, Lilydale about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Warburton Water World in Warburton is about 17 minutes' drive from Reefton. The closest library to Reefton is Street Library in Millgrove, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Reefton is the skate park at Yarra Junction Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Yarra Junction, about 31 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Lyre Bird enclosure in Badger Creek is about 45 minutes' drive from Reefton. Healesville is the closest bigger centre to Reefton, about 49 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Reefton are the playground at Yarra River Heritage River in Warburton (17 min), Warburton Water World in Warburton (17 min), the playground at Warburton Recreation Reserve in Warburton (18 min), Street Library in Millgrove (24 min), Yarra Junction public library in Yarra Junction (31 min) and the skate park at Yarra Junction Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Yarra Junction (31 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Reefton on the Upper Yarra Walking Track.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Reefton, Noojee has 1 playground. Cambarville also borders Reefton.
