Playground at King Street Chain

Playground in Balwyn · Basic info

Playground at King Street Chain is a public playground in Balwyn, in the Boroondara City area. The Little Glass Creek Trail passes about 20 m away, and Playground on Nicholson Street is another option about 600 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 840 m away, and buses 302 and 304 stop at Para St/Belmore Rd, a 7-minute walk away.
