King Street Chain

Park in Balwyn · Basic info

King Street Chain is a park in Balwyn, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Para St/Belmore Rd (bus) is a 8-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 737 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed on a lead.
