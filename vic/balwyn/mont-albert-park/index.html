Mont Albert Park

Park in Balwyn · Basic info

Mont Albert Park is a park in Balwyn, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Yarrbat Ave/Union Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 914 m away with a baby change table.
