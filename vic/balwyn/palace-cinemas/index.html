Palace Cinemas

Cinema in Balwyn · Official details

Palace Cinemas is a cinema in Balwyn, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for school-age kids and teens. Balwyn Cinema/Whitehorse Rd #46 (tram) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 342 m away with a baby change table.
