Hilda Street Reserve

Park in Balwyn · Basic info

Hilda Street Reserve is a park in Balwyn, in the Boroondara City Council area, good for all ages. Macleay Park/Belmore Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 791 m away with a baby change table.
