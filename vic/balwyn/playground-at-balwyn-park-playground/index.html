Playground at Balwyn Park

Playground in Balwyn · Basic info

Playground at Balwyn Park in Balwyn is a public playground in the Boroondara City area. Balwyn Library is about 540 m away, and for food, Gu Sam Chinese-Korean Style BBQ is about 110 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 110 m from the playground, and bus 285 stops at Whitehorse Rd/Balwyn Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
