Wrathung is in the Wellington area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Wrathung is Valencia State Forest, covering about 29,649 hectares.

Getting around

Wrathung has no train station; Stratford Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 44 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wrathung, so most trips start by car. By car from Wrathung, Sale is about 2.5 hours away and Bairnsdale about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest places for kids by road from Wrathung are Anzac Park Playground in Briagolong (113 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wrathung.
