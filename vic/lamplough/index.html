Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Lamplough in the Pyrenees area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lamplough. The closest to Lamplough is Talbot Railway Station (V/Line), about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lamplough, so most trips start by car. By car from Lamplough, Maryborough is about 28 minutes away, Ballarat about 54 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lamplough is Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot, about 32 minutes' drive. For a library, Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough is about 28 minutes' drive from Lamplough. For a skate park, the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort is about 42 minutes' drive from Lamplough. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 28 minutes from Lamplough by car, with a library. From Lamplough, the nearest places for kids by road are Courthouse in Avoca (5 min), the playground at Avoca Lions Park in Avoca (6 min), the playground at Toll Bar Park in Lexton (15 min), Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (32 min), Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (28 min) and the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (42 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lamplough.
