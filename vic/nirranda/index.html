Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Nirranda in the Moyne area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it. Nirranda does have a beach.

Landmarks

In Nirranda you'll also find Flaxmans Hill Beach.

Getting around

There's no train station in Nirranda. The closest to Nirranda is Sherwood Park Railway Station (V/Line), about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nirranda, so most trips start by car. By car from Nirranda, Warrnambool is about 31 minutes away and Colac about 76 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Nirranda is Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon, about 23 minutes' drive. The closest library to Nirranda is Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Nirranda is Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Nirranda is Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool, about 31 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, The Bowl in Warrnambool is about 32 minutes' drive from Nirranda. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Nirranda is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 24 minutes' drive. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Nirranda, about 31 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Nirranda are Buttress Point in The Cove (7 min), the playground at Peterborough Coastal Reserve (COM) in Peterborough (14 min), Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell (22 min), the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (22 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (23 min) and Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (24 min).

Best for

Beach days at Flaxmans Hill Beach.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Nirranda, Nullawarre has 1 playground and a beach. Nirranda South, next to Nirranda, has a beach.
