Playground on Attenborough Square

Playground in Wantirna · Basic info

This is a public playground in Wantirna. The Stud Road Trail is about 60 m away, and for food, Coconut Tree is about 210 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 740 m from the playground, and buses 664, 745 and 901 stop at Teddington Way/Stud Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
