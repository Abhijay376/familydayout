Playground at Newlands Court Reserve

Playground in Wantirna · Basic info

This is a public playground in Wantirna. The Stud Road Trail is about 210 m away, and Playground at Barrington Court Reserve is another option about 250 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 920 m from the playground, and bus 901 stops at Roxburgh Rd/Boronia Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
