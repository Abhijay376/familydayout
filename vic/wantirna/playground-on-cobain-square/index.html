Playground on Cobain Square

Playground in Wantirna · Basic info

Playground on Cobain Square in Wantirna is a public playground in the Knox City area. The Stud Road Trail is about 240 m away, and for food, Murasaki Tanuki Japanese is about 330 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 720 m from the playground, and buses 664 and 901 stop at Harold St/Stud Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
