Georgian Gardens Reserve

Park in Wantirna · Basic info

Georgian Gardens Reserve is a park in Wantirna, in the Knox City Council area, good for all ages. Marlo St/Templeton St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1407 m away with a baby change table.
