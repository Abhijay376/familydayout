Playground on Eton Square

Playground in Wantirna · Basic info

Playground on Eton Square is a public playground in Wantirna, in the Knox City area. Playground at Burlington Crescent Reserve is another option about 130 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.1 km from the playground, and bus 901 stops at Amesbury Ave/Boronia Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
