Courtney Square Reserve

Park in Wantirna · Basic info

Courtney Square Reserve is a park in Wantirna, in the Knox City Council area, good for all ages. Yorkminster Ave/Boronia Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 739 m away with a baby change table.
