Merivale Drive Reserve

Park in Wantirna · Basic info

Merivale Drive Reserve is a park in Wantirna, in the Knox City Council area, good for all ages. Thaxted Pde/Boronia Rd (bus) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 935 m away with a baby change table.
