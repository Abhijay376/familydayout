Playground at Hamsterley Square Reserve

Playground in Wantirna · Basic info

Playground at Hamsterley Square Reserve in Wantirna is a public playground in the Knox City area. The Burwood Highway Trail passes about 250 m away, and Knox Library – Ngarrgoo is about 540 m away. There's a public toilet about 1.4 km away, and buses 732, 738, 757 and 967 stop at Hartington Dr/Burwood Hwy, a 7-minute walk away.
