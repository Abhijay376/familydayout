Playground on Inchcape Avenue

Playground in Wantirna · Basic info

Playground on Inchcape Avenue is a public playground in Wantirna, in the Knox City area. The Dandenong Creek Trail passes about 260 m away, and Bellbird Drive Playground is another option about 180 m away. There's a public toilet about 730 m away, and bus 901 stops at Clarence Rd/Wantirna Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
