Ultima Recreation Reserve

Park in Ultima · Basic info

Ultima Recreation Reserve is a park in Ultima, in the Swan Hill Rural City Council area, good for all ages. McCelland St/Dillon St (bus) is a 14-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1204 m away.
