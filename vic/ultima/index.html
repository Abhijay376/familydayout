Ultima is in the Swan Hill area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Ultima is Ultima Recreation Reserve, covering about 9 hectares. 14 Mile Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Ultima.

Getting around

There's no train station in Ultima. The closest to Ultima is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 30 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Swan Hill - Sea Lake via Ultima route stop in Ultima. By car from Ultima, Swan Hill is about 27 minutes away and Echuca about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Ultima is Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest library to Ultima is Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Ultima is Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South, about 25 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill is about 27 minutes' drive from Ultima. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 27 minutes from Ultima by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Ultima are Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South (25 min), the playground at Jaycee Park in Swan Hill (27 min), Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill (27 min), Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill (27 min), Swan Hill Outdoor Pool in Swan Hill (27 min) and the playground at Riverside Park in Swan Hill (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Ultima.

Nearby suburbs

Murnungin, Meatian, Gowanford and Goschen also border Ultima.
