Pearsondale is in the Wellington area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Pearsondale, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Pearsondale. The closest to Pearsondale is Sale Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.2 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Pearsondale, so most trips start by car. By car from Pearsondale, Sale is about 16 minutes away, Traralgon about 48 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Indoor pool in Sale is about 15 minutes' drive from Pearsondale. The closest library to Pearsondale is Sale Library in Sale, about 13 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Sale Skate Park in Sale is about 13 minutes' drive from Pearsondale. For a cinema, Sale Cinemas in Sale is about 14 minutes' drive from Pearsondale. The nearest larger centre is Sale, about 16 minutes from Pearsondale by car, with 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Pearsondale, the nearest places for kids by road are Wurruk Playground in Wurruk (12 min), Sale Skate Park in Sale (13 min), Sale Library in Sale (13 min), Gippsland Art Gallery in Sale (13 min), Old train signals in Sale (13 min) and Sale Cinemas in Sale (14 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Pearsondale.

Nearby suburbs

Sale, next to Pearsondale, has 14 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Rosedale, next to Pearsondale, has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Wurruk, next to Pearsondale, has 1 playground. Kilmany and Fulham also border Pearsondale.
