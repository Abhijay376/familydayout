Middle Park Beach

Beach in Middle Park · Basic info

Middle Park Beach is a beach in Middle Park, in the Port Phillip City Council area, good for all ages. Harold St/Danks St #137 (tram) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 282 m away with a baby change table.
