Middle Park in the Port Phillip area has 3 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in our data. Beyond the parks, Middle Park has 2 libraries and a beach.

Landmarks

Middle Park also has Neville Street Reserve, Ashworth Street Reserve and Frank & Mary Crean Reserve. Playgrounds around Middle Park include the playground at Neville Street Reserve, the playground at Ashworth Street Reserve and the playground at Frank & Mary Crean Reserve. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Middle Park. In Middle Park you'll also find Carmelite Library, Middle Park Library and Middle Park Beach. For walks and rides, Bay Trail and St Kilda to Port Melbourne cross Middle Park. Our data lists 6 cafes and family restaurants in Middle Park for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Middle Park. The closest to Middle Park is Anzac Railway Station (metro), about 2.2 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Tram 12 stops in Middle Park. Bus 606 stops in Middle Park. By car from Middle Park, Sunshine is about 22 minutes away, Box Hill about 26 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 8 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Middle Park is Melbourne Sports and Aquatic Centre in Albert Park, about 4 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Middle Park is the pump track at Windsor Siding in Windsor, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Middle Park is Palace George in St Kilda, about 3 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Middle Park is PLAYit in Southbank, about 7 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Luna Park in St Kilda is about 4 minutes' drive from Middle Park. The nearest larger centre is Sunshine, about 22 minutes from Middle Park by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Middle Park's 3 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the library in Middle Park. Beach days at Middle Park Beach. Free days on the Bay Trail and in Middle Park's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Middle Park, Albert Park has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a library. St Kilda West, next to Middle Park, has 2 playgrounds and a beach.
