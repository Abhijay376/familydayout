Rocklands is in the Southern Grampians area, and our data lists 7 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Rocklands is Rocklands State Forest, covering about 4,853 hectares. State Forest and State Forest are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Rocklands include Turkey Neck, Brodies Campsite and Glendinning Campsite. Our data shows 8 public toilets and 7 off-leash dog parks in Rocklands. Picnic areas in Rocklands include Brodies Campsite, Glendinning Campsite and Mountain Dam Campsite. For camping, there's Turkey Neck.

Getting around

Rocklands has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 87 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Rocklands, so most trips start by car. By car from Rocklands, Horsham is about 1.5 hours away and Hamilton about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Rocklands is Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral, about 35 minutes' drive. From Rocklands, the nearest places for kids by road are Glendinning Street Playground in Balmoral (35 min), Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral (35 min), Harrow Discovery Centre in Harrow (65 min) and Bake House Studio in Harrow (65 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Rocklands.

Nearby suburbs
