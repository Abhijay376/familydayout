Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Catani in the Cardinia area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Catani has no train station; Garfield Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Catani, so most trips start by car. By car from Catani, Pakenham is about 23 minutes away, Warragul about 33 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 67 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Koo Wee Rup Swimming Pool in Koo Wee Rup is about 15 minutes' drive from Catani. For a library, Pakenham Library in Pakenham is about 23 minutes' drive from Catani. For a beach, Corinella Beach in Corinella is about 44 minutes' drive from Catani. For a skate park, the skate park at Cochrane Park in Koo Wee Rup is about 14 minutes' drive from Catani. The closest cinema to Catani is Warragul Cinema in Warragul, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Catani is Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Catani is MonkeyG's in Pakenham, about 21 minutes' drive. Pakenham is the closest bigger centre to Catani, about 23 minutes away by car, and has 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Catani are the playground at Bayles Recreation Reserve in Bayles (7 min), the playground at James Street Reserve in Lang Lang (16 min), the skate park at Cochrane Park in Koo Wee Rup (14 min), Koo Wee Rup Swimming Pool in Koo Wee Rup (15 min), Skate BMX Park in Garfield (23 min) and Garfield Swimming Pool in Garfield (23 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Catani.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Catani, Bayles has 1 playground. Heath Hill and Cora Lynn also border Catani.
