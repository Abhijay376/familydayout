Wongungarra in the Wellington area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Tea Tree Range State Forest is the largest mapped park in Wongungarra, at about 26,431 hectares. Mount Sarah Natural Features and Scenic Reserve and Mount McAdam Reference Area are the next largest. Wongungarra also has Crooked River - Howittville Camping Area 1 (CLWF). Our data shows 1 public toilet in Wongungarra. For camping, there's Crooked River - Howittville Camping Area 1 (CLWF). For walks and rides, McMillans Walking Track, McMillan Alternative Walking Track and Brewery Creek Jeep Track cross Wongungarra.

Getting around

Wongungarra has no train station; Stratford Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 70 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wongungarra, so most trips start by car. By car from Wongungarra, Bairnsdale is about 1.5 hours away and Sale about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Wongungarra, the nearest places for kids by road are Dargo Public Hall Playground in Dargo (27 min) and Dargo Museum in Dargo (29 min).

Best for

Free days on the McMillans Walking Track and in Wongungarra's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Hotham Heights, next to Wongungarra, has 1 playground. Dargo, next to Wongungarra, has 1 playground. Crooked River, Cowa, Billabong and Arbuckle also border Wongungarra.
