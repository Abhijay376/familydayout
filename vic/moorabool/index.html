Moorabool is in the Greater Geelong area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Moorabool, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Moorabool. The closest to Moorabool is North Shore Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Moorabool, so most trips start by car. By car from Moorabool, Geelong is about 17 minutes away, Torquay about 33 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 62 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Moorabool is Goodfellow Aquatic Centre in Bell Post Hill, about 7 minutes' drive. For a library, John Drysdale Library in Norlane is about 9 minutes' drive from Moorabool. For a beach, Limeburners Lagoon Beach in Corio is about 15 minutes' drive from Moorabool. For a skate park, the bmx track at Stead Park in Corio is about 10 minutes' drive from Moorabool. The closest cinema to Moorabool is Village Cinemas in Geelong, about 15 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, General Public in Corio is about 11 minutes' drive from Moorabool. For a zoo or wildlife park, Fairy Park in Anakie is about 27 minutes' drive from Moorabool. Geelong is the closest bigger centre to Moorabool, about 17 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Moorabool, the nearest places for kids by road are Abe Wood Reserve in Lovely Banks (6 min), the playground at Monastery Reserve in Lovely Banks (5 min), Goodfellow Aquatic Centre in Bell Post Hill (7 min), John Drysdale Library in Norlane (9 min), the bmx track at Stead Park in Corio (10 min) and Corio Bowls in Norlane (8 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Moorabool.

Nearby suburbs

Bell Post Hill, next to Moorabool, has 8 playgrounds and a pool. Lovely Banks, next to Moorabool, has 2 playgrounds. Batesford, next to Moorabool, has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Moorabool, Sutherlands Creek has 1 playground. Anakie and Gheringhap also border Moorabool.
