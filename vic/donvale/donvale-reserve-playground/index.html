Donvale Reserve Playground

Playground in Donvale · Basic info

Donvale Reserve Playground in Donvale is one of the council's larger playgrounds in the Manningham City area. Lucky Corner is about 280 m away if you need food, and Argyle Street Playground is another option about 550 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 80 m away, and buses 902 and 907 stop at Donvale Reserve/Mitcham Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
