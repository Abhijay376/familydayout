Argyle Street Playground

Playground in Donvale · Basic info

Argyle Street Playground is a small local playground in Donvale, in the Manningham City area. The St Clems Trail passes about 340 m away, and Darvall Playspace Playground is another option about 340 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 790 m away, and bus 280 stops at Tunstall Rd/St Clems Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
