Hillcrest Reserve Playground

Playground in Donvale · Basic info

Hillcrest Reserve Playground in Donvale is a small local playground in the Manningham City area. The Whitehorse NS10 passes about 10 m away, and Cobham Road Playground is another option about 480 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.4 km away, and buses 271 and 303 stop at Savaris Ct/Park Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
