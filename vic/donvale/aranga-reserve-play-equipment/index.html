Aranga Reserve Play Equipment

Playground in Donvale · Basic info

This is a small local playground in Donvale, looked after by Manningham City Council. The Koonung Creek Trail passes about 130 m away, and Playground at Astelot Drive Reserve is another option about 350 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 1.0 km away, and buses 271, 273 and 902 stop at Heatherwood School/Springvale Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
