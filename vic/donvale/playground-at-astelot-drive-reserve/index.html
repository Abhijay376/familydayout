Playground at Astelot Drive Reserve

Playground in Donvale · Basic info

This is a small local playground in Donvale, looked after by Manningham City Council. The EastLink Trail passes about 220 m away, and Laksa Village is about 490 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 960 m away, and buses 271 and 907 stop at William St/Mitcham Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
