Laksa Village, Donvale

Cafe or restaurant in Donvale · Basic info

Laksa Village is a malaysian cafe or restaurant in Donvale, in the Manningham City Council area, a place to eat during a day out. Donvale Terminus/Mitcham Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 495 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: opening_hours: Mo-Su 11:00-15:00,16:30-21:00.
