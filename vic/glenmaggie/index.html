Our data lists 6 parks and reserves in Glenmaggie, in the Wellington area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Glenmaggie is Mount Useful State Forest, covering about 23,318 hectares. Macalister Gorge Natural Features and Scenic Reserve and Stringybark Creek Reference Area are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Glenmaggie include Cheynes Bridge, The Holding Paddock and Glenmaggie Natural Features and Scenic Reserve. Our data shows 6 public toilets in Glenmaggie. Picnic areas in Glenmaggie include Cheynes Bridge. For camping, there's The Holding Paddock. For walks and rides, Blores Hill Tramway crosses Glenmaggie.

Getting around

There's no train station in Glenmaggie. The closest to Glenmaggie is Rosedale Railway Station (V/Line), about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glenmaggie, so most trips start by car. By car from Glenmaggie, Sale is about 43 minutes away, Traralgon about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield is about 11 minutes' drive from Glenmaggie. The closest library to Glenmaggie is Sale Library in Sale, about 42 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Pump Track in Heyfield is about 11 minutes' drive from Glenmaggie. For a cinema, Sale Cinemas in Sale is about 42 minutes' drive from Glenmaggie. Sale is the closest bigger centre to Glenmaggie, about 43 minutes away by car, and has 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Glenmaggie are Pump Track in Heyfield (11 min), Apex Park Playground in Heyfield (11 min), Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield (11 min), Heyfield and District Museum in Heyfield (11 min), Graves Court - Perry Court - Wickham Court Playground in Heyfield (12 min) and Rosedale Swimming Pool in Rosedale (27 min).

Best for

Free days on the Blores Hill Tramway and in Glenmaggie's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Heyfield, next to Glenmaggie, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Glenmaggie, Coongulla has 1 playground. Toombon, Tinamba West, Tinamba and Seaton also border Glenmaggie.
