Acacia Crescent Reserve Playground

Playground in Melton South · Basic info

This is a public playground in Melton South. The Toolern Creek Trail is about 390 m away, and Melton Swimmers is about 110 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 550 m from the playground, and buses 455 and 458 stop at Garry St/Station Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
