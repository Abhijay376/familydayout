Walton St Reserve Playground

Playground in Melton South · Basic info

This is a public playground in Melton South. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. You'll find a public toilet about 1.1 km from the playground, and bus 452 stops at Turf Club Bvd/Exford Rd , a 5-minute walk away.
