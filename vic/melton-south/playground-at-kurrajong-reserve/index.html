Playground at Kurrajong Reserve

Playground in Melton South · Basic info

Playground at Kurrajong Reserve is a public playground in Melton South, in the Melton City area. The Toolern Creek Trail is about 310 m away, and Melton Swimmers is about 180 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 150 m from the playground, and buses 455, 458 and 943 stop at First Ave/Station Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
