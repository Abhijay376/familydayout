Skate park on Staughton Street

Skate, BMX or pump track in Melton South · Basic info

Skate park on Staughton Street is a skate, bmx or pump track in Melton South, in the Melton City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Al Iman College/Rees Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 950 m away with a baby change table.
