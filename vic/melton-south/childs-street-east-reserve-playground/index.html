Childs Street East Reserve Playground

Playground in Melton South · Basic info

Childs Street East Reserve Playground is a public playground in Melton South, in the Melton City area. Melton Swimmers is about 380 m away, and for food, Ready Roasts is about 470 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.0 km from the playground, and buses 455, 458 and 943 stop at Garry St/Station Rd, a 8-minute walk away.
