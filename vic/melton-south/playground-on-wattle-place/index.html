Wattle Place Reserve Playground

Playground in Melton South · Basic info

Wattle Place Reserve Playground is a public playground in Melton South, in the Melton City area. Childs Street West Reserve Playground is another option about 460 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 870 m away, and buses 457 and 459 stop at Brooklyn Rd/Coburns Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
