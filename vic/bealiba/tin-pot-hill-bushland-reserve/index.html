Tin Pot Hill Bushland Reserve

Park in Bealiba · Basic info

Tin Pot Hill Bushland Reserve is a park in Bealiba, in the Central Goldfields Shire Council area, good for all ages. Corner Store/Main St (coach) is a 14-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1047 m away.
