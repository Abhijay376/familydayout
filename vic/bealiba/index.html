Bealiba in the Central Goldfields area has 1 playground and 8 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bealiba is Tunstalls Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 1,559 hectares. Bealiba Bushland Reserve and Bealiba H42 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Bealiba also has Archdale Bushland Reserve, Bealiba H43 Bushland Reserve and Tin Pot Hill Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Bealiba. The Dunolly Cycle Tracks and Bealiba Cycle Track all pass through Bealiba.

Getting around

Bealiba has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 33 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Bealiba. By car from Bealiba, Maryborough is about 45 minutes away, Bendigo about 73 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bealiba is Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest library to Bealiba is Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough, about 45 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 45 minutes from Bealiba by car, with a library. The closest places for kids by road from Bealiba are Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (21 min), Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (21 min), Stuart Mill Recreation Reserve Playground in Stuart Mill (43 min), Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (45 min), the playground at Market Reserve in Carisbrook (50 min) and St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (45 min).

Best for

Free days on the Dunolly Cycle Tracks and in Bealiba's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Dalyenong, Cochranes Creek and Archdale also border Bealiba.
