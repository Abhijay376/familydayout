Coomoora in the Hepburn area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Coomoora is Glenlyon Bushland Reserve, covering about 2 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Coomoora. The closest to Coomoora is Malmsbury Railway Station (V/Line), about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Coomoora, so most trips start by car. By car from Coomoora, Daylesford is about 6 minutes away, Castlemaine about 32 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford is about 6 minutes' drive from Coomoora. For a library, Daylesford Library in Daylesford is about 6 minutes' drive from Coomoora. The closest beach to Coomoora is Canoeing in Tylden, about 37 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton is about 34 minutes' drive from Coomoora. The closest cinema to Coomoora is Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central, about 45 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Coomoora is Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek, about 38 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Daylesford, about 6 minutes from Coomoora by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Coomoora, the nearest places for kids by road are Daylesford Spa Country Railway in Daylesford (5 min), Daylesford Library in Daylesford (6 min), Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford (6 min), the playground at Hepburn Regional Park in Daylesford (11 min), the playground at J P Gleeson Memorial Park in Lyonville (16 min) and Little Gallery in Trentham (25 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Coomoora.

Nearby suburbs

Daylesford, next to Coomoora, has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Glenlyon also borders Coomoora.
