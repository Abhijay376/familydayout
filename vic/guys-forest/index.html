Guys Forest is in the Towong area, and our data lists 4 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Burrowa - Pine Mountain National Park is the largest mapped park in Guys Forest, at about 18,964 hectares. Mount Burrowa Area Remote and Natural Area and Jemba Reference Area are the next largest. Guys Forest also has Burrowa - Pine Mountain NP (VPC addition).

Getting around

There's no train station in Guys Forest. The closest to Guys Forest is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 65 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Guys Forest, so most trips start by car. By car from Guys Forest, Wodonga is about 80 minutes away and Wangaratta about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a beach, Pebble Beach in Walwa is about 19 minutes' drive from Guys Forest. From Guys Forest, the nearest places for kids by road are The Spotted Goanna in Walwa (13 min) and Pebble Beach in Walwa (19 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Guys Forest.

Nearby suburbs

Walwa, next to Guys Forest, has 1 playground and a beach. Cudgewa, Burrowye and Berringama also border Guys Forest.
