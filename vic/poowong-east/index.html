Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Poowong East in the South Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Poowong East has no train station; Drouin Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Warragul Station - Poowong East via Drouin, Ripplebrook route stop in Poowong East. By car from Poowong East, Warragul is about 26 minutes away, Leongatha about 31 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Poowong East is Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest library to Poowong East is Poowong library in Poowong, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Poowong East is Nyora skate park in Nyora, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Poowong East is Warragul Cinema in Warragul, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Poowong East is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 24 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Wildlife Trail in Tynong North is about 38 minutes' drive from Poowong East. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Poowong East, about 26 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Poowong East are Poowong library in Poowong (11 min), Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong (12 min), the playground at Coleman Park in Korumburra (19 min), Korumburra Outdoor Pool in Korumburra (19 min), the playground at Peterson Street Reserve in Korumburra (20 min) and Coal Creek Community Park & Museum in Korumburra (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Poowong East.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Poowong East, Poowong has a pool and a library. Korumburra, next to Poowong East, has 3 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Poowong East, Hallora has 1 playground. Ranceby, Poowong North and Mountain View also border Poowong East.
