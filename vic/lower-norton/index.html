Our data lists 4 parks and reserves in Lower Norton, in the Horsham area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lower Norton is John Smith Memorial Flora Reserve, covering about 35 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Lower Norton include Wimmera River - Horsham Camping Area 2 (CLWF), Wimmera River - Horsham Camping Area 3 (CLWF) and Wimmera River - Horsham Camping Area 4 (CLWF). For camping, there's Wimmera River - Horsham Camping Area 2 (CLWF) and Wimmera River - Horsham Camping Area 3 (CLWF).

Getting around

There's no train station in Lower Norton. The closest to Lower Norton is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 94 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lower Norton, so most trips start by car. By car from Lower Norton, Horsham is about 16 minutes away and Stawell about 69 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lower Norton is Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Lower Norton is Horsham Skate Park in Horsham, about 14 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Lower Norton is Centre Cinemas in Horsham, about 15 minutes' drive. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to Lower Norton, about 16 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Lower Norton, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Weir Park in Horsham (14 min), the playground at Windsor Court Park in Horsham (13 min), Horsham Skate Park in Horsham (14 min), Centre Cinemas in Horsham (15 min), Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham (15 min) and Velodrome in Horsham (15 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lower Norton.

Nearby suburbs

Natimuk, next to Lower Norton, has 4 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Lower Norton, Vectis has 1 playground. Haven, next to Lower Norton, has 1 playground. Quantong, Nurrabiel, Noradjuha and McKenzie Creek also border Lower Norton.
