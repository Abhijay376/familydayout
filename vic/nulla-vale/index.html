Nulla Vale is in the Mitchell area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Langley Flora Reserve is the largest mapped park in Nulla Vale, at about 29 hectares. Baynton I17 Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Nulla Vale has no train station; Kilmore East Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 25 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nulla Vale, so most trips start by car. By car from Nulla Vale, Seymour is about 48 minutes away, Kyneton about 51 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 85 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Kilmore Leisure Centre in Kilmore is about 31 minutes' drive from Nulla Vale. For a library, Romsey Library in Romsey is about 23 minutes' drive from Nulla Vale. For a skate park, Romsey Skate Park in Romsey is about 24 minutes' drive from Nulla Vale. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 48 minutes from Nulla Vale by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Nulla Vale are Tooborac Recreation Reserve Playground in Tooborac (20 min), Romsey Library in Romsey (23 min), Romsey Skate Park in Romsey (24 min), the playground at Pyalong Recreation Reserve in Pyalong (26 min), Mudwood Studio in Heathcote (32 min) and Heathcote Skate Park in Heathcote (32 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Nulla Vale.

Nearby suburbs

Tooborac, next to Nulla Vale, has 1 playground. Pyalong, next to Nulla Vale, has 1 playground. High Camp, Goldie, Benloch and Baynton also border Nulla Vale.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Nulla Vale, without traffic.
