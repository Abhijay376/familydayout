Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Freeburgh, in the Alpine area.

Landmarks

Freeburgh Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Freeburgh, at about 34 hectares. For walks and rides, Bright - Mount Beauty, Great Valley Trail and Germantown Trail cross Freeburgh.

Getting around

There's no train station in Freeburgh. The closest to Freeburgh is Wodonga Railway Station (V/Line), about 75 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Omeo - Bright via Hotham Heights route stop in Freeburgh. By car from Freeburgh, Bright is about 9 minutes away and Wangaratta about 77 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Freeburgh is Bright Splash Park in Bright, about 8 minutes' drive. For a library, Bright Library in Bright is about 9 minutes' drive from Freeburgh. The closest beach to Freeburgh is Pebble Beach in Tawonga South, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Freeburgh is Bright BMX Track in Bright, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Freeburgh is Sun Cinema Bright in Bright, about 9 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bright, about 9 minutes from Freeburgh by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Freeburgh are Bright Art Gallery in Bright (8 min), Bright Splash Park in Bright (8 min), Sun Cinema Bright in Bright (9 min), the playground at Centenary Park in Bright (9 min), Bright Library in Bright (9 min) and Bright BMX Track in Bright (10 min).

Best for

Free days on the Bright - Mount Beauty and in Freeburgh's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Freeburgh, Bright has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Tawonga South, next to Freeburgh, has a beach. Germantown also borders Freeburgh.
