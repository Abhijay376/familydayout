Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Nine Mile, in the Loddon area.

Landmarks

Nine Mile Historic Area is the largest mapped park in Nine Mile, at about 15 hectares.

Getting around

Nine Mile has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 74 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nine Mile, so most trips start by car. By car from Nine Mile, Bendigo is about 1.5 hours away and Maryborough about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Nine Mile is Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Nine Mile is Rex Theatre in Charlton, about 28 minutes' drive. From Nine Mile, the nearest places for kids by road are Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (29 min), Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (29 min), Rex Theatre in Charlton (28 min), Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (28 min), the playground at Rotary Park in Charlton (28 min) and the playground at Gordon Park in Charlton (28 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Nine Mile.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Nine Mile, Wedderburn has 1 playground and a pool. Berrimal also borders Nine Mile.
