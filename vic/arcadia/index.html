Our data lists 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in Arcadia, in the Greater Shepparton area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Arcadia is Arcadia Streamside Reserve, covering about 1,058 hectares. Arcadia Nature Conservation Reserve and Arcadia H59 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Playgrounds around Arcadia include the playground at Arcadia Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Arcadia.

Getting around

Arcadia has no train station; Murchison East Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 10.0 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Arcadia, so most trips start by car. By car from Arcadia, Shepparton is about 22 minutes away, Seymour about 47 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Arcadia is Aquamoves in Shepparton, about 21 minutes' drive. For a library, Shepparton Library in Shepparton is about 22 minutes' drive from Arcadia. For a beach, Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton is about 23 minutes' drive from Arcadia. For a skate park, the skate park at Skate Park in Shepparton is about 20 minutes' drive from Arcadia. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 22 minutes' drive from Arcadia. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 22 minutes' drive from Arcadia. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Arcadia is Emerald Bank Leisure Land in Kialla, about 15 minutes' drive. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Arcadia, about 22 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Arcadia, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Toolamba Rec Res Youth Spot in Toolamba (13 min), Emerald Bank Leisure Land in Kialla (15 min), Museum of Vehicle Evolution - MOVE in Kialla (15 min), Shepparton Art Museum in Shepparton (20 min), the skate park at Skate Park in Shepparton (20 min) and Aquamoves in Shepparton (21 min).
