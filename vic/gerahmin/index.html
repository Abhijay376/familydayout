Gerahmin in the Swan Hill area has 5 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Gerahmin is Lake Daytrap Lake Reserve, covering about 102 hectares. Daytrap Bushland Reserve and Gerahmin Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Gerahmin also has Grants Bushland Reserve and Gerahmin North Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Gerahmin has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 73 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Gerahmin, so most trips start by car. By car from Gerahmin, Swan Hill is about 68 minutes away and Mildura about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Gerahmin is Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang, about 27 minutes' drive. From Gerahmin, the nearest places for kids by road are Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Gerahmin.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Gerahmin, Manangatang has 1 playground and a pool. Cocamba and Chinkapook also border Gerahmin.
