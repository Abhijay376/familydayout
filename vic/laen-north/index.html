Laen North is in the Buloke area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Laen North Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Laen North, at about 9 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Laen North. The closest to Laen North is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 99 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Laen North, so most trips start by car. By car from Laen North, Horsham is about 58 minutes away and Stawell about 76 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Donald Swimming Pool in Donald is about 17 minutes' drive from Laen North. The closest skate park to Laen North is Donald BMX Track in Donald, about 19 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Horsham, about 58 minutes from Laen North by car, with 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Laen North are the playground at Donald Public Park in Donald (17 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (17 min), Donald BMX Track in Donald (19 min), the playground at Minyip Wetlands in Minyip (19 min), Minyip Swimming Pool in Minyip (19 min) and Woods' Farming and Heritage Museum in Rupanyup (37 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Laen North.

Nearby suburbs

Minyip, next to Laen North, has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Donald, next to Laen North, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Lawler, Laen East and Laen also border Laen North.
