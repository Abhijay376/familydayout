Mount Toolebewong in the Yarra Ranges area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Mount Toolebewong is Mallesons Lookout Bushland Reserve, covering about 7 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mount Toolebewong. The closest to Mount Toolebewong is Lilydale Railway Station (metro), about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mount Toolebewong, so most trips start by car. By car from Mount Toolebewong, Healesville is about 22 minutes away, Lilydale about 41 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 82 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Healesville Outdoor Pool in Healesville is about 20 minutes' drive from Mount Toolebewong. The closest library to Mount Toolebewong is Yarra Junction public library in Yarra Junction, about 27 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Yarra Junction Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Yarra Junction is about 27 minutes' drive from Mount Toolebewong. The closest cinema to Mount Toolebewong is The Memo, Healesville in Healesville, about 21 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Healesville Sanctuary in Badger Creek is about 17 minutes' drive from Mount Toolebewong. Healesville is the closest bigger centre to Mount Toolebewong, about 22 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Mount Toolebewong are the playground at Roma Reserve in Badger Creek (16 min), the playground at Coranderrk Nature Conservation Reserve in Badger Creek (17 min), Healesville Sanctuary in Badger Creek (17 min), Lyre Bird enclosure in Badger Creek (17 min), Healesville Outdoor Pool in Healesville (20 min) and Toddlers Pool in Healesville (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mount Toolebewong.

Nearby suburbs

Healesville, next to Mount Toolebewong, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. Next door to Mount Toolebewong, Woori Yallock has 1 playground.
