California Gully in the Greater Bendigo area has 3 playgrounds and 4 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, California Gully has a pool.

Landmarks

Truscott Reserve is the largest mapped park in California Gully, at about 13 hectares. Jobs Gully Bushland Reserve and Johnsons and Golden Age Mines Historic and Cultural Features Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in California Gully include Bright Street Park. Playgrounds in California Gully include Pirate Playground, the playground at Truscott Reserve and the playground at Bright Street Park. In California Gully you'll also find Sloan Swim School. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in California Gully for a lunch stop.

Getting around

California Gully has no train station; Eaglehawk Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 1.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 52 and 53 stop in California Gully. By car from California Gully, Bendigo is about 8 minutes away, Castlemaine about 38 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Bendigo Library in Bendigo is about 7 minutes' drive from California Gully. The closest skate park to California Gully is the bmx track on Victoria Street in Eaglehawk, about 4 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to California Gully is Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to California Gully is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 11 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo is about 10 minutes' drive from California Gully. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 8 minutes from California Gully by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at California Gully's 3 local playgrounds.

Nearby suburbs

Eaglehawk, next to California Gully, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to California Gully, Long Gully has 4 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to California Gully, Jackass Flat has 2 playgrounds. Next door to California Gully, North Bendigo has 1 playground. Maiden Gully also borders California Gully.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of California Gully, without traffic.
