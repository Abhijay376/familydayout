Badger Creek in the Yarra Ranges area has 4 playgrounds and 5 parks and reserves in our data. On top of that, Badger Creek has 2 zoos or wildlife parks.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Badger Creek is Coranderrk Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 179 hectares. Healesville Sanctuary and Healesville G158 B.R. are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Badger Creek include Roma Reserve and Badger Creek Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds in Badger Creek include the playground at Roma Reserve, the playground at Coranderrk Nature Conservation Reserve and the playground at Coranderrk Nature Conservation Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Badger Creek. Places to visit in Badger Creek include Healesville Sanctuary and Lyre Bird enclosure. For walks and rides, Main Track crosses Badger Creek.

Getting around

Badger Creek has no train station; Lilydale Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 685 and 965 stop in Badger Creek. By car from Badger Creek, Healesville is about 7 minutes away, Lilydale about 25 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 66 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Badger Creek is Healesville Outdoor Pool in Healesville, about 6 minutes' drive. For a library, Yarra Junction public library in Yarra Junction is about 19 minutes' drive from Badger Creek. The closest skate park to Badger Creek is the skate park at Yarra Junction Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Yarra Junction, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Badger Creek is The Memo, Healesville in Healesville, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Badger Creek is GAMR Laser Tag & VR in Chirnside Park, about 30 minutes' drive. Healesville is the closest bigger centre to Badger Creek, about 7 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Badger Creek's 4 local playgrounds. Free days on the Main Track and in Badger Creek's parks.
