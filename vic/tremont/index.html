Landmarks

Playgrounds around Tremont include the playground at Dandenong Ranges National Park and the playground at Dandenong Ranges National Park. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Tremont. The Resilience (ngurrak barring | RidgeWalk), Belview Loop and Lyrebird Loop all pass through Tremont. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Tremont for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Tremont has no train station; Upper Ferntree Gully Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 2.0 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 688 and 755 stop in Tremont. By car from Tremont, Ringwood is about 21 minutes away, Dandenong about 23 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 39 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tremont is Aquatic Achievers in Ferntree Gully, about 7 minutes' drive. For a library, Belgrave Library in Belgrave is about 8 minutes' drive from Tremont. For a beach, Edithvale Beach in Edithvale is about 33 minutes' drive from Tremont. For a skate park, Upwey Skate Park in Upwey is about 6 minutes' drive from Tremont. The closest cinema to Tremont is Cameo Belgrave in Belgrave, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Tremont is Little Rascals Play Centre in Ferntree Gully, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Tremont is Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale, about 28 minutes' drive. Ringwood is the closest bigger centre to Tremont, about 21 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Tremont, the nearest places for kids by road are Upwey / Belgrave RSL in Upwey (5 min), Upwey Skate Park in Upwey (6 min), Little Rascals Play Centre in Ferntree Gully (6 min), Cameo Belgrave in Belgrave (8 min), Belgrave Library in Belgrave (8 min) and Burrinja Cultural Centre - Galleries in Upwey (9 min).

Best for
