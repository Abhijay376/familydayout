Earlston is in the Strathbogie area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Earlston is Gowangardie Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 3 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Earlston. The closest to Earlston is Violet Town Railway Station (V/Line), about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Earlston, so most trips start by car. By car from Earlston, Benalla is about 44 minutes away, Shepparton about 44 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Earlston is Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa, about 38 minutes' drive. For a library, Euroa Library in Euroa is about 38 minutes' drive from Earlston. The closest skate park to Earlston is Euroa Skate Park in Euroa, about 37 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Earlston is Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa, about 37 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 44 minutes' drive from Earlston. The nearest larger centre is Benalla, about 44 minutes from Earlston by car, with 5 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Earlston are Violet Town Lions Park Playground in Violet Town (20 min), Violet Town Recreation Reserve West Playground in Violet Town (21 min), Farmers Arms Hotel Museum in Euroa (37 min), Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa (37 min), Euroa Skate Park in Euroa (37 min) and Euroa Library in Euroa (38 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Earlston.

Nearby suburbs
