Curdies River is in the Corangamite area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Curdies River, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Camperdown to Timboon Rail Trail crosses Curdies River.

Getting around

There's no train station in Curdies River. The closest to Curdies River is Terang Railway Station (V/Line), about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Curdies River, so most trips start by car. By car from Curdies River, Warrnambool is about 46 minutes away and Colac about 66 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon is about 12 minutes' drive from Curdies River. For a beach, Wild Dog Cove 5 in Peterborough is about 30 minutes' drive from Curdies River. The closest skate park to Curdies River is the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon, about 12 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Curdies River is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 40 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Warrnambool, about 46 minutes from Curdies River by car, with 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Curdies River, the nearest places for kids by road are the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (12 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (12 min), Port Campbell Skate Park in Port Campbell (26 min), the playground at Peterborough Coastal Reserve (COM) in Peterborough (29 min), Wild Dog Cove 5 in Peterborough (30 min) and Falls of Halladale in Peterborough (31 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Curdies River on the Camperdown to Timboon Rail Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Curdies River, Timboon has a pool and a skate park.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Curdies River, without traffic.
