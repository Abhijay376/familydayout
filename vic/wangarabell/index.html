Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Wangarabell in the East Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Wangarabell has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 171 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wangarabell, so most trips start by car. By car from Wangarabell, Lakes Entrance is about 3 hours away and Bairnsdale about 3.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Wangarabell, the nearest places for kids by road are Davis Creek Beach in Mallacoota (74 min) and Betka Beach in Mallacoota (74 min).
