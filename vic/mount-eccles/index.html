Mount Eccles is in the South Gippsland area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Mount Eccles, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mount Eccles. The closest to Mount Eccles is Yarragon Railway Station (V/Line), about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mount Eccles, so most trips start by car. By car from Mount Eccles, Leongatha is about 31 minutes away, Warragul about 51 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Mount Eccles is South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha, about 29 minutes' drive. For a library, Leongatha Library in Leongatha is about 30 minutes' drive from Mount Eccles. The closest skate park to Mount Eccles is BMX track in Leongatha, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Mount Eccles is Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha, about 30 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Leongatha, about 31 minutes from Mount Eccles by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Mount Eccles are the playground at Mossvale Park in Berrys Creek (30 min), BMX track in Leongatha (28 min), Leongatha Skate Park in Leongatha (28 min), South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha (29 min), Splash Pools and Stadium in Leongatha (30 min) and the playground at Coal Creek Historic and Cultural Features Reserve in Korumburra (40 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mount Eccles.

Nearby suburbs

Hallston and Fairbank also border Mount Eccles.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Mount Eccles, without traffic.
