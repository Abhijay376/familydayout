Naringal East is in the Moyne area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Naringal East is Unnamed P0126 Private Nature Reserve, covering about 92 hectares. Brucknell Creek Flora and Fauna Reserve and Nullawarre Flora Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

Naringal East has no train station; Terang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Naringal East, so most trips start by car. By car from Naringal East, Warrnambool is about 30 minutes away and Colac about 67 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon is about 26 minutes' drive from Naringal East. The closest library to Naringal East is Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool, about 31 minutes' drive. For a beach, Buttress Point in The Cove is about 29 minutes' drive from Naringal East. For a skate park, the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon is about 25 minutes' drive from Naringal East. For a cinema, Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool is about 30 minutes' drive from Naringal East. The closest indoor play centre to Naringal East is The Bowl in Warrnambool, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Naringal East is Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee, about 23 minutes' drive. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Naringal East, about 30 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Naringal East, the nearest places for kids by road are Buttress Point in The Cove (29 min), Flaxman Beach in Nullawarre (31 min), Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (23 min), the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (25 min), Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (26 min) and Grieve Street Playground in Warrnambool (28 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Naringal East.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Naringal East, Nullawarre has 1 playground and a beach. Panmure, next to Naringal East, has 1 playground. Mepunga East, Laang, Ecklin South and Ayrford also border Naringal East.
