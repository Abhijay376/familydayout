Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Carron in the Buloke area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Carron has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 111 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Carron, so most trips start by car. By car from Carron, Horsham is about 70 minutes away and Stawell about 87 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Carron is Donald Swimming Pool in Donald, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Carron is Donald BMX Track in Donald, about 23 minutes' drive. From Carron, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Donald Public Park in Donald (21 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (21 min), Donald BMX Track in Donald (23 min), the playground at Minyip Wetlands in Minyip (31 min), Minyip Swimming Pool in Minyip (32 min) and Historical Centre in Warracknabeal (26 min).
