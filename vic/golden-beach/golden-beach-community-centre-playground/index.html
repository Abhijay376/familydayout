Golden Beach Community Centre Playground

Playground in Golden Beach · Basic info

Golden Beach Community Centre Playground in Golden Beach is a public playground in the Wellington Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. You'll find a public toilet about 90 m from the playground, and bus 7 stops at Golden Beach Community Hall/Longford-Golden Beach Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
