Veronica Maybury Reserve

Park in Golden Beach · Basic info

Veronica Maybury Reserve is a park in Golden Beach, in the Wellington Shire Council area, good for all ages. Golden Beach Community Hall/Longford-Golden Beach Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 650 m away.
