Landmarks

The biggest green space in Golden Beach is Veronica Maybury Reserve, covering about 6 hectares. Playgrounds in Golden Beach include Golden Beach Community Centre Playground. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Golden Beach. In Golden Beach you'll also find Golden Beach.

Getting around

There's no train station in Golden Beach. The closest to Golden Beach is Sale Railway Station (V/Line), about 33 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 7 stops in Golden Beach. By car from Golden Beach, Sale is about 42 minutes away and Traralgon about 77 minutes, without traffic.
