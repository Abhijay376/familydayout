Zero Latency VR

Indoor play in Docklands · Official details

Zero Latency VR is a indoor play in Docklands, in the Melbourne City Council area, best for younger kids (check the venue's age and height rules). Moonee Ponds Creek/Footscray Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 146 m away.
