Playground at Point Park

Playground in Docklands · Official details

Playground at Point Park in Docklands is a public playground in the Melbourne City area. The River Esplanade is about 40 m away, and Library at the Dock is about 340 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 60 m from the playground, and bus 237 stops at Point Park Cres/Lorimer St, a 3-minute walk away.
