Playground at Ron Barassi Senior Park

Playground in Docklands · Official details

This is a public playground in Docklands. Zero Latency VR is about 540 m away, and Alma Doepel Restoration Project is about 350 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 80 m away, and trams 35, 70 and 86 stop at Waterfront City/Docklands Dr #D11, a 7-minute walk away.
