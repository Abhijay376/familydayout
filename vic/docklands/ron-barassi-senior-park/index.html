Ron Barassi Senior Park

Park in Docklands · Official details

Ron Barassi Senior Park is a park in Docklands, in the Melbourne City Council area, good for all ages. Waterfront City/Docklands Dr #D11 (tram) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 238 m away with a baby change table.
