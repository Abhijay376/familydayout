Fish N Chips On Docks, Docklands

Cafe or restaurant in Docklands · Basic info

Fish N Chips On Docks is a fish and chips cafe or restaurant in Docklands, in the Melbourne City Council area, a place to eat during a day out. Central Pier/Harbour Esp #D2 (tram) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 148 m away with a baby change table.
