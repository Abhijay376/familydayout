Fish Market

Cafe or restaurant in Docklands · Basic info

Fish Market is a chinese cafe or restaurant in Docklands, in the Melbourne City Council area, a place to eat during a day out. Waterfront City/Docklands Dr #D11 (tram) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 279 m away with a baby change table.
