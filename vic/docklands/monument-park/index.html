Monument Park

Park in Docklands · Basic info

Monument Park is a park in Docklands, in the Melbourne City Council area, good for all ages. Central Pier/Harbour Esp #D2 (tram) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 156 m away.
