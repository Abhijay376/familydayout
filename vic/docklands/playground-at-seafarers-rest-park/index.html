Playground at Seafarers Rest Park

Playground in Docklands · Basic info

This is a public playground in Docklands. The Main Yarra Trail passes about 110 m away, and Fox Classic Car Museum is about 310 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 430 m away, and trams 35, 70 and 75 stop at South Wharf/Wurundjeri Way #D5, a 4-minute walk away.
