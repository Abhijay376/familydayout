Wharf Landing Park

Park in Docklands · Official details

Wharf Landing Park is a park in Docklands, in the Melbourne City Council area, good for all ages. Regatta Way/Lorimer St (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 2200 m away.

Details

Good to know
Public BBQ about 20 m away (Council)

Questions parents ask

Where is Wharf Landing Park?
Wharf Landing Park is in Docklands, Victoria.
Is Wharf Landing Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Wharf Landing Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 2200 m away.
How do I get to Wharf Landing Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Regatta Way/Lorimer St (routes 237), a 2-minute walk.
