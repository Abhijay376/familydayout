Buluk Park Playground

Playground in Docklands · Basic info

Buluk Park Playground in Docklands is a public playground in the Melbourne City area. The River Esplanade is about 260 m away, and Library at the Dock is about 40 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 90 m from the playground, and trams 11 and 48 stop at Bourke St/Collins St #D18, a 2-minute walk away.
