Docklands Park

Park in Docklands · Official details

Docklands Park is a park in Docklands, in the Melbourne City Council area, good for all ages. Harbour Esp/Collins St #D16 (tram) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 227 m away.

Details

Good to know
Public BBQ about 70 m away (Council)

Questions parents ask

Where is Docklands Park?
Docklands Park is in Docklands, Victoria.
Is Docklands Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Docklands Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 227 m away.
How do I get to Docklands Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Harbour Esp/Collins St #D16 (routes 11, 48), a 3-minute walk.
