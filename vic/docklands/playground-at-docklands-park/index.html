Playground at Docklands Park

Playground in Docklands · Official details

Playground at Docklands Park in Docklands is a public playground in the Melbourne City area. The Capital City Trail is about 20 m away, and Fox Classic Car Museum is about 120 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 60 m from the playground, and trams 35, 70 and 75 stop at Docklands Park/Harbour Esp #D4, a 1-minute walk away.
