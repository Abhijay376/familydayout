ArtVo

Museum or gallery in Docklands · Official details

ArtVo is a museum or gallery in Docklands, in the Melbourne City Council area, best for school-age kids. Waterfront City/Docklands Dr #D11 (tram) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 215 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: Monday – 10am to 5pm; Tuesday – 10am to 5pm; Wednesday – 10am to 5pm; Thursday – 10am to 5pm; Friday – 10am to 5pm; Saturday – 10am to 8pm; Sunday  – 10am to 5pm.
