Patyah in the West Wimmera area has 14 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Murrays State Forest is the largest mapped park in Patyah, at about 1,067 hectares. Burns State Forest and Keelys State Forest are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Patyah include Morea I5 Bushland Reserve, Awonga I19 Bushland Reserve and Bringalbert Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Patyah has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 156 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Patyah, so most trips start by car. By car from Patyah, Horsham is about 87 minutes away and Hamilton about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Goroke Swimming Centre in Goroke is about 30 minutes' drive from Patyah. For a library, West Wimmera Shire Goroke Library in Goroke is about 31 minutes' drive from Patyah. The closest places for kids by road from Patyah are West Wimmera Community Association - Red Tail Gallery in Edenhope (20 min), Dachshund Museum & Gallery in Edenhope (20 min), the playground at Lions Park in Edenhope (20 min), Henley Park Playground in Edenhope (21 min), Goroke Swimming Centre in Goroke (30 min) and West Wimmera Shire Goroke Library in Goroke (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Patyah.

Nearby suburbs

Edenhope, next to Patyah, has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Patyah, Apsley has 1 playground. Bringalbert also borders Patyah.
