Hampton Park is in the Casey area, and our data lists 20 playgrounds and 27 parks and reserves in the suburb. On top of that, Hampton Park has a library.

Landmarks

Robert Booth Reserve is the largest mapped park in Hampton Park, at about 4 hectares. Oaktree Drive Reserve and Marjorie Eastick Reserve are the next largest. Hampton Park also has Scott Park, Redwood Avenue and Peppercorn Park. Playgrounds in Hampton Park include the playground at Peppercorn Park, the playground at Amber Drive Reserve and the playground at The Parkway Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Hampton Park. Places to visit in Hampton Park include Hampton Park Library. Hampton Park Central is the local shopping centre. The Hallam Valley Trail, River Gum Creek Trail and Dandenong Bypass Extension Trail all pass through Hampton Park. Our data lists 14 cafes and family restaurants in Hampton Park for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Hampton Park. The closest to Hampton Park is Hallam Railway Station (metro), about 1.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 863, 891, 892, 893, 894, 895 and 982 stop in Hampton Park. By car from Hampton Park, Dandenong is about 9 minutes away, Cranbourne about 12 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 34 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Hampton Park is Paul Sadler Swimland in Narre Warren South, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Hampton Park is Edithvale Beach in Edithvale, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Hampton Park is Narre Warren Skatepark in Narre Warren, about 6 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Narre Warren is about 8 minutes' drive from Hampton Park.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 20 playgrounds across Hampton Park. Rainy days at the library in Hampton Park. Free days on the Hallam Valley Trail and in Hampton Park's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Narre Warren, next to Hampton Park, has 30 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Hampton Park, Narre Warren South has 18 playgrounds and a pool. Hallam, next to Hampton Park, has 12 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Hampton Park, Cranbourne North has 12 playgrounds and a skate park. Lynbrook and Dandenong South also border Hampton Park.
