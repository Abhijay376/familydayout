Playground at Victor Court Reserve

Playground in Hampton Park · Basic info

Playground at Victor Court Reserve is a public playground in Hampton Park, in the Casey City area. The River Gum Creek Trail passes about 260 m away, and Playground at Henry Joseph Moran Park is another option about 540 m away. There's a public toilet about 590 m away, and bus 895 stops at Strong Dr/Warana Dr, a 2-minute walk away.
