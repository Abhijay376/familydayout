Playground on Affra Place

Playground in Hampton Park · Basic info

Playground on Affra Place is a public playground in Hampton Park, in the Casey City area. The Hallam Valley Trail passes about 280 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 190 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 310 m away, and buses 892 and 894 stop at Sinclair Ct/Pound Rd, a 6-minute walk away.
