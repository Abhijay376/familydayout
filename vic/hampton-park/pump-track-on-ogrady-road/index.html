Pump track on O'Grady Road

Skate, BMX or pump track in Hampton Park · Basic info

Pump track on O'Grady Road is a skate, bmx or pump track in Hampton Park, in the Casey City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Sinclair Ct/Pound Rd (bus) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 508 m away with a baby change table.
