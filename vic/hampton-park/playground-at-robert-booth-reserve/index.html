Playground at Robert Booth Reserve

Playground in Hampton Park · Basic info

Playground at Robert Booth Reserve in Hampton Park is a public playground in the Casey City area. Altaqi Pakwan Centre is about 130 m away if you need food, and Playground on Cody Avenue is another option about 440 m away. There's a public toilet about 900 m away, and bus 894 stops at Robert Booth Reserve/Somerville Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
