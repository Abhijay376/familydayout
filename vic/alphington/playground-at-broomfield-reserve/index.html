Playground at Broomfield Reserve

Playground in Alphington · Basic info

Playground at Broomfield Reserve in Alphington is a public playground in the Darebin City area. The Bike: Heidelberg Road Link passes about 160 m away, and Fairfield Library is about 580 m away. There's a public toilet about 110 m away, and buses 546 and 609 stop at Yarraford Ave/Heidelberg Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
