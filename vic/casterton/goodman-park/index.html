Goodman Park

Park in Casterton · Basic info

Goodman Park is a park in Casterton, in the Glenelg Shire Council area, good for all ages. Casterton Memorial Hospital/Russell St (coach) is a 9-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1054 m away.
