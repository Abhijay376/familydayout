Skate park on Murray Street

Skate, BMX or pump track in Casterton · Basic info

Skate park on Murray Street is a skate, bmx or pump track in Casterton, in the Glenelg Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Wathen St/Henty St (coach) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 214 m away with a baby change table.
