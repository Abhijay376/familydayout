Barwon Heads Skatepark

Skate, BMX or pump track in Barwon Heads · Basic info

Barwon Heads Skatepark is a skate, bmx or pump track in Barwon Heads, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Barwon Heads Bowling Club/Sheepwash Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 146 m away.
