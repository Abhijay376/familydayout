Barwon Bluff Marine Sanctuary

Park in Barwon Heads · Official details

Barwon Bluff Marine Sanctuary is a park in Barwon Heads, in the Greater Geelong City Council area, good for all ages. Grandview Pde/Bridge Rd (bus) is a 14-minute walk away.

Details

Good to know
Keep clear of cliff bases after a recent rockfall; High incoming tides, check tide times; Stay on the trail around the Bluff (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Barwon Bluff Marine Sanctuary?
Barwon Bluff Marine Sanctuary is in Barwon Heads, Victoria.
Is Barwon Bluff Marine Sanctuary free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
How do I get to Barwon Bluff Marine Sanctuary by public transport?
The closest stop is Grandview Pde/Bridge Rd (routes 55), a 14-minute walk.
