Playground at Dromana Park

Playground in Dromana · Basic info

This is a public playground in Dromana. The Mornington Peninsula Bay Trail passes about 100 m away, and Dromana and District Historical Society Museum is about 210 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 110 m away, and buses 788 and 887 stop at Visitor Information/Point Nepean Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
