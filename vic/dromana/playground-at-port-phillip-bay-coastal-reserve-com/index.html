Playground at Port Phillip Bay Coastal Reserve (COM)

Playground in Dromana · Basic info

Playground at Port Phillip Bay Coastal Reserve (COM) in Dromana is a public playground in the Mornington Peninsula Shire area. The Mornington Peninsula Bay Trail passes about 30 m away, and Dromana Beach is about 350 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and buses 788 and 887 stop at Pier St/Point Nepean Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
