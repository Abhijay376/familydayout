Playground at Dromana Recreation Reserve

Playground in Dromana · Basic info

Playground at Dromana Recreation Reserve in Dromana is a public playground in the Mornington Peninsula Shire area. The Mornington Peninsula Bay Trail passes about 390 m away, and Dromana Beach is about 480 m away. There's a public toilet about 80 m away, and buses 781, 788 and 887 stop at Dromana Shops/Point Nepean Rd, a 5-minute walk away.
