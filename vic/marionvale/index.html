Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Marionvale in the Greater Shepparton area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Marionvale. The closest to Marionvale is Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line), about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Marionvale, so most trips start by car. By car from Marionvale, Shepparton is about 26 minutes away, Benalla about 67 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Aquamoves in Shepparton is about 27 minutes' drive from Marionvale. The closest library to Marionvale is Shepparton Library in Shepparton, about 25 minutes' drive. For a beach, Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton is about 29 minutes' drive from Marionvale. For a skate park, Shepparton Velodrome in Shepparton is about 24 minutes' drive from Marionvale. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 26 minutes' drive from Marionvale. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 26 minutes' drive from Marionvale. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kidstown in Mooroopna is about 29 minutes' drive from Marionvale. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 26 minutes from Marionvale by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Marionvale are the playground at Katandra West Recreation Reserve in Katandra West (9 min), the playground at Katandra Rec Res Youth Spot in Katandra West (8 min), Shepparton Velodrome in Shepparton (24 min), BMX track in Shepparton (24 min), Shepparton Library in Shepparton (25 min) and Village Cinemas in Shepparton (26 min).
