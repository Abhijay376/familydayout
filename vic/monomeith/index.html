Monomeith is in the Cardinia area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Monomeith, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Monomeith. The closest to Monomeith is East Pakenham Railway Station (V/Line), about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Monomeith, so most trips start by car. By car from Monomeith, Pakenham is about 20 minutes away, Cranbourne about 26 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 63 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Koo Wee Rup Swimming Pool in Koo Wee Rup is about 6 minutes' drive from Monomeith. The closest library to Monomeith is Pakenham Library in Pakenham, about 19 minutes' drive. For a beach, Corinella Beach in Corinella is about 33 minutes' drive from Monomeith. For a skate park, the skate park at Cochrane Park in Koo Wee Rup is about 6 minutes' drive from Monomeith. The closest cinema to Monomeith is Village Cinemas in Frankston, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Monomeith is Jump Crazy Trampoline Park in Pakenham, about 19 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, MonkeyG's in Pakenham is about 18 minutes' drive from Monomeith. Pakenham is the closest bigger centre to Monomeith, about 20 minutes away by car, and has 46 playgrounds, a pool, a library and indoor play. From Monomeith, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Cochrane Park in Koo Wee Rup (5 min), the skate park at Cochrane Park in Koo Wee Rup (6 min), Koo Wee Rup Swimming Pool in Koo Wee Rup (6 min), the playground at Alexandra Avenue Park in Koo Wee Rup (6 min), Fisherman's Cottage in Tooradin (12 min) and MonkeyG's in Pakenham (18 min).
