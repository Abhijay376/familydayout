Creswick North is in the Hepburn area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Creswick North, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Daylesford - North Creswick railway crosses Creswick North.

Getting around

There's no train station in Creswick North. The closest to Creswick North is Creswick Railway Station (V/Line), about 3.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Creswick North, so most trips start by car. By car from Creswick North, Ballarat is about 23 minutes away, Daylesford about 28 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, (Diving tower, restricted) in Creswick is about 4 minutes' drive from Creswick North. The closest library to Creswick North is Creswick Library in Creswick, about 4 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick is about 5 minutes' drive from Creswick North. For a cinema, Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central is about 23 minutes' drive from Creswick North. The closest indoor play centre to Creswick North is Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Creswick North is Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek, about 22 minutes' drive. Ballarat is the closest bigger centre to Creswick North, about 23 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Creswick North, the nearest places for kids by road are Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick (5 min), (Diving tower, restricted) in Creswick (4 min), Infants in Creswick (4 min), Creswick Library in Creswick (4 min), Skate Park in Creswick (5 min) and Apex Park Playground in Creswick (5 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Creswick North on the Daylesford - North Creswick railway.

Nearby suburbs

Creswick, next to Creswick North, has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Clunes, next to Creswick North, has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Broomfield and Allendale also border Creswick North.
