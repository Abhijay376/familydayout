Mount Buffalo National Park

Park in Mount Buffalo · Official details

Mount Buffalo National Park is a park in Mount Buffalo, in the Alpine Shire Council area, good for all ages.

Details

Seasons
Reservoir Road closed from the Thursday after the King's Birthday long weekend to the Thursday before the Melbourne Cup long weekend (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
Tobogganing and snow play at Cresta Valley and Dingo Dell in winter; Lake Catani suitable for a swim (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Mount Buffalo National Park?
Mount Buffalo National Park is in Mount Buffalo, Victoria.
Is Mount Buffalo National Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
