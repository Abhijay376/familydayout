Bonnie Brook in the Melton area has 6 playgrounds and 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bonnie Brook is Kororoit Creek K36 Streamside Reserve, covering about 2 hectares. Monument Bonnie Brook Public Playground is the next largest. Bonnie Brook also has Alvaston View Reserve and Sinclairs Road Wetland Reserve. Playgrounds in Bonnie Brook include Mushu St Reserve Playground, Opera Bvd Reserve Playground and Shiloh St Reserve Playground. For walks and rides, Kororoit Creek Trail crosses Bonnie Brook.

Getting around

Bonnie Brook has no train station; Rockbank Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 3.8 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 464 stops in Bonnie Brook. By car from Bonnie Brook, Melton is about 10 minutes away, Sunshine about 20 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 35 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Paul Sadler Swimland in Aintree is about 5 minutes' drive from Bonnie Brook. For a library, Melton Library & Learning Hub in Melton is about 10 minutes' drive from Bonnie Brook. The closest beach to Bonnie Brook is Altona Beach in Altona, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Bonnie Brook is Rockbank Skate Park in Rockbank, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Bonnie Brook is Hoyts in Taylors Lakes, about 13 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes is about 15 minutes' drive from Bonnie Brook. For a zoo or wildlife park, Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville is about 33 minutes' drive from Bonnie Brook.

Best for

Younger kids at Bonnie Brook's 6 local playgrounds. Free days on the Kororoit Creek Trail and in Bonnie Brook's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Melton, next to Bonnie Brook, has 15 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Bonnie Brook, Aintree has 16 playgrounds and a pool. Fraser Rise, next to Bonnie Brook, has 13 playgrounds. Diggers Rest, next to Bonnie Brook, has 11 playgrounds. Deanside, Plumpton and Grangefields also border Bonnie Brook.
