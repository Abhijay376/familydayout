Playground at Bourke Street Reserve

Playground in Essendon West · Basic info

Playground at Bourke Street Reserve in Essendon West is a public playground in the Moonee Valley City area. The Maribyrnong River Trail is about 90 m away, and for food, Hampton Road Fish & Chips is about 470 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 800 m from the playground, and buses 465 and 903 stop at Bourke St/Buckley St, a 2-minute walk away.
