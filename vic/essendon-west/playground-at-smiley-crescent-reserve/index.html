Playground at Smiley Crescent Reserve

Playground in Essendon West · Basic info

Playground at Smiley Crescent Reserve is a public playground in Essendon West, in the Moonee Valley City area. The Maribyrnong River Trail passes about 30 m away, and Playground at Bourke Street Reserve is another option about 300 m away. There's a public toilet about 620 m away, and buses 465 and 903 stop at Hoffmans Rd/Buckley St, a 6-minute walk away.
