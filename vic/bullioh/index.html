Bullioh in the Towong area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bullioh is Granya Bushland Reserve, covering about 62 hectares. Tallangatta Creek K29 Streamside Reserve and Rail Bridge (Dry Forest Creek) Historic Reserve are the next largest. Bullioh also has Trestle Bridge, Tucker Box Creek Historic Reserve. For walks and rides, High Country Rail Trail and Tallangatta - Firebrace Loop cross Bullioh.

Getting around

Bullioh has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 38 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bullioh, so most trips start by car. By car from Bullioh, Wodonga is about 47 minutes away and Wangaratta about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta is about 14 minutes' drive from Bullioh. For a library, Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta is about 13 minutes' drive from Bullioh. For a skate park, Skate Park in Baranduda is about 39 minutes' drive from Bullioh. The nearest larger centre is Wodonga, about 47 minutes from Bullioh by car, with 36 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Bullioh, the nearest places for kids by road are Granya Museum in Granya (15 min), Tallangatta Library in Tallangatta (13 min), Tallangatta Art & Photography in Tallangatta (13 min), Tallangatta Swimming Pool in Tallangatta (14 min), the playground at Banksia Park in Tangambalanga (26 min) and the playground at Ludlows Reserve in Ebden (31 min).

Best for

Free days on the High Country Rail Trail and in Bullioh's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Georges Creek and Bungil also border Bullioh.
