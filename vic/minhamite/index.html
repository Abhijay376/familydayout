Minhamite is in the Moyne area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Minhamite, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Minhamite. The closest to Minhamite is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 45 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Minhamite, so most trips start by car. By car from Minhamite, Hamilton is about 39 minutes away and Warrnambool about 48 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Minhamite is Hawkesdale Swimming Pool in Hawkesdale, about 13 minutes' drive. For a library, Hamilton Library in Hamilton is about 39 minutes' drive from Minhamite. The closest beach to Minhamite is Reef Point Beach in Port Fairy, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Minhamite is Koroit Youth Space in Koroit, about 34 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton is about 39 minutes' drive from Minhamite. Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Minhamite, about 39 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Minhamite are Hawkesdale Swimming Pool in Hawkesdale (13 min), Volcanoes Discovery Centre in Penshurst (13 min), Penshurst Botanic Gardens Playground in Penshurst (14 min), Penshurst Swimming Pool in Penshurst (14 min), Penshurst Rec Reserve Playground in Penshurst (15 min) and Koroit Youth Space in Koroit (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Minhamite.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Minhamite, Penshurst has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Hawkesdale, next to Minhamite, has a pool. Gerrigerrup and Caramut also border Minhamite.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Minhamite, without traffic.
