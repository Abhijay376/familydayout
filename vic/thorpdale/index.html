Thorpdale in the Baw Baw area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Pioneer Park is the largest mapped park in Thorpdale, at about 1 hectares. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Thorpdale.

Getting around

There's no train station in Thorpdale. The closest to Thorpdale is Trafalgar Railway Station (V/Line), about 9.4 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Thorpdale, so most trips start by car. By car from Thorpdale, Warragul is about 29 minutes away, Traralgon about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar is about 11 minutes' drive from Thorpdale. For a library, Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North is about 17 minutes' drive from Thorpdale. The closest skate park to Thorpdale is Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North in Mirboo North, about 18 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 27 minutes' drive from Thorpdale. The closest indoor play centre to Thorpdale is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 28 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Warragul, about 29 minutes from Thorpdale by car, with 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Thorpdale, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Brindlewood Reserve in Trafalgar (10 min), Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar (11 min), Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North in Mirboo North (18 min), the playground at Baromi Park in Mirboo North (18 min), Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North (17 min) and ARC Yinnar in Yinnar (23 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Thorpdale.

Nearby suburbs

Mirboo North, next to Thorpdale, has 1 playground, a library and a skate park. Delburn, Childers and Allambee South also border Thorpdale.
