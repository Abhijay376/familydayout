Lardner is in the Baw Baw area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Lardner, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lardner. The closest to Lardner is Warragul Railway Station (V/Line), about 6.3 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lardner, so most trips start by car. By car from Lardner, Warragul is about 13 minutes away, Pakenham about 41 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 86 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul is about 11 minutes' drive from Lardner. The closest library to Lardner is Warragul Library in Warragul, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Lardner is Warragul Skate Park in Warragul, about 11 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 11 minutes' drive from Lardner. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 12 minutes' drive from Lardner. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Lardner is Wildlife Trail in Tynong North, about 29 minutes' drive. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Lardner, about 13 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Lardner, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at McMillan Heights Reserve in Warragul (12 min), the playground at Kelly Koochew Park in Warragul (13 min), Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul (11 min), Warragul Skate Park in Warragul (11 min), Warragul Leisure Centre in Warragul (11 min) and Burke Street Pump Track in Warragul (11 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lardner.

Nearby suburbs

Drouin, next to Lardner, has 19 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Hallora, next to Lardner, has 1 playground. Drouin South also borders Lardner.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Lardner, without traffic.
