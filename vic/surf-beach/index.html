Surf Beach in the Bass Coast area has 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Surf Beach also has Surf Beach Community Park. Playgrounds around Surf Beach include the playground at Surf Beach Community Park. The Phillip Island Bike Path passes through Surf Beach.

Getting around

There's no train station in Surf Beach. The closest to Surf Beach is Stony Point Railway Station (metro), about 16 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Cowes - Anderson - Wonthaggi and Cowes - Fountain Gate via Anderson routes stop in Surf Beach. V/Line coaches stop in Surf Beach. By car from Surf Beach, Wonthaggi is about 33 minutes away, Leongatha about 63 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Surf Beach is Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Wonthaggi, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest library to Surf Beach is San Remo Library in San Remo, about 9 minutes' drive. For a beach, Smiths Beach in Rhyll is about 7 minutes' drive from Surf Beach. The closest skate park to Surf Beach is the skate park at Wonthaggi Railway Historic Reserve in Kilcunda, about 20 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi is about 32 minutes' drive from Surf Beach. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Surf Beach is A Maze'N Things in Cowes, about 5 minutes' drive. Wonthaggi is the closest bigger centre to Surf Beach, about 33 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Surf Beach are the playground at Sunset Strip Reserve in Sunset Strip (5 min), National Vietnam Veterans Museum in Newhaven (4 min), A Maze'N Things in Cowes (5 min), Koala Conservation Reserve in Rhyll (7 min), Smiths Beach in Rhyll (7 min) and YCW in Smiths Beach (7 min).

Best for

Free days on the Phillip Island Bike Path and in Surf Beach's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Surf Beach, Rhyll has 2 playgrounds, a beach and a zoo or wildlife park. Next door to Surf Beach, Newhaven has 1 playground.
