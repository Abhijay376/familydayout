Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lerderderg is Lerderderg State Park, covering about 20,542 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Lerderderg. For walks and rides, Walk 5, Wombat MTB Trail Two and Lerderderg Gorge Track cross Lerderderg.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lerderderg. The closest to Lerderderg is Bacchus Marsh Railway Station (V/Line), about 8.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lerderderg, so most trips start by car. By car from Lerderderg, Bacchus Marsh is about 12 minutes away, Melton about 20 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 56 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lerderderg is Paul Saddler Swimland in Darley, about 9 minutes' drive. For a library, Lerderderg Library in Bacchus Marsh is about 12 minutes' drive from Lerderderg. For a beach, Lionhead Beach in Pentland Hills is about 18 minutes' drive from Lerderderg. For a skate park, the bmx track on Dundas Street in Darley is about 9 minutes' drive from Lerderderg. The closest cinema to Lerderderg is Reading Cinemas in Melton West, about 19 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes is about 39 minutes' drive from Lerderderg. The nearest larger centre is Bacchus Marsh, about 12 minutes from Lerderderg by car, with 6 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Lerderderg are the playground at Federation Park Recreation Reserve in Darley (7 min), the playground at Darley Park Recreation Reserve in Darley (9 min), the bmx track on Dundas Street in Darley (9 min), Paul Saddler Swimland in Darley (9 min), the skate park at Rotary Park in Bacchus Marsh (11 min) and Lerderderg Library in Bacchus Marsh (12 min).
