Landmarks

Other parks and reserves in Mount Beauty include 5 ways, Rockpools and Hydro Park. Our data shows 5 public toilets in Mount Beauty. Picnic areas in Mount Beauty include Mermaid Beach and Rockpools. In Mount Beauty you'll also find Mt Beauty Library. For older kids in Mount Beauty there's Mount Beauty BMX Bike Track. The Trappers Gap: Mitta Mitta to Mount Beauty, Bright - Mount Beauty and Grey Hills Track all pass through Mount Beauty. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Mount Beauty for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mount Beauty. The closest to Mount Beauty is Albury Railway Station (V/Line), about 76 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mount Beauty, so most trips start by car. By car from Mount Beauty, Bright is about 30 minutes away and Wodonga about 72 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bright River Pool in Bright is about 30 minutes' drive from Mount Beauty. The closest beach to Mount Beauty is Pebble Beach in Tawonga South, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Mount Beauty is Sun Cinema Bright in Bright, about 31 minutes' drive. Bright is the closest bigger centre to Mount Beauty, about 30 minutes away by car, and has 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Mount Beauty, the nearest places for kids by road are Pebble Beach in Tawonga South (5 min), Falls Creek Museum in Falls Creek (33 min), Bright River Pool in Bright (30 min), Bright Art Gallery in Bright (30 min), Bright Splash Park in Bright (31 min) and Sun Cinema Bright in Bright (31 min).
