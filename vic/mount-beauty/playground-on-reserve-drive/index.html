Playground on Reserve Drive

Playground in Mount Beauty · Basic info

This is a public playground in Mount Beauty. The Trappers Gap: Mitta Mitta to Mount Beauty is about 100 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 100 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 50 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 2 minutes from Mount Beauty.
