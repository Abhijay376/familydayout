Hydro Park

Park in Mount Beauty · Basic info

Hydro Park is a park in Mount Beauty, in the Alpine Shire Council area, good for all ages. Falls Creek Coaches/233 Kiewa Valley Hwy (coach) is a 12-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 237 m away with a baby change table.
