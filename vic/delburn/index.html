Delburn is in the Latrobe area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Delburn, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Delburn has no train station; Trafalgar Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Delburn, so most trips start by car. By car from Delburn, Traralgon is about 29 minutes away, Leongatha about 31 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Delburn is Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar, about 22 minutes' drive. For a library, Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North is about 9 minutes' drive from Delburn. For a skate park, Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North in Mirboo North is about 9 minutes' drive from Delburn. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 20 minutes' drive from Delburn. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 39 minutes' drive from Delburn. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Delburn, about 29 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. From Delburn, the nearest places for kids by road are Railway Trolley Shed in Yinnar (13 min), ARC Yinnar in Yinnar (13 min), the playground at Yinnar Centenary Garden in Yinnar (14 min), Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North in Mirboo North (9 min), the playground at Baromi Park in Mirboo North (9 min) and Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North (9 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Delburn.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Delburn, Mirboo North has 1 playground, a library and a skate park. Next door to Delburn, Yinnar has 3 playgrounds. Boolarra, next to Delburn, has 1 playground and a skate park. Narracan, Driffield and Darlimurla also border Delburn.
