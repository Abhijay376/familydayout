Beazleys Bridge in the Northern Grampians area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Beazleys Bridge is Tottington Bushland Reserve, covering about 57 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Beazleys Bridge. The closest to Beazleys Bridge is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 67 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Beazleys Bridge, so most trips start by car. By car from Beazleys Bridge, Stawell is about 51 minutes away and Maryborough about 73 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Beazleys Bridge is St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud, about 12 minutes' drive. Stawell is the closest bigger centre to Beazleys Bridge, about 51 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds and a pool. From Beazleys Bridge, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Queen Mary Botanic Gardens in St Arnaud (11 min), Market Square Park Playground in St Arnaud (11 min) and St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (12 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Beazleys Bridge.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Beazleys Bridge, Stuart Mill has 1 playground. Gre Gre and Carapooee West also border Beazleys Bridge.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Beazleys Bridge, without traffic.
