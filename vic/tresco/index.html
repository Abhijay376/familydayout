Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Tresco in the Swan Hill area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Tresco has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tresco, so most trips start by car. By car from Tresco, Swan Hill is about 20 minutes away and Echuca about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill is about 19 minutes' drive from Tresco. The closest library to Tresco is Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill, about 20 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Riverside Park in Swan Hill is about 19 minutes' drive from Tresco. The closest cinema to Tresco is Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill, about 19 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Swan Hill, about 20 minutes from Tresco by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Tresco are the playground at Gray Park in Lake Boga (6 min), Lake Boga Flying Boat Museum in Lake Boga (7 min), the playground at Guastalegname Park in Swan Hill (17 min), Swan Hill Regional Art Gallery in Swan Hill (18 min), the skate park at Riverside Park in Swan Hill (19 min) and Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill (19 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tresco.
