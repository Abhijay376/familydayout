Wharparilla is in the Campaspe area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb. On top of that, Wharparilla has a pool.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Wharparilla is Wharparilla Bushland Reserve, covering about 10 hectares. Places to visit in Wharparilla include Aqua Island.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wharparilla. The closest to Wharparilla is Echuca Railway Station (V/Line), about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wharparilla, so most trips start by car. By car from Wharparilla, Echuca is about 13 minutes away and Shepparton about 75 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca is about 12 minutes' drive from Wharparilla. The closest skate park to Wharparilla is Moama Echuca Community Skate Park in Echuca, about 14 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca is about 12 minutes' drive from Wharparilla. Echuca is the closest bigger centre to Wharparilla, about 13 minutes away by car, and has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Wharparilla are Holden Museum in Echuca (10 min), the playground at Echuca Lions Park in Echuca (11 min), Hopwood Gardens Playground in Echuca (11 min), Port of Echuca Discovery Centre in Echuca (11 min), Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca (12 min) and Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca (12 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wharparilla.

Nearby suburbs

Echuca, next to Wharparilla, has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. Bamawm Extension also borders Wharparilla.
