Weering in the Colac Otway area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Lake Weering Lake Reserve is the largest mapped park in Weering, at about 426 hectares. Cressy Lake Reserve and Weering Lake Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Weering. The closest to Weering is Birregurra Railway Station (V/Line), about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Weering, so most trips start by car. By car from Weering, Colac is about 37 minutes away, Geelong about 66 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rokewood Lagoon in Rokewood is about 44 minutes' drive from Weering. The closest library to Weering is Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac, about 38 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Rokewood Skate Park in Rokewood is about 44 minutes' drive from Weering. For a cinema, Colac Cinemas in Colac is about 38 minutes' drive from Weering. The closest indoor play centre to Weering is Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac, about 37 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Colac, about 37 minutes from Weering by car, with 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. The closest places for kids by road from Weering are the playground at Cressy Picnic Area in Cressy (20 min), the playground at Red Rock Scenic Reserve in Alvie (41 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (37 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (38 min), Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (38 min) and Rokewood Skate Park in Rokewood (44 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Weering.

Nearby suburbs

Cressy, next to Weering, has 1 playground. Cundare North, Cundare, Beeac and Barunah Plains also border Weering.

Getting there
