Prairie is in the Loddon area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Hayanmi Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Prairie, at about 37 hectares. Prairie Nature Conservation Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

Prairie has no train station; Dingee Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 8.1 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Prairie, so most trips start by car. By car from Prairie, Bendigo is about 49 minutes away, Echuca about 49 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Prairie is Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo, about 8 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 49 minutes from Prairie by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Prairie are the playground at R J Phelan Reserve in Mitiamo (7 min), East Loddon Historical Society in Mitiamo (8 min), Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo (8 min), Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood (23 min), Pyramid Hill & Districts Historical Museum in Pyramid Hill (28 min) and the playground at Apex Park in Rochester (37 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Prairie.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Prairie, Mitiamo has 1 playground and a pool. Calivil also borders Prairie.
