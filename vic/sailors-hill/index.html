Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Sailors Hill in the Hepburn area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Goldfields Track and Three Lost Children Walk cross Sailors Hill.

Getting around

There's no train station in Sailors Hill. The closest to Sailors Hill is Creswick Railway Station (V/Line), about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Sailors Hill, so most trips start by car. By car from Sailors Hill, Daylesford is about 5 minutes away, Castlemaine about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford is about 4 minutes' drive from Sailors Hill. The closest library to Sailors Hill is Daylesford Library in Daylesford, about 4 minutes' drive. For a beach, Canoeing in Tylden is about 42 minutes' drive from Sailors Hill. The closest skate park to Sailors Hill is Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick, about 23 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central is about 38 minutes' drive from Sailors Hill. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 39 minutes' drive from Sailors Hill. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Sailors Hill is Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek, about 31 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Daylesford, about 5 minutes from Sailors Hill by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Sailors Hill, the nearest places for kids by road are Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford (4 min), Daylesford Library in Daylesford (4 min), Daylesford Spa Country Railway in Daylesford (7 min), the playground at Hepburn Regional Park in Daylesford (11 min), the playground at Korweinguboora Recreation Reserve in Spargo Creek (19 min) and Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick (23 min).

Best for
