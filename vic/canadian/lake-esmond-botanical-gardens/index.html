Lake Esmond Botanical Gardens

Park in Canadian · Official details

Lake Esmond Botanical Gardens is a park in Canadian, in the Ballarat City Council area, good for all ages. Clayton St/Joseph St (bus) is a 7-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 118 m away.
