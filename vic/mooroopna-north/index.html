Mooroopna North is in the Greater Shepparton area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Mooroopna North, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

The Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks passes through Mooroopna North.

Getting around

There's no train station in Mooroopna North. The closest to Mooroopna North is Mooroopna Railway Station (V/Line), about 9.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mooroopna North, so most trips start by car. By car from Mooroopna North, Shepparton is about 15 minutes away, Echuca about 48 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Aquamoves in Shepparton is about 16 minutes' drive from Mooroopna North. For a library, Mooroopna Library in Mooroopna is about 9 minutes' drive from Mooroopna North. For a beach, Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton is about 17 minutes' drive from Mooroopna North. For a skate park, the skate park at Skate Park in Shepparton is about 16 minutes' drive from Mooroopna North. The closest cinema to Mooroopna North is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Mooroopna North is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 15 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kidstown in Mooroopna is about 13 minutes' drive from Mooroopna North. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Mooroopna North, about 15 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Mooroopna North, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Craigmuir Lake Park in Mooroopna (6 min), the playground at Arrowsmith Crescent Reserve in Mooroopna (7 min), Mooroopna Library in Mooroopna (9 min), Kidstown in Mooroopna (13 min), Shepparton Heritage Centre in Shepparton (14 min) and Shepparton Library in Shepparton (15 min).
