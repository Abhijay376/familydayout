Kirrip Park

Park in South Melbourne · Basic info

Kirrip Park is a park in South Melbourne, in the Port Phillip City Council area, good for all ages. City Rd/Light Rail #126 (tram) is a 3-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 628 m away with a baby change table and dogs are allowed on a lead.
