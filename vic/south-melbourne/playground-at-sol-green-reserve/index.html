Playground at Sol Green Reserve

Playground in South Melbourne · Basic info

This is a public playground in South Melbourne. For food, The Bengal Tiger is about 110 m away, and Playground at Lyell Iffla Reserve is another option about 330 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 550 m from the playground, and bus 234 stops at Montague St/City Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
