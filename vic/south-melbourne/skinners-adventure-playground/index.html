Skinners Adventure Playground

Playground in South Melbourne · Basic info

Skinners Adventure Playground in South Melbourne is a public playground in the Port Phillip City area. The Moray Street is about 60 m away, and Emerald Hill Library is about 280 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 360 m from the playground, and tram 1 stops at Moray St/Park St #23, a 3-minute walk away.
