Park Towers Reserve

Playground in South Melbourne · Basic info

Park Towers Reserve is a public playground in South Melbourne, in the Port Phillip City area. The Cecil Street passes about 40 m away, and Emerald Hill Library is about 200 m away. There's a public toilet about 170 m away, and tram 1 stops at Cecil St/Park St #25, a 2-minute walk away.
