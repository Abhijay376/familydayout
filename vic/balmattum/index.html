Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Balmattum, in the Strathbogie area.

Landmarks

Balmattum Flora Reserve is the largest mapped park in Balmattum, at about 12 hectares. Balmattum Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Balmattum.

Getting around

Balmattum has no train station; Euroa Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 8.2 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Balmattum, so most trips start by car. By car from Balmattum, Benalla is about 39 minutes away, Seymour about 58 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Euroa Swimming Pool in Euroa is about 20 minutes' drive from Balmattum. For a library, Euroa Library in Euroa is about 20 minutes' drive from Balmattum. The closest skate park to Balmattum is Euroa Skate Park in Euroa, about 19 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa is about 19 minutes' drive from Balmattum. Benalla is the closest bigger centre to Balmattum, about 39 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Balmattum are Moglonemby Hall Playground in Moglonemby (32 min), Violet Town Recreation Reserve East Playground in Violet Town (18 min), Farmers Arms Hotel Museum in Euroa (19 min), Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa (19 min), Euroa Skate Park in Euroa (19 min) and Euroa Library in Euroa (20 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Balmattum.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Balmattum, Euroa has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Violet Town, next to Balmattum, has 3 playgrounds. Marraweeney, Kithbrook and Boho also border Balmattum.
