Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Swanwater West, in the Northern Grampians area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Swanwater West is Rich Avon East Bushland Reserve, covering about 59 hectares.

Getting around

Swanwater West has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 86 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Swanwater West, so most trips start by car. By car from Swanwater West, Stawell is about 65 minutes away and Horsham about 77 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Donald Swimming Pool in Donald is about 16 minutes' drive from Swanwater West. The closest skate park to Swanwater West is Donald BMX Track in Donald, about 19 minutes' drive. From Swanwater West, the nearest places for kids by road are Guthrie Park Playground in Rich Avon West (21 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (16 min), the playground at Donald Public Park in Donald (16 min), Donald BMX Track in Donald (19 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (27 min) and Woods' Farming and Heritage Museum in Rupanyup (42 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Swanwater West.

Nearby suburbs

Avon Plains also borders Swanwater West.
