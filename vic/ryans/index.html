Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Ryans in the East Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Ryans has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 34 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Ryans, so most trips start by car. By car from Ryans, Bairnsdale is about 81 minutes away and Lakes Entrance about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

From Ryans, the nearest places for kids by road are Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale (80 min), Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale (80 min), Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale (80 min), Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale (82 min), Krowathunkoolong Keeping Place in Bairnsdale (82 min) and All Abilities Playground in Bairnsdale (82 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Ryans.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Ryans, Dargo has 1 playground. Deptford, Cobbannah and Bullumwaal also border Ryans.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Ryans, without traffic.
