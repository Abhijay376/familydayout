Warne in the Buloke area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Torneys Tank Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Warne, at about 31 hectares.

Getting around

Warne has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 63 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Warne, so most trips start by car. By car from Warne, Swan Hill is about 59 minutes away and Horsham about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Warne is Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock, about 16 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Birchip Skate Park in Birchip is about 34 minutes' drive from Warne. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Warne, about 59 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Warne, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Culgoa Memorial Park in Culgoa (7 min), the playground at Hall Corner Park in Nullawil (9 min), Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock (16 min), Birchip Skate Park in Birchip (34 min), Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof (27 min) and Birchip History Museum in Birchip (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Warne.

Nearby suburbs

Nullawil, next to Warne, has 1 playground. Next door to Warne, Culgoa has 1 playground. Jil Jil and Cokum also border Warne.
