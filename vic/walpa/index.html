Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Walpa in the East Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Walpa. The closest to Walpa is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 17 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Walpa, so most trips start by car. By car from Walpa, Bairnsdale is about 20 minutes away and Sale about 46 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Walpa is Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale, about 19 minutes' drive. For a library, Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale is about 21 minutes' drive from Walpa. The closest beach to Walpa is Boyfriend Beach in Forge Creek, about 35 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Gilsenan Reserve in Paynesville is about 35 minutes' drive from Walpa. For a cinema, Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale is about 21 minutes' drive from Walpa. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow is about 27 minutes' drive from Walpa. The nearest larger centre is Bairnsdale, about 20 minutes from Walpa by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Walpa, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Amy Shand Park in Lindenow (2 min), Bairnsdale Aquatic & Recreation Centre in Bairnsdale (19 min), Sun Cinema Bairnsdale in Bairnsdale (21 min), Bairnsdale swimming pool in Bairnsdale (21 min), Bairnsdale Library in Bairnsdale (21 min) and Krowathunkoolong Keeping Place in Bairnsdale (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Walpa.

Nearby suburbs

Lindenow, next to Walpa, has 1 playground. Iguana Creek, Glenaladale and Fernbank also border Walpa.
