Carisbrook is in the Central Goldfields area, and our data lists 1 playground and 6 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Carisbrook Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Carisbrook, at about 19 hectares. Carisbrook Streamside Reserve and Carisbrook Recreation Complex are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Carisbrook include Bland Reserve, Market Reserve and C.L Bucknall Park. Playgrounds around Carisbrook include the playground at Market Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Carisbrook. For walks and rides, Castlemaine-Maryborough Rail Trail (Under Construction), Cycle Central Goldfields and Carrisbrook Settlers Trail cross Carisbrook.

Getting around

Carisbrook has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 6.3 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Maryborough - Bendigo route stop in Carisbrook. V/Line coaches stop in Carisbrook. By car from Carisbrook, Maryborough is about 7 minutes away, Castlemaine about 38 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Carisbrook is Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot, about 24 minutes' drive. For a library, Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough is about 8 minutes' drive from Carisbrook. The closest skate park to Carisbrook is Bike Jumps in Castlemaine, about 38 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 7 minutes from Carisbrook by car, with a library. The closest places for kids by road from Carisbrook are Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (8 min), Talbot Pioneers Memorial Playground in Talbot (24 min), Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (24 min), Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (30 min), Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (30 min) and Lee Medlyn Bottle Museum in Clunes (33 min).

Best for

Free days on the Castlemaine-Maryborough Rail Trail (Under Construction) and in Carisbrook's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Maryborough, next to Carisbrook, has a library. Havelock, Flagstaff, Eddington and Craigie also border Carisbrook.
