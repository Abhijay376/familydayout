C.L. Bucknall Park

Park in Carisbrook · Basic info

C.L. Bucknall Park is a park in Carisbrook, in the Central Goldfields Shire Council area, good for all ages. Carisbrook General Store/Green St (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 293 m away.
