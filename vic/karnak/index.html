Our data lists 6 parks and reserves in Karnak, in the West Wimmera area.

Landmarks

Lake Ratzcastle Lake Reserve is the largest mapped park in Karnak, at about 412 hectares. Lake Karnak Wildlife Reserve (hunting) and Sheepwash, Charam Wildlife Reserve (hunting) are the next largest. Karnak also has Maryvale and Charam Swamp Lake Reserve, Lake Koynock Wildlife Reserve (hunting) and Woolshed Swamp, Karnak Wildlife Reserve (no hunting). Our data shows 1 public toilet in Karnak.

Getting around

There's no train station in Karnak. The closest to Karnak is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 138 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Karnak, so most trips start by car. By car from Karnak, Horsham is about 66 minutes away and Stawell about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Karnak is Goroke Swimming Centre in Goroke, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest library to Karnak is West Wimmera Shire Goroke Library in Goroke, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Karnak is the skate park at Natimuk Soldiers Park Reserve in Natimuk, about 44 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Karnak are West Wimmera Shire Goroke Library in Goroke (29 min), Goroke Little Desert Nature Playspace in Goroke (29 min), Goroke Swimming Centre in Goroke (29 min), West Wimmera Community Association - Red Tail Gallery in Edenhope (31 min), Dachshund Museum & Gallery in Edenhope (31 min) and the playground at Lions Park in Edenhope (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Karnak.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Karnak, Goroke has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Gymbowen and Charam also border Karnak.
