Playground at Kelvin Close Reserve

Playground in Niddrie · Basic info

Playground at Kelvin Close Reserve is a public playground in Niddrie, in the Moonee Valley City area. The Steele Creek Trail is about 50 m away, and Paul Sadler's Swimland Essendon is about 360 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 1.3 km from the playground, and bus 476 stops at Rosehill Sec Col/Ida St, a 5-minute walk away.
