Grosvenor Street Reserve

Park in Niddrie · Basic info

Grosvenor Street Reserve is a park in Niddrie, in the Moonee Valley City Council area, good for all ages. Florence St/Hotham Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 883 m away with a baby change table.
