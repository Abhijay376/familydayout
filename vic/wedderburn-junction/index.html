Wedderburn Junction is in the Loddon area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Wedderburn Junction rail line Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Wedderburn Junction, at about 38 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wedderburn Junction. The closest to Wedderburn Junction is Raywood Railway Station (V/Line), about 48 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wedderburn Junction, so most trips start by car. By car from Wedderburn Junction, Bendigo is about 64 minutes away, Castlemaine about 80 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Wedderburn Junction is Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Wedderburn Junction is Rex Theatre in Charlton, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Wedderburn Junction are Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (10 min), Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (10 min), Rex Theatre in Charlton (35 min), Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (35 min), the playground at Rotary Park in Charlton (36 min) and the playground at Gordon Park in Charlton (36 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wedderburn Junction.

Nearby suburbs

Wedderburn, next to Wedderburn Junction, has 1 playground and a pool. Fiery Flat also borders Wedderburn Junction.
