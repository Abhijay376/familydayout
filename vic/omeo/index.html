Our data lists 2 playgrounds and 11 parks and reserves in Omeo, in the East Gippsland area. Beyond the parks, Omeo has 3 museums or galleries and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Omeo is Cobungra State Forest, covering about 21,541 hectares. Marble Gully - Mount Tambo Nature Conservation Reserve and Mount Sam State Forest are the next largest. Omeo also has Memorial Park, Omeo Bushland Reserve and Livingstone Creek Reserve. Playgrounds in Omeo include the playground at Omeo Historical Park & Museum. Our data shows 8 public toilets in Omeo. For camping, there's Livingstone Creek - Omeo Camping Area 13 (CLWF) and Livingstone Creek - Omeo Camping Area 7 (CLWF). Places to visit in Omeo include Old Omeo Courthouse and Omeo Historical Park & Museum. For older kids in Omeo there's the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage. The Flomeo, Saddle Up and Billygoat all pass through Omeo. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Omeo for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Omeo has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 81 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the 14 and Omeo - Bright via Hotham Heights routes stop in Omeo. By car from Omeo, Bright is about 2 hours away and Bairnsdale about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek is about 21 minutes' drive from Omeo. From Omeo, the nearest places for kids by road are Swifts Creek Memorial Pool in Swifts Creek (21 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the museum or gallery in Omeo. Teens who skate or ride, with the pump track at Livingstone Creek Water Frontage in Omeo. Free days on the Flomeo and in Omeo's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Swifts Creek, next to Omeo, has 1 playground and a pool. Dargo, next to Omeo, has 1 playground. Omeo Valley, Hinnomunjie, Glen Valley and Cobungra also border Omeo.
