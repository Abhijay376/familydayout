Nariel Valley in the Towong area has 16 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Nariel Valley is Wabba Wilderness Park, covering about 19,396 hectares. Log Bridge Ck - East Branch Natural Catchment Area and Mount Gibbo Natural Features and Scenic Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Nariel Valley include Gray's, Ohagens and Ski Hut. Our data shows 7 public toilets in Nariel Valley. Picnic areas in Nariel Valley include Dunstans Lookout, Ohagens and Thowgla Falls. For camping, there's Dart River and Gray's. The Australian Alps Walking Track and Thowgla Falls Walking Track all pass through Nariel Valley.

Getting around

Nariel Valley has no train station; Albury Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 90 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nariel Valley, so most trips start by car. By car from Nariel Valley, Wodonga is about 2 hours away and Wangaratta about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Nariel Valley is Corryong Public swimming pool in Corryong, about 34 minutes' drive. For a library, Corryong Elyne Mitchell Library in Corryong is about 34 minutes' drive from Nariel Valley. For a cinema, Corryong Cinema in Corryong is about 33 minutes' drive from Nariel Valley. The closest places for kids by road from Nariel Valley are Corryong Cinema in Corryong (33 min), Corryong Elyne Mitchell Library in Corryong (34 min) and Corryong Public swimming pool in Corryong (34 min).

Best for

Free days on the Australian Alps Walking Track and in Nariel Valley's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Cudgewa, Colac Colac, Cobberas and Biggara also border Nariel Valley.
