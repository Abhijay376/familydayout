Our data lists 5 parks and reserves in Percydale, in the Pyrenees area.

Landmarks

Percydale Historic & Cultural Features Res. is the largest mapped park in Percydale, at about 1,476 hectares. Percydale Bushland Reserve and Yehrip Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Percydale include Waterfall Picnic Area and Frasers Trail Bike Visitor Area. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Percydale. Picnic areas in Percydale include Waterfall Picnic Area. The Pyrenees Endurance Walk and Valley Walk all pass through Percydale.

Getting around

Percydale has no train station; Talbot Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Percydale, so most trips start by car. By car from Percydale, Maryborough is about 37 minutes away, Ararat about 64 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Percydale is Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot, about 41 minutes' drive. For a library, Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough is about 37 minutes' drive from Percydale. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 37 minutes from Percydale by car, with a library. From Percydale, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Avoca Lions Park in Avoca (15 min), Courthouse in Avoca (15 min), the playground at Toll Bar Park in Lexton (35 min), Talbot Outdoor Pool in Talbot (41 min), Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough (37 min) and Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (43 min).

Best for

Free days on the Pyrenees Endurance Walk and in Percydale's parks.
