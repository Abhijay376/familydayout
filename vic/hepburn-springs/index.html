Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Hepburn Springs in the Hepburn area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Goldfields Track and Dry Diggings Track cross Hepburn Springs.

Getting around

Hepburn Springs has no train station; Castlemaine Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Daylesford - Hepburn Springs, Ballan - Hepburn via Daylesford and Hepburn - Creswick via Daylesford routes stop in Hepburn Springs. By car from Hepburn Springs, Daylesford is about 5 minutes away, Castlemaine about 31 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford is about 4 minutes' drive from Hepburn Springs. For a library, Daylesford Library in Daylesford is about 4 minutes' drive from Hepburn Springs. The closest beach to Hepburn Springs is Canoeing in Tylden, about 41 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Hepburn Springs is Creswick Skate and Splash Park in Creswick, about 28 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central is about 43 minutes' drive from Hepburn Springs. The closest indoor play centre to Hepburn Springs is Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point, about 44 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek is about 36 minutes' drive from Hepburn Springs. The nearest larger centre is Daylesford, about 5 minutes from Hepburn Springs by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Hepburn Springs, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Hepburn Regional Park in Daylesford (3 min), Daylesford Library in Daylesford (4 min), Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford (4 min), Daylesford Spa Country Railway in Daylesford (6 min), the playground at J P Gleeson Memorial Park in Lyonville (18 min) and Little Gallery in Trentham (27 min).

Best for
