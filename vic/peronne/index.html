Our data lists 6 parks and reserves in Peronne, in the West Wimmera area.

Landmarks

Little Desert National Park is the largest mapped park in Peronne, at about 131,629 hectares. Lemon Springs Bushland Reserve and Mortat I35 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Peronne include Morea I36 Bushland Reserve, Peronne Recreation Reserve and Dopewora I6 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Peronne has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 153 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Peronne, so most trips start by car. By car from Peronne, Horsham is about 65 minutes away and Stawell about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Peronne is Goroke Swimming Centre in Goroke, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest library to Peronne is West Wimmera Shire Goroke Library in Goroke, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Peronne are Goroke Swimming Centre in Goroke (8 min), Goroke Little Desert Nature Playspace in Goroke (9 min), West Wimmera Shire Goroke Library in Goroke (9 min), West Wimmera Community Association - Red Tail Gallery in Edenhope (31 min), Dachshund Museum & Gallery in Edenhope (31 min) and the playground at Lions Park in Edenhope (31 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Peronne.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Peronne, Goroke has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Next door to Peronne, Kaniva has 1 playground and a library. Little Desert and Lawloit also border Peronne.
