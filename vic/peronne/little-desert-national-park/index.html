Little Desert National Park

Park in Peronne · Official details

Little Desert National Park is a park in Peronne, in the West Wimmera Shire Council area, good for all ages.

Details

Seasons
Best between late winter and early summer when temperatures are comfortable and wildflowers bloom (Parks Victoria)

Where is Little Desert National Park?

Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
