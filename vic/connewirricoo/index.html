Connewirricoo is in the West Wimmera area, and our data lists 3 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Connewirricoo is Connewirrecoo I33 Bushland Reserve, covering about 90 hectares. Connewirrecoo I32 Bushland Reserve and Mooree Streamside Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Connewirricoo. The closest to Connewirricoo is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 132 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Connewirricoo, so most trips start by car. By car from Connewirricoo, Hamilton is about 72 minutes away and Horsham about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral is about 37 minutes' drive from Connewirricoo. The closest places for kids by road from Connewirricoo are Bake House Studio in Harrow (13 min), Harrow Discovery Centre in Harrow (14 min), the playground at Lions Park in Edenhope (24 min), Henley Park Playground in Edenhope (25 min) and Balmoral Swimming Pool in Balmoral (37 min).
