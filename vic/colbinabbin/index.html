Colbinabbin in the Campaspe area has 1 playground and 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Colbinabbin is Runnymede Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 249 hectares. Wanalta Bushland Reserve and Colbinabbin Recreation Reserve are the next largest. Colbinabbin also has Wanalta Weir Historic and Cultural Features Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Colbinabbin.

Getting around

There's no train station in Colbinabbin. The closest to Colbinabbin is Elmore Railway Station (V/Line), about 20 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Bendigo Station - Shepparton Station via Kyabram route stop in Colbinabbin. V/Line coaches stop in Colbinabbin. By car from Colbinabbin, Echuca is about 50 minutes away, Bendigo about 57 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Colbinabbin is Rushworth Pool in Rushworth, about 19 minutes' drive. For a library, Rushworth Library in Rushworth is about 19 minutes' drive from Colbinabbin. For a skate park, the skate park at Lions Park in Rushworth is about 19 minutes' drive from Colbinabbin. The nearest larger centre is Echuca, about 50 minutes from Colbinabbin by car, with 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Colbinabbin, the nearest places for kids by road are Rushworth Museum in Rushworth (18 min), Rushworth Library in Rushworth (19 min), Rushworth Pool in Rushworth (19 min), the skate park at Lions Park in Rushworth (19 min), Goornong Swimming Pool in Goornong (30 min) and Campaspe Run Rural Discovery Centre in Elmore (23 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Colbinabbin.

Nearby suburbs

Rushworth, next to Colbinabbin, has a pool, a library and a skate park. Gobarup, Creek View, Cornella and Burramboot also border Colbinabbin.
