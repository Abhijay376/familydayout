Playground on Meade Lane

Playground in Colbinabbin · Basic info

This is a public playground in Colbinabbin. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus Bendigo Station - Shepparton Station via Kyabram stops at Colbinabbin General Store/Mitchell St, a 1-minute walk away.
