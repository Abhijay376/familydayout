Landmarks

Fiery Flat Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Fiery Flat, at about 10 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Fiery Flat. The closest to Fiery Flat is Raywood Railway Station (V/Line), about 38 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Fiery Flat, so most trips start by car. By car from Fiery Flat, Bendigo is about 70 minutes away, Castlemaine about 86 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn is about 36 minutes' drive from Fiery Flat. From Fiery Flat, the nearest places for kids by road are Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (36 min), Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (36 min), A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park in Boort (54 min), Boort Court House Historical Museum in Boort (54 min) and Boort Swimming Pool in Boort (55 min).
