Great Otway National Park

Park in Wye River · Official details

Great Otway National Park is a park in Wye River, in the Colac Otway Shire Council area, good for all ages. Dogs are not allowed.

not allowed (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Great Otway National Park?
Great Otway National Park is in Wye River, Victoria.
Is Great Otway National Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Great Otway National Park?
No, dogs are not allowed.
