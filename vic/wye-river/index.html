Wye River is in the Colac Otway area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Wye River is Great Otway National Park, covering about 110,433 hectares. Wye River Coastal Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Wye River. The River Walk passes through Wye River.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wye River. The closest to Wye River is Birregurra Railway Station (V/Line), about 35 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Wye River. By car from Wye River, Torquay is about 70 minutes away, Colac about 72 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Lorne Sea Baths in Lorne is about 19 minutes' drive from Wye River. The closest beach to Wye River is Godfrey Creek in Separation Creek, about 5 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Kay Langan Skate Park in Lorne is about 19 minutes' drive from Wye River. For a cinema, Lorne Theatre in Lorne is about 19 minutes' drive from Wye River. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Wye River is Live Wire Park in Lorne, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Wye River are Godfrey Creek in Separation Creek (5 min), Grey River in Grey River (10 min), the playground at Lorne - Queenscliff Coastal Reserve in Lorne (19 min), Kay Langan Skate Park in Lorne (19 min), Lorne Theatre in Lorne (19 min) and Lorne Sea Baths in Lorne (19 min).

Best for

Free days on the River Walk and in Wye River's parks.
