J.J. Holland Park

Park in Kensington · Official details

J.J. Holland Park is a park in Kensington, in the Melbourne City Council area, good for all ages. Devon St/Kensington Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 205 m away.
