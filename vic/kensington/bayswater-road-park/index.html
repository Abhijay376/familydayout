Bayswater Road Park

Park in Kensington · Official details

Bayswater Road Park is a park in Kensington, in the Melbourne City Council area, good for all ages. Altona St/Kensington Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 452 m away.
