Warun Biik Playground

Playground in Kensington · Official details

Warun Biik Playground in Kensington is a public playground in the Melbourne City area. For food, Fifty-Six Threads is about 140 m away, and Playground on Derby Street is another option about 160 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 330 m from the playground, and bus 402 stops at Derby St/Kensington Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
