Carapook also has Carapook Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 1 off-leash dog park in Carapook.

Getting around

There's no train station in Carapook. The closest to Carapook is Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line), about 124 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Carapook, so most trips start by car. By car from Carapook, Hamilton is about 43 minutes away and Portland about 82 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Casterton Pool in Casterton is about 11 minutes' drive from Carapook. The closest skate park to Carapook is coleraine skate park in Coleraine, about 16 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Hamilton, about 43 minutes from Carapook by car, with 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Carapook, the nearest places for kids by road are Gorman's art gallery in Casterton (11 min), Casterton Pool in Casterton (11 min), coleraine skate park in Coleraine (16 min), the playground at Queens Park in Coleraine (16 min), Silvester Oval Playground in Coleraine (16 min) and Merino Swimming Pool in Merino (23 min).

Nearby suburbs

Casterton, next to Carapook, has a pool. Coleraine, next to Carapook, has 4 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Carapook, Wando Vale has 1 playground. Muntham and Konongwootong also border Carapook.
