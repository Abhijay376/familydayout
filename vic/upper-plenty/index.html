Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Upper Plenty in the Mitchell area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

There's no train station in Upper Plenty. The closest to Upper Plenty is Wallan Railway Station (V/Line), about 4.7 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Upper Plenty, so most trips start by car. By car from Upper Plenty, Broadmeadows is about 38 minutes away, Seymour about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 52 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Whittlesea Swimming Centre in Whittlesea is about 14 minutes' drive from Upper Plenty. For a library, Wallan Library in Wallan is about 8 minutes' drive from Upper Plenty. The closest skate park to Upper Plenty is BMX Track in Wallan, about 6 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Upper Plenty is Reading Epping Cinema in Epping, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Upper Plenty is Timezone in Epping, about 26 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Upper Plenty is Funfields in Whittlesea, about 15 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Broadmeadows, about 38 minutes from Upper Plenty by car, with 12 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Upper Plenty, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Merri Creek Park in Wallan (7 min), BMX Track in Wallan (6 min), Wallan Park Skate Park in Wallan (7 min), the playground at Hadfield Park in Wallan (7 min), Wallan Library in Wallan (8 min) and Whittlesea Swimming Centre in Whittlesea (14 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Upper Plenty.

Nearby suburbs

Wallan, next to Upper Plenty, has 13 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Whittlesea, next to Upper Plenty, has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a zoo or wildlife park. Next door to Upper Plenty, Beveridge has 4 playgrounds and a skate park. Eden Park and Clonbinane also border Upper Plenty.
