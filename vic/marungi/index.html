Marungi is in the Moira area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Marungi, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Marungi has no train station; Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Marungi, so most trips start by car. By car from Marungi, Shepparton is about 24 minutes away, Benalla about 65 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah is about 26 minutes' drive from Marungi. The closest library to Marungi is Numurkah Library in Numurkah, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Marungi is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 27 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Marungi is Riverland Skate Park in Numurkah, about 26 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 24 minutes' drive from Marungi. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 24 minutes' drive from Marungi. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Marungi is Kidstown in Mooroopna, about 27 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 24 minutes from Marungi by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Marungi, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Katandra Rec Res Youth Spot in Katandra West (11 min), the playground at Katandra West Recreation Reserve in Katandra West (12 min), Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah (26 min), Riverland Skate Park in Numurkah (26 min), Numurkah Library in Numurkah (27 min) and Shepparton Velodrome in Shepparton (22 min).
