Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Corack, in the Buloke area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Corack is Corack East I139 Bushland Reserve, covering about 130 hectares. Corack East I140 Bushland Reserve and Corack East Wildlife Reserve (no hunting) are the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Corack. The closest to Corack is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 106 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Corack, so most trips start by car. By car from Corack, Stawell is about 86 minutes away and Horsham about 89 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem is about 31 minutes' drive from Corack. The closest skate park to Corack is Donald BMX Track in Donald, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Corack is Rex Theatre in Charlton, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Corack are Donald BMX Track in Donald (17 min), Watchem Swimming Pool in Watchem (31 min), the playground at Donald Public Park in Donald (18 min), Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (18 min), the playground at Lake Watchem Lake Reserve in Watchem (33 min) and Willandra Museum in Wycheproof (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Corack.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Corack, Donald has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Corack, Watchem has 1 playground and a pool. Morton Plains, Jeffcott North, Corack East and Chirrip also border Corack.
