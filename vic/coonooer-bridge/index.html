Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Coonooer Bridge, in the Buloke area.

Landmarks

Gowar I56 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Coonooer Bridge, at about 10 hectares. Avoca River K15 Streamside Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Coonooer Bridge. The closest to Coonooer Bridge is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 74 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Coonooer Bridge, so most trips start by car. By car from Coonooer Bridge, Stawell is about 77 minutes away and Maryborough about 80 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud is about 15 minutes' drive from Coonooer Bridge. The closest skate park to Coonooer Bridge is Donald BMX Track in Donald, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Coonooer Bridge is Rex Theatre in Charlton, about 20 minutes' drive. From Coonooer Bridge, the nearest places for kids by road are Apex Park Playground in St Arnaud (14 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (15 min), Market Square Park Playground in St Arnaud (15 min), Rex Theatre in Charlton (20 min), Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (20 min) and Donald Swimming Pool in Donald (32 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Coonooer Bridge.
