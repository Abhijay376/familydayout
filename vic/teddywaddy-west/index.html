Teddywaddy West is in the Buloke area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Teddywaddy West is Teddywaddy I152 Bushland Reserve, covering about 3 hectares.

Getting around

Teddywaddy West has no train station; Kerang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 83 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Teddywaddy West, so most trips start by car. By car from Teddywaddy West, Swan Hill is about 1.5 hours away and Stawell about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof is about 15 minutes' drive from Teddywaddy West. The closest skate park to Teddywaddy West is Donald BMX Track in Donald, about 31 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 23 minutes' drive from Teddywaddy West. The closest places for kids by road from Teddywaddy West are Willandra Museum in Wycheproof (13 min), the playground at Centenary Park in Wycheproof (14 min), Wycheproof Swimming Pool in Wycheproof (15 min), the playground at Gordon Park in Charlton (23 min), Rex Theatre in Charlton (23 min) and Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (23 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Teddywaddy West.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Teddywaddy West, Charlton has 3 playgrounds and a cinema. Wycheproof, next to Teddywaddy West, has 1 playground and a pool. Teddywaddy and Chirrip also border Teddywaddy West.
