Emu in the Northern Grampians area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Summerhayes Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Emu, at about 255 hectares. Emu Station Bushland Reserve and Emu Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

Emu has no train station; Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 44 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Emu. By car from Emu, Maryborough is about 67 minutes away and Bendigo about 89 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Emu is St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Emu are Bi-Centennial Park Playground in St Arnaud (28 min), the playground at Queen Mary Botanic Gardens in St Arnaud (30 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (30 min), Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (43 min), Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (43 min) and Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (45 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Emu.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Emu, Bealiba has 1 playground. Cochranes Creek and Carapooee also border Emu.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Emu, without traffic.
