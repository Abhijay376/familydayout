Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Orrvale in the Greater Shepparton area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Orrvale has no train station; Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 4.3 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Orrvale, so most trips start by car. By car from Orrvale, Shepparton is about 8 minutes away, Benalla about 49 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Orrvale is Aquamoves in Shepparton, about 9 minutes' drive. For a library, Shepparton Library in Shepparton is about 9 minutes' drive from Orrvale. For a beach, Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton is about 11 minutes' drive from Orrvale. The closest skate park to Orrvale is the skate park at Skate Park in Shepparton, about 8 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 8 minutes' drive from Orrvale. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 8 minutes' drive from Orrvale. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Orrvale is Emerald Bank Leisure Land in Kialla, about 9 minutes' drive. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Orrvale, about 8 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Orrvale, the nearest places for kids by road are Windsor Park - Hillsborough Avenue Playground in Shepparton (3 min), the playground at WG Parker-Central Park in Shepparton East (4 min), Shepparton Art Museum in Shepparton (8 min), the skate park at Skate Park in Shepparton (8 min), Village Cinemas in Shepparton (8 min) and StarBowl in Shepparton (8 min).
