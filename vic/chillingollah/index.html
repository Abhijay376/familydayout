Chillingollah in the Swan Hill area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Chillingollah is Chillingollah Flora and Fauna Reserve, covering about 244 hectares. Chillingollah Bushland Reserve and Timboram West Tank Bushland Reserve are the next largest.

Getting around

Chillingollah has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 47 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Chillingollah, so most trips start by car. By car from Chillingollah, Swan Hill is about 42 minutes away and Mildura about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Chillingollah is Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang, about 24 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South is about 34 minutes' drive from Chillingollah. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Chillingollah, about 42 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Chillingollah, the nearest places for kids by road are Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang (24 min), Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South (34 min), the playground at Nyah Heritage Park in Nyah (39 min) and the playground at River Murray Reserve (non-PV) in Nyah (40 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Chillingollah.
