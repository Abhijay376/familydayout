Boolarra South is in the Latrobe area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Boolarra South, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Boolarra South has no train station; Morwell Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 25 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Boolarra South, so most trips start by car. By car from Boolarra South, Leongatha is about 39 minutes away, Traralgon about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Latrobe Leisure - Churchill in Churchill is about 29 minutes' drive from Boolarra South. The closest library to Boolarra South is Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Boolarra South is Boolarra Skate Park in Boolarra, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Boolarra South is Village 8 in Morwell, about 37 minutes' drive. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Boolarra South, about 39 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Boolarra South, the nearest places for kids by road are Boolarra Skate Park in Boolarra (10 min), Boolarra BMX track in Boolarra (10 min), the playground at Railway Park in Boolarra (10 min), Boolarra Old School Museum in Boolarra (10 min), the playground at Baromi Park in Mirboo North (16 min) and Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North (16 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Boolarra South.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Boolarra South, Boolarra has 1 playground and a skate park. Darlimurla and Budgeree also border Boolarra South.
