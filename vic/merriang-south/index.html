Merriang South is in the Alpine area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Merriang South, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Merriang South. The closest to Merriang South is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 42 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Merriang South, so most trips start by car. By car from Merriang South, Bright is about 36 minutes away and Wangaratta about 50 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Merriang South is the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford, about 9 minutes' drive. For a library, Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford is about 10 minutes' drive from Merriang South. The closest beach to Merriang South is Sandy Beach in Beechworth, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Merriang South is Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford, about 10 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Sun Cinema Bright in Bright is about 36 minutes' drive from Merriang South. The nearest larger centre is Bright, about 36 minutes from Merriang South by car, with 2 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Merriang South, the nearest places for kids by road are the water play at Jubilee Park in Myrtleford (9 min), Myrtleford Swimming Pool in Myrtleford (10 min), The Old School Museum in Myrtleford (9 min), Myrtleford Library in Myrtleford (10 min), Myrtleford Skate Park in Myrtleford (10 min) and Cundy Park Playground in Myrtleford (10 min).
