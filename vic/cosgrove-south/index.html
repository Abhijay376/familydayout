Our data lists 4 parks and reserves in Cosgrove South, in the Greater Shepparton area.

Landmarks

Currawa H57 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Cosgrove South, at about 14 hectares. Caniambo Bridge Streamside Reserve and Cosgrove South H56 Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Cosgrove South also has Cosgrove South H55 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cosgrove South. The closest to Cosgrove South is Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line), about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cosgrove South, so most trips start by car. By car from Cosgrove South, Shepparton is about 19 minutes away, Benalla about 31 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cosgrove South is Aquamoves in Shepparton, about 21 minutes' drive. For a library, Shepparton Library in Shepparton is about 21 minutes' drive from Cosgrove South. For a beach, Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton is about 23 minutes' drive from Cosgrove South. The closest skate park to Cosgrove South is the skate park at Skate Park in Shepparton, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Cosgrove South is Village Cinemas in Shepparton, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Cosgrove South is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 19 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kidstown in Mooroopna is about 23 minutes' drive from Cosgrove South. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 19 minutes from Cosgrove South by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Cosgrove South are the playground at O'Donnell Park in Shepparton East (12 min), the playground at CWA Gardens in Dookie (17 min), Village Cinemas in Shepparton (20 min), StarBowl in Shepparton (19 min), Shepparton Heritage Centre in Shepparton (20 min) and the skate park at Skate Park in Shepparton (20 min).

Best for
