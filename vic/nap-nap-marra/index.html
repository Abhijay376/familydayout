Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Nap Nap Marra, in the Wellington area.

Landmarks

Avon Wilderness Park is the largest mapped park in Nap Nap Marra, at about 39,577 hectares. Avon, Turton, and Dolodrook Rivers;; Ben Cruachan Ck Natural Catchment Area is the next largest. For walks and rides, Razorback Walking Track, Riggall Spur Track and Spion Kopje Walking Track cross Nap Nap Marra.

Getting around

Nap Nap Marra has no train station; Stratford Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 51 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Nap Nap Marra, so most trips start by car. By car from Nap Nap Marra, Sale is about 4.5 hours away and Traralgon about 4.5 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Free days on the Razorback Walking Track and in Nap Nap Marra's parks.

Moroka and Buragwonduc also border Nap Nap Marra.
