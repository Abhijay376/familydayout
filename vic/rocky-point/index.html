Rocky Point is in the Ararat area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Rocky Point, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Rocky Point has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 10 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Rocky Point, so most trips start by car. By car from Rocky Point, Ararat is about 20 minutes away, Stawell about 48 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat is about 20 minutes' drive from Rocky Point. For a library, Ararat Library in Ararat is about 20 minutes' drive from Rocky Point. For a beach, Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans is about 44 minutes' drive from Rocky Point. For a skate park, David Blackie Velodrome in Ararat is about 24 minutes' drive from Rocky Point. The closest cinema to Rocky Point is Astor Cinema in Ararat, about 20 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Halls Gap Zoo in Halls Gap is about 40 minutes' drive from Rocky Point. The nearest larger centre is Ararat, about 20 minutes from Rocky Point by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Rocky Point, the nearest places for kids by road are Ararat Gallery TAMA in Ararat (20 min), Ararat Library in Ararat (20 min), Astor Cinema in Ararat (20 min), Langi Morgala Museum in Ararat (20 min), Ararat Recreation and Aquatic Centre in Ararat (20 min) and Ararat Outdoor Pool in Ararat (23 min).
