Swan Hill West is in the Swan Hill area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Swan Hill West, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Swan Hill West. The closest to Swan Hill West is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Swan Hill West, so most trips start by car. By car from Swan Hill West, Swan Hill is about 12 minutes away and Echuca about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Swan Hill West is Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill, about 13 minutes' drive. For a library, Swan Hill Regional Library in Swan Hill is about 13 minutes' drive from Swan Hill West. The closest skate park to Swan Hill West is Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Swan Hill West is Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill, about 13 minutes' drive. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Swan Hill West, about 12 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Swan Hill West, the nearest places for kids by road are Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South (11 min), the playground at Jaycee Park in Swan Hill (13 min), Showbiz Cinemas Swan Hill in Swan Hill (13 min), Swan Hill Waterslide in Swan Hill (13 min), Swan Hill Outdoor Pool in Swan Hill (13 min) and the playground at Riverside Park in Swan Hill (13 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Swan Hill West.

Nearby suburbs

Swan Hill, next to Swan Hill West, has 9 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Woorinen South, next to Swan Hill West, has a skate park. Nowie, Goschen and Bulga also border Swan Hill West.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Swan Hill West, without traffic.
