Playground at Barber Reserve

Playground in Somerville · Basic info

Playground at Barber Reserve is a public playground in Somerville, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire area. The Western Port Bay Trail passes about 380 m away, and Playground at Todd Grove Reserve is another option about 540 m away. There's a public toilet about 600 m away, and buses 782 and 783 stop at Somerville Station/Frankston-Flinders Rd, a 8-minute walk away.
