Somerville Recreation Reserve

Park in Somerville · Basic info

Somerville Recreation Reserve is a park in Somerville, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire Council area, good for all ages. Somerville Recreation Reserve/Park Lane (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 51 m away.
