Playground at Eramosa Park

Playground in Somerville · Basic info

Playground at Eramosa Park is a public playground in Somerville, in the Mornington Peninsula Shire area. For food, McDonald's is about 210 m away, and Playground at Colchester Park is another option about 440 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 670 m from the playground, and buses 782 and 783 stop at Graf Rd/Eramosa Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
