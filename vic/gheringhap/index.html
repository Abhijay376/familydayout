Gheringhap is in the Golden Plains area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Gheringhap, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Getting around

Gheringhap has no train station; North Geelong Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 12 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 19 stops in Gheringhap. V/Line coaches stop in Gheringhap. By car from Gheringhap, Geelong is about 19 minutes away, Torquay about 33 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 67 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Goodfellow Aquatic Centre in Bell Post Hill is about 10 minutes' drive from Gheringhap. The closest library to Gheringhap is Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn, about 7 minutes' drive. For a beach, Eastern Beach in Geelong is about 18 minutes' drive from Gheringhap. The closest skate park to Gheringhap is Bannockburn Skate Park in Bannockburn, about 8 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Geelong is about 18 minutes' drive from Gheringhap. The closest indoor play centre to Gheringhap is All 4 Kids Play Centre in Geelong West, about 15 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Adventure Park in Wallington is about 35 minutes' drive from Gheringhap. The nearest larger centre is Geelong, about 19 minutes from Gheringhap by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Gheringhap are the playground at Dog Rocks Reserve in Batesford (6 min), Bannockburn Lions Park Playground in Bannockburn (6 min), Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn (7 min), Bannockburn Skate Park in Bannockburn (8 min), Goodfellow Aquatic Centre in Bell Post Hill (10 min) and Corio Bowls in Norlane (11 min).
