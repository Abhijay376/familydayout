Karabeal is in the Southern Grampians area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Karabeal, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Karabeal. The closest to Karabeal is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 73 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Karabeal, so most trips start by car. By car from Karabeal, Hamilton is about 28 minutes away and Ararat about 71 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Hamilton Indoor Leisure & Aquatic Centre in Hamilton is about 28 minutes' drive from Karabeal. The closest library to Karabeal is Hamilton Library in Hamilton, about 27 minutes' drive. For a skate park, hamilton skate park in Hamilton is about 27 minutes' drive from Karabeal. For a cinema, Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton is about 27 minutes' drive from Karabeal. Hamilton is the closest bigger centre to Karabeal, about 28 minutes away by car, and has 23 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Karabeal, the nearest places for kids by road are Dunkeld Memorial Park Playground in Dunkeld (11 min), the playground at Cavendish Town Square Memorial Park in Cavendish (14 min), hamilton skate park in Hamilton (27 min), Sir Reginald Ansett Transport Museum in Hamilton (27 min), Hamilton Library in Hamilton (27 min) and Hamilton Cinema in Hamilton (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Karabeal.

Nearby suburbs

Dunkeld, next to Karabeal, has 1 playground. Cavendish, next to Karabeal, has 1 playground. Moutajup and Hensley Park also border Karabeal.
