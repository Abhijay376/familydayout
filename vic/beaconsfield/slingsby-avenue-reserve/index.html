Slingsby Avenue Reserve

Park in Beaconsfield · Basic info

Slingsby Avenue Reserve is a park in Beaconsfield, in the Casey City Council area, good for all ages. Brookvale Cl/Soldiers Rd (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1365 m away.
