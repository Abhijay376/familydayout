Beaconsfield Skate and BMX Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Beaconsfield · Basic info

Beaconsfield Skate and BMX Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Beaconsfield, in the Cardinia Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Sanctuary Way/Fieldstone Bvd (bus) is a 12-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1274 m away.
