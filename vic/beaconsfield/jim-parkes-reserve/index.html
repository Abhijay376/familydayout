Jim Parkes Reserve

Park in Beaconsfield · Basic info

Jim Parkes Reserve is a park in Beaconsfield, in the Cardinia Shire Council area, good for all ages. Beaconsfield Plaza/Woods St (bus) is a 6-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 497 m away.
