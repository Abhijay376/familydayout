Our data lists 8 playgrounds and 17 parks and reserves in Beaconsfield, in the Cardinia area. On top of that, Beaconsfield has 2 skate parks.

Landmarks

Cardinia Creek Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Beaconsfield, at about 29 hectares. Beaconsfield Flora and Fauna Reserve and Beaconsfield Recreational Reserve are the next largest. Beaconsfield also has Manna Gum Park, Bonette Reserve and Beaconsfield Park. Playgrounds in Beaconsfield include Kath Roberts Reserve Playground, the playground at Jim Parkes Reserve and the playground at Slingsby Avenue Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Beaconsfield. For older kids in Beaconsfield there's the bmx track at Kath Roberts Reserve and Beaconsfield Skate and BMX Park. Our data lists 27 cafes and family restaurants in Beaconsfield for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Beaconsfield Railway Station (metro: Pakenham Line) in Beaconsfield. Buses 836, 837, 838, 926 and 928 stop in Beaconsfield. By car from Beaconsfield, Pakenham is about 12 minutes away, Cranbourne about 17 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 40 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Splash's Swimming School in Berwick is about 6 minutes' drive from Beaconsfield. The closest library to Beaconsfield is Berwick Library in Berwick, about 4 minutes' drive. For a beach, Edithvale Beach in Edithvale is about 30 minutes' drive from Beaconsfield. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Narre Warren is about 9 minutes' drive from Beaconsfield. For an indoor play centre, Oz Tenpin in Narre Warren is about 8 minutes' drive from Beaconsfield. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Beaconsfield is MonkeyG's in Pakenham, about 13 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 8 playgrounds across Beaconsfield. Teens who skate or ride, with the bmx track at Kath Roberts Reserve in Beaconsfield.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Beaconsfield, Berwick has 58 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Officer, next to Beaconsfield, has 14 playgrounds. Next door to Beaconsfield, Beaconsfield Upper has 3 playgrounds and a skate park.
