Bulla in the Hume area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bulla is Organ Pipes National Park, covering about 152 hectares. Bulla Hill Railway is the next largest. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Bulla for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Bulla has no train station; Watergardens Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 7.5 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Bus 479 stops in Bulla. By car from Bulla, Broadmeadows is about 15 minutes away, Sunshine about 22 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 31 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Sunbury Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Sunbury is about 10 minutes' drive from Bulla. The closest library to Bulla is Sunbury Library in Sunbury, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Bulla is Horse Arena in Abbotsford, about 34 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Bulla is Sunbury Bmx Club in Sunbury, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Bulla is Reading Cinema in Sunbury, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Bulla is Timezone in Craigieburn, about 17 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville is about 26 minutes' drive from Bulla. Broadmeadows is the closest bigger centre to Bulla, about 15 minutes away by car, and has 12 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Bulla are the playground at Apex Park in Sunbury (8 min), the playground at Turnberry Drive Reserve in Sunbury (9 min), Sunbury Library in Sunbury (10 min), Sunbury Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Sunbury (10 min), Reading Cinema in Sunbury (10 min) and URBNSURF Wave Park in Melbourne Airport (12 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bulla.

Nearby suburbs

Sunbury, next to Bulla, has 43 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Bulla, Greenvale has 21 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Bulla, Diggers Rest has 11 playgrounds. Melbourne Airport, next to Bulla, has a pool. Keilor North, Wildwood and Oaklands Junction also border Bulla.
