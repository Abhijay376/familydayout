Organ Pipes National Park

Park in Bulla · Official details

Organ Pipes National Park is a park in Bulla, in the Hume City Council area, good for all ages. The nearest public toilet is about 258 m away.

Details

Toilets
yes (Parks Victoria)
Wheelchair access
no (Parks Victoria)
Pram access
partly (Parks Victoria)
Seasons
Visit morning to mid afternoon in summer to avoid heat (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
Only way in is a short steep bitumen path from the carpark; Rosette Rock is about 400m past the Organ Pipes (Parks Victoria)
