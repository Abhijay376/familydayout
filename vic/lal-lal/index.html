Our data lists 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in Lal Lal, in the Moorabool area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lal Lal is Lal Lal - Bungal Historic Area, covering about 284 hectares. Lal Lal Falls Scenic Reserve is the next largest. Playgrounds in Lal Lal include the playground at Lal Lal Falls Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Lal Lal.

Getting around

Lal Lal has no train station; Ballarat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 19 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Lal Lal. By car from Lal Lal, Ballarat is about 27 minutes away, Bacchus Marsh about 54 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lal Lal is Buninyong Swimming Pool in Buninyong, about 13 minutes' drive. For a library, L Building: E J Barker Library in Mount Helen is about 18 minutes' drive from Lal Lal. The closest beach to Lal Lal is Disability beach in Steiglitz, about 38 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the bmx track at Buninyong Recreation Park in Buninyong is about 13 minutes' drive from Lal Lal. For a cinema, Victoria Theatre in Golden Point is about 24 minutes' drive from Lal Lal. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 24 minutes' drive from Lal Lal. For a zoo or wildlife park, Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East is about 25 minutes' drive from Lal Lal. Ballarat is the closest bigger centre to Lal Lal, about 27 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lal Lal.
