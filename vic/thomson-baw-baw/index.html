Thomson (Baw Baw) is in the Baw Baw area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Other parks and reserves in Thomson (Baw Baw) include Junction and Meringtons. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Thomson (Baw Baw). Picnic areas in Thomson (Baw Baw) include Junction. For camping, there's Meringtons. For walks and rides, Australian Alps Walking Track, Whitelaw Track and Meringtons Walk cross Thomson (Baw Baw).

Getting around

There's no train station in Thomson (Baw Baw). The closest to Thomson (Baw Baw) is Moe Railway Station (V/Line), about 46 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Thomson (Baw Baw), so most trips start by car. By car from Thomson (Baw Baw), Traralgon is about 69 minutes away, Warragul about 1.5 hours and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.
