Landmarks

The biggest green space in Vite Vite North is Vite Vite Streamside Reserve, covering about 3 hectares.

Getting around

Vite Vite North has no train station; Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 43 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Vite Vite North, so most trips start by car. By car from Vite Vite North, Ballarat is about 71 minutes away, Ararat about 75 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Vite Vite North is Lismore Pool in Lismore, about 35 minutes' drive. The closest library to Vite Vite North is Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum, about 27 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Linton Skate Park in Linton is about 41 minutes' drive from Vite Vite North. The closest places for kids by road from Vite Vite North are Derrinallum Library in Derrinallum (27 min), the playground at Simpson Park in Streatham (30 min), Lismore Pool in Lismore (35 min), Linton Skate Park in Linton (41 min), Linton BMX park in Linton (41 min) and Linton Recreation Reserve Playground in Linton (41 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Vite Vite North.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Vite Vite North, Lismore has a pool. Next door to Vite Vite North, Derrinallum has a library. Pura Pura, Mingay, Carranballac and Bradvale also border Vite Vite North.
