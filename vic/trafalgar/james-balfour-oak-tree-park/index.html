James Balfour Oak Tree Park

Park in Trafalgar · Basic info

James Balfour Oak Tree Park is a park in Trafalgar, in the Baw Baw Shire Council area, good for all ages. Trafalgar Station/Princes Hwy (bus) is a 10-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 650 m away.
