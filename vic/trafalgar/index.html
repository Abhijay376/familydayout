Our data lists 4 playgrounds and 4 parks and reserves in Trafalgar, in the Baw Baw area. Beyond the parks, Trafalgar has a pool.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Trafalgar is Trafalgar Bushland Reserve, covering about 6 hectares. James Balfour Oak Tree Park and Brindlewood Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Trafalgar include McGregor Park. Playgrounds around Trafalgar include the playground at Brindlewood Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Trafalgar. In Trafalgar you'll also find Trafalgar Swimming Pool. The Donaldson Track passes through Trafalgar. Our data lists 4 cafes and family restaurants in Trafalgar for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Trafalgar Railway Station (V/Line: Bairnsdale - Melbourne via Sale & Traralgon and Traralgon - Melbourne via Morwell, Moe & Pakenham) in Trafalgar. Buses on the Garfield Station - Traralgon Plaza route stop in Trafalgar. V/Line coaches stop in Trafalgar. By car from Trafalgar, Warragul is about 19 minutes away, Traralgon about 31 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Trafalgar is Warragul Library in Warragul, about 19 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Moe Skate Park in Moe is about 10 minutes' drive from Trafalgar. For a cinema, Warragul Cinema in Warragul is about 17 minutes' drive from Trafalgar. The closest indoor play centre to Trafalgar is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 18 minutes' drive. Warragul is the closest bigger centre to Trafalgar, about 19 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Trafalgar's 4 local playgrounds. Free days on the Donaldson Track and in Trafalgar's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Yarragon, next to Trafalgar, has 2 playgrounds. Willow Grove, next to Trafalgar, has 2 playgrounds. Trafalgar South, Trafalgar East and Shady Creek also border Trafalgar.
