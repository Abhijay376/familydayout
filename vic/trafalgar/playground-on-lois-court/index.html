Playground on Lois Court

Playground in Trafalgar · Basic info

Playground on Lois Court is a public playground in Trafalgar, in the Baw Baw Shire area. Trafalgar Swimming Pool is about 180 m away, and Fish Bits Trafalgar is about 390 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 800 m away. It's easiest by car, about 2 minutes from Trafalgar.
