Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Tanjil South, in the Baw Baw area.

Landmarks

Tanjil State Forest is the largest mapped park in Tanjil South, at about 31,402 hectares. Tanjil East G86 Bushland Reserve is the next largest. For walks and rides, Moe–Walhalla narrow-gauge railway crosses Tanjil South.

Getting around

Tanjil South has no train station; Moe Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 7.0 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tanjil South, so most trips start by car. By car from Tanjil South, Traralgon is about 32 minutes away, Warragul about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tanjil South is Diving Pool in Moe, about 9 minutes' drive. For a library, Morwell Library in Morwell is about 21 minutes' drive from Tanjil South. The closest skate park to Tanjil South is Moe Skate Park in Moe, about 8 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 23 minutes' drive from Tanjil South. The closest indoor play centre to Tanjil South is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 34 minutes' drive. Traralgon is the closest bigger centre to Tanjil South, about 32 minutes away by car, and has 38 playgrounds and a pool. From Tanjil South, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Ferguson Street Reserve in Moe (7 min), the playground at Hennessey Street Park in Moe (7 min), Moe Skate Park in Moe (8 min), Diving Pool in Moe (9 min), Moe Outdoor Pool in Moe (10 min) and Old Brown Coal Mine Museum in Yallourn North (13 min).

Best for

Free days on the Moe–Walhalla narrow-gauge railway and in Tanjil South's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Tanjil South, Moe has 14 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Tanjil South, Newborough has 11 playgrounds. Yallourn North, next to Tanjil South, has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Willow Grove, next to Tanjil South, has 2 playgrounds. Westbury, Tanjil and Moondarra also border Tanjil South.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Tanjil South, without traffic.
