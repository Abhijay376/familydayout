Cabarita is in the Mildura area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Cabarita, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Cabarita has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 185 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the 250/300 route stop in Cabarita. By car from Cabarita, Mildura is about 12 minutes away and Swan Hill about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cabarita is Merbein Pool in Merbein, about 9 minutes' drive. For a library, Merbein Library in Merbein is about 8 minutes' drive from Cabarita. For a beach, Apex Beach in Mildura is about 19 minutes' drive from Cabarita. The closest skate park to Cabarita is Merbein Skate Park in Merbein, about 12 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Deakin Cinema Complex in Mildura is about 11 minutes' drive from Cabarita. For an indoor play centre, Laserpunk Laserforce in Mildura is about 12 minutes' drive from Cabarita. For a zoo or wildlife park, Putt Putt Family Fun Centre in Mildura is about 12 minutes' drive from Cabarita. Mildura is the closest bigger centre to Cabarita, about 12 minutes away by car, and has 26 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Cabarita, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Chaffey Park in Merbein (11 min), Merbein Skate Park in Merbein (12 min), the playground at Hollywood Boulevard Drainage Reserve in Mildura (7 min), The RAAF Air Museum in Mildura (8 min), Merbein Library in Merbein (8 min) and Merbein Pool in Merbein (9 min).
