Fred Gration Reserve

Park in Coronet Bay · Basic info

Fred Gration Reserve is a park in Coronet Bay, in the Bass Coast Shire Council area, good for all ages. Bantering Bay Rd/Gellibrand St (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 338 m away.
