Playground on Cutty Sark Road

Playground in Coronet Bay · Basic info

This is a public playground in Coronet Bay. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet about 60 m away, and buses Coronet Bay - Grantville via Corinella and Coronet Bay - Wonthaggi via Corinella stop at General Store/Champ Elysees Esp, a 2-minute walk away.
