Landmarks

The biggest green space in Staceys Bridge is Binginwarri H19 Bushland Reserve, covering about 4 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Staceys Bridge. The closest to Staceys Bridge is Morwell Railway Station (V/Line), about 37 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Staceys Bridge, so most trips start by car. By car from Staceys Bridge, Sale is about 75 minutes away and Traralgon about 79 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Staceys Bridge is Yarram Public Pool in Yarram, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest library to Staceys Bridge is Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram, about 20 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram is about 20 minutes' drive from Staceys Bridge. The closest places for kids by road from Staceys Bridge are Yarram Historical Society Museum in Yarram (19 min), the skate park at Yarram Tarra Trail Reserve in Yarram (20 min), Yarram Public Pool in Yarram (20 min), Yarram Library - Temporary Site in Yarram (20 min), the playground at Memorial Park in Yarram (20 min) and Port Albert Maritime Museum in Port Albert (32 min).
