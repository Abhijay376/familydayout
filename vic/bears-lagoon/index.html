Bears Lagoon is in the Loddon area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Bears Lagoon is Serpentine Creek Weir Historic and Cultural Features Reserve, covering about 1 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bears Lagoon. The closest to Bears Lagoon is Dingee Railway Station (V/Line), about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Bears Lagoon. By car from Bears Lagoon, Bendigo is about 49 minutes away, Echuca about 71 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo is about 28 minutes' drive from Bears Lagoon. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 49 minutes from Bears Lagoon by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Bears Lagoon are the playground at R J Phelan Reserve in Mitiamo (28 min), East Loddon Historical Society in Mitiamo (28 min), Mitiamo Swimming Pool in Mitiamo (28 min), Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood (32 min), Pyramid Hill & Districts Historical Museum in Pyramid Hill (32 min) and A S Salter Memorial Playground / Nolan's Park in Boort (35 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Bears Lagoon.

Nearby suburbs

Calivil and Borung also border Bears Lagoon.
