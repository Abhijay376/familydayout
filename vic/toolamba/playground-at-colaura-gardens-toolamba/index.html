Playground at Colaura Gardens Toolamba

Playground in Toolamba · Official details

This is a neighbourhood playground in Toolamba, looked after by Greater Shepparton City Council. The Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks is about 20 m away, and Playground at Toolamba Rec Res Youth Spot is another option about 440 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 80 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 1 minute from Toolamba.
