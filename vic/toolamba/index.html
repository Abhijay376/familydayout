Toolamba is in the Greater Shepparton area, and our data lists 2 playgrounds and 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Shepparton Regional Park is the largest mapped park in Toolamba, at about 2,800 hectares. Toolamba also has Colaura Gardens. Playgrounds in Toolamba include the playground at Colaura Gardens Toolamba and the playground at Toolamba Rec Res Youth Spot. Our data shows 1 public toilet and 2 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by in Toolamba. For walks and rides, Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks crosses Toolamba.

Getting around

Toolamba has no train station; Mooroopna Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 10 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Toolamba, so most trips start by car. By car from Toolamba, Shepparton is about 18 minutes away, Seymour about 55 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Toolamba is Tatura Swimming complex in Tatura, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest library to Toolamba is Mooroopna Library in Mooroopna, about 12 minutes' drive. For a beach, Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton is about 20 minutes' drive from Toolamba. The closest skate park to Toolamba is the skate park at Tatura Memorial Park in Tatura, about 15 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 18 minutes' drive from Toolamba. The closest indoor play centre to Toolamba is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Toolamba is Emerald Bank Leisure Land in Kialla, about 13 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 18 minutes from Toolamba by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Free days on the Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks and in Toolamba's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Mooroopna, next to Toolamba, has 12 playgrounds, a library and a zoo or wildlife park. Next door to Toolamba, Kialla has 11 playgrounds and a zoo or wildlife park. Kialla West, next to Toolamba, has 1 playground. Arcadia, next to Toolamba, has 1 playground. Toolamba West, Tatura East, Murchison North and Dhurringile also border Toolamba.
