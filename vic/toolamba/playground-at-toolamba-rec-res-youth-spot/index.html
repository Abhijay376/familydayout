Playground at Toolamba Rec Res Youth Spot

Playground in Toolamba · Basic info

This is a neighbourhood playground in Toolamba, looked after by Greater Shepparton City Council. The Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks is about 100 m away, and Playground at Colaura Gardens Toolamba is another option about 440 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 600 m from the playground. It's easiest by car, about 2 minutes from Toolamba.
