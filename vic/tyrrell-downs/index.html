Our data lists 4 parks and reserves in Tyrrell Downs, in the Buloke area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tyrrell Downs is Lake Wahpool Lake Reserve, covering about 2,643 hectares. Pooks Bushland Reserve and Lake Wahpool West Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Tyrrell Downs also has Chillingollah West Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

Tyrrell Downs has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 55 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tyrrell Downs, so most trips start by car. By car from Tyrrell Downs, Swan Hill is about 55 minutes away and Mildura about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake is about 16 minutes' drive from Tyrrell Downs. Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Tyrrell Downs, about 55 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Tyrrell Downs are Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake (16 min), the playground at Apex Park in Sea Lake (16 min) and Manangatang Swimming Pool in Manangatang (28 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tyrrell Downs.

Nearby suburbs

Sea Lake, next to Tyrrell Downs, has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Chinkapook and Chillingollah also border Tyrrell Downs.
