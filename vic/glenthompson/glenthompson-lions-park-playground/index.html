Glenthompson Lions Park Playground

Playground in Glenthompson · Basic info

Glenthompson Lions Park Playground is a public playground in Glenthompson, in the Southern Grampians Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet about 90 m away, and coaches stop at Cameron St/Glenelg Hwy, a 2-minute walk away.
