Cheshunt in the Wangaratta area has 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Cheshunt is Mount Pleasant Reference Area, covering about 610 hectares. Wabonga Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Cheshunt. The Whitfield - Lake Buffalo passes through Cheshunt.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cheshunt. The closest to Cheshunt is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 50 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the King Valley-Wangaratta route stop in Cheshunt. By car from Cheshunt, Wangaratta is about 52 minutes away and Mansfield about 62 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Cheshunt, about 52 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Cheshunt, the nearest places for kids by road are Myrrhee Recreation Reserve in Myrrhee (29 min) and the playground at Apex Park in Moyhu (27 min).

Best for

Free days on the Whitfield - Lake Buffalo and in Cheshunt's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Cheshunt, Whitfield has 1 playground. Next door to Cheshunt, Tolmie has 1 playground. Edi Upper and Cheshunt South also border Cheshunt.
