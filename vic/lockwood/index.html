Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lockwood is Bendigo Regional Park, covering about 8,797 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lockwood. The closest to Lockwood is Kangaroo Flat Railway Station (V/Line), about 7.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lockwood, so most trips start by car. By car from Lockwood, Bendigo is about 17 minutes away, Castlemaine about 26 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Faith Leech Aquatic Centre in Bendigo is about 18 minutes' drive from Lockwood. The closest library to Lockwood is Kangaroo Flat Library in Kangaroo Flat, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Lockwood is Long Gully BMX Track in Long Gully, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Lockwood is Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo, about 17 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 14 minutes' drive from Lockwood. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Lockwood is The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo, about 22 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Bendigo, about 17 minutes from Lockwood by car, with 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Lockwood are Graemar Court Playspace in Kangaroo Flat (9 min), Kangaroo Flat Library in Kangaroo Flat (9 min), William Drive Playspace in Kangaroo Flat (10 min), Intagolf in Golden Gully (14 min), Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo (17 min) and Bendigo RSL Museum in Bendigo (17 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lockwood.
