Our data lists 6 parks and reserves in Raglan, in the Pyrenees area.

Landmarks

Raglan also has Richards, The Glut and Mugwamp Hut. Our data shows 10 public toilets in Raglan. Picnic areas in Raglan include Ditchfield Camp, Mugwamp Hut and Richards. For camping, there's Beeripmo Campground. The Beeripmo Walk, Paradise Walk and Cave Hill Circuit all pass through Raglan.

Getting around

Raglan has no train station; Beaufort Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 8.0 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Raglan, so most trips start by car. By car from Raglan, Ararat is about 39 minutes away, Ballarat about 52 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Raglan is Beaufort Pool in Beaufort, about 16 minutes' drive. For a library, Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort is about 15 minutes' drive from Raglan. The closest skate park to Raglan is the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Raglan is Astor Cinema in Ararat, about 38 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Ararat, about 39 minutes from Raglan by car, with 5 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Raglan, the nearest places for kids by road are Beaufort Community Resource Centre in Beaufort (15 min), the playground at Wotherspoon Reserve in Beaufort (15 min), the playground at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (16 min), Beaufort Pool in Beaufort (16 min), the skate park at Beaufort Skate Park in Beaufort (16 min) and Courthouse in Avoca (33 min).

Best for

Free days on the Beeripmo Walk and in Raglan's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Raglan, Beaufort has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Middle Creek, Main Lead, Glenlogie and Chute also border Raglan.
