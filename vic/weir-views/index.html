Weir Views in the Melton area has 4 playgrounds in our data.

Landmarks

Playgrounds around Weir Views include Embleton Chase Reserve Playground, Tamboritha Bvd Reserve Playground and Conservation Av Reserve Playground. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Weir Views. Opalia Plaza is the local shopping centre. For walks and rides, Toolern Creek Trail crosses Weir Views. Our data lists 7 cafes and family restaurants in Weir Views for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Weir Views. The closest to Weir Views is Melton Railway Station (V/Line), about 2.4 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 452 stops in Weir Views. By car from Weir Views, Melton is about 8 minutes away, Bacchus Marsh about 18 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 45 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Weir Views is Melton Swimmers in Melton South, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest library to Weir Views is Melton Library & Learning Hub in Melton, about 8 minutes' drive. For a beach, Lionhead Beach in Pentland Hills is about 25 minutes' drive from Weir Views. The closest skate park to Weir Views is the skate park at Cambrian Way Reserve in Melton West, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Weir Views is Reading Cinemas in Melton West, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Weir Views is Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes, about 28 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Werribee Open Range Zoo in Werribee South is about 39 minutes' drive from Weir Views. The nearest larger centre is Melton, about 8 minutes from Weir Views by car, with 15 playgrounds, a pool and a library.

Best for

Younger kids at Weir Views's 4 local playgrounds. Free days on the Toolern Creek Trail and in Weir Views's parks.
