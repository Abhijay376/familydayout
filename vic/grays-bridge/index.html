Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Grays Bridge, in the Northern Grampians area.

Landmarks

Creswick Swamp Wildlife Reserve (no hunting) is the largest mapped park in Grays Bridge, at about 18 hectares.

Getting around

Grays Bridge has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 75 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Grays Bridge, so most trips start by car. By car from Grays Bridge, Stawell is about 53 minutes away and Horsham about 68 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Rupanyup Swimming Pool in Rupanyup is about 31 minutes' drive from Grays Bridge. The closest skate park to Grays Bridge is Donald BMX Track in Donald, about 34 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Stawell, about 53 minutes from Grays Bridge by car, with 6 playgrounds and a pool. From Grays Bridge, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Shanahan Park in Marnoo (13 min), Guthrie Park Playground in Rich Avon West (18 min), Rupanyup Swimming Pool in Rupanyup (31 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (32 min), Woods' Farming and Heritage Museum in Rupanyup (31 min) and Donald BMX Track in Donald (34 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Grays Bridge.

Nearby suburbs

Marnoo, next to Grays Bridge, has 1 playground. Avon Plains also borders Grays Bridge.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Grays Bridge, without traffic.
