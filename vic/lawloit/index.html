Our data lists 1 park or reserve in Lawloit, in the West Wimmera area.

Landmarks

Lake Lawloit Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Lawloit, at about 116 hectares.

Getting around

Lawloit has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 162 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lawloit, so most trips start by car. By car from Lawloit, Horsham is about 77 minutes away and Stawell about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Nhill Library in Nhill is about 19 minutes' drive from Lawloit. For a skate park, Nhill Skate Park in Nhill is about 18 minutes' drive from Lawloit. From Lawloit, the nearest places for kids by road are Nhill Playground in Nhill (18 min), Nhill Skate Park in Nhill (18 min), Wagonin in Nhill (18 min), Gallery Central in Nhill (18 min), Nhill Library in Nhill (19 min) and the playground at Goldsworthy Park in Nhill (19 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lawloit.

Nearby suburbs

Nhill, next to Lawloit, has 3 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Kaniva, next to Lawloit, has 1 playground and a library. Little Desert also borders Lawloit.
