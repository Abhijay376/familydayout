Playground at Angus Facey Reserve

Playground in Narre Warren · Basic info

Playground at Angus Facey Reserve is a public playground in Narre Warren, in the Casey City area. The Hallam Bypass Trail passes about 120 m away, and Playground on Wallowa Crescent is another option about 540 m away. There's a public toilet about 820 m away, and bus 842 stops at Prospect Hill Rd/Narre Warren North Rd, a 8-minute walk away.
