Bunjil Place Playground

Playground in Narre Warren · Basic info

This is a public playground in Narre Warren. Narre Warren Library is about 120 m away, and Casey ARC is about 50 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and buses 828, 834, 891 and 981 stop at Casey ARC/Princes Hwy, a 4-minute walk away.
