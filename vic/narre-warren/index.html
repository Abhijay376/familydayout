Our data lists 30 playgrounds and 24 parks and reserves in Narre Warren, in the Casey area. On top of that, Narre Warren has a library, a pool, a museum or gallery and a cinema.

Landmarks

Troups Creek West Wetlands is the largest mapped park in Narre Warren, at about 22 hectares. Max Pawsey Reserve and Sweeney Reserve are the next largest. Narre Warren also has Brechin Gardens, John Byron Reserve and Angus Facey Reserve. Playgrounds around Narre Warren include Croc's Playcenter, Ray Bastin Reserve and BOUNCE Fountain Gate. Our data shows 9 public toilets in Narre Warren. In Narre Warren you'll also find Bunjil Place, Village Cinemas, Oz Tenpin, Casey ARC and Narre Warren Library. For older kids in Narre Warren there's BMX Track and Narre Warren Skatepark. Westfield Fountain Gate Shopping Centre is the local shopping centre. The Hallam Bypass Trail, Hallam Valley Trail and Hallam Valley Trail Extension all pass through Narre Warren. Our data lists 35 cafes and family restaurants in Narre Warren for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Narre Warren Railway Station (metro: Pakenham Line) in Narre Warren. Buses on the 695, 697, 828 and 834 routes stop in Narre Warren. By car from Narre Warren, Cranbourne is about 11 minutes away, Dandenong about 11 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 34 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a beach, Edithvale Beach in Edithvale is about 23 minutes' drive from Narre Warren. For a zoo or wildlife park, MonkeyG's in Pakenham is about 16 minutes' drive from Narre Warren.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 30 playgrounds across Narre Warren. Rainy days at the library, cinema, indoor play centre and museum or gallery in Narre Warren. Teens who skate or ride, with BMX Track in Narre Warren. Free days on the Hallam Bypass Trail and in Narre Warren's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Berwick, next to Narre Warren, has 58 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Narre Warren, Hampton Park has 20 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Narre Warren, Narre Warren South has 18 playgrounds and a pool. Next door to Narre Warren, Hallam has 12 playgrounds and a skate park. Narre Warren North also borders Narre Warren.
