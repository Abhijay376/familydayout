James Reserve Playground

Playground in Narre Warren · Basic info

James Reserve Playground in Narre Warren is a public playground in the Casey City area. Oz Tenpin is about 500 m away, and KFC is about 350 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 710 m away, and buses 828, 838, 895 and 926 stop at Stewart Ave/Princes Hwy, a 5-minute walk away.
