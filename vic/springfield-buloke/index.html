Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Springfield (Buloke), in the Buloke area.

Landmarks

Stony Plain Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Springfield (Buloke), at about 61 hectares. Twin Dams Bushland Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Springfield (Buloke). The closest to Springfield (Buloke) is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 48 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Springfield (Buloke), so most trips start by car. By car from Springfield (Buloke), Swan Hill is about 40 minutes away and Horsham about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock is about 13 minutes' drive from Springfield (Buloke). For a skate park, Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South is about 38 minutes' drive from Springfield (Buloke). Swan Hill is the closest bigger centre to Springfield (Buloke), about 40 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Springfield (Buloke) are the playground at Culgoa Memorial Park in Culgoa (13 min), Berriwillock Swimming Pool in Berriwillock (13 min), the playground at Hall Corner Park in Nullawil (25 min), Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake (26 min) and Woorinen South Skatepark in Woorinen South (38 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Springfield (Buloke).

Nearby suburbs

Sea Lake, next to Springfield (Buloke), has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Berriwillock, next to Springfield (Buloke), has a pool. Next door to Springfield (Buloke), Culgoa has 1 playground. Waitchie, Tyrrell, Murnungin and Chinangin also border Springfield (Buloke).
