Playground at Quarries Park

Playground in Clifton Hill · Basic info

Playground at Quarries Park is a public playground in Clifton Hill, in the Yarra City area. The Capital City Trail passes about 180 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 80 m away. There's a public toilet about 720 m away, and bus 546 stops at Dwyer St/Heidelberg Rd, a 9-minute walk away.
