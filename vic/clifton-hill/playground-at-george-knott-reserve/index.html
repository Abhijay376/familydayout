Playground at George Knott Reserve

Playground in Clifton Hill · Basic info

Playground at George Knott Reserve is a public playground in Clifton Hill, in the Yarra City area. The Capital City Trail is about 20 m away, and Playground on The Parade is another option about 150 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 80 m from the playground, and bus 546 stops at Dwyer St/Heidelberg Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
