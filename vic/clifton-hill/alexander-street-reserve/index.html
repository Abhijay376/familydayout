Alexander Street Reserve

Park in Clifton Hill · Basic info

Alexander Street Reserve is a park in Clifton Hill, in the Yarra City Council area, good for all ages. Noone St/Hoddle St (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 478 m away.
