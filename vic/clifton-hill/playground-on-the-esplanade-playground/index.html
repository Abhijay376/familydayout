Playground on The Esplanade

Playground in Clifton Hill · Basic info

This is a public playground in Clifton Hill. The Capital City Trail is about 30 m away, and Playground on Spensley Street is another option about 170 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 360 m from the playground, and bus 546 stops at The Esplanade/Heidelberg Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
