Playground on Wellington Street

Playground in Clifton Hill · Basic info

Playground on Wellington Street in Clifton Hill is a public playground in the Yarra City area. The Bike: Roseneath St is about 170 m away, and for food, Thai Ma-Me is about 140 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 290 m from the playground, and tram 86 stops at Wellington St/Queens Pde #23, a 1-minute walk away.
