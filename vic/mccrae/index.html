McCrae is in the Mornington Peninsula area, and our data lists 1 playground and 3 parks and reserves in the suburb. On top of that, McCrae has a beach.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in McCrae is Benbenjie Reserve, covering about 1 hectares. The Boulevard Reserve is the next largest. McCrae also has Bushland Reserve. Playgrounds in McCrae include Little Lighthouse. Our data shows 6 public toilets in McCrae. Places to visit in McCrae include McCrae Beach. McCrae Plaza is the local shopping centre. The Two Bays Walking Track, The Mornington Peninsula Walk and Mornington Peninsula Bay Trail all pass through McCrae. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in McCrae for a lunch stop.

Getting around

McCrae has no train station; Bittern Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 22 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 788 and 887 stop in McCrae. By car from McCrae, Mornington is about 20 minutes away, Frankston about 26 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 68 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Yawa Aquatic Centre in Rosebud is about 7 minutes' drive from McCrae. For a library, Rosebud Library in Rosebud is about 3 minutes' drive from McCrae. The closest skate park to McCrae is Rosebud Skate Park in Rosebud, about 5 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Rosebud Cinemas in Rosebud is about 2 minutes' drive from McCrae. The closest indoor play centre to McCrae is Top Fun Shack in Rosebud, about 4 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to McCrae is Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale, about 31 minutes' drive. Mornington is the closest bigger centre to McCrae, about 20 minutes away by car, and has 22 playgrounds, a library, indoor play and a beach.

Best for

Beach days at McCrae Beach. Free days on the Two Bays Walking Track and in McCrae's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to McCrae, Rosebud has 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to McCrae, Dromana has 7 playgrounds, a beach and a cinema. Next door to McCrae, Arthurs Seat has 3 playgrounds.
