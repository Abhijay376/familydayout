Millers Creek Bushland Reserve

Park in Pomonal · Basic info

Millers Creek Bushland Reserve is a park in Pomonal, in the Ararat Rural City Council area, good for all ages. Lake Fyans Tourist Rd/Ararat - Pomonal Rd (coach) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 402 m away with a baby change table.
