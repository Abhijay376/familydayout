Pomonal in the Ararat area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Lady Somers Bridge Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Pomonal, at about 16 hectares. Moyston West Bushland Reserve and Rowes Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Pomonal also has Millers Creek Bushland Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Pomonal. For walks and rides, Grampians Peaks Trail, Boronia Trail and Mount William Track cross Pomonal.

Getting around

There's no train station in Pomonal. The closest to Pomonal is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 31 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Pomonal. By car from Pomonal, Stawell is about 19 minutes away and Ararat about 30 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Halls Gap Swimming Pool in Halls Gap is about 12 minutes' drive from Pomonal. For a library, Ararat Library in Ararat is about 29 minutes' drive from Pomonal. For a beach, Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans is about 11 minutes' drive from Pomonal. For a skate park, the pump track on Sutherland Street in Stawell is about 18 minutes' drive from Pomonal. The closest cinema to Pomonal is Astor Cinema in Ararat, about 29 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Halls Gap Zoo in Halls Gap is about 7 minutes' drive from Pomonal. Stawell is the closest bigger centre to Pomonal, about 19 minutes away by car, and has 6 playgrounds and a pool. From Pomonal, the nearest places for kids by road are Halls Gap Zoo in Halls Gap (7 min), Kelleys Beach in Lake Fyans (11 min), Halls Gap Swimming Pool in Halls Gap (12 min), the playground at Halls Gap Picnic Reserve in Halls Gap (12 min), Brambuk Cultural Centre in Halls Gap (16 min) and MOCO Gallery in Halls Gap (16 min).

Best for

Free days on the Grampians Peaks Trail and in Pomonal's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Halls Gap, next to Pomonal, has 3 playgrounds, a pool and a zoo or wildlife park. Lake Fyans, next to Pomonal, has a beach. Mafeking, Black Range, Bellfield and Bellellen also border Pomonal.
