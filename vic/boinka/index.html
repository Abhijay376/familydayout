Boinka in the Mildura area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Boinka Flora Reserve is the largest mapped park in Boinka, at about 141 hectares. Boinka Bushland Reserve and Worooa Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Boinka.

Getting around

There's no train station in Boinka. The closest to Boinka is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 179 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Ouyen - Pinnaroo via Mallee Hwy route stop in Boinka. By car from Boinka, Mildura is about 2 hours away and Swan Hill about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Underbool Swimming Pool in Underbool is about 16 minutes' drive from Boinka. The closest library to Boinka is Walpeup Library in Walpeup, about 30 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Boinka are the playground at Outen Park in Underbool (15 min), Underbool Swimming Pool in Underbool (16 min), Walpeup Library in Walpeup (30 min), the playground at Walpeup Wayside Stop in Walpeup (30 min) and Murrayville Library in Murrayville (30 min).
