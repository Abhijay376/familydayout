Arcadia South is in the Strathbogie area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Arcadia South, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Arcadia South has no train station; Murchison East Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Arcadia South, so most trips start by car. By car from Arcadia South, Shepparton is about 21 minutes away, Seymour about 44 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Aquamoves in Shepparton is about 21 minutes' drive from Arcadia South. For a library, Shepparton Library in Shepparton is about 21 minutes' drive from Arcadia South. The closest beach to Arcadia South is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 22 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Skate Park in Shepparton is about 19 minutes' drive from Arcadia South. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 22 minutes' drive from Arcadia South. The closest indoor play centre to Arcadia South is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 21 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Arcadia South is Emerald Bank Leisure Land in Kialla, about 14 minutes' drive. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Arcadia South, about 21 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Arcadia South, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Arcadia Recreation Reserve in Arcadia (4 min), the playground at Toolamba Rec Res Youth Spot in Toolamba (12 min), Emerald Bank Leisure Land in Kialla (14 min), Museum of Vehicle Evolution - MOVE in Kialla (14 min), Shepparton Art Museum in Shepparton (19 min) and the skate park at Skate Park in Shepparton (19 min).
