Delahey in the Brimbank area has 5 playgrounds and 5 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Goldsmith Avenue Reserve is the largest mapped park in Delahey, at about 8 hectares. Cowley Street Reserve and Delahey Emerald Park are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Delahey include Robinson Court Reserve and Taylors Road Linear Reserve. Playgrounds around Delahey include the playground at Delahey Emerald Park, the playground at Cowley Street Reserve and the playground at Robinson Court Reserve. Our data shows 3 public toilets, 3 parks and playgrounds with a council BBQ close by and 2 off-leash dog parks in Delahey. Delahey Village is the local shopping centre. The Hume Drive Trail passes through Delahey. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Delahey for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Delahey. The closest to Delahey is Watergardens Railway Station (metro), about 1.9 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 418, 420, 425, 461 and 464 stop in Delahey. By car from Delahey, Sunshine is about 13 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 20 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 31 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Delahey is Flippers Swim School in Sydenham, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest library to Delahey is Brimbank Library in Taylors Lakes, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Delahey is Altona Beach in Altona, about 27 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the skate park at Packard Street Reserve in Keilor Downs is about 5 minutes' drive from Delahey. The closest cinema to Delahey is Hoyts in Taylors Lakes, about 5 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Zone Bowling in Taylors Lakes is about 5 minutes' drive from Delahey. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Delahey is Melbourne Zoological Gardens in Parkville, about 26 minutes' drive.

Best for

Younger kids at Delahey's 5 local playgrounds. Free days on the Hume Drive Trail and in Delahey's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Delahey, St Albans has 33 playgrounds and a library. Taylors Lakes, next to Delahey, has 16 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Keilor Downs, next to Delahey, has 16 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Delahey, Taylors Hill has 18 playgrounds. Sydenham, Kings Park and Burnside Heights also border Delahey.
