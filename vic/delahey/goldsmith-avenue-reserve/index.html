Goldsmith Avenue Reserve

Park in Delahey · Official details

Goldsmith Avenue Reserve is a park in Delahey, in the Brimbank City Council area, good for all ages. Silvertree Ave/Copperfield Dr (bus) is a 5-minute walk away, the nearest public toilet is about 253 m away and dogs are allowed.
