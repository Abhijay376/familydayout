Aleppo Crescent Reserve

Park in Frankston North · Basic info

Aleppo Crescent Reserve is a park in Frankston North, in the Frankston City Council area, good for all ages. Monterey Community Park/Forest Dr (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 406 m away with a baby change table.
