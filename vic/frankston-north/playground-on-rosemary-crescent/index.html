Playground on Rosemary Crescent

Playground in Frankston North · Basic info

Playground on Rosemary Crescent is a public playground in Frankston North, in the Frankston City area. Older kids have a skate park about 350 m away, and Pines Forest Aquatic Centre is about 340 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 270 m away, and bus 833 stops at Mahogany Rise Primary School/Monterey Bvd, a 1-minute walk away.
