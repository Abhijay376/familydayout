Playground at Monterey Reserve

Playground in Frankston North · Basic info

This is a public playground in Frankston North. Older kids have a skate park about 100 m away, and Pines Forest Aquatic Centre is about 160 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and bus 833 stops at Monterey Community Park/Forest Dr, a 1-minute walk away.
