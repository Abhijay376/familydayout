Playground at Manna Reserve

Playground in Frankston North · Basic info

Playground at Manna Reserve in Frankston North is a public playground in the Frankston City area. For food, Guzman y Gomez is about 440 m away, and Playground on Pimpala Avenue is another option about 380 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 560 m from the playground, and bus 832 stops at Holy Family Catholic Church/35 Moreton St, a 4-minute walk away.
