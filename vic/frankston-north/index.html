Our data lists 7 playgrounds and 8 parks and reserves in Frankston North, in the Frankston area. Beyond the parks, Frankston North has a pool and a skate park.

Landmarks

Monterey Reserve is the largest mapped park in Frankston North, at about 9 hectares. Pat Rollo Reserve and Eric Bell Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Frankston North include Armata Park, Manna Reserve and Indigo Court Reserve. Playgrounds in Frankston North include the playground at Armata Park, the playground at Manna Reserve and the playground at Monterey Reserve. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Frankston North. In Frankston North you'll also find Pines Forest Aquatic Centre. For older kids in Frankston North there's the skate park at Monterey Reserve. For walks and rides, Peninsula Link Trail, Boundary Track and Link Track cross Frankston North. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Frankston North for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Frankston North. The closest to Frankston North is Kananook Railway Station (metro), about 1.6 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses 760, 832, 833 and 901 stop in Frankston North. By car from Frankston North, Frankston is about 6 minutes away, Cranbourne about 15 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 41 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Frankston North is Frankston Library in Frankston, about 6 minutes' drive. For a beach, Seaford Beach in Seaford is about 7 minutes' drive from Frankston North. The closest cinema to Frankston North is Hoyts in Frankston, about 7 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to Frankston North is Strike in Frankston, about 7 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale is about 19 minutes' drive from Frankston North.

Best for

Younger kids at Frankston North's 7 local playgrounds. Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at Monterey Reserve in Frankston North. Free days on the Peninsula Link Trail and in Frankston North's parks.
