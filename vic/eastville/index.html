Eastville is in the Loddon area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Eastville, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Eastville. The closest to Eastville is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 28 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Eastville, so most trips start by car. By car from Eastville, Bendigo is about 40 minutes away, Maryborough about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Eastville is Maldon Swimming Pool in Maldon, about 24 minutes' drive. The closest library to Eastville is Athenaeum Library in Maldon, about 24 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Bike Jumps in Castlemaine is about 43 minutes' drive from Eastville. The closest cinema to Eastville is Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo, about 40 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Intagolf in Golden Gully is about 37 minutes' drive from Eastville. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Eastville, about 40 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Eastville, the nearest places for kids by road are Athenaeum Library in Maldon (24 min), Maldon Swimming Pool in Maldon (24 min), the playground at Maldon Shire Gardens in Maldon (24 min), Maldon District Museum in Maldon (24 min), Maldon Vintage Machinery Museum in Maldon (25 min) and Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (26 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Eastville.
