Lockington in the Campaspe area has 4 parks and reserves in our data. Beyond the parks, Lockington has 2 pools.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Lockington is Lockington Recreation Reserve, covering about 8 hectares. Lockington Bushland Reserve and Lions Apex Park are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Lockington include Clare Avenue Park. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Lockington. Places to visit in Lockington include Lockington District Swimming Pool and Lockington Pool. For walks and rides, Lockington – Kotta Rail Trail crosses Lockington.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lockington. The closest to Lockington is Rochester Railway Station (V/Line), about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Lockington. By car from Lockington, Echuca is about 29 minutes away, Bendigo about 61 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca is about 30 minutes' drive from Lockington. The closest skate park to Lockington is Moama Echuca Community Skate Park in Echuca, about 32 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca is about 30 minutes' drive from Lockington. Echuca is the closest bigger centre to Lockington, about 29 minutes away by car, and has 8 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. From Lockington, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Apex Park in Rochester (19 min), Campaspe Run Rural Discovery Centre in Elmore (24 min), the playground at Echuca Lions Park in Echuca (27 min), Holden Museum in Echuca (29 min), Echuca Paramount Cinemas in Echuca (30 min) and Campaspe Regional Library in Echuca (30 min).

Best for

Free days on the Lockington – Kotta Rail Trail and in Lockington's parks.

Diggora, Bamawm Extension and Bamawm also border Lockington.
