Skate park on Singer Road

Skate, BMX or pump track in Lockington · Basic info

Skate park on Singer Road is a skate, bmx or pump track in Lockington, in the Campaspe Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Singer Rd/Lockington Rd (coach) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 274 m away.
