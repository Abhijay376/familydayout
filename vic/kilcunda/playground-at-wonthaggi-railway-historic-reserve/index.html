Playground at Wonthaggi Railway Historic Reserve

Playground in Kilcunda · Basic info

Playground at Wonthaggi Railway Historic Reserve in Kilcunda is a public playground in the Bass Coast Shire area. The Bass Coast Rail Trail passes about 20 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 20 m away. There's a public toilet about 300 m away, and buses Coronet Bay - Wonthaggi via Corinella and Cowes - Anderson - Wonthaggi stop at Peppermint Rd/Bass Hwy, a 8-minute walk away.
