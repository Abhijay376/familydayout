Our data lists 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in Kilcunda, in the Bass Coast area. On top of that, Kilcunda has 5 beaches and a skate park.

Landmarks

Kilcunda Coastal Reserve is the largest mapped park in Kilcunda, at about 35 hectares. Kilcunda Coal-mine Historic and Cultural Features Reserve is the next largest. Playgrounds around Kilcunda include the playground at Wonthaggi Railway Historic Reserve. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Kilcunda. Places to visit in Kilcunda include Kilcunda East Beach and Kilcunda West 1 Beach. For older kids in Kilcunda there's the skate park at Wonthaggi Railway Historic Reserve. For walks and rides, Bass Coast Rail Trail and George Bass Coastal Walk cross Kilcunda.

Getting around

Kilcunda has no train station; Stony Point Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 30 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Cowes - Anderson - Wonthaggi and Coronet Bay - Wonthaggi via Corinella routes stop in Kilcunda. V/Line coaches stop in Kilcunda. By car from Kilcunda, Wonthaggi is about 14 minutes away, Leongatha about 44 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Bass Coast Aquatic and Leisure Centre in Wonthaggi is about 13 minutes' drive from Kilcunda. The closest library to Kilcunda is San Remo Library in San Remo, about 10 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Kilcunda is Wonthaggi Union Community Arts Centre in Wonthaggi, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Kilcunda is Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville, about 15 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Wonthaggi, about 14 minutes from Kilcunda by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at Wonthaggi Railway Historic Reserve in Kilcunda. Beach days at Kilcunda East Beach and Kilcunda West 1 Beach. Free days on the Bass Coast Rail Trail and in Kilcunda's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Kilcunda, San Remo has 4 playgrounds, a library and a beach. Next door to Kilcunda, Dalyston has 1 playground and a beach.
