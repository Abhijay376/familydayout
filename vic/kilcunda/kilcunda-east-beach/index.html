Kilcunda East Beach

Beach in Kilcunda · Basic info

Kilcunda East Beach is a beach in Kilcunda, in the Bass Coast Shire Council area, good for all ages. Peppermint Rd/Bass Hwy (bus) is a 20-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 197 m away.

Where is Kilcunda East Beach?
