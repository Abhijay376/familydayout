Shelly Beach

Beach in Kilcunda · Basic info

Shelly Beach is a beach in Kilcunda, in the Bass Coast Shire Council area, good for all ages. Peppermint Rd/Bass Hwy (bus) is a 11-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 556 m away.

Where is Shelly Beach?
Shelly Beach is in Kilcunda, Victoria.
