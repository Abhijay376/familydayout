Our data lists 3 parks and reserves in Turtons Creek, in the South Gippsland area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Turtons Creek is Turtons Creek Scenic Reserve, covering about 125 hectares. Mirboo South H39 Bushland Reserve is the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Turtons Creek include Turtons Creek Falls. Picnic areas in Turtons Creek include Turtons Creek Falls.

Getting around

There's no train station in Turtons Creek. The closest to Turtons Creek is Morwell Railway Station (V/Line), about 37 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Turtons Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Turtons Creek, Leongatha is about 56 minutes away, Traralgon about 76 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Turtons Creek is Foster Outdoor Pool in Foster, about 39 minutes' drive. For a library, Foster Library in Foster is about 38 minutes' drive from Turtons Creek. For a skate park, Skate Bowl Baromi Park Mirboo North in Mirboo North is about 42 minutes' drive from Turtons Creek. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Turtons Creek, about 56 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Turtons Creek are Luminous Glass Studio in Foster (38 min), Stockyard Gallery in Foster (38 min), Foster Library in Foster (38 min), Foster Outdoor Pool in Foster (39 min), the playground at Baromi Park in Mirboo North (42 min) and Mirboo North Library in Mirboo North (42 min).
