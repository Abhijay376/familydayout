Playground on Emerald Lake Road

Playground in Emerald · Basic info

This is a public playground in Emerald. The Eastern Dandenong Ranges Trail passes about 200 m away, and older kids have a skate park about 90 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and buses 695 and 695F stop at Beaconsfield-Emerald Rd/Belgrave-Gembrook Rd, a 1-minute walk away.
