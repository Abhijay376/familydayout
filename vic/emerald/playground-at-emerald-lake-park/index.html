Playground at Emerald Lake Park

Playground in Emerald · Basic info

Playground at Emerald Lake Park is a public playground in Emerald, in the Cardinia Shire area. The Pine Track passes about 10 m away. There's a public toilet about 210 m away, and buses 695 and 695F stop at Lawson Rd/Belgrave-Gembrook Rd, a 10-minute walk away.
