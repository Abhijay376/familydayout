Cardinia Reservoir Park

Park in Emerald · Official details

Cardinia Reservoir Park is a park in Emerald, in the Cardinia Shire Council area, good for all ages. The nearest public toilet is about 163 m away and dogs are allowed on a lead. Opening hours: Daylight hours.
