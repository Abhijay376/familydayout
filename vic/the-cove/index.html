Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside The Cove in the Moyne area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it. The Cove does have a beach.

Landmarks

In The Cove you'll also find Buttress Point.

Getting around

There's no train station in The Cove. The closest to The Cove is Sherwood Park Railway Station (V/Line), about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside The Cove, so most trips start by car. By car from The Cove, Warrnambool is about 32 minutes away and Colac about 82 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to The Cove is Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest library to The Cove is Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool, about 33 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to The Cove is the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to The Cove is Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool, about 32 minutes' drive. The closest indoor play centre to The Cove is The Bowl in Warrnambool, about 32 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee is about 25 minutes' drive from The Cove. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to The Cove, about 32 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From The Cove, the nearest places for kids by road are Flaxman Beach in Nullawarre (15 min), Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (25 min), the playground at Peterborough Coastal Reserve (COM) in Peterborough (23 min), the skate park at Power Creek Reserve in Timboon (28 min), Grieve Street Playground in Warrnambool (30 min) and Timboon Outdoor Swimming Pool in Timboon (28 min).

Best for

Beach days at Buttress Point.
