Invergordon is in the Moira area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Invergordon, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Invergordon.

Getting around

Invergordon has no train station; Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 29 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Invergordon, so most trips start by car. By car from Invergordon, Shepparton is about 27 minutes away, Benalla about 58 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Invergordon is Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest library to Invergordon is Numurkah Library in Numurkah, about 26 minutes' drive. For a beach, Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton is about 30 minutes' drive from Invergordon. The closest skate park to Invergordon is Riverland Skate Park in Numurkah, about 25 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 27 minutes' drive from Invergordon. For an indoor play centre, StarBowl in Shepparton is about 27 minutes' drive from Invergordon. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kidstown in Mooroopna is about 30 minutes' drive from Invergordon. Shepparton is the closest bigger centre to Invergordon, about 27 minutes away by car, and has 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Invergordon, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Katandra Rec Res Youth Spot in Katandra West (11 min), the playground at Katandra West Recreation Reserve in Katandra West (12 min), Katamatite Historical Society Museum in Katamatite (13 min), Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah (25 min), Riverland Skate Park in Numurkah (25 min) and Numurkah Library in Numurkah (26 min).
