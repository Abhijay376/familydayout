Our data lists 4 playgrounds and 7 parks and reserves in Cobram, in the Moira area. Cobram also has a library, a cinema, 13 beaches and a skate park.

Landmarks

Cobram Regional Park is the largest mapped park in Cobram, at about 472 hectares. Kennedy Park and Barooga Botanical Gardens are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Cobram include Apex Park, Mivo Park and Colgan Park. Playgrounds around Cobram include the playground at Federation Park, the playground at Cobram Regional Park and the playground at Barooga Botanical Gardens. Our data shows 13 public toilets in Cobram. Places to visit in Cobram include Cobram Cinema, Barooga Library, Cobrooga Beach and Davies Beach. For older kids in Cobram there's the skate park at Federation Park. Cobram village is the local shopping centre. Our data lists 7 cafes and family restaurants in Cobram for a lunch stop.

Getting around

There's no train station in Cobram. The closest to Cobram is Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line), about 57 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Cobram Town Service route stop in Cobram. V/Line coaches stop in Cobram. By car from Cobram, Shepparton is about 57 minutes away and Benalla about 68 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cobram is Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah, about 35 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Tunzafun in Yarrawonga is about 34 minutes' drive from Cobram. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 57 minutes from Cobram by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Cobram's 4 local playgrounds. Rainy days at the library and cinema in Cobram. Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at Federation Park in Cobram. Beach days at Cobrooga Beach and Davies Beach.

Nearby suburbs

Koonoomoo, next to Cobram, has 1 playground, a library and a beach. Cobram East, next to Cobram, has a beach.
