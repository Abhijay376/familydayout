Skate park at Federation Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Cobram · Basic info

Skate park at Federation Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Cobram, in the Moira Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Cobram Station/Punt Rd (coach) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 189 m away.
