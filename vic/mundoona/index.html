Mundoona in the Moira area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Mundoona is Mundoona Bushland Reserve, covering about 20 hectares.

Getting around

Mundoona has no train station; Shepparton Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Mundoona, so most trips start by car. By car from Mundoona, Shepparton is about 36 minutes away, Echuca about 65 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah is about 17 minutes' drive from Mundoona. For a library, Numurkah Library in Numurkah is about 19 minutes' drive from Mundoona. The closest beach to Mundoona is Canoe Club Boat Launch Site in Shepparton, about 38 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Riverland Skate Park in Numurkah is about 18 minutes' drive from Mundoona. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Shepparton is about 36 minutes' drive from Mundoona. The closest indoor play centre to Mundoona is StarBowl in Shepparton, about 36 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Mundoona is Kidstown in Mooroopna, about 38 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Shepparton, about 36 minutes from Mundoona by car, with 47 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Mundoona, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Bunbartha Recreation Reserve in Bunbartha (16 min), Numurkah Swimming Centre in Numurkah (17 min), Riverland Skate Park in Numurkah (18 min), Numurkah Library in Numurkah (19 min), the playground at Larsen Reserve in Tallygaroopna (20 min) and Barmah Forest Heritge and Education Centre in Nathalia (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Mundoona.

Nearby suburbs

Numurkah, next to Mundoona, has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Tallygaroopna, next to Mundoona, has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Mundoona, Bunbartha has 1 playground. Waaia and Kaarimba also border Mundoona.
