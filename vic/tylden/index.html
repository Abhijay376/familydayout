Getting around

There's no train station in Tylden. The closest to Tylden is Kyneton Railway Station (V/Line), about 8.3 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Bus 4 stops in Tylden. V/Line coaches stop in Tylden. By car from Tylden, Kyneton is about 14 minutes away, Daylesford about 32 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 71 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tylden is Gisborne Aquatic Centre in Gisborne, about 26 minutes' drive. For a library, Kyneton Library in Kyneton is about 14 minutes' drive from Tylden. The closest skate park to Tylden is Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton, about 12 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Reading Cinema in Sunbury is about 39 minutes' drive from Tylden. Kyneton is the closest bigger centre to Tylden, about 14 minutes away by car, and has 10 playgrounds and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Tylden are Kyneton Velodrome in Kyneton (12 min), Bolton Reserve Lane Playground in Kyneton (13 min), Kyneton Community Park in Kyneton (13 min), Kyneton Library in Kyneton (14 min), Little Gallery in Trentham (11 min) and Kyneton Museum in Kyneton (14 min).

Best for

Beach days at Canoeing.

Nearby suburbs

Trentham, next to Tylden, has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Tylden, Woodend has 1 playground. Kyneton South, Fern Hill, Carlsruhe and Ashbourne also border Tylden.
