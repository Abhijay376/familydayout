Playground on Main Road

Playground in Buchan · Basic info

Playground on Main Road in Buchan is a public playground in the East Gippsland Shire area. We haven't found a cafe or trail within a short walk, so it's worth packing snacks. There's a public toilet right by the playground, and bus 12 stops at Buchan - Orbost Rd/Buchan Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
