Playground at Ferndale Reserve

Playground in Frankston · Basic info

Playground at Ferndale Reserve in Frankston is a public playground in the Frankston City area. The Peninsula Link Trail passes about 140 m away, and Donut King is about 440 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet with a baby change table about 660 m away, and buses 789, 790 and 791 stop at Karingal Hub SC/Cranbourne Rd, a 4-minute walk away.
