Karingal Hub Shopping Centre

Shopping centre in Frankston · Official details

Karingal Hub Shopping Centre is a shopping centre in Frankston, in the Frankston City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Karingal Hub SC/Karingal Dr (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 233 m away with a baby change table.
