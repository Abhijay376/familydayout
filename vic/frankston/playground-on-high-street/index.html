Playground on High Street

Playground in Frankston · Basic info

Playground on High Street is a public playground in Frankston, in the Frankston City area. The Kananook Creek Walk passes about 280 m away, and Frankston Beach is about 150 m away. There's a public toilet with a baby change table right by the playground, and buses 781, 784, 785 and 788 stop at Frankston Pier/Nepean Hwy, a 3-minute walk away.
