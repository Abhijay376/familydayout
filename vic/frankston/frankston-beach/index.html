Frankston Beach

Beach in Frankston · Basic info

Frankston Beach is a beach in Frankston, in the Frankston City Council area, good for all ages. Fernery Lane/Nepean Hwy (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 136 m away.
