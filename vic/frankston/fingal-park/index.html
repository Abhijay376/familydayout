Fingal Park

Park in Frankston · Basic info

Fingal Park is a park in Frankston, in the Frankston City Council area, good for all ages. Amber Ave/Skye Rd (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 984 m away with a baby change table.
