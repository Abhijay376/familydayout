Ballam Park Homestead

Museum or gallery in Frankston · Official details

Ballam Park Homestead is a museum or gallery in Frankston, in the Frankston City Council area, best for school-age kids. Ballam Park/Cranbourne Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 55 m away with a baby change table. Opening hours: The Tea Rooms are open every Sunday from 12pm – 4pm.; Guided tours through the house and museum available every Sunday from 12pm – 3pm.
