Playground at O'Grady Avenue Reserve

Playground in Frankston · Basic info

Playground at O'Grady Avenue Reserve is a public playground in Frankston, in the Frankston City area. The Kananook Creek Walk passes about 260 m away, and Muffin Break is about 370 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 440 m away, and buses 779 and 780 stop at O'Grady Ave/Nepean Hwy, a 2-minute walk away.
