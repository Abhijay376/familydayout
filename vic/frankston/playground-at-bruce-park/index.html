Playground at Bruce Park

Playground in Frankston · Basic info

Playground at Bruce Park in Frankston is a public playground in the Frankston City area. For food, Sawadekaa is about 320 m away, and Playground on Sycamore Road is another option about 580 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 400 m from the playground, and bus 775 stops at Bruce Park/Bondi Ave, a 3-minute walk away.
