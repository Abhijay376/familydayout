Frankston Power Centre

Shopping centre in Frankston · Official details

Frankston Power Centre is a shopping centre in Frankston, in the Frankston City Council area, handy for a rainy day or a quick stop. Frankston Power Centre SC/Cranbourne Rd (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 105 m away with a baby change table.
