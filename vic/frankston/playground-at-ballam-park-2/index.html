Playground at Ballam Park

Playground in Frankston · Basic info

This is a public playground in Frankston. Zone Bowling is about 340 m away, and Ballam Park Homestead is about 200 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 110 m from the playground, and buses 789, 790 and 791 stop at Ballam Park/Cranbourne Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
