Playground on Karingal Drive

Playground in Frankston · Basic info

Playground on Karingal Drive is a public playground in Frankston, in the Frankston City area. The Peninsula Link Trail is about 320 m away, and Ballam Park Homestead is about 480 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 260 m from the playground, and buses 770, 771 and 777 stop at Karingal Hub SC/Karingal Dr, a 2-minute walk away.
