Bathumi is in the Moira area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Bathumi, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Bathumi has no train station; Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 42 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Bathumi, so most trips start by car. By car from Bathumi, Wangaratta is about 39 minutes away and Benalla about 60 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Bathumi is Rutherglen War Memorial Swimming Pool in Rutherglen, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest library to Bathumi is Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga, about 8 minutes' drive. For a beach, Forges Beach No 2 in Yarrawonga is about 18 minutes' drive from Bathumi. For a skate park, the pump track on Victoria Street in Wahgunyah is about 32 minutes' drive from Bathumi. For a cinema, Cobram Cinema in Cobram is about 37 minutes' drive from Bathumi. For a zoo or wildlife park, Tunzafun in Yarrawonga is about 10 minutes' drive from Bathumi. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Bathumi, about 39 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Bathumi, the nearest places for kids by road are Yarrawonga Library in Yarrawonga (8 min), Tunzafun in Yarrawonga (10 min), Yarrawonga Mulwala Pioneer Museum in Yarrawonga (11 min), Lonsdale Reserve Playground in Yarrawonga (12 min), Mulwala Waterslide in Yarrawonga (12 min) and Swimming Pool Reserve in Yarrawonga (12 min).
