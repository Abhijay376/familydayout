Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Auchmore in the Loddon area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Auchmore has no train station; Raywood Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 13 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Auchmore, so most trips start by car. By car from Auchmore, Bendigo is about 48 minutes away, Castlemaine about 76 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Auchmore is Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood, about 25 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Auchmore is McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk, about 40 minutes' drive. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Auchmore, about 48 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Auchmore are Raywood Aquatic Centre in Raywood (25 min), McKern Skate Park in Eaglehawk (40 min), Peter Krenz Leisure Centre in Eaglehawk (40 min), Eaglehawk Playspace in Eaglehawk (40 min), the playground at Canterbury Park in Eaglehawk (40 min) and the bmx track on Victoria Street in Eaglehawk (41 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Auchmore.

Nearby suburbs

Raywood, next to Auchmore, has 1 playground and a pool. Pompapiel and Bridgewater North also border Auchmore.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Auchmore, without traffic.
