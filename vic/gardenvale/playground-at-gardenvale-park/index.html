Playground at Gardenvale Park

Playground in Gardenvale · Basic info

Playground at Gardenvale Park in Gardenvale is a public playground in the Glen Eira City area. The Nepean Highway Trail passes about 380 m away, and KFC is about 450 m away if you need food. There's a public toilet about 490 m away, and bus 630 stops at Magnolia Rd/Gardenvale Rd, a 3-minute walk away.
