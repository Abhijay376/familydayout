Gardenvale Park

Park in Gardenvale · Official details

Gardenvale Park is a park in Gardenvale, in the Glen Eira City Council area, good for all ages. Magnolia Rd/Gardenvale Rd (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 499 m away.

Details

Good to know
Facilities: BBQs, Picnic shelter (Council)

Questions parents ask

Where is Gardenvale Park?
Gardenvale Park is in Gardenvale, Victoria.
Is Gardenvale Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Are there toilets at Gardenvale Park?
The nearest public toilet is about 499 m away.
How do I get to Gardenvale Park by public transport?
The closest stop is Magnolia Rd/Gardenvale Rd (routes 630), a 3-minute walk.
