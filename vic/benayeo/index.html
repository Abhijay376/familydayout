Benayeo in the West Wimmera area has 4 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Mullinger Swamp Wildlife Reserve (no hunting) is the largest mapped park in Benayeo, at about 31 hectares. Mullinger Swamp Conservation Park and Tallageira Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Benayeo include Benayeo Recreation Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Benayeo. The closest to Benayeo is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 179 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Benayeo, so most trips start by car. By car from Benayeo, Horsham is about 2 hours away and Hamilton about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest places for kids by road from Benayeo are the playground at War Memorial Park in Apsley (12 min), the playground at Lions Park in Edenhope (29 min), Dachshund Museum & Gallery in Edenhope (29 min) and West Wimmera Community Association - Red Tail Gallery in Edenhope (29 min).
