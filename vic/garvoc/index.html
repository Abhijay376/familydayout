Getting around

Garvoc has no train station; Terang Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 11 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Garvoc, so most trips start by car. By car from Garvoc, Warrnambool is about 30 minutes away, Colac about 65 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Garvoc is Deep Blue Hot Springs Sanctuary in Warrnambool, about 33 minutes' drive. For a library, Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool is about 32 minutes' drive from Garvoc. The closest beach to Garvoc is Buttress Point in The Cove, about 37 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Garvoc is Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake, about 31 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Garvoc is Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool, about 31 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, The Bowl in Warrnambool is about 31 minutes' drive from Garvoc. For a zoo or wildlife park, Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee is about 17 minutes' drive from Garvoc. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Garvoc, about 30 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Garvoc are Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee (17 min), Grieve Street Playground in Warrnambool (28 min), the playground at Bushfield Recreation Reserve in Bushfield (32 min), SCOPE Galleries in Warrnambool (30 min), Buttress Point in The Cove (37 min) and Flaxman Beach in Nullawarre (39 min).
