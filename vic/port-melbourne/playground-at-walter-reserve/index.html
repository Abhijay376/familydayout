Playground at Walter Reserve

Playground in Port Melbourne · Basic info

Playground at Walter Reserve is a public playground in Port Melbourne, in the Port Phillip City area. The Sandridge Rail Trail is about 10 m away, and Port Melbourne Library is about 330 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 540 m from the playground, and bus 606 stops at Evans St/Bridge St, a 1-minute walk away.
