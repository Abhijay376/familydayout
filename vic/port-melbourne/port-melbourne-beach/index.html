Port Melbourne Beach

Beach in Port Melbourne · Basic info

Port Melbourne Beach is a beach in Port Melbourne, in the Port Phillip City Council area, good for all ages. Port Melbourne Station/Beach St (bus) is a 4-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 255 m away.
