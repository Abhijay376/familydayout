Our data lists 3 playgrounds and 3 parks and reserves in Ironbark, in the Greater Bendigo area.

Landmarks

Victoria Hill Diggings Historic and Cultural Features Reserve is the largest mapped park in Ironbark, at about 15 hectares. Garden Gully Recreation Reserve and South Garden Gully Recreation Reserve are the next largest. Playgrounds in Ironbark include Garden Gully Recreation Reserve Playspace. Our data lists 2 cafes and family restaurants in Ironbark for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Ironbark has no train station; Bendigo Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 2.1 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 53, 54 and 55 stop in Ironbark. By car from Ironbark, Bendigo is about 3 minutes away, Castlemaine about 33 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Faith Leech Aquatic Centre in Bendigo is about 3 minutes' drive from Ironbark. The closest library to Ironbark is Bendigo Library in Bendigo, about 4 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Long Gully BMX Track in Long Gully is about 2 minutes' drive from Ironbark. For a cinema, Bendigo Cinemas in Bendigo is about 4 minutes' drive from Ironbark. The closest indoor play centre to Ironbark is Intagolf in Golden Gully, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Ironbark is The Zone Fun Park in East Bendigo, about 8 minutes' drive. Bendigo is the closest bigger centre to Ironbark, about 3 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Ironbark's 3 local playgrounds.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Ironbark, Bendigo has 4 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Ironbark, Long Gully has 4 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Ironbark, Golden Square has 4 playgrounds.
