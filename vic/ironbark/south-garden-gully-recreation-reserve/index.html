South Garden Gully Recreation Reserve

Park in Ironbark · Basic info

South Garden Gully Recreation Reserve is a park in Ironbark, in the Greater Bendigo City Council area, good for all ages. Don St/Eaglehawk Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 944 m away with a baby change table.
