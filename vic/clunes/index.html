Clunes in the Hepburn area has 2 playgrounds and 9 parks and reserves in our data. Clunes also has a pool and 3 museums or galleries.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Clunes is Merin Merin and Middle Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 499 hectares. Dunach Nature Conservation Reserve and Clunes Common Nature Conservation Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Clunes include Pinetum, Esmond Park and Queens Park. Playgrounds in Clunes include the playground at Queens Park. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Clunes. Places to visit in Clunes include Clunes Museum, Lee Medlyn Bottle Museum and Clunes Swimming Pool. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Clunes for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Clunes has a train station: Clunes Railway Station (V/Line: Maryborough - Melbourne via Ballarat). Buses on the Ballarat - Maryborough route stop in Clunes. V/Line coaches stop in Clunes. By car from Clunes, Ballarat is about 30 minutes away, Maryborough about 31 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.
