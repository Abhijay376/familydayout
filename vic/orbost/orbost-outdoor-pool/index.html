Orbost Outdoor Pool

Pool or water play in Orbost · Official details

Orbost Outdoor Pool is a pool or water play in Orbost, in the East Gippsland Shire Council area, good for all ages. Nicholson St/Wolseley St (coach) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 382 m away. Opening hours: 9.00am – 5.00pm (Closed Christmas Day and Good Friday. Shorter hours on Anzac Day.); Free entry to the pool every Friday from 3:30pm - 6:45pm.; Wednesday mornings 9.00am -10:00am; Thursday 8:00am - 8:45am.
