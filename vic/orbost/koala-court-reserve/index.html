Koala Court Reserve

Park in Orbost · Basic info

Koala Court Reserve is a park in Orbost, in the East Gippsland Shire Council area, good for all ages. Nicholson St/Wolseley St (coach) is a 20-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 553 m away.
