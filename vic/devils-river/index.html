Devils River is in the Murrindindi area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Devils River, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

Our data shows 9 public toilets in Devils River. The Wallaby Bay Track, Bolte Bay Walking Track and Coller Bay Walking/Bicycle Track all pass through Devils River.

Getting around

There's no train station in Devils River. The closest to Devils River is Euroa Railway Station (V/Line), about 57 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Devils River, so most trips start by car. By car from Devils River, Mansfield is about 66 minutes away, Seymour about 69 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Devils River is Eildon Swimming Pool in Eildon, about 8 minutes' drive. For a library, Alexandra Library in Alexandra is about 12 minutes' drive from Devils River. The closest skate park to Devils River is the skate park at Yea railway station Historic & Cultural Features Res in Yea, about 40 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Devils River are Eildon Swimming Pool in Eildon (8 min), Baanh ba Djila Splash Park in Eildon (9 min), the playground at W.J Moore Park in Eildon (9 min), Alexandra Outdoor Swimming Pool in Alexandra (12 min), Alexandra Timber Tramway and Museum in Alexandra (13 min) and Alexandra Library in Alexandra (12 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Devils River on the Wallaby Bay Track.

Nearby suburbs

Alexandra, next to Devils River, has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Bonnie Doon, next to Devils River, has 1 playground. Maintongoon and Lake Eildon also border Devils River.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Devils River, without traffic.
