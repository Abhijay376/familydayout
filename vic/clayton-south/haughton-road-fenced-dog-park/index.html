Haughton Road Fenced Dog Park

Park in Clayton South · Basic info

Haughton Road Fenced Dog Park is a park in Clayton South, in the Kingston City Council area, good for all ages. Moriah St/Centre Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 704 m away with a baby change table.
