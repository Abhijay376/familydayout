Our data lists 12 playgrounds and 12 parks and reserves in Clayton South, in the Kingston area. On top of that, Clayton South has a pool and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Clayton South is Heatherton Park, covering about 22 hectares. Namatjira Park and Deals Road Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Clayton South include Clayton Urban Park, Jackson Green Park and The Grange Reserve. Playgrounds in Clayton South include the playground at Namatjira Park, the playground at Heatherton Park and the playground at Jackson Green Park. Our data shows 7 public toilets in Clayton South. In Clayton South you'll also find The Little Swim School. For older kids in Clayton South there's the skate park at Namatjira Park. The Djerring Trail passes through Clayton South. Our data lists 13 cafes and family restaurants in Clayton South for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Westall Railway Station (metro: Cranbourne Line and Pakenham Line) in Clayton South. Buses 631, 703, 704, 705, 733, 811, 824, 978 and 979 stop in Clayton South. By car from Clayton South, Dandenong is about 16 minutes away, Box Hill about 22 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 27 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Clarinda Library in Clarinda is about 5 minutes' drive from Clayton South. The closest beach to Clayton South is Mordialloc Beach in Parkdale, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Clayton South is Village Cinemas in Clayton, about 9 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Småland in Springvale is about 8 minutes' drive from Clayton South. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale is about 13 minutes' drive from Clayton South.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 12 playgrounds across Clayton South. Teens who skate or ride, with the skate park at Namatjira Park in Clayton South. Free days on the Djerring Trail and in Clayton South's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Clayton, next to Clayton South, has 7 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Clayton South, Springvale has 16 playgrounds and a library. Next door to Clayton South, Dingley Village has 18 playgrounds and a skate park. Clarinda, next to Clayton South, has 6 playgrounds and a library. Springvale South also borders Clayton South.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Clayton South, without traffic.
