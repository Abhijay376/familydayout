Clayton Urban Park

Park in Clayton South · Basic info

Clayton Urban Park is a park in Clayton South, in the Kingston City Council area, good for all ages. Centre Rd/Haughton Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 666 m away.
