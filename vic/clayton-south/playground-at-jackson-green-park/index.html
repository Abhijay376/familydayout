Playground at Jackson Green Park

Playground in Clayton South · Basic info

Playground at Jackson Green Park in Clayton South is a public playground in the Kingston City area. The Djerring Trail is about 100 m away, and for food, For Heaven's Steaks is about 220 m away. You'll find a public toilet with a baby change table about 640 m from the playground, and bus 824 stops at Centre Rd/Haughton Rd, a 2-minute walk away.
