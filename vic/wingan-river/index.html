Wingan River in the East Gippsland area has 8 parks and reserves in our data. Beyond the parks, Wingan River has 13 beaches.

Landmarks

Sandpatch Wilderness Zone is the largest mapped park in Wingan River, at about 15,434 hectares. East Gippsland Coastal streams Natural Catchment Area and Rame Head Remote and Natural Area are the next largest. Wingan River also has Alfred National Park, Baawang Reference Area and Seal Creek Reference Area. Our data shows 2 public toilets in Wingan River. In Wingan River you'll also find Rame Head 1 and Rame Head 2. For walks and rides, Heathland Walking Track, Seal Creek Walk and Sandpatch Link cross Wingan River.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wingan River. The closest to Wingan River is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 164 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wingan River, so most trips start by car. By car from Wingan River, Lakes Entrance is about 2 hours away and Bairnsdale about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a skate park, the skate park at Mick Baum Park in Cann River is about 24 minutes' drive from Wingan River. From Wingan River, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Mick Baum Park in Cann River (24 min), the skate park at Mick Baum Park in Cann River (24 min), Davis Creek Beach in Mallacoota (34 min), Betka Beach in Mallacoota (34 min) and WWII Bunker Museum in Mallacoota (36 min).

Best for

Beach days at Rame Head 1 and Rame Head 2. Free days on the Heathland Walking Track and in Wingan River's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Wingan River, Tamboon has a beach. Mallacoota, next to Wingan River, has a beach. Next door to Wingan River, Genoa has 1 playground. Noorinbee also borders Wingan River.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Wingan River, without traffic.
