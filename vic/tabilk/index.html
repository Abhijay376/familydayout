Our data lists 5 parks and reserves in Tabilk, in the Strathbogie area.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Tabilk is Tabilk Lagoon Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 138 hectares. Hughes Creek Hill Bushland Reserve and Tabilk Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Tabilk also has Wormangal Creek B.R and Mitchellstown Streamside Reserve. For walks and rides, Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks crosses Tabilk.

Getting around

Tabilk has no train station; Nagambie Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 7.0 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tabilk, so most trips start by car. By car from Tabilk, Seymour is about 21 minutes away, Shepparton about 51 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tabilk is Nagambie Pool in Nagambie, about 10 minutes' drive. For a library, Nagambie Library in Nagambie is about 12 minutes' drive from Tabilk. For a skate park, Seymour Skate Park in Seymour is about 22 minutes' drive from Tabilk. The closest cinema to Tabilk is Euroa Community Cinema in Euroa, about 44 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Seymour, about 21 minutes from Tabilk by car, with 10 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Tabilk, the nearest places for kids by road are Nagambie Recreation Reserve Playground in Nagambie (10 min), Nagambie Pool in Nagambie (10 min), Blayney Reserve Playground in Nagambie (11 min), Nagambie Historical Society in Nagambie (11 min), Nagambie Library in Nagambie (12 min) and Memorial Swimming pool in Seymour (20 min).

Best for

Free days on the Yellowbelly Trail - Tracks and in Tabilk's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Tabilk, Nagambie has 5 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Tabilk, Avenel has 1 playground. Mangalore and Locksley also border Tabilk.
