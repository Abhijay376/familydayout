Wychitella is in the Loddon area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Ngulumbarra Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Wychitella, at about 346 hectares. Buckrabanyule Nature Conservation Reserve is the next largest.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wychitella. The closest to Wychitella is Pyramid Railway Station (V/Line), about 52 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wychitella, so most trips start by car. By car from Wychitella, Bendigo is about 84 minutes away and Maryborough about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn is about 22 minutes' drive from Wychitella. For a cinema, Rex Theatre in Charlton is about 26 minutes' drive from Wychitella. From Wychitella, the nearest places for kids by road are Coach House Gallery & Museum in Wedderburn (22 min), Wedderburn Swimming Pool in Wedderburn (22 min), Rex Theatre in Charlton (26 min), Golden Grains Museum in Charlton (26 min), the playground at Rotary Park in Charlton (27 min) and the playground at Gordon Park in Charlton (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wychitella.

Nearby suburbs

Buckrabanyule and Borung also border Wychitella.
