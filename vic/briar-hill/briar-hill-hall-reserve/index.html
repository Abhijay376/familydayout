Briar Hill Hall Reserve

Park in Briar Hill · Basic info

Briar Hill Hall Reserve is a park in Briar Hill, in the Banyule City Council area, good for all ages. Community Hall/Mountain View Rd (bus) is a 1-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 1238 m away.
