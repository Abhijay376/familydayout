Boundary Bend in the Swan Hill area has 1 playground and 2 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Passage Camp Nature Conservation Reserve is the largest mapped park in Boundary Bend, at about 21 hectares. Boundary Bend Memorial Park is the next largest. Playgrounds around Boundary Bend include the playground at Boundary Bend Memorial Park. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Boundary Bend.

Getting around

Boundary Bend has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 78 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Boundary Bend. By car from Boundary Bend, Swan Hill is about 71 minutes away and Mildura about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a skate park, Robinvale Skatepark in Robinvale is about 38 minutes' drive from Boundary Bend. The closest places for kids by road from Boundary Bend are the playground at Pisasale Park in Robinvale (36 min), Rural Life Museum in Robinvale (36 min), Robinvale Skatepark in Robinvale (38 min) and the bmx track at Piangil Memorial Park in Piangil (39 min).
