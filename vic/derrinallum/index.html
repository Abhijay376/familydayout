Derrinallum is in the Corangamite area, and our data lists 6 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Derrinallum has a library.

Landmarks

Logans Lake Lake Reserve is the largest mapped park in Derrinallum, at about 296 hectares. Unnamed P0245 Private Nature Reserve and Deep Lake Lake Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Derrinallum include Derrinallum Town Park, Lake Koonangurt Lake Reserve and Derrinallum Recreation Reserve. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Derrinallum. In Derrinallum you'll also find Derrinallum Library.

Getting around

Derrinallum has no train station; Camperdown Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 32 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Derrinallum. By car from Derrinallum, Colac is about 65 minutes away, Ballarat about 71 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Derrinallum is Lismore Pool in Lismore, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Derrinallum is Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown, about 37 minutes' drive. From Derrinallum, the nearest places for kids by road are Lismore Pool in Lismore (11 min), Camperdown Skate Park in Camperdown (37 min), the playground at Cressy Picnic Area in Cressy (33 min), Market Square Playground in Mortlake (32 min), Mortlake Community Centre in Mortlake (33 min) and Mortlake Skate Park in Mortlake (34 min).

Best for

Rainy days at the library in Derrinallum.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Derrinallum, Lismore has a pool. Kariah, Darlington and Bookaar also border Derrinallum.
