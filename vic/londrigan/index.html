Londrigan is in the Wangaratta area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Londrigan, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

The Murray to Mountains Rail Trail: Wangaratta – Bright passes through Londrigan.

Getting around

There's no train station in Londrigan. The closest to Londrigan is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 9.3 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Londrigan, so most trips start by car. By car from Londrigan, Wangaratta is about 15 minutes away and Benalla about 42 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta is about 16 minutes' drive from Londrigan. For a library, Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta is about 13 minutes' drive from Londrigan. The closest beach to Londrigan is Sandy Beach in Beechworth, about 28 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Londrigan is the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta, about 16 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Londrigan is Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta, about 12 minutes' drive. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Londrigan, about 15 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Londrigan, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Apex Park in Wangaratta (11 min), the playground at Gunhouse Park Reserve in Eldorado (10 min), Eldorado Museum in Eldorado (10 min), Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta (12 min), Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (13 min) and Wangaratta Indoor Sports & Aquatic Centre in Wangaratta (16 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Londrigan on the Murray to Mountains Rail Trail: Wangaratta – Bright.

Nearby suburbs

Eldorado, next to Londrigan, has 1 playground. Byawatha also borders Londrigan.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Londrigan, without traffic.
