Cranbourne South is in the Casey area, and our data lists 5 playgrounds and 1 park or reserve in the suburb. On top of that, Cranbourne South has a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Cranbourne South is Morning Mist Recreation Reserve, covering about 15 hectares. Playgrounds around Cranbourne South include Brompton Playground, Brompton Playground and Brompton Playground. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Cranbourne South. For older kids in Cranbourne South there's Pump Track.

Getting around

Cranbourne South has no train station; Cranbourne Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 7.2 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 791 and 792 stop in Cranbourne South. By car from Cranbourne South, Cranbourne is about 12 minutes away, Frankston about 17 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 48 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Cranbourne South is Kingswim Langwarrin in Langwarrin, about 11 minutes' drive. The closest library to Cranbourne South is Cranbourne Library in Cranbourne East, about 13 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Cranbourne South is Frankston Beach in Frankston, about 19 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Frankston is about 15 minutes' drive from Cranbourne South. For an indoor play centre, Zone Bowling in Frankston is about 14 minutes' drive from Cranbourne South. For a zoo or wildlife park, Moonlit Sanctuary in Pearcedale is about 8 minutes' drive from Cranbourne South.

Best for

Younger kids at Cranbourne South's 5 local playgrounds. Teens who skate or ride, with Pump Track in Cranbourne South.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Cranbourne South, Langwarrin has 22 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Cranbourne South, Cranbourne has 27 playgrounds and a skate park. Next door to Cranbourne South, Cranbourne West has 21 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Skye, next to Cranbourne South, has 7 playgrounds. Pearcedale, Botanic Ridge and Devon Meadows also border Cranbourne South.
