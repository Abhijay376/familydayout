Pump Track

Skate, BMX or pump track in Cranbourne South · Basic info

Pump Track is a skate, bmx or pump track in Cranbourne South, in the Casey City Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Stanhill Dr/Cranbourne-Frankston Rd (bus) is a 10-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 491 m away with a baby change table.
