Cannie is in the Gannawarra area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Unnamed P0325 Private Nature Reserve is the largest mapped park in Cannie, at about 2,759 hectares.

Getting around

Cannie has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 47 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Cannie, so most trips start by car. By car from Cannie, Swan Hill is about 65 minutes away and Echuca about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Quambatook Memorial Pool in Quambatook is about 19 minutes' drive from Cannie. The closest library to Cannie is Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook, about 18 minutes' drive. The closest places for kids by road from Cannie are Quambatook Historical Centre in Quambatook (18 min), Gannawarra Library Service - Quambatook Branch in Quambatook (18 min), Quambatook Lions Club Playground in Quambatook (18 min), Quambatook Memorial Pool in Quambatook (19 min) and the playground at Culgoa Memorial Park in Culgoa (40 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Cannie.

Nearby suburbs

Quambatook, next to Cannie, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Budgerum East and Beauchamp also border Cannie.
