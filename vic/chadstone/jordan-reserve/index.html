Jordan Reserve

Park in Chadstone · Basic info

Jordan Reserve is a park in Chadstone, in the Monash City Council area, good for all ages. Burton St/Huntingdale Rd (bus) is a 2-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 745 m away.
