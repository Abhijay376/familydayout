Batesford Reserve

Park in Chadstone · Basic info

Batesford Reserve is a park in Chadstone, in the Monash City Council area, good for all ages. Batesford Reserve/Power Ave (bus) is a 3-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 24 m away with a baby change table.
