Our data lists 2 parks and reserves in Wonnangatta, in the Alpine area.

Landmarks

Wonnangatta River Reference Area is the largest mapped park in Wonnangatta, at about 489 hectares. Other parks and reserves in Wonnangatta include Rileys Hut. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Wonnangatta. Picnic areas in Wonnangatta include Rileys Hut. The Australian Alps Walking Track, Selwyn Track and Blue Hills Track all pass through Wonnangatta.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wonnangatta. The closest to Wonnangatta is Stratford Railway Station (V/Line), about 95 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wonnangatta, so most trips start by car. By car from Wonnangatta, Bright is about 4 hours away and Wangaratta about 4 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Free days on the Australian Alps Walking Track and in Wonnangatta's parks.
