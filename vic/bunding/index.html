Bunding is in the Moorabool area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Bunding also has Werribee River Picnic Area. Picnic areas in Bunding include Werribee River Picnic Area. For walks and rides, Werribee River Walk and indistinct track cross Bunding.

Getting around

There's no train station in Bunding. The closest to Bunding is Ballan Railway Station (V/Line), about 7.7 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. Buses on the Ballan - Hepburn via Daylesford route stop in Bunding. By car from Bunding, Daylesford is about 22 minutes away, Bacchus Marsh about 24 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 68 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Ballan Swimming Pool in Ballan is about 9 minutes' drive from Bunding. For a library, Daylesford Library in Daylesford is about 22 minutes' drive from Bunding. For a beach, Lionhead Beach in Pentland Hills is about 20 minutes' drive from Bunding. The closest skate park to Bunding is the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan, about 9 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Bunding is Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central, about 31 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 32 minutes' drive from Bunding. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kryal Castle in Leigh Creek is about 23 minutes' drive from Bunding. The nearest larger centre is Daylesford, about 22 minutes from Bunding by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool and a library. From Bunding, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at McLean Recreation Reserve in Ballan (8 min), the playground at Ballan Recreation Reserve in Ballan (9 min), the skate park at Mill Park in Ballan (9 min), Ballan Swimming Pool in Ballan (9 min), Daylesford Swimming Pool in Daylesford (22 min) and Daylesford Library in Daylesford (22 min).

Best for

Free days on the Werribee River Walk and in Bunding's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Ballan, next to Bunding, has 4 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Next door to Bunding, Gordon has 2 playgrounds. Spargo Creek, next to Bunding, has 1 playground. Bolwarrah and Blakeville also border Bunding.
