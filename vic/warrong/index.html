Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Warrong in the Moyne area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Warrong has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 24 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Warrong, so most trips start by car. By car from Warrong, Warrnambool is about 29 minutes away and Hamilton about 57 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Warrong is Hawkesdale Swimming Pool in Hawkesdale, about 10 minutes' drive. For a library, Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool is about 30 minutes' drive from Warrong. The closest beach to Warrong is Reef Point Beach in Port Fairy, about 14 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Koroit Youth Space in Koroit is about 12 minutes' drive from Warrong. The closest cinema to Warrong is Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy, about 21 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, The Bowl in Warrnambool is about 30 minutes' drive from Warrong. For a zoo or wildlife park, Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee is about 42 minutes' drive from Warrong. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Warrong, about 29 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Warrong, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Kirkstall Recreation Reserve in Kirkstall (6 min), Hawkesdale Swimming Pool in Hawkesdale (10 min), Koroit Youth Space in Koroit (12 min), Reef Point Beach in Port Fairy (14 min), Killarney Beach in Killarney (18 min) and Reardon Theatre in Port Fairy (21 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Warrong.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Warrong, Hawkesdale has a pool. Koroit, next to Warrong, has 1 playground and a skate park. Kirkstall, next to Warrong, has 1 playground. Willatook and Tarrone also border Warrong.
