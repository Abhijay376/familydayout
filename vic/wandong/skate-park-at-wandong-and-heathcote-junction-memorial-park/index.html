Skate park at Wandong and Heathcote Junction Memorial Park

Skate, BMX or pump track in Wandong · Basic info

Skate park at Wandong and Heathcote Junction Memorial Park is a skate, bmx or pump track in Wandong, in the Mitchell Shire Council area, best for older kids and teens on scooters, bikes or boards. Wandong Station/Rail St (coach) is a 8-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 158 m away.
