Our data lists 1 playground and 1 park or reserve in Wandong, in the Mitchell area. Beyond the parks, Wandong has a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Wandong is Wandong and Heathcote Junction Memorial Park, covering about 1 hectares. Playgrounds around Wandong include L B Davern Reserve Playground. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Wandong. For older kids in Wandong there's the skate park at Wandong and Heathcote Junction Memorial Park. Wandong Plaza is the local shopping centre. For walks and rides, Say G'day Trail crosses Wandong.

Getting around

Trains stop at Heathcote Junction Railway Station (V/Line: Seymour - Melbourne via Broadmeadows and Shepparton - Melbourne via Seymour) and Wandong Railway Station (V/Line: Seymour - Melbourne via Broadmeadows and Shepparton - Melbourne via Seymour) in Wandong. V/Line coaches stop in Wandong. By car from Wandong, Seymour is about 34 minutes away, Broadmeadows about 40 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 58 minutes, without traffic.
