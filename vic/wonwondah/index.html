Wonwondah is in the Horsham area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Wonwondah is Kingcourt Swamp Wildlife Reserve (hunting), covering about 34 hectares. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Wonwondah.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wonwondah. The closest to Wonwondah is Ararat Railway Station (V/Line), about 82 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wonwondah, so most trips start by car. By car from Wonwondah, Horsham is about 20 minutes away and Stawell about 60 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham is about 19 minutes' drive from Wonwondah. The closest skate park to Wonwondah is Velodrome in Horsham, about 19 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Wonwondah is Centre Cinemas in Horsham, about 20 minutes' drive. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to Wonwondah, about 20 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Wonwondah are the playground at Uebergang Park in Horsham (19 min), Velodrome in Horsham (19 min), Horsham Aquatic Centre in Horsham (19 min), Horsham Water Play in Horsham (20 min), Centre Cinemas in Horsham (20 min) and Horsham Skate Park in Horsham (20 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Wonwondah.
