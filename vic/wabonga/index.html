Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Wabonga in the Wangaratta area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 4 public toilets in Wabonga. For walks and rides, Australian Alps Walking Track, Queen Spur Road and Bennies Walking Track cross Wabonga.

Getting around

There's no train station in Wabonga. The closest to Wabonga is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 81 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Wabonga, so most trips start by car. By car from Wabonga, Wangaratta is about 2.5 hours away and Mansfield about 3 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Wabonga on the Australian Alps Walking Track.

Nearby suburbs

Mount Buller, next to Wabonga, has a cinema. Tolmie, next to Wabonga, has 1 playground. Merrijig, Howitt Plains, Cheshunt South and Abbeyard also border Wabonga.
