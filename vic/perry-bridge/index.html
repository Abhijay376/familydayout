Perry Bridge is in the Wellington area, and our data lists 9 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

Providence Ponds Flora and Fauna Reserve is the largest mapped park in Perry Bridge, at about 2,535 hectares. Unnamed P0190 Private Nature Reserve and Unnamed P0207 Private Nature Reserve are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Perry Bridge include Yeerung Bushland Reserve, Avon River Streamside Reserve and Meerlieu I15 Bushland Reserve.

Getting around

There's no train station in Perry Bridge. The closest to Perry Bridge is Stratford Railway Station (V/Line), about 18 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Perry Bridge, so most trips start by car. By car from Perry Bridge, Sale is about 29 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 34 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford is about 16 minutes' drive from Perry Bridge. For a library, Sale Library in Sale is about 30 minutes' drive from Perry Bridge. The closest skate park to Perry Bridge is Skate Park in Stratford, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest cinema to Perry Bridge is Roulette Cinema in East Sale, about 32 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Bairnsdale Archery, Mini Golf, Games Park in Lucknow is about 42 minutes' drive from Perry Bridge. The nearest larger centre is Sale, about 29 minutes from Perry Bridge by car, with 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Perry Bridge, the nearest places for kids by road are Skate Park in Stratford (15 min), Market Reserve Playground in Stratford (15 min), Soldiers Memorial Park Playground in Stratford (15 min), Stratford Swimming Pool in Stratford (16 min), Roulette Cinema in East Sale (32 min) and Former Drive-in site in Sale (27 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Perry Bridge.

Nearby suburbs

Stratford, next to Perry Bridge, has 8 playgrounds, a pool and a skate park. Fernbank and Clydebank also border Perry Bridge.
