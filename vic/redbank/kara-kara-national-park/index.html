Kara Kara National Park

Park in Redbank · Official details

Kara Kara National Park is a park in Redbank, in the Northern Grampians Shire Council area, good for all ages. Dogs are not allowed.

Details

Dogs
not allowed (Parks Victoria)
Toilets
yes (Parks Victoria)
Prices
Camping free (Parks Victoria)
Good to know
No mobile reception in the campground; Park closed on Catastrophic Fire Danger days; Bring own drinking water and firewood (Parks Victoria)

Questions parents ask

Where is Kara Kara National Park?
Kara Kara National Park is in Redbank, Victoria.
Is Kara Kara National Park free?
Yes. It is a public park, free to visit.
Can I bring my dog to Kara Kara National Park?
No, dogs are not allowed.
