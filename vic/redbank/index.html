Redbank in the Northern Grampians area has 3 parks and reserves in our data.

Landmarks

Kara Kara National Park is the largest mapped park in Redbank, at about 13,980 hectares. Redbank Nature Conservation Reserve and Moyreisk Bushland Reserve are the next largest. Our data shows 4 public toilets in Redbank.

Getting around

There's no train station in Redbank. The closest to Redbank is Maryborough Railway Station (V/Line), about 39 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Redbank, so most trips start by car. By car from Redbank, Maryborough is about 42 minutes away, Stawell about 59 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud is about 35 minutes' drive from Redbank. The closest library to Redbank is Maryborough Resource Centre in Maryborough, about 42 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Maryborough, about 42 minutes from Redbank by car, with a library. From Redbank, the nearest places for kids by road are Stuart Mill Recreation Reserve Playground in Stuart Mill (17 min), the playground at Avoca Lions Park in Avoca (20 min), Courthouse in Avoca (20 min), St Arnaud Swimming Pool in St Arnaud (35 min), Dunolly Museum in Dunolly (40 min) and Dunolly Swimming Pool in Dunolly (40 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Redbank.

Nearby suburbs

Barkly, next to Redbank, has 2 playgrounds. Next door to Redbank, Stuart Mill has 1 playground. Moyreisk and Moonambel also border Redbank.
