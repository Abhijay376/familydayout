Landmarks

The biggest green space in Walhalla East is Stony State Forest, covering about 11,399 hectares.

Getting around

Walhalla East has no train station; Traralgon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 27 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Walhalla East, so most trips start by car. By car from Walhalla East, Traralgon is about 2 hours away and Sale about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest places for kids by road from Walhalla East are Fire station in Walhalla (102 min), the playground at Ned Stringer's Park in Walhalla (102 min), Apex Park Playground in Heyfield (101 min), Heyfield and District Museum in Heyfield (102 min), Pump Track in Heyfield (102 min) and Memorial Heyfield Pool in Heyfield (102 min).
