Laceby is in the Wangaratta area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Laceby, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Murray to Mountains Rail Trail: Wangaratta – Bright and Murray to Mountains Rail Trail: Wangaratta – Milawa cross Laceby.

Getting around

There's no train station in Laceby. The closest to Laceby is Wangaratta Railway Station (V/Line), about 5.5 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Laceby, so most trips start by car. By car from Laceby, Wangaratta is about 8 minutes away, Benalla about 28 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Moore than Swimming in Wangaratta is about 4 minutes' drive from Laceby. The closest library to Laceby is Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta, about 8 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Laceby is Sandy Beach in Beechworth, about 34 minutes' drive. For a skate park, the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta is about 6 minutes' drive from Laceby. For a cinema, Wangaratta Cinema Centre in Wangaratta is about 8 minutes' drive from Laceby. Wangaratta is the closest bigger centre to Laceby, about 8 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Laceby are Moore than Swimming in Wangaratta (4 min), Splashpark in Wangaratta (6 min), the pump track on Brash Avenue in Wangaratta (6 min), the playground at Batchelors Green in Wangaratta (6 min), Wangaratta Library in Wangaratta (8 min) and Donald Street Playground in Wangaratta (8 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Laceby on the Murray to Mountains Rail Trail: Wangaratta – Bright.

Nearby suburbs

Wangaratta, next to Laceby, has 9 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Glenrowan, next to Laceby, has 1 playground. Greta West, Greta and Docker also border Laceby.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Laceby, without traffic.
