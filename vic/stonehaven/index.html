Stonehaven in the Golden Plains area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Barwon River Streamside Reserve is the largest mapped park in Stonehaven, at about 4 hectares.

Getting around

Stonehaven has no train station; North Geelong Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 8.1 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Stonehaven, so most trips start by car. By car from Stonehaven, Geelong is about 13 minutes away, Torquay about 26 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 65 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Stonehaven is Geelong Aquatic Centre in Newtown, about 11 minutes' drive. For a library, Western Heights College Library in Hamlyn Heights is about 9 minutes' drive from Stonehaven. For a beach, Eastern Beach in Geelong is about 14 minutes' drive from Stonehaven. For a skate park, the pump track at Western Oval in North Geelong is about 11 minutes' drive from Stonehaven. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Geelong is about 12 minutes' drive from Stonehaven. For an indoor play centre, Laserquest in Geelong is about 13 minutes' drive from Stonehaven. For a zoo or wildlife park, Adventure Park in Wallington is about 29 minutes' drive from Stonehaven. Geelong is the closest bigger centre to Stonehaven, about 13 minutes away by car, and has 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Stonehaven are the playground at Dog Rocks Reserve in Batesford (8 min), Elderslie Reserve in Newtown (7 min), Shearers Arms Gallery in Geelong West (8 min), Western Heights College Library in Hamlyn Heights (9 min), Geelong West Library in Geelong West (10 min) and Geelong Aquatic Centre in Newtown (11 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Stonehaven.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Stonehaven, Bannockburn has 6 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Fyansford, next to Stonehaven, has 3 playgrounds. Next door to Stonehaven, Ceres has 2 playgrounds. Murgheboluc, next to Stonehaven, has 1 playground. Gnarwarre, Gheringhap and Barrabool also border Stonehaven.
