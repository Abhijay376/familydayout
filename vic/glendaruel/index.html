Glendaruel is in the Ballarat area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Glendaruel, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Glendaruel. The closest to Glendaruel is Clunes Railway Station (V/Line), about 7.2 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Glendaruel, so most trips start by car. By car from Glendaruel, Ballarat is about 37 minutes away, Maryborough about 46 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Glendaruel is Clunes Swimming Pool in Clunes, about 20 minutes' drive. The closest library to Glendaruel is Creswick Library in Creswick, about 29 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Glendaruel is the skate park at Alexander Park in Learmonth, about 21 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Regent Cinemas in Ballarat Central is about 37 minutes' drive from Glendaruel. For an indoor play centre, Empire Bowling Saloon in Golden Point is about 41 minutes' drive from Glendaruel. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Glendaruel is Ballarat Wildlife Park in Ballarat East, about 40 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Ballarat, about 37 minutes from Glendaruel by car, with 4 playgrounds, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Glendaruel are the skate park at Alexander Park in Learmonth (21 min), the playground at Learmonth Recreation Reserve in Learmonth (21 min), the playground at Ascot Hall Reserve in Ascot (19 min), Lee Medlyn Bottle Museum in Clunes (19 min), The Warehouse in Clunes (20 min) and Clunes Swimming Pool in Clunes (20 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Glendaruel.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Glendaruel, Clunes has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Coghills Creek also borders Glendaruel.
