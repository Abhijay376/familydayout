Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Irrewillipe in the Colac Otway area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Landmarks

Our data shows 1 public toilet in Irrewillipe.

Getting around

There's no train station in Irrewillipe. The closest to Irrewillipe is Colac Railway Station (V/Line), about 14 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Irrewillipe, so most trips start by car. By car from Irrewillipe, Colac is about 19 minutes away, Geelong about 84 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest library to Irrewillipe is Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac, about 22 minutes' drive. The closest skate park to Irrewillipe is the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt, about 24 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Colac Cinemas in Colac is about 20 minutes' drive from Irrewillipe. For an indoor play centre, Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac is about 20 minutes' drive from Irrewillipe. Colac is the closest bigger centre to Irrewillipe, about 19 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a library, a cinema and indoor play. From Irrewillipe, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Donaldson Street Park in Colac (18 min), the playground at Western Bay Playground in Colac (19 min), Colac Cinemas in Colac (20 min), Colac Tenpin Bowl in Colac (20 min), Colac Community Library and Learning Centre in Colac (22 min) and the pump track on Garden Close in Elliminyt (24 min).
