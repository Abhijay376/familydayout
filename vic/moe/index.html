Our data lists 14 playgrounds and 25 parks and reserves in Moe, in the Latrobe area. Beyond the parks, Moe has 2 pools and a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Moe is Edward Hunter Heritage Bush Reserve, covering about 57 hectares. Ted Summerton Reserve and Olympic Park are the next largest. Moe also has Apex Park, Lions Park and TB Drew Park. Playgrounds in Moe include the playground at Apex Park, the playground at Scott Reserve and the playground at Scorpio Reserve. Our data shows 10 public toilets in Moe. Places to visit in Moe include Diving Pool and Moe Outdoor Pool. For older kids in Moe there's Moe Skate Park. For walks and rides, Moe-Yallourn Rail Trail crosses Moe. Our data lists 11 cafes and family restaurants in Moe for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Trains stop at Moe Railway Station (V/Line: Bairnsdale - Melbourne via Sale & Traralgon and Traralgon - Melbourne via Morwell, Moe & Pakenham) in Moe. Buses on the 1, 5, 8 and 11 routes stop in Moe. V/Line coaches stop in Moe. By car from Moe, Traralgon is about 26 minutes away, Warragul about 28 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a library, Morwell Library in Morwell is about 15 minutes' drive from Moe. For a cinema, Village 8 in Morwell is about 17 minutes' drive from Moe. The closest indoor play centre to Moe is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 27 minutes' drive.

Best for

Toddlers and younger kids, with 14 playgrounds across Moe. Teens who skate or ride, with Moe Skate Park in Moe. Free days on the Moe-Yallourn Rail Trail and in Moe's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Newborough, next to Moe, has 11 playgrounds. Moe South and Hernes Oak also border Moe.

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Moe, without traffic.
