Ted Summerton Reserve

Park in Moe · Basic info

Ted Summerton Reserve is a park in Moe, in the Latrobe City Council area, good for all ages. Bristol St/Wirraway St (bus) is a 5-minute walk away and the nearest public toilet is about 978 m away with a baby change table.
