Lima is in the Benalla area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Lima, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lima. The closest to Lima is Benalla Railway Station (V/Line), about 21 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lima, so most trips start by car. By car from Lima, Benalla is about 29 minutes away, Mansfield about 42 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Lima is Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla, about 28 minutes' drive. For a library, Mansfield Library in Mansfield is about 42 minutes' drive from Lima. The closest skate park to Lima is Pump Track in Benalla, about 30 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Swanpool Cinema in Swanpool is about 13 minutes' drive from Lima. Benalla is the closest bigger centre to Lima, about 29 minutes away by car, and has 5 playgrounds and a pool. The closest places for kids by road from Lima are Swanpool Cinema in Swanpool (13 min), the playground at Waminda Park in Benalla (26 min), the playground at Benalla Botanical Gardens in Benalla (28 min), Benalla Art Gallery in Benalla (27 min), Benalla Costume and Pioneer Museum in Benalla (28 min) and Benalla Aquatic Centre in Benalla (28 min).
