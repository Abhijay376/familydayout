Shady Creek in the Baw Baw area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

Neerim East G210 Bushland Reserve is the largest mapped park in Shady Creek, at about 5 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Shady Creek. The closest to Shady Creek is Yarragon Railway Station (V/Line), about 9.2 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Shady Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Shady Creek, Warragul is about 21 minutes away, Traralgon about 45 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Shady Creek is Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar, about 15 minutes' drive. The closest library to Shady Creek is Warragul Library in Warragul, about 21 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South is about 27 minutes' drive from Shady Creek. The closest cinema to Shady Creek is Warragul Cinema in Warragul, about 20 minutes' drive. For an indoor play centre, The Alley Warragul in Warragul is about 20 minutes' drive from Shady Creek. The nearest larger centre is Warragul, about 21 minutes from Shady Creek by car, with 22 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Shady Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are Yarragon Childrens Playground Reserve in Yarragon (10 min), Station Gallery in Yarragon (9 min), Conventry Drive Playground in Warragul (21 min), Trafalgar Swimming Pool in Trafalgar (15 min), Craig Watts Skate Park in Neerim South (27 min) and Warragul Swimming Pool in Warragul (19 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Shady Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Trafalgar, next to Shady Creek, has 4 playgrounds and a pool. Yarragon, next to Shady Creek, has 2 playgrounds. Willow Grove, next to Shady Creek, has 2 playgrounds. Darnum, next to Shady Creek, has 2 playgrounds. Nilma North, Neerim East and Buln Buln East also border Shady Creek.
