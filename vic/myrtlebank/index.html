Myrtlebank is in the Wellington area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Myrtlebank, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Myrtlebank has no train station; Sale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 4.7 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Myrtlebank, so most trips start by car. By car from Myrtlebank, Sale is about 6 minutes away, Traralgon about 42 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 3 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Indoor pool in Sale is about 8 minutes' drive from Myrtlebank. The closest library to Myrtlebank is Sale Library in Sale, about 7 minutes' drive. For a beach, 90 Mile Beach in Seaspray is about 44 minutes' drive from Myrtlebank. The closest skate park to Myrtlebank is Sale Skate Park in Sale, about 8 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Former Drive-in site in Sale is about 6 minutes' drive from Myrtlebank. Sale is the closest bigger centre to Myrtlebank, about 6 minutes away by car, and has 14 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Myrtlebank are Boulevard Playground in Sale (3 min), Apex Park Playground in Sale (6 min), Former Drive-in site in Sale (6 min), Sale Historical Museum in Sale (7 min), Old train signals in Sale (7 min) and Sale Library in Sale (7 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Myrtlebank.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Myrtlebank, Sale has 14 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Next door to Myrtlebank, Wurruk has 1 playground. Cobains and Bundalaguah also border Myrtlebank.
