Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Boorool in the South Gippsland area, so this guide covers the closest places for kids in the suburbs around it.

Getting around

Boorool has no train station; Yarragon Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Boorool, so most trips start by car. By car from Boorool, Leongatha is about 11 minutes away, Wonthaggi about 42 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha is about 9 minutes' drive from Boorool. For a library, Leongatha Library in Leongatha is about 10 minutes' drive from Boorool. For a beach, The Caves in Inverloch is about 37 minutes' drive from Boorool. The closest skate park to Boorool is BMX track in Leongatha, about 8 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha is about 10 minutes' drive from Boorool. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Boorool, about 11 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Boorool, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Mossvale Park in Berrys Creek (6 min), BMX track in Leongatha (8 min), Leongatha Skate Park in Leongatha (8 min), South Gippsland Splash in Leongatha (9 min), Splash Pools and Stadium in Leongatha (10 min) and Leongatha Library in Leongatha (10 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Boorool.

Nearby suburbs
