Tyenna is in the Buloke area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Tyenna, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Getting around

Tyenna has no train station; Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 90 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Tyenna, so most trips start by car. By car from Tyenna, Swan Hill is about 1.5 hours away and Mildura about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Tyenna is Tempy Outdoor Swimming Pool in Tempy, about 22 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Roxy in Ouyen is about 44 minutes' drive from Tyenna. From Tyenna, the nearest places for kids by road are Tempy Outdoor Swimming Pool in Tempy (22 min), the playground at Tempy Recreation Reserve in Tempy (22 min), the playground at Patchewollock Rec Reserve in Patchewollock (44 min), Ouyen Swimming Pool in Ouyen (44 min) and Roxy in Ouyen (44 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Tyenna.
