Woolsthorpe is in the Moyne area, and our data lists 2 parks and reserves in the suburb.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Woolsthorpe is Woolsthorpe Nature Conservation Reserve, covering about 32 hectares. Woolsthorpe Cemetery Reserve is the next largest. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Woolsthorpe.

Getting around

Woolsthorpe has no train station; Warrnambool Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 23 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. V/Line coaches stop in Woolsthorpe. By car from Woolsthorpe, Warrnambool is about 25 minutes away and Hamilton about 60 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Hawkesdale Swimming Pool in Hawkesdale is about 13 minutes' drive from Woolsthorpe. For a library, Warrnambool Library in Warrnambool is about 26 minutes' drive from Woolsthorpe. The closest beach to Woolsthorpe is Killarney Beach in Killarney, about 28 minutes' drive. For a skate park, Koroit Youth Space in Koroit is about 13 minutes' drive from Woolsthorpe. For a cinema, Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool is about 25 minutes' drive from Woolsthorpe. For an indoor play centre, The Bowl in Warrnambool is about 26 minutes' drive from Woolsthorpe. For a zoo or wildlife park, Cudgee Wildlife Park in Cudgee is about 33 minutes' drive from Woolsthorpe. Warrnambool is the closest bigger centre to Woolsthorpe, about 25 minutes away by car, and has 45 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. The closest places for kids by road from Woolsthorpe are Koroit Youth Space in Koroit (13 min), Hawkesdale Swimming Pool in Hawkesdale (13 min), the playground at Kirkstall Recreation Reserve in Kirkstall (18 min), the playground at Bushfield Recreation Reserve in Bushfield (21 min), Killarney Beach in Killarney (28 min) and Capitol Cinema in Warrnambool (25 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Woolsthorpe.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Woolsthorpe, Hawkesdale has a pool. Koroit, next to Woolsthorpe, has 1 playground and a skate park. Minjah, Hexham, Grassmere and Ellerslie also border Woolsthorpe.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Woolsthorpe, without traffic.
