Lake Tyrrell is in the Buloke area, and our data lists 1 park or reserve in the suburb.

Landmarks

Lake Tyrrell Wildlife Reserve (hunting) is the largest mapped park in Lake Tyrrell, at about 12,752 hectares.

Getting around

There's no train station in Lake Tyrrell. The closest to Lake Tyrrell is Swan Hill Railway Station (V/Line), about 70 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Lake Tyrrell, so most trips start by car. By car from Lake Tyrrell, Swan Hill is about 79 minutes away and Mildura about 2 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake is about 22 minutes' drive from Lake Tyrrell. From Lake Tyrrell, the nearest places for kids by road are Sea Lake Swimming Pool in Sea Lake (22 min), the playground at Apex Park in Sea Lake (22 min), the playground at Tempy Recreation Reserve in Tempy (37 min) and Tempy Outdoor Swimming Pool in Tempy (37 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Lake Tyrrell.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Lake Tyrrell, Sea Lake has 2 playgrounds and a pool. Gerahmin, Chinkapook and Bimbourie also border Lake Tyrrell.
