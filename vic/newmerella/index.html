Newmerella in the East Gippsland area has 1 park or reserve in our data.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Newmerella is Newmerella Recreation Reserve, covering about 41 hectares. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Newmerella. For walks and rides, East Gippsland Rail Trail crosses Newmerella.

Getting around

There's no train station in Newmerella. The closest to Newmerella is Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line), about 72 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Newmerella. By car from Newmerella, Lakes Entrance is about 45 minutes away and Bairnsdale about 72 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost is about 5 minutes' drive from Newmerella. The closest library to Newmerella is Orbost Library in Orbost, about 5 minutes' drive. The closest beach to Newmerella is Ewings Marsh in Waygara, about 17 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Lakes Entrance, about 45 minutes from Newmerella by car, with 3 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a beach. From Newmerella, the nearest places for kids by road are the playground at Forest Park in Orbost (5 min), Orbost Library in Orbost (5 min), Orbost Outdoor Pool in Orbost (5 min), Ewings Marsh in Waygara (17 min) and Sailors Grave Beach in Cape Conran (33 min).

Best for

Free days on the East Gippsland Rail Trail and in Newmerella's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Orbost, next to Newmerella, has 2 playgrounds, a pool and a library. Marlo, next to Newmerella, has 1 playground. Corringle and Bete Bolong also border Newmerella.
