Antwerp is in the Hindmarsh area. Our data has no playgrounds or parks inside Antwerp, so this guide is mostly about the closest places for kids.

Landmarks

For walks and rides, Wimmera River Discovery Trail crosses Antwerp.

Getting around

Antwerp has no train station; Ararat Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 136 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses on the Horsham - Hopetoun via Dimboola route stop in Antwerp. By car from Antwerp, Horsham is about 42 minutes away and Stawell about 1.5 hours, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Dimboola Swimming Pool in Dimboola is about 15 minutes' drive from Antwerp. The closest library to Antwerp is Dimboola Library in Dimboola, about 14 minutes' drive. Horsham is the closest bigger centre to Antwerp, about 42 minutes away by car, and has 13 playgrounds, a pool and a cinema. From Antwerp, the nearest places for kids by road are Wimmera-Mallee Pioneer Museum in Jeparit (14 min), Dimboola Library in Dimboola (14 min), Dimboola Swimming Pool in Dimboola (15 min), Fitness Course in Jeparit (16 min), the playground at Wimmera River Heritage River in Jeparit (16 min) and Historical Centre in Warracknabeal (31 min).

Best for

Longer rides and walks from Antwerp on the Wimmera River Discovery Trail.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Antwerp, Dimboola has 1 playground, a pool and a library. Next door to Antwerp, Jeparit has 3 playgrounds. Gerang Gerung also borders Antwerp.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Antwerp, without traffic.
