Our data lists 8 parks and reserves in Deddick Valley, in the East Gippsland area.

Landmarks

Bowen Wilderness Zone is the largest mapped park in Deddick Valley, at about 17,636 hectares. Upper Snowy Area Remote and Natural Area and Tingaringy Wilderness Zone are the next largest. Other parks and reserves in Deddick Valley include Beehive Creek Reference Area, Gelantipy Plateau Reference Area and Wallaby Creek Natural Catchment Area. Our data shows 3 public toilets in Deddick Valley.

Getting around

Deddick Valley has no train station; Bairnsdale Railway Station (V/Line) is the closest, about 112 kilometres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. No bus or tram routes stop inside Deddick Valley, so most trips start by car. By car from Deddick Valley, Lakes Entrance is about 3 hours away and Bairnsdale about 3 hours, without traffic.

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Deddick Valley.

Nearby suburbs

Next door to Deddick Valley, Wulgulmerang East has 1 playground. Suggan Buggan, Gelantipy and Bonang also border Deddick Valley.
