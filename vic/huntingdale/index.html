Huntingdale is in the Monash area, and our data lists 1 playground in the suburb.

Landmarks

Our data shows 2 public toilets in Huntingdale. The Djerring Trail and North Road Trail all pass through Huntingdale. Our data lists 1 cafe or family restaurant in Huntingdale for a lunch stop.

Getting around

Huntingdale has no train station; Huntingdale Railway Station (metro) is the closest, about 200 metres from the suburb centre as the crow flies. Buses 630, 704, 800, 802, 804, 862 and 900 stop in Huntingdale. By car from Huntingdale, Box Hill is about 14 minutes away, Dandenong about 15 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 19 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Oakleigh Recreation Centre in Oakleigh is about 4 minutes' drive from Huntingdale. The closest library to Huntingdale is Manga Library in Clayton, about 5 minutes' drive. For a beach, Sandown Street Beach in Brighton is about 15 minutes' drive from Huntingdale. The closest skate park to Huntingdale is the skate park at Clayton Community Space West in Clayton, about 4 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Clayton is about 5 minutes' drive from Huntingdale. The closest indoor play centre to Huntingdale is Zone Bowling in Clayton, about 5 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Kami Neko no Uchi in Hughesdale is about 5 minutes' drive from Huntingdale. The nearest larger centre is Box Hill, about 14 minutes from Huntingdale by car, with 11 playgrounds, a pool and a library. The closest places for kids by road from Huntingdale are the playground at Jack Edwards Reserve in Oakleigh (2 min), the playground at Princes Highway Reserve in Oakleigh East (2 min), Monash Federation Centre in Oakleigh (4 min), Oakleigh Recreation Centre in Oakleigh (4 min), Clayton Aquatics & Health Club in Clayton (5 min) and Manga Library in Clayton (5 min).

Best for

Free days on the Djerring Trail and in Huntingdale's parks.
