Landmarks

Playgrounds in Sutherlands Creek include Sutherlands Creek Tennis Reserve Playground.

Getting around

There's no train station in Sutherlands Creek. The closest to Sutherlands Creek is North Shore Railway Station (V/Line), about 15 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. No bus or tram routes stop inside Sutherlands Creek, so most trips start by car. By car from Sutherlands Creek, Geelong is about 26 minutes away, Torquay about 43 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 70 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

The closest pool or water play area to Sutherlands Creek is Goodfellow Aquatic Centre in Bell Post Hill, about 17 minutes' drive. The closest library to Sutherlands Creek is Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn, about 17 minutes' drive. For a beach, Limeburners Lagoon Beach in Corio is about 23 minutes' drive from Sutherlands Creek. For a skate park, Corio Bowls in Norlane is about 16 minutes' drive from Sutherlands Creek. For a cinema, Village Cinemas in Geelong is about 25 minutes' drive from Sutherlands Creek. For an indoor play centre, General Public in Corio is about 18 minutes' drive from Sutherlands Creek. The closest zoo or wildlife park to Sutherlands Creek is Fairy Park in Anakie, about 27 minutes' drive. The nearest larger centre is Geelong, about 26 minutes from Sutherlands Creek by car, with 9 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema. From Sutherlands Creek, the nearest places for kids by road are Maude Tennis Reserve Playground in Maude (11 min), Bannockburn Library in Bannockburn (17 min), Corio Bowls in Norlane (16 min), Goodfellow Aquatic Centre in Bell Post Hill (17 min), John Drysdale Library in Norlane (17 min) and Bannockburn Skate Park in Bannockburn (18 min).

Best for

Families happy to drive a little for a day out from Sutherlands Creek.

Nearby suburbs

Bannockburn, next to Sutherlands Creek, has 6 playgrounds, a library and a skate park. Next door to Sutherlands Creek, Maude has 2 playgrounds. Anakie, next to Sutherlands Creek, has 1 playground and a zoo or wildlife park. Moorabool and Gheringhap also border Sutherlands Creek.
