Playground on Grundy Avenue

Playground in Nyora · Basic info

Playground on Grundy Avenue in Nyora is a public playground in the South Gippsland Shire area. The Great Southern Rail Trail is about 210 m away, and Playground at Nyora Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve is another option about 560 m away. You'll find a public toilet about 670 m from the playground, and coaches stop at Mitchell St/Davis St, a 5-minute walk away.
