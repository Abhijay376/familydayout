Nyora is in the South Gippsland area, and our data lists 3 playgrounds and 2 parks and reserves in the suburb. Beyond the parks, Nyora has a skate park.

Landmarks

The biggest green space in Nyora is Wuchatsch Reserve, covering about 15 hectares. Nyora Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve is the next largest. Playgrounds in Nyora include the playground at Nyora Railway Station Historic and Cultural Features Reserve. Our data shows 1 public toilet in Nyora. For older kids in Nyora there's Nyora skate park. The Great Southern Rail Trail, Gippsland Odyssey Extension - Woolamai to Nyora (Proposed) and Fern Gully Track all pass through Nyora.

Getting around

There's no train station in Nyora. The closest to Nyora is Bunyip Railway Station (V/Line), about 26 kilometres from the suburb centre in a straight line. V/Line coaches stop in Nyora. By car from Nyora, Leongatha is about 31 minutes away, Pakenham about 36 minutes and the Melbourne CBD about 80 minutes, without traffic.

Nearby places for kids

For a pool or water play area, Poowong Swimming Pool in Poowong is about 9 minutes' drive from Nyora. For a library, Poowong library in Poowong is about 9 minutes' drive from Nyora. The closest beach to Nyora is Corinella Beach in Corinella, about 37 minutes' drive. For a cinema, Stadium 4 Cinema in Leongatha is about 30 minutes' drive from Nyora. The closest indoor play centre to Nyora is The Alley Warragul in Warragul, about 38 minutes' drive. For a zoo or wildlife park, Maru Koala & Animal Park in Grantville is about 25 minutes' drive from Nyora. Leongatha is the closest bigger centre to Nyora, about 31 minutes away by car, and has 4 playgrounds, a pool, a library and a cinema.

Best for

Younger kids at Nyora's 3 local playgrounds. Teens who skate or ride, with Nyora skate park in Nyora. Free days on the Great Southern Rail Trail and in Nyora's parks.

Nearby suburbs

Poowong, next to Nyora, has a pool and a library. Next door to Nyora, Lang Lang has 4 playgrounds. Next door to Nyora, Loch has 1 playground. Lang Lang East and Heath Hill also border Nyora.

Getting there

Distances on this page are by road from the centre of Nyora, without traffic.
