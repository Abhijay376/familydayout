Privacy

Family Day Out uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages are useful. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects details such as the pages you view, your approximate location, your device and browser. It does not give us your name or contact details. You can block it with your browser settings or the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

The day planner keeps your kids' ages, reviews and ideas in your own browser only. They are not sent to us.

If you use a business form, we keep the details you send (business name, contact name, email, phone and suburbs) to set up your listing, and we do not sell them.

Questions about privacy: use the contact page.
