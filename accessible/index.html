Access details on every place page

Every park, playground and venue page now has an Access box: wheelchair access where it is recorded, the nearest toilet and whether it is accessible, and the nearest Changing Places within 5 km. Where we don't know, we say so.

Questions families ask

What is a Changing Places toilet?
A Changing Places toilet is a larger accessible toilet with a ceiling hoist, an adult-sized height-adjustable change table, a peninsula toilet and room for two carers. They are for people who cannot use a standard accessible toilet.
What is an MLAK key?
MLAK (Master Locksmiths Access Key) opens many accessible and Changing Places toilets in Australia. People with a disability and their carers can buy one from Master Locksmiths. Toilets that need one are marked MLAK key.
Where does this information come from?
Toilets come from the National Public Toilet Map. Wheelchair access comes from venue, council and Parks Victoria pages, government open data and OpenStreetMap. We show the source on every place page.
Can I suggest a place or fix a detail?
Yes. Use the Report a problem link on any page and tell us what is wrong or missing. We check it and update the page.
